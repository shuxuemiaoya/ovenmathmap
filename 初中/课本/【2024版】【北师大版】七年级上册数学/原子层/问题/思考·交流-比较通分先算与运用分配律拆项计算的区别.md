> [!question] 思考·交流
> 下面是计算 $\left(\frac{1}{3} +\frac{1}{4} -\frac{1}{6}\right)\times 24$ 的两种解法。
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{r l} \text {解法一:} & (\frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{4} - \frac {1}{6}) \times 24 \\ & = (\frac {4}{12} + \frac {3}{12} - \frac {2}{12}) \times 24 \\ & = \frac {5}{12} \times 24 \\ & = 10 _ {\circ} \end{array}
> $$
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{r l} \text {解法二:} & (\frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{4} - \frac {1}{6}) \times 24 \\ & = \frac {1}{3} \times 24 + \frac {1}{4} \times 24 - \frac {1}{6} \times 24 \\ & = 8 + 6 - 4 \\ & = 10 _ {\circ} \end{array}
> $$
>
> 比较两种解法，说说它们的区别，并与同伴进行交流。
