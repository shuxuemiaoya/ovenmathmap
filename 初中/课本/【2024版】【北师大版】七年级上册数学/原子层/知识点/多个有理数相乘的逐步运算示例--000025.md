计算：

(1) $(-4)\times 5\times (-0.25);$

(2) $\left(-\frac{3}{5}\right)\times\left(-\frac{5}{6}\right)\times(-2)$ 。

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & 1) (- 4) \times 5 \times (- 0.25) \\ & = [ - (4 \times 5) ] \times (- 0.25) \\ & = (- 20) \times (- 0.25) \\ & = + (20 \times 0.25) \\ & = 5; \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} (2) & (- \frac {3}{5}) \times (- \frac {5}{6}) \times (- 2) \\ & = [ + (\frac {3}{5} \times \frac {5}{6}) ] \times (- 2) \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \times (- 2) \\ & = - (\frac {1}{2} \times 2) \\ & = - 1 _ {\circ} \end{array}
$$
