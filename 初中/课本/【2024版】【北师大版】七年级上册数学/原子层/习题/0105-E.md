(3) $100 \times (-3) \times (-5) \times 0.01$ ;

(5) $(\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{8}) \times 128;$

(7) $2.25 \times (-2.3) \times \frac{3}{25}$ ;
