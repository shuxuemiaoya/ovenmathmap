7. 请在下列括号里填写运算的依据：

$$
{ \begin{array}{r l} & {10 \times (- {\frac {3}{7}}) \times ({\frac {5}{2}} - {\frac {6}{5}} + {\frac {1}{10}})} \\ & {= (- {\frac {3}{7}}) \times 10 \times ({\frac {5}{2}} - {\frac {6}{5}} + {\frac {1}{10}})} \\ & {= (- {\frac {3}{7}}) \times [ 10 \times ({\frac {5}{2}} - {\frac {6}{5}} + {\frac {1}{10}}) ]} \\ & {= (- {\frac {3}{7}}) \times [ 10 \times {\frac {5}{2}} + 10 \times (- {\frac {6}{5}}) + 10 \times {\frac {1}{10}} ]} \\ & {= (- {\frac {3}{7}}) \times (25 - 12 + 1)} \\ & {= (- {\frac {3}{7}}) \times 14} \\ & {= - 6 _ {\circ}} \end{array} }
$$
