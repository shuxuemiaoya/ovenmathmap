例5 计算:

$$

(1) (- 18) \div (- \frac {2}{3});
$$

$$

(2) 16 \div (- \frac {5}{3}) \div (- \frac {16}{9}) _ {\circ}
$$

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $(-18)\div\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)=(-18)\times\left(-\frac{3}{2}\right)=18\times\frac{3}{2}=27;$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (2) 16 \div (- \frac {5}{3}) \div (- \frac {16}{9}) & = 16 \times (- \frac {3}{5}) \times (- \frac {9}{16}) \\ & = 16 \times (- \frac {9}{16}) \times (- \frac {3}{5}) \\ & = \frac {27}{5}. \end{array}
> > $$
