例3 计算：

(1) $\left(-\frac{5}{6}+\frac{3}{8}\right)\times(-24);$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \text {
解: (1)} & (- \frac {5}{6} + \frac {3}{8}) \times (- 24) \\ & = (- \frac {5}{6}) \times (- 24) + \frac {3}{8} \times (- 24) \\ & = 20 + (- 9) \\ & = 11; \end{array}
$$

(2) $(-7)\times \left(-\frac{4}{3}\right)\times \frac{5}{14}$ 。

$$
\begin{array}{r l} (2) (- 7) \times \left(- \frac {4}{3}\right) \times \frac {5}{14} & = (- 7) \times \frac {5}{14} \times \left(- \frac {4}{3}\right) \\ & = \left(- \frac {5}{2}\right) \times \left(- \frac {4}{3}\right) \\ & = \frac {10}{3} 。 \end{array} \tag {2}
$$
