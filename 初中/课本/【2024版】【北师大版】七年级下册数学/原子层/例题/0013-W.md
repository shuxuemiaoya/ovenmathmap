例4 计算：

(1) $a^2 (a + b)(a - b) + a^2 b^2;$

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $a^2 (a + b)(a - b) + a^2 b^2$ $= a^{2}(a^{2} - b^{2}) + a^{2}b^{2}$ $= a^{4} - a^{2}b^{2} + a^{2}b^{2}$ $= a^{4};$
> >
> > (2) $(2x - 5)(2x + 5) - 2x(2x - 3)$ 。
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (2) (2 x - 5) (2 x + 5) - 2 x (2 x - 3) & = (2 x) ^ {2} - 5 ^ {2} - (4 x ^ {2} - 6 x) \\ & = 4 x ^ {2} - 25 - 4 x ^ {2} + 6 x \\ & = 6 x - 25 _ {\circ} \end{array}
> > $$
