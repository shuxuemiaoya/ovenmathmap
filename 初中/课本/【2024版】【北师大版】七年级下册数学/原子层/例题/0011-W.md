(1) $\left(-\frac{1}{4}x-y\right)\left(-\frac{1}{4}x+y\right);$

(2) $(ab+8)(ab-8)$ 。

>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $\left(-\frac{1}{4}x-y\right)\left(-\frac{1}{4}x+y\right)=\left(-\frac{1}{4}x\right)^{2}-y^{2}=\frac{1}{16}x^{2}-y^{2};$
> >
> > $$
> > (a b + 8) (a b - 8) = (a b) ^ {2} - 8 ^ {2} = a ^ {2} b ^ {2} - 64 。
> > $$
