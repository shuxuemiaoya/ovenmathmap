例3 计算：

(1) $(1 - x)(0.6 - x)$ ;

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $(1 - x)(0.6 - x)$ $= 1\times 0.6 - 1\cdot x - x\cdot 0.6 + x\cdot x$ $= 0.6 - x - 0.6x + x^2$ $= 0.6 - 1.6x + x^{2};$
> >
> > (2) $(2x + y)(x - y)$ 。
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (2) (2 x + y) (x - y) & = 2 x \cdot x - 2 x \cdot y + y \cdot x - y \cdot y \\ & = 2 x ^ {2} - 2 x y + x y - y ^ {2} \\ & = 2 x ^ {2} - x y - y ^ {2}. \end{array} \tag {2}
> > $$
