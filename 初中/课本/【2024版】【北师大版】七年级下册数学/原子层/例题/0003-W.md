例3 计算：

(1) $(10^{2})^{3}$ ;

(2) $(b^{5})^{5}$ ;

(3) $(a^{n})^{3}$ ;

(4) $-(x^{2})^{m}$ ;

(5) $(y^{2})^{3}\cdot y$ ;

(6) $2(a^{2})^{6}-(a^{3})^{4}$ 。

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $(10^{2})^{3}=10^{2\times3}=10^{6};$
> > (2) $(b^{5})^{5}=b^{5\times5}=b^{25};$
> > (3) $(a^{n})^{3}=a^{n\cdot3}=a^{3n};$
> > (4) $-(x^{2})^{m}=-x^{2\cdot m}=-x^{2m};$
> >
> > (5) $(y^{2})^{3} \cdot y = y^{2 \times 3} \cdot y = y^{6} \cdot y = y^{7}$ ;
> >
> > (6) $2(a^{2})^{6} - (a^{3})^{4} = 2a^{2\times 6} - a^{3\times 4} = 2a^{12} - a^{12} = a^{12}$
