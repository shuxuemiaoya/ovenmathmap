例1 计算：

(1) $(-3)^{7}\times(-3)^{6};$

(2) $\left(\frac{1}{111}\right)^{3}\times\frac{1}{111};$

(3) $-x^{3}\cdot x^{5};$

(4) $b^{2m}\cdot b^{2m+1}$

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $(-3)^{7}\times(-3)^{6}=(-3)^{7+6}=(-3)^{13}$ ;
> >
> > (2) $(\frac{1}{111})^3 \times \frac{1}{111} = (\frac{1}{111})^{3 + 1} = (\frac{1}{111})^4;$
> >
> > (3) $-x^{3} \cdot x^{5} = -x^{3+5} = -x^{8}$ ;
> >
> > (4) $b^{2m}\cdot b^{2m+1}=b^{2m+2m+1}=b^{4m+1}$
