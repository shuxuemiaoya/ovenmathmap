例6 计算：

(1) $(x + 3)^{2} - x^{2}$ ;

(3) $(x + 5)^{2} - (x - 2)(x - 3)$ ;

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $(x+3)^{2}-x^{2}$ $=x^{2}+6x+9-x^{2}$ $=6x+9;$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (3) (x + 5) ^ {2} - (x - 2) (x - 3) & = x ^ {2} + 10 x + 25 - (x ^ {2} - 5 x + 6) \\ & = x ^ {2} + 10 x + 25 - x ^ {2} + 5 x - 6 \\ & = 15 x + 19; \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $(a + b + 3)(a + b - 3)$ ;
> >
> > (4) $\left[(a + b)(a - b)\right]^2$ 。
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (2) (a + b + 3) (a + b - 3) & = [ (a + b) + 3 ] [ (a + b) - 3 ] \\ & = (a + b) ^ {2} - 3 ^ {2} \\ & = a ^ {2} + 2 a b + b ^ {2} - 9; \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (4) & [ (a + b) (a - b) ] ^ {2} \\ & = (a ^ {2} - b ^ {2}) ^ {2} \\ & = a ^ {4} - 2 a ^ {2} b ^ {2} + b ^ {4} \text {。} \end{array}
> > $$
