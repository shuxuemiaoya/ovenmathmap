6. 按照下面的步骤，你会折出一朵漂亮的纸花。动手做一做。

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_f2d2e511fb3d1d794b354e3d051ec1bb0d2c77d02bb58de9dd56c440c4bcca64.jpg)

(1) 将正方形对折;

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_087269eef8a24c773e3277df3dcab568eaeeeeaa4dd055b2280d89505f739f18.jpg)

(2) 再对折;

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_e42e410f3e63108c92b1b7de69a73d54d04e6ffe5950e88c0732990e6d7a7930.jpg)

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_41c44ca826329252f95a5e7f4670db71de2de028e5cc6ef4e80c30dd5f64088e.jpg)

(3) 把得到的两个等腰直角三角形分别折成正方形；

(4) 将正方形的边隆起, 折成一个等腰三角形;

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_c9c5d4eff6c7b340d6276c5c41028f4065d78f69dfd7986984b12659ccc2f8d3.jpg)

(5) 其他三边也重复同样的步骤；

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_34f1794ddb67d6ee712495fffeed57930a90c5fdb672dc29c8a6ee9aa2bca88b.jpg)

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_5a9187807ece627f3905739cc01cff81a83a11d9569dd3c50c4d907abaf10bd9.jpg)

(7) 折成直角；

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_91c48c49d95e348f5bf472049cdd954688c5c71a1dd9ea35a0b87ee71c03ad33.jpg)

(8) 将尾部向内折;

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_18d43a4933b2c13e6e90890d9fa056cc8efb77729bca169fe997edd70e2bb424.jpg)

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_ebb07a976192a52b3e848f2b2b487d983c5f33ab87e6997cfa5897ae8a8bd077.jpg)

(9) 打开;

![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_4fc2e8071e673a413cb2d7e0360ba87afa9303df3c36f963c584f1d2284f6d45.jpg)

(10) 纸花做成了。

将纸花铺平，观察折痕中是否有线段的垂直平分线和角的平分线。分别有几条呢？
