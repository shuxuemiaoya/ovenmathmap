怎样计算 $102^{2}$ , $197^{2}$ 更简单呢?

$$
\begin{array}{r l} 102 ^ {2} & = (100 + 2) ^ {2} \\ & = 100 ^ {2} + 2 \times 100 \times 2 + 2 ^ {2} \\ & = 10000 + 400 + 4 \\ & = 10404; \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} 197 ^ {2} & = (200 - 3) ^ {2} \\ & = 200 ^ {2} - 2 \times 200 \times 3 + 3 ^ {2} \\ & = 40000 - 1200 + 9 \\ & = 38809 _ {\circ} \end{array}
$$
