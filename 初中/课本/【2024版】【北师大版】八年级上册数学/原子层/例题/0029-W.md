例1 解方程组:

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3 x + 2 y = 14, \\ x = y + 3. \end{array} \right.\tag{①}
$$

②

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：将②代入①, 得 $3(y + 3) + 2y = 14$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > 3 y + 9 + 2 y = 14,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > 5 y = 5,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y = 1 _ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > 将 $y = 1$ 代入②，得
> >
> > $$
> > x = 4 _ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以原方程组的解是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 4, \\ y = 1. \end{array} \right.$
