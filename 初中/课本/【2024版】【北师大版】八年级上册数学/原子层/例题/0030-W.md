例2 解方程组:

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 x + 3 y = 16, \\ x + 4 y = 13 _ {\circ} \end{array} \right.\tag{①}
$$

②

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：由②, 得
> >
> > $$
> > x = 13 - 4 y _ {\circ}\tag{③}
> > $$
> >
> > 将③代入①，得 $2(13 - 4y) + 3y = 16$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} 26 - 8 y + 3 y & = 16, \\ - 5 y & = - 10, \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y = 2 _ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > 将 $y = 2$ 代入③，得
> >
> > $$
> > x = 5 _ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以原方程组的解是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 5, \\ y = 2. \end{array} \right.$
