1. 用代入消元法解下列方程组：

(1) $\left\{\begin{aligned}x-3y&=2,\\ y&=x;\end{aligned}\right.$

(2) $\left\{\begin{aligned}x+y&=5,\\ 2x+y&=8;\end{aligned}\right.$

(3) $\left\{\begin{aligned}4x+3y&=5,\\ x-2y&=4;\end{aligned}\right.$

(4) $\left\{\begin{aligned}m-\frac{n}{2}&=2,\\ 2m+3n&=12.\end{aligned}\right.$
