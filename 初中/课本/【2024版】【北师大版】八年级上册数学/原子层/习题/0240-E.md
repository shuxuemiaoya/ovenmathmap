1. 用加减消元法解下列方程组：

(1) $\left\{\begin{aligned}7x-2y&=3,\\ 9x+2y&=-19;\end{aligned}\right.$

(2) $\left\{\begin{aligned}6x-5y&=3,\\ 6x+y&=-15;\end{aligned}\right.$

(3) $\left\{\begin{aligned}4s+3t&=5,\\ 2s-t&=-5;\end{aligned}\right.$

(4) $\left\{\begin{aligned}5x-6y&=9,\\ 7x-4y&=-5.\end{aligned}\right.$
