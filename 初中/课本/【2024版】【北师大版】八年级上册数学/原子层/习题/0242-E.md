2. 用加减消元法解下列方程组：

(1) $\left\{\begin{aligned}&4x-3y=14,\\ &5x+3y=31;\end{aligned}\right.$

(2) $\left\{\begin{aligned}&2x-5y=-21,\\ &4x+3y=23;\end{aligned}\right.$

(3) $\left\{\begin{aligned}&4x+7y=-19,\\ &4x-5y=17;\end{aligned}\right.$

(4) $\left\{\begin{aligned}&3(x-1)=y+5,\\ &5(y-1)=3(x+5)\end{aligned}\right.$
