1. 用代入消元法解下列方程组：

(1) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = 2x, \\ x + y = 12; \end{array} \right.$

$$

(2) \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \frac {y - 5}{2}, \\ 4 x + 3 y = 65; \end{array} \right.
$$

$$

(3) \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + y = 11, \\ x - y = 7; \end{array} \right.
$$

$$

(4) \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3 x - 2 y = 9, \\ x + 2 y = 3 _ {\circ} \end{array} \right.
$$
