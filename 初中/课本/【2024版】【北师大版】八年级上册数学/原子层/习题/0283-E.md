1. 解下列方程组:

$$

(1) \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 x - y = 5, \\ 7 x - 3 y = 20; \end{array} \right.
$$

(2) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 4x - y = 1, \\ y = 2x + 3; \end{array} \right.$

$$

(3) \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 5 x + y = 2, \\ x - 3 y = 4; \end{array} \right.
$$

(4) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3x - 2y = 1, \\ 2x + 3y = -7; \end{array} \right.$ $^*(5)\left\{ \begin{array}{l} z = x + y, \\ 2x - 3y + 2z = 5, \\ x + 2y - z = 3. \end{array} \right.$
