二次根式的乘法法则和除法法则

$$
\sqrt {a} \cdot \sqrt {b} = \sqrt {a b} (a \geqslant 0, b \geqslant 0), \frac {\sqrt {a}}{\sqrt {b}} = \sqrt {\frac {a}{b}} (a \geqslant 0, b > 0) _ {\circ}
$$
