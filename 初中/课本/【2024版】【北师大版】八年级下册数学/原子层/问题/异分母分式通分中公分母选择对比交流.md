> [!question] 观察·交流
> 在计算 $\frac{3}{a} + \frac{1}{4a}$ 时，小颖和小亮的做法如下。
>
> (1) 你明白他们是怎么做的吗?
>
> (2) 你对这两种做法有何评论？与同伴进行交流。
>
> ![](../../%E8%B5%84%E6%BA%90/images/part001_095a470479a556e22f5efc8b55c2f79bf1a89f3c939d195179fef524babf94f1.jpg)
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{r l} \frac {3}{a} + \frac {1}{4 a} & = \frac {3 \times 4 a}{a \cdot 4 a} + \frac {a}{4 a \cdot a} \\ & = \frac {12 a}{4 a ^ {2}} + \frac {a}{4 a ^ {2}} = \frac {13 a}{4 a ^ {2}} = \frac {13}{4 a}. \end{array}
> $$
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{r l} \frac {3}{a} + \frac {1}{4 a} & = \frac {3 \times 4}{a \cdot 4} + \frac {1}{4 a} \\ & = \frac {12}{4 a} + \frac {1}{4 a} = \frac {13}{4 a}. \end{array}
> $$
