例5 计算：

(1) $\frac{3}{a} + \frac{a - 15}{5a}$ ;

(2) $\frac{1}{x - 3} - \frac{1}{x + 3}$ ;

(3) $\frac{2a}{a^2 - 4} - \frac{1}{a - 2}$ 。

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1） $\frac{3}{a} +\frac{a - 15}{5a} = \frac{15}{5a} +\frac{a - 15}{5a} = \frac{15 + a - 15}{5a} = \frac{a}{5a} = \frac{1}{5};$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \frac {1}{x - 3} - \frac {1}{x + 3} & = \frac {x + 3}{(x - 3) (x + 3)} - \frac {x - 3}{(x - 3) (x + 3)} \\ & = \frac {x + 3 - (x - 3)}{(x - 3) (x + 3)} = \frac {6}{(x - 3) (x + 3)}; \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \frac {2 a}{a ^ {2} - 4} - \frac {1}{a - 2} & = \frac {2 a}{(a - 2) (a + 2)} - \frac {a + 2}{(a - 2) (a + 2)} \\ & = \frac {2 a - (a + 2)}{(a - 2) (a + 2)} \\ & = \frac {a - 2}{(a - 2) (a + 2)} \\ & = \frac {1}{a + 2}. \end{array} \tag {3}
> > $$
