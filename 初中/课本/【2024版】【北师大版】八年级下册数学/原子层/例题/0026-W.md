例1 把下列各式因式分解:

(1) $2mn + 4m^2$ ;

(2) $7p^{2} - 21p$ ;

(3) $8a^{3}b^{2} - 12ab^{3}c + ab;$

(4) $-24x^{3} + 12x^{2} - 28x$

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1） $2mn+4m^{2}=2m\cdot n+2m\cdot2m=2m(n+2m)$ ;
> >
> > (2) $7p^{2}-21p=7p\cdot p-7p\cdot3=7p(p-3);$
> >
> > (3) $8a^{3}b^{2} - 12ab^{3}c + ab = ab\cdot 8a^{2}b - ab\cdot 12b^{2}c + ab\cdot 1$ $= ab(8a^{2}b - 12b^{2}c + 1);$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (4) - 24 x ^ {3} + 12 x ^ {2} - 28 x & \\ = - (24 x ^ {3} - 12 x ^ {2} + 28 x) & \\ = - (4 x \cdot 6 x ^ {2} - 4 x \cdot 3 x + 4 x \cdot 7) & \\ = - 4 x (6 x ^ {2} - 3 x + 7) 。 \end{array}
> > $$
