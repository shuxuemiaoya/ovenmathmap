例1 把下列各式因式分解:

(1) $25 - 16x^{2}$ ;

(2) $9a^{2} - \frac{1}{4} b^{2}$ .

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $25 - 16x^{2} = 5^{2} - (4x)^{2} = (5 + 4x)(5 - 4x)$ ;
> >
> > (2) $9a^{2} - \frac{1}{4} b^{2} = (3a)^{2} - \left(\frac{1}{2} b\right)^{2} = \left(3a + \frac{1}{2} b\right)\left(3a - \frac{1}{2} b\right)$ 。
