例4 把下列各式因式分解:

(1) $3ax^{2} + 6axy + 3ay^{2}$ ;

(2) $-x^{2} - 4y^{2} + 4xy$ 。

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1） $3ax^{2}+6axy+3ay^{2}=3a(x^{2}+2xy+y^{2})=3a(x+y)^{2}$ ;
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} - x ^ {2} - 4 y ^ {2} + 4 x y & = - (x ^ {2} + 4 y ^ {2} - 4 x y) \\ & = - (x ^ {2} - 4 x y + 4 y ^ {2}) \\ & = - [ x ^ {2} - 2 \cdot x \cdot 2 y + (2 y) ^ {2} ] \\ & = - (x - 2 y) ^ {2}. \end{array}
> > $$
