7. 解下列不等式组:

(1) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{2x - 1}{3} - \frac{5x + 1}{2} \leqslant 1, \\ 5x - 1 < 3(x + 1); \end{array} \right.$

(2) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2x - 7 < 3(x - 1), \\ \frac{4}{3} x + 3 \geqslant 1 - \frac{2}{3} x_{\circ} \end{array} \right.$
