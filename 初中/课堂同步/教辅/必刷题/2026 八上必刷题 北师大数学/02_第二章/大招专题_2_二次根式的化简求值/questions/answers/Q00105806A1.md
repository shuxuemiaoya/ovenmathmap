> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】由数轴知 -1 < x < 2, 所以 $\left|x - 2\right| - \sqrt{(x - 3)^{2}} + \sqrt{4x^{2} - 20x + 25} = 2 - x - (3 - x) + 2x - 5 \mid = 2 - x - 3 + x - 2x + 5 = 4 - 2x.$
> >
