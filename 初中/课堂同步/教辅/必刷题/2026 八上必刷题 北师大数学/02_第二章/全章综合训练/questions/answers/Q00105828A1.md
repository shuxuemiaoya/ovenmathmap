>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $-2\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sqrt{12} - \sqrt{8} \cdot \sqrt{6} = 2\sqrt{3} - 4\sqrt{3} = -2\sqrt{3}$ . 故答案为 $-2\sqrt{3}$ .
