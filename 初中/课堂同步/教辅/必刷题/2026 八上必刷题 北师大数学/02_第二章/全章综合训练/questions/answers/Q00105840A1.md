> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 3√2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sqrt{16-x^{2}}=m$ $\sqrt{4-x^{2}}=n$ → m-n=2√2,
> > m+n=? → m+n=(m+n)(m-n)/m-n $\sqrt{16-x^{2}}+\sqrt{4-x^{2}}=\frac{(16-x^{2})-(4-x^{2})}{\sqrt{16-x^{2}}-\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}$ $\sqrt{16-x^{2}}+\sqrt{4-x^{2}}=\frac{12}{2\sqrt{2}}$ $\sqrt{16-x^{2}}+\sqrt{4-x^{2}}=3\sqrt{2}$
