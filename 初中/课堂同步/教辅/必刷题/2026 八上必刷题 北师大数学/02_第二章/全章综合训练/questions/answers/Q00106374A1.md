> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $a=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}-1}=\sqrt{2}+1$ , $b=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}+1}=\sqrt{2}-1$ ,
> > 原式 $=\sqrt{a}\cdot\sqrt{b}\left(\frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}}-\frac{\sqrt{b}}{\sqrt{a}}\right)=a-b=(\sqrt{2}+1)-$ ( $\sqrt{2}-1$ ) = 2.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
