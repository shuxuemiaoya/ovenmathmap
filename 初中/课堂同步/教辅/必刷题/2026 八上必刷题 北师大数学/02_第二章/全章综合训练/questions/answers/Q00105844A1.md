> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) 原式 = $\frac{4-\sqrt{15}}{(4+\sqrt{15})(4-\sqrt{15})}=$ $\frac{4-\sqrt{15}}{16-15}=4-\sqrt{15}.$
> >
> > (2) 因为 $a = \frac{1}{3 - \sqrt{7}} = \frac{3 + \sqrt{7}}{(3 - \sqrt{7})(3 + \sqrt{7})} = 1$ $\frac{3 + \sqrt{7}}{2}$ ，所以原式 $= 4 \times \left( \frac{3 + \sqrt{7}}{2} \right)^{2} - 12 \times \frac{3 + \sqrt{7}}{2} + 5 = 4 \times \frac{(3 + \sqrt{7})^{2}}{4} - 6(3 + \sqrt{7}) + 5 = (3 + \sqrt{7})^{2} - 18 - 6\sqrt{7} + 5 = 9 + 6\sqrt{7} + 7 - 18 - 6\sqrt{7} + 5 = 3.$ (3) $a = \frac{1}{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{5}}{(\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{5})(\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{5})} = \sqrt{6} + \sqrt{5}, b = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5} - 2} = \frac{\sqrt{5} + 2}{(\sqrt{5} - 2)(\sqrt{5} + 2)} = \sqrt{5} + 2.$ 因为 $\sqrt{6} > 2$ ，所以 $\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{5} > 2 + \sqrt{5}$ ，
> > 所以 a > b.
