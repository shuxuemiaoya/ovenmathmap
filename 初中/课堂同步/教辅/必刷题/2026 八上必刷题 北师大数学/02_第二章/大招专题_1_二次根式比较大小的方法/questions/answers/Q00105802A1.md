> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】 $\frac{1 + \sqrt{2}}{3 + \sqrt{2}} = \frac{(1 + \sqrt{2})(3 - \sqrt{2})}{(3 + \sqrt{2})(3 - \sqrt{2})} = \frac{2\sqrt{2} + 1}{7},$ $\frac{2 + \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2} + 1} = \frac{(2 + \sqrt{2})(\sqrt{2} - 1)}{(\sqrt{2} + 1)(\sqrt{2} - 1)} = \sqrt{2} = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{7}$ . 因为 $\frac{2\sqrt{2} + 1}{7} < \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{7}$ , 所以 $\frac{1 + \sqrt{2}}{3 + \sqrt{2}} < \frac{2 + \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2} + 1}$ .
> >
