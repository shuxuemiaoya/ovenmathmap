> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】因为 $\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{3}} - \frac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}} =$ $\frac{(\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{5})\sqrt{2} - (2 - \sqrt{2})\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{6}} = \frac{2\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{10} - 2\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{6}}{\sqrt{6}}$ $\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{10}}{\sqrt{6}} < 0$ , 所以 $\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{5}}{\sqrt{3}} < \frac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}}$ .
> >
> > 大招解读 | 取近似值后比较
> > 牢记常见无理数的近似值： $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.414; \sqrt{3} \approx 1.732; \sqrt{5} \approx 2.236; \sqrt{6} \approx 2.449;$ $\sqrt{7} \approx 2.646.$
