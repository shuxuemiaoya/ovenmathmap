> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】因为 $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2009} - \sqrt{2008}} = \sqrt{2009} + \sqrt{2008}$ ,$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2008} - \sqrt{2007}} = \sqrt{2008} + \sqrt{2007}, \sqrt{2009} + \sqrt{2008} > \sqrt{2008} + \sqrt{2007}$ ，所以 $\frac{1}{\sqrt{2009} - \sqrt{2008}} > \frac{1}{\sqrt{2008} - \sqrt{2007}}$ ，所以 $\sqrt{2009} - \sqrt{2008} < \sqrt{2008} - \sqrt{2007}$ .
