> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $-a^{2} \geqslant 0, a^{2} \geqslant 0$ , 所以 $a = 0$ , 所以原式 $= \sqrt{8} - \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{0} = 2\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{2} + 0 = \sqrt{2}$ .
