> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由非负性可知 $b - 4 = 0, a - 3 = 0$ , 所以 $b = 4, a = 3$ , 所以 $\sqrt{\frac{a}{b}} = \sqrt{\frac{3}{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
