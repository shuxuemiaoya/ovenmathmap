> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{0.11} = \sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{\frac{11}{100}} = \sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{11} \times \sqrt{\frac{1}{100}} = \frac{1}{10} \times \sqrt{7} \times \sqrt{11}$ . 因为 $\sqrt{7} = a$ ,$\sqrt{11} = b$ ，所以原式 $= \frac{ab}{10}$ 故选D.
