> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** ab
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\sqrt{90} = \sqrt{3 \times 30} = \sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{30}$ , $\sqrt{3}=a,\sqrt{30}=b$ ,所以 $\sqrt{90}=ab$ .故答案为ab.
