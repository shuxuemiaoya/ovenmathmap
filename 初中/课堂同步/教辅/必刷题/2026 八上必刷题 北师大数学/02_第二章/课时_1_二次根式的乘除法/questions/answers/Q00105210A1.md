> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\sqrt{7} -\sqrt{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a = \sqrt{7} +\sqrt{6},b = \sqrt{7} -\sqrt{6}$ 所以 $ab = (\sqrt{7} +\sqrt{6})(\sqrt{7} -\sqrt{6}) = 7 - 6 = 1$ ，则 $a^{2023}\cdot b^{2024} = (ab)^{2023}b = 1\times (\sqrt{7} -\sqrt{6}) = \sqrt{7}-$ $\sqrt{6}$ .故答案为 $\sqrt{7} -\sqrt{6}$
