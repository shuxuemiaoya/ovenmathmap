> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $12 \text{※} 4 = \frac{\sqrt{12 + 4}}{\sqrt{12 - 4}} = \frac{\sqrt{16}}{\sqrt{8}} = \sqrt{\frac{16}{8}} = \sqrt{2}$ . 故答案为 $\sqrt{2}$ .
