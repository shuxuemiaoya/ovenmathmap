> > 【解】(1) 原式 $= \sqrt{\frac{1}{3} \times \frac{27}{4}} = \sqrt{\frac{9}{4}} = \frac{3}{2}$ . (2) $(\sqrt{24} - \sqrt{6}) \times \sqrt{\frac{1}{6}} = \sqrt{24 \times \frac{1}{6}} - \sqrt{6 \times \frac{1}{6}} = \sqrt{4} - 1 = 2 - 1 = 1.$ (3) 原式 $= (\sqrt{3} + 2)(\sqrt{3} - 2) + \sqrt{2 \times 18} = (\sqrt{3})^2 - 2^2 + \sqrt{36} = 3 - 4 + 6 = 5.$
