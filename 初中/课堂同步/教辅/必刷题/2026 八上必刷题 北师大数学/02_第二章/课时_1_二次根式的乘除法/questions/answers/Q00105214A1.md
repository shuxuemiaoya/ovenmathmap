> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $S = ab$ ，所以 $b = \frac{S}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{3}$ . 故答案为 $\sqrt{3}$ .
