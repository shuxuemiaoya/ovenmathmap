> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $m = \sqrt{3} + 1$ , $n = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3} - 1} = \frac{2(\sqrt{3} + 1)}{(\sqrt{3} - 1)(\sqrt{3} + 1)} = \sqrt{3} + 1$ , 所以 m = n.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
