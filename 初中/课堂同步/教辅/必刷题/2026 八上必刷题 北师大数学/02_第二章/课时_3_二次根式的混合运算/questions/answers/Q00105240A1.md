> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{5}{2}\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a \otimes b = \sqrt{a} \cdot \sqrt{b} + \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}$ ，所以 $2 \otimes 4 = \sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{4} + \sqrt{\frac{2}{4}} = 2\sqrt{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{5}{2}\sqrt{2}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{5}{2}\sqrt{2}$ .
