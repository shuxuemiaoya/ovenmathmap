> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n}}=\frac{1\times(\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n})}{(\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n})(\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n})}=$ $\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}$ . 故答案为 $\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}$ .
> >
> > (2) $\frac{1}{1+\sqrt{2}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{4}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{\sqrt{98}+\sqrt{99}}+$ $\frac{1}{\sqrt{99}+\sqrt{100}}=\sqrt{2}-1+\sqrt{3}-\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{4}-\sqrt{3}+\cdots+$ $\sqrt{99}-\sqrt{98}+\sqrt{100}-\sqrt{99}=\sqrt{100}-1=10-1=9.$ (3)能. $\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}}=\frac{1\times(\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n})}{(\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n})(\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n})}=$ $\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n}.$
