> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{1}{2\sqrt{3}-3\sqrt{2}}=$ $\frac{2\sqrt{3}+3\sqrt{2}}{(2\sqrt{3}-3\sqrt{2})(2\sqrt{3}+3\sqrt{2})}=\frac{2\sqrt{3}+3\sqrt{2}}{(2\sqrt{3})^{2}-(3\sqrt{2})^{2}}=$ $\frac{2\sqrt{3}+3\sqrt{2}}{-6}=-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ . 故答案为 $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
