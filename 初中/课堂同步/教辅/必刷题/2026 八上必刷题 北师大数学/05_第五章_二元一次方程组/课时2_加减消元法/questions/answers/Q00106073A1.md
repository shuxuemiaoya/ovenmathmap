> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=2,\\ y&=-2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left\{\begin{aligned}x+y&=0,①\\ 2x-y&=6,②\end{aligned}\right.$ ①+②，得 3x=6，解得 x=2。把 x=2 代入①，得 $2+y=0$ ，解得 y=-2，所以原方程组的解是 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=2,\\ y&=-2.\end{aligned}\right.$ 故答案为 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=2,\\ y&=-2.\end{aligned}\right.$
