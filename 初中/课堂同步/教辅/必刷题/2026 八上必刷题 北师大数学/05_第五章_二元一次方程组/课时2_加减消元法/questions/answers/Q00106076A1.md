> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left\{\begin{aligned}&3x-y=4m+1,①\\&x+y=2m-5,②\end{aligned}\right.$ ①-②得 2x-2y=2m+6,所以 x-y=m+3.
> > 由 x-y=4 , 可得 m+3=4 , 解得 m=1 ,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
