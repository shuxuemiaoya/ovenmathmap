>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $x = 2, y = -1$ 时, $2x - y = 5, x + y = 1$ , 则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 2, \\ y = -1 \end{array} \right.$ 是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2x - y = 5, \\ x + y = 1 \end{array} \right.$ 的解, 故 A 符合题意. 当 $x = 2, y = -1$ 时, $3y = -3 \neq 2, 3y + 1 = -3 + 1 = -2 \neq 2$ , 则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 2, \\ y = -1 \end{array} \right.$ 不是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 3y, \\ x = 3y + 1 \end{array} \right.$ 的解, 故 B 不符合题意. 当 $x = 2, y = -1$ 时, $x - 3 = -1, y - 2x = -1 - 4 = -5 \neq 5$ , 则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 2, \\ y = -1 \end{array} \right.$ 不是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = x - 3, \\ y - 2x = 5 \end{array} \right.$ 的解, 故 C 不符合题意. 当 $x = 2, y = -1$ 时, $x - 3y = 5, 2x + y = 4 - 1 = 3 \neq 5$ , 则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 2, \\ y = -1 \end{array} \right.$ 不是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x - 3y = 5, \\ 2x + y = 5 \end{array} \right.$ 的解, 故 D 不符合题意.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
