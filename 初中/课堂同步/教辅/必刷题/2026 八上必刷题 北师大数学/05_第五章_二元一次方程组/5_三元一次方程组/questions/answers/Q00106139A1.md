> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3x - y + z = 4, \\ 2x + 3y - z = 12, \\ x + y + z = 6, \end{array} \right.$ ①+②,得 $5x+$ $x + y + z = 6, \textcircled{3}$ $2y = 16, \textcircled{4}$ ②+③,得 $3x + 4y = 18, \textcircled{5}$ ④×2-⑤,得 $7x = 14$ ,解得 $x = 2$ . 把 $x = 2$ 代入④,得 $10+$ $2y = 16$ ,解得 $y = 3$ . 把 $x = 2, y = 3$ 代入③,得 $2+$ $3 + z = 6$ ,解得 $z = 1$ . 所以原方程组的解为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 2, \\ y = 3, \\ z = 1. \end{array} \right.$ (2) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3x + 4y - 3z = 3, \\ 2x - 3y - 2z = 2, \\ 5x - 3y + 4z = -22, \end{array} \right.$ ③-②,得 $3x + 6z =$ -24,④①×3+②×4,得 $17x - 17z = 17$ ,即 $x - z=$ 1,⑤④与⑤组成方程组,得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3x + 6z = -24, \\ x - z = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = -2, \\ z = -3. \end{array} \right.$ 将 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = -2, \\ z = -3. \end{array} \right.$ 代入①,得 $-6 + 4y+$ $9 = 3$ ,解得 $y = 0$ ,所以原方程组的解为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = -2, \\ y = 0, \\ z = -3. \end{array} \right.$
