> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** -1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + y = 5, \\ 2x - y = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 2, \\ y = 3. \end{array} \right.$ 把 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 2, \\ y = 3 \end{array} \right.$ 分别代入 $4ax + 5by = -22$ 和 $ax - by - 8 = 0$ 中，整理得方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 8a + 15b = -22, \\ 2a - 3b = 8, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 1, \\ b = -2, \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $a + b = -1$ .
