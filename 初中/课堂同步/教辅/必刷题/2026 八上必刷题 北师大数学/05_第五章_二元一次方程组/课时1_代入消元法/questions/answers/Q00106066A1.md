> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\left|\begin{array}{cc}3 & -1 \\ y & x\end{array}\right|=3x+y=17$ , $\left|\begin{array}{cc}1 & 2 \\ x & y\end{array}\right|=y-2x=-3$ , 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&3x+y=17,①\\&y-2x=-3.\end{aligned}\right.$ 由②
> >
> > 得 y=2x-3, ③ 把③代入①, 得 $3x+2x-3=17$ ,
> >
> > 解得 x=4. 把 x=4 代入③, 得 $y=2\times4-3=5$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x &= 4, \\ y &= 5.\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
