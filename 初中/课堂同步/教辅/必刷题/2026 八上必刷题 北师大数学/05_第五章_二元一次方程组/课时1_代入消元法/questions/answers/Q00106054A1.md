> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2x - (1 + x) = 5$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2x - y = 5, \\ y = 1 + x, \end{array} \right.$ 将②代入①得 $2x - (1 + x) = 5$ , 故答案为 $2x - (1 + x) = 5$ .
