> > [!success] **【答案】** 3

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)由题意得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}-4a+7b&=9,\\2a+b&=9,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=3,\\b&=3,\end{aligned}\right.$ 则原方程为 $3x+3y=9$ ，即 y=3-x。当 x=0 时，y=3-0=3，即 p=3。故答案为 3。
> > (2)由(1)得 $x+y=3$ 。方程组 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x+y&=3,\\2x+y&=-5\end{aligned}\right.$ 的解为 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=-8,\\y&=11,\end{aligned}\right.$ 故①不符合题意。方程组 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x+y&=3,\\x+2y&=-4\end{aligned}\right.$ 的解为 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=10,\\y&=-7,\end{aligned}\right.$ 故②不符合题意。方程组$\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + y = 3, \\ 3x - y = 1 \end{array} \right.$ 的解为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 1, \\ y = 2, \end{array} \right.$ 故③符合题意. 故答案为③. (3) 把 $x = -7, y = -2$ 和 $x = 8, y = 13$ 代入 $cx+$ $dy = 1$ , 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} -7c - 2d = 1, \\ 8c + 13d = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} c = -\frac{1}{5}, \\ d = \frac{1}{5}, \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $cx + dy = 1$ 可化为 $-\frac{1}{5} x + \frac{1}{5} y = 1$ , 即 $y - x = 5$ , 所以原方程组变为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + y = 3, \\ y - x = 5, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = -1, \\ y = 4. \end{array} \right.$ 故方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + y = 3, \\ y - x = 5 \end{array} \right.$ 的解为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = -1, \\ y = 4. \end{array} \right.$
