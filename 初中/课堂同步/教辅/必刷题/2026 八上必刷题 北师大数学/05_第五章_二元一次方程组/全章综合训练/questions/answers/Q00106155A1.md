> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + 2y = 4k, \\ x - y = k, \end{array} \right.$ ①+②得 $2x + 1$ $y = 5k$ . 又因为 $2x + y = 10$ , 所以 $5k = 10$ , 所以 $k = 2$ . 故答案为 2.
