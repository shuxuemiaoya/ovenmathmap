> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 5, \\ y = -1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 把 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 3, \\ y = -2 \end{array} \right.$ 代入 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} ax + y = b, \\ cx - y = d, \end{array} \right.$ 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3a - 2 = b, \\ 3c + 2 = d. \end{array} \right.$ 因为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} ax + 2y = 2a + b, \\ cx - 2y = 2c + d, \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} ax + 2y = 2a + 3a - 2, \\ cx - 2y = 2c + 3c + 2, \end{array} \right.$ 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} ax + 2y = 5a - 2, \\ cx - 2y = 5c + 2, \end{array} \right.$ ① + ②，得 $(a + c)x = 5(a + c)$ . 因为方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} ax + y = b, \\ cx - y = d \end{array} \right.$ 有解，所以 $a + c \neq 0$ ，所以 $x = 5$ . 把 $x = 5$ 代入①，得 $5a + 2y = 5a - 2$ ，解得 $y = -1$ ，所以方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} ax + 2y = 2a + b, \\ cx - 2y = 2c + d \end{array} \right.$ 的解为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 5, \\ y = -1. \end{array} \right.$ 故答案为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 5, \\ y = -1. \end{array} \right.$
> >
