> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)因为 $2 \times 1 - 1 = 1 \neq -1, 2 \times (-3) - 4 = -10 \neq -1, 2 \times \frac{1}{2} - 2 = -1$ , 所以点 $C$ 在方程 $2x - y = -1$ 的图象上. 故答案为 $C$ . (2)由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2x + 3y = 9, \\ 3x - 4y = 5, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 3, \\ y = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 所以方程 $2x + 3y = 9$ 和方程 $3x - 4y = 5$ 图象的交点坐标为 (3,1). (3)由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 5x + 3y = 20 + 7m, \\ 3x + 4y = 19 - 14m, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \frac{23 + 70m}{11}, \\ y = \frac{35 - 91m}{11}. \end{array} \right.$ 因为 $x + y = 5$ , 所以 $\frac{23 + 70m}{11} + \frac{35 - 91m}{11} = 5$ , 所以 $m = \frac{1}{7}$ . 当 $t > \frac{1}{7}$ 时, $\sqrt{(-t - 2)^2} - |1 - 7t| = t + 2 + 7t = 3 - 6t$ .
