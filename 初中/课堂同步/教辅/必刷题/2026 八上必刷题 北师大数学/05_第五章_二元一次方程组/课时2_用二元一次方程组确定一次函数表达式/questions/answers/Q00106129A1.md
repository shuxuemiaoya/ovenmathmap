> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 y 与 x 间的关系式为 $y = kx + b$ . 将 (5,12.5), (20,20) 代入, 得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}12.5 &= 5k + b, \\ 20 &= 20k + b,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}k &= \frac{1}{2}, \\ b &= 10,\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $y = \frac{1}{2}x + 10$ . 当 x = 0 时, y = 10, 即弹簧不挂物体时的长度是 10 cm.
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
