> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ AB = AC, ∴ ∠ABC = ∠ACB. 若 $\angle DCB = \angle EBC, \because BC = BC, \therefore \triangle DCB \cong$ $\triangle EBC(\mathrm{ASA}), \therefore CD = BE, BD = CE$ , 故选项 B、D 是真命题, 不符合题意. 若 $BD = CE, \because BC = BC, \angle ABC = \angle ACB, \therefore \triangle DCB \cong \triangle EBC$ (SAS), $\therefore \angle DCB = \angle EBC$ , 故选项 C 是真命题, 不符合题意. $CD = BE$ 时, 不能证明 $\angle DCB = \angle EBC$ , 故选项 A 是假命题, 符合题意.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
