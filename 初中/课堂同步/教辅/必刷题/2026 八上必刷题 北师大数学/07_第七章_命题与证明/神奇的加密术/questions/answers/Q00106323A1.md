> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** AB
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】选择①. 理由: $\because AE \parallel BF, CE \parallel DF, \therefore \angle A$ $\angle FBD, \angle D = \angle ECA.\because AE = BF, \therefore \triangle AEC \cong \triangle BFD(AAS), \therefore AC = BD, \therefore AC - BC = BD - BC,$ 即 $AB = CD.$ （答案不唯一，选择①或③均可使结论成立）
