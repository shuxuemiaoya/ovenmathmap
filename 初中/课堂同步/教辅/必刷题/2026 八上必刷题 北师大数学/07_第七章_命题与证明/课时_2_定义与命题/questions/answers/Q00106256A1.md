> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\angle 1 = \angle 2 = 45^{\circ}$ 满足 $\angle 1 + \angle 2 = 90^{\circ}$ ,但不满足 $\angle 1 \neq \angle 2$ ,能说明命题“如果 $\angle 1 + \angle 2 = 90^{\circ}$ ,那么 $\angle 1 \neq \angle 2$ ”是假命题,故A选项符合题意; $\angle 1 = 40^{\circ}, \angle 2 = 50^{\circ}$ 满足命题“如果 $\angle 1 + \angle 2 = 90^{\circ}$ ,那么 $\angle 1 \neq \angle 2$ ”,故B选项不符合题意; $\angle 1 = 50^{\circ}, \angle 2 = 50^{\circ}$ 不满足 $\angle 1 + \angle 2 = 90^{\circ}$ ,故C选项不符合题意; $\angle 1 = 40^{\circ}, \angle 2 = 40^{\circ}$ 不满足 $\angle 1 + \angle 2 = 90^{\circ}$ ,故D选项不符
> >
> > 合题意.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
