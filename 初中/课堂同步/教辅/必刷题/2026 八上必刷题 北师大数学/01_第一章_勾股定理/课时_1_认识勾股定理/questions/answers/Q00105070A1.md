> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{5}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $CD \perp AB, BD = 3, BC = 5$ , 所以 $CD^2 = BC^2 - BD^2 = 5^2 - 3^2 = 16$ . 设 $AE = CE = x$ , 则 $DE = 6 - x$ , 所以在 Rt $\triangle CDE$ 中, $CE^2 = DE^2 + CD^2$ , 即 $x^2 = (6 - x)^2 + 16$ , 解得 $x = \frac{13}{3}$ , 所以 $DE = 6 - \frac{13}{3} = \frac{5}{3}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{5}{3}$ .
