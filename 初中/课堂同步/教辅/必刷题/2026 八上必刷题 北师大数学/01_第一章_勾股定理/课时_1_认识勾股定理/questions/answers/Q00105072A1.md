>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 24
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由勾股定理, 得 $AB^{2} = BC^{2} + AC^{2}$ , 则阴影部分的面积为 $\frac{1}{2}\pi \times \left(\frac{BC}{2}\right)^{2} + \frac{1}{2}\pi \times \left(\frac{AC}{2}\right)^{2} + S_{\triangle ABC} - \frac{1}{2}\pi \times \left(\frac{AB}{2}\right)^{2} = \frac{1}{8}\pi \times (BC^{2} + AC^{2} - AB^{2}) + 24 = 24\mathrm{cm}^{2}$ .
