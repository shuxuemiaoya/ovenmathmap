> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)在长方形ABCD中, $AB = 6, AD = 8$ , 所以 $BC = AD = 8, CD = AB = 6, \angle B = \angle D = 90^{\circ}$ . 在 Rt△ABC 中, 由勾股定理得 $AC^{2} = AB^{2} + BC^{2} = 6^{2} + 8^{2} = 100$ , 所以 $AC = 10$ .
> >
> > (2)由折叠的性质可知, $AF = AD = 8, DE = EF$ , $\angle AFE = \angle D = 90^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\angle CFE = 90^{\circ}, CF = AC - AF = 2$ . 设 $DE = EF = x$ , 则 $CE = 6 - x$ . 在 Rt△CEF 中, 由勾股定理得 $EF^{2} + CF^{2} = CE^{2}$ , 即 $x^{2} + 2^{2} = (6 - x)^{2}$ , 解得 $x = \frac{8}{3}$ , 所以 $DE = \frac{8}{3}$ .
