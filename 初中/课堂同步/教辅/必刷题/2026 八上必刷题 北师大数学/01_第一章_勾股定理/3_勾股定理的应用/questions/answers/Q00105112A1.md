> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 5
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $BC = BD = 25 \mathrm{~cm}$ , $CE = 20 \mathrm{~cm}$ , $DF = 15 \mathrm{~cm}$ , $\angle BEC = \angle BFD = 90^{\circ}$ . 在 Rt $\triangle BEC$ 中, 由勾股定理, 得 $BE^{2} = BC^{2} - CE^{2} = 25^{2} - 20^{2} = 225$ , 所以 $BE = 15 \mathrm{~cm}$ . 在 Rt $\triangle BFD$ 中, 由勾股定理得 $BF^{2} = BD^{2} - DF^{2} = 25^{2} - 15^{2} = 400$ , 所以 $BF = 20 \mathrm{~cm}$ , 所以 $EF = BF - BE = 20 - 15 = 5 (\mathrm{~cm})$ . 故答案为 5.
