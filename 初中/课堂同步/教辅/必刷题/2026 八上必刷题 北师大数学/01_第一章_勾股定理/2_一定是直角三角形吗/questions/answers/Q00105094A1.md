>
> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 AB = 8, AC = 15, BC = 17, 所以 $AB^{2} + AC^{2} = 289$ , $BC^{2} = 289$ , 所以 $AB^{2} + AC^{2} = BC^{2}$ , 所以 $\angle BAC = 90^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\triangle ABC$ 的面积为 $\frac{1}{2} \times AB \times AC = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 15 = 60$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
