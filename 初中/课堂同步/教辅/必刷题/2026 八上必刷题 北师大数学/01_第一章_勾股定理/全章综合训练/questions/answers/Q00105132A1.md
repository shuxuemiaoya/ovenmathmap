> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 AC = 3, BC = 4, $\angle ACB = 90^{\circ}$ , 所以 $AB^{2} = AC^{2} + BC^{2} = 3^{2} + 4^{2} = 25$ , 所以 AB = 5. 由折叠可得 AE = AB = 5, BD = DE, 所以 CE = AE - AC = 2. 设 CD = x, 则 BD = DE = 4 - x. 在 Rt $\triangle CDE$ 中, $CD^{2} + CE^{2} = DE^{2}$ , 所以 $x^{2} + 2^{2} = (4 - x)^{2}$ , 解得 $x = \frac{3}{2}$ , 所以 $CD = \frac{3}{2}$ , 所以阴影部分的面积是 $\frac{1}{2}AC \cdot CD = \frac{1}{2} \times 3 \times \frac{3}{2} = \frac{9}{4}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
