> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 98
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $2 + 3 + 5 = 10$ ， $\left(90 - 85 \times \frac{2}{10} - 80 \times \frac{3}{10}\right) \div \frac{5}{10} = (90 - 17 - 24) \div \frac{5}{10} = 49 \div \frac{5}{10} = 98$ （分）. 故答案为98.
