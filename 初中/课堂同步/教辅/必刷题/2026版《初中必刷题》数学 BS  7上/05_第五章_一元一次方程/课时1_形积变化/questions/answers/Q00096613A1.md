> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2500\pi \mathrm{cm}^3$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设该玻璃密封容器的总容积为 $V \mathrm{~cm}^3$ . 根据题意得 $\pi \times \left(\frac{20}{2}\right)^2 \times 15 = V - \pi \times \left(\frac{20}{2}\right)^2 \times (30 - 20)$ , 解得 $V = 2500\pi$ , 即该玻璃密封容器的总容积为 $2500\pi \mathrm{cm}^3$ . 故答案为 $2500\pi \mathrm{cm}^3$ .
