> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $90^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) $\angle ACM + \angle BCN = 180^{\circ} - \angle ACB = 180^{\circ} - 90^{\circ} = 90^{\circ}$ . 故答案为 $90^{\circ}$ . (2) 因为 $\angle BCN = 180^{\circ} - \angle BCM, \angle ACM = 90^{\circ} - \angle BCM$ , 所以 $\angle BCN - \angle ACM = (180^{\circ} - \angle BCM) - (90^{\circ} - \angle BCM) = 90^{\circ}$ . 故答案为 $\angle BCN - \angle ACM = 90^{\circ}$ . (3) 因为 $\angle BCN = 180^{\circ} - \angle BCM, \angle ACM = 90^{\circ} + \angle BCM$ , 所以 $\angle BCN + \angle ACM = (180^{\circ} - \angle BCM) + (90^{\circ} + \angle BCM) = 270^{\circ}$ . 故答案为 $\angle ACM + \angle BCN = 270^{\circ}$ .
