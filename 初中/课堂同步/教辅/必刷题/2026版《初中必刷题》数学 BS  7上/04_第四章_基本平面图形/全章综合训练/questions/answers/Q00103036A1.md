> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 3 000π
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $S_{阴影}=S_{扇形AOD}-S_{扇形BOC}=\pi\times120^{2}\times\frac{100^{\circ}}{360^{\circ}}-\pi\times60^{2}\times\frac{100^{\circ}}{360^{\circ}}=3\ 000\pi(\mathrm{~cm}^{2})$ ，故答案为 3 000π.
