> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\angle MON = 20^{\circ}, \angle AOM = 35^{\circ}$ ，所以 $\angle AON = \angle AOM - \angle MON = 35^{\circ} - 20^{\circ} = 15^{\circ}$ 。因为 OM 平分 $\angle BON$ ，所以 $\angle BON = 2\angle MON = 2\times20^{\circ} = 40^{\circ}$ ，所以 $\angle AOB = \angle BON + \angle AON = 40^{\circ} + 15^{\circ} = 55^{\circ}$ 。
