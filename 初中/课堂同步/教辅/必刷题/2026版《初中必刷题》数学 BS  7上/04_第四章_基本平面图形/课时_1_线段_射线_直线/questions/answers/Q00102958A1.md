> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 8 cm
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 M 是 AC 的中点, 所以 $MC = AM = \frac{1}{2}AC = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 = 3 \, (\text{cm})$ . 又因为 CN:NB = 1:2, BC = 15 cm, 所以 $CN = \frac{1}{3}BC = \frac{1}{3} \times 15 = 5 \, (\text{cm})$ , 所以 $MN = MC + NC = 3 + 5 = 8 \, (\text{cm})$ .
