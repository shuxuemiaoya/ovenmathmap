>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $26.38^{\circ} = 26^{\circ} + 0.38 \times 60' = 26^{\circ} + 22.8' = 26^{\circ} + 22' + 0.8 \times 60'' = 26^{\circ} + 22' + 48'' = 26^{\circ}22'48''$ .
> >
> > (2) $30'' = \left(\frac{1}{60}\right)' \times 30 = 0.5'$ , $40.5' = \left(\frac{1}{60}\right)^{\circ} \times 40.5 = 0.675^{\circ}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $35^{\circ}40'30'' = 35.675^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 钟表上有 12 个数字, 每相邻两个数字之间的夹角均为 $30^{\circ}$ .
