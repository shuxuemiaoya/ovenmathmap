> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{40}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $x = \frac{2}{3}, y = 6$ 时, $2x^{2}y + 3xy - 4 = 2 \times \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{2} \times 6 + 3 \times \frac{2}{3} \times 6 - 4 = \frac{16}{3} + 12 - 4 = \frac{40}{3}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{40}{3}$ .
