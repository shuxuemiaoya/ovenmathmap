> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $-\frac{15}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $a = -5 \times (-3) = 15, b = \frac{4}{-1} = -4$ ，所以 $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{15}{-4} = -\frac{15}{4}$ . 故答案为 $-\frac{15}{4}$ .
