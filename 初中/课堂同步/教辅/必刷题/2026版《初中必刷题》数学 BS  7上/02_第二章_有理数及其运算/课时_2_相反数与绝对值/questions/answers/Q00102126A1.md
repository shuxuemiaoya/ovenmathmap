> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $<, >, <$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $-\frac{3}{4} = -\frac{9}{12}, -\frac{2}{3} = -\frac{8}{12}$ , 因为 $\left| -\frac{9}{12} \right| = \frac{9}{12} > \left| -\frac{8}{12} \right| = \frac{8}{12}$ , 所以 $-\frac{9}{12} < -\frac{8}{12}$ , 所以 $-\frac{3}{4} < -\frac{2}{3}; -\left(-\frac{2}{7}\right) = \frac{2}{7} > -\frac{3}{8}; -\left| -1 \frac{3}{4} \right| = -1 \frac{3}{4} < -(-1.8) = 1.8$ . 故答案为 $<, >, <$ .
