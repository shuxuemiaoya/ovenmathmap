> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{8}{15}$ (答案不唯一)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 观察分母, 在计算 $\frac{7}{15} + \left(-\frac{2}{7}\right) + \blacksquare$ 时, $\blacksquare$ 中可以填 $\frac{8}{15}$ , 则 $\frac{7}{15} + \left(-\frac{2}{7}\right) + \frac{8}{15} = \frac{7}{15} + \frac{8}{15} + \left(-\frac{2}{7}\right) = 1 + \left(-\frac{2}{7}\right) = \frac{5}{7}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{8}{15}$ (答案不唯一).
