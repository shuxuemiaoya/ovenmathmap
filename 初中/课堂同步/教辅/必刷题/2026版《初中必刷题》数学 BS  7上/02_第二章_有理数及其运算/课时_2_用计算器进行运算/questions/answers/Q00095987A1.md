> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $-\frac{9}{10}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $n = 1\frac{1}{3} - \frac{7}{12} + \frac{9}{20} - \frac{11}{30} + \frac{13}{42} - \frac{15}{56} + \frac{17}{72} = 1 + \frac{1}{3} - \left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{5}\right) - \left(\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{6}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{7}\right) - \left(\frac{1}{7} + \frac{1}{8}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{9}\right) = 1 + \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{5} - \frac{1}{5} - \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{7} - \frac{1}{7} - \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{9} = 1 + \frac{1}{9} = \frac{10}{9}$ , 所以 $n$ 的负倒数是 $-\frac{9}{10}$ .
