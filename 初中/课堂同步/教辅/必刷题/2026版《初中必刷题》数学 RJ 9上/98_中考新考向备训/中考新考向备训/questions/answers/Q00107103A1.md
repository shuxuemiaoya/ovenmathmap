>
> > [!success] **【答案】** ①,②
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)选择①作为已知条件,②作为结论.
> > 故答案为①,②.
> > 证明:连接 $OD$ . $\because DE$ 与 $\odot O$ 相切于点 $D$ , $\therefore \angle ODE = 90^\circ$ . $\because AD$ 平分 $\angle BAC$ , $\therefore \angle EAD = \angle DAB$ . $\because OA = OD$ , $\therefore \angle DAB = \angle ADO$ , $\therefore \angle EAD = \angle ADO$ , $\therefore AE // DO$ , $\therefore \angle AED = 180^\circ - \angle ODE = 90^\circ$ , $\therefore DE \perp AC$ .
> > 选择②作为已知条件,①作为结论. 故答案为②,①.
> > 证明:连接 $OD$ . $\because DE \perp AC$ , $\therefore \angle AED = 90^\circ$ . $\because AD$ 平分 $\angle BAC$ , $\therefore \angle EAD = \angle DAB$ . $\because OA = OD$ , $\therefore \angle DAB = \angle ADO$ , $\therefore \angle EAD = \angle ADO$ , $\therefore AE // DO$ , $\therefore \angle ODE = 180^\circ - \angle AED = 90^\circ$ . $\because OD$ 是 $\odot O$ 的半径, $\therefore DE$ 与 $\odot O$ 相切. (任选一种回答即可)
> > (2)连接 $OF, DF$ . $\because AB$ 是 $\odot O$ 的直径, $\therefore \angle ADB = 90^\circ$ . $\because AB = 6$ , $\angle BAD = 30^\circ$ , $\therefore BD = \frac{1}{2} AB = 3$ , $\therefore AD = 3\sqrt{3}$ . $\because AD$ 平分 $\angle BAC$ , $\therefore \angle EAD = \angle DAB = 30^\circ$ , $\therefore$ 在 Rt $\triangle AED$ 中, $DE = \frac{1}{2} AD = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , $\therefore AE = \frac{9}{2}$ . $\because \angle DOB = 2\angle DAB = 60^\circ$ , $\angle DOF = 2\angle EAD = 60^\circ$ , $OD = OF = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 = 3$ , $\therefore \triangle DOF$ 是等边三角形, $\therefore \angle ODF = 60^\circ = \angle DOB$ , $\therefore DF // AB$ , $\therefore S_{\triangle ADF} = S_{\triangle ODF}$ , $\therefore S_{阴影部分} = S_{\triangle AED} - S_{扇形DOF} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{9}{2} \times \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{60\times\pi\times3^2}{360} = \frac{27\sqrt{3}}{8} - \frac{3\pi}{2}$ .
