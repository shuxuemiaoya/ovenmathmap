> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\angle ABC = 110^{\circ}$ ，∴ 优弧 $\widehat{ADC}$ 所对的圆心角的度数为 $110^{\circ} \times 2 = 220^{\circ}$ . ∵ CD 是直径，∴ $\angle COD = 180^{\circ}$ . ∵ $\angle COD + \angle AOD = 220^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle AOD = 40^{\circ}$ . ∵ ⊙O 的半径为 3, ∴ 扇形 AOD 的面积为 $\frac{40 \times \pi \times 3^{2}}{360} = \pi$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
