> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ CA = CB, CD ⊥ AB, ∴ AD = DB = $\frac{1}{2}AB$ . 由旋转的性质知, $AB' = AB$ , ∴ $AD = \frac{1}{2}AB'$ ,
> >
> > ∴ $\angle AB'D = 30^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle B'AD = 90^{\circ} - \angle AB'D = 60^{\circ} = 2\alpha$ , ∴ $\alpha = 30^{\circ}$ . ∵ AC = 4, ∴ CD = 2,
> >
> > 首先证明 $\alpha=30^{\circ}$ ，根据已知可算出 AD 的长度，进而可得 AB 的长度，再根据弧长公式即可得出答案.
> >
> > $\therefore AD = 2\sqrt{3}, \therefore AB = 2AD = 4\sqrt{3}, \therefore \widehat{BB'}$ 的长是 $\frac{60 \times \pi \times 4\sqrt{3}}{180} = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}\pi.$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
