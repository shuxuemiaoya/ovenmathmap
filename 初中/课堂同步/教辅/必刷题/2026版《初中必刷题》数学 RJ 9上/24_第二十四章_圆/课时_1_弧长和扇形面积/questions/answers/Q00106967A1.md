> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】∵△ABC为等边三角形,且内接于⊙O,
> > ∴ $S_{\triangle BOC} = S_{\triangle AOC}$ , ∠AOC = ∠BOC = 120°. 在
> > △OBC中, OB=OC, ∠BOC=120°, BC=2√3,
> > ∴ 易得 OB = OC = 2, ∴ $S_{阴影部分} = S_{扇形AOC} = \frac{120\pi \times 2^{2}}{360} = \frac{4\pi}{3}$ .
