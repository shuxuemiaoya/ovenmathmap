> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{26}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图, 连接 $CE, CD, AC$ . $\because$ 每个小正方形的边长均为 $1, \therefore AD = \sqrt{1^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{26}, AC = \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{13}, CD = \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{13}, \therefore CD^2 + AC^2 = AD^2, AC = CD, \therefore \angle ACD = 90^\circ, \therefore \triangle ACD$ 是等腰直角三角形. $\because \angle ABC = 90^\circ, \therefore AC$ 是圆弧所在圆的直径, $\therefore \angle AEC = 90^\circ, \therefore CE \perp AD, \therefore AE = \frac{1}{2} AD = \frac{\sqrt{26}}{2}$ , 故答案为 $\frac{\sqrt{26}}{2}$ .
