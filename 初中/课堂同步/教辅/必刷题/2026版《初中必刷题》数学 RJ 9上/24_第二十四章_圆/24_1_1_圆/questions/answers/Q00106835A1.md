> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 圆 O 的周长为 $4\pi, \therefore OD = 2$ .
> >
> > ∵ OC = OD, ∴ $\angle C = \angle D$ (等边对等角).
> >
> > ∵ BE // OC, ∴ $\angle EBD = \angle C$ (平行线的性质),
> >
> > ∴ $\angle EBD = \angle D$ (等量代换), ∴ BE = DE (等角对等边), ∴ EO + EB = OD = 2. 故答案为 2.
