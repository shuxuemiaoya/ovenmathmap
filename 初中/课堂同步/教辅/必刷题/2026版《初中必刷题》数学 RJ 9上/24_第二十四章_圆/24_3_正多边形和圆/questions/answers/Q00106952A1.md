> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $10 - 2\sqrt{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 连接 $AG$ . $\because H$ 为 $OF$ 中点, $\therefore OH = \frac{1}{2} OF = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 = 1$ . 在 Rt $\triangle OAH$ 中, 由勾股定理, 得 $AH = \sqrt{OA^2 + OH^2} = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}$ . $\because AH = HG = \sqrt{5}, \therefore OG = GH - OH = \sqrt{5} - 1$ . 在 Rt $\triangle AOG$ 中, 由勾股定理得, $AG^2 = OA^2 + OG^2 = 4 + (\sqrt{5} - 1)^2 = 10 - 2\sqrt{5}$ . $\because AB = AG, \therefore AB^2 = 10 - 2\sqrt{5}$ . 故答案为 $10 - 2\sqrt{5}$ .
