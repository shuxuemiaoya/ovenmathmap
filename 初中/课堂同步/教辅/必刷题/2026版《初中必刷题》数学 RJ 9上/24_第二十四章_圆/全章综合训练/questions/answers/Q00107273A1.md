> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 连接 OP. 由题意得 $OQ = 35 \, cm$ , $PQ = 135 \, cm$ , $\therefore 100 \, cm \leqslant OP \leqslant 170 \, cm$ , $\therefore OP$ 的最大值为 $170 \, cm$ . $\because OA \perp AP, OA = 80 \, cm$ , $\therefore AP = \sqrt{OP^{2} - OA^{2}} = \sqrt{170^{2} - 80^{2}} = 150 (\, \text{cm})$ , 故选 B.
> >
> > 连接 OP, 求出 OP 的最大值, 进而根据勾股定理求出 PA 的最大值.
