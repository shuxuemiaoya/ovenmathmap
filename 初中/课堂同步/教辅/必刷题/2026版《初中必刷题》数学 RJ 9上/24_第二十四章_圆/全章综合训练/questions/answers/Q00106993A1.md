> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $90^{\circ}
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\angle A$ 是 $\widehat{BC}$ 所对的圆周角， $\angle BOC$ 是 $\widehat{BC}$ 所对的圆心角，∴ $\angle BOC = 2\angle A$ . ∵ $\angle BOC + \angle OBC + \angle OCB = 180^{\circ}$ ,
> > ∴ $2\angle A + \angle OBC + \angle OCB = 180^{\circ}$ . ∵ OB = OC,
> > ∴ $\angle OBC = \angle OCB, \therefore 2\angle A + \angle OBC + \angle OBC = 180^{\circ}, \therefore 2\angle A + 2\angle OBC = 180^{\circ}, \therefore \angle A + \angle OBC = 90^{\circ}.$ 故答案为 $90^{\circ}.$
