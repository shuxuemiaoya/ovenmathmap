> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because$ 六边形 $ABCDEF$ 是边长为 6 的正六边形, $\therefore \angle BAF = \angle AFE = \angle E = \frac{(6 - 2) \times 180^\circ}{6} = 120^\circ$ , $AB = AF = EF = DE = 6$ , $\therefore \angle AFB = \angle ABF = \frac{1}{2} \times (180^\circ - 120^\circ) = 30^\circ$ , $\angle EFD = \angle EDF = \frac{1}{2} \times (180^\circ - 120^\circ) = 30^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle BFD = 120^\circ - 2 \times 30^\circ = 60^\circ$ . 如图, 过点 $A$
> >
> > 掌握正六边形的性质及弧长的计算公式是解题关键.
> >
> > 作 $AG \perp BF$ 于点 G，则 $AG = \frac{1}{2} AF = 3, BF = 2FG, \therefore FG = \sqrt{6^2 - 3^2} = 3\sqrt{3}, \therefore BF = 6\sqrt{3}$ . 设圆锥的底面半径为 r，则 $2\pi r = \frac{60\pi}{180} \times 6\sqrt{3}, \therefore r = \sqrt{3}$ . 故答案为 $\sqrt{3}$ .
