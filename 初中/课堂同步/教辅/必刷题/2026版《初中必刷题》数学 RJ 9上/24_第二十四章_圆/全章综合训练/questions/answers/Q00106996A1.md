>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ C 为 $\widehat{AB}$ 的中点， $\angle AOB = 72^{\circ}$ ，
> > ∴ $\angle AOC = \angle BOC = 36^{\circ}$ . ∵ OA = OC,
> > ∴ $\angle ACO = \angle OAC = 72^{\circ}$ . ∵ 直线 MN 与 $\odot O$ 相切, 切点为 C, ∴ $\angle OCM = 90^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle ACM = \angle OCM - \angle ACO = 90^{\circ} - 72^{\circ} = 18^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
