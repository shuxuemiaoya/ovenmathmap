> > [!success] **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】∵ FA=FE, ∴ $\angle FAE=\angle AEF.$ ∵ $\angle FAE$ 与 $\angle BCE$ 都是 $\widehat{BF}$ 所对的圆周角，
> > ∴ $\angle FAE = \angle BCE.$ 又∵ $\angle AEF = \angle CEB, \therefore \angle CEB = \angle BCE.$ ∵ CE 平分 $\angle ACD, \therefore \angle ACE = \angle DCE.$ ∵ AB 是直径, ∴ $\angle ACB = 90^{\circ}$ ,
> >
> > $\therefore \angle CEB + \angle DCE = \angle BCE + \angle ACE = \angle ACB = 90^{\circ}, \therefore \angle CDE = 90^{\circ}, \therefore CD \perp AB.$
> >
> > (2)【解】由(1)知, $\angle BEC=\angle BCE,\therefore BE=BC.$ $\because AF=EF,FM\perp AB,OM=OE=1,\therefore MA=ME=2,\therefore AE=4,\therefore$ 圆的半径 $OA=OB=AE-OE=3,\therefore BC=BE=OB-OE=2.$ 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中,AB=6,BC=2, $\angle ACB=90^{\circ}$ , $\therefore AC=\sqrt{AB^{2}-BC^{2}}=\sqrt{6^{2}-2^{2}}=4\sqrt{2}.$
