> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】∵ ⊙O 与 AB 相切, ∴ OE⊥AB. 求解.
> >
> > ∵ AO 平分 $\angle BAD, OF \perp AD, \therefore OE = OF.$ ∵ OE 为 $\odot O$ 的半径, ∴ OF 为 $\odot O$ 的半径, ∴ $\odot O$ 与 AD 相切.
> >
> > (2)【解】设 $\odot O$ 的半径为 $r$ , 则 $OC = OE = OF = r$ . $\because$ 四边形 $ABCD$ 是平行四边形, $\therefore AD = BC$ , $AD \parallel BC$ , $\angle B = \angle D > 45^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle FAO = \angle AOB$ . $\because AO$ 平分 $\angle BAD$ , $\therefore \angle BAO = \angle FAO$ , $\therefore \angle BAO = \angle AOB$ , $\therefore AB = OB = 5$ . $\because DF = 7$ , $BO = 5$ , $\therefore AF + 7 = 5 + r$ , $\therefore AF = AE = r - 2$ , $\therefore BE = AB - AE = 7 - r$ . 在 Rt $\triangle OBE$ 中, $BE^2 + OE^2 = OB^2$ , $\therefore (7 - r)^2 + r^2 = 5^2$ , 解得 $r = 4$ 或 3. 当 $r = 4$ 时, $BE = 3$ , 当 $r = 3$ 时, $BE = 4$ . $\because \angle B > 45^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle BOE < 45^\circ$ , $\therefore OE > BE$ , $\therefore r = 4$ , 即 $OC = OE = OF = 4$ . $\because OF \perp AD, AD \parallel BC$ , $\therefore OF \perp BC$ , 即 $\angle COF = 90^\circ$ , $\therefore S_{\text{阴影}} = S_{\text{梯形}CDFO} - S_{\text{扇形}COF} = \frac{1}{2}(OC + DF) \times OF - \frac{90\pi \times OC^2}{360} = \frac{1}{2} \times (4 + 7) \times 4 - \frac{90\pi \times 4^2}{360} = 22 - 4\pi$ .
