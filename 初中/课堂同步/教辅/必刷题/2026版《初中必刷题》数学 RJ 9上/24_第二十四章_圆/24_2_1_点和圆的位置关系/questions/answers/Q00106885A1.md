> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 AB 与 OC 交于点 D. ∵ 弦 AB 的长为 $4\sqrt{3}$ , $OC \perp AB$ , ∴ $AD = BD = \frac{1}{2}AB = 2\sqrt{3}$ .
> >
> > ∵ $\angle ABC = 30^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle AOD = 2\angle ABC = 60^{\circ}$ ,
> >
> > ∴ $\angle A = 180^{\circ} - 90^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} = 30^{\circ}$ , ∴ OA = 2OD. 设 OD = x, 则 OA = 2x. 在 Rt△AOD 中, $OD^{2} + AD^{2} = OA^{2}$ , 即 $x^{2} + (2\sqrt{3})^{2} = (2x)^{2}$ , 解得 x = 2 (负值已舍去), ∴ OA = 2x = 4. ∵ OP = 5, ∴ OP > OA,
> >
> > ∴ 点 P 在 ⊙O 外.
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
