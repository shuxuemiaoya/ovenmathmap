> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $219^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 连接 AB. ∵ PA, PB 是 $\odot O$ 的切线, ∴ PA = PB. ∵ $\angle P = 102^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle PAB = \angle PBA = \frac{1}{2}(180^{\circ} - 102^{\circ}) = 39^{\circ}$ . ∵ $\angle DAB + \angle C = 180^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle PAD + \angle C = \angle PAB + \angle DAB + \angle C = 39^{\circ} + 180^{\circ} = 219^{\circ}$ , 故答案为 $219^{\circ}$ .
