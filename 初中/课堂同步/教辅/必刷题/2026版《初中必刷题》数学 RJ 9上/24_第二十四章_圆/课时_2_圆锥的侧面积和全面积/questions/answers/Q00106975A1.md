> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 OA = r. 由题意得  \( S\_{甲} = \pi \cdot \left( \frac{\pi x r}{180 \times 2\pi} \right)^{2} = \frac{\pi x^{2} r^{2}}{360^{2}}, S\_{乙} = \pi \cdot \left[ \frac{\pi r (164 - x)}{180 \times 2\pi} \right]^{2} = \frac{\pi (164 - x)^{2} r^{2}}{360^{2}}. \because S\_{乙} = 9S\_{甲}, \therefore \frac{\pi (164 - x)^{2} r^{2}}{360^{2}} = 9 \times \frac{\pi x^{2} r^{2}}{360^{2}}, \therefore (164 - x)^{2} = 9x^{2} \text{，解得 } x = 41 \text{（负值已舍去），
> >
> > 故选 **A**.}
