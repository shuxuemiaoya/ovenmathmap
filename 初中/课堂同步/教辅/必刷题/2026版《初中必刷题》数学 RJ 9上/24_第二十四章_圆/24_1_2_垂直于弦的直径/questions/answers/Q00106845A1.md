> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 分别作线段 BC, $A'D'$ 的垂直平分线 MH, NH, 两线的交点为 H 点, 连接 BH, 如图, $\therefore BM = \frac{1}{2} BC = \frac{5}{2}$ , $A'N = \frac{1}{2} A'D' = \frac{5}{2}$ , $\therefore HM = A'N - 1 = \frac{5}{2} - 1 = \frac{3}{2}$ , $\therefore BH = \sqrt{HM^{2} + BM^{2}} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{2} + \left(\frac{5}{2}\right)^{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{34}}{2}$ , 故选 C.
