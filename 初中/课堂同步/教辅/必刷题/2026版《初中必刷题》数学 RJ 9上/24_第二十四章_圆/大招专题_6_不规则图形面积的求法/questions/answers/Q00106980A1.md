> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意知 $AC = \sqrt{AB^{2} - BC^{2}} = \sqrt{(\sqrt{5})^{2} - 2^{2}} = 1$ ，则 BE = BF = AD = AC = 1。设 $\angle B = n^{\circ}, \angle A = m^{\circ}$ 。∵ $\angle ACB = 90^{\circ}, \therefore \angle B + \angle A = 90^{\circ}$ ，即 $n + m = 90, \therefore S_{阴影部分} = S_{\triangle ABC} - (S_{扇形EBF} + S_{扇形DAC}) = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 1 - \left( \frac{n\pi \times 1^{2}}{360} + \frac{m\pi \times 1^{2}}{360} \right) = 1 - \frac{(n+m)\pi}{360} = 1 - \frac{\pi}{4}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
