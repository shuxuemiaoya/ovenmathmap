>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】设抛物线的函数解析式为 $y=ax^{2}+bx+c$ .
> > 由题意得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a-b+c&=0,\\ c&=3,\\ 4a+2b+c&=-3,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=-2,\\ b&=1,\\ c&=3,\end{aligned}\right.$ 则抛物线的解析式为 $y=-2x^{2}+x+3$ .
