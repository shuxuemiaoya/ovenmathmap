> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 抛物线 $C: y = \frac{1}{2}(x - 1)^{2} - 1$ 沿水平方向向右（或向左）平移 $|m|$ 个单位长度得到抛物线 $C_{1}: y = \frac{1}{2}(x - m - 1)^{2} - 1, \therefore D(1, -1), D_{1}(m + 1, -1), \therefore Q$ 点的横坐标为 $\frac{m + 2}{2}$ ，代入 $y = \frac{1}{2}(x - 1)^{2} - 1$ ，得 $Q\left(\frac{m + 2}{2}, \frac{m^{2}}{8} - 1\right)$ .
> >
> > ∵ $\angle DQD_{1} = 60^{\circ}, \therefore$ 易知 $\triangle DQD_{1}$ 是等边三角形，∴ $QD = DD_{1} = |m|$ . 由勾股定理，得 $\left(\frac{m + 2}{2} - 1\right)^{2} + \left(\frac{m^{2}}{8} - 1 + 1\right)^{2} = m^{2}$ ，解得 $m = \pm 4\sqrt{3}$ (m = 0 已舍去).
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
