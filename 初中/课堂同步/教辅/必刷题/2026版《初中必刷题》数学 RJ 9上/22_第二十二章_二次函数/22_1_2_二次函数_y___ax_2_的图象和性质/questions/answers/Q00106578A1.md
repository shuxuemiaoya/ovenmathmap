> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 过点 $P_{i}(i,0)$ ( $i=1,2,\cdots,2024$ ) 作 x 轴的垂线，交直线 y=x 于点 $A_{i}$ ，交抛物线 $y=-x^{2}$ 于点 $B_{i},\therefore A_{i}(i,i),B_{i}(i,-i^{2})$ ，
> > ∴ $A_{i}B_{i}=i+i^{2},\therefore\frac{1}{A_{i}B_{i}}=\frac{1}{i+i^{2}}=\frac{1}{i(1+i)}=\frac{1}{i}-\frac{1}{1+i},$ ∴ $\frac{1}{A_{1}B_{1}} + \frac{1}{A_{2}B_{2}} + \cdots + \frac{1}{A_{2024}B_{2024}} = 1 - \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} + \cdots + \frac{1}{2024} - \frac{1}{2025} = 1 - \frac{1}{2025} = \frac{2024}{2025}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
