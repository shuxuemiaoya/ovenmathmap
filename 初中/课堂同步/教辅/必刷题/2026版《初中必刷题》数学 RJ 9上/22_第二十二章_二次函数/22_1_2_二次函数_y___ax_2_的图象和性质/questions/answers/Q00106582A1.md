> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{3}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵点 $A$ 的横坐标为-1, ∴将 $x = -1$ 分别代入 $y = \frac{1}{2} x^2$ , $y = -\frac{1}{4} x^2$ , 得 $y = \frac{1}{2} \times (-1)^2 = \frac{1}{2}$ , $y = -\frac{1}{4} \times (-1)^2 = -\frac{1}{4}$ , ∴ $A(-1, \frac{1}{2}), B(-1, -\frac{1}{4})$ , ∴ $AB = \frac{1}{2} - (-\frac{1}{4}) = \frac{3}{4}$ . 根据抛物线的对称性, 得 $AD = 1 \times 2 = 2$ , $S_{\text{阴影}} = \frac{1}{2} S_{\text{矩形}ABCD}$ , ∴ $S_{\text{阴影}} = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{3}{4}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{3}{4}$ .
