> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 抛物线 $y=ax^{2}+bx+c(a>0)$ 的对称轴为直线 x=-2, $\therefore -\frac{b}{2a}=-2$ , $\therefore b=4a$ . $\because m=a+b, n=a-b, \therefore m=a+b=a+4a=5a, n=a-b=a-4a=-3a, \therefore m-n=5a-(-3a)=8a.$ $\because a>0, \therefore m-n=8a>0, \therefore m>n.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
