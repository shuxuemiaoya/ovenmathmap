> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $y=\frac{1}{4}x^{2}-x+3=-\frac{1}{4}(x^{2}+4x)+3=-\frac{1}{4}(x^{2}+4x+4-4)+3=-\frac{1}{4}(x+2)^{2}+4,\therefore k=$ 4, 故答案为 4.
