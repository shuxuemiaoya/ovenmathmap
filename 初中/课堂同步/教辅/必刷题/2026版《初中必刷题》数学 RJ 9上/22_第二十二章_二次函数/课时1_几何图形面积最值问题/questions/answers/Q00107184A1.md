> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设运动时间为 x s, 四边形 APQC 的面积为 $y \, cm^{2}$ , 则 AP = x cm, $BQ = 2x \, cm$ ,
> >
> > ∴ $BP = (4 - x) \, \text{cm}$ , ∴ $y = S_{\triangle ABC} - S_{\triangle PBQ} = \frac{1}{2} BC \cdot AB - \frac{1}{2} BQ \cdot BP$ , 即 $y = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 4 - \frac{1}{2} \times 2x (4 - x) = x^{2} - 4x + 16 = (x - 2)^{2} + 12$ , ∴ 当 x = 2 时, y 有最小值, 为 12,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
