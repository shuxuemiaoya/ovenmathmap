> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设△PCD 的面积为 $y \, cm^{2}$ . 由题意得 AP = t, PD = 5 - t, ∴ $y = \frac{1}{2} CD \cdot PD = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times (5 - t) = 5 - t$ . ∵ 四边形 EFPC 是正方形, ∴ $S_{\triangle DEF} + S_{\triangle PDC} = \frac{1}{2} S_{\text {正方形} EFPC}$ . ∵ $PC^{2} = PD^{2} + CD^{2} = 2^{2} + (5 - t)^{2} = t^{2} - 10t + 29$ , ∴ $S_{\triangle DEF} = \frac{1}{2}(t^{2} - 10t + 29) - (5 - t) = \frac{1}{2} t^{2} - 4t + \frac{19}{2} = \frac{1}{2}(t - 4)^{2} + \frac{3}{2}$ , ∴ 当 t = 4 时, △DEF 的面积最小, 为 $\frac{3}{2} \, cm^{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
