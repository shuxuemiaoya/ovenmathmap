> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $BE = AD$ . 证明: $\because \triangle C'DE$ 绕点 $C$ 按顺时针方向旋转 $20^{\circ}, \therefore \angle BCE = \angle ACD = 20^{\circ}$ . $\because \triangle ABC$ 与 $\triangle C'DE$ 是等边三角形, $\therefore CA = CB, CE = CD, \therefore \triangle BCE \cong \triangle ACD, \therefore BE = AD$ . (2) 是. 证明: $\because \triangle C'DE$ 绕点 $C$ 按顺时针方向旋转的角度为 $\alpha, \therefore \angle BCE = \angle ACD = \alpha$ . $\because \triangle ABC$ 与 $\triangle C'DE$ 是等边三角形, $\therefore CA = CB, CE = CD, \therefore \triangle BCE \cong \triangle ACD, \therefore BE = AD$ .
