> >
> > (3) 先利用勾股定理的逆定理证明 $\triangle PCQ$ 是直角三角形, 得 $\angle QPC = 90^{\circ}$ , 由 $\triangle PBQ$ 是等边三角形得 $\angle BPQ = 60^{\circ}$ , 然后计算 $\angle BPQ + \angle QPC$ 的度数即可.
> >
> > $\therefore \angle PBQ = \angle ABC.\because \triangle ABC$ 为等边三角形， $\therefore \angle PBQ = \angle ABC = 60^{\circ},\therefore$ 旋转角为 $60^{\circ}$ ，故答案为60.
> >
> > (2) 连接 $PQ$ . $\because \triangle ABC$ 是等边三角形, $\therefore \angle ABC = 60^{\circ}$ . $\because \triangle QCB$ 是 $\triangle PAB$ 绕点 $B$ 逆时针旋转得到的, $\therefore BP = BQ = 4$ , $\angle PBQ = \angle ABC = 60^{\circ}$ , $\therefore \triangle PBQ$ 是等边三角形, $\therefore PQ = PB = 4$ , 即点 $P$ 与点 $Q$ 之间的距离是 4. (3) 由旋转得 $QC = 5$ , $PQ = 4$ , 又 $\because PC = 3$ , 而 $3^{2} + 4^{2} = 5^{2}$ , $\therefore PC^{2} + PQ^{2} = CQ^{2}$ , $\therefore \triangle PCQ$ 是直角三角形, 且 $\angle QPC = 90^{\circ}$ . $\because \triangle PBQ$ 是等边三角形, $\therefore \angle BPQ = 60^{\circ}$ , $\therefore \angle BPC = \angle BPQ + \angle QPC = 60^{\circ} + 90^{\circ} = 150^{\circ}$ .
