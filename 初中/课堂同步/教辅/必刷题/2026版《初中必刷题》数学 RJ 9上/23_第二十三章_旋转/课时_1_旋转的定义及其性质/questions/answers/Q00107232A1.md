> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\angle B = 30^{\circ}$ , $\angle C = 90^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle CAB = 180^{\circ} - \angle B - \angle C = 60^{\circ}$ . ∵ 将直角三角板 ABC 绕顶点 A 顺时针旋转到 $\triangle AB'C'$ , ∴ $\angle C'AB' = \angle CAB = 60^{\circ}$ . ∵ 点 $B'$ 恰好落在 CA 的延长线上, ∴ $\angle BAC' = 180^{\circ} - \angle CAB - \angle C'AB' = 60^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
