> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 将 $\triangle ABC$ 绕点 A 顺时针旋转 $60^{\circ}$ 后得到 $\triangle ADE, \therefore AC = AE, BC = DE, AB = AD, \angle BAD = \angle CAE = 60^{\circ}, \angle C = \angle E$ ，故选项 A, C, D 正确，不符合题意；∵ $\angle BAC = \angle BAD + \angle DAC = 60^{\circ} + \angle DAC, \therefore \angle BAC$ 不等于 $60^{\circ}$ ，故 B 选项错误，符合题意。故选 B.
