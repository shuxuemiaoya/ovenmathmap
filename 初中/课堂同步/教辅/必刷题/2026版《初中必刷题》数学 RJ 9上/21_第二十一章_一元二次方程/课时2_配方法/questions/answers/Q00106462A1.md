> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 原方程可化为 $x^{2}-2x-3=m,\therefore(x-1)^{2}=4+m,\therefore x-1=\pm\sqrt{4+m},\therefore x=1\pm\sqrt{4+m}.$ $\because m>0,\therefore 4+m>4,\therefore \sqrt{4+m}>2.\because \alpha<\beta,\therefore \alpha=1-\sqrt{4+m}<-1,\beta=1+\sqrt{4+m}>3,$ 即 $\alpha<-1$ 且 $\beta>3,$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
