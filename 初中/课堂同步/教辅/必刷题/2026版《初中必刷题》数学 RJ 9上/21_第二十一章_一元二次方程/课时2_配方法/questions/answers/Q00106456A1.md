> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $2x^{2}-2x=1$ , ∴ $x^{2}-x+\frac{1}{4}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}$ ,
> > ∴ $\left(x-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}=\frac{3}{4},\therefore x-\frac{1}{2}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\therefore x_{1}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},$ $x_{2}=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\therefore1<x_{1}<2,$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
