>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 6
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ Rt△ABC 中，∠BAC=90°，AB=AC=16 cm，AD 为 BC 边上的高，∴ ∠ABC=∠ACB=45°，AD=BD=CD=8√2 cm，∠ADC=90°。又∵ AP=√2 t cm，∴ S₁=½AP·BD=½×√2 t×8√2 = 8t (cm²)，PD=(8√2-√2 t) cm。
> > ∵ PE∥BC，∴ ∠AEP = ∠C = 45°，∠APE = ∠ADC = 90°，∴ ∠PAE = ∠PEA = 45°，∴ PE = AP = √2 t cm，∴ S₂ = PD · PE = (8√2 - √2 t) · √2 t = (16t - 2t²) cm²。∵ S₁ = 2S₂，∴ 8t = 2(16t - 2t²)，解得 t = 6 或 t = 0（舍去），故答案为 6。
