> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $3, \frac{13}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意得 $S_{1} = S_{\text{长方形}ABCD} - S_{\text{大正方形}} - S_{\text{小正方形}} + S_{\text{大、小正方形重叠部分}}, \therefore S_{1} = 6 \times 5 - a^{2} - b^{2} + (a + b - 6)b = -a^{2} + ab - 6b + 30.$ 同理可得， $S_{2} = S_{\text{长方形}ABCD} - S_{\text{大正方形}} - S_{\text{小正方形}} + S_{\text{大、小正方形重叠部分}}, \therefore S_{2} = 6 \times 5 - a^{2} - b^{2} + (a + b - 5)b = -a^{2} + ab - 5b + 30, \therefore S_{2} - S_{1} = (-a^{2} + ab - 5b + 30) - (-a^{2} + ab - 6b + 30) = b.$ 又 $\because S_{2} - S_{1} = 3, \therefore b = 3.$ 又 $\because S_{1} = 8, \therefore -a^{2} + ab - 6b + 30 = 8.$ 将 $b = 3$ 代入 $-a^{2} + ab - 6b + 30 = 8$ ，得 $-a^{2} + 3a - 6 \times 3 + 30 = 8$ ，即 $a^{2} - 3a - 4 = 0$ ，解得 $a_{1} = 4, a_{2} = -1$ （舍去）， $\therefore a = 4.$ 根据题意，得 $S_{3} = S_{\text{大正方形}} + S_{\text{小正方形}} - S_{\text{左上空白直角三角形}} - S_{\text{右下空白直角三角形}}, \therefore S_{3} = a^{2} + b^{2} - \frac{1}{2}(a + b)b - \frac{1}{2}a^{2} = \frac{1}{2}a^{2} + \frac{1}{2}b^{2} - \frac{1}{2}ab = \frac{1}{2} \times 4^{2} + \frac{1}{2} \times 3^{2} - \frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 3 = \frac{13}{2}$ . 故答案为 $3, \frac{13}{2}$ .
