> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知 BC = BD = m, AC = 6. 在 Rt△ACB 中，∠ACB = 90°, ∴ AB = $\sqrt{BC^{2} + AC^{2}} = \sqrt{m^{2} + 36}$ , ∴ AD = AB - BD = $\sqrt{m^{2} + 36} - m$ .
> >
> > ∵ $\frac{S_{\triangle BCD}}{S_{\triangle ACD}} = \frac{5}{8}$ , ∴ $\frac{BD}{AD} = \frac{5}{8}$ , 即 $\frac{m}{\sqrt{m^{2} + 36} - m} = \frac{5}{8}$ ,
> >
> > ∴ $169m^{2} = 25(m^{2} + 36)$ , 解得 m = 2.5 或 m = -2.5, 经检验, m = 2.5 是原方程的解, 且符合题意.
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
