> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $4x^{2} + 6x + 1 = 0$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设一元二次方程为 $ax^2 + bx + c = 0 (a \neq 0)$ , 则 $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$ , 与 $x = \frac{-6 \pm \sqrt{6^2 - 4 \times 4 \times 1}}{2 \times 4}$ 对比, $\therefore a = 4, b = 6, c = 1$ , $\therefore$ 该一元二次方程为 $4x^{2} + 6x + 1 = 0$ . 故答案为 $4x^{2} + 6x + 1 = 0$ .
