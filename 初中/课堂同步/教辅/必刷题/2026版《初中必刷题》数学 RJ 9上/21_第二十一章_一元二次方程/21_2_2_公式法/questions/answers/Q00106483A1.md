> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\triangle ABC$ 是等腰三角形, 理由: $\because x = 1$ 是方程的一个根, $\therefore (a + b) - 2c + (a - b) = 0$ , $\therefore a + b - 2c + a - b = 0$ , $\therefore a - c = 0$ , $\therefore a = c$ , $\therefore \triangle ABC$ 是等腰三角形.
> > (2) 若 $\triangle ABC$ 是等边三角形, 则 $a = b = c \neq 0$ , 原方程可化为 $2ax^{2} - 2ax = 0$ , $\therefore x^{2} - x = 0$ , 由求根公式得 $x_{1} = 0, x_{2} = 1$ .
