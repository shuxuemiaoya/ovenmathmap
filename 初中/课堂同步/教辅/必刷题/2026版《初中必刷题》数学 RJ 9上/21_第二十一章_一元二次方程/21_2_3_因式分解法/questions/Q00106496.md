①拆分二次项与常数项: $x^{2}=x\cdot x,-35=(-5)\times(+7)$ .

$$
\begin{array}{l} x \\ x \end{array} \times \begin{array}{l} + 7 = 7 x \\ - 5 = - 5 x \end{array}
