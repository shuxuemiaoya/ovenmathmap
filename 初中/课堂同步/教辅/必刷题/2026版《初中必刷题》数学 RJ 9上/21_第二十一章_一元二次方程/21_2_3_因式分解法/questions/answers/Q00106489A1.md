> > 【解】(1)因式分解,得 $(y+7)(y-8)=0,\therefore y+7=0$ 或y-8=0, $\therefore y_{1}=-7,y_{2}=8.$ (2) 因式分解, 得 $[(x+2)+2(x-3)][(x+2)-2(x-3)]=0,\therefore(3x-4)(-x+8)=0,\therefore3x-4=0$ 或 $-x+8=0,\therefore x_{1}=\frac{4}{3},x_{2}=8.$
> >
> > (3) 整理得 $3y^{2}-2\sqrt{3}y+1=0$ ，因式分解，得 $(\sqrt{3}y-1)^{2}=0,\therefore y_{1}=y_{2}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$
