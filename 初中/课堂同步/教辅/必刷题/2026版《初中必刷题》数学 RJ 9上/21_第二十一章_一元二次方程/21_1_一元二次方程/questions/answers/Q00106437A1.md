> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $x_{1} = -4, x_{2} = -1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) $\because$ 方程 $a(x + k)^{2} + 2023 = 0$ 的解是 $x_{1} = -2, x_{2} = 1, \therefore a(x + k + 2)^{2} + 2023 = 0$ 中 $x + 2 = -2$ 或 $x + 2 = 1$ , 解得 $x_{1} = -4, x_{2} = -1$ , 故答案为 $x_{1} = -4, x_{2} = -1$ .
> >
> > (2) $\because$ 方程 $a(x + k)^{2} + 2023 = 0$ 的解是 $x_{1} = -2, x_{2} = 1, \therefore a(x - k + 2)^{2} + 2023 = 0$ 中 $x + 2 = 2$ 或 $x + 2 = -1, \therefore x_{1} = 0, x_{2} = -3$ , 故答案为 $x_{1} = 0, x_{2} = -3$ .
> >
> > (3) $\because$ 方程 $a(x + k)^{2} + 2023 = 0$ 的解是 $x_{1} = -2, x_{2} = 1, \therefore a(2x + k + 1)^{2} + 2023 = 0$ 中 $2x + 1 = -2$ 或 $2x + 1 = 1$ , 解得 $x_{1} = -\frac{3}{2}, x_{2} = 0$ , 故答案为 $x_{1} = -\frac{3}{2}, x_{2} = 0$ .
