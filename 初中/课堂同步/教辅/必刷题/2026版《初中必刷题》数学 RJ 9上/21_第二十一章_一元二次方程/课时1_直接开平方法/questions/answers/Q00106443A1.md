> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得两根不相等. $\because x^{2} = \frac{b}{a}$ , $\therefore x = \pm \sqrt{\frac{b}{a}}$ , $\therefore$ 方程的两个根互为相反数, $\therefore m + 1 + 2m - 4 = 0$ , 解得 $m = 1$ , $\therefore$ 一元二次方程 $ax^{2} = b$ 的两个根分别是 2 与 -2, $\therefore \sqrt{\frac{b}{a}} = 2$ , $\therefore \frac{b}{a} = 4$ . 故答案为 4.
