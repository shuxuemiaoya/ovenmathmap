> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $BE \parallel AD, \therefore \triangle BCE \sim \triangle ACD,$ ∴ $\frac{CB}{CA} = \frac{CE}{CD}$ ，即 $\frac{BC}{AB + BC} = \frac{EC}{EC + DE}$ 。又 BC = 1，
> >
> > DE = 1.8, EC = 1.2, ∴ $\frac{1}{AB + 1} = \frac{1.2}{1.2 + 1.8}, \therefore AB = 1.5 \, m.$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
