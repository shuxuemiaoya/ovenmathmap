> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可知, $\triangle A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$ 与 $\triangle ABC$ 是位似图形, 且相似比为 $\frac{2}{2 + 3} = \frac{2}{5}, \therefore \triangle A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$ 的面积是 $60 \div \left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^{2} = 375 (\mathrm{~cm}^{2})$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
