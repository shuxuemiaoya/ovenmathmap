> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)将 $A(-3,0)$ 代入 $y = x + m$ 得 $-3 + m = 0$ , 解得 $m = 3, \therefore y = x + 3$ . 将 $B(n,4)$ 代入 $y = x + 3$ 得 $n + 3 = 4$ , 解得 $n = 1, \therefore B(1,4)$ . 将 $B(1,4)$ 代入 $y = \frac{k}{x}$ 得 $k = 1 \times 4 = 4$ . (2) $a > 1$ . $S_{\triangle AOB} = \frac{1}{2} AO \times |y_B| = \frac{1}{2} \times 3 \times 4 = 6, S_{\triangle AOC} = \frac{1}{2} AO \times |y_C| = \frac{3}{2} y_C$ , 由题意得 $\frac{3}{2} y_C < 6, \therefore y_C < 4, \therefore a > 1$ .
