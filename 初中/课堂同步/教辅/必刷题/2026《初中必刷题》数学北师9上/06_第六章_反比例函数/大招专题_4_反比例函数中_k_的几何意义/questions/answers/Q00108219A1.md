> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $PC \perp x$ 轴, $PD \perp y$ 轴, ∴ $S_{\triangle AOC} = S_{\triangle BOD} = \frac{1}{2} \times |1| = \frac{1}{2}$ , $S_{矩形PCOD} = |2| = 2$ , ∴ 四边形 PAOB 的面积为 $2 - 2 \times \frac{1}{2} = 1$ .
