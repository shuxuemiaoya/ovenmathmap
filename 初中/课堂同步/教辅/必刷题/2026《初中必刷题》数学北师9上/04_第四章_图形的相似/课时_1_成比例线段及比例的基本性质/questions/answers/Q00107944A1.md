> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{3}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意得 $4x = 3y$ . $\because y \neq 0$ , $\therefore \frac{x}{y} = \frac{3}{4}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{3}{4}$ .
