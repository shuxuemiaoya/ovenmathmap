> > 【解】(1)能成比例.∵在□ABCD中, $DE\perp AB,BF\perp AD,\therefore S_{\square ABCD}=AB\cdot DE=AD\cdot BF,\therefore AB\cdot DE=BC\cdot BF$ ,即 $\frac{AB}{BC}=\frac{BF}{DE},\therefore AB,BC,BF,DE$ 四条线段成比例.
> > (2)∵ $AB \cdot DE = BC \cdot BF$ ,∴ $10 \times 2.5 = 5BC$ ,解得BC=5.
