> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} = \frac{e}{f} = \frac{1}{3}$ , ∴ $\frac{3a}{3b} = \frac{-2c}{-2d} = \frac{e}{f} = \frac{1}{3}$ , ∴ $\frac{3a - 2c + e}{3b - 2d + f} = \frac{1}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
