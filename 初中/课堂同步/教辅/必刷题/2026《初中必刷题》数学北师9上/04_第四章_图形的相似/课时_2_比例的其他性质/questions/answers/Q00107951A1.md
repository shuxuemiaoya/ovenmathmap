> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{5}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\frac{a - b}{a} = \frac{1}{3}$ , 得 $1 - \frac{b}{a} = \frac{1}{3}, \therefore \frac{b}{a} = a, \therefore a = \frac{3}{2} b,$ $\frac{2}{3}, \therefore \frac{a}{b} = \frac{3}{2}, \therefore \frac{a + b}{b} = \frac{3 + 2}{2} = \frac{5}{2}$ , 故答案为 $\frac{5}{2}$ .
