> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\frac{x}{2} = \frac{y}{7} = \frac{z}{5} \neq 0$ , ∴ $\frac{x + y - z}{4} = \frac{x}{2}$ , $\frac{x+x+z}{9}=\frac{x}{2},\therefore x+y-z=2x,2x+z=\frac{9}{2}x,$ ∴ $\frac{x+y-z}{2x+z}=\frac{2x}{9}\frac{1}{2}x=\frac{4}{9}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
