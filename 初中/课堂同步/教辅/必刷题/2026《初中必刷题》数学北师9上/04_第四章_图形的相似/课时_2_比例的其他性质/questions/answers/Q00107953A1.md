> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $\frac{a}{2} = \frac{b}{3} = \frac{c}{4} = k \neq 0$ , 则 $a = 2k, b = 3k, c = 4k$ . $\because a + 2b = 16, \therefore 2k + 6k = 16$ , 解得 $k = 2, \therefore c = 4 \times 2 = 8$ . 故选 C.
> >
> > 另解 $\because \frac{a}{2} = \frac{b}{3} = \frac{c}{4} \neq 0, \therefore \frac{a + 2b}{2 + 2 \times 3} = \frac{c}{4}$ . 又 $\because a + 2b = 16, \therefore \frac{16}{8} = \frac{c}{4}, \therefore c = 8$ . 此类题用“设 $k$ 法”求解更简便.
