> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \frac{2}{3} m = \frac{5}{6} n (n \neq 0), \therefore \frac{m}{n} = \frac{5}{4}, \therefore \frac{m - n}{n} = \frac{5 - 4}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$ .
