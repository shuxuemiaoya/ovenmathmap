> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \frac{x}{2} = \frac{y}{3} = \frac{z}{4} \neq 0, \therefore \frac{x + y - z}{2 + 3 - 4} = x + y - z = \frac{x}{2}, \frac{x + y + z}{2 + 3 + 4} = \frac{x + y + z}{9} = \frac{x}{2}$ , 则 $x + y + z = \frac{9x}{2}$ . $\therefore \frac{x + y - z}{x + y + z} = \frac{\frac{x}{2}}{\frac{9x}{2}} = \frac{1}{9}$ .
