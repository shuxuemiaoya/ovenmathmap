> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\angle BAE$ 与 $\angle CAD$ 相等. 理由如下: $\because \frac{AB}{AE} = \frac{BC}{ED} = \frac{AC}{AD}, \therefore \triangle ABC \sim \triangle AED, \therefore \angle BAC = \angle EAD, \therefore \angle BAE = \angle CAD.$ (2) $\triangle ABE$ 与 $\triangle ACD$ 相似. 理由: $\because \frac{AB}{AE} = \frac{AC}{AD}, \therefore \frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{AE}{AD}$ . 在 $\triangle ABE$ 与 $\triangle ACD$ 中, $\frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{AE}{AD}, \angle BAE = \angle CAD, \therefore \triangle ABE \sim \triangle ACD.$
