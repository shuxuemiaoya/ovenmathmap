> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 45°
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设正方形的边长为 a. 由勾股定理得 $AC^{2}=a^{2}+a^{2}=2a^{2}, \therefore AC=\sqrt{2}a.$ 同理可得 $AF=\sqrt{5}a, AG=\sqrt{10}a.$ $\because \frac{\sqrt{2}a}{2a}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}a}=\frac{\sqrt{5}a}{\sqrt{10}a}$ ，即 $\frac{AC}{CG}=\frac{CF}{AC}=\frac{AF}{AG}, \therefore \triangle ACF \sim \triangle GCA, \therefore \angle 2 = \angle CAF.$ $\because \angle ACB = \angle CAF + \angle 1 = 45^{\circ}, \therefore \angle 1 + \angle 2 = 45^{\circ}.$
