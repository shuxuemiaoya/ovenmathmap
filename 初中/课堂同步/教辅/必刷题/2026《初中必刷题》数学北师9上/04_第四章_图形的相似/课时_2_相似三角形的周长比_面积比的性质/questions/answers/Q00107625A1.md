> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\because \frac{CB}{CD} = \frac{CA}{CB} = \frac{3}{2}$ , 且 $\angle C = \angle C$ , $\therefore \triangle BCD \sim \triangle ACB, \therefore \frac{C_{\triangle ABC}}{C_{\triangle BCD}} = \frac{AC}{BC} = \frac{3}{2},$ $\therefore \triangle ABC$ 的周长为 $\frac{3}{2} \times 24 = 36 (\mathrm{~cm})$ . (2) $\because \triangle BCD \sim \triangle ACB, \therefore \frac{S_{\triangle BCD}}{S_{\triangle ABC}} = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9},$ $\therefore \triangle BCD$ 与 $\triangle ABD$ 的面积比为4:5.
