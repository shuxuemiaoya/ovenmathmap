> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 16
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 在□ABCD 中, CD = AB = 10, BC = AD = 15, ∠BAD 的平分线交 BC 于点 E, ∴ AB // DF, ∠BAF = ∠DAF, ∴ ∠BAF = ∠F, ∴ ∠DAF = ∠F, ∴ DF = AD = 15, ∴ CF = DF - CD = 15 - 10 = 5. 同理, BE = AB = 10. 在 Rt△ABG 中, BG ⊥ AE, AB = 10, BG = 8, ∴ AG = $\sqrt{AB^2 - BG^2} = \sqrt{100 - 64} = 6$ , ∴ AE = 2AG = 12, ∴ △ABE 的周长为 AB + BE + AE = 10 + 10 + 12 = 32. ∵ ∠BAF = ∠F, ∠AEB = ∠CEF, ∴ △CEF ∼ △BEA, 且相似比为 CF : AB = 5 : 10 = 1 : 2, ∴ C△CEF : C△BEA = 1 : 2, ∴ C△CEF : 32 = 1 : 2, ∴ △CEF 的周长为 16.
