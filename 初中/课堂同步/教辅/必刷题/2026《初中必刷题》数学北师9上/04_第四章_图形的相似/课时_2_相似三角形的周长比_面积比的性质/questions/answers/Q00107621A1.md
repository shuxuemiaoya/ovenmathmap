> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $S_{\triangle ABC}:S_{四边形BDEC}=1:3,\therefore S_{\triangle ABC}:$ $S_{\triangle ADE}=1:4.\because\triangle ABC\sim\triangle ADE,\therefore\left(\frac{BC}{DE}\right)^{2}=\frac{1}{4},$ $\therefore \frac{BC}{DE} = \frac{1}{2}$ （负值已舍去）. ∵ BC = $\sqrt{2}$ , ∴ DE = $2\sqrt{2}$ .
> >
