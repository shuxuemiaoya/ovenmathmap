> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 四边形 ABCD 为平行四边形，
> > ∴ $S_{\triangle PBC} = \frac{1}{2} S_{\square ABCD}$ . ∵ M, N 分别是 PB, PC 的中点, $\therefore MN // BC, MN = \frac{1}{2} BC, \therefore$ 易得 $\triangle PMN \sim \triangle PBC, \therefore \frac{S_{\triangle PMN}}{S_{\triangle PBC}} = \left(\frac{MN}{BC}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}, \therefore S_{\text{四边形 } BMNC} = \frac{3}{4} S_{\triangle PBC}$ . 又 $\because S_{\text{四边形 } BMNC} = 6, \therefore S_{\triangle PBC} = 8,$ $\therefore S_{\square ABCD} = 16.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
