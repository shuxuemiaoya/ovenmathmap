> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because AC \parallel BD, \therefore \triangle AOC \sim \triangle BOD,$ $\therefore \frac{OA + OC + AC}{OB + OD + BD} = \frac{AC}{BD}.\quad \because \frac{OA + OC + AC}{OB + OD + BD} = \frac{1}{2},$ $\therefore \frac{AC}{BD} = \frac{1}{2}$ , 故答案为 $\frac{1}{2}$ .
