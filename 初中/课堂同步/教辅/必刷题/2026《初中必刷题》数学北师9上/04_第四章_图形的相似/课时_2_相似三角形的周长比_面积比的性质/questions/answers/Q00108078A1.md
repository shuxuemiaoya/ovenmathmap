> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 5 个正方形中的小正方形的边长为 $a$ ，大正方形的边长为 $b$ ，则 $AG = b, BG = b + a, BE = 2b - a, CE = 2b, \therefore AB = 2b + a, BC = AD = 2b + 2b - a = 4b - a$ . ∵ 矩形 $BEFG \sim$ 矩形 $ABCD, \therefore \frac{BG}{AD} = \frac{BE}{AB}$ ，即 $\frac{b + a}{4b - a} = \frac{2b - a}{2b + a}, \therefore b = \frac{3}{2}a,$ ∴ $BG = b + a = \frac{5}{2}a, AD = 4b - a = 5a$ . ∵ 矩形 $BEFG \sim$ 矩形 $ABCD, \therefore \frac{S_{\text{矩形}BEFG}}{S_{\text{矩形}ABCD}} = \left(\frac{BG}{AD}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{\frac{5}{2}a}{\frac{5a}{5a}}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{1}{4}$ .
