> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2 \sqrt{5} + 4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because BC = AC + 2, \therefore BC > AC,$ $\therefore BC = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2} AB, AC = AB - BC = \frac{3 - \sqrt{5}}{2} AB,$ $\therefore \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2} AB = \frac{3 - \sqrt{5}}{2} AB + 2, \therefore (\sqrt{5} - 2) AB = 2,$ $\therefore AB = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5} - 2} = \frac{2(\sqrt{5} + 2)}{(\sqrt{5} - 2)(\sqrt{5} + 2)} = 2\sqrt{5} + 4.$ 故答案为 $2 \sqrt{5} + 4.$
> >
