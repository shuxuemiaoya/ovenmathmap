> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** BC
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】∵ $\angle A = 36^{\circ}, AB = AC, \therefore \angle ABC = \angle C = 72^{\circ}$ . 又∵ BD 平分 $\angle ABC, \therefore \angle DBC = \angle ABD = 36^{\circ} = \angle A, \therefore AD = BD. \because \angle BDC = \angle A + \angle ABD = 72^{\circ} = \angle C, \therefore BD = BC, \therefore AD = BD = BC.$ 在 $\triangle ABC$ 与 $\triangle BDC$ 中，∵ $\angle A = \angle DBC, \angle C = \angle C, \therefore \triangle ABC \sim \triangle BDC, \therefore \frac{AC}{BC} = \frac{BC}{DC}$ ，即 $BC^{2} = AC \cdot DC$ ，即 $AD^{2} = AC \cdot DC, \therefore$ 点 D 是线段 AC 的黄金分割点.
> > (2)【解】设 AD = x，则 DC = 1 - x。由(1)中的结论，得 $x^{2} = 1 - x$ 。解得 $x = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} - \frac{1}{2}$ 或 $x = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} - \frac{1}{2}$ （舍去），∴ $AD = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}$ 。
