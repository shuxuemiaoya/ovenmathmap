> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】(1)∵ $AF^{2}=FG\cdot FE,\therefore\frac{AF}{FE}=\frac{FG}{AF}.$ ∵ $\angle AFG=\angle EFA,\therefore\triangle AFG\sim\triangle EFA,$ ∴ $\angle FAG=\angle E,$ 即 $\angle DAC=\angle E.$ ∵ $AE\parallel BC,\therefore\angle CBG=\angle E,\therefore\angle DAC=\angle CBG.$ 又∵ $\angle C=\angle C,\therefore\triangle CAD\sim\triangle CBG.$ (2)由(1)知 $\triangle CAD\sim\triangle CBG,\therefore\frac{CA}{CB}=\frac{CD}{CG}.$ ∵ $\angle DCG=\angle ACB,\therefore\triangle CDG\sim\triangle CAB,\therefore\frac{DG}{AB}=$ $\frac{CG}{CB}.\because AE\parallel BC,\therefore$ 易证 $\triangle AGE\sim\triangle CGB,$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{AE}{CB} = \frac{AG}{GC}, \therefore \frac{AG}{AE} = \frac{CG}{CB}, \therefore \frac{DG}{AB} = \frac{AG}{AE},$ $\therefore DG\cdot AE = AB\cdot AG.$
