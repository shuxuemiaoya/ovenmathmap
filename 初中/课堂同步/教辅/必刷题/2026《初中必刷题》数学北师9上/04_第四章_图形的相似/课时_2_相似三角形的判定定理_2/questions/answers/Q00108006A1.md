> > 【证明】∵ $\angle AEC + \angle ACB = 180^{\circ}$ ， $\angle AEC + \angle AED = 180^{\circ}$ ，∴ $\angle AED = \angle ACB$ 。∵ $\angle DAE = \angle BAC$ ，∴ $\triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC$ ，∴ $\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{AE}{AC}$ ，∴ $\frac{AD}{AE} = \frac{AB}{AC}$ 。∵ $\angle DAE = \angle BAC$ ，即 $\angle DAB + \angle BAE =$ $\angle BAE + \angle EAC, \therefore \angle DAB = \angle EAC,$ $\therefore \triangle DAB \sim \triangle EAC.$
