> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 连接 DE. ∵ $AD = \frac{1}{3} AB, AE = \frac{1}{3} AC,$ ∴ $\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{AE}{AC} = \frac{1}{3}.$ 又∵ $\angle DAE = \angle BAC, \therefore \triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC, \therefore \frac{DE}{BC} = \frac{1}{3}, \angle ADE = \angle ABC, \therefore DE // BC, \therefore \angle OED = \angle OBC, \angle ODE = \angle OCB,$ ∴ $\triangle DOE \sim \triangle COB, \therefore \frac{DE}{BC} = \frac{OE}{OB} = \frac{1}{3}, \therefore S_{\triangle COE}: S_{\triangle BOC} = \frac{1}{3}.$ 故选 D.
> >
> > 用判定定理2判定两个三角形相似时，一定要找准对应角以及夹对应角的两边.
