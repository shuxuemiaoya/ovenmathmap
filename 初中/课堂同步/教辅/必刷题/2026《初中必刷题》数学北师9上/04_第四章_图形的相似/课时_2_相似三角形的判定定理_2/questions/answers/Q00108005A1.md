>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\angle D = 60^{\circ}$ , $\angle E = 80^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle F = 40^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle F = \angle A$ . ∵ $\frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{FD}{FE}$ , ∴ $\triangle BCA \sim \triangle DEF$ , ∴ $\angle B$ 与 $\angle D$ 是对应角, ∴ $\angle B = \angle D = 60^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
