> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** EF
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > : 将矩形沿 $AC$ 折叠, 点 $B$ 落在点 $E$ 处, $\therefore \angle BAC = \angle EAC, AE = AB = CD$ . $\because AB // CD, \therefore \angle DCA = \angle BAC, \therefore \angle EAC = \angle DCA$ . 设 $AE$ 与 $CD$ 相交于 $F$ , 则 $AF = CF, \therefore AE - AF = CD - CF$ , 即 $EF = DF, \therefore \frac{EF}{AF} = \frac{DF}{FC}$ . 又 $\because \angle AFC = \angle EFD, \therefore \triangle ACF \sim \triangle EDF, \therefore \frac{DF}{FC} = \frac{DE}{AC} = \frac{3}{5}$ . 设 $DF = 3x, FC = 5x$ , 则 $AF = 5x$ . 在 Rt $\triangle ADF$ 中, $AD = \sqrt{AF^2 - DF^2} = 4x$ . 又 $\because AB = CD = DF + FC = 3x + 5x = 8x, \therefore \frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{4x}{8x} = \frac{1}{2}$ .
> >
> > ## 思路分析
> >
> > 设 AE 与 CD 相交于 F, 根据“两边成比例且夹角相等的两个三角形相似”证明 $\triangle ACF \sim \triangle EDF$ ,
> > 得出 $\frac{DF}{FC}=\frac{3}{5}$ .
> > 设 DF = 3x,
> > FC = 5x, 用含
> > x 的式子表示
> > 出 AD, AB 的
> > 长即可得解.
