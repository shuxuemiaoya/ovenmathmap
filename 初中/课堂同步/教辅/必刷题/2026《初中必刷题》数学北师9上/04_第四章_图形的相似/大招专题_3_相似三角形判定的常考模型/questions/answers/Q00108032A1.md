> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $3$ 或 $\frac{4}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because D$ 为 $AC$ 中点, $\therefore AD = \frac{1}{2} AC = 2$ . 当 $\frac{AE}{AD} = \frac{AB}{AC}$ 时, $\because \angle A = \angle A, \therefore \triangle AED \sim \triangle ABC, \therefore AE = \frac{AB \cdot AD}{AC} = \frac{6 \times 2}{4} = 3$ ; 当 $\frac{AD}{AE} = \frac{AB}{AC}$ 时, $\because \angle A = \angle A, \therefore \triangle ADE \sim \triangle ABC, \therefore AE = \frac{AC \cdot AD}{AB} = \frac{4 \times 2}{6} = \frac{4}{3}$ . 综上, $AE = 3$ 或 $\frac{4}{3}$ . 故答案为 $3$ 或 $\frac{4}{3}$ .
