> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\sqrt{3} - 1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在 Rt $\triangle ABE$ 中, $\angle B = 30^{\circ}$ , $AB = \sqrt{3}$ , $\therefore AE = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , 由勾股定理得, $BE = \frac{3}{2}$ . 根据翻折性质可得 $BF = 2BE = 3$ , $\therefore CF = 3 - \sqrt{3}$ . $\because AD \parallel CF$ , $\therefore$ 易证 $\triangle ADG \sim \triangle FCG$ , $\therefore \frac{AD}{CF} = \frac{DG}{CG}$ . 设 $CG = x$ , 则 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3 - \sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3} - x}{x}$ , 解得 $x = \sqrt{3} - 1$ , 故答案为 $\sqrt{3} - 1$ .
