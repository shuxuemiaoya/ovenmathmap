> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ OC:CF = 1:2, ∴ OC:OF = 1:3.
> > ∵ △ABC 与 △DEF 位似, ∴ AC∥DF, △ABC ∽$\triangle DEF, \therefore$ 易证 $\triangle AOC \sim \triangle DOF, \therefore \frac{AC}{DF} = \frac{OC}{OF} = \frac{1}{3}, \therefore \triangle ABC$ 与 $\triangle DEF$ 的周长之比为 $1:3$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
