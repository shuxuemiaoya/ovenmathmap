> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ △ABC 中, AB = AC, ∠A = 36°,
> > ∴ ∠ABC = ∠ACB = 72°. ∵ BC₁ 平分 ∠ABC,
> > ∴ ∠C₁BC = ∠ABC₁ = 36°, ∴ AC₁ = BC₁, △ABC ∽
> > △BCC₁, ∠CC₁B = 72° = ∠ACB, ∴ $\frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{BC}{CC_1}$ ,
> > BC = BC₁, ∴ AC₁ = BC₁ = BC = 1. 设 CC₁ = x, 则
> > AB = AC = 1 + x, ∴ $\frac{1 + x}{1} = \frac{1}{x}$ , ∴ x = $\frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$ . ∵ x > 0, ∴ CC₁ = x = $\frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}$ . ∵ B₁C₂ // BC₁, ∴ ∠C₂B₁C₁ =
> > ∠B₁C₁B. 又 ∵ C₁B₁ // BC, ∴ ∠B₁C₁B =
> > ∠C₁BC, ∠B₁C₁C₂ = ∠BCC₁, ∴ ∠C₂B₁C₁ =
> > ∠C₁BC, ∴ △BCC₁ ∽ △B₁C₁C₂, △ABC ∽
> > △AB₁C₁, ∴ $\frac{C_1C_2}{CC_1} = \frac{B_1C_1}{BC}, \frac{B_1C_1}{BC} = \frac{AC_1}{AC}$ . ∵ AC₁ =
> > BC₁ = BC = 1, ∴ $\frac{C_1C_2}{CC_1} = \frac{AC_1}{AC} = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}$ , ∴ C₁C₂ = $\left(\frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{3 - \sqrt{5}}{2}$ , 同理得 C₂C₃ = $\left(\frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}\right)^3$ $√5 - 2, \cdots, \therefore C_5C_6 = \left(\frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{2}\right)^6 = (\sqrt{5} - 2)^2 = 9 - 4\sqrt{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
