> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设正方形城池的边长为 $x$ 步, 则 $AE = CE = \frac{1}{2} x$ . $\because AE \parallel CD$ , $\therefore \angle BEA = \angle EDC$ , $\therefore Rt \triangle BEA \sim Rt \triangle EDC$ , $\therefore \frac{AB}{EC} = \frac{AE}{CD}$ , 即 $\frac{40}{\frac{1}{2} x} = \frac{\frac{1}{2} x}{810}$ , $\therefore x = 360$ (负值已舍去), 即正方形城池的边长为 360 步.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
