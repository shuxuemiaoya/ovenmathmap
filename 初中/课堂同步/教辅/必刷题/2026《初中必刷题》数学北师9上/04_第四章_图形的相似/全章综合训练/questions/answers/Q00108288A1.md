>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{4\sqrt{7}}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵菱形ABCD的边长为6， $\angle BAD = 120^{\circ}, \therefore AD = BC = CD = 6, AD \parallel BC,$ $\angle BCD = 120^{\circ}, \therefore \angle DCE = 60^{\circ}.$ ∵ $DE \perp BC$ 交BC的延长线于点E，∴ $\angle DEC = 90^{\circ}, \therefore \angle CDE =$
> >
> > $90^{\circ} - \angle DCE = 30^{\circ}, \therefore CE = \frac{1}{2} CD = 3, \therefore DE =$ $3\sqrt{3}, BE = BC + CE = 9. \because AD \parallel BE, \therefore \angle ADE =$ $180^{\circ} - \angle DEC = 90^{\circ}$ . 在 Rt $\triangle ADE$ 中, $AE =$ $\sqrt{DE^{2} + AD^{2}} = \sqrt{(3\sqrt{3})^{2} + 6^{2}} = 3\sqrt{7}. \because AD \parallel BE,$ $\therefore$ 易得 $\triangle AFD \sim \triangle EFB, \therefore \frac{AF}{FE} = \frac{AD}{BE} = \frac{6}{9} = \frac{2}{3},$ $\therefore AF = \frac{2}{5} AE = \frac{2}{5} \times 3\sqrt{7} = \frac{6\sqrt{7}}{5}. \because AD \parallel CE,$ $\therefore \triangle AGD \sim \triangle EGC, \therefore \frac{AG}{EG} = \frac{AD}{CE} = \frac{6}{3} = 2, \therefore AG =$ $\frac{2}{3} AE = \frac{2}{3} \times 3\sqrt{7} = 2\sqrt{7}, \therefore FG = AG - AF = 2\sqrt{7}-$ $\frac{6\sqrt{7}}{5} = \frac{4\sqrt{7}}{5}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{4\sqrt{7}}{5}$ .
