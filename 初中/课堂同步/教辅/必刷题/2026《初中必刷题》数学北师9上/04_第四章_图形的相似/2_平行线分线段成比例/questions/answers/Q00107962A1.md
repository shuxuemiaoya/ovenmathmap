> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 直线 $l_{1} \parallel l_{2} \parallel l_{3}$ ，∴ $\frac{DE}{EF} = \frac{AB}{BC}$ .
> > ∵ AB = 5, BH = 1, CH = 2, ∴ BC = BH + CH = 3,
> > ∴ $\frac{BC}{AB} = \frac{3}{5}$ , ∴ $\frac{EF}{DE} = \frac{3}{5}$ . 故选 D.
> >
> > 易错警示
> > 本题容易因忽视 $a + b + c = 0$ 的情况漏解.
