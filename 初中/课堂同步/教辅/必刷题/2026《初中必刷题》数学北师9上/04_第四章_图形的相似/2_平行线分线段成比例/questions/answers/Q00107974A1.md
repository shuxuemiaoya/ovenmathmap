>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由作图可知, $AD$ 平分 $\angle CAB$ ,∴ $\angle CAD = \angle BAD.$ ∵ EF 垂直平分线段 AD，
> > ∴ EA = ED, FA = FD, ∴ $\angle EAD = \angle EDA$ , $\angle FAD = \angle FDA, \therefore \angle EDA = \angle DAF, \angle EAD = \angle ADF, \therefore DE \parallel AF, AE \parallel DF, \therefore$ 四边形 AEDF 是平行四边形. ∵ EA = ED, ∴ 四边形 AEDF 是菱形, ∴ AF = AE = DE = DF. ∵ $DF \parallel AB, \therefore CF: AF = CD: DB = 4:5, \therefore S_{\triangle ADF}: S_{\triangle CDF} = 5:4$ , 故选项 A、B、D 正确. 若 DF = 5, 则 AF = 5, CF = 3,
> > ∴ CF:AF=3:5,与已知条件矛盾,∴DF不能为5,故选项C错误.故选C.
