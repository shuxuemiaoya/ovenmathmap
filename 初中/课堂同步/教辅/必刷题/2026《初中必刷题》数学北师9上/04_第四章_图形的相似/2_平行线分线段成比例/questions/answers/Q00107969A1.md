> > 【解】(1)∵ $FE \parallel CD$ , ∴ $\frac{AE}{AC} = \frac{AF}{AD}$ , 即 $\frac{4}{AC} = \frac{3}{5}$ , 解得 $AC = \frac{20}{3}$ , 则 $CE = AC - AE = \frac{20}{3} - 4 = \frac{8}{3}$ . (2) ∵ $DE \parallel BC$ , ∴ $\frac{AD}{AB} = \frac{AE}{AC}$ , 即 $\frac{5}{AB} = \frac{4}{\frac{20}{3}}$ , 解得 $AB = \frac{25}{3}$ .
