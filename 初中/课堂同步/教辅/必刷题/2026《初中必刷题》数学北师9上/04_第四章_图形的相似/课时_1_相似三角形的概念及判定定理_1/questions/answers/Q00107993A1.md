> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $CD$ 是 $AB$ 的垂线时, $\triangle ACD \sim \triangle CBD$ . $\because CD \perp AB, \therefore \angle CDA = \angle BDC = 90^\circ$ . $\because \angle ACB = 90^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle A + \angle ACD = \angle ACD + \angle BCD = 90^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle A = \angle BCD, \therefore \triangle ACD \sim \triangle CBD$ . 根据作图痕迹可知, C 选项中, $CD$ 是 $AB$ 的垂线, 符合题意.
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
