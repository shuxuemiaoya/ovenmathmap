> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2 \sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵点 $D$ 是 $AC$ 的中点, $CD = 2$ , $\therefore AD = CD = 2, AC = 4$ . $\because \triangle ABC \sim \triangle ADB$ , $\therefore \frac{AB}{AD} = \frac{AC}{AB}, \therefore AB^2 = AD \cdot AC = 2 \times 4 = 8$ . $\because AB > 0, \therefore AB = 2 \sqrt{2}$ . 故答案为 $2 \sqrt{2}$ .
> >
> > ## 易错警示
> >
> > 题中未明确相似三角形的对应关系,要进行分类讨论,避免漏解.
