> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{5}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because BP = 5, BC = 4, \therefore CP = 1.$ $\because PQ \perp AP, \therefore \angle APQ = 90^{\circ} = \angle ABC,$ $\therefore \angle APB + \angle BAP = 90^{\circ} = \angle APB + \angle BPQ,$ $\therefore \angle BAP = \angle BPQ.$ 又 $\because \angle ABP = \angle PCQ = 90^{\circ},$ $\therefore \triangle ABP \sim \triangle PCQ, \therefore \frac{AB}{CP} = \frac{BP}{CQ}, \therefore \frac{3}{1} = \frac{5}{CQ},$ $\therefore CQ = \frac{5}{3}$ , 故答案为 $\frac{5}{3}$ .
