>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 易知 AB = 2, CD = 3, AD = 5, AB // CD, ∴ ∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠C. 在 △AOB 和 △DOC 中, ∵ ∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠C, ∴ △AOB ∼ △DOC, ∴ $\frac{AO}{DO} = \frac{AB}{DC}$ , 即 $\frac{AO}{5 - AO} = \frac{2}{3}$ , 解得 AO = 2.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
