> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\triangle ABC \sim \triangle AED$ , $\triangle AEG \sim \triangle ABF$ , $\triangle ADG \sim \triangle ACF$ . 理由如下: $\because \angle AED = \angle ABC$ , $\angle EAD = \angle BAC$ , $\therefore \triangle ABC \sim \triangle AED$ , $\therefore \angle ADE = \angle ACB$ . $\because AF$ 是 $\angle BAC$ 的平分线, $\therefore \angle BAF = \angle CAF$ . $\because \angle AED = \angle ABC$ , $\angle EAG = \angle BAF$ , $\therefore \triangle AEG \sim \triangle ABF$ . $\because \angle ADG = \angle ACF$ , $\angle DAG = \angle CAF$ , $\therefore \triangle ADG \sim \triangle ACF$ .
> >
> > (2) $\because \frac{AG}{GF} = \frac{3}{2}$ , $\therefore \frac{AG}{AF} = \frac{3}{5}$ . $\because \triangle ABC \sim \triangle AED$ , $\therefore \frac{DE}{BC} = \frac{AD}{AC}$ . $\because \triangle ADG \sim \triangle ACF$ , $\therefore \frac{AD}{AC} = \frac{AG}{AF} = \frac{DE}{BC}$ , $\therefore \frac{DE}{BC} = \frac{3}{5}$ .
> >
