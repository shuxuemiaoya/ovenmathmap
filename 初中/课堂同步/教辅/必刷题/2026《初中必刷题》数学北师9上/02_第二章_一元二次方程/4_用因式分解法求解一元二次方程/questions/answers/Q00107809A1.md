> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $x^{2}-2mx+m^{2}=4$ , ∴ $(x-m)^{2}=4$ ,
> > ∴ x-m=2 或 x-m=-2. ∵ $x_{1}>x_{2}$ , ∴ $x_{1}=m+2$ , $x_{2}=m-2.\because x_{1}=2x_{2}+3,\therefore m+2=2(m-2)+3,$ 解得 m=3.
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
