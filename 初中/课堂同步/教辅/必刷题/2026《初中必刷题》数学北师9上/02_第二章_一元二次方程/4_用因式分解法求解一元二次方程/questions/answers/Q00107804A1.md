> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $x = -2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 把 $x = 0$ 代入方程得 $a - 1 = 0$ , $\therefore a = 1, \therefore$ 方程为 $x^{2} + 2x = 0, \therefore x(x + 2) = 0$ , $\therefore x_{1} = 0, x_{2} = -2$ , 故答案为 $x = -2$ .
