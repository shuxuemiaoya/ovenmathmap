> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $x_{1} = x_{2} = -3$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $x^{2} + 6x + 9 = 0, (x + 3)^{2} = 0$ , 解得 $x_{1} = x_{2} = -3$ .
