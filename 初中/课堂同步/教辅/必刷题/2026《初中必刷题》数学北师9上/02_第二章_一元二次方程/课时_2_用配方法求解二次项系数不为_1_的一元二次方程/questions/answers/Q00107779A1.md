> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $P < Q$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $Q - P = (m^2 - 5m + 4) - (-m^2 - m + 1) = m^2 - 5m + 4 + m^2 + m - 1 = 2m^2 - 4m + 3 = 2(m - 1)^2 + 1$ . $\because (m - 1)^2 \geqslant 0, \therefore 2(m - 1)^2 + 1 > 0, \therefore Q - P > 0, \therefore P < Q$ , 故答案为 $P < Q$ .
