> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $2x^{2}-2x-1=0$ , ∴ $2x^{2}-2x=1$ , $x^{2}-x=\frac{1}{2}$ , 配方, 得 $x^{2}-x+\frac{1}{4}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{4}$ , ∴ $(x-\frac{1}{2})^{2}=\frac{3}{4}$ , ∴ $x-\frac{1}{2}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , ∴ $x_{1}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , $x_{2}=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , ∴ $1<x_{1}<2$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
