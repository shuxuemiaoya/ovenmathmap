> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 等边
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}-ab-bc-ca=0,$ $\therefore 2a^{2}+2b^{2}+2c^{2}-2ab-2bc-2ca=0, \therefore (a^{2}-2ab+b^{2})+(b^{2}-2bc+c^{2})+(c^{2}-2ca+a^{2})=0,$ $\therefore (a-b)^{2}+(b-c)^{2}+(c-a)^{2}=0, \therefore a-b=0, b-c=0, c-a=0, \therefore a=b=c, \therefore \triangle ABC$ 是等边三角形.
