> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2 \pm \sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because$ 原式 $= (3x)^{2} \pm 2 \times 3x \times 3\sqrt{2n} + (3\sqrt{2n})^{2} = (3x \pm 3\sqrt{2n})^{2}, \therefore \pm 18\sqrt{2n} = 18(n - 1)$ , 即 $(n - 1)^{2} = 2n, \therefore n^{2} - 4n + 1 = 0$ , 则 $n^{2} - 4n + 4 - 4 + 1 = 0$ , 即 $(n - 2)^{2} = 3, \therefore n - 2 = \pm \sqrt{3}, \therefore n = 2 \pm \sqrt{3}$ . 故答案为 $2 \pm \sqrt{3}$ .
