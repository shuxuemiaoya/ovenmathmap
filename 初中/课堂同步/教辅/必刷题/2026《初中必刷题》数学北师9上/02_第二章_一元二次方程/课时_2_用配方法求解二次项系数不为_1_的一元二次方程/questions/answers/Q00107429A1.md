> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{4}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $-3x^{2} + 12x - 2 = 0$ ，二次项系数化为
> >
> > 1, 得 $x^{2} - 4x + \frac{2}{3} = 0$ , 移项, 得 $x^{2} - 4x = -\frac{2}{3}$ , 配方, 得 $x^{2} - 4x + 4 = -\frac{2}{3} + 4$ , 即 $(x - 2)^{2} = \frac{10}{3}$ , $\therefore a = -2, b = \frac{10}{3}, \therefore a + b = -2 + \frac{10}{3} = \frac{4}{3}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{4}{3}$ .
