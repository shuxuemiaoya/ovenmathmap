> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 16 - 4(m - 2) \times 2 \geqslant 0, \\ m - 2 \neq 0, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $m \leqslant 4$ 且 $m \neq 2$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
