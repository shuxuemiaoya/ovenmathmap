> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** -2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $5x^{2} + (8 - 4y)x + (y^{2} - 2y + 3 - W) = 0$ . $\because x$ 为实数, $\therefore (8 - 4y)^{2} - 20(y^{2} - 2y + 3 - W) \geqslant 0$ , 即 $5W \geqslant (y + 3)^{2} - 10 \geqslant -10$ , $\therefore W \geqslant -2$ , $\therefore W$ 的最小值为 -2.
