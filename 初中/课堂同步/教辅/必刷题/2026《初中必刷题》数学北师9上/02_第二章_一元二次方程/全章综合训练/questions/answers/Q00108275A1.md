> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 1 或 $\frac{3}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意, 得 $(2x - 1) \star 3x = (2x - 1)^{2} - 6x = -5$ , 整理, 得 $4x^{2} - 10x + 6 = 0$ , $\therefore (4x - 6)(x - 1) = 0, \therefore x_{1} = \frac{3}{2}, x_{2} = 1$ , 故答案为 1 或 $\frac{3}{2}$ .
