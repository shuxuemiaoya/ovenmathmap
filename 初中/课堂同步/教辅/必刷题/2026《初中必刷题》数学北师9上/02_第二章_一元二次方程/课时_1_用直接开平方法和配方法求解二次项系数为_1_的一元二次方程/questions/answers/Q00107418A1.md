> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $x_{1} = 2025, x_{2} = -2023$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $x^{2} - 2x - 4096575 = 0$ , 则 $x^{2} - 2x = 4096575, \therefore x^{2} - 2x + 1 = 4096575 + 1, \therefore (x - 1)^{2} = 4096576, \therefore x - 1 = \pm 2024, \therefore x_{1} = 2025, x_{2} = -2023$ . 故答案为 $x_{1} = 2025, x_{2} = -2023$ .
