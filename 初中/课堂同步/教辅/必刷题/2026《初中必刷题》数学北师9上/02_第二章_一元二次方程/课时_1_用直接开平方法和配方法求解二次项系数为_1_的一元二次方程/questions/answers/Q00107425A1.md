> > [!success] **【答案】** 7, 2, -4, -10
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\because(x+5)(x+9)=5,\therefore[(x+7)-2][(x+7)+2]=5,\therefore(x+7)^{2}-2^{2}=5,\therefore(x+7)^{2}=9,\therefore x+7=3$ 或 $x+7=-3$ , 解得 $x_{1}=-4$ , $x_{2}=-10$ , $\therefore$ 题中 a, b, c, d 表示的数分别为 7, 2, -4, -10. 故答案为 7, 2, -4, -10.
> > (2) $\because(x-5)(x+7)=12,\therefore[(x+1)-6][(x+1)+6]=12,\therefore(x+1)^{2}-36=12,\therefore(x+1)^{2}=48,\therefore x+1=4\sqrt{3}$ 或 $x+1=-4\sqrt{3}$ ，解得 $x_{1}=-1+4\sqrt{3}, x_{2}=-1-4\sqrt{3}$ .
