> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $6 + \sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $(x - 2)^{2} = 3, \therefore x - 2 = \pm \sqrt{3}$ , 解得 $x_{1} = 2 + \sqrt{3}, x_{2} = 2 - \sqrt{3}$ . ∵ 方程 $(x - 2)^{2} = 3$ 的两根为 $a, b$ , 且 $a > b, \therefore a = 2 + \sqrt{3}, b = 2 - \sqrt{3}, \therefore 2a + b = 2(2 + \sqrt{3}) + 2 - \sqrt{3} = 6 + \sqrt{3}$ . 故答案为 $6 + \sqrt{3}$ .
