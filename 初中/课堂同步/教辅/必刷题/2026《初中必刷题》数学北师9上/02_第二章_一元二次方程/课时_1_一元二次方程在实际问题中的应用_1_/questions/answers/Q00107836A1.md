> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 题图(1)的面积为 $(a + b)^2 = \overline{a^2 + 2ab + b^2}$ , 题图(2)的面积为 $b(a + 2b) = ab + 2b^2$ , $\therefore a^2 + 2ab + b^2 = ab + 2b^2$ , $\therefore b^2 - ab - a^2 = 0$ , $\therefore \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^2 - \frac{b}{a} - 1 = 0$ , $\therefore \frac{b}{a} = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$ . $\because \frac{b}{a} > 0$ , $\therefore \frac{b}{a} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$ .
