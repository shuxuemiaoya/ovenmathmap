> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设经过 $t$ 秒时, $\triangle PCQ$ 的面积等于 4. 由题意得 $BP = t$ , $CQ = 2t$ , 则 $CP = 5 - t$ . $\because S_{\triangle PCQ} = \frac{1}{2} CQ \cdot CP, \therefore 4 = \frac{1}{2} \times 2t \times (5 - t)$ , 整理得 $t^2 - 5t + 4 = 0$ , 解得 $t_1 = 1, t_2 = 4$ (不合题意, 舍去), 即经过 1 秒时, $\triangle PCQ$ 的面积等于 4, 故选 A.
> >
> > ## 7. 思路分析
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \boxed {A C = 5 \sqrt {2}} \\ \boxed {A P = \sqrt {2} t} \\ \text {作} P H \perp B C \end{array} \xrightarrow [ ]{\boxed {P C = 5 \sqrt {2} - \sqrt {2} t}} \begin{array}{l} \boxed {P H = 5 - t} \\ \boxed {C Q = t} \\ \boxed {H Q = | 5 - 2 t |} \\ \boxed {P Q = \sqrt {1 0}} \end{array} \xrightarrow {\text {勾股定理}} t \text {值}
> > $$
> >
> > 【解】能. 在正方形 $ABCD$ 中, $\because AB = 5\mathrm{cm}$ , $\therefore AC = 5\sqrt{2}\mathrm{cm}$ . 由题意得 $AP = \sqrt{2} t\mathrm{cm}, CQ = t\mathrm{cm}, \therefore PC = (5\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{2} t)\mathrm{cm}, BQ = (5 - t)\mathrm{cm}$ . 过点 $P$ 作 $PH \perp BC$ 于点 $H$ , 则易得 $PH = HC = (5 - t)\mathrm{cm}, \therefore HQ = |HC - CQ| = |5 - 2t|\mathrm{cm}$ . 在 Rt△PHQ 中, $\because \angle PHQ = 90^{\circ}, \therefore PH^{2} + HQ^{2} = PQ^{2}$ . $\because PQ$ 的长度等于 $\sqrt{10}\mathrm{cm}, \therefore (5 - t)^{2} + (5 - 2t)^{2} = (\sqrt{10})^{2}$ , 解得 $t_{1} = 2, t_{2} = 4$ . 故在 $P, Q$ 两点移动的过程中, $PQ$ 的长度能等于 $\sqrt{10}\mathrm{cm}$ , 此时 $t$ 的值为 2 或 4.
