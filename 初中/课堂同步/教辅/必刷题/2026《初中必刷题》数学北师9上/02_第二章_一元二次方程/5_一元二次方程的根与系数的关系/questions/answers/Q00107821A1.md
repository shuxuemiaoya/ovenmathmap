> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ a, b 是一元二次方程 $2x^{2} + 3x - 4 = 0$ 的两个根，∴ $a + b = -\frac{3}{2}$ ，ab = -2，∴ $ab^{2} + a^{2}b = ab(a + b) = -2 \times \left(-\frac{3}{2}\right) = 3$ ，故答案为 3.
