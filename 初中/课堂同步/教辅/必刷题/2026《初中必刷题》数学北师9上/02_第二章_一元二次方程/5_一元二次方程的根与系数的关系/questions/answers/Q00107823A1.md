> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 20
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可知 $x_{1} + x_{2} = -6, x_{1}x_{2} = 4$ , $\therefore (x_{1} - x_{2})^{2} = (x_{1} + x_{2})^{2} - 4x_{1}x_{2} = (-6)^{2} - 4 \times 4 = 36 - 16 = 20$ , 故答案为 20.
