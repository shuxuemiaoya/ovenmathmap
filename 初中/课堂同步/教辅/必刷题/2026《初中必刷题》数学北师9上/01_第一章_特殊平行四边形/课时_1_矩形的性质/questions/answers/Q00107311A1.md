> > [!success] **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】∵四边形ABCD是矩形,∴OA=OC,OB=OD,AC=BD,∠ABC=90°.∵BE=DF,∴OE=OF.
> > 在△AOE和△COF中, $\left\{\begin{aligned}OA&=OC,\\ \angle AOE&=\angle COF,\\ OE&=OF,\end{aligned}\right.$ ∴△AOE≌△COF(SAS),∴AE=CF.
> > (2)【解】∵OA=OC,OB=OD,AC=BD,∴OA=OB.∵∠AOB=∠COD=60°,∴△AOB是等边三角形,∴OA=AB=6,∴AC=2OA=12.在Rt△ABC中,BC= $\sqrt{AC^{2}-AB^{2}}$ =6 $\sqrt{3}$ ,∴矩形ABCD的面积为AB·BC=6×6 $\sqrt{3}$ =36 $\sqrt{3}$ .
