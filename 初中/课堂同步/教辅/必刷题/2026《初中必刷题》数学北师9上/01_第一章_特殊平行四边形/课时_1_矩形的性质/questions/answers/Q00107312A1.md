> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 连接 $OM'$ . $\because OA = 12 \mathrm{~m}, OB = 5 \mathrm{~m}$ , $\therefore AB = \sqrt{OA^2 + OB^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + 5^2} = 13 (\mathrm{~m})$ , $\therefore A'B' = AB = 13 \mathrm{~m}$ . $\because M'$ 为 $A'B'$ 的中点, $\triangle A'OB'$ 是直角三角形, $\therefore OM' = \frac{1}{2} A'B' = 6.5 \mathrm{~m}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
