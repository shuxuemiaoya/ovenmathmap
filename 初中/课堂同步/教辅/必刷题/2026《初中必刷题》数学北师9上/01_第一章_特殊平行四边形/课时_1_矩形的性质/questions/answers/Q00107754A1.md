> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 四边形 ABCD 是矩形, ∴ $\angle DAB = \angle ABE = 90^{\circ}$ , OA = OB. ∵ AE 平分 $\angle BAD$ , ∴ $\angle BAE = 45^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle BAO = 45^{\circ} + 15^{\circ} = 60^{\circ}$ , $\angle AEB = 45^{\circ}$ , ∴ AB = BE, $\triangle BAO$ 是等边三角形, $\therefore AB = BO = BE.\because \angle OBE = 90^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} = 30^{\circ},$ $\therefore \angle OEB = (180^{\circ} - 30^{\circ})\div 2 = 75^{\circ},\therefore \angle AEO =$ $75^{\circ} - 45^{\circ} = 30^{\circ}.$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
