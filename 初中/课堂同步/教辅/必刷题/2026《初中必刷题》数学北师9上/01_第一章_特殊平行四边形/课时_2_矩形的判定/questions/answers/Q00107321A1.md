> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** BC
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】(1)在平行四边形ABCD中, $AD = BC$ , $AB = CD$ , $AB \parallel CD$ , 则 $BE \parallel CD$ . 又 $\because AB = BE$ , $\therefore BE = DC$ , $\therefore$ 四边形BECD为平行四边形, $\therefore BD = EC$ . 在 $\triangle ABD$ 与 $\triangle BEC$ 中, $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} AB = BE, \\ BD = EC, \\ AD = BC, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \triangle ABD \cong \triangle BEC (\mathrm{SSS})$ .
> >
> > (2)由(1)知,四边形BECD为平行四边形,则 $OD = OE, OC = OB$ . $\because$ 四边形ABCD为平行四边形, $\therefore \angle A = \angle BCD$ . 又 $\because \angle BOD = 2\angle A, \angle BOD = \angle OCD + \angle ODC, \therefore \angle OCD = \angle ODC, \therefore OC = OD, \therefore OC + OB = OD + OE$ , 即 $BC = ED$ , $\therefore$ 平行四边形BECD为矩形.
