> > [!success] **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】∵四边形ABCD是菱形,∴AB=AD,∠B=∠D.又∵AE⊥BC于点E,AF⊥CD于点F,∴∠AEB=∠AFD=90°.
> > 在△ABE与△ADF中,∵ $\left\{\begin{aligned}\angle B&=\angle D,\\\angle AEB&=\angle AFD,\\AB&=AD,\end{aligned}\right.$ ∴△ABE≌△ADF(AAS),∴AE=AF.
> > (2)【解】∵四边形ABCD是菱形,∴AD∥BC,
> > ∴∠B+∠BAD=180°.∵∠B=60°,∴∠BAD=120°.又∵∠AEB=90°,∠B=60°,∴∠BAE=30°.由(1)知△ABE≌△ADF,∴∠BAE=∠DAF=30°,∴∠EAF=120°-30°-30°=60°.
> > ∵由(1)知AE=AF,∴△AEF是等边三角形,
> > ∴∠AEF=60°.
