> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{48}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 四边形 $ABCD$ 是菱形, $\therefore AC \perp BD$ , $AO = \frac{1}{2}AC = 8 \text{ cm}$ , $BO = \frac{1}{2}BD = 6 \text{ cm}$ , $\therefore AB = \sqrt{AO^2 + BO^2} = 10 \text{ cm}$ . $\because S_{\text{菱形}ABCD} = \frac{1}{2}AC \cdot BD = AB \cdot EF$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{2} \times 16 \times 12 = 10EF$ , $\therefore EF = \frac{48}{5} \text{ cm}$ , 故答案为 $\frac{48}{5}$ .
