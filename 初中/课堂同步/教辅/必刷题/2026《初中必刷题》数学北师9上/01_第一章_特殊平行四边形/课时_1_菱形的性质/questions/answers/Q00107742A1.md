> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2 \sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 已知四边形 $AECF$ 是菱形, $AB = 3$ , 设 $BE = x$ , 则 $AE = 3 - x = CE$ . $\because$ 四边形 $AECF$ 是菱形, $\therefore \angle FCO = \angle ECO$ . $\because \angle ECO = \angle ECB$ , $\therefore \angle ECO = \angle ECB = \angle FCO = 30^\circ$ , $\therefore 2BE = CE$ , $\therefore CE = 2x$ , $\therefore 2x = 3 - x$ , 解得 $x = 1$ , $\therefore CE = 2$ . 由勾股定理得 $BC^2 + BE^2 = EC^2$ , $\therefore BC = \sqrt{EC^2 - BE^2} = \sqrt{2^2 - 1^2} = \sqrt{3}$ . 又 $\because AE = 3 - x = 3 - 1 = 2$ , $\therefore$ 菱形的面积是 $AE \cdot BC = 2 \sqrt{3}$ . 故答案为 $2 \sqrt{3}$ .
