> > △EPM和△BPN中, $\left\{\begin{aligned}\angle PMA&=\angle PNB=90^{\circ},\\ PM&=PN,\\ \angle MPE&=\angle NPB,\end{aligned}\right.$ ∴△EPM≌△BPN(ASA),∴EM=BN.
