> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】(1)∵四边形ABCD为正方形，∴AB=BC,∠ABE=∠CBE=45°.
> > 在△EAB和△ECB中， $\left\{\begin{aligned}AB&=BC,\\ \angle ABE&=\angle CBE,\\ BE&=BE,\end{aligned}\right.$ ∴ $\triangle EAB \cong \triangle ECB(SAS)$ .
> > (2)∵四边形ABCD为正方形，∴ $\angle BDC = \frac{1}{2} \angle CDA = 45^{\circ}$ . ∵ $\triangle EAB \cong \triangle ECB, \angle AEC = 45^{\circ}, \therefore \angle CED = \angle AED = \frac{1}{2} \angle AEC = 22.5^{\circ}.$ ∵ $\angle BDC = \angle CED + \angle DCE = 45^{\circ}, \therefore \angle DCE = 45^{\circ} - 22.5^{\circ} = 22.5^{\circ}, \therefore \angle CED = \angle DCE,$ ∴ DC=DE.
