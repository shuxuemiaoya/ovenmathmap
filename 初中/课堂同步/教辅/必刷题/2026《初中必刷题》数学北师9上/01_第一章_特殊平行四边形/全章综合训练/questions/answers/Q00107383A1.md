> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $S_{正方形AMEF} = 16$ , ∴ AM = 4. ∵ M 是斜边 BC 的中点, ∴ BC = 2AM = 8, ∴ AC = $\sqrt{BC^{2}-AB^{2}} = \sqrt{8^{2}-4^{2}} = 4\sqrt{3}$ , ∴ $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2}AB \cdot AC = 8\sqrt{3}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
