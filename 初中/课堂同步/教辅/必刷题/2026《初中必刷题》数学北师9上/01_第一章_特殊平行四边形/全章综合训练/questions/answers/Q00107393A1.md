> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】∵ $AD \parallel BC$ , ∴ $\angle ADO = \angle CBO$ .
> > 在 $\triangle ADO$ 和 $\triangle CBO$ 中， $\left\{\begin{aligned}\angle ADO&=\angle CBO,\\ \angle AOD&=\angle COB,\\ OA&=OC,\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > ∴ $\triangle ADO \cong \triangle CBO$ (AAS), ∴ OD = OB, ∴ 四边形 ABCD 是平行四边形. ∵ AB = BC, ∴ 四边形 ABCD 是菱形.
> >
> > (2)【解】与线段 $CE$ 相等的线段有 $AE, DE$ , $AG, CF$ . 由 (1) 知, 四边形 $ABCD$ 是菱形, $\therefore AB = BC = CD = AD, AC \perp BD$ . $\because AB = AC$ , $\therefore AB = BC = CD = AD = AC, \therefore \triangle ABC$ 和 $\triangle ADC$ 为等边三角形. $\because CH \perp AD, \therefore AH = DH$ , 即 $CH$ 为 $AD$ 的垂直平分线, $\therefore AE = DE$ . 同理可得 $CE = AE, \therefore AE = DE = EC$ . $\because \triangle ADC$ 为等边三角形, $CH \perp AD, \therefore \angle ACH = \frac{1}{2} \angle ACD = 30^\circ$ . $\because \angle FEC = 75^\circ, \therefore \angle EFC = 180^\circ - \angle ACH - \angle FEC = 75^\circ, \therefore \angle EFC = \angle FEC, \therefore CF = CE$ . $\because \triangle ABC$ 和 $\triangle ADC$ 为等边三角形, $\therefore \angle BAC = \angle CAD = 60^\circ$ . $\because CE = AE, \therefore \angle EAC = \angle ECA = 30^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle BAE = \angle BAC + \angle CAE = 90^\circ$ , $\angle AEC = 180^\circ - \angle EAC - \angle ECA = 120^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle AEG = \angle AEC - \angle FEC = 45^\circ, \therefore \angle AGE = \angle AEG, \therefore AE = AG, \therefore AG = EC$ .
