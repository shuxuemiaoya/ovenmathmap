> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 四边形 ABCD 是正方形, ∴ AC ⊥ BD, ∠ABD = 45°. ∵ EF ⊥ CF, ∴ ∠COF =$\angle EFC = 90^{\circ}, \therefore \angle EFG = 90^{\circ} - \angle CFO = \angle FCO.$ $\because EG \perp BD, \therefore \angle EGF = \angle FOC = 90^{\circ}$ . 在 $\triangle EFG$ 和 $\triangle FCO$ 中， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \angle EGF = \angle FOC = 90^{\circ}, \\ \angle EFG = \angle FCO, \\ FE = CF, \end{array} \right.$ $\triangle FCO(AAS), \therefore EG = OF = 3.$ $\because \angle ABD = 45^{\circ},$ $\therefore \triangle EBG$ 是等腰直角三角形， $\therefore BE = \sqrt{2}EG = 3\sqrt{2}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
