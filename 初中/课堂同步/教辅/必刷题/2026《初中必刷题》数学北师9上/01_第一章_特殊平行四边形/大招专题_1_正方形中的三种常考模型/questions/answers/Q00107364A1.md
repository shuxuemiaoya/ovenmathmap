> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{9}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $OE = OF$ , 证明: $\because$ 四边形 $ABCD$ 是正方形, $\therefore OA = OB$ , $\angle AOB = 90^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle OAB = \angle OBA = \angle OBF = 45^\circ$ , $\angle AOE + \angle BOE = 90^\circ$ . $\because \angle MOP = 90^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle BOE + \angle BOF = 90^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle BOF = \angle AOE$ . 在 $\triangle AOE$ 和 $\triangle BOF$ 中, $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \angle OAE = \angle OBF = 45^\circ, \\ OA = OB, \\ \angle AOE = \angle BOF, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \triangle AOE \cong \triangle BOF (\text {ASA}), \therefore OE = OF$ . (2) $\because \triangle AOE \cong \triangle BOF$ , $\therefore S_{\triangle AOE} = S_{\triangle BOF}$ , $\therefore S_{\triangle AOE} + S_{\triangle OBE} = S_{\triangle BOF} + S_{\triangle OBE}$ , 即 $S_{\triangle AOB} = S_{\text {四边形 } OEBF}$ . $\because S_{\triangle AOB} = \frac{1}{4} S_{\text {四边形 } ABCD} = \frac{1}{4} \times 3^2 = \frac{9}{4}$ , $\therefore S_{\text {四边形 } OEBF} = \frac{9}{4}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{9}{4}$ .
> >
> > 思路分析
> > (1)②根据旋转及正方形的性质得 $\angle ABG=\angle D=\angle BAD=\angle ABE=90^{\circ}$ , 可得 $G, B, E$ 共线. 再证明 $\triangle EAF \cong \triangle EAG$ , 得出 $EF=EG$ , 进而可得出 $BE, EF, DF$ 之间的数量关系.
