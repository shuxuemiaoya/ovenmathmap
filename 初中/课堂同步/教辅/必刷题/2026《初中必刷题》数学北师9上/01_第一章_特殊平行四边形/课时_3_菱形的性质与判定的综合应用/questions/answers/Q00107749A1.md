> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2^{2021}}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 点 D, E 分别为 AC, BC 边的中点, ∴ DE 是 $\triangle ABC$ 的中位线, ∴ DE = $\frac{1}{2}AB = \frac{1}{2}$ , $AD = \frac{1}{2}AC = \frac{1}{2}$ , $DE \parallel AF$ , ∴ DE = AD. ∵ EF // AC, ∴ 四边形 EDAF 是平行四边形, ∴ AD = EF, DE = AF. 又 ∵ DE = AD, ∴ AD = EF = DE = AF, ∴ 四边形 EDAF 是菱形, ∴ $C_{1} = 4 \times \frac{1}{2} = 2$ . 同理求得 $C_{2} = 4 \times \frac{1}{2^{2}} = 1, \cdots, C_{n} = 4 \times \frac{1}{2^{n}} = \frac{1}{2^{n-2}} (n \text{ 为正整数})$ , ∴ $C_{2023} = \frac{1}{2^{2023-2}} = \frac{1}{2^{2021}}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{1}{2^{2021}}$ .
