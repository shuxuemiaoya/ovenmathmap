> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left(\frac{18}{5}, \frac{24}{5}\right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 连接 $EF$ , 设 $AD$ 交 $y$ 轴于点 $H, AE, BF$ 交于点 $G$ . $\because$ 四边形 $ABCD$ 为平行四边形, $\therefore AD // BC, \therefore \angle FAE = \angle BEA$ . 由作图可知 $AE$ 平分 $\angle BAF, \therefore \angle BAE = \angle FAE$ , $\therefore \angle BAE = \angle AEB, \therefore AB = BE = 5$ . $\because AF = AB$ , $\therefore AF = BE$ . 又 $\because AF // BE, \therefore$ 四边形 $AFEB$ 为平行四边形. $\because AB = BE, \therefore$ 四边形 $AFEB$ 为菱形, $\therefore AE \perp BF, BG = \frac{1}{2} BF = 3, EG = \frac{1}{2} AE$ . 在 Rt $\triangle BGE$ 中, $EG = \sqrt{BE^2 - BG^2} = 4$ , $\therefore AE = 2EG = 8$ . $\because S_{菱形AFEB} = \frac{1}{2} AE \cdot BF = AF \cdot OH$ , $\therefore OH = \frac{24}{5}$ . 在 Rt $\triangle OFH$ 中, $HF = \sqrt{BF^2 - OH^2} = \frac{18}{5}, \therefore F\left(\frac{18}{5}, \frac{24}{5}\right)$ . 故答案为 $\left(\frac{18}{5}, \frac{24}{5}\right)$ .
