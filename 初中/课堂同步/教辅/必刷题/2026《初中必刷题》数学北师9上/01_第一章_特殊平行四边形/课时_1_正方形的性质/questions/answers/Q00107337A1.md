> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 四边形 ABCD 是正方形, ∴ AB = AD. 又∵ $\triangle ADE$ 是等边三角形, ∴ AE = AD = DE, $\angle DAE = 60^{\circ}$ , ∴ AB = AE, ∴ $\angle ABE = \angle AEB$ , $\angle BAE = 90^{\circ} + 60^{\circ} = 150^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle ABE = (180^{\circ} - 150^{\circ}) \div 2 = 15^{\circ}$ . 又∵ $\angle BAC = 45^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle BFC = 45^{\circ} + 15^{\circ} = 60^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
