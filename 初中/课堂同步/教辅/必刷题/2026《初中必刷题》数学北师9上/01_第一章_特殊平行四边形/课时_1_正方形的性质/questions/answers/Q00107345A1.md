> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 四边形 $EDFC$ 是菱形， $\therefore EF \perp CD, DG = CG, \therefore \angle DGE = \angle CGE = 90^\circ$ . ∵ 四边形 $ABCD$ 是正方形， $\therefore \angle A = \angle ADC = 90^\circ, AB = BC = CD = AD, \therefore$ 四边形 $ADGE$ 是矩形， $\therefore DG = AE = 2, EG = AD, \therefore CG = DG = 2, \therefore CD = 4, \therefore AD = CD = EG = 4.$ 在 Rt△CGE 中，由勾股定理得 $CE = \sqrt{CG^2 + EG^2} = \sqrt{2^2 + 4^2} = 2\sqrt{5}$ . ∵ 四边形 $HGNM$ 是矩形， $\therefore GN \perp CE, HM = GN, \therefore S_{\triangle CGE} = \frac{1}{2} CG \cdot EG = \frac{1}{2} CE \cdot GN, \therefore \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 4 = \frac{1}{2} \times 2\sqrt{5} \cdot GN, \therefore GN = \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}, \therefore HM = GN = \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}$ , 故答案为 $\frac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}$ .
