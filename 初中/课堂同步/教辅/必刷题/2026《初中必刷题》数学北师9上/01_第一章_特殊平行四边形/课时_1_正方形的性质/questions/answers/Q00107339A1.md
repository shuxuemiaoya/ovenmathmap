>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 四边形 ABCD 是正方形，
> > ∴ AC = BD, OC ⊥ OD, OA = OC, OB = OD,
> > ∴ OC = OD, ∠ COD = 90°, ∴ ∠ ODC = ∠ OCD = 45°. 由作图可知 OC = CE, ∴ ∠ COE = ∠ CEO = $\frac{180^{\circ}-45^{\circ}}{2}$ = 67. 5°, ∴ ∠ DOE = ∠ COD - ∠ COE = 90° - 67. 5° = 22. 5°. 故答案为 22.5.
