> > 【证明】∵ BD 垂直平分 AC, ∴ OA = OC, $\angle DOC = \angle DOA = \angle AOE = 90^{\circ}$ . 在 $\triangle AEO$ 与 $\triangle CDO$ 中， $\left\{\begin{aligned}\angle AEO &=\angle CDO,\\ \angle AOE &=\angle COD,\\ AO &=OC,\end{aligned}\right.$ $\triangle CDO(AAS),\therefore CD=AE.\because\angle AEO=\angle CDO,$ $\therefore CD \parallel AE, \therefore$ 四边形 AECD 是平行四边形. $\because AC \perp DE, \therefore \square AECD$ 是菱形.
