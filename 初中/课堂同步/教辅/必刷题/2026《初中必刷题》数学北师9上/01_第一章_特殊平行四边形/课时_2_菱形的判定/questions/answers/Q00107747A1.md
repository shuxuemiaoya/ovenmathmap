> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $60^{\circ}
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 延长 AB, FG 交于 H, 连接 HD.
> > ∵ AD ∥ BC, BC ∥ GF, ∴ AD ∥ GF. ∵ AB ∥ DF,
> > ∴ 四边形 AHFD, 四边形 BCFH 为平行四边形.
> >
> > $\because \angle ABC = 120^{\circ}, AF$ 平分 $\angle BAD, \therefore \angle DAF = \frac{1}{2} (180^{\circ} - 120^{\circ}) = 30^{\circ}, \angle ADC = 120^{\circ}, \therefore \angle DFA = 30^{\circ}, \therefore \angle DAF = \angle DFA, \therefore AD = DF, \therefore$ 平行四边形 $AHFD$ 为菱形， $\therefore$ 易得 $\triangle ADH \cong \triangle FDH$ ，且均为等边三角形， $\therefore DH = DF, \angle BHD = \angle GFD = \angle HDF = 60^{\circ}. \because AD // EC, \therefore \angle CEF = \angle DAF = \angle DFA = 30^{\circ}, \therefore CE = CF.$ 又 $\because$ 四边形 $ECFG$ 为平行四边形， $\therefore$ 四边形 $ECFG$ 为菱形， $\therefore CE = CF = FG = EG.$ $\because$ 四边形 $BCFH$ 为平行四边形， $\therefore BH = CF, \therefore BH = GF.$ 在 $\triangle BHD$ 与 $\triangle GFD$ 中， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}DH = DF, \\ \angle BHD = \angle GFD, \therefore \triangle BHD \cong \triangle GFD (\text {SAS}), \\ BH = GF, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \angle BDH = \angle GDF, \therefore \angle BDG = \angle BDH + \angle HDG = \angle GDF + \angle HDG = 60^{\circ}.$ 故答案为 $60^{\circ}.$
