> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\angle A = \angle C$ (答案不唯一)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \angle A = \angle C$ , $\angle AOB = \angle COD, \therefore \triangle AOB \sim \triangle COD$ . 故答案为 $\angle A = \angle C$ (答案不唯一).
