> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $x = -40$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{x}{-4 \times 5} = -\frac{x}{4} + \frac{x}{5}, \frac{x}{-5 \times 6} = -\frac{x}{5} + \frac{x}{6}, \frac{x}{-6 \times 7} = -\frac{x}{6} + \frac{x}{7}, \cdots, \frac{x}{-9 \times 10} = -\frac{x}{9} + \frac{x}{10}$ , 所以原方程可化为 $-\frac{x}{4} + \frac{x}{5} - \frac{x}{5} + \frac{x}{6} - \frac{x}{6} + \frac{x}{7} + \cdots + \left(-\frac{x}{9}\right) + \frac{x}{10} = 6$ , 化简得 $-\frac{x}{4} + \frac{x}{10} = 6$ , 合并同类项, 得 $-\frac{3}{20} x = 6$ , 系数化为 1, 得 $x = -40$ . 故答案为 $x = -40$ .
