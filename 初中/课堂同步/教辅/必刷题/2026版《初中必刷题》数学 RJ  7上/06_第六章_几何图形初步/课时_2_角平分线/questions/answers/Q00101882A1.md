> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $11^{\circ}15'$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $180^{\circ}\div16=11.25^{\circ}$ . 因为 $0.25^{\circ}=(0.25\times60)'=15'$ , 所以 $11.25^{\circ}=11^{\circ}15'$ , 故答案为 $11^{\circ}15'$ .
