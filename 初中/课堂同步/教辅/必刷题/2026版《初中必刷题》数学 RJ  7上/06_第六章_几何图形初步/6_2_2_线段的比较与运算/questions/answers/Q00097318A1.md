> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)因为 $AB = 6\mathrm{cm}, BC = 4\mathrm{cm}$ , 所以 $AC = AB + BC = 6 + 4 = 10(\mathrm{cm})$ . 又因为 $D$ 为线段 $AC$ 的中点, 所以 $DC = \frac{1}{2} AC = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 = 5(\mathrm{cm})$ , 所以 $DB = DC - BC = 5 - 4 = 1(\mathrm{cm})$ . (2) 因为 $BD = \frac{1}{4} AB = \frac{1}{3} CD$ , 所以设 $BD = x\mathrm{cm}$ , 则 $AB = 4BD = 4x\mathrm{cm}, CD = 3BD = 3x\mathrm{cm}$ , 所以 $BC = DC - DB = 3x - x = 2x(\mathrm{cm})$ , 所以 $AC = AB + BC = 4x + 2x = 6x(\mathrm{cm})$ . 因为 $E$ 为线段 $AB$ 的中点, 所以 $BE = \frac{1}{2} AB = \frac{1}{2} \times 4x = 2x(\mathrm{cm})$ , 所以 $EC = BE + BC = 2x + 2x = 4x(\mathrm{cm})$ . 又因为 $EC = 8\mathrm{cm}$ , 所以 $4x = 8$ , 解得 $x = 2$ , 所以 $AC = 6 \times 2 = 12(\mathrm{cm})$ .
