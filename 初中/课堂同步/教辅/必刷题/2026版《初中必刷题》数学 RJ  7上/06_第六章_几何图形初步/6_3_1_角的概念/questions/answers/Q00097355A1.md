> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** (1)(3t) 5 (2)(30+6t) 5
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)根据题意得 $\angle AON = (3t)^{\circ}$ . 因为 $\angle AOC = 30^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\angle BOC = 150^{\circ}$ . 因为 $OM$ 平分 $\angle BOC$ , $\angle MON = 90^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\angle COM = \frac{1}{2} \times 150^{\circ} = 75^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\angle CON = 90^{\circ} - 75^{\circ} = 15^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\angle AON = \angle AOC - \angle CON = 30^{\circ} - 15^{\circ} = 15^{\circ}$ , 所以 $3t = 15$ , 解得 $t = 5$ .
> >
> > 关键点拨
> > 解题的关键是掌握正方体有8个顶点, 12条棱, 6个面.
> >
> > (2) 根据题意得 $\angle AOC = (30 + 6t)^{\circ}$ . 因为 $\angle MON = 90^{\circ}, OC$ 平分 $\angle MON$ , 所以 $\angle CON = \angle COM = 45^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\angle AOC - \angle AON = \angle CON = 45^{\circ}$ , 所以 $30 + 6t - 3t = 45$ , 解得 $t = 5$ .
