> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\angle AOB = \alpha, OA_{1}, OB_{1}$ 分别是 $\angle AOM$ 和 $\angle MOB$ 的平分线，所以 $\angle A_{1}OM = \frac{1}{2}\angle AOM, \angle B_{1}OM = \frac{1}{2}\angle BOM$ ，所以 $\angle A_{1}OB_{1} = \frac{1}{2} (\angle AOM + \angle BOM) = \frac{1}{2}\angle AOB =$ $\frac{1}{2}\alpha.$ 因为 $OA_{2}, OB_{2}$ 分别是 $\angle A_{1}OM$ 和 $\angle MOB_{1}$ 的平分线，所以 $\angle A_{2}OM = \frac{1}{2} \angle A_{1}OM, \angle B_{2}OM = \frac{1}{2} \angle B_{1}OM,$ 所以 $\angle A_{2}OB_{2} = \frac{1}{2} (\angle A_{1}OM + \angle B_{1}OM) = \frac{1}{2} \angle A_{1}OB_{1} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \alpha = \frac{1}{4} \alpha = \frac{\alpha}{2^{2}}.$ 因为 $OA_{3}, OB_{3}$ 分别是 $\angle A_{2}OM$ 和 $\angle MOB_{2}$ 的平分线，所以 $\angle A_{3}OM = \frac{1}{2} \angle A_{2}OM, \angle B_{3}OM = \frac{1}{2} \angle B_{2}OM,$ 所以 $\angle A_{3}OB_{3} = \frac{1}{2} (\angle A_{2}OM + \angle B_{2}OM) = \frac{1}{2} \angle A_{2}OB_{2} = \frac{1}{8} \alpha = \frac{\alpha}{2^{3}}, \cdots,$ 由此规律得 $\angle A_{n}OB_{n} = \frac{\alpha}{2^{n}}.$ 故选 C.
