> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 108
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 OD 是 $\angle AOC$ 的平分线，所以 $\angle COD = \frac{1}{2} \angle AOC$ . 因为 OE 是 $\angle BOC$ 的平分线，所以 $\angle EOC = \frac{1}{2} \angle BOC$ ，所以 $\angle COD + \angle EOC = \frac{1}{2} \angle AOC + \frac{1}{2} \angle BOC$ ，即 $\angle DOE = \frac{1}{2} \angle AOB = 90^{\circ}$ . 因为 $\angle AOE = 162^{\circ}$ ，所以 $\angle AOD = \angle AOE - \angle DOE = 72^{\circ}$ ，所以 $\angle BOD = 180^{\circ} - \angle AOD = 108^{\circ}$ ，故答案为 108.
