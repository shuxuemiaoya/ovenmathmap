> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\angle AOD = 110^{\circ}$ , OC 平分 $\angle AOD$ , 所以 $\angle COD = \frac{1}{2} \angle AOD = 55^{\circ}$ . 因为 $\angle BOC$ 与 $\angle COD$ 互余, 所以 $\angle BOC + \angle COD = 90^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\angle BOC = 90^{\circ} - \angle COD = 90^{\circ} - 55^{\circ} = 35^{\circ}$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
