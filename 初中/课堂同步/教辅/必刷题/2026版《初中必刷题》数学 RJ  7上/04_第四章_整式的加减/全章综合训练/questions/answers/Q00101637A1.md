> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $y^{2}-1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $3xy+2y^{2}-5-(y^{2}+3xy-4)=3xy+2y^{2}-5-y^{2}-3xy+4=y^{2}-1$ . 故答案为 $y^{2}-1$ .
