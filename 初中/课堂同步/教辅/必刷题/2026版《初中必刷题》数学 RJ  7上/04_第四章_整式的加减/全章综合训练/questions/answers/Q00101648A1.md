> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 22
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $(3ab+6a+4b)-(2a-2ab)=3ab+6a+4b-2a+2ab=5ab+4a+4b=5ab+4(a+b)$ .
> > 当 $a + b = -7, ab = 10$ 时，原式 $= 5 \times 10 + 4 \times (-7) = 22$ . 故答案为 22.
