> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2023m - 6068$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $a_1 = m, a_2 = 1 (m \geqslant 3, m$ 为整数)时， $a_3 = m - 2, a_4 = 2m - 5, a_5 = 3m - 8, a_6 = 4m - 11, \cdots, a_n = (n - 2)m - 3n + 7$ ，所以 $a_{2025} = (2025 - 2)m - 3 \times 2025 + 7 = 2023m - 6068$ ，故答案为 $2023m - 6068$ .
