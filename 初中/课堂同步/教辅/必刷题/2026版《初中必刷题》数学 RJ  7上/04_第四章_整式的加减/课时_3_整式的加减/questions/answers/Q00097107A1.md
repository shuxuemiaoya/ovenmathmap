> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{48 - 9\pi}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 剩余铁皮的面积 $S = S_{\text{三角形}} - S_{\text{半圆}} = \frac{1}{2} ah - \frac{1}{2}\pi r^2$ 。当 $a = 8, h = 6, r = 3$ 时， $S = \frac{1}{2} \times 8 \times 6 - \frac{1}{2}\pi \times 3^2 = 24 - \frac{9}{2}\pi = \frac{48 - 9\pi}{2}$ 。
