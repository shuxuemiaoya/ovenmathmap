> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{6}{7}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $(7mxy - 0.75y^3) - 2(2x^2y + 3xy) = 7mxy - 0.75y^3 - 4x^2y - 6xy = -0.75y^3 + (7m - 6)xy - 4x^2y.$ 因为化简后不含二次项，所以 $7m - 6 = 0$ ，解得 $m = \frac{6}{7}$ .
