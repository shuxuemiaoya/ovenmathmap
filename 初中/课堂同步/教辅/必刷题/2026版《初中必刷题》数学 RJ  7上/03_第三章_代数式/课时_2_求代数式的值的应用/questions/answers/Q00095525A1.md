> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)窗户的总面积是 $\left(4a^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\pi a^{2}\right)\mathrm{m}^{2}$ .
> > (2)窗框的总长为 $15a + \frac{1}{2} \times 2\pi a = (15a + \pi a) \, \mathrm{m}$ .
> > (3) 当 a = 0.5 时， $\left(4a^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\pi a^{2}\right) \times 40 + (15a + \pi a) \times 24 = \left(4 \times 0.5^{2} + \frac{1}{2} \times 3 \times 0.5^{2}\right) \times 40 + (15 \times 0.5 + 3 \times 0.5) \times 24 = (1 + 0.375) \times 40 + (7.5 + 1.5) \times 24 = 271$ （元），即制作一扇窗户需要的费用是271元.
