> > 【解】(1) $2^{100}-2^{99}=2^{99}\times(2-1)=2^{99}$ . 故答案为 $2^{99}$ .
> > (2) $2-2^{2}-2^{3}-2^{4}-\cdots-2^{2023}+2^{2024}=2^{2024}-2^{2023}-2^{2022}-\cdots-2^{2}+2=2^{2023}-2^{2022}-\cdots-2^{2}+2=2^{2022}-2^{2021}-\cdots-2^{3}-2^{2}+2=2^{2}+2=6.$
