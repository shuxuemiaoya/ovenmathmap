> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{5}{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意可得, 横线上应填 $\left(-\frac{5}{2}\right)\div(-3)=\frac{5}{2}\times\frac{1}{3}=\frac{5}{6}.$
