>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left[\left(-\frac{3}{10}\right)+\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)+\frac{3}{5}+\frac{1}{2}\right],\frac{3}{10},\frac{3}{10}
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) 原式 = $\left[-3+\left(-\frac{3}{10}\right)\right]+\left[-1+\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)\right]+\left(2+\frac{3}{5}\right)+\left(2+\frac{1}{2}\right)=\left[(-3)+(-1)+2+2\right]+\left[\left(-\frac{3}{10}\right)+\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)+\frac{3}{5}+\frac{1}{2}\right]=0+\frac{3}{10}=\frac{3}{10}.$ 故答案为 $\left[\left(-\frac{3}{10}\right)+\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)+\frac{3}{5}+\frac{1}{2}\right],\frac{3}{10},\frac{3}{10}.$
> >
> > (2) 原式 = $\left[(-2025) + 2024 + (-2023) + 2020\right] + \left[\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) + \frac{3}{4} + \left(-\frac{5}{6}\right) + \frac{1}{2}\right] = (-4) + \left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) = -\frac{17}{4}.$
