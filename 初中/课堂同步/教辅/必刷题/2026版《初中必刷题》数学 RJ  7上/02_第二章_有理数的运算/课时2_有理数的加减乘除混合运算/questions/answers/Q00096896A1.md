> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > - **①**：添加“×”“÷”两个运算符号,得到的算式:先乘后除: $1 \times 2 \div 345 = \frac{2}{345}$ , $1 \times 23 \div 45 = \frac{23}{45}$ , $1 \times 234 \div 5 = \frac{234}{5}$ , $12 \times 3 \div 45 = \frac{36}{45} = \frac{4}{5}$ , $12 \times 34 \div 5 = \frac{12 \times 34}{5} = \frac{408}{5}$ , $123 \times 4 \div 5 = \frac{123 \times 4}{5} = \frac{492}{5}$ , 先除后乘: $1 \div 2 \times 345 = \frac{345}{2}$ , $1 \div 23 \times 45 = \frac{45}{23}$ , $1 \div 234 \times 5 = \frac{5}{234}$ , $12 \div 3 \times 45 = 180$ , $12 \div 34 \times 5 = \frac{30}{17}$ , $123 \div 4 \times 5 = \frac{615}{4}$ , 总共 12 种不同的结果, 故①正确;
> > - **②**：$1 + 2 \times 3 - 4 \div 5 = \frac{31}{5}$ , 故②正确;
> > - **③**：$1 + 23 \times 4 - 5 = 88$ , 故③错误. 综上, 正确的个数是 2,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
