> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $-2^{2} = -4, \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} = \frac{1}{4},$ $\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)^{3} = -\frac{1}{27}, |-4| = 4, \left|-\frac{1}{27}\right| = \frac{1}{27},$ 且 $4 > \frac{1}{27},$ 所以 $-\frac{1}{27} > -4$ ，所以 $\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 > \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 > -2^2$ 。
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
