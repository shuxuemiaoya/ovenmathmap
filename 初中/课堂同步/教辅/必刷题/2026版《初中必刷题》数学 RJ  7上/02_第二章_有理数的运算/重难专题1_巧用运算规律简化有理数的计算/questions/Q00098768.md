11[2024广东东莞质检,中]阅读下列材料,回答问题.
计算: $50\div\left(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{12}\right)$ .
解法1:原式= $50\div\frac{1}{3}-50\div\frac{1}{4}+50\div\frac{1}{12}=50\times3-50\times4+50\times12$ .该解法对吗?答:\_\_\_\_.
解法2:先计算原式的倒数, $\left(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{4}+\frac{1}{12}\right)\div50=\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{1}{50}-\frac{1}{4}\times\frac{1}{50}+\frac{1}{12}\times\frac{1}{50}=\frac{1}{300}$ ,故原式=300.
(1)请你用解法2的方法计算: $\left(-\frac{1}{30}\right)\div\left(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{10}+\frac{1}{6}-\frac{2}{5}\right)$ ;
