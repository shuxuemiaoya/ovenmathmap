> > 【解】因为除法没有分配律,所以解法1不对.故答案为不对.
> > (1)先计算原式的倒数, $\left(\frac{2}{3}-\frac{1}{10}+\frac{1}{6}-\frac{2}{5}\right)\div$ $\left(-\frac{1}{30}\right) = \frac{2}{3} \times (-30) - \frac{1}{10} \times (-30) + \frac{1}{6} \times$ $(-30) - \frac{2}{5} \times (-30) = -20 - (-3) + (-5) -$ $(-12) = -20 + 3 - 5 + 12 = -10$ ，故原式 $= -\frac{1}{10}$ . (2) $\left(1\frac{3}{4} - \frac{7}{8} - \frac{7}{12}\right) \div \left(-\frac{7}{8}\right) = \frac{7}{4} \times \left(-\frac{8}{7}\right) -$ $\frac{7}{8} \times \left(-\frac{8}{7}\right) - \frac{7}{12} \times \left(-\frac{8}{7}\right) = -2 - (-1) -$ $\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) = -2 + 1 + \frac{2}{3} = -\frac{1}{3}$ ，所以 $\left(-\frac{7}{8}\right) \div$ $\left(1\frac{3}{4} - \frac{7}{8} - \frac{7}{12}\right) = -3$ ，所以原式 $= -\frac{1}{3} +$ $(-3) = -\frac{10}{3}$ .
