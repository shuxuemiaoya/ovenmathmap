> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\angle P = \frac{1}{2}\angle A$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \angle ACE$ 是 $\triangle ABC$ 的一个外角, $\therefore \angle A = \angle ACE - \angle ABC.$ $\because CP$ 是 $\angle ACE$ 的平分线, $\therefore \angle PCE = \frac{1}{2}\angle ACE.$ $\because BP$ 是 $\angle ABC$ 的平分线, $\therefore \angle PBE = \frac{1}{2}\angle ABC,$ $\therefore \angle P = \angle PCE - \angle PBC = \frac{1}{2} \times (\angle ACE - \angle ABC) = \frac{1}{2}\angle A$ , 故答案为 $\angle P = \frac{1}{2}\angle A$ .
