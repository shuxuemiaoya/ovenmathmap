> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\angle ABC = 2\angle C, BP$ 平分 $\angle ABC,$ ∴ $\angle PBC = \angle C.$ 设 $\angle C = x$ ，则 $\angle PBC = x.$ ∵ $\angle FEC = 28^{\circ}, \therefore \angle AFE = x + 28^{\circ}.$ ∵ $\angle AEF = 2\angle AFE,$ ∴ $\angle AEF = 2x + 56^{\circ}.$ ∵ EP 平分 $\angle AEF, \therefore \angle FEP = x + 28^{\circ}.$ ∵ $\angle PEC = \angle P + \angle PBC, \therefore x + 28^{\circ} + 28^{\circ} = \angle P + x,$ ∴ $\angle P = 56^{\circ}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
