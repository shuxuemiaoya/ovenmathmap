> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{360}{7}
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ：BD平分∠CBA，且∠CBA=$m^{\circ},\therefore \angle CBD=\frac{1}{2}\angle CBA=\frac{1}{2}m^{\circ}.\because$ 延长DB至点G, $\therefore \angle CBD+\angle CBG=180^{\circ},\therefore \angle CBG=180^{\circ}-\angle CBD=180^{\circ}-\frac{1}{2}m^{\circ}.\because BE$ 平分 $\angle CBG,\therefore \angle CBE=\frac{1}{2}\angle CBG=\frac{1}{2}\left(180^{\circ}-\frac{1}{2}m^{\circ}\right)=90^{\circ}-\frac{1}{4}m^{\circ}.\because$ 延长AC至点F, $\therefore \angle FCB$ 是 $\triangle ABC$ 的外角. $\because \angle CAB=n^{\circ},\angle CBA=m^{\circ},\therefore \angle FCB=\angle CBA+\angle CAB=m^{\circ}+n^{\circ}.$ 又 $\because BE//AC,\therefore \angle FCB+\angle CBE=180^{\circ},\therefore m^{\circ}+n^{\circ}+90^{\circ}-\frac{1}{4}m^{\circ}=180^{\circ},\therefore\frac{3}{4}m+n=90,\therefore\frac{4}{7}n+\frac{3}{7}m=\frac{4}{7}\left(n+\frac{3}{4}m\right)=\frac{4}{7}\times90=\frac{360}{7}.$ 故答案为 $\frac{360}{7}.$
