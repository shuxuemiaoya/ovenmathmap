> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $0^{\circ} < \angle A < 60^{\circ}$ 或 $90^{\circ} < \angle A < 150^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意, 分两种情况: 若 $\angle A$ 为钝角, 则 $90^{\circ} < \angle A < 180^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}$ , 即 $90^{\circ} < \angle A < 150^{\circ}$ . 若 $\angle A$ 为锐角, 则 $0^{\circ} < \angle A < 90^{\circ}, 90^{\circ} < \angle APO < 180^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}$ , 即 $90^{\circ} < \angle APO < 150^{\circ}, \therefore 30^{\circ} < \angle APN < 90^{\circ}$ . $\because \angle APN = \angle O + \angle A, \therefore 30^{\circ} < \angle A + 30^{\circ} < 90^{\circ}, \therefore 0^{\circ} < \angle A < 60^{\circ}$ . 综上, $\angle A$ 的取值范围为 $0^{\circ} < \angle A < 60^{\circ}$ 或 $90^{\circ} < \angle A < 150^{\circ}$ .
