> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\angle ABC = n^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle BAC + \angle BCA =$
> >
> > $180^{\circ} - \angle ABC = 180^{\circ} - n^{\circ}$ . $\because O$ 是三个内角的平分线的交点, $\therefore \angle OBC = \frac{1}{2} \angle ABC = \frac{1}{2} n^{\circ}$ , $\angle OCA = \frac{1}{2} \angle BCA$ , $\angle OAC = \frac{1}{2} \angle BAC$ , $\therefore \angle OAC + \angle OCA = \frac{1}{2} (\angle BAC + \angle BCA) = \frac{1}{2} (180^{\circ} - n^{\circ}) = 90^{\circ} - \frac{1}{2} n^{\circ}, \therefore \angle AOC = 180^{\circ} - (\angle OAC + \angle OCA) = 180^{\circ} - \left(90^{\circ} - \frac{1}{2} n^{\circ}\right) = 90^{\circ} + \frac{1}{2} n^{\circ}$ . $\because \angle ODC = \angle AOC$ , $\therefore \angle ODC = 90^{\circ} + \frac{1}{2} n^{\circ}$ . $\because \angle ODC = \angle OBC + \angle BOD$ , $\angle OBC = \frac{1}{2} n^{\circ}$ , $\therefore \angle BOD = 90^{\circ}$ , 故选 D.
