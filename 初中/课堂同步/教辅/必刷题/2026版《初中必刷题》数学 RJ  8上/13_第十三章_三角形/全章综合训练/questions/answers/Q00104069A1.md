> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $110^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图, 延长 ED 交 BC 于点 G.
> > ∵ $\angle BAC = 90^{\circ}, \angle B = 60^{\circ}, \therefore \angle C = 90^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} = 30^{\circ}.$ ∵ $\angle EDF = 100^{\circ}, \angle F = 40^{\circ}, \therefore \angle E = 180^{\circ} - 40^{\circ} - 100^{\circ} = 40^{\circ}.$ ∵ BC//EF, ∴ $\angle EGC = \angle E = 40^{\circ}, \therefore \angle DMC = 180^{\circ} - \angle EGC - \angle C = 110^{\circ}.$ 故答案为 $110^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 归纳总结
> > 见平行,延长线段构造同位角、内错角或同旁内角.
