> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l}&\triangle A D B \rightarrow \text {底} A D \leftarrow \triangle A C D\\&\downarrow\\&\text {高} B E \quad \text {高} C F\\&\downarrow\\&S _ {\triangle A D B} + S _ {\triangle A C D} = \frac {1}{2} A D (B E + C F)\\&\downarrow\\&S _ {\triangle A B C} = \frac {1}{2} A D (B E + C F)\end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > ∵ $\triangle ABC$ 的面积不变, 点 D 沿 CB 自点 C 向点 B 运动时 AD 逐渐减小, ∴ $BE + CF$ 的值逐渐变大,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
