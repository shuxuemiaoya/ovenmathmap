>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ BD 是边 AC 上的中线, ∴ AD = CD, ∴ $S_{\triangle ABD} = S_{\triangle BDC} = \frac{1}{2} S_{\triangle ABC}$ . 又∵ $S_{\triangle ABC} = 18 \, cm^2$ , ∴ $S_{\triangle ABD} = S_{\triangle BDC} = 9 \, cm^2$ . ∵ E 是 BD 的中点, ∴ BE = DE, ∴ $S_{\triangle ABE} = S_{\triangle ADE} = \frac{1}{2} S_{\triangle ABD} = 4.5 \, cm^2$ , $S_{\triangle CBE} = S_{\triangle CDE} = \frac{1}{2} S_{\triangle BDC} = 4.5 \, cm^2$ , ∴ $S_{阴影} = S_{\triangle ABE} + S_{\triangle CDE} = 9 \, cm^2$ . 故选 B.
> >
> > 3.8 【解析】∵ AD 是 BC 边上的中线, ∴ BD = CD, ∴ △ABD 的周长 - △ADC 的周长 = (AB + AD + BD) - (AC + AD + CD) = AB - AC = 3, 即 AB - AC = 3. ① 又∵ AB + AC = 13, ② ∴ 由① + ②得 2AB = 16, 解得 AB = 8. 故答案为 8.
