> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】∵ AD 是 BC 边上的高， $\angle B = 50^{\circ}$ ，
> > ∴ $\angle ADB = 90^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle BAD = 90^{\circ} - 50^{\circ} = 40^{\circ}$ .
> > ∵ $\angle EAD = 15^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle BAE = \angle BAD - \angle EAD = 40^{\circ} - 15^{\circ} = 25^{\circ}$ . ∵ AE 是 $\angle BAC$ 的平分线，
> > ∴ $\angle BAC = 2 \angle BAE = 50^{\circ}$ . 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中， $\angle BAC + \angle B + \angle C = 180^{\circ}, \angle B = 50^{\circ}, \angle BAC = 50^{\circ}, \therefore \angle C = 180^{\circ} - \angle B - \angle BAC = 180^{\circ} - 50^{\circ} - 50^{\circ} = 80^{\circ}.$
