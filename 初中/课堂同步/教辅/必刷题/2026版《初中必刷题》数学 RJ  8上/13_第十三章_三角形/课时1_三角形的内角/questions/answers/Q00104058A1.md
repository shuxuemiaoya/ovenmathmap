> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $52^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图, 由题意可得 $\angle1 = \angle3 = 120^{\circ}, \therefore \angle4 = 180^{\circ} - 120^{\circ} = 60^{\circ}. \because \angle2 = 68^{\circ}, \therefore \angle P = 180^{\circ} - \angle2 - \angle4 = 180^{\circ} - 68^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} = 52^{\circ}.$ 故答案为 $52^{\circ}$ .
