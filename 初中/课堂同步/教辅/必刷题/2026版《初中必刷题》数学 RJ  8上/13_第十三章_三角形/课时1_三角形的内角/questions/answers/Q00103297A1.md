> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $240^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图. ∵ $\angle1 + \angle DEC = 180^{\circ}, \angle2 + \angle EDC = 180^{\circ}, \therefore \angle1 + \angle2 + \angle DEC + \angle EDC = 360^{\circ}. \because \angle C = 60^{\circ},$ ∴ $\angle DEC + \angle EDC = 180^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} = 120^{\circ}, \therefore \angle1 + \angle2 = 360^{\circ} - (\angle DEC + \angle EDC) = 240^{\circ}$ ，故答案为 $240^{\circ}$ .
