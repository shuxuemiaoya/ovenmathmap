> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $(a - 1)(a^2 + a + 1)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 将题图(2)分成三个长方体, 可得体积为 $a \times a \times (a - 1) + 1 \times 1 \times (a - 1) + 1 \times a \times (a - 1) = a^2(a - 1) + a(a - 1) + (a - 1) = (a - 1)(a^2 + a + 1), \therefore a^3 - 1 = (a - 1)(a^2 + a + 1)$ . 故答案为 $(a - 1)(a^2 + a + 1)$ .
