> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** -3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $m^{2}-2m-2011=0$ , ∴ $m(m-2)=2011$ . $m^{3}-m^{2}-2013m-2014=m^{3}-m^{2}-m^{2}+m^{2}-2013m-2014=m^{3}-2m^{2}+m^{2}-2013m-$
> >
> > $2014=m^{2}(m-2)+m(m-2013)-2014=m^{2}(m-2)+m(m-2-2011)-2014=m^{2}(m-2)+m(m-2)-2011m-2014.$ 把 $m(m-2)=2011$ 代入上式，得原式 $=2011m+2011-2011m-2014=-3.$ 故答案为-3.
