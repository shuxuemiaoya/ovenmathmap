> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $(2m + n)(m + 2n)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) 由图形可知, $2m^{2} + 5mn + 2n^{2} = (2m + n)(m + 2n)$ . 故答案为 $(2m + n)(m + 2n)$ . (2) ①依题意得, $2m^{2} + 2n^{2} = 58$ , $mn = 10$ , $\therefore m^{2} + n^{2} = 29$ , $\therefore (m + n)^{2} = m^{2} + n^{2} + 2mn = 29 + 20 = 49$ . $\because m + n > 0$ , $\therefore m + n = 7$ . ②所有裁剪线长之和为 $7 \times 6 = 42$ (cm). 故答案为 42.
