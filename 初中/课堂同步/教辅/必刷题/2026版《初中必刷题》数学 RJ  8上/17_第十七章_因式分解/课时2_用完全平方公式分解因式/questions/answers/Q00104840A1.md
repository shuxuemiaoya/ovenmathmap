> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $a = 2023x + 2022$ , $b = 2023x + 2023$ , $c = 2023x + 2024$ , ∴ $a - b = (2023x + 2022) - (2023x + 2023) = 2023x + 2022 - 2023x - 2023 = -1$ , $a - c = (2023x + 2022) - (2023x + 2024) = 2023x + 2022 - 2023x - 2024 = -2$ , $b - c = (2023x + 2023) - (2023x + 2024) = 2023x + 2023 - 2023x - 2024 = -1$ ,
> >
> > ∴ $a^{2} + b^{2} + c^{2} - ab - ac - bc = \frac{1}{2} [2(a^{2} + b^{2} + c^{2} - ab - ac - bc)] = \frac{1}{2}(2a^{2} + 2b^{2} + 2c^{2} - 2ab - 2ac - 2bc) =$
> >
> > ## 关键点拨
> >
> > 对于 $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}-ab-ac-bc$ ，要用完全平方公式求值，需要将各项系数扩大2倍，然后再将整体乘 $\frac{1}{2}$ ，再运用完全平方公式因式分解，根据已知条件可得a-b,a-c,b-c的值，然后代入求值.
> >
> > $\frac{1}{2} \left[ (a^2 - 2ab + b^2) + (a^2 - 2ac + c^2) + (b^2 - 2bc + c^2) \right] = \frac{1}{2} \left[ (a - b)^2 + (a - c)^2 + (b - c)^2 \right] = \frac{1}{2} \times \left[ (-1)^2 + (-2)^2 + (-1)^2 \right] = \frac{1}{2} \times (1 + 4 + 1) = \frac{1}{2} \times 6 = 3$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
