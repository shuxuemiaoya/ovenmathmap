> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 8
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $2m^{3}n-4m^{2}n^{2}+2mn^{3}=2mn(m^{2}-2mn+n^{2})=2mn(m-n)^{2}$ . $\because mn=4, m-n=-1,$ $\therefore 2mn(m-n)^{2}=2\times4\times(-1)^{2}=8,\therefore 2m^{3}n-4m^{2}n^{2}+2mn^{3}=8.$ 故答案为 8.
