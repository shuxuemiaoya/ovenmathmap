> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ OA = OB = OC, ∠ABC = ∠ADC = 70°, ∴ ∠ABO = ∠BAO, ∠OBC = ∠OCB, ∴ ∠AOB + ∠BOC = 360° - 2 (∠ABO + ∠OBC) = 220°, ∴ ∠AOC = 360° - 220° = 140°. ∵ ∠OAD + ∠ADC + ∠OCD + ∠AOC = 360°, ∴ ∠DAO + ∠DCO = 150°.
