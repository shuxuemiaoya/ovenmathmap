> > 易错警示
> > 本题中未指出腰,所以需要对腰进行分类讨论,避免漏解.
> >
> > $A_{1}D, \angle BA_{1}C$ 是 $\triangle A_{1}A_{2}D$ 的外角， $\therefore \angle DA_{2}A_{1} = \frac{1}{2}\angle BA_{1}C = \frac{1}{2} \times 75^{\circ} = 37.5^{\circ}$ . 同理可得， $\angle EA_{3}A_{2} = \left(\frac{75}{4}\right)^{\circ}, \angle FA_{4}A_{3} = \left(\frac{75}{8}\right)^{\circ}, \therefore$ 第 $n$ 个等腰三角形的底角的度数是 $\left(\frac{75}{2^{n-1}}\right)^{\circ}$ .
