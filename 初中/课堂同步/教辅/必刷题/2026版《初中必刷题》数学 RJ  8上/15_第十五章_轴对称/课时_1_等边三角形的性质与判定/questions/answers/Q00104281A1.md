> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\angle B = \angle A$ (答案不唯一)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $\angle B = \angle A$ 时, $\because \angle B = \angle C, \therefore \angle A = \angle B = \angle C, \therefore \triangle ABC$ 是等边三角形. 故答案为 $\angle B = \angle A$ (答案不唯一).
