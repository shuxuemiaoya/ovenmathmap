> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ AD = DE = DF, ∴ $\angle DAE = \angle DEA$ , $\angle DAF = \angle DFA$ . ∵ $\angle DAE + \angle DAF = \angle BAC = 60^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle DEA + \angle DFA = 60^{\circ}$ . ∵ $\angle ABC = \angle DEA + \angle EDB = 60^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle EDB = \angle DFA$ . ∵ $\angle ACB = \angle CFD + \angle CDF = 60^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle CDF = \angle BED$ , ∴ $\triangle BDE \cong \triangle CFD$ , ∴ BD = CF, BE = CD, ∴ $\triangle BED$ 的周长为 BD + BE + DE = BD + CD + AD = BC + AD. ∵ 点 D 在 BC 边上从 B 至 C 的运动过程中, AD 的长先变小后变大, ∴ $\triangle BED$ 的周长先变小后变大,
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
