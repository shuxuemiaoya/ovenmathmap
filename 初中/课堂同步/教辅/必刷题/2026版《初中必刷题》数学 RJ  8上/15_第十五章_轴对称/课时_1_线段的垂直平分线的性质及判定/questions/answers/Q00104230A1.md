> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)当 $x = 5$ 时, 点 $E$ 在线段 $CD$ 的垂直平分线上. 理由: 当 $x = 5$ 时, $AE = 2 \times 5 = 10(\mathrm{~cm})$ . $\because AB = 25 \mathrm{~cm}$ , $DA = 15 \mathrm{~cm}$ , $CB = 10 \mathrm{~cm}$ , $\therefore BE = AD = 15 \mathrm{~cm}$ , $AE = BC = 10 \mathrm{~cm}$ . 在 $\triangle ADE$ 和 $\triangle BEC$ 中, $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}AD = BE, \\ \angle A = \angle B, \\ AE = BC, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \triangle ADE \cong \triangle BEC(\mathrm{SAS}), \therefore DE = CE, \therefore$ 点 $E$ 在线段 $CD$ 的垂直平分线上. 故当 $x = 5$ 时, 点 $E$ 在线段 $CD$ 的垂直平分线上. (2) $DE$ 与 $CE$ 的位置关系是 $DE \perp CE$ . 理由: $\because \triangle ADE \cong \triangle BEC, \therefore \angle ADE = \angle CEB.$ $\because \angle A = 90^{\circ}, \therefore \angle ADE + \angle AED = 90^{\circ},$ $\therefore \angle AED + \angle CEB = 90^{\circ}, \therefore \angle DEC = 180^{\circ} - (\angle AED + \angle CEB) = 90^{\circ}, \therefore DE \perp CE.$
