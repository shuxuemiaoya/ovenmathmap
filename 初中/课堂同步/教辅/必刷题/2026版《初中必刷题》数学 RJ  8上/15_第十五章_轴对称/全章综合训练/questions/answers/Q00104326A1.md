> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中, $\angle BAC = 75^{\circ}$ , $\angle ACB = 35^{\circ}$ , $\therefore \angle ABC = 180^{\circ} - \angle BAC - \angle ACB = 70^{\circ}$ . $\because BD$ 平分 $\angle ABC$ , $\therefore \angle DBC = \frac{1}{2} \angle ABC = 35^{\circ}$ , $\therefore \angle DBC = \angle ACB$ , $\therefore BD = CD$ , $\therefore AD + BD = AD + CD = AC = 8$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
