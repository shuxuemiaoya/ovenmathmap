> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 130 或 100 或 160
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由旋转的性质得 $BD = AB = BC$ . $\because \triangle ADC$ 为等腰三角形, $\therefore$ 分三种情况: ① 当 $DA = DC$ 时, 易得 $\triangle ABD \cong \triangle CBD$ , $\therefore \angle ABD = \angle CBD = \frac{1}{2} (360^\circ - \angle ABC) = 130^\circ$ , $\therefore m = 130$ ;
> > - **②**：当 $AD = AC$ 时, 易得 $\triangle ABD \cong \triangle ABC$ , $\therefore \angle ABD = \angle ABC = 100^\circ$ , $\therefore m = 100$ ;
> > - **③**：当 $CA = CD$ 时, 易得 $\triangle CBA \cong \triangle CBD$ , $\therefore \angle CBD = \angle ABC = 100^\circ$ , $\therefore \angle ABD = 360^\circ - 100^\circ - 100^\circ = 160^\circ$ , $\therefore m = 160$ . 综上所述, $m$ 所有可能的取值为 130 或 100 或 160. 故答案为 130 或 100 或 160.
