> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】∵ $\angle BGE = \angle ADE, \angle BGE = \angle CGF, \therefore \angle ADE = \angle CGF.$ ∵ $AC \perp BD, BF \perp CD, \therefore \angle ADE + \angle DAE = \angle CGF + \angle GCF = 90^{\circ}, \therefore \angle DAE = \angle GCF.$ 在 $\triangle ADE$ 和 $\triangle CDE$ 中， $\left\{\begin{aligned}\angle DAE&=\angle DCE,\\ \angle AED&=\angle CED,\\ DE&=DE,\end{aligned}\right.$ ∴ $\triangle ADE \cong \triangle CDE(AAS), \therefore AD = CD.$ (2)【解】面积等于 $\triangle ADE$ 面积的2倍的三角形有 $\triangle ACD, \triangle ABE, \triangle BCE, \triangle BHG.$ 设 DE = a，则 AE = 2DE = 2a, EG = DE = a,
> > ∴ $S_{\triangle ADE} = \frac{1}{2} AE \cdot DE = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2a \cdot a = a^{2}.$ ∵ BH 是 $\triangle ABE$ 的中线，∴ AH = HE = a. ∵ AC ⊥ BD, ∴ $\angle AED = \angle CED = 90^{\circ}.$ ∵ AD = CD, DE = DE, ∴ Rt $\triangle ADE \cong Rt\triangle CDE(HL), \therefore CE = AE = 2a,$ 则 $S_{\triangle ADC} = \frac{1}{2} AC \cdot DE = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (2a + 2a) \cdot a = 2a^{2} = 2S_{\triangle ADE}.$ 在 $\triangle ADE$ 和 $\triangle BGE$ 中， $\left\{\begin{aligned}\angle AED &=\angle BEG,\\ DE &=GE,\\ \angle ADE &=\angle BGE,\end{aligned}\right.$ ∴ $\triangle ADE \cong \triangle BGE(ASA), \therefore BE = AE = 2a,$ ∴ $S_{\triangle ABE} = \frac{1}{2} AE \cdot BE = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2a \cdot 2a = 2a^{2} = 2S_{\triangle ADE}, S_{\triangle BCE} = \frac{1}{2} CE \cdot BE = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 2a \cdot 2a = 2a^{2} = 2S_{\triangle ADE}, S_{\triangle BHG} = \frac{1}{2} HG \cdot BE = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (a + a) \cdot 2a = 2a^{2} = 2S_{\triangle ADE}.$ 综上所述，面积等于 $\triangle ADE$ 面积的2倍的三角形有 $\triangle ACD,$ $\triangle ABE, \triangle BCE, \triangle BHG.$
