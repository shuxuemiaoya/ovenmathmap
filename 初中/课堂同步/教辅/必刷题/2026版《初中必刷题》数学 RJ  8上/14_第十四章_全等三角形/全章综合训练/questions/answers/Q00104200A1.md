> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 12
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图, 延长 $AB, CD$ 交于点 $E$ . $\because AD$ 平分 $\angle BAC$ , $\therefore \angle CAD = \angle EAD$ . $\because CD \perp AD$ , $\therefore \angle ADC = \angle ADE = 90^\circ$ .
> >
> > 在 $\triangle ADE$ 和 $\triangle ADC$ 中, $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \angle ADE = \angle ADC, \\ AD = AD, \\ \angle EAD = \angle CAD, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \triangle ADE \cong \triangle ADC (\text { ASA })$ , $\therefore AC = AE, DE = CD$ . $\because AC - AB = 5$ , $\therefore AE - AB = 5$ , 即 $BE = 5$ . $\because DE = DC$ , $\therefore S_{\triangle BDC} = \frac{1}{2} S_{\triangle BCE}$ , $\therefore$ 当 $BE \perp BC$ 时, $S_{\triangle BEC}$ 取最大值, 即 $S_{\triangle BDC}$ 取最大值, 此时 $S_{\triangle BDC} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 5 = 12.5$ . 故答案为 12.5.
> >
> > 关键点拨
> > (2) 设 DE = a，
> > 用含 a 的代数式表示出各相关线段的长，
> > 易证 $S_{\triangle ADC} = S_{\triangle ABE} = S_{\triangle BCE} = S_{\triangle BHG} = 2a^{2} = 2S_{\triangle ADE}$ .
