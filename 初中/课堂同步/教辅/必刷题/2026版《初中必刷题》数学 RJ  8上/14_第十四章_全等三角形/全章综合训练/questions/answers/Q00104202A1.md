> > [!success] **【答案】** (1)【解】图(2): $BC+BE=BF$ , 图(3): BE-BC=BF. (2)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】图(2):∵ AB = DF, ∠A = ∠D, ∠B = ∠F, ∴ △ABC ≅ △DFE (ASA), ∴ BC = EF.
> > ∵ BE = BC + CE, ∴ BC + BE = EF + BC + CE = BF.
> > 图(3): ∵ AB = DF, ∠A = ∠D, ∠ABC = ∠DFE, ∴ △ABC ≅ △DFE (ASA), ∴ BC = EF. ∵ BE = BF + EF, ∴ BE - BC = BF + EF - BC = BF + BC - BC = BF. (任选一种回答即可)
