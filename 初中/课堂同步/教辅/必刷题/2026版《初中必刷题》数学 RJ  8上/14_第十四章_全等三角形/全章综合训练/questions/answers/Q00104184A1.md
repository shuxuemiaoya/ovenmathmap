> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\angle A = 60^{\circ}$ , $\angle B = 40^{\circ}$ , $\angle A + \angle B + \angle ACB = 180^{\circ}$ , ∴ $\angle ACB = 180^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} - 40^{\circ} = 80^{\circ}$ . ∵ $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEC$ , ∴ $\angle DCE = \angle ACB = 80^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
