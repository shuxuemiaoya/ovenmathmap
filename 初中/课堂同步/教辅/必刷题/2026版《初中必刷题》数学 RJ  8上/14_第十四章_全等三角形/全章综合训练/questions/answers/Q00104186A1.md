> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** DE = EF (答案不唯一)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ CF // AB,
> > ∴ $\angle A = \angle ECF, \angle ADE = \angle CFE,$ ∴ 添加条件
> > DE = EF, 可以使得 $\triangle ADE \cong \triangle CFE$ (AAS),
> > ∴ AE = CE. 故答案为 DE = EF (答案不唯一).
