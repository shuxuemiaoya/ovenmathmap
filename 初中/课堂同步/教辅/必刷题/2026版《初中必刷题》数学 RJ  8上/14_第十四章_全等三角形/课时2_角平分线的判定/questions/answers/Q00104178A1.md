> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $80^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意, 得 OP 平分 $\angle AOB$ , $\therefore \angle AOB = 2\angle POB = 2\times 40^{\circ} = 80^{\circ}$ . 由长方形直尺可知 $CP \parallel OB, \therefore \angle ACP = \angle AOB = 80^{\circ}$ . 故答案为 $80^{\circ}$ .
