> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 平分线
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】由作图过程可得, $OA = OB, AC = BC$ . 在 $\triangle AOC$ 和 $\triangle BOC$ 中, $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} OA = OB, \\ OC = OC, \\ AC = BC, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \triangle AOC \cong \triangle BOC (\mathrm{SSS}), \therefore \angle AOC = \angle BOC,$ $\therefore$ 射线 $OC$ 为 $\angle MON$ 的平分线.
> >
