> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $BD \perp CD, \therefore \angle BDC = 90^{\circ},$ ∴ $\angle C + \angle CBD = 90^{\circ}.$ ∵ $\angle A = 90^{\circ}, \therefore \angle ABD + \angle ADB = 90^{\circ}.$ ∵ $\angle ADB = \angle C, \therefore \angle ABD = \angle CBD, \therefore BD$ 是 $\angle ABC$ 的平分线. 当 $DP \perp BC$ 时, DP 的长度最小. ∵ $\angle A = 90^{\circ}, \therefore DA \perp AB,$ ∴ DP 长的最小值与 AD 长相等. ∵ AD = 4,
> >
> > ∴ DP 长的最小值是 4,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
