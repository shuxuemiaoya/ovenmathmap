> > [!success] **【答案】** 4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【证明】∵ $\triangle ACD \cong \triangle BED, \therefore \angle ADC = \angle BDE, \angle CAD = \angle DBE.$ ∵ $\angle ADC + \angle BDE = 180^\circ$ , ∴ $\angle ADC = \angle BDE = 90^\circ$ . ∵ $\angle AEF + \angle AFE + \angle EAF = \angle BED + \angle BDE + \angle DBE = 180^\circ$ , $\angle AEF = \angle BED, \therefore \angle AFE = \angle BDE = 90^\circ$ . (3)【解】∵ $S_{\triangle BCF} = 20$ , $S_{\text{四边形}CFED} = 8$ , ∴ $S_{\triangle BDE} = S_{\triangle BCF} - S_{\text{四边形}CFED} = 12$ . ∵ $\triangle ACD \cong \triangle BED$ , ∴ $S_{\triangle ACD} = S_{\triangle BED} = 12$ , ∴ $S_{\triangle AEF} = S_{\triangle ACD} - S_{\text{四边形}CFED} = 12 - 8 = 4$ . 故答案为 4.
