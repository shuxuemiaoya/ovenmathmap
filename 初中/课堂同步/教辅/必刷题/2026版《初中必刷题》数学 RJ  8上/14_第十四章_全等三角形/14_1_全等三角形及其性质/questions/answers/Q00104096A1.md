> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle A'B'C$ , ∴ $\angle A = \angle A'$ , $\angle CBA = \angle CB'A', \therefore \angle A = 40^\circ, \angle CBA = 60^\circ,$ ∴ $\angle ACB = 180^\circ - \angle A - \angle CBA = 180^\circ - 40^\circ - 60^\circ = 80^\circ.$ ∵ BO, CO 分别平分 $\angle ABC, \angle PCB,$ ∴ $\angle OBC = \frac{1}{2} \angle ABC, \angle OCB = \frac{1}{2} \angle PCB,$ ∴ $\angle BOC = 180^\circ - \angle OBC - \angle OCB = 180^\circ - \frac{1}{2} (\angle ABC + \angle PCB) = 180^\circ - \frac{1}{2} (180^\circ - \angle BPC) = 90^\circ + \frac{1}{2} \angle BPC = 90^\circ + \frac{1}{2} (\angle A + \angle ACP) = 110^\circ + \frac{1}{2} \angle ACP,$ 则 $\angle ACP = 2\angle BOC - 220^\circ.$ ∵ P 点在 AB 边上且不与 A, B 重合，∴ $0^\circ < \angle ACP < 80^\circ, \therefore 0^\circ < 2\angle BOC - 220^\circ < 80^\circ, \therefore 110^\circ < \angle BOC < 150^\circ, \therefore m = 110, n = 150, \therefore n - m = 40.$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
