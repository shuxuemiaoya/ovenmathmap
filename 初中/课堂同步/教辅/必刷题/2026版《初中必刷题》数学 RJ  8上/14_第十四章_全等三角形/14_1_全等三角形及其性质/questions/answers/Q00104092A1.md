> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** (6, -5)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ A(-6, 0), B(0, 5), ∴ OA = 6, OB = 5, ∠AOB = 90°. ∵ △OA'B' ≅ △AOB, ∴ OA' = OA = 6, A'B' = OB = 5, ∠B'A'O = 90°. ∵ 点 B' 在第四象限, ∴ 点 B' 的坐标是 (6, -5).
