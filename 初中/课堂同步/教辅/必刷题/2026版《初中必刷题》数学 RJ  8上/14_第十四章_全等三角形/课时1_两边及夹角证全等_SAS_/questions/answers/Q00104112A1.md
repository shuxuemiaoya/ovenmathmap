> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $AD = \frac{1}{2} BC$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 延长 $AD$ 到点 $E$ , 使 $DE = \overline{AD}$ , 连接 $BE$ . $\because AD$ 是 $BC$ 边上的中线, $\therefore CD = BD$ . 在 $\triangle ACD$ 和 $\triangle EBD$ 中, $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} AD = DE, \\ \angle ADC = \angle EDB, \\ CD = BD, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \triangle ACD \cong \triangle EBD(SAS)$ , $\therefore AC = BE, \angle DAC = \angle DEB, \therefore AC // BE, \therefore \angle BAC + \angle ABE = 180^\circ$ . $\because \angle BAC = 90^\circ, \therefore \angle BAC = \angle ABE = 90^\circ$ . 在 $\triangle BAC$ 和 $\triangle ABE$ 中, $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} AC = BE, \\ \angle BAC = \angle ABE, \\ AB = AB, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \triangle BAC \cong \triangle ABE(SAS)$ , $\therefore BC = AE$ . $\because AD = DE = \frac{1}{2} AE, \therefore AD = \frac{1}{2} BC$ .
> >
> > 归纳总结
> > 遇中线问题常运用倍长中线法构造全等三角形, 将条件转化到同一个三角形中.
