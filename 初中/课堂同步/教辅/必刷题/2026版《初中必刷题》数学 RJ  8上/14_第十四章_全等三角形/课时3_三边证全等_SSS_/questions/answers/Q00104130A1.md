> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意得, $BP = BD = FQ = FH$ , $DP = QH$ . 在 $\triangle PBD$ 和 $\triangle QFH$ 中, $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} BP = FQ, \\ BD = FH, \\ DP = QH, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \triangle PBD \cong \triangle QFH (\text{SSS}), \therefore \angle GFE = \angle ABC$ , 故选 A.
> >
> > 关键点拨
> > 解决本题的关键是掌握三角形全等的判定方法.
