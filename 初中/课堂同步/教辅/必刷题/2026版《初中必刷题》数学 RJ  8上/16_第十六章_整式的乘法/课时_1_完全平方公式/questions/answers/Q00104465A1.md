> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 27
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $m - \frac{1}{m} = 5$ , ∴ $m^{2} - 2 \times m \times \frac{1}{m} + \frac{1}{m^{2}} = 25$ , ∴ $m^{2} - 2 + \frac{1}{m^{2}} = 25$ , ∴ $m^{2} + \frac{1}{m^{2}} = 25 + 2 = 27$ , 故 $m^{2} + \frac{1}{m^{2}}$ 的值为 27.
