> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\pm8x$ 或 $64x^{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > - **①**：∵ $16x^{2}+M+1=(4x)^{2}+M+1^{2}=(4x\pm1)^{2},\therefore M=\pm2\times4x\times1=\pm8x.$ ②∵ $M+16x^{2}+1=M+2\times8x^{2}\times1+1^{2}=(8x^{2}+1)^{2}$ ,
> > ∴ $M = (8x^{2})^{2} = 64x^{4}$ . 故答案为 $\pm8x$ 或 $64x^{4}$ .
