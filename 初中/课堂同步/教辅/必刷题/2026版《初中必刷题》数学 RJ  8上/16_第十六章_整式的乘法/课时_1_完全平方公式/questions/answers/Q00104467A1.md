> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 16
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $m^2 - S_1 = n^2 - S_2, \therefore S_1 - S_2 = m^2 - n^2 = (m + n)(m - n)$ . $\because m + n = 8, mn = 15, \therefore (m - n)^2 = (m + n)^2 - 4mn = 64 - 60 = 4.$ $\because m > n, \therefore m - n = 2, \therefore S_1 - S_2 = 8 \times 2 = 16.$ 故答案为 16.
