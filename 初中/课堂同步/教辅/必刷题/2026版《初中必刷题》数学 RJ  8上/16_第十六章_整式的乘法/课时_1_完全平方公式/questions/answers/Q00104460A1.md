> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $x + y = 2a, x - y = 2b, \therefore (x + y)^{2} = 4a^{2}, (x - y)^{2} = 4b^{2}, \therefore x^{2} + 2xy + y^{2} = 4a^{2},$ ① $x^{2} - 2xy + y^{2} = 4b^{2},$ ② ∴ ① - ② 得 $4xy = 4a^{2} - 4b^{2},$ ∴ $xy = a^{2} - b^{2}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
