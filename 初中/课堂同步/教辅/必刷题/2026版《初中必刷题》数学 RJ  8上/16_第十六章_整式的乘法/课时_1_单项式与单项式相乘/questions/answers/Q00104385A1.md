> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left(\frac{\pi}{8} + 2\right)x^2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{\left(\frac{\pi}{8} + 2\right)x^2}{\frac{1}{2}\pi \times \left(\frac{x}{2}\right)^2 + x \times 2x = \frac{\pi}{8}x^2 + 2x^2 = \left(\frac{\pi}{8} + 2\right)x^2 (\mathrm{cm})}$ , 故答案为 $\left(\frac{\pi}{8} + 2\right)x^2$ .
