> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 2304
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $2(mn \cdot 3m) \cdot 3(2n \cdot mn) = 36m^{3}n^{3} = 36(mn)^{3} = 36 \times 4^{3} = 36 \times 64 = 2304$ . 故答案为 2304.
