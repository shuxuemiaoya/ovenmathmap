> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】原式 $= [4a^{2} + 4ab + b^{2} - (4a^{2} - b^{2})]\div 2b =$ $(4a^{2} + 4ab + b^{2} - 4a^{2} + b^{2})\div 2b = (4ab + 2b^{2})\div$ $2b = 2a + b.$ 当 $a = 2,b = -1$ 时，原式 $= 2\times 2-$ $1 = 3.$
