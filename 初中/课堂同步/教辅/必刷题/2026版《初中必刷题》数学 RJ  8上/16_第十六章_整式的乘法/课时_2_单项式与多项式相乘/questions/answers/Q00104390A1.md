> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)∵ $A=-2x^{2},B=x^{2}-3x-1,C=-x+1$ ,
> > ∴ $A \cdot B + A \cdot C = -2x^{2} \cdot (x^{2}-3x-1) - 2x^{2} \cdot (-x+1) = -2x^{4} + 6x^{3} + 2x^{2} + 2x^{3} - 2x^{2} = -2x^{4} + 8x^{3}$ .
> > (2) $\because A=-2x^{2},B=x^{2}-3x-1,C=-x+1,$ ∴ $A \cdot (B - C) = -2x^{2}(x^{2}-3x-1+x-1) = -2x^{2}(x^{2}-2x-2) = -2x^{4} + 4x^{3} + 4x^{2}.$ (3) $\because A=-2x^{2},B=x^{2}-3x-1,C=-x+1,$ ∴ $A \cdot C - B = -2x^{2}(-x + 1) - (x^{2} - 3x - 1) = 2x^{3} - 2x^{2} - x^{2} + 3x + 1 = 2x^{3} - 3x^{2} + 3x + 1.$
