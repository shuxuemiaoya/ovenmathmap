> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{2}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because a(x^{2}+3x+b)=5x^{2}+15x+10,$ $\therefore ax^{2}+3ax+ab=5x^{2}+15x+10,\therefore a=5,ab=10,$ 则 $b=2,\therefore \frac{b}{a}=\frac{2}{5}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{2}{5}$ .
