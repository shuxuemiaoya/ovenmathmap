> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 27, $2a+b=c$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $x^{a}=3, x^{b}=8, x^{c}=72$ ,
> > ∴ $x^{a-b+c} = x^{a} \div x^{b} \cdot x^{c} = 3 \div 8 \times 72 = 27$ . ∵ $(x^{a})^{2} = x^{2a} = 9$ , ∴ $x^{2a} \times x^{b} = 9 \times 8 = 72 = x^{c}$ , ∴ $x^{2a+b} = x^{c}$ ,
> > ∴ $2a+b=c$ , 故答案为 27, $2a+b=c$ .
