> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $x^{2}-2x+3,1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)由题意,得
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{c} x ^ {2} - 2 x + 3 \\ x - 2 \sqrt {x ^ {3} - 4 x ^ {2} + 7 x - 5} \\ \frac {x ^ {3} - 2 x ^ {2}}{- 2 x ^ {2} + 7 x} \\ \frac {- 2 x ^ {2} + 4 x}{3 x - 5} \\ \frac {3 x - 6}{1} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为 $x^{2}-2x+3,1$ .
> >
> > (2)由题意,得
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{c} x - 3 \\ x ^ {2} + 2 x + 2 \sqrt {x ^ {3} - x ^ {2} + a x + b} \\ \frac {x ^ {3} + 2 x ^ {2} + 2 x}{- 3 x ^ {2} + (a - 2) x + b} \\ \frac {- 3 x ^ {2} - 6 x - 6}{0} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 关键点按
> > 观察 a, b, c,
> > 根据平方差公式可得 a = 1950 $^{2}$ - 16, b = 1950 $^{2}$ - 49, c = 1950 $^{2}$ - 1, 再进行比较即可.
> >
> > ∴ a-2=-6, b=-6, 即 a=-4, b=-6.
