> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $(9a^{3})^{m} \div (3a) = (3^{2})^{m} \cdot a^{3m} \div (3a) = 3^{2m} \cdot a^{3m} \div (3a) = 3^{2m-1} \cdot a^{3m-1} = 3a^{n}$ ,
> > ∴ $2m-1=1,3m-1=n$ , 解得 m=1, n=2, ∴ $m+n=3$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
