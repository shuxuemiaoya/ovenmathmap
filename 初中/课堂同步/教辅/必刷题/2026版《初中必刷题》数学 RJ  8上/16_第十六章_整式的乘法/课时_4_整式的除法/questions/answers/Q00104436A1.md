> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 正确结果: 原式 = $(6x^{3}y) \div (3xy) - (3x^{2}y^{2}) \div (3xy) = 2x^{2} - xy$ ; 错误结果: 原式 = $(6x^{3}y) \div (3xy) + (3x^{2}y^{2}) \div (3xy) = 2x^{2} + xy$ , $\therefore (2x^{2} - xy)(2x^{2} + xy) = 4x^{4} + 2x^{3}y - 2x^{3}y - x^{2}y^{2} = 4x^{4} - x^{2}y^{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
