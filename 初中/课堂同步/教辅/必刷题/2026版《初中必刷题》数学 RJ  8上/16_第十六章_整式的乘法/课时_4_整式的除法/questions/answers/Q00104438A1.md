> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $b + \frac{1}{8}\pi a$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设新窗框的高度为 $x$ . 根据题意, 得 $ab + \frac{1}{2}\pi \cdot \left(\frac{a}{2}\right)^2 = ax$ , $\therefore x = \left[ab + \frac{1}{2}\pi \cdot \left(\frac{a}{2}\right)^2\right] \div a = \left(ab + \frac{1}{8}\pi a^2\right) \div a = b + \frac{1}{8}\pi a$ .
