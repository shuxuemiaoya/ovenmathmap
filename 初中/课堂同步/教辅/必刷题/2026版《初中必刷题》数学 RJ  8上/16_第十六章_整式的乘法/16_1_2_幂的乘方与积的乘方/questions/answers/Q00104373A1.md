> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2^{6}-2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) $2+2^{2}=2^{3}-2,2+2^{2}+2^{3}=2^{4}-2,2+2^{2}+2^{3}+2^{4}=2^{5}-2,\cdots,2+2^{2}+2^{3}+2^{4}+\cdots+2^{n}=2^{n+1}-2,\therefore2+2^{2}+2^{3}+2^{4}+2^{5}=2^{6}-2$ ，故答案为 $2^{6}-2$ .
> > (2) $2^{21}+2^{22}+2^{23}+2^{24}+2^{25}=2+2^{2}+2^{3}+2^{4}+\cdots+2^{24}+2^{25}-(2+2^{2}+2^{3}+2^{4}+\cdots+2^{19}+2^{20})=2^{26}-2-(2^{21}-2)=2^{26}-2^{21}$ ，故答案为 $2^{26}-2^{21}$ .
> > (3) $2^{100}+2^{101}+2^{102}+\cdots+2^{199}+2^{200}=2+2^{2}+2^{3}+2^{4}+\cdots+2^{199}+2^{200}-(2+2^{2}+2^{3}+2^{4}+\cdots+2^{98}+2^{99})=2^{201}-2-(2^{100}-2)=2^{201}-2^{100}\cdot\because 2^{100}=S,\therefore$ 这组数据的和为 $2^{201}-2^{100}=2\times(2^{100})^{2}-2^{100}=2S^{2}-S.$
