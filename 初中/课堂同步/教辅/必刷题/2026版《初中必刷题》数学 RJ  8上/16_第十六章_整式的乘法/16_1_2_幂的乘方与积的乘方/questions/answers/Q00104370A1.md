> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $(x - 1)^{3} - 3$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because x = 4^{m} + 1, \therefore 4^{m} = x - 1,$ $\therefore 64^{m} = 4^{3m} = (4^{m})^{3} = (x - 1)^{3}, \therefore y = 64^{m} - 3 =$ $(x - 1)^{3} - 3.$
