> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 6
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\because n^{200}=(n^{2})^{100},3^{300}=(3^{3})^{100}=27^{100},$ $n^{200}>3^{300},\therefore n^{2}>27$ , 故 n 的最小正整数值为 6.
> > 故答案为 6.
> > (2) 当 $x^{a}=2, x^{b}=3$ 时， $x^{3a+2b}=x^{3a}\cdot x^{2b}=(x^{a})^{3}\cdot(x^{b})^{2}=2^{3}\times3^{2}=8\times9=72.$ (3) $2^{2023}\times\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{2022}\times(-3)^{2021}=2^{2}\times2^{2021}\times\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^{2021}\times\frac{1}{6}\times(-3)^{2021}=4\times\frac{1}{6}\times\left(-3\times2\times\frac{1}{6}\right)^{2021}=4\times\frac{1}{6}\times(-1)^{2021}=4\times\frac{1}{6}\times(-1)=-\frac{2}{3}.$
