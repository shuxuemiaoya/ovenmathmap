> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** -5
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $3^{a} \times 27^{b} = 3^{a} \times (3^{3})^{b} = 3^{a} \times 3^{3b} = 3^{a+3b} = 81 = 3^{4}, \therefore a + 3b = 4, \therefore 3 - 2a - 6b = 3 - 2(a + 3b) = 3 - 2 \times 4 = 3 - 8 = -5.$ 故答案为 -5.
