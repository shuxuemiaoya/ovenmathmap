> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $m^3 n^4 - 5m^4 n^8$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because 3^x = m, 3^y = n,$ $\therefore 3^{3x + 4y} - 5 \times 81^{x + 2y} = 3^{3x} \times 3^{4y} - 5 \times (3^4)^{x + 2y} = (3^x)^3 \times (3^y)^4 - 5 \times 3^{4x + 8y} = (3^x)^3 \times (3^y)^4 - 5 \times (3^x)^4 \times (3^y)^8 = m^3 n^4 - 5m^4 n^8.$ 故答案为 $m^3 n^4 - 5m^4 n^8$ .
