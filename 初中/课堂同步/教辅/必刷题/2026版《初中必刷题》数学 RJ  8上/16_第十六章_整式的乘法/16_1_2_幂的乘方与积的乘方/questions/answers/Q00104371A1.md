> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $4^{2x} \cdot 5^{2x+1} - 4^{2x+1} \cdot 5^{2x} = 20^{3x-4}$ ,
> > ∴ $5 \times 4^{2x} \cdot 5^{2x} - 4 \times 4^{2x} \cdot 5^{2x} = 20^{3x-4}$ , ∴ $4^{2x} \cdot 5^{2x} = 20^{3x-4}$ , ∴ $20^{2x} = 20^{3x-4}$ , ∴ 2x = 3x - 4, ∴ x = 4.
