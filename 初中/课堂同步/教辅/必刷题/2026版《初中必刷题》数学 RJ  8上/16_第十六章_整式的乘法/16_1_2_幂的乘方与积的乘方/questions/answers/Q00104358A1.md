> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 64
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $x + 3y = 3$ , ∴ $4^{x} \cdot 8^{2y} = 4^{x} \cdot (8^{2})^{y} = 4^{x} \cdot 64^{y} = 4^{x} \cdot 4^{3y} = 4^{x+3y} = 4^{3} = 64$ .
