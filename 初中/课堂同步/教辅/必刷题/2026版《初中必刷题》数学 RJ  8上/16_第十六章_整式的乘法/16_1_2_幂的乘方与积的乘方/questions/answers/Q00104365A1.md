> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $2^{x} \cdot 5^{y} = 125, 5^{x} \cdot 2^{y} = 8, \therefore 2^{x} \cdot 5^{y} \cdot 5^{x} \cdot 2^{y} = 1000 = 10^{3}, \therefore 2^{x+y} \cdot 5^{x+y} = 10^{3},$ ∴ $10^{x+y} = 10^{3}, \therefore x + y = 3.$ 故答案为 3.
