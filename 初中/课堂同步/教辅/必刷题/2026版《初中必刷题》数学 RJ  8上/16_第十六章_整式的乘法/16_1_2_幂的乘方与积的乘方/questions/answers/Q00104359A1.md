> > 【解】(1) $\because a^{m}=2,a^{n}=3,\therefore a^{2m+n}=a^{2m}\cdot a^{n}=(a^{m})^{2}\cdot a^{n}=2^{2}\times3=4\times3=12.$ (2) $\because x^{2n}=2,\therefore(x^{3n})^{2}-4(x^{2})^{2n}=x^{6n}-4x^{4n}=(x^{2n})^{3}-4(x^{2n})^{2}=2^{3}-4\times2^{2}=8-4\times4=8-16=-8.$
