> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 5
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $(x+1)(x-1)+x(x-2)=x^{2}-1+x^{2}-2x=2x^{2}-2x-1$ . $\because x^{2}-x-3=0,\therefore x^{2}-x=3$ , 则 $2x^{2}-2x=6,\therefore$ 原式 =6-1=5, 故答案为 5.
