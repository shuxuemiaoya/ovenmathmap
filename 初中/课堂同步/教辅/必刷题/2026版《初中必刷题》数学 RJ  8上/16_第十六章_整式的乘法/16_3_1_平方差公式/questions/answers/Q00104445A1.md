> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $(a^{2}+b^{2}+1)(a^{2}+b^{2}-1)=8$ ,
> > ∴ $(a^{2}+b^{2})^{2}-1=8,\therefore(a^{2}+b^{2})^{2}=9,\therefore a^{2}+b^{2}=3$ (负值舍去)，故答案为3.
