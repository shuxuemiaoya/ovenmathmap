> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $S_{1}=(m+7)(m+1)=m^{2}+8m+7$ , $S_{2}=(m+4)(m+2)=m^{2}+6m+8,\therefore S_{1}-S_{2}=2m-1.\because m$ 为正整数，∴ m 最小为 1，∴ 2m-1>0，
> > ∴ $|S_{1}-S_{2}|=|2m-1|=2m-1.\because 0<n<|S_{1}-S_{2}|$ ∴ 0 < n < 2m - 1. 由题意得 $8 < 2m - 1 \leqslant 9$ ，解得 $\frac{9}{2}<m \leqslant 5$ . ∵ m 为正整数，∴ m = 5.
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
