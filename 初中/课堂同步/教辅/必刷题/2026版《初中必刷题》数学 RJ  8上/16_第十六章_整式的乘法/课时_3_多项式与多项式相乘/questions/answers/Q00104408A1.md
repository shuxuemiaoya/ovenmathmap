> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】原式 $= 2x^{2} + xy + 4xy + 2y^{2} - (3x^{2} + 6xy - xy - 2y^{2}) = 2x^{2} + xy + 4xy + 2y^{2} - 3x^{2} - 5xy + 2y^{2} = -x^{2} + 4y^{2}$ . 当 $x = 3, y = \frac{1}{2}$ 时，原式 $= -3^{2} + 4 \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} = -9 + 1 = -8$ .
