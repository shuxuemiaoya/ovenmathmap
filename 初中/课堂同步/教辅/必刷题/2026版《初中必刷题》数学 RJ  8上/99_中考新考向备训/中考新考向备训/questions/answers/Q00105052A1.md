> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** AB
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】选择①,理由:
> > ∵ AE∥BF, CE∥DF, ∴ ∠A = ∠FBD, ∠D = ∠ECA.
> > ∵ AE = BF, ∴ △AEC ≅ △BFD(AAS),
> > ∴ AC = BD, ∴ AC - BC = BD - BC, 即 AB = CD.
> > (答案不唯一, 选择①或③均可使结论成立)
> > 【一题多解】
> > 选择③,理由:
> > ∵ AE∥BF, ∴ ∠A = ∠FBD.
> > ∵ AE = BF, ∠E = ∠F, ∴ △AEC ≅ △BFD(ASA),
> > ∴ AC = BD, ∴ AC - BC = BD - BC, 即 AB = CD.
