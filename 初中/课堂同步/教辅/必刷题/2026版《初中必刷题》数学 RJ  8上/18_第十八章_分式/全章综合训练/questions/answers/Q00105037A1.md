> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $a_{1}=2,\therefore a_{2}=\frac{1+a_{1}}{1-a_{1}}=\frac{1+2}{1-2}=-3,$ $a_{3}=\frac{1+a_{2}}{1-a_{2}}=\frac{1+(-3)}{1-(-3)}=-\frac{1}{2},a_{4}=\frac{1+a_{3}}{1-a_{3}}=$ $\frac{1+\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)}{1-\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)}=\frac{1}{3},a_{5}=\frac{1+a_{4}}{1-a_{4}}=\frac{1+\frac{1}{3}}{1-\frac{1}{3}}=2,\cdots,\therefore$ 这列数按照 $2,-3,-\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{3}$ 的循环规律出现.
