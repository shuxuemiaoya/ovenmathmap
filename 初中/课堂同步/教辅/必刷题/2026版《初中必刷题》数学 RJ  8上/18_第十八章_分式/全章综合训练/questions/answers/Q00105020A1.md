> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > : 计算 $\frac{A}{xy + y^2} - \frac{y}{x^2 + xy}$ 的结果为 $\frac{x - y}{xy}$ , $\therefore \frac{y}{x^2 + xy} + \frac{x - y}{xy} = \frac{A}{xy + y^2}, \therefore \frac{y^2}{xy(x + y)} + \frac{(x - y)(x + y)}{xy(x + y)} = \frac{x^2}{xy(x + y)} = \frac{x}{xy + y^2} = \frac{A}{xy + y^2}, \therefore A = x$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
