> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】原式 $= \frac{3a - 6b + 3b}{(a - b)^2} = \frac{3(a - b)}{(a - b)^2} = \frac{3}{a - b}$ ∵ $a - b - 1 = 0,\therefore a - b = 1,\therefore$ 原式 $= \frac{3}{1} = 3.$
