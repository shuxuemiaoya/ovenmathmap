> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1) $\because\frac{a}{b}=3,\therefore a=3b,\therefore\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}}{ab}=\frac{(3b)^{2}+b^{2}}{3b\cdot b}=$ $\frac{9b^{2}+b^{2}}{3b^{2}}=\frac{10b^{2}}{3b^{2}}=\frac{10}{3}.$ (2) $\left(1+\frac{1}{x}\right)\div\frac{x^{2}-1}{x}=\frac{x+1}{x}\cdot\frac{x}{(x+1)(x-1)}=\frac{1}{x-1}.$ ∵当 $x=0,\pm1$ 时,原分式无意义,∴x=2.当x=2时,原式 $=\frac{1}{2-1}=1.$
