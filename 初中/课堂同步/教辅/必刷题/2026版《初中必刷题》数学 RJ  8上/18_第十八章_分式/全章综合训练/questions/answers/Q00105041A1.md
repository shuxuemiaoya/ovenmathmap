> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** -4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\frac{ab}{a+b} = -2$ , $\frac{bc}{b+c} = \frac{5}{3}$ , $\frac{ac}{a+c} = -\frac{5}{3}$ ,
> > ∴ $\frac{a+b}{ab}=-\frac{1}{2}$ ，① $\frac{b+c}{bc}=\frac{3}{5}$ ，② $\frac{a+c}{ac}=-\frac{3}{5}$ ，③
> > 由①+②+③可得 $\frac{a+b}{ab}+\frac{b+c}{bc}+\frac{a+c}{ac}=-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{3}{5}-$ $\frac{3}{5}=-\frac{1}{2},\therefore\frac{2(ab+bc+ac)}{abc}=-\frac{1}{2},\therefore\frac{ab+bc+ac}{abc}=$ $-\frac{1}{4},\therefore\frac{abc}{ab+bc+ac}=-4.$ 故答案为-4.
