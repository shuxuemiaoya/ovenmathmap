> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 选项, $\frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 3} \div \frac{x - 3}{x^2 - 9} = \frac{(x + 2)(x - 2)}{x - 3}$ . $\frac{(x + 3)(x - 3)}{x - 3} = \frac{(x + 2)(x - 2)(x + 3)}{x - 3}$ , 不是整式, 符合题意; B 选项, $\frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 3} \div \frac{x - 2}{x^2 - 9} = \frac{(x + 2)(x - 2)}{x - 3} \cdot \frac{(x + 3)(x - 3)}{x - 2} = (x + 2)(x + 3)$ , 是整式, 不符合题意; C 选项, $\frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 3} \div \frac{x + 3}{x^2 - 9} = \frac{(x + 2)(x - 2)}{x - 3} \cdot \frac{(x + 3)(x - 3)}{x + 3} = (x + 2)(x - 2)$ , 是整式, 不符合题意; D 选项, $\frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 3} \div \frac{x + 2}{x^2 - 9} = \frac{(x + 2)(x - 2)}{x - 3} \cdot \frac{(x + 3)(x - 3)}{x + 2} = (x - 2)(x + 3)$ , 是整式, 不符合题意.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
