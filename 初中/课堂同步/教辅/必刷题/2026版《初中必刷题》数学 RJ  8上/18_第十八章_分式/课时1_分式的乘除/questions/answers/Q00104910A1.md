> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 选项, $\frac{a^2}{b^5} \cdot \frac{b^3}{a^5} = \frac{1}{a^3 b^2}$ , 故此选项不正确; B 选项, $\frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{d}{c} = \frac{ad}{bc}$ , 故此选项不正确; C 选项, $\frac{7b}{2a^3} \cdot \frac{8a^3}{7b^2} = \frac{4}{b}$ , 故此选项不正确; D 选项, $a \cdot \frac{b}{a} \cdot \frac{1}{a} = \frac{b}{a}$ , 故此选项正确.
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
