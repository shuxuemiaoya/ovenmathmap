> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > - **①**：$\frac{3x}{x^2} \cdot \frac{x}{3x} = \frac{1}{x}$ , 符合题意
> > - **②**：$a \div b \times \frac{1}{b} = a \times \frac{1}{b} \times \frac{1}{b} = \frac{a}{b^2}$ , 不符合题意
> > - **③**：$\frac{a}{a^2 - 1} \div \frac{a^2}{a^2 + a} = \frac{a}{(a - 1)(a + 1)} \cdot \frac{a(a + 1)}{a^2} = \frac{1}{a - 1}$ , 符合题意
> > - **④**：$8a^2b^2 \div \left(-\frac{3a}{4b^2}\right) = 8a^2b^2 \times \left(-\frac{4b^2}{3a}\right) = -\frac{32ab^4}{3}$ , 不符合题意
