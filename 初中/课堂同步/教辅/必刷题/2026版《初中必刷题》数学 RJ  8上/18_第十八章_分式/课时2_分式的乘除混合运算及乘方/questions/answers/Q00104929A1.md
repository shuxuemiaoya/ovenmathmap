> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】原式 $= \frac{(x + y)^2(x - y)^2}{x^2y^2}\cdot \frac{1}{x + y}\cdot \frac{x^3}{(x - y)^3} =$ $\frac{x(x + y)}{y^2(x - y)} = \frac{x^2 + xy}{xy^2 - y^3}.$ 当 $x = -\frac{1}{2},y = -1$ 时，原式 $= \frac{3}{2}.$
