> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $-\frac{5}{4b}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left(-\frac{a}{b}\right)^2 \div \left(\frac{2a^2}{5b}\right)^2 \cdot \left(-\frac{a^2}{5b}\right) = \frac{a^2}{b^2} \div \frac{4a^4}{25b^2} \cdot \left(-\frac{a^2}{5b}\right) = \frac{a^2}{b^2} \cdot \frac{25b^2}{4a^4} \cdot \left(-\frac{a^2}{5b}\right) = -\frac{5}{4b}$ .
