> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{2a}{3b^2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $\frac{2a^2b}{3x} \cdot \frac{2xy}{3ab^3} \div \left(\frac{2}{3}y\right) = \frac{4a^2bxy}{9ab^3x} \cdot \frac{3}{2y} = \frac{2a}{3b^2}$ , 故答案为 $\frac{2a}{3b^2}$ .
