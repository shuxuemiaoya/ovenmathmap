> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $-\frac{1}{a+3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left(1-\frac{5}{a+2}\right)\div\left(\frac{5}{a+2}-a+2\right)=\frac{a-3}{a+2}\div\frac{5-(a^{2}-4)}{a+2}=\frac{a-3}{a+2}\cdot\frac{a+2}{-(a-3)(a+3)}=-\frac{1}{a+3}$ ，故答案为 $-\frac{1}{a+3}$ .
