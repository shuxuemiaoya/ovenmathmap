> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{x+6}{x+7}-\frac{x+5}{x+6}=\frac{1}{x+6}-\frac{1}{x+7}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)【解】由题意可得,第⑤个等式是 $\frac{x+6}{x+7}$ $\frac{x+5}{x+6}=\frac{1}{x+6}-\frac{1}{x+7}$ ，故答案为 $\frac{x+6}{x+7}-\frac{x+5}{x+6}=\frac{1}{x+6}-\frac{1}{x+7}$ .
> >
> > (2)【解】由题意可得,第⑩个等式是 $\frac{x+n+1}{x+n+2}-\frac{x+n}{x+n+1}=\frac{1}{x+n+1}-\frac{1}{x+n+2}$ ，故答案为 $\frac{x+n+1}{x+n+2}-\frac{x+n}{x+n+1}=\frac{1}{x+n+1}-\frac{1}{x+n+2}$ .
> > (3)【证明】∵ $\frac{x+n+1}{x+n+2}-\frac{x+n}{x+n+1}=\frac{(x+n+2)-1}{x+n+2}-\frac{(x+n+1)-1}{x+n+1}=1-\frac{1}{x+n+2}-1+\frac{1}{x+n+1}=\frac{1}{x+n+1}-\frac{1}{x+n+2}$ ，∴等式 $\frac{x+n+1}{x+n+2}-\frac{x+n}{x+n+1}=\frac{1}{x+n+1}-\frac{1}{x+n+2}$ 成立.
