> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{5}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because x = -1$ 时, 分式 $\frac{x - 2b}{2x + a}$ 无意义, $\therefore 2 \times (-1) + a = 0, \therefore a = 2.$ $\because x = 1$ 时, 此分式的值为 $0, \therefore 1 - 2b = 0, \therefore b = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore a + b = 2 + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{2}$ .
> >
> > 8.2 【解析】∵ 分式 $\frac{|x|-2}{|x+2|}$ 的值为 0, ∴ $|x|-2=0$ 且 $|x+2|\neq0$ , 解得 x=2.
