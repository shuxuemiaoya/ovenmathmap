> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** ②
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 原式 $= \frac{x(x + y)}{(x + y)(x - y)} - \frac{y(x - y)}{(x + y)(x - y)} =$ $\frac{x^2 + xy - xy + y^2}{(x + y)(x - y)} = \frac{x^2 + y^2}{(x + y)(x - y)} = \frac{x^2 + y^2}{x^2 - y^2}$ , 所以他从第②步开始出现错误, 故答案为②.
