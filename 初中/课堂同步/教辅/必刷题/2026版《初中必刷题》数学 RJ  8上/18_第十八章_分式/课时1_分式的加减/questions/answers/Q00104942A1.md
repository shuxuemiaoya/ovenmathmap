> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \frac{z}{x+y}+\frac{x}{y+z}+\frac{y}{z+x}=11, \therefore 1+\frac{z}{x+y}+1+\frac{x}{y+z}+1+\frac{y}{z+x}=14, \frac{x+y+z}{x+y}+\frac{x+y+z}{y+z}+\frac{x+y+z}{z+x}=14, \therefore (x+y+z)\left(\frac{1}{x+y}+\frac{1}{y+z}+\frac{1}{z+x}\right)=14, \therefore \frac{1}{x+y}+\frac{1}{y+z}+\frac{1}{z+x}=\frac{14}{x+y+z}$ . 而 $\frac{1}{x+y}+\frac{1}{y+z}+\frac{1}{z+x}=\frac{7}{6}, \therefore \frac{14}{x+y+z}=\frac{7}{6}, \therefore x+y+z=12$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
