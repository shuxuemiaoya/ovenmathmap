> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $p = \frac{a}{a+1} - \frac{b}{b+1}$ , $q = \frac{1}{a+1} - \frac{1}{b+1}$ ,
> > ∴ $p + q = \frac{a}{a+1} - \frac{b}{b+1} + \left( \frac{1}{a+1} - \frac{1}{b+1} \right) = \frac{a}{a+1} - \frac{b}{b+1} + \frac{1}{a+1} - \frac{1}{b+1} = \frac{a+1}{a+1} - \frac{b+1}{b+1} = 1 - 1 = 0, \therefore p = -q.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
