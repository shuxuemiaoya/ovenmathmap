7 老师布置了一道分式计算题: $\frac{x}{x - y} - \frac{y}{x + y}$ , 小强是这样解答的:

$$
\frac {x}{x - y} - \underbrace {\frac {y}{x + y}} _ {\textcircled {1}} = \underbrace {\frac {x (x + y)}{(x - y) (x + y)}} _ {\textcircled {2}} - \underbrace {\frac {y (x - y)}{(x - y) (x + y)}} _ {\textcircled {3}} = \underbrace {\frac {x ^ {2} + x y - x y - y ^ {2}}{(x - y) (x + y)}} _ {\textcircled {4}} = \underbrace {\frac {x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}}{(x - y) (x + y)}} _ {\textcircled {4}} = 1.
$$
