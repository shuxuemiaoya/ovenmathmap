> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \frac{a}{2n-1} + \frac{b}{2n+1} = \frac{(2a+2b)n+a-b}{(2n-1)(2n+1)} = \frac{1}{(2n-1)(2n+1)}, \therefore \begin{cases} 2a+2b=0, \\ a-b=1, \end{cases}$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned} a &= \frac{1}{2}, \\ b &= -\frac{1}{2}, \end{aligned}\right.$ 故答案为 $\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}$ .
