> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{3x - 1}{x^2 - 7x + 2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 将分式的分子、分母同时乘-1，得 $\frac{(-3x + 1) \times (-1)}{(-x^2 + 7x - 2) \times (-1)} = \frac{3x - 1}{x^2 - 7x + 2}$ ，故答案为 $\frac{3x - 1}{x^2 - 7x + 2}$ .
