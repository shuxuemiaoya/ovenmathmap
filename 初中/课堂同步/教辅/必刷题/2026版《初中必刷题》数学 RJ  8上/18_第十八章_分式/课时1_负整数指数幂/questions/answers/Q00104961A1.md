> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】 $(x^{-1}-y^{-1})\div(x^{-2}-y^{-2})-xy(x+y)^{-1}=\left(\frac{1}{x}-\right.\left.\frac{1}{y}\right)\div\left(\frac{1}{x^{2}}-\frac{1}{y^{2}}\right)-\frac{xy}{x+y}=\left(\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{y}\right)\div\left[\left(\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}\right)\left(\frac{1}{x}-\frac{1}{y}\right)\right]-\frac{xy}{x+y}=1\div\left(\frac{1}{x}+\frac{1}{y}\right)-\frac{xy}{x+y}=1\div\frac{x+y}{xy}-\frac{xy}{x+y}=\frac{xy}{x+y}-\frac{xy}{x+y}=0.$
