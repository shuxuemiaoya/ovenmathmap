> > [!success] **【答案】** $3a(2a-b)^{-2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{3a}{(2a-b)^{2}}=3a(2a-b)^{-2}$ .
> > 故答案为 $3a(2a-b)^{-2}$ .
