>
> > [!success] **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $4^{3n} \times 8^{n} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-9}$ , ∴ $2^{6n} \times 2^{3n} = 2^{9}$ ,
> > ∴ $2^{9n}=2^{9}$ , ∴ 9n=9, 解得 n=1.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
