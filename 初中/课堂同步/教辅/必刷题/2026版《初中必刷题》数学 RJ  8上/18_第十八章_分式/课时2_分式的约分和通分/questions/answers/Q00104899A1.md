> > 【解】(1) $\frac{2a(a-1)}{8ab^{2}(1-a)}=\frac{2a(a-1)}{-8ab^{2}(a-1)}=-\frac{1}{4b^{2}}.$ (2) $\frac{a^{2}-4ab+4b^{2}}{a^{2}-4b^{2}}=\frac{(a-2b)^{2}}{(a+2b)(a-2b)}=\frac{a-2b}{a+2b}.$
