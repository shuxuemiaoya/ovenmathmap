> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{(a - 1)^2}{(a + 1)^2(a - 1)}, \frac{-5(a + 1)}{(a + 1)^2(a - 1)}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \frac{a - 1}{a^2 + 2a + 1} = \frac{a - 1}{(a + 1)^2}, \frac{5}{1 - a^2} = \frac{-5}{a^2 - 1} = \frac{-5}{(a + 1)(a - 1)}, \therefore$ 最简公分母为 $(a + 1)^2(a - 1)$ ， $\therefore$ 通分后分别为 $\frac{(a - 1)^2}{(a + 1)^2(a - 1)}, \frac{-5(a + 1)}{(a + 1)^2(a - 1)}.$
> >
> > (1) 理解题中“巧分式”的定义是解题关键.
