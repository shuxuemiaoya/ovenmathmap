> > 【解】∵ abc = 1, ∴ $\frac{1}{ab+b+1} = \frac{abc}{ab+b+abc} =$ $\frac{ac}{a+1+ac}, \frac{1}{bc+c+1} = \frac{abc}{bc+c+abc} = \frac{ab}{b+1+ab} =$ $\frac{ab}{b+abc+ab}=\frac{a}{1+ac+a}.$
