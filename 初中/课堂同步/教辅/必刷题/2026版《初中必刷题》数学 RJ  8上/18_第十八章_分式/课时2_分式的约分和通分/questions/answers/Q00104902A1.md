> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $10x^{2}(x-y)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\frac{1}{2x-2y}=\frac{1}{2(x-y)}$ , ∴ 分式 $\frac{2}{5x^{2}(x-y)}$ 与 $\frac{1}{2x-2y}$ 的最简公分母是
> >
> > $10x^{2}(x-y)$ ，故答案为 $10x^{2}(x-y)$ .
