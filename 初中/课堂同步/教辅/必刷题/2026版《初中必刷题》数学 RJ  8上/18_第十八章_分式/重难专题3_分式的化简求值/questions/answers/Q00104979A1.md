> [!success]- 必刷题
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** -1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 实数 m, n 满足 $m^{2} + 2m - 2 = 0$ ,
> > m-n=2, ∴ n=m-2, ∴ 原式= $\frac{1}{m+1}+\frac{2}{m-2}=$ $\frac{m-2+2m+2}{(m+1)(m-2)}=\frac{3m}{m^{2}-m-2}=\frac{3m}{m^{2}+2m-2-3m}=$ $\frac{3m}{-3m}=-1.$
