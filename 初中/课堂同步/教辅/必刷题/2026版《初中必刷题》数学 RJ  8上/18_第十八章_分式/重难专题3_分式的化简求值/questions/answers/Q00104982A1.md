> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】由已知得 $x \neq 0, \therefore \frac{x^2 - 3x + 1}{x} = x + \frac{1}{x} - 3 = -1, \therefore x + \frac{1}{x} = 2,$ $\therefore \frac{x^4 - 9x^2 + 1}{x^2} = x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} - 9 = \left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 - 11 = 2^2 - 11 = -7, \therefore \frac{x^2}{x^4 - 9x^2 + 1} = -\frac{1}{7}.$
