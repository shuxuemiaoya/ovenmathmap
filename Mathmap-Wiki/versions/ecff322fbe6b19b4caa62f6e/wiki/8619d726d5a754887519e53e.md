# 例 2 化简·解析

对应题目：[例 2 化简](#node=Q8792079371395825989)

解：

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \tan (- \alpha - 1 8 0 ^ {\circ}) & = \tan [ - (1 8 0 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) ] \\ & = - \tan (1 8 0 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) \\ & = - \tan \alpha , \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \cos (- 1 8 0 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) & = \cos [ - (1 8 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) ] \\ & = \cos (1 8 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) \\ & = - \cos \alpha , \end{array}
$$

$$
\text {原式} = \frac {- \cos \alpha \sin \alpha}{(- \tan \alpha) (- \cos \alpha)} = - \cos \alpha .
$$
