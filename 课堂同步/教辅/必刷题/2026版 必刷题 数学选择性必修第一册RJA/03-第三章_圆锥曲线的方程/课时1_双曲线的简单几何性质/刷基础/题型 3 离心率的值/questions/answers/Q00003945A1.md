> **【解析】**
> 由已知椭圆的离心率为 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , 得 $\frac{a^2 - b^2}{a^2} = \frac{3}{4}, \therefore a^2 = 4b^2$ . 双曲线的离心率 $e = \sqrt{\frac{a^2 + b^2}{a^2}} = \sqrt{\frac{5b^2}{4b^2}} =$ 敲黑板: 双曲线的离心率 $e = \frac{c}{a} = \sqrt{\frac{c^2}{a^2}} = \sqrt{\frac{a^2 + b^2}{a^2}}$ $\sqrt{\frac{5}{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$ .
>
> 故选 **A**.
