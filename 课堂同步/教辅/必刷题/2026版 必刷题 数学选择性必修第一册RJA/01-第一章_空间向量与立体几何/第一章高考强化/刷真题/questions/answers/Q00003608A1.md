> **【解析】**
> (1)【证明】∵ $AD = 5\sqrt{3}$ , AB = 8, $\overrightarrow{AE} = \frac{2}{5}\overrightarrow{AD}, \overrightarrow{AF} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$ ,
∴ $AE = 2\sqrt{3}$ , AF = 4.
在 $\triangle AEF$ 中，由余弦定理得 $EF^{2}=AF^{2}+AE^{2}-2AF\cdot AE\cdot\cos A=16+$ $12-2\times4\times2\sqrt{3}\times\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}=4,$
则 EF=2,
∴ 在 $\triangle AEF$ 中, $EF^{2}+AE^{2}=AF^{2}$ ,
∴ $EF \perp AE$ .
又∵ $\triangle AEF$ 沿 EF 翻折至 $\triangle PEF$ ,
∴ $EF \perp PE$ .
又∵ $AE \cap PE = E, AE, PE \subset$ 平面
PED, ∴ $EF \perp$ 平面 PED.
又∵ $PD \subset$ 平面 PED,
∴ $EF \perp PD$ .
(2)【解】由(1)知 $EF \perp AE$ ,
又∵ $CD \perp AD, \therefore CD // EF.$ ∵ $EF \perp$ 平面 PED, ∴ $CD \perp$ 平面 PED.
又∵ $PD \subset$ 平面 PED, ∴ $CD \perp PD.$ 在 Rt $\triangle PCD$ 中, $PD = \sqrt{PC^{2} - CD^{2}} = \sqrt{39}$ .
又∵ ED = AD - AE = $3\sqrt{3}$ ,
∴ $PE^{2} + ED^{2} = PD^{2}$ ,
∴ $PE \perp ED.$ ∴ 以 E 为原点, EF, ED, EP 所在直线分别为 x, y, z 轴建立如图所示的空间直角坐标系,
则 $P(0,0,2\sqrt{3})$ , $F(2,0,0)$ , $D(0,3\sqrt{3},0)$ , $B(4,2\sqrt{3},0)$ , $C(3,3\sqrt{3},0)$ ,
∴ $\overrightarrow{FP} = (-2, 0, 2\sqrt{3}), \overrightarrow{BP} = (-4, -2\sqrt{3}, 2\sqrt{3}), \overrightarrow{CD} = (-3, 0, 0), \overrightarrow{DP} = (0, -3\sqrt{3}, 2\sqrt{3}).$ 设平面 PBF 的法向量为 $n = (x_{1}, y_{1}, z_{1})$ ,
则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\overrightarrow{FP} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{BP} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{DP} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{DP} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{DP} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n}_1 &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{n}_1 &=(\sqrt{3},-1,1).$ 设平面 PCD 的法向量为 $m = (x_{2}, y_{2}, z_{2})$ ,
则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\overrightarrow{CD} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{DP} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{n} &\cdot \\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ \overrightarrow{m} &=1,\\ z_{1}=1,\text { 则 }z_{1}=1,\text { 则 }n=(\sqrt{3},-1,1).$ 设平面 PCD 与平面 PBF 所成的二面角的平面角为 θ，
则 $|\cos\theta| = |\cos\langle n, m\rangle| = |\frac{|n\cdot m|}{|n||m|}| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{65}}.$ ∴ $\sin\theta=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}\theta}=\frac{8}{65}\sqrt{65}.$
