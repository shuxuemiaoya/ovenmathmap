> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{1}{3}$ 【解析】由 $\tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) = \frac{3}{2} \cos 2\alpha$ ，
→①思：也可由 $= \frac{1 + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan \alpha} = \frac{3}{2} \times \frac{\cos^2 \alpha - \sin^2 \alpha}{\cos^2 \alpha + \sin^2 \alpha} = \frac{3}{2} \times \frac{1 - \tan^2 \alpha}{1 + \tan^2 \alpha}$ 求解
可得 $\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)} = \frac{3}{2} \cos 2\alpha = \frac{3}{2} \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+2\alpha\right)$ ，即 $\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)} = 3 \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right) \cdot \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)$ .
由于 $\alpha \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$ ，故 $\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha \in (\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4})$ ，
则 $\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right) \neq 0$
所以 $\frac{1}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)} = 3\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)$ ，则 $\cos^2\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) = \frac{1}{3}$ ，所以 $\frac{1 + \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} + 2\alpha\right)}{2} = \frac{1 - \sin 2\alpha}{2} = \frac{1}{3}$ ，解得 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{1}{3}$ .
