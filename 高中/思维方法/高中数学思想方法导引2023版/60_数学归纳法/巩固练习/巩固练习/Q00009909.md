2. 已知数列 $\{x_{n}\}$ 满足 $x_{1} = 1, x_{n} = x_{n + 1} + \ln (1 + x_{n + 1}), n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$ . 证明：

(1) $0 < x_{n + 1} < x_n$ ;

(2) $2x_{n+1}-x_{n}\leqslant\frac{x_{n}x_{n+1}}{2};$

(3) $\frac{1}{2^{n-1}}\leqslant x_{n}\leqslant\frac{1}{2^{n-2}}.$
