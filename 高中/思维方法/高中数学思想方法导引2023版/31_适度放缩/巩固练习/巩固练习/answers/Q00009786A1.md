> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** 18
>
> **【解析】**
> $2(\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n})=\frac{2}{\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n}}$ $<\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{n}+\sqrt{n}}<\frac{2}{\sqrt{n}+\sqrt{n-1}}$ $=2(\sqrt{n}-\sqrt{n-1})$ ,
> 取 n=100，累加即得.
