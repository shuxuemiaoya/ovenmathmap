> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 令 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=\sqrt{5}\cos\theta,\\ y&=\sqrt{5}\sin\theta,\end{aligned}\right.$ $\theta\in[0,2\pi]$ ,
> 则 $t=4x^{2}-5xy+4y^{2}=20-\frac{25}{2}\sin2\theta\in\left[\frac{15}{2},\frac{65}{2}\right]$ ，
>
> 故选 **B**.
