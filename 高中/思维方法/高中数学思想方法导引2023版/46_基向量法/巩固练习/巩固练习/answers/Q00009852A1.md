> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** $\sqrt{11}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中，由余弦定理得 $\cos \angle ACB = \frac{AC^2 + BC^2 - AB^2}{2 \cdot AC \cdot BC} = \frac{1}{2}$ ，所以 $\angle ACB = 60^\circ$ ，即 $\langle \overrightarrow{BC}, \overrightarrow{CA} \rangle = 120^\circ$ 。同理可求得 $\langle \overrightarrow{CA}, \overrightarrow{AD} \rangle = 120^\circ$ 。又 $AD \perp BC$ ，所以 $\langle \overrightarrow{BC}, \overrightarrow{AD} \rangle = 90^\circ$ 。取 $\overrightarrow{BC}, \overrightarrow{CA}, \overrightarrow{AD}$ 为一组基底，则 $\overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{CA} + \overrightarrow{AD}$ ，所以 $|\overrightarrow{BD}|^2 = (\overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{CA} + \overrightarrow{AD})^2 = \overrightarrow{BC^2} + \overrightarrow{CA^2} + \overrightarrow{AD^2} + 2\overrightarrow{BC} \cdot \overrightarrow{CA} + 2\overrightarrow{CA} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD} + 2\overrightarrow{BC} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD} = 2^2 + 3^2 + 4^2 + 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times \cos 120^\circ + 2 \times 3 \times 4 \times \cos 120^\circ + 2 \times 2 \times 4 \times \cos 90^\circ = 11$ ，所以 $|\overrightarrow{BD}| = \sqrt{11}$ 。
