> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 易得 $a_{n} = 2n$ 则 $b_{n} = \frac{n + 1}{4n^{2}(n + 2)^{2}} = \frac{1}{16}\left[\frac{1}{n^{2}} -\frac{1}{(n + 2)^{2}}\right],$ $T_{n} = \frac{1}{16}\left[1 + \frac{1}{2^{2}} -\frac{1}{(n + 1)^{2}} -\frac{1}{(n + 2)^{2}}\right] <   \frac{5}{64}.$
