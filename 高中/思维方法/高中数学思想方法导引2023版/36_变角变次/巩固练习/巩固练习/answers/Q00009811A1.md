> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题意知 $\sin^{2}\alpha + \sin^{2}\beta = \sin\alpha\cos\beta + \cos\alpha\sin\beta,$ $\sin\alpha(\sin\alpha - \cos\beta) = \sin\beta(\cos\alpha - \sin\beta),$ 则 $\sin\alpha - \cos\beta$ 与 $\cos\alpha - \sin\beta$ 同号或同为零.
> 若 $\sin\alpha > \cos\beta$ 且 $\cos\alpha > \sin\beta,$ 则 $1 = \sin^{2}\alpha + \cos^{2}\alpha > \cos^{2}\beta + \sin^{2}\beta = 1$ ，矛盾；
> 若 $\sin\alpha < \cos\beta$ 且 $\cos\alpha < \sin\beta,$ 则 $1 = \sin^{2}\alpha + \cos^{2}\alpha < \cos^{2}\beta + \sin^{2}\beta = 1$ ，矛盾.
> 故 $\cos\alpha = \sin\beta$ 且 $\sin\alpha = \cos\beta,$ 即 $\alpha + \beta = \frac{\pi}{2}.$
