> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** (0,1)
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题意， $f(x)=\left|e^{x}-1\right|=$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}1-e^{x},&x<0,\\ e^{x}-1,&x\geqslant0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 则 $f'(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned}-\mathrm{e}^{x},&x<0,\\ \mathrm{e}^{x},&x\geqslant0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $A(x_{1},1-\mathrm{e}^{x_{1}}),B(x_{2},\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}-1)$ , $k_{AM}=-e^{x_{1}},k_{BN}=e^{x_{2}}$ 如图，由 $AM \perp BN$ 知 $-e^{x_{1}}e^{x_{2}}=-1$ 得 $x_{1}+x_{2}=0$
> $AM: y-1+e^{x_{1}}=-e^{x_{1}}(x-x_{1}),$ $M(0, e^{x_{1}} x_{1} - e^{x_{1}} + 1),$ $|AM|=\sqrt{x_{1}^{2}+(e^{x_{1}}x_{1})^{2}}=\sqrt{1+e^{2x_{1}}}|x_{1}|,$ 同理， $|BN|=\sqrt{1+e^{2x_{2}}}|x_{2}|$ 所以 $\frac{|AM|}{|BN|}=\frac{\sqrt{1+e^{2x_{1}}}|x_{1}|}{\sqrt{1+e^{2x_{2}}}|x_{2}|}$ $=\sqrt{\frac{1+e^{2x_{1}}}{1+e^{2x_{2}}}}=\sqrt{\frac{1+e^{2x_{1}}}{1+e^{-2x_{1}}}}=e^{x_{1}}\in(0,1).$
