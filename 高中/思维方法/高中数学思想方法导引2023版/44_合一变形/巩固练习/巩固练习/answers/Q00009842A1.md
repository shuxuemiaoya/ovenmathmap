> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 合一变形得 $f(x) = \sqrt{a^2 + (1 - a)^2} \sin (x + \varphi)$ , 由题意, $f\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \pm \sqrt{a^2 + (1 - a)^2} = \pm \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ , 平方后解得 $a = \frac{1}{2}$ , 则 $g(x) = \frac{1}{2} \sin x + \frac{3}{2} \cos x = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2} \sin (x + \alpha)$ , 故 $g(x)_{\max} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$ .
