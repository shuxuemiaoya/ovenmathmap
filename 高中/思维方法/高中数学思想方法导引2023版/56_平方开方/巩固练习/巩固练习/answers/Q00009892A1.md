> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> $|2a+b|=\sqrt{(2a+b)^{2}}$ $=\sqrt{4a^{2}+4a\cdot b+b^{2}}$
> $$
> \begin{array}{l} = \sqrt {1 2 + 1 6 + 4 \times \sqrt {3} \times 4 \times \cos 1 5 0 ^ {\circ}} \\ = 2. \end{array}
> $$
