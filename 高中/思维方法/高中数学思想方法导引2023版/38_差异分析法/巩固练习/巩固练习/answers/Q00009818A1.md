> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** 4
>
> **【解析】**
> 由 $\frac{b}{a} + \frac{a}{b} = 6\cos C$ 得 $6ab\cos C = a^2 + b^2$ 即 $6ab \cdot \frac{a^2 + b^2 - c^2}{2ab} = a^2 + b^2$ 得 $a^2 + b^2 = \frac{3c^2}{2}$ $\frac{\tan C}{\tan A} + \frac{\tan C}{\tan B} = \frac{\sin C}{\cos C} \cdot \frac{\cos B\sin A + \sin B\cos A}{\sin A\sin B} = \frac{\sin C}{\cos C} \cdot \frac{\sin(A + B)}{\sin A\sin B} = \frac{1}{\cos C} \cdot \frac{\sin^2C}{\sin A\sin B},$ 由正弦定理可知，上式 $= \frac{1}{\cos C} \cdot \frac{c^2}{ab} = \frac{c^2}{\frac{1}{6}(a^2 + b^2)} = \frac{c^2}{\frac{1}{6} \cdot \frac{3c^2}{2}} = 4.$
