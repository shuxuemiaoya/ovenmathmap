> [!faq]- 巩固练习答案
> **【答案】** 2
>
> **【解析】**
> 原式 $= \frac{2\sin 30^{\circ}\cos 20^{\circ}}{-\frac{1}{2}(\cos 90^{\circ} - \cos 20^{\circ})}$ $= \frac{\cos 20^{\circ}}{\frac{1}{2}\cos 20^{\circ}} = 2.$
