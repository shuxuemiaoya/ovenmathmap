例 5 计算 $(1+2i)\div(3-4i)$ .

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \text {:} (1 + 2 \mathrm{i}) \div (3 - 4 \mathrm{i}) & = \frac {1 + 2 \mathrm{i}}{3 - 4 \mathrm{i}} \\ & = \frac {(1 + 2 \mathrm{i}) (3 + 4 \mathrm{i})}{(3 - 4 \mathrm{i}) (3 + 4 \mathrm{i})} = \frac {3 - 8 + 6 \mathrm{i} + 4 \mathrm{i}}{3 ^ {2} + 4 ^ {2}} \\ & = \frac {- 5 + 1 0 \mathrm{i}}{2 5} = - \frac {1}{5} + \frac {2}{5} \mathrm{i}. \end{array}
$$
