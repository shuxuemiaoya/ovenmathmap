例5 已知 $\sin (53^{\circ} - \alpha) = \frac{1}{5}$ ，且 $-270^{\circ} < \alpha < -90^{\circ}$ ，求 $\sin (37^{\circ} + \alpha)$ 的值.

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 联系条件与结论，注意到 $(53^{\circ} - \alpha) + (37^{\circ} + \alpha) = 90^{\circ}$ ，由此可利用诱导公式解决问题.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：因为 $(53^{\circ} - \alpha) + (37^{\circ} + \alpha) = 90^{\circ}$ ，所以由诱导公式五，得
> > 因为
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \sin (37 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) & = \sin [ 90 ^ {\circ} - (53 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) ] \\ & = \cos (53 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) \\ & - 270 ^ {\circ} <   \alpha <   - 90 ^ {\circ}, \\ & 143 ^ {\circ} <   53 ^ {\circ} - \alpha <   323 ^ {\circ}. \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以
> > 由 $\sin (53^{\circ} - \alpha) = \frac{1}{5} >0$ ，得 $143^{\circ} <   53^{\circ} - \alpha <  180^{\circ}.$
> > 所以
> >
> > $$
> > \cos (53 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) = - \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} (53 ^ {\circ} - \alpha)} = - \sqrt {1 - \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {2}} = - \frac {2 \sqrt {6}}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以
> >
> > $$
> > \sin (37 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) = - \frac {2 \sqrt {6}}{5}.
> > $$
