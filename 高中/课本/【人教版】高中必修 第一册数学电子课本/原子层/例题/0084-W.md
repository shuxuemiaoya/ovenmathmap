例2 化简

$$
\frac {\cos (180 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) \sin (\alpha + 360 ^ {\circ})}{\tan (- \alpha - 180 ^ {\circ}) \cos (- 180 ^ {\circ} + \alpha)}.
$$

> [!success]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \tan (- \alpha - 180 ^ {\circ}) & = \tan [ - (180 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) ] \\ & = - \tan (180 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) \\ & = - \tan \alpha , \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \cos (- 180 ^ {\circ} + \alpha) & = \cos [ - (180 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) ] \\ & = \cos (180 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) \\ & = - \cos \alpha , \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {原式} = \frac {- \cos \alpha \sin \alpha}{(- \tan \alpha) (- \cos \alpha)} = - \cos \alpha .
> > $$
