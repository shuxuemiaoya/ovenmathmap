8. 确定下列式子的符号：

(1) $\tan 125^{\circ}\sin 273^{\circ}$ ;

(2) $\frac{\tan 108^{\circ}}{\cos 305^{\circ}}$ ;

(3) $\sin \frac{5\pi}{4}\cos \frac{4\pi}{5}\tan \frac{11\pi}{6}$ ;

(4) $\frac{\cos \frac{5\pi}{6}\tan \frac{11\pi}{6}}{\sin \frac{2\pi}{3}}$ .
