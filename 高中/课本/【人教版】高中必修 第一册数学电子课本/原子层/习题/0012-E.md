2. 用适当的符号填空：

(1) $a$ \_\_\_\_ $\{a, b, c\}$ ;

(2) 0 $\underline{\quad}$ {x | x $^{2}$ =0};

(3) $\varnothing$ $\{x\in \mathbf{R}|x^2 +1 = 0\}$

(4) $\{0, 1\}$ \_\_\_\_ N;

(5) $\{0\}$ \_\_\_\_ $\{x \mid x^2 = x\}$ ;

(6) $\{2, 1\} \underline{\quad}\{x \mid x^2 - 3x + 2 = 0\}$ .
