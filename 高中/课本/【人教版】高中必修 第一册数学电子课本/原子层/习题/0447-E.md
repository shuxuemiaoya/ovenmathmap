5. 求下列各式的值：

(1) $\sin 15^{\circ}\cos 15^{\circ}$ ; (2) $\cos^{2}\frac{\pi}{8} -\sin^{2}\frac{\pi}{8}$ ;

(3) $\frac{\tan 22.5^{\circ}}{1 - \tan^{2}22.5^{\circ}};$

(4) $2\cos^2 22.5^\circ - 1.$
