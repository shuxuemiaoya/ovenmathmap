4. 求使下列函数取得最大值、最小值的自变量 $x$ 的集合，并求出最大值、最小值：(1) $y = 1 - \frac{1}{2}\cos \frac{\pi}{3} x, x \in \mathbf{R}$ ; (2) $y = 3\sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right), x \in \mathbf{R}$ ; (3) $y = -\frac{3}{2}\cos \left(\frac{1}{2} x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right), x \in \mathbf{R}$ ; (4) $y = \frac{1}{2}\sin \left(\frac{1}{2} x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right), x \in \mathbf{R}$ .
