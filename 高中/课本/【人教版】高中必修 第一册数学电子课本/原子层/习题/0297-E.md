6. 设 $f(x) = \frac{\mathrm{e}^x - \mathrm{e}^{-x}}{2}$ , $g(x) = \frac{\mathrm{e}^x + \mathrm{e}^{-x}}{2}$ , 求证:

(1) $[g(x)]^{2}-[f(x)]^{2}=1;$

(2) $f(2x)=2f(x)g(x);$

(3) $g(2x) = [g(x)]^2 + [f(x)]^2.$
