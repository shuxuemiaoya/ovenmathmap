2. 证明：

(1) $\cos\left(\frac{5}{2}\pi-\alpha\right)=\sin\alpha;$

(2) $\cos\left(\frac{7}{2}\pi+\alpha\right)=\sin\alpha;$

(3) $\sin\left(\frac{9}{2}\pi-\alpha\right)=\cos\alpha;$

(4) $\sin\left(\frac{11}{2}\pi-\alpha\right)=-\cos\alpha.$

$$
\frac {\cos \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{2}\right)}{\sin \left(\frac {5 \pi}{2} + \alpha\right)} \sin (\alpha - 2 \pi) \cos (2 \pi - \alpha);
$$

$$
\cos^ {2} (- \alpha) - \frac {\tan (2 \pi + \alpha)}{\cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)};
$$

$$
\frac {\cos (\alpha - 3 \pi) \cos \left(\frac {3 \pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{\sin^ {2} \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{2}\right)}
$$
