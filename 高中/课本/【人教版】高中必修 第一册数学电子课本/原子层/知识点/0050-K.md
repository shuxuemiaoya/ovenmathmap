将 $y = 60$ 代入 $y = 60 \times 0.9227^{x} + 25$ ，得

$$
6 0 \times 0. 9 2 2 7 ^ {x} + 2 5 = 6 0.
$$

解得

$$
x = \log_ {0. 9 2 2 7} \frac {7}{1 2}.
$$
