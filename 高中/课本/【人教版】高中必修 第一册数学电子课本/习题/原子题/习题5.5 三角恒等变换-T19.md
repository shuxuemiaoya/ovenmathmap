19. 你能利用所给图形，证明下列两个等式吗？

$\frac {1}{2} (\sin \alpha + \sin \beta) = \sin \frac {\alpha + \beta}{2} \cos \frac {\alpha - \beta}{2};$
$\frac {1}{2} (\cos \alpha + \cos \beta) = \cos \frac {\alpha + \beta}{2} \cos \frac {\alpha - \beta}{2}.$
