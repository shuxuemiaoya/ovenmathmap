4. 根据下列数列的前5项，写出数列的一个通项公式：

(1) $1, \frac{1}{3}, \frac{1}{5}, \frac{1}{7}, \frac{1}{9}, \cdots;$

(2) $1, \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}, \frac{1}{4}, \cdots$ .
