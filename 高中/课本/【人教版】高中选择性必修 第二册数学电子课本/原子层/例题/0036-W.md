例 1 求下列函数的导数:

(1) $y = x^{\frac{2}{3}}$ ; (2) $y = \log_2 x$ .

解：(1) $y' = \left(x^{\frac{2}{3}}\right)' = \frac{2}{3} x^{\frac{2}{3} - 1} = \frac{2}{3} x^{-\frac{1}{3}}$ ;

(2) $y' = (\log_2 x)' = \frac{1}{x \ln 2}.$
