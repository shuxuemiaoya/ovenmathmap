例 1 设 $f(x)=\frac{1}{x}$ ，求 $f'(1)$ .

解：

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \text {解:} f ^ {\prime} (1) & = \lim _ {\Delta x \to 0} \frac {f (1 + \Delta x) - f (1)}{\Delta x} = \lim _ {\Delta x \to 0} \frac {\frac {1}{1 + \Delta x} - 1}{\Delta x} \\ & = \lim _ {\Delta x \to 0} \left(- \frac {1}{1 + \Delta x}\right) = - 1. \end{array}
$$
