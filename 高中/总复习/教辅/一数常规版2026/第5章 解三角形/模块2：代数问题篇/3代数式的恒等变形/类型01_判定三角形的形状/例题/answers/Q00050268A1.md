>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解法2：也可将题干所给等式角化边，从边的层面来分析三角形形状，
> >
> > 因为 $c - a\cos B = (2a - b)\cos A$ ，所以 $c - a\cdot \frac{a^2 + c^2 - b^2}{2ac} = (2a - b)\cdot \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc}$
> >
> > $$
> > b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - a ^ {2} = (2 a - b) \cdot \frac {b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}{b}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - a ^ {2}\right) \left(1 - \frac {2 a - b}{b}\right) = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - a ^ {2} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > 1 - \frac {2 a - b}{b} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - a ^ {2} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} = a ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \triangle A B C
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > 1 - \frac {2 a - b}{b} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
