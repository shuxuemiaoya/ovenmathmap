【例2】（2019·新课标Ⅱ卷）已知函数 $f(x) = (x - 1)\ln x - x - 1$ ，
(1) <   0, \quad f \left(\mathrm{e} ^ {2}\right) = \left(\mathrm{e} ^ {2} - 1\right) \ln \mathrm{e} ^ {2} - \mathrm{e} ^ {2} - 1 = \mathrm{e} ^ {2} - 3 > 0
$$

$$
(1, \mathrm{e} ^ {2})
$$

$$
f (x) = 0
$$

（怎样证明两根互为倒数？可以假设其中一根为 $x_{1}$ ，再由此出发论证 $f\left(\frac{1}{x_1}\right) = 0$ ）设 $f(x)$ 在 $(\mathrm{e}^{-2},1)$ 上的零点为 $x_{1}$ ，即 $f(x_{1}) = (x_{1} - 1)\ln x_{1} - x_{1} - 1 = 0$ ，

$$
f \left(\frac {1}{x _ {1}}\right) = \left(\frac {1}{x _ {1}} - 1\right) \ln \frac {1}{x _ {1}} - \frac {1}{x _ {1}} - 1 = - \left(\frac {1}{x _ {1}} - 1\right) \ln x _ {1} - \frac {1}{x _ {1}} - 1 = \frac {1}{x _ {1}} [ (x _ {1} - 1) \ln x _ {1} - 1 - x _ {1} ] = \frac {1}{x _ {1}} f (x _ {1}) = 0
$$
