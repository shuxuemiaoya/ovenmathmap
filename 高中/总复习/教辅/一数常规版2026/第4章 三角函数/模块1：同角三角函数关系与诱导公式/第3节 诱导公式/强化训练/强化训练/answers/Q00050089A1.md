> [!faq]- 《第3节 诱导公式》参考答案
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{89}{2}$ ; (2) 0
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：（1） $\sin^{2}1^{\circ}$ ， $\sin^{2}2^{\circ}$ 等无法直接计算，考虑组合计算，注意到 $\sin^{2}1^{\circ}+\sin^{2}89^{\circ}=\sin^{2}1^{\circ}+\cos^{2}1^{\circ}=1$ ，类似的， $\sin^{2}2^{\circ}+\sin^{2}88^{\circ}=\sin^{2}2^{\circ}+\cos^{2}2^{\circ}=1,\cdots$ ，计算的方法就出来了，
> > 记 $S = \sin^{2}1^{\circ} + \sin^{2}2^{\circ} + \sin^{2}3^{\circ} + \cdots + \sin^{2}89^{\circ}$ ①，
> > 因为 $\sin l^{\circ} = \sin(90^{\circ} - 89^{\circ}) = \cos 89^{\circ}$ $\sin 2^{\circ} = \sin(90^{\circ} - 88^{\circ}) = \cos 88^{\circ}, \cdots,$ $\sin 89^{\circ} = \sin(90^{\circ} - 1^{\circ}) = \cos 1^{\circ}$ ，代入式①得：
> >
> > $$
> > S = \cos^ {2} 8 9 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 8 8 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 8 7 ^ {\circ} + \dots + \cos^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \cos^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 2 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 3 ^ {\circ} + \dots + \cos^ {2} 8 9 ^ {\circ} \quad ②,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} ① + ② \text {得:} 2 S = (\sin^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ}) + (\sin^ {2} 2 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 2 ^ {\circ}) +
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \dots + (\sin^ {2} 8 9 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 8 9 ^ {\circ}) = 8 9, \text {故} S = \frac {8 9}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 分子为同底数对数相加, 可以合并, 故先合并,
> >
> > 原式的分子 = $\lg(\tan1^{\circ}\tan2^{\circ}\cdots\tan89^{\circ})$ ,
> >
> > 因为 $\tan 1^{\circ}\tan 2^{\circ}\dots \tan 89^{\circ} = \frac{\sin 1^{\circ}}{\cos 1^{\circ}}\cdot \frac{\sin 2^{\circ}}{\cos 2^{\circ}}\dots \frac{\sin 89^{\circ}}{\cos 89^{\circ}}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sin 1 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 8 9 ^ {\circ}} \cdot \frac {\sin 2 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 8 8 ^ {\circ}} \dots \frac {\sin 8 9 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 1 ^ {\circ}} = 1,
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\lg(\tan1^{\circ}\tan2^{\circ}\cdots\tan89^{\circ})=\lg1=0$ ，故原式 =0 .
> >
