> [!faq]- 《第2节 同角三角函数基本关系》参考答案
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** BC
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > BC
> >
> > 解析：A项， $\sin \alpha +\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{5}\Rightarrow (\sin \alpha +\cos \alpha)^2 =$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha + 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 1 + \sin 2 \alpha = \frac {1}{2 5}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin 2\alpha = -\frac{24}{25}$ ，故A项错误；
> >
> > $$
> > (\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha) ^ {2} = \sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 - \sin 2 \alpha = 1 - \left(- \frac {2 4}{2 5}\right) = \frac {4 9}{2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = - \frac {1 2}{2 5} <   0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \alpha \in (0, \pi)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \alpha > 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \alpha <   0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha = \frac {7}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos 2 \alpha = \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha = (\cos \alpha + \sin \alpha) (\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{5} \times \left(- \frac {7}{5}\right) = - \frac {7}{2 5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin^ {4} \alpha - \cos^ {4} \alpha = (\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha) (\sin^ {2} \alpha - \cos^ {2} \alpha)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sin^ {2} \alpha - \cos^ {2} \alpha = - \cos 2 \alpha = \frac {7}{2 5}
> > $$
