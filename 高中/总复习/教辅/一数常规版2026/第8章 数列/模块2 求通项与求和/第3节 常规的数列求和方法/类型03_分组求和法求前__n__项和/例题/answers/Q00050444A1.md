> [!faq]- 《第3节 常规的数列求和方法》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解: (由于 $2^{n}$ 和 $\frac{1}{n(n + 2)}$ 均能求和, 故分别求和, 再相减, 即可得到 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $S_{n}$ )
> >
> > 由题意， $a_{n} = 2^{n} - \frac{1}{n(n + 2)} = 2^{n} - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 2}\right),$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = 2 ^ {1} - \frac {1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) + 2 ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} \times \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{4}\right) + 2 ^ {3} - \frac {1}{2} \times \left(\frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{5}\right) + \dots + 2 ^ {n} - \frac {1}{2} \times \left(\frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(2 ^ {1} + 2 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {3} + \dots + 2 ^ {n}\right) - \frac {1}{2} \left(1 - \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{4} + \frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{5} + \dots + \frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {2 (1 - 2 ^ {n})}{1 - 2} - \frac {1}{2} \left(1 + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{n + 1} - \frac {1}{n + 2}\right) = 2 ^ {n + 1} - 2 - \frac {1}{2} \left[ \frac {3}{2} - \frac {2 n + 3}{(n + 1) (n + 2)} \right] = 2 ^ {n + 1} - \frac {1 1}{4} + \frac {2 n + 3}{2 (n + 1) (n + 2)}.
> > $$
