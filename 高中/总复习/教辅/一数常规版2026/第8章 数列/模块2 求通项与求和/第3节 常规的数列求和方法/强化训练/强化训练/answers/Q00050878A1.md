> [!faq]- 《第3节 常规的数列求和方法》参考答案
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1）因为 $S_{n} = 2^{n + 1} - 2$ ，所以 $a_1 = S_1 = 2^2 - 2 = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > \left( \begin{array}{c} a _ {1} b _ {1}, a _ {1} b _ {2}, a _ {1} b _ {3}, \dots , a _ {1} b _ {n} \\ a _ {2} b _ {1}, a _ {2} b _ {2}, a _ {2} b _ {3}, \dots , a _ {2} b _ {n} \\ a _ {3} b _ {1}, a _ {3} b _ {2}, a _ {3} b _ {3}, \dots , a _ {3} b _ {n} \\ \dots \\ a _ {n} b _ {1}, a _ {n} b _ {2}, a _ {n} b _ {3}, \dots , a _ {n} b _ {n} \end{array} \right)
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $n \geq 2$ 时， $a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n-1} = 2^{n+1} - 2 - (2^{n} - 2) = 2^{n+1} - 2^{n}$
> >
> > $= 2 \times 2^{n} - 2^{n} = 2^{n}$ ，又因为 $a_{1} = 2$ 也满足 $a_{n} = 2^{n}$ ，所以对任意的 $n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$ ，都有 $a_{n} = 2^{n}$ ，
> >
> > 由题意， $b_{n}=\log_{2}a_{n}=\log_{2}2^{n}=n$ .
> >
> > （2）（观察发现所给数阵中第 $k$ 行每项都有 $a_{k}$ ，故可先求出每一行的和，再把各行的和相加，即可得到数阵中所有项的和）设数阵中第 $k$ 行的和为 $c_{k}$ ，
> >
> > 则 $c_{k} = a_{k}b_{1} + a_{k}b_{2} + a_{k}b_{3} + \dots +a_{k}b_{n} = a_{k}(b_{1} + b_{2} + b_{3} + \dots +b_{n})$
> >
> > $$
> > = a _ {k} (1 + 2 + 3 + \dots + n) = a _ {k} \cdot \frac {n (1 + n)}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以该数阵中所有项的和 $T_{n} = c_{1} + c_{2} + c_{3} + \dots +c_{n}$
> >
> > $$
> > = a _ {1} \cdot \frac {n (1 + n)}{2} + a _ {2} \cdot \frac {n (1 + n)}{2} + a _ {3} \cdot \frac {n (1 + n)}{2} + \dots + a _ {n} \cdot \frac {n (1 + n)}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} = (a _ {1} + a _ {2} + a _ {3} + \dots + a _ {n}) \cdot \frac {n (1 + n)}{2} = \frac {2 (1 - 2 ^ {n})}{1 - 2} \cdot \frac {n (1 + n)}{2} \\ = (2 ^ {n} - 1) n (1 + n). \end{array}
> > $$
> >
