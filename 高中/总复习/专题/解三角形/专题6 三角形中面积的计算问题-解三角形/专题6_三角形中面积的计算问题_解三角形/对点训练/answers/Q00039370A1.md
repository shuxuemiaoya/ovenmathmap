> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角形中面积的计算问题-解三角形》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求 $\cos C$ 的值；
> > (2)若 $b = \sqrt{5}$ ，求 $\triangle ABC$ 的面积.
> > 3. 解析 (1) $\because A + B + C = \pi, A + 3C = \pi, \therefore B = 2C.$
> > 由 $\frac{b}{c}=\frac{\sin B}{\sin C}$ ，得 $\frac{4\sqrt{5}}{5}=\frac{2\sin C\cos C}{\sin C}$ ，化简得 $\cos C=\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$ .
> > (2) $\because C\in(0,\pi),\quad\therefore\sin C=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}C}=\sqrt{1-\frac{4}{5}}=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}.$ $\because B=2C,\quad\therefore\cos B=\cos2C=2\cos^{2}C-1=2\times\frac{4}{5}-1=\frac{3}{5},\quad\therefore\sin B=\frac{4}{5}.$ $\because A+B+C=\pi,$ $\therefore \sin A = \sin (B + C) = \sin B \cos C + \cos B \sin C = \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5} + \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} = \frac{11\sqrt{5}}{25}.$ $\because \frac{b}{c}=\frac{4\sqrt{5}}{5},\quad b=\sqrt{5},\quad\therefore c=\frac{5}{4}.$ $\therefore\triangle ABC$ 的面积 $S=\frac{1}{2}bc\sin A=\frac{1}{2}\times\sqrt{5}\times\frac{5}{4}\times\frac{11\sqrt{5}}{25}=\frac{11}{8}.$
