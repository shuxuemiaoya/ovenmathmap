> [!faq]- 《专题1 三角形中基本量的计算问题-解三角形》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 $\because a\sin A\sin B + b\cos^2 A = \sqrt{2} a, \therefore \sin A\sin A\sin B + \sin B\cos^2 A = \sqrt{2}\sin A, \therefore \sin B = \sqrt{2}\sin A,$ $\therefore \frac{b}{a} = \frac{\sin B}{\sin A} = \sqrt{2}.$
