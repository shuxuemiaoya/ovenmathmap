> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 由 $\sin C = 1, \therefore C = \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 由 $A: B = 1: 2$ , 故 $A + B = 3A = \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 得 $A = \frac{\pi}{6}, B = \frac{\pi}{3}$ , 由正弦定理得, $a: b: c = \sin A: \sin B: \sin C = \frac{1}{2}: \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}: \frac{2}{2} = 1: \sqrt{3}: 2$ .
