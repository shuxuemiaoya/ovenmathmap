> > [!note]- **【分析】**

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 $\sin \angle BAC = \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \angle BAD\right) = \cos \angle BAD, \therefore \cos \angle BAD = \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{2}. BD^{2} = AB^{2} + AD^{2} - 2AB \cdot AD\cos \angle BAD = (3\sqrt{2})^{2} + 3^{2} - 2 \times 3\sqrt{2} \times 3 \times \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{2}$ , 即 $BD^{2} = 3, BD = \sqrt{3}$ .
