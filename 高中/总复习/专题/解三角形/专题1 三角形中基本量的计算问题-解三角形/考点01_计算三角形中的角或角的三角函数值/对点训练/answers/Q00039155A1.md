> [!faq]- 《专题1 三角形中基本量的计算问题-解三角形》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{c} \sqrt {1 5} \\ \hline 4 \end{array}
> > $$
> > $$
> > 2 b ^ {2} - 2 a ^ {2} = a c + 2 c ^ {2}
> > $$
> > $$
> > 2 \left(a ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - b ^ {2}\right) + a c = 0
> > $$
> > $$
> > a ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - b ^ {2} = 2 a c \cos
> > $$
> > $$
> > \therefore 4 a c \cos B + a c = 0
> > $$
> > $$
> > \because a c \neq 0
> > $$
> > $$
> > \therefore 4 \cos B + 1 = 0, \cos B = - \frac {1}{4},
> > $$
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin B = \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} B} = \frac {\sqrt {1 5}}{4}
> > $$
