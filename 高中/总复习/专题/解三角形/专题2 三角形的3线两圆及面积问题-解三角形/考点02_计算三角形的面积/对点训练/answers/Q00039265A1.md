> [!faq]- 《专题2 三角形的3线两圆及面积问题-解三角形》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ 解析 $\because a = 2b\cos A, \therefore$ 由正弦定理可得 $\sin A = 2\sin B \cdot \cos A.$ $\because B = \frac{\pi}{3}, \therefore \sin A = \sqrt{3}\cos A,$ $\therefore \tan A = \sqrt{3}.$ 又 $\because A$ 为 $\triangle ABC$ 的内角， $\therefore A = \frac{\pi}{3}.$ 又 $B = \frac{\pi}{3}, \therefore C = \pi - A - B = \frac{\pi}{3}, \therefore \triangle ABC$ 为等边三角形， $\therefore S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} ac\sin B = \frac{1}{2} \times 1 \times 1 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}.$
