> [!faq]- 《专题2 三角形的3线两圆及面积问题-解三角形》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 由正弦定理 $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$ , 得 $\frac{2}{\sin A} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}$ , 所以 $\sin A = \frac{1}{2}$ , 又 $a < c$ , 所以 $A = \frac{\pi}{6}$ , $\sin B = \sin (A + C) = \sin A\cos C + \cos A\sin C = \frac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}{4}$ , 所以 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} ac\sin B = \frac{1}{2} \times 2 \times 2\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}{4} = \sqrt{3} + 1$ .
