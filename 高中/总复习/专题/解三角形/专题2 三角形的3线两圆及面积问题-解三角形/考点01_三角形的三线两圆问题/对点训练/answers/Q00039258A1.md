> [!faq]- 《专题2 三角形的3线两圆及面积问题-解三角形》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{7\sqrt{6}}{3}$ 解析 $\cos A = \frac{3^2 + 5^2 - 7^2}{2 \times 3 \times 5} = -\frac{1}{2}, \therefore A = 120^\circ, \therefore C = 60^\circ.$ 从而 $\frac{BC}{\sin 45^\circ} = \frac{7}{\sin 60^\circ}, \therefore BC = \frac{7\sin 45^\circ}{\sin 60^\circ} = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{7\sqrt{6}}{3}.$
