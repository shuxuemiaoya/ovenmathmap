> [!faq]- 《专题2 三角形的3线两圆及面积问题-解三角形》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $5\sqrt{2}$ 解析 $\because S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} ac \cdot \sin B = \frac{1}{2} c \cdot \sin 45^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} c = 2, \therefore c = 4\sqrt{2}, \therefore b^{2} = a^{2} + c^{2} - 2ac\cos 45^{\circ} = 25, \therefore b = 5, \therefore \triangle ABC$ 的外接圆直径为 $\frac{b}{\sin B} = 5\sqrt{2}$ .
