> [!faq]- 《专题5 定点直线线段长度问题》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) 直线 $l$ 的参数方程为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = -2 + t\cos \alpha, \\ y = -4 + t\sin \alpha \end{array} \right.$ (t 为参数),
> >
> > $$
> > \rho \sin^ {2} \theta = 2 \cos \theta
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \rho^ {2} \sin^ {2} \theta = 2 \rho \cos \theta
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x = \rho \cos \theta ,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y ^ {2} = 2 x
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y ^ {2} = 2 x
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > t ^ {2} \sin^ {2} \alpha - (2 \cos \alpha + 8 \sin \alpha) t + 2 0 = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 设 A, B 对应的参数分别为 $t_{1}$ , $t_{2}$ ，由一元二次方程根与系数的关系得， $t_{1}t_{2}=\frac{20}{\sin^{2}\alpha}$ ,
> >
> > 根据直线的参数方程中参数的几何意义，得 $|MA|\cdot|MB|=|t_{1}t_{2}|=\frac{20}{\sin^{2}\alpha}=40$ ，得 $\alpha=\frac{\pi}{4}$ 或 $\alpha=\frac{3\pi}{4}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \Delta = (2 \cos \alpha + 8 \sin \alpha) ^ {2} - 8 0 \sin^ {2} \alpha > 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \alpha = \frac {\pi}{4}.
> > $$
