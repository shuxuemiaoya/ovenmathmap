> [!faq]- 《专题5 定点直线线段长度问题》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) $\because \rho = 2\cos \theta, \therefore \rho^2 = 2\rho \cos \theta,$ 又 $\because \rho^2 = x^2 +y^2,\rho \cos \theta = x,$ $\therefore$ 曲线 $C_1$ 的直角坐标方程为 $x^{2} + y^{2} - 2x = 0$ ，曲线 $C_2$ 的普通方程为 $x^{2} + (y - 1)^{2} = t^{2}.$
> >
> > (2)将 $C_2$ 的参数方程 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = t\cos \alpha, \\ y = 1 + t\sin \alpha \end{array} \right.$ （ $t$ 为参数）代入 $C_1$ 的方程 $x^2 + y^2 - 2x = 0$ ，得 $t^2 + (2\sin \alpha - 2\cos \alpha)t + 1 = 0$ . $\because A = (2\sin \alpha - 2\cos \alpha)^2 - 4 = 8\sin^2\left[\frac{\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}}{4}\right] - 4 > 0,$ $\therefore \left| \sin \left(\frac{\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}}{4}\right) \right| \in \left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, 1\right), \quad \therefore \sin \left(\frac{\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}}{4}\right) \in \left[-1, -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) \cup \left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, 1\right].$ $t_1 + t_2 = -(2\sin \alpha - 2\cos \alpha) = -2\sqrt{2}\sin \left(\frac{\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}}{4}\right), t_1t_2 = 1 > 0,$ $\because t_1t_2 = 1 > 0, \quad \therefore t_1, t_2$ 同号， $\therefore |t_1| + |t_2| = |t_1 + t_2|$ ，由点 $A$ 在曲线 $C_2$ 上，根据 $t$ 的几何意义，可得 $\frac{1}{|PA|} + \frac{1}{|AQ|} = \frac{1}{|t_1|} + \frac{1}{|t_2|} = \frac{|t_1| + |t_2|}{|t_1||t_2|} = \frac{|t_1| + |t_2|}{|t_1t_2|} = \frac{|t_1 + t_2|}{1} = 2\sqrt{2}\left|\sin \left(\frac{\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}}{4}\right)\right| \in (2, 2\sqrt{2}].$ $\therefore \frac{1}{|PA|} + \frac{1}{|AQ|} \in (2, 2\sqrt{2}].$
> >
