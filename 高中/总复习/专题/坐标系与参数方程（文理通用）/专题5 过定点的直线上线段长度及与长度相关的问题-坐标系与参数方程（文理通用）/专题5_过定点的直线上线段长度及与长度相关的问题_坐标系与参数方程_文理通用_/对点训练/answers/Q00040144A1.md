> [!faq]- 《专题5 过定点的直线上线段长度及与长度相关的问题-坐标系与参数方程（文理通用）》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 (1) $l$ 的普通方程为 $x + y - 1 = 0$ ；又 $\because \rho^2 + \rho^2 \sin^2 \theta = 2, \therefore x^2 + y^2 + y^2 = 2$ ，即曲线 $C$ 的直角坐标方程为 $\frac{x^2}{2} + y^2 = 1$ .
> > (2) 点 $P$ 的直角坐标为 $\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)$ .
> > 法一 $P\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)$ 在直线 $l$ 上，直线 $l$ 的参数方程为 $\left\{\begin{aligned} & x = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} t', \\ & y = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} t' \end{aligned}\right.$ ( $t'$ 为参数)，代入曲线 $C$ 的直角坐标方程得 $\left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} t'\right)^2 + 2\left(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} t'\right)^2 - 2 = 0$ ，即 $\frac{3}{2} t'^2 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} t' - \frac{5}{4} = 0, |PA| \cdot |PB| = |t_1'| \cdot |t_2'| = |t_1't_2'| = \frac{5}{6}$ .
> > 法二 $\left\{\begin{aligned} & y = 1 - x, \\ & x^2 + 2y^2 = 2 \end{aligned}\right.$ $3x^2 - 4x = 0, x_1 = 0, x_2 = \frac{4}{3}, \therefore A(0, 1), B\left(\frac{4}{3}, -\frac{1}{3}\right), \therefore |PA| = \sqrt{\left(0 - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, |PB| = \sqrt{\left(\frac{4}{3} - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(-\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2} = \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{6}, \\ & |PA| \cdot |PB| = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{6} = \frac{5}{6}. \end{aligned}\right.$
