> [!faq]- 《专题24  长度和距离型取值范围模型-圆锥曲线》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) 设 $l: ty = x - a, A(x_1, y_1), B(x_2, y_2)$ . 由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y^2 = 2px, \\ ty = x - a \end{array} \right.$ 消去 $x$ 得 $y^2 - 2pty - 2pa = 0$ .
> >
> > $\therefore y_{1} + y_{2} = 2pt, y_{1}y_{2} = -2pa$ ，由 $OA \perp OB$ 得 $x_{1}x_{2} + y_{1}y_{2} = 0$ ，即 $-\frac{y_{1}y_{2}}{4p^{2}} + y_{1}y_{2} = 0,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a ^ {2} - 2 p a = 0. \quad \because a > 0, \quad \therefore a = 2 p.
> > $$
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)可得 $|AB|=\sqrt{1+t^{2}}|y_{1}-y_{2}|=2p\sqrt{1+t^{2}}\cdot\sqrt{t^{2}+4}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > | A M | \cdot | M B | = \overrightarrow {A M} \cdot \overrightarrow {M B} = (a - x _ {1}) (x _ {2} - a) - y _ {1} y _ {2} = - x _ {1} x _ {2} + a (x _ {1} + x _ {2}) - a ^ {2} - y _ {1} y _ {2} = a \cdot \frac {y _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2}}{2 p} - a ^ {2} = 4 p ^ {2} (1 + t ^ {2}).
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \lambda = \frac {\sqrt {4 + t ^ {2}}}{\sqrt {1 + t ^ {2}}} = \sqrt {1 + \frac {3}{1 + t ^ {2}}}. \because t ^ {2} \geq 0, \therefore \lambda \in (1, 2 ].
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because | O M | \cdot | A B | = \lambda | A M | \cdot | M B |, \therefore a \cdot 2 p \sqrt {1 + t ^ {2}} \sqrt {t ^ {2} + 4} = \lambda \cdot 4 p ^ {2} (1 + t ^ {2}),
> > $$
