> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 由题意知直线 AB 的斜率 $k=\frac{0-(-1)}{3-1}=\frac{1}{2}$ ，设 $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1,\quad①\\ \frac{x_{2}^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y_{2}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1,\quad②\end{aligned}\right.$ ①-②整理得 $\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}=-\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}\cdot\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{y_{1}+y_{2}}$ ，即 $k=-\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}\times\frac{2}{-2}=\frac{1}{2},\therefore\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}=\frac{1}{2}.$ 又 $a^{2}-b^{2}=c^{2}=9,$ ∴ $a^{2}=18,\ b^{2}=9.\therefore$ 椭圆E的方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{18}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1.$
