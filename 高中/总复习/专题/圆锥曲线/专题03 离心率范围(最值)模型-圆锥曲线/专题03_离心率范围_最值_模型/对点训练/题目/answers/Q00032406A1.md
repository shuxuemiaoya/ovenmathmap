> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 $\because F_{1}, F_{2}$ 是椭圆 $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1 (a > b > 0)$ 的左、右两个焦点， $\therefore$ 离心率 $0 < e < 1, F_{1}(-c, 0), F_{2}(c, 0), c^{2} = a^{2} - b^{2}$ . 设点 $P(x, y)$ ，由 $PF_{1} \perp PF_{2}$ ，得 $(x + c, y) \cdot (x - c, y) = 0$ ，化简得 $x^{2} + y^{2} = c^{2}$ . 联立方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x^{2} + y^{2} = c^{2}, \\ \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 整理得， $x^{2} = (2c^{2} - a^{2}) \cdot \frac{a^{2}}{c^{2}} \geq 0$ ，解得 $e \geq \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ . 又 $0 < e < 1, \therefore \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \leq e < 1$ .
