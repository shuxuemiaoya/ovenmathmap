> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > [规范解答] (1)由题意可设椭圆方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ ，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{c}{a}&=\frac{1}{2},\\\frac{1}{a^{2}}&+\frac{9}{4b^{2}}=1,\\a^{2}&=b^{2}+c^{2},\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $a^{2}=4,\ b^{2}=3$ .
> > ∴椭圆方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$ .
