> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{\sqrt{7}}{14}$ 解析 设双曲线的标准方程为 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1 (a > 0, b > 0)$ ，由 $e = \frac{c}{a} = 2$ 知， $c = 2a$ ，又 $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$ ，故 $b = \sqrt{3}a$ ，所以 $A(0, \sqrt{3}a), C(0, -\sqrt{3}a), B(-a, 0), F(-2a, 0)$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{BA} = (a, \sqrt{3}a), \overrightarrow{CF} = (-$
> > $2a, \sqrt{3} a)$ , 结合题图可知, $\cos \angle BDF = \cos < \overrightarrow{BA}, \overrightarrow{CF} > = \frac{\overrightarrow{BA} \cdot \overrightarrow{CF}}{|BA| \cdot |CF|} = \frac{-2a^2 + 3a^2}{2a \cdot \sqrt{7} a} = \frac{\sqrt{7}}{14}$ .
