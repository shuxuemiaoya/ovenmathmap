>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $y = \pm 2\sqrt{2} x$ 解析 由题意得 $|AB| = \frac{2b^2}{a}$ , $\because S_{\triangle AOB} = \frac{8}{3}$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2b^2}{a} \times 1 = \frac{8}{3}$ , $\therefore \frac{b^2}{a} = \frac{8}{3}$ ①, 又 $a^2 + b^2 = 1$
> > - **②**：，由①②得 $a = \frac{1}{3}$ ， $b = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ，∴双曲线的渐近线方程为 $y = \pm \frac{b}{a} x = \pm 2\sqrt{2} x.$
