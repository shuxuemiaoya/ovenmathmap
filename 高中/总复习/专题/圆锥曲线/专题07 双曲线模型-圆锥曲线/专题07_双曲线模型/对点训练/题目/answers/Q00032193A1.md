>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 $\because e = \frac{c}{a} = \sqrt{2}, \therefore c = \sqrt{2} a, \therefore b^2 = c^2 - a^2 = a^2, \therefore$ 双曲线方程可变形为 $x^2 - y^2 = a^2$ . 设 $B(x_0, y_0)$ , 由对称性可知 $C(-x_0, y_0)$ , $\because$ 点 $B(x_0, y_0)$ 在双曲线上, $\therefore x_0^2 - y_0^2 = a^2$ . $\because A(a, 0), \therefore \overrightarrow{AB} = (x_0 - a, y_0), \overrightarrow{AC} = (-x_0 - a, y_0), \therefore \overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = (x_0 - a) \cdot (-x_0 - a) + y_0^2 = a^2 - x_0^2 + y_0^2 = 0, \therefore \overrightarrow{AB} \perp \overrightarrow{AC}$ , 即 $\theta = \frac{\pi}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
