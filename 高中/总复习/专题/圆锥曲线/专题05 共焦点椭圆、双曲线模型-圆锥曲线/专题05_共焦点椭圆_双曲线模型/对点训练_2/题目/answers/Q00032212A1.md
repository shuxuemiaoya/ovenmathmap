> > [!note]- **【分析】**

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 秒杀 由椭圆和双曲线焦点三角形面积公式得， $b^{2}\tan\frac{\theta}{2}=b^{2}\frac{1}{\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}$ ，即， $4\tan\frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{1}{\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}$ ，解得 $\tan\frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{1}{2}$ . $\therefore\frac{1}{e_{1}^{2}}+\frac{4}{e_{2}^{2}}=5$ , $\therefore5=\frac{1}{e_{1}^{2}}+\frac{4}{e_{2}^{2}}\geq2\sqrt{\frac{4}{e_{1}^{2}e_{2}^{2}}}\geq\frac{4}{e_{1}e_{2}}$ , $\therefore5\geq\frac{4}{e_{1}e_{2}}$ ，当且仅当 $2e_{1}=e_{2}$ 时，等号成立. $\therefore e_{1}e_{2}\geq\frac{4}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
