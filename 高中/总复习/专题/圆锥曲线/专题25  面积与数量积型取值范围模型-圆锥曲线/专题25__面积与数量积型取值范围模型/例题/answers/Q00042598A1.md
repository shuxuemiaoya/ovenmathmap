> [!faq]- 《专题25  面积与数量积型取值范围模型-圆锥曲线》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) 设 $P(x_{0}, y_{0})$ , $A\left(\frac{1}{4}y_{1}^{2}, y_{1}\right)$ , $B\left(\frac{1}{4}y_{2}^{2}, y_{2}\right)$ .
> >
> > 因为 PA, PB 的中点在抛物线上，所以 $y_{1}$ ， $y_{2}$ 为方程 $\left(\frac{y+y_{0}}{2}\right)^{2}=4\cdot\frac{\frac{1}{4}y^{2}+x_{0}}{2}$
> >
> > 即 $y^{2} - 2y_{0}y + 8x_{0} - y_{0}^{2} = 0$ 的两个不同的实根．所以 $y_{1} + y_{2} = 2y_{0}$ ，所以 $PM$ 垂直于 $y$ 轴.
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)可知 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y_{1} + y_{2} = 2y_{0}, \\ y_{1}y_{2} = 8x_{0} - y_{0}^{2}, \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $|PM| = \frac{1}{8}(y_{1}^{2} + y_{2}^{2}) - x_{0} = \frac{3}{4} y_{0}^{2} - 3x_{0}, |y_{1} - y_{2}| = 2\sqrt{2(y_{0}^{2} - 4x_{0})}$ .
> >
> > 所以 $\triangle PAB$ 的面积 $S_{\triangle PAB} = \frac{1}{2}|PM|\cdot |y_1 - y_2| = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{4}(y_0^2 -4x_0)\frac{3}{2},$
> >
> > 因为 $x_0^2 +\frac{y_0^2}{4} = 1(-1\leq x_0 <   0)$ ，所以 $y_{0}^{2} - 4x_{0} = -4x_{0}^{2} - 4x_{0} + 4\in [4,5],$
> >
> > 所以 $\triangle PAB$ 面积的取值范围是 $\left[6\sqrt{2}, \frac{15\sqrt{10}}{4}\right]$ .
