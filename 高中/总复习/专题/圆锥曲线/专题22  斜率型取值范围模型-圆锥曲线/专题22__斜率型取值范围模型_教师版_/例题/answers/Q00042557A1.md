> > (2)法一：由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1, \\ y = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} x, \end{array} \right.$ 得 $\left(b^2 + \frac{1}{8} a^2\right)x^2 - a^2 b^2 = 0.$
