> [!faq]- 教师版对点训练解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 5 解析 设 $A(x_{1}, y_{1}), B(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，由 $\overrightarrow{AP} = 2\overrightarrow{PB}$ ，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} -x_{1} = 2x_{2}, \\ 1 - y_{1} = 2(y_{2} - 1), \end{array} \right.$ 即 $x_{1} = -2x_{2}, y_{1} = 3$ $-2y_{2}$ . 因为点 $A, B$ 在椭圆上，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{4x_2^2}{4} + (3 - 2y_2)^2 = m, \\ \frac{x_2^2}{4} + y_2^2 = m, \end{array} \right.$ 得 $y_{2} = \frac{1}{4}m + \frac{3}{4}$ ，所以 $x_2^2 = m - (3 - 2y_2)^2 = -\frac{1}{4}m^2 + \frac{5}{2}m - \frac{9}{4} = -\frac{1}{4}(m - 5)^2 + 4 \leqslant 4$ ，所以当 $m = 5$ 时，点 $B$ 横坐标的绝对值最大，最大值为 2.
