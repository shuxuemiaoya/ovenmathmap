> [!faq]- 教师版对点训练解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $(0, \sqrt{3}]$ 解析 由双曲线的定义及题意可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} PF_1 | - |PF_2| = 2a, \\ |PF_1| = t|PF_2|, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} |PF_1| = \frac{2at}{t-1}, \\ |PF_2| = \frac{2a}{t-1}. \end{array} \right.$ 又 $|PF_1|$ $+ |PF_2| \geqslant 2c$ , $\therefore |PF_1| + |PF_2| = \frac{2at}{t-1} + \frac{2a}{t-1} \geqslant 2c$ , 整理得 $e = \frac{c}{a} \leqslant \frac{t+1}{t-1} = 1 + \frac{2}{t-1}$ , $\because 1 < t \leqslant 3$ , $\therefore 1 + \frac{2}{t-1} \geqslant 2$ , $\therefore 1 < e \leqslant 2$ . 又 $\frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{c^2 - a^2}{a^2} = e^2 - 1$ , $\therefore 0 < \frac{b^2}{a^2} \leqslant 3$ , 故 $0 < \frac{b}{a} \leqslant \sqrt{3}$ . $\therefore$ 双曲线经过一、三象限的渐近线的斜率的取值范围是 $(0, \sqrt{3}]$ .
