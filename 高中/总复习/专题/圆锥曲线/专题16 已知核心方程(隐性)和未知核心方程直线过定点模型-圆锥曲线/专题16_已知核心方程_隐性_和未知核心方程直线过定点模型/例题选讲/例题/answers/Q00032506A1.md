> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > [规范解答] (1)由题可知当点 P 在椭圆 O 的上顶点(或下顶点)时， $S_{\triangle PAB}$ 最大，
> > 此时 $S_{\triangle PAB} = \frac{1}{2}\times 2ab = ab = 2\sqrt{3},\quad \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l}ab = 2\sqrt{3},\\ \frac{c}{a} = \frac{1}{2},\\ a^2 -b^2 = c^2 \end{array} \right.$ $\Rightarrow a = 2,b = \sqrt{3},c = 1,$
> > ∴椭圆 O 的标准方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{4} + \frac{y^{2}}{3} = 1$ .
