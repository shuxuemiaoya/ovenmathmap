> > (1)由抛物线定义知 $|AF|=y_{1}+\frac{1}{2},|BF|=y_{2}+\frac{1}{2},\therefore|AF|-|BF|=y_{1}-y_{2}=2$ ，又知 $x_{1}^{2}=2y_{1}$ ， $x_{2}^{2}=2y_{2},\therefore x_{1}^{2}-x_{2}^{2}=2(y_{1}-y_{2})=4,\therefore y_{1}+x_{1}^{2}-y_{2}-x_{2}^{2}=(y_{1}-y_{2})+(x_{1}^{2}-x_{2}^{2})=2+4=6.$
