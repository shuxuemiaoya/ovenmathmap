> [!faq]- 教师版对点训练解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 0 解析 设 $A(x_{1}, y_{1}), B(x_{2}, y_{2}), C(x_{3}, y_{3}), F\left(\frac{p}{2}, 0\right)$ , 由 $\overrightarrow{PA} + \overrightarrow{FB} = -\overrightarrow{FC}$ , 得 $\left(x_{1}-\frac{p}{2}, y_{1}\right) + \left(x_{2}-\frac{p}{2}, y_{2}\right) = -\left(x_{3}-\frac{p}{2}, y_{3}\right), y_{1} + y_{2} + y_{3} = 0.$ 因为 $k_{AB} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{2p}{y_1 + y_2}, k_{AC} = \frac{y_3 - y_1}{x_3 - x_1} = \frac{2p}{y_1 + y_3}, k_{BC} = \frac{y_3 - y_2}{x_3 - x_2} = \frac{2p}{y_2 + y_3}$ , 所以 $\frac{1}{k_{AB}} + \frac{1}{k_{AC}} + \frac{1}{k_{BC}} = \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2p} + \frac{y_3 + y_1}{2p} + \frac{y_2 + y_3}{2p} = \frac{y_1 + y_2 + y_3}{p} = 0.$
