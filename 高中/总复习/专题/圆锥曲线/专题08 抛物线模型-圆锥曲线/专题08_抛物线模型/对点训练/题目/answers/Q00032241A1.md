> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\left(-\frac{2\sqrt{3}p}{3},0\right)$ 解析 联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y^{2}&=2px,\\ y&=kx+b\end{aligned}\right.$ 消去 y，得 $k^{2}x^{2}+(2kb-2p)x+b^{2}=0,\therefore x_{1}+x_{2}=\frac{-2kb+2p}{k^{2}}$ $x_{1}x_{2}=\frac{b^{2}}{k^{2}},\quad\because k_{OA}\cdot k_{OB}=\sqrt{3},\quad\therefore y_{1}y_{2}=\sqrt{3}x_{1}x_{2},\quad 又 \because y_{1}y_{2}=(kx_{1}+b)(kx_{2}+b)=k^{2}x_{1}x_{2}+kb(x_{1}+x_{2})+b^{2}=\frac{2bp}{k},$ $\therefore \frac{2bp}{k}=\sqrt{3}\cdot\frac{b^{2}}{k^{2}}$ ，解得 $b=\frac{2\sqrt{3}pk}{3},\quad\therefore y=kx+\frac{2\sqrt{3}pk}{3}=k\left(x+\frac{2\sqrt{3}p}{3}\right).$ 令 $x=-\frac{2\sqrt{3}p}{3}$ ，得 y=0，∴直线 l 过
> > 定点 $\left(-\frac{2\sqrt{3}p}{3},0\right)$ .
