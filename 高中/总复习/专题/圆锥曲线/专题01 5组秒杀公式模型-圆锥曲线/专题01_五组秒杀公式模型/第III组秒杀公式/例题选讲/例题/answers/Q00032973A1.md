> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 秒杀 $\because k_{1}\cdot k_{2}=e^{2}-1.\therefore-\frac{1}{2}=e^{2}-1.\therefore e=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ . 故选 C.
> > 通法 设 $P(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $Q(-x_1, - y_1)$ ， $A(a,0)$ .所以 $k_{AP}\cdot k_{AQ} = \frac{y_1}{x_1 - a}\cdot \frac{-y_1}{-x_1 - a} = \frac{y_1^2}{x_1^2 - a^2}$ 又因为 $\frac{x_1^2 + y_1^2}{a^2} = 1\Rightarrow y_1^2 = \frac{b^2(a^2 - x_1^2)}{a^2}$ 所以 $k_{AP}\cdot k_{AQ} = -\frac{b^2}{a^2}$ 即 $\frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{1}{2}$ 所以椭圆 $C$ 的离心率 $e = \frac{c}{a} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{b^2}{a^2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
