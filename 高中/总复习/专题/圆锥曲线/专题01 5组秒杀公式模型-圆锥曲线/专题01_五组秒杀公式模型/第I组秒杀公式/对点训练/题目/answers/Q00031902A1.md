> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 秒杀 $\triangle PEF_{2}$ 的周长为 $|PE| + |PF_{2}| + |EF_{2}| = |PE| + 2a - |PF_{1}| + |EF_{2}| = 2a + |EF_{2}| + |PE|$ $|PF_{1}| \geq 2a + |EF_{2}| - |EF_{1}| = 2a = 4b$ , $\therefore e = \frac{c}{a} = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^{2}} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{1}{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
