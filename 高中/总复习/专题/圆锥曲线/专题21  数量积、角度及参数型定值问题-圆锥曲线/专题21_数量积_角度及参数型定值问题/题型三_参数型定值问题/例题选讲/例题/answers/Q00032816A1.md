> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > [规范解答] (1)设椭圆 C 的方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1 (a > b > 0)$ ，则 $\left\{\frac{1}{a^{2}} + \frac{\left(\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\right)^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1, \right.$
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {c}{a} = \frac {2 \sqrt {5}}{5}, \\ \frac {1}{a ^ {2}} + \frac {\left(\frac {2 \sqrt {5}}{5}\right) _ {2}}{b ^ {2}} = 1 \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> > $$
> > \therefore a ^ {2} = 5, b ^ {2} = 1,
> > $$
> > ∴椭圆 C 的标准方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{5} + y^{2} = 1$ .
