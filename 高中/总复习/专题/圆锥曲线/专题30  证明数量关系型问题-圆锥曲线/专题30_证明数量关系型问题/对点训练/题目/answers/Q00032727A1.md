>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析

> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 (1)由题意，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} c = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \\ a = 2, \\ 2c = 2\sqrt{3}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 2, \\ c = \sqrt{3}, \end{array} \right.$ 又 $b^{2} = a^{2} - c^{2} = 1$ ， $\therefore$ 椭圆 $C$ 的方程为 $\frac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$ （2）证明：设直线 $l$ 的方程为 $y = -\frac{1}{2} x + m$ ， $P(x_1, y_1)$ ， $Q(x_2, y_2)$ ，由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = -\frac{1}{2} x + m, \\ \frac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 消去 $y$ ，得 $2x^{2} - 4mx + 4(m^{2} - 1) = 0$ 则 $\Delta = 16m^{2} - 32(m^{2} - 1) = 16(2 - m^{2}) > 0$ ，且 $x_{1} + x_{2} = 2m$ ， $x_{1}x_{2} = 2(m^{2} - 1)$ . 故 $y_{1}y_{2} = \left(-\frac{1}{2} x_{1} + m\right)\left(-\frac{1}{2} x_{2} + m\right) = \frac{1}{4} x_{1}x_{2} - \frac{1}{2} m(x_{1} + x_{2}) + m^{2}$ . $\therefore k_{OP} \cdot k_{OQ} = \frac{y_{1}y_{2}}{x_{1}x_{2}} = \frac{\frac{1}{4}x_{1}x_{2} - \frac{1}{2}m(x_{1} + x_{2}) + m^{2}}{x_{1}x_{2}} = \frac{1}{4} = k_{PQ}^{2}$ ,
> > 即直线 $OP, PQ, OQ$ 的斜率依次成等比数列.
