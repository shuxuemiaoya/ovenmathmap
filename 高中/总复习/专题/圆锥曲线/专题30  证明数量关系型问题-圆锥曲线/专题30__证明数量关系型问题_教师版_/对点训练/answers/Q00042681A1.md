> [!faq]- 《专题30  证明数量关系型问题(教师版)》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求椭圆 C 的方程;
> >
> > (2)若斜率为 $-\frac{1}{2}$ 的直线与椭圆 C 交于 P, Q 两点(点 P, Q 均在第一象限), O 为坐标原点, 证明: 直线 OP, PQ, OQ 的斜率依次成等比数列.
> >
> > ![](../../../images/questions/f44ff3b8357533bf115c8200bb4b40450c19330947c8078331094c5a8f0b0940.jpg)
> >
> > 7. 解析
> > (1)由题意，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} c = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \\ a = 2, \\ 2c = 2\sqrt{3}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 2, \\ c = \sqrt{3}, \end{array} \right.$ 又 $b^{2} = a^{2} - c^{2} = 1$ ， $\therefore$ 椭圆 $C$ 的方程为 $\frac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$
> > （2）证明：设直线 $l$ 的方程为 $y = -\frac{1}{2} x + m$ ， $P(x_1, y_1)$ ， $Q(x_2, y_2)$ ，由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = -\frac{1}{2} x + m, \\ \frac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 消去 $y$ ，得 $2x^{2} - 4mx + 4(m^{2} - 1) = 0$ 则 $\Delta = 16m^{2} - 32(m^{2} - 1) = 16(2 - m^{2}) > 0$ ，且 $x_{1} + x_{2} = 2m$ ， $x_{1}x_{2} = 2(m^{2} - 1)$ . 故 $y_{1}y_{2} = \left(-\frac{1}{2} x_{1} + m\right)\left(-\frac{1}{2} x_{2} + m\right) = \frac{1}{4} x_{1}x_{2} - \frac{1}{2} m(x_{1} + x_{2}) + m^{2}$ . $\therefore k_{OP} \cdot k_{OQ} = \frac{y_{1}y_{2}}{x_{1}x_{2}} = \frac{\frac{1}{4}x_{1}x_{2} - \frac{1}{2}m(x_{1} + x_{2}) + m^{2}}{x_{1}x_{2}} = \frac{1}{4} = k_{PQ}^{2}$ ,
> >
> > 即直线 $OP, PQ, OQ$ 的斜率依次成等比数列.
