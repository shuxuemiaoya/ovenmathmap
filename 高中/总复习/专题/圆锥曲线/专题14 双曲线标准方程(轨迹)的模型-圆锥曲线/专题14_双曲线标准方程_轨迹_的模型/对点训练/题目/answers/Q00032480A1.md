> > [!note]- **【分析】**

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析: 设所求双曲线方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{y^{2}}{7 - a^{2}} = 1$ . 由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{y^{2}}{7 - a^{2}} &= 1, \\ y &= x - 1,\end{aligned}\right.$ 得 $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{(x - 1)^{2}}{7 - a^{2}} = 1, (7 - a^{2})x^{2} - a^{2}(x - 1)^{2} = a^{2}(7 - a^{2})$ , 整理得 $(7 - 2a^{2})x^{2} + 2a^{2}x - 8a^{2} + a^{4} = 0$ . 又 MN 中点的横坐标为 $-\frac{2}{3}$ , 故 $x_{0} = \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2} = -\frac{2a^{2}}{2(7 - 2a^{2})} = -\frac{2}{3}$ , 即 $3a^{2} = 2(7 - 2a^{2})$ , 所以 $a^{2} = 2$ , 故所求双曲线方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{2} - \frac{y^{2}}{5} = 1$ .
