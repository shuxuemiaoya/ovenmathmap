> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 设 $A(x_{1}, y_{1})$ , $B(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，则 $\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{9} + \frac{y_{1}^{2}}{4} = 1$ , $\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{9} + \frac{y_{2}^{2}}{4} = 1$ ，两式作差并化简整理得 $\frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} = -\frac{4}{9} \times \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{y_{1} + y_{2}}$ ，而 $x_{1} + x_{2} = -1$ , $y_{1} + y_{2} = 2$ ，所以 $\frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} = -\frac{4}{9} \times \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{y_{1} + y_{2}} = \frac{2}{9}$ ，直线 l 的方程为 $y - 1 = \frac{2}{9} \left( x + \frac{1}{2} \right)$ ，即 $2x - 9y + 10 = 0$ 。经验证可知符合题意。
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
