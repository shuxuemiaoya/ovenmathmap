>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 由椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$ 得 $a^2 = 25, b^2 = 9$ ，则 $c = \sqrt{a^2 - b^2} = \sqrt{25 - 9} = 4, \therefore |F_1F_2| = 2c = 8$ . 由椭圆的定义可得 $|PF_1| + |PF_2| = 2a = 10, \because PF_1 \perp PF_2, \therefore |PF_1|^2 + |PF_2|^2 = 64. \therefore 2|PF_1| \cdot |PF_2| = (|PF_1| + |PF_2|)^2 - (|PF_1|^2 + |PF_2|^2) = 100 - 64 = 36, \therefore |PF_1| \cdot |PF_2| = 18.$ 又 $S \triangle PF_1F_2 = \frac{1}{2} |PF_1| \cdot |PF_2| = \frac{1}{2} |F_1F_2| \cdot |PH|$ , $\therefore |PH| = \frac{|PF_1| \cdot |PF_2|}{|F_1F_2|} = \frac{9}{4}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
