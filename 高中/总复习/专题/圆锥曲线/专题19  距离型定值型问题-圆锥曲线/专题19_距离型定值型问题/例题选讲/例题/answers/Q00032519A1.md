> > [规范解答] (1)由题可知 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} b = c \\ \frac{1}{2a^2} + \frac{3}{4b^2} = 1 \\ a^2 = b^2 + c^2 \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a^2 = 2, \\ b^2 = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 所以椭圆 $C$ 的方程为 $\frac{x^2}{2} + y^2 = 1$ .
