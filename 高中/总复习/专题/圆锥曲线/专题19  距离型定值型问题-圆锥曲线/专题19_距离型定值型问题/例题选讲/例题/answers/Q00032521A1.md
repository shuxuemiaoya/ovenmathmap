> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > [规范解答] (1)由题意可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \\ \frac{4}{a^2} + \frac{1}{b^2} = 1, \\ a^2 = b^2 + c^2, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $a^2 = 6, b^2 = c^2 = 3$ ，故椭圆 $C$ 的方程为 $\frac{x^2}{6} + \frac{y^2}{3} = 1.$
