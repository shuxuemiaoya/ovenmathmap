> > [规范解答] (1)由题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \\ \frac{1}{2} ab = 1, \\ a^2 = b^2 + c^2, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 2, \\ b = 1, \\ c = \sqrt{3}. \end{array} \right.$ 所以椭圆 $C$ 的方程为 $\frac{x^2}{4} + y^2 = 1$ .
