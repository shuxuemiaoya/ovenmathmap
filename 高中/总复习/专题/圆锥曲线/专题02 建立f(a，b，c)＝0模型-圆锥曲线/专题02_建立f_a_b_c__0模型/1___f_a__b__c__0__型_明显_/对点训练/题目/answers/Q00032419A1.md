> [!faq]- 教师版对点训练解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\sqrt{5}$ 解析 $\because$ 点 $M$ 的纵坐标为 $\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$ , $\therefore$ 点 $M$ 在渐近线 $y = \frac{b}{a} x$ 上. $\because$ 双曲线方程为 $x^{2} - \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1$ , $\therefore a = 1, F(c, 0)$ , 渐近线方程为 $y = \pm bx$ . 则 $|FM| = \frac{|bc|}{\sqrt{1 + b^2}}$ , $\because c^{2} = a^{2} + b^{2} = 1 + b^{2}, \therefore |FM| = b$ . $\because \triangle OMF$ 为直角三角形, $\therefore OM = \sqrt{OF^2 - FM^2} = \sqrt{c^2 - b^2} = a$ . $\therefore OM \times FM = OF \times y_M$ , 即 $cy_M = ab$ , $\therefore c^2 y_M^2 = b^2$ . $\because y_M = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$ , $\therefore b^2 = \frac{4}{5} c^2$ . 又 $\because c^2 = a^2 + b^2$ , $\therefore a^2 = \frac{1}{5} c^2$ , $\therefore e = \sqrt{5}$ .
