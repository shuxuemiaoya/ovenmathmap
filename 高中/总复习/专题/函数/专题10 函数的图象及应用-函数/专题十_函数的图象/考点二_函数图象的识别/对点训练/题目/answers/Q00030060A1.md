> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 当 $x \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right]$ 时， $f(x) = \tan x + \sqrt{4 + \tan^2 x}$ ，图象不会是直线段，从而排除 A、C。当 $x \in \left[\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right]$ 时， $f(\frac{\pi}{4}) = f(\frac{3\pi}{4}) = 1 + \sqrt{5}$ ， $f(\frac{\pi}{2}) = 2\sqrt{2}$ 。\$\because 2\sqrt{2} < 1 + \sqrt{5}\$，\$\therefore f(\frac{\pi}{2}) < f(\frac{\pi}{4}) = f(\frac{3\pi}{4})\$，从而排除 D，故
> > 选B.
