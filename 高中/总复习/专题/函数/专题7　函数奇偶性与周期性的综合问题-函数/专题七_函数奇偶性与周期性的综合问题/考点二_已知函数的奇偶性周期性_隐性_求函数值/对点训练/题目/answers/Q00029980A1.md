> > [!note]- **【分析】**

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 0 解析 当 $x \geq 0$ 时, $f(x + 2) = -\frac{1}{f(x)}$ , $\therefore f(x + 4) = f(x)$ , 即 4 是 $f(x)(x \geq 0)$ 的一个周期. $\therefore f(-2017) = f(2017) = f(1) = \log_2 2 = 1$ , $f(2019) = f(3) = -\frac{1}{f(1)} = -1$ , $\therefore f(-2017) + f(2019) = 0$ .
