> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $f(x) = x^2 - 6x + 8$ 解析 $\because f(x + 2) = -f(x), \therefore f(x + 4) = -f(x + 2) = f(x). \therefore f(x)$ 是周期为 4 的周期函数. $\because x \in [2, 4], \therefore -x \in [-4, -2], \therefore 4 - x \in [0, 2], \therefore f(4 - x) = 2(4 - x) - (4 - x)^2 = -x^2 + 6x - 8. \because f(4 - x) = f(-x) = -f(x), \therefore -f(x) = -x^2 + 6x - 8, \text{即} f(x) = x^2 - 6x + 8, x \in [2, 4].$
