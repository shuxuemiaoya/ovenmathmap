> [!faq]- 教师版对点训练解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 1 解析 $f(x) = \frac{2tx^2 + \sqrt{2}t\sin\left(x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) + x}{2x^2 + \cos x} = t + \frac{t\sin x + x}{2x^2 + \cos x}$ ，设 $g(x) = \frac{t\sin x + x}{2x^2 + \cos x}$ ，则 $g(x)$ 为奇函数， $g(x)_{\max} = a - t, g(x)_{\min} = b - t. \quad \because g(x)_{\max} + g(x)_{\min} = 0, \therefore a + b - 2t = 0$ ，即 $2 - 2t = 0$ ，解得 $t = 1$ .
