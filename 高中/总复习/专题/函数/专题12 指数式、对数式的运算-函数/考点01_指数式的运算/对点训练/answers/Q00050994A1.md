> [!faq]- 《专题12 指数式、对数式的运算-函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解
> > (1) $\left(-x^{\frac{1}{3}}y^{-\frac{1}{3}}\right)\left(3x^{-\frac{1}{2}}y^{\frac{2}{3}}\right)\left(-2x^{\frac{1}{6}}y^{\frac{2}{3}}\right) = [-1\times 3\times (-2)]x^{\frac{1}{3} -\frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{6}}y^{-\frac{1}{3} +\frac{2}{3} +\frac{2}{3}} = 6x^{0}y^{1} = 6y.$
> >
> > $$
> > 2 x ^ {\frac {1}{4}} \left(- 3 x ^ {\frac {1}{4}} y ^ {- \frac {1}{3}}\right) \div \left(- 6 x ^ {- \frac {3}{2}} y ^ {- \frac {4}{3}}\right) = [ 2 \times (- 3) \div (- 6) ] x ^ {\frac {1}{4} + \frac {1}{4} + \frac {3}{2}} y ^ {- \frac {1}{3} + \frac {4}{3}} = x ^ {2} y. \tag {2}
> > $$
