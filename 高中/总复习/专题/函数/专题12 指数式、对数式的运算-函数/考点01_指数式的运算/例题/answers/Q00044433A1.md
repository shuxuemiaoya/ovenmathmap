> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $-\frac{5\sqrt{ab}}{4ab^2}$ 解析 原式 $= -\frac{5}{2} a^{-\frac{1}{6}}b^{-3}\div (4a_{3}^{2}\cdot b^{-3})_{2}^{1} = -\frac{5}{4} a^{-\frac{1}{6}}b^{-3}\div (a_{3}^{1}b^{-\frac{3}{2}}) = -\frac{5}{4} a^{-\frac{1}{2}}\cdot b^{-\frac{3}{2}} = -$ $\frac{5}{4}\cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{ab^3}} = -\frac{5\sqrt{ab}}{4ab^2}.$
