>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 设点 $P(x_0, y_0)$ , 可得 $M\left(\frac{1}{2}x_0 + y_0, \frac{1}{4}x_0 + \frac{1}{2}y_0\right), N\left(\frac{1}{2}x_0 - y_0, -\frac{1}{4}x_0 + \frac{1}{2}y_0\right)$ . 由于 $|MN| = \sqrt{\frac{1}{4}x_0^2 + 4y_0^2}$ 为定值, 所以 $\frac{a^2}{b^2} = \frac{4}{\frac{1}{4}} = 16, \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}} = 2$ , 因此选 C.
