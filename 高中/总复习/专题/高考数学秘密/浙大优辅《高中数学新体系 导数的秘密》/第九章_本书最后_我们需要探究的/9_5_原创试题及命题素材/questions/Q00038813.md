## 4. 双变量不等式

当 $x > 0, y > 0$ 时， $\frac{1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x - y}}{(x + y)(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-x})(1 - \mathrm{e}^{-y})} - \frac{1}{xy} \leqslant \frac{1}{12}$ ;

$$
\frac {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2}}{a + b} > \sqrt {\frac {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2}}{2}} > \frac {a + b}{2} > \frac {a - b}{\ln a - \ln b} > \sqrt {a b} > \frac {2 a b}{a + b}.
$$
