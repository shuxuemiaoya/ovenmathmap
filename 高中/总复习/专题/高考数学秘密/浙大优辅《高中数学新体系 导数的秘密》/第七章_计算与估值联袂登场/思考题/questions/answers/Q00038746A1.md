> > 【思考题72】解答 $24\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{3}}}}$ $= 24\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2\left(1 + \cos{\frac{\pi}{6}}\right)}}}$ $= 24\sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2 + 2\cos{\frac{\pi}{12}}}} = 24\sqrt{2 - 2\cos{\frac{\pi}{24}}}$ $= 48\sin {\frac{\pi}{48}},$ 由不等式 $\sin x <   x(x > 0)$ ，知 $48\sin {\frac{\pi}{48}} <  \pi .$
