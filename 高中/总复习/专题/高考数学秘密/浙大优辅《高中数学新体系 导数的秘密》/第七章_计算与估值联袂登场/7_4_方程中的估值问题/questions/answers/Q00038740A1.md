> [!faq]- 《高中数学新体系·导数的秘密》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 证明 构造函数 $f(x) = x + (1 - x^{2019})^{2019} - \left(1 - \frac{1}{300}\right)$ ，则
> > $$
> > f ^ {\prime} (x) = 1 - 2 0 1 9 ^ {2} (1 - x ^ {2 0 1 9}) ^ {2 0 1 8} x ^ {2 0 1 8}.
> > $$
> > 而
> > $$
> > f (0) = f (1) = \frac {1}{3 0 0} > 0,
> > $$
> > 则 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[0,1]$ 上不单调，故存在 $x_0 \in (0,1)$ 使得
> > $$
> > f ^ {\prime} (x _ {0}) = 1 - 2 0 1 9 ^ {2} (1 - x _ {0} ^ {2 0 1 9}) ^ {2 0 1 8} x _ {0} ^ {2 0 1 8} = 0.
> > $$
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \text {此} f (x _ {0}) & = x _ {0} + (1 - x _ {0} ^ {2 0 1 9}) ^ {2 0 1 9} - \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 0 0}\right) = x _ {0} + \frac {1 - x _ {0} ^ {2 0 1 9}}{2 0 1 9 ^ {2} x _ {0} ^ {2 0 1 8}} - \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 0 0}\right) \\ & = \frac {1}{2 0 1 9 ^ {2}} \Big [ \underbrace {\frac {(2 0 1 9 ^ {2} - 1) x _ {0}}{2 0 1 8} + \cdots + \frac {(2 0 1 9 ^ {2} - 1) x _ {0}}{2 0 1 8}} _ {\text {2018个}} + \frac {1}{x _ {0} ^ {2 0 1 8}} \Big ] - \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 0 0}\right) \\ & \geqslant \frac {2 0 2 0 ^ {\frac {2 0 1 8}{2 0 1 9}} - 2 0 1 9 \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 0 0}\right)}{2 0 1 9} > 0. \end{array}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\text {这是因为} \frac {2 0 1 8}{2 0 1 9} \times \ln 2 0 2 0 - \ln 2 0 1 9 - \ln \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 0 0}\right)} \\ & {= - \frac {2 0 1 8}{2 0 1 9} \times \ln \frac {2 0 1 9}{2 0 2 0} - \ln \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 0 0}\right) - \frac {1}{2 0 1 9} \times \ln 2 0 1 9} \\ & {> - \frac {2 0 1 8}{2 0 1 9} \times \left(\frac {2 0 1 9}{2 0 2 0} - 1\right) - \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 0 0} - 1\right) - \frac {1}{2 0 1 9} \times 7. 7 = \frac {2 9 3}{2 0 3 9 1 9 0 0} > 0.} \end{array}
> > $$
> > 注 本题为全国高中数学联赛(重庆)预赛题.
