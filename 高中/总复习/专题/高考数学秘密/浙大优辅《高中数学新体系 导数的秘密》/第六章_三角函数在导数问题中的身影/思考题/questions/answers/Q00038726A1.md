> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【思考题67】解答（1）对 $f(x)$ 求导，得 $f^{\prime}(x-$ $x\sin x$ ，则 $f^{\prime}\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = -\frac{\pi}{2}$ 而 $f\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = -1.$ 切线方程为 $y + 1 = -\frac{\pi}{2}\left(x - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$
> > 整理得 $\frac{\pi}{2} x + y + 1 - \frac{\pi^2}{4} = 0.$ (2)令 $f^{\prime}(x) = -x\sin x = 0$ ，其中 $x > 0$ 解得 $x = n\pi (n\in \mathbb{N}^{*})$ 当 $n = 1$ 时， $\frac{1}{x_1^2} = \frac{1}{\pi^2} < \frac{7}{4\pi^2}$ 当 $n\geqslant 2$ 时， $\frac{1}{x_n^2} = \frac{1}{n^2\pi^2} <   \frac{1}{\pi^2}\cdot \frac{1}{n^2 - 1}$ $= \frac{1}{2\pi^2}\left(\frac{1}{n - 1} -\frac{1}{n + 1}\right),$ 则 $\frac{1}{x_1^2} +\frac{1}{x_2^2} +\dots +\frac{1}{x_n^2} <   \frac{1}{\pi^2} +\frac{1}{2\pi^2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{3} +\frac{1}{2} -\frac{1}{4}\right.$ $+\dots +\frac{1}{n - 2} -\frac{1}{n} +\frac{1}{n - 1} -\frac{1}{n + 1})$ $= \frac{1}{\pi^2} +\frac{1}{2\pi^2}\left(1 + \frac{1}{2} -\frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1}\right)$ $<  \frac{1}{\pi^2} +\frac{3}{4\pi^2} = \frac{7}{4\pi^2}.$ 综上： $\frac{1}{x_1^2} +\frac{1}{x_2^2} +\dots +\frac{1}{x_n^2} <   \frac{7}{4\pi^2}.$
