> [!faq]- 《高中数学新体系·导数的秘密》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > \text {解答} & f (x) = \frac {x ^ {4} - x ^ {3} (\cos \theta + 1) - x (\sin \theta + 1) + 1}{x ^ {2}} \\ & = x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}} - \left(x + \frac {1}{x}\right) - \left(x \cos \theta + \frac {1}{x} \sin \theta\right) \\ & \geqslant x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}} - \left(x + \frac {1}{x}\right) - \sqrt {x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}} \\ & = x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}} - \sqrt {x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}} + 2} - \sqrt {x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}}. \end{array}
> > $$
> > 令 $t = \sqrt{x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}}$ ，则 $t \geqslant \sqrt{2}$ . 设
> > $$
> > g (t) = t ^ {2} - \sqrt {t ^ {2} + 2} - t,
> > $$
> > 则 $g^{\prime}(t) = 2t - 1 - \frac{t}{\sqrt{t^{2} + 2}} >2t - 1 - 1 > 0$ ，故 $g(t)$ 单调递增，
> > $$
> > g (t) _ {\min} = g (\sqrt {2}) = - \sqrt {2}.
> > $$
> > 上述不等式均可取等号,故 $m \in (-\infty, -\sqrt{2}]$ .
> > 注 本题节选自 2018—2019 学年浙江省温州九校联考高二(上)期中数学卷.
