> [!faq]- 《高中数学新体系·导数的秘密》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 令 $f(x) = a(x - x_{1})(x - x_{2})(x - x_{3})$ ，其中 $a \neq 0, x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}$ 互不相等，则
> > $$
> > f ^ {\prime} (x) = a \left[ \left(x - x _ {2}\right) \left(x - x _ {3}\right) + \left(x - x _ {1}\right) \left(x - x _ {3}\right) + \left(x - x _ {1}\right) \left(x - x _ {2}\right) \right].
> > $$
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \frac {1}{k _ {1}} + \frac {1}{k _ {2}} + \frac {1}{k _ {3}} & = \frac {1}{a} \left[ \frac {1}{(x _ {1} - x _ {2}) (x _ {1} - x _ {3})} + \frac {1}{(x _ {2} - x _ {1}) (x _ {2} - x _ {3})} + \frac {1}{(x _ {3} - x _ {1}) (x _ {3} - x _ {2})} \right] \\ & = \frac {x _ {2} - x _ {3} + x _ {3} - x _ {1} + x _ {1} - x _ {2}}{a (x _ {1} - x _ {2}) (x _ {1} - x _ {3}) (x _ {2} - x _ {3})} = 0. \end{array}
> > $$
> > 注 如果方程 $x^{3} + ax^{2} + bx + c = 0$ 有三个成等差数列的实数根, 实数 $a, b, c$ 应满足什么样的充要条件?
