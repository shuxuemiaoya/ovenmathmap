> [!faq]- 《高中数学新体系·导数的秘密》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 (1)由题意知 $f'(x) = (ax - x^2 + a - 2x)e^x \leqslant 0$ 在区间 $[2, +\infty)$ 上恒成立，即
> > $$
> > a \leqslant x + 1 - \frac {1}{x + 1}.
> > $$
> > 令 $g(x) = x + 1 - \frac{1}{x + 1}$ ，则 $g(x)$ 在区间 $[2, +\infty)$ 上单调递增， $g(x)_{\min} = g(2) = \frac{8}{3}$ ，故
> > $$
> > a \in \left[ 0, \frac {8}{3} \right].
> > $$
> > (2)由题意知 $f'(x)=(ax-x^{2}+a-2x)\mathrm{e}^{x}=0$ ，即
> > $$
> > x ^ {2} - (a - 2) x - a = 0
> > $$
> > 的两个零点为 $x_{1}, x_{2}$ . 由韦达定理知
> > $$
> > x _ {1} + x _ {2} = a - 2, x _ {1} x _ {2} = - a.
> > $$
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\text {所以} f (x _ {1}) + f (x _ {2}) = (a x _ {1} - x _ {1} ^ {2}) \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {1}} + (a x _ {2} - x _ {2} ^ {2}) \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {2}} = (2 x _ {1} - a) \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {1}} + (2 x _ {2} - a) \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {2}}} \\ & {\qquad = [ 2 x _ {1} - (x _ {1} + x _ {2} + 2) ] \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {1}} + [ 2 x _ {2} - (x _ {1} + x _ {2} + 2) ] \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {2}}} \\ & {\qquad = \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {2}} [ (x _ {1} - x _ {2} - 2) \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {1} - x _ {2}} + x _ {2} - x _ {1} - 2 ].} \end{array}
> > $$
> > 令 $t = x_{1} - x_{2}(t < 0)$ ，则
> > $$
> > (x _ {1} - x _ {2} - 2) \mathrm{e} ^ {x _ {1} - x _ {2}} + x _ {2} - x _ {1} - 2 = (t - 2) \mathrm{e} ^ {t} - t - 2,
> > $$
> > $$
> > t = - \sqrt {\left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} - 4 x _ {1} x _ {2}} = - \sqrt {(a - 2) ^ {2} - 4 (- a)} = - \sqrt {a ^ {2} + 4} \leqslant - \sqrt {\left(\frac {2 \sqrt {1 1}}{5}\right) ^ {2} + 4} = - \frac {1 2}{5},
> > $$
> > 则 $t \in \left(-\infty, -\frac{12}{5}\right]$ .
> > 构造函数
> > $$
> > g (t) = (t - 2) \mathrm{e} ^ {t} - t - 2,
> > $$
> > 则 $g'(t)=(t-1)e^{t}-1<0$ ，所以 $g(t)$ 单调递减，
> > $$
> > g (t) \geqslant g \left(- \frac {1 2}{5}\right) = \frac {2}{5} \times (1 - 1 1 \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1 2}{5}}) > \frac {2}{5} \times (1 - \mathrm{e} ^ {\frac {1 2}{5}} \times \mathrm{e} ^ {- \frac {1 2}{5}}) = 0,
> > $$
> > 即 $f(x_{1})+f(x_{2})>0.$
