> [!faq]- 《高中数学新体系·概率统计的秘密》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (I)由题意可知
> > $$
> > p _ {1} = \frac {1}{3}, q _ {1} = \frac {2}{3}.
> > $$
> > 从而
> > $$
> > p _ {2} = \frac {1}{3} p _ {1} + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} q _ {1} = \frac {7}{2 7},
> > $$
> > $$
> > q _ {2} = \frac {2}{3} p _ {1} + \left(\frac {2}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{3}\right) q _ {1} = \frac {1 6}{2 7}.
> > $$
> > (Ⅱ)由题意,根据全概率公式可知
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} p _ {n + 1} = \frac {1}{3} p _ {n} + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} q _ {n} + (1 - p _ {n} - q _ {n}) \times 0, \\ q _ {n + 1} = \frac {2}{3} p _ {n} + q _ {n} \left(\frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {2}{3}\right) + \frac {2}{3} (1 - p _ {n} - q _ {n}), \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> > 整理可得
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} p _ {n + 1} = \frac {1}{3} p _ {n} + \frac {2}{9} q _ {n}, \\ q _ {n + 1} = \frac {2}{3} - \frac {1}{9} q _ {n}. \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> > 根据题设式子结构,在第一个式子的等号两边同乘以 2 并相加可得
> > $$
> > 2 p _ {n + 1} + q _ {n + 1} = \frac {2}{3} p _ {n} + \frac {1}{3} q _ {n} + \frac {2}{3} = \frac {1}{3} (2 p _ {n} + q _ {n}) + \frac {2}{3},
> > $$
> > 则
> > $$
> > 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} = \frac {1}{3} (2 p _ {n - 1} + q _ {n - 1}) + \frac {2}{3},
> > $$
> > 其中 $n \geqslant 2$ 且 $n \in \mathbb{N}^*$ , 所以
> > $$
> > 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} - 1 = \frac {1}{3} (2 p _ {n - 1} + q _ {n - 1} - 1).
> > $$
> > 因为 $2p_{1} + q_{1} - 1 = \frac{1}{3}$ ，数列 $\{2p_n + q_n - 1\}$ 是首项为 $\frac{1}{3}$ 、公比为 $\frac{1}{3}$ 的等比数列，所以 $2p_{n} + q_{n} - 1 = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{n}$ ，即
> > $$
> > 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} = \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {n} + 1.
> > $$
> > 依题意, $X_{n}$ 的取值为0,1,2,其分布列为
> > <table><tr><td> $X_n$ </td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $1 - p_n - q_n$ </td><td> $q_n$ </td><td> $p_n$ </td></tr></table>
> > 所以
> > $$
> > E (X _ {n}) = 0 \times (1 - p _ {n} - q _ {n}) + q _ {n} + 2 p _ {n} = 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} = \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {n} + 1.
> > $$
> > 【探讨】本题是2020年江苏卷第25题,应该是受了上述例题1全国卷考题的影响,纠正了概率统计情境以及应用在构造递推式方面的不足,不是直接给出递推关系式,而是让考生运用全概率公式来求递推式,在递推式的处理上也借鉴了全国卷,给出了研究递推式的方向,为期望问题的解决奠定了基础.事实上,利用递推式求通项,可得
> > $$
> > k p _ {n + 1} + q _ {n + 1} + m = \frac {k}{3} p _ {n} + \frac {2 k - 1}{9} q _ {n} + \frac {2}{3} + m.
> > $$
> > 令
> > $$
> > k: 1: m = \frac {k}{3}: \frac {2 k - 1}{9}: \left(\frac {2}{3} + m\right),
> > $$
> > 则有 $\left\{\begin{aligned}k&=0,\\ m&=-\frac{3}{5}\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}k&=2,\\ m&=-1,\end{aligned}\right.$ 于是
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} - 1 = \frac {1}{3} (2 p _ {n - 1} + q _ {n - 1} - 1), \\ q _ {n} - \frac {3}{5} = - \frac {1}{9} \left(q _ {n - 1} - \frac {3}{5}\right), \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> > 整理得
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} - 1 = \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {n}, \\ q _ {n} - \frac {3}{5} = - \frac {3}{5} \times \left(- \frac {1}{9}\right) ^ {n}. \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> > 不难得到数列的通项为
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} p _ {n} = \frac {3}{1 0} \times \left(- \frac {1}{9}\right) ^ {n} + \frac {1}{2} \times \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {n} + \frac {1}{5}, \\ q _ {n} = \frac {1}{1 5} \times \left(- \frac {1}{9}\right) ^ {n - 1} + \frac {3}{5}. \end{array} \right.
> > $$
