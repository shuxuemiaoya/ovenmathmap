>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 题 2-8 【解析】已知 $(2-\sqrt{x})^{n}$ 的展开式中 x 的系数为 $f(n)=\mathrm{C}_{n}^{2}\times2^{n-2}$ ，则
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & \frac {2}{f (2)} + \frac {2 ^ {2}}{f (3)} + \frac {2 ^ {3}}{f (4)} + \dots + \frac {2 ^ {2 0 1 9}}{f (2 0 2 0)} \\ & = \frac {2}{C _ {2} ^ {2}} + \frac {2 ^ {2}}{C _ {3} ^ {2} \times 2} + \frac {2 ^ {3}}{C _ {4} ^ {2} \times 2 ^ {2}} + \dots + \frac {2 ^ {2 0 1 9}}{C _ {2 0 2 0} ^ {2} \times 2 ^ {2 0 1 8}} \\ & = \frac {2}{C _ {2} ^ {2}} + \frac {2}{C _ {3} ^ {2}} + \frac {2}{C _ {4} ^ {2}} + \dots + \frac {2}{C _ {2 0 2 0} ^ {2}} \end{array}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} = \frac {2}{\frac {2 \times 1}{2}} + \frac {2}{\frac {3 \times 2}{2}} + \frac {2}{\frac {4 \times 3}{2}} + \dots + \frac {2}{\frac {2 0 0 0 \times 2 0 1 9}{2}} \\ = \frac {4}{2 \times 1} + \frac {4}{3 \times 2} + \frac {4}{4 \times 3} + \dots + \frac {4}{2 0 2 0 \times 2 0 1 9} \\ = 4 \times \left(\frac {1}{1} - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{4} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 0 1 9} - \frac {1}{2 0 2 0}\right) \\ = 4 \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2 0 2 0}\right) = \frac {2 0 1 9}{5 0 5}. \end{array}
> > $$
> > 故选B.
> > 【探讨】先对通项进行研究方便我们判断下一步的解决方法， $\frac{2^{n-1}}{f(n)}=\frac{2^{n-1}}{C_{n}^{2}\times2^{n-2}}=\frac{2}{C_{n}^{2}}=\frac{4}{n(n-1)}=4\times\left(\frac{1}{n-1}-\frac{1}{n}\right)$ .
