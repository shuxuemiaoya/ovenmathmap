> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {3}{4} = \frac {1}{4},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {3}{4} + \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {3}{4} + \frac {1}{2} \times
> > $$
> > $$
> > \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{4} = \frac {1 1}{2 4},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{4} + \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{4} + \frac {1}{2} \times
> > $$
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {3}{4} = \frac {1}{4},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{4} = \frac {1}{2 4}.
> > $$
> > 所以 $X$ 的分布列为
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{4}$ </td><td> $\frac{11}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{4}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{24}$ </td></tr></table>
> > 所以 $X$ 的数学期望 $E(X) = 0 \times \frac{1}{4} + 1 \times \frac{11}{24} + 2 \times \frac{1}{4} + 3 \times \frac{1}{24} = \frac{13}{12}$ .

> > $$
> > P (Y + Z = 1) = P (Y = 0, Z = 1) + P (Y = 1,
> > $$
> > $$
> > Z = 0) = P (Y = 0) P (Z = 1) + P (Y = 1) P (Z = 0)
> > $$
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \times \frac {1 1}{2 4} + \frac {1 1}{2 4} \times \frac {1}{4} = \frac {1 1}{4 8}.
