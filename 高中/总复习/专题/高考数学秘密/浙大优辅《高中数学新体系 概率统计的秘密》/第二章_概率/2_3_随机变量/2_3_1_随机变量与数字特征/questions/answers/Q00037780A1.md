> [!faq]- 《高中数学新体系·概率统计的秘密》参考答案
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 题 2
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (Ⅰ)记“该甲、乙、丙三项新技术被攻克”分别为事件 A, B, C,
> > 则 $P(A)=\frac{3}{5}, P(B)=\frac{2}{3}, P(C)=\frac{1}{2}.$
> > 该科研团队获得 60 万元科研经费的概率为 $P(A\overline{B}\overline{C})+P(\overline{A}BC)=\frac{3}{5}\times\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{1}{2}+\frac{2}{5}\times\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{1}{2}=$ $\frac{1}{10}+\frac{2}{15}=\frac{7}{30}.$
> > （Ⅱ）X 所有可能的取值为 0,20,40,60,80,100,120,则
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = P (\overline {{{A}}} \overline {{{B}}} \overline {{{C}}}) = \frac {2}{5} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{1 5},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 2 0) = P (\overline {{{A}}} \overline {{{B}}} C) = \frac {2}{5} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{1 5},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 4 0) = P (\overline {{{A}}} B \overline {{{C}}}) = \frac {2}{5} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {2}{1 5},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 6 0) = \frac {7}{3 0},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 8 0) = P (A \overline {{{B}}} C) = \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{1 0},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 1 0 0) = P (A B \overline {{{C}}}) = \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{5},
> > $$
> > $$
> > P (X = 1 2 0) = P (A B C) = \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{5}.
> > $$
> > 所以 $X$ 的分布列为
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>20</td><td>40</td><td>60</td><td>80</td><td>100</td><td>120</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{15}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{15}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{15}$ </td><td> $\frac{7}{30}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{10}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{5}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{5}$ </td></tr></table>
> > 所以 $E(X)=0\times\frac{1}{15}+20\times\frac{1}{15}+40\times\frac{2}{15}+60\times$ $\frac{7}{30}+80\times\frac{1}{10}+100\times\frac{1}{5}+120\times\frac{1}{5}=\frac{218}{3}$ （万元）.
