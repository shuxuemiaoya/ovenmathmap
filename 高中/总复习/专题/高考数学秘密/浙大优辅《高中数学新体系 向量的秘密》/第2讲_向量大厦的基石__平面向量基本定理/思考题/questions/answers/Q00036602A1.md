> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 代数法
> > 由 $|a+b|=3,|a-b|=2$ 得
> > $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a^{2} + 2a \cdot b + b^{2} = 9, \\ a^{2} - 2a \cdot b + b^{2} = 4, \end{array} \right.$ 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a^{2} + b^{2} = \frac{13}{2}, \\ a \cdot b = \frac{5}{4}. \end{array} \right.$
> > 所以 $\frac{|a|}{a\cdot b} = \frac{4|a|}{5}$
> > 转化为关于 $|a|$ 的函数, 那么 $|a|$ 的取值范围怎么得到呢?
> > $$
> > (a \cdot b) ^ {2} = | a | ^ {2} | b | ^ {2} \cos^ {2} \theta = \frac {2 5}{1 6} \leqslant
> > $$
> > $$
> > \mid \boldsymbol {a} \mid^ {2} \mid \boldsymbol {b} \mid^ {2} = \mid \boldsymbol {a} \mid^ {2} \left(\frac {1 3}{2} - \mid \boldsymbol {a} \mid^ {2}\right)
> > $$
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{2} \leqslant | a | \leqslant \frac {5}{2}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \frac {| \boldsymbol {a} |}{\boldsymbol {a} \cdot \boldsymbol {b}} = \frac {4 | \boldsymbol {a} |}{5} \in \left[ \frac {2}{5}, 2 \right].
> > $$
> > $$
> > (a + b) + (a - b) = 2 a
> > $$
> > $$
> > \left| \left| a + b \right| - \left| a - b \right| \right| \leqslant | 2 a | \leqslant
> > $$
> > $$
> > \vert a + b \vert + \vert a - b \vert
> > $$
> > $$
> > 1 \leqslant | 2 a | \leqslant 5
> > $$
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{2} \leqslant | a | \leqslant \frac {5}{2}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \frac {| \boldsymbol {a} |}{\boldsymbol {a} \cdot \boldsymbol {b}} = \frac {4 | \boldsymbol {a} |}{5} \in \left[ \frac {2}{5}, 2 \right].
> > $$
