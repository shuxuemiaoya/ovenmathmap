> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 已知 $|c| = 2$ 为定值，设 $\overrightarrow{OA} = a, \overrightarrow{OB} = b, \overrightarrow{OC} = c = (2, 0)$ ,
> > 结合 $a \cdot c = 2, b \cdot c = 6$ ，可设 $A(1, m), B(3, n)$ ，则 $a \cdot b = 3 + mn = 2$ ，得 $mn = -1$ . $\cos \theta = \frac{2}{\sqrt{1 + m^2} \sqrt{9 + n^2}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{1 + m^2} \sqrt{9 + \frac{1}{m^2}}} =$ $\frac{2}{\sqrt{10 + 9m^2 + \frac{1}{m^2}}} \leqslant \frac{1}{2},$ 故 $\theta \in \left[\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ .
