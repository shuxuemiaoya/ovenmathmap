8. 解答 $(c - a) \cdot (c - b) = -8 \Leftrightarrow c^2 - (a + b) \cdot c + 8 = 0.$ 配方: 可得 $\left(c - \frac{a + b}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{(a + b)^2}{4} - 8.$ 因为 $a \perp b, |a - b| = 6$ , 所以 $|a + b| = 6$ , 故 $\left|c - \frac{a + b}{2}\right| = 1.$ 换元: $m = c - \frac{a + b}{2}, |m| = 1.$ 目标: $c \cdot (a + b) = \left(m + \frac{a + b}{2}\right) \cdot (a + b) = m \cdot (a + b) + 18 \in [12, 24]$ .
