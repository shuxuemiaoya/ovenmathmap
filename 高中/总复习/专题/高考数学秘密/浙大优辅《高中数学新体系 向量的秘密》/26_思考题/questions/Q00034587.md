5. 解答 (1) 记 $\overrightarrow{AB} = a, \overrightarrow{AC} = b, \overrightarrow{AD} = c$ ,

则 $|a|=3,|b|=3\sqrt{2},|c|=3.$

由 $\sqrt{3} = |\overrightarrow{BC}| = |a - b| = \sqrt{9 + 18 - 2a\cdot b}$ ，得 $a\cdot b = 12.$

$$
\text {又} \overrightarrow {C F} = \overrightarrow {C A} + \overrightarrow {A D} + \overrightarrow {D F} = - \frac {1}{2} b + c,
$$

$$
\frac {\sqrt {6}}{2} = | \overrightarrow {C F} | = \sqrt {\frac {9}{2} + 9 - \boldsymbol {b} \cdot \boldsymbol {c}}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol {b} \cdot \boldsymbol {c} = 1 2
$$

$$
\overrightarrow {B D} = \overrightarrow {B A} + \overrightarrow {A D} = - a + c.
$$

所以 $\overrightarrow{CA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = b \cdot (-a + c) = b \cdot c - a \cdot b = 0$ 故 $CA \perp BD$ .

(2)设平面 ABC 的法向量为 n，由 $ACFD \perp$ 平面 ABC 可知， $n \parallel$ 平面 ACDF，所以 $n = xb + yc$ .

由 $n \perp a, n \perp b$ ，可得 $n \cdot a = xa \cdot b + ya \cdot c = 12x + ya \cdot c = 0$ , $n \cdot b = xb^2 + yb \cdot c = 18x + 12y = 0$ .

取 $x = 2$ ，则 $y = -3, n = 2b - 3c, a \cdot c = 8$ ， $|n| = \sqrt{(2b - 3c)^2} = 3$ .

另一方面， $\overrightarrow{AE} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{DE} = \frac{1}{2}a + c$ ，

所以 $|\overrightarrow{AE}| = \left|\frac{1}{2}a + c\right| = \frac{\sqrt{77}}{2}$ .

设直线 $AE$ 与平面 $ABC$ 所成的角为 $\theta$ ，

则 $\sin \theta = \frac{|n \cdot \overrightarrow{AE}|}{|n||\overrightarrow{AE}|} = \frac{|n \cdot \left(\frac{1}{2}a + c\right)|}{|n||\frac{1}{2}a + c|}$ $= \frac{|n \cdot c|}{|n||\frac{1}{2}a + c|} = \frac{|(2b - 3c) \cdot c|}{|n||\frac{1}{2}a + c|} = \frac{|24 - 27|}{3 \times \frac{\sqrt{77}}{2}}$ $= \frac{2\sqrt{77}}{77}$ .
