> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 解法一: 错位相减法, 略.
> > 解法二: (阿贝尔变换法)
> > 令 $a_{n}=n, b_{n}=q^{n-1}$ , $S_{n}=b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n}=\frac{1-q^{n}}{1-q}$ 则 $T_{n}=1+2q+\cdots+nq^{n-1}$ $=a_{1}b_{1}+a_{2}b_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}b_{n}$ $=a_{1}S_{1}+a_{2}(S_{2}-S_{1})+\cdots+a_{n}(S_{n}-S_{n-1})$ $=S_{1}(a_{1}-a_{2})+S_{2}(a_{2}-a_{3})+\cdots+S_{n-1}(a_{n-1}-a_{n})$ $+S_{n}a_{n}$ $=-\left(1+\frac{1-q^{2}}{1-q}+\cdots+\frac{1-q^{n-1}}{1-q}\right)+n\frac{1-q^{n}}{1-q}$ $=\left(\frac{q}{1-q}+\frac{q^{2}}{1-q}+\cdots+\frac{q^{n-1}}{1-q}\right)+\frac{n-1}{q-1}+n\frac{q^{n}-1}{q-1}$ $=\frac{q-q^{n}}{(q-1)^{2}}+\frac{n-1}{q-1}+n\frac{q^{n}-1}{q-1}$ $=\frac{1-q^{n}}{(1-q)^{2}}-\frac{nq^{n}}{1-q}.$
