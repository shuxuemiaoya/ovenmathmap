> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 (1) 由题意知 $a_{n} = \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{n}, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ . 因为 $b_{n} = 3\log_{\frac{1}{4}} a_{n} - 2, b_{1} = 3\log_{\frac{1}{4}} a_{1} - 2 = 1$ , 所以 $b_{n+1} - b_{n} = 3\log_{\frac{1}{4}} a_{n+1} - 3\log_{\frac{1}{4}} a_{n}$ $= 3\log_{\frac{1}{4}} \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = 3\log_{\frac{1}{4}} q = 3,$
> > $$
> > b _ {1} = 1
> > $$
> > $$
> > b _ {n} = 3 n - 2
> > $$
> > $$
> > c _ {n} = (3 n - 2) \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n}, n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*}
> > $$
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = 1 \times \frac {1}{4} + 4 \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {2} + 7 \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {3} + \dots +
> > $$
> > $$
> > (3 n - 5) \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n - 1} + (3 n - 2) \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{4} S _ {n} = 1 \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {2} + 4 \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {3} + 7 \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {4} +
> > $$
> > $$
> > \dots + (3 n - 5) \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n} + (3 n - 2) \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n + 1},
> > $$
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \frac {3}{4} S _ {n} & = \frac {1}{4} + 3 \times \left[ \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {3} + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n - 1} \right. \\ & \quad \left. + \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n} \right] - (3 n - 2) \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n + 1} \\ & = - \frac {1}{2} + 3 \left[ \frac {1}{4} + \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {3} + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n - 1} \right. \\ & \quad \left. + \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n} \right] - (3 n - 2) \times \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n + 1} \\ & = \frac {1}{2} - (3 n + 2) \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n + 1}, \end{array}
> > $$
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = \frac {2}{3} - \frac {1 2 n + 8}{3} \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {n + 1}
> > $$
> > (3) 因为 $c_{n+1}-c_{n}=(3n+1)\times\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{n+1}-(3n-2)\times\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{n}=9(1-n)\times\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{n+1}, n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}$ .
> > 所以，当 n=1 时， $c_{2}=c_{1}=\frac{1}{4}$ 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时， $c_{n+1} < c_{n}$ ，
> > 即 $c_{1}=c_{2}>c_{3}>c_{4}>\cdots>c_{n}$ .
> > 所以，当 n=1 或 2 时， $c_{n}$ 取最大值 $\frac{1}{4}$ .
> > 又 $c_{n} \leqslant \frac{1}{4} m^{2} + m - 1$ 对一切正整数 n 恒成立，
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{4}m^{2}+m-1\geqslant\frac{1}{4}$ ,
> > 即 $m^{2}+4m-5\geqslant0$ ，得 $m\geqslant1$ 或 $m\leqslant-5$ .
