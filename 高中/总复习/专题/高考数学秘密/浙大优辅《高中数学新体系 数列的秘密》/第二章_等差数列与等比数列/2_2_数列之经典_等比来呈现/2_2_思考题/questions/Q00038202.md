C. $a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+\cdots+a_{n}=\frac{a_{1}(1-q^{n})}{1-q}$ D. $1+2+2^{2}+2^{3}+\cdots+2^{n}=\frac{1\times(1-2^{n+1})}{1-2}$
