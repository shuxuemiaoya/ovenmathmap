6. 设数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足 $a_{1} = a, a_{n+1}a_{n} - a_{n}^{2} = 1 (n \in \mathbb{N}^{*})$ .

(1) 若 $a_3 = \frac{5}{2}$ , 求实数 $a$ 的值.

(2) 设 $b_n = \frac{a_n}{\sqrt{n}}$ , 若 $a = 1$ , 证明: $\sqrt{2} \leqslant b_n < \frac{3}{2} (n \geqslant 2)$ .
