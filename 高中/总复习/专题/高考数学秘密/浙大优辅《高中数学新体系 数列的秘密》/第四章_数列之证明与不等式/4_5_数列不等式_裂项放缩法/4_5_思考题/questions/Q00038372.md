7. 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足 $a_{1} = 1, a_{n+1} = \frac{n^{2}a_{n}}{n^{2} + 1} (n \in \mathbb{N}^{*})$ . 证明：

(1) $a_{n+1}<a_{n}.$

(2) $\frac{a_{1}}{a_{2}}+\frac{a_{2}}{a_{3}}+\cdots+\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n+1}}\leqslant n+2-\frac{1}{n}.$

(3) $a_{n}>\frac{1}{4}.$
