> [!faq]- 《高中数学新体系·数列的秘密》参考答案
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 (1) 因为 $a_{2} = 4, a_{3} = 17, a_{4} = 72$ , 所以 $b_{1} = 4, b_{2} = \frac{17}{4}, b_{3} = \frac{72}{17}$ . (2) 由 $a_{n+2} = 4a_{n+1} + a_{n}$ 得 $\frac{a_{n+2}}{a_{a+1}} = 4 + \frac{a_{n}}{a_{n+1}}$ , 即 $b_{n+1} = 4 + \frac{1}{b_n}$ . 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, $b_{n} > 4$ , 于是 $c_{1} = b_{1}b_{2} = 17$ , $c_{n} = b_{n}b_{n+1} = 4b_{n} + 1 > 17 (n \geqslant 2)$ , 所以 $S_{n} = c_{1} + c_{2} + \cdots + c_{n} \geqslant 17n$ . (3) 当 $n = 1$ 时, 结论 $|b_{2} - b_{1}| = \frac{1}{4} < \frac{17}{64}$ 成立. 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, 有 $|b_{n+1} - b_{n}| = \left|4 + \frac{1}{b_n} - 4 - \frac{1}{b_{n-1}}\right| = \left|\frac{b_n - b_{n-1}}{b_nb_{n-1}}\right| \leqslant \frac{1}{17}|b_n - b_{n-1}|$ $\leqslant \frac{1}{17^2}|b_{n-1} - b_{n-2}| \leqslant \cdots \leqslant \frac{1}{17^{n-1}}|b_2 - b_1| = \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{1}{17^{n-1}}$ . 所以 $|b_{2n} - b_n| \leqslant |b_{n+1} - b_n| + |b_{n+2} - b_{b+1}| + \cdots + |b_{2n} - b_{2n-1}|$ $\leqslant \frac{1}{4} \times \left[\left(\frac{1}{17}\right)^{n-1} + \left(\frac{1}{17}\right)^n + \cdots + \left(\frac{1}{17}\right)^{2n-2}\right]$ $= \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{\left(\frac{1}{17}\right)^{n-1} \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{17^n}\right)}{1 - \frac{1}{17}} < \frac{1}{64} \times \frac{1}{17^{n-2}} (n \in \mathbb{N}^*)$ .
