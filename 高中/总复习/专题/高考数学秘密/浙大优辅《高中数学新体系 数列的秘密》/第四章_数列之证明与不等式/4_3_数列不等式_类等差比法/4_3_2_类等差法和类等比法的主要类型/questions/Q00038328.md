例5 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足 $a_1 = 1, a_n = \frac{1}{a_{n+1}} - \frac{1}{2}, n \in \mathbb{N}^*$ . 证明：

(1) $\frac{2}{3}\leqslant a_{n}\leqslant1;$

(2) $\left|a_{n+1}-a_{n}\right|\leqslant\frac{1}{3};$

(3) $\left|a_{2n}-a_{n}\right|\leqslant\frac{10}{27}.$
