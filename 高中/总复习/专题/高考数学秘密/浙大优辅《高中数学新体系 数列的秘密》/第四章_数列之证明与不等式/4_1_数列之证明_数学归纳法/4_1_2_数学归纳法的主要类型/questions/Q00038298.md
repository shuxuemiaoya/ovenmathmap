例 5 设 $x_{1}, x_{2}, \cdots, x_{n}$ 均为非负实数，证明：若 $x_{1} + x_{2} + \cdots + x_{n} \leqslant \frac{1}{2}$ ，则均有 $(1 - x_{1}) \cdot (1 - x_{2}) \cdots (1 - x_{n}) \geqslant \frac{1}{2}$ .
