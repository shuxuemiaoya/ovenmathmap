(1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项 $a_{n}$ .

(2) 证明: $\frac{1}{2S_1} + \frac{1}{3S_2} + \cdots + \frac{1}{(n+1)S_n} < 2\left(1 - \frac{1}{S_{n+1}}\right)$ .
