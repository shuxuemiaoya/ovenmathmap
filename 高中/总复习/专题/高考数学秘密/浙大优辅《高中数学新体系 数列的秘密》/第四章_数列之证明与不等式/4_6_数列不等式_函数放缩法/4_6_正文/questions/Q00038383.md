例 9 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}, a_{n} \geqslant 0, a_{1} = 0, a_{n+1}^{2} + a_{n+1} - 1 = a_{n}^{2} (n \in \mathbb{N}^{*})$ . 记 $S_{n} = a_{1} + a_{2} + \cdots + a_{n}$ , $T_{n} = \frac{1}{1 + a_{1}} + \frac{1}{(1 + a_{1})(1 + a_{2})} + \cdots + \frac{1}{(1 + a_{1})(1 + a_{2}) \cdots (1 + a_{n})}$ . 证明：

(1) $a_{n}<a_{n+1}$ ;

(2) $S_{n}>n-2;$

(3) $T_{n}<3.$
