例 12 证明：

$$
(1) 1 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {2} + \dots + n ^ {2} = \frac {n (n + 1) (2 n + 1)}{6};
$$

$$
(2) 1 ^ {3} + 2 ^ {3} + \dots + n ^ {3} = \left[ \frac {n (n + 1)}{2} \right] ^ {2}.
$$
