> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析

> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 (1) 由原式变形得 $a_{n+1}=\frac{2(t^{n+1}-1)(a_{n}+1)}{a_{n}+2t^{n}-1}-1,$ 则 $\frac{a_{n+1}+1}{t^{n+1}-1}=\frac{2(a_{n}+1)}{a_{n}+2t^{n}-1}=\frac{\frac{2(a_{n}+1)}{t^{n}-1}}{\frac{a_{n}+1}{t^{n}-1}+2}.$ 记 $\frac{a_{n}+1}{t^{n}-1}=b$ ，则 $b_{n+1}=\frac{2b_{n}}{b_{n}+2}$ $b_{1}=\frac{a_{1}+1}{t-1}=\frac{2t-2}{t-1}=2.$ 又 $\frac{1}{b_{n+1}}=\frac{1}{b_n}+\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{b_1}=\frac{1}{2},$ 从而有 $\frac{1}{b_n}=\frac{1}{b_1}+\frac{1}{2}(n-1)=\frac{n}{2},$ 故 $\frac{a_{n}+1}{t^{n}-1}=\frac{2}{n}$ ，于是有 $a_{n}=\frac{2(t^{n}-1)}{n}-1.$ (2) $a_{n+1}-a_{n}=\frac{2(t^{n+1}-1)}{n+1}-\frac{2(t^{n}-1)}{n}$ $=\frac{2(t-1)}{n(n+1)}[n(1+t+\cdots+t^{n-1}+t^{n})-(n+1)(1+t+\cdots+t^{n-1})]$ $=\frac{2(t-1)}{n(n+1)}[nt^{n}-(1+t+\cdots+t^{n-1})]$ $=\frac{2(t-1)}{n(n+1)}[(t^{n}-1)+(t^{n}-t)+\cdots+(t^{n}-t^{n-1})]$ $=\frac{2(t-1)^{2}}{n(n+1)}[(t^{n-1}+t^{n-2}+\cdots+1)+t(t^{n-2}+t^{n-3}+\cdots+1)+\cdots+t^{n-1}]$ 显然，当 $t>0(t\neq1)$ 时，恒有 $a_{n+1}-a_{n}>0$
> > $$
> > a _ {n + 1} > a _ {n}
> > $$
