> [!faq]- 《浙大优辅《高中数学新体系 立体几何的秘密》》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 由已知得
> > $$
> > \boldsymbol {a} \cdot \boldsymbol {c} = | \boldsymbol {a} |, \boldsymbol {b} \cdot \boldsymbol {c} = 2 | \boldsymbol {b} |, | \boldsymbol {a} + \boldsymbol {b} | = \sqrt {| \boldsymbol {a} | ^ {2} + | \boldsymbol {b} | ^ {2}},
> > $$
> > 所以
> > $$
> > \cos \theta = \frac {| \textbf {a} | + 2 | \textbf {b} |}{\sqrt {1 0} \sqrt {| \textbf {a} | ^ {2} + | \textbf {b} | ^ {2}}} \leqslant \frac {\sqrt {(1 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {2}) (| \textbf {a} | ^ {2} + | \textbf {b} | ^ {2})}}{\sqrt {1 0} \sqrt {| \textbf {a} | ^ {2} + | \textbf {b} | ^ {2}}} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} (\text {柯西不等式}).
> > $$
> > 由此得 c 与 $a + b$ 的夹角的最小值为 $45^{\circ}$ .
