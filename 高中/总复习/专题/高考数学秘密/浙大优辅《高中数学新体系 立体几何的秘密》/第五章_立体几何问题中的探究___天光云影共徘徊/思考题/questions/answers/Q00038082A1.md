>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析

> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 作 $G_{1}A \perp NH, G_{1}B \perp FH \Rightarrow \alpha = \angle G_{1}AG, \beta = \pi - \angle G_{1}BG \Rightarrow \tan \alpha - \tan \beta = \tan \angle G_{1}AG + \tan \angle G_{1}BG = \frac{2}{G_{1}A} + \frac{2}{G_{1}B}$ .
> > 如答图， $\mathrm{Rt}\triangle NAG_{1} \cong \mathrm{Rt}\triangle FBG_{1}$ ，
> > 则 $AG_{1} = BG_{1}$ .
> > 原式 $= \frac{4}{G_1A}\in (4, + \infty),G_1A\in (0,1),$
> > 从而 $\tan\alpha-\tan\beta$ 的取值范围是 $(4,+\infty)$ .
