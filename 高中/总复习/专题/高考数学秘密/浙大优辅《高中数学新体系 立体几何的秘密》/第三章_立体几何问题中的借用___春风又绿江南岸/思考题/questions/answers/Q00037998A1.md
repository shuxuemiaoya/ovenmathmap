> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答 菱形边长为2,过点E作EH垂直BD于点H, $\overrightarrow{CF} \cdot \overrightarrow{BE} = \overrightarrow{CF} \cdot (\overrightarrow{BH} + \overrightarrow{HE}) = \overrightarrow{CF} \cdot \overrightarrow{HE} = -\frac{1}{2},$ 所以 $\cos\langle\overrightarrow{CF},\overrightarrow{BE}\rangle=-\frac{1}{6},$ 故所求的正弦值为 $\frac{\sqrt{35}}{6}$ ，选A.
