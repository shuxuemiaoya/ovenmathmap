> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 ▶ 函数 $f(x)$ 的图像如图所示, 易知 $x_{2} + x_{3} = 4, x_{2} \in (0, 2)$ , 所以 $y = \frac{x_{2} f(x_{1})}{x_{2} + x_{3}} = \frac{x_{2} f(x_{2})}{4} = \frac{1}{4} x_{2}(2 - x_{2})$ .
> > ![](../../../../images/combined/part-001/2ea35d73e2add49dbde333100292217f31be09f3265ffd4f558cb27d4a38d4f4.jpg)
> > ![](../../../../images/combined/part-001/b9fe72018f8903fc88d8878472f8a096bfe4115622adacf242773964ba80f9eb.jpg)
> > 当 $x = 1$ 时， $y_{\max} = \frac{1}{4}$ . 故 $y \in \left(0, \frac{1}{4}\right]$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
