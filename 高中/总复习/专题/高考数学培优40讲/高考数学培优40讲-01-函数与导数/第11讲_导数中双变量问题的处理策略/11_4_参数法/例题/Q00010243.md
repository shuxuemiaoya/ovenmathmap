例 11.7 设 $x_{1}, x_{2}$ 是方程 $x - \ln x = a$ 的两个不同的根，求证：
(1) $x_{1}x_{2}<1;$ (2) $x_{1}+x_{2}>2;$ (3) $2x_{1}+x_{2}>e;$ (4) $a+1<x_{1}+x_{2}<2a;$ (5) $x_{1}x_{2}>e^{1-a};$ (6) $x_{1}+x_{2}<\frac{4a+2}{3};$ (7) $2e^{-a-1}<\frac{1}{x_{1}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}}<e^{a};$ (8) $e^{\frac{a}{3}}<\frac{1}{\sqrt{x_{1}}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{x_{2}}}<2e^{\frac{a}{3}}.$
