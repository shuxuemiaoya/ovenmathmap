> [!faq]- 《高考数学培优40讲》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 $\triangleright u = a + ab + abc = a(1 + b + bc)$ , 又 $b + bc = b(1 + c) \leqslant \left(\frac{b + 1 + c}{2}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{4 - a}{2}\right)^2 = \left(2 - \frac{a}{2}\right)^2$ , 所以 $u \leqslant a\left(1 + 4 - 2a + \frac{a^2}{4}\right) = \frac{a^3}{4} - 2a^2 + 5a = f(a), f'(a) = \frac{3}{4}a^2 - 4a + 5 = \frac{3}{4}(a - 2) \cdot (a - \frac{10}{3})$ , 依题意 $a \leqslant 3$ , 所以 $f(a)_{\max} = f(2) = 4 \Rightarrow u \leqslant 4$ , 当 $a = 2, b = 1, c = 0$ 时取到等号,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
