> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 原式 $= \cos \frac{\pi}{11} +\cos \frac{3\pi}{11} +\cos \frac{5\pi}{11} +\cos \frac{7\pi}{11} +\cos \frac{9\pi}{11}$ $= \frac{2\sin\frac{\pi}{11}\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{11} + \cos\frac{3\pi}{11} + \cos\frac{5\pi}{11} + \cos\frac{7\pi}{11} + \cos\frac{9\pi}{11}\right)}{2\sin\frac{\pi}{11}}$ $= \frac{\left(\sin\frac{2\pi}{11} - \sin 0\right) + \left(\sin\frac{4\pi}{11} - \sin\frac{2\pi}{11}\right) + \cdots +\left(\sin\frac{10\pi}{11} - \sin\frac{8\pi}{11}\right)}{2\sin\frac{\pi}{11}} = \frac{\sin\frac{10\pi}{11}}{2\sin\frac{\pi}{11}} = \frac{1}{2}.$
> > $$
