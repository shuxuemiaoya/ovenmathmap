> [!faq]- 《高考数学培优40讲》参考答案
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解法一:由已知得 $\sin \alpha \cos \beta + \cos \alpha \sin \beta + \cos \alpha \cos \beta - \sin \alpha \sin \beta = 2 (\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha) \sin \beta$ , 即 $\sin \alpha \cos \beta - \cos \alpha \sin \beta + \cos \alpha \cos \beta + \sin \alpha \sin \beta = 0, \sin (\alpha - \beta) + \cos (\alpha - \beta) = 0$ , 所以 $\tan (\alpha - \beta) = -1$ . 故选 D.
> > 解法二（从角的关系入手）： $\sin (\alpha +\beta) + \cos (\alpha +\beta) = 2\sqrt{2}\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\sin \beta$ ，得 $\sqrt{2}\sin \left(\alpha +\beta +\frac{\pi}{4}\right) =$ $2\sqrt{2}\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\sin \beta$ ，即 $\sin \left[\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right) + \beta \right] = 2\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\sin \beta ,\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\cos \beta = \cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\sin \beta$ ，故 $\tan \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \tan \beta$ ，所以 $\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4} = \beta +k\pi (k\in \mathbf{Z})$ ，即 $\alpha -\beta = k\pi -\frac{\pi}{4}(k\in \mathbf{Z})$ ，得 $\tan (\alpha -\beta) = \tan \left(k\pi -\frac{\pi}{4}\right) =$ -1.
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
