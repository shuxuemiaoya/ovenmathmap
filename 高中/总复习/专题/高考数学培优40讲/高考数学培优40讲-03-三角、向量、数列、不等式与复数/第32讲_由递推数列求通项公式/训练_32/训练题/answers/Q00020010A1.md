> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 其特征方程为 $4x^{2}=4x-1$ ，解得 $x_{1}=x_{2}=\frac{1}{2}$ ，令 $a_{n}=(c_{1}+nc_{2})\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n}$ ，由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=(c_{1}+c_{2})\times\frac{1}{2}=1\\ a_{2}&=(c_{1}+2c_{2})\times\frac{1}{4}=2\end{aligned}\right.$ 得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}c_{1}&=-4\\ c_{2}&=6\end{aligned}\right.$ ，所以 $a_{n}=\frac{3n-2}{2^{n-1}}(n\in\mathbb{N}^{*})$ .
