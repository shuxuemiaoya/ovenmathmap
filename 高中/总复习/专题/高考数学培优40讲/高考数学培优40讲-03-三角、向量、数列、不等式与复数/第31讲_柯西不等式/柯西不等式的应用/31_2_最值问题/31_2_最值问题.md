## 31.2 最值问题

## 1. 处理整式结构

研究密钥

对于 $ax + by + cz$ 形式求最值, $ax + by + cz$ 可以理解为不同的乘积之和:

(1) $(ax + by + cz)^2 \leqslant (a^2 + b^2 + c^2)(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)$ .

$$
(2) (a x + b y + c z) ^ {2} \leqslant (1 ^ {2} + 1 ^ {2} + 1 ^ {2}) [ (a x) ^ {2} + (b y) ^ {2} + (c z) ^ {2} ].
$$

$$
(3) (a x + b y + c z) ^ {2} \leqslant (\sqrt {a} ^ {2} + \sqrt {b} ^ {2} + \sqrt {c} ^ {2}) [ (\sqrt {a} x) ^ {2} + (\sqrt {b} y) ^ {2} + (\sqrt {c} z) ^ {2} ].
$$

$$
(4) (a x + b y + c z) ^ {2} \leqslant [ (a ^ {2}) ^ {2} + (b ^ {2}) ^ {2} + (c ^ {2}) ^ {2} ] \left[ \left(\frac {x}{a}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {y}{b}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {z}{c}\right) ^ {2} \right].
$$

关键在于乘积的分解方法,需要满足不等号右边含未知项的平方和公式为常数.
