>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 证明：由柯西不等式易得 $\left[\sum_{k=1}^{n-2}(a_k^2 + a_{k+1}^2 + a_{k+2}^2)\right]\left[\sum_{k=1}^{n-2}(a_k^2 + a_{k+1}^2 + a_{k+2}^2)^{-1}\right] \geqslant (n-2)^2$ ，则 $\sum_{k=1}^{n-2}(a_k^2 + a_{k+1}^2 + a_{k+2}^2)^{-1} \geqslant \frac{(n-2)^2}{3\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_k^2 - 2(a_1^2 + a_n^2) - (a_2^2 + a_{n-1}^2)} > (n-2)^2 \left(3\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_k^2\right)^{-1} = \frac{2(n-2)^2}{n(n+1)(2n+1)}.$
