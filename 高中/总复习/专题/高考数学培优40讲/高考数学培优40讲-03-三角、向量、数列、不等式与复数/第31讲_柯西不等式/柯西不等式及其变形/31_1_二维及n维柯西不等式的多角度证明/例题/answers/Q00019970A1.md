> [!faq]- 《高考数学培优40讲》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\text {解析}} \\ & {\quad \left(a _ {1} + a _ {2} + \dots + a _ {n}\right) \left(\frac {b _ {1} ^ {2}}{a _ {1}} + \frac {b _ {2} ^ {2}}{a _ {2}} + \dots + \frac {b _ {n} ^ {2}}{a _ {n}}\right)} \\ & {\qquad = \left[ (\sqrt {a _ {1}}) ^ {2} + (\sqrt {a _ {2}}) ^ {2} + \dots + (\sqrt {a _ {n}}) ^ {2} \right] \left[ \left(\frac {b _ {1}}{\sqrt {a _ {1}}}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {b _ {2}}{\sqrt {a _ {2}}}\right) ^ {2} + \dots + \left(\frac {b _ {n}}{\sqrt {a _ {n}}}\right) ^ {2} \right]} \end{array}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \geqslant \left(\sqrt {a _ {1}} \cdot \frac {b _ {1}}{\sqrt {a _ {1}}} + \sqrt {a _ {2}} \cdot \frac {b _ {2}}{\sqrt {a _ {2}}} + \dots + \sqrt {a _ {n}} \cdot \frac {b _ {n}}{\sqrt {a _ {n}}}\right) ^ {2} \\ = (b _ {1} + b _ {2} + \dots + b _ {n}) ^ {2}. \end{array}
> > $$
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{\sqrt{a_1}}{\frac{b_1}{\sqrt{a_1}}} = \frac{\sqrt{a_2}}{\frac{b_2}{\sqrt{a_2}}} = \cdots = \frac{\sqrt{a_n}}{\frac{b_n}{\sqrt{a_n}}}$ , 即 $\frac{a_1}{b_1} = \frac{a_2}{b_2} = \cdots = \frac{a_n}{b_n}$ 时取等号.
