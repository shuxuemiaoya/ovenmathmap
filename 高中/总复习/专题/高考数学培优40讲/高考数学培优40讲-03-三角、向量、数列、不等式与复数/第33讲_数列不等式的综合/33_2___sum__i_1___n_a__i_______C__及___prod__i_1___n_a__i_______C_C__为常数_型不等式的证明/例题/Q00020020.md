变式(1) 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足: $a_{1} = \frac{1}{3}, a_{n+1} = \ln (a_{n} - a_{n}^{2}) + 1, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ . 证明: 当 $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ 时, (1) $a_{1}^{2} + a_{2}^{2} + \cdots + a_{n}^{2} \leqslant \frac{1}{3}$ ; (2) $a_{n} \leqslant \frac{1}{n+2}$ ; (3) $\mathrm{e}^{a_{1} + a_{2} + \cdots + a_{n}} \leqslant \frac{n+2}{2}$ .
