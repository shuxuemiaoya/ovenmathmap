> [!faq]- 《圆锥曲线专题(下册)》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 设 AB: $y=kx+t$ 过 $(0, t) \Rightarrow x_{1}y_{2}-x_{2}y_{1}=t(x_{1}-x_{2})$ ，从而有 $x_{1}y_{2}+x_{2}y_{1}=\frac{4}{t}(x_{1}+x_{2})$ 过 $(0, 4)$ 因此有 $x_{1}y_{2}-x_{2}y_{1}=4(x_{1}-x_{2})\Rightarrow|x_{1}-x_{2}|=\frac{1}{4}|x_{1}y_{2}-x_{2}y_{1}|$ ,
> > $$
> > S _ {\triangle A B P} = \frac {1}{2} x (4 - 1) \left| x _ {1} - x _ {2} \right| = \frac {3}{2} \times \frac {1}{4} \left| x _ {1} y _ {2} - x _ {2} y _ {1} \right|,
> > $$
> > $$
> > \left(\frac {x _ {1} ^ {2}}{8} + \frac {y _ {1} ^ {2}}{4}\right) \left(\frac {x _ {2} ^ {2}}{8} + \frac {y _ {2} ^ {2}}{4}\right) = 1 \Rightarrow \frac {(x _ {1} y _ {2} - x _ {2} y _ {1}) ^ {2}}{3 2} = 1 - \left(\frac {x _ {1} x _ {2}}{8} + \frac {y _ {1} y _ {2}) ^ {2}}{4}\right) ^ {2} \leqslant 1
> > $$
> > $$
> > \mid x _ {1} y _ {2} - x _ {2} y _ {1} \mid \leqslant 4 \sqrt {2} \quad S _ {\triangle A B P} \leqslant \frac {3}{8} \times 4 \sqrt {2} = \frac {3 \sqrt {2}}{2}.
> > $$
