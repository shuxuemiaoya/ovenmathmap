## 第三节 椭圆双曲线的两点对合式

由于 $\cos \alpha \cos \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} + \sin \alpha \sin \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} = \cos \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2}$ ,

$$
\cos \beta \cos \frac {\alpha + \beta}{2} + \sin \beta \sin \frac {\alpha + \beta}{2} = \cos \frac {\alpha - \beta}{2},
$$
