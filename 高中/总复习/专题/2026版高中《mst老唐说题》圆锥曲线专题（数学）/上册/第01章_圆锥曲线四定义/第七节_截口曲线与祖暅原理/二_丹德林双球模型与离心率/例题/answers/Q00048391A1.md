> [!faq]- 《圆锥曲线专题(上册)》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 解法一: 设 $O_{1} O_{2} \cap E F = D$ , 由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{|D O_{2}|}{|D O_{1}|} = \frac{|F O_{2}|}{|E O_{1}|} = \frac{1}{2} \\ |D O_{2}| + |D O_{1}| = 2 \sqrt {10} \end{array} \right.$ , 解得 $|O_{2} D| = \frac{2 \sqrt {10}}{3}$ , $|O_{1} D| = \frac{4 \sqrt {10}}{3}$ , $|D E| = \sqrt {\left(\frac{4 \sqrt {10}}{3}\right)^{2} - 4^{2}} = \frac{4}{3}$ , $|D F| = \sqrt {\left(\frac{2 \sqrt {10}}{3}\right)^{2} - 2^{2}} = \frac{2}{3}$ , 所以 $2 c = \frac{4}{3} + \frac{2}{3} = 2, c = 1$ , 设直线 $E F$ 与圆锥的母线相交于点 $A$ , 圆锥的母线与球相切于 $B, C$ 两点, 如图所示, 则 $|A B| = |A E|$ , $|A C| = |A F|$ , 两式相加得 $|A B| + |A C| = |A E| + |A F| = a - c + a + c = 2 a$ , 即 $|B C| = 2 a$ , 过 $O_{2}$ 作 $O_{2} G \perp O_{1} B$ , 垂直为 $G$ , 则四边形 $B G O_{2} C$ 为矩形, 所以 $2 a = |B C| = \sqrt {(2 \sqrt {10})^{2} - 2^{2}} = 6, a = 3$ , 所以椭圆的离心率为 $\frac{c}{a} = \frac{1}{3}$ . 故答案为 $\frac{1}{3}$ .
