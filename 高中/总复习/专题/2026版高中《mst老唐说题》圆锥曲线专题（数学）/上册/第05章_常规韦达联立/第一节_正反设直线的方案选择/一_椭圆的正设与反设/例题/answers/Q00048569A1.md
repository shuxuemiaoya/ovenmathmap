> [!faq]- 《圆锥曲线专题(上册)》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 正设： $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ 则 $\left|AB\right|=\sqrt{1+k^{2}}\sqrt{\left|x_{2}-x_{1}\right|^{2}}=\sqrt{1+k^{2}}\sqrt{(x_{1}+x_{2})^{2}-4x_{1}x_{2}}$ 将 $y = x + \frac{1}{2}$ 代入 $\frac{x^{2}}{2} + y^{2} = 1$ 得： $3x^{2} + 2x - \frac{3}{2} = 0$ 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x_{1}+x_{2}&=-\frac{2}{3}\\ x_{1}\cdot x_{2}&=-\frac{1}{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $\left|AB\right|=\sqrt{1+k^{2}}$ $\sqrt{\left|x_{2}-x_{1}\right|^{2}}=\frac{2\sqrt{11}}{3}.$
