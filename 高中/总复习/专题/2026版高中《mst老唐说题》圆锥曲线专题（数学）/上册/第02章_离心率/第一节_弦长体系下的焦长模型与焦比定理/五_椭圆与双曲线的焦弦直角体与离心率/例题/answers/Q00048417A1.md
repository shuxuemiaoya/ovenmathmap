> [!faq]- 《圆锥曲线专题(上册)》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 设 $\angle MF_{1}F_{2} = \alpha, \lambda = \frac{|MF_{1}|}{|NF_{1}|} = 2, e\cos \alpha = \frac{\lambda - 1}{\lambda + 1}$ 可得 $\frac{c}{a} \cdot \frac{\lambda + 1}{2} \cdot \frac{b^{2}}{a} \cdot \frac{1}{2c} = \frac{\lambda - 1}{\lambda + 1} \Rightarrow \frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}} = \frac{4}{9}$ , 所以 $e = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
