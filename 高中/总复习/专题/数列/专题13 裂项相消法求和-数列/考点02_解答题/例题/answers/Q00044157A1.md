> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) $\because a_{2} = 8, S_{n} = \frac{a_{n + 1}}{2} - n - 1, \therefore a_{1} = S_{1} = \frac{a_{2}}{2} - 2 = 2,$
> >
> > 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时， $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1} = \frac{a_{n+1}}{2} - n - 1 - \left(\frac{a_n}{2} - n\right)$ ，即 $a_{n+1} = 3a_n + 2$ ，又 $a_2 = 8 = 3a_1 + 2$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n + 1} = 3 a _ {n} + 2, n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*}, \therefore a _ {n + 1} + 1 = 3 (a _ {n} + 1),
> > $$
> >
> > ∴数列 $\{a_{n}+1\}$ 是等比数列，且首项为 $a_{1}+1=3$ ，公比为3，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} + 1 = 3 \times 3 ^ {n - 1} = 3 ^ {n}, \quad \therefore a _ {n} = 3 ^ {n} - 1.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \frac {2 \times 3 ^ {n}}{a _ {n} a _ {n + 1}} = \frac {2 \times 3 ^ {n}}{(3 ^ {n} - 1) (3 ^ {n + 1} - 1)} = \frac {1}{3 ^ {n} - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {n + 1} - 1}.
> > $$
> >
> > ∴数列 $\left\{\frac{2\times3^{n}}{a_{n}a_{n+1}}\right\}$ 的前n项和
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = \left(\frac {1}{3 - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {2} - 1}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{3 ^ {2} - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {3} - 1}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{3 ^ {n} - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {n + 1} - 1}\right) = \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {n + 1} - 1}.
> > $$
> >
> > 点评：本例第
> > (1)问在求解通项公式时运用了构造法，形如 $a_{n + 1} = \lambda a_n + \mu$ 的数列递推关系求通项公式都可以采用此法；第
> > (2)问运用了裂项相消法求和， $b_{n} = \frac{(q - 1)a_{n}}{(a_{n} + k)(a_{n + 1} + k)} = \frac{1}{a_{n} + k} -\frac{1}{a_{n + 1} + k}.$
