> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解得 $a_{1}=1,\ d=2.\ \therefore a_{n}=2n-1.$
> >
> > (2)由题意知， $b_{n}=\frac{1}{(2n-1)(2n+1)}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2n-1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = b _ {1} + b _ {2} + \dots + b _ {n} = \frac {1}{2} \left[ \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{5}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{2 n - 1} - \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right) \right] = \frac {1}{2} \left(1 - \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right) = \frac {n}{2 n + 1}.
> > $$
