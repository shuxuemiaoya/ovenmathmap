[例 17] 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为各项非零的等差数列，其前 n 项和为 $S_{n}$ ，满足 $S_{2n-1}=a_{n}^{2}$ .

(1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式;

(2)记 $b_{n} = \frac{n}{a_{n}a_{n + 1}} (-1)^{n}$ ，求数列 $\{b_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n}$

(1) $S_{2n - 1} = \frac{(2n - 1)(a_1 + a_{2n - 1})}{2} = a_n(2n - 1) = a_n^2,\quad \because a_n\neq 0,\quad \therefore a_n = 2n - 1(n\in \mathbf{N}^*)$

$$
(2) b _ {n} = \frac {n}{a _ {n} a _ {n + 1}} (- 1) ^ {n} = \frac {n}{(2 n - 1) (2 n + 1)} (- 1) ^ {n} = \frac {1}{4} \left(\frac {1}{2 n - 1} + \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right) (- 1) ^ {n},
$$

当 n 为偶数时 $T_{n}=\frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{1}{1}-\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{7}+\cdots+\frac{1}{2n-1}+\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)=\frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{1}{1}+\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)=\frac{-n}{4n+2}$

当 $n$ 为奇数时 $T_{n} = \frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{1}{1} -\frac{1}{3} +\frac{1}{3} +\frac{1}{5} -\frac{1}{5} -\frac{1}{7} +\dots -\frac{1}{2n - 1} -\frac{1}{2n + 1}\right) = \frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{1}{1} -\frac{1}{2n + 1}\right) = \frac{-n - 1}{4n + 2}.$

所以 $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}-\frac{n}{4n+2},&n\text{为偶数,}\\ -\frac{n+1}{4n+2},&n\text{为奇数.}\end{aligned}\right.$
