[例6] 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足 $a_1 = \frac{1}{2}$ ，且 $a_{n + 1} = \frac{2a_n}{2 + a_n}$ .

(1)求证：数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n}\right\}$ 是等差数列；

(2)若 $b_{n} = a_{n}a_{n + 1}$ ，求数列 $\{b_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $S_{n}$

(1) 易知 $a_{n} \neq 0$ , $\because a_{n+1} = \frac{2a_{n}}{2 + a_{n}}$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = \frac{2 + a_{n}}{2a_{n}}$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{a_{n+1}} - \frac{1}{a_{n}} = \frac{1}{2}$ ,

又 $\because a_{1} = \frac{1}{2},\therefore \frac{1}{a_{1}} = 2,\therefore$ 数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n}\right\}$ 是以2为首项， $\frac{1}{2}$ 为公差的等差数列.

(2)由
(1)知， $\frac{1}{a_{n}}=2+\frac{1}{2}(n-1)=\frac{n+3}{2}$ ，即 $a_{n}=\frac{2}{n+3}$ ,

$\therefore b_{n} = \frac{4}{(n + 3)(n + 4)} = 4\left(\frac{1}{n + 3} -\frac{1}{n + 4}\right),$

$S_{n} = 4\left[\left(\frac{1}{4} -\frac{1}{5}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{5} -\frac{1}{6}\right) + \dots +\left(\frac{1}{n + 3} -\frac{1}{n + 4}\right)\right] = 4\left(\frac{1}{4} -\frac{1}{n + 4}\right) = \frac{n}{n + 4}.$
