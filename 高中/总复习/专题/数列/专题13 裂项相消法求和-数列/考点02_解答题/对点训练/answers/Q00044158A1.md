> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式;
> > (2)设 $b_{n} = \frac{a_{n}}{S_{n}S_{n + 1}}$ ，求数列 $\{b_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n}$
> > 27. 解析
> > (1) $\because S_{n} = 2a_{n} - a_{1}$ , ①, $\therefore$ 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, $S_{n-1} = 2a_{n-1} - a_{1}$ , ②
> > - **①**：-②得, $a_{n} = 2a_{n} - 2a_{n-1}$ , 即 $a_{n} = 2a_{n-1}$ .
> > 由 $a_{1}, a_{2} + 1, a_{3}$ 成等差数列, 得 $2(a_{2} + 1) = a_{1} + a_{3}, \therefore 2(2a_{1} + 1) = a_{1} + 4a_{1}$ , 解得 $a_{1} = 2$ . $\therefore$ 数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是首项为 2, 公比为 2 的等比数列. $\therefore a_{n} = 2^{n}$ .
> > (2) $\because a_{n} = 2^{n}, \therefore S_{n} = 2a_{n} - a_{1} = 2^{n+1} - 2, S_{n+1} = 2^{n+2} - 2$ . $\therefore b_{n} = \frac{a_{n}}{S_{n}S_{n+1}} = \frac{2^{n}}{(2^{n+1} - 2)(2^{n+2} - 2)} = \frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{1}{2^{n}-1} - \frac{1}{2^{n+1}-1}\right]$ . $\therefore$ 数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n} = \frac{1}{2}\left[\left(\frac{1}{2-1} - \frac{1}{2^{2}-1}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{2^{2}-1} - \frac{1}{2^{3}-1}\right) + \cdots + \left(\frac{1}{2^{n}-1} - \frac{1}{2^{n+1}-1}\right)\right] = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{2^{n+1}-1}\right) = \frac{2^{n}-1}{2^{n+1}-1}$ .
