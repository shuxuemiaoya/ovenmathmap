> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1)设数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为d，由题意得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&3a_{1}+3d=a_{1}+6d,\\ &(a_{1}+7d)-2(a_{1}+2d)=3,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $a_{1}=3,\ d=2,$ $\therefore a_{n}=a_{1}+(n-1)d=2n+1.$
> > (2)由
> > (1)得 $S_{n}=na_{1}+\frac{n(n-1)}{2}d=n(n+2),\quad\therefore b_{n}=\frac{1}{n(n+2)}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+2}\right).$ $\therefore T_{n}=b_{1}+b_{2}+\cdots+b_{n-1}+b_{n}=\frac{1}{2}\left[\left(\frac{1-\frac{1}{3}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\right)+\cdots+\left(\frac{1}{n-1}-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+2}\right)\right]$ $=\frac{1}{2}\left(1+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{n+1}-\frac{1}{n+2}\right)=\frac{3}{4}-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}\right).$
