> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) 因为 $S_{3} = 2S_{2} + 4$ ，所以 $a_{1} = d - 4$ ，又因为 $a_{5} = 36$ ，
> > 所以 $a_{1} + 4d = 36$ ，解得 $d = 8, a_{1} = 4$ ，
> > 所以 $a_{n} = 4 + 8(n - 1) = 8n - 4, S_{n} = \frac{n(4 + 8n - 4)}{2} = 4n^{2}$ .
> >
> > (2) $b_{n} = 4n^{2} - 1 = (2n - 1)(2n + 1)$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{b_n} = \frac{1}{(2n - 1)(2n + 1)} = \frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{1}{2n - 1} - \frac{1}{2n + 1}\right]$ . $T_{n} = \frac{1}{b_1} + \frac{1}{b_2} + \frac{1}{b_3} + \cdots + \frac{1}{b_n} = \frac{1}{2}\left[1 - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{5} + \cdots + \frac{1}{2n - 1} - \frac{1}{2n + 1}\right] = \frac{1}{2}\left[1 - \frac{1}{2n + 1}\right] = \frac{n}{2n + 1}$ .
