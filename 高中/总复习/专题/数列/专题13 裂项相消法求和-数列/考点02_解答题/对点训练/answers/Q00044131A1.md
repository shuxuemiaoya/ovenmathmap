> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) 设正项等比数列 $\{a_n\}$ 的公比 $q > 0$ ，由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2a_3 + a_4 = a_5, \\ a_1 + a_2 = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2a_1q^2 + a_1q^3 = a_1q^4, \\ a_1 + a_1q = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_1 = \frac{1}{3}, \\ q = 2, \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n} = \frac{2^{n - 1}}{3}, n\in \mathbf{N}^{*}$
> >
> > (2)由题知 $t_n = \log_2(3a_n) = \log_22^{n - 1} = n - 1$ ，则 $\frac{1}{t_{n + 1}t_{n + 2}} = \frac{1}{n(n + 1)} = \frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1},$
> >
> > 所以数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{t_{n+1}t_{n+2}}\right\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n}=\left(1-\frac{1}{2}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{4}\right)+\ldots+\left(\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)=1-\frac{1}{n+1}=\frac{n}{n+1}$ .
