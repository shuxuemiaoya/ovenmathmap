> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)证明： $\{a_{n} + 1\}$ 是等比数列；
> > (2)若数列 $b_{n} = \log_{2}(a_{n} + 1)$ ，求数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{b_{2n - 1}\cdot b_{2n + 1}}\right\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n}$ .
> > 20. 解析
> > (1) 当 n=1 时， $S_{1}=2a_{1}-1,\quad\therefore a_{1}=1$ .
> > $$
> > \because S _ {n} = 2 a _ {n} - n, \therefore S _ {n + 1} = 2 a _ {n + 1} - (n + 1), \therefore a _ {n + 1} = 2 a _ {n} + 1, \therefore a _ {n + 1} + 1 = 2 \left(a _ {n} + 1\right),
> > $$
> > ∴ $\{a_{n}+1\}$ 是以 $a_{1}+1=2$ 为首项，2 为公比的等比数列.
> > (2)由
> > (1)得 $a_{n}+1=2^{n},\quad\therefore b_{n}=\log_{2}2^{n}=n,$
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {1}{b _ {2 n - 1} \cdot b _ {2 n + 1}} = \frac {1}{(2 n - 1) (2 n + 1)} = \frac {1}{2} \left(\frac {1}{2 n - 1} - \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right)
> > $$
> > $\therefore T_{n} = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{3} +\frac{1}{3} -\frac{1}{5} +\dots +\frac{1}{2n - 1} -\frac{1}{2n + 1}\right) = \frac{n}{2n + 1}.$
