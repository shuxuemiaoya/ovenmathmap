> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 $\because 2a_{1} + 2^{2}a_{2} + \ldots + 2^{n}a_{n} = n (n \in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ ， $\therefore 2a_{1} + 2^{2}a_{2} + \ldots + 2^{n-1}a_{n-1} = n - 1 (n \geq 2)$ ，两式相减得 $2^{n}a_{n} = 1 (n \geq 2)$ ， $a_{1} = \frac{1}{2}$ 也满足上式，故 $a_{n} = \frac{1}{2^{n}}$ ，故 $\frac{1}{\log_2 a_n \log_2 a_{n+1}} = \frac{1}{n (n+1)} = \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}$ ， $S_{n} = \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\right) + \ldots + \left(\frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}\right) = 1 - \frac{1}{n+1} = \frac{n}{n+1}$ ， $\therefore S_{1} \cdot S_{2} \cdot S_{3} \cdot \ldots \cdot S_{10} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} \times \ldots \times \frac{9}{10} \times \frac{10}{11} = \frac{1}{11}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
