> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 $\because a_{n}=\frac{1+2+3+\cdots+n}{n+1}=\frac{n}{2},\quad\therefore b_{n}=\frac{1}{a_{n}a_{n+1}}=\frac{4}{n(n+1)}=4\left(\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}\right),\quad\therefore S_{n}=$ $4\left[\left(1-\frac{1}{2}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}\right)+\cdots+\left(\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)\right]=4\left(1-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)=\frac{4n}{n+1}.$
