> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 $a_{n}=\frac{1}{(n+1)\sqrt{n}+n\sqrt{n+1}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{n(n+1)}(\sqrt{n+1}+\sqrt{n})}=\frac{\sqrt{n+1}-\sqrt{n}}{\sqrt{n(n+1)}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}}$ ，所以 $S_{n}=错误!_{i}=1-\frac{1}{\sqrt{n+1}}$ . $44^{2}<2021<45^{2}$ ，所以 $[\sqrt{n+1}]_{\min}=2$ , $[\sqrt{n+1}]_{\max}=44$ ，即 $S_{1}, S_{2}, \ldots, S_{2021}$ 共有 43 个有理项．
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
