> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{2n}{n+1}$ 解析 设公差为 d，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}+2d=3,\\ 4a_{1}+6d=10,\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}=1,\\ d=1,\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore a_{n}=n.\therefore$ 前 n 项和 $S_{n}=1+2+\cdots$ $+n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2},\quad\therefore\frac{1}{S_{n}}=\frac{2}{n(n+1)}=2\left(\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}\right),\quad\therefore$ 错误! $\frac{1}{S_{k}}=2\left(1-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)=2\left(1-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)=2\cdot\frac{n}{n+1}=\frac{2n}{n+1}.$
