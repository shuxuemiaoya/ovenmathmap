> [!faq]- 《专题13 裂项相消法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{3(2^n - 1)}{2^{n+1} - 1}$ 解析 $S_n = \frac{4}{3} \times (2^{2n} - 1) - 2 \times (2^n - 1) = \frac{2}{3} \times [2(2^n + 1)(2^n - 1) - 3 \times (2^n - 1)] = \frac{2}{3} \times (2^{n+1} - 1)(2^n - 1)$ . 所以 $b_n = \frac{2^n}{S_n} = \frac{3}{2} \times \frac{2^n}{(2^{n+1} - 1)(2^n - 1)} = \frac{3}{2} \left[ \frac{1}{2^n - 1} - \frac{1}{2^{n+1} - 1} \right]$ . 所以 $T_n =$ 错误！ $i = \frac{3}{2} \left[ \left( \frac{1}{2^1 - 1} - \frac{1}{2^2 - 1} \right) + \left( \frac{1}{2^2 - 1} - \frac{1}{2^3 - 1} \right) + \cdots + \left( \frac{1}{2^n - 1} - \frac{1}{2^{n+1} - 1} \right) \right] = \frac{3}{2} \left[ 1 - \frac{1}{2^{n+1} - 1} \right] = \frac{3(2^n - 1)}{2^{n+1} - 1}$ .
> >
> > (7)已知 $S_{n}$ 为数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和， $a_{n} = 2 \cdot 3^{n - 1}(n \in \mathbb{N}^{*})$ ，若 $b_{n} = \frac{a_{n + 1}}{S_{n} S_{n + 1}}$ ，则 $b_{1} + b_{2} + \ldots + b_{n} =$ \_\_\_\_。答案 $\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3^{n + 1} - 1}$ 解析由 $a_{n} = 2 \cdot 3^{n - 1}$ 可知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是以2为首项，3为公比的等比数列，所以 $S_{n} = \frac{2(1 - 3^{n})}{1 - 3} = 3^{n} - 1$ ，则 $b_{n} = \frac{a_{n + 1}}{S_{n} S_{n + 1}} = \frac{S_{n + 1} - S_{n}}{S_{n} S_{n + 1}} = \frac{1}{S_{n}} - \frac{1}{S_{n + 1}}$ ，则 $b_{1} + b_{2} + \ldots + b_{n} = \left(\frac{1}{S_{1}} - \frac{1}{S_{2}}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{S_{2}} - \frac{1}{S_{3}}\right) + \ldots + \left(\frac{1}{S_{n}} - \frac{1}{S_{n + 1}}\right) = \frac{1}{S_{1}} - \frac{1}{S_{n + 1}} = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3^{n + 1} - 1}$ .
