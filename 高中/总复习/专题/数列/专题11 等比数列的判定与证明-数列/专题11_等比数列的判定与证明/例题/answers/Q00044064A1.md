> [!faq]- 《专题11 等比数列的判定与证明-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) $\because a_{n + 1} = S_{n + 1} - S_n, S_n^2 = a_{n + 1}^2 -\lambda S_{n + 1},$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {n} ^ {2} = \left(S _ {n + 1} - S _ {n}\right) ^ {2} - \lambda S _ {n + 1}, \quad \therefore S _ {n + 1} \left(S _ {n + 1} - 2 S _ {n} - \lambda\right) = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because a _ {n} > 0, \therefore S _ {n + 1} > 0, \therefore S _ {n + 1} - 2 S _ {n} - \lambda = 0; \therefore S _ {n + 1} = 2 S _ {n} + \lambda .
> > $$
> >
> > (2)存在 $\lambda = 1$ ，使得数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为等比数列，理由如下：
> >
> > $S_{n+1}=2S_{n}+\lambda,\quad S_{n}=2S_{n-1}+\lambda(n\geq2)$ ，相减得 $a_{n+1}=2a_{n}(n\geq2)$ ,
> >
> > $\therefore \{a_{n}\}$ 从第二项起成等比数列， $\because S_2 = 2S_1 + \lambda$ ，即 $a_2 + a_1 = 2a_1 + \lambda$ ， $\therefore a_2 = 1 + \lambda >0$ ，得 $\lambda > - 1$ $\therefore a_{n} = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}1, & n = 1,\\ (\lambda +1)2^{n - 2}, & n\geq 2, \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 若使 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是等比数列，则 $a_{1}a_{3}=a_{2}^{2},\quad\therefore2(\lambda+1)=(\lambda+1)^{2},\quad\therefore\lambda=-1(舍)或\lambda=1$ ，经检验符合题意.
