> [!faq]- 《专题11 等比数列的判定与证明-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) 因为 $4S_{n+2} + 5S_{n} = 8S_{n+1} + S_{n-1}, a_{1} = 1, a_{2} = \frac{3}{2}, a_{3} = \frac{5}{4}$ ,
> >
> > 当 $n = 2$ 时， $4S_{4} + 5S_{2} = 8S_{3} + S_{1}$ ，即 $4 \times \left(1 + \frac{3}{2} + \frac{5}{4} + a_{4}\right) + 5 \times \left(1 + \frac{3}{2}\right) = 8 \times \left(1 + \frac{3}{2} + \frac{5}{4}\right) + 1$ ，解得 $a_{4} = \frac{7}{8}$ .
> >
> > (2)由 $4S_{n + 2} + 5S_n = 8S_{n + 1} + S_{n - 1}(n\geqslant 2)$ ，得 $4S_{n + 2} - 4S_{n + 1} + S_n - S_{n - 1} = 4S_{n + 1} - 4S_n(n\geqslant 2),$
> >
> > 即 $4a_{n + 2} + a_n = 4a_{n + 1}(n\geqslant 2)$ 当 $n = 1$ 时，有 $4a_{3} + a_{1} = 4\times \frac{5}{4} +1 = 6 = 4a_{2},\therefore 4a_{n + 2} + a_n = 4a_{n + 1},$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {a _ {n + 2} - \frac {1}{2} a _ {n + 1}}{a _ {n + 1} - \frac {1}{2} a _ {n}} = \frac {4 a _ {n + 2} - 2 a _ {n + 1}}{4 a _ {n + 1} - 2 a _ {n}} = \frac {4 a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n} - 2 a _ {n + 1}}{4 a _ {n + 1} - 2 a _ {n}} = \frac {2 a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n}}{2 (2 a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n})} = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > ∴数列 $\left\{a_{n+1}-\frac{1}{2}a_{n}\right\}$ 是以 $a_{2}-\frac{1}{2}a_{1}=1$ 为首项， $\frac{1}{2}$ 为公比的等比数列.
