[例6] 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $S_{n}$ 满足 $S_{n} = 2a_{n} + (-1)^{n}(n\in \mathbf{N}^{*})$

(1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前三项 $a_1, a_2, a_3$ ;

(2)求证：数列 $\left\{a_{n} + \frac{2}{3} (-1)^{n}\right\}$ 为等比数列，并求出 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式.

(1) 在 $S_{n} = 2a_{n} + (-1)^{n}(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ 中分别令 $n = 1, 2, 3$ ，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_{1} = 2a_{1} - 1, \\ a_{1} + a_{2} = 2a_{2} + 1, \\ a_{1} + a_{2} + a_{3} = 2a_{3} - 1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a _ {1} = 1, \\ a _ {2} = 0, \\ a _ {3} = 2. \end{array} \right.
$$

(2)由 $S_{n} = 2a_{n} + (-1)^{n}(n\in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ ，得 $S_{n - 1} = 2a_{n - 1} + (-1)^{n - 1}(n\geqslant 2),$

两式相减，得 $a_{n} = 2a_{n - 1} - 2(-1)^{n}(n\geqslant 2),$

$$
a _ {n} = 2 a _ {n - 1} - \frac {4}{3} (- 1) ^ {n} - \frac {2}{3} (- 1) ^ {n} = 2 a _ {n - 1} + \frac {4}{3} (- 1) ^ {n - 1} - \frac {2}{3} (- 1) ^ {n} (n \geqslant 2),
$$

$$
\therefore a _ {n} + \frac {2}{3} (- 1) ^ {n} = 2 \left[ a _ {n - 1} + \frac {2}{3} (- 1) ^ {n - 1} \right] (n \geqslant 2).
$$

故数列 $\left\{a_{n}+\frac{2}{3}(-1)^{n}\right\}$ 是以 $a_{1}-\frac{2}{3}=\frac{1}{3}$ 为首项，2 为公比的等比数列.

$$
\therefore a _ {n} + \frac {2}{3} (- 1) ^ {n} = \frac {1}{3} \times 2 ^ {n - 1}, \quad \therefore a _ {n} = \frac {1}{3} \times 2 ^ {n - 1} - \frac {2}{3} \times (- 1) ^ {n} = \frac {2 ^ {n - 1}}{3} - \frac {2}{3} (- 1) ^ {n}.
$$
