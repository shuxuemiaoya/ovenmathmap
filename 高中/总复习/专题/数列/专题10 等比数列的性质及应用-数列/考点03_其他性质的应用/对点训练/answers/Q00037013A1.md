> [!faq]- 《专题10 等比数列的性质及应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 2 解析 由题意，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} S_{\text{奇}} + S_{\text{偶}} = -240, \\ S_{\text{奇}} - S_{\text{偶}} = 80, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} S_{\text{奇}} = -80, \\ S_{\text{偶}} = -160, \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $q = \frac{S_{\text{偶}}}{S_{\text{奇}}} = \frac{-160}{-80} = 2.$
