> [!faq]- 《专题10 等比数列的性质及应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 $\because a_{1}a_{2020}=1,\quad\therefore f(a_{1})+f(a_{2020})=\frac{2}{1+a_{1}^{2}}+\frac{2}{1+a_{2020}^{2}}=\frac{2}{1+a_{1}^{2}}+\frac{2}{1+\frac{1}{a_{1}^{2}}}=\frac{2}{1+a_{1}^{2}}+\frac{2a_{1}^{2}}{1+a_{1}^{2}}=2,$
> >
> > $\because \{a_{n}\}$ 为等比数列，则 $a_1a_{2020} = a_2a_{2019} = \dots = a_{1010}a_{1011} = 1$ ， $\therefore f(a_2) + f(a_{2019}) = 2, \dots, f(a_{1010}) + f(a_{1011}) = 2,$
> >
> > 即 $f(a_{1})+f(a_{2})+f(a_{3})+\cdots+f(a_{2020})=2\times1010=2020.$
> >
