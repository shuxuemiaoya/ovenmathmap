> [!faq]- 《专题10 等比数列的性质及应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 50 解析 因为 $a_{10}a_{11} + a_9a_{12} = 2a_{10}a_{11} = 2\mathrm{e}^5$ ，所以 $a_{10}a_{11} = \mathrm{e}^5$ 。所以 $\ln a_1 + \ln a_2 + \ldots + \ln a_{20} = \ln (a_1a_2\dots a_{20}) = \ln [(a_1a_{20})\cdot (a_2a_{19})\cdot \ldots \cdot (a_{10}a_{11})] = \ln (a_{10}a_{11})^{10} = 10\ln (a_{10}a_{11}) = 10\ln \mathrm{e}^5 = 50\ln \mathrm{e} = 50.$
