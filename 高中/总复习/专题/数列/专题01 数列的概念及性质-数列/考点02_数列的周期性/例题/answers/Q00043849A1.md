> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 由 $a_1 = \frac{1}{2}$ , $a_{n+1} = \frac{1}{1 - a_n}$ , 得 $a_2 = \frac{1}{1 - a_1} = 2$ , $a_3 = \frac{1}{1 - a_2} = -1$ , $a_4 = \frac{1}{1 - a_3} = \frac{1}{2}$ , $a_5 = \frac{1}{1 - a_4} = 2$ , $\cdots$ , 于是可知数列 $\{a_n\}$ 是以 3 为周期的周期数列, 因此 $a_{2022} = a_{3 \times 674} = a_3 = -1$ .
