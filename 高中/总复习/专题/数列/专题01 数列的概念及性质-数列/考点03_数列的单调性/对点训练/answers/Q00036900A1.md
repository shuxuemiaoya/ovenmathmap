> [!faq]- 《专题01 数列的概念及性质-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 $\because a_{n+1}=2a_n-1, \therefore a_{n+1}-1=2(a_n-1), \therefore \frac{a_{n+1}-1}{a_n-1}=2$ , 又 $\because a_1-1=a-1, \therefore$ 数列 $\{a_n-1\}$ 是首项为 $a-1$ , 公比为 2 的等比数列, $\therefore a_n-1=(a-1)2^{n-1}, \therefore a_n=(a-1)2^{n-1}+1$ , 又 $\because \{a_n\}$ 为递增数列, $\therefore a_{n+1}-a_n=(a-1)2^n-(a-1)2^{n-1}=\frac{1}{2}(a-1)2^n>0, \therefore a-1>0, \therefore a>1$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
