> [!faq]- 《专题01 数列的概念及性质-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 AD 解析 $\because a_{n} + 4S_{n - 1}S_{n} = 0(n\geq 2),\therefore S_{n} - S_{n - 1} + 4S_{n - 1}S_{n} = 0(n\geq 2).$ 又 $S_{n}\neq 0$ ，故 $\frac{1}{S_n} -\frac{1}{S_{n - 1}} = 4(n\geq 2)$ 即数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{S_n}\right\}$ 是首项为 $\frac{1}{S_1} = 4$ ，公差为 $d = 4$ 的等差数列，且数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{S_n}\right\}$ 也是递增数列， $\therefore \frac{1}{S_n} = 4 + 4(n - 1) = 4n,$ 即 $S_{n} = \frac{1}{4n}$ ，故A，D正确；又当 $n\geq 2$ 时， $a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n - 1} = \frac{1}{4n} -\frac{1}{4(n - 1)} = -\frac{1}{4n(n - 1)}$ ，且 $a_1 = \frac{1}{4}$ ，故 $a_{n} =$ $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll} \frac{1}{4}, & n = 1,\\ -\frac{1}{4n(n - 1)}, & n\geq 2, \end{array} \right.$ 数列 $\{a_n\}$ 不是递增数列，故B，C错误．
> >
> > 故选 **AD**.
