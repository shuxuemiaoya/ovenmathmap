> [!faq]- 《专题12 等差、等比数列的综合应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 已知等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的首项和公差均不为 0，且满足 $a_{2}, a_{5}, a_{7}$ 成等比数列， $\therefore a_{5}^{2} = a_{2}a_{7}$ ， $\therefore (a_{1} + 4d)^{2} = (a_{1} + d)(a_{1} + 6d)$ ， $\therefore 10d^{2} = -a_{1}d$ ， $\because d \neq 0$ ， $\therefore -10d = a_{1}$ ， $\therefore \frac{a_{3} + a_{6} + a_{11}}{a_{1} + a_{8} + a_{10}} = \frac{3a_{1} + 17d}{3a_{1} + 16d} = \frac{-30d + 17d}{-30d + 16d} = \frac{13}{14}$ .
