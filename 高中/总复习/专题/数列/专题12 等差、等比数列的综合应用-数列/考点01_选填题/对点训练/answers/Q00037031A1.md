> [!faq]- 《专题12 等差、等比数列的综合应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 不妨设 $a > b$ ，由题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a + b = p > 0, \\ ab = q > 0, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore a > 0, b > 0$ ，则 $a, -2, b$ 成等比数列， $a, b, -2$ 成等差数列， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} ab = (-2)^2, \\ a - 2 = 2b, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 4, \\ b = 1, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore p = 5, q = 4, \therefore p + q = 9$ .
