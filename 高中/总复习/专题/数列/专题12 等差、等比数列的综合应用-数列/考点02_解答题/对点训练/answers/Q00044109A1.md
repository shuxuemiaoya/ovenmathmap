> [!faq]- 《专题12 等差、等比数列的综合应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：(1) $\because a_{1} + a_{2} = 4, a_{3} - a_{2} = 6, \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_{1}(1 + q) = 4, \\ a_{1}(q^{2} - q) = 6, \end{array} \right.\quad \because q > 0, \therefore q = 3, a_{1} = 1.$
> >
> > $\therefore a_{n} = 1 \times 3^{n - 1} = 3^{n - 1}$ ，故数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n} = 3^{n - 1}$ .
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)知 $a_{n} = 3^{n - 1}$ ， $S_{n} = \frac{1\times(1 - 3^{n})}{1 - 3} = \frac{3^{n} - 1}{2},\quad \because ka_{n},S_{n}, - 1$ 成等差数列，
> >
> > $\therefore 2S_{n} = ka_{n} - 1$ ，即 $2\times \frac{3^{n} - 1}{2} = k\times 3^{n - 1} - 1$ ，解得 $k = 3$
