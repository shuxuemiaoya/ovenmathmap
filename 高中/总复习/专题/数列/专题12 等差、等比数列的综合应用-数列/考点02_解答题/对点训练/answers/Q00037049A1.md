> [!faq]- 《专题12 等差、等比数列的综合应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求 $a_{n}$ 和 $S_{n}$ ;
> > (2)若 $b_{n} = 2^{a_{n}}$ ，求 $\{b_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n}$
> > 7. 解析 (1)设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的首项为 $a_{1}$ ，公差为d，由 $a_{3}=3,\ S_{7}=14$
> > 得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{3}&=a_{1}+2d=3,\\ S_{7}&=7a_{1}+\frac{7\times(7-1)}{2}d=14,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=5,\\ d&=-1,\end{aligned}\right.$
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = a _ {1} + (n - 1) d = - n + 6, S _ {n} = \frac {(a _ {1} + a _ {n}) n}{2} = \frac {(5 + 6 - n) n}{2} = \frac {(1 1 - n) n}{2}.
> > $$
> > (2)由(1)知 $a_{n} = -n + 6$ ， $b_{n} = 2^{a_{n}}$ ，得 $b_{n} = 2^{6 - n} = 2^{6}\times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)_{n}$
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = \frac {2 ^ {5} \left[ 1 - \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) n \right]}{1 - \frac {1}{2}} = 2 ^ {6} - 2 ^ {6} \times \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n} = 6 4 - 2 ^ {6 - n}.
> > $$
