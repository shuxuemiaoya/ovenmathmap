> [!faq]- 《专题09 等比数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 设正数的等比数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公比为 $q$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_{1} + a_{1}q + a_{1}q^{2} + a_{1}q^{3} = 15, \\ a_{1}q^{4} = 3a_{1}q^{2} + 4a_{1}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_{1} = 1, \\ q = 2, \end{array} \right.\therefore a_{3} = a_{1}q^{2} = 4$ 。
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
