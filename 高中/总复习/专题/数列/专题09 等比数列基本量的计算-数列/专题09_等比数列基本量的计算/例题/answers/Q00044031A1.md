> [!faq]- 《专题09 等比数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1)由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} S_{2} = 6a_{2} - 42, \\ S_{3} = a_{4} - 42 \end{array} \right.$ 得 $a_{3} = a_{4} - 6a_{2}$ ，即 $q^{2} - q - 6 = 0$
> >
> > 又∵q>0，∴q=3．把q=3代入 $S_{2}=6a_{2}-42$ 得 $a_{1}=3$ ，∴ $a_{n}=3^{n}$
> >
> > 又 $\because \frac{b_1}{a_1} +\frac{b_2}{a_2} +\dots +\frac{b_n}{a_n} = 1 - \frac{n + 1}{3^n} (n\in \mathbf{N}^*)$
> >
> > 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时， $\frac{b_{1}}{a_{1}} + \frac{b_{2}}{a_{2}} + \cdots + \frac{b_{n-1}}{a_{n-1}} = 1 - \frac{n}{3^{n-1}}$ ,
> >
> > ∴两式相减得 $\frac{b_{n}}{a_{n}}=1-\frac{n+1}{3^{n}}-\left(1-\frac{n}{3^{n-1}}\right)=\frac{2n-1}{3^{n}}.$
> >
> > 因为当 $n = 1$ 时， $\frac{b_1}{a_1} = \frac{1}{3}$ 也满足， $\therefore \frac{b_n}{a_n} = \frac{2n - 1}{3^n} (n\in \mathbf{N}^*)$ 又 $\because a_{n} = 3^{n}$ ， $\therefore b_{n} = 2n - 1(n\in \mathbf{N}^{*})$
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)知 $c_{n} = \frac{1}{ab_{n}} = \frac{1}{3^{2n - 1}},$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = c _ {1} + c _ {2} + c _ {3} + \dots + c _ {n} = \frac {1}{3 ^ {1}} + \frac {1}{3 ^ {3}} + \frac {1}{3 ^ {5}} + \dots + \frac {1}{3 ^ {2 n - 1}} = \frac {\frac {1}{3} \left(1 - \frac {1}{9 ^ {n}}\right)}{1 - \frac {1}{9}} = \frac {3}{8} \left(1 - \frac {1}{9 ^ {n}}\right).
> > $$
