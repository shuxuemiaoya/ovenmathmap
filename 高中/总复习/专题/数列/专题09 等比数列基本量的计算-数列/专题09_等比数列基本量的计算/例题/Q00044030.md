[例 3] 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 中， $a_{1}=1,\quad a_{n}\cdot a_{n+1}=\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n}$ ，记 $T_{2n}$ 为 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 2n 项的和， $b_{n}=a_{2n}+a_{2n-1},\quad n\in\mathbb{N}^{*}$ .
(1)判断数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 是否为等比数列，并求出 $b_{n}$ ;

(2)求 $T_{2n}$ .

(1) $\because a_{n} \cdot a_{n+1} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n}, \therefore a_{n+1} \cdot a_{n+2} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n+1}, \therefore \frac{a_{n+2}}{a_{n}} = \frac{1}{2}$ , 即 $a_{n+2} = \frac{1}{2} a_{n}$ .

$$
\because b _ {n} = a _ {2 n} + a _ {2 n - 1}, \therefore \frac {b _ {n + 1}}{b _ {n}} = \frac {a _ {2 n + 2} + a _ {2 n + 1}}{a _ {2 n} + a _ {2 n - 1}} = \frac {\frac {1}{2} a _ {2 n} + \frac {1}{2} a _ {2 n - 1}}{a _ {2 n} + a _ {2 n - 1}} = \frac {1}{2},
$$

$\because a_{1} = 1, a_{1} \cdot a_{2} = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore a_{2} = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore b_{1} = a_{1} + a_{2} = \frac{3}{2}. \therefore \{b_{n}\}$ 是首项为 $\frac{3}{2}$ , 公比为 $\frac{1}{2}$ 的等比数列.

$$
\therefore b _ {n} = \frac {3}{2} \times \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n - 1} = \frac {3}{2 ^ {n}}.
$$

(2)由
(1)可知， $a_{n+2}=\frac{1}{2}a_{n},\quad\therefore a_{1},\quad a_{3},\quad a_{5},\quad\ldots$ 是以 $a_{1}=1$ 为首项，以 $\frac{1}{2}$ 为公比的等比数列；

$a_{2}, a_{4}, a_{6}, \ldots$ 是以 $a_{2} = \frac{1}{2}$ 为首项，以 $\frac{1}{2}$ 为公比的等比数列，

$$
\therefore T _ {2 n} = \left(a _ {1} + a _ {3} + \dots + a _ {2 n - 1}\right) + \left(a _ {2} + a _ {4} + \dots + a _ {2 n}\right) = \frac {1 - \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n}}{1 - \frac {1}{2}} + \frac {\frac {1}{2} \left[ 1 - \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n} \right]}{1 - \frac {1}{2}} = 3 - \frac {3}{2 ^ {n}}.
$$
