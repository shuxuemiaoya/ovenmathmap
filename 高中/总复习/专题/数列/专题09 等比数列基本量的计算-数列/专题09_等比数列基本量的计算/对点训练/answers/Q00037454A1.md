> [!faq]- 《专题09 等比数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{5}{8}$ 解析 设等比数列 $\{a_n\}$ 的公比为 $q$ ，则 $a_n = a_1 q^{n-1} = q^{n-1}$ . $\because a_1 = 1, S_3 = \frac{3}{4}, \therefore a_1 + a_2 + a_3 = 1 + q + q^2 = \frac{3}{4}$ ，则 $4q^2 + 4q + 1 = 0, \therefore q = -\frac{1}{2}, \therefore S_4 = \frac{1 \times \left[1 - \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^4\right]}{1 - \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)} = \frac{5}{8}$ .
