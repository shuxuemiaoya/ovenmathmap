> [!faq]- 《专题09 等比数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 1024 解析 $\because b_{1} = \frac{a_{2}}{a_{1}} = a_{2}, b_{2} = \frac{a_{3}}{a_{2}}, \therefore a_{3} = b_{2}a_{2} = b_{1}b_{2}, \because b_{3} = \frac{a_{4}}{a_{3}}, \therefore a_{4} = b_{1}b_{2}b_{3}, \cdots, a_{n} = b_{1}b_{2}b_{3}\cdots b_{n - 1}, \therefore a_{21} = b_{1}b_{2}b_{3}\cdots b_{20} = (b_{10}b_{11})^{10} = 2^{10} = 1024.$
