> [!faq]- 《专题17 数列不等式的证明-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1)设数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 $d(d \neq 0)$ ，由已知得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_{10} = 15, \\ a_{4}^{2} = a_{3}a_{7}, \end{array} \right.$ 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_{1} + 9d = 15, \\ (a_{1} + 3d)^{2} = (a_{1} + 2d)(a_{1} + 6d), \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=-3,\\ d&=2.\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore a_{n}=2n-5(n\in\mathbf{N}^{*}).$
> >
> > $$
> > (2) \because b _ {n} = \frac {a _ {n}}{2 ^ {n}} = \frac {2 n - 5}{2 ^ {n}}, n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = \frac {- 3}{2} + \frac {- 1}{2 ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{2 ^ {3}} + \dots + \frac {2 n - 5}{2 ^ {n}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{2} T _ {n} = \frac {- 3}{2 ^ {2}} + \frac {- 1}{2 ^ {3}} + \frac {1}{2 ^ {4}} + \dots + \frac {2 n - 7}{2 ^ {n}} + \frac {2 n - 5}{2 ^ {n + 1}}, \tag {②}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > - **①**：- ② \text {得} \frac {1}{2} T _ {n} = \frac {- 3}{2} + 2 \left(\frac {1}{2 ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{2 ^ {3}} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 ^ {n}}\right) - \frac {2 n - 5}{2 ^ {n + 1}} = - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1 - 2 n}{2 ^ {n + 1}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = - 1 - \frac {2 n - 1}{2 ^ {n}} (n \in \mathrm{N} ^ {*}), \quad \because \frac {2 n - 1}{2 ^ {n}} > 0 (n \in \mathrm{N} ^ {*}), \quad \therefore T _ {n} <   - 1.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n + 1} - T _ {n} = \left[ - 1 - \frac {2 n + 1}{2 ^ {n + 1}} \right] - \left[ - 1 - \frac {2 n - 1}{2 ^ {n}} \right] = \frac {2 n - 3}{2 ^ {n + 1}}, \therefore T _ {n} <   T _ {n + 1} (n \geqslant 2).
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $T_{1}=-1-\frac{1}{2}=-\frac{3}{2},\quad T_{2}=-1-\frac{4-1}{4}=-\frac{7}{4}.$ $\because T_{1}>T_{2},\quad\therefore T_{2}$ 最小，即 $T_{n}\geqslant T_{2}=-\frac{7}{4}.$
> >
> > 综上所述， $-\frac{7}{4}\leqslant T_{n}<-1(n\in\mathbf{N}^{*})$ .
