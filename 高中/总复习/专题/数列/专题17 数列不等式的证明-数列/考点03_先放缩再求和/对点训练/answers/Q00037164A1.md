> [!faq]- 《专题17 数列不等式的证明-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)证明：数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n}\right\}$ 是等差数列；
> > (2)求数列 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&1\\ &a_{n}\end{aligned}\right\}$ 的前n项和 $S_{n}$ ，并证明： $\frac{1}{S_{1}}+\frac{1}{S_{2}}+\cdots+\frac{1}{S_{n}}>\frac{n}{n+1}.$
> > 14. 解析 $\because a_{n+1} = \frac{a_n}{2a_n + 1}, \therefore \frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = \frac{2a_n + 1}{a_n}$ , 化简得 $\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = 2 + \frac{1}{a_n}$ , 即 $\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} - \frac{1}{a_n} = 2$ ,
> > 故数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n}\right\}$ 是以1为首项，2为公差的等差数列.
> > (2)由(1)知 $\frac{1}{a_n} = 2n - 1$ ，所以 $S_{n} = \frac{n(1 + 2n - 1)}{2} = n^{2}$
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{S _ {1}} + \frac {1}{S _ {2}} + \dots + \frac {1}{S _ {n}} = \frac {1}{1 ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{2 ^ {2}} + \dots + \frac {1}{n ^ {2}} > \frac {1}{1 \times 2} + \frac {1}{2 \times 3} + \dots + \frac {1}{n (n + 1)}
> > $$
> > $$
> > = \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) = 1 - \frac {1}{n + 1} = \frac {n}{n + 1}.
> > $$
