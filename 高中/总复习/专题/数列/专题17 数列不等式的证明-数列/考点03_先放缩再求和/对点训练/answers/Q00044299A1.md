> [!faq]- 《专题17 数列不等式的证明-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) $a_1 = 2, a_2 = 2(2 + 2) = 8, a_{n+1} = 2(S_n + n + 1)(n \in \mathbb{N}^*)$ , $a_n = 2(S_{n-1} + n)(n \geqslant 2)$ ,
> >
> > 两式相减，得 $a_{n+1}=3a_{n}+2(n\geqslant2)$ 。经检验，当 n=1 时上式也成立，
> >
> > 即 $a_{n + 1} = 3a_n + 2(n\geqslant 1)$ ，所以 $a_{n + 1} + 1 = 3(a_n + 1)$ ，即 $b_{n + 1} = 3b_{n}$ ，且 $b_{1} = 3$
> >
> > 故 $\{b_{n}\}$ 是首项为3，公比为3的等比数列.
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)得 $b_{n}=3^{n}$ ， $nb_{n}=n\cdot3^{n}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = 1 \times 3 + 2 \times 3 ^ {2} + 3 \times 3 ^ {3} + \dots + n \times 3 ^ {n},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > 3 T _ {n} = 1 \times 3 ^ {2} + 2 \times 3 ^ {3} + 3 \times 3 ^ {4} + \dots + n \times 3 ^ {n + 1},
> > $$
> >
> > 两式相减，得 $-2T_{n}=3+3^{2}+3^{3}+\cdots+3^{n}-n\times3^{n+1}=\frac{3(1-3^{n})}{1-3}-n\times3^{n+1}$
> >
> > 化简得 $T_{n}=\left(\frac{3}{2}n-\frac{3}{4}\right)\times3^{n}+\frac{3}{4}.$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{a _ {k}} = \frac {1}{3 ^ {k} - 1} > \frac {1}{3 ^ {k}}, \text {得} \frac {1}{a _ {1}} + \frac {1}{a _ {2}} + \frac {1}{a _ {3}} + \dots + \frac {1}{a _ {n}} > \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{3 ^ {2}} + \dots + \frac {1}{3 ^ {n}} = \frac {\frac {1}{3} \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 ^ {n}}\right)}{1 - \frac {1}{3}} = \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3 ^ {n}}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {又} \frac {1}{a _ {k}} = \frac {1}{3 ^ {k} - 1} = \frac {3 ^ {k + 1} - 1}{(3 ^ {k} - 1) (3 ^ {k + 1} - 1)} <   \frac {3 ^ {k + 1}}{(3 ^ {k} - 1) (3 ^ {k + 1} - 1)} = \frac {3}{2} \left[ \frac {1}{3 ^ {k} - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {k + 1} - 1} \right],
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \frac {1}{a _ {1}} + \frac {1}{a _ {2}} + \frac {1}{a _ {3}} + \dots + \frac {1}{a _ {n}} <   \frac {1}{2} + \frac {3}{2} \left[ \left(\frac {1}{3 ^ {2} - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {3} - 1}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{3 ^ {3} - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {4} - 1}\right) + \dots + \quad \left(\frac {1}{3 ^ {n} - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {n + 1} - 1}\right) \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {3}{2} \left(\frac {1}{3 ^ {2} - 1} - \frac {1}{3 ^ {n + 1} - 1}\right) = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {3}{1 6} - \frac {3}{2} \times \frac {1}{3 ^ {n + 1} - 1} <   \frac {1 1}{1 6},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{2 \times 3 ^ {n}} <   \frac {1}{a _ {1}} + \frac {1}{a _ {2}} + \frac {1}{a _ {3}} + \dots + \frac {1}{a _ {n}} <   \frac {1 1}{1 6}.
> > $$
