[例15] 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和为 $S_{n}$ , $a_{1} = \frac{3}{2}$ , $2S_{n} = (n + 1)a_{n} + 1(n \geqslant 2)$ .

(1)求 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式;

(2)设 $b_{n} = \frac{1}{(a_{n} + 1)^{2}} (n\in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ ，数列 $\{b_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和为 $T_{n}$ ，证明： $T_{n} <   \frac{7}{10} (n\in \mathbf{N}^{*}).$

$$
2 \left(a _ {1} + a _ {2}\right) = 3 a _ {2} + 1
$$

$$
a _ {2} = 2
$$

当 $n \geqslant 3$ 时， $2a_{n} = 2S_{n} - 2S_{n-1} = (n + 1)a_{n} - na_{n-1}$ ， $\therefore (n - 1)a_{n} = na_{n-1}$ ， $\therefore \frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{n}{n - 1}$

$\therefore\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}}=\frac{n-1}{n-2},\quad\frac{a_{n-2}}{a_{n-3}}=\frac{n-2}{n-3},\quad\cdots,\quad\frac{a_{3}}{a_{2}}=\frac{3}{2},\quad 将以上各式相乘得\frac{a_{n}}{a_{2}}=\frac{n}{2},\quad\therefore a_{n}=n.$

显然，当 n=1 时，上式不成立，当 n=2 时，上式成立． $\therefore a_{n}=\begin{cases}\frac{3}{2},&n=1,\\n,&n\geqslant2.\end{cases}$

$$
b _ {n} = \frac {1}{(a _ {n} + 1) ^ {2}} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} \frac {4}{2 5}, & n = 1, \\ \frac {1}{(n + 1) ^ {2}}, & n \geqslant 2, \end{array} \right.
$$

$$
n \geqslant 2
$$

$$
b _ {n} = \frac {1}{(n + 1) ^ {2}} <   \frac {1}{n (n + 1)} = \frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1},
$$

$$
\therefore T _ {n} = \frac {4}{2 5} + \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{4}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) = \frac {4}{2 5} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{n + 1} = \frac {3 3}{5 0} - \frac {1}{n + 1} <   \frac {3 3}{5 0} <   \frac {7}{1 0}.
$$
