[例5] 已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 中， $a_{1} = 1$ ，其前 $n$ 项的和为 $S_{n}$ ，且满足 $a_{n} = \frac{2S_{n}^{2}}{2S_{n} - 1} (n \geq 2, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ .

(1)求证：数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{S_n}\right\}$ 是等差数列；

(2)证明： $\frac{1}{3} S_1 + \frac{1}{5} S_2 + \frac{1}{7} S_3 + \ldots +\frac{1}{2n + 1} S_n <   \frac{1}{2}.$

(1) 当 $n \geq 2$ 时， $S_n - S_{n-1} = \frac{2S_n^2}{2S_n - 1}$ ， $S_{n-1} - S_n = 2S_n \cdot S_{n-1}$ ， $\frac{1}{S_n} - \frac{1}{S_{n-1}} = 2$

所以数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{S_n}\right\}$ 是以1为首项，2为公差的等差数列.

(2)由
(1)可知， $\frac{1}{S_{n}}=\frac{1}{S_{1}}+(n-1)\cdot2=2n-1$ ，所以 $S_{n}=\frac{1}{2n-1}$ .

$$
\frac {1}{3} S _ {1} + \frac {1}{5} S _ {2} + \frac {1}{7} S _ {3} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 n + 1} S _ {n} = \frac {1}{1 \times 3} + \frac {1}{3 \times 5} + \frac {1}{5 \times 7} + \dots + \frac {1}{(2 n - 1) (2 n + 1)}
$$

$$
= \frac {1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{5} + \frac {1}{5} - \frac {1}{7} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 n - 1} - \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right) = \frac {1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right) <   \frac {1}{2}.
$$
