> [!faq]- 《专题17 数列不等式的证明-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) $\because a_{n}^{2}-a_{n}+1=\left(a_{n}-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}+\frac{3}{4}>0$ ，且 $a_{1}=\frac{1}{2}>0$ ， $\therefore a_{n}>0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n} = \frac {a _ {n} ^ {2}}{a _ {n} ^ {2} - a _ {n} + 1} - a _ {n} = \frac {- a _ {n} (a _ {n} - 1) ^ {2}}{a _ {n} ^ {2} - a _ {n} + 1} <   0. \quad \therefore a _ {n + 1} <   a _ {n}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because 1 - a _ {n + 1} = 1 - \frac {a _ {n} ^ {2}}{a _ {n} ^ {2} - a _ {n} + 1} = \frac {1 - a _ {n}}{a _ {n} ^ {2} - a _ {n} + 1}, \quad \therefore \frac {1}{1 - a _ {n + 1}} = \frac {a _ {n} ^ {2} - a _ {n} + 1}{1 - a _ {n}} = \frac {1}{1 - a _ {n}} - a _ {n}.
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore a_{n}=\frac{1}{1-a_{n}}-\frac{1}{1-a_{n+1}}$ ，则 $a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}=2-\frac{1}{1-a_{n+1}}$ ，由
> > (1)可知 $0<a_{n}\leqslant\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {n} = a _ {1} + a _ {2} + \dots + a _ {n} = 2 - \frac {1}{1 - a _ {n + 1}} <   1.
> > $$
