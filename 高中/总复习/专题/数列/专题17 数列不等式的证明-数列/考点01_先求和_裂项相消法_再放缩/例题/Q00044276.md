[例 9] 已知正项数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 n 项和为 $S_{n}$ ，且 $a_{n}^{2}+2a_{n}=4S_{n}-1$ .

(1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式;

(2)若 $b_{n} = \frac{a_{n} + 1}{S_{2n - 1}\cdot S_{2n + 1}}$ ，数列 $\{b_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和为 $T_{n}$ ，求证： $\frac{2}{9}\leq T_n <   \frac{1}{4}.$

(1)由题意，当 n=1 时， $a_{1}^{2}+2a_{1}=4S_{1}-1=4a_{1}-1$ ，整理，得 $a_{1}^{2}-2a_{1}+1=0$ ，解得 $a_{1}=1$ 。

当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时，由 $a_{n}^{2} + 2a_{n} = 4S_{n} - 1$ ，得 $a_{n-1}^{2} + 2a_{n-1} = 4S_{n-1} - 1$ ，

两式相减，得 $a_{n}^{2} + 2a_{n} - a_{n - 1}^{2} - 2a_{n - 1} = 4S_{n} - 1 - 4S_{n - 1} + 1 = 4a_{n}$ ，即 $a_{n}^{2} - a_{n - 1}^{2} = 2a_{n} + 2a_{n - 1}$

$$
\therefore \left(a _ {n} + a _ {n - 1}\right) \left(a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1}\right) = 2 \left(a _ {n} + a _ {n - 1}\right). \quad \because a _ {n} + a _ {n - 1} > 0, \quad \therefore a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1} = 2,
$$

∴数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是以1为首项，2为公差的等差数列．∴ $a_{n}=1+2(n-1)=2n-1$ .

(2)由
(1)知， $S_{n}=n+\frac{n(n-1)}{2}\cdot2=n^{2}$

$$
b _ {n} = \frac {a _ {n} + 1}{S _ {2 n - 1} \cdot S _ {2 n + 1}} = \frac {2 n - 1 + 1}{(2 n - 1) ^ {2} \cdot (2 n + 1) ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{4} \cdot \frac {8 n}{(2 n - 1) ^ {2} \cdot (2 n + 1) ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{4} \left[ \frac {1}{(2 n - 1) ^ {2}} - \frac {1}{(2 n + 1) ^ {2}} \right].
$$

$$
\therefore T _ {n} = b _ {1} + b _ {2} + \dots + b _ {n} = \frac {1}{4} \left(1 - \frac {1}{3 ^ {2}}\right) + \frac {1}{4} \left(\frac {1}{3 ^ {2}} - \frac {1}{5 ^ {2}}\right) + \dots + \frac {1}{4} \left[ \frac {1}{(2 n - 1) ^ {2}} - \frac {1}{(2 n + 1) ^ {2}} \right]
$$

$$
= \frac {1}{4} \left[ 1 - \frac {1}{3 ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{3 ^ {2}} - \frac {1}{5 ^ {2}} + \dots + \frac {1}{(2 n - 1) ^ {2}} - \frac {1}{(2 n + 1) ^ {2}} \right] = \frac {1}{4} \left[ 1 - \frac {1}{(2 n + 1) ^ {2}} \right]
$$

$\therefore T_{n} < \frac{1}{4}$ . 又 $\because a_{n} > 0, \therefore b_{n} > 0$ ，易证 $\{T_{n}\}$ 单调递增， $\therefore T_{n} \geqslant T_{1} = b_{1} = \frac{1}{4} \left(1 - \frac{1}{3^{2}}\right) = \frac{2}{9}, \therefore \frac{2}{9} \leqslant T_{n} < \frac{1}{4}$ .
