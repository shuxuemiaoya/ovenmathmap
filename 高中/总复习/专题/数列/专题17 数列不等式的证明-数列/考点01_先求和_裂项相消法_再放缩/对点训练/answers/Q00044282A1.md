> [!faq]- 《专题17 数列不等式的证明-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) $\because 4S_{n} = 5a_{n} - 5, \therefore 4a_{1} = 5a_{1} - 5, \therefore a_{1} = 5.$ 当 $n \geq 2$ 时， $4S_{n-1} = 5a_{n-1} - 5, \therefore 4a_{n} = 5a_{n} - 5a_{n-1}, \therefore a_{n} = 5a_{n-1}$ ， $\therefore \{a_{n}\}$ 是以 5 为首项，5 为公比的等比数列， $\therefore a_{n} = 5 \cdot 5^{n-1} = 5^{n}$ . $\therefore b_{n} = \log_5 5^{n} = n.$
> > (2) $\because c_{n} = \frac{1}{n(n+1)} = \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}, \therefore T_{n} = \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\right) + \ldots + \left(\frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}\right) = 1 - \frac{1}{n+1} < 1.$
