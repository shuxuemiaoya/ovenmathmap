> [!faq]- 《专题20 数列不等式恒成立与存在性问题大题-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1)设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 $d$ ，由 $\left\{\begin{array}{l} a_{22}-3a_{7}=2, \\ (\sqrt{S_{2}-3})^{2}=\frac{1}{a_{2}}\cdot S_{3}, \end{array}\right.$ 得 $\left\{\begin{array}{l} (a_{1}+21d)-3(a_{1}+6d)=2, \\ (2a_{1}+d-3)\cdot(a_{1}+d)=3a_{1}+3d, \end{array}\right.$ 即 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}-2a_{1}+3d=2, \\ (a_{1}+d)(2a_{1}+d-6)=0, \end{array}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{array}{l} a_{1}=2, \\ d=2 \end{array}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{array}{l} a_{1}=-\frac{2}{5}, \\ d=\frac{2}{5}. \end{array}\right.$
> >
> > 当 $a_{1}=-\frac{2}{5}$ ， $d=\frac{2}{5}$ 时， $\sqrt{S_{2}-3}=\sqrt{-\frac{17}{5}}$ 没有意义， $\therefore a_{1}=2,\quad d=2$ ，此时 $a_{n}=2+2(n-1)=2n$ .
> > (2) $b_{n}=\frac{2}{a_{n}a_{n+2}}=\frac{1}{2n(n+2)}=\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+2}\right),$ $T_{n}=b_{1}+b_{2}+b_{3}+\cdots+b_{n}$ $=\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1-1}{1}-\frac{1}{3}\right)+\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\right)+\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{5}\right)+\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{6}\right)+\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{7}\right)+\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{6}-\frac{1}{8}\right)+\cdots+\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{n-1}-\frac{1}{n+1}\right)+\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+2}\right)$ $=\frac{1}{4}\left(1+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{n+1}-\frac{1}{n+2}\right)=\frac{3}{8}-\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}\right),$ $\therefore 8T_{n}=3-2\left(\frac{1}{n+1}+\frac{1}{n+2}\right)<3.$ 为满足题意，必须使 $2\lambda^{2}+5\lambda\geqslant3,\quad\therefore\lambda\geqslant\frac{1}{2}$ 或 $\lambda\leqslant-3.$
