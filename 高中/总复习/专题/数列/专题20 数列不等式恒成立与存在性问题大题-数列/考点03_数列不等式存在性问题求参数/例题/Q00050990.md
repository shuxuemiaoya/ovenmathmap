(1) $S_{n}=\frac{1}{2}(a_{n}^{2}+a_{n})$ ，即 $a_{n}^{2}+a_{n}-2S_{n}=0$ ，①

当 $n \geq 2$ 时， $S_{n-1} = \frac{1}{2}(a_{n-1}^2 + a_{n-1})$ ，即 $a_{n-1}^2 + a_{n-1} - 2S_{n-1} = 0$ ，②

①一②得 $(a_{n}-a_{n-1})(a_{n}+a_{n-1})+a_{n}-a_{n-1}-2a_{n}=0,\quad(a_{n}+a_{n-1})(a_{n}-a_{n-1}-1)=0,$ ∵ $a_{n}>0,\quad\therefore a_{n}-a_{n-1}=1,$

当 n=1 时， $a_{1}^{2}+a_{1}-2a_{1}=0,\quad\because a_{n}>0,\quad\therefore a_{1}=1,\quad\therefore a_{n}=1+(n-1)=n.$

(2)由
(1)知 $b_{n} = \frac{n}{2^{n - 1}}$ ，所以 $T_{n} = 1\times (\frac{1}{2})^{0} + 2\times (\frac{1}{2})^{1} + \ldots +n(\frac{1}{2})^{n - 1}$ ，③

$\frac{1}{2} T_{n} = 1\times (\frac{1}{2})^{1} + 2\times (\frac{1}{2})^{2} + \dots +n(\frac{1}{2})^{n},$ ④

③-④得 $\frac{1}{2} T_n = 1 + \frac{1}{2} +\ldots +(\frac{1}{2})^{n - 1} - n(\frac{1}{2})^n = 2[1 - (\frac{1}{2})^n ] - n(\frac{1}{2})^n,$

故 $T_{n} = 4[1 - (\frac{1}{2})^{n}] - 2n(\frac{1}{2})^{n} = 4 - 4 \times (\frac{1}{2})^{n} - 2n(\frac{1}{2})^{n} = 4 - (2n + 4)(\frac{1}{2})^{n}$ . 易知 $T_{n} < 4$ ,

$$
\because T _ {n + 1} - T _ {n} = 4 - (2 n + 6) \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n + 1} - 4 + (2 n + 4) \cdot \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n} = (n + 1) \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n} > 0,
$$

$\therefore T_{n}\geq T_{1}=1$ ，故存在正整数 m=1 满足题意.
