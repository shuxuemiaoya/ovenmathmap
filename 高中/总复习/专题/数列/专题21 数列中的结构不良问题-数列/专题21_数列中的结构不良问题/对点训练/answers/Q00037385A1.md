> [!faq]- 《专题21 数列中的结构不良问题-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 若选择①,
> >
> > $$
> > \because 2 S _ {n} = (n + 1) a _ {n}, \quad n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*}, \quad \therefore 2 S _ {n + 1} = (n + 2) a _ {n + 1}, \quad n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*}.
> > $$
> >
> > 两项相减得 $2a_{n + 1} = (n + 2)a_{n + 1} - (n + 1)a_n$ ，整理得 $na_{n + 1} = (n + 1)a_n$ 即 $\frac{a_{n + 1}}{n + 1} = \frac{a_n}{n}, n\in \mathbf{N}^*$
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{\frac{a_n}{n}\right\}$ 为常数列. $\frac{a_n}{n} = \frac{a_1}{1} = 1, \therefore a_n = n.$ （或由 $\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{n+1}{n}$ ，利用相乘相消法，求得 $a_n = n$ ）
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {k} = k, S _ {k + 2} = (k + 2) \times 1 + \frac {(k + 2) (k + 1)}{2} \times 1 = \frac {(k + 2) (k + 3)}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $a_1, a_k, S_{k+2}$ 成等比数列， $\therefore (k+2)(k+3)=2k^2, k^2-5k-6=0$ ，解得 $k=6$ 或 $k=-1$ （舍去）。 $\therefore k=6$ 。若选择②，
> >
> > 由 $\sqrt{S_n} +\sqrt{S_{n - 1}} = a_n(n\geq 2)$ 变形得 $\sqrt{S_n} +\sqrt{S_{n - 1}} = S_n - S_{n - 1},\quad \sqrt{S_n} +\sqrt{S_{n - 1}} = (\sqrt{S_n} +\sqrt{S_{n - 1}})(\sqrt{S_n} -\sqrt{S_{n - 1}}),$
> >
> > 易知 $S_{n} > 0$ ， $\therefore \sqrt{S_n} -\sqrt{S_{n - 1}} = 1$ ， $\{\sqrt{S_n}\}$ 为等差数列，而 $\sqrt{S_1} = \sqrt{a_1} = 1$ ， $\therefore \sqrt{S_n} = n$ ， $S_{n} = n^{2}$
> >
> > $\therefore a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n - 1} = 2n - 1(n\geq 2)$ ，且 $n = 1$ 时也满足， $\therefore a_n = 2n - 1$
> >
> > $\because a_{1},\quad a_{k},\quad S_{k+2}$ 成等比数列， $\therefore (k+2)^{2}=(2k-1)^{2},\quad \therefore k=3$ 或 $k=-\frac{1}{3}$ ，又 $k\in N^{*},\quad \therefore k=3$ .
> >
> > 若选择③，
> >
> > $$
> > \because a _ {n} ^ {2} + a _ {n} = 2 S _ {n} (n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*}), \quad \therefore a _ {n - 1} ^ {2} + a _ {n - 1} = 2 S _ {n - 1} (n \geq 2).
> > $$
> >
> > 两式相减得 $a_{n}^{2}-a_{n-1}^{2}+a_{n}-a_{n-1}=2S_{n}-2S_{n-1}=2a_{n}(n\geq2)$ ,
> >
> > 整理得 $(a_{n}-a_{n-1})(a_{n}+a_{n-1})=a_{n}+a_{n-1}(n\geq2)$ .
> >
> > $\because a_{n}>0,\quad\therefore a_{n}-a_{n-1}=1(n\geq2),\quad\therefore\{a_{n}\}$ 是等差数列， $\therefore a_{n}=1+(n-1)\times1=n,$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {k + 2} = (k + 2) \times 1 + \frac {(k + 2) (k + 1)}{2} \times 1 = \frac {(k + 2) (k + 3)}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $a_{1}$ ， $a_{k}$ ， $S_{k+2}$ 成等比数列， $\therefore (k+2)(k+3)=2k^{2}$ ，
> >
> > 解得 k=6 或 k=-1，又 $k \in N^{*}$ ，∴ k=6.
