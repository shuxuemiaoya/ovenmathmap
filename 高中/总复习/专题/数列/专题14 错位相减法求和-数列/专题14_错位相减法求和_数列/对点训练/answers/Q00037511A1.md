> [!faq]- 《专题14 错位相减法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ ， $\{b_{n}\}$ 的通项公式；
> > (2)设 $c_{n} = \frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}}$ ，求数列 $\{c_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n}$
> > 10. 解析 (1) $\because a_{2} = 2, a_{3} + a_{5} = 8, \therefore 2 + d + 2 + 3d = 8, \therefore d = 1, \therefore a_{n} = n(n \in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ . $\because b_{n+1} = S_{n} + 2 (n \in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ , ① $\therefore b_{n} = S_{n-1} + 2 (n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}, n \geqslant 2)$ . ②
> > 由①-②，得 $b_{n+1} - b_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n-1} = b_{n} (n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}, n \geqslant 2)$ , $\therefore b_{n+1} = 2b_{n} (n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}, n \geqslant 2)$ . $\because b_{1} = 2, b_{2} = 2b_{1}$ , $\therefore \{b_{n}\}$ 是首项为 2，公比为 2 的等比数列， $\therefore b_{n} = 2^{n} (n \in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ .
> > (2)由 $c_{n} = \frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}} = \frac{n}{2^{n}}$ ，得 $T_{n} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{2}{2^{2}} + \frac{3}{2^{3}} + \cdots + \frac{n - 1}{2^{n - 1}} + \frac{n}{2^{n}}$ , $\frac{1}{2} T_{n} = \frac{1}{2^{2}} + \frac{2}{2^{3}} + \frac{3}{2^{4}} + \cdots + \frac{n - 1}{2^{n}} + \frac{n}{2^{n + 1}}$ ,
> > 两式相减，得 $\frac{1}{2} T_{n} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2^{2}} + \cdots + \frac{1}{2^{n}} - \frac{n}{2^{n + 1}} = 1 - \frac{2 + n}{2^{n + 1}}$ ,
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = 2 - \frac {n + 2}{2 ^ {n}} (n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*})
> > $$
> > 3. $f(a_{n + 1}, a_n) = 0+$ 错位相减法型
