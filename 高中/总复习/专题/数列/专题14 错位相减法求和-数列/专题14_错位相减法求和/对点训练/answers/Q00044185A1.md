> [!faq]- 《专题14 错位相减法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1)由题意知 $a_{1}=1,\quad\because a_{n}=-3S_{n}+4,\quad\therefore a_{n+1}=-3S_{n+1}+4.$
> >
> > 两式相减并化简得 $a_{n + 1} = \frac{1}{4} a_n$ ， $\therefore a_{n} = \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{n - 1}$ $b_{n} = -\log_{2}a_{n + 1} = -\log_{2}\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{n} = 2n.$
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)知 $c_{n} = \frac{n}{2^{n}}$ 则 $T_{n} = \frac{1}{2} +\frac{2}{2^{2}} +\frac{3}{2^{3}} +\ldots +\frac{n}{2^{n}},$ ①
> >
> > $\frac{1}{2} T_{n} = \frac{1}{2^{2}} +\frac{2}{2^{3}} +\dots +\frac{n - 1}{2^{n}} +\frac{n}{2^{n + 1}},$ ②
> > - **①**：-②得， $\frac{1}{2}T_{n}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2^{2}}+\frac{1}{2^{3}}+\ldots+\frac{1}{2^{n}}-\frac{n}{2^{n+1}}=1-\frac{n+2}{2^{n+1}}.$
> >
> > $\therefore T_{n} = 2 - \frac{n + 2}{2^{n}}$ 可得 $T_{n} + \frac{n + 2}{2^{n}} = 2$
