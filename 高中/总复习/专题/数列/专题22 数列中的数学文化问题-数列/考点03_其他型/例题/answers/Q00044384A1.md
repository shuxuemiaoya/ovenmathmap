> [!faq]- 《专题22 数列中的数学文化问题-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 由题意知所得新数列为 $1 \times \frac{n}{2}, \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{n}{2}, \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{n}{2}, \ldots, \frac{1}{n} \times \frac{n}{2}$ ，所以 $a_1 a_2 + a_2 a_3 + a_3 a_4 + \ldots + a_{n-1} a_n = \frac{n^2}{4} \left[ \frac{1}{1 \times 2} + \frac{1}{2 \times 3} + \frac{1}{3 \times 4} + \ldots + \frac{1}{(n-1) \times n} \right] = \frac{n^2}{4} \left[ \left[ 1 - \frac{1}{2} \right] + \left( \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} \right) + \left( \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4} \right) + \ldots + \left[ \frac{1}{n-1} - \frac{1}{n} \right] \right] = \frac{n^2}{4} \left[ 1 - \frac{1}{n} \right] = \frac{n(n-1)}{4}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
