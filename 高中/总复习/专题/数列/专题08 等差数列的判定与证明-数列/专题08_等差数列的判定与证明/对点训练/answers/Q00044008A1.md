> [!faq]- 《专题08 等差数列的判定与证明-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) $\because na_{n+1}-(n+1)a_n=1+2+3+\cdots+n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2},$ $\therefore \frac{na_{n+1}}{n(n+1)}-\frac{(n+1)a_n}{n(n+1)}=\frac{a_{n+1}}{n+1}-\frac{a_n}{n}=\frac{1}{2},$ $\therefore$ 数列 $\left\{\frac{a_n}{n}\right\}$ 是首项为 1，公差为 $\frac{1}{2}$ 的等差数列.
> > (2)由
> > (1)知， $\frac{a_n}{n}=1+\frac{1}{2}(n-1)=\frac{n+1}{2}, \therefore a_n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}.$ $\therefore b_n=\frac{1}{a_n}=\frac{2}{n(n+1)}=2\left[\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}\right].$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {n} = b _ {1} + b _ {2} + \dots + b _ {n} = 2 \left(1 - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3} + \dots + \frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) = \frac {2 n}{n + 1}.
> > $$
