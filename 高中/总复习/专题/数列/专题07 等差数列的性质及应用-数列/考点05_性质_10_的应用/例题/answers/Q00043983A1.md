>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析

> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{29}{43}$ 解析 $\frac{a_{11}}{b_6 + b_{10}} +\frac{a_5}{b_7 + b_9} = \frac{a_{11} + a_5}{2b_8} = \frac{2a_8}{2b_8} = \frac{a_8}{b_8},\therefore \frac{a_8}{b_8} = \frac{S_{2\times 8 - 1}}{T_{2\times 8 - 1}} = \frac{S_{15}}{T_{15}} = \frac{2\times 15 - 1}{3\times 15 - 2} = \frac{29}{43}.$
