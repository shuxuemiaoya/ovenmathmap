> [!faq]- 《专题07 等差数列的性质及应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{11}{17}$ 解析 由 $\frac{a_n}{b_n} = \frac{2a_n}{2b_n} = \frac{a_1 + a_{2n - 1}}{b_1 + b_{2n - 1}} = \frac{\frac{(2n - 1)(a_1 + a_{2n - 1})}{2}}{\frac{(2n - 1)(b_1 + b_{2n - 1})}{2}} = \frac{S_{2n - 1}}{T_{2n - 1}} = \frac{2n}{3n + 1}$ . 则 $\frac{S_{21}}{T_{21}} = \frac{S_{2\times 11 - 1}}{T_{2\times 11 - 1}} = \frac{a_{11}}{b_{11}} = \frac{2\times 11}{3\times 11 + 1} = \frac{11}{17}$ .
