> [!faq]- 《专题07 等差数列的性质及应用-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 2018 解析 $\because S_{n}$ 是等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和， $\therefore \left\{\frac{S_n}{n}\right\}$ 是等差数列，设其公差为 $d$ . $\because \frac{S_{2022}}{2022} - \frac{S_{2020}}{2020} = 2, \therefore 2d = 2, d = 1.$ $\because a_1 = -2, \therefore \frac{S_1}{1} = -2.$ $\therefore \frac{S_n}{n} = -2 + (n - 1) \times 1 = n - 3.$ $\therefore \frac{S_{2021}}{2021} = 2018.$
