> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 -110 解析 由 $\left\{\frac{S_n}{n}\right\}$ 也是等差数列，构造新的等差数列 $b_{1} = \frac{S_{10}}{10} = 10, b_{10} = \frac{S_{100}}{100} = \frac{1}{10}$ ，则 $d = \frac{1}{9}(b_{10} - b_{1}) = \frac{1}{9}\left(-\frac{99}{10}\right) = -\frac{11}{10}$ ，所以 $b_{11} = \frac{S_{110}}{110} = b_{10} + d = \frac{1}{10} + \left(-\frac{11}{10}\right) = -1$ ，所以 $S_{110} = -110$ 。
