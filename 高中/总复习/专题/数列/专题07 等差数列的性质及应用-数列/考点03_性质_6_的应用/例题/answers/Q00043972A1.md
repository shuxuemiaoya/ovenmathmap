> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 2n-110 解析 设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 d， $\because\frac{S_{9}}{9}-\frac{S_{7}}{7}=2,\quad\therefore\frac{9-1}{2}d-\frac{7-1}{2}d=2,\quad\therefore d=2,$ $\because a_{1}=-9,\quad\therefore a_{n}=-9+2(n-1)=2n-11,\quad S_{10}=10\times(-9)+\frac{10\times9}{2}\times2=0.$
