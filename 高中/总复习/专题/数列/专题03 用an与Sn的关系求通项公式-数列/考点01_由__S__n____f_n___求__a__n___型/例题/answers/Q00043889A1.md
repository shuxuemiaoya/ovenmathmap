> [!faq]- 《专题03 用an与Sn的关系求通项公式-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 4, n = 1, \\ 2 \times 3^{n - 1}, n \geq 2 \end{array} \right.$ 解析 当 $n = 1$ 时， $a_1 = S_1 = 3 + 1 = 4$ ；当 $n \geq 2$ 时， $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1} = (3^n + 1) - (3^n - 1 + 1) = 2 \times 3^{n - 1}$ 。当 $n = 1$ 时， $2 \times 3^{1 - 1} = 2 \neq a_1$ ，所以 $a_n = \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 4, n = 1, \\ 2 \times 3^{n - 1}, n \geq 2. \end{array} \right.$
