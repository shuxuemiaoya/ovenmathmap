> [!faq]- 《专题06 等差数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $3n^{2} - 2n$ 解析 设 $b_{n} = 2n - 1, c_{m} = 3m - 2, b_{n} = c_{m}$ , 则 $2n - 1 = 3m - 2$ , 得 $n = \frac{3m - 1}{2} = \frac{3m - 3 + 2}{2} = \frac{3(m - 1)}{2} + 1$ , 于是 $m - 1 = 2k, k \in \mathbf{N}$ , 所以 $m = 2k + 1, k \in \mathbf{N}$ , 则 $a_{k} = 3(2k + 1) - 2 = 6k + 1, k \in \mathbf{N}$ , 得 $a_{n} = 6n - 5, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$ . 故 $S_{n} = \frac{1 + 6n - 5}{2} \times n = 3n^{2} - 2n$ .
