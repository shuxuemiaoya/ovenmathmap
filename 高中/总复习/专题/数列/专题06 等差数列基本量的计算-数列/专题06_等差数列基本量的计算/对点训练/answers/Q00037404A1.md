> [!faq]- 《专题06 等差数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 设 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 $d$ ，由 $\left\{\begin{aligned} & a_{1} + 3d + 5a_{1} + \frac{5 \times 4}{2} d = 2, \\ & 7a_{1} + \frac{7 \times 6}{2} d = 14, \end{aligned}\right.$ 可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned} & 6a_{1} + 13d = 2, \\ & a_{1} + 3d = 2, \end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned} & a_{1} = -4, \\ & d = 2, \end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $a_{10} = -4 + 9 \times 2 = 14$ ，选 C.
