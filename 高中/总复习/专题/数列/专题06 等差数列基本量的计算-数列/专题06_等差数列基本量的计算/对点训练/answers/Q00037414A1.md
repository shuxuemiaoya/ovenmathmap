> [!faq]- 《专题06 等差数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 $d$ ，依题意得 $\left\{\begin{aligned} & S_{11}=11a_{1}+\frac{11\times(11-1)}{2}d=22,\\ & a_{4}=a_{1}+3d=-12,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned} & a_{1}=-33,\\ & d=7,\end{aligned}\right.\therefore a_{m}=a_{1}+(m-1)d=7m-40=30,\therefore m=10.$
