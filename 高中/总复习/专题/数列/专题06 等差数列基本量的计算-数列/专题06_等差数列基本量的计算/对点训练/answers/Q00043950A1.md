> [!faq]- 《专题06 等差数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1)设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为d，由 $S_{3}+S_{4}=S_{5}$ ，可得 $a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}=a_{5}$
> >
> > 即 $3a_{2} = a_{5}$ ，故 $3(1 + d) = 1 + 4d$ ，解得 $d = 2$ ： $\therefore a_{n} = 1 + (n - 1)\times 2 = 2n - 1.$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {
> > (2) \text {由}
> > (1) \text {可得} b _ {n} = (- 1) ^ {n - 1} \cdot (2 n - 1) \cdot (2 n + 1) = (- 1) ^ {n - 1} \cdot (4 n ^ {2} - 1).} \\ & {\therefore T _ {2 n} = (4 \times 1 ^ {2} - 1) - (4 \times 2 ^ {2} - 1) + (4 \times 3 ^ {2} - 1) - (4 \times 4 ^ {2} - 1) + \dots + (- 1) ^ {2 n - 1} \cdot [ 4 \times (2 n) ^ {2} - 1 ]} \\ & {= 4 [ 1 ^ {2} - 2 ^ {2} + 3 ^ {2} - 4 ^ {2} + \dots + (2 n - 1) ^ {2} - (2 n) ^ {2} ] = - 4 (1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + \dots + 2 n - 1 + 2 n)} \\ & {= - 4 \times \frac {2 n (2 n + 1)}{2} = - 8 n ^ {2} - 4 n.} \end{array}
> > $$
