> [!faq]- 《专题06 等差数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 解析 由题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_{m} = a_{1} + (m - 1)d = 4, \\ S_{m} = ma_{1} + \frac{m(m - 1)}{2} d = 0, \\ S_{m + 2} - S_{m} = a_{m + 1} + a_{m + 2} = 2a_{1} + (m + m + 1)d = 14, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_{1} = -4, \\ m = 5, \\ d = 2, \end{array} \right.$ $a_{n}$ $= -4 + (n - 1)\times 2 = 2n - 6,\therefore a_{2022} = 2\times 2022 - 6 = 4038.$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
