> [!faq]- 《专题06 等差数列基本量的计算-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 $\because$ 对于任意 $n > 1, n \in \mathbf{N}^*$ , 满足 $S_{n+1} + S_{n-1} = 2(S_n + 1)$ , $\therefore S_{n+1} - S_n = S_n - S_{n-1} + 2$ , $\therefore a_n + 1 - a_n = 2$ . $\therefore$ 数列 $\{a_n\}$ 在 $n > 1, n \in \mathbf{N}^*$ 时是等差数列, 公差为 2, 又 $a_1 = 1, a_2 = 2, a_n = 2 + (n - 2) \times 2 = 2n - 2(n > 1, n \in \mathbf{N}^*)$ , $\therefore a_9 = 2 \times 9 - 2 = 16, a_{10} = 2 \times 10 - 2 = 18, S_9 = 1 + 8 \times 2 + \frac{8 \times 7}{2} \times 2 = 73, S_{10} = 1 + 9 \times 2 + \frac{9 \times 8}{2} \times 2 = 91$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
