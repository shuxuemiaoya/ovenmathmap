> [!faq]- 《专题16 数列的奇偶项讨论问题-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \{a _ {n} \}
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = a _ {1} + (n - 1) d, \quad a _ {n + 1} = a _ {1} + n d.
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {n + 1} + a _ {n} = 4 n - 3
> > $$
> > $$
> > (a _ {1} + n d) + [ a _ {1} + (n - 1) d ] = 4 n - 3
> > $$
> > $$
> > 2 d = 4, \quad 2 a _ {1} - d = - 3
> > $$
> > $$
> > d = 2, a _ {1} = - \frac {1}{2}.
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {n + 1} + a _ {n} = 4 n - 3 (n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*})
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {n + 2} + a _ {n + 1} = 4 n + 1 (n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*})
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {n + 2} - a _ {n} = 4
> > $$
> > $$
> > \left\{a _ {2 n - 1} \right\}
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {1}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \{a _ {2 n} \}
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {2} + a _ {1} = 1, \quad a _ {1} = 2
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {2} = - 1
> > $$
> > 所以 $a_{n}=\begin{cases}2n(n\text{为奇数}),\\2n-5(n\text{为偶数}).\end{cases}$
> > - **①**：当 n 为奇数时， $S_{n}=a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+\ldots+a_{n}=(a_{1}+a_{2})+(a_{3}+a_{4})+\ldots+(a_{n-2}+a_{n-1})+a_{n}=1+9+\ldots+(4n-11)+2n=\frac{\frac{n-1}{2}\times(1+4n-11)}{2}+2n=\frac{2n^{2}-3n+5}{2};$
> > - **②**：当 n 为偶数时， $S_{n}=a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+\ldots+a_{n}=(a_{1}+a_{2})+(a_{3}+a_{4})+\ldots+(a_{n-1}+a_{n})=1+9+\ldots+(4n-7)=\frac{2n^{2}-3n}{2}.$ $\therefore S_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{2n^{2}-3n+5}{2}\quad(n\text{为奇数}),\\&\frac{2n^{2}-3n}{2}\quad(n\text{为偶数}).\end{aligned}\right.$
