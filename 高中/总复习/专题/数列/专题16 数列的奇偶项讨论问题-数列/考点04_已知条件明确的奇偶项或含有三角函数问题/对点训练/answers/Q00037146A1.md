> [!faq]- 《专题16 数列的奇偶项讨论问题-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 $a_{n}=n^{2}\sin\left(\frac{2n+1}{2}\pi\right)=\left\{\begin{aligned}-n^{2},&n\text{为奇数,}\\ n^{2},&n\text{为偶数,}\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore a_{1}+a_{2}+a_{3}+\cdots+a_{2018}=-1^{2}+2^{2}-3^{2}+4^{2}-\cdots-2017^{2}+2018^{2}=(2^{2}-1^{2})+(4^{2}-3^{2})+\cdots+(2018^{2}-2017^{2})=1+2+3+4+\cdots+2018=\frac{2018\times2019}{2}.$
