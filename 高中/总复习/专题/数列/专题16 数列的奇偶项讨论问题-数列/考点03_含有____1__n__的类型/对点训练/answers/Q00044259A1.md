> [!faq]- 《专题16 数列的奇偶项讨论问题-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求 $a_3$ ;
> >
> > (2)求 $S_{1}+S_{2}+\cdots+S_{100}$ .
> >
> > 20. 解析
> > (1) 令 $n = 4$ ，则 $S_{4} = a_{4} - \frac{1}{2^{4}}$ , $\therefore S_{3} = -\frac{1}{2^{4}}$ .
> >
> > 令 $n = 3$ ，则 $S_{3} = -a_{3} - \frac{1}{2^{3}}$ ， $\therefore a_3 = -S_3 - \frac{1}{2^3} = -\frac{1}{2^4}.$
> >
> > (2)当 $n = 1$ 时， $a_1 = -\frac{1}{4}$ ;
> >
> > 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时， $a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n-1} = (-1)^{n} \cdot a_{n} - \frac{1}{2^{n}} - (-1)^{n-1} \cdot a_{n-1} + \frac{1}{2^{n-1}} = (-1)^{n} \cdot a_{n} + (-1)^{n} \cdot a_{n-1} + \frac{1}{2^{n}}$ ,
> >
> > 即 $a_{n} = (-1)^{n}\cdot a_{n} + (-1)^{n}\cdot a_{n - 1} + \frac{1}{2^{n}}.$ (\*)
> > - **①**：当 $n$ 为偶数时，由\*式可得 $a_{n - 1} + \frac{1}{2^n} = 0$ ，则 $a_{n - 1} = -\frac{1}{2^n}$ ， $\therefore a_n = -\frac{1}{2^{n + 1}}$ ，此时 $n$ 为奇数.
> > - **②**：当 $n$ 为奇数时，由\*式可得 $a_{n - 1} = -2a_{n} + \frac{1}{2^{n}} = -2\cdot \left(-\frac{1}{2^{n + 1}}\right) + \frac{1}{2^n} = \frac{1}{2^{n - 1}},\therefore a_n = \frac{1}{2^n}$ ，此时 $n$ 为偶数.
> >
> > 综上所述， $a_{n} = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} - \frac{1}{2^{n + 1}}, & n\text{为奇数},\\ \frac{1}{2^{n}}, & n\text{为偶数}. \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore S_1 + S_2 + \dots +S_{100} = (-a_1 + a_2) + (-a_3 + a_4) + \dots +( - a_{99} + a_{100}) - \left(\frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{2^2} +\dots +\frac{1}{2^{100}}\right)$ $= 2\left(\frac{1}{4} +\frac{1}{16} +\dots +\frac{1}{2^{100}}\right) - \left(\frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{2^2} +\dots +\frac{1}{2^{100}}\right) = \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2^{100}} -1\right).$
