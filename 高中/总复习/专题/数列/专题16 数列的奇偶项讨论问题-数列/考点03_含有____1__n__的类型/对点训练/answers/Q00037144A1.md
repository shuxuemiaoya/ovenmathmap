> [!faq]- 《专题16 数列的奇偶项讨论问题-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 (1) $S_{2n-1}=\frac{(2n-1)(a_1+a_{2n-1})}{2}=a_n(2n-1)=a_n^2,\quad\because a_n\neq0,\quad\therefore a_n=2n-1(n\in\mathbf{N}^*)$ .
> > (2) $b_{n}=\frac{n}{a_{n}a_{n+1}}(-1)^{n}=\frac{n}{(2n-1)(2n+1)}(-1)^{n}=\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{2n-1}+\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)(-1)^{n},$ 当 n 为偶数时 $T_{n}=\frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{1}{1}-\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{7}+\cdots+\frac{1}{2n-1}+\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)=\frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{1}{1}+\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)=\frac{-n}{4n+2},$ 当 n 为奇数时 $T_{n}=\frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{1}{1}-\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{7}+\cdots-\frac{1}{2n-1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)=\frac{1}{4}\left(-\frac{1}{1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)=\frac{-n-1}{4n+2}.$ 所以 $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}-\frac{n}{4n+2},&n\text{为偶数,}\\-\frac{n+1}{4n+2},&n\text{为奇数.}\end{aligned}\right.$
