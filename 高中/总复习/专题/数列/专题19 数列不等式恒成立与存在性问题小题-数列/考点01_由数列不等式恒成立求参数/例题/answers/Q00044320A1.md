> [!faq]- 《专题19 数列不等式恒成立与存在性问题小题-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 $\because 2S_{n} = a_{n}^{2} + a_{n}$ , ①, 且 $a_{n} > 0$ , $\therefore$ 当 $n = 1$ 时, $2S_{1} = a_{1}^{2} + a_{1}$ , 解得 $a_{1} = 1$ 或 $a_{1} = 0$ (舍去). 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, $2S_{n-1} = a_{n-1}^{2} + a_{n-1}$ , ②, ①-②得 $2a_{n} = a_{n}^{2} + a_{n} - (a_{n-1}^{2} + a_{n-1})$ , $a_{n}^{2} - a_{n-1}^{2} - a_{n} - a_{n-1} = 0$ , 即 $(a_{n} + a_{n-1})(a_{n} - a_{n-1} - 1) = 0$ , $\because a_{n} > 0$ , $\therefore a_{n} - a_{n-1} = 1$ , $\therefore \{a_{n}\}$ 是以 1 为首项, 1 为公差的等差数列, $\therefore a_{n} = n$ , $\therefore b_{n} = \frac{2^{a_{n}}}{(2^{a_{n}} - 1)(2^{a_{n+1}} - 1)} = \frac{2^{n}}{(2^{n} - 1)(2^{n+1} - 1)} = \frac{1}{2^{n} - 1} - \frac{1}{2^{n+1} - 1}$ , $\therefore T_{n} = \frac{1}{2^{1} - 1} - \frac{1}{2^{2} - 1} + \frac{1}{2^{2} - 1} - \frac{1}{2^{3} - 1} + \cdots + \frac{1}{2^{n} - 1} - \frac{1}{2^{n+1} - 1} = \frac{1}{2^{1} - 1} - \frac{1}{2^{n+1} - 1} < 1$ , $\because k > T_{n}$ 恒成立, $\therefore k \geqslant 1$ , 即 $k$ 的最小值为 1.
