> [!faq]- 《专题04 用累加法与累乘法求通项公式-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $4 - \frac{1}{n}$ 解析 原递推公式可化为 $a_{n + 1} - a_n = \frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1}$ 则 $a_{n} = (a_{n} - a_{n - 1}) + (a_{n - 1} - a_{n - 2}) + \ldots +(a_2$ $-a_{1}) + a_{1} = \left(\frac{1}{n - 1} -\frac{1}{n}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{n - 2} -\frac{1}{n - 1}\right) + \ldots +(1 - \frac{1}{2}) + 3 = 1 - \frac{1}{n} +3 = 4 - \frac{1}{n}.$
