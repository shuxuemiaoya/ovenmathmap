> [!faq]- 《专题04 用累加法与累乘法求通项公式-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1)由题知， $\frac{b_{n+1}+2}{b_{n}+2}=\frac{2b_{n}+2+2}{b_{n}+2}=2,$
> >
> > $\because b_{1}=a_{2}-a_{1}=4-2=2,\quad\therefore b_{1}+2=4,\quad\therefore$ 数列 $\{b_{n}+2\}$ 是以4为首项，2为公比的等比数列.
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)可得， $b_{n}+2=4\cdot2^{n-1}$ ，故 $b_{n}=2^{n+1}-2$ ． $\because a_{n+1}-a_{n}=b_{n}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {2} - a _ {1} = b _ {1}, \quad a _ {3} - a _ {2} = b _ {2}, \quad a _ {4} - a _ {3} = b _ {3}, \quad \dots \dots a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1} = b _ {n - 1}.
> > $$
> >
> > 累加得， $a_{n}-a_{1}=b_{1}+b_{2}+b_{3}+\cdots+b_{n-1}(n\geqslant2)$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = 2 + (2 ^ {2} - 2) + (2 ^ {3} - 2) + (2 ^ {4} - 2) + \dots + (2 ^ {n} - 2) = 2 + \frac {2 ^ {2} (1 - 2 ^ {n - 1})}{1 - 2} - 2 (n - 1) = 2 ^ {n + 1} - 2 n,
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $a_{n} = 2^{n + 1} - 2n(n\geqslant 2)$
> >
> > $\because a_{1} = 2 = 2^{1 + 1} - 2\times 1,\therefore$ 数列 $\{a_n\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n} = 2^{n + 1} - 2n(n\in \mathbf{N}^{*})$
