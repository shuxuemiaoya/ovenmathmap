> [!faq]- 《专题04 用累加法与累乘法求通项公式-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{2n}{n + 1}$ 解析 根据 $a_1 + \frac{a_2}{2^2} + \frac{a_3}{3^2} + \ldots + \frac{a_n}{n^2} = a_n$ ，①，有 $a_1 + \frac{a_2}{2^2} + \frac{a_3}{3^2} + \ldots + \frac{a_{n-1}}{(n-1)^2} = a_{n-1}(n \geq 2)$ ，②，① - ② 得 $\frac{a_n}{n^2} = a_n - a_{n-1}(n \geq 2) \Rightarrow n^2 a_{n-1} = (n^2 - 1)a_n(n \geq 2) \Rightarrow \frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{n^2}{n^2 - 1}(n \geq 2)$ ，所以 $\frac{a_2}{a_1} \times \frac{a_3}{a_2} \times \ldots \times \frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{2^2}{2^2 - 1} \times \frac{3^2}{3^2 - 1} \times \ldots \times \frac{n^2}{n^2 - 1}$ ，所以 $a_n = a_1 \times \frac{2^2}{2^2 - 1} \times \frac{3^2}{3^2 - 1} \times \ldots \times \frac{n^2}{n^2 - 1} = \frac{2^2 \times 3^2 \times 4^2 \times \ldots \times n^2}{(2 - 1) \times (2 + 1) \times (3 - 1) \times (3 + 1) \times (4 - 1) \times (4 + 1) \times (\ldots \times (n - 1) \times (n + 1)} = \frac{2^2 \times 3^2 \times 4^2 \times \ldots \times n^2}{1 \times 3 \times 2 \times 4 \times 3 \times 5 \times \ldots \times (n - 1) \times (n + 1)} = \frac{2n}{n + 1}(n \geq 2)$ . $a_1 = 1$ 满足上式， $\therefore a_n = \frac{2n}{n + 1}$ .
