> [!faq]- 《专题04 用累加法与累乘法求通项公式-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $n \cdot 2^{n-1}$ 解析 由 $2(n+1) \cdot a_n^2 + (n+2) \cdot a_n \cdot a_{n+1} - n \cdot a_{n+1}^2 = 0$ 得 $n(2a_n^2 + a_n \cdot a_{n+1} - a_{n+1}^2) + 2a_n(a_n + a_{n+1}) = 0$ , $\therefore n(a_n + a_{n+1})(2a_n - a_{n+1}) + 2a_n(a_n + a_{n+1}) = 0$ , $(a_n + a_{n+1})[(2a_n - a_{n+1}) \cdot n + 2a_n] = 0$ , 又 $a_n > 0$ , $\therefore 2n \cdot a_n + 2a_n - n \cdot a_{n+1} = 0$ , $\therefore \frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{2(n+1)}{n}$ , 又 $a_1 = 1$ , $\therefore$ 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, $a_n = \frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} \cdot \frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}} \cdots \frac{a_3}{a_2} \cdot \frac{a_2}{a_1} \cdot a_1 = \frac{2n}{n-1} \times \frac{2(n-1)}{n-2} \times \frac{2(n-2)}{n-3} \times \cdots \times \frac{2 \times 3}{2} \times \frac{2 \times 2}{1} \times 1 = 2^{n-1} \cdot n$ . 又 $n=1$ 时, $a_1 = 1$ 适合上式, $\therefore a_n = n \cdot 2^{n-1}$ .
