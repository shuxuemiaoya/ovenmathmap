> [!faq]- 《专题04 用累加法与累乘法求通项公式-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $n \cdot 2^n$ 解析 $a_{n+1} = 2\left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\right)a_n = \frac{2(n+1)}{n}a_n$ ，即 $\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{2(n+1)}{n}$ . $\therefore a_n = \frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} \cdot \frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}} \cdots \frac{a_2}{a_1} \cdot a_1 = \frac{2n}{n-1} \cdot \frac{2(n-1)}{n-2} \cdots \frac{2(1+1)}{1} \cdot 2 = n \cdot 2^n$ .
