> [!faq]- 《专题15 分组转化法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 令数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和为 $S_{n}$ ，则 $S_{20} = a_{1} + a_{2} + \dots + a_{20} = 2(1 + 2 + \dots + 20) - 3\left(\frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{5^{2}} + \dots + \frac{1}{5^{20}}\right) = 2 \times \frac{20 \times (20 + 1)}{2} - 3 \times \frac{\frac{1}{5}\left(1 - \frac{1}{5^{20}}\right)}{1 - \frac{1}{5}} = 420 - \frac{3}{4}\left(1 - \frac{1}{5^{20}}\right)$ .
