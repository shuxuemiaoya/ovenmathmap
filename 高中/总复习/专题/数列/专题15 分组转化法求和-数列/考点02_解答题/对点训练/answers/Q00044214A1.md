> [!faq]- 《专题15 分组转化法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式；
> >
> > (2)设 $b_{n} = a_{n}^{2} + \log_{2}a_{n}$ ，求数列 $\{b_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n}$ .
> >
> > 9. 解析
> > (1) 设等比数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公比为 $q(q>0)$ ，则 $a_{n}=a_{1}q^{n-1}$ ，且 $a_{n}>0$ ，
> >
> > 由已知得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_{1} + a_{1}q = 2\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}} +\frac{1}{a_{1}q}\right),\\ a_{1}q^{2} + a_{1}q^{3} = 32\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}q^{2}} +\frac{1}{a_{1}q^{3}}\right), \end{array} \right.$ 化简得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_1^2 q(q + 1) = 2(q + 1),\\ a_1^2 q^5 (q + 1) = 32(q + 1), \end{array} \right.$ 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_1^2 q = 2,\\ a_1^2 q^5 = 32, \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 又∵ $a_{1}>0,\quad q>0,\quad\therefore a_{1}=1,\quad q=2,\quad\therefore$ 数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n}=2^{n-1}$ .
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)知 $b_{n} = a_{n}^{2} + \log_{2}a_{n} = 4^{n - 1} + n - 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = (1 + 4 + 4 ^ {2} + \dots + 4 ^ {n - 1}) + (0 + 1 + 2 + 3 + \dots + n - 1) = \frac {4 ^ {n} - 1}{4 - 1} + \frac {n (n - 1)}{2} = \frac {4 ^ {n} - 1}{3} + \frac {n (n - 1)}{2}.
> > $$
