> [!faq]- 《专题15 分组转化法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式；
> > (2)设 $b_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{\log_{2}a_{n}}{n^{2}(n+2)},&n\text{为奇数,}\\ \frac{n}{a_{n}},&n\text{为偶数,}\end{aligned}\right.$
> > $$
> > T _ {n}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \{b _ {n} \}
> > $$
> > $$
> > T _ {2 n}
> > $$
> > 22. 解析
> > (1) $\because S_{2} = 2a_{2} - 2$ ，①， $S_{3} = a_{4} - 2$ ，②
> > - **②**：—①得 $a_{3}=a_{4}-2a_{2}$ ，即 $q^{2}-q-2=0$ 。又 q>0，∴q=2。
> > $\because S_{2} = 2a_{2} - 2, \therefore a_{1} + a_{2} = 2a_{2} - 2,$ 即 $a_{1} + a_{1}q = 2a_{1}q - 2, \therefore a_{1} = 2, \therefore a_{n} = 2^{n}$ .
> > (2)由
> > (1)知 $b_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{\log_{2}2^{n}}{n^{2}(n+2)},&n\text{为奇数,}\\ \frac{n}{2^{n}},&n\text{为偶数,}\end{aligned}\right.$
> > 即 $b_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{1}{n(n+2)},&n\text{为奇数,}\\ &\frac{n}{2^{n}},&n\text{为偶数,}\end{aligned}\right.$
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {2 n} = b _ {1} + b _ {2} + b _ {3} + \dots + b _ {2 n}
> > $$
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} \left(\frac {1}{1} - \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{5} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 n - 1} - \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right) + [ 2 \times 2 ^ {- 2} + 4 \times 2 ^ {- 4} + 6 \times 2 ^ {- 6} + \dots + (2 n) \times 2 ^ {- 2 n} ]
> > $$
> > $$
> > = \frac {n}{2 n + 1} + [ 2 \times 2 ^ {- 2} + 4 \times 2 ^ {- 4} + 6 \times 2 ^ {- 6} + \dots + (2 n) \times 2 ^ {- 2 n} ].
> > $$
> > 设 $A=2\times2^{-2}+4\times2^{-4}+6\times2^{-6}+\cdots+(2n)\times2^{-2n}$ ,
> > $$
> > 2 ^ {- 2} A = 2 \times 2 ^ {- 4} + 4 \times 2 ^ {- 6} + 6 \times 2 ^ {- 8} + \dots + (2 n - 2) \times 2 ^ {- 2 n} + (2 n) \times 2 ^ {- 2 n - 2}
> > $$
> > 两式相减得 $\frac{3}{4}A=\frac{1}{2}+2(2^{-4}+2^{-6}+2^{-8}+\cdots+2^{-2n})-(2n)\times2^{-2n-2}$ ，整理得 $A=\frac{8}{9}-\frac{6n+8}{9\times2^{2n}}$ $\therefore T_{2n}=\frac{8}{9}-\frac{6n+8}{9\times2^{2n}}+\frac{n}{2n+1}.$
