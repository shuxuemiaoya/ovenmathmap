> [!faq]- 《专题15 分组转化法求和-数列》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1)设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为d，等比数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的公比为 $q(q>0)$ .
> >
> > 依题意，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}3q = 3 + 2d,\\ 3q^2 = 15 + 4d, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}d = 3,\\ q = 3, \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 故 $a_{n}=3+3(n-1)=3n,\quad b_{n}=3\times3^{n-1}=3^{n}.$
> >
> > 所以 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n}=3n,\quad\{b_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $b_{n}=3^{n}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > (2) a _ {1} c _ {1} + a _ {2} c _ {2} + \dots + a _ {2 n} c _ {2 n} = (a _ {1} + a _ {3} + a _ {5} + \dots + a _ {2 n - 1}) + (a _ {2} b _ {1} + a _ {4} b _ {2} + a _ {6} b _ {3} + \dots + a _ {2 n} b _ {n})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left[ \underbrace {n \times 3 + \frac {n (n - 1)}{2} \times 6} _ {\text {   }} \right] + (6 \times 3 ^ {1} + 1 2 \times 3 ^ {2} + 1 8 \times 3 ^ {3} + \dots + 6 n \times 3 ^ {n}) = 3 n ^ {2} + 6 (1 \times 3 ^ {1} + 2 \times 3 ^ {2} + \dots + n \times 3 ^ {n}).
> > $$
> >
> > 记 $T_{n}=1\times3^{1}+2\times3^{2}+\cdots+n\times3^{n}$ ，①
> >
> > 则 $3T_{n}=1\times3^{2}+2\times3^{3}+\cdots+n\times3^{n+1}$ ，②
> >
> > $$
> > 2 T _ {n} = - 3 - 3 ^ {2} - 3 ^ {3} - \dots - 3 ^ {n} + n \times 3 ^ {n + 1} = - \frac {3 (- 3 ^ {n})}{1 - 3} + n \times 3 ^ {n + 1} = \frac {(2 n - 1) 3 ^ {n + 1} + 3}{2}. \tag {②-①}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {1} c _ {1} + a _ {2} c _ {2} + \dots + a _ {2 n} c _ {2 n} = 3 n ^ {2} + 6 T _ {n} = 3 n ^ {2} + 3 \times \frac {(2 n - 1) 3 ^ {n ^ {+ 1}} + 3}{2} = \frac {(2 n - 1) 3 ^ {n ^ {+ 2}} + 6 n ^ {2} + 9}{2} (n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {*}).
> > $$
