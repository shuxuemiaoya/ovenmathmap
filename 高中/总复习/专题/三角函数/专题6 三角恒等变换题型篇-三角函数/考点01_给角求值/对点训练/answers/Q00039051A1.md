> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\sqrt{6}$ 解析 $\left(2\sin 50^{\circ} + \sin 10^{\circ}\frac{\cos 10^{\circ} + \sqrt{3}\sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}}\right)\sqrt{2}\sin 80^{\circ} = \left(2\sin 50^{\circ} + 2\sin 10^{\circ}\frac{\frac{1}{2}\cos 10^{\circ} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}}\right).$ $\sqrt{2}\cos 10^{\circ} = 2\sqrt{2} [\sin 50^{\circ}\cdot \cos 10^{\circ} + \sin 10^{\circ}\cdot \cos (60^{\circ} - 10^{\circ})] = 2\sqrt{2}\sin (50^{\circ} + 10^{\circ}) = 2\sqrt{2}\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \sqrt{6}.$
