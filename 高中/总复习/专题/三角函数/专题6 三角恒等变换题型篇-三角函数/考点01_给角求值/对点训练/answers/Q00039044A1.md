> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 原式 $= \frac{\cos 20^{\circ}}{\cos 35^{\circ}|\sin 10^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}|} = \frac{\cos^{2}10^{\circ} - \sin^{2}10^{\circ}}{\cos 35^{\circ}(\cos 10^{\circ} - \sin 10^{\circ})} = \frac{\cos 10^{\circ} + \sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 35^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{\sqrt{2}\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cos 10^{\circ} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin 10^{\circ}\right)}{\cos 35^{\circ}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}\cos(45^{\circ} - 10^{\circ})}{\cos 35^{\circ}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}\cos 35^{\circ}}{\cos 35^{\circ}} = \sqrt{2}.$
