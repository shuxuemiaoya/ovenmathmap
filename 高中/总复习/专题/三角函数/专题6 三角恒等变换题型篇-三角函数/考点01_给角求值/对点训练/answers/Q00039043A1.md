> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 $\frac{1 - \cos^2 10^\circ}{\cos 80^\circ \sqrt{1 - \cos 20^\circ}} = \frac{\sin^2 10^\circ}{\sin 10^\circ \sqrt{1 - (1 - 2 \sin^2 10^\circ)}} = \frac{\sin^2 10^\circ}{\sqrt{2} \sin^2 10^\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
