> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 $\sin20^{\circ}\cos10^{\circ}-\cos160^{\circ}\sin10^{\circ}=\sin20^{\circ}\cos10^{\circ}+\cos20^{\circ}\sin10^{\circ}=\sin(20^{\circ}+10^{\circ})=\sin30^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2}$ ，故选 D.
> > (2) $\frac{\cos 10^{\circ}(1 + \sqrt{3}\tan 10^{\circ})}{\cos 50^{\circ}}$ 的值是 \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 2 解析 原式= $\frac{\cos10^{\circ}+\sqrt{3}\sin10^{\circ}}{\cos50^{\circ}}=\frac{2\sin(10^{\circ}+30^{\circ})}{\cos50^{\circ}}=\frac{2\sin40^{\circ}}{\sin40^{\circ}}=2.$
> > (3)计算： $\frac{\cos10^{\circ}-\sqrt{3}\cos(-100^{\circ})}{\sqrt{1-\sin10^{\circ}}}=\underline{\quad}$
> > 答案 $\sqrt{2}$ 解析 $\frac{\cos 10^{\circ} - \sqrt{3}\cos(-100^{\circ})}{\sqrt{1 - \sin 10^{\circ}}} = \frac{\cos 10^{\circ} + \sqrt{3}\cos 80^{\circ}}{\sqrt{1 - \cos 80^{\circ}}} = \frac{\cos 10^{\circ} + \sqrt{3}\sin 10^{\circ}}{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sin 40^{\circ}} = \frac{2\sin(10^{\circ} + 30^{\circ})}{\sqrt{2} \cdot \sin 40^{\circ}} = \sqrt{2}.$
> > (4) $\sin 50^{\circ}(1 + \sqrt{3}\tan 10^{\circ}) =$
> > 答案 1 解析 $\sin 50^{\circ}(1 + \sqrt{3}\tan 10^{\circ}) = \sin 50^{\circ}(1 + \tan 60^{\circ}\cdot \tan 10^{\circ}) = \sin 50^{\circ}\cdot \frac{\cos 60^{\circ}\cos 10^{\circ} + \sin 60^{\circ}\sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 60^{\circ}\cos 10^{\circ}}$
> > $$
> > = \sin 5 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot \frac {\cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {2 \sin 5 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 5 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\sin 1 0 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = 1.
> > $$
> > (5) 计算: $\frac{1 + \cos 20^{\circ}}{2\sin 20^{\circ}} - \sin 10^{\circ} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{\tan 5^{\circ}} - \tan 5^{\circ}\right) =$ \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 解析 原式 $= \frac{2\cos^210^\circ}{4\sin 10^\circ\cos 10^\circ} -\sin 10^\circ \cdot \frac{\cos^25^\circ - \sin^25^\circ}{\sin 5^\circ\cos 5^\circ} = \frac{\cos 10^\circ}{2\sin 10^\circ} -\frac{\sin 20^\circ}{\sin 10^\circ} = \frac{\cos 10^\circ - 2\sin 20^\circ}{2\sin 10^\circ}$ $= \frac{\cos 10^\circ - 2\sin(30^\circ - 10^\circ)}{2\sin 10^\circ} = \frac{\cos 10^\circ - 2\sin 30^\circ\cos 10^\circ + 2\cos 30^\circ\sin 10^\circ}{2\sin 10^\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$
