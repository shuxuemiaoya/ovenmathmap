> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 因为 $\sin 2\alpha = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = -\frac{24}{25}$ ，即 $1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{49}{25}$ ，所以 $(\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)^2 = \frac{49}{25}$ ，又 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角，所以 $\cos \alpha < \sin \alpha$ ，则 $\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha = -\frac{7}{5}$ .
> > (2)已知 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{3}{5}\left(\frac{\pi}{2} < 2\alpha < \pi\right)$ , $\tan (\alpha -\beta) = \frac{1}{2}$ , 则 $\tan (\alpha +\beta)$ 等于（ ）A. -2 B. -1 C. $-\frac{2}{11}$ D. $\frac{2}{11}$
> > 答案 A 解析 由题意，可得 $\cos 2\alpha = -\frac{4}{5}$ ，则 $\tan 2\alpha = -\frac{3}{4}$ ， $\tan (\alpha + \beta) = \tan [2\alpha - (\alpha - \beta)] = \frac{\tan 2\alpha - \tan (\alpha - \beta)}{1 + \tan 2\alpha \tan (\alpha - \beta)} = -2.$
> > (3) 已知 $\tan 2\alpha = -2\sqrt{2}$ ，且 $\frac{\pi}{4} < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，则 $\frac{2\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2} - \sin\alpha - 1}{\sqrt{2}\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)}$ 的值是（ ）A. $\sqrt{2}$ B. $-\sqrt{2}$ C. $-3 + 2\sqrt{2}$ D. $3 - 2\sqrt{2}$
> > 答案 C 解析 因为 $\frac{\pi}{4} < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，所以 $\tan \alpha > 0$ ，所以由 $\tan 2\alpha = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 - \tan^2\alpha} = -2\sqrt{2}$ 可得 $\tan \alpha = \sqrt{2}$ 。原式 $= \frac{2\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2} - 1 - \sin\alpha}{\sqrt{2}\left[\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\cos\alpha + \cos\frac{\pi}{4}\sin\alpha\right]} = \frac{\cos\alpha - \sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha + \sin\alpha} = \frac{1 - \tan\alpha}{1 + \tan\alpha} = \frac{1 - \sqrt{2}}{1 + \sqrt{2}} = -3 + 2\sqrt{2}$ 。故选 C.
> > (4) 已知 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ , $\beta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ , 且 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) = \frac{3}{5}$ , $\sin \left(\frac{5\pi}{4} + \beta\right) = -\frac{12}{13}$ , 则 $\cos (\alpha + \beta) =$ \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 $-\frac{33}{65}$ 解析 $\because \alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right), \therefore \frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, 0\right)$ ，又 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) = \frac{3}{5}, \therefore \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) = -\frac{4}{5}, \because$ $\sin \left(\frac{5\pi}{4} + \beta\right) = -\frac{12}{13}, \therefore \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta\right) = \frac{12}{13}$ ，又 $\because \beta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right), \frac{\pi}{4} + \beta \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \therefore \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta\right) = \frac{5}{13}, \therefore \cos (\alpha + \beta) =$ $\cos \left[\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta\right) - \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha\right)\right] = \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta\right) \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) + \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta\right) \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) = \frac{5}{13} \times \frac{3}{5} - \frac{12}{13} \times \frac{4}{5} = -\frac{33}{65}.$
> > (5)设 $\alpha$ 为锐角，若 $\cos \left[\frac{\alpha + \pi}{6}\right] = \frac{4}{5}$ ，则 $\sin \left[2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{12}\right]$ 的值为 \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 $\frac{17\sqrt{2}}{50}$ 解析 $\because \alpha$ 为锐角且 $\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{4}{5} >0,\therefore \alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\in \left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{\pi}{2}\right),\therefore \sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{3}{5}.\therefore \sin \left(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{12}\right)$ $= \sin \left[2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \frac{\pi}{4}\right] = \sin 2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\cos \frac{\pi}{4} -\cos 2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \sqrt{2}\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left[2\cos^2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) - 1\right] =$ $\sqrt{2}\times \frac{3}{5}\times \frac{4}{5} -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left[2\times \left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^2 -1\right] = \frac{12\sqrt{2}}{25} -\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{50} = \frac{17\sqrt{2}}{50}.$
> > (6) 已知 $\alpha \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ ，且 $2\sin^2\alpha - \sin\alpha \cdot \cos\alpha - 3\cos^2\alpha = 0$ ，则 $\frac{\sin\left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}\right)}{\sin 2\alpha + \cos 2\alpha + 1} =$ \_\_\_\_。
> > 答案 $\frac{\sqrt{26}}{8}$ 解析 $\because \alpha \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ ，且 $2\sin^2\alpha - \sin\alpha \cdot \cos\alpha - 3\cos^2\alpha = 0$ ，则 $(2\sin\alpha - 3\cos\alpha) \cdot (\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha) = 0$ ，又 $\because \alpha \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ ， $\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha > 0$ ， $\therefore 2\sin\alpha = 3\cos\alpha$ ，又 $\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha = 1$ ， $\therefore \cos\alpha = \frac{2}{\sqrt{13}}$ ， $\sin\alpha = \frac{3}{\sqrt{13}}$ ， $\therefore \frac{\sin\left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}\right)}{\sin 2\alpha + \cos 2\alpha + 1} = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}(\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha)}{(\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha)^2 + (\cos^2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha)} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4\cos\alpha} = \frac{\sqrt{26}}{8}$ .
