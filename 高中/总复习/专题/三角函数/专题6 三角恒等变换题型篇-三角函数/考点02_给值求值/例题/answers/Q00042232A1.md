> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{17\sqrt{2}}{50}$ 解析 $\because \alpha$ 为锐角且 $\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{4}{5} >0,\therefore \alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\in \left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{\pi}{2}\right),\therefore \sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{3}{5}.\therefore \sin \left(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{12}\right)$ $= \sin \left[2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \frac{\pi}{4}\right] = \sin 2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\cos \frac{\pi}{4} -\cos 2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \sqrt{2}\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left[2\cos^2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) - 1\right] =$ $\sqrt{2}\times \frac{3}{5}\times \frac{4}{5} -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left[2\times \left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^2 -1\right] = \frac{12\sqrt{2}}{25} -\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{50} = \frac{17\sqrt{2}}{50}.$
