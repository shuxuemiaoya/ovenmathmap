> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 因为 $\alpha$ 为锐角，且 $\cos \left(\frac{\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}}{6}\right) = \frac{4}{5}$ ，所以 $\sin \left(\frac{\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}}{6}\right) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \left(\frac{\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}}{6}\right)} = \frac{3}{5}$ ，所以 $\sin \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \sin 2\left(\frac{\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}}{6}\right) = 2\sin \left(\frac{\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}}{6}\right)\cos \left(\frac{\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}}{6}\right) = 2 \times \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{24}{25}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
