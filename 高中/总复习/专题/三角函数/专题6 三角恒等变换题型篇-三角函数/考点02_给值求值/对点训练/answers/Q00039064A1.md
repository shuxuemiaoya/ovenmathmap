> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{5}{8}$ 解析 因为 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \theta\right)\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} - \theta\right) = \left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cos \theta - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin \theta\right)\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cos \theta + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin \theta\right) = \frac{1}{2} (\cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta)$ $= \frac{1}{2}\cos 2\theta = \frac{1}{4}$ . 所以 $\cos 2\theta = \frac{1}{2}$ . 故 $\sin^4\theta + \cos^4\theta = \left(\frac{1 - \cos 2\theta}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1 + \cos 2\theta}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{16} + \frac{9}{16} = \frac{5}{8}$ .
