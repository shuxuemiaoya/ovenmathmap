> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 $\because \tan\left(\frac{\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}}{4}\right)=\frac{\tan\alpha+1}{1-\tan\alpha}=\frac{1}{2},\quad\therefore \tan\alpha=-\frac{1}{3},\quad\because \tan\alpha=\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=-\frac{1}{3},\quad\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha=1,$ $\alpha\in\left(-\frac{\pi}{2},0\right),\quad\therefore \sin\alpha=-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}.$ $\therefore \frac{2\sin^{2}\alpha+\sin2\alpha}{\cos\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}=\frac{2\sin\alpha(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\alpha\right)}=\frac{4\sin\alpha(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)}{\sqrt{2}(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)}=2\sqrt{2}\sin\alpha$ $=2\sqrt{2}\times\left(-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}\right)=-\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}.$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
