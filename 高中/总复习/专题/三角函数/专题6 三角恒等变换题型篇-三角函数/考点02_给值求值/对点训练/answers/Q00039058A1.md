> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 由题意知 $\tan \alpha = \tan[(\alpha + \beta) - \beta] = \frac{\tan(\alpha + \beta) - \tan \beta}{1 + \tan(\alpha + \beta)\tan \beta} = -\frac{1}{7}$ , 所以 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{2\sin \alpha\cos \alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{\tan^2\alpha + 1} = -\frac{7}{25}$ .
