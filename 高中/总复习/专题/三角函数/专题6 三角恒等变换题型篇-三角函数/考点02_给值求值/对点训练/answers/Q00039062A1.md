> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 $\because \sin \alpha = -\frac{4}{5}, \alpha \in \left[\frac{3\pi}{2}, 2\pi\right], \therefore \cos \alpha = \frac{3}{5}$ . 又 $\because \frac{\sin(\alpha + \beta)}{\cos \beta} = 2, \therefore \sin (\alpha + \beta) = 2\cos[(\alpha + \beta) - \alpha]$ . 展开并整理，得 $\frac{6}{5}\cos (\alpha + \beta) = \frac{13}{5}\sin (\alpha + \beta), \therefore \tan (\alpha + \beta) = \frac{6}{13}$ .
