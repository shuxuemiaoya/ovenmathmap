> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{\pi}{2}$ 解析 由 $\alpha - \beta \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right]$ ，且 $\cos (\alpha - \beta) = -\frac{12}{13}$ ，得 $\sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{5}{13}$ . 由 $\alpha + \beta \in \left[\frac{3\pi}{2}, 2\pi\right]$ ，且 $\cos (\alpha + \beta) = \frac{12}{13}$ ，得 $\sin (\alpha + \beta) = -\frac{5}{13}$ . $\therefore \cos 2\beta = \cos [(\alpha + \beta) - (\alpha - \beta)] = \cos (\alpha + \beta)\cos (\alpha - \beta) + \sin (\alpha + \beta)\sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{12}{13} \times \left[-\frac{12}{13}\right] + \left[-\frac{5}{13}\right] \times \frac{5}{13} = -1$ . 又 $\because \alpha + \beta \in \left[\frac{3\pi}{2}, 2\pi\right], \alpha - \beta \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right], \therefore 2\beta \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3\pi}{2}\right]. \therefore 2\beta = \pi$ 则 $\beta = \frac{\pi}{2}$ .
