> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $-\frac{3}{4}\pi$ 解析 $\because \tan \beta = -\frac{1}{7}, \tan (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore \tan \alpha = \tan [(\alpha - \beta) + \beta] = \frac{\tan (\alpha - \beta) + \tan \beta}{1 - \tan (\alpha - \beta)\tan \beta} =$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{7}}{1 - \frac {1}{2} \times \left(- \frac {1}{7}\right)} = \frac {1}{3}, \tan (2 \alpha - \beta) = \tan [ (\alpha - \beta) + \alpha ] = \frac {\tan (\alpha - \beta) + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan (\alpha - \beta) \tan \alpha} = \frac {\frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{3}}{1 - \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2}} = 1. \because \tan \alpha = \frac {1}{3} > 0, \tan \beta = -
> > $$
> >
> > $\frac{1}{7}<0,\quad\therefore\alpha\in\left(0,\quad\frac{\pi}{2}\right),\quad\beta\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\quad\pi\right),\quad\therefore\alpha-\beta\in(-\pi,0).$ 又 $\because\tan(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{1}{2}>0,\quad\therefore\alpha-\beta\in\left(-\pi,-\frac{\pi}{2}\right),\quad2\alpha-$ $-\beta=\alpha+(\alpha-\beta)\in(-\pi,0).$ 而 $\tan(2\alpha-\beta)=1,\quad\therefore2\alpha-\beta=-\frac{3}{4}\pi.$
