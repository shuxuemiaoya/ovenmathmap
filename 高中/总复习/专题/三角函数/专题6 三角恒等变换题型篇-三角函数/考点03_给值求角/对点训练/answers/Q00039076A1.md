> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{\pi}{3}$ 解析 由题意有 $\sin \alpha \cos \beta - \cos \alpha \sin \beta = \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{14}$ , 又 $0 < \beta < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ , $\therefore 0 < \alpha - \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 故 $\cos (\alpha - \beta) = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 (\alpha - \beta)} = \frac{13}{14}$ , 而 $\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{7}$ , $\therefore \sin \alpha = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{7}$ , 于是 $\sin \beta = \sin [\alpha - (\alpha - \beta)] = \sin \alpha \cos (\alpha - \beta) - \cos \alpha \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{7} \times \frac{13}{14} - \frac{1}{7} \times \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{14} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ . 又 $0 < \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 故 $\beta = \frac{\pi}{3}$ .
