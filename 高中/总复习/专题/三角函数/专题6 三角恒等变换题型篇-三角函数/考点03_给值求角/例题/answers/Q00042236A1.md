> [!faq]- 《专题6 三角恒等变换题型篇-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{\pi}{3}$ 解析 由 $\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{7}, 0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 得 $\sin \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \alpha} = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{1}{7}\right)^2} = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{7}$ . 由 $0 < \beta < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 得 $0 < \alpha - \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}$ . 又 $\because \cos (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{13}{14}, \therefore \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 (\alpha - \beta)} = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{13}{14}\right)^2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{14}$ . $\because \beta = \alpha - (\alpha - \beta), \therefore \cos \beta = \cos [\alpha - (\alpha - \beta)] = \cos \alpha \cos (\alpha - \beta) + \sin \alpha \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{1}{7} \times \frac{13}{14} + \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{7} \times \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{14} = \frac{1}{2}$ . $\because 0 < \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}, \therefore \beta = \frac{\pi}{3}$ .
