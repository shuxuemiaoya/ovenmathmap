> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 选 原式= $\sin20^{\circ}\cos10^{\circ}+\cos20^{\circ}\sin10^{\circ}=\sin(20^{\circ}+10^{\circ})=\sin30^{\circ}=\frac{1}{2}$ .
> > (2) (2018·全国II)已知 $\sin \alpha + \cos \beta = 1$ ， $\cos \alpha + \sin \beta = 0$ ，则 $\sin (\alpha + \beta) =$ \_\_\_\_。
> > 答案 $-\frac{1}{2}$ 解析 $\because \sin \alpha + \cos \beta = 1, ①, \cos \alpha + \sin \beta = 0, ②. \therefore ①^2 + ②^2$ 得 $1 + 2 (\sin \alpha \cos \beta + \cos \alpha \sin$ $\beta) + 1 = 1, \therefore \sin \alpha \cos \beta + \cos \alpha \sin \beta = -\frac{1}{2}, \therefore \sin (\alpha + \beta) = -\frac{1}{2}.$
> > (3) $\tan 25^{\circ} + \tan 35^{\circ} + \sqrt{3}\tan 25^{\circ}\tan 35^{\circ} =$ \_\_\_\_ .
> > 答案 $\sqrt{3}$ 解析 原式 $= \tan (25^{\circ} + 35^{\circ})(1 - \tan 25^{\circ}\tan 35^{\circ}) + \sqrt{3}\tan 25^{\circ}\cdot \tan 35^{\circ} = \sqrt{3}(1 - \tan 25^{\circ}\tan 35^{\circ})$ $+\sqrt{3}\tan 25^{\circ}\tan 35^{\circ} = \sqrt{3}$ .
> > (4) 设 $a = \cos 50^{\circ} \cos 127^{\circ} + \cos 40^{\circ} \cos 37^{\circ}$ , $b = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} (\sin 56^{\circ} - \cos 56^{\circ})$ , $c = \frac{1 - \tan^{2} 39^{\circ}}{1 + \tan^{2} 39^{\circ}}$ , 则 $a, b, c$ 的大小关系是 ( )
> > A. $a > b > c$ B. $b > a > c$ C. $c > a > b$ D. $a > c > b$
> > 答案 D 解析 由两角和与差的正、余弦公式及诱导公式, 可得 $a=\cos50^{\circ}\cos127^{\circ}+\cos40^{\circ}\cos37^{\circ}$ $=\cos50^{\circ}\cos127^{\circ}+\sin50^{\circ}\sin127^{\circ}=\cos(50^{\circ}-127^{\circ})=\cos(-77^{\circ})=\cos77^{\circ}=\sin13^{\circ},\quad b=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}(\sin56^{\circ}-\cos56^{\circ})$ $=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin56^{\circ}-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cos56^{\circ}=\sin(56^{\circ}-45^{\circ})=\sin11^{\circ},\quad c=\frac{1-\tan^{2}39^{\circ}}{1+\tan^{2}39^{\circ}}=\frac{1-\frac{\sin^{2}39^{\circ}}{\cos^{2}39^{\circ}}}{1+\frac{\sin^{2}39^{\circ}}{\cos^{2}39^{\circ}}}=\cos^{2}39^{\circ}-\sin^{2}39^{\circ}=\cos78^{\circ}=$
> > $\sin 12^{\circ}$ ．因为函数 $y = \sin x$ ， $x \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ 为增函数，所以 $\sin 13^{\circ} > \sin 12^{\circ} > \sin 11^{\circ}$ ，所以 $a > c > b$ 。
> > (5) 已知 $\cos\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)+\sin\alpha=\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{5}$ ，则 $\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=$ \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 $\frac{4}{5}$ 解析 由 $\cos \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \sin \alpha = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{5}$ ，可得 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos \alpha +\frac{1}{2}\sin \alpha +\sin \alpha = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{5}$ ，即 $\frac{3}{2}\sin \alpha +\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos \alpha =$ $\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{5},\therefore \sqrt{3}\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{5},$ 即 $\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{4}{5}.$
> > (6) $\cos 20^{\circ} \cdot \cos 40^{\circ} \cdot \cos 80^{\circ} =$ \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 $\frac{1}{8}$ 解析 原式 $= \frac{2\sin 20^{\circ}\cos 20^{\circ}\cos 40^{\circ}\cos 80^{\circ}}{2\sin 20^{\circ}} = \frac{2\sin 40^{\circ}\cos 40^{\circ}\cos 80^{\circ}}{4\sin 20^{\circ}} = \frac{2\sin 80^{\circ}\cos 80^{\circ}}{8\sin 20^{\circ}} =$ $\frac{\sin 160^{\circ}}{8\sin 20^{\circ}} = \frac{\sin 20^{\circ}}{8\sin 20^{\circ}} = \frac{1}{8}.$
> > (7) 化简 $\sin^{2}\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)+\sin^{2}\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)-\sin^{2}\alpha$ 的结果是 \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 $\frac{1}{2}$ 解析 原式 $= \frac{1 - \cos\left(2\alpha - \frac{\pi}{3}\right)}{2} + \frac{1 - \cos\left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)}{2} - \sin^2\alpha = 1 - \frac{1}{2}\left[\cos\left(2\alpha - \frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \cos\left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right] - \sin^2\alpha = 1 - \cos 2\alpha \cdot \cos \frac{\pi}{3} - \sin^2\alpha = 1 - \frac{\cos 2\alpha}{2} - \frac{1 - \cos 2\alpha}{2} = \frac{1}{2}.$
