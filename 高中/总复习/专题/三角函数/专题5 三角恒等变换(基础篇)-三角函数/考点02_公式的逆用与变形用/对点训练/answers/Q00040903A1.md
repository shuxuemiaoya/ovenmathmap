> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换(基础篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解 $\because \sin (\alpha - \beta) \cos \alpha - \cos (\beta - \alpha) \sin \alpha = \sin (\alpha - \beta) \cos \alpha - \cos (\alpha - \beta) \sin \alpha = \sin (\alpha - \beta - \alpha) = \sin (-\beta) = -\sin \beta = \frac{4}{5},$ $\therefore \sin \beta = -\frac{4}{5}$ ，又 $\beta$ 是第三象限角， $\therefore \cos \beta = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2 \beta} = -\frac{3}{5},$ $\therefore \sin \left[\beta + \frac{\pi}{4}\right] = \sin \beta \cos \frac{\pi}{4} + \cos \beta \sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \left[-\frac{4}{5}\right] \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \left[-\frac{3}{5}\right] \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = -\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}.$
