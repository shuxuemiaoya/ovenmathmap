> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 由题意可知 $\cos \alpha = \frac{4}{5}, \cos \left(\frac{7\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) = \cos \left(2\pi - \frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) = \cos \left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \cos \alpha \cos \frac{\pi}{4} + \sin \alpha \sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$ .
