> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换(基础篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{\sqrt{15}}{7}$ 解析 $\because \tan \left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \theta\right) = 4\cos (2\pi - \theta), \therefore \frac{\cos \theta}{\sin \theta} = 4\cos \theta,$ 又 $\because |\theta| < \frac{\pi}{2}, \therefore \sin \theta = \frac{1}{4}, \therefore 0 < \theta < \frac{\pi}{2}, \cos$ $\theta = \frac{\sqrt{15}}{4}, \tan \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{15}},$ 从而 $\tan 2\theta = \frac{2\tan\theta}{1 - \tan^2\theta} = \frac{\sqrt{15}}{7}.$
