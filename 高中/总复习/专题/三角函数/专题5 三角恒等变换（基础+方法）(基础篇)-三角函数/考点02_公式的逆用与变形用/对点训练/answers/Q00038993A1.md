> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换（基础+方法）(基础篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 $\because \tan 30^{\circ} = \tan(18^{\circ} + 12^{\circ}) = \frac{\tan 18^{\circ} + \tan 12^{\circ}}{1 - \tan 18^{\circ} \tan 12^{\circ}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}, \therefore \tan 18^{\circ} + \tan 12^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}(1 - \tan 18^{\circ} \tan 12^{\circ}), \therefore \text{原式} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$
