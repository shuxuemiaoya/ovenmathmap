> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换（基础+方法）(基础篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 $\because \alpha$ 为锐角，且 $\cos \alpha = \frac{12}{13}$ ， $\therefore \sin \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \alpha} = \frac{5}{13}$ 。 $\because \beta$ 为第三象限角，且 $\sin \beta = -\frac{3}{5}$ ， $\therefore \cos \beta = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2 \beta} = -\frac{4}{5}$ ， $\therefore \cos (\alpha - \beta) = \cos \alpha \cos \beta + \sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{12}{13} \times \left(-\frac{4}{5}\right) + \frac{5}{13} \times \left(-\frac{3}{5}\right) = -\frac{63}{65}$ .
