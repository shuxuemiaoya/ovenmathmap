(2) 函数 $y=(\cos x+\sin x)\cdot\cos\left(x-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ 的单调递增区间是( )

A. $\left[2k\pi-\frac{\pi}{8},\quad2k\pi+\frac{3\pi}{8}\right](k\in\mathbf{Z})$ B. $\left[k\pi-\frac{\pi}{8},\quad k\pi+\frac{3\pi}{8}\right](k\in\mathbf{Z})$ C. $\left[k\pi-\frac{\pi}{4},\quad k\pi+\frac{\pi}{4}\right](k\in\mathbf{Z})$ D. $\left[2k\pi-\frac{\pi}{2},\quad2k\pi+\frac{\pi}{2}\right](k\in\mathbf{Z})$

$$
y = (\cos x + \sin x) \cdot \sin x = \sin x \cos x + \sin^ {2} x = \frac {1}{2} \sin 2 x + \frac {1 - \cos 2 x}{2} = \frac {1}{2} +
$$

$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ . 函数的单调递增区间满足 $2k\pi - \frac{\pi}{2} \leq 2x - \frac{\pi}{4} \leq 2k\pi + \frac{\pi}{2} (k \in \mathbf{Z})$ ，解得 $k\pi - \frac{\pi}{8} \leq x \leq k\pi + \frac{3}{8}\pi (k \in \mathbf{Z})$ ，表示

为区间形式即 $\left[k\pi -\frac{\pi}{8}, k\pi +\frac{3\pi}{8}\right](k\in \mathbf{Z})$ ，故选B.
