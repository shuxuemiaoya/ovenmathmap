> [!faq]- 《专题7 三角恒等变换应用篇(解答题)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解
> > (1) $f(\frac{\pi}{6})=\cos^{2}\frac{\pi}{6}+\sin\frac{\pi}{6}\cos\frac{\pi}{6}=\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}=\frac{3+\sqrt{3}}{4}.$
> > (2) 因为 $f(x)=\cos^{2}x+\sin x\cos x=\frac{1+\cos 2x}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\sin 2x=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}(\sin 2x+\cos 2x)=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right),$ 所以 $f\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{\pi}{24}\right)=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{12}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}\sin \alpha+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos \alpha\right).$ 又因为 $\sin \alpha=\frac{3}{5},$ 且 $\alpha\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right)$ , 所以 $\cos \alpha=-\frac{4}{5},$ 所以 $f\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}+\frac{\pi}{24}\right)=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{3}{5}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\times\frac{4}{5}\right)=\frac{10+3\sqrt{2}-4\sqrt{6}}{20}.$
