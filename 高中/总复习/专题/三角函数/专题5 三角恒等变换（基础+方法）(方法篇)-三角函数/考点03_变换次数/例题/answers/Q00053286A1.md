> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 $\cos^{2}\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{1+\cos2\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}{2}=\frac{1+\cos\left(2\alpha-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{2}=\frac{1+\sin2\alpha}{2}=\frac{1-\frac{1}{3}}{2}=\frac{1}{3}.$
