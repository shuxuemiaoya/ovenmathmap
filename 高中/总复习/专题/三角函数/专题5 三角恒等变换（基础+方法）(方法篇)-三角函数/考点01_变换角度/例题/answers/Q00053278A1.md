> > 答案 A 解析 $\because \frac{\pi}{3} + 2\alpha = \pi - 2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \alpha\right), \therefore \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} + 2\alpha\right) = \cos \left[\pi - 2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \alpha\right)\right] = -\cos 2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \alpha\right) = -\left[1 - 2\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \alpha\right)\right] = -\left(1 - 2 \times \frac{2}{9}\right) = -\frac{5}{9}.$
