(1) $\frac{\sin 47^{\circ}-\sin 17^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ}}{\cos 17^{\circ}}=$ \_\_\_\_.

$$
\text { 答案 } \quad \frac {1}{2} \quad \text { 解析 } \quad \frac {\sin 4 7 ^ {\circ} - \sin 1 7 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 7 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\sin (1 7 ^ {\circ} + 3 0 ^ {\circ}) - \sin 1 7 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 7 ^ {\circ}}
$$

$$
= \frac {\sin 1 7 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 1 7 ^ {\circ} \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} - \sin 1 7 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 7 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\cos 1 7 ^ {\circ} \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 7 ^ {\circ}} = \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {1}{2}.
$$
