> [!faq]- 《专题7 三角恒等变换应用篇(选填+解答)(解答题)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解
> > (1) $\because \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} + \alpha\right) \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \alpha\right) = \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} + \alpha\right) \sin \left[\frac{\pi}{6} + \alpha\right) = \frac{1}{2} \sin \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{1}{4}, \therefore \sin \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{1}{2}.$ $\because \alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \therefore 2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \in \left(\pi, \frac{4\pi}{3}\right), \therefore \cos \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},$ $\therefore \sin 2\alpha = \sin \left[\left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) - \frac{\pi}{3}\right] = \sin \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) \cos \frac{\pi}{3} - \cos \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) \sin \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{1}{2}.$
> > (2) $\because \alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \therefore 2\alpha \in \left(\frac{2\pi}{3}, \pi\right)$ . 又由
> > (1) 知 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore \cos 2\alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$ $\therefore \tan \alpha - \frac{1}{\tan \alpha} = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} - \frac{\cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha} = \frac{\sin^2\alpha - \cos^2\alpha}{\sin \alpha\cos \alpha} = -\frac{2\cos 2\alpha}{\sin 2\alpha} = -2\times \frac{-\sqrt{3}}{\frac{1}{2}} = 2\sqrt{3}.$
