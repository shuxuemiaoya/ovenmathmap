> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换(方法篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$ 解析 因为 $\sin 2\theta = \frac{2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta} = \frac{2\tan\theta}{\tan^2\theta + 1} = \frac{4}{5}$ , 又由 $\theta \in (0, \frac{\pi}{4})$ , 得 $2\theta \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$ , 所以 $\cos 2\theta = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 2\theta} = \frac{3}{5}$ , 所以 $\sin (2\theta + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \sin 2\theta \cos \frac{\pi}{4} + \cos 2\theta \sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$ .
