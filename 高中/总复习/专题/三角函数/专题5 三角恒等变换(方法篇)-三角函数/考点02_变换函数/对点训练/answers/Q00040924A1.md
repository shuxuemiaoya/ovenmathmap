> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换(方法篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 由 $\tan \alpha + \frac{1}{\tan \alpha} = \frac{10}{3}$ 得 $\frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} + \frac{\cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha} = \frac{10}{3}, \therefore \frac{1}{\sin \alpha \cos \alpha} = \frac{10}{3}, \therefore \sin 2\alpha = \frac{3}{5}.\because \alpha \in (\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2}),$ $\therefore 2\alpha \in (\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi), \therefore \cos 2\alpha = -\frac{4}{5}.\therefore \sin (2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \sin 2\alpha \cos \frac{\pi}{4} + \cos 2\alpha \sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times (\frac{3}{5} - \frac{4}{5}) = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}.$
