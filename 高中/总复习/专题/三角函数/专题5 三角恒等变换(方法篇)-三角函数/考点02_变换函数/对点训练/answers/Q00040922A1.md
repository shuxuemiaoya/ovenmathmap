> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换(方法篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 $\frac{\sin 10^{\circ}}{1 - \sqrt{3} \tan 10^{\circ}} = \frac{\sin 10^{\circ} \cos 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ} - \sqrt{3} \sin 10^{\circ}} \frac{2 \sin 10^{\circ} \cos 10^{\circ}}{4 \left( \frac{1}{2} \cos 10^{\circ} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \sin 10^{\circ} \right)} = \frac{\sin 20^{\circ}}{4 \sin 30^{\circ} - 10^{\circ}} = \frac{1}{4}$ .
