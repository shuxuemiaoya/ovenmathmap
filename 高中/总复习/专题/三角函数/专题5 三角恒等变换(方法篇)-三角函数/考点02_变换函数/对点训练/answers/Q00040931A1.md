> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换(方法篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $-\frac{28}{75}$ 解析 $\frac{\sin 2\alpha + 2\sin^2\alpha}{1 - \tan\alpha} = \frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha + 2\sin^2\alpha}{1 - \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}} = \frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha(\cos\alpha + \sin\alpha)}{\cos\alpha - \sin\alpha} = \sin 2\alpha \cdot \frac{1 + \tan\alpha}{1 - \tan\alpha}$ $= \sin 2\alpha \cdot \tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)$ . 由 $\frac{17\pi}{12} < \alpha < \frac{7\pi}{4}$ , 得 $\frac{5\pi}{3} < \alpha + \frac{\pi}{4} < 2\pi$ , 又 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) = \frac{3}{5}$ , 所以 $\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) = -\frac{4}{5}$ , $\tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)$ $= -\frac{4}{3}$ . $\cos \alpha = \cos \left[\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) - \frac{\pi}{4}\right] = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$ , $\sin \alpha = -\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$ , $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{7}{25}$ . 所以 $\frac{\sin 2\alpha + 2\sin^2\alpha}{1 - \tan\alpha} = \frac{7}{25} \times \left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) = -\frac{28}{75}$ .
