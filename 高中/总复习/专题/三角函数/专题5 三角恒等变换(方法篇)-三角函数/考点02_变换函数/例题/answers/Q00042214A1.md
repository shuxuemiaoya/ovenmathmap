>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 -2 解析 原式 $= (\tan 10^{\circ} - \tan 60^{\circ})\cdot \frac{\cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 50^{\circ}} = \left(\frac{\sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}} -\frac{\sin 60^{\circ}}{\cos 60^{\circ}}\right)\cdot \frac{\cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 50^{\circ}}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - \sin 6 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}}. \frac {\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 5 0 ^ {\circ}} = - \frac {\sin (6 0 ^ {\circ} - 1 0 ^ {\circ})}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}}. \frac {\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 5 0 ^ {\circ}} = - \frac {1}{\cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}} = - 2.
> > $$
