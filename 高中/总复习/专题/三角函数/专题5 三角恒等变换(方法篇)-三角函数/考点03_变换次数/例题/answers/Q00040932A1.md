> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换(方法篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\cos 20^{\circ}$ 解析 原式 $= \sqrt{(\sin 20^{\circ} - \cos 20^{\circ})^{2}} +\sqrt{\frac{1 - (1 - 2\sin^{2}20^{\circ})}{2}} = |\sin 20^{\circ} - \cos 20^{\circ}| + \sqrt{\sin^{2}20^{\circ}}$ $= \cos 20^{\circ} - \sin 20^{\circ} + \sin 20^{\circ} = \cos 20^{\circ}.$
> > (2) 已知 $0 < \theta < \pi$ ，化简： $\frac{(1 + \sin \theta + \cos \theta)\left[\frac{\sin \theta}{2} - \cos \frac{\theta}{2}\right]}{\sqrt{2 + 2 \cos \theta}} =$ \_\_\_\_. 答案 $-\cos \theta$ 解析 由 $\theta \in (0, \pi)$ 得 $0 < \frac{\theta}{2} < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，所以 $\cos \frac{\theta}{2} > 0$ ，所以 $\sqrt{2 + 2 \cos \theta} = \sqrt{4 \cos^2 \frac{\theta}{2}} = 2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2}$ . 又 $(1 + \sin \theta + \cos \theta)\left[\frac{\sin \frac{\theta}{2} - \cos \frac{\theta}{2}}{2}\right] = \left[2 \sin \frac{\theta}{2} \cos \frac{\theta}{2} + 2 \cos^2 \frac{\theta}{2}\right] \cdot \left[\frac{\sin \frac{\theta}{2} - \cos \frac{\theta}{2}}{2}\right] = 2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2} \left[\sin^2 \frac{\theta}{2} - \cos^2 \frac{\theta}{2}\right] = -2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2} \cos \theta.$ 故原式 $= \frac{-2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2} \cos \theta}{2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2}} = -\cos \theta.$ (3) $\sqrt{3} \cos 15^\circ - 4 \sin^2 15^\circ \cos 15^\circ = (\quad)$ A. $\frac{1}{2}$ B. $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ C. 1
> > D. $\sqrt{2}$
> > 答案 D 解析 $\sqrt{3}\cos15^{\circ}-4\sin^{2}15^{\circ}\cos15^{\circ}=\sqrt{3}\cos15^{\circ}-2\sin15^{\circ}\cdot2\sin15^{\circ}\cos15^{\circ}=\sqrt{3}\cos15^{\circ}-2\sin15^{\circ}\cdot\sin30^{\circ}=\sqrt{3}\cos15^{\circ}-\sin15^{\circ}=2\cos(15^{\circ}+30^{\circ})=2\cos45^{\circ}=\sqrt{2}$ . 故选 D.
> > (4) $\sin 2\alpha = -\frac{1}{3}$ , 则 $\cos^2\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ 的值为 ( ) A. $-\frac{2}{3}$ B. $-\frac{1}{3}$ C. $\frac{1}{3}$ D. $\frac{2}{3}$
> > 答案 C 解析 $\cos^{2}\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{1+\cos2\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}{2}=\frac{1+\cos\left(2\alpha-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{2}=\frac{1+\sin2\alpha}{2}=\frac{1-\frac{1}{3}}{2}=\frac{1}{3}.$
> > (5)化简： $\sin^{2}\alpha\cdot\sin^{2}\beta+\cos^{2}\alpha\cdot\cos^{2}\beta-\frac{1}{2}\cos2\alpha\cdot\cos2\beta=$ \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 $\frac{1}{2}$ 解析 (利用降幂公式) 原式 $= \frac{1 - \cos 2\alpha}{2} \cdot \frac{1 - \cos 2\beta}{2} + \frac{1 + \cos 2\alpha}{2} \cdot \frac{1 + \cos 2\beta}{2} - \frac{1}{2} \cos 2\alpha \cdot \cos 2\beta = \frac{1}{4}(1 + \cos 2\alpha \cdot \cos 2\beta - \cos 2\alpha - \cos 2\beta) + \frac{1}{4}(1 + \cos 2\alpha \cdot \cos 2\beta + \cos 2\alpha + \cos 2\beta) - \frac{1}{2} \cos 2\alpha \cdot \cos 2\beta = \frac{1}{2}$ .
