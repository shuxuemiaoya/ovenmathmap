(2) 已知 $0 < \theta < \pi$ ，化简： $\frac{(1 + \sin \theta + \cos \theta)\left[\frac{\sin \theta}{2} - \cos \frac{\theta}{2}\right]}{\sqrt{2 + 2 \cos \theta}} =$ \_\_\_\_. 答案 $-\cos \theta$ 解析 由 $\theta \in (0, \pi)$ 得 $0 < \frac{\theta}{2} < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，所以 $\cos \frac{\theta}{2} > 0$ ，所以 $\sqrt{2 + 2 \cos \theta} = \sqrt{4 \cos^2 \frac{\theta}{2}} = 2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2}$ . 又 $(1 + \sin \theta + \cos \theta)\left[\frac{\sin \frac{\theta}{2} - \cos \frac{\theta}{2}}{2}\right] = \left[2 \sin \frac{\theta}{2} \cos \frac{\theta}{2} + 2 \cos^2 \frac{\theta}{2}\right] \cdot \left[\frac{\sin \frac{\theta}{2} - \cos \frac{\theta}{2}}{2}\right] = 2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2} \left[\sin^2 \frac{\theta}{2} - \cos^2 \frac{\theta}{2}\right] = -2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2} \cos \theta.$ 故原式 $= \frac{-2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2} \cos \theta}{2 \cos \frac{\theta}{2}} = -\cos \theta.$
