> [!faq]- 《专题5 三角恒等变换(方法篇)-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\tan\alpha$ 解析 $\frac{(\sin2\alpha+\cos2\alpha-1)(\sin2\alpha-\cos2\alpha+1)}{\sin4\alpha}=\frac{\sin^{2}2\alpha-(\cos2\alpha-1)^{2}}{2\sin2\alpha\cdot\cos2\alpha}$
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sin^ {2} 2 \alpha - \cos^ {2} 2 \alpha + 2 \cos 2 \alpha - 1}{2 \sin 2 \alpha \cdot \cos 2 \alpha} = \frac {- 2 \cos^ {2} 2 \alpha + 2 \cos 2 \alpha}{2 \sin 2 \alpha \cdot \cos 2 \alpha} = \frac {1 - \cos 2 \alpha}{\sin 2 \alpha} = \frac {2 \sin^ {2} \alpha}{2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha} = \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = \tan \alpha .
> > $$
