> [!faq]- 《专题2 同角三角函数基本关系式及诱导公式-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 将 $\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{5}$ , ①. 左右两边平方, 得 $1 + 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{25}$ , 即 $2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = -\frac{24}{25} < 0$ . 又 $\alpha \in [0, \pi]$ , $\therefore \sin \alpha > 0$ , $\cos \alpha < 0$ , 即 $\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha > 0$ , $\therefore (\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)^2 = 1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{49}{25}$ , $\therefore \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha = \frac{7}{5}$ , ②. 联立①②解得 $\sin \alpha = \frac{4}{5}$ , $\cos \alpha = -\frac{3}{5}$ , 则 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = -\frac{4}{3}$ .
