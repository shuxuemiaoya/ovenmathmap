> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 由题意知 $\sin \theta + \cos \theta = -\frac{m}{2}$ , $\sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac{m}{4}$ , 又 $(\sin \theta + \cos \theta)^2 = 1 + 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta$ , $\therefore \frac{m^2}{4} = 1 + \frac{m}{2}$ , 解得 $m = 1 \pm \sqrt{5}$ , 又 $\Delta = 4m^2 - 16m \geq 0$ , $\therefore m \leq 0$ 或 $m \geq 4$ , $\therefore m = 1 - \sqrt{5}$ .
