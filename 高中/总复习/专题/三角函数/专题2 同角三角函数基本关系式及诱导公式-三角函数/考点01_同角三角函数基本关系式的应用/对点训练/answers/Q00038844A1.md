>

> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 $\because \tan \theta = 2, \therefore \frac{\sin^{2}\theta - \sin \theta \cos \theta}{2 \cos^{2} \theta} = \frac{\tan^{2}\theta - \tan \theta}{2} = \frac{4 - 2}{2} = 1.$
