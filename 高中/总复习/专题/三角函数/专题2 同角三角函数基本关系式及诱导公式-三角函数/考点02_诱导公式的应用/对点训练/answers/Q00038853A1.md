> [!faq]- 《专题2 同角三角函数基本关系式及诱导公式-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 1 解析 原式 $= -\sin 1200^{\circ}\cos 1290^{\circ} - \cos 1020^{\circ}\sin 1050^{\circ} = -\sin (3\times 360^{\circ} + 120^{\circ})\cos (3\times 360^{\circ} + 210^{\circ}) - \cos (2\times 360^{\circ} + 300^{\circ})\sin (2\times 360^{\circ} + 330^{\circ}) = -\sin 120^{\circ}\cos 210^{\circ} - \cos 300^{\circ}\sin 330^{\circ} = -\sin (180^{\circ} - 60^{\circ})\cos (180^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}) - \cos (360^{\circ} - 60^{\circ})\cdot \sin (360^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}) = \sin 60^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ} + \cos 60^{\circ}\sin 30^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} +\frac{1}{2}\times \frac{1}{2} = 1.$
