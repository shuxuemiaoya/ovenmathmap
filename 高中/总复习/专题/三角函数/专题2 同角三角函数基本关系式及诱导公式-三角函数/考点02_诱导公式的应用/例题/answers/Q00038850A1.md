> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 原式 $= \frac{\cos(360^{\circ} - 10^{\circ}) - 2\sin(90^{\circ} - 10^{\circ})}{-\sin(270^{\circ} + 10^{\circ})} = \frac{\cos 10^{\circ} - 2\cos 10^{\circ}}{-(-\cos 10^{\circ})} = \frac{-\cos 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}} = -1$
> > (2) $\sin \frac{4}{3}\pi \cdot \cos \frac{5}{6}\pi \cdot \tan \left(-\frac{4}{3}\pi\right)$ 的值是 \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 $-\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}$ 解析 原式 $= \sin \left(\pi +\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\cdot \cos \left(\pi -\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\cdot \tan \left(-\pi -\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \left(-\sin \frac{\pi}{3}\right)\cdot \left(-\cos \frac{\pi}{6}\right)\cdot \left(-\tan \frac{\pi}{3}\right) =$ $\left[-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right]\times \left[-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right]\times (-\sqrt{3}) = -\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}.$
> > (3) 计算： $2\sin\left(-\frac{31\pi}{6}\right)+\cos12\pi+\tan\frac{7\pi}{4}=$ \_\_\_\_.
> > 答案 1 解析 原式 $= 2\sin \left(-6\pi + \frac{5\pi}{6}\right) + \cos 0 + \tan \left(2\pi - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 2\sin \frac{5\pi}{6} + 1 - \tan \frac{\pi}{4} = 2\sin \left(\pi - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + 1 - 1 = 2\sin \frac{\pi}{6} = 1.$
> > (4) 若 $\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha\right) = \frac{1}{3}$ , 则 $\cos \left(\frac{2\pi}{3} + 2\alpha\right) = (\quad)$ A. $-\frac{7}{9}$ B. $-\frac{1}{3}$ C. $\frac{1}{3}$ D. $\frac{7}{9}$
> > 答案 A 解析 $\cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}+2\alpha\right)=\cos\left[\pi-\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-2\alpha\right)\right]=-\cos\left[\frac{\pi}{3}-2\alpha\right]=-1+2\sin^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}-\alpha\right)=-\frac{7}{9}.$ 故选 A.
> > (5) 化简: $\frac{\tan(\pi + \alpha)\cos(2\pi + \alpha)\sin\left[\alpha - \frac{3\pi}{2}\right]}{\cos(-\alpha - 3\pi)\sin(-3\pi - \alpha)} = \underline{\quad}.$
> > 答案 -1 解析 原式 $= \frac{\tan \alpha \cos \alpha \sin \left[-2\pi + \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\right]}{\cos(3\pi + \alpha) [-\sin(3\pi + \alpha)]} = \frac{\tan \alpha \cos \alpha \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)}{(-\cos \alpha) \sin \alpha} = \frac{\tan \alpha \cos \alpha \cos \alpha}{(-\cos \alpha) \sin \alpha} = -\frac{\tan \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha} = -\frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} \cdot \frac{\cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha} = -1.$
> > (6)已知 $k \in \mathbf{Z}$ ，化简： $\frac{\sin(k\pi - \alpha)\cos[(k-1)\pi - \alpha]}{\sin[(k+1)\pi + \alpha]\cos(k\pi + \alpha)}$ .
> > 答案 -1 解析 当 $k = 2n(n \in \mathbf{Z})$ 时，原式 $= \frac{\sin(2n\pi - \alpha)\cos[(2n - 1)\pi - \alpha]}{\sin[(2n + 1)\pi + \alpha]\cos(2n\pi + \alpha)} = \frac{\sin(-\alpha) \cdot \cos(-\pi - \alpha)}{\sin(\pi + \alpha) \cdot \cos \alpha} = \frac{-\sin \alpha(-\cos \alpha)}{-\sin \alpha \cdot \cos \alpha} = -1$ ；当 $k = 2n + 1 (n \in \mathbf{Z})$ 时，原式 $= \frac{\sin[(2n + 1)\pi - \alpha] \cdot \cos[(2n + 1 - 1)\pi - \alpha]}{\sin[(2n + 1 + 1)\pi + \alpha] \cdot \cos[(2n + 1)\pi + \alpha]} = \frac{\sin(\pi - \alpha) \cdot \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha \cdot \cos(\pi + \alpha)} = \frac{\sin \alpha \cdot \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha(-\cos \alpha)} = -1.$
