> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 $\because \tan x=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}=-\frac{4}{3},\therefore \cos x=-\frac{3}{4}\sin x,\therefore \sin^{2}x+\cos^{2}x=\sin^{2}x+\frac{9}{16}\sin^{2}x=\frac{25}{16}\sin^{2}x=1,$
> >
> > $\therefore \sin^2 x = \frac{16}{25}$ . 又 $x \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right]$ , $\therefore \sin x = \frac{4}{5}$ , $\therefore \cos \left[-x - \frac{\pi}{2}\right] = \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{2} + x\right) = -\sin x = -\frac{4}{5}$ .
