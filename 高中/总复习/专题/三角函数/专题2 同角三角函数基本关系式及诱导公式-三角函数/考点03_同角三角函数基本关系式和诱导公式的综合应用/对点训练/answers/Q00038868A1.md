> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 C 解析 $\because \alpha \in \left[\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right]$ , 且 $\cos \alpha = -\frac{5}{13}$ , $\therefore \sin \alpha = \frac{12}{13}$ , 则 $\frac{\tan \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{\cos (\alpha + \pi)} = \frac{-\cos \alpha}{-\cos \alpha} = \frac{1}{\sin \alpha} = \frac{13}{12}$ .
