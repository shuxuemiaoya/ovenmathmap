> [!faq]- 《专题2 同角三角函数基本关系式及诱导公式-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解 $\because\sin(3\pi+\alpha)=2\sin\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}+\alpha\right)$ ， $\therefore-\sin\alpha=-2\cos\alpha$ ，即 $\sin\alpha=2\cos\alpha$ .
> >
> > (1)原式= $\frac{2\cos\alpha-4\cos\alpha}{10\cos\alpha+2\cos\alpha}=\frac{-2}{12}=-\frac{1}{6}.$
> >
> > $$
> > (2) \because \sin \alpha = 2 \cos \alpha , \therefore \tan \alpha = 2, \therefore \text {原式} = \frac {\sin^ {2} \alpha + 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {\tan^ {2} \alpha + 2 \tan \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = \frac {4 + 4}{4 + 1} = \frac {8}{5}.
> > $$
