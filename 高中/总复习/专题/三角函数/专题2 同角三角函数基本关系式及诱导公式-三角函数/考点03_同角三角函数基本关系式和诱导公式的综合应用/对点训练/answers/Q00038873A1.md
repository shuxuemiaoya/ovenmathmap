> [!faq]- 《专题2 同角三角函数基本关系式及诱导公式-三角函数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\sqrt{3}$ 解析 $\because f(a) = \frac{(-2\sin a)(-\cos a) + \cos a}{1 + \sin^2 a + \sin a - \cos^2 a} = \frac{2\sin a\cos a + \cos a}{2\sin^2 a + \sin a} = \frac{\cos a(1 + 2\sin a)}{\sin a(1 + 2\sin a)} = \frac{1}{\tan a},$
> > $$
> > \therefore f \left[ - \frac {2 3 \pi}{6} \right] = \frac {1}{\tan \left(- \frac {2 3 \pi}{6}\right)} = \frac {1}{\tan \left(- 4 \pi + \frac {\pi}{6}\right)} = \frac {1}{\tan \frac {\pi}{6}} = \sqrt {3}.
> > $$
