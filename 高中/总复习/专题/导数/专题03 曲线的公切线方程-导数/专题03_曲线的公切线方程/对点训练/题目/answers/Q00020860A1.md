> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析

> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $1 - \ln 2$ 解析 $y = \ln x + 2$ 的切线为 $y = \frac{1}{x_1} \cdot x + \ln x_1 + 1$ (设切点横坐标为 $x_1$ ). $y = \ln (x + 1)$ 的切线为 $y = \frac{1}{x_2 + 1} x + \ln (x_2 + 1) - \frac{x_2}{x_2 + 1}$ (设切点横坐标为 $x_2$ ). $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{1}{x_1} = \frac{1}{x_2 + 1}, \\ \ln x_1 + 1 = \ln (x_2 + 1) - \frac{x_2}{x_2 + 1}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $x_1 = \frac{1}{2}, x_2 = -\frac{1}{2},$ $\therefore b = \ln x_1 + 1 = 1 - \ln 2$ .
