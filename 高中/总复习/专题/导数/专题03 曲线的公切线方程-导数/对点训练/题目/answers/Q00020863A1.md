> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 设直线 $y = kx + b$ 与曲线 $y = \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{\mathrm{e}^2}$ 相切于点 $P(x_1, y_1), y' = \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{\mathrm{e}^2} = \mathrm{e}^{x - 2}, k_1 = \mathrm{e}^{x_1 - 2}$ ; 直线 $y =$ $kx+b$ 与曲线 $y=e^{x}-1$ 相切于点 $Q(x_{2},y_{2})$ , $y'=\mathrm{e}^{x}$ , $k_{2}=\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}$ , $\therefore l_{1}:y=\mathrm{e}^{x_{1}-2}x+\mathrm{e}^{x_{1}-2}-x_{1}\mathrm{e}^{x_{1}-2}$ , $l_{2}:y=\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}x+\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}-1-x_{2}\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}$ , $\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}\mathrm{e}^{x_{1}-2}&=\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}},\\ \mathrm{e}^{x_{1}-2}-x_{1}\mathrm{e}^{x_{1}-2}&=\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}-x_{2}\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}-1,\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore x_{2}=-\ln 2,$ $\therefore k+b=\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}+\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}-1-x_{2}\mathrm{e}^{x_{2}}=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}-1-(-\ln 2)\times\frac{1}{2}=\frac{\ln 2}{2}.$
