> [!faq]- 《专题01 导数的运算-导数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > y = \mathrm{e} ^ {x} \ln x; (2) y = x \left(x ^ {2} + \frac {1}{x} + \frac {1}{x ^ {3}}\right); (3) y = x - \sin \frac {x}{2} \cos \frac {x}{2}; (4) y = \ln \sqrt {1 + 2 x}. (5) f (x) = \frac {x ^ {3} + 2 x - x ^ {2} \ln x - 1}{x ^ {2}}.
> > $$
> > 16. 解析 $(1)y' = (\mathrm{e}^x)'\ln x + \mathrm{e}^x(\ln x)' = \mathrm{e}^x\ln x + \mathrm{e}^x\cdot \frac{1}{x} = \left(\ln x + \frac{1}{x}\right)\mathrm{e}^x.$
> > $$
> > \because y = x ^ {3} + 1 + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}, \quad \therefore y ^ {\prime} = 3 x ^ {2} - \frac {2}{x ^ {3}}.
> > $$
> > $$
> > \because y = x - \frac {1}{2} \sin x, \therefore y ^ {\prime} = 1 - \frac {1}{2} \cos x.
> > $$
> > $$
> > \because y = \ln \sqrt {1 + 2 x} = \frac {1}{2} \ln (1 + 2 x), \quad \therefore y ^ {\prime} = \frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {1}{1 + 2 x} \cdot (1 + 2 x) ^ {\prime} = \frac {1}{1 + 2 x}.
> > $$
> > (5)由已知 $f(x) = x - \ln x + \frac{2}{x} - \frac{1}{x^2}$ . 所以 $f'(x) = 1 - \frac{1}{x} - \frac{2}{x^2} + \frac{2}{x^3} = \frac{x^3 - x^2 - 2x + 2}{x^3}$ .
