> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $-\frac{7}{4}$ 解析 $\because f(x) = 2x^2 - 3xf'(1) + \ln x, \therefore f'(x) = 4x - 3f'(1) + \frac{1}{x}$ , 将 $x = 1$ 代入, 得 $f'(1) = 4 - 3f'(1) + 1$ , 得 $f'(1) = \frac{5}{4}.$ $\therefore f(x) = 2x^2 - \frac{15}{4}x + \ln x, \therefore f(1) = 2 - \frac{15}{4} = -\frac{7}{4}.$
