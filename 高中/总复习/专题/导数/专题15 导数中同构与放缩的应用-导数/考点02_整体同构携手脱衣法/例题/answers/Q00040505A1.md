> [!faq]- 《专题15 导数中同构与放缩的应用-导数》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) $\log_2 x - k \cdot 2^{kx} \geq 0$
> > $$
> > \log_ {2} x - k \cdot 2 ^ {k x} \geq 0 \Leftrightarrow x \log_ {2} x \geq k x \cdot 2 ^ {k x} \Leftrightarrow (\log_ {2} x) \cdot 2 ^ {\log_ {2} x} \geq k x \cdot 2 ^ {k x}, f (x) = x \cdot 2 ^ {x}
> > $$
> > (2) $x^{2}\ln x - m\mathrm{e}^{\frac{m}{x}}\geq 0$
> > 解析 $x^{2}\ln x - m\mathrm{e}^{\frac{m}{x}}\geq 0\Leftrightarrow x\ln x\geq \frac{m}{x}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{m}{x}}\Leftrightarrow \ln x + \ln (\ln x)\geq \frac{m}{x} +\ln \frac{m}{x},f(x) = x + \ln x.$
> > (3) $a(\mathrm{e}^{ax} + 1)\geq 2(x + \frac{1}{x})\ln x$ 解析 $a(\mathrm{e}^{ax} + 1)\geq 2(x + \frac{1}{x})\ln x\Leftrightarrow ax\mathrm{e}^{ax} + ax\geq 2x^2\ln x + 2\ln x\Leftrightarrow ax\mathrm{e}^{ax} + ax\geq x^2\ln x^2 +\ln x^2$ $\Leftrightarrow ax\mathrm{e}^{ax} + ax\geq \ln x^2\cdot \mathrm{e}^{\ln x^2} + \ln x^2,f(x) = xe^x +x$ (4) $x + a\ln x + \mathrm{e}^{-x}\geq x^{a}(x > 1)$
> > 解析 $x + a\ln x + \mathrm{e}^{-x}\geq x^{a}\Leftrightarrow x + \mathrm{e}^{-x}\geq x^{a} - \ln x^{a}\Leftrightarrow \mathrm{e}^{-x} - \ln \mathrm{e}^{-x}\geq x^{a} - \ln x^{a},f(x) = x - \ln x.$
> > (5) $x^{2}e^{x}+\ln x=0$
> > 解析 $x^{2}\mathrm{e}^{x} + \ln x = 0\Leftrightarrow x\mathrm{e}^{x} = -\frac{\ln x}{x}\Leftrightarrow x\mathrm{e}^{x} = -\frac{1}{x}\ln \frac{1}{x}\Leftrightarrow \mathrm{e}^{x}\ln \mathrm{e}^{x} = -\frac{1}{x}\ln \frac{1}{x},f(x) = x\ln x.$
