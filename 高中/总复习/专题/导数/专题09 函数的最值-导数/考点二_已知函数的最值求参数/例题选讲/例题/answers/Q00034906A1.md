> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 -71 解析 $f'(x) = 3x^2 - 6x - 9 = 3(x - 3)(x + 1)$ . 由 $f'(x) = 0$ 得 $x = 3$ 或 $x = -1$ . 又 $f(-4) = k - 76$ , $f(3) = k - 27$ , $f(-1) = k + 5$ , $f(4) = k - 20$ . 由 $f(x)_{\max} = k + 5 = 10$ , 得 $k = 5$ , $\therefore f(x)_{\min} = k - 76 = -71$ .
