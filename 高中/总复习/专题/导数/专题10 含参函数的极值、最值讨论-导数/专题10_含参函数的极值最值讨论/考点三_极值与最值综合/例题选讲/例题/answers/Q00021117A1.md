> [!faq]- 教师版例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 (1) $\because g'(x) = x + \frac{1}{x} + 1, g'(x) = x + \frac{1}{x} + 1 \geq 2\sqrt{x \cdot \frac{1}{x}} + 1 = 3, g'(x) \geq a, \therefore a \leq 3.$
> > (2) $\therefore f'(x)=x+1-m+\frac{1}{x}=\frac{x^{2}+(1-m)x+1}{x}$ ，又 $\because f'(x)<0$ 在 $(0,+\infty)$ 上有解，
> > 令 $h(x)=x^{2}+(1-m)x+1$ ，则 $h(0)=1>0$ ，只需 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{m-1}{2}>0,\\ (m-1)^{2}-4>0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}m>1,\\ m>0\text{ 或 }m<-1,\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 m>3
> > (3) $\because f'(x)=\frac{x^{2}+(1-m)x+1}{x}$ ，令 $f'(x)=0$ ，即 $x^{2}+(1-m)x+1=0$ ，
> > 两根分别为 $x_{1}$ ， $x_{2}$ ，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x_{1}+x_{2}&=m-1,\\ x_{1}x_{2}&=1,\end{aligned}\right.$
> > $$
> > \text {又} \because f (x _ {1}) - f (x _ {2}) = \frac {1}{2} (x _ {1} ^ {2} - x _ {2} ^ {2}) + (1 - m) (x _ {1} - x _ {2}) + \ln \frac {x _ {1}}{x _ {2}} = \frac {1}{2} (x _ {1} ^ {2} - x _ {2} ^ {2}) - (x _ {1} ^ {2} - x _ {2} ^ {2}) + \ln \frac {x _ {1}}{x _ {2}},
> > $$
> > $$
> > = \ln \frac {x _ {1}}{x _ {2}} - \frac {1}{2} (x _ {1} ^ {2} - x _ {2} ^ {2}) = \ln \frac {x _ {1}}{x _ {2}} - \frac {1}{2} \left(\frac {x _ {1}}{x _ {2}} - \frac {x _ {2}}{x _ {1}}\right).
> > $$
> > 令 $t = \frac{x_1}{x_2}$ ，由于 $x_{1} <   x_{2}$ ， $\therefore 0 <   t <   1$
> > 又 $\because m \geq \frac{7}{2}, (x_1 + x_2)^2 = (m - 1)^2 \geq \frac{25}{4}$ , 即 $\frac{(x_1 + x_2)^2}{x_1x_2} = \frac{x_1}{x_2} + 2 + \frac{x_2}{x_1}$ , 即 $t + 2 + \frac{1}{t} \geq \frac{25}{4}$ ,
> > ∴ $4t^{2}-17t+4\geq0$ ，解得 $t\geq4$ 或 $t\leq\frac{1}{4}$ ，即 $0<t\leq\frac{1}{4}$ .
> > $$
> > h (t) = \ln t - \frac {1}{2} \left[ t - \frac {1}{t} \right] (0 <   t \leq \frac {1}{4}), h ^ {\prime} (t) = \frac {1}{t} - \frac {1}{2} \left[ 1 + \frac {1}{t ^ {2}} \right] = - \frac {(t - 1) ^ {2}}{2 t ^ {2}} <   0,
> > $$
> > ∴ $h(t)$ 在 $(0, \frac{1}{4}]$ 上单调递减， $h(t)_{\min}=h(\frac{1}{4})=-2\ln2+\frac{15}{8}$ .
> > $\therefore f(x_{1}) - f(x_{2})$ 的最小值为 $-2\ln 2 + \frac{15}{8}$ .
