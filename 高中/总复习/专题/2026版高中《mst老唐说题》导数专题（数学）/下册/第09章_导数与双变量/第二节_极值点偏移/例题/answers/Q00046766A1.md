> > [!note]- **【分析】**

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > 解析 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x_{1}^{2}-x_{1}-\ln x_{1}-1=0\\ x_{2}^{2}-x_{2}-\ln x_{2}-1=0\end{aligned}\right.$ ，作差得 $x_{1}^{2}-x_{1}-\ln x_{1}-x_{2}^{2}+x_{2}+\ln x_{2}=0$ ，即 $(x_{1}+x_{2}-1)(x_{1}-x_{2})=$
> > $\ln x_{1} - \ln x_{2}$ ，即 $\frac{x_1 - x_2}{\ln x_1 - \ln x_2} = \frac{1}{(x_1 + x_2 - 1)} < \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2} \Rightarrow 2 < (x_1 + x_2)(x_1 + x_2 - 1)$ ，即 $0 < (x_{1} + x_{2})^{2} - (x_{1} + x_{2}) - 2 = (x_{1} + x_{2} + 1)(x_{1} + x_{2} - 2), x_{1} + x_{2} < -1$ （舍）， $x_{1} + x_{2} > 2$ 得证！
