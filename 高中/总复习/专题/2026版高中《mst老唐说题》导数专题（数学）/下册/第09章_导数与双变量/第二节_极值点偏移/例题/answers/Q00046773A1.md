> [!faq]- 《导数专题(下)》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 $1 < x_{1} < \mathrm{e} < x_{2}$ (自证), 由对数均值不等式:
> > $$
> > \left(\frac {x _ {1} - \mathrm{e}}{\ln x _ {1} - \ln \mathrm{e}} <   \frac {x _ {1} + \mathrm{e}}{2} \Rightarrow 2 (x _ {1} - \mathrm{e}) > (x _ {1} + \mathrm{e}) (\ln x _ {1} - 1) = (x _ {1} + \mathrm{e}) (a x _ {1} - 1) \right.
> > $$
> > $$
> > \left\{\frac {x _ {2} - \mathrm{e}}{\ln x _ {2} - \ln \mathrm{e}} > \frac {x _ {2} + \mathrm{e}}{2} \Rightarrow 2 (x _ {2} - \mathrm{e}) <   (x _ {2} + \mathrm{e}) (\ln x _ {2} - 1) = (x _ {2} + \mathrm{e}) (a x _ {2} - 1) \right.
> > $$
> > 即 $2(x_{1} - \mathrm{e}) - (x_{1} + \mathrm{e})(ax_{1} - 1) = 0 > 2(x_{2} - \mathrm{e}) - (x_{2} + \mathrm{e})(ax_{2} - 1)\Leftrightarrow x_{1} + x_{2} > \frac{3}{a} -\mathrm{e}$ 得证！
> > 本题的拟合思想我们在后续拟合篇给出!
