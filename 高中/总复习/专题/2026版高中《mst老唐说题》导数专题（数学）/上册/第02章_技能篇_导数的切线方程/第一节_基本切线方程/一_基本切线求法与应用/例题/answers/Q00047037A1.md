> [!faq]- 《导数专题(上册)》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** BCD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 $y = \begin{cases} -\ln x, & 0 < x < 1 \\ \ln x, & x \geqslant 1 \end{cases}$ , $0 < x < 1$ 时, $y' = -\frac{1}{x}$ ; $x \geqslant 1$ 时, $y' = \frac{1}{x}$ . 不妨设 $P_{1}(x_{1}, -\ln x_{1})(0 < x_{1} < 1)$ , $P_{2}(x_{2}, \ln x_{2})$ , $(x_{2} > 1)$ , 因为切线 $l_{1} \perp l_{2}$ , 所以 $k_{l_1} \cdot k_{l_2} = -\frac{1}{x_1} \times \frac{1}{x_2} = -1$ , 所以 $x_{1}x_{2} = 1$ . 切线 $l_{1}$ 、 $l_{2}$ 的方程分别为: $y + \ln x_{1} = -\frac{1}{x_{1}} (x - x_{1})$ , $y - \ln x_{2} = \frac{1}{x_{2}} (x - x_{2})$ , 联立解得 $x_{P} = \frac{2x_{1}x_{2}}{x_{1} + x_{2}} = \frac{2}{x_{1} + \frac{1}{x_{1}}} \in (0, 1)$ , $A(0, 1 - \ln x_{1})$ , $B(0, \ln x_{2} - 1)$ , 所以 $|AB| = |1 - \ln x_{1} - (\ln x_{2} + 1)| = |2 + \ln (x_{1}x_{2})| = 2$ 为定值, 所以 $\Delta ABP$ 面积 $S = \frac{1}{2} \times |AB| \cdot x_{P} = x_{P} \in (0, 1)$ ;
> >
> > $P_{1}$ 、 $P_{2}$ 两点的纵坐标之积为 $-\ln x_{1}\ln x_{2} = \ln^{2}x_{1}$ 不为定值；直线 $P_{1}P_{2}$ 的斜率为 $\frac{-\ln x_1 - \ln x_2}{x_1 - x_2} =$ $\frac{-\ln(x_1x_2)}{x_1 - x_2} = 0$ 为值．综上可得：只有BCD正确．
> >
> > 故选 **BCD**.
