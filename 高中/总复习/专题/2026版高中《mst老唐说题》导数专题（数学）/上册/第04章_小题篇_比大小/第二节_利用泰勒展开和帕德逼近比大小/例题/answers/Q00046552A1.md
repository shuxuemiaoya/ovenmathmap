> [!faq]- 《导数专题(上)》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 构造 $A(x) = xe^{x}$ , $B(x) = \frac{x}{1 - x}$ , $C(x) = -\ln (1 - x)$ , 由于有相同的 $\Delta x = 0.1$ , $A'(x) = (x + 1)e^{x}$ , $A'(0) = 1$ , $A''(x) = (x + 2)e^{x}$ , $A''(0) = 2$ , $B'(x) = \frac{1}{(x - 1)^2}$ , $B'(0) = 1$ , $B''(x) = \frac{-2}{(x - 1)^3}$ , $B''(0) = 2$ , $C'(x) = \frac{1}{1 - x}$ , $C'(0) = 1$ , $C''(x) = \frac{1}{(x - 1)^2}$ , $C''(0) = 1$ , $A''(0) = B''(0) > C''(0)$ , 所以 $a > c$ , 比较 $A'''(0)$ 和 $B'''(0)$ , $A'''(x) = (x + 3)e^{x}$ , $B'''(x) = \frac{6}{(x - 1)^4}$ , $A'''(0) < B'''(0)$ . 所以 $b > a > c$ . 法二: 由泰勒展开: $a = 0.1e^{0.1} \approx 0.1 \times \left(0.1 + 1 + \frac{0.1^2}{2} + \frac{0.1^3}{b}\right)$ , $b = \frac{1}{9}$ , $c = \ln (1 - 0.1) \approx -0.1 + \frac{1}{2} \times 0.1^2 - \frac{1}{3} \times 0.1^3$ , 计算得: $b > a > c$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
