> [!faq]- 《专题2 平面向量的数量积-平面向量》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 (0, 3] 解析 设 $\angle BAO = \theta$ , $\theta \in (0^{\circ}, 90^{\circ})$ , 则 $B(0, 2\sin \theta)$ , $C(2\cos \theta + 2\cos (120^{\circ} - \theta)$ , $2\sin (120^{\circ} - \theta))$ , 则 $\overrightarrow{OC} \cdot \overrightarrow{OB} = (0, 2\sin \theta) \cdot (2\cos \theta + 2\cos (120^{\circ} - \theta)$ , $2\sin (120^{\circ} - \theta)) = 2\sin \theta \cdot 2\sin (120^{\circ} - \theta) = 2\sqrt{3}\sin \theta\cos \theta + 2\sin^{2}\theta = 2\sin (2\theta - 30^{\circ}) + 1$ 。因为 $\theta \in (0^{\circ}, 90^{\circ})$ , 所以 $\overrightarrow{OC} \cdot \overrightarrow{OB} \in (0, 3]$ .
