>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 2 解析 因为 $\overrightarrow{AE} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$ , $\overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AB}$ , 所以 $\overrightarrow{AE} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = (\overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}) \cdot (\overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AB})$
> > $$
> > = \overrightarrow {A D} ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {A D} \cdot \overrightarrow {A B} - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2} = 2.
> > $$
