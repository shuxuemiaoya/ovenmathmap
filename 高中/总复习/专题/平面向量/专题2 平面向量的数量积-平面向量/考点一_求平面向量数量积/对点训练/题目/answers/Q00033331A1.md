> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 -7 解析 ∵ 在平面四边形 ABCD 中， $|AC|=3, |BD|=4, \therefore \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{DC} = \overrightarrow{AC} + \overrightarrow{CB} + \overrightarrow{DB} + \overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AC} + \overrightarrow{DB} = \overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{BD}, \overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{BD} + \overrightarrow{DC} + \overrightarrow{AC} + \overrightarrow{CD} = \overrightarrow{AC} + \overrightarrow{BD}, \therefore (\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{DC}) \cdot (\overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{AD}) = (\overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{BD})(\overrightarrow{AC} + \overrightarrow{BD}) = \overrightarrow{AC^2} - \overrightarrow{BD^2} = 9 - 16 = -7.$
