> [!faq]- 《专题6 平面向量与三角函数-平面向量》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析
> > (1) $\because m \perp n, \therefore 3\cos^2 A - \sin^2 A = 0, \therefore 3\cos^2 A - 1 + \cos^2 A = 0, \therefore \cos^2 A = \frac{1}{4}$ . 又 $\because \triangle ABC$ 为锐角三角形， $\therefore \cos A = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore A = \frac{\pi}{3}$ .
> >
> > (2)由
> > (1)可得 $m=\left(\frac{3}{4},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ ， $n=\left(1,-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ 。∴ $|\overrightarrow{AB}|=\frac{\sqrt{21}}{4}p,\quad|\overrightarrow{AC}|=\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}q$ $\therefore S_{\triangle ABC}=\frac{1}{2}|\overrightarrow{AB}|\cdot|\overrightarrow{AC}|\cdot\sin A=\frac{21}{32}pq.$ 又∵ $p+q=6$ ，且p>0，q>0， $\therefore\sqrt{p}\cdot\sqrt{q}\leqslant\frac{p+q}{2},\quad\therefore\sqrt{p}\cdot\sqrt{q}\leqslant3.\quad\therefore p\cdot q\leqslant9.$ $\therefore \triangle ABC$ 面积的最大值为 $\frac{21}{32} \times 9 = \frac{189}{32}.$
