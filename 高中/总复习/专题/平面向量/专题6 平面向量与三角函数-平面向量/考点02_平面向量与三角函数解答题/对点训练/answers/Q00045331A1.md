> [!faq]- 《专题6 平面向量与三角函数-平面向量》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求函数 $f(x)$ 的解析式，并指出它的定义域；
> > (2)若 $f(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{8}) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{5},\alpha \in (0,\frac{\pi}{2})$ ，求 $f(\alpha)$ 6. 解析
> > (1) $f(x) = a\cdot b = (1 - \tan x)(1 + \sin 2x + \cos 2x)$ $= \frac{\cos x - \sin x}{\cos x}\cdot (2\cos^2 x + 2\sin x\cos x) = 2(\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x) = 2\cos 2x,$ ∴定义域为 $\left\{x|x\neq k\pi +\frac{\pi}{2},k\in \mathbf{Z}\right\} .$
> > (2)∵ $f(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{8}) = 2\cos (2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{5},\therefore \cos (2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10} >0,$ 且 $2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\in (\frac{\pi}{4},\frac{5\pi}{4}),\therefore \sin (2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}.$ ∴ $f(\alpha) = 2\cos 2\alpha = 2\cos [(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}) - \frac{\pi}{4}] = 2\cos (2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4})\cos \frac{\pi}{4} +2\sin (2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4})\sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{8}{5}.$
