> [!faq]- 《专题6 平面向量与三角函数-平面向量》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)求 $A$ 的大小;
> >
> > (2)求函数 $y = 2\sin^2 B + \cos \left(\frac{C - 3B}{2}\right)$ 取最大值时， $B$ 的大小.
> >
> > $$
> > (1) \because p / / q, \therefore (2 - 2 \sin A) (1 + \sin A) - (\cos A + \sin A) (\sin A - \cos A) = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \sin^{2}A = \frac{3}{4}, \sin A = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \because \triangle ABC$ 为锐角三角形， $\therefore A = 60^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {
> > (2) y = 2 \sin^ {2} B + \cos \left(\frac {C - 3 B}{2}\right) = 2 \sin^ {2} B + \cos \left(\frac {1 8 0 ^ {\circ} - B - A - 3 B}{2}\right) = 2 \sin^ {2} B + \cos (2 B - 6 0 ^ {\circ})} \\ & {= 1 - \cos 2 B + \cos (2 B - 6 0 ^ {\circ}) = 1 - \cos 2 B + \cos 2 B \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin 2 B \sin 6 0 ^ {\circ} = 1 - \frac {1}{2} \cos 2 B + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin 2 B} \\ & {= 1 + \sin (2 B - 3 0 ^ {\circ}), \text {当} 2 B - 3 0 ^ {\circ} = 9 0 ^ {\circ}, \text {即} B = 6 0 ^ {\circ} \text {时, 函数取最大值} 2.} \end{array}
> > $$
