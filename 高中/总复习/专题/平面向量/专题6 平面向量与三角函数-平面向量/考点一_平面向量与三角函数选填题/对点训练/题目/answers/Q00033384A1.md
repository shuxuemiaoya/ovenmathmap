> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $150^{\circ}$ 解析 $\because \overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} < 0, \therefore \angle BAC$ 为钝角，又 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} |a||b| \sin \angle BAC = \frac{15}{4}.$ $\therefore \sin \angle BAC = \frac{1}{2},$ $\therefore \angle BAC = 150^{\circ}.$
