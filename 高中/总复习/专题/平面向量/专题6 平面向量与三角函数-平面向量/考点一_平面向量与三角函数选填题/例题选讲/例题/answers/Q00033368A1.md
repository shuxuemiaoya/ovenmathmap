> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $2\cos x$ 解析 $\because a + b = (\cos \frac{3x}{2} +\cos \frac{x}{2},\sin \frac{3x}{2} -\sin \frac{x}{2}),\therefore |a + b| = \sqrt{(\cos\frac{3x}{2} +\cos\frac{x}{2})^2 + (\sin\frac{3x}{2} -\sin\frac{x}{2})^2}$ $= \sqrt{2 + 2\cos 2x} = 2|\cos x|$ . $\because x\in [-\frac{\pi}{3},\frac{\pi}{4}],\therefore \cos x > 0,\therefore |a + b| = 2\cos x.$
