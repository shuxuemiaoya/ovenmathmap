> [!faq]- 教师版对点训练解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 (1) $\because m \perp n, \therefore m \cdot n = \sqrt{3} \sin A + (\cos A + 1) \times (-1) = 0, \therefore \sqrt{3} \sin A - \cos A = 1,$
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \left[ A - \frac {\pi}{6} \right] = \frac {1}{2}. \quad \because 0 <   A <   \pi , \quad \therefore - \frac {\pi}{6} <   A - \frac {\pi}{6} <   \frac {5 \pi}{6}, \quad \therefore A - \frac {\pi}{6} = \frac {\pi}{6}, \quad \therefore A = \frac {\pi}{3}.
> > $$
> > (2)在 $\triangle ABC$ 中， $A = \frac{\pi}{3}$ ， $a = 2$ ， $\cos B = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ， $\therefore \sin B = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2B} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{1}{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}.$
> > 由正弦定理知 $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B}, \therefore b = \frac{a\sin B}{\sin A} = \frac{2\times\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}, \therefore b = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}$ .
