> [!faq]- 《专题6 平面向量与三角函数-平面向量》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{5\pi}{4}$ 解析 $\because \overrightarrow{AC} = (\cos \alpha - 3, \sin \alpha), \overrightarrow{BC} = (\cos \alpha, \sin \alpha - 3), \therefore |\overrightarrow{AC}| = \sqrt{(\cos \alpha - 3)^2 + \sin^2\alpha} = \sqrt{10 - 6\cos \alpha}, |\overrightarrow{BC}| = \sqrt{10 - 6\sin \alpha}$ . 由 $|\overrightarrow{AC}| = |\overrightarrow{BC}|$ 得 $\sin \alpha = \cos \alpha$ . 又 $\alpha \in (\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3\pi}{2}), \therefore \alpha = \frac{5\pi}{4}$ .
