$$

$$

$$
\sin 2 A \cdot \overrightarrow {O A} + \sin 2 B \cdot \overrightarrow {O B} + \sin 2 C \cdot \overrightarrow {O C} = 0, \quad ④ \overrightarrow {O A} + \overrightarrow {O B} + \overrightarrow {O C} = 0
