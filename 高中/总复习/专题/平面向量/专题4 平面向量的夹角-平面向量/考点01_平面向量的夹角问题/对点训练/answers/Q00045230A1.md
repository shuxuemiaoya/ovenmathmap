> [!faq]- 《专题4 平面向量的夹角-平面向量》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 $|\overrightarrow{BA}| = 1, |\overrightarrow{BC}| = 1, \cos \angle ABC = \frac{\overrightarrow{BA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC}}{|\overrightarrow{BA}||\overrightarrow{BC}|} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ . 又 $\because 0^{\circ} \leqslant \angle ABC \leqslant 180^{\circ}, \therefore \angle ABC = 30^{\circ}$ .
