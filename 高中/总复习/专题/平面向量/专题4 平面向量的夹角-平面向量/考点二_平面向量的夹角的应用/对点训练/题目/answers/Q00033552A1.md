> [!faq]- 教师版对点训练解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 解析 由题意知 $|e_1| = |e_2| = 1, e_1 \cdot e_2 = 0, |\sqrt{3}e_1 - e_2| = \sqrt{(\sqrt{3}e_1 - e_2)^2} = \sqrt{3e_1^2 - 2\sqrt{3}e_1 \cdot e_2 + e_2^2} = \sqrt{3 - 0 + 1} = 2$ 。同理 $|e_1 + \lambda e_2| = \sqrt{1 + \lambda^2}$ 。所以 $\cos 60^\circ = \frac{(\sqrt{3}e_1 - e_2) \cdot (e_1 + \lambda e_2)}{|\sqrt{3}e_1 - e_2||e_1 + \lambda e_2|} = \frac{\sqrt{3}e_1^2 + (\sqrt{3}\lambda - 1)e_1 \cdot e_2 - \lambda e_2^2}{2\sqrt{1 + \lambda^2}} = \frac{\sqrt{3} - \lambda}{2\sqrt{1 + \lambda^2}} = \frac{1}{2}$ ，解得 $\lambda = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 。
