> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 解析 $\because e_1 \cdot e_2 = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore |e_1||e_2|\cos < e_1, e_2 > = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore <e_1, e_2 > = 60^\circ.$ 又 $\because b \cdot e_1 = b \cdot e_2 = 1 > 0,$ $\therefore <b, e_1> = <b, e_2> = 30^\circ.$ 由 $b \cdot e_1 = 1$ ，得 $|b||e_1|\cos 30^\circ = 1,\therefore |b| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}.$
