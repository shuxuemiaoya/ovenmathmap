> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $3\sqrt{2}$ 解析 依题意，可知 $|2a - b|^2 = 4|a|^2 - 4a \cdot b + |b|^2 = 4 - 4|a||b|\cdot \cos 45^\circ + |b|^2 = 4 - 2\sqrt{2} |b| + |b|^2 = 10$ ，即 $|b|^2 - 2\sqrt{2} |b| - 6 = 0$ ， $\therefore |b| = \frac{2\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{32}}{2} = 3\sqrt{2}$ (负值舍去).
