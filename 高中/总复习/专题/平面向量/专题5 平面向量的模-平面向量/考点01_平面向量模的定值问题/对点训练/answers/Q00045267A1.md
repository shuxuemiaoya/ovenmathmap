> [!faq]- 《专题5 平面向量的模-平面向量》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 依题意得 $|a| = \sqrt{2}$ , $a \cdot b = \sqrt{2} \times 2 \times \cos 45^\circ = 2$ , $\therefore |3a + b| = \sqrt{(3a + b)^2} = \sqrt{9a^2 + 6a \cdot b + b^2} = \sqrt{18 + 12 + 4} = \sqrt{34}$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
