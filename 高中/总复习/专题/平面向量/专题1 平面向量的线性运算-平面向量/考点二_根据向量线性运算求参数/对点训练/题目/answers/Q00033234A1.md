> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 B 解析 根据题意设 $\overrightarrow{BP}=n\overrightarrow{BN}(n\in\mathbf{R})$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{AP}=\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{BP}=\overrightarrow{AB}+n\overrightarrow{BN}=\overrightarrow{AB}+n(\overrightarrow{AN}-\overrightarrow{AB})=\overrightarrow{AB}+$ $n(\frac{2}{5}\overrightarrow{AC}-\overrightarrow{AB})=(1-n)\overrightarrow{AB}+\frac{n}{5}\overrightarrow{AC}$ . 又 $\overrightarrow{AP}=m\overrightarrow{AB}+\frac{2}{5}\overrightarrow{AC}, \therefore \left\{\begin{aligned}&1-n=m,\\ &\frac{n}{5}=\frac{2}{5},\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&n=2,\\ &m=-1.\end{aligned}\right.$
