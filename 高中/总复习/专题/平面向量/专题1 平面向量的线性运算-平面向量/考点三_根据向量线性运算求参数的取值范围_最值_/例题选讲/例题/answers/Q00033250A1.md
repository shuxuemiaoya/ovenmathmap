> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 A 解析 建立如图所示的直角坐标系，则 $C$ 点坐标为(2, 1). 设 $BD$ 与圆 $C$ 切于点 $E$ , 连接 $CE$ , 则 $CE \perp BD$ . 因为 $CD = 1$ , $BC = 2$ , 所以 $BD = \sqrt{1^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{5}$ , $EC = \frac{BC \cdot CD}{BD} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$ , 所以 $P$ 点的轨迹方程为 $(x - 2)^{2} + (y - 1)^{2} = \frac{4}{5}$ . 设 $P(x_0, y_0)$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_0 = 2 + \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\cos \theta, \\ y_0 = 1 + \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\sin \theta \end{array} \right.$ （ $\theta$ 为参数），而 $\overrightarrow{AP} = (x_0, y_0), \overrightarrow{AB} = (0, 1), \overrightarrow{AD} = (2, 0)$ . 因为 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \lambda \overrightarrow{AB} + \mu \overrightarrow{AD} = \lambda (0, 1) + \mu (2, 0) = (2\mu, \lambda)$ ，所以 $\mu = \frac{1}{2} x_0 = 1 + \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\cos \theta, \lambda = y_0 = 1 + \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\sin \theta$ . 两式相加，得 $\lambda + \mu = 1 + \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\sin \theta + 1 + \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\cos \theta = 2 + \sin (\theta + \varphi) \leq 3$ （其中 $\sin \varphi = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}, \cos \varphi = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$ ），当且仅当 $\theta = \frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi - \varphi, k \in \mathbf{Z}$ 时， $\lambda + \mu$ 取得最大值3．故选A.
