> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 D 解析 设 $\overrightarrow{CO}=y\overrightarrow{BC}, \because \overrightarrow{AO}=\overrightarrow{AC}+\overrightarrow{CO}=\overrightarrow{AC}+y\overrightarrow{BC}=\overrightarrow{AC}+y(\overrightarrow{AC}-\overrightarrow{AB})=-y\overrightarrow{AB}+(1+y)\overrightarrow{AC}.$ $\because \overrightarrow{BC}=3\overrightarrow{CD}$ ，点 O 在线段 CD 上（与点 C, D 不重合）， $\therefore y\in\left(0,\frac{1}{3}\right),$ $\because \overrightarrow{AO}=x\overrightarrow{AB}+(1-x)\overrightarrow{AC}, \therefore x=-y,$
> > $$
> > x \in \left[ - \frac {1}{3}, 0 \right].
> > $$
