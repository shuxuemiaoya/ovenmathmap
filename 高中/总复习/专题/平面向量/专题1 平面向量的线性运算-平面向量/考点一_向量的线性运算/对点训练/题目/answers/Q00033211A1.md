> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $-\frac{1}{4}a + \frac{1}{4}b$ 解析 由 $\overrightarrow{AN} = 3\overrightarrow{NC}$ 得， $\overrightarrow{AN} = \frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{AC} = \frac{3}{4}(a + b)$ ， $\overrightarrow{AM} = a + \frac{1}{2}b$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{MN} = \overrightarrow{AN} - \overrightarrow{AM} = \frac{3}{4}(a + b) - \left(a + \frac{1}{2}b\right) = -\frac{1}{4}a + \frac{1}{4}b$ .
