> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\left(\frac{3}{5}, -\frac{4}{5}\right)$ 解析 $\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{OB} - \overrightarrow{OA} = (4, -1) - (1, 3) = (3, -4)$ ，∴与 $\overrightarrow{AB}$ 同方向的单位向量为 $\frac{\overrightarrow{AB}}{|\overrightarrow{AB}|} = \left(\frac{3}{5}, -\frac{4}{5}\right)$
