> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案 $\frac{1}{2}$ 解析 $\because \lambda a + b$ 与 $a + 2b$ 平行， $\therefore \lambda a + b = t(a + 2b)$ ，即 $\lambda a + b = ta + 2tb$ ， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \lambda = t, \\ 1 = 2t, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \lambda = \frac{1}{2}, \\ t = \frac{1}{2}. \end{array} \right.$
