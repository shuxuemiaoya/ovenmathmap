> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 18
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $k_{2} = -2$ ，即 $k_{2} = a(x_{2} - x_{3})(x_{2} - x_{1}) < 0$ ，知 $x_{2}$ 位于 $x_{1},x_{3}$ 之间，
> >
> > 不妨设 $x_{1} < x_{2} < x_{3}$ ，则 $k_{1} > 0, k_{3} > 0$
> >
> > $$
> > k _ {1} + 4 k _ {3} = 2 \left(k _ {1} + 4 k _ {3}\right) \left(\frac {1}{k _ {1}} + \frac {1}{k _ {3}}\right) = 2 \left(5 + \frac {k _ {1}}{k _ {3}} + \frac {4 k _ {2}}{k _ {1}}\right) \geqslant 2 \left(5 + 2 \sqrt {\frac {k _ {1}}{k _ {3}} \cdot \frac {4 k _ {2}}{k _ {1}}}\right) = 1 8.
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{k_1}{k_2} = \frac{4k_2}{k_1} \\ \frac{1}{k_1} + \frac{1}{k_2} = \frac{1}{2} \end{array} \right.$ ，即 $k_{1} = 6, k_{3} = 3$ 时等号成立，故则 $k_{1} + 4k_{3}$ 的最小值为18，故答案为：18
