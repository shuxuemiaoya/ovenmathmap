## 考点四_裂项相消法

## 常见裂项相消

1. 若 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为等差数列, 且首项为 $a_{1}$ , 公差为 $d$ , 数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和为 $S_{n}$ , 则有:

① $b_{n}=\frac{1}{a_{n}a_{n+1}}=\frac{1}{d}\left(\frac{1}{a_{n}}-\frac{1}{a_{n+1}}\right)$ （接龙型）， $S_{n}=\frac{1}{d}\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}}-\frac{1}{a_{n+1}}\right)=\frac{n}{a_{1}a_{n+1}}$

② $b_{n}=\frac{1}{a_{n}a_{n+2}}=\frac{1}{2d}\left(\frac{1}{a_{n}}-\frac{1}{a_{n+2}}\right)$ (隔项型) $S_{n}=\frac{1}{2d}\left(\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}-\frac{1}{a_{n+1}}-\frac{1}{a_{n+2}}\right)$

③ $b_{n}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{a_{n}}+\sqrt{a_{n+1}}}=\frac{1}{d}(\sqrt{a_{n+1}}-\sqrt{a_{n}})$ （根式型） $S_{n}=\frac{1}{d}(\sqrt{a_{n+1}}-\sqrt{a_{1}})$

## 4. 等差与等比混合型

$$
① a _ {n} = \frac {g ^ {n} [ 1 + (1 - q) n ]}{n (n + 1)} = \frac {q ^ {n}}{n} - \frac {g ^ {n + 1}}{n + 1}; ② a _ {n} = \frac {g ^ {n} [ k + (1 - q ^ {k}) n ]}{n (n + k)} = \frac {q ^ {n}}{n} - \frac {g ^ {n + k}}{n + k}
$$

通常采用反推法,就是从右边往左边推导,具体情况将会在例题中说明。

## 5. 正切裂项相消

若 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为等差数列，则 $\tan (a_{n + 1} - a_n) = \tan d = \frac{\tan a_{n + 1} - \tan a_n}{1 + \tan a_{n + 1} \cdot \tan a_n}$ ，故 $\tan a_{n + 1} \cdot \tan a_n = \frac{\tan a_{n + 1} - \tan a_n}{\tan d}$ $= -1, \sum_{i=1}^{n} \tan a_{i+1} \tan a_i = \frac{\tan a_{n+1} - \tan a_1}{\tan d} - n$

例(2024·安徽月考)已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前n项和为 $S_{n}, a_{1}=\frac{\pi}{5}, S_{n}=\frac{n+1}{2}a_{n}, b_{n}=\tan(a_{n})\tan(a_{n+1})$ .

(1)求数列 $(a_{n})$ 的通项公式；

(2)求数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的前2024项和 $T_{2024}$ .

解析 (1) 因为 $S_{n} = \frac{n + 1}{2} a_{n}$ , 所以当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, $S_{n - 1} = \frac{n}{2} a_{n - 1}$ . 所以 $S_{n} - S_{n - 1} = \frac{n + 1}{2} a_{n} - \frac{n}{2} a_{n - 1}$ , 即 $a_{n} = \frac{n + 1}{2} a_{n} - \frac{n}{2} a_{n - 1}$ , 所以 $\frac{n - 1}{2} a_{n} = \frac{n}{2} a_{n - 1}$ , 所以 $\frac{a_n}{n} = \frac{a_{n - 1}}{n - 1}$ , 所以数列 $\left\{\frac{a_n}{n}\right\}$ 是常数列, 所以 $\frac{a_n}{n} = \frac{a_1}{1} = \frac{\pi}{5}$ , 所以 $a_{n} = \frac{\pi}{5} n$ , 即数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n} = \frac{\pi}{5} n$ .

(2) 因为 $\tan \frac{\pi}{5} = \tan \left[ \left( \frac{\pi n}{5} + \frac{\pi}{5} \right) - \frac{\pi n}{5} \right] = \frac{\tan \left( \frac{\pi n}{5} + \frac{\pi}{5} \right) - \tan \frac{\pi n}{5}}{1 + \tan \left( \frac{\pi n}{5} + \frac{\pi}{5} \right) \tan \frac{\pi n}{5}}$ ，所以 $\tan \left( \frac{\pi n}{5} + \frac{\pi}{5} \right) \tan \frac{\pi n}{5} = \frac{\tan \left( \frac{\pi n}{5} + \frac{\pi}{5} \right) - \tan \frac{\pi n}{5}}{\tan \frac{\pi}{5}}$

-1, 所以 $b_n = \tan \left( \frac{\pi n}{5} + \frac{\pi}{5} \right) \tan \frac{\pi n}{5} = \frac{\tan \left( \frac{\pi n}{5} + \frac{\pi}{5} \right) - \tan \frac{\pi n}{5}}{\tan \frac{\pi}{5}} - 1.$

$$
T _ {2 0 2 4} = b _ {1} + b _ {2} + \dots + b _ {2 0 2 4} = \frac {\left(\tan \frac {2 \pi}{5} - \tan \frac {\pi}{5}\right) + \left(\tan \frac {3 \pi}{5} - \tan \frac {2 \pi}{5}\right) + \cdots + \left(\tan \frac {2 0 2 5 \pi}{5} - \tan \frac {2 0 2 4 \pi}{5}\right)}{\tan \frac {\pi}{5}} - 2 0 2 4
$$

$$
= \frac {\tan \frac {2 0 2 5 \pi}{5} - \tan \frac {\pi}{5}}{\tan \frac {\pi}{5}} - 2 0 2 4 = - 1 - 2 0 2 4 = - 2 0 2 5.
$$
