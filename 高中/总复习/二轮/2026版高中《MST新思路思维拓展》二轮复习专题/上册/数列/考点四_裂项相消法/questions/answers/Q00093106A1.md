> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ABD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 由 $a_1 + a_2 = 8, \frac{2a_n}{a_{n+1}} = \frac{n-1}{n+1}$ . 可得 $\frac{2a_1}{a_2} = 0$ , 即 $a_1 = 0, a_2 = 8$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = a _ {2} \cdot \frac {a _ {3}}{a _ {2}} \cdot \frac {a _ {4}}{a _ {3}} \cdot \dots \cdot \frac {a _ {n}}{a _ {n - 1}} = 8 \cdot \frac {2 \times 3}{1} \cdot \frac {2 \times 4}{2} \cdot \dots \cdot \frac {2 n}{n - 2} = 8 \cdot 2 ^ {n - 2} \cdot \frac {n (n - 1)}{1 \times 2} = (n ^ {2} - n) \cdot 2 ^ {n},
> > $$
> >
> > 上式对 $n = 1,2$ 都成立，所以 $a_{n} = (n^{2} - n)\cdot 2^{n},n\in \mathbf{N}^{*}$
> >
> > 则 $a_5 = (25 - 5) \times 32 = 640, a_{n+1} - a_n = (n^2 + n) \cdot 2^{n+1} - (n^2 - n) \cdot 2^n = (n^2 + 3n) \cdot 2^n > 0$ ，即 $a_{n+1} > a_n$ ，故 AB 正确；当 $n = 1$ 时， $S_1 = 0$ ，而 $4 + (n-1) \cdot 2^{n+1} = 4$ ，可得 $S_n \geqslant 4 + (n-1) \cdot 2^{n+1}$ 不恒成立，故 C 错误；
> >
> > 对于D,法一:由 $a_{n} = (n^{2} - n)\cdot 2^{n}\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l}S_{n} = (An^{2} + Bn + C)2^{n + 1} + D\\ S_{n - 1} = [A(^{n} - 1)2 + B(n - 1) + C)2^{n} + D \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以 $[An^2 +(B + 2A)n + C + B - A]\cdot 2^n = (n^2 -n)\cdot 2^n$ ，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}A = 1\\ B = -3,\\ a_1 = (1 - 3 + 4)\times 2^2 +D = 0,D = -8,\\ C = 4 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {二}: a _ {n} = (n ^ {2} - n) \cdot 2 ^ {n} = (a n ^ {2} + b n + c) \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1} - [ a (n - 1) ^ {2} + b (n - 1) + c ] \cdot 2 ^ {n} = [ a n ^ {2} + (2 a + b) n + c + b - a ] \cdot 2 ^ {n},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $a = 1, b = -3, c = 4$ ，所以 $a_{n} = (n^{2} - n) \cdot 2^{n} = h(n) - h(n - 1), S_{n} = h(n) - h(1) + a_{1} = (n^{2} - 3n + 4) \cdot 2^{n + 1} - 8,$ 则 $S_{n} - [4 + (n - 2) \cdot 2^{n + 1}] = (n^{2} - 4n + 6) \cdot 2^{n + 1} - 12, n = 1$ 时， $3 \cdot 4 - 12 = 0; n = 2$ 时， $2 \cdot 8 - 12 > 0; n = 3$ 时， $3 \cdot 16 - 12 > 0$ ，而 $n^{2} - 4n + 6 \geqslant 2$ ，所以 $n \geqslant 2$ 时， $S_{n} - [4 + (n - 2) \cdot 2^{n + 1}] > 0$ 恒成立，故 D 正确。
> >
> > 故选 **ABD**.
