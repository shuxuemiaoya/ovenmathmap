> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 因为 $\frac{1}{c_n} = f(n) = n - \frac{1}{n}$ ( $n \geqslant 2$ ), 所以 $c_n = \frac{n}{n^2 - 1} = \frac{n}{(n + 1)(n - 1)}$ , ( $n \geqslant 2$ ),
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = c _ {1} \cdot c _ {2} \dots \cdot c _ {n} = 1 \times \frac {2}{3 \times 1} \times \frac {3}{4 \times 2} \times \dots \times \frac {n - 1}{n \times (n - 2)} \times \frac {n}{(n + 1) \times (n - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {n}{3 \times 4 \times 5 \times \cdots \times (n + 1)} = \frac {2 n}{(n + 1) !} = 2 \cdot \frac {n + 1 - 1}{(n + 1) !} = 2 \left[ \frac {1}{n !} - \frac {1}{(n + 1) !} \right],
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $S_{n}=T_{1}+T_{2}+\cdots+T_{n}=2\left[1-\frac{1}{2!}+\frac{1}{2!}-\frac{1}{3!}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n!}-\frac{1}{(n+1)!}\right]=2-\frac{2}{(n+1)!}<2,$
> >
> > 因为 $T_{n} > 0$ ，所以 $\{S_n\}$ 为递增数列，因为 $S_{2} = 2 - \frac{2}{3!} = \frac{5}{3}$ 所以当 $n\geqslant 2$ 时， $\frac{5}{3}\leqslant S_n <   2.$ 故选：A.

> > 错位相减的裂项分析法
> >
> > 若 $a_{n} = An + B, b_{n} = q^{n}, c_{n} = a_{n}b_{n} = (An + B)q^{n}$ , 求 $c_{n}$ 的前 $n$ 项和, 可以采用以下三种方法:
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = (A + B) q + (2 A + B) q ^ {2} + (3 A + B) q ^ {3} + \dots + (A n + B) q ^ {n}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > q T _ {n} = (A + B) q ^ {2} + (2 A + B) q ^ {3} + (3 A + B) q ^ {4} + \dots + (A n + B) q ^ {n + 1} \tag {②}
> > $$
> > - **①**：一②得： $(1-q)T_{n}=(A+B)q-(An+B)q^{n+1}+A(q^{2}+q^{3}+\cdots+q^{n})$
> >
> > 整理得: $T_{n}=\left(\frac{An}{q-1}+\frac{B}{q-1}-\frac{A}{(q-1)^{2}}\right)q^{n+1}-\left(\frac{B}{q-1}-\frac{A}{(q-1)^{2}}\right)q.$
