> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 (1) 由题知, $a_{n} > 0$ , 设数列 $\langle a_n \rangle$ 的公比为 $q(q > 0)$ , $\langle b_n \rangle$ 的公差为 $d$ , 因为 $2a_5, a_4, 4a_6$ 成等差数列, 且满足 $a_1 = 4a_3^2$ , 所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2a_4 = 2a_5 + 4a_6 \\ a_4 = 4a_3^2 \end{array} \right.$ , 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_4 = a_4(q + 2q^2) \\ a_3 \cdot q = 4a_3^2 \end{array} \right.$ , 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} q = \frac{1}{2} \\ a_3 = \frac{1}{8} \end{array} \right.$ , 所以 $a_n = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^n$ ,
> >
> > 又因为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} b_{2} + b_{4} = 6 \\ S_{4} = 10 \end{array} \right.$ ，即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2b_{1} + 4d = 6 \\ 4a_{1} + 6d = 10 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} b_{1} = 1 \\ d = 1 \end{array} \right.$ ，所以 $b_{n} = n$ 。所以数列 $\langle a_{n} \rangle$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n} = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{n}$ ，数列 $\langle b_{n} \rangle$ 的
> >
> > 通项公式为 $b_{n}=n$ .
> >
> > (2) 证明: 由(1)知, $a_{n} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n}, b_{n} = n$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > d _ {n} = \frac {2 n + 5}{(2 n + 1) (2 n + 3)} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {\circ} = 2 \left[ \frac {1}{(2 n + 1) \cdot 2 ^ {n}} - \frac {1}{(2 n + 3) \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1}} \right], n \in \mathbf {N} ^ {\circ},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = 2 \left[ \left(\frac {1}{3 \times 2} - \frac {1}{5 \times 4}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{5 \times 4} - \frac {1}{7 \times 8}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{7 \times 8} - \frac {1}{9 \times 1 6}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{(2 n + 1) \cdot 2 ^ {n}} - \frac {1}{(2 n + 3) \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1}}\right) \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $= 2\left[\frac{1}{c} - \frac{1}{(2n + 3)\cdot 2^{n + 1}}\right] = \frac{1}{3} -\frac{1}{(2n + 3)\cdot 2^n}$ 因为 $\frac{1}{(2n + 3)\cdot 2^n} >0$ ，所以 $\frac{1}{3} -\frac{1}{(2n + 3)\cdot 2^n} < \frac{1}{3},$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {   现   } \tau_ {m} <   \frac {1}{3}
> > $$
