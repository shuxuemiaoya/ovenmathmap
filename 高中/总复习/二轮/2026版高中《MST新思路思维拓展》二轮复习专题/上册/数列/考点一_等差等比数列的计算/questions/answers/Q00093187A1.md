> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 对 A, 由题意得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}q^{2}&=1\\ a_{1}+a_{1}q+a_{1}q^{2}&=7\end{aligned}\right.$ ，结合 q>0，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=4\\ q&=\frac{1}{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=9\\ q&=-\frac{1}{3}\end{aligned}\right.$ （舍去），故 A 正确；
> >
> > 对 B, 则 $a_{6}=a_{1}q^{4}=4\times\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{4}=\frac{1}{4}$ ，故 B 错误；
> >
> >
> > 对 $C, S_{5} = \frac{a_{1}(1 - q^{6})}{1 - q} = \frac{4 \times \left(1 - \frac{1}{32}\right)}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = \frac{31}{4}$ , 故 $C$ 错误;
> >
> > 对 $D, a_{n} = 4 \times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n-1} = 2^{3-n}, S_{n} = \frac{4 \times \left[1 - \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n}\right]}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = 8 - 2^{-n+3}$ , 则 $a_{n} + S_{n} = 2^{3-n} + 8 - 2^{3-n} = 8$ , 故 D 正确;
> >
> > 故选 **AD**.
