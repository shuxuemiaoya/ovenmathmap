> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 因为知 $a_{n} = \sqrt{\frac{a_{n + 1} + 1}{2}}, a_{n} \in [-1, 1]$ , 两边平方可得: $2a_{n}^{2} = a_{n + 1} + 1$ , 令 $\cos b_{n} = a_{n}$ , 则 $2\cos^2 b_n = \cos b_{n + 1} + 1 \Leftrightarrow \cos (2b_n) = \cos b_{n + 1} = a_{n + 1}$ , 不妨取 $b_{1} = \frac{\pi}{9}$ , 则 $a_{2} = \cos \frac{2\pi}{9}, a_{3} = \cos \frac{4\pi}{9}$ ,
> >
> > $a_{1}a_{2}a_{3} = \cos \frac{\pi}{9}\cos \frac{2\pi}{9}\cos \frac{4\pi}{9} = \frac{\sin \frac{\pi}{9}\cos \frac{\pi}{9}\cos \frac{2\pi}{9}\cos \frac{4\pi}{9}}{\sin \frac{\pi}{9}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}\sin \frac{2\pi}{9}\cos \frac{2\pi}{9}\cos \frac{4\pi}{9}}{\sin \frac{\pi}{9}} = \frac{\frac{1}{4}\sin \frac{4\pi}{9}\cos \frac{4\pi}{9}}{\sin \frac{\pi}{9}} = \frac{\frac{1}{8}\sin \frac{8\pi}{9}}{\sin \frac{\pi}{9}}$ $= \frac{\frac{1}{8}\sin\left(\pi - \frac{\pi}{9}\right)}{\sin\frac{\pi}{9}} = \frac{\frac{1}{8}\sin\frac{\pi}{9}}{\sin\frac{\pi}{9}} = \frac{1}{8}.$ 故选：C.

> > 例(2025·济南月考)数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足 $a_{1}\in[-1,1]$ ， $a_{n+1}=2a_{n}^{2}-1$ ，记 $T_{n}=a_{1}a_{2}a_{3}\cdots a_{n}$ ，则 $\sqrt{1-a_{1}^{2}}\cdot T_{2025}$ 的最大值为\_\_\_\_。
> >
> > 解析 因为 $a_{1} \in [-1, 1]$ , $a_{n+1} = 2a_{n}^{2} - 1$ , 设 $a_{1} = \cos b_{1}, a_{n} = \cos b_{n}, a_{n+1} = 2\cos^{2}b_{n} - 1 = \cos b_{n+1} = \cos 2b_{n}$ , $T_{2025} = \cos b_{1} \times \cos b_{2} \times \cos b_{3} \times \cdots \times \cos b_{2025} = \frac{2\sin b_{1} \times \cos b_{1} \times \cos b_{2} \times \cos b_{3} \times \cdots \times \cos b_{2025}}{2\sin b_{1}}$ $= \frac{2\sin b_{2} \times \cos b_{2} \times \cos b_{3} \times \cdots \times \cos b_{2025}}{2^{2} \times \sin b_{1}} \frac{2\sin b_{3} \times \cos b_{3} \times \cdots \times \cos b_{2025}}{2^{3} \times \sin b_{1}} \cdots = \frac{2\sin b_{2025} \times \cos b_{2025}}{2^{2025}\sin b_{1}} = \frac{\sin b_{2026}}{2^{2025}\sin b_{1}},$ $\sqrt{1 - a_1^2} \cdot T_{2025} = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 b_1} \times \cos b_1 \times \cos b_2 \times \cos b_3 \times \cdots \times \cos b_{2025} = |\sin b_1| \times \cos b_1 \times \cos b_2 \times \cos b_3 \times \cdots \times \cos b_{2025}$ $= |\sin b_1| \times \frac{\sin b_{2026}}{2^{2025}\sin b_1} <slantslant \frac{\sin b_{2026}}{2^{2025}} <slantslantslant \frac{1}{2^{2025}} = 2^{-2025}$ , 当 $\sin b_{2026} = 1; \sin b_1 > 0$ 时取等号，故答案为： $2^{-2025}$ .
