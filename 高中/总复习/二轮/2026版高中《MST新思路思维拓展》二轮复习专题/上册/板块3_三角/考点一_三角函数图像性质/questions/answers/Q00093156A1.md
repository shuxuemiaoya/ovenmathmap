> > 解析 如图, 连接 $CD, OD$ , 作 $DE$ 垂直 $x$ 轴于点 $E$ , 因为 $\overrightarrow{BO} + \overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{BD}$ , 所以四边形 $OBCD$ 为平行四边形, $|OB| = |CD| = |OE|$ , $\overrightarrow{BC} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = (\overrightarrow{BO} + \overrightarrow{OC}) \cdot (\overrightarrow{BE} + \overrightarrow{ED}) = 2 |OB|^2 + |OC|^2 = 10$ , 又 $|BD|^2 = |BE|^2 + |ED|^2 = 4 |OB|^2 + |OC|^2 = 14$ , 解得 $|OB| = |CD| = |OE| = \sqrt{2}$ , $|OC| = \sqrt{6}$ , 由对称性得 $|OB| = |EF|$ , $f(x)$ 的最小正周期 $T = 2(2|OB| + |OE|) = 6|OB| = 6\sqrt{2}$ , 取 $\omega > 0$ ( $\omega < 0$ 不影响结果), 则 $\omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} = \frac{\sqrt{2}\pi}{6}$ , 由点 $B(-\sqrt{2}, 0)$ 在图象上, 可知 $\sin\left(-\frac{\pi}{3} + \varphi\right) = 0$ , 如图所示, 故 $-\frac{\pi}{3} + \varphi = 0$ , $\varphi = \frac{\pi}{3}$ , 由 $f(0) = \sqrt{6}$ 得, $A \sin \frac{\pi}{3} = \sqrt{6}$ , 解得 $A = 2\sqrt{2}$ , 所以 $f(x) = 2\sqrt{2} \sin\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}\pi}{6} x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ , 故 $f\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\right) = 2\sqrt{2} \sin \frac{5\pi}{12} = 2\sqrt{2} \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{6} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 2\sqrt{2}\left(\sin \frac{\pi}{6} \cos \frac{\pi}{4} + \cos \frac{\pi}{6} \sin \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \sqrt{3} + 1$ ; 故答案
> >
> >
> > 为： $\sqrt{3} + 1$
