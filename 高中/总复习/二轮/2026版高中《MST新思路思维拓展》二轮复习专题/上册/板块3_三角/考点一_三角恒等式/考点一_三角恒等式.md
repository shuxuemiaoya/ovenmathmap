## 考点一_三角恒等式

## 一、射影定理与隐藏直角三角形

射影定理:在 $\triangle ABC$ 中,求证: $a=b\cos C+c\cos B,b=c\cos A+a\cos C,c=a\cos B+b\cos A$ .

证明:在 $\triangle ABC$ 中,由 $A+B+C=\pi$ ,得 $A=\pi-(B+C)$ .

所以 $\sin A = \sin(B + C) = \sin B \cos C + \cos B \sin C.$

设 $\triangle ABC$ 的外接圆半径为 $R$ ,由正弦定理得, $\frac{a}{2R}=\sin(B+C)=\frac{b}{2R}\cos C+\frac{c}{2R}\cos B$ ,

即 $a=b\cos C+c\cos B$ . 同理可证: $b=c\cos A+a\cos C, c=a\cos B+b\cos A$ .

## 二、正弦平方和与余弦平方和

1. 正弦平方和: $\sin^2 A + \sin^2 B + \sin^2 C = 2 + 2\cos A\cos B\cos C$

$$
\sin^ {2} A + \sin^ {2} B + \sin^ {2} C = 1 - \frac {1}{2} (\cos 2 A + \cos 2 B) + 1 - \cos^ {2} C = 2 - \cos (A + B) \cos (A - B) - \cos^ {2} C
$$

$$
= 2 + \cos C [ \cos (A - B) + \cos (A + B) ] = 2 + 2 \cos A \cos B \cos C
$$

往题 $\sin^2 A + \sin^2 B + \sin^2 C\left\{ \begin{array}{ll} > 2 & \text{锐角三角形}\\ = 2 & \text{直角三角形}\\ < 2 & \text{钝角三角形} \end{array} \right.$

2. 余弦平方和: $\cos^2 A + \cos^2 B + \cos^2 C = 1 - 2\cos A\cos B\cos C$ .

证明： $\cos^2 A + \cos^2 B + \cos^2 C = 3 - \sin^2 A - \sin^2 B - \sin^2 C$

$$
= 3 - (2 + 2 \cos A \cos B \cos C) = 1 - 2 \cos A \cdot \cos B \cos C.
$$

## 三、正余弦平方差与倍角三角形

1. 正余弦平方差公式：

$$
\begin{array}{l} \sin^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \beta = \sin (\alpha + \beta) \cdot \sin (\alpha - \beta) \\ \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \beta = \cos (\alpha + \beta) \cdot \cos (\alpha - \beta) \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} {\text {证明:} \sin (\alpha + \beta) \cdot \sin (\alpha - \beta)} & {= (\sin \alpha \cdot \cos \beta + \sin \beta \cdot \cos \alpha) \cdot (\sin \alpha \cdot \cos \beta - \sin \beta \cdot \cos \alpha)} \\ & {= (\sin \alpha \cdot \cos \beta) ^ {2} - (\sin \beta \cdot \cos \alpha) ^ {2}} \\ & {= \sin^ {2} \alpha   \cos^ {2} \beta - \sin^ {2} \beta   \cos^ {2} \alpha} \\ & {= \sin^ {2} \alpha (1 - \sin^ {2} \beta) - \sin^ {2} \beta (1 - \sin^ {2} \alpha)} \\ & {= \sin^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha   \sin^ {2} \beta - \sin^ {2} \beta + \sin^ {2} \alpha   \sin^ {2} \beta} \\ & {= \sin^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \beta} \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \cos (\alpha + \beta) \cdot \cos (\alpha - \beta) & = (\cos \alpha \cdot \cos \beta - \sin \alpha \sin \beta) \cdot (\cos \alpha \cdot \cos \beta + \sin \alpha \sin \beta) \\ & = \cos^ {2} \alpha \cdot \cos^ {2} \beta - \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta \\ & = \cos^ {2} \alpha \cdot (1 - \sin^ {2} \beta) - (1 - \cos^ {2} \alpha) \sin^ {2} \beta \end{array}
$$

$$
= \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \beta
$$

2. 倍角三角形：

$$
B = 2 A \Leftrightarrow b ^ {2} = a (a + c)
$$

$C=2B\Leftrightarrow c^{2}=b(b+a)$ ，这样的三角形称为“倍角三角形”.

$$
A = 2 C \Leftrightarrow a ^ {2} = c (c + b)
$$

(必要性)法一:余弦定理,已知 $b^{2}=a(a+c)$ ,证 B=2A, $b^{2}=a(a+c)$ ①, $b^{2}=a^{2}+c^{2}-2ac\cdot\cos B$ ②

得 $a = c - 2a\cos B, \sin A = \sin C - 2\sin A\cos B = \sin (A + B) - 2\sin A\cos B = \sin (B - A)$ 得 $B - A = A$ ，所以 $B = 2A$ .

法二: 当 $b^{2}=a(a+c)a^{2}-b^{2}=bc$ 时, 由正弦定理得 $\sin^{2}B-\sin^{2}A=\sin A\sin C$ , 由正弦平方差公式得: $\sin(B-A)\sin(A+B)=\sin A\sin C$ 由 $A+B+C=\pi$ 得 $\sin(A+B)=\sin C$ , 所以 $\sin(B-A)=\sin A$ ,

$$
A, B \in (0, \pi)
$$

$$
B = 2 A
$$

(充分性): 因为 B=2A, 所以 $\sin B=\sin2A=2\sin A\cos A$ , 根据正弦定理可得: $b=2a\cos A$ , 利用余弦定理: $b=2a\frac{b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}}{2bc}$ , 即 $b^{2}c=ab^{2}+ac^{2}-a^{3}$ , 所以 $b^{2}(c-a)=a(c^{2}-a^{2})$ , 即 $b^{2}=a(a+c)$ .
