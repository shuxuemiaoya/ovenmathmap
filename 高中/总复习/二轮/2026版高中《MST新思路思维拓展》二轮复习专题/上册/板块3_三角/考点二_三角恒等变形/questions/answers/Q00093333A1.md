>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** BCD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 依题意， $\lambda_0 = \frac{\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \theta_0\right) + \sin^2(0 - \theta_0)}{2} = \frac{\left(\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\cos\theta_0 - \cos\frac{\pi}{3}\sin\theta_0\right)^2 + \sin^2\theta_0}{2}$
> >
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\left(\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos \theta_ {0} - \frac {1}{2} \sin \theta_ {0}\right) ^ {2} + \sin^ {2} \theta_ {0}}{2} = \frac {\frac {3}{4} \cos^ {2} \theta_ {0} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos \theta_ {0} \sin \theta_ {0} + \frac {1}{4} \sin^ {2} \theta_ {0} + \sin^ {2} \theta_ {0}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\frac {1}{2} \sin^ {2} \theta_ {0} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos \theta_ {0} \sin \theta_ {0} + \frac {3}{4}}{2} = \frac {1 - \frac {1}{4} \cos 2 \theta_ {0} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} \sin 2 \theta_ {0}}{2} = \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{4} \left(\frac {1}{2} \cos 2 \theta_ {0} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin 2 \theta_ {0}\right) = \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{4} \sin \left(2 \theta_ {0} + \frac {\pi}{6}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $\theta_0 \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ , 得 $2\theta_0 + \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left[\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{7\pi}{6}\right]$ , 故 $\sin \left(2\theta_0 + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \in \left[-\frac{1}{2}, 1\right]$ , 即 $\lambda_0 \in \left[\frac{1}{4}, \frac{5}{8}\right]$ . 故选: BCD.
> >
> > 例 (2026 届广西柳州开学考试 T18) 将函数 $f(x) = \sin \left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$ 图象上各点向右平移 $\frac{\pi}{6}$ 个单位长度后得到函数 $g(x)$ 的图象.
> >
> > (1)求曲线 $y=g(x)$ 的对称轴方程;
> >
> > (2) 若 $f(x)$ 在 $\left[-m, 2m + \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ 上单调递减， $g(x)$ 在 $\left[-m, 2m + \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ 上单调递增，求 $m$ 的取值范围；
> >
> > (3)若关于 $\theta$ 的方程 $g(2\theta) + g\left(\frac{3}{2}\theta\right) = 0$ 在 $(0,\pi)$ 上的三个根分别为 $\theta_1, \theta_2, \theta_3$ ，求 $\cos \theta_1 + \cos \theta_2 + \cos \theta_3$ 。
> >
> > 解析 (1) 依题意, $g(x) = \sin \left[2\left(x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \frac{\pi}{3}\right] = \sin 2x$ , 由 $2x = \frac{\pi}{2} + k\pi (k \in Z)$ , 得 $x = \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{k\pi}{2} (k \in Z)$ ,
> >
> > 所以曲线 $y=g(x)$ 的对称轴方程为 $x=\frac{\pi}{4}+\frac{k\pi}{2}(k\in Z)$ .
> >
> > (2)由 $\frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi \leqslant 2x + \frac{\pi}{3} \leqslant \frac{3\pi}{2} + 2k\pi (k \in Z)$ , 得 $\frac{\pi}{12} + k\pi \leqslant x \leqslant \frac{7\pi}{12} + k\pi (k \in Z)$ , 因此函数 $f(x)$ 的单调递减区间为
> >
> > $\left[\frac{\pi}{12} + k\pi, \frac{7\pi}{12} + k\pi\right](k \in Z)$ ，由 $-\frac{\pi}{2} + 2k_1\pi \leqslant 2x \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2} + 2k_1\pi (k_1 \in Z)$ ，得 $-\frac{\pi}{4} + k_1\pi \leqslant x \leqslant \frac{\pi}{4} + k_1\pi (k_1 \in Z)$ ，
> >
> > 函数 $g(x)$ 的单调递增区间为 $\left[-\frac{\pi}{4} + k_1\pi, \frac{\pi}{4} + k_1\pi\right](k_1 \in Z)$ ，由 $-m < 2m + \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，得 $m > -\frac{\pi}{6}$ ，则 $-m < \frac{\pi}{6} < 2m + \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 由 $f(x)$ 在 $\left[-m, 2m + \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ 上单调递减， $g(x)$ 在 $\left[-m, 2m + \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ 上单调递增，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} m > -\frac{\pi}{6} \\ -m \geqslant \frac{\pi}{12} \\ 2m + \frac{\pi}{2} \leqslant \frac{\pi}{4} \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $-\frac{\pi}{6} < m \leqslant -\frac{\pi}{8}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 m 的取值范围为 $\left(-\frac{\pi}{6}, -\frac{\pi}{8}\right]$ .
> >
> > (3) 由 $g(2\theta) + g\left(\frac{3}{2}\theta\right) = 0$ ，得 $\sin 4\theta + \sin 3\theta = 0$ ，由 $\theta \in (0, \pi)$ ，得 $3\theta \in (0, 3\pi), 4\theta \in (0, 4\pi)$ ，
> >
> > 则 $\sin 4\theta = \sin (3\theta +\pi)$ ，于是 $4\theta +3\theta +\pi = \pi +2k\pi ,k = 1,2,3,$ 解得 $\theta \in \left\{\frac{2\pi}{7},\frac{4\pi}{7},\frac{6\pi}{7}\right\}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \theta_{1} + \cos \theta_{2} + \cos \theta_{3} = \cos \frac{2\pi}{7} +\cos \frac{4\pi}{7} +\cos \frac{6\pi}{7} = \frac{\sin\frac{\pi}{7}\left(\cos\frac{2\pi}{7} +\cos\frac{4\pi}{7} +\cos\frac{6\pi}{7}\right)}{\sin\frac{\pi}{7}}$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} = \frac {\sin \frac {\pi}{7} \cos \frac {2 \pi}{7} + \sin \frac {\pi}{7} \cos \frac {4 \pi}{7} + \sin \frac {\pi}{7} \cos \frac {6 \pi}{7}}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}} \\ = \frac {\frac {1}{2} \left[ \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{7} + \frac {2 \pi}{7}\right) + \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{7} - \frac {2 \pi}{7}\right) + \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{7} + \frac {4 \pi}{7}\right) + \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{7} - \frac {4 \pi}{7}\right) + \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{7} + \frac {6 \pi}{7}\right) + \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{7} - \frac {6 \pi}{7}\right) \right]}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}} \\ = \frac {\frac {1}{2} \left(\sin \frac {3 \pi}{7} - \sin \frac {\pi}{7} + \sin \frac {5 \pi}{7} - \sin \frac {3 \pi}{7} + \sin \pi - \sin \frac {5 \pi}{7}\right)}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}} = \frac {- \frac {1}{2} \sin \frac {\pi}{7}}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}} = - \frac {1}{2}. \end{array}
> > $$
