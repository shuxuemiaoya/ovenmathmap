> > 解析 因为 $0 < \alpha < \pi, \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ , 所以 $0 < \frac{\alpha}{2} < \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 所以 $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}\right)} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} > \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \sin \frac{\pi}{4}$ , 所以 $\frac{\alpha}{2} > \frac{\pi}{4}$ , 即 $\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha < \pi$ , 所以 $\sin \alpha = 2\sin \frac{\alpha}{2}\cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{4}{5}, \cos \alpha = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2\alpha} = -\frac{3}{5}$ , 则 $\sin \left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \sin \alpha \cos \frac{\pi}{4} - \cos \alpha \sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$ . 故选: D.
