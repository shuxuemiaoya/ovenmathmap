> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ABD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 法一: 以 BC 中点 O 为坐标原点, 以 BC, OA 所在直线建立平面直角坐标系, 如图,
> >
> > 则 $O(0,0), B\left(-\frac{3}{2}, 0\right), C\left(\frac{3}{2}, 0\right), A\left(0, \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ ，由 $\overrightarrow{BC} = 3\overrightarrow{BD}, \overrightarrow{AD} = 2\overrightarrow{AE}$ ，可得 $D\left(-\frac{1}{2}, 0\right), E\left(-\frac{1}{4}, \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > : \overrightarrow {B E} = \left(\frac {5}{4}, \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{4}\right), - \frac {2}{3} \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {A C} = - \frac {2}{3} \left(- \frac {3}{2}, - \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{2}\right) + \frac {1}{6} \left(\frac {3}{2}, - \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{2}\right) = \left(\frac {5}{4}, \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{4}\right) = \overrightarrow {B E}
> > $$
> >
> > $B_{1}\overrightarrow{CE} = \left(-\frac{7}{4},\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\right),\overrightarrow{AD} = \left(-\frac{1}{2}, - \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{CE}\cdot \overrightarrow{AD} = -\frac{7}{4}\times \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) + \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\times \left(-\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -\frac{5}{2}$ ，故B正确；
> >
> > $C_{2}\overrightarrow{AE} = \left(-\frac{1}{4}, - \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\right),\overrightarrow{AB} = \left(-\frac{3}{2}, - \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{AE}\cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = -\frac{1}{4}\times \left(-\frac{3}{2}\right) + \left(-\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)\times \left(-\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = \frac{15}{4}$ 故C错误；
> >
> > $D$ : 设 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \lambda \overrightarrow{AC}, P\left(\frac{3}{2}\lambda, \frac{3\sqrt{3}(1-\lambda)}{2}\right), \overrightarrow{PC} = \left(\frac{3}{2} - \frac{3}{2}\lambda, -\frac{3\sqrt{3}(1-\lambda)}{2}\right), \overrightarrow{PD} = \left(-\frac{1}{2} - \frac{3}{2}\lambda, -\frac{3\sqrt{3}(1-\lambda)}{2}\right)$ , 所以 $\overrightarrow{PC} \cdot \overrightarrow{PD} = \left(\frac{3}{2} - \frac{3}{2}\lambda\right) \times \left(-\frac{1}{2} - \frac{3}{2}\lambda\right) + \frac{27}{4}(1-\lambda)^2 = 9\lambda^2 - 15\lambda + 6 = 9\left(\lambda - \frac{5}{6}\right)^2 - \frac{1}{4}$ , 显然 $0 < \lambda < 1$ , 所以当 $\lambda = \frac{5}{6}$ 时 $\overrightarrow{PC} \cdot \overrightarrow{PD}$ 取得最小值 $-\frac{1}{4}$ , 故 D 正确.
> >
> > 法二： $\overrightarrow{BE} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BA} +\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BD} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BA} +\frac{1}{6}\overrightarrow{BC} = -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} +\frac{1}{6} (\overrightarrow{AC} -\overrightarrow{AB}) = -\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} +\frac{1}{6}\overrightarrow{AC},$ A正确；
> >
> > 利用对角线向量定理， $\overrightarrow{CE}\cdot \overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{|CD|^2 + |AE|^2 - |AC|^2 - |ED|^2}{2} = \frac{|CD|^2 - |AC|^2}{2} = -\frac{5}{2}$ 故B正确；
> >
> > $\overrightarrow{AE} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AD} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AC} + \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AB}\right) = \frac{15}{4}$ , 故 C 错误;
> >
> > 取 $CD$ 中点 $M$ , 利用极化恒等式, $\overrightarrow{PC} \cdot \overrightarrow{PD} = |PM|^2 - |MC|^2 = |PM|^2 - 1 \geqslant |CM|^2 \sin^2 60^\circ - 1 = -\frac{1}{4}$ , 故 $D$ 正确.
> >
> > 故选 **ABD**.
