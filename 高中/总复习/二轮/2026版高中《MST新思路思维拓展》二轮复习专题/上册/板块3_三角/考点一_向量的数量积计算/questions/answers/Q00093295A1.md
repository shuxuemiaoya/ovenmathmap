> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{41}{18}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > \- 由一，以点 $B$ 为坐标原点，建立如下图所示平面直角坐标系，因为 $\overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BC},\angle ABC = 60^{\circ},|AD| = |AB| = 1,$ 则 $B(0,0),A\left(\frac{1}{2},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),D\left(\frac{3}{2},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),C(2,0)$ ，设点 $E(x,y)$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{DE} = \left(x - \frac{3}{2},y - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),\overrightarrow{EC} = (2 - x, - y)$ ，因为 $\overrightarrow{DE} = 2\overrightarrow{EC}$ ，所以 $\left(x - \frac{3}{2},y - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = (4 - 2x, - 2y)$ ，即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x - \frac{3}{2} = 4 - 2x\\ y - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = -2y \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x = \frac{11}{6}\\ y = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6} \end{array} \right.$ ，所以 $E\left(\frac{11}{6},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\right),\overrightarrow{AE} =$ $\left(\frac{4}{3}, - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right),\overrightarrow{BE} = \left(\frac{11}{6},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\right)$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{AE}\cdot \overrightarrow{BE} = \frac{4}{3}\times \frac{11}{6} -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6} = \frac{44 - 3}{18} = \frac{41}{18}.$ 法二： $\overrightarrow{AE}\cdot \overrightarrow{BE} = (\overrightarrow{AD} +\overrightarrow{DE})(\overrightarrow{BC} +\overrightarrow{CE}) = 2 + \overrightarrow{AD}\cdot \overrightarrow{CE} +\overrightarrow{BC}\cdot \overrightarrow{DE} +\overrightarrow{DE}\cdot \overrightarrow{CE} = 2 + 1\times \frac{1}{3}\times \cos 120^{\circ} + 2\times \frac{2}{3}\times \cos 60^{\circ}\\ -\frac{2}{9}\\ 2 - \frac{1}{6} +\frac{2}{3} -\frac{2}{9} = \frac{41}{18},$ 故答案为： $\frac{41}{18}$ .
