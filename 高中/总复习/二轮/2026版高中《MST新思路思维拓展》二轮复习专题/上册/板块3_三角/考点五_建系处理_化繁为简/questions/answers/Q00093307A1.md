> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{5}{12}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 如图所示, 建立平面直角坐标系, 设 $A\left(-\frac{c}{2},0\right)$ , $B\left(\frac{c}{2},0\right)$ , $C(x,y)$ (c>0),
> >
> > 则 $8BC^{2} + 8AC^{2} + 5AB^{2} = 8\left[\left(x - \frac{c}{2}\right)^{2} + y^{2}\right] + 8\left[\left(x + \frac{c}{2}\right)^{2} + y^{2}\right] + 5c^{2} = 20$ ，化简得 $x^{2} + y^{2} = \frac{5}{4} -\frac{9}{16} c^{2},$
> >
> >
> > 显然 $\frac{5}{4} -\frac{9}{16} c^2\geqslant 0$ ，所以 $0 <   c^{\circ}\leqslant \frac{20}{9}$ 且 $|y|\leqslant \sqrt{\frac{5}{4} - \frac{9}{16}c^2}$ ，而 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} c|y|\leqslant \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{c^2\left(\frac{5}{4} - \frac{9}{16}c^2\right)} = \frac{1}{2}\times \sqrt{\frac{16}{9}}\times$ $\sqrt{\frac{9}{16}c^2\left(\frac{5}{4} - \frac{9}{16}c^2\right)}\leqslant \frac{1}{2}\times \frac{4}{3}\times \frac{\frac{9}{16}c^2 + \left(\frac{5}{4} - \frac{9}{16}c^2\right)}{2} = \frac{5}{12},$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{9}{16} c^2 = \frac{5}{4} -\frac{9}{16} c^2$ ，即 $c^2 = \frac{10}{9}$ 时取得最大值.故答案为： $\frac{5}{12}$
