> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $5\sqrt{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 $\frac{1}{\cos\alpha} + \frac{8}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{1^{+}}{(\cos^{2}\alpha)^{+}} + \frac{4^{+}}{(\sin^{2}\alpha)^{+}} \geqslant \frac{(1 + 4)^{+}}{(\cos^{2}\alpha + \sin^{2}\alpha)^{+}} = 5\sqrt{5}$ , 当且仅当: $\frac{1}{\cos^2\alpha} = \frac{4}{\sin^2\alpha}$ 时等号成立, 故答案为: $5\sqrt{5}$ .
