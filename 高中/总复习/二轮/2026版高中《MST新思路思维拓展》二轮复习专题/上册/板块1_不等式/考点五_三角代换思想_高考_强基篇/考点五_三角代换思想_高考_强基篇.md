## 考点五_三角代换思想_高考_强基篇

三角换元:① $a^{2}+b^{2}=r^{2}$ ，可令 $a=r\cos\alpha,b=r\sin\alpha;$

② $\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}$ ，令 $x = a\cos \alpha / a\sin \alpha$

③ $\sqrt{a^2 + x^2}$ ，令 $x = a\tan a$

④ $\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}$ , 令 $x = \frac{a}{\cos a}$

⑤ $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=r^{2}$ ，令 $a=r\cos\alpha,b=r\sin\alpha\cos\beta,c=r\sin\alpha\sin\beta.$


例 i0 (2022 · 选 · 新高考Ⅱ) 若 x, y 满足 $x^{2} + y^{2} - xy = 1$ ，则
y<1 B. $x+y\geq-2$ C. $x^{2}+y^{2}\leq2$

解析 三角代换: $x^{2} + y^{2} - xy = 1 \Rightarrow \left(x - \frac{y}{2}\right)^{2} + \frac{3y}{4} = 1$ , 令 $x - \frac{y}{2} = \cos \alpha, \frac{\sqrt{3}y}{2} = \sin \alpha$ , 则 $x + y = \cos \alpha + \sqrt{3} \sin \alpha = 2 \sin (\alpha + \beta), \tan \beta = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ , 故 $x + y \in [-2, 2]$ :

$$
x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = 1 + x y = 1 + \frac {2}{3} \sin^ {2} \alpha + \frac {2 \sqrt {3}}{3} \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 1 + \frac {1}{3} (1 - \cos 2 \alpha) + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \sin 2 \alpha = \frac {4}{3} + \frac {2}{3} \sin \left(2 \alpha - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \in \left[ \frac {2}{3}, 2 \right];
$$

注：若 $xy \geqslant 0, x^2 + y^2 - xy = 1 \Rightarrow (x + y)^2 - 1 = 3xy \leqslant \frac{3}{4}(x + y)^2, -2 \leqslant x + y \leqslant 2, x^2 + y^2 - 1 = xy \leqslant \frac{x^2 + y^2}{2},$

$x^{2} + y^{2}\leqslant 2,xy <   0,x^{2} + y^{2} - xy = 1\Rightarrow 1 - (x^{2} + y^{2}) = -xy\leqslant \frac{x^{2} + y^{2}}{2},$ 解得 $x^{2} + y^{2}\geqslant \frac{2}{3}$ ，故答案为：BC.
