> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \tan A + 2 \tan B > 0, \tan B = \frac {\cos A}{1 + \sin A} = \frac {\cos^ {2} \frac {A}{2} - \sin^ {2} \frac {A}{2}}{\cos^ {2} \frac {A}{2} + 2 \sin \frac {A}{2} \cos \frac {A}{2} + \sin^ {2} \frac {A}{2}} = \frac {\left(\cos \frac {A}{2} - \sin \frac {A}{2}\right) \left(\cos \frac {A}{2} + \sin \frac {A}{2}\right)}{\left(\sin \frac {A}{2} + \cos \frac {A}{2}\right) ^ {2}} =
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\cos \frac {A}{2} - \sin \frac {A}{2}}{\cos \frac {A}{2} + \sin \frac {A}{2}} = \frac {1 - \tan \frac {A}{2}}{1 + \tan \frac {A}{2}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan A + 2 \tan B = \frac {2 \tan \frac {A}{2}}{1 - \tan^ {2} \frac {A}{2}} + 2 \cdot \frac {1 - \tan \frac {A}{2}}{1 + \tan \frac {A}{2}} = \frac {2 \tan \frac {A}{2} + 2 (1 - \tan \frac {A}{2}) ^ {2}}{1 - \tan^ {2} \frac {A}{2}} = \frac {2 \tan^ {2} \frac {A}{2} - 2 \tan \frac {A}{2} + 2}{1 - \tan^ {2} \frac {A}{2}},
> > $$
> >
> > 【解析】
> > 令 $\tan \frac{A}{2} = t$ ，因为 $A \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\frac{A}{2} \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right), t = \tan \frac{A}{2} \in (0, 1)$ ，
> >
> > 则 $\tan A + 2\tan B = \frac{2t^2 - 2t + 2}{1 - t^2} = \frac{(2t^2 - 2) - 2t + 4}{1 - t^2} = \frac{-2(t - 2)}{1 - t^2} -2$ ，令 $u = t - 2\in (-2, - 1)$ ，则 $t = u + 2$
> >
> > $\tan A + 2\tan B = \frac{-2u}{1 - (u + 2)^2} -2 = \frac{-2u}{-u^2 - 4u - 3} -2 = \frac{2}{u + \frac{3}{u} + 4} -2$ ，其中 $u + \frac{3}{u}\leqslant -2\sqrt{(-u)\cdot\left(-\frac{3}{u}\right)} = -2\sqrt{3}$ ，当
> >
> > 且仅当 $-u = -\frac{3}{u}$ ，即 $u = -\sqrt{3}$ 时，等号成立，故 $\tan A + 2\tan B = \frac{2}{u + \frac{3}{u} + 4} - 2 \geqslant \frac{2}{-2\sqrt{3} + 4} - 2 = \sqrt{3}$ . 故答案为： $\sqrt{3}$
