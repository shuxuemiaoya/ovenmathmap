> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 倾关系数, $5a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=ma^{2}+b^{2}+pa^{2}+c^{2}\geqslant2\sqrt{m}ab+2\sqrt{p}ac\Rightarrow\left\{\begin{aligned}&m=4p\\ &m+p=5\end{aligned}\right.$ ，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&m=4\\ &p=1\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 由基本不等式可得: $a(2b + c) = 2ab + ac \leqslant \frac{4a^2 + b^2}{2} + \frac{a^2 + c^2}{2} = \frac{5a^2 + b^2 + c^2}{2} = 2$ , 当且仅当 $2a = b, a = c$ 时等号成立, 可取 $a = c = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}, b = \frac{2\sqrt{10}}{5}$ , 所以 $a(2b + c)$ 的最大值为 2.
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
