> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 (特定系数法)令 $a(a + b + c) = a^2 + ab + ac \leqslant a^2 + \frac{1}{2} (\lambda a^2 + \frac{1}{\lambda} b^2) + \frac{1}{2} (\mu a^2 + \frac{1}{\mu} c^2)$ , 则对比系数可知: $1 + \frac{\lambda}{2} + \frac{\mu}{2} = \frac{1}{2\lambda} = \frac{1}{2\mu}$ , 解得: $\lambda = \frac{\sqrt{3} - 1}{2}$ , 故 $a^2 + ab + ac \leqslant 1 + \frac{\lambda}{2} + \frac{\mu}{2} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{3}}{2}$ ;
> >
> > 法二：(三角换元) $a^{2}+b^{2}+c^{2}=1,a=\cos\alpha,b=\sin\alpha\cos\beta,c=\sin\alpha\sin\beta$ ，则 $a(a+b+c)=\cos\alpha(\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\sin\alpha\sin\beta)=$ 10  羌唐说题
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1 + \cos 2 a}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \sin l _ {\alpha} (\sin \beta + \cos \beta),
> > $$
> >
> > 由辅助意可得 $\frac{1 + \cos\beta g}{2} +\frac{1}{2}\sin 2a(\sin \beta +\cos \beta) = \frac{1 + \cos 2a}{2} +\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin \left(\beta +\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\sin 2a =$
> >
> > $\frac{1 + \cos 2a}{8} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin \left( \beta + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) \sin 2a = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{1 + 2\sin^2 \left( \beta + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)}}{2}\sin (2a + \gamma) \leqslant \frac{1 + \sqrt{3}}{2}$ , 故答案为 $\frac{1 + \sqrt{3}}{2}$ .
