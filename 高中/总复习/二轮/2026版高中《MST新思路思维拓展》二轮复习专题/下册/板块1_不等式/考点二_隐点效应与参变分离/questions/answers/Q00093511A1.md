> >
> > $$
> > = 0 \Rightarrow x = 1 + \ln \frac {2}{3}, \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x <   1 + \ln \frac {2}{3}, g ^ {\prime \prime} (x) <   0, g ^ {\prime} (x) \downarrow \\ x > 1 + \ln \frac {2}{3}, g ^ {\prime \prime} (x) > 0, g ^ {\prime} (x) \uparrow \end{array} , g ^ {\prime} (0) = 2 > 0, g ^ {\prime} (1) = 0, \right.
