> >
> > $0, k'(t) = \frac{\sqrt{a}}{2} e^+ - 1 = 0, t = 2\ln \frac{2}{\sqrt{a}}, k\left(2\ln \frac{2}{\sqrt{a}}\right) < 0 \Rightarrow a < \frac{4}{e^2}$ , 当 $a \in \left(0, \frac{4}{e^2}\right)$ , $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} t > 2\ln \frac{2}{\sqrt{a}}, k(t) \text{单调递增} \\ 0 < t < 2\ln \frac{2}{\sqrt{a}}, k(t) \text{单调递减} \end{array} \right.$ , 则存在唯一
