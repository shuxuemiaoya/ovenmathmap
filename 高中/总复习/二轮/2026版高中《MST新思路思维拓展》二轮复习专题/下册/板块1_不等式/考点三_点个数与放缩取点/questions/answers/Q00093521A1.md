> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 $f(x) = x \ln x + ax^2, f'(x) = 1 + \ln x + 2ax, f''(x) = \frac{1}{x} + 2a$ ，当 $a \geqslant 0, f''(x) > 0, f'(x)$ 单调递增，至多一个极值点，
> >
> > 故 $a < 0, \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x < -\frac{1}{2a}, f''(x) > 0, f'(x) \text{单调递增} \\ x > -\frac{1}{2a}, f''(x) < 0, f'(x) \text{单调递减} \end{array} , f'(x)_{\max} = f' \left(-\frac{1}{2a}\right) = \ln \left(-\frac{1}{2a}\right) > 0 \Rightarrow 0 > a > -\frac{1}{2}\right.$ , 此时 $f' \left(\frac{1}{e}\right) = \frac{2a}{e}$
> >
> > <0, 存在唯一 $x_1 \in \left(\frac{1}{e}, -\frac{1}{2a}\right)$ 使得 $f'(x_1) = 0$ , 当 $x > 1$ , $f'(x) = 1 + \ln x + 2ax = \sqrt{x} \left(\frac{\ln ex}{\sqrt{x}} + 2a\sqrt{x}\right) = \sqrt{x} \left(\frac{2\sqrt{e}\ln\sqrt{ex}}{\sqrt{ex}} + 2a\sqrt{x}\right) < \sqrt{x} \left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{e}} + 2a\sqrt{x}\right) = 0$ , 取 $x = \frac{1}{ea^2}$ , 存在唯一 $x_2 \in \left(-\frac{1}{2a}, \frac{1}{ea^3}\right)$ 使得 $f'(x_2) = 0$ , 所以 $0 > a > -\frac{1}{2}$ , $f(x)$ 存在两个极值点.
