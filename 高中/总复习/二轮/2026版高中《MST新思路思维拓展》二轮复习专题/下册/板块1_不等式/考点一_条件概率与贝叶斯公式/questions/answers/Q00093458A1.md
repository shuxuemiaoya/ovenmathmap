> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 根据 $P(A) = \frac{1}{2}, P(B) = \frac{1}{3}$ 可得 $P(\bar{A}) = 1 - P(A) = \frac{1}{2}, P(\bar{B}) = 1 - P(B) = \frac{2}{3}$ . 又 $P(\bar{A} | B) = \frac{P(\bar{A} B)}{P(B)} = \frac{1}{2}$ , 可得 $P(\bar{A} B) = \frac{1}{2} P(B) = \frac{1}{6} = P(\bar{A}) P(B)$ , 则事件 $\bar{A}$ 与事件 $B$ 相互独立, $P(\bar{B}) \neq P(\bar{A} B)$ , 故 $AB$ 正确, $D$ 错误; 由 $P(\bar{A}) = P(\bar{A} B) + P(\bar{A} \bar{B}) = \frac{1}{2}$ , 则 $P(\bar{A} \bar{B}) = \frac{1}{2} - P(\bar{A} B) = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$ , 故 $C$ 正确.
> >
