> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 $\frac{1 - \cos\theta}{e_1^2} +\frac{1 + \cos\theta}{e_2^2} = 2$ ，取 $\theta = 60^{\circ}$ ，可得 $\frac{1}{e_1^2} +\frac{3}{e_2^2} = 4$ ，令 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\frac{1}{e_1} = 2\cos \alpha \\ \frac{\sqrt{3}}{e_2} = 2\sin \alpha \end{array} \right.$ ， $\alpha \in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{3}\right),\frac{1}{e_1} +\frac{1}{e_2} = 2\cos \alpha +\frac{2\sin\alpha}{\sqrt{3}} =$ $\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\leqslant \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 当且仅当 $\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = 1$ ，即 $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{6}$ 时，不等式取等号，
