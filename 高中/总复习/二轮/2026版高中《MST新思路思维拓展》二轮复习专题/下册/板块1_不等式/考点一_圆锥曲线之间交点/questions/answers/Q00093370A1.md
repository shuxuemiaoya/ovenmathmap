> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left(\frac{1}{2},+\infty\right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 因为 $\left\{\begin{aligned}|PF_{1}|+|PF_{2}|&=2a_{1}\\ |PF_{1}|-|PF_{2}|&=2a_{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}|PF_{1}|&=a_{1}+a_{2}\\ |PF_{2}|&=a_{1}-a_{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，且 $\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|=2c=2\left|PF_{2}\right|=2(a_{1}-a_{2})\Rightarrow\frac{1}{e_{1}}-\frac{1}{e_{2}}=2,e_{1}=$ $=\frac{e_{2}}{e_{2}+1}$ ，所以 $e_{2}-e_{1}=e_{2}-\frac{e_{2}}{e_{2}+1}=\frac{e_{2}^{2}}{e_{2}+1}=\frac{1}{\left(\frac{1}{e_{2}}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{e_{2}}}$ 。因为 $e_{2}>1$ ，所以 $0<\frac{1}{e_{2}}<1$ ，设 $\frac{1}{e_{2}}=x,0<x<1$ ，
> > 所以 $0<\left(\frac{1}{e_{2}}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{e_{2}}=x^{2}+x<2$ ，所以 $e_{2}-e_{1}>\frac{1}{2}$ 。故答案为： $\left(\frac{1}{2},+\infty\right)$ 。
