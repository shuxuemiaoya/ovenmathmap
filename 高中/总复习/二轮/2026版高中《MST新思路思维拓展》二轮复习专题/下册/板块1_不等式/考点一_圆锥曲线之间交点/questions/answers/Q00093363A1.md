> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ACD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 对于 A, 令 $AB: x = my + a$ , $A(x_{1}, y_{1})$ , $B(x_{2}, y_{2})$ , 联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x &= my + a \\ y^{2} &= 2px\end{aligned}\right.$ ，消 x 可得 $y^{2} - 2pmy - 2pa = 0$ ，则 $\Delta = (2pm)^{2} + 8pa > 0$ , $y_{1}y_{2} = -2pa$ , $y_{1} + y_{2} = 2pm$ ,
> >
> > $x_{1} + x_{2} = m(y_{1} + y_{2}) + 2a = 2pm^{2} + 2a$ ，则 $k_{OA} = \frac{y_1}{x_1}, OA: y = \frac{y_1}{x_1} x, M\left(-a, -\frac{ay_1}{x_1}\right)$
> >
> > 故 $k_{BM} = \frac{y_2 + \frac{ay_1}{x_1}}{x_2 + a} = \frac{y_2 + \frac{2pa}{y_1}}{x_2 + a} = \frac{y_1y_1 + 2pa}{y_1(x_2 + a)} = 0$ ，同理 $k_{AN} = 0$ ，所以 $BM / / AN$ ，故A正确；
> >
> > 对于 C, 设 x = -a 与 x 轴交于 P, $S_{\triangle FON} = S_{\triangle AOC}$ , $S_{\triangle MOP} = S_{\triangle BOC}$ ，则 $S_{\triangle FON} + S_{\triangle MOP} = S_{\triangle AOC}$ , $+S_{\triangle BOC}$ , $S_{\triangle AOB} = S_{\triangle MDN}$ ，故 C 正确；
> >
> > 对于 $D, S_{\triangle ANC} = \frac{1}{2}(x_1 + a)y_1, S_{\triangle BCM} = -\frac{1}{2}(x_2 + a)y_2$
> >
> > 则 $S_{\triangle ANC} \cdot S_{\triangle BCM} = -\frac{1}{4}(x_1 + a)(x_2 + a)y_1y_2 = -\frac{1}{4}(my_1 + 2a)(my_2 + 2a)y_1y_2$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{4} [ m ^ {2} y _ {1} y _ {2} + 2 a m (y _ {1} + y _ {2}) + 4 a ^ {2} ] y _ {1} y _ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{4} [ m ^ {2} (- 2 p a) + 2 a m (2 p m) + 4 a ^ {2} ] (- 2 p a) = p a ^ {2} (p m ^ {2} + 2 a),
> > $$
> >
> > 而 $S_{\triangle MCN} = S_{\triangle MPC} + S_{\triangle NPC} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 2a|y_1 - y_2| = a|y_1 - y_2|$
> >
> > 所以 $S_{\triangle MCN}^2 = a^2 (y_1 - y_2)^2 = a^2 [(y_1 + y_2)^2 - 4y_1y_2] = 4pa^2 (pm^2 +2a) = 4S_{\triangle ANC}\cdot S_{\triangle BCM}$ ，故D正确；
> >
> > 对于 B, AB 中点 $Q\left(\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}, \frac{y_{1}+y_{2}}{2}\right)$ ，即 $Q(pm^{2}+a, -pa)$ ，则 Q 到直线 x = -a 的距离 $d = pm^{2} + 2a$ ，
> >
> > 以 $AB$ 为直径的圆的半径 $\frac{|AB|}{2} = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{1 + m^2}|y_1 - y_2| = \sqrt{p^2m^4 + p^2m^2 + 2pam^2 + 2pa}$
> >
> > 所以 $d^2 -\frac{AB^2}{4} = (p + 2a)(2a - p)m^2$ ，当 $a = \frac{p}{2}$ 时相切，当 $a\neq \frac{p}{2}$ 时不相切，故B错误.
> >
> > 故选 **ACD**.
