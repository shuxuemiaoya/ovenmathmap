> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $P_{2} = \frac{3}{8};P_{n} = \frac{3}{7} +\frac{9}{28}\times \left(-\frac{1}{6}\right)^{n - 1},$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时， $P_n = \frac{1}{3} P_{n-1} + \frac{1}{2}(1 - P_{n-1}) = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{6} P_{n-1}$ ，所以 $P_n - \frac{3}{7} = -\frac{1}{6}\left(P_{n-1} - \frac{3}{7}\right)$ ，
> >
> > 又因为 $P_{1} - \frac{3}{7} = \frac{9}{28}$ 即 $\{P_n - \frac{3}{7}\}$ 是以 $\frac{9}{28}$ 为首项，以 $-\frac{1}{6}$ 为公比的等比数列，所以 $P_{n} - \frac{3}{7} = \frac{9}{28} \times \left(-\frac{1}{6}\right)^{n-1}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $P_{n} = \frac{3}{7} +\frac{9}{28}\times \left(-\frac{1}{6}\right)^{n - 1}$ 故答案为： $P_{2} = \frac{3}{8};P_{n} = \frac{3}{7} +\frac{9}{28}\times \left(-\frac{1}{6}\right)^{n - 1},$
