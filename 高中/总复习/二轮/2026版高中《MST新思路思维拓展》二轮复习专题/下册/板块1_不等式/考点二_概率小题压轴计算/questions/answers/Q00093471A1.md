> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{27}{16}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 人的可能取值为 1,2,3,其中 $P(X = 1) = \frac{C_4^3A_3^3}{4^3} = \frac{3}{8}, P(X = 2) = \frac{C_4^2C_3^2C_2^1}{4^3} = \frac{9}{16}, P(X = 3) = \frac{C_4^1}{4^3} = \frac{1}{16}$ , 故 $F(X) = \frac{3}{8} \times 1 + \frac{9}{16} \times 2 + \frac{1}{16} \times 3 = \frac{27}{16}$ . 故答案为: $\frac{27}{16}$
