> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ACD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2}),l:(y_{1} + y_{2})y = 2px + y_{1}y_{2}$ ，代入 $F\left(\frac{p}{2},0\right)$ ，则 $y_{1}y_{2} = -p^{2}$ ，设 $M(x_0,y_0),(y_1 + y_2)$ $y_0 = 2px - p^2$ ，且 $y_0 = \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}$ 点 $M$ 到 $y$ 轴的最短距离为 $x_0 = \frac{(y_1 + y_2)^2}{4p} +\frac{p}{2}$ 显然当 $y_{1} + y_{2} = 0$ 时，取得最小值，故 $\frac{p}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$ 解得 $p = 1,l:(y_1 + y_2)y = 2x - 1;$
> >
> > $$
> > 2 x - 2 y - 1 = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y _ {1} + y _ {2} = 2
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y _ {0} = \frac {y _ {1} + y _ {2}}{2} = 1, x _ {0} = \frac {3}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > M \left(\frac {3}{2}, 1\right)
> > $$
> >
> > B选项, 设 $\angle A F x = \alpha$ , 由焦半径公式得 $|A F| = \frac{p}{1 - \cos \alpha}, |B F| = \frac{p}{1 + \cos \alpha}$ , 所以 $\frac{1}{|A F|} + \frac{1}{|B F|} = \frac{1 - \cos \alpha}{p} + \frac{1 + \cos \alpha}{p} = \frac{2}{p} = 2$ , 所以 $|A F| + |B F| = 2|A F| \cdot |B F|$ , B 错误;
> >
> > C选项，由于 $y_0 = \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}, x_0 = \frac{(y_1 + y_2)^2}{4p} + \frac{p}{2} = y_0^2 + \frac{1}{2}$ ，故点 $M$ 的轨迹方程是 $y^2 = x - \frac{1}{2}, C$ 正确；
> >
> > D选项， $|AB| = \frac{2p}{\sin^2\alpha} = \frac{2}{\sin^2\alpha}, |OM|^2 = |OF|^2 + |MF|^2 - 2|OF| \cdot |MF|\cos (\pi - \alpha), |OF| = \frac{p}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > $|MF|=\frac{|AF|-|BF|}{2}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{p}{1-\cos\alpha}-\frac{p}{1+\cos\alpha}\right)=\frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha}$ ，所以 $|OM|^{2}=\frac{1}{4}+\frac{\cos^{2}\alpha}{\sin^{4}\alpha}+\frac{\cos^{2}\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha},4|OM|^{2}+3=1+$ $\frac{4(1-\sin^{2}\alpha)}{\sin^{4}\alpha}+\frac{4(1-\sin^{2}\alpha)}{\sin^{2}\alpha}+3=\frac{4}{\sin^{4}\alpha}=|AB|^{2},D$ 正确.
> >
> > 故选 **ACD**.
