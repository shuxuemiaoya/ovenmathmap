> [!faq]- 例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 (1) 当 $a = 1$ 时, $f(x) = e^{x} - x - \sin x = e^{x} - x - 1 + 1 - \sin x, x \in \mathbb{R}$ . $e^{x} - x - 1 \geqslant 0, 1 - \sin x \geqslant 0$ , 当且仅当 $x = 0$ 时等号成立, 得证!
> >
> > (2) 法一: 令 $\varphi(x) = f(x) + \sqrt{2} \sin\left(x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) - 2, x \in \mathbf{R}$ , 则 $\varphi(x) = e^{x} - (a-1)x^{3} - x - \sin x + \sin x + \cos x - 2 = e^{x} + \cos x - (a-1)x^{2} - x - 2.$
> >
> > $\varphi(0)=0.$ 令 $\varphi'(x)=e^{x}-\sin x-3(a-1)x^{2}-1,\varphi'(0)=0,$ (故本题原函数极点效应失效)
> >
> > $$
> > \varphi^ {\prime \prime} (x) = e ^ {2} - \cos x - 6 (a - 1) x \varphi^ {\prime \prime \prime} (x) = e ^ {2} + \sin x - 6 (a - 1), \varphi^ {\prime \prime} (0) = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > 本题属于二阶导极点效应: 在 0 的邻域内, $\varphi'(x)$ 先负后正, $\varphi''(x) \geqslant 0$ , 若 $\varphi''(0) = 0$ , 则 $\varphi'''(0) = 0$ , 故 $\varphi'''(0) = 1 - 6(a - 1) = 0 \Rightarrow a = \frac{7}{6}$ .
> > - **①**：当 $a > \frac{7}{6}$ 时， $\varphi^{\prime \prime}(0) = 1 - 6(a - 1) < 0$ 。当 $x \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, +\infty\right)$ 时， $\varphi^{\prime \prime}(x)$ 单调递增；
> >
> > $x > 0, \varphi^{m}(x) = e^{x} + \sin x - 6(a - 1) > e^{x} - 1 - 6(a - 1) = 0 \Rightarrow x = \ln (6a - 5)$ ，当然利用 $e^{x} - x - 1 \geqslant 0$ 放缩也可以：
> >
> > $$
> > \varphi^ {\prime} \left(e ^ {6 k a - 1 \lambda}\right) > e ^ {6 k a - 1 \lambda} + 1 + \sin e ^ {6 k a - 1 \lambda} - 6 (a - 1) > 6 (a - 1) + 2 + \sin e ^ {6 k a - 1 \lambda} - 6 (a - 1) = 2 + \sin e ^ {6 k a - 1 \lambda} > 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x _ {0} \in (0, e ^ {\delta k a - 1 \lambda})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \varphi^ {\prime \prime \prime} (x _ {0}) = 0.
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $0 < x < x_0$ 时， $\varphi''(x) < 0$ ，则 $\varphi''(x)$ 在 $(0, x_0)$ 上单调递减； $\varphi''(x) < \varphi''(0) = 0$ ，则 $\varphi'(x)$ 在 $(0, x_0)$ 上单调递减；故当 $0 < x < x_0$ 时， $\varphi'(x) < \varphi'(0) = 0, \varphi(x) \downarrow, \varphi(x) < \varphi(0) = 0$ 不合题意.
> > - **②**：当 $a = \frac{7}{6}$ 时， $\varphi''(x) = e^x - \cos x - x \geqslant 0, \varphi'(x)$ 单调递增， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x < 0, \varphi'(x) < \varphi'(0) = 0, \varphi(x) \downarrow \\ x > 0, \varphi'(x) > \varphi'(0) = 0, \varphi(x) \uparrow \end{array} \right.$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\varphi(x)\geqslant\varphi(0)=0$ , 符合题意.
> > - **③**：当 $a < \frac{7}{6}$ 时, $\varphi''(0) = 7 - 6a > 0$ , 当 $x \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, +\infty\right)$ 时, $\varphi''(x)$ 单调递增. 则存在 $x_1 \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, 0\right)$ , 使得 $\varphi''(x)$ 单调递增, $\varphi''(x) < \varphi''(0) = 0$ , $\varphi'(x)$ 单调递减, $\varphi'(x) > \varphi'(0) = 0$ , $\varphi(x)$ 在 $(x, 0)$ 上单调递增; $\varphi(x) < \varphi(0) = 0$ 矛盾! 所以实数 $a$ 的取值范围为 $\left\{\frac{7}{6}\right\}$ .
> >
> > 注意:本题 $a<\frac{7}{6}$ 时最好严格说明一下 $\varphi^{\prime\prime}\left(-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ 的符号,以它作为分类依据.
> >
> > 法二:保值性+泰勒展开 $e^{x}-(a-1)x^{3}-x-2+\cos x=e^{x}-1-\frac{1}{2}x^{2}-\frac{1}{6}x^{3}+\cos x-1+\frac{1}{2}x^{2}+\left(\frac{7}{6}-a\right)x^{3}\geqslant0,a=$ $\frac{7}{6}$ 显然成立,矛盾区间同法一自证!
