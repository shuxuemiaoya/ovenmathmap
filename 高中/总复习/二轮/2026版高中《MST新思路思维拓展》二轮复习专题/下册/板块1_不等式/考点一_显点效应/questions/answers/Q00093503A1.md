>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析 $\forall g(x) = \frac{e^x - 1}{x} - a^x$ ，显然 $\lim_{x \to 0} g(x) \to 0, g'(x) = \frac{x e^x - e^x + 1}{x^2} - a^x \ln a$ ，由极点效应： $\lim_{x \to 0} g'(x) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x e^x - e^x + 1}{x^2} - a^x \ln a = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x e^x - e^x + 1}{x^2} - a^x \ln a = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x e^x - e^x + 1}{x^2} - a^x \ln a = \sqrt{e}$ .
