> [!faq]- 《专题03 平面向量》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 【分析】利用向量模的平方等于向量的平方化简条件得 $e_{1}\cdot e_{2}$ $\frac{3}{4}$ ，再根据向量夹角公式求 $\cos^{2}\theta$ 函数关系式，根据函数单调性求最值·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【详解】 $Q|2e_{1}-e_{2}|\leq\sqrt{2}$
> > $$
> > \therefore 4 - 4 \stackrel {{\mathbf {u}}} {{e _ {1}}} \cdot \stackrel {{\mathbf {u}}} {{e _ {2}}} + 1 \leq 2
> > $$
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{c c} \mathbf {u} & \mathbf {u} \\ \therefore e _ {1} \cdot e _ {2} & \frac {3}{4} \end{array}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos^ {2} \theta = \frac {(a \cdot b) ^ {2}}{a \cdot b} = \frac {(4 + 4 e _ {1} \cdot e _ {2}) ^ {2}}{(2 + 2 e _ {1} \cdot e _ {2}) (1 0 + 6 e _ {1} \cdot e _ {2})} = \frac {4 (1 + e _ {1} \cdot e _ {2})}{5 + 3 e _ {1} \cdot e _ {2}}
> > $$
> > $$
> > = \frac {4}{3} \left(1 - \frac {2}{5 + 3 e _ {1} \cdot e _ {2}}\right) \quad \frac {4}{3} \left(1 - \frac {2}{5 + 3 \times \frac {3}{4}}\right) = \frac {2 8}{2 9}.
> > $$
> > 故答案为： $\frac{28}{29}$
