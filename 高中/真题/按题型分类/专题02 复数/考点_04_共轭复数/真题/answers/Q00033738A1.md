> [!faq]- 《专题02 复数》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 【分析】由共轭复数的概念及复数的运算即可得解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【详解】 $\overline{z} = -1 - \sqrt{3}\mathrm{i}, z\overline{z} = (-1 + \sqrt{3}\mathrm{i})(-1 - \sqrt{3}\mathrm{i}) = 1 + 3 = 4.$
> > $$
> > \frac {z}{z \bar {z} - 1} = \frac {- 1 + \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}}{3} = - \frac {1}{3} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \mathrm{i}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **C**
