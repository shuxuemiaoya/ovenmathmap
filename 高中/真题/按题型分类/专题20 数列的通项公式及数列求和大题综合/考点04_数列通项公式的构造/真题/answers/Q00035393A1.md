> [!faq]- 《专题20 数列的通项公式及数列求和大题综合》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) 若 $\lambda = 0, \mu = -2$ , 求数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的通项公式； (2) 若 $\lambda = \frac{1}{k_0} (k_0 \in N_+, k_0, 2), \mu = -1$ , 证明: $2 + \frac{1}{3k_0 + 1} < a_{k_0 + 1} < 2 + \frac{1}{2k_0 + 1}$ 【答案】（1） $a_{n}=3\cdot2^{n-1}$ ；（2）证明见解析·
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 试题分析：（1）由于 $\lambda=0,\mu=-2$ ，因此把已知等式具体化得 $a_{n+1}a_{n}=2a_{n}^{2}$ ，显然由于 $a_{1}=3$ ，则 $a_{n}\neq0$ （否则会得出 $a_{1}=0$ ），从而 $a_{n+1}=2a_{n}$ ，所以 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是等比数列，由其通项公式可得结论；（2）本小题是数列与不等式的综合性问题，数列的递推关系是 $a_{n+1}a_{n}+\frac{1}{k_{0}}a_{n+1}-a_{n}^{2}=0$ ，可变形为 $a_{n+1}\left(a_{n}+\frac{1}{k_{0}}\right)=a_{n}^{2}$ $(n\in N_{+})$ ，
> > 由于 $k_{0} > 0$ ，因此 $\frac{a_n}{a_n + \frac{1}{k_0}} < 1$ ，于是可得 $a_{n+1} < a_n$ ，即有 $3 = a_1 > a_2 > \dots > a_n > a_{n+1} > \dots > 0$ ，又
> > $$
> > a _ {n + 1} = \frac {a _ {n} ^ {2}}{a _ {n} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}}} = \frac {a _ {n} ^ {2} - \frac {1}{k _ {0} ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0} ^ {2}}}{a _ {n} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}}} = a _ {n} - \frac {1}{k _ {0}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {n} + 1}, \text {于是有}
> > $$
> > $$
> > a _ {k _ {0} + 1} = a _ {1} + \left(a _ {2} - a _ {1}\right) + \dots + \left(a _ {k _ {0} + 1} - a _ {k _ {0}}\right)
> > $$
> > $$
> > = a _ {1} - k _ {0} \cdot \frac {1}{k _ {0}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {1} + 1} + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {2} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {k _ {0}} + 1}\right) > 2 + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1} + \frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1}\right)
> > $$
> > $= 2 + \frac{1}{3k_{0} + 1}$ ，这里应用了累加求和的思想方法，由这个结论可知 $a_{n} > 2(n\in N^{*})$ ，因此 $a_{k_0 + 1} =$
> > $$
> > = a _ {1} - k _ {0} \cdot \frac {1}{k _ {0}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {1} + 1} + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {2} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {k _ {0}} + 1}\right) <   2 + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1} + \frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1}\right) = 2 + \frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1}
> > $$
> > 样结论得证，本题不等式的证明应用了放缩法·（1）由 $\lambda=0,\quad\mu=-2$ ，有 $a_{n+1}a_{n}=2a_{n}^{2},(n\in N_{+})$
> > 若存在某个 $n_0 \in N_+$ ，使得 $a_{n_0} = 0$ ，则由上述递推公式易得 $a_{n_0 + 1} = 0$ ，重复上述过程可得 $a_1 = 0$ ，此与 $a_1 = 3$ 矛盾，所以对任意 $n \in \mathbf{N}_+$ ， $a_n \neq 0$ 。
> > 从而 $a_{n + 1} = 2a_{n}\left(n\in N_{+}\right)$ ，即 $\{a_n\}$ 是一个公比 $q = 2$ 的等比数列
> > 故 $a_{n} = a_{1}q^{n - 1} = 3\cdot 2^{n - 1}$
> > (2) 由 $\lambda = \frac{1}{k_0}$ , $\mu = -1$ ，数列 $\{a_n\}$ 的递推关系式变为
> > $a_{n + 1}a_n + \frac{1}{k_0} a_{n + 1} - a_n^2 = 0,$ 变形为 $a_{n + 1}\left(a_n + \frac{1}{k_0}\right) = a_n^2$ $(n\in N_{+})$
> > 由上式及 $a_{1}=3$ ，归纳可得
> > $$
> > 3 = a _ {1} > a _ {2} > \dots > a _ {n} > a _ {n + 1} > \dots > 0
> > $$
> > 因为 $a_{n+1}=\frac{a_{n}^{2}}{a_{n}+\frac{1}{k_{0}}}=\frac{a_{n}^{2}-\frac{1}{k_{0}^{2}}+\frac{1}{k_{0}^{2}}}{a_{n}+\frac{1}{k_{0}}}=a_{n}-\frac{1}{k_{0}}+\frac{1}{k_{0}}\cdot\frac{1}{k_{0}a_{n}+1}$ ，所以对 $n=1,2\cdots k_{0}$
> > 求和得 $a_{k_{0}+1}=a_{1}+(a_{2}-a_{1})+\cdots+(a_{k_{0}+1}-a_{k_{0}})$
> > $$
> > = a _ {1} - k _ {0} \cdot \frac {1}{k _ {0}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {1} + 1} + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {2} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {k _ {0}} + 1}\right)
> > $$
> > $$
> > > 2 + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1} + \frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1}\right) = 2 + \frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1}
> > $$
> > 另一方面，由上已证的不等式知 $a_{1} > a_{2} > \cdots > a_{k_{0}} > a_{k_{0}+1} > 2$ 得
> > $$
> > a _ {k _ {0} + 1} = a _ {1} - k _ {0} \cdot \frac {1}{k _ {0}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {1} + 1} + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {2} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {k _ {0}} + 1}\right)
> > $$
> > $$
> > <   2 + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1} + \frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1}\right) = 2 + \frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1}
> > $$
> > 综上： $2+\frac{1}{3k_{0}+1}<a_{k_{0}+1}<2+\frac{1}{2k_{0}+1}$
> > 考点：等比数列的通项公式，数列的递推公式，不等式的证明，放缩法·，考查探究能力和推理论证能力，考查创新意识.
