> [!faq]- 《专题20 数列的通项公式及数列求和大题综合》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 【分析】（ $^{1}$ ）利用退位法可求公比，再求出首项后可求通项；
> >
> > (2) 利用分组求和法即可求 $S_{n}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【详解】（1）因为 $2S_{n}=3a_{n+1}-3$ ，故 $2S_{n-1}=3a_{n}-3$ ，
> > 所以 $2a_{n} = 3a_{n + 1} - 3a_{n}(n - 2)$ 即 $5a_{n} = 3a_{n + 1}$ 故等比数列的公比为 $q = \frac{5}{3}$
> > 故 $2a_{1} = 3a_{2} - 3 = 3a_{1}\times \frac{5}{3} -3 = 5a_{1} - 3$ ，故 $a_1 = 1$ ，故 $a_{n} = \left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^{n - 1}$
> > （2）由等比数列求和公式得 $S_{n} = \frac{1\times\left[1 - \left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^{n}\right]}{1 - \frac{5}{3}} = \frac{3}{2}\left(\frac{5}{3}\right)^{n} - \frac{3}{2}$
> > 所以数列 $\{S_n\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = S _ {1} + S _ {2} + S _ {3} + \dots + S _ {n} = \frac {3}{2} \left[ \left(\frac {5}{3}\right) + \left(\frac {5}{3}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {5}{3}\right) ^ {3} + \dots + \left(\frac {5}{3}\right) ^ {n} \right] - \frac {3}{2} n
> > $$
> > $$
> > = \frac {3}{2} \cdot \frac {\left(\frac {5}{3}\right) \left[ 1 - \left(\frac {5}{3}\right) ^ {n} \right]}{1 - \left(\frac {5}{3}\right)} - \frac {3}{2} n = \frac {1 5}{4} \cdot \left(\frac {5}{3}\right) ^ {n} - \frac {3}{2} n - \frac {1 5}{4}.
> > $$
