> [!faq]- 《zhenti-专题10 三角恒等变换与解三角形小题综合》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{7}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\tan \alpha = \tan \left[\left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{4}\right) + \frac{\pi}{4}\right] = \frac{\tan\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) + \tan\frac{\pi}{4}}{1 - \tan\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}\right)\tan\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{\frac{1}{6} + 1}{1 - \frac{1}{6}} = \frac{7}{5}$
> >
> > 故答案为 $\frac{7}{5}$
