> [!faq]- 《zhenti-专题10 三角恒等变换与解三角形小题综合》例题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\tan \beta = \tan (\alpha +\beta -\alpha) = \frac{\tan(\alpha + \beta) - \tan\alpha}{1 + \tan(\alpha + \beta)\tan\alpha} = \frac{\frac{1}{7} + 2}{1 + \frac{1}{7}\times(-2)} = 3$ ，故答案为3.
