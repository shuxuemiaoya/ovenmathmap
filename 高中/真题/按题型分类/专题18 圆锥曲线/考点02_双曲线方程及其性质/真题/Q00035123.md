A. $\frac{x^2}{3} - \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$ B. $\frac{x^2}{9} - \frac{y^2}{3} = 1$ C. $\frac{x^2}{4} - \frac{y^2}{12} = 1$ D. $\frac{x^2}{12} - \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$
