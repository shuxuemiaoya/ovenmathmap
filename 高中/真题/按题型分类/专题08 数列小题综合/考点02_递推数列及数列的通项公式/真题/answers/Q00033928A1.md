> [!faq]- 《专题08 数列小题综合》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 2
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $a_{n} = n^{2}$ ，若对于任意 $n\in N^{+},\{b_{n}\}$ 的第 $a_{n}$ 项等于 $\{a_n\}$ 的第 $b_{n}$ 项，则 $b_{a_n} = a_{b_n} = (b_n)^2$ ，则 $b_{1} = 1 = (b_{1})^{2},b_{4} = (b_{2})^{2},b_{9} = (b_{3})^{2},b_{16} = (b_{4})^{2}$ 所以 $b_{1}b_{4}b_{9}b_{16} = (b_{1}b_{2}b_{3}b_{4})^{2}$ ，所以 $\frac{\lg(b_1b_4b_9b_{16})}{\lg(b_1b_2b_3b_4)} = \frac{\lg(b_1b_2b_3b_4)^2}{\lg(b_1b_2b_3b_4)} = \frac{2\lg(b_1b_2b_3b_4)}{\lg(b_1b_2b_3b_4)} = 2.$
