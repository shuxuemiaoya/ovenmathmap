> [!faq]- 《专题19 解三角形大题综合》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）先表示出 $S_{1}, S_{2}, S_{3}$ ，再由 $S_{1} - S_{2} + S_{3} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 求得 $a^{2} + c^{2} - b^{2} = 2$ ，结合余弦定理及平方关系求得 $ac$ ，再由面积公式求解即可；
> >
> > (2) 由正弦定理得 $\frac{b^2}{\sin^2 B} = \frac{ac}{\sin A \sin C}$ ，即可求解
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意得 $S_{1} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot a^{2}\cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} a^{2},S_{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} b^{2},S_{3} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} c^{2}$ ，则
> > $$
> > S _ {1} - S _ {2} + S _ {3} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} a ^ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} b ^ {2} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} c ^ {2} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
> > $$
> > 即 $a^2 + c^2 - b^2 = 2$ ，由余弦定理得 $\cos B = \frac{a^2 + c^2 - b^2}{2ac}$ ，整理得 $ac \cos B = 1$ ，则 $\cos B > 0$ ，又 $\sin B = \frac{1}{3}$ ，
> > 则 $\cos B = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ，ac = $\frac{1}{\cos B} = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{4}$ ，则 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2} ac \sin B = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{8}$ ;
> > (2) 由正弦定理得： $\frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$ ，则 $\frac{b^2}{\sin^2 B} = \frac{a}{\sin A} \cdot \frac{c}{\sin C} = \frac{ac}{\sin A \sin C} = \frac{\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{4}}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}} = \frac{9}{4}$ ，则 $\frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{3}{2}$ ，
> > $$
> > b = \frac {3}{2} \sin B = \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
