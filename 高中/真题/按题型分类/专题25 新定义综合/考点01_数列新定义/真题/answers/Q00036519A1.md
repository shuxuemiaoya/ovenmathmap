> [!faq]- 《专题25 新定义综合》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据新定义，逐一检验即可
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $a_{i + m} = a_i$ 知，序列 $a_{i}$ 的周期为 $m$ ，由已知， $m = 5$
> > $$
> > C (k) = \frac {1}{5} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} a _ {i} a _ {i + k}, k = 1, 2, 3, 4
> > $$
> > 对于选项 A，
> > $$
> > C (1) = \frac {1}{5} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} a _ {i} a _ {i + 1} = \frac {1}{5} \left(a _ {1} a _ {2} + a _ {2} a _ {3} + a _ {3} a _ {4} + a _ {4} a _ {5} + a _ {5} a _ {6}\right) = \frac {1}{5} (1 + 0 + 0 + 0 + 0) = \frac {1}{5} \leq \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> > $C(2) = \frac{1}{5}\sum_{i = 1}^{5}a_{i}a_{i + 2} = \frac{1}{5}\left(a_{1}a_{3} + a_{2}a_{4} + a_{3}a_{5} + a_{4}a_{6} + a_{5}a_{7}\right) = \frac{1}{5}(0 + 1 + 0 + 1 + 0) = \frac{2}{5}$ ，不满足；
> > 对于选项 B，
> > $C(1)=\frac{1}{5}\sum_{i=1}^{5}a_{i}a_{i+1}=\frac{1}{5}(a_{1}a_{2}+a_{2}a_{3}+a_{3}a_{4}+a_{4}a_{5}+a_{5}a_{6})=\frac{1}{5}(1+0+0+1+1)=\frac{3}{5}$ ，不满足；
> > 对于选项 D，
> > $C(1)=\frac{1}{5}\sum_{i=1}^{5}a_{i}a_{i+1}=\frac{1}{5}(a_{1}a_{2}+a_{2}a_{3}+a_{3}a_{4}+a_{4}a_{5}+a_{5}a_{6})=\frac{1}{5}(1+0+0+0+1)=\frac{2}{5}$ ，不满足；
> > 故选：C
