> [!faq]- 《专题25 新定义综合》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $^{(1)}$ 见解析 $^{(2)}$ 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 试题分析：（Ⅰ）分别代入求 $c_{1}, c_{2}, c_{3}$ ，观察规律，再证明当 $n \geq 3$ 时，
> > $(b_{k+1}-na_{k+1})-(b_{k}-na_{k})=2-n<0$ ，所以 $b_{k}-na_{k}$ 关于 $k\in N^{*}$ 单调递减·所以
> > $c_{n}=\max\left\{b_{1}-a_{1}n,b_{2}-a_{2}n,\cdots,b_{n}-a_{n}n\right\}=b_{1}-a_{1}n=1-n$ ，从而得证；（Ⅱ）首先求 $\left\{c_{n}\right\}$ 的通项公式，分
> > $d_{1}>0,d_{1}=0,d_{1}<0$ 三种情况讨论证明.
> > 试题解析：（1） $c_{1} = b_{1} - a_{1} = 1 - 1 = 0,$
> > $$
> > c _ {2} = \max \left\{b _ {1} - 2 a _ {1}, b _ {2} - 2 a _ {2} \right\} = \max \left\{1 - 2 \times 1, 3 - 2 \times 2 \right\} = - 1
> > $$
> > $$
> > c _ {3} = \max \left\{b _ {1} - 3 a _ {1}, b _ {2} - 3 a _ {2}, b _ {3} - 3 a _ {3} \right\} = \max \left\{1 - 3 \times 1, 3 - 3 \times 2, 5 - 3 \times 3 \right\} = - 2.
> > $$
> > 当 $n$ 3时， $\left(b_{k + 1} - na_{k + 1}\right) - \left(b_k - na_k\right) = \left(b_{k + 1} - b_k\right) - n\left(a_{k + 1} - a_k\right) = 2 - n <   0$
> > 所以 $b_{k}-na_{k}$ 关于 $k\in N^{*}$ 单调递减.
> > 所以 $c_{n}=\max\left\{b_{1}-a_{1}n,b_{2}-a_{2}n,\cdots,b_{n}-a_{n}n\right\}=b_{1}-a_{1}n=1-n$
> > 所以对任意 $n = 1, c_n = 1 - n$ ，于是 $c_{n+1} - c_n = -1$
> > 所以 $\{c_n\}$ 是等差数列·
> > （Ⅱ）设数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 和 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的公差分别为 $d_{1}, d_{2}$ ，则
> > $$
> > b _ {k} - n a _ {k} = b _ {1} + (k - 1) d _ {2} - \left\lceil a _ {1} + (k - 1) d _ {1} \right\rceil n = b _ {1} - a _ {1} n + \left(d _ {2} - n d _ {1}\right) (k - 1).
> > $$
> > 所以 $c_{n} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l}b_{1} - a_{1}n + (n - 1)(d_{2} - nd_{1}),\text{当} d_{2} > nd_{1}\text{时}\\ b_{1} - a_{1}n,\text{当} d_{2}\leq nd_{1}\text{时} , \end{array} \right.$
> > - **①**：当 $d_{1} > 0$ 时，取正整数 $m > \frac{d_2}{d_1}$ ，则当 $n - m$ 时， $nd_{1} > d_{2}$ ，因此 $c_{n} = b_{1} - a_{1}n$ 。
> > 此时， $c_{m}, c_{m+1}, c_{m+2}, \cdots$ 是等差数列·
> > - **②**：当 $d_{1}=0$ 时，对任意 n=1 ，
> > $$
> > c _ {n} = b _ {1} - a _ {1} n + (n - 1) \max \left\{d _ {2}, 0 \right\} = b _ {1} - a _ {1} + (n - 1) (\max \left\{d _ {2}, 0 \right\} - a _ {1}).
> > $$
> > 此时， $c_{1},c_{2},c_{3},\cdots,c_{n},\cdots$ 是等差数列·
> > - **③**：当 $d_{1}<0$ 时，
> > 当 $n > \frac{d_2}{d_1}$ 时，有 $nd_{1} < d_{2}$
> > 所以 $\frac{c_{n}}{n}=\frac{b_{1}-a_{1}n+(n-1)(d_{2}-nd_{1})}{n}=n(-d_{1})+d_{1}-a_{1}+d_{2}+\frac{b_{1}-d_{2}}{n}$
> > $n\left(-d_{1}\right) + d_{1} - a_{1} + d_{2} - \left|b_{1} - d_{2}\right|$ .
> > 对任意正数 $M$ ，取正整数 $m > \max \left\{\frac{M + \left|b_1 - d_2\right| + a_1 - d_1 - d_2}{-d_1},\frac{d_2}{d_1}\right\}$
> > 故当 $n$ 时， $\frac{c_n}{n} >M$
