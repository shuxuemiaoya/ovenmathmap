> [!faq]- 《专题01 集合与常用逻辑用语》真题解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 求出集合 $M, N$ 后可求 $M \cap N$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $M = \{x\mid 0\leq x <   16\} ,N = \{x\mid x\quad \frac{1}{3}\} ,$ 故 $M\cap N = \left\{x\Big|\frac{1}{3}\leq x <   16\right\} .$
> >
> > 故选 **D**
