> [!faq]- 2014年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（新课标ⅰ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （Ⅰ）利用 $a_{n}a_{n+1}=\lambda S_{n}-1,\quad a_{n+1}a_{n+2}=\lambda S_{n+1}-1$ ，相减即可得出；
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 分析】（Ⅰ）利用 $a_{n}a_{n+1}=\lambda S_{n}-1,\quad a_{n+1}a_{n+2}=\lambda S_{n+1}-1$ ，相减即可得出；
> >
> > （II）假设存在 $\lambda$ ，使得 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为等差数列，设公差为 $d$ .可得 $\lambda = a_{n + 2} - a_n = (a_{n + 2} - a_{n + 1}) +$
> >
> > $(a_{n+1} - a_n) = 2d, d = \frac{\lambda}{2}$ . 得到 $\lambda S_n = \frac{\lambda^2}{4} n^2 + (\lambda - \frac{\lambda^2}{4})n + 2 - \frac{\lambda}{2}$ , 根据 $\{a_n\}$ 为等差数
> >
> > 列的充要条件是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \lambda \neq 0 \\ 2 - \frac{\lambda}{2} = 0 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\lambda$ 即可.
> >
> > 【
> > 解答】（Ⅰ）证明：∵ $a_{n}a_{n+1}=\lambda S_{n}-1$ ， $a_{n+1}a_{n+2}=\lambda S_{n+1}-1$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n + 1} \left(a _ {n + 2} - a _ {n}\right) = \lambda a _ {n + 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because a _ {n + 1} = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n + 2} - a _ {n} = \lambda .
> > $$
> >
> > （II）解：假设存在 $\lambda$ ，使得 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为等差数列，设公差为 d.
> >
> > 则 $\lambda=a_{n+2}-a_{n}=\left(a_{n+2}-a_{n+1}\right)+\left(a_{n+1}-a_{n}\right)=2d,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore d = \frac {\lambda}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = 1 + \frac {\lambda (n - 1)}{2}, \quad a _ {n + 1} = 1 + \frac {\lambda n}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \lambda S _ {n} = 1 + [ 1 + \frac {\lambda (n - 1)}{2} ] [ 1 + \frac {\lambda n}{2} ] = \frac {\lambda^ {2}}{4} n ^ {2} + (\lambda - \frac {\lambda^ {2}}{4}) n + 2 - \frac {\lambda}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 根据 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为等差数列的充要条件是 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\lambda&\neq0\\2-\frac{\lambda}{2}&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\lambda=4$ .
> >
> > 此时可得 $S_{n}=n^{2}$ ， $a_{n}=2n-1$ 。
> >
> > 因此存在 $\lambda=4$ ，使得 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为等差数列.
> >

> >

> >
