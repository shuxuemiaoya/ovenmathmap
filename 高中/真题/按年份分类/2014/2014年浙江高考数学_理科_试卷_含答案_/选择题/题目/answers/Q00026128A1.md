> [!faq]- 2014年浙江高考数学(理科)试卷(含答案)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题为考场高考真题，
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $y = \sin 3x + \cos 3x = \sqrt{2} \sin (3x + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \sqrt{2} \sin [3(x + \frac{\pi}{12})]$ 而 $y=\sqrt{2}\cos3x=\sqrt{2}\sin(3x+\frac{\pi}{2})=\sqrt{2}\sin[3(x+\frac{\pi}{6})]$ 由 $3(x+\frac{\pi}{6})\rightarrow3(x+\frac{\pi}{12})$ ，即 $x\rightarrow x-\frac{\pi}{12}$ 故只需将 $y=\sqrt{2}\cos3x$ 的图象向右平移 $\frac{\pi}{12}$ 个单位. 故选 C
> >
