> [!faq]- 2014年浙江高考数学(理科)试卷(含答案)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\left|\left(\frac{i}{99}\right)^{2}-\left(\frac{i-1}{99}\right)^{2}\right|=\frac{1}{99}\frac{2i-1}{99}$ ，
> > 故 $I_{1}=\frac{1}{99}\left(\frac{1}{99}+\frac{3}{99}+\frac{5}{99}+\cdots+\frac{2\times99-1}{99}\right)=\frac{1}{99}\frac{99^{2}}{99}=1$ 由 $2\left|\frac{i}{99}-\frac{i-1}{99}-\left(\frac{i}{99}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{i-1}{99}\right)^{2}\right|=2\times\frac{1}{99}\left|\frac{99-(2i-1)}{99}\right|$ 故 $I_{2}=2\times\frac{1}{99}\times2\times\frac{50(98+0)}{2\times99}=\frac{98}{99}\frac{100}{99}<1$ $I_{3}=\frac{1}{3}\left(\left|\sin(2\pi\frac{1}{99})\right|-\left|\sin(2\pi\frac{0}{99})\right|\right)+\left|\sin(2\pi\frac{2}{99})\right|-\left|\sin(2\pi\frac{1}{99})\right|+\cdots+\left|\sin(2\pi\frac{99}{99})\right|-\left|\sin(2\pi\frac{98}{99})\right|$ $=\frac{1}{3}[2\sin(2\pi\frac{25}{99})-2\sin(2\pi\frac{74}{99})]>1$ 故 $I_{2}<I_{1}<I_{3}$ ，故选B
> >
