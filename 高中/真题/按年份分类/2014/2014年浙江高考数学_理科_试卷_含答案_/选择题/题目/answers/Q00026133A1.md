> [!faq]- 2014年浙江高考数学(理科)试卷(含答案)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 1】 $p_1 = \frac{m}{m + n} +\frac{n}{m + n}\times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{2m + n}{2(m + n)}$
> >
> > $$
> > p _ {2} = \frac {C _ {n} ^ {2}}{C _ {m + n} ^ {2}} \square \frac {1}{3} + \frac {C _ {m} ^ {1} C _ {n} ^ {1}}{C _ {m + n} ^ {2}} \square \frac {2}{3} + \frac {C _ {m} ^ {2}}{C _ {m + n} ^ {2}} = \frac {3 m ^ {2} - 3 m + 2 m n + n ^ {2} - n}{3 (m + n) (m + n - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore p _ {1} - p _ {2} = \frac {2 m + n}{2 (m + n)} - \frac {3 m ^ {2} - 3 m + 2 m n + n ^ {2} - n}{3 (m + n) (m + n - 1)} = \frac {5 m n + n (n - 1)}{6 (m + n) (m + n - 1)} > 0,
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $p_1 > p_2$
> >
> > $$
> > \because P \left(\xi_ {1} = 1\right) = \frac {n}{m + n}, P \left(\xi_ {1} = 2\right) = \frac {m}{m + n}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore E (\xi_ {1}) = 1 \times \frac {n}{m + n} + 2 \times \frac {m}{m + n} = \frac {2 m + n}{m + n}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(\xi_ {2} = 1\right) = \frac {C _ {n} ^ {2}}{C _ {m + n} ^ {2}} = \frac {n (n - 1)}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(\xi_ {2} = 2\right) = \frac {C _ {n} ^ {1} C _ {m} ^ {1}}{C _ {m + n} ^ {2}} = \frac {2 m n}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(\xi_ {2} = 3\right) = \frac {C _ {m} ^ {2}}{C _ {m + n} ^ {2}} = \frac {m (\mathrm{m} - 1)}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore E \left(\xi_ {2}\right) = 1 \times \frac {n (n - 1)}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)} + 2 \times \frac {2 m n}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)} + 3 \times \frac {m (m - 1)}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {3 m ^ {2} + n ^ {2} - 3 m - n + 4 m n}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > E \left(\xi_ {2}\right) - E \left(\xi_ {1}\right) = \frac {3 m ^ {2} + n ^ {2} - 3 m - n + 4 m n}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)} - \frac {2 m + n}{m + n} = \frac {m (m - 1) + m n}{(m + n) (m + n - 1)} > 0
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $E(\xi_{2}) > E(\xi_{1})$ ，故选 A
> >
