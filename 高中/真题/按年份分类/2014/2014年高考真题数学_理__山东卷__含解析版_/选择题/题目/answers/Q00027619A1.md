> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > e _ {1} ^ {2} = \frac {c ^ {2}}{a ^ {2}} = \frac {a ^ {2} - b ^ {2}}{a ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > e _ {2} ^ {2} = \frac {c ^ {2}}{a ^ {2}} = \frac {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2}}{a ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left(e _ {1} e _ {2}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {a ^ {4} - b ^ {4}}{a ^ {4}} = \frac {3}{4} \therefore a ^ {4} = 4 b ^ {4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {b}{a} = \pm \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}
