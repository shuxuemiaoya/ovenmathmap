18、解：（Ⅰ）设恰有一次的落点在乙上为事件 A

$$
P (A) = \frac {5}{6} \times \frac {1}{5} + \frac {1}{6} \times \frac {4}{5} = \frac {3}{1 0}
$$

(II) $\xi$ 的可能取值为 0,1,2,3,4,6

$$
P (\xi = 0) = \frac {1}{6} \times \frac {1}{5} = \frac {1}{3 0}, P (\xi = 1) = \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{5} + \frac {1}{6} \times \frac {3}{5} = \frac {1}{6}
$$

$$
P (\xi = 2) = \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {3}{5} = \frac {1}{5}, P (\xi = 3) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{5} + \frac {1}{6} \times \frac {1}{5} = \frac {2}{1 5}
$$

$$
P (\xi = 4) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {3}{5} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{5} = \frac {1 1}{3 0}, P (\xi = 6) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{5} = \frac {1}{1 0}
$$

$\therefore \xi$ 的分布列为

<table><tr><td>ξ</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>4</td><td>6</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{30}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{6}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{5}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{15}$ </td><td> $\frac{11}{30}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{10}$ </td></tr></table>

∴其数学期望为 $E(\xi)=0\times\frac{1}{30}+1\times\frac{1}{6}+2\times\frac{1}{5}+3\times\frac{2}{15}+4\times\frac{11}{30}+6\times\frac{1}{10}=\frac{91}{30}$
