> [!faq]- 2014年高考浙江文科数学试题及答案(精校版)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案: (I) $\because a_{1}=1, S_{2} \cdot S_{3}=36$ , $\therefore (2 \times 1 + \frac{2 \times 1}{2} \times d)(3 \times 1 + \frac{3 \times 2}{2} \times d)=36$ , $\therefore (d+5)(d-2)=0$ , 而 $d>0$ , $\therefore d=2$ , $\therefore S_{n}=na_{1}+\frac{n(n-1)}{2}d=n+\frac{n(n-1)}{2} \times 2=n^{2}$ .
> > (Ⅱ) 由 $a_{m}+a_{m+1}+a_{m+2}+\ldots+a_{m+k}=65$ , 得 $S_{m+k}-S_{m-1}=65$ , $\therefore (m+k)^{2}-(m-1)^{2}=65$ , $\therefore (2m+k-1)(k+1)=65=5 \times 13$ , $\therefore k+1=5$ , 且 $2m+k-1=13$ , $\therefore k=4, m=5$ .
