> [!faq]- 2014年山东省高考数学试卷(理科)word版试卷及解析解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由条件可知 $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = cb \cos A = \tan A$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > A = \frac {\pi}{6}, b c = \frac {2}{3}, S _ {\triangle A B C} = \frac {1}{2} b c \sin A = \frac {1}{6}
> > $$
