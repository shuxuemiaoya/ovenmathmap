> > $e_{1}^{2}=\frac{c^{2}}{a^{2}}=\frac{a^{2}-b^{2}}{a^{2}}$ $e_{2}^{2}=\frac{c^{2}}{a^{2}}=\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}}{a^{2}}$ $\therefore(e_{1}e_{2})^{2}=\frac{a^{4}-b^{4}}{a^{4}}=\frac{3}{4}\therefore a^{4}=4b^{4}$ $\therefore\frac{b}{a}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
