> > 本题解析推导如下：
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：由题意 $2c = |AB|$ ，所以 $|AC| = 2 \times 2c \times \sin 60^{\circ} = 2\sqrt{3}c$ ，由双曲线的定义
> >
> > 有 $2a = |AC| - |BC| = 2\sqrt{3}c - 2c \Rightarrow a = (\sqrt{3} - 1)c$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore e = \frac {c}{a} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {3} - 1} = \frac {1 + \sqrt {3}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
