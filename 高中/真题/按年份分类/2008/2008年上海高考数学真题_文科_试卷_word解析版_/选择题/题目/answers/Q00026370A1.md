> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}S&=\frac{a_{1}}{1-q}\\ |q|<1\end{aligned}\right.$ $\Rightarrow$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=\frac{1}{1-a+\frac{3}{2}}\\ |a-\frac{3}{2}|<1\end{aligned}\right.$ $\Rightarrow$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=\frac{1}{2}\text{或}a=2\\ \frac{1}{2}<a<\frac{5}{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ $\Rightarrow$ $a=2$ .
