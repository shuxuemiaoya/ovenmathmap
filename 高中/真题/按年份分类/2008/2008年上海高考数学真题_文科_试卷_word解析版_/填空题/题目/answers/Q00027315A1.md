> [!faq]- 2008年上海高考数学真题（文科）试卷（word解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{7}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $|a+b|^{2}=(a+b)\square(a+b)=a\square a+b\square b+2a\square b$
> >
> > $$
> > \Rightarrow a \mid^ {2} + | b \mid^ {2} + 2 | a | | b | \cos \frac {\pi}{3} = 7 \Rightarrow | a + b | = \sqrt {7}.
> > $$
