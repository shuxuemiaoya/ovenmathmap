18. [解] (1) $|MN| = \left|\sin\left(2 \times \frac{\pi}{4}\right) - \cos\left(2 \times \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right|$

$$
= \left| 1 - \cos \frac {2 \pi}{3} \right| = \frac {3}{2}.
$$

……2分

(2) $|MN| = \left|\sin 2t - \cos \left(2t + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right|$

$$
= \left| \frac {3}{2} \sin 2 t - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos 2 t \right|
$$

……8分

$$
= \sqrt {3} \left| \sin \left(2 t - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \right|.
$$

$\because t\in\left[0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right],\quad2t-\frac{\pi}{6}\in\left[-\frac{\pi}{6},\pi-\frac{\pi}{6}\right],$ ……13分

∴ $\left|MN\right|$ 的最大值为 $\sqrt{3}$ . ..... 15 分
