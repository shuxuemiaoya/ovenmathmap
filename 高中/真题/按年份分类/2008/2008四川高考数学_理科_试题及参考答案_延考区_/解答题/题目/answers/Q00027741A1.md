> [!faq]- 2008四川高考数学(理科)试题及参考答案（延考区）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （12分）(2008·四川)设椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} +\frac{y^2}{b^2} =
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （Ⅰ）设 $M(\sqrt{2}a, y_{1})$ ， $N(\sqrt{2}a, y_{2})$ ，根据题意由 $\overrightarrow{F_{1}M} \cdot \overrightarrow{F_{2}N}=0$ 得
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：由 $a^2 - b^2 = c^2$ 与 $e = \frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，得 $a^2 = 2b^2$ ， $F_1(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} a, 0)$ ， $F_2(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} a, 0)$ ，1的方程为 $x = \sqrt{2} a$ 设 $\mathbb{M}(\sqrt{2} a, y_1)$ ， $N(\sqrt{2} a, y_2)$ 则 $\overrightarrow{F_1M} = (\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2} a, y_1)$ ， $\overrightarrow{F_2N} = (\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} a, y_2)$ 由 $\overrightarrow{F_1M} \cdot \overrightarrow{F_2N} = 0$ 得 $y_1y_2 = -\frac{3}{2} a^2 < 0$ ①
> > （1）由 $|\overrightarrow{\mathrm{F}_1\mathbb{M}}| = |\overrightarrow{\mathrm{F}_2\mathbb{N}}| = 2\sqrt{5}$ ，得 $\sqrt{\left(\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}a\right)^2 + y_1^2} = 2\sqrt{5}^{②}\sqrt{\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}a\right)^2 + y_2^2} = 2\sqrt{5}$
> >
> > 由①、②、③三式，消去 $y_{1}$ ， $y_{2}$ ，并求得 $a^{2}=4$
> >
> > 故 $a = 2$ ， $b = \frac{2}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$
> >
> > （Ⅱ）证明： $|MN|^{2}=(y_{1}-y_{2})^{2}=y_{1}^{2}+y_{2}^{2}-2y_{1}y_{2}\geq-2y_{1}y_{2}-2y_{1}y_{2}=-4y_{1}y_{2}=6a^{2}$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $y_{1} = -y_{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2} a$ 或 $y_{2} = -y_{1} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2} a$ 时，|MN|取最小值 $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2} a$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {\mathrm{F} _ {1} \mathrm{M}} + \overrightarrow {\mathrm{F} _ {2} \mathrm{N}} = (\frac {3 \sqrt {2}}{2} \mathrm{a}, \mathrm{y} _ {1}) + (\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \mathrm{a}, \mathrm{y} _ {2}) = (2 \sqrt {2} \mathrm{a}, \mathrm{y} _ {1} + \mathrm{y} _ {2}) = (2 \sqrt {2} \mathrm{a}, 0) = 2 \overrightarrow {\mathrm{F} _ {1} \mathrm{F} _ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{F}_1\mathrm{M}} +\overrightarrow{\mathrm{F}_2\mathrm{N}}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{\mathrm{F}_1\mathrm{F}_2}$ 共线.
