> [!faq]- 2008年上海高考数学真题（理科）试卷（word解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{7}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \left| a + b \right| ^ {2} = (a + b) \square (a + b) = a \square a + b \square b + 2 a \square b \neq | a | ^ {2} + | b | ^ {2} + 2 | a | | b | \cos \frac {\pi}{3} = 7 \Rightarrow | a + b | = 7.
> > $$
