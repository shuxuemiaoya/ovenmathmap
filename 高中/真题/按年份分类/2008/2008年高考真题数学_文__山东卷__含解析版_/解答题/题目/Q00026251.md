18. 解: (Ⅰ) 从 8 人中选出日语、俄语和韩语志愿者各 1 名, 其一切可能的结果组成的基本事件空间

$$
\Omega = ^ {\left\{\left(A _ {1}, B _ {1}, C _ {1}\right) \left(A _ {1}, B _ {1}, C _ {2}\right) \left(A _ {1}, B _ {2}, C _ {1}\right) ^ {\prime} \left(A _ {1}, B _ {2}, C _ {2}\right) \left(A _ {1}, B _ {3}, C _ {1}\right) ^ {\prime} \right.}
$$

$$
\left(A _ {1}, B _ {3}, C _ {2}\right) ^ {\prime} \left(A _ {2}, B _ {1}, C _ {1}\right) \left(A _ {2}, B _ {1}, C _ {2}\right) \left(A _ {2}, B _ {2}, C _ {1}\right) ^ {\prime} \left(A _ {2}, B _ {2}, C _ {2}\right) ^ {\prime}
$$

$$
\left(A _ {2}, B _ {3}, C _ {1}\right) ^ {\prime} \left(A _ {2}, B _ {3}, C _ {2}\right) ^ {\prime} \left(A _ {3}, B _ {1}, C _ {1}\right) \left(A _ {3}, B _ {1}, C _ {2}\right) \left(A _ {3}, B _ {2}, C _ {1}\right) ^ {\prime}
$$

$$
\left(A _ {3}, B _ {2}, C _ {2}\right) \left(A _ {3}, B _ {3}, C _ {1}\right) \left(A _ {3}, B _ {3}, C _ {2}\right) ^ {\}}
$$

由18个基本事件组成。由于每一个基本事件被抽取的机会均等，因此这些基本事件的发生是等可能的。

用 $M$ 表示“ $A_{1}$ 恰被选中”这一事件，则

$$
M = ^ {\{} \left(A _ {1}, B _ {1}, C _ {1}\right) \left(A _ {1}, B _ {1}, C _ {2}\right) \left(A _ {1}, B _ {2}, C _ {1}\right),
$$

$$
\left. \left(A _ {1}, B _ {2}, C _ {2}\right) \left(A _ {1}, B _ {3}, C _ {1}\right) \left(A _ {1}, B _ {3}, C _ {2}\right) ^ \} \right.
$$

事件 M 由 6 个基本事件组成，

因而 $P(M)=\frac{6}{18}=\frac{1}{3}$ .

（II）用 $N$ 表示“ $B_{1}$ ， $C_{1}$ 不全被选中”这一事件，则其对立事件 $\overline{N}$ 表示“ $B_{1}$ ， $C_{1}$ 全被选中”这一事件，

由于 $\overline{N} = \{(A_1, B_1, C_1)(A_2, B_1, C_1)(A_3, B_1, C_1)\}$ ，事件 $\overline{N}$ 有3个基本事件组成，

所以 $P(\overline{N}) = \frac{3}{18} = \frac{1}{6}$ ，由对立事件的概率公式得 $P(N) = 1 - P(\overline{N}) = 1 - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$ .
