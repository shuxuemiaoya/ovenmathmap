> [!faq]- 2008年高考真题数学【文】(山东卷)（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 主要考查三角函数变换与求值。
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + \sin \alpha = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos \alpha + \frac {3}{2} \sin \alpha = \frac {4}{5} \sqrt {3} \frac {1}{2} \cos \alpha + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin \alpha = \frac {4}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {7 \pi}{6}\right) = - \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = - \left(\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin \alpha + \frac {1}{2} \cos \alpha\right) = - \frac {4}{5}.
> > $$
