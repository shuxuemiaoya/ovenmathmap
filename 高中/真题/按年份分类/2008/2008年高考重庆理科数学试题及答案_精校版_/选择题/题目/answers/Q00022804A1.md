> > 解：由定比分点坐标公式 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x=\frac{x_{1}+\lambda x_{2}}{1+\lambda}\\&y=\frac{y_{1}+\lambda y_{2}}{1+\lambda}\end{aligned}\right.$ 得 $\lambda=\frac{x_{1}-x}{x-x_{2}}=\frac{y_{1}-y}{y-y_{2}}$ 不妨设点 P(x,0)，
> > 则 $\lambda=\frac{0-2}{6-0}=-\frac{1}{3}$ 故答案选 A
