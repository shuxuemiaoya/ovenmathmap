> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 本小题主要考查平均数、方差、标准差的概念及其运算。

> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\because \overline {{{x}}} = \frac {1 0 0 + 4 0 + 9 0 + 6 0 + 1 0}{1 0 0} = 3,} \\ & {\therefore S ^ {2} = \frac {1}{n} [ (x _ {1} - x) ^ {2} + (x _ {2} - x) ^ {2} + \dots + (x _ {n} - x) ^ {2} ]} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {1}{1 0 0} [ 2 0 \times 2 ^ {2} + 1 0 \times 1 ^ {2} + 3 0 \times 1 ^ {2} + 1 0 \times 2 ^ {2} ] = \frac {1 6 0}{1 0 0} = \frac {8}{5}, \Rightarrow S = \frac {2 \sqrt {1 0}}{5}.} \\ & {\text {选B.}} \end{array}
