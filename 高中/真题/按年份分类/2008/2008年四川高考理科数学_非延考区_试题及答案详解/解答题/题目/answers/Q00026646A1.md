> [!faq]- 2008年四川高考理科数学(非延考区)试题及答案详解解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 题目这么容易，估计今年的评分标准要偏严了.
> >
> > (Ⅰ) $P=0.5\times(1-0.6)+(1-0.5)\times0.6=0.2+0.3=0.5$
> >
> > (II) $P = 1 - (1 - 0.5)(1 - 0.6) = 0.8$
> >
> > (III) $\xi$ 可取 0, 1, 2, 3.
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 0) = C _ {3} ^ {0} \times (1 - 0. 8) ^ {3} = 0. 0 0 8
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 1) = C _ {3} ^ {1} \times (1 - 0. 8) ^ {2} \times 0. 8 = 0. 0 9 6
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 2) = C _ {3} ^ {2} \times (1 - 0. 8) \times 0. 8 ^ {2} = 0. 3 8 4 \quad P (\xi = 3) = C _ {3} ^ {3} \times 0. 8 ^ {3} = 0. 5 1 2
> > $$
> >
> > $\xi$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>ξ</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td></tr><tr><td rowspan="2">P</td><td>0.00</td><td>0.09</td><td>0.38</td><td>0.51</td></tr><tr><td>8</td><td>6</td><td>4</td><td>2</td></tr></table>
> >
> > $$
> > E \xi = 3 \times 0. 8 = 2. 4
> > $$
