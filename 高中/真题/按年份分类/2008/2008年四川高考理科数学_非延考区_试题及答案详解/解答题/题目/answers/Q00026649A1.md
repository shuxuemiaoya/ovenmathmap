> [!faq]- 2008年四川高考理科数学(非延考区)试题及答案详解解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 数列和解几位列倒数第三和第二，意料之中．开始挤牙膏吧.
> >
> > （Ⅰ）由已知， $F_{1}(-c,0)$ ， $F_{2}(c,0)$ ．由 $e=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ， $\frac{c^{2}}{a^{2}}=\frac{1}{2}$ ， $\therefore a^{2}=2c^{2}$
> >
> > 又 $a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2},\therefore b^{2}=c^{2},a^{2}=2b^{2}.$
> >
> > $\therefore l: x = \frac{a^2}{c} = \frac{2c^2}{c} = 2c, M(2c, y_1), N(2c, y_2).$
> >
> > 延长 $NF_{2}$ 交 $MF_{1}$ 于 P，记右准线 l 交 x 轴于 Q。
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore F _ {1} M \cdot F _ {2} N = 0, \therefore F _ {1} M \perp F _ {2} N, F _ {1} M \perp F _ {2} N
> > $$
> >
> > 由平几知识易证 $Rt\Delta MQF_{1}\cong Rt\Delta F_{2}QN$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | Q N | = \left| F _ {1} Q \right| = 3 c, \quad | Q M | = \left| F _ {2} Q \right| = c
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $\left|y_{1}\right|=c,\quad\left|y_{2}\right|=3c$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| F _ {1} M \right| = \left| F _ {2} N \right| = 2 \sqrt {5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 9 c ^ {2} + c ^ {2} = 2 0, \quad c ^ {2} = 2, \quad b ^ {2} = 2, \quad a ^ {2} = 4.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a = 2, b = \sqrt {2}.
> > $$
> >
> > （Ⅰ）另解：∵ $F_{1}M \cdot F_{2}N = 0$ ，∴ $(3c, y_{1}) \cdot (c, y_{2}) = 0$ ， $y_{1}y_{2} = -3c^{2} < 0$ 。
> > 又 $\left|F_{1}M\right| = \left|F_{2}N\right| = 2\sqrt{5}$ 联立 $\begin{cases} y_{1}y_{2} = -3c^{2} \\ 9c^{2} + y_{1}^{2} = 20 \\ c^{2} + y_{2}^{2} = 20 \end{cases}$ ，消去 $y_{1}$ 、 $y_{2}$ 得： $(20 - 9c^{2})(20 - c^{2}) = 9c^{2}$ ，
> >
> > 整理得： $9c^{4}-209c^{2}+400=0$ ， $(c^{2}-2)(9c^{2}-200)=0$ ．解得 $c^{2}=2$ ．但解此方程组要考倒不少人.
> >
> > (II) $\because F_{1}M\cdot F_{2}N = (3c,y_{1})\cdot (c,y_{2}) = 0,\therefore y_{1}y_{2} = -3c^{2} <   0.$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| M N \right| ^ {2} = \left| y _ {1} - y _ {2} \right| ^ {2} = y _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2} - 2 y _ {1} y _ {2} \geq - 2 y _ {1} y _ {2} - 2 y _ {1} y _ {2} = - 4 y _ {1} y _ {2} = 1 2 c ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $y_{1}=-y_{2}=\sqrt{3}c$ 或 $y_{2}=-y_{1}=\sqrt{3}c$ 时，取等号．此时 $\left|MN\right|$ 取最小值 $2\sqrt{3}c$ .
> >
> > 此时 $F_{1}M + F_{2}N = (3c, \pm \sqrt{3} c) + (c, \mp \sqrt{3} c) = (4c, 0) = 2F_{1}F_{2}$ .
> >
> > $\therefore F_{1}M + F_{2}N$ 与 $F_{1}F_{2}$ 共线.
> >
> > （Ⅱ）另解： $\because F_{1}M \cdot F_{2}N = 0$ ， $\therefore (3c, y_{1}) \cdot (c, y_{2}) = 0$ ， $y_{1}y_{2} = -3c^{2}$ .
> >
> > 设 $MF_{1}$ ， $NF_{2}$ 的斜率分别为k， $-\frac{1}{k}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \text {由} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = k (x + c) \\ x = 2 c \end{array} \Rightarrow y _ {1} = 3 k c \right., \text {由} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = - \frac {1}{k} (x - c) \\ x = 2 c \end{array} \Rightarrow y _ {2} = - \frac {c}{k} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| M N \right| = \left| y _ {1} - y _ {2} \right| = c \cdot \left| 3 k + \frac {1}{k} \right| \geq 2 \sqrt {3} c. \text {当且仅当} 3 k = \frac {1}{k} \text {即} k ^ {2} = \frac {1}{3}, k = \pm \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \text {时}
> > $$
> >
> > 取等号.
> >
> > 即当 $\left|MN\right|$ 最小时， $k=\pm\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > F _ {1} M + F _ {2} N = (3 c, 3 k c) + (c, - \frac {c}{k}) = (3 c, \pm \sqrt {3} c) + (c, \mp \sqrt {3} c) = (4 c, 0) = 2 F _ {1} F _ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore F _ {1} M + F _ {2} N \text {与} F _ {1} F _ {2} \text {共线}.
> > $$
