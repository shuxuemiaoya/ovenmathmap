> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设等差数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 公差为 $d$ ，而 $b_{n} = \begin{cases} a_{n} - 6, & n = 2k - 1 \\ 2a_{n}, & n = 2k \end{cases}, k \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ ，
> >
> > 则 $b_{1} = a_{1} - 6, b_{2} = 2a_{2} = 2a_{1} + 2d, b_{3} = a_{3} - 6 = a_{1} + 2d - 6$
> >
> > 于是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} S_{4} = 4a_{1} + 6d = 32 \\ T_{3} = 4a_{1} + 4d - 12 = 16, \text{解得} a_{1} = 5, d = 2, a_{n} = a_{1} + (n - 1)d = 2n + 3, \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的通项公式是 $a_{n}=2n+3$ .
