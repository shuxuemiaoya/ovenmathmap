> > $$

> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > A E = A B + B E = A B + \lambda B C
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > A F = A B + B C + C F = A B + B C + \frac {1 - 9 \lambda}{1 8 \lambda} A B = \frac {1 + 9 \lambda}{1 8 \lambda} A B + B C
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > A E \cdot A F = (A B + \lambda B C) \cdot (\frac {1 + 9 \lambda}{1 8 \lambda} A B + B C)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1 + 9 \lambda}{1 8 \lambda} A B ^ {2} + \lambda B C ^ {2} + (1 + \lambda \frac {1 + 9 \lambda}{1 8 \lambda}) A B \cdot B C
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1 + 9 \lambda}{1 8 \lambda} \times 4 + \lambda + \frac {1 9 + 9 \lambda}{1 8 \lambda} \times 2 \times 1 \times \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}

> > $$
> > = \frac {2}{9 \lambda} + \frac {1}{2} \lambda + \frac {1 7}{1 8} \geq 2 \sqrt {\frac {2}{9 \lambda} \cdot \frac {1}{2} \lambda} + \frac {1 7}{1 8} = \frac {2 9}{1 8}
