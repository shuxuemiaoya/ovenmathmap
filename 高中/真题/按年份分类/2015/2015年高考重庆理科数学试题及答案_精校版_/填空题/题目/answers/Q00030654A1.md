> [!faq]- 2015年高考重庆理科数学试题及答案(精校版)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 过程:
> >
> > (I)由 $\lambda=0,\mu=-2,$ 有 $a_{n+1}a_{n}=2a_{n}^{2}(n\in N_{+})$ ,
> >
> > 若存在某个 $n_0 \in N_+$ , 使得 $a_{n_0} = 0$ ,
> >
> > 则由上述递推公式易得 $a_{n_0 - 1} = 0$
> >
> > 重复上述过程可得 $a_{1} = 0$ ，与已知矛盾，
> >
> > 所以对任意的 $n \in N_{+}$ ， $a_{n} \neq 0$ .
> >
> > 从而 $a_{n + 1} = 2a_{n}(n\in N_{+})$ ，即 $\{a_n\}$ 是一个公比 $q = 2$ 的等比数列.
> >
> > 故 $a_{n} = a_{1}q^{n - 1} = 3\cdot 2^{n - 1}$
> >
> > $\lambda=\frac{1}{k_{0}},\mu=-1,$ (Ⅱ)由 数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的递推关系式变为
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n + 1} a _ {n} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n} ^ {2} \leq 0, \quad a _ {n + 1} (a _ {n} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}}) = a _ {n} ^ {2} (n \in N _ {+}).
> > $$
> >
> > 由上式及 $a_1 = 3 > 0$ ，归纳可得
> >
> > $$
> > 3 = a _ {1} > a _ {2} > \dots > a _ {n} > a _ {n + 1} > \dots > 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n + 1} = \frac {a _ {n} ^ {2}}{a _ {n} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}}} = \frac {a _ {n} ^ {2} - \frac {1}{k _ {0} ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0} ^ {2}}}{a _ {n} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}}} = a _ {n} - \frac {1}{k _ {0}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \text { 口 } \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {n} + 1},
> > $$
> >
> > 因为
> >
> > 所以对 $n = 1,2,\dots ,k_{0}$
> >
> > 求和得 $a_{k_0 + 1} = a_1 + (a_2 - a_1) + \dots + (a_{k_0 + 1} - a_{k_0})$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {1} - k _ {0} \cdot \frac {1}{k _ {0}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {1} + 1} + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {2} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {k _ {0}} + 1}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > > 2 + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1} + \frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1}\right) = 2 + \frac {1}{3 k _ {0} + 1}
> > $$
> >
> > 另一方面，由上面已证的不等式知
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {1} > a _ {2} > \dots > a _ {k _ {0}} > a _ {k _ {0} + 1} > \dots > 2,
> > $$
> >
> > 得
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {k _ {0} + 1} = a _ {1} - k _ {0} \cdot \frac {1}{k _ {0}} + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {1} + 1} + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {2} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{k _ {0} a _ {k _ {0}} + 1}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > <   2 + \frac {1}{k _ {0}} \cdot \left(\frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1} + \frac {1}{2 k _ {\text {个}} + 1} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1}\right) = 2 + \frac {1}{2 k _ {0} + 1}
> > $$
> >
> > 综上， $2+\frac{1}{3k_{0}+1}<a_{k_{0}+1}<2+\frac{1}{2k_{0}+1}.$
