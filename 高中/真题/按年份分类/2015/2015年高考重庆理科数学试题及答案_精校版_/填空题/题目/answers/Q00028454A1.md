> [!faq]- 2015年高考重庆理科数学试题及答案(精校版)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 过程:
> >
> > (I)由椭圆的定义，
> >
> > $$
> > 2 a \neq \left| P F _ {1} \right| + \left| P F _ {2} \right| = (2 + \sqrt {2}) + (2 - \sqrt {2}) = 4,
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $a = 2$
> >
> > 设椭圆的半焦距为 c，由已知 $PF_{1} \perp PF_{2}$ ，因此
> >
> > $$
> > 2 c \neq \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = \sqrt {\left| P F _ {1} \right| ^ {2} + \left| P F _ {2} \right| ^ {2}} = \sqrt {(2 + \sqrt {2}) ^ {2} + (2 - \sqrt {2}) ^ {2}} = 2 \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $c = \sqrt{3}$ ，从而 $b = \sqrt{a^2 - c^2} = 1.$
> >
> > 故所求椭圆的标准方程为 $\frac{x^2}{4} +\frac{y^2}{1} = 1$
> >
> > (Ⅱ)解法一：设点 $P(x_0,y_0)$ 在椭圆上，且 $PF_{1}\perp PF_{2}$ ，则
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {x _ {0} ^ {2}}{a ^ {2}} + \frac {y _ {0} ^ {2}}{b ^ {2}} = 1, x _ {0} ^ {2} + y _ {0} ^ {2} = c ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > 求得 $x_0 = \pm \frac{a}{c}\sqrt{a^2 - 2b^2}, y_0 = \pm \frac{b^2}{c}$ .
> >
> > 由 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=|PQ|>\left|PF_{2}\right|$ 得 $x_{0}>0,$ 从而
> >
> > $$
> > \left| P F _ {1} \right| ^ {2} = \left(\frac {a \sqrt {a ^ {2} - 2 b ^ {2}}}{c} + c\right) ^ {2} + \frac {b ^ {2}}{c ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \left(a ^ {2} - b ^ {2}\right) + 2 a \sqrt {a ^ {2} - 2 b ^ {2}} = \left(a + \sqrt {a ^ {2} - 2 b ^ {2}}\right) ^ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > 由椭圆的定义，
> >
> > $\left|PF_{1}\right| + \left|PF_{2}\right| = 2a, \left|QF_{1}\right| + \left|QF_{2}\right| = 2a.$ 从而由
> >
> > $$
> > \mid P F _ {1} \mid \rightleftharpoons P Q \mid \rightleftharpoons \mid P F _ {2} \mid + \mid Q F _ {2} \mid , _ {\text {有}} \mid Q F _ {1} \mid = 4 a - 2 \mid P F _ {1} \mid ,
> > $$
> >
> > 又由 $PQ \perp PF_{1}, |PF_{1}| = |PQ|,$ 知 $|QF_{1}| = \sqrt{2} |PF_{1}|$ , 因此
> >
> > $(2 + \sqrt{2})|PF_{1}| = 4a,$ 即 $(2 + \sqrt{2})(a + \sqrt{a^2 - 2b^2}) = 4a,$
> >
> > 于是 $(2 + \sqrt{2})(1 + \sqrt{2c^2 - 1}) = 4$ ，解得
> >
> > $e = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2} [1 + (\frac{4}{2 + \sqrt{2}} - 1)^2] = \sqrt{6} - \sqrt{3}}.$
> >
> > 解法二：
> >
> > 由椭圆定义， $\left|PF_{1}\right|+\left|PF_{2}\right|=2a,\left|QF_{1}\right|+\left|QF_{2}\right|=2a.$
> >
> > 从而由 $\left|PF_{1}\right|\neq PQ\left|\neq PF_{2}\right|+\left|QF_{2}\right|,$
> >
> > 有 $|QF_{1}|=4a-2|PF_{1}|,$
> >
> > 又由 $PQ \perp PF_{1}, |PF_{1}| = |PQ|,$ 知 $|QF_{1}| = \sqrt{2} |PF_{1}|$ , 得
> >
> > $$
> > \left| P F _ {1} \right| = 2 (2 - \sqrt {2}) a,
> > $$
> >
> > 从而 $\left|PF_{2}\right|=2a-\left|PF_{1}\right|=2a-\left|PF_{1}\right|=2(\sqrt{2}-1)a.$
> >
> > 由 $PF_{1} \perp PF_{2}$ , 知 $|PF_{1}|^{2} + |PF_{2}|^{2} = |F_{1}F_{2}|^{2} = (2c)^{2}$ .
> >
> > 因此 $e = \frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{|PF_1|^2 + |PF_2|^2}}{2a} = \sqrt{(2 - \sqrt{2})^2 + (\sqrt{2} - 1)^2}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {9 - 6 \sqrt {2}} = \sqrt {6} - \sqrt {3}.
> > $$
