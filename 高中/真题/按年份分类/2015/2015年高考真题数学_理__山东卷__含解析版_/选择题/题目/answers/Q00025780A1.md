> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 过程:
> >
> > 原不等式可转化为以下三个不等式的并集：
> >
> > (1) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x < 1 \\ 1 - x + x - 5 < 2 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ x \mid x < 1 \right\}$
> >
> > (II) $\left\{\begin{aligned}1&\leq x<5\\ x-1+x-5&<2\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{x\mid1\leq x<4\right\}$
> >
> > (III) $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&\geq5\\ x&-1-x+5<2\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\phi$
> >
> > 综上，原不等式的解集为 $\left\{x\mid x < 4\right\}$ ，选A
