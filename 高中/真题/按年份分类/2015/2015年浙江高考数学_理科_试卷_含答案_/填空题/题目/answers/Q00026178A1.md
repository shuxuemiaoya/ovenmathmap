>

>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解: $\because a = \log_{4} 3$ , 可知 $4^{a} = 3$ ,
> >
> > 即 $2^{\mathrm{a}} = \sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $2^{a}+2^{-a}=\sqrt{3}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}=\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故
