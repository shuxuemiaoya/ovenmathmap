> [!faq]- 2015年浙江高考数学(理科)试卷(含答案)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答：解：
> > （1） $\because A = \frac{\pi}{4}$ ， $\therefore$ 由余弦定理可得： $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos \frac{\pi}{4}$ ， $\therefore b^2 - a^2 = \sqrt{2}bc - c^2$
> >
> > 又 $b^{2}-a^{2}=\frac{1}{2}c^{2}$ . $\therefore\sqrt{2}bc-c^{2}=\frac{1}{2}c^{2}$ . $\therefore\sqrt{2}b=\frac{3}{2}c$ . 可得 $b=\frac{3\sqrt{2}c}{4}$ ,
> >
> > $\therefore a^{2} = b^{2} - \frac{1}{2} c^{2} = \frac{5}{8} c^{2}$ 即 $a = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{4} c.$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos C = \frac {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2} - c ^ {2}}{2 a b} = \frac {\frac {5}{8} c ^ {2} + \frac {9}{8} c ^ {2} - c ^ {2}}{2 \times \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{4} c \times \frac {3 \sqrt {2}}{4} c} = \frac {\sqrt {5}}{5}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \because C \in (0, \pi), \\ \therefore \sin C = \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} C} = \frac {2 \sqrt {5}}{5}. \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \tan C = \frac {\sin C}{\cos C} = 2.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because S _ {\triangle A B C} = \frac {1}{2} a b \sin C = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{4} c \times \frac {3 \sqrt {2}}{4} c \times \frac {2 \sqrt {5}}{5} = 3,
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $c=2\sqrt{2}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b = \frac {3 \sqrt {2} c}{4} = 3.
> > $$
> >
> > 点评：本题考查了正弦定理余弦定理、同角三角形基本关系式、三角形面积计算公式，考查了推理能力与计算能力，属于中档题.
