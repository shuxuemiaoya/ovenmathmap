> [!faq]- 精品解析：2022年全国高考乙卷数学（理）试题（解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先算出 $\overline{z}$ ，再代入计算，实部与虚部都为零解方程组即可
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $Z = 1 + 2\mathrm{i}$
> >
> > $$
> > z + a z + b = 1 - 2 \mathrm{i} + a (1 + 2 \mathrm{i}) + b = (1 + a + b) + (2 a - 2) \mathrm{i}
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $z + a\overline{z} +b = 0$ ，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}1 + a + b = 0\\ 2a - 2 = 0 \end{array} \right.$ 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a = 1\\ b = -2 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 故选: A
