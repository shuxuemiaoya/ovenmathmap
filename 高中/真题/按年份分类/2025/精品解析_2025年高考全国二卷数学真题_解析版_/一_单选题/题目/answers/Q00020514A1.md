> [!faq]- 精品解析：2025年高考全国二卷数学真题（解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由余弦定理 $\cos A = \frac{AB^2 + AC^2 - BC^2}{2AB\square AC}$ 直接计算求解即可.详解】由题意得 $\cos A = \frac{AB^2 + AC^2 - BC^2}{2AB\square AC} = \frac{(\sqrt{6})^2 + (1 + \sqrt{3})^2 - 2^2}{2\times\sqrt{6}\times(1 + \sqrt{3})} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},$ 又 $0^{\circ} < A < 180^{\circ}$ ，所以 $A = 45^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 故选：A
