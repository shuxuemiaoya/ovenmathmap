>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 移项后转化为求一元二次不等式的解即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{x - 4}{x - 1} \geq 2$ 即为 $\frac{x + 2}{x - 1} \leq 0$ 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}(x + 2)(x - 1) \leq 0 \\ x - 1 \neq 0 \end{array} \right.$ ，故 $-2 \notin x < 1$
> >
> > 故解集为 $\{x - 2 \leq x < 1\}$ .
> >
> > 故选：C.
> > 5. $\forall ABC$ 中， $BC = 2$ ， $AC = 1 + \sqrt{3}$ ， $AB = \sqrt{6}$ ，则 $A = ()$ A. $45^{\circ}$ B. $60^{\circ}$ C. $120^{\circ}$ D. $135^{\circ}$
> >
> > 【
> >
> > 由题意得 $\cos A = \frac{AB^2 + AC^2 - BC^2}{2AB\square AC} = \frac{(\sqrt{6})^2 + (1 + \sqrt{3})^2 - 2^2}{2\times\sqrt{6}\times(1 + \sqrt{3})} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},$ 又 $0^{\circ} < A < 180^{\circ}$ ，所以 $A = 45^{\circ}$
> >
> > 故选：A
