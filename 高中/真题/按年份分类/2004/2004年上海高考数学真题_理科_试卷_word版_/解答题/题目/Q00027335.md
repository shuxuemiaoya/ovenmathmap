18. 【解】由题意得 $xy + \frac{1}{4}x^{2} = 8, \therefore y = \frac{8 - \frac{x^{2}}{4}}{x} = \frac{8}{x} - \frac{x}{4} (0 < x < 4\sqrt{2})$ .

于是, 框架用料长度为 $I=2x+2y+2\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x\right)=\left(\frac{3}{2}+\sqrt{2}\right)x+\frac{16}{x}\geq2\sqrt{16\left(\frac{3}{2}+\sqrt{2}\right)}=4\sqrt{6+4\sqrt{2}}$ 当 $\left(\frac{3}{2}+\sqrt{2}\right)x=\frac{16}{x}$ ，即 $x=8-4\sqrt{2}$ 时等号成立.
此时， $x \approx 2.343, y = 2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.
828.$ 故当 x 为 2.343m,y 为 2.828m 时, 用料最省.
