> [!faq]- 2020年高考浙江高考数学试题及答案(精校版)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题第（Ⅰ）题先根据等比数列的通项公式将 b2=q, b3=q2 代入 $b1+b2=6b3$ ，计
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （Ⅰ）解：由题意，b2=q，b3=q2，
> >
> > $$
> > \because b 1 + b 2 = 6 b 3, \therefore 1 + q = 6 q 2,
> > $$
> >
> > 整理，得 $6q2 - q - 1 = 0$
> >
> > 解得 $q = -\frac{1}{3}$ （舍去），或 $q = \frac{1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore c n + 1 = \frac {b _ {n}}{b _ {n + 2}} \cdot c n = \frac {\frac {1}{b _ {n + 2}}}{b _ {n}} \cdot c n = \frac {1}{q ^ {2}} \cdot c n = \frac {1}{(\frac {1}{2}) ^ {2}} \cdot c n = 4 \cdot c n,
> > $$
> >
> > ∴数列{cn}是以1为首项，4为公比的等比数列，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{cn} = 1 \cdot 4 n - 1 = 4 n - 1, n \in \mathrm{N} ^ {*}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a n + 1 - a n = c n + 1 = 4 n,
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $a1 = 1$
> >
> > $$
> > a n - a n - 1 = 4 n - 1,
> > $$
> >
> > 各项相加，可得
> >
> > $$
> > a n = 1 + 4 1 + 4 2 + \dots + 4 n - 1 = \frac {1 - 4 ^ {\mathrm{n}}}{1 - 4} = \frac {4 ^ {\mathrm{n}} - 1}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > （Ⅱ）证明：依题意，由 $cn + 1 = \frac{b_n}{b_{n+2}} \cdot cn (n \in \mathbb{N}^*)$ ，可得
> >
> > $$
> > b n + 2 \cdot c n + 1 = b n \cdot c n,
> > $$
> >
> > 两边同时乘以 $bn + 1$ ，可得
> >
> > $$
> > b n + 1 b n + 2 c n + 1 = b n b n + 1 \mathrm{cn},
> > $$
> >
> > $\because b1b2c1=b2=1+d,$
> >
> > ∴数列 $\{b n b n+1 c n\}$ 是一个常数列，且此常数为 $1+d$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & b n b n + 1 \mathrm{cn} = 1 + d, \\ & \therefore \mathrm{cn} = \frac {1 + \mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}}} = \frac {1 + \mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}} \cdot \frac {\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}}} = (1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}) \cdot \frac {\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}} - \mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n}}}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n}} \mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}}} = (1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}) (\frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n}}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}}}), \\ & \therefore c 1 + c 2 + \ldots + \mathrm{cn} \\ & = (1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}) (\frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {1}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {2}}) + (1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}) (\frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {2}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {3}}) + \ldots + (1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}) (\frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n}}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}}}) \\ & = (1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}) (\frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {1}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {2}} + \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {2}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {3}} + \ldots + \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n}}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}}}) \\ & = (1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}) (\frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {1}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}}}) \\ & = (1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}) (1 - \frac {1}{\mathrm{b} _ {\mathrm{n+1}}}) \\ & <   1 + \frac {1}{\mathrm{d}}, \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore c1 + c2 + \ldots + cn < 1 + \frac{1}{d}$ ，故得证.
