> [!faq]- 2020年江苏高考数学试题及答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $$ \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 2; \\ b = 2 \end{array} \right. $$ $$ M ^ {- 1} = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} \frac {2}{5} & - \frac {1}{5} \\ \frac {1}{5} & \frac {2}{5} \end{array} \right]. $$ 【
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > (1) 根据变换写出具体的矩阵关系式，然后进行矩阵的计算可得出实数 a, b 的值；
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）∵平面上点 $A(2,-1)$ 在矩阵 $M=\begin{bmatrix}a&1\\-1&b\end{bmatrix}$ 对应的变换作用下得到点 $B(3,-4)$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left[ \begin{array}{c c} a & 1 \\ - 1 & b \end{array} \right] \left[ \begin{array}{c} 2 \\ - 1 \end{array} \right] = \left[ \begin{array}{c} 3 \\ - 4 \end{array} \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2a - 1 = 3 \\ -2 - b = -4, \text{解得} \end{array} \right.$ $a = 2$ $b = 2$
> >
> > (2) 设 $M^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} m & n \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}$ , 则 $MM^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 2m + c & 2n + d \\ -m + 2c & -n + 2d \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
> >
> > $$
> > m = \frac {2}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > n = - \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 m + c = 1 \\ 2 n + d = 0 \\ - m + 2 c = 0 \\ - n + 2 d = 1 \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > c = \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > d = \frac {2}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore M ^ {- 1} = \left[ \begin{array}{c c} \frac {2}{5} & - \frac {1}{5} \\ \frac {1}{5} & \frac {2}{5} \end{array} \right]
> > $$
> >
