> [!faq]- 2020年江苏高考数学试题及答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $$ p _ {1} = \frac {1}{3}, q _ {1} = \frac {2}{3}; p _ {2} = \frac {7}{2 7}, q _ {2} = \frac {1 6}{2 7} (2) 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} = \frac {1}{3} (2 p _ {n - 1} + q _ {n - 1}) + \frac {2}{3} $$ 【
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > (1) 直接根据操作，根据古典概型概率公式可得结果；
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) $p_{1}=\frac{1\times3}{3\times3}=\frac{1}{3},q_{1}=\frac{2\times3}{3\times3}=\frac{2}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > p _ {2} = p _ {1} \times \frac {1 \times 3}{3 \times 3} + q _ {1} \times \frac {1 \times 2}{3 \times 3} = \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {2}{9} = \frac {7}{2 7},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > q _ {2} = p _ {1} \times \frac {2 \times 3}{3 \times 3} + q _ {1} \times \frac {1 \times 1 + 2 \times 2}{3 \times 3} + 0 = \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {5}{9} = \frac {1 6}{2 7}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > p _ {n} = p _ {n - 1} \times \frac {1 \times 3}{3 \times 3} + q _ {n - 1} \times \frac {1 \times 2}{3 \times 3} = \frac {1}{3} p _ {n - 1} + \frac {2}{9} q _ {n - 1},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > q _ {n} = p _ {n - 1} \times \frac {2 \times 3}{3 \times 3} + q _ {n - 1} \times \frac {1 \times 1 + 2 \times 2}{3 \times 3} + (1 - p _ {n - 1} - q _ {n - 1}) \times \frac {3 \times 2}{3 \times 3} = - \frac {1}{9} q _ {n - 1} + \frac {2}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > 因此 $2p_{n} + q_{n} = \frac{2}{3} p_{n-1} + \frac{1}{3} q_{n-1} + \frac{2}{3}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \text {从而} 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} = \frac {1}{3} \left(2 p _ {n - 1} + q _ {n - 1}\right) + \frac {2}{3}, \therefore 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} - 1 = \frac {1}{3} \left(2 p _ {n - 1} + q _ {n - 1} - 1\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > _ {\text {即}} 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} - 1 = \left(2 p _ {1} + q _ {1} - 1\right) \frac {1}{3 ^ {n - 1}}, \therefore 2 p _ {n} + q _ {n} = 1 + \frac {1}{3 ^ {n}}.
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $X_{n}$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td> $X_n$ </td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $1 - p_n - q_n$ </td><td> $q_n$ </td><td> $p_n$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > 故 $E(X_{n}) = 2p_{n} + q_{n} = 1 + \frac{1}{3^{n}}.$
