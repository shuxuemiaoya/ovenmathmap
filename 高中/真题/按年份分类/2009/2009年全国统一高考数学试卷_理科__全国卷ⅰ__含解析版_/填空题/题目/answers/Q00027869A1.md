> [!faq]- 2009年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（全国卷ⅰ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 见到二倍角 $2x$ 就想到用二倍角公式，之后转化成关于 $\tan x$ 的函数，将
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：令 $\tan x = t$ ， $\because \frac{\pi}{4} < x < \frac{\pi}{2} \therefore t > 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{y} = \tan 2 \mathrm{x} \tan^ {3} \mathrm{x} = \frac {2 \tan^ {4} \mathrm{x}}{1 - \tan^ {2} \mathrm{x}} = \frac {2 \mathrm{t} ^ {4}}{1 - \mathrm{t} ^ {2}} = \frac {2}{\frac {1}{\mathrm{t} ^ {4}} - \frac {1}{\mathrm{t} ^ {2}}} = \frac {2}{\left(\frac {1}{\mathrm{t} ^ {2}} - \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} - \frac {1}{4}} \leqslant \frac {2}{- \frac {1}{4}} = - 8
> > $$
> >
> > 故填：-8.
