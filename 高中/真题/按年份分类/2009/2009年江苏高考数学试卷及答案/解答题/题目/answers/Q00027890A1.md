> [!faq]- 2009年江苏高考数学试卷及答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)
> >
> > 当 $m_{A} = \frac{3}{5} m_{B}$ 时，
> >
> > $$
> > h _ {\text {甲}} = \sqrt {\frac {\frac {3}{5} m _ {B}}{\frac {3}{5} m _ {B} + 1 2} \cdot \frac {m _ {B}}{m _ {B} + 5}} = \sqrt {\frac {m _ {B} ^ {2}}{(m _ {B} + 2 0) (m _ {B} + 5)}}, h _ {\text {乙}} = \sqrt {\frac {\frac {3}{5} m _ {B}}{\frac {3}{5} m _ {B} + 3} \cdot \frac {m _ {B}}{m _ {B} + 2 0}} = \sqrt {\frac {m _ {B} ^ {2}}{(m _ {B} + 5) (m _ {B} + 2 0)}},
> > $$
> >
> > 显然 $h_{\text{甲}} = h_{\text{乙}}$
> >
> > $$
> > m _ {A} = \frac {3}{5} m _ {B} \text {时，} h _ {\text {甲}} = \sqrt {\frac {m _ {B} ^ {2}}{(m _ {B} + 2 0) (m _ {B} + 5)}} = \sqrt {\frac {1}{(1 + \frac {2 0}{m _ {B}}) (1 + \frac {5}{m _ {B}})}} = \sqrt {\frac {1}{1 0 0 (\frac {1}{m _ {B}}) ^ {2} + 2 5 \frac {1}{m _ {B}} + 1}},
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $m_B \in [5, 20]$ 得 $\frac{1}{m_B} \in [\frac{1}{20}, \frac{1}{5}]$ ，故当 $\frac{1}{m_B} = \frac{1}{20}$ 即 $m_B = 20, m_A = 12$ 时，甲乙两人同时取到最大的综合满意度为 $\frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}$
