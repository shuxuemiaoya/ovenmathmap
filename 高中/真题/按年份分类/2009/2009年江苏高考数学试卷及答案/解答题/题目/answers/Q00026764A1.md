> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）若 $f(0)\geq 1$ ，则 $-a\mid a|\geq 1\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l}a <   0\\ a^2\geq 1 \end{array} \right.\Rightarrow a\leq -1$
> >
> > (2) 当 $x \geq a$ 时， $f(x) = 3x^{2} - 2ax + a^{2}$ ， $f(x)_{\min} = \begin{cases} f(a), & a \geq 0 \\ f(\frac{a}{3}), & a < 0 \end{cases} = \begin{cases} 2a^{2}, & a \geq 0 \\ \frac{2a^{2}}{3}, & a < 0 \end{cases}$
> >
> > 当 $x \leq a$ 时， $f(x) = x^2 + 2ax - a^2, f(x)_{\min} = \begin{cases} f(-a), & a \geq 0 \\ f(a), & a < 0 \end{cases} = \begin{cases} -2a^2, & a \geq 0 \\ 2a^2, & a < 0 \end{cases}$
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) _ {\min} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l} - 2 a ^ {2}, a \geq 0 \\ \frac {2 a ^ {2}}{3}, a <   0 \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 综上
> >
> > (3) $x \in (a, +\infty)$ 时， $h(x) \geq 1$ 得 $3x^{2} - 2ax + a^{2} - 1 \geq 0$ ， $\Delta = 4a^{2} - 12(a^{2} - 1) = 12 - 8a^{2}$
> >
> > 当 $a \leq -\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$ 或 $a \geq \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$ 时， $\Delta \leq 0, x \in (a, +\infty)$ ;
> >
> > 当 $-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2} < a < \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$ 时， $\Delta > 0$ 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} (x - \frac{a - \sqrt{3 - 2a^2}}{3})(x - \frac{a + \sqrt{3 - 2a^2}}{3}) \geq 0 \\ x > a \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 1) $a \in (\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2})$ 时， $x \in (a, +\infty)$
> >
> > 2) $a \in [-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}]_{\text{时}}, x \in [\frac{a + \sqrt{3 - 2a^2}}{3}, +\infty)$
> >
> > 3) $a \in (-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}, -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}]_{\text{时}}, x \in (a, \frac{a - \sqrt{3 - 2a^2}}{3}] \cup [\frac{a + \sqrt{3 - 2a^2}}{3}, +\infty)$
