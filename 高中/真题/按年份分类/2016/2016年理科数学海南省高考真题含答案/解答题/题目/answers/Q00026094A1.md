> [!faq]- 2016年理科数学海南省高考真题含答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** \~24 题为选考题。考生根据要求作答。
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题解析推导如下：
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （I）证明：∵ $DF \perp CE$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{Rt} \triangle D E F \sim \mathrm{Rt} \triangle C E D
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \angle G D F = \angle D E F = \angle B C F
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {D F}{D G} = \frac {C F}{B C}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because D E = D G, C D = B C
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {D F}{D G} = \frac {C F}{B C}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \triangle G D F \sim \triangle B C F
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \angle C F B = \angle D F G
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \angle GFB = \angle GFC + \angle CFB = \angle GFC + \angle DFG = \angle DFC = 90^{\circ}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \angle G F B + \angle G C B = 1 8 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore B, C, G, F$ 四点共圆.
> >
> > (II) $\because E$ 为 AD 中点，AB = 1，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore D G = C G = D E = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > ∴在 Rt△GFC 中，GF =GC，
> >
> > 连接 GB，Rt△BCG≌Rt△BFG，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {\text {四边形} B C G F} = 2 S _ {\triangle B C G} = 2 \times \frac {1}{2} \times 1 \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
