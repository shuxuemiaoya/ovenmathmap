> >
> > 【
> > 解答】解：(I) “星队” 至少猜对 3 个成语包含 “甲猜对 1 个，乙猜对 2 个”，“甲猜对 2 个，乙猜对 1 个”，“甲猜对 2 个，乙猜对 2 个” 三个基本事件，

> > 故概率 $P = C_{2}^{1}\cdot \frac{3}{4}\cdot (1 - \frac{3}{4})\cdot (\frac{2}{3})^{2} + (\frac{3}{4})^{2}\cdot C_{2}^{1}\cdot \frac{2}{3}\cdot (1 - \frac{2}{3}) + (\frac{3}{4})^{2}\cdot (\frac{2}{3})^{2}$ $= \frac{1}{6} +\frac{1}{4} +\frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{3},$
> >
> > (II) “星队”两轮得分之和为 X 可能为：0，1，2，3，4，6，
> >
> > 则 $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X} = 0) = (1 - \frac{3}{4})^2 \cdot (1 - \frac{2}{3})^2 = \frac{1}{144},$ $\mathrm{P}(\mathrm{X} = 1) = 2 \times [\frac{3}{4} \cdot (1 - \frac{3}{4}) \cdot (1 - \frac{2}{3})^2 + (1 - \frac{3}{4})^2 \cdot \frac{2}{3} \cdot (1 - \frac{2}{3})] = \frac{10}{144},$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \frac {3}{4} \cdot (1 - \frac {2}{3}) \cdot \frac {3}{4} \cdot (1 - \frac {2}{3}) + \frac {3}{4} \cdot (1 - \frac {2}{3}) \cdot (1 - \frac {3}{4}) \cdot \frac {2}{3} +
> > $$

> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{P} (\mathrm{X} = 3) = 2 \times \frac {3}{4} \cdot \frac {2}{3} \cdot (1 - \frac {3}{4}) \cdot (1 - \frac {2}{3}) = \frac {1 2}{1 4 4},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 4) = 2 \times \left[ \frac {3}{4} \cdot \left(1 - \frac {3}{4}\right) \cdot \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {2}{3} \cdot \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) \cdot \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {2} \right] = \frac {6 0}{1 4 4}
