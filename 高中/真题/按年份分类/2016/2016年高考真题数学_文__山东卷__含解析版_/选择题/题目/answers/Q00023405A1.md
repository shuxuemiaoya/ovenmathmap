> > 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中, 由余弦定理得 $a^{2} = b^{2} + c^{2} - 2bc\cos A$ ,
> >
> > $\because b = c, \therefore a^{2} = 2b^{2}(1 - \cos A)$ , 又 $\because a^{2} = 2b^{2}(1 - \sin A)$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos A = \sin A, \quad \therefore \tan A = 1,
> > $$
> >
