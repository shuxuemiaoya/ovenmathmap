> > （II）根据 $b_{n}=[a_{n}]$ ，列出数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的前 10 项，相加可得
> > 答案.
> >
> > 【
> > 解答】解：（Ⅰ）设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为d，
> >
> > $$
> > \because a _ {3} + a _ {4} = 4, \quad a _ {5} + a _ {7} = 6.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 a _ {1} + 5 d = 4 \\ 2 a _ {1} + 1 0 d = 6 \end{array} , \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 解得： $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=1\\ d&=\frac{2}{5}\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = \frac {2}{5} n + \frac {3}{5};
> > $$
> >
> > (II) $\because b_{n}=[a_{n}]$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b _ {1} = b _ {2} = b _ {3} = 1,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathsf {b} _ {4} = \mathsf {b} _ {5} = 2,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathsf {b} _ {6} = \mathsf {b} _ {7} = \mathsf {b} _ {8} = 3,
> > $$
> >
> > $b_{9}=b_{10}=4.$
> >
> > 故数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的前10项和 $S_{10}=3\times1+2\times2+3\times3+2\times4=24$ .
> >
> > 【点评】本题考查的知识点是等差数列的通项公式，等差数列的性质，难度中档.
