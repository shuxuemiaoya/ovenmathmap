>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析

> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $1 - \ln 2$
> >
> > $y=\ln x+2$ 的切线为： $y=\frac{1}{x_{1}}\cdot x+\ln x_{1}+1$ （设切点横坐标为 $x_{1}$ ）
> >
> > $y=\ln(x+1)$ 的切线为： $y=\frac{1}{x_{2}+1}x+\ln(x_{2}+1)-\frac{x^{2}}{x_{2}+1}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {1}{x _ {1}} = \frac {1}{x _ {2} + 1} \\ \ln x _ {1} + 1 = \ln (x _ {2} + 1) - \frac {x _ {2}}{x _ {2} + 1} \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $x_{1} = \frac{1}{2}$ $x_{2} = -\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} b = \ln x _ {1} + 1 = 1 - \ln 2 \\ \therefore \end{array} .
> > $$
