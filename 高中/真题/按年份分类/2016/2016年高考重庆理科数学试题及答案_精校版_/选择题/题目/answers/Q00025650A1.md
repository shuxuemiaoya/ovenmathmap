> > 离心率 $e = \frac{F_1F_2}{MF_2 - MF_1}$ ，由正弦定理得 $e = \frac{F_1F_2}{MF_2 - MF_1} = \frac{\sin M}{\sin F_1 - \sin F_2} = \frac{\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}}{1 - \frac{1}{3}} = \sqrt{2}$ 。
> >
