> [!faq]- 2016年高考重庆理科数学试题及答案(精校版)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 若 $\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\alpha\right)=\frac{3}{5}$ ，则 $\sin2\alpha=$ (A) $\frac{7}{25}$ (B) $\frac{1}{5}$ (C) $-\frac{1}{5}$ (D) $-\frac{7}{25}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题解析推导如下：
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) = \frac {3}{5}, \sin 2 \alpha = \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - 2 \alpha\right) = 2 \cos^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) - 1 = \frac {7}{2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > 故选D.
