> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （Ⅰ）已知等式利用正弦定理化简，整理后利用两角和与差的正弦函数公
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（Ⅰ）∵在△ABC中，0<C<π，∴sinC=0
> >
> > 已知等式利用正弦定理化简得： $2\cos C$ （ $\sin A\cos B + \sin B\cos A$ ）=sinC，
> >
> > 整理得： $2\cos C\sin(A+B)=\sin C$ ,
> >
> > 即 $2\cos C \sin (\pi - (A + B)) = \sin C$
> >
> > 2cosCsinC=sinC
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos C = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore C = \frac {\pi}{3};
> > $$
> >
> > （II）由余弦定理得 $7 = a^{2} + b^{2} - 2ab\cdot \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore (a + b) ^ {2} - 3 a b = 7,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because S = \frac {1}{2} a b \sin C = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} a b = \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a b = 6,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore (a + b) ^ {2} - 1 8 = 7,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore a+b=5,$
> >
> > $\therefore\triangle ABC$ 的周长为 $5+\sqrt{7}$ .
> >
