> [!faq]- 2007年上海高考数学真题（文科）试卷（word解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\arccos \frac{\sqrt{6}}{6}$ ![](images/combined/7174629905c52302c3b4626b971ff8c259affa0669f694fd7e759e969181fbb5.jpg)
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $A_{1}C_{1} \parallel AC, \therefore$ 异面直线 $A_{1}B$ 与 AC 所成角为 $\angle BA_{1}C_{1}$ ,
> >
> > 易求 $A_{1}B = \sqrt{6}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos \angle B A _ {1} C _ {1} = \frac {A _ {1} C _ {1}}{A _ {1} B} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {6}} = \frac {\sqrt {6}}{6} \Rightarrow \angle B A _ {1} C _ {1} = \arccos \frac {\sqrt {6}}{6} 。
> > $$
