> [!faq]- 2007年上海高考数学真题（文科）试卷（word解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设数列 $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为 $d$ ，则 $b_{4} = b_{1} + 3d = 2 + 3d = 11$ ，解得 $d = 3$
> >
> > ∴ 数列 $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ 为2,5,8,11,8,5,2.
> >
> > $$
> > S = c _ {1} + c _ {2} + \dots + c _ {4 9} = 2 (c _ {2 5} + c _ {2 6} + \dots + c _ {4 9}) - c _ {2 5} \tag {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \left(1 + 2 + 2 ^ {2} + \dots + 2 ^ {2 4}\right) - 1 = 2 \left(2 ^ {2 5} - 1\right) - 1 = 2 ^ {2 6} - 3 = ^ {6 7 1 0 8 8 6 1}.
> > $$
> > (3) $d_{51} = 2, d_{100} = 2 + 3 \times (50 - 1) = 149$ .
> >
> > 由题意得 $d_{1}, d_{2}, \cdots, d_{50}$ 是首项为 149，公差为 -3 的等差数列.
> >
> > 当 $n \leq 50$ 时， $S_{n} = d_{1} + d_{2} + \dots + d_{n} = 149n + \frac{n(n - 1)}{2} (-3) = -\frac{3}{2} n^{2} + \frac{301}{2} n$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \text {当} 5 1 \leq n \leq 1 0 0 ^ {\text {时}}, S _ {n} = d _ {1} + d _ {2} + \dots + d _ {n} = S _ {5 0} + \left(d _ {5 1} + d _ {5 2} + \dots + d _ {n}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 3 7 7 5 + 2 \square (n - 5 0) + \frac {(n - 5 0) (n - 5 1)}{2} \times 3 = \frac {3}{2} n ^ {2} - \frac {2 9 9}{2} n + 7 5 0 0
> > $$
> >
> > 综上所述， $S_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}-\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{301}{2}n,\quad&1\leq n\leq50,\\ \frac{3}{2}n^{2}-\frac{299}{2}n+7500,\quad&51\leq n\leq100\cdot\end{aligned}\right.$
