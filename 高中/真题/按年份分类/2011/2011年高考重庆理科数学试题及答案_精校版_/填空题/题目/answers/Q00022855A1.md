> [!faq]- 2011年高考重庆理科数学试题及答案(精校版)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 为： $-\frac{\sqrt{14}}{2}$ 点评：本题主要考查了三角函数恒等变换及化简求值．主要是利用了同角三角函数中平方关系解题.
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用题设等式, 两边平方后即可求得 $\sin 2\alpha$ , 进而根据同角三角函数的基本关系求得 $\cos 2\alpha$ , 利用正弦的两角和公式把原式的分母展开, 把 $\cos 2\alpha$ 和 $\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha$ 的值代入即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解： $\because \sin \alpha = \frac{1}{2} + \cos \alpha$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 两边平方后求得 $1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{4}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin 2 \alpha = \frac {3}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha = \frac {1}{2} > 0
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \alpha \in (\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2})$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 2 \alpha \in (\frac {\pi}{2}, \pi)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos 2 \alpha = - \sqrt {1 - \frac {9}{1 6}} = - \frac {\sqrt {7}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{\sin (\alpha - \frac {\pi}{4})} = \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} (\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)} = \frac {- \frac {\sqrt {7}}{4}}{\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {1}{2}} = - \frac {\sqrt {1 4}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故
