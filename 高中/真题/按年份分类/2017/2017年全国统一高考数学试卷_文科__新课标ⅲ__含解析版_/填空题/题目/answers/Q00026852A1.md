> [!faq]- 2017年全国统一高考数学试卷（文科）（新课标ⅲ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $75^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据正弦定理和三角形的内角和计算即可
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：根据正弦定理可得 $\frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$ ， $C = 60^{\circ}$ ， $b = \sqrt{6}$ ， $c = 3$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin B = \frac {\sqrt {6} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}}{3} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $\because b < c,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore B = 4 5 ^ {\circ},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore A = 1 8 0 ^ {\circ} - B - C = 1 8 0 ^ {\circ} - 4 5 ^ {\circ} - 6 0 ^ {\circ} = 7 5 ^ {\circ},
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $75^{\circ}$
