> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 分析】由条件，两边平方，根据二倍角公式和平方关系即可求出.

> > 解答】解：∵sinα - cosα= $\frac{4}{3}$ ,
> >
> > $\therefore (\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)^2 = 1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 1 - \sin 2\alpha = \frac{16}{9},$ $\therefore \sin 2\alpha = -\frac{7}{9},$
> >
> > 故选：A.
