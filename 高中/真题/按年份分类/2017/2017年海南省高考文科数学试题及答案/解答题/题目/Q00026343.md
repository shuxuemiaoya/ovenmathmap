23. 解:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} (1) (a + b) (a ^ {3} + b ^ {3}) & = a ^ {6} + a b ^ {5} + a ^ {5} b + b ^ {6} \\ & = (a ^ {3} + b ^ {3}) ^ {2} - 2 a ^ {3} b ^ {3} + a b (a ^ {4} + b ^ {4}) \\ & = 4 + a b (a ^ {2} - b ^ {2}) ^ {2} \\ & \geq 4. \end{array}
$$

(2) 因为 $(a + b)^{3} = a^{3} + 3a^{2}b + 3ab^{2} + b^{3}$

$$
= 2 + 3 a b (a + b)
$$

$$
\leq 2 + \frac {3 (a + b) ^ {2}}{4} (a + b)
$$

$$
= 2 + \frac {3 (a + b) ^ {3}}{4}
$$

所以 $(a + b)^{3} \leq 8$ ，因此 $a + b \leq 2$
