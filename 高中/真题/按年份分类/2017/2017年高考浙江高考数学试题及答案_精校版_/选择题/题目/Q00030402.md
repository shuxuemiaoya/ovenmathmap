22. (15 分) 已知数列 $\{x_{n}\}$ 满足: $x_{1} = 1, x_{n} = x_{n+1} + \ln (1 + x_{n+1}) (n \in \mathbb{N}^{*})$ , 证明: 当 $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ 时,

(1) $0 < x_{n+1} < x_{n};$

(II) $2x_{n+1} - x_n \leq \frac{x_n x_{n+1}}{2}$ ;

(III) $\frac{1}{2^{n - 1}}\leqslant x_n\leq \frac{1}{2^{n - 2}}.$
