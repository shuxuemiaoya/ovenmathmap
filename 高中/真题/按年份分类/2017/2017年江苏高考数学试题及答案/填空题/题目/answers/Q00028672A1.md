>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解答】证明：（1）∵直线PC切半圆O于点C，∴∠ACP=∠ABC.
> >
> > ∵AB为半圆O的直径，∴∠ACB=90°.
> >
> > $$
> > \because A P \perp P C, \therefore \angle A P C = 9 0 ^ {\circ}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \angle P A C = 9 0 ^ {\circ} - \angle A C P, \angle C A B = 9 0 ^ {\circ} - \angle A B C,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \angle PAC = \angle CAB.$
