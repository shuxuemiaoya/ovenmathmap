> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：
> > （1）设事件 $A_{i}$ 表示编号为 $i$ 的抽屉里放的是黑球，
> >
> > 则 $p = p(A_{2}) = P(A_{2}|A_{1})P(A_{1}) + P(A_{2}|A_{1})P(A_{1})$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {n - 1}{\pi + n - 1} \times \frac {n}{\pi + n} \times \frac {n}{\pi + n - 1} \times \frac {\pi}{\pi + n}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {n ^ {2} - n + \pi n}{(\pi + n) (\pi + n - 1)} = \frac {n}{\pi + n}.
> > $$
> >
> > 证明：（2）∵x的所有可能取值为 $\frac{1}{n}$ ， $\frac{1}{n+1}$ ，…， $\frac{1}{n+m}$ ，
> >
> > P $(x=\frac{1}{k})=\frac{C_{k-1}^{n-1}}{C_{m+n}^{n}},\quad k=n,\quad n+1,\quad n+2,\quad\cdots,\quad n+m,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore E (X) = \sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n + m} \left(\frac {1}{k} \cdot \frac {C _ {k - 1} ^ {n - 1}}{C _ {n + m} ^ {n}}\right) = \frac {1}{C _ {n + m} ^ {n}} \cdot \sum_ {k = n} ^ {n + m} \frac {C _ {k - 1} ^ {n - 1}}{k}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{C _ {n + m} ^ {n}} \cdot \sum_ {k = n} ^ {n + m} \frac {C _ {k - 1} ^ {n - 1}}{k} <   \frac {1}{C _ {n + m} ^ {n}} \cdot \sum_ {k = n} ^ {n + m} \frac {C _ {k - 1} ^ {n - 1}}{k - 1} = \frac {1}{C _ {n + m} ^ {n}} \cdot \sum_ {k = n} ^ {n + m} \frac {C _ {k - 2} ^ {n - 2}}{n - 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{(n - 1) C _ {n + m} ^ {n}} \cdot \left(C _ {n - 2} ^ {n - 2} + C _ {n - 1} ^ {n - 2} + \dots + C _ {n + m - 2} ^ {n - 2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{(n - 1) C _ {m + n} ^ {n}} \cdot C _ {m + n - 1} ^ {n - 1} = \frac {n}{(m + n) (n - 1)},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore E (X) < \frac{n}{(n + n)(n - 1)}.$
