> [!faq]- 2017年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（新课标ⅱ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）利用三角形的内角和定理可知 $A+C=\pi-B$ ，再利用诱导公式化简
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：
> > （1） $\sin(A+C)=8\sin^{2}\frac{B}{2}$
> >
> > $\therefore \sin B = 4(1 - \cos B)$
> >
> > $\because\sin^{2}B+\cos^{2}B=1,$
> >
> > ∴16 $(1 - \cos B)^{2} + \cos^{2}B = 1,$
> >
> > ∴16 $(1 - \cos B)^{2} + \cos^{2}B - 1 = 0,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 1 6 (\cos B - 1) ^ {2} + (\cos B - 1) (\cos B + 1) = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ $(17\cos B - 15)$ $(\cos B - 1) = 0,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos B = \frac {1 5}{1 7};
> > $$
> > (2) 由
> > (1) 可知 $\sin B = \frac{8}{17}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \because S _ {\triangle A B C} = \frac {1}{2} a c \bullet \sin B = 2,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a c = \frac {1 7}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b ^ {2} = a ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - 2 \text {accosB} = a ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - 2 \times \frac {1 7}{2} \times \frac {1 5}{1 7}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = a ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - 1 5 = (a + c) ^ {2} - 2 a c - 1 5 = 3 6 - 1 7 - 1 5 = 4,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b = 2.
> > $$
