> [!faq]- 2017年上海高考数学真题试卷（word解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{\pi}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 考查三角函数的性质和值域， $\frac{1}{2 + \sin\alpha_1} \in \left[\frac{1}{3}, 1\right], \frac{1}{2 + \sin(2\alpha_2)} \in \left[\frac{1}{3}, 1\right]$ ，要使 $\frac{1}{2 + \sin\alpha_1} + \frac{1}{2 + \sin(2\alpha_2)} = 2$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{1}{2 + \sin\alpha_1} = 1 \\ \frac{1}{2 + \sin(2\alpha_2)} = 1 \end{array} \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \alpha_1 = -\frac{\pi}{2} + 2k_1\pi \\ \alpha_2 = -\frac{\pi}{4} + k_2\pi \end{array} , k_1, k_2 \in Z \right. \right.$ $|10\pi - \alpha_1 - \alpha_2|_{\min} = |10\pi + \frac{3}{4}\pi - (2k_1 + k_2)\pi|_{\min} = \frac{\pi}{4}$ ，当 $2k_1 + k_2 = 11$ 时成立
> > 【
