> [!faq]- 2017年全国统一高考数学试卷（文科）（新课标ⅰ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据诱导公式和两角和的正弦公式以及正弦定理计算即可
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解： $\sin B=\sin(A+C)=\sin A\cos C+\cos A\sin C,$
> >
> > $\because \sin B + \sin A (\sin C - \cos C) = 0,$
> >
> > ∴sinAcosC+cosAsinC+sinAsinC - sinAcosC=0,
> >
> > $\therefore \cos A \sin C + \sin A \sin C = 0,$
> >
> > $\because \sin C = 0,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos A = - \sin A,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \tan A = - 1,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \frac {\pi}{2} <   A <   \pi ,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore A = \frac {3 \pi}{4},
> > $$
> >
> > 由正弦定理可得 $\frac{c}{\sin C} = \frac{a}{\sin A}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin C = \frac {c \sin A}{a},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because a = 2, c = \sqrt {2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin C = \frac {c \sin A}{a} = \frac {\sqrt {2} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}{2} = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because a > c,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore C = \frac {\pi}{6},
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
> >
> > 【点评】本题考查了诱导公式和两角和的正弦公式以及正弦定理，属于基础题
