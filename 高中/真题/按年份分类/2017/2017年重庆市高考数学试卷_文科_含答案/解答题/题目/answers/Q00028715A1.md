> [!faq]- 2017年重庆市高考数学试卷(文科)含答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (a + b) (a ^ {5} + b ^ {5}) & = a ^ {6} + a b ^ {5} + a ^ {5} b + b ^ {6} \\ & = (a ^ {3} + b ^ {3}) ^ {2} - 2 a ^ {3} b ^ {3} + a b (a ^ {4} + b ^ {4}) \\ & = 4 + a b (a ^ {2} - b ^ {2}) ^ {2} \\ & \geq 4. \end{array}
> > $$
> > (2) 因为
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} (a + b) ^ {3} & = a ^ {3} + 3 a ^ {2} b + 3 a b ^ {2} + b ^ {3} \\ & = 2 + 3 a b (a + b) \\ & \leq 2 + \frac {3 (a + b) ^ {2}}{4} (a + b) \\ & = 2 + \frac {3 (a + b) ^ {3}}{4}. \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $(a+b)^{3}\leq8$ ，因此 $a+b\leq2$
