> > 解答】解：x、y、z 为正数，
> >
> > 令 $2^{x}=3^{y}=5^{z}=k>1$ . $\lg k>0$ .
> >
> > 则 $x = \frac{\lg k}{\lg 2}, y = \frac{\lg k}{\lg 3}, z = \frac{\lg k}{\lg 5}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 3 y = \frac {1 g k}{1 g \sqrt [ 3 ]{3}}, 2 x = \frac {1 g k}{1 g \sqrt {2}}, 5 z = \frac {1 g k}{1 g \sqrt [ 5 ]{5}}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \sqrt [ 3 ]{3} = \sqrt [ 6 ]{9} > \sqrt [ 6 ]{8} = \sqrt {2}, \sqrt {2} = \sqrt [ 1 0 ]{3 2} > \sqrt [ 1 0 ]{2 5} = \sqrt [ 5 ]{5}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \lg \sqrt [ 3 ]{3} > \lg \sqrt {2} > \lg \sqrt [ 5 ]{5} > 0.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 3 y <   2 x <   5 z.
> > $$
> >
> > 另解：x、y、z 为正数，
> >
> > 令 $2^{x}=3^{y}=5^{z}=k>1$ . $\lg k>0$ .
> >
> > 则 $x = \frac{\lg k}{\lg 2}, y = \frac{\lg k}{\lg 3}, z = \frac{\lg k}{\lg 5}$ .
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{2x}{3y} = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1g3}{1g2} = \frac{1g9}{1g8} > 1$ ，可得 $2x > 3y$
> >
> > $\frac{5z}{2x} = \frac{5}{2} \times \frac{\lg 2}{\lg 5} = \frac{\lg 2^5}{\lg 5^2} > 1$ 。可得 $5z > 2x$ 。
> >
> > 综上可得： $5z>2x>3y$ .
