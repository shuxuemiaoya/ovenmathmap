>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(Ⅰ) 记接受甲种心理暗示的志愿者中包含 $A_{1}$ 但不包含 $B_{1}$ 的事件为 M，
> >
> > 则 P (M) = $\frac{C_{8}^{4}}{C_{10}^{5}} = \frac{5}{18}$ .
> >
> > (Ⅱ) X 的可能取值为: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4,
> >
> > $\therefore P (X = 0) = \frac{C_{6}^{5}}{C_{10}^{5}} = \frac{1}{42},$
> >
> > P (X=1) = $\frac{C_{6}^{4}C_{4}^{1}}{C_{10}^{5}}=\frac{5}{21}$ ,
> >
> > P (X=2) = $\frac{C_{6}^{3}C_{4}^{2}}{C_{10}^{5}}=\frac{10}{21}$ ,
> >
> > P (X=3) = $\frac{C_{6}^{2}C_{4}^{3}}{C_{10}^{5}}=\frac{5}{21}$ ,
> >
> > P (X=4) = $\frac{C_{6}^{1}C_{4}^{4}}{C_{10}^{5}}=\frac{1}{42}$ .
> >
> > ∴x 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>4</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{42}$ </td><td> $\frac{5}{21}$ </td><td> $\frac{10}{21}$ </td><td> $\frac{5}{21}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{42}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > X 的数学期望 $EX=0\times\frac{1}{42}+1\times\frac{5}{21}+2\times\frac{10}{21}+3\times\frac{5}{21}+4\times\frac{1}{42}=2$ .
