> [!faq]- 2018年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（新课标ⅲ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）设 A $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ ，B $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，利用点差法得 6 $(x_{1} - x_{2}) + 8m(y_{1} - y_{2}) = 0$ ，k=
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：
> > （1）设 A $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ ，B $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，
> >
> > ∵线段 AB 的中点为 M（1，m），
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore x _ {1} + x _ {2} = 2, \quad y _ {1} + y _ {2} = 2 m
> > $$
> >
> > 将A，B代入椭圆C： $\frac{x^2 + y^2}{4} = 1$ 中，可得
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3 x _ {1} ^ {2} + 4 y _ {1} ^ {2} = 1 2 \\ 3 x _ {2} ^ {2} + 4 y _ {2} ^ {2} = 1 2 \end{array} , \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 两式相减可得， $3(x_{1}+x_{2})(x_{1}-x_{2})+4(y_{1}+y_{2})(y_{1}-y_{2})=0,$
> >
> > 即 $6(x_{1}-x_{2})+8m(y_{1}-y_{2})=0,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore k = \frac {y _ {1} - y _ {2}}{x _ {1} - x _ {2}} = - \frac {6}{8 m} = - \frac {3}{4 m}
> > $$
> >
> > 点 M（1，m）在椭圆内，即 $\frac{1}{4} + \frac{m^{2}}{3} < 1$ ，(m > 0)，
> >
> > 解得 $0 < m < \frac{3}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore k = - \frac {3}{4 m} <   - \frac {1}{2}.
> > $$
> > (2) 证明：设 A $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ ，B $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，P $(x_{3}, y_{3})$ ，
> >
> > 可得 $x_{1} + x_{2} = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {F P} + \overrightarrow {F A} + \overrightarrow {F B} = \overrightarrow {0}, F (1, 0), \therefore x _ {1} - 1 + x _ {2} - 1 + x _ {3} - 1 = 0, y _ {1} + y _ {2} + y _ {3} = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore x _ {3} = 1, \quad y _ {3} = - \quad \left(y _ {1} + y _ {2}\right) = - 2 m
> > $$
> >
> > $\because m > 0$ ，可得 $\mathsf{P}$ 在第四象限，故 $y_{3} = -\frac{3}{2}$ ， $m = \frac{3}{4}$ ， $k = -1$
> >
> > 由椭圆的焦半径公式得则 $|\mathsf{FA}| = a - \mathrm{ex}_1 = 2 - \frac{1}{2} x_1, |\mathsf{FB}| = 2 - \frac{1}{2} x_2, |\mathsf{FP}| = 2 - \frac{1}{2} x_3 = \frac{3}{2}$ .
> >
> > 则 $|\mathrm{FA}| + |\mathrm{FB}| = 4 - \frac{1}{2} (\mathbf{x}_1 + \mathbf{x}_2) = 3,\therefore |\mathrm{FA}| + |\mathrm{FB}| = 2|\mathrm{FP}|$
> >
> > 联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = -x + \frac{7}{4} \\ 3x^2 + 4y^2 = 12 \end{array} \right.$ ，可得 $|x_1 - x_2| = \sqrt{(x_1 + x_2)^2 - 4x_1x_2} = \frac{3\sqrt{21}}{7}$
> >
> > 所以该数列的公差 d 满足 $2d = \pm \frac{1}{2} |x_{1} - x_{2}| = \pm \frac{3\sqrt{21}}{14}$ ,
> >
> > ∴该数列的公差为 $\pm\frac{3\sqrt{21}}{28}$ .
> >
