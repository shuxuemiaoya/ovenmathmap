> [!faq]- 2018年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（新课标ⅲ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先根据点到直线的距离求出 $|\mathrm{PF}_2| = \mathrm{b}$ , 再求出 $|\mathrm{OP}| = \mathrm{a}$ , 在三角形 $\mathrm{F}_1\mathrm{PF}_2$ 中,
> > 由余弦定理可得 $|\mathrm{PF}_1|^2 = |\mathrm{PF}_2|^2 + |\mathrm{F}_1\mathrm{F}_2|^2 - 2|\mathrm{PF}_2| \bullet |\mathrm{F}_1\mathrm{F}_2| \cos \angle \mathrm{PF}_2\mathrm{O}$ , 代值化简整
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：双曲线 C: $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1 (a > 0, b > 0)$ 的一条渐近线方程为 $y = \frac{b}{a} x$
> >
> > ∴点 $F_{2}$ 到渐近线的距离 $d=\frac{bc}{\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}}=b$ ，即 $\left|PF_{2}\right|=b$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | O P | = \sqrt {\left| O F _ {2} \right| ^ {2} - \left| P F _ {2} \right| ^ {2}} = \sqrt {c ^ {2} - b ^ {2}} = a, \cos \angle P F _ {2} O = \frac {b}{c},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \left| P F _ {1} \right| = \sqrt {6} | O P |,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \left|PF_{1}\right| = \sqrt{6} a,$
> >
> > 在三角形 $F_{1}PF_{2}$ 中，由余弦定理可得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|^{2}=\left|PF_{2}\right|^{2}+\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|^{2}-2\left|PF_{2}\right|\bullet\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{COS} \angle \mathrm{PF} _ {2} \mathrm{O},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 6 a ^ {2} = b ^ {2} + 4 c ^ {2} - 2 \times b \times 2 c \times \frac {b}{c} = 4 c ^ {2} - 3 b ^ {2} = 4 c ^ {2} - 3 (c ^ {2} - a ^ {2}),
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $3a^{2}=c^{2}$ ,
> >
> > 即 $\sqrt{3}a=c$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore e = \frac {c}{a} = \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：C.
