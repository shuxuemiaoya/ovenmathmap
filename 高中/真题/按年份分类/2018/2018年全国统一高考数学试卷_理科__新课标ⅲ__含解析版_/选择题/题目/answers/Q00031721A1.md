> [!faq]- 2018年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（新课标ⅲ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 2
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由已知可求过 A, B 两点的直线方程为 $y=k(x-1)$ ，然后联立直线与抛物线
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：∵抛物线 C: $y^{2}=4x$ 的焦点 F（1，0），
> >
> > ∴过 A，B 两点的直线方程为 y=k（x-1），
> >
> > 联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y^{2}&=4x\\ y=k(x-1)\end{aligned}\right.$ 可得， $k^{2}x^{2}-2(2+k^{2})x+k^{2}=0,$
> >
> > 设 A $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ ，B $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，
> >
> > 则 $x_{1} + x_{2} = \frac{4 + 2k^{2}}{k^{2}}, x_{1}x_{2} = 1,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{y} _ {1} + \mathrm{y} _ {2} = \mathrm{k} (\mathrm{x} _ {1} + \mathrm{x} _ {2} - 2) = \frac {4}{\mathrm{k}}, \quad \mathrm{y} _ {1} \mathrm{y} _ {2} = \mathrm{k} ^ {2} (\mathrm{x} _ {1} - 1) (\mathrm{x} _ {2} - 1) = \mathrm{k} ^ {2} [ \mathrm{x} _ {1} \mathrm{x} _ {2} - (\mathrm{x} _ {1} + \mathrm{x} _ {2}) + 1 ] = - 4,
> > $$
> >
> > ∵M $(-1, 1)$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {\mathrm{MA}} = \left(x _ {1} + 1, y _ {1} - 1\right), \overrightarrow {\mathrm{MB}} = \left(x _ {2} + 1, y _ {2} - 1\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \angle A M B = 9 0 ^ {\circ}, \therefore \overrightarrow {M A} \cdot \overrightarrow {M B} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left(x _ {1} + 1\right) \quad \left(x _ {2} + 1\right) + \left(y _ {1} - 1\right) \quad \left(y _ {2} - 1\right) = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > 整理可得， $x_{1}x_{2}+(x_{1}+x_{2})+y_{1}y_{2}-(y_{1}+y_{2})+2=0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 1 + 2 + \frac {4}{k ^ {2}} - 4 - \frac {4}{k} + 2 = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $k^{2}-4k+4=0$ ，
> >
> > ∴k=2.
> >
> > 故答案为：2
