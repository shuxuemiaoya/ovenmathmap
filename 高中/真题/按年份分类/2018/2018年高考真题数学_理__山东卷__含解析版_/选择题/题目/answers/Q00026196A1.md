> [!faq]- 2018年高考真题数学【理】(山东卷)（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 如图：设 $BC = 2r_{1}$ ， $AB = 2r_{2}$ ， $AC = 2r_{3}$ ，分别求出I，II，III所对应的面积，即可
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：如图：设 $BC=2r_{1}$ ， $AB=2r_{2}$ ， $AC=2r_{3}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore r _ {1} ^ {2} = r _ {2} ^ {2} + r _ {3} ^ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {1} = \frac {1}{2} \times 4 r _ {2} r _ {3} = 2 r _ {2} r _ {3}, \quad S _ {\text {III}} = \frac {1}{2} \times \pi r _ {1} ^ {2} - 2 r _ {2} r _ {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\parallel} = \frac {1}{2} \times \pi r _ {3} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} \times \pi r _ {2} ^ {2} - S _ {\parallel \parallel} = \frac {1}{2} \times \pi r _ {3} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} \times \pi r _ {2} ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} \times \pi r _ {1} ^ {2} + 2 r _ {2} r _ {3} = 2 r _ {2} r _ {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {1} = S _ {\parallel},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore P _ {1} = P _ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A.
> >
> > 【点评】本题考查了几何概型的概率问题, 关键是求出对应的面积, 属于基础题.
