> [!faq]- 2018年理科数学海南省高考真题含答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据离心率得 a,c 关系，进而得 a,b 关系，再根据双曲线方程求渐近线方程，得结果.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \mathrm{e} = \frac{\mathrm{c}}{\mathrm{a}} = \sqrt{3},\therefore \frac{\mathrm{b}^2}{\mathrm{a}^2} = \frac{\mathrm{c}^2 - \mathrm{a}^2}{\mathrm{a}^2} = \mathrm{e}^2 -1 = 3 - 1 = 2,\therefore \frac{\mathrm{b}}{\mathrm{a}} = \sqrt{2},$
> >
> > 因为渐近线方程为 $y = \pm \frac{b}{a} x$ ，所以渐近线方程为 $y = \pm \sqrt{2} x$ ，选A.
> >
> > 点睛：已知双曲线方程 $\frac{\mathrm{x}^2 - \mathrm{y}^2}{\mathrm{a}^2 - \mathrm{b}^2} = 1(a, b > 0)$ 求渐近线方程： $\frac{\mathrm{x}^2 - \mathrm{y}^2}{\mathrm{a}^2 - \mathrm{b}^2} = 0 \Rightarrow \mathrm{y} = \pm \frac{\mathrm{b}}{\mathrm{a}}$
