> [!faq]- 2018年上海高考数学真题试卷（word解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （14 分）（2018•上海）设常数 $a \in R$ ，函数 $f(x) = \sin
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）根据函数的奇偶性和三角形的函数的性质即可求出，
> > (2) 先求出 $a$ 的值，再根据三角形函数的性质即可求出.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1）∵f(x)=asin2x+2cos²x，
> >
> > $\therefore f(-x) = -a \sin 2x + 2 \cos^2 x,$
> >
> > ∵f(x)为偶函数，
> >
> > $\therefore f(-x) = f(x)$
> >
> > ∴ $-\sin2x+2\cos^{2}x=\sin2x+2\cos^{2}x,$
> >
> > ∴2asin2x=0,
> >
> > ∴a=0;
> > (2) $\because f\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\sqrt{3}+1,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{asin} \frac {\pi}{2} + 2 \cos^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4}\right) = a + 1 = \sqrt {3} + 1,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a = \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore f(x) = \sqrt{3}\sin 2x + 2\cos^2 x = \sqrt{3}\sin 2x + \cos 2x + 1 = 2\sin (2x + \frac{\pi}{6}) + 1,$
> >
> > $\because f(x) = 1 - \sqrt{2},$
> >
> > $\therefore 2 \sin \left(2 x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + 1 = 1 - \sqrt{2},$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin (2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}) = - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore 2x + \frac{\pi}{6} = -\frac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi$ ，或 $2x + \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{5}{4}\pi + 2k\pi, k \in \mathbb{Z}$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore x = -\frac{5\pi}{24}\pi + k\pi$ ，或 $x = \frac{13}{24}\pi + k\pi, k \in \mathbb{Z}$ ，
> >
> > $\because x \in [-\pi, \pi]$ ,
> >
> > $\therefore x = \frac{13\pi}{24}$ 或 $x = \frac{19\pi}{24}$ 或 $x = -\frac{5\pi}{24}$ 或 $x = -\frac{11\pi}{24}$
