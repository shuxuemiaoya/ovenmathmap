> > 分析】（1）设 A $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ ，B $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，利用点差法得

> > $$
> > 6 \left(\mathrm{x} _ {1} - \mathrm{x} _ {2}\right) + 8 \mathrm{m} \left(\mathrm{y} _ {1} - \mathrm{y} _ {2}\right) = 0, \mathrm{k} = \frac {\mathrm{y} _ {1} - \mathrm{y} _ {2}}{\mathrm{x} _ {1} - \mathrm{x} _ {2}} = - \frac {6}{8 \mathrm{m}} = - \frac {3}{4 \mathrm{m}}

> > 解答】解：（1）设 A $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ ，B $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，
> >
> > ∵线段 AB 的中点为 M（1，m），
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{x} _ {1} + \mathrm{x} _ {2} = 2, \quad \mathrm{y} _ {1} + \mathrm{y} _ {2} = 2 \mathrm{m}
> > $$
> >
> > 将A，B代入椭圆C： $\frac{x^2 + y^2}{4} = 1$ 中，可得
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3 x _ {1} ^ {2} + 4 y _ {1} ^ {2} = 1 2 \\ 3 x _ {2} ^ {2} + 4 y _ {2} ^ {2} = 1 2 \end{array} , \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 两式相减可得， $3\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}+\mathrm{x}_{2}\right)\quad\left(\mathrm{x}_{1}-\mathrm{x}_{2}\right)+4\left(\mathrm{y}_{1}+\mathrm{y}_{2}\right)\quad\left(\mathrm{y}_{1}-\mathrm{y}_{2}\right)=0,$
> >
> > 即 $6(x_{1}-x_{2})+8m(y_{1}-y_{2})=0,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{k} = \frac {\mathrm{y} _ {1} - \mathrm{y} _ {2}}{\mathrm{x} _ {1} - \mathrm{x} _ {2}} = - \frac {6}{8 \mathrm{m}} = - \frac {3}{4 \mathrm{m}}
> > $$
> >
> > 点 M（1，m）在椭圆内，即 $\frac{1}{4} + \frac{m^{2}}{3} < 1$ ，(m > 0)，
> >
> > 解得 $0 < \mathrm{m} < \frac{3}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore k = - \frac {3}{4 m} <   - \frac {1}{2}.
> > $$

> >

> > \because \overrightarrow {F P} + \overrightarrow {F A} + \overrightarrow {F B} = \overrightarrow {0}, F (1, 0), \therefore x _ {1} - 1 + x _ {2} - 1 + x _ {3} - 1 = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$

> > $$

> >
> > 则 $|FA|+|FB|=4-\frac{1}{2}(x_{1}+x_{2})=3,$

> > $$
> > \therefore | \mathrm{FA} | + | \mathrm{FB} | = 2 | \mathrm{FP} |,
