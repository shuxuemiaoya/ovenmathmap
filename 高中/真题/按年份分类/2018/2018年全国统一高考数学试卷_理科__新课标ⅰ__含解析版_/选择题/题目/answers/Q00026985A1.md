> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：如图：设 $BC=2r_{1}$ ， $AB=2r_{2}$ ， $AC=2r_{3}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{r} _ {1} ^ {2} = \mathrm{r} _ {2} ^ {2} + \mathrm{r} _ {3} ^ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{S} _ {\mathrm{I}} = \frac {1}{2} \times 4 \mathrm{r} _ {2} \mathrm{r} _ {3} = 2 \mathrm{r} _ {2} \mathrm{r} _ {3}, \quad \mathrm{S} _ {\mathrm{III}} = \frac {1}{2} \times \pi \mathrm{r} _ {1} ^ {2} - 2 \mathrm{r} _ {2} \mathrm{r} _ {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{S} _ {\mathrm{II}} = \frac {1}{2} \times \pi \mathrm{r} _ {3} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} \times \pi \mathrm{r} _ {2} ^ {2} - \mathrm{S} _ {\mathrm{III}} = \frac {1}{2} \times \pi \mathrm{r} _ {3} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} \times \pi \mathrm{r} _ {2} ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} \times \pi \mathrm{r} _ {1} ^ {2} + 2 \mathrm{r} _ {2} \mathrm{r} _ {3} = 2 \mathrm{r} _ {2} \mathrm{r} _ {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {I} = S _ {I I},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \mathrm{P} _ {1} = \mathrm{P} _ {2},
> > $$
