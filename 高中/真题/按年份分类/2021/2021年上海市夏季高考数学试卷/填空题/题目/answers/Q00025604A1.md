> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $S=\frac{a_{1}}{1-q}=\frac{3}{1-q}=9\Rightarrow q=\frac{2}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > b _ {n} = a _ {2 n} = a _ {1} \times q ^ {2 n - 1} = 3 \times \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {2 n - 1} \Rightarrow b _ {1} = 2, q _ {0} = \frac {4}{9} \Rightarrow S _ {b} = \frac {b _ {1}}{1 - q _ {0}} = \frac {2}{1 - \frac {4}{9}} = \frac {1 8}{5}
> > $$
