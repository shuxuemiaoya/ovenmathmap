> [!faq]- 2021年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（新课标ⅰ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) 由已知 $\frac{2}{S_n} + \frac{1}{b_n} = 2$ ，则 $\frac{b_n}{b_{n-1}} = S_n (n \geq 2)$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \Rightarrow \frac {2 b _ {n - 1}}{b _ {n}} + \frac {1}{b _ {n}} = 2 \Rightarrow 2 b _ {n - 1} + 2 = 2 b _ {n} \Rightarrow b _ {n} - b _ {n - 1} = \frac {1}{2} (n \geq 2), \quad b _ {1} = \frac {3}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $\{b_n\}$ 是以 $\frac{3}{2}$ 为首项， $\frac{1}{2}$ 为公差的等差数列.
> > (2) 由
> > (1) 知 $b_{n} = \frac{3}{2} + (n - 1)\frac{1}{2} = \frac{n + 2}{2}$ ，则 $\frac{2}{S_n} + \frac{2}{n + 2} = 2 \Rightarrow S_n = \frac{n + 2}{n + 1}$ ，
> >
> > $n = 1$ 时， $a_{1} = S_{1} = \frac{3}{2}, n\geq 2$ 时， $a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n - 1} = \frac{n + 2}{n + 1} -\frac{n + 1}{n} = -\frac{1}{n(n + 1)},$
> >
> > 故 $a_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{3}{2},n=1\\&-\frac{1}{n(n+1)},n\geq2\end{aligned}\right.$
