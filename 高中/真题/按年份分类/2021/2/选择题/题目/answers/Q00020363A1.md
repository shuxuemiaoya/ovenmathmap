> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $P(\text{甲})=\frac{1}{6},\quad P(\text{乙})=\frac{1}{6},\quad P(\text{丙})=\frac{5}{36},\quad P(\text{丁})=\frac{6}{36}=\frac{1}{6},$

> >
