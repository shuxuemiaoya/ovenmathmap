> [!faq]- 2021年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（全国甲卷）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because f(x + 1)$ 为奇函数， $\therefore f(x)$ 关于 $(1,0)$ 中心对称， $\therefore f(1) = 0$
> >
> > 因 $f(x + 2)$ 为偶函数，故 $f(x)$ 关于 $x = 2$ 轴对称，周期为4.
> >
> > $\therefore f(0) = -f(2), f(3) = f(1)$ . 即 $f(1) - f(2) = 6, f(2) = -6$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a + b = 0 \\ 4 a + b = - 6 \end{array} , \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = - 2 \\ b = 2 \end{array} \right. \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $f\left(\frac{9}{2}\right) = f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = -f\left(\frac{3}{2}\right) = -(-2 \times \frac{9}{4} + 2) = \frac{5}{2}.$
> >
> > 故选D.
