> [!faq]- 2021年全国统一高考数学试卷（文科）（全国甲卷）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** \- $\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由图可知 $\frac{3}{4} T = \frac{13}{12}\pi -\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{3}{4}\pi \Rightarrow T = \pi = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}\Rightarrow \omega = 2$ ，由
> >
> > $$
> > f \left(\frac {1 3}{1 2} \pi\right) = 2 \Rightarrow 2 \cos \left(\frac {1 3 \pi}{6} + \varphi\right) = 2 \Rightarrow \frac {1 3 \pi}{6} + \varphi = 2 \pi \Rightarrow \varphi = - \frac {\pi}{6},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $f(\frac{\pi}{2})=2\cos(2\times\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{\pi}{6})=-\sqrt{3}$
