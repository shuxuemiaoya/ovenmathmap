> [!faq]- 2021年全国统一高考数学试卷（文科）（全国甲卷）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $3\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $|a - b| = 5$ , $\therefore a^2 - 2ab + b^2 = 25$ , $\therefore |a|^2 - 2ab + |b|^2 = 25$ , $\therefore 9 - 2 + |b|^2 = 25$ , $\therefore |b|^2 = 18$ , $\therefore |b| = 3\sqrt{2}$ .
