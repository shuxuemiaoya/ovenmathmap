> [!faq]- 2021年全国统一高考数学试卷（文科）（全国甲卷）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \{\sqrt{S_n}\}$ 为等差数列，设公差为 $d$ $\therefore \sqrt{S_2} -\sqrt{S_1} = d$ ，即 $\sqrt{4a_1} -\sqrt{a_1} = d$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sqrt {a _ {1}} = d = \sqrt {S _ {1}}, \therefore \sqrt {S _ {n}} = \sqrt {S _ {1}} + (n - 1) d = n d
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = n ^ {2} d ^ {2}, \therefore a _ {n} = S _ {n} - S _ {n - 1} = n ^ {2} d ^ {2} - (n - 1) ^ {2} d ^ {2} = (2 n - 1) d ^ {2} (n \geq 2),
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $a_{n} = 2d^{2}\cdot n - d^{2}(n\geq 2)$ ，又 $a_1 = S_1 = d^2$ 同样满足通项公式，所以 $\{a_n\}$ 是等差数列.
