> [!faq]- 精品解析：2024年高考全国甲卷数学(理)真题（解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 5
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先设展开式中第项系数最大，则根据通项公式有 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&C_{10}^{r}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{10-r}\geq C_{10}^{r+1}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{9-r}\\&C_{10}^{r}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{10-r}\geq C_{10}^{r-1}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{11-r}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，进而求出即
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题展开式通项公式为 $T_{r+1} = C_{10}^{r}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{10-r}x^{r}, \quad 0 \leq r \leq 10$ 且 $r \in Z$ ，
> >
> > 设展开式中第项系数最大，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} C_{10}^{r}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{10 - r} \geq C_{10}^{r + 1}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{9 - r}\\ C_{10}^{r}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{10 - r} \geq C_{10}^{r - 1}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{11 - r} \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > $\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} r \geq \frac{29}{4} \\ r \leq \frac{33}{4}, \text{即} \frac{29}{4} \leq r \leq \frac{33}{4}, \text{又} \\ r \in \mathbf{Z}, r = 8 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以展开式中系数最大的项是第9项，且该项系数为 $C_{10}^{8}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2}=5$ .
> >
> > 故答案为：5.
