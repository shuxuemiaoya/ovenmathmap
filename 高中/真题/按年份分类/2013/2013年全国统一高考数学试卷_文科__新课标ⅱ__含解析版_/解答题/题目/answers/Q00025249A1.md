> > 解答】解：（1）设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为d=0，
> >
> > 由题意 $a_1, a_{11}, a_{13}$ 成等比数列， $\therefore a_{11}^2 = a_1 a_{13}$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore (a_{1} + 10d)^{2} = a_{1}(a_{1} + 12d)$ ，化为 $d(2a_{1} + 25d) = 0$
> >
> > $\because d = 0, \therefore 2 \times 25 + 25d = 0$ ，解得 $d = -2$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = 2 5 + (n - 1) \times (- 2) = - 2 n + 2 7.
> > $$
> >
> > (Ⅱ) 由 (Ⅰ) 可得 $a_{3n-2} = -2(3n-2) + 27 = -6n + 31$ , 可知此数列是以 25 为首项, -6
> >
> > 为公差的等差数列.
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {n} = a _ {1} + a _ {4} + a _ {7} + \dots + a _ {3 n - 2} = \frac {n (a _ {1} + a _ {3 n - 2})}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\mathrm{n} (2 5 - 6 \mathrm{n} + 3 1)}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - 3 n ^ {2} + 2 8 n.
> > $$
