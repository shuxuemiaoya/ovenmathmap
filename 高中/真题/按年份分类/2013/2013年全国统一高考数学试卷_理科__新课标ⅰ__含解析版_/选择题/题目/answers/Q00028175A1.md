> [!faq]- 2013年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（新课标ⅰ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由 $a_{n+1}=a_{n}$ 可知 $\triangle A_{n}B_{n}C_{n}$ 的边 $B_{n}C_{n}$ 为定值 $a_{1}$ ，由 $b_{n+1}+c_{n+1}-2a_{1}=$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解： $b_{1}=2a_{1}-c_{1}$ 且 $b_{1}>c_{1}$ ，∴ $2a_{1}-c_{1}>c_{1}$ ，∴ $a_{1}>c_{1}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b _ {1} - a _ {1} = 2 a _ {1} - c _ {1} - a _ {1} = a _ {1} - c _ {1} > 0, \quad \therefore b _ {1} > a _ {1} > c _ {1},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b _ {1} - c _ {1} <   a _ {1}, \therefore 2 a _ {1} - c _ {1} - c _ {1} <   a _ {1}, \therefore 2 c _ {1} > a _ {1}, \therefore c _ {1} > \frac {a _ {1}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 由题意， $b_{n+1} + c_{n+1} = \frac{b_n + c_n}{2} + a_n, \therefore b_{n+1} + c_{n+1} - 2a_n = \frac{1}{2}(b_n + c_n - 2a_n)$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b _ {n} + c _ {n} - 2 a _ {n} = 0, \quad \therefore b _ {n} + c _ {n} = 2 a _ {n} = 2 a _ {1}, \quad \therefore b _ {n} + c _ {n} = 2 a _ {1},
> > $$
> >
> > 由此可知顶点 $A_{n}$ 在以 $B_{n}$ 、 $C_{n}$ 为焦点的椭圆上，
> >
> > 又由题意， $b_{n+1}-c_{n+1}=\frac{c_{n}-b_{n}}{2},\quad\therefore b_{n+1}-(2a_{1}-b_{n+1})=\frac{2a_{1}-b_{n}-b_{n}}{2}=a_{1}-b_{n},$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b _ {n + 1} - a _ {1} = \frac {1}{2} (a _ {1} - b _ {n}), \quad \therefore b _ {n} - a _ {1} = (- \frac {1}{2}) ^ {n - 1},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b _ {n} = a _ {1} + \left(b _ {1} - a _ {1}\right) \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n - 1}, c _ {n} = 2 a _ {1} - b _ {n} = a _ {1} - \left(b _ {1} - a _ {1}\right) \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n - 1},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {n} ^ {2} = \frac {3 a _ {1}}{2} \left(\frac {3 a _ {1}}{2} - a _ {1}\right) \left[ \frac {3 a _ {1}}{2} - a _ {1} - \left(b _ {1} - a _ {1}\right) \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n - 1} \right] [
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {3 a _ {1}}{2} - a _ {1} + (b _ {1} - a _ {1}) \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n - 1} ]
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{3}{4} a_{1}^{2}\left[\frac{a_{1}^{2}}{2} -\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{n - 1}\left(b_{1} - a_{1}\right)^{2}\right]$ 单调递增（可证当 $n = 1$ 时 $\frac{a_1^2}{4} -(b_1 - a_1)^2 >0)$
> >
> > 故选：B.
