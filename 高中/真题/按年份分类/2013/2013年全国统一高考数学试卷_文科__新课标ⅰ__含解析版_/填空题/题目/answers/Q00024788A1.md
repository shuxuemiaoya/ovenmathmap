> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > . 0 + 3. 1 + 2. 3 + 2. 4) = 2. 3.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overline {{{y}}} = \frac {1}{2 0} \times (3. 2 + 1. 7 + 1. 9 + 0. 8 + 0. 9 + 2. 4 + 1. 2 + 2. 6 + 1. 3 + 1. 4 + 1. 6 + 0. 5 + 1. 8 + 0. 6 + 2. 1 + 1. 1 + 2. 5

> >
