> [!faq]- 2013年上海高考数学真题（文科）试卷（word解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** -2 【
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $(x^{2} + \frac{a}{x})^{5}\Rightarrow C_{5}^{r}(x^{2})^{5 - r}(\frac{a}{x})^{r} = -10x^{7}\Rightarrow r = 1,C_{5}^{1}a = -10$ $\Rightarrow 5a = -10,a = -2$
