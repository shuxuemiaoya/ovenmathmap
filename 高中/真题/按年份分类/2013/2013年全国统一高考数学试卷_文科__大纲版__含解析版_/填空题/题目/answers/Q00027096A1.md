> [!faq]- 2013年全国统一高考数学试卷（文科）（大纲版）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （10 分）等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 中， $a_{7}=4,\quad a_{19}=2a_{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （I）由 $a_{7}=4,\quad a_{19}=2a_{9}$ ，结合等差数列的通项公式可求 $a_{1}, d$ ，进而可求 $a_{n}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：
> > （1）设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 $d$
> >
> > $\because a_{7} = 4, a_{19} = 2a_{9},$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a _ {1} + 6 d = 4 \\ a _ {1} + 1 8 d = 2 (a _ {1} + 8 d) \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 解得， $a_{1}=1,\quad d=\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = 1 + \frac {1}{2} (n - 1) = \frac {1 + n}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > (II) $\because b_{n}=\frac{1}{na_{n}}=\frac{2}{n(n+1)}=\frac{2}{n}-\frac{2}{n+1}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore s _ {n} = 2 \left(1 - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3} + \dots + \frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \left(1 - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) = \frac {2 n}{n + 1}
> > $$
