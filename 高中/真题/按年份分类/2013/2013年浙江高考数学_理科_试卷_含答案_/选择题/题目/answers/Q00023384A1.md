> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 分析：
> > 不妨设 $|AF_{1}|=x,|AF_{2}|=y$ ，依题意 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x+y&=4\\ x^{2}+y^{2}&=12\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解此方程组可求得x，y的值，利用双曲线的定义及性质即可求得 $C_{2}$ 的离心率.
> >
> > 解答：解：设 $|AF_{1}|=x$ ， $|AF_{2}|=y$ ，∵点A为椭圆 $C_{1}:\frac{x^{2}}{4}+y^{2}=1$ 上的点，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 2 a = 4, b = 1, c = \sqrt {3};
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| \mathrm{AF} _ {1} \right| + \left| \mathrm{AF} _ {2} \right| = 2 \mathrm{a} = 4
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{x} + \mathrm{y} = 4;
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{AF} _ {1} \mathrm{BF} _ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| A F _ {1} \right| ^ {2} + \left| A F _ {2} \right| ^ {2} = \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{x} ^ {2} + \mathrm{y} ^ {2} = (2 \mathrm{c}) ^ {2} = (2 \sqrt {3}) ^ {2} = 1 2
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x + y = 4 \\ x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = 1 2 \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{x} = 2 - \sqrt {2}, \mathrm{y} = 2 + \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{C} _ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > 2 a = , \left| A F _ {2} \right| - \left| A F _ {1} \right| = y - x = 2 \sqrt {2}, 2 c = 2 \sqrt {2 ^ {2} - 1 ^ {2}} = 2 \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{C} _ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{e} = \frac {\mathrm{c}}{\mathrm{a}} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{\sqrt {2}} = \frac {\sqrt {6}}{2}.
> > $$
