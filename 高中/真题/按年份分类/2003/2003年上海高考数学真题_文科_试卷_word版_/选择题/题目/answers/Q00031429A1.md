> [!faq]- 2003年上海高考数学真题（文科）试卷（word版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** \sqrt{2}\approx
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题解析推导如下：
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > [解]
> > (1)
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} a _ {1} C _ {2} ^ {0} - a _ {2} C _ {2} ^ {1} + a _ {3} C _ {2} ^ {2} = a _ {1} - 2 a _ {1} q + a _ {1} q ^ {2} = a _ {1} (1 - q) ^ {2}, \\ a _ {1} C _ {3} ^ {0} - a _ {2} C _ {3} ^ {1} + a _ {3} C _ {3} ^ {2} - a _ {4} C _ {3} ^ {3} = a _ {1} - 3 a _ {1} q + 3 a _ {1} q ^ {2} - a _ {1} q ^ {3} = a _ {1} (1 - q) ^ {3}. \end{array}
> > $$
> > (2) 归纳概括的结论为:
> >
> > 若数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是首项为 $a_1$ ，公比为q的等比数列，则
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {a _ {1} C _ {n} ^ {0} - a _ {2} C _ {n} ^ {1} + a _ {3} C _ {n} ^ {2} - a _ {4} C _ {n} ^ {3} + \ldots + (- 1) ^ {n} a _ {n + 1} C _ {n} ^ {n} = a _ {1} (1 - q) ^ {n}, n \text {为正整数}.} \\ & {\text {证明}: a _ {1} C _ {n} ^ {0} - a _ {2} C _ {n} ^ {1} + a _ {3} C _ {n} ^ {2} - a _ {4} C _ {n} ^ {3} + \ldots + (- 1) ^ {n} a _ {n + 1} C _ {n} ^ {n}} \\ & {\quad = a _ {1} C _ {n} ^ {0} - a _ {1} q C _ {n} ^ {1} + a _ {1} q ^ {2} C _ {n} ^ {2} - a _ {1} q ^ {3} C _ {n} ^ {3} + \ldots + (- 1) ^ {n} a _ {1} q ^ {n} C _ {n} ^ {n}} \\ & {\quad = a _ {1} [ C _ {n} ^ {0} - q C _ {n} ^ {1} + q ^ {2} C _ {n} ^ {2} - q ^ {3} C _ {n} ^ {3} + \ldots + (- 1) ^ {n} q ^ {n} C _ {n} ^ {n} ] = a _ {1} (1 - q) ^ {n}} \end{array}
> > $$
> > (3) 因为 $S_{n}=\frac{a_{1}-a_{1}q^{n}}{1-q}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\text {所以} S _ {1} C _ {n} ^ {0} - S _ {2} C _ {n} ^ {1} + S _ {3} C _ {n} ^ {2} - S _ {4} C _ {n} ^ {3} + \ldots + (- 1) ^ {n} S _ {n + 1} C _ {n} ^ {n}} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {a _ {1} - a _ {1} q}{1 - q} C _ {n} ^ {0} - \frac {a _ {1} - a _ {1} q ^ {2}}{1 - q} C _ {n} ^ {1} + \frac {a _ {1} - a _ {1} q ^ {3}}{1 - q} C _ {n} ^ {2} + \ldots + (- 1) ^ {n} \frac {a _ {1} - a _ {1} q ^ {n + 1}}{1 - q} C _ {n} ^ {n}} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {a _ {1}}{1 - q} [ C _ {n} ^ {0} - C _ {n} ^ {1} + C _ {n} ^ {2} - C _ {n} ^ {3} + \ldots + (- 1) ^ {n} C _ {n} ^ {n} ] -} \\ & {\qquad \frac {a _ {1} q}{1 - q} [ C _ {n} ^ {0} - q C _ {n} ^ {1} + q ^ {2} C _ {n} ^ {2} - q ^ {3} C _ {n} ^ {3} + \ldots + (- 1) ^ {n} q ^ {n} C _ {n} ^ {n} ] = \frac {a _ {1} q}{q - 1} (1 - q) ^ {n}.} \end{array}
> > $$
