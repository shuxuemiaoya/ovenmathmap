> [!faq]- 2003年上海高考数学真题（理科）试卷（word版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** [解]
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题解析推导如下：
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > [解]
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} \mid z _ {1} \cdot z _ {2} \mid & = \mid 1 + \sin \theta \cos \theta + (\cos \theta - \sin \theta) i \mid \\ & = \sqrt {(1 + \sin \theta \cos \theta) ^ {2} + (\cos \theta - \sin \theta) ^ {2}} \\ & = \sqrt {2 + \sin^ {2} \theta \cos^ {2} \theta} = \sqrt {2 + \frac {1}{4} \sin^ {2} 2 \theta}. \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $|z_{1}\cdot z_{2}|$ 的最大值为 $\frac{3}{2}$ ，最小值为 $\sqrt{2}$ .
