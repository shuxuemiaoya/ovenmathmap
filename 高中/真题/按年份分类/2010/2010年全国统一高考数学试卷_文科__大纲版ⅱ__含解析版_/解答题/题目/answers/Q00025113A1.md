> > [!note]- **【解析】**

> >
> > 【
> > 解答】解：（1）设正等比数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 首项为 $a_{1}$ ，公比为q，由题意得：
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a _ {1} (1 + q) = 2 \cdot \frac {1}{a _ {1}} \cdot \frac {1}{q} (1 + q) \\ a _ {1} q ^ {2} (1 + q + q ^ {2}) = 6 4 \cdot \frac {1}{a _ {1} q ^ {4}} (1 + q + q ^ {2}) \end{array} \Leftrightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a _ {1} ^ {2} q = 2 \\ a _ {1} ^ {2} q ^ {6} = 6 4 \end{array} \Leftrightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a _ {1} = 1 \\ q = 2 \end{array} \right. \therefore a _ {n} = 2 ^ {n - 1} (6 \text {分}) \right. \right.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $b_n = (2^{n-1} + \frac{1}{2^{n-1}})^2 = 4^{n-1} + (\frac{1}{4})^{n-1} + 2$
> >
> > $\therefore b_{n}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n} = \frac{1(1 - 4^{n})}{1 - 4} +\frac{1(1 - \frac{1}{4^{n}})}{1 - \frac{1}{4}} +2n = \frac{1}{3}\cdot 4^n -\frac{4}{3}\cdot (\frac{1}{4})^n +2n + 1$ (12分）
