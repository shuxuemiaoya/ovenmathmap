> [!faq]- 2010年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（大纲版ⅰ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 设点 P（ $x_{0}$ ， $y_{0}$ ）在双曲线的右支，由双曲线的第二定义得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=e\left[x_{0}-\left(\frac{a^{2}}{c}\right)\right]=a+ex_{0}=1+\sqrt{2}x_{0},$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 分析】设点 P（ $x_{0}$ ， $y_{0}$ ）在双曲线的右支，由双曲线的第二定义得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=e\left[x_{0}-\left(\frac{a^{2}}{c}\right)\right]=a+ex_{0}=1+\sqrt{2}x_{0},$
> >
> > $\left|\mathrm{PF}_{2}\right| = \mathrm{e}\left[\mathrm{x}_{0} - \frac{\mathrm{a}^{2}}{\mathrm{c}}\right] = \mathrm{ex}_{0} - \mathrm{a} = \sqrt{2}\mathrm{x}_{0} - 1.$ 由余弦定理得 $\cos \angle F_1PF_2 =$
> >
> > $\frac{\left|\mathrm{PF}_1\right|^2 + \left|\mathrm{PF}_2\right|^2 - \left|\mathrm{F}_1\mathrm{F}_2\right|^2}{2\left|\mathrm{PF}_1\right| \left|\mathrm{PF}_2\right|}$ ，由此可求出 $P$ 到 $x$ 轴的距离。
> >
> > 【
> > 解答】解：不妨设点 $P(x_0, y_0)$ 在双曲线的右支，由双曲线的第二定义得
> >
> > $$
> > \left| P F _ {1} \right| = e \left[ x _ {0} - \left(\frac {a ^ {2}}{c}\right) \right] = a + e x _ {0} = 1 + \sqrt {2} x _ {0},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \mathrm{PF} _ {2} \right| = e \left[ x _ {0} - \frac {a ^ {2}}{c}\right) ] = e x _ {0} - a = \sqrt {2} x _ {0} - 1.
> > $$
> >
> > 由余弦定理得
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \angle F _ {1} P F _ {2} = \frac {\left| P F _ {1} \right| ^ {2} + \left| P F _ {2} \right| ^ {2} - \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| ^ {2}}{2 \left| P F _ {1} \right| \left| P F _ {2} \right|},
> > $$
> >
> > 即
> >
> > $$
> > \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} =
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\left(1 + \sqrt {2} x _ {0}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\sqrt {2} x _ {0} - 1\right) ^ {2} - \left(2 \sqrt {2}\right) ^ {2}}{2 \left(1 + \sqrt {2} x _ {0}\right) \left(\sqrt {2} x _ {0} - 1\right)},
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $x_0^2 = \frac{5}{2}$ ，所以 $y_0^2 = x_0^2 - 1 = \frac{3}{2}$ ，故 P 到 x 轴的距离为 $|y_0| = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$
> >
> > 故选：B.
