> [!faq]- 2012年重庆市高考数学试卷(文科)含答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 式为 $f(x) = 2\sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ ;
> >
> > (II) 函数 g (x) = $\frac{6\cos^{4}x - \sin^{2}x - 1}{f(x + \frac{\pi}{6})}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {6 \cos^ {4} x - \sin^ {2} x - 1}{2 \sin (2 x + \frac {\pi}{2})}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {6 \cos^ {4} x - \sin^ {2} x - 1}{2 \cos 2 x}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {6 \cos^ {4} x - \sin^ {2} x - 1}{2 (2 \cos^ {2} x - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {6 \cos^ {4} x + \cos^ {2} x - 2}{2 (2 \cos^ {2} x - 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\left(3 \cos^ {2} x + 2\right) \quad \left(2 \cos^ {2} x - 1\right)}{2 \left(2 \cos^ {2} x - 1\right)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {3}{2} \cos^ {2} x + 1 (\cos^ {2} x \neq \frac {1}{2})
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $\cos^2\mathrm{x}\in [0,1]$ ，且 $\cos^2\mathrm{x}\neq \frac{1}{2},$
> >
> > 故 $g(x)$ 的值域为 $[1, \frac{7}{4}) \cup (\frac{7}{4}, \frac{5}{2}]$ .
> >
