> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【试题解析】
> >
> > $$
> > \because f (x) = \lg x, f (a b) = 1 ^ {\prime} \therefore \lg (a b) = 1 ^ {\prime} \therefore f \left(a ^ {2}\right) + f \left(b ^ {2}\right) = \lg a ^ {2} + \lg b ^ {2} = 2 \lg (a b) = 2.
> > $$
