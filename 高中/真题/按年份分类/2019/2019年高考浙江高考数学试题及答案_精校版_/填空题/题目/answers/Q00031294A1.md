> [!faq]- 2019年高考浙江高考数学试题及答案(精校版)解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1).0 (2). $2\sqrt{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题主要考查平面向量的应用，题目难度较大.从引入“基向量”入手，简化模的表现形式，利用转化与化归思想将问题逐步简化.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详
> >
> > 解
> >
> > $$
> > \lambda_ {1} A B + \lambda_ {2} B C + \lambda_ {3} C D + \lambda_ {4} D A + \lambda_ {5} A C + \lambda_ {6} B D = (\lambda_ {1} - \lambda_ {3} + \lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6}) A B + (\lambda_ {2} - \lambda_ {4} + \lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6}) A D
> > $$
> >
> > 要使 $\left|\lambda_{1}AB + \lambda_{2}BC + \lambda_{3}CD + \lambda_{4}DA + \lambda_{5}AC + \lambda_{6}BD\right|$ 的最小，只需要
> >
> > $\left|\lambda_{1}-\lambda_{3}+\lambda_{5}-\lambda_{6}\right|=\left|\lambda_{2}-\lambda_{4}+\lambda_{5}+\lambda_{6}\right|=0$ ，此时只需要取 $\lambda_{1}=1, \lambda_{2}=-1, \lambda_{3}=1, \lambda_{4}=1, \lambda_{5}=1, \lambda_{6}=1$
> >
> > 此时 $\left|\lambda_{1}AB + \lambda_{2}BC + \lambda_{3}CD + \lambda_{4}DA + \lambda_{5}AC + \lambda_{6}BD\right|_{\min} = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \left| \lambda_ {1} \overline {{A B}} + \lambda_ {2} \overline {{B C}} + \lambda_ {3} \overline {{C D}} + \lambda_ {4} \overline {{D A}} + \lambda_ {5} \overline {{A C}} + \lambda_ {6} \overline {{B D}} \right| ^ {2} = \left| \left(\lambda_ {1} - \lambda_ {3} + \lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6}\right) \overline {{A B}} + \left(\lambda_ {2} - \lambda_ {4} + \lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6}\right) \overline {{A D}} \right| ^ {2} \\ = \left(\lambda_ {1} - \lambda_ {3} + \lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\lambda_ {2} - \lambda_ {4} + \lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6}\right) ^ {2} \leq \left(\left| \lambda_ {1} \right| + \left| \lambda_ {3} \right| + \left| \lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6} \right|\right) ^ {2} + \left(\left| \lambda_ {2} \right| + \left| \lambda_ {4} \right| + \left| \lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6} \right|\right) ^ {2} \\ = (2 + | \lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6} |) ^ {2} + (2 + | \lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6} |) ^ {2} = 8 + 4 (| \lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6} | + | \lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6} |) + (\lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6}) ^ {2} + (\lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6}) ^ {2} \\ = 8 + 4 \sqrt {\left(| \lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6} | + | \lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6} |\right) ^ {2}} + 2 (\lambda_ {5} ^ {2} + \lambda_ {6} ^ {2}) = 1 2 + 4 \sqrt {\left(\lambda_ {5} - \lambda_ {6}\right) ^ {2} + (\lambda_ {5} + \lambda_ {6}) ^ {2} + 2 | \lambda_ {5} ^ {2} - \lambda_ {6} ^ {2}} \\ = 1 2 + 4 \sqrt {2 (\lambda_ {5} ^ {2} + \lambda_ {6} ^ {2}) + 2 | \lambda_ {5} ^ {2} - \lambda_ {6} ^ {2}} = 2 0 \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 等号成立当且仅当 $\lambda_{1}, -\lambda_{3}, \lambda_{5} - \lambda_{6}$ 均非负或者均非正，并且 $\lambda_{2}, -\lambda_{4}, \lambda_{5} + \lambda_{6}$ 均非负或者均非正。
> >
> > 比如 $\lambda_{1} = 1, \lambda_{2} = 1, \lambda_{3} = -1, \lambda_{4} = -1, \lambda_{5} = 1, \lambda_{6} = 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} \left| \lambda_ {1} A B + \lambda_ {2} B C + \lambda_ {3} C D + \lambda_ {4} D A + \lambda_ {5} A C + \lambda_ {6} B D \right| _ {\max} = \sqrt {2 0} = 2 \sqrt {5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 点睛：对于此题需充分利用转化与化归思想，从“基向量”入手，最后求不等式最值，是一道向量和不等式的综合题。
> >
> > 【点睛】对于平面向量的应用问题，需充分利用转化与化归思想、数形结合思想.
