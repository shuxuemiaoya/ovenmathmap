> > $$
> > a _ {n + 1} \geq \left(\frac {1 7}{1 6}\right) ^ {2 ^ {n - 1}} (n \geq 4), \text {则} a _ {1 0} \geq \left(\frac {1 7}{1 6}\right) ^ {2 ^ {6}} = \left(1 + \frac {1}{1 6}\right) ^ {6 4} = 1 + \frac {6 4}{1 6} + \frac {6 4 \times 6 3}{2} \times \frac {1}{1 6 ^ {2}} + \dots > 1 + 4 + 7 > 1 0.
