> [!faq]- 2019年理科数学海南省高考真题含答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** -3
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \cos B = \cos \frac {\pi}{3} = \frac {a ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - b ^ {2}}{2 a c} = \frac {4 c ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - 3 6}{4 c ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore c = 2 \sqrt {3}, a = 4 \sqrt {3}, \therefore S = \frac {1}{2} a c \sin B = \frac {1}{2} \times 4 \sqrt {3} \times 2 \sqrt {3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = 6 \sqrt {3}
> > $$
