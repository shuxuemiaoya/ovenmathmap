> > $$
> > \therefore | P Q | \neq O F | = c, \therefore \angle P O Q = 9 0 ^ {\circ},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {又} \mid O P \mid \nexists O Q \mid = a, \therefore a ^ {2} + a ^ {2} = c ^ {2}
