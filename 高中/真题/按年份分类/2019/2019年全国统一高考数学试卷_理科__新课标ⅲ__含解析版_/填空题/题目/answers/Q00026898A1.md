> [!faq]- 2019年全国统一高考数学试卷（理科）（新课标ⅲ）（含解析版）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{2}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据向量数量积的应用, 求出相应的长度和数量积即可得到结论.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解： $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{c}=\vec{a}\cdot(2\vec{a}-\sqrt{5}\vec{b})=2\vec{a}^{2}-\sqrt{5}\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}=2,$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {c} ^ {2} = (2 \overrightarrow {a} - \sqrt {5 b}) ^ {2} = 4 \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} - 4 \sqrt {5 a} \cdot \overrightarrow {b} + 5 \overrightarrow {b} ^ {2} = 9,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | \stackrel {\rightarrow} {c} | = 3,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos <   \overrightarrow {a}, \overrightarrow {c} > = \frac {\overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {c}}{| \overrightarrow {a} | | \overrightarrow {c} |} = \frac {2}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{2}{3}$
