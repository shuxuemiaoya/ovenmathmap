> [!faq]- 2019年江苏高考数学试题及答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{a_A}{a_C} = \frac{\frac{v_A^2}{r_A}}{\frac{v_C^2}{r_C}} = \frac{v_A^2}{v_C^2} = 1:4.$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由题意首先求得 $\tan \alpha$ 的值，然后利用两角和差正余弦公式和二倍角公式将原问题转化为齐次式求值的问题，最后切化弦求得三角函数式的值即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\frac{\tan\alpha}{\tan\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}=\frac{\tan\alpha}{\frac{\tan\alpha+1}{1-\tan\alpha}}=\frac{\tan\alpha(1-\tan\alpha)}{\tan\alpha+1}=-\frac{2}{3}$
> >
> > 得 $3\tan^2\alpha - 5\tan \alpha - 2 = 0$
> >
> > 解得 $\tan\alpha=2$ ，或 $\tan\alpha=-\frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \sin 2 \alpha \cos \frac {\pi}{4} + \cos 2 \alpha \sin \frac {\pi}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} (\sin 2 \alpha + \cos 2 \alpha) = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \left(\frac {2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \left(\frac {2 \tan \alpha + 1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $\tan \alpha = 2$ 时，上式 $= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left(\frac{2\times 2 + 1 - 2^2}{2^2 + 1}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$
> >
> > 当 $\tan\alpha=-\frac{1}{3}$ 时，上式 $=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left(\frac{2\times\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)+1-\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2}}{\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2}+1}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}.$
> >
> > 综上， $\sin \left(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}.$
> >
> > 【点睛】本题考查三角函数的化简求值，渗透了逻辑推理和数学运算素养.采取转化法，利用分类讨论和转化与化归思想解题.
