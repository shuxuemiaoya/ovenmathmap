>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意可知 X 所有可能的取值为：-1，0，1
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore P (X = - 1) = (1 - \alpha) \beta ; P (X = 0) = \alpha \beta + (1 - \alpha) (1 - \beta); P (X = 1) = \alpha (1 - \beta)
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $X$ 的分布列如下：
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>-1</td><td>0</td><td>1</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td>(1-α)β</td><td>αβ+(1-α)(1-β)</td><td>α(1-β)</td></tr></table>
> >
> > (2) $\because \alpha = 0.5, \beta = 0.8$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a = 0. 5 \times 0. 8 = 0. 4, b = 0. 5 \times 0. 8 + 0. 5 \times 0. 2 = 0. 5, c = 0. 5 \times 0. 2 = 0. 1
> > $$
> >
> > (i) $\because p_{i}=ap_{i-1}+bp_{i}+cp_{i+1}(i=1,2,\cdots;7)$
> >
> > 即 $p_{i}=0.4p_{i-1}+0.5p_{i}+0.1p_{i+1}(i=1,2,\cdots;7)$
> >
> > 整理可得： $5p_{i}=4p_{i-1}+p_{i+1}(i=1,2,\cdots;7)\therefore p_{i+1}-p_{i}=4(p_{i}-p_{i-1})(i=1,2,\cdots;7)$
