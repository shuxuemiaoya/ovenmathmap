> [!faq]- 2019年山东高考文科数学真题及答案解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because f (- x) = \frac {\sin (- x) - x}{\cos (- x) + (- x) ^ {2}} = - \frac {\sin x + x}{\cos x + x ^ {2}} = - f (x),
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore f(x)$ 为奇函数，排除 A.
> >
> > 又 $f(\frac{\pi}{2}) = \frac{\sin\frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2}}{\cos\frac{\pi}{2} + \left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)^2} = \frac{4 + 2\pi}{\pi^2} >0$ ，排除C，
> >
> > $f(\pi)=\frac{\sin\pi+\pi}{\cos\pi+(\pi)^{2}}=\frac{\pi}{1+\pi^{2}}>0$ ，排除B，故选D.
