>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 答案:
> >
> > B
> >
> > 解答：
> >
> > $\alpha \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$ ， $2\sin 2\alpha = \cos 2\alpha + 1 \Rightarrow 4\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 2\cos^2 \alpha$
> >
> > 则 $2\sin \alpha = \cos \alpha \Rightarrow \tan \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$ ，所以 $\cos \alpha = \sqrt{\frac{1}{1 + \tan^2\alpha}} = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin\alpha=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}\alpha}=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
