> [!faq]- 《第四章 指数函数与对数函数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $m = a + \sqrt{a^2 + 1}$ ， $n = b + \sqrt{b^2 + 1}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {1}{m} = \frac {1}{a + \sqrt {a ^ {2} + 1}} = \sqrt {a ^ {2} + 1} - a, \frac {1}{n} = \frac {1}{b + \sqrt {b ^ {2} + 1}} = \sqrt {b ^ {2} + 1} - b
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore m - \frac {1}{m} = (a + \sqrt {a ^ {2} + 1}) - (\sqrt {a ^ {2} + 1} - a) = 2 a
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > n - \frac {1}{n} = (b + \sqrt {b ^ {2} + 1}) - (\sqrt {b ^ {2} + 1} - b) = 2 b.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a = \frac {m - \frac {1}{m}}{2}, b = \frac {n - \frac {1}{n}}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\because m \cdot n = 1$ ， $\therefore n = \frac{1}{m}$ ， $m = \frac{1}{n}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a = \frac {m - n}{2}, b = \frac {n - m}{2}, \therefore a + b = \frac {m - n}{2} + \frac {n - m}{2} = 0.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **D**
