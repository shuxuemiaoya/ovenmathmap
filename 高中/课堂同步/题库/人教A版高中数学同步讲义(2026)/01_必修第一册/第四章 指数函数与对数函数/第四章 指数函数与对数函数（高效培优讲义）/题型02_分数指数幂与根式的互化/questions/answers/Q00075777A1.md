> [!faq]- 《第四章 指数函数与对数函数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)112 (2)21 (3)4 (4) $4a$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）原式 $= 2^{(-3)\times \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)}\times 1 + \left(2^{3}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}\times 2^{\frac{1}{4}} + \left(2^{\frac{1}{3}}\right)^{6}\times \left(3^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)^{6} = 2 + 2^{\frac{3}{4} +\frac{1}{4}} + 2^{2}\times 3^{3} = 112.$
> >
> > (2) $(0.25)^{-\frac{1}{2}} - (-2)^{2} \times (2^{3})^{-\frac{2}{3}} + 10(2 - \sqrt{3})^{-1} - 10 \times 3^{0.5} = \left[(\frac{1}{2})^{2}\right]^{-\frac{1}{2}} - (-2)^{2} \times 2^{-2} + 10 \times \frac{1}{2 - \sqrt{3}} - 10 \times 3^{\frac{1}{2}}$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 - 4 \times \frac {1}{4} + 1 0 (2 + \sqrt {3}) - 1 0 \sqrt {3} = 2 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(7 + 4 \sqrt {3}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} - 8 1 ^ {\frac {1}{8}} + 3 2 ^ {\frac {3}{5}} - 2 \times \left(\frac {1}{8}\right) ^ {- \frac {2}{3}} + \sqrt [ 3 ]{2} \times \left(4 ^ {- \frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {- 1} = \left[ (2 + \sqrt {3}) ^ {2} \right] ^ {\frac {1}{2}} - \left(3 ^ {4}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{8}} + \left(2 ^ {5}\right) ^ {\frac {3}{5}} - 2 \times \left(2 ^ {- 3}\right) ^ {- \frac {2}{3}} + 2 ^ {\frac {1}{3}} \times \left(2 ^ {2}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{3}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 + \sqrt {3} - \sqrt {3} + 8 - 8 + 2 = 4
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(2 a ^ {\frac {2}{3}} b ^ {\frac {1}{2}}\right) \left(- 6 a ^ {\frac {1}{2}} b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) \div \left(- 3 a ^ {\frac {1}{6}} b ^ {\frac {5}{6}}\right) = \frac {2 \times (- 6)}{- 3} \cdot a ^ {\frac {2}{3} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{6}} b ^ {\frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{3} - \frac {5}{6}} = 4 a. \tag {4}
