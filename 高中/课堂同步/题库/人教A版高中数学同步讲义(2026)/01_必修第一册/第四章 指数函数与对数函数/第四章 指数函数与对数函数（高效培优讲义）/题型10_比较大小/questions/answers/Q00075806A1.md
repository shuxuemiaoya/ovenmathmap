> [!faq]- 《第四章 指数函数与对数函数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由于 $a = \log_{64}3 < \log_{64}4 = \frac{1}{3}\log_44 = \frac{1}{3}, a = \log_{64}3 > \log_{64}2\sqrt{2} = \frac{1}{4}\log_{2\sqrt{2}}2\sqrt{2} = \frac{1}{4}$
> >
> > 所以 $a \in \left(\frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{3}\right)$ ，又 $c = \frac{1}{3} \log_3 4 > \frac{1}{3} \log_3 3 = \frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > $b=\lg2\cdot\lg5=\lg2\cdot(1-\lg2)<\left[\frac{\lg2+(1-\lg2)}{2}\right]^{2}=\frac{1}{4}$ ，所以c>a>b.
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
