> [!faq]- 《专题01 指数及指数函数的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $x = \frac{1}{2}\left(5^{\frac{1}{n}} - 5^{-\frac{1}{n}}\right)$ $\therefore x^{2} = \frac{1}{4}\left(5^{\frac{2}{n}} - 2 + 5^{-\frac{2}{n}}\right)$ $\therefore 1 + x^{2} = \frac{1}{4}\left(5^{\frac{2}{n}} + 2 + 5^{-\frac{2}{n}}\right) = \frac{1}{4}\left(5^{\frac{1}{n}} + 5^{-\frac{1}{n}}\right)^{2}$ $\therefore (x + \sqrt{1 + x^2})^n = \left[\frac{1}{2}\left(5^{\frac{1}{n}} - 5^{-\frac{1}{n}}\right) + \frac{1}{2}\left(5^{\frac{1}{n}} + 5^{-\frac{1}{n}}\right)\right]^n = \left(5^{\frac{1}{n}}\right)^n = 5.$
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
