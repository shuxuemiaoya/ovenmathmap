> [!faq]- 《专题01 指数及指数函数的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $[-3, 3]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sqrt{(a - 3)(a^2 - 9)} = \sqrt{(a - 3)^2(a + 3)} = |a - 3|\sqrt{a + 3}$
> >
> > 要使 $\left|a-3\right|\sqrt{a+3}=\left(3-a\right)\sqrt{a+3}\mid$ 成立，
> >
> > 需 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a-3&\leq0\\ a+3&0\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $a\in[-3,3]$
