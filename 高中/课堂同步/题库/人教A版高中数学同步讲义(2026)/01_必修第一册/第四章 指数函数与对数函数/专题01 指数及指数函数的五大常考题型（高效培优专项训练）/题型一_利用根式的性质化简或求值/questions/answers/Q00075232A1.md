> [!faq]- 《专题01 指数及指数函数的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \frac{1}{1 - a} 0$ ，即 $1 - a > 0$ ， $\therefore a - 1 < 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore (a - 1) \cdot \sqrt {\frac {1}{1 - a}} = - (1 - a) \cdot \sqrt {\frac {1}{1 - a}} = - \sqrt {(1 - a) ^ {2}} \times \sqrt {\frac {1}{1 - a}} = - \sqrt {(1 - a) ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{1 - a}} = - \sqrt {1 - a}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
