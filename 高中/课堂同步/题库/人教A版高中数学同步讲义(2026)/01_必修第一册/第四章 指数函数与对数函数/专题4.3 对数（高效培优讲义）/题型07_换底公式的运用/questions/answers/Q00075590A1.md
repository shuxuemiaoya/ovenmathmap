> [!faq]- 《专题4.3 对数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{39}{16}$ (2)1 (3)52
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）原式 $= 3\cdot 3^{\log_36} - 2^4\cdot 2^{\log_23} + 10^{\lg 27} + 3^{-2(\log_34 - 1)}$
> >
> > $$
> > = 3 \cdot 3 ^ {\log_ {3} 6} - 2 ^ {4} \cdot 2 ^ {\log_ {2} 3} + 1 0 ^ {\lg 2 7} + 3 ^ {2 - 2 \log_ {3} 4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 3 \cdot 6 - 1 6 \cdot 3 + 2 7 + \frac {3 ^ {2}}{3 ^ {\log_ {3} 1 6}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 8 - 4 8 + 2 7 + \frac {9}{1 6} = - \frac {3 9}{1 6}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > (2) \text {原式} = (\lg 2 + \lg 5) \left[ (\lg 2) ^ {2} - \lg 2 \cdot \lg 5 + (\lg 5) ^ {2} \right] + 3 \lg 2 \cdot \lg 5
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = (\lg 2) ^ {2} - \lg 2 \cdot \lg 5 + (\lg 5) ^ {2} + 3 \lg 2 \cdot \lg 5 = (\lg 2 + \lg 5) ^ {2} = 1.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \lg \left(5 0 0 \times \frac {8}{5}\right) - \lg \sqrt {6 4} + 5 0 \left[ \lg (2 \times 5) \right] ^ {2} \tag {3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \lg 8 0 0 - \lg 8 + 5 0 = \lg \frac {8 0 0}{8} + 5 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \lg 1 0 0 + 5 0 = 2 + 5 0 = 5 2
> > $$
> >

> >
> > $$
> > = \lg 5 + \lg 1 0 0 + \lg 8 - \lg 5 - \lg 8 + 5 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \lg 1 0 0 + 5 0 = 5 2
