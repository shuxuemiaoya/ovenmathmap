> [!faq]- 《专题4.3 对数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 函数 $f(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{cccc} & \mathrm{e}^x, & x & 0\\ & \ln [f(x + 2)], & x <   0 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 则 $f\left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) - f(\ln 2) = \ln \left[f\left(-\frac{1}{4} + 2\right)\right] - \mathrm{e}^{\ln 2} = \ln \left[f\left(\frac{7}{4}\right)\right] - 2 = \ln \mathrm{e}^{\frac{7}{4}} - 2 = \frac{7}{4} - 2 = -\frac{1}{4}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
