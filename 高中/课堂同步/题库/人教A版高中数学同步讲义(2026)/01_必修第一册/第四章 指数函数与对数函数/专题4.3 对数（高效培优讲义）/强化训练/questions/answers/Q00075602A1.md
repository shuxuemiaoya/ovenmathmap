> [!faq]- 《专题4.3 对数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{\log_43}{\log_54} = \log_43\cdot \log_45 <   \left(\frac{\log_43 + \log_45}{2}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{\log_415}{2}\right)^2 <  \left(\frac{\log_416}{2}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{\log_44^2}{2}\right)^2 = 1$ ，所以 $a <   b$ 又 $a = \log_43 > \log_42 = \frac{1}{2} >\pi^{-1} = c$
> >
> > 所以 b > a > c.
> >
> > 故选 **A**
