> [!faq]- 《专题4.3 对数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{3}{4}$ (2)18
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）原式 $= \frac{1}{2}\log_{2}2^{\frac{3}{2}} - 3^{\log_32^{-1}} - \frac{\ln e}{\ln 6}\cdot \ln (2\times 3) = \frac{1}{2}\times \frac{3}{2} -2^{-1} - 1 = \frac{3}{4} -\frac{1}{2} -1 = -\frac{3}{4};$
> >
> > (2) 原式 $= 2 \times 3^{\frac{1}{2}} \times 3 \times \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} \times \left(2^{2} \times 3\right)^{\frac{1}{6}} = 2 \times 3^{\frac{1}{2}} \times 3 \times \frac{3^{\frac{1}{3}}}{2^{\frac{1}{3}}} \times 2^{2 \times \frac{1}{6}} \times 3^{\frac{1}{6}} = 2^{1 - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3}} \times 3^{\frac{1}{2} + 1 + \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6}} = 2 \times 3^{2} = 18.$
