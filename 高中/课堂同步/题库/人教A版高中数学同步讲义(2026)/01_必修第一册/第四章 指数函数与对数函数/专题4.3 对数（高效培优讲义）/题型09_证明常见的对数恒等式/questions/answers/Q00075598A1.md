> [!faq]- 《专题4.3 对数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $2^{x}=3^{y}=6^{z}=k(k\neq1)$
> >
> > $$
> > x = \log_ {2} k = \frac {\lg k}{\lg 2}, y = \log_ {3} k = \frac {\lg k}{\lg 3}, z = \log_ {6} k = \frac {\lg k}{\lg 6}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $\frac{1}{x} +\frac{1}{y} = \frac{\lg 2 + \lg 3}{\lg k} = \frac{\lg 6}{\lg k} = \frac{1}{z}$
