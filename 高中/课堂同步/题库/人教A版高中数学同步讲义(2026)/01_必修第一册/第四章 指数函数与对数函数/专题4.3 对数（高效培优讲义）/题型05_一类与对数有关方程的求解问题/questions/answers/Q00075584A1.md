> [!faq]- 《专题4.3 对数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{95}{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $t = \lg x$ ，则原方程化为 $3t^{2} - 5t + 1 = 0, \Delta = (-5)^{2} - 4 \times 3 \times 1 = 13 > 0, t_{1} + t_{2} = \frac{5}{3}$ ， $t_{1}t_{2} = \frac{1}{3}$ ，即
> >
> > $\lg x_{1} + \lg x_{2} = \frac{5}{3},\lg x_{1}\lg x_{2} = \frac{1}{3}$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \lg \left(x _ {1} x _ {2}\right) \cdot \left(\log_ {x _ {1}} x _ {2} + \log_ {x _ {2}} x _ {1}\right) = \left(\lg x _ {1} + \lg x _ {2}\right) \left(\frac {\lg x _ {1}}{\lg x _ {2}} + \frac {\lg x _ {2}}{\lg x _ {1}}\right) = \frac {5}{3} \times \frac {\left(\lg x _ {1}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\lg x _ {2}\right) ^ {2}}{\lg x _ {1} \lg x _ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {5}{3} \times \frac {\left(\lg x _ {1} + \lg x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} - 2 \lg x _ {1} \lg x _ {2}}{\lg x _ {1} \lg x _ {2}} = \frac {5}{3} \times \frac {\left(\frac {5}{3}\right) ^ {2} - 2 \times \frac {1}{3}}{\frac {1}{3}} = \frac {9 5}{9}.
> > $$
