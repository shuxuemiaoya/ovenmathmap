> [!faq]- 《专题4.3 对数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{19}{10}$ (2)3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）原式 $= \left[\left(\frac{1}{10}\right)^3\right]^{\frac{1}{3}} - 1 + \left(2^4\right)^{\frac{3}{4}} - \left(3^{\frac{1}{3}}\right)^6 = \frac{1}{10} - 1 + 8 - 9 = -\frac{19}{10}$
> >
> > (2) 原式 $=4\lg10^{\frac{1}{2}}-3+\frac{\lg3}{\lg2}\cdot\frac{\lg16}{\lg3}=2-3+\frac{\lg16}{\lg2}=-1+\frac{4\lg2}{\lg2}=3$
