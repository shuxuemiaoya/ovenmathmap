> [!faq]- 《专题02 对数及对数函数的六大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(1)^{5}$ ; $(2)^{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $(\lg 5)^2 - (\lg 2)^2 + 8^{\frac{2}{3}} \times \lg \sqrt{2} - 0.6^0 + 0.2^{-1}$
> >
> > $$
> > = (\lg 5 + \lg 2) (\lg 5 - \lg 2) + (2 ^ {3}) ^ {\frac {2}{3}} \times \frac {1}{2} \lg 2 - 1 + \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {- 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \lg 1 0 \times (\lg 5 - \lg 2) + 2 \lg 2 - 1 + 5 = \lg 5 + \lg 2 + 4 = 1 + 4 = 5.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $16^{a} = 2^{b} = 27$ ，故 $a = \log_{16}27,b = \log_227$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {故} \mathrm{e} ^ {\ln (\log_ {2} 9)} \times \left(\frac {1}{a} - \frac {1}{b}\right) = \log_ {2} 9 \times \left(\frac {1}{\log_ {1 6} 2 7} - \frac {1}{\log_ {2} 2 7}\right) = 2 \log_ {2} 3 \times \left(\log_ {2 7} 1 6 - \log_ {2 7} 2\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \log_ {2} 3 \times \left(\log_ {3 ^ {3}} 2 ^ {4} - \log_ {3 ^ {3}} 2\right) = 2 \log_ {2} 3 \times \left(\frac {4}{3} \log_ {3} 2 - \frac {1}{3} \log_ {3} 2\right) = 2 \log_ {2} 3 \times \log_ {3} 2 = 2.
> > $$
