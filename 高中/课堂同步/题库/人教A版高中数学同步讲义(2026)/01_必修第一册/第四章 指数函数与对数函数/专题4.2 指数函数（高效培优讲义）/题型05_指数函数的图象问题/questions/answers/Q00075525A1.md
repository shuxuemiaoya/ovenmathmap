> [!faq]- 《专题4.2 指数函数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/6a99eee42ef9e65f3584ad50ffef0ec90080fd8474a5b79a6e63a715da7feedf.jpg) B ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/4eb7ad4323781c15b1125e6176000b40b093bd182e1a678b7f0dafef30b3742e.jpg) C. ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/ab43ba43f46483f08d19644300675ff30f60af694431e2a023d39ee8e2aee3b4.jpg) D ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/0675be65d7600273c6a3f638bce40c14b6cbd6da0fd9e2acfc7d2b723cdef065.jpg)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $f(-x)=\frac{-2x}{e^{-x}+e^{x}}=-\frac{2x}{e^{x}+e^{-x}}=-f(x)$ ，且定义域为 R，故 $f(x)$ 为奇函数，排除 B、D；
> >
> > $x \to +\infty$ 时， $2x, e^x + e^{-x}$ 都趋向于 $+\infty$ ，且 $e^x + e^{-x}$ 增长快于 $2x$ ，所以 $f(x)$ 趋向于0，排除C.
> >
> > 故选 **A**
