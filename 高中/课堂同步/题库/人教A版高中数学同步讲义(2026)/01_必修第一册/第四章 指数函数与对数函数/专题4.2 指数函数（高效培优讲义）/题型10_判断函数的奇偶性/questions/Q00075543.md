C. $f_{3}(x)=\frac{1}{2^{x}-1}+\frac{1}{2}$

D. $f_{4}(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}x + 1, & x <   - 1\\ 0, & -1\leq x\leq 1\\ -x + 1,x > 1 \end{array} \right.$
