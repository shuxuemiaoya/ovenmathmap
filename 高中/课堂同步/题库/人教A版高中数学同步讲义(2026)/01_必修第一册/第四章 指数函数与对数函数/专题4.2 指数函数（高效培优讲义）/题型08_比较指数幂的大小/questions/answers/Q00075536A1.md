> [!faq]- 《专题4.2 指数函数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ b > a > 0 ，∴ $a^{b} > 0$
> >
> > ∴则函数 $f(x)=\frac{-a^{b}}{x}(a,b)$ 为常数，且 $b>a>0)$ 在 $(0,+\infty)$ 单调递增，
> >
> > 又∵函数的定义域为 $\begin{bmatrix}a,&b\end{bmatrix}$
> >
> > ∴函数的值域为 $\left[f(a),f(b)\right]$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} f (a) = - a ^ {b - 1} = 2 a - \frac {5}{2} \\ f (b) = - \frac {a ^ {b}}{b} = 2 b - \frac {5}{2} \end{array} \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} - a ^ {b} = 2 a ^ {2} - \frac {5}{2} a \\ - a ^ {b} = 2 b ^ {2} - \frac {5}{2} b \end{array} \Rightarrow b ^ {2} - \frac {5}{4} b = a ^ {2} - \frac {5}{4} a, \right. \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a + b = \frac {5}{4}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
