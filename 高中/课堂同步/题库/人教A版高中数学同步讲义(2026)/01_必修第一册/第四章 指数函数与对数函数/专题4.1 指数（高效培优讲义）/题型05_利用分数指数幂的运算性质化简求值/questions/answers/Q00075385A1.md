> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{9}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \frac {a ^ {\frac {2}{3}} + 3 a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} b ^ {\frac {1}{3}} + \left(3 \sqrt [ 3 ]{b}\right) ^ {2}}{a ^ {\frac {4}{3}} - 2 7 a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} b} \div \frac {a ^ {\frac {1}{3}}}{\sqrt [ 3 ]{a} - 3 \sqrt [ 3 ]{b}} = \frac {\left(a ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {2} + 3 a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} b ^ {\frac {1}{3}} + \left(3 b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {2}}{a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} \left[ \left(a ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {3} - \left(3 b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {3} \right]} \times \frac {a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} - 3 b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}}{a ^ {\frac {1}{3}}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\left(a ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {2} + 3 a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} b ^ {\frac {1}{3}} + \left(3 b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {2}}{a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} \left(a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} - 3 b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) \left[ \left(a ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {2} + 3 a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} b ^ {\frac {1}{3}} + \left(3 b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {2} \right]} \times \frac {a ^ {\frac {1}{3}} - 3 b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}}{a ^ {\frac {1}{3}}} = a ^ {- \frac {2}{3}}
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $a = -\frac{8}{27}$ ，所以原式 $\left(-\frac{8}{27}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}} = \left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)^{3\times \left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)} = \frac{9}{4}$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{9}{4}$
> >
> > ## 方法技巧
> >
> > 根式的化简结果应写为最简根式．（1）被开方数的指数与根指数互质；（2）被开方数分母为1，且不含非
> >
> > 正整数指数幂；（3）被开方数的每个因数的指数小于根指数。
