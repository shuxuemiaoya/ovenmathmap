> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{167}{9}$ ; (2) $\frac{15}{4}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）解：原式 $= (-1)^{-\frac{2}{3}} \times (3\frac{3}{8})^{-\frac{2}{3}} + (\frac{1}{500})^{-\frac{1}{2}} - \frac{10}{\sqrt{5} - 2} + 1$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(\frac {2 7}{8}\right) ^ {- \frac {2}{3}} + (5 0 0) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} - 1 0 \times (\sqrt {5} + 2) + 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {4}{9} + 1 0 \sqrt {5} - 1 0 \sqrt {5} - 2 0 + 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1 6 7}{9}.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 解：原式 $=\log_{3}3^{-\frac{1}{4}}+\lg100+1=-\frac{1}{4}+2+2=\frac{15}{4}$
