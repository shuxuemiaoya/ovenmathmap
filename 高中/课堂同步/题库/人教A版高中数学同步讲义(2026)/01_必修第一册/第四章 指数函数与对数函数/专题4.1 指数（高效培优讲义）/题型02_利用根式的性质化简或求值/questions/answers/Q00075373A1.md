> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $m = a + \sqrt{a^2 + 1}$ ， $n = b + \sqrt{b^2 + 1}$ $\therefore \frac{1}{m} = \frac{1}{a + \sqrt{a^2 + 1}} = \sqrt{a^2 + 1} - a$ ， $\frac{1}{n} = \frac{1}{b + \sqrt{b^2 + 1}} = \sqrt{b^2 + 1} - b$ $\therefore m - \frac{1}{m} = (a + \sqrt{a^2 + 1}) - (\sqrt{a^2 + 1} - a) = 2a$ $n - \frac{1}{n} = (b + \sqrt{b^2 + 1}) - (\sqrt{b^2 + 1} - b) = 2b$ $\therefore a = \frac{m - \frac{1}{m}}{2}, b = \frac{n - \frac{1}{n}}{2}$ 又 $\because m \cdot n = 1$ ， $\therefore n = \frac{1}{m}$ ， $m = \frac{1}{n}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a = \frac {m - n}{2}, b = \frac {n - m}{2} \quad \therefore a + b = \frac {m - n}{2} + \frac {n - m}{2} = 0.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D
> >
> > ## 方法技巧
> >
> > 此类问题应熟练应用 $a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m} (a > 0, m, n \in N^*$ , 且 $n > 1)$ 。当所求根式含有多重根号时，要搞清被开方数，
> >
> > 由里向外或由外向里，用分数指数幂写出，然后再用性质进行化简。
