> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{16}{15}$ (2)100 (3) $-\frac{a}{3c}$ (4) $\frac{3}{2}a^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{\frac{4}{3}}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）原式 $= 1 + \frac{1}{4}\times \left(\frac{4}{9}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} - \left(\frac{1}{100}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = 1 + \frac{1}{6} -\frac{1}{10} = \frac{16}{15}$
> >
> > (2) 原式 $= \left(\frac{25}{9}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} + \left(\frac{1}{10}\right)^{-2} + \left(\frac{64}{27}\right)^{-\frac{2}{3}} - 3 + \frac{37}{48}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {5}{3} + 1 0 0 + \frac {9}{1 6} - 3 + \frac {3 7}{4 8} = 1 0 0
> > $$
> >
> > (3) 原式 $= -4a^{-2 - 1}b^{-3 + 1}\div (12a^{-4}b^{-2}c)$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{3} a ^ {- 3 - (- 4)} b ^ {- 2 - (- 2)} c ^ {- 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{3} a c ^ {- 1} = - \frac {a}{3 c};
> > $$
> >
> > (4) 原式 $=2a^{\frac{2}{3}}\div4\left(a^{\frac{1}{6}}b^{\frac{1}{6}}\right)\left(3b^{\frac{3}{2}}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} a ^ {\frac {2}{3} - \frac {1}{6}} \cdot b ^ {- \frac {1}{6}} \cdot \left(3 b ^ {\frac {3}{2}}\right) = \frac {3}{2} a ^ {\frac {1}{2}} b ^ {\frac {4}{3}}
> > $$
