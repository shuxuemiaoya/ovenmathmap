> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $2 \sqrt{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：由题意，得 $a + b = 5, ab = 5$ 。又 $(a - b)^2 = (a + b)^2 - 4ab = 5^2 - 4 \times 5 = 5$ ，而 $a > b$ ，所以 $a - b = \sqrt{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\sqrt {a} - \sqrt {b}}{\sqrt {a} + \sqrt {b}} + \frac {\sqrt {a} + \sqrt {b}}{\sqrt {a} - \sqrt {b}} = \frac {(\sqrt {a} - \sqrt {b}) ^ {2} + (\sqrt {a} + \sqrt {b}) ^ {2}}{(\sqrt {a} + \sqrt {b}) (\sqrt {a} - \sqrt {b})} = \frac {a - 2 \sqrt {a b} + b + a + 2 \sqrt {a b} + b}{a - b} = \frac {2 (a + b)}{\sqrt {5}} = 2 \sqrt {5}
> > $$
