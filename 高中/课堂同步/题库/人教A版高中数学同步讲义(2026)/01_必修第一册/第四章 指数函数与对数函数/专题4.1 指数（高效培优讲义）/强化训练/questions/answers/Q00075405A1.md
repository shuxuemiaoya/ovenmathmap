> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a^{\frac{11}{3}}$ (2) $a^{\frac{1}{4}}b^{-\frac{3}{4}}$ (3) $a^{-\frac{11}{6}}b^{\frac{4}{3}}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $a^{3}\times\sqrt[3]{a^{2}}=a^{3}\times a^{\frac{2}{3}}=a^{3+\frac{2}{3}}=a^{\frac{11}{3}}$
> >
> > (2) 因为 a > 0, b > 0
> >
> > 所以 $\sqrt{\sqrt{\frac{b^3}{a}}\cdot\sqrt{\frac{a^2}{b^6}}}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {\left(a ^ {- 1} b ^ {3}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} \cdot \left(a ^ {2} b ^ {- 6}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {\left(a ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} b ^ {\frac {3}{2}}\right) \left(a b ^ {- 3}\right)}
> > $$
> >
> > $= \sqrt{a^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{-\frac{3}{2}}} = \left(a^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{-\frac{3}{2}}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$
> >
> > $= a^{\frac{1}{4}}b^{-\frac{3}{4}}$
> >
> > (3) 因为 a > 0, b > 0,
> >
> > 所以 $\sqrt{a^{-4}b^2\sqrt[3]{ab^2}} = \sqrt{a^{-4}b^2a^{\frac{1}{3}}b^{\frac{2}{3}}}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {a ^ {- \frac {1 1}{3}} \cdot b ^ {\frac {8}{3}}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(a ^ {- \frac {1 1}{3}} \cdot b ^ {\frac {8}{3}}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} = a ^ {- \frac {1 1}{6}} b ^ {\frac {4}{3}}
> > $$
