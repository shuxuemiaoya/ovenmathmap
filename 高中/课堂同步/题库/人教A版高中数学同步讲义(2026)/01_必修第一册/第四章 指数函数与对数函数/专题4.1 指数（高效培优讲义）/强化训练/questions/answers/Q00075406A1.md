> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)±2 (2) $10\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $x + \frac{1}{x} = 2\sqrt{2}$ 所以 $\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 = x^2 + 2x \cdot \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x^2} = x^2 + 2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = 8$
> >
> > 即 $x^{2}+2+\frac{1}{x^{2}}=8,\quad x^{2}+\frac{1}{x^{2}}=8-2=6$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(x - \frac {1}{x}\right) ^ {2} = x ^ {2} - 2 x \cdot \frac {1}{x} + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}} = x ^ {2} - 2 + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}.
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $x^{2} + \frac{1}{x^{2}} = 6$ ，所以 $\left(x - \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 = 6 - 2 = 4$ ，则 $x - \frac{1}{x} = \pm \sqrt{4} = \pm 2.$
> >
> > $$
> > x ^ {3} + \frac {1}{x ^ {3}} = \left(x + \frac {1}{x}\right) \left(x ^ {2} - x \cdot \frac {1}{x} + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}\right) = \left(x + \frac {1}{x}\right) \left(x ^ {2} - 1 + \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}\right).
> > $$
> >
> > 已知 $x + \frac{1}{x} = 2\sqrt{2}, x^{2} + \frac{1}{x^{2}} = 6$ ，所以 $x^{3} + \frac{1}{x^{3}} = 2\sqrt{2} \times (6 - 1) = 2\sqrt{2} \times 5 = 10\sqrt{2}$
