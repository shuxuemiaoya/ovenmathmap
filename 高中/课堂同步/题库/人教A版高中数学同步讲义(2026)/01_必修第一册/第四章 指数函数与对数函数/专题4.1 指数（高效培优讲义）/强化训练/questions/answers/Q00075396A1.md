> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $a + b = 1$ B. $ab = 1$ C. $a - b = 1$ D. $a = b$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为幂函数 $f(x) = x^{a}, g(x) = x^{b} (a, b \in \mathbf{R})$ 的图象分别经过 $M\left(\frac{1}{3}, \frac{2}{3}\right), N\left(\frac{2}{3}, \frac{1}{3}\right)$ 两点，所以把 $M\left(\frac{1}{3}, \frac{2}{3}\right), N\left(\frac{2}{3}, \frac{1}{3}\right)$ 两点分别代入 $f(x) = x^{a}, g(x) = x^{b}$ 可得 $\frac{2}{3} = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{a}, \frac{1}{3} = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{b}$ ，故 $\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{ab} = \left[\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{a}\right]^{b} = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{b} = \frac{1}{3}$ ，故 $ab = 1$ 。
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
