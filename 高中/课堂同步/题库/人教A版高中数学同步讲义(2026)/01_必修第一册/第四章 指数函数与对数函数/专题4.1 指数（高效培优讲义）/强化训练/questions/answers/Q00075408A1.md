> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 指数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(1)^{-10y}$ ; $(2)^{8}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1）原式 $= \frac{3x^{-\frac{3}{4}}y^{\frac{1}{2}}}{-\frac{3}{10}x^{-\frac{3}{4}}y^{-\frac{1}{2}}} = -10y$
> >
> > (2) 令 $a^{\frac{1}{3}} = A, b^{\frac{1}{3}} = B$ ，则 $x = A^3 + 3AB^2, y = B^3 + 3A^2B$
> >
> > $$
> > x + y = A ^ {3} + 3 A B ^ {2} + 3 A ^ {2} B + B ^ {3} = (A + B) ^ {3}, x - y = A ^ {3} + 3 A B ^ {2} - 3 A ^ {2} B - B ^ {3} = (A - B) ^ {3}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $(x + y)^{\frac{2}{3}} + (x - y)^{\frac{2}{3}} = (A + B)^2 + (A - B)^2 = 2(A^2 + B^2) = 2(a^{\frac{2}{3}} + b^{\frac{2}{3}}) = 8$
