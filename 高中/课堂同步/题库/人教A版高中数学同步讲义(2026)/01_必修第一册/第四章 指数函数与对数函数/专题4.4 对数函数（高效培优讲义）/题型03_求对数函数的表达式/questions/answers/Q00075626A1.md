> [!faq]- 《专题4.4 对数函数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $F(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned}x^{-\frac{1}{2}},&0<x\leq4\\ \log_{16}x,&x>4\end{aligned}\right.$ (2) $\left(\frac{1}{3},\frac{3}{2}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）依题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 4^{a} = \frac{1}{2} \\ \log_{b}4 = \frac{1}{2} \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = -\frac{1}{2} \\ b = 16 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以 $F(x) = \begin{cases} x^{-\frac{1}{2}}, & 0 < x \leq 4 \\ \log_{16} x, & x > 4 \end{cases}$
> >
> > (2) 由 $\left(m + 2\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} > \left(3 - 2m\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$ 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll} m + 2 & 0 \\ 3 - 2m & 0 \\ m + 2 > 3 - 2m \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 解得 $\frac{1}{3} < m \leq \frac{3}{2}$ ， $\therefore m$ 取值范围为 $\left(\frac{1}{3}, \frac{3}{2}\right]$
