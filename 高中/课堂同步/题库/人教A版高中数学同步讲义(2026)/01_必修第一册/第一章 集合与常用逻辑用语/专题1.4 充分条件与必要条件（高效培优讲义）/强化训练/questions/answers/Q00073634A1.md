> [!faq]- 《专题1.4 充分条件与必要条件（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A.-2 B.-1 C.1 D.2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 1 + a + b = 0 \\ \Delta = a^2 - 4b = 0 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = -2 \\ b = 1 \end{array} \right.$ ，所以 $ab = -2$
