> [!faq]- 《第一章 集合与常用逻辑用语（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意，要使集合含有“孤立元”，则集合中的元素不是3个一致连续的整数即可，
> > 故满足条件的集合有： $\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,2,4\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,2,5\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,2,6\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,3,4\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,3,5\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,3,6\end{aligned}\right\}$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,4,5\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,4,6\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&1,5,6\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&2,3,5\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&2,3,6\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&2,4,5\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&2,4,6\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&2,5,6\end{aligned}\right\}$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}&3,4,6\end{aligned}\right\},\left\{\begin{aligned}&3,5,6\end{aligned}\right\}$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
