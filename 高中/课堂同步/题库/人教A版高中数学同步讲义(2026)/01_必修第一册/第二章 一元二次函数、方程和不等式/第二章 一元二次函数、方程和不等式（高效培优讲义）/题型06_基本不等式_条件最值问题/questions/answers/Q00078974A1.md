> [!faq]- 《第二章 一元二次函数、方程和不等式（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $9\sqrt{11}-32$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $xy + 2z = 1$ ，得 $z = \frac{1 - xy}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $5 = x^{2} + y^{2} + \left(\frac{1 - xy}{2}\right)^{2}$ $2|xy| + \left(\frac{1 - xy}{2}\right)^{2}$ ,
> >
> > 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}xy & 0\\ (xy)^2 +6xy - 19\leq 0 \end{array} \right.$ 或 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}xy <   0\\ (xy)^2 -10xy - 19\leq 0 \end{array} \right.,$
> >
> > 解得 $0 \leq xy \leq -3 + 2\sqrt{7}$ 或 $5 - 2\sqrt{11} \leq xy < 0$
> >
> > 所以 $xyz = xy \cdot \frac{1 - xy}{2} = -\frac{1}{2}\left(xy - \frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} + \frac{1}{8}$ .
> >
> > 综上，当 $xy = 5 - 2\sqrt{11}$ 时，xyz取得最小值 $9\sqrt{11} -32$
