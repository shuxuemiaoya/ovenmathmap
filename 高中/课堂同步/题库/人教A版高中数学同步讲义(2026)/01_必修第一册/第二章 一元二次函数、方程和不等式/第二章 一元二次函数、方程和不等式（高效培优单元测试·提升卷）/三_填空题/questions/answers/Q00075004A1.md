> [!faq]- 《第二章 一元二次函数、方程和不等式（高效培优单元测试·提升卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)1 (2) $\frac{27}{4}$ (3) $2\sqrt{10}+2\sqrt{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）∵ $2x^{2}+5xy+2y^{2}=2x+y$ ，∴ $(2x+y)(x+2y)=2x+y$
> >
> > $\because x, y$ 为正数， $\therefore 2x + y > 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore x + 2 y = 1
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $\because x + 2y = 1, \therefore 3x + 1 + 6y = 4$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {3}{3 x + 1} + \frac {2}{y} = \frac {3}{3 x + 1} + \frac {1 2}{6 y} = \frac {1}{4} (3 x + 1 + 6 y) \left(\frac {3}{3 x + 1} + \frac {1 2}{6 y}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \left[ 1 5 + \frac {1 8 y}{3 x + 1} + \frac {1 2 (3 x + 1)}{6 y} \right] \quad \frac {1}{4} \left[ 1 5 + 2 \sqrt {\frac {1 8 y}{3 x + 1} \cdot \frac {1 2 (3 x + 1)}{6 y}} \right] = \frac {2 7}{4},
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{18y}{3x+1}&=\frac{12(3x+1)}{6y}\\ x+2y&=1\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=\frac{1}{9}\\ y&=\frac{4}{9}\end{aligned}\right.$ 时等号成立，
> >
> > 故 $\frac{3}{3x + 1} +\frac{2}{y}$ 的最小值为 $\frac{27}{4}$
> >
> > (3) $\because x + 2y = 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {y z}{x} + \frac {z}{x y} + \frac {\sqrt {5}}{z - 1} - 4 z = z \left(\frac {y}{x} + \frac {1}{x y} - 4\right) + \frac {\sqrt {5}}{z - 1} = z \left[ \frac {y}{x} + \frac {(x + 2 y) ^ {2}}{x y} - 4 \right] + \frac {\sqrt {5}}{z - 1} = z \left(\frac {x}{y} + \frac {5 y}{x}\right) + \frac {\sqrt {5}}{z - 1} \quad z \cdot 2 \sqrt {5} + \frac {\sqrt {5}}{z - 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \sqrt {5} \cdot (z - 1) + \frac {\sqrt {5}}{z - 1} + 2 \sqrt {5} \quad 2 \sqrt {2 \sqrt {5} \cdot (z - 1) \cdot \frac {\sqrt {5}}{z - 1}} + 2 \sqrt {5} = 2 \sqrt {1 0} + 2 \sqrt {5}
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x+2y=1\\ \frac{x}{y}&=\frac{5y}{x}\\ 2\sqrt{5}\cdot(z-1)&=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{z-1}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=5-2\sqrt{5}\\ y&=\sqrt{5}-2\\ z&=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}+1\end{aligned}\right.$ 时等号成立，
> >
> > 故 $\frac{yz}{x} +\frac{z}{xy} +\frac{\sqrt{5}}{z - 1} -4z$ 的最小值为 $2\sqrt{10} +2\sqrt{5}$
