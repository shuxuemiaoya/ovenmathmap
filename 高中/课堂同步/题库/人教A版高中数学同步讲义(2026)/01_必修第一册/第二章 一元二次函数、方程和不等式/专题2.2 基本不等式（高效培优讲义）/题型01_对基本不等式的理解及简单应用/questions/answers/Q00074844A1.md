> [!faq]- 《专题2.2 基本不等式（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a > b > c, n \in \mathbb{N}^*$ ，则 $a - c > 0, a - b > 0, b - c > 0$ ，又 $\frac{1}{a - b} + \frac{1}{b - c} = \frac{n}{a - c}$ 恒成立，
> >
> > 即 $n \leq \frac{a - c}{a - b} + \frac{a - c}{b - c}$ 恒成立，
> >
> > $$
> > \text {又} \frac {a - c}{a - b} + \frac {a - c}{b - c} = \frac {a - b + b - c}{a - b} + \frac {a - b + b - c}{b - c} = 2 + \frac {b - c}{a - b} + \frac {a - b}{b - c} \quad 2 + 2 \sqrt {\frac {b - c}{a - b} \times \frac {a - b}{b - c}} = 4
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{b - c}{a - b} = \frac{a - b}{b - c}$ ，即 $2b = a + c$ 时取等号，所以 $n\leq 4$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
