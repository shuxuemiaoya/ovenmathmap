> [!faq]- 《第二章 一元二次函数、方程和不等式（高效培优单元测试·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 不等式 $ax^{2} + 4x + b$ 0 对于一切实数 x 恒成立，且 a > 1
> >
> > $\therefore \Delta = 16 - 4ab\leq 0\quad \therefore ab\quad 4$
> >
> > $\therefore \exists x_{0} \in R$ ，使 $ax_{0}^{2} + 4x_{0} + b = 0$ 成立，
> >
> > $\therefore \Delta = 0 \quad \therefore ab = 4$
> >
> > $\frac{4a + b - 4}{b(a - 1)} = \frac{4(a - 1) + b}{b(a - 1)} = \frac{4}{b} + \frac{1}{a - 1} = a + \frac{1}{a - 1} = (a - 1) + \frac{1}{a - 1} + 1 \quad 2\sqrt{(a - 1) \cdot \frac{1}{a - 1}} + 1 = 3$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $a - 1 = \frac{1}{a - 1}$ 即 $a = b = 2$ 时等号成立
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
