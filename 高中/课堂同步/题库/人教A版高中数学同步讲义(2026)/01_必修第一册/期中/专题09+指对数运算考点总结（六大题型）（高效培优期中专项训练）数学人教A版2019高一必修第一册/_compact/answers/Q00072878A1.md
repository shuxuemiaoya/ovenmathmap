> [!faq]- 《专题09+指对数运算考点总结（六大题型）（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(1)^{1}$ ; $(2)a^{-1}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用指数运算法则和根式运算法则计算即可·
> >
> > $$
> > \text {
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > } (1) \left(\frac {9}{4}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} - \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {2} + 6 ^ {- 2} \times \left(\frac {8}{2 7}\right) ^ {- \frac {2}{3}} = \left[ \left(\frac {3}{2}\right) ^ {2} \right] ^ {\frac {1}{2}} - \frac {9}{1 6} + \frac {1}{3 6} \times \left[ \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {3} \right] ^ {- \frac {2}{3}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {3}{2} - \frac {9}{1 6} + \frac {1}{3 6} \times \frac {9}{4} = \frac {3}{2} - \frac {9}{1 6} + \frac {1}{1 6} = 1;
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(^ {2}\right) \frac {\left(a ^ {\frac {2}{3}} \cdot b ^ {- 1}\right) ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \cdot a ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \cdot b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}}{\sqrt [ 6 ]{a \cdot b ^ {5}}} = \frac {a ^ {- \frac {1}{3}} \cdot b ^ {\frac {1}{2}} \cdot a ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \cdot b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}}{a ^ {\frac {1}{6}} \cdot b ^ {\frac {5}{6}}} = \frac {a ^ {- \frac {5}{6}} \cdot b ^ {\frac {5}{6}}}{a ^ {\frac {1}{6}} \cdot b ^ {\frac {5}{6}}} = a ^ {- 1}.
> > $$
