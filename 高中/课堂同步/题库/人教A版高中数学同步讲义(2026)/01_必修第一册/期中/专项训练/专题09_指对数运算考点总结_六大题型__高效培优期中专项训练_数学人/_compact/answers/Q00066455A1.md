> > 20. 计算下列各式：
> >
> > (1) $\left(2\frac{1}{4}\right)^{0.5} + \frac{(-3)^{0}}{\sqrt{2}} - 0.75^{2} + 2^{-\frac{1}{2}};$
> >
> > (2) $\sqrt{\frac{25}{4}} + \sqrt{24} \times \sqrt{\frac{1}{3}} - \sqrt[3]{3\frac{3}{8}} + \sqrt[4]{(-2)^2}$
> >
> > 【答案】(1) $\frac{15}{16}+\sqrt{2}$
> >
> > (2) $1+3\sqrt{2}$
> >
> > 【分析】利用分数指数幂和根式运算法则计算出结果·
> >
> > 【详解】（1） $\left(2\frac{1}{4}\right)^{0.5}+\frac{(-3)^{0}}{\sqrt{2}}-0.75^{2}+2^{-\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{9}{4}}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}-\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{2}+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ $=\frac{3}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}-\frac{9}{16}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}=\frac{15}{16}+\sqrt{2}$ ;
> >
> > (2) $\sqrt{\frac{25}{4}}+\sqrt{24}\times\sqrt{\frac{1}{3}}-\sqrt[3]{3\frac{3}{8}}+\sqrt[4]{(-2)^{2}}=\frac{5}{2}+\sqrt{8}-\sqrt[3]{\frac{27}{8}}+(2^{2})^{\frac{1}{4}}$ $=\frac{5}{2}+2\sqrt{2}-\frac{3}{2}+\sqrt{2}=1+3\sqrt{2}$
