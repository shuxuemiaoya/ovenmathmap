> [!faq]- 《专题09_指对数运算考点总结_六大题型__高效培优期中专项训练_数学人》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(1)^{2}$ ; $(2)\frac{16}{15}$ ; $(3)\frac{a}{b}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 将根式化为分数指数幂，根据分数指数幂的运算法则进行计算；
> >
> > $$
> > \text {
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > } (1) \left[ \left(\frac {3}{2}\right) ^ {\sqrt {3}} \right] ^ {- \frac {\sqrt {3}}{9}} \times \left(- 6 ^ {\sqrt {2}}\right) ^ {0} + 8 ^ {\frac {1}{4}} \times \sqrt [ 4 ]{2} - \sqrt {\left(- \frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {\frac {2}{3}}} = \left(\frac {3}{2}\right) ^ {- \frac {1}{3}} \times 1 + 2 ^ {\frac {3}{4}} \times 2 ^ {\frac {1}{4}} - \left[ \left(- \frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {\frac {2}{3}} \right] ^ {\frac {1}{2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{3}} + 2 ^ {\frac {3}{4} + \frac {1}{4}} - \left[ \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {\frac {2}{3}} \right] ^ {\frac {1}{2}} = \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{3}} + 2 - \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{3}} = 2;
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(^ {2}\right) \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {0} + 2 ^ {- 2} \times \left(2 \frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} - (0. 0 1) ^ {0. 5} = 1 + \frac {1}{4} \times \left(\frac {9}{4}\right) ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} - [ (0. 1) ^ {2} ] ^ {0. 5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 + \frac {1}{4} \times \left[ \left(\frac {3}{2}\right) ^ {2} \right] ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} - 0. 1 = 1 + \frac {1}{4} \times \frac {2}{3} - 0. 1 = \frac {9}{1 0} + \frac {1}{6} = \frac {2 7 + 5}{3 0} = \frac {1 6}{1 5};
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(^ {3}\right) \frac {\left(\sqrt [ 3 ]{a ^ {2} b}\right) ^ {2} \cdot \sqrt [ 3 ]{a}}{\sqrt [ 3 ]{b} \cdot \sqrt [ 3 ]{a ^ {2} b ^ {4}}} = \frac {\left(a ^ {\frac {2}{3}} b ^ {\frac {1}{3}}\right) ^ {2} \cdot a ^ {\frac {1}{3}}}{b ^ {\frac {1}{3}} \cdot a ^ {\frac {2}{3}} b ^ {\frac {4}{3}}} = \frac {a ^ {\frac {5}{3}} b ^ {\frac {2}{3}}}{a ^ {\frac {2}{3}} b ^ {\frac {5}{3}}} = \frac {a}{b}.
> > $$
