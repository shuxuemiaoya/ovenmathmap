$$
= \log_ {3} 4 - \log_ {3} \frac {3 2}{9} + \log_ {3} 8 + 5 ^ {\log_ {5} 9}
$$

$$
= \log_ {3} \frac {4 \times 8}{\frac {3 2}{9}} + 9 = \log_ {3} 9 + 9 = 2 + 9 = 1 1
$$

故答案为：11
