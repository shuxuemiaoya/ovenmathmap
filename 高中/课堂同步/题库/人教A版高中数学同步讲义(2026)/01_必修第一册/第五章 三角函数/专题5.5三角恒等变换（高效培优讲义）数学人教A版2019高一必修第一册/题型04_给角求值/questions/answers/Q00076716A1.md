> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\csc 10^{\circ} - \sqrt{3}\sec 10^{\circ} = \frac{1}{\sin 10^{\circ}} -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\cos 10^{\circ}} = \frac{\cos 10^{\circ} - \sqrt{3}\sin 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}\cos 10^{\circ}} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}\sin 10^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}\cos 10^{\circ}}$ $= -\frac{2\sin(10^{\circ} - 30^{\circ})}{\frac{1}{2}\sin 20^{\circ}} = \frac{4\sin 20^{\circ}}{\sin 20^{\circ}} = 4.$
> >
> > 故选：C.
