> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\overrightarrow{m}=\left(\sin\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right),1\right)$ ， $\overrightarrow{n}=\left(1,\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right)$
> >
> > 根据题中定义可得 $\overline{m} \oplus n = \sin \left( \alpha - \frac{\pi}{6} \right) \sin \left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \sin \left( \alpha - \frac{\pi}{6} \right) \sin \left[ \left( \alpha - \frac{\pi}{6} \right) + \frac{\pi}{2} \right]$ $= \sin \left( \alpha - \frac{\pi}{6} \right) \cos \left( \alpha - \frac{\pi}{6} \right) = \frac{1}{2} \sin \left( 2\alpha - \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{10}$ ，故 $\sin \left( 2\alpha - \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
