> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $0^{\circ} \leq \alpha \leq 180^{\circ}$ 满足 $\frac{\tan 39^{\circ} + \tan 81^{\circ} + \tan \alpha}{\tan 39^{\circ} \tan 81^{\circ}} = \sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan \alpha = \sqrt{3}\tan 39^{\circ}\tan 81^{\circ} - (\tan 39^{\circ} + \tan 81^{\circ})$
> >
> > 因为 $\tan 120^{\circ} = \tan (39^{\circ} + 81^{\circ}) = \frac{\tan 39^{\circ} + \tan 81^{\circ}}{1 - \tan 39^{\circ}\tan 81^{\circ}} = -\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 故 $\tan 39^{\circ} + \tan 81^{\circ} = -\sqrt{3}\left(1 - \tan 39^{\circ}\tan 81^{\circ}\right)$
> >
> > 故 $\tan \alpha = \sqrt{3} \tan 39^{\circ} \tan 81^{\circ} - (\tan 39^{\circ} + \tan 81^{\circ}) = \sqrt{3} \tan 39^{\circ} \tan 81^{\circ} + \sqrt{3} (1 - \tan 39^{\circ} \tan 81^{\circ}) = \sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 因此， $\alpha=60^{\circ}$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
