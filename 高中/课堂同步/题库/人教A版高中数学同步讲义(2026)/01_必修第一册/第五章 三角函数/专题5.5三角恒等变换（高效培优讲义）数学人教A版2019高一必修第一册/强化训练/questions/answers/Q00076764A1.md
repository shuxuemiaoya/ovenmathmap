> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{1}{3}$ ; $-\frac{3}{5}$ (2) $\frac{\pi}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $m \perp n$ ，所以 $-3\sin\alpha - \cos\alpha = 0$ ，即 $\tan\alpha = -\frac{1}{3}$ ;
> >
> > $$
> > \sin 2 \alpha = 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac {2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {2 \tan \alpha}{1 + \tan^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {2 \times \left(- \frac {1}{3}\right)}{1 + \frac {1}{9}} = - \frac {3}{5};
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 得 $\sin \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}, \cos \alpha = -\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \beta = \sin [ (\alpha + \beta) - \alpha ] = \sin (\alpha + \beta) \cos \alpha - \cos (\alpha + \beta) \sin \alpha
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)$ ， $\beta \in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\alpha +\beta \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$
> >
> > 因为 $\sin(\alpha+\beta)=-\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ ，所以 $\alpha+\beta\in\left(\pi,\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ ， $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=-\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin \beta = \sin (\alpha +\beta)\cos \alpha -\cos (\alpha +\beta)\sin \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\times \left(-\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}\right) - \left(-\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\right)\times \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\beta=\frac{\pi}{4}$ .
