> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $-\frac{\pi}{2} < \frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha < 0, 0 < \frac{3\pi}{4} + \beta < \pi$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) = - \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right)} = - \frac {3}{5}, \sin \left(\frac {3 \pi}{4} + \beta\right) = \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} \left(\frac {3 \pi}{4} + \beta\right)} = \frac {1 2}{1 3}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin(\alpha+\beta)=-\cos\left(\alpha+\beta+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=-\cos\left[\left(\frac{3\pi}{4}+\beta\right)-\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\alpha\right)\right]$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \left[ \cos \left(\frac {3 \pi}{4} + \beta\right) \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) + \sin \left(\frac {3 \pi}{4} + \beta\right) \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \left[ \left(- \frac {5}{1 3}\right) \times \frac {4}{5} + \frac {1 2}{1 3} \times \left(- \frac {3}{5}\right) \right] = \frac {5 6}{6 5}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **B**
