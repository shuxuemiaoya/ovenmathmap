> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{3}{5}$ (2) $\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{10} - \frac{2}{5}$ (3) $\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因 $\alpha$ 是锐角，且 $\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{4}{5} < 0$
> >
> > 所以 $\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}$ 是钝角，且 $\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right)} = \frac{3}{5}.$
> >
> > (2) $\cos \alpha = \cos \left[ \left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) - \frac{\pi}{3} \right] = \cos \left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) \cos \frac{\pi}{3} + \sin \left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) \sin \frac{\pi}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(- \frac {4}{5}\right) \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{1 0} - \frac {2}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > (3)
> >
> > $$
> > \tan \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) = \frac {\sin \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)}{\cos \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)} = \frac {2 \sin^ {2} \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)}{2 \sin \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) \cos \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)} = \frac {1 - \cos \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{6}\right)}{\sin \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{6}\right)} = \frac {1 - \cos \left[ \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) - \frac {\pi}{2} \right]}{\sin \left[ \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) - \frac {\pi}{2} \right]} = \frac {1 - \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right)}{- \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right)} = \frac {1 - \frac {3}{5}}{\frac {4}{5}} = \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
