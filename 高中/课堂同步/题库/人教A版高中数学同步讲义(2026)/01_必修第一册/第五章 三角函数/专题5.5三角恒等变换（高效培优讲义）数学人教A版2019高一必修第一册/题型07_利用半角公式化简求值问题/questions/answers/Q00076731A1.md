> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{17\sqrt{2}}{50}$ (2) $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = -2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)=-\cos\alpha=\frac{3}{5}$ ，所以 $\cos\alpha=-\frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > 因为 $\alpha \in \left(\pi, \frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\sin \alpha = -\sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \alpha} = -\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2} = -\frac{4}{5}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \sin 2 \alpha = 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 2 \times \left(- \frac {3}{5}\right) \times \left(- \frac {4}{5}\right) = \frac {2 4}{2 5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos 2 \alpha = 2 \cos^ {2} \alpha - 1 = 2 \times \left(- \frac {3}{5}\right) ^ {2} - 1 = - \frac {7}{2 5}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin2\alpha+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cos2\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\times\frac{24}{25}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\times\left(-\frac{7}{25}\right)=\frac{17\sqrt{2}}{50}$
> >
> > (2) 解：方法一：因为 $\alpha\in\left(\pi,\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\frac{\alpha}{2}\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ ，则 $\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}>0,\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}<0$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} = \sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos\alpha}{2}} = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5},\cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = -\sqrt{\frac{1 + \cos\alpha}{2}} = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> >
> > 则 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}}{-\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}} = -2$
> >
> > 方法二： $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha + 1} = \frac{-\frac{4}{5}}{-\frac{3}{5} + 1} = -2$
> >
> > 方法三： $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2} - \sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{2\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}}{1 - \tan^2\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{4}{3}$ ，解得 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$ 或-2
> >
> > 因为 $\alpha \in \left(\pi, \frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\frac{\alpha}{2} \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ ，则 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} < 0$ ，故 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = -2$
