> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $f(x)=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\left(x\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right)$ $$ f (x) _ {\max} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{6}, x = \frac {\pi}{6}; \tag {2} $$ (3) $\left[\frac{\pi}{24},\frac{7\pi}{24}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）根据题意可知 $AD = BC = \sin x$ ， $OB = \cos x$ ， $OA = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} AD = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin x$
> >
> > 所以 $f(x)=AB\cdot AD=(OB-OA)\cdot AD=\left(\cos x-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin x\right)\cdot\sin x$
> >
> > 整理得 $f(x)=\cos x\sin x-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin^{2}x=\frac{1}{2}\sin 2x+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}(\cos 2x-1)=\frac{1}{2}\sin 2x+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}\cos 2x-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{6} \left(x \in \left(0, \frac {\pi}{3}\right)\right).
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{6} \left(x \in \left(0, \frac {\pi}{3}\right)\right).
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 知 $f(x) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6} \left(x \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right)$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left( \frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{5\pi}{6} \right)$ ，显然 $2x + \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{2}$ 时， $f(x)_{\max} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$ ，此时 $x = \frac{\pi}{6}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > (3) \text {由} f (x) = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{6} \quad \frac {\sqrt {6} - \sqrt {3}}{6}, \text {可得} \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \quad \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left(\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{5\pi}{6}\right)$ ，所以 $\frac{\pi}{4} \leq 2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \leq \frac{3\pi}{4}$ ，解得 $\frac{\pi}{24} \leq x \leq \frac{7\pi}{24}$
> >
> > 即不等式 $f(x) \frac{\sqrt{6}-\sqrt{3}}{6}$ 的解集为 $\left[\frac{\pi}{24},\frac{7\pi}{24}\right]$ .
