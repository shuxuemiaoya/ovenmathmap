> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $S_{\triangle AEF}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}n^{2}$ (2) $\sqrt{3}n^{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $DG = \sqrt{3}n$ ， $DE = \frac{1}{2}AD = n$ ，故 $\tan \angle DEG = \frac{DG}{DE} = \sqrt{3}$ ，故 $\angle DEG = 60^\circ$ 。
> >
> > 又 $\angle FEG = 60^{\circ}$ ，故 $\angle AEF = 60^{\circ}$ ， $AF = AE\tan \angle AEF = \sqrt{3} n$
> >
> > 所以 $S_{\triangle AEF} = \frac{1}{2} AE \cdot AF = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} n^{2}$ .
> >
> > (2) 设 $\angle GED = \alpha$ ，由题意 $30^{\circ} < \alpha < 90^{\circ}$ ，则 $DG = n\tan \alpha$ ， $\angle AEF = 120^{\circ} - \alpha$ ， $AF = n\tan (120^{\circ} - \alpha)$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\triangle A E F} = \frac {1}{2} A E \cdot A F = \frac {1}{2} n ^ {2} \tan (1 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha), S _ {\triangle A C G} = \frac {1}{2} D E \cdot D G = \frac {1}{2} n ^ {2} \tan \alpha ,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\text {梯形} A D G F} = \frac {1}{2} (A F + D G) A D = \frac {1}{2} \left[ n \tan \left(1 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha\right) + n \tan \alpha \right] 2 n
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = n ^ {2} \left[ \tan (1 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) + \tan \alpha \right],
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $S_{\triangle EFG} = S_{梯形ADGF} - S_{\triangle AEF} - S_{\triangle DEG} = \frac{1}{2} n^{2} \left[ \tan \left( 120^{\circ} - \alpha \right) + \tan \alpha \right]$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} n ^ {2} \frac {\sin (1 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) \cos \alpha + \cos (1 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha) \sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha \cos (1 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha)} = \frac {2 n ^ {2} \sin 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}}{2 \cos \alpha (- \cos \alpha + \sqrt {3} \sin \alpha)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {3} n ^ {2}}{\sqrt {3} \sin 2 \alpha - \cos 2 \alpha - 1} = \frac {\sqrt {3} n ^ {2}}{2 \sin (2 \alpha - 3 0 ^ {\circ}) - 1}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because 3 0 ^ {\circ} <   \alpha <   9 0 ^ {\circ} \quad \therefore 3 0 ^ {\circ} <   2 \alpha - 3 0 ^ {\circ} <   1 5 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {1}{2} <   \sin (2 \alpha - 3 0 ^ {\circ}) \leq 1, 0 <   2 \sin (2 \alpha - 3 0 ^ {\circ}) - 1 \leq 1,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {\sqrt {3} n ^ {2}}{2 \sin (2 a - 3 0 ^ {\circ}) - 1} \quad \sqrt {3} n ^ {2}, \text {当} _ {\alpha = 6 0 ^ {\circ}} \text {时取等号，}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \triangle EFG_{的面积的最小值} \sqrt{3}n^{2}.$
