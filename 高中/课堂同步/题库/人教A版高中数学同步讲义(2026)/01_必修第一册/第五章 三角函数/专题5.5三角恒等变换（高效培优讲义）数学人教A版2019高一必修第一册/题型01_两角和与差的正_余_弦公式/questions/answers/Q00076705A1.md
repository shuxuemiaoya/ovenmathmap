> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} -\alpha\right) = \sin \frac{\pi}{4}\cos \alpha -\cos \frac{\pi}{4}\sin \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\bigl (\cos \alpha -\sin \alpha\bigr) = \frac{5}{13}$
> >
> > 则 $\cos \alpha -\sin \alpha = \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{13}$
> >
> > 则 $\left(\cos \alpha -\sin \alpha\right)^2 = 1 - 2\cos \alpha \sin \alpha = \frac{50}{169}$ ，即 $2\cos \alpha \sin \alpha = \frac{119}{169}$
> >
> > 则 $\left(\cos \alpha +\sin \alpha\right)^2 = 1 + 2\cos \alpha \sin \alpha = 1 + \frac{119}{169} = \frac{288}{169}$
> >
> > 又因为 $0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{4}$ ，所以 $\sin \alpha > 0, \cos \alpha > 0$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha=\frac{12\sqrt{2}}{13}$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{\cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)} = \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos \frac {\pi}{4} \cos \alpha - \sin \frac {\pi}{4} \sin \alpha} = \frac {(\cos \alpha + \sin \alpha) (\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha)}{\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} (\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {2} (\cos \alpha + \sin \alpha) = \sqrt {2} \times \frac {1 2 \sqrt {2}}{1 3} = \frac {2 4}{1 3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
