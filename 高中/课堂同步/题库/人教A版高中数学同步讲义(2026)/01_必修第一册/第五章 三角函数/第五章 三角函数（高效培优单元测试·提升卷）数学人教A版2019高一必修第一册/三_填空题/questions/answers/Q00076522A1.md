> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优单元测试·提升卷）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2} / 0.5$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because f(x)$ 在 $\left(-\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ 上单调递增
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore x \in \left(- \frac {\pi}{3}, \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \Rightarrow \omega x - \frac {\pi}{6} \in \left(- \frac {\pi}{3} \omega - \frac {\pi}{6}, \frac {\pi}{6} \omega - \frac {\pi}{6}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} - \frac {\pi}{3} \omega - \frac {1}{6} \omega & - \frac {\pi}{2} \\ \frac {\pi}{6} \omega - \frac {\pi}{6} \leq \frac {\pi}{2} \end{array} \Rightarrow 0 <   \omega \leq 1 \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 又∵ $f(x)$ 关于点 $\left(\frac{\pi}{3},0\right)$ 对称
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{3} \omega - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = 0 \Rightarrow k = \frac {1}{3} \omega - \frac {1}{6} \Rightarrow k \leq \frac {1}{6}, k \in Z
> > $$
> >
> > ∴当k=0时， $\omega=\frac{1}{2}$ ， $f(x)=\sin\left(\omega x-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)(\omega>0)$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore f \left(\frac {2 \pi}{3}\right) = \sin \left(\frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2 \pi}{3} - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{1}{2}$
