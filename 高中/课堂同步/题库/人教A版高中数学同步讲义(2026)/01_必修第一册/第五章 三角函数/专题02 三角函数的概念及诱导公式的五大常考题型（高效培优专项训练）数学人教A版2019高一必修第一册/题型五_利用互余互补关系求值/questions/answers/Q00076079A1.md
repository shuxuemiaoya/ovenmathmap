> [!faq]- 《专题02 三角函数的概念及诱导公式的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{1}{3}$ $\frac{8}{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意，因为 $\sin (\alpha -\frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin(\alpha+\frac{3\pi}{4})=\sin[(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4})+\pi]=-\sin(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4})=-\frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) \cdot \cos \left(\alpha - \frac {9 \pi}{4}\right) = \cos \left[ - \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) \right] \cdot \cos \left[ \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) - 2 \pi \right] = \cos^ {2} \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = 1 - \sin^ {2} \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \frac {8}{9}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为：① $-\frac{1}{3}$ ，② $\frac{8}{9}$
