> [!faq]- 《专题02 三角函数的概念及诱导公式的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $0 < x < \frac{\pi}{2}, \therefore -\frac{\pi}{6} < \frac{\pi}{3} - x < \frac{\pi}{3}$
> >
> > $\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - x\right) = \frac{1}{3}, \therefore \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - x\right) = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} - x\right)} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{6} + x\right) - \cos \left(\frac {2 \pi}{3} + x\right) = \sin \left[ \frac {\pi}{2} - \left(\frac {\pi}{3} - x\right) \right] - \cos \left[ \pi - \left(\frac {\pi}{3} - x\right) \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{3} - x\right) + \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{3} - x\right) = 2 \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{3} - x\right) = \frac {4 \sqrt {2}}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **A**
