> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $f(2\pi - x) = \cos \frac{2\pi - x}{2} = \cos \left(\pi - \frac{x}{2}\right) = -\cos \frac{x}{2} = -f(x)$ ，①不成立，
> >
> > $f(2\pi + x) = \cos \frac{2\pi + x}{2} = \cos \left(\pi + \frac{x}{2}\right) = -\cos \frac{x}{2} = -f(x)$ ，②不成立，
> >
> > $f(-x) = \cos \left(-\frac{x}{2}\right) = \cos \frac{x}{2} = f(x)$ ，③不成立，④成立.
