> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 证明：左边 $= \frac{\cos\theta\sin(-\theta)\tan(-\theta)}{\sin\theta(-\cos\theta)} = \frac{\cos\theta\sin\theta\tan\theta}{-\sin\theta\cos\theta} = -\tan\theta =$ 右边
> >
> > 所以原等式成立
