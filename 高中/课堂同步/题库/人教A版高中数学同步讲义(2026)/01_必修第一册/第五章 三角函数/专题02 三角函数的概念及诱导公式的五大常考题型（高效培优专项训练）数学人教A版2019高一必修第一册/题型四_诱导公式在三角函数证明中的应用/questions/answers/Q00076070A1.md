> [!faq]- 《专题02 三角函数的概念及诱导公式的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 左边 $= \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} +\frac{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\alpha}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\alpha}{2}\right)} = \frac{\sin\alpha\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\alpha}{2}\right) + \cos\alpha\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\alpha}{2}\right)}{\cos\alpha\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\alpha}{2}\right)}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \frac {\alpha}{2} - \alpha\right)}{\cos \alpha \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)} = \frac {\cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)}{\cos \alpha \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)} = \frac {\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)}{\cos \alpha \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)} = \frac {1}{\cos \alpha} = \text {右边.}
> > $$
> >
> > 故原式得证.
