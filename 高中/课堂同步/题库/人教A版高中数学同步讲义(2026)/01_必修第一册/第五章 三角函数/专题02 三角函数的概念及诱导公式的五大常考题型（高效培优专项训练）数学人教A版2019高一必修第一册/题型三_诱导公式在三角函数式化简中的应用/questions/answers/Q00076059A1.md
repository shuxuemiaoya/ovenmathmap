> [!faq]- 《专题02 三角函数的概念及诱导公式的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(1)^{0}$ ; $(2)\frac{18}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）原式 $= \sin \frac{25\pi}{6} -\cos \frac{10\pi}{3} +\tan \left(-\frac{13\pi}{4}\right) = \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{6} +4\pi\right) - \cos \left(-\frac{2\pi}{3} +4\pi\right) + \tan \left(-\frac{\pi}{4} -3\pi\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sin \frac {\pi}{6} - \cos \frac {2 \pi}{3} - \tan \frac {\pi}{4} = \frac {1}{2} - \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) - 1 = 0
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 由题意得到 $\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{3}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $3 \sin^2 \alpha - 4 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha + 5 \cos^2 \alpha$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {3 \sin^ {2} \alpha - 4 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha + 5 \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {3 \tan^ {2} \alpha - 4 \tan \alpha + 5}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = \frac {1 8}{5}.
> > $$
