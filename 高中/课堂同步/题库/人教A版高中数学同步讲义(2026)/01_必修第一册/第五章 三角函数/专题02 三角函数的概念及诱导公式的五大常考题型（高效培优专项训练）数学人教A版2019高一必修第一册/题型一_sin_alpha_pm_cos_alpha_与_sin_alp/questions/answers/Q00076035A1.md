> [!faq]- 《专题02 三角函数的概念及诱导公式的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\frac{12}{5}$ B. $\frac{5}{12}$ C. $-\frac{12}{5}$ D. $-\frac{5}{12}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\cos A + \sin A = -\frac{7}{13}$
> >
> > $1 + 2\cos A\sin A = \left(-\frac{7}{13}\right)^{2}$ 可得 $2\sin A\cos A = -\frac{120}{169}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore (\cos A - \sin A) ^ {2} = 1 - 2 \sin A \cos A = \frac {2 8 9}{1 6 9}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos A - \sin A = \pm \frac {1 7}{1 3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $A$ 为第四象限角， $\therefore \cos A - \sin A = \frac{17}{13}$
> >
> > 又 $\cos A + \sin A = -\frac{7}{13}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos A=\frac{5}{13}$ ， $\sin A=-\frac{12}{13}$ .
> >
> > 所以 $\tan A = -\frac{12}{5}$ .
> >
> > 答案：C.
