> [!faq]- 《专题02 三角函数的概念及诱导公式的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)3； (2) $-\frac{8}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $\because \tan \alpha = 2$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha - \cos\alpha} = \frac{\tan\alpha + 1}{\tan\alpha - 1} = \frac{2 + 1}{2 - 1} = 3$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos^ {2} \alpha - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha - 1 = \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha + \sin^ {2} \alpha} - 1 = \frac {1 - 2 \tan \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} - 1 = \frac {1 - 4}{5} - 1 = - \frac {8}{5}.
> > $$
