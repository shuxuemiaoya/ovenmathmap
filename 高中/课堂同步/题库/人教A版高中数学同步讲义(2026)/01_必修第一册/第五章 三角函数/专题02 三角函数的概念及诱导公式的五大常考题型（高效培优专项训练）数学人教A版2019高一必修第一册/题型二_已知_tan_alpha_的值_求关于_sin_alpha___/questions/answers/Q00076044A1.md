> [!faq]- 《专题02 三角函数的概念及诱导公式的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{\sin^3\theta + \sin\theta}{\cos^3\theta + \sin\theta\cos^2\theta} = \frac{\sin^3\theta + \sin\theta (\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta)}{\cos^3\theta + \sin\theta\cos^2\theta}$ $= \frac{2\sin^3\theta + \sin\theta\cos^2\theta}{\cos^3\theta + \sin\theta\cos^2\theta}$ $= \frac{2\tan^3\theta + \tan\theta}{1 + \tan\theta}$ $= \frac{2\times \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 + \frac{1}{2}}{1 + \frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\frac{3}{4}}{\frac{3}{2}} = \frac{1}{2}.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
