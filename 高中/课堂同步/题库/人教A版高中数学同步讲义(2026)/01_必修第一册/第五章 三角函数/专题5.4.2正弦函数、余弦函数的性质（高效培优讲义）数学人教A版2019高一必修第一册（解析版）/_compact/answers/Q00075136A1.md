> [!faq]- 《专题5.4.2正弦函数、余弦函数的性质（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\left[2k\pi,2k\pi+\frac{2\pi}{3}\right](k\in\mathbf{Z})$ B. $\left[2k\pi+\pi,2k\pi+\frac{4\pi}{3}\right](k\in\mathbf{Z})$ C. $\left[2k\pi, 2k\pi + \frac{2\pi}{3}\right], \left[2k\pi + \pi, 2k\pi + \frac{4\pi}{3}\right] (k \in \mathbf{Z})$ D. 不存在
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $y = \sin^2 x - \cos x = -\cos^2 x - \cos x + 1$ 即 $y = -\left(\cos x + \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{5}{4}$
> >
> > 由此可知 $f(x) = \cos x$ 与 $y = -\left(\cos x + \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{5}{4}$ 同增同减即可得函数 $y = \sin^2 x - \cos x$ 的单调增区间，
> >
> > 则增区间满足 $\left\{\begin{aligned}-1&\leq\cos x\leq-\frac{1}{2}\\ -\pi+2k_{2}\pi&\leq x\leq2k_{2}\pi\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}-\frac{1}{2}&\leq\cos x\leq1\\ 2k_{4}\pi&\leq x\leq\pi+2k_{4}\pi,\quad k_{2},k_{4}\in Z\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{2\pi}{3}+2k_{1}\pi\leq x\leq\frac{4\pi}{3}+2k_{1}\pi\\ -\pi+2k_{2}\pi\leq x\leq2k_{2}\pi\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}-\frac{2\pi}{3}+2k_{3}\pi\leq x\leq\frac{2\pi}{3}+2k_{3}\pi\\ 2k_{4}\pi\leq x\leq\pi+2k_{4}\pi\end{aligned}\right.$ ， $k_{1},k_{2},k_{3},k_{4}\in Z$
> >
> > 解得 $x \in \left[2k\pi, 2k\pi + \frac{2\pi}{3}\right]$ 或 $x \in \left[2k\pi + \pi, 2k\pi + \frac{4\pi}{3}\right](k \in \mathbf{Z})$
> >
> > 所以函数的单调增区间为： $\left[2k\pi ,2k\pi +\frac{2\pi}{3}\right],\left[2k\pi +\pi ,2k\pi +\frac{4\pi}{3}\right](k\in \mathbf{Z})$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
