> [!faq]- 《专题5.7 三角函数的应用（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $\frac{1}{2}\left(t_1 + t_2\right) = 1, \frac{1}{2}\left(t_2 + t_3\right) = 3$
> >
> > 故函数的周期为 $T = 2 \times (3 - 1) = 4$
> >
> > 则 $\omega = \frac{2\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > s (t) = 3 \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} t + \varphi\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 位移的大小即 $\left|S(t)\right|$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {故令} \left| 3 \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} t + \varphi\right) \right| <   1. 5
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > - \frac {1}{2} <   \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} t + \varphi\right) <   \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {5 \pi}{6} + 2 k \pi <   \frac {\pi}{2} t + \varphi <   \frac {7 \pi}{6} + 2 k \pi , k \in Z,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1 1 \pi}{6} + 2 k \pi <   \frac {\pi}{2} t + \varphi <   \frac {1 3 \pi}{6} + 2 k \pi , k \in Z \text {或}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} 4 k + \frac {5}{3} - \frac {2}{\pi} \varphi <   t <   4 k + \frac {7}{3} - \frac {2}{\pi} \varphi , k \in \mathbb {Z}
> > $$
> >
> > 或者 $4k + \frac{11}{3} -\frac{2}{\pi}\varphi <  t <   4k + \frac{13}{3} -\frac{2}{\pi}\varphi ,k\in Z$
> >
> > 故总时间为：
> >
> > $$
> > \left[ \left(4 k + \frac {7}{3} - \frac {2}{\pi} \varphi\right) - \left(4 k + \frac {5}{3} - \frac {2}{\pi} \varphi\right) \right] + \left[ \left(4 k + \frac {1 3}{3} - \frac {2}{\pi} \varphi\right) - \left(4 k + \frac {1 1}{3} - \frac {2}{\pi} \varphi\right) \right] = \frac {4}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
