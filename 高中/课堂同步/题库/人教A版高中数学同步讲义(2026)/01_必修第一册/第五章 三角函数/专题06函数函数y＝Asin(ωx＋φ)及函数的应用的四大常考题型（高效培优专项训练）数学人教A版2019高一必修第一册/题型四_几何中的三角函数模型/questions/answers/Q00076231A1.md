> [!faq]- 《专题06函数函数y＝Asin(ωx＋φ)及函数的应用的四大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{64}{5} / 12.8$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由已知及对称性知 $GF = BF = l\cos \theta ,GE = BE = l\sin \theta .$ 又 $\angle GEA = \angle GFB = 2\theta$
> >
> > 所以 $AE = GE\cos 2\theta = l\sin \theta \cos 2\theta .$ 由 $AE + BE = l\sin \theta \cos 2\theta +l\sin \theta = 6,$
> >
> > $$
> > l = \frac {6}{\sin \theta (1 + \cos 2 \theta)} = \frac {6}{\sin \theta (2 - 2 \sin^ {2} \theta)} = \frac {6}{\frac {1}{4} \times \left[ 2 - 2 \times (\frac {1}{4}) ^ {2} \right]} = \frac {6 4}{5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{64}{5}$
