> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{5\sqrt{10}}{14}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：因为 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{3\pi}{2}, 2\pi\right)$ ，且 $\cos \alpha = \frac{3}{5}$ ，所以 $\sin \alpha = -\sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \alpha} = -\sqrt{1 - \frac{9}{25}} = -\frac{4}{5}$ ，
> >
> > 因为 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{3\pi}{2}, 2\pi\right)$ ，所以 $\frac{\alpha}{2} + \frac{\pi}{4} \in \left(\pi, \frac{5\pi}{4}\right)$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin \left(\frac{\alpha}{2} +\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = -\sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos 2\left(\frac{\alpha}{2} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right)}{2}} = -\sqrt{\frac{1 + \sin\alpha}{2}} = -\sqrt{\frac{1 - \frac{4}{5}}{2}} = -\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$
> >
> > 因为 $\cos2\alpha=2\cos^{2}\alpha-1=2\times\frac{9}{25}-1=-\frac{7}{25}$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\alpha}{2} + \frac{\pi}{4}\right)}{\cos 2\alpha} = \frac{-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}}{-\frac{7}{25}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10} \times \frac{25}{7} = \frac{5\sqrt{10}}{14}$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{5\sqrt{10}}{14}$
