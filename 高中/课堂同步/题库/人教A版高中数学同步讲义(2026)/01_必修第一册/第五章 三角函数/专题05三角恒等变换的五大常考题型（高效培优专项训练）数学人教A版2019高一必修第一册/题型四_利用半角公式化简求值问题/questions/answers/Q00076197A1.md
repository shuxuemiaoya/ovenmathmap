> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{7}{25}$ (2) $\frac{63}{16}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $\cos (\alpha -\beta) - \cos (\alpha +\beta) = \frac{8}{13}$ ，得 $\cos \alpha \cos \beta +\sin \alpha \sin \beta -\cos \alpha \cos \beta +\sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{8}{13}$
> >
> > 即 $\sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{4}{13}$
> >
> > 因为 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 - \cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{\sin\alpha}{1 + \cos\alpha}$ ，所以 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} +\frac{1}{\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{1 - \cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha} +\frac{1 + \cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{2}{\sin\alpha}$
> >
> > 又因为 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} +\frac{1}{\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{26}{5}$ ，所以 $\frac{26}{5} = \frac{2}{\sin\alpha}$ ，即 $\sin \alpha = \frac{5}{13}$ ，所以 $\sin \beta = \frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos2\beta=1-2\sin^{2}\beta=1-2\times(\frac{4}{5})^{2}=-\frac{7}{25}$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 知， $\sin\alpha=\frac{5}{13}$ ， $\sin\beta=\frac{4}{5}$ ，
> >
> > 又因为 $\alpha \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ， $\beta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2\alpha} = \sqrt{1 - (\frac{5}{13})^2} = \frac{12}{13}$ ， $\cos \beta = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2\beta} = \sqrt{1 - (\frac{4}{5})^2} = \frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{\frac{5}{13}}{\frac{12}{13}} = \frac{5}{12}$ ， $\tan \beta = \frac{\sin\beta}{\cos\beta} = \frac{\frac{4}{5}}{\frac{3}{5}} = \frac{4}{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan(\alpha+\beta)=\frac{\tan\alpha+\tan\beta}{1-\tan\alpha\tan\beta}=\frac{\frac{5}{12}+\frac{4}{3}}{1-\frac{5}{12}\times\frac{4}{3}}=\frac{63}{16}$
