> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** -3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\sin(\alpha-\beta)\cos\alpha-\cos(\alpha-\beta)\sin\alpha=\frac{3}{5}=\sin(\alpha-\beta-\alpha)=-\sin\beta,\therefore\sin\beta=-\frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > 结合 $\beta$ 为第三象限角，∴ $\cos\beta = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^{2}\beta} = -\frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 则 $\tan \frac{\beta}{2} = \frac{\sin\beta}{1 + \cos\beta} = \frac{-\frac{3}{5}}{1 - \frac{4}{5}} = -3,\tan \left(\frac{\beta}{2} -\pi\right) = \tan \frac{\beta}{2} = -3.$
> >
> > 故答案为：-3.
