> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $b > a > c$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $a = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left(\sin 56^{\circ} - \cos 56^{\circ}\right) = \sin \left(56^{\circ} - 45^{\circ}\right) = \sin 11^{\circ} = \cos 79^{\circ}$
> >
> > $$
> > b = \cos 5 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot \cos 1 2 8 ^ {\circ} + \cos 4 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot \cos 3 8 ^ {\circ} = - \cos 5 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot \cos 5 2 ^ {\circ} + \sin 5 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot \sin 5 2 ^ {\circ} = - \cos 1 0 2 ^ {\circ} = \cos 7 8 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $c = \cos 80^{\circ}$ ，由 $\cos 78^{\circ} > \cos 79^{\circ} > \cos 80^{\circ}$ ，即 $b > a > c$
> >
> > 故答案为：b > a > c。
