> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\sin \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{7}{12}$ ， $\cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{4}$ (2) $\frac{7}{3}$ (3) $\frac{2\sqrt{22}-5}{18}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sin (\alpha + \beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta + \cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{5}{6} \\ \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta - \cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{3} \end{array} \right.$ ，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sin \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{7}{12} \\ \cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{4} \end{array} \right.$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \left(\frac {2 (1 - \cos 2 \alpha) (1 + \cos 2 \beta)}{\sin 2 \alpha \sin 2 \beta} = \frac {1 - (1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \alpha)}{2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha} \cdot \frac {(1 + 2 \cos^ {2} \beta - 1)}{2 \sin \beta \cos \beta} = \frac {2 \sin^ {2} \alpha}{2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha} \cdot \frac {2 \cos^ {2} \beta}{2 \sin \beta \cos \beta} = \frac {\sin \alpha \cos \beta}{\cos \alpha \sin \beta} = \frac {\frac {7}{1 2}}{\frac {1}{4}} = \frac {7}{3} \right..
> > $$
> >
> > (3) 由 $\tan \beta < \tan \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，得 $0 < \beta < \frac{\pi}{6}$
> >
> > 由 $\frac{\tan\alpha}{\tan\beta} = \frac{\sin\alpha\cos\beta}{\cos\alpha\sin\beta} = \frac{7}{3}$ ，得 $\tan \alpha = \frac{7}{3}\tan \beta <  \frac{7\sqrt{3}}{9} <  \sqrt{3}$ ，得 $0 <   \alpha <  \frac{\pi}{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $0 < \alpha + \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ， $-\frac{\pi}{6} < \alpha - \beta < \frac{\pi}{3}$ ，
> >
> > 由 $\sin (\alpha +\beta) = \frac{5}{6}$ ， $\sin (\alpha -\beta) = \frac{1}{3}$ 得 $\cos (\alpha +\beta) = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2(\alpha + \beta)} = \frac{\sqrt{11}}{6}$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos (\alpha - \beta) = \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} (\alpha - \beta)} = \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos 2\alpha = \cos (\alpha +\beta +\alpha -\beta) = \cos (\alpha +\beta)\cos (\alpha -\beta) - \sin (\alpha +\beta)\sin (\alpha -\beta)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {1 1}}{6} \times \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3} - \frac {5}{6} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {2 \sqrt {2 2} - 5}{1 8}
> > $$
