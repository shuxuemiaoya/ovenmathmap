> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ 0, $\frac{\pi}{6}$ , $\pi$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题 $f(\alpha) = \sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{2}\sin \alpha \cos \alpha +\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos^2\alpha$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \sin 2 \alpha + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} (1 + \cos 2 \alpha) = \frac {1}{2} \sin \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $f\left(\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\sin \left(2\times \frac{\pi}{3} +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$
> >
> > 令 $f(\alpha) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，即 $\frac{1}{2}\sin \left(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \Rightarrow \sin \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $\Rightarrow 2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$ 或 $2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi (k \in \mathbb{Z})$
> >
> > 即 $\alpha = k\pi$ 或 $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{6} + k\pi (k\in \mathbb{Z})$ ，又 $\alpha \in [0,\pi ]$
> >
> > 所以 $\alpha=0,\frac{\pi}{6},\pi.$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ ； $\alpha = 0, \frac{\pi}{6}, \pi$
