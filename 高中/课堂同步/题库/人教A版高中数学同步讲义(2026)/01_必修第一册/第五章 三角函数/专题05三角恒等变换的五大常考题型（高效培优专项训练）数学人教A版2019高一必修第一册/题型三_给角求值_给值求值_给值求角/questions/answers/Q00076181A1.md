> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{3}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\frac{\sin3\alpha}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{\sin(2\alpha + \alpha)}{\sin\alpha}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sin 2 \alpha \cos \alpha + \cos 2 \alpha \sin \alpha}{\sin \alpha}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {2 \sin \alpha \cos^ {2} \alpha + (2 \cos^ {2} \alpha - 1) \sin \alpha}{\sin \alpha}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {4 \sin \alpha \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin \alpha}{\sin \alpha}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 4 c o s ^ {2} \alpha_ {\mathrm{一}} 1 = 2 (2 c o s ^ {2} \alpha_ {\mathrm{一}} 1) + 1 = 2 c o s 2 \alpha_ {\mathrm{一}} + 1
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{13}{5}$ ，所以cos $2\alpha = \frac{4}{5}\cdot$
> >
> > 又 $\alpha$ 是第四象限角，所以 $\sin 2\alpha = -\frac{3}{5}$ ， $\tan 2\alpha = -\frac{3}{4}$ 。
