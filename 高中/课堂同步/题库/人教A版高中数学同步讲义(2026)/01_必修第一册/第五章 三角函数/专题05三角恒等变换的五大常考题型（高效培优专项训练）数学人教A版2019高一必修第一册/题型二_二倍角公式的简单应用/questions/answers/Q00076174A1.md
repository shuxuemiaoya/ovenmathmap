> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\theta \in \left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\in \left(\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)$
> >
> > 可知 $\cos \left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) <   0$ ， $\sin \left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) > 0$
> >
> > 且由二倍角公式得 $\cos \left(2\theta +\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 2\cos^2\left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) - 1 = -\frac{7}{25}$
> >
> > 解得 $\cos \left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{3}{5}$ ，则 $\sin \left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\left(\theta + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)} = \frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 可得 $\tan\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{\sin\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)}{\cos\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)}=\frac{\frac{4}{5}}{-\frac{3}{5}}=-\frac{4}{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan \left( \theta - \frac{\pi}{6} \right) = \tan \left[ \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) - \frac{\pi}{2} \right] = \frac{\sin \left[ \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) - \frac{\pi}{2} \right]}{\cos \left[ \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) - \frac{\pi}{2} \right]} = \frac{\cos \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)}{\sin \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)} = -\frac{1}{\tan \left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)} = \frac{3}{4}$ .
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{3}{4}$
