> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \frac {\tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} \times \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ}}{\tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} + \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ} - \sqrt {3}} = \frac {\tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} \times \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ}}{\tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} + \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ} - \frac {\tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} + \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ}}{1 - \tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} \times \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ}}} = \frac {- (\tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} + \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ})}{1 - \tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} \times \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ}} = - \frac {1}{\tan (2 0 ^ {\circ} + 4 0 ^ {\circ})} = - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
