> [!faq]- 《专题05三角恒等变换的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3}{4}/0.75$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\tan \alpha -\tan \beta = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} -\frac{\sin\beta}{\cos\beta} = \frac{\sin\alpha\cos\beta - \cos\alpha\sin\beta}{\cos\alpha\cos\beta} = \frac{\sin(\alpha - \beta)}{\cos\alpha\cos\beta} = 6$
> >
> > 由 $\cos\alpha\cos\beta=\frac{1}{10}$ ，则 $\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{3}{5}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \because \alpha - \beta \in \left(- \frac {\pi}{2}, \frac {\pi}{2}\right), \therefore \cos (\alpha - \beta) = \frac {4}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \tan (\alpha - \beta) = \frac {\sin (\alpha - \beta)}{\cos (\alpha - \beta)} = \frac {3}{4}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{3}{4}$
