> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优单元测试·强化卷）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $x_{0}=\frac{\pi}{6}$ ， $y_{0}=\sqrt{3}$ (2) $\frac{19}{16}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意可得 $2\cos x_0 = 3\tan x_0 = \frac{3\sin x_0}{\cos x_0}$ ，则 $2\cos^2 x_0 - 3\sin x_0 = 0$
> >
> > 即 $2\left(1 - \sin^2 x_0\right) - 3\sin x_0 = 0$ ，整理可得 $2\sin^2 x_0 + 3\sin x_0 - 2 = 0$
> >
> > 即 $\left(2\sin x_0 - 1\right)\left(\sin x_0 + 2\right) = 0$
> >
> > 因为 $x_0 \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，则 $0 < \sin x_0 < 1$ ，解得 $\sin x_0 = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 所以， $x_{0}=\frac{\pi}{6}$ ， $y_{0}=2\cos\frac{\pi}{6}=\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > (2) 因为 $\sin\left(x+x_{0}\right)=\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{1}{4}$
> >
> > 所以， $\sin \left(\frac{5\pi}{6} -x\right) + \sin^2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} -x\right) = \sin \left[\pi -\left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right] + \sin^2\left[\frac{\pi}{2} -\left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right]$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sin \left(x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + \cos^ {2} \left(x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left(x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + 1 - \sin^ {2} \left(x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \frac {1}{4} + 1 - \frac {1}{1 6} = \frac {1 9}{1 6}.
> > $$
