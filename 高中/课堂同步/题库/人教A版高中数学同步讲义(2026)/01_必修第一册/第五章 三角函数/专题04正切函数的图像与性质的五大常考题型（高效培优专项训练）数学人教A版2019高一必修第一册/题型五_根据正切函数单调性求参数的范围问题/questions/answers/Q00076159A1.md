> [!faq]- 《专题04正切函数的图像与性质的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $\varphi=k\pi+\frac{\pi}{2},k\in Z$ ；（2） $\theta\in\left(k\pi-\frac{\pi}{2},k\pi-\arctan\frac{1}{2}\right],k\in Z$ ；（3） $\omega=2k-1,k\in N^{*}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1）因为函数 $f(x) = \sin (x + \varphi)$ 为偶函数，所以 $\sin (x + \varphi) = \sin (-x + \varphi)$
> >
> > 得 $2\sin x\cos \varphi = 0$ 对 $x\in R$ 恒成立，即 $\cos \varphi = 0$
> >
> > 所以 $\varphi = k\pi + \frac{\pi}{2}, k \in Z$ .
> >
> > (2) $f(x) = \sin \left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right) + \sqrt{3} \sin \left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$
> >
> > $= \sqrt{3}\sin 2x + 2\cos 2x = \sqrt{7}\sin (2x + \varphi_{1})\in [-\sqrt{7},\sqrt{7} ]$ ，即 $A = [-\sqrt{7},\sqrt{7} ]$
> >
> > $g(x) = x^{2} - (4\sqrt{7}\tan \theta)x + 1 = (x - 2\sqrt{7}\tan \theta)^{2} + 1 - 28\tan^{2}\theta$
> >
> > 由题意可知： $2\sqrt{7}\tan\theta\leq-\sqrt{7}$ 得 $\tan\theta\leq-\frac{1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \theta \in \left(k \pi - \frac {\pi}{2}, k \pi - \arctan \frac {1}{2} \right], k \in Z.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = a _ {1} \sin (\omega x + \varphi_ {1}) + a _ {2} \sin (\omega x + \varphi_ {2}) + \dots + a _ {n} \sin (\omega x + \varphi_ {n}) \tag {3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = a _ {1} \left(\sin \omega x \cos \varphi_ {1} + \cos \omega x \sin \varphi_ {1}\right) + a _ {2} \left(\sin \omega x \cos \varphi_ {2} + \cos \omega x \sin \varphi_ {2}\right) + \dots + a _ {n} \left(\sin \omega x \cos \varphi_ {n} + \cos \omega x \sin \varphi_ {n}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(a _ {1} \cos \varphi_ {1} + a _ {2} \cos \varphi_ {2} + \dots + a _ {n} \cos \varphi_ {n}\right) \sin \omega x + \left(a _ {1} \sin \varphi_ {1} + a _ {2} \sin \varphi_ {2} + \dots + a _ {n} \sin \varphi_ {n}\right) \cos \omega x
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text { 又 } \because f ^ {2} (0) + f ^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{2 \omega}\right) \neq 0, f (0) = a _ {1} \sin \varphi_ {1} + a _ {2} \sin \varphi_ {2} + \dots + a _ {n} \sin \varphi_ {n},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f \left(\frac {\pi}{2 \omega}\right) = a _ {1} \cos \varphi_ {1} + a _ {2} \cos \varphi_ {2} + \dots + a _ {n} \cos \varphi_ {n},
> > $$
> >
> > 不妨设 $a_{1}\cos\varphi_{1}+a_{2}\cos\varphi_{2}+\cdots+a_{s}\cos\varphi_{n}=p$ ， $a_{1}\sin\varphi_{1}+a_{2}\sin\varphi_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}\sin\varphi_{n}=q$
> >
> > 则 $f(x) = p\sin \omega x + q\cos \omega x = \sqrt{p^2 + q^2}\sin (\omega x + \varphi)$ ，其中 $p^2 + q^2 \neq 0$
> >
> > 由函数 $f(x)$ 的图像关于点 $\left(\frac{\pi}{2},0\right)$ 对称，在 $x = \pi$ 处取得最小值得 $\frac{2k - 1}{4} T = \pi -\frac{\pi}{2}$
> >
> > 即 $\frac{2k - 1}{4} \cdot \frac{2\pi}{\omega} = \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，故 $\omega = 2k - 1, k \in N^{*}$
