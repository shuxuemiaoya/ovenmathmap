> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 若 $\tan (\pi +\alpha) = 2$ ，则 $\tan \alpha = 2$
> >
> > $\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi}{2} -\alpha\right) - 4\sin (\pi -\alpha)\cos (-\alpha) = \cos^2\alpha -4\sin \alpha \cos \alpha$ $= \frac{\cos^2\alpha - 4\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{1 - 4\tan\alpha}{\tan^2\alpha + 1} = \frac{1 - 4\times 2}{4 + 1} = -\frac{7}{5}.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
