> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\alpha -\frac{3\pi}{4} +\frac{\pi}{4} -\alpha = -\frac{\pi}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} -\alpha\right) = \cos \left[-\frac{\pi}{2} -\left(\alpha -\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)\right] = \cos \left[\frac{\pi}{2} +\left(\alpha -\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)\right] = -\sin \left(\alpha -\frac{3\pi}{4}\right) = -\frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > 故选 **A**
