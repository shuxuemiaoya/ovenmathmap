> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(1)-1$ ; $(2)\frac{11}{5}$ ; $(3)-\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $2\lg 5 + \lg 4 - \frac{\log_38}{\log_32} = \lg 5^2 +\lg 4 - \frac{\log_32^3}{\log_32} = \lg (5^2\times 4) - \frac{3\log_32}{\log_32} = 2 - 3 = -1$
> >
> > (2) 因为 $\cos(\pi+\alpha)=\frac{4}{5}$ ，所以 $\cos\alpha=-\frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 因为 $\alpha \in (0,\pi)$ ，所以 $\sin \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \left(-\frac{4}{5}\right)^2} = \frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos(3\pi-\alpha)+\cos\left(\frac{3\pi}{2}+\alpha\right)-\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\alpha\right)=-\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha=\frac{4}{5}+\frac{3}{5}+\frac{4}{5}=\frac{11}{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1 - 2 \sin x \cos x}{2 \cos^ {2} x - \sin^ {2} x} = \frac {\sin^ {2} x + \cos^ {2} x - 2 \sin x \cos x}{2 \cos^ {2} x - \sin^ {2} x} = \frac {\tan^ {2} x + 1 - 2 \tan x}{2 - \tan^ {2} x} = \frac {4 + 1 - 4}{2 - 4} = - \frac {1}{2}. \tag {3}
> > $$
