> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $1 + \sin 1 / \sin 1 + 1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意： $6=2r+l=2r+r\alpha$ ，故 $r=\frac{6}{2+\alpha}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > S = \frac {1}{2} \alpha r ^ {2} = \frac {1}{2} \alpha \left(\frac {6}{2 + \alpha}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1 8}{\frac {4}{\alpha} + \alpha + 4} \leq \frac {1 8}{2 \sqrt {\frac {4}{\alpha} \cdot \alpha} + 4} = \frac {9}{4},
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $\frac{4}{\alpha} = \alpha$ ，即 $\alpha = 2$ 时等号成立·
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \left(\cos \frac {2 0 2 5 \pi}{\alpha}\right) + \sin \left(\sin \frac {2 0 2 5 \pi}{\alpha}\right) = \cos \left(\cos \frac {\pi}{2}\right) + \sin \left(\sin \frac {\pi}{2}\right) = \cos 0 + \sin 1 = 1 + \sin 1.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $1+\sin1$
