> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意：令 $2x - \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ ，∴ 函数 $f(x)$ 对称轴方程为： $x = \frac{5\pi}{12} + \frac{k\pi}{2}(k \in Z)$ ，
> >
> > 又 $\therefore$ 方程 $f(x)=\frac{1}{3}$ 在 $(0,\pi)$ 的解为 $x_{1}$ , $x_{2}(x_{1}<x_{2})$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2} = \frac {5 \pi}{1 2}, \therefore x _ {2} = \frac {5 \pi}{6} - x _ {1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \left(x _ {1} - x _ {2}\right) = \sin \left(2 x _ {1} - \frac {5 \pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left[ \left(2 x _ {1} - \frac {\pi}{3}\right) - \frac {\pi}{2}\right) = - \cos \left(2 x _ {1} - \frac {\pi}{3}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\therefore x_{2} = \frac{5\pi}{6} -x_{1}$ ， $x_{1} <   x_{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 0 <   x _ {1} <   \frac {5 \pi}{1 2}, \therefore 0 <   2 x _ {1} <   \frac {5 \pi}{6}, \therefore - \frac {\pi}{3} <   2 x _ {1} - \frac {\pi}{3} <   \frac {\pi}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 又∵ $\sin(2x_{1}-\frac{\pi}{3})=\frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \left(x _ {1} - x _ {2}\right) = - \cos \left(2 x _ {1} - \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = - \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} \left(2 x _ {1} - \frac {\pi}{3}\right)} = - \sqrt {1 - \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2}} = - \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $-\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$
