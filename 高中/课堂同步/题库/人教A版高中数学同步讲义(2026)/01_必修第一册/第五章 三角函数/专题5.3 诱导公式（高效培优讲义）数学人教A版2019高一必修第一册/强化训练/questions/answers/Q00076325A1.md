> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\cos \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ (2) $\frac{4}{3}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）依题意， $\tan \alpha = \tan (\pi + \alpha) = -2$ ，由 $\alpha$ 是第二象限角，得 $\sin \alpha > 0, \cos \alpha < 0$
> >
> > 又 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sin \alpha = -2\cos \alpha \\ \sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\sin \alpha = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5},\cos \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ ，所以 $\cos \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> >
> > (2) $\frac{2\sin(\pi + \alpha)\cos(-2\pi - \alpha)}{\sin^2(-\alpha) - \sin^2(\frac{3\pi}{2} - \alpha)} = \frac{-2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha - \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{-2\tan\alpha}{\tan^2\alpha - 1} = \frac{-2\times(-2)}{4 - 1} = \frac{4}{3}$ .
