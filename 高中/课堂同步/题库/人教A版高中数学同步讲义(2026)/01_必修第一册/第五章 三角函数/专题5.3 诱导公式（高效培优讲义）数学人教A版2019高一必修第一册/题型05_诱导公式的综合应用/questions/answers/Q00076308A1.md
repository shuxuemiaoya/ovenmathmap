> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{12}{13}$ (2) $-\frac{4}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因 $f(\alpha) = \frac{\sin(2\pi - \alpha)\tan(\pi + \alpha)\sin\left(\frac{3\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{\sin\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\tan(3\pi - \alpha)} = \frac{-\sin\alpha\tan\alpha(-\cos\alpha)}{-\cos\alpha\cdot(-\tan\alpha)} = \sin \alpha$
> >
> > 又角 $\alpha$ 的终边过点 $P(5, -12)$ ，则 $\sin \alpha = \frac{-12}{\sqrt{5^2 + (-12)^2}} = -\frac{12}{13}$
> >
> > 故 $f(\alpha) = -\frac{12}{13}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > f (\alpha) - f \left(\frac {3 \pi}{2} + \alpha\right) = \sin \alpha - \sin \left(\frac {3 \pi}{2} + \alpha\right) = \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha = \frac {1}{5} ①,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = (\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha) ^ {2} - 1 = \frac {1}{2 5} - 1 = - \frac {2 4}{2 5}
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $\left(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha\right)^{2}=1-2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=1+\frac{24}{25}=\frac{49}{25}$ ，
> >
> > 因 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ ，则 $\cos \alpha < 0$ ，又 $\sin \alpha \cos \alpha < 0$ ，则 $\sin \alpha >0$
> >
> > 故 $\alpha\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)$ ， $\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha=\frac{7}{5}$ ②，
> >
> > 由①②解得 $\sin\alpha=\frac{4}{5},\cos\alpha=-\frac{3}{5}$ ，故 $\tan\alpha=\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=-\frac{4}{3}$
> >
> > $f(\alpha) = \frac{\sin(\pi - \alpha)\sin(-\pi - \alpha)}{\sin(2\pi + \alpha)\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)}$ ，若 $f(\alpha) = 2$ ，求 $\sin^2\alpha - 2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha + 3\cos^2\alpha$ 的值。
> >
> > 【答案】 $\frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > 【详解】由题设 $f(\alpha)=\frac{\sin^{2}\alpha}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}=\tan\alpha=2$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin^ {2} \alpha - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha + 3 \cos^ {2} \alpha = \frac {\sin^ {2} \alpha - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha + 3 \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {\tan^ {2} \alpha - 2 \tan \alpha + 3}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = \frac {4 - 4 + 3}{4 + 1} = \frac {3}{5}.
> > $$
