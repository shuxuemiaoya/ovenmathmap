> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)答案见解析 (2) $-\frac{4}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）根据三角函数的定义得 $\sin \alpha = \frac{\lambda}{\sqrt{3^2 + \lambda^2}} = \frac{\lambda}{5}$ ，解得 $\lambda = 0$ 或 $\lambda = \pm 4$
> >
> > 当 $\lambda=0$ 时， $P(3,0)$ ， $\cos\alpha=1$ ，
> >
> > 当 $\lambda = \pm 4$ 时， $\cos\alpha = \frac{3}{\sqrt{3^{2} + \lambda^{2}}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt{3^{2} + 4^{2}}} = \frac{3}{5}$ .
> >
> > (2) 由 $\lambda > 0$ 可知 $\lambda = 4$ ，此时 $\sin \alpha = \frac{4}{5}$ ， $\cos \alpha = \frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sin (\alpha + \pi) + \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {5}{2} \pi\right)}{\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - \alpha\right) - \cos (\alpha + 3 \pi)} = \frac {- \sin \alpha + \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{2}\right)}{\cos \alpha - \cos (\alpha + \pi)} = \frac {- \sin \alpha - \sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha + \cos \alpha} = - \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = - \frac {\frac {4}{5}}{\frac {3}{5}} = - \frac {4}{3}.
> > $$
