> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{8}{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由于 $\frac{\pi}{3} - x = \frac{\pi}{2} - \left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} -x\right) = \sin^2\left[\frac{\pi}{2} -\left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right] = \cos^2\left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = 1 - \sin^2\left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = 1 - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{8}{9}.$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{8}{9}$
