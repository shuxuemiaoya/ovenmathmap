> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\sin (30^{\circ} + \alpha) = \frac{3}{5}$ ， $60^{\circ} < a < 150^{\circ}$ ，则 $90^{\circ} < 30^{\circ} + \alpha < 180^{\circ}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos(30^{\circ}+\alpha)=-\frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 则 $\cos (150^{\circ} - \alpha) = \cos [180^{\circ} - (30^{\circ} + \alpha)] = -\cos (30^{\circ} + \alpha) = \frac{4}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**
