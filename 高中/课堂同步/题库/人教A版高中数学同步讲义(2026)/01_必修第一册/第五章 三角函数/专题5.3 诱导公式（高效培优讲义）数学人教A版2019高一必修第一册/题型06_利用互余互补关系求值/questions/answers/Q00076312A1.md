> [!faq]- 《专题5.3 诱导公式（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\sin \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6} -\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = -\sin \left[\frac{\pi}{2} -(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6})\right] = -\cos (\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6})$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{3} + \frac {\pi}{2}\right) = \cos \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \frac {2}{5} \in (0, \frac {1}{2})
> > $$
> >
> > 而 $\alpha\in(0,\pi)$ ，则 $\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\in(-\frac{\pi}{3},\frac{2\pi}{3})$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\in (\frac{\pi}{3},\frac{\pi}{2})$ ，则 $\alpha \in (\frac{2\pi}{3},\frac{5\pi}{6})$ ，可得 $\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\in \left(\frac{5\pi}{6},\pi\right)$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6})=-\frac{\sqrt{21}}{5}$ ，则 $\sin\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{21}}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**
