> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $a=\frac{2\tan13^{\circ}}{1-\tan^{2}13^{\circ}}=\tan26^{\circ}$
> >
> > $$
> > b = \frac {1}{2} \cos 6 ^ {\circ} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin 6 ^ {\circ} = \cos (6 0 ^ {\circ} + 6 ^ {\circ}) = \cos 6 6 ^ {\circ} = \sin 2 4 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > c = \sqrt {\frac {1 - \cos 5 0 ^ {\circ}}{2}} = \sqrt {\frac {1 - (1 - 2 \sin^ {2} 2 5 ^ {\circ})}{2}} = \sqrt {\sin^ {2} 2 5 ^ {\circ}} = \sin 2 5 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > 根据正弦函数的单调性可知 c > b
> >
> > 根据正切函数的单调性可知 $a = \tan 26^{\circ} > \tan 25^{\circ} = \frac{\sin 25^{\circ}}{\cos 25^{\circ}} > \sin 25^{\circ} = c$
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
