> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\left[k\pi-\frac{\pi}{6},k\pi+\frac{\pi}{3}\right](k\in Z)$ (2) 当 $x=\frac{\pi}{3}$ 时， $f(x)_{\max}=2$ ，当 $x=-\frac{\pi}{12}$ 时， $f(x)_{\min}=-\sqrt{3}$ (3) $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $f(x) = \sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \sin \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \cos 2x$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {3} \sin 2 x - \cos 2 x = 2 \sin \left(2 x - \frac {\pi}{6}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 令 $2k\pi - \frac{\pi}{2} \leq 2x - \frac{\pi}{6} \leq 2k\pi + \frac{\pi}{2}(k \in \mathbb{Z})$ ，解得 $k\pi - \frac{\pi}{6} \leq x \leq k\pi + \frac{\pi}{3}(k \in \mathbb{Z})$
> >
> > ∴ 函数 $f(x)$ 的单调递增区间为 $\left[k\pi - \frac{\pi}{6}, k\pi + \frac{\pi}{3}\right] (k \in \mathbb{Z})$ .
> >
> > (2) $\because x\in \left[-\frac{\pi}{12},\frac{5\pi}{12}\right],\therefore 2x - \frac{\pi}{6}\in \left[-\frac{\pi}{3},\frac{2\pi}{3}\right]$
> >
> > $\therefore 2x - \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{2}$ 时，即 $x = \frac{\pi}{3}$ 时， $f(x)_{\max} = 2$
> >
> > $\therefore 2x - \frac{\pi}{6} = -\frac{\pi}{3}$ 时，即 $x = -\frac{\pi}{12}$ 时， $f(x)_{\min} = 2\sin \left(-\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\sqrt{3}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \because f \left(\frac {\theta}{2} - \frac {\pi}{2 4}\right) = 2 \sin \left(\theta - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \frac {6}{5}, \therefore \sin \left(\theta - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \frac {3}{5} \tag {3}
> > $$
> >
> > $\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\in\left(-\frac{\pi}{4},\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\therefore\cos\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos \theta = \cos \left(\theta - \frac {\pi}{4} + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cos \left(\theta - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \sin \left(\theta - \frac {\pi}{4}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cdot \frac {4}{5} - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cdot \frac {3}{5} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{1 0}.
> > $$
