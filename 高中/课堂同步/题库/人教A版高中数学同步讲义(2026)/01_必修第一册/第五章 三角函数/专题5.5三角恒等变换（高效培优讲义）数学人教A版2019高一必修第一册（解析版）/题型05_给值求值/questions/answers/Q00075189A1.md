> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $f(x)=\sqrt{2}\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ (2) $\left[-1, \sqrt{2}\right]$ (3) $-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）根据题意（1）化简可得
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = \sin 2 \omega x + \cos 2 \omega x = \sqrt {2} \left(\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \sin 2 \omega x + \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cos 2 \omega x\right) = \sqrt {2} \sin \left(2 \omega x + \frac {\pi}{4}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 又因为函数 $f(x)$ 的最小正周期为 $\pi$ ，所以 $\frac{2\pi}{2\omega} = \pi$ ，可得 $\omega = 1$
> >
> > 所以 $f(x)=\sqrt{2}\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$
> >
> > (2) 设 $t = 2x + \frac{\pi}{4}, t \in \left[\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{5\pi}{4}\right]$ ，所以 $\sin t \in \left[-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, 1\right]$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $f(x) = \sqrt{2}\sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ 的值域为 $\left[-1,\sqrt{2}\right]$
> >
> > (3) 因为 $f(\alpha)=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}$ ，所以 $f(\alpha)=\sqrt{2}\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}$ ，
> >
> > 可得 $\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> >
> > 因为 $\frac{\pi}{8} < \alpha < \frac{3\pi}{8}$ ，可得 $\frac{\pi}{2} < 2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4} < \pi$ ，所以 $\cos \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = -\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos 2 \alpha = \cos \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cos \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) + \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \sin \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{4}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {2 \sqrt {5}}{5} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} + \frac {\sqrt {5}}{5} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} = - \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{1 0}
> > $$
