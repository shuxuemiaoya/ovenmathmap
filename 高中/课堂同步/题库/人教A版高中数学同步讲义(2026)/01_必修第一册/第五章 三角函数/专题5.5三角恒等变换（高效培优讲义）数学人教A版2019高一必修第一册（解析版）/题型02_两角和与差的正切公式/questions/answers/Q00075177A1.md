> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)3 (2)1 $$ f (x) = \frac {\sin (3 \pi + x) \sin (x - \pi) \cos (\pi + x)}{\cos (\pi - x) \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + x\right) \sin \left(x - \frac {3 \pi}{2}\right)} = \frac {(- \sin x) (- \sin x) (- \cos x)}{(- \cos x) (- \sin x) \cos x} = - \tan x $$ $$ \because f (\alpha) = 2, \therefore \tan \alpha = - 2 $$ $$ \therefore \frac {\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha} = \frac {\tan \alpha - 1}{\tan \alpha + 1} = \frac {- 2 - 1}{- 2 + 1} = 3 $$ (2) 依题意，由 $f(\alpha - \beta) = -\frac{1}{2}, f(\alpha) = -\frac{1}{3}$ ，可得 $\tan (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{1}{2}, \tan \alpha = \frac{1}{3}$ $\therefore \tan (2\alpha - \beta) = \tan [(\alpha - \beta) + \alpha] = \frac{\tan (\alpha - \beta) + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan (\alpha - \beta) \tan \alpha} = \frac{\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}}{1 - \frac{1}{6}} = 1$
>
