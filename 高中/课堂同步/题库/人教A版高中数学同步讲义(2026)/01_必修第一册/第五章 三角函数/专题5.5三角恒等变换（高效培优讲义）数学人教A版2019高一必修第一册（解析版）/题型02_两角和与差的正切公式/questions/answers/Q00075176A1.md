> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)1 (2) $\frac{p}{p^2 + 1}$ , $\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）当 $p = 1$ 时， $x^{2} - 4x - 3 = 0$
> >
> > 由题意知： $\tan \alpha +\tan \beta = 4$ ， $\tan \alpha \tan \beta = -3$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan\left(\alpha+\beta\right)=\frac{\tan\alpha+\tan\beta}{1-\tan\alpha\tan\beta}=\frac{4}{1+3}=1$
> >
> > (2) 由题知： $\tan\alpha+\tan\beta=4p,\quad\tan\alpha\tan\beta=-3$
> >
> > 则 $\tan (\alpha +\beta) = \frac{\tan\alpha + \tan\beta}{1 - \tan\alpha\tan\beta} = \frac{4p}{1 + 3} = p$
> >
> > 因为 $\cos 2\alpha \cos 2\beta +\sin^2 (\alpha -\beta) = (\cos^2\alpha -\sin^2\alpha)(\cos^2\beta -\sin^2\beta) + (\sin \alpha \cos \beta -\cos \alpha \sin \beta)^2$
> >
> > $$
> > = \cos^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta - \cos^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta - \sin^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta + \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta + \sin^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \beta \cos \alpha \sin \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta
> > $$
> >
> > $= \cos^2\alpha \cos^2\beta +\sin^2\alpha \sin^2\beta -2\sin \alpha \cos \beta \cos \alpha \sin \beta$
> >
> > $$
> > = (\cos \alpha \cos \beta - \sin \alpha \sin \beta) ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \cos^ {2} (\alpha + \beta) = \frac {\cos^ {2} (\alpha + \beta)}{\sin^ {2} (\alpha + \beta) + \cos^ {2} (\alpha + \beta)} = \frac {1}{\tan^ {2} (\alpha + \beta) + 1},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan (\alpha + \beta) \left[ \cos 2 \alpha \cos 2 \beta + \sin^ {2} (\alpha - \beta) \right] = \frac {\tan (\alpha + \beta)}{\tan^ {2} (\alpha + \beta) + 1} = \frac {p}{p ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {1}{p + \frac {1}{p}}
> > $$
> >
> > 由于 $p > 0$ ，故 $p + \frac{1}{p} 2\sqrt{p\cdot\frac{1}{p}} = 2$ ，当且仅当 $p = 1$ 时，等号成立，
> >
> > 所以当 $p = 1$ 时， $\tan (\alpha +\beta)\left[\cos 2\alpha \cos 2\beta +\sin^2 (\alpha -\beta)\right]$ 取得最大值为 $\frac{1}{2}\cdot$
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = \frac {\sin (3 \pi + x) \sin (x - \pi) \cos (\pi + x)}{\cos (\pi - x) \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + x\right) \sin \left(x - \frac {3 \pi}{2}\right)}
> > $$
