> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5},\tan \alpha = -\frac{3}{4}$ $$ \cos \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \frac {3 - 4 \sqrt {3}}{1 0}, \sin 2 \alpha = - \frac {2 4}{2 5}, \tan 2 \alpha = - \frac {2 4}{7} \tag {2} $$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）已知 $\alpha$ 是第二象限角， $\cos \alpha = -\frac{4}{5}$ ，则 $\sin \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \left(-\frac{4}{5}\right)^2} = \frac{3}{5}, \tan \alpha = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = -\frac{3}{4}$ ；
> >
> > (2) 由题意 $\cos\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos\alpha+\frac{1}{2}\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\times\left(-\frac{4}{5}\right)+\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{3}{5}=\frac{3-4\sqrt{3}}{10}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \sin 2 \alpha = \frac {2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{1} = \frac {2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {2 \tan \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {2 \times \left(- \frac {3}{4}\right)}{\left(- \frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {2} + 1} = - \frac {3}{2} \times \frac {1 6}{2 5} = - \frac {2 4}{2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan 2 \alpha = \frac {2 \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {2 \times \left(- \frac {3}{4}\right)}{1 - \left(- \frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {2}} = - \frac {3}{2} \times \frac {1 6}{7} = - \frac {2 4}{7}.
> > $$
