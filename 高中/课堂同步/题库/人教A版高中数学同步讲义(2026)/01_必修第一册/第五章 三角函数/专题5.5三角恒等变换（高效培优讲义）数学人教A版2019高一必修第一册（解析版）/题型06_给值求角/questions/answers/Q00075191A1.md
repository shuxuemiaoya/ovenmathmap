> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sin \frac{\pi}{7} = 2\sin \frac{\pi}{14}\cos \frac{\pi}{14}(1 + \sin 2\alpha)\sin \frac{\pi}{7} = 2\cos^2\frac{\pi}{14}\cos 2\alpha$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin\frac{\pi}{14}\sin2\alpha+\sin\frac{\pi}{14}=\cos\frac{\pi}{14}\cos2\alpha$
> >
> > 则 $\cos \frac{\pi}{14}\cos 2\alpha -\sin \frac{\pi}{14}\sin 2\alpha = \sin \frac{\pi}{14}$
> >
> > 即 $\cos \left(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{14}\right) = \sin \frac{\pi}{14}$
> >
> > 因为 $0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，所以 $\frac{\pi}{14} < 2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{14} < \frac{15\pi}{14}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{14} = \frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{\pi}{14}$ ，
> >
> > 解得 $\alpha = \frac{5\pi}{28}$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
