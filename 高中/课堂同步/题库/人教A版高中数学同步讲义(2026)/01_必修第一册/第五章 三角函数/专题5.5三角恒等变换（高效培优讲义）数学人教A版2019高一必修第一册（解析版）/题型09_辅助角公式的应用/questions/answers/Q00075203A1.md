> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C $$ f (x) = 5 \sin x - 1 2 \cos x = 1 3 \left[ \sin x \cdot \frac {5}{1 3} + \cos x \cdot \left(- \frac {1 2}{1 3}\right) \right] = 1 3 \sin (x + \varphi) $$ 其中， $\cos \varphi = \frac{5}{13}$ ， $\sin \varphi = -\frac{12}{13}$ 依题意得， $f(\theta)=13\sin(\theta+\varphi)=-13$ ， $\therefore\sin(\theta+\varphi)=-1$ $\therefore \theta +\varphi = 2k\pi -\frac{\pi}{2}, k\in \mathbf{Z},\therefore \theta = 2k\pi -\frac{\pi}{2} -\varphi ,k\in \mathbf{Z},$ $$ \therefore \cos \theta = \cos \left(2 k \pi - \frac {\pi}{2} - \varphi\right) = \cos \left(- \frac {\pi}{2} - \varphi\right) = - \sin \varphi = \frac {1 2}{1 3}, $$ 故选：C.
