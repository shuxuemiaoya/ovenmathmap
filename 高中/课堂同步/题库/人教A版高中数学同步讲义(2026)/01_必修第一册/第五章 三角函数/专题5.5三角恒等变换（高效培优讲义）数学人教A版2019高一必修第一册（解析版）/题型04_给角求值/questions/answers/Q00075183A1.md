> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 原式 $= \frac{2\sin 18^{\circ}\left(3\cos^{2}9^{\circ} - \sin^{2}9^{\circ} - \cos^{2}9^{\circ} - \sin^{2}9^{\circ}\right)}{2\sin\left(6^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}\right)}$ $= \frac{2\sin 18^{\circ}\left(2\cos^{2}9^{\circ} - 2\sin^{2}9^{\circ}\right)}{2\sin 36^{\circ}} = \frac{2\sin 18^{\circ}\cos 18^{\circ}}{\sin 36^{\circ}} = \frac{\sin 36^{\circ}}{\sin 36^{\circ}} = 1.$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
