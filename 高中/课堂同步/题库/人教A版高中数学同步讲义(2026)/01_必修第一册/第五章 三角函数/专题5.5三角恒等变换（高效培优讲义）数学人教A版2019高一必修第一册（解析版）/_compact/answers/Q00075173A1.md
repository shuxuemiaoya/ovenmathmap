> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \cos 2\alpha = 4\sin \alpha \cos \alpha + 2 \\ \sqrt{2}\sin \beta = \sin \left( \beta + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > $$
> > \left[ \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha - 4 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha - 4 \tan \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = 2 \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sqrt {2} \sin \beta = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \sin \beta + \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cos \beta \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \tan \alpha = -1 \text{或} - \frac{1}{3} \\ \tan \beta = 1 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 当 $\tan\alpha=-1$ 时， $\cos2\alpha=0$ ，A 在 y 轴上，舍去.
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\tan\alpha&=-\frac{1}{3}\\ \tan\beta&=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，故 $\tan(\alpha+\beta)=\frac{\tan\alpha+\tan\beta}{1-\tan\alpha\tan\beta}=\frac{1}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**
