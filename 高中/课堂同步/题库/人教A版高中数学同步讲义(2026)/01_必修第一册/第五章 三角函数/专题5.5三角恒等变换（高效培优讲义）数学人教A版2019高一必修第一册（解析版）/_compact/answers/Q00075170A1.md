> [!faq]- 《专题5.5三角恒等变换（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册（解析版）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{1}{2}\tan 40^{\circ} - 2\cos 50^{\circ} = \frac{\sin 40^{\circ}}{2\cos 40^{\circ}} -\frac{4\cos 40^{\circ}\sin 40^{\circ}}{2\cos 40^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{\sin 40^{\circ} - 2\sin 80^{\circ}}{2\cos 40^{\circ}} = \frac{\sin 40^{\circ} - 2\cos 10^{\circ}}{2\cos 40^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{\sin 40^{\circ} - 2\cos(40^{\circ} - 30^{\circ})}{2\cos 40^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{\sin 40^{\circ} - 2\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos 40^{\circ} + \frac{1}{2}\sin 40^{\circ}\right)}{2\cos 40^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{-\sqrt{3}\cos 40^{\circ}}{2\cos 40^{\circ}} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$
> >
> > 故选：B.
