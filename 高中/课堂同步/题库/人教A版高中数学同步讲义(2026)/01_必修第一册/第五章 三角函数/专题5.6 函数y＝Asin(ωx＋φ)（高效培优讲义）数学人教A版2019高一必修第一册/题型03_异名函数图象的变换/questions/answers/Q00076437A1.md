> [!faq]- 《专题5.6 函数y＝Asin(ωx＋φ)（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $f(x)=2\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{3}+\varphi\right)\left(\left|\varphi\right|<\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ 的图像关于 y 轴对称，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {\pi}{3} + \varphi = k \pi + \frac {\pi}{2} (k \in Z), \therefore \varphi = k \pi + \frac {\pi}{6} (k \in Z)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because | \varphi | <   \frac {\pi}{2}, \therefore \varphi = \frac {\pi}{6}, \therefore f (x) = 2 \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{3} + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = 2 \cos 2 x
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore f(x) = 2\cos 2x$ 的图像可由 $g(x) = 2\cos \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = 2\cos \left[2\left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right]$ 的图像向右平移 $\frac{\pi}{6}$ 个单位长度得到.
> >
> > 故选 **B**
