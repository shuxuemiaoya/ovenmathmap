> [!faq]- 《专题5.6 函数y＝Asin(ωx＋φ)（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $^{1}$ 或 $-\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可得 $|\omega| = 2$ ，故 $\omega = \pm 2$ ，故 $f(x) = \sin (2x + \varphi)$ 或 $f(x) = \sin (-2x + \varphi)$
> >
> > 又因为 $f\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{2}$ 故 $\sin \left(2\times \frac{\pi}{2} +\varphi\right) = \sin (\pi +\varphi) = -\sin \varphi = \frac{1}{2}$ 或 $\sin \left(-2\times \frac{\pi}{2} +\varphi\right) = \sin (-\pi +\varphi) = -\sin \varphi = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin\varphi=-\frac{1}{2}$ ，故 $\varphi=2k\pi-\frac{\pi}{6}, k\in Z$ 或 $\varphi=2k\pi+\frac{7\pi}{6}, k\in Z$
> >
> > 故 $f(x) = \sin \left(2x + 2k\pi -\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right),k\in Z$
> >
> > 或 $f(x) = \sin \left(2x + 2k\pi +\frac{7\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left(2x + \frac{7\pi}{6}\right),k\in Z$
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = \sin \left(- 2 x + 2 k \pi + \frac {7 \pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left(- 2 x + \frac {7 \pi}{6}\right), k \in Z
> > $$
> >
> > 或 $f(x) = \sin \left(-2x + 2k\pi -\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left(-2x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right),k\in Z$
> >
> > $$
> > f \left(\frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \sin \frac {\pi}{2} = 1 \text {或} f \left(\frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \sin \frac {1 1 \pi}{6} = - \frac {1}{2} \text {或} f \left(\frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \sin \frac {\pi}{2} = 1 \text {或} f \left(\frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \sin \left(- \frac {5 \pi}{6}\right) = - \frac {1}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $^{1}$ 或 $-\frac{1}{2}$ .
