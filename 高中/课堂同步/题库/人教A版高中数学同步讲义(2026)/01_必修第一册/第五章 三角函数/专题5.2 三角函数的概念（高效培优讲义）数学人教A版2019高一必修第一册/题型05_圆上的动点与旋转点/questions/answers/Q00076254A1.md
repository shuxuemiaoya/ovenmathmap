> [!faq]- 《专题5.2 三角函数的概念（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $P\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)$ (2) $\tan \alpha = 2 \pm \sqrt{3}$ (3)16
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意得 $P(\cos\alpha,\sin\alpha)$ ，所以 $P\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{6},\sin\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ ，即 $P\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)$
> >
> > (2) 由题意得 $\alpha$ 为锐角，故 $P$ 在第一象限，则 $P_{1}, P_{2}$ 在 $x, y$ 轴正半轴上，
> >
> > 由题意可知 $OP \perp P_{1}P_{2}$ ，所以 $\cos\alpha=\frac{1}{|OP_{1}|}\Rightarrow x_{0}=\frac{1}{\cos\alpha},\angle OP_{2}P=\alpha,$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin\alpha=\frac{1}{\left|OP_{2}\right|}\Rightarrow y_{0}=\frac{1}{\sin\alpha}$ ，由 $\triangle OP_{1}P_{2}$ 的面积为 2，
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{2} x _ {0} y _ {0} = 2 \Rightarrow \frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {1}{\cos \alpha} \cdot \frac {1}{\sin \alpha} = 2 \Rightarrow \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac {1}{4},
> > $$
> >
> > 又因为 $\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha=1$ ，所以 $\frac{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha}=\frac{1}{4}\Rightarrow\frac{\tan\alpha}{\tan^{2}\alpha+1}=\frac{1}{4}$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan^{2}\alpha-4\tan\alpha+1=0\Rightarrow\tan\alpha=2\pm\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > (3) 由题意 $\alpha$ 是锐角，则 $x_0 > 0, y_0 > 0,$
> >
> > $$
> > x _ {0} ^ {2} + 9 y _ {0} ^ {2} = \frac {1}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {9}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} = \left(\frac {1}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {9}{\sin^ {2} \alpha}\right) (\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 0 + \frac {\sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {9 \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} \quad 1 0 + 2 \sqrt {\frac {\sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} \times \frac {9 \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha}} = 1 6,
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{\sin^{2}\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha}=\frac{9\cos^{2}\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha}$ ，即 $\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\alpha=\frac{\pi}{3}$ 时取等号，
> >
> > 所以 $x_{0}^{2} + 9y_{0}^{2}$ 的最小值为 16.
