> [!faq]- 《专题5.2 三角函数的概念（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 1 $\left[1,\frac{4\sqrt{2}+5}{7}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意 $a^2 - ab + b^2 = 1$ ， $2ab - ab = ab$ ，等号成立当且仅当 $a = b = \pm 1$ ，即 $ab$ 的最大值为 $1$ ；
> >
> > $$
> > \text {由题意} \frac {1}{a ^ {2} + 1} + \frac {1}{b ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {a ^ {2} + 1 + b ^ {2} + 1}{(a ^ {2} + 1) (b ^ {2} + 1)} = \frac {a b + 3}{(a b) ^ {2} + a ^ {2} + b ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {a b + 3}{(a b) ^ {2} + a b + 2},
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $a^2 - ab + b^2 = \left(a - \frac{b}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{3}{4} b^2 = 1$ ，所以设 $a - \frac{1}{2} b = \cos \theta, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} b = \sin \theta$
> >
> > $$
> > a = \cos \theta + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \sin \theta , b = \frac {2 \sqrt {3}}{3} \sin \theta
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a b = \frac {2 \sqrt {3}}{3} \sin \theta \cos \theta + \frac {2}{3} \sin^ {2} \theta = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \sin 2 \theta - \frac {1}{3} \cos 2 \theta + \frac {1}{3} = \frac {2}{3} \sin \left(2 \theta - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + \frac {1}{3} \in \left[ - \frac {1}{3}, 1 \right] ，
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \frac {(a b) ^ {2} + a b + 2}{a b + 3} = \frac {\left[ (a b + 3) - 3 \right] ^ {2} + \left[ (a b + 3) - 3 \right] + 2}{a b + 3} = a b + 3 + \frac {8}{a b + 3} - 5,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > u = a b + 3 \in \left[ \frac {8}{3}, 4 \right]    ,    f (u) = u + \frac {8}{u} - 5    ,    \text {所以}    f (u) _ {\min} = f (2 \sqrt {2}) = 4 \sqrt {2} - 5
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f \left(\frac {8}{3}\right) = \frac {2}{3} <   f (4) = 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \frac {(a b) ^ {2} + a b + 2}{a b + 3} = a b + 3 + \frac {8}{a b + 3} - 5 = f (u) = u + \frac {8}{u} - 5 \in [ 4 \sqrt {2} - 5, 1 ]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \frac {1}{a ^ {2} + 1} + \frac {1}{b ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {a b + 3}{(a b) ^ {2} + a b + 2} \in \left[ 1, \frac {4 \sqrt {2} + 5}{7} \right].
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $1$ ； $\left[1, \frac{4\sqrt{2} + 5}{7}\right]$ .
> >
> > 17. 计算：
> >
> > (1)已知 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角， $\tan \alpha = -2$ ，求 $\sin \alpha, \cos \alpha$
> >
> > (2) $\tan \alpha = 2$
> >
> > (i) 求 $2\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha$ 的值
> >
> > (ii) 求 $\sin^2\alpha + \sin \alpha \cos \alpha$ 的值
> >
> > 【答案】(1) $\sin\alpha=\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5},\cos\alpha=-\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> >
> > (2) (i) $\frac{1}{5}$ ; (ii) $\frac{6}{5}$ .
> >
> > 【详解】（1）由 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角，得 $\sin \alpha >0,\cos \alpha < 0$ ，由 $\tan \alpha = -2$ ，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\sin \alpha = -2\cos \alpha \\ \sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin \alpha = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5},\cos \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}.$
> >
> > (2) 由 $\tan \alpha = 2$ ，得 (i) $\frac{\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha}{2 \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha} = \frac{\tan \alpha - 1}{2 \tan \alpha + 1} = \frac{2 - 1}{2 \times 2 + 1} = \frac{1}{5}$ ；
> >
> > (ii) $\sin^{2}\alpha + \sin\alpha\cos\alpha = \frac{\sin^{2}\alpha + \sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha + \cos^{2}\alpha} = \frac{\tan^{2}\alpha + \tan\alpha}{\tan^{2}\alpha + 1} = \frac{2^{2} + 2}{2^{2} + 1} = \frac{6}{5}$
