> [!faq]- 《专题5.2 三角函数的概念（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $\cos \alpha = -\frac{12}{13}$ ， $\tan \alpha = -\frac{5}{12}$ ；(2) $\sin \alpha = -\frac{3}{5}, \cos \alpha = -\frac{4}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $\alpha$ 是第二象限角，
> >
> > 所以 $\cos\alpha=-\sqrt{1-\sin^{2}\alpha}=-\frac{12}{13}$ ; $\tan\alpha=\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=-\frac{5}{12}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \left( \begin{array}{l} 2 \end{array} \right) \left\{ \begin{array}{c} \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = \frac {3}{4}, \\ \sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha = 1 \end{array} \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sin \alpha = \frac {3}{5}, \\ \cos \alpha = \frac {4}{5} \text {或} \end{array} \right. \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sin \alpha = - \frac {3}{5}, \\ \cos \alpha = - \frac {4}{5}. \end{array} \right. \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 是第三象限角，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\sin\alpha&=-\frac{3}{5},\\ \cos\alpha&=-\frac{4}{5}.\end{aligned}\right.$
