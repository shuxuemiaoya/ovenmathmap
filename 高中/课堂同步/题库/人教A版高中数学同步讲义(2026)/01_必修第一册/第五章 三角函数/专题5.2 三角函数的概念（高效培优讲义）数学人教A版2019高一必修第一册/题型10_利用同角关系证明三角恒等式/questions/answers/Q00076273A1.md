> [!faq]- 《专题5.2 三角函数的概念（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \text {右边} = \frac {1 - \tan 2 x}{1 + \tan 2 x} = \frac {1 - \frac {\sin 2 x}{\cos 2 x}}{1 + \frac {\sin 2 x}{\cos 2 x}} = \frac {\cos 2 x - \sin 2 x}{\cos 2 x + \sin 2 x} = \frac {(\cos 2 x - \sin 2 x) ^ {2}}{(\cos 2 x + \sin 2 x) (\cos 2 x - \sin 2 x)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\cos^ {2} 2 x + \sin^ {2} 2 x - 2 \sin 2 x \cos 2 x}{\cos^ {2} 2 x - \sin^ {2} 2 x} = \frac {1 - 2 \sin 2 x \cos 2 x}{\cos^ {2} 2 x - \sin^ {2} 2 x} = \text {左边}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以原等式成立．
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1 - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \tan \alpha}{1 + \tan \alpha}
> > $$
