> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $f(x)=\left\{\begin{aligned}\cos x,x&\in\left[-\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\\ \sin x,x&\in\left[\frac{\pi}{4},\pi\right]\end{aligned}\right.$ (2) $\frac{3-\sqrt{3}}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）当 $-\frac{\pi}{2} \leq x < \frac{\pi}{4}$ 时， $\frac{\pi}{4} < \frac{\pi}{2} - x \leq \pi$
> >
> > 又函数 $y = f(x)$ 的图象关于直线 $x = \frac{\pi}{4}$ 对称，∴ $f(x) = f\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - x\right) = \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - x\right) = \cos x$
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{c} \cos x, x \in \left[ - \frac {\pi}{2}, \frac {\pi}{4}\right) \\ \sin x, x \in \left[ \frac {\pi}{4}, \pi \right] \end{array} . \right.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 当 $\alpha \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, -\frac{\pi}{4}\right]$ , $-\alpha \in \left[\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ , $\therefore f(\alpha) = \cos \alpha$ , $f(-\alpha) = \sin (-\alpha) = -\sin \alpha$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {由} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} f (\alpha) - \sqrt {2} \cos \beta = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \\ f (- \alpha) + \sqrt {2} \sin \beta = - \frac {1}{2} \text {可得}: \therefore \left(\cos \alpha - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \left(- \sin \alpha + \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} = 2 \cos^ {2} \beta + 2 \sin^ {2} \beta = 2, \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 2 - \sqrt {3} \cos \alpha - \sin \alpha = 2, \therefore \sin \alpha = - \sqrt {3} \cos \alpha , \therefore \tan \alpha = - \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \alpha (\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha) = \frac {\sin^ {2} \alpha + \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {\tan^ {2} \alpha + \tan \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = \frac {3 - \sqrt {3}}{4}.
> > $$
> >
> > 另解，由 $\alpha \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, -\frac{\pi}{4}\right]$ ， $\tan \alpha = -\sqrt{3}$ 得 $\alpha = -\frac{\pi}{3}$ ， $\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$ ， $\sin \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \alpha (\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha) = - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cdot \left(- \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} + \frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {3 - \sqrt {3}}{4}.
> > $$
