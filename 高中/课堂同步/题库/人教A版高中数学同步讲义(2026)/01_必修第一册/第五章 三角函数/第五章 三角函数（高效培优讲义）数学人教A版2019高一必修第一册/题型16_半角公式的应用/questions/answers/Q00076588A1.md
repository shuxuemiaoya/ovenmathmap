> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析

> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)1 (2) $\frac{1}{2}$ $$ = \frac {\cos \theta (1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \theta)}{\cos \theta \cos 2 \theta} = \frac {\cos 2 \theta}{\cos 2 \theta} = 1. $$ $$ \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta - \frac {1}{2} \cos 2 \alpha \cos 2 \beta \tag {2} $$ $$ = \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta - \frac {1}{2} (2 \cos^ {2} \alpha - 1) (2 \cos^ {2} \beta - 1) $$ $$ = \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta - \frac {1}{2} (4 \cos^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta - 2 \cos^ {2} \alpha - 2 \cos^ {2} \beta + 1) $$ $$ = \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta - 2 \cos^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \beta - \frac {1}{2} $$ $$ = \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta - \cos^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \beta - \frac {1}{2} $$ $$ = \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta + (1 - \cos^ {2} \alpha) \cos^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha - \frac {1}{2} $$ $$ = \sin^ {2} \alpha \sin^ {2} \beta + \sin^ {2} \alpha \cos^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \alpha - \frac {1}{2} $$ $$ = \sin^ {2} \alpha (\sin^ {2} \beta + \cos^ {2} \beta) + \cos^ {2} \alpha - \frac {1}{2} $$ $$ = \sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha - \frac {1}{2} = 1 - \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{2}. $$
