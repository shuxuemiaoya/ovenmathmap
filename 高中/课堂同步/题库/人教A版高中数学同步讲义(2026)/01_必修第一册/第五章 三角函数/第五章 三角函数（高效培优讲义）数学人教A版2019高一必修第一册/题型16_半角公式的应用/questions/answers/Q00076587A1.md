> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)-5 (2) $\frac{33}{65}$ 或 $\frac{63}{65}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）方法一，因为 $\alpha$ 是锐角， $\cos\alpha=\frac{3}{5}$ ，所以 $\sin\alpha=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^{2}}=\frac{4}{5}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {2 \cos \frac {\alpha}{2} + \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}}{\cos \frac {\alpha}{2} - 3 \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {(2 \cos \frac {\alpha}{2} + \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}) \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}}{(\cos \frac {\alpha}{2} - 3 \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}) \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {\sin \alpha + \sin^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}}{\frac {1}{2} \sin \alpha - 3 \sin^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {\sin \alpha + \frac {1}{2} (1 - \cos \alpha)}{\frac {1}{2} \sin \alpha - \frac {3}{2} (1 - \cos \alpha)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\frac {4}{5} + \frac {1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac {3}{5}\right)}{\frac {1}{2} \times \frac {4}{5} - \frac {3}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac {3}{5}\right)} = \frac {1}{- \frac {1}{5}} = - 5.
> > $$
> >
> > 方法二，因为 $0 < \frac{\alpha}{2} < \frac{\pi}{4}$ ，所以 $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} = \sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos\alpha}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}, \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = \sqrt{\frac{1 + \cos\alpha}{2}} = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {2 \cos \frac {\alpha}{2} + \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}}{\cos \frac {\alpha}{2} - 3 \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {\frac {4 \sqrt {5}}{5} + \frac {\sqrt {5}}{5}}{\frac {2 \sqrt {5}}{5} - \frac {3 \sqrt {5}}{5}} = - 5.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 因为 $\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\frac{12}{13}$ ，所以 $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\pm\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{12}{13}\right)^{2}}=\pm\frac{5}{13}$
> >
> > 若 $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=-\frac{5}{13}$
> >
> > 则 $\cos \beta = \cos (\alpha +\beta -\alpha) = \cos (\alpha +\beta)\cos \alpha +\sin (\alpha +\beta)\sin \alpha$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {5}{1 3} \times \frac {3}{5} + \frac {1 2}{1 3} \times \frac {4}{5} = \frac {3 3}{6 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 若 $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\frac{5}{13}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \beta = \cos [ (\alpha + \beta) - \alpha ] = \cos (\alpha + \beta) \cos \alpha + \sin (\alpha + \beta) \sin \alpha
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {5}{1 3} \times \frac {3}{5} + \frac {1 2}{1 3} \times \frac {4}{5} = \frac {6 3}{6 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \beta = \frac {3 3}{6 5} \quad \text {或} \frac {6 3}{6 5}.
> > $$
