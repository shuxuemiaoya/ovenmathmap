题型 05 $\sin\alpha\pm\cos\alpha$ 和 $\sin\alpha\cdot\cos\alpha$ 之间的关系【典例1】已知 $\sin \alpha +\cos \alpha = -\frac{1}{5}$ ，且 $\alpha \in (0,\pi)$ ，则 $\frac{\sin\alpha\cdot\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha - \cos\alpha} = (\quad)$ A. $-\frac{12}{5}$ B. $\frac{12}{5}$ C. $-\frac{12}{35}$ D. $\frac{12}{35}$

【答案】C

【详解】因为 $\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha=-\frac{1}{5}$

所以 $\left(\sin \alpha +\cos \alpha\right)^2 = \frac{1}{25}$

故 $\sin^2\alpha + 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha + \cos^2\alpha = \frac{1}{25}$ ，又 $\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha = 1$ ，

所以 $\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = -\frac{12}{25}$ ，又 $\alpha \in (0,\pi)$

所以 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)$

所以 $\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha>0$

又 $\left(\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha\right)^2 = 1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 1 + \frac{24}{25} = \frac{49}{25}$

所以 $\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha=\frac{7}{5}$ ，所以 $\frac{\sin\alpha\cdot\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha}=\frac{-\frac{12}{25}}{\frac{7}{5}}=-\frac{12}{35}$ ·故选：C.

## 方法技巧

$(\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha)^2 = 1 + 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 1 + \sin 2\alpha$

$(\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)^2 = 1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 1 - \sin 2\alpha$

$(\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha)^2 + (\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)^2 = 2$

$$
\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha) (\cos \alpha + \sin \alpha) = \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha = \cos 2 \alpha
$$

$$
\frac {1}{\cos 2 \alpha} = \frac {1}{\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1}{(\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha) (\cos \alpha + \sin \alpha)}
$$

$$
\tan \alpha + \frac {1}{\tan \alpha} = \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} + \frac {\cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha} = \frac {1}{\sin \alpha \cos \alpha}
$$

注：利用 $\sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1$ 可实现正弦、余弦的互化，开方时要根据角 $\alpha$ 所在象限确定符号；利用 $= \tan \alpha$ 可以实现角 $\alpha$ 的弦切互化；利用 $(\sin \alpha \pm \cos \alpha)^{2} = 1\pm 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha$ 的关系可实现和积转化．
