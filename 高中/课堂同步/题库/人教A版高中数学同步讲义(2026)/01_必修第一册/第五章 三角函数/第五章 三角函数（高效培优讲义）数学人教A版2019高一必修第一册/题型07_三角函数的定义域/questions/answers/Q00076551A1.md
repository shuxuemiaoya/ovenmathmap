> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\left\{x\mid k\pi +\frac{\pi}{4} < x < \frac{3\pi}{4} +k\pi ,k\in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$ B. $\left\{x\mid k\pi +\frac{\pi}{4}\leq x < \frac{3\pi}{4} +k\pi ,k\in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$ C. $\left\{x\mid k\pi < x < \frac{\pi}{2} +k\pi ,k\in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$ D. $\left\{x\mid k\pi \leq x < \frac{\pi}{2} +k\pi ,k\in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得， $\tan \left(x - \frac{\pi}{4}\right)0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore k \pi \leq x - \frac {\pi}{4} <   \frac {\pi}{2} + k \pi , k \in \mathbf {Z}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > k \pi + \frac {\pi}{4} \leq x <   \frac {3 \pi}{4} + k \pi , k \in \mathbf {Z}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴函数的定义域为 $\left\{x\mid k\pi+\frac{\pi}{4}\leq x<\frac{3\pi}{4}+k\pi,k\in\mathbf{Z}\right\}$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
