> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $a = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos 6^{\circ} - \frac{1}{2}\sin 6^{\circ} = \sin 60^{\circ}\cos 6^{\circ} - \cos 60^{\circ}\sin 6^{\circ} = \sin (60^{\circ} - 6^{\circ}) = \sin 54^{\circ}$
> >
> > $$
> > b = \frac {2 \tan 2 7 ^ {\circ}}{1 - \tan^ {2} 2 7 ^ {\circ}} = \tan 5 4 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > c = \sqrt {\frac {1 - \cos 1 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{2}} = \sqrt {\frac {1 - (1 - 2 \sin^ {2} 5 5 ^ {\circ})}{2}} = \sqrt {\sin^ {2} 5 5 ^ {\circ}} = \sin 5 5 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > 因为函数 $y = \sin x$ ， $y = \tan x$ 在 $\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ 上单调递增，
> >
> > 所以 $\sin 54^{\circ} < \sin 55^{\circ} < \sin 90^{\circ} = 1$ ， $\tan 54^{\circ} > \tan 45^{\circ} = 1$
> >
> > 则 $a < c < b$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
