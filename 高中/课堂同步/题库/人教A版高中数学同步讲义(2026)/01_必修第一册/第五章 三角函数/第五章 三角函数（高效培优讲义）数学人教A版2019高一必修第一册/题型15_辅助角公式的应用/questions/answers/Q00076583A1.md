> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\left(-\frac{1}{2},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ (2) $-\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $f(x)=\left(\sqrt{3}\tan\frac{x}{2}+1\right)\cos^{2}\frac{x}{2}-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{3}\sin\frac{x}{2}+\cos\frac{x}{2}}{\cos\frac{x}{2}}\cdot\cos^{2}\frac{x}{2}-\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {3} \sin \frac {x}{2} \cos \frac {x}{2} + \cos^ {2} \frac {x}{2} - \frac {1}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin x + \frac {1}{2} \cos x = \sin \left(x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $x \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right)$ ，所以 $x + \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left(\frac{2\pi}{3}, \frac{7\pi}{6}\right)$ ，所以 $\sin \left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \in \left(-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ ，
> >
> > 即函数 $f(x)$ 的值域为 $\left(-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ .
> >
> > (2) 由 $f(x) = \sin \left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ , $x + \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left( \frac{2\pi}{3}, \frac{7\pi}{6} \right)$ ,
> >
> > 得 $\cos \left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = -\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)^2} = -\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos\left(2x-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\cos\left[2\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}\right]=\sin2\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=2\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\cos\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \times \left(- \frac {\sqrt {6}}{3}\right) = - \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}.
> > $$
