> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\cos \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} -\alpha\right) + \cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sqrt {2} \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6} + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin \left( \alpha + \frac{5\pi}{12} \right) = \cos \left( \alpha - \frac{\pi}{12} \right) = \frac{1}{4}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos\left(2\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=2\cos^{2}\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{12}\right)-1=-\frac{7}{8}$
> >
> > 故选 **D**
