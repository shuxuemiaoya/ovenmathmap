> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \sin \alpha \cdot \cos \alpha = \frac {\sin \alpha \cdot \cos \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {\tan \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = \frac {\frac {4}{3}}{\left(\frac {4}{3}\right) ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {1 2}{2 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
