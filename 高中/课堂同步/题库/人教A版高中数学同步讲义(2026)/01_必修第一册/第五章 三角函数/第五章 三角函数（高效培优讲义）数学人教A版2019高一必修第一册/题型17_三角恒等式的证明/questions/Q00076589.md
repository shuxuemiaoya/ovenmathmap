【典例1】已知 $\sin \alpha \sin \beta \neq 0$ ，且 $\sin (\alpha -\beta) = 2\sin \alpha \sin \beta$ ，证明：

(1) $\frac{1}{\tan\beta} - \frac{1}{\tan\alpha} = 2$

(2) $\cos (\alpha +\beta) = \sqrt{2}\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} +\alpha -\beta\right).$
