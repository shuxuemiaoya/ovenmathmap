> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1）证明见解析；（2）2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because 2\tan \alpha = 3\tan \beta$
> >
> > $\therefore \tan \alpha = \frac{3}{2} \tan \beta$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \tan (\alpha - \beta) = \frac {\tan \alpha - \tan \beta}{1 + \tan \alpha \tan \beta} = \frac {\frac {3}{2} \tan \beta - \tan \beta}{1 + \frac {3}{2} \tan^ {2} \beta}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan (\alpha - \beta) = \frac {\tan \beta}{2 + 3 \tan^ {2} \beta} = \frac {\frac {\sin \beta}{\cos \beta}}{2 + 3 \frac {\sin^ {2} \beta}{\cos^ {2} \beta}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan (\alpha - \beta) = \frac {\sin \beta \cos \beta}{2 \cos^ {2} \beta + 3 \sin^ {2} \beta} = \frac {\sin 2 \beta}{2 (1 + \cos 2 \beta) + 3 (1 - \cos 2 \beta)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan (\alpha - \beta) = \frac {\sin 2 \beta}{5 - \cos 2 \beta}
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $\because 3\sin^2\alpha + 2\sin^2\beta = 1$
> >
> > $\therefore 3\sin^{2}\alpha = \cos 2\beta$ ①
> >
> > $\because 3 \sin 2 \alpha - 2 \sin 2 \beta = 0$
> >
> > $\therefore 3 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \sin 2\beta$ ②
> > - **①**：、②式两式相除得： $\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=\frac{\cos2\beta}{\sin2\beta}$
> >
> > $\therefore \cos \alpha \cos 2\beta - \sin \alpha \sin 2\beta = 0$ ，即 $\cos (\alpha + 2\beta) = 0$
> >
> > 又 $Q\alpha$ ， $\beta$ 均为锐角，
> >
> > $$
> > 0 <   \alpha + 2 \beta <   \frac {3 \pi}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \alpha + 2 \beta = \frac {\pi}{2}.
> > $$
