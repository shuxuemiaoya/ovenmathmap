> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(1)^{\sqrt{3}}$ ; $(2)^{\frac{63}{65}}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）原式 $= \tan (20^{\circ} + 40^{\circ})(1 - \tan 20^{\circ}\cdot \tan 40^{\circ}) + \sqrt{3}\tan 20^{\circ}\cdot \tan 40^{\circ}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {3} - \sqrt {3} \tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ} + \sqrt {3} \tan 2 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot \tan 4 0 ^ {\circ} = \sqrt {3}
> > $$
> >
> > $\sin\alpha=\frac{3}{5},\alpha$ 是锐角，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \alpha = \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} \alpha} = \sqrt {1 - \left(\frac {3}{5}\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {4}{5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $\alpha, \beta$ 是锐角，所以 $\alpha + \beta \in (0, \pi)$ .
> >
> > 又因为 $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\frac{5}{13}$ ，所以 $\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}(\alpha+\beta)}=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{5}{13}\right)^{2}}=\frac{12}{13}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\sin (2\alpha +\beta) = \sin [(\alpha +\beta) + \alpha ] = \sin (\alpha +\beta)\cos \alpha +\cos (\alpha +\beta)\sin \alpha$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1 2}{1 3} \times \frac {4}{5} + \frac {5}{1 3} \times \frac {3}{5} = \frac {6 3}{6 5}.
> > $$
