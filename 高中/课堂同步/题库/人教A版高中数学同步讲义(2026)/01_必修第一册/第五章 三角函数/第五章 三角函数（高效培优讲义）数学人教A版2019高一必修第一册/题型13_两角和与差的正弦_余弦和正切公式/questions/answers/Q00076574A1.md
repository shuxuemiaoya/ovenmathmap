> [!faq]- 《第五章 三角函数（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版2019高一必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\frac{16}{65}$ B. $\frac{33}{65}$ C. $\frac{56}{65}$ D. $\frac{63}{65}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\alpha\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ， $\beta\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)$ ，所以 $\alpha+\beta\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$
> >
> > 又 $\sin (\alpha +\beta) = -\frac{3}{5}$ ，则 $\alpha +\beta \in \left(\pi ,\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ ， $\cos (\alpha +\beta) = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2(\alpha + \beta)} = -\frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 又 $\cos \beta = -\frac{5}{13}$ ，所以 $\sin \beta = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\beta} = \frac{12}{13}$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin \alpha = \sin \left[\left(\alpha +\beta\right) - \beta \right] = \sin \left(\alpha +\beta\right)\cos \beta -\cos \left(\alpha +\beta\right)\sin \beta$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {3}{5} \times \left(- \frac {5}{1 3}\right) - \left(- \frac {4}{5}\right) \times \frac {1 2}{1 3} = \frac {6 3}{6 5}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
