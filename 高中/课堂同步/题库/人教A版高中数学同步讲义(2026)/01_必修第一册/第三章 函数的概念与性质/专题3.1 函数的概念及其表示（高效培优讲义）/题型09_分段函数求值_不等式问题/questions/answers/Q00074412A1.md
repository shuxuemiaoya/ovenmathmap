> [!faq]- 《专题3.1 函数的概念及其表示（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意， $f\left(-\frac{1}{1024}\right) = 2f\left(\frac{1}{512}\right) = 2^2 f\left(-\frac{1}{256}\right) = \dots = 2^{10}f\left[(-2)^{10}\times \left(-\frac{1}{1024}\right)\right]$ $= 2^{10}f(-1) = 1024\times \frac{1}{4} = 256.$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
