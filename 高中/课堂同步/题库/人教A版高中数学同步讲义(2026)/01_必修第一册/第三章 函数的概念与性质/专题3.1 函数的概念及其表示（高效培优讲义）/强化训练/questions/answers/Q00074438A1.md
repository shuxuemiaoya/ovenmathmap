> [!faq]- 《专题3.1 函数的概念及其表示（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)1；1 (2)证明见解析 (3)1098
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）解：因为 $f(x) = \frac{x^2}{1 + x^2}$
> >
> > 所以 $f(2)+f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)=\frac{2^{2}}{1+2^{2}}+\frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}}{1+\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}}=1$
> >
> > $$
> > f (3) + f \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) = \frac {3 ^ {2}}{1 + 3 ^ {2}} + \frac {\left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2}}{1 + \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2}} = 1
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 证明： $f(x)+f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)=\frac{x^{2}}{1+x^{2}}+\frac{\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^{2}}{1+\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^{2}}=\frac{x^{2}}{1+x^{2}}+\frac{1}{x^{2}+1}=\frac{x^{2}+1}{x^{2}+1}=1$
> >
> > (3) 解：由 (2) 知 $f(x)+f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)=1$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > f (2) + f \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) = 1, f (3) + f \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) = 1, f (4) + f \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) = 1, \dots , f (1 0 9 9) + f \left(\frac {1}{1 0 9 9}\right) = 1 \text {, 所以}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f (2) + f \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) + f (3) + f \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) + \dots + f (1 0 9 9) + f \left(\frac {1}{1 0 9 9}\right) = 1 0 9 8
> > $$
