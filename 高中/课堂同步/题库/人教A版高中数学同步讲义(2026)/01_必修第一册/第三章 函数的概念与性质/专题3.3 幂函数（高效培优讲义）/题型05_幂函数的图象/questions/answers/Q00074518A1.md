> [!faq]- 《专题3.3 幂函数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/9a8075f50d3cf088ba483ded59d431da6e0275e8293f4df4a9e4742dfb031058.jpg) B ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/e8a45b986e5124bac2434adf806e12c182984cb171accdc4eb2582124b84aeeb.jpg) C ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/00aed7085f468eec518ed06102c253095a914aa9fd4c58cd1168c5e69783ff75.jpg) D ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/5e4b1a78c1c66b070592c2ebc0659537da9a3bb5b97d0211c46a3c4abf45a335.jpg)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $x = 0$ 时，因为 $y = \sqrt{x}$ 为单调递增函数，
> >
> > $y = -\sqrt{x}$ 与 $y = \sqrt{x}$ 关于 x 轴对称，所以 $y = -\sqrt{x}$ 单调递减，
> >
> > 当 $x < 0$ 时，因为 $y = \frac{2}{x}$ 为单调递减函数，
> >
> > $y = -\frac{2}{x}$ 与 $y = \frac{2}{x}$ 关于 $x$ 轴对称，所以 $y = -\frac{2}{x}$ 单调递增，
> >
> > 综上所述只有选项 $^{C}$ 满足条件·
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
