> [!faq]- 《专题3.3 幂函数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $f(x)$ 为增函数，不妨设 $x_{1} > x_{2}$ ，
> >
> > 则 $\left|f\left(x_1\right) - f\left(x_2\right)\right| = f\left(x_1\right) - f\left(x_2\right) = 2$ ，即 $x_{1}^{3} - x_{2}^{3} = \left(x_{1} - x_{2}\right)\left(x_{1}^{2} + x_{1}x_{2} + x_{2}^{2}\right) = \left(x_{1} - x_{2}\right)\left[\left(x_{1} - x_{2}\right)^{2} + 3x_{1}x_{2}\right] = 2$
> >
> > $3x_{1}x_{2}=\frac{2}{x_{1}-x_{2}}-\left(x_{1}-x_{2}\right)^{2}$ 变形得
> >
> > 若 $x_{1}, x_{2}$ 异号，则 $3x_{1}x_{2} = -3x_{1}(-x_{2}) - 3\left(\frac{x_{1} - x_{2}}{2}\right)^{2}$
> >
> > 即 $\frac{2}{x_1 - x_2} - (x_1 - x_2)^2 - 3\left(\frac{x_1 - x_2}{2}\right)^2$
> >
> > 解得 $x_{1} - x_{2}\leq 2$ ，当且仅当 $x_{1} = 1,x_{2} = -1$ 时，等号成立.
> >
> > 若 $x_{1}, x_{2}$ 同号或 $x_{1}, x_{2}$ 中有一个为 0，则 $3x_{1}x_{2} = \frac{2}{x_{1} - x_{2}} - (x_{1} - x_{2})^{2} = 0$ ，解得 $x_{1} - x_{2} \leq 2^{\frac{1}{3}}$ .
> >
> > 综上， $\left|x_{1}-x_{2}\right|$ 的最大值为2.
> >
> > 故答案为：2.
