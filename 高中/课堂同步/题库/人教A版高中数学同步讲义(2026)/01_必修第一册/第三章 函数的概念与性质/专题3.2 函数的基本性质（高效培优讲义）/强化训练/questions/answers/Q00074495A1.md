> [!faq]- 《专题3.2 函数的基本性质（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) a = 1, b = 0 ; (2)证明见解析； (3) $t \in \left[\frac{1}{2}, 1\right)$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题设 $f(0) = b = 0$ ， $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{a + b}{\frac{5}{4}} = \frac{4}{5}$ ，则 $a = 1$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $f(x)=\frac{2x}{x^{2}+1}$ ，则 $f(-x)=\frac{-2x}{(-x)^{2}+1}=-\frac{2x}{x^{2}+1}=-f(x)$ ，满足题设，
> >
> > 所以 a=1, b=0 ;
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) $f(x)=\frac{2x}{x^{2}+1}$ ，令 $-1£x_{1}<x_{2}£1$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > f \left(x _ {2}\right) - f \left(x _ {1}\right) = \frac {2 x _ {2}}{x _ {2} ^ {2} + 1} - \frac {2 x _ {1}}{x _ {1} ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {2 x _ {2} \left(x _ {1} ^ {2} + 1\right) - 2 x _ {1} \left(x _ {2} ^ {2} + 1\right)}{\left(x _ {1} ^ {2} + 1\right) \left(x _ {2} ^ {2} + 1\right)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {2 x _ {1} x _ {2} \left(x _ {1} - x _ {2}\right) + 2 \left(x _ {2} - x _ {1}\right)}{\left(x _ {1} ^ {2} + 1\right) \left(x _ {2} ^ {2} + 1\right)} = \frac {2 x _ {1} x _ {2} \left(x _ {1} - x _ {2}\right) + 2 \left(x _ {2} - x _ {1}\right)}{\left(x _ {1} ^ {2} + 1\right) \left(x _ {2} ^ {2} + 1\right)} = \frac {2 \left(x _ {2} - x _ {1}\right) \left(1 - x _ {1} x _ {2}\right)}{\left(x _ {1} ^ {2} + 1\right) \left(x _ {2} ^ {2} + 1\right)}
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $x_{2} - x_{1} > 0,1 - x_{1}x_{2} > 0,(x_{1}^{2} + 1)(x_{2}^{2} + 1) > 0$ ，则 $f(x_{2}) > f(x_{1})$
> >
> > 所以函数 $f(x)$ 在 $[-1, 1]$ 上单调递增；
> >
> > (3) 由题设 $f(t^2 - 1) < -f(2t - 2) = f(2 - 2t)$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} t ^ {2} - 1 <   2 - 2 t \\ - 1 \leq t ^ {2} - 1 \leq 1 \\ - 1 \leq 2 - 2 t \leq 1 \end{array} \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} t ^ {2} + 2 t - 3 = (t + 3) (t - 1) <   0 \\ - \sqrt {2} \leq t \leq \sqrt {2} \\ \frac {1}{2} \leq t \leq \frac {3}{2} \end{array} \right. \Rightarrow \frac {1}{2} \leq t <   1 \right.,
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{2} \leq t < 1$ ，即 $t \in \left[\frac{1}{2}, 1\right)$ .
