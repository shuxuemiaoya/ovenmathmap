> [!faq]- 《专题01_函数的概念及其表示的四大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 函数 $f(x) = \frac{x}{x + 1} (x > 0)$ ，
> >
> > 则 $f_{1}(x) = f(x) = \frac{x}{x + 1} (x > 0)$
> >
> > $$
> > f _ {2} (x) = f \left(\frac {x}{x + 1}\right) = \frac {\frac {x}{x + 1}}{\frac {x}{x + 1} + 1} = \frac {x}{2 x + 1} (x > 0),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f _ {3} (x) = f \left(f _ {2} (x)\right) = \frac {\frac {x}{2 x + 1}}{\frac {x}{2 x + 1} + 1} = \frac {x}{3 x + 1} (x > 0),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f _ {n} (x) = f \left(f _ {n - 1} (x)\right) = \frac {x}{n x + 1} (x > 0),
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $f_{2024}(x)=\frac{x}{2024x+1}(x>0)$
> >
> > 故选 **A**
