> [!faq]- 《第三章 函数的概念与性质（高效培优单元测试·提升卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $f(x)$ 是偶函数 B. $f(x)$ 的图象关于点 $(1,2)$ 对称 C. $f(3)=6$ D. $f(1) + f(2) + f(3) + \mathrm{L} + f(10) = 110$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 令 x = y = 0 ，得 $f^{2}(0) + f^{2}(0) = 2\left[f^{2}(0) + f^{2}(0)\right], \therefore f(0) = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {令} x = 0 \text {, 得} f ^ {2} (y) + f ^ {2} (- y) = 2 [ f ^ {2} (0) + f ^ {2} (y) ] \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (y) + f ^ {2} (- y) = 2 f ^ {2} (y) \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (- y) = f ^ {2} (y)
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $f(x) = 0$ ，所以 $f(-y) = f(y)$ ，所以 $f(x)$ 是偶函数，故 $A$ 对；
> >
> > $$
> > x = y = 1 \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (2) + f ^ {2} (0) = 2 [ f ^ {2} (1) + f ^ {2} (1) ] \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (2) = 2 (4 + 4) = 1 6 \Rightarrow f (2) = 4
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f ^ {2} (2 + 1) + f ^ {2} (2 - 1) = 2 [ f ^ {2} (2) + f ^ {2} (1) ], \therefore f ^ {2} (3) = 3 6 \therefore f (3) = 6
> > $$
> >
> > 令 $x = 2, y = 1$ ，得
> >
> > $$
> > f (- 1) = f (1) = 2, f (- 1) + f (3) \neq 4
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $f(x)$ 的图象不关于点 $(1,2)$ 对称，故 B 错，C 对；
> >
> > $$
> > \text {令} x = y = 2 \quad , \text {得} x = y = 2 \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (4) + f ^ {2} (0) = 2 [ f ^ {2} (2) + f ^ {2} (2) ] \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (4) = 2 (1 6 + 1 6) = 6 4 \Rightarrow f (4) = 8
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x = 3, y = 2 \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (5) + f ^ {2} (1) = 2 [ f ^ {2} (3) + f ^ {2} (2) ] \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (5) = 1 0 0 \Rightarrow f (5) = 1 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x = y = 3 \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (6) + f ^ {2} (0) = 2 [ f ^ {2} (3) + f ^ {2} (3) ] \Rightarrow f ^ {2} (6) = 1 4 4 \Rightarrow f (6) = 1 2
> > $$
> >
> > 同理可得 $f(7)=14, f(8)=16, f(9)=18, f(10)=20$
> >
> > 所以 $f(1)+f(2)+f(3)+\cdots+f(10)=2+4+6+8+\cdots+20=110$ ，故 D 对；
> >
> > 故选 **ACD**
