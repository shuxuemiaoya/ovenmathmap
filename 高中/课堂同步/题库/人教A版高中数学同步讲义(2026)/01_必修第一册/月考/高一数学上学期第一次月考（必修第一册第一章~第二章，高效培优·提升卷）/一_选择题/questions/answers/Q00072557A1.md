> [!faq]- 《高一数学上学期第一次月考（必修第一册第一章~第二章，高效培优·提升卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ① $\left\{x\mid x^{2}+1=0\right\}=\left\{x\left|\begin{array}{l}\left\{2x+4>0\right\}\\x+3<0\end{array}\right.\right\}$ ; ② $\left\{y\mid y=2x^{2}+1\right\}=\left\{x\mid y=2x^{2}+1\right\}$ ; ③ $\left\{x\left|x=\frac{1-(-1)^{n}}{2},n\in N\right\}=\left\{x\left|-1<x<2,x\in N\right.\right\}\right.$ ④ $\left\{(x,y)\mid y=\sqrt{x-1}+\sqrt{1-x}\right\}=\left\{0,1\right\}$ A.0个 B.1个 C.2个 D.3个
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\left\{x\mid x^{2}+1=0\right\}=\varnothing,\left\{x\left|\begin{array}{l}2x+4>0\\x+3<0\end{array}\right.\right\}=\varnothing$ ，所以①正确；
> >
> > 因为 $\left\{y\mid y=2x^{2}+1\right\}=\left[1,+\infty\right)$ ， $\left\{x\mid y=2x^{2}+1\right\}=R$ ，所以②不正确；
> >
> > 因为 $\left\{x\mid x=\frac{1-(-1)^{n}}{2},n\in\mathbb{N}\right\}=\left\{0,1\right\}$ ， $\left\{x\mid-1<x<2,x\in\mathbb{N}\right\}=\left\{0,1\right\}$ ，故③正确； $\left\{(x,y)\mid y=\sqrt{x-1}+\sqrt{1-x}\right\}=\left\{(1,0)\right\}\neq\left\{0,1\right\}$ ，故④错误.
> >
> > 故选 **C**
