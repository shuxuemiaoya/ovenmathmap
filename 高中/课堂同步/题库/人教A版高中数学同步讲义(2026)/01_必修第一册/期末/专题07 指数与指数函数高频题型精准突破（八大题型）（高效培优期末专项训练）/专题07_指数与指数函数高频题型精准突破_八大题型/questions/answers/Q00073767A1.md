> [!faq]- 《专题07 指数与指数函数高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据各函数解析式，应用作差法、基本不等式、指数的运算性质比较大小，即可判断
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 对于 $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ 且 $x=0$ ， $f\left(\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}\right)-\frac{f(x_{1})+f(x_{2})}{2}=\sqrt{\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}}-\frac{\sqrt{x_{1}}+\sqrt{x_{2}}}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 而 $2(x_{1} + x_{2})(\sqrt{x_{1}} + \sqrt{x_{2}})^{2}\Leftrightarrow \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2} (\frac{\sqrt{x_{1}} + \sqrt{x_{2}}}{2})^{2}\Leftrightarrow \sqrt{\frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2}}\frac{\sqrt{x_{1}} + \sqrt{x_{2}}}{2},$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $x_{1} = x_{2}$ 时取等号，故 $f\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}\right) - \frac{f(x_1) + f(x_2)}{2} 0$ ，A不是；
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = \frac {1}{x} (x > 0), f \left(\frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2}\right) - \frac {f \left(x _ {1}\right) + f \left(x _ {2}\right)}{2} = \frac {2}{x _ {1} + x _ {2}} - \frac {\frac {1}{x _ {1}} + \frac {1}{x _ {2}}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{2}{x_1 + x_2} -\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2x_1x_2} = \frac{4x_1x_2 - (x_1 + x_2)^2}{2x_1x_2(x_1 + x_2)} = -\frac{(x_1 - x_2)^2}{2x_1x_2(x_1 + x_2)}\leq 0$ ，当且仅当 $x_{1} = x_{2}$ 时取等号，B是，
> >
> > 对于 $f(x) = x^2$ ， $f\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}\right) - \frac{f(x_1) + f(x_2)}{2} = \frac{(x_1 + x_2)^2}{4} - \frac{x_1^2 + x_2^2}{2}$
> >
> > $= \frac{(x_{1} + x_{2})^{2} - 2(x_{1}^{2} + x_{2}^{2})}{4} = \frac{2x_{1}x_{2} - x_{1}^{2} - x_{2}^{2}}{4} = -\frac{(x_{1} - x_{2})^{2}}{4}\leq 0$ ，当且仅当 $x_{1} = x_{2}$ 时取等号，C是；
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = 2 ^ {x}, f \left(\frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2}\right) - \frac {f \left(x _ {1}\right) + f \left(x _ {2}\right)}{2} = 2 ^ {\frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2}} - \frac {2 ^ {x _ {1}} + 2 ^ {x _ {2}}}{2} = \sqrt {2 ^ {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}} - \frac {2 ^ {x _ {1}} + 2 ^ {x _ {2}}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $\leq \sqrt{2^{x_1 + x_2}} - \frac{2\sqrt{2^{x_1} \cdot 2^{x_2}}}{2} = \sqrt{2^{x_1 + x_2}} - \sqrt{2^{x_1 + x_2}} = 0$ ，当且仅当 $x_1 = x_2$ 时取等号， $D$ 是·
> >
> > 故选 **A**
