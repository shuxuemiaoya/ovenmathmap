> [!faq]- 《高一数学上学期期末模拟卷（必修第一册，高效培优·提升卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\frac{7}{3}$ B. $\frac{10}{3}$ C.4 D.6
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据诱导公式可得 $\tan (\pi +\theta) = \tan \theta = 2$
> >
> > 即 $\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} = 2$ ，所以 $\sin \theta = 2\cos \theta$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\sin^ {3} \theta + \sin \theta}{\cos^ {3} \theta + \sin \theta \cos^ {2} \theta} = \frac {\sin \theta (\sin^ {2} \theta + 1)}{\cos^ {2} \theta (\cos \theta + \sin \theta)} = \frac {2 \cos \theta [ (2 \cos \theta) ^ {2} + 1 ]}{\cos^ {2} \theta (\cos \theta + 2 \cos \theta)} = \frac {2 (4 \cos^ {2} \theta + 1)}{3 \cos^ {2} \theta}
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $\sin \theta = 2\cos \theta$ ，则 $\sin^2\theta = 4\cos^2\theta$ ，而又因为 $\sin^2\theta +\cos^2\theta = 1$
> >
> > 所以 $4\cos^{2}\theta + \cos^{2}\theta = 1, \cos^{2}\theta = \frac{1}{5}$
> >
> > 将 $\cos^{2}\theta=\frac{1}{5}$ 代入得： $\frac{\sin^{3}\theta+\sin\theta}{\cos^{3}\theta+\sin\theta\cos^{2}\theta}=\frac{2(4\cos^{2}\theta+1)}{3\cos^{2}\theta}=\frac{2(4\times\frac{1}{5}+1)}{3\times\frac{1}{5}}=6$
> >
> > 故选 **D**
