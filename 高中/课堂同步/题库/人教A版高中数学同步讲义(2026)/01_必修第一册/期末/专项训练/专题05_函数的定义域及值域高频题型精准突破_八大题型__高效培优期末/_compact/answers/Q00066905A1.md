> [!faq]- 《专题05_函数的定义域及值域高频题型精准突破_八大题型__高效培优期末》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D $$ f (x) = \max \left\{x + 1, - 2 x + 4 \right\} = \left\{ \begin{array}{c c} x + 1, x & 1, \\ - 2 x + 4, x < 1, \end{array} \right. $$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 令 $x + 1 - 2x + 4$ ，解得 $x - 1$ ，则 $f(x) = \max \{x + 1, - 2x + 4\} = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll}x + 1,x & 1,\\ -2x + 4,x <   1, \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 因此 $f(-2) = -2 \times (-2) + 4 = 8$ ，故 $f[f(-2)] = f(8) = 9$
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
