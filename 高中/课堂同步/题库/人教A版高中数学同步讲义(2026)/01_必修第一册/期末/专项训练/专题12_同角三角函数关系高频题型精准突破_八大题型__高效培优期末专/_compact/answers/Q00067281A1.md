> [!faq]- 《专题12_同角三角函数关系高频题型精准突破_八大题型__高效培优期末专》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用同角的正余弦平方关系化为齐次式可求值·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin^3\alpha + \sin\alpha\cos^2\alpha + \cos\alpha} = \frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha (\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha) + \cos\alpha} = \frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha}$ $= \frac{1}{\tan\alpha + 1} = \frac{1}{\frac{1}{4} + 1} = \frac{4}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
