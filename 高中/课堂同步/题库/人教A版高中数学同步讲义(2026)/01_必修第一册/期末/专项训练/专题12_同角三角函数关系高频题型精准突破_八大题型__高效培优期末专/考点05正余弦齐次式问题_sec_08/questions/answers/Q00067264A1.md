> [!faq]- 《专题12_同角三角函数关系高频题型精准突破_八大题型__高效培优期末专》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{16}{13}$ (2) $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \mid \cos \alpha \mid = - \cos \alpha \cdot f (\alpha) = \sqrt {\frac {1 + \sin \alpha}{1 - \sin \alpha}} - \sqrt {\frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{1 + \sin \alpha}} = \sqrt {\frac {(1 + \sin \alpha) ^ {2}}{(1 - \sin \alpha) (1 + \sin \alpha)}} - \sqrt {\frac {(1 - \sin \alpha) ^ {2}}{(1 + \sin \alpha) (1 - \sin \alpha)}} \\ = \sqrt {\frac {(1 + \sin \alpha) ^ {2}}{1 - \sin^ {2} \alpha}} - \sqrt {\frac {(1 - \sin \alpha) ^ {2}}{1 - \sin^ {2} \alpha}} = \frac {1 + \sin \alpha}{| \cos \alpha |} - \frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{| \cos \alpha |} = \frac {2 \sin \alpha}{| \cos \alpha |} = - 2 \tan \alpha . \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $f(\alpha) = -2 \tan \alpha = 3$ ，所以 $\tan \alpha = -\frac{3}{2}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {4}{3} \sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha = \frac {\frac {4}{3} \sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {\frac {4}{3} \tan^ {2} \alpha + 1}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = \frac {\frac {4}{3} \times \left(- \frac {3}{2}\right) ^ {2} + 1}{\left(- \frac {3}{2}\right) ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {1 6}{1 3}.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $\cos^2\alpha f(\alpha) = \cos^2\alpha \times (-2\tan \alpha) = -\cos^2\alpha \times \frac{2\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = -2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$ ，则 $\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = -\frac{1}{4}$ .
> >
> > 因为 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角，所以 $\sin \alpha > 0, \cos \alpha < 0, \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha > 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha = \sqrt {(\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {1 - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha} = \sqrt {1 + 2 \times \frac {1}{4}} = \frac {\sqrt {6}}{2}
> > $$
