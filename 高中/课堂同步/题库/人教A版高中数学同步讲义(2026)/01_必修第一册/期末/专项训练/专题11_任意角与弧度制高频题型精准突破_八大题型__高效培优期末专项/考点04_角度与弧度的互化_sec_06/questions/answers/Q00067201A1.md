> [!faq]- 《专题11_任意角与弧度制高频题型精准突破_八大题型__高效培优期末专项》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\left\{\alpha \mid \alpha = \frac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$ B. $\left\{\alpha \mid \alpha = \frac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$ C. $\left\{\alpha \mid \alpha = -\frac{\pi}{4} + 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$ D. $\left\{\alpha \mid \alpha = -\frac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 将 $2025^{\circ}$ 化成弧度结合选项即可求解；
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $2025^{\circ}$ 用弧度制可表示为 $12\pi - \frac{3\pi}{4}$ ，
> >
> > 所以与 $2025^{\circ}$ 角的终边相同的角构成的集合为 $\left\{\alpha \mid \alpha = -\frac{3\pi}{4} + 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}\right\}$
> >
> > 故选 **D**
