> [!faq]- 《专题12 同角三角函数关系高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\frac{1 + \sqrt{2}}{2}$ B. $\sqrt{2} - 1$ C. $\frac{1 + \sqrt{2}}{8}$ D. $\frac{\sqrt{2} - 1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 把 $\frac{\pi}{4}$ 代入函数解析式，化简可得答案
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $f\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{1-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}+50\sqrt{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}+100+50\sqrt{2}}=\frac{2+99\sqrt{2}}{200+101\sqrt{2}}$ $=\frac{2+99\sqrt{2}}{2+99\sqrt{2}+99\times2+2\sqrt{2}}=\frac{2+99\sqrt{2}}{(2+99\sqrt{2})(\sqrt{2}+1)}$ $=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}+1}=\sqrt{2}-1.$
> >
> > 故选 **B**
