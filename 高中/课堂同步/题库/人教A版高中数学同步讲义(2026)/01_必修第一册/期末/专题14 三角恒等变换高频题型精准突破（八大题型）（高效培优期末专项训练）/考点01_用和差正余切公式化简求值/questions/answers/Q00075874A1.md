> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{2\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{3}}{6}$ (2) $\frac{4+6\sqrt{2}}{15}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）由同角三角函数基本关系可得 $\cos \alpha = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ，然后由两角差的余弦公式可得答案；
> >
> > (2) 由同角三角函数基本关系可得 $\sin \beta = -\frac{3}{5}$ ，然后由两角差的正弦公式可得答案.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因 $\sin \alpha = \frac{1}{3},\alpha \in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，则 $\cos \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2\alpha} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{1}{9}} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}.$
> >
> > 从而 $\cos \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \cos \alpha \cos \frac{\pi}{3} +\sin \alpha \sin \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\times \frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{3}\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{2\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}}{6};$
> >
> > (2) 因 $\cos\beta=\frac{4}{5},\beta\in\left(-\frac{\pi}{2},0\right)$ ，则 $\sin\beta=-\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}\beta}=-\sqrt{1-\frac{16}{25}}=-\frac{3}{5}$ .
> >
> > 从而 $\sin (\alpha -\beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta -\cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{3}\times \frac{4}{5} -\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\times \left(-\frac{3}{5}\right) = \frac{4 + 6\sqrt{2}}{15}.$
