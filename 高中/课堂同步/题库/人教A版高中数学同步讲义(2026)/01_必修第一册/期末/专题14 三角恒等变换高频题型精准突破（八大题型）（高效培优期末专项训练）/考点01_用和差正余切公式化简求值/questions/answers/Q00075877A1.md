> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{117}{125}$ (2) $\frac{7}{25}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）根据 $\frac{3(\alpha - \beta)}{2} = \left(\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2}\right) + \left(\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta\right)$ ，然后根据两角和的正弦公式计算即可；
> >
> > (2) 先计算 $\sin\left(\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}\right)$ ，然后利用二倍角的余弦公式计算即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $\alpha -\frac{\beta}{2},\frac{\alpha}{2} -\beta$ 均为第一象限的角， $\sin \left(\alpha -\frac{\beta}{2}\right) = \frac{3}{5},\cos \left(\frac{\alpha}{2} -\beta\right) = \frac{7}{25}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos\left(\alpha-\frac{\beta}{2}\right)=\sqrt{1-\sin^{2}\left(\alpha-\frac{\beta}{2}\right)}=\frac{4}{5},\sin\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}-\beta\right)=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}-\beta\right)}=\frac{24}{25}$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \frac {3 (\alpha - \beta)}{2} = \sin \left[ \left(\alpha - \frac {\beta}{2}\right) + \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \beta\right) \right] = \sin \left(\alpha - \frac {\beta}{2}\right) \cos \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \beta\right) + \cos \left(\alpha - \frac {\beta}{2}\right) \sin \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} - \beta\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin \frac{3(\alpha - \beta)}{2} = \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{7}{25} + \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{24}{25} = \frac{117}{125}$
> >
> > (2) $\sin \left(\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}\right) = \sin \left[\left(\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2}\right) - \left(\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta\right)\right] = \sin \left(\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2}\right) \cos \left(\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta\right) - \cos \left(\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2}\right) \sin \left(\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta\right)$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\sin\left(\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}\right)=\frac{3}{5}\times\frac{7}{25}-\frac{4}{5}\times\frac{24}{25}=-\frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=1-2\sin^{2}\left(\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}\right)=\frac{7}{25}$
