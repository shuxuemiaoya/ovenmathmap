> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先根据三角恒等变换求 $a, b, c^2$ 的值，再利用作差法比较 $a, b, c$ 的大小
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $a = \sin^2\frac{\pi}{6} -\sin^2\frac{\pi}{12} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 -\frac{1 - \cos\frac{\pi}{6}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3} - 1}{4} >0$
> >
> > $$
> > b = \tan \frac {\pi}{1 2} = \tan \left(\frac {\pi}{3} - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \frac {\tan \frac {\pi}{3} - \tan \frac {\pi}{4}}{1 + \tan \frac {\pi}{3} \tan \frac {\pi}{4}} = \frac {\sqrt {3} - 1}{1 + \sqrt {3}} = 2 - \sqrt {3} > 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore a - b = \frac{\sqrt{3} - 1}{4} - (2 - \sqrt{3}) = \frac{5\sqrt{3} - 9}{4} < 0$ ，则 $a < b$ ，
> >
> > 又： $c = \sin \frac{\pi}{8} >0,\cos \frac{\pi}{4} = 1 - 2\sin^2\frac{\pi}{8} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，则 $c^2 = \sin^2\frac{\pi}{8} = \frac{2 - \sqrt{2}}{4}$
> >
> > $c^{2}-b^{2}=\frac{2-\sqrt{2}}{4}-\left(2-\sqrt{3}\right)^{2}>\frac{2-1.5}{4}-0.3^{2}>0$ ，则 $c^{2}>b^{2}$ ，即c>b
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a <   b <   c
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
