> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 令 $\beta=\frac{\alpha}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}$ ，联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\tan\beta&=\frac{\sin\beta}{\cos\beta}=\frac{1}{2}\\ \sin^{2}\beta+\cos^{2}\beta=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解出 的值，再利用诱导公式以及倍角公式即得
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 令 $\beta=\frac{\alpha}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}$ ，则 $\tan\beta=\frac{1}{2}$ ，
> > 由 $\beta=\frac{\alpha}{2}-\frac{\pi}{4}$ ，可得 $\frac{\alpha}{2}=\beta+\frac{\pi}{4}$ ，进而 $\alpha=2\beta+\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，
> > 因此， $2\alpha=4\beta+\pi$ 利用诱导公式， $\sin(4\beta+\pi)=-\sin4\beta$
> >
> > 联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\tan\beta&=\frac{\sin\beta}{\cos\beta}=\frac{1}{2}\\ \sin^{2}\beta+\cos^{2}\beta&=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得：
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} {\sin \beta = \frac {1}{\sqrt {5}}} \\ {\cos \beta = \frac {2}{\sqrt {5}}} \end{array} \text {或} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} {\sin \beta = - \frac {1}{\sqrt {5}}} \\ {\cos \beta = - \frac {2}{\sqrt {5}}} \end{array} \right. \right..
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\sin\beta&=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\\ \cos\beta&=\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}\end{aligned}\right.$ 时，
> >
> > $$
> > \sin 2 \beta = 2 \sin \beta \cos \beta = 2 \cdot \frac {1}{\sqrt {5}} \cdot \frac {2}{\sqrt {5}} = \frac {4}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos 2 \beta = \cos^ {2} \beta - \sin^ {2} \beta = \left(\frac {2}{\sqrt {5}}\right) ^ {2} - \left(\frac {1}{\sqrt {5}}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {4}{5} - \frac {1}{5} = \frac {3}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $\sin 4\beta = 2\sin 2\beta \cos 2\beta = 2 \cdot \frac{4}{5} \cdot \frac{3}{5} = \frac{24}{25}$
> >
> > 代入得 $\sin 2\alpha = -\sin 4\beta = -\frac{24}{25}$ ;
> >
> > $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sin \beta = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} \\ \cos \beta = -\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}} \end{array} \right.$ 时，
> >
> > $$
> > \sin 2 \beta = 2 \sin \beta \cos \beta = 2 \cdot \left(- \frac {1}{\sqrt {5}}\right) \cdot \left(- \frac {2}{\sqrt {5}}\right) = \frac {4}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos 2 \beta = \cos^ {2} \beta - \sin^ {2} \beta = \left(- \frac {2}{\sqrt {5}}\right) ^ {2} - \left(- \frac {1}{\sqrt {5}}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {4}{5} - \frac {1}{5} = \frac {3}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin 2 \alpha = - \sin 4 \beta = - 2 \sin 2 \beta \cos 2 \beta = - \frac {2 4}{2 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **B**
