> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 3
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由诱导公式结合和差角公式求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \tan \alpha = 2\tan 10^{\circ},\therefore \frac{\cos\left(\alpha - 80^{\circ}\right)}{\sin\left(\alpha - 10^{\circ}\right)} = \frac{\cos\left(\alpha + 10^{\circ} - 90^{\circ}\right)}{\sin\left(\alpha - 10^{\circ}\right)} = \frac{\sin\left(\alpha + 10^{\circ}\right)}{\sin\left(\alpha - 10^{\circ}\right)}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sin \alpha \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos \alpha \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\sin \alpha \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} - \cos \alpha \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\tan \alpha + \tan 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\tan \alpha - \tan 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {3 \tan 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\tan 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = 3
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为：3
