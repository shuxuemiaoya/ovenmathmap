> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由 $\frac{\pi}{12}=\frac{\pi}{3}-\frac{\pi}{4}$ 及余弦差公式求值.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\cos \frac{\pi}{12} = \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} -\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} +\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
