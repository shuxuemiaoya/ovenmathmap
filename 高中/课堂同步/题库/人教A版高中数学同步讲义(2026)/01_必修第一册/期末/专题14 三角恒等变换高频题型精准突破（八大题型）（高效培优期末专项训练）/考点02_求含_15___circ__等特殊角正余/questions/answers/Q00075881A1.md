> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\tan 165^{\circ} = 2 - \sqrt{3}$ B. $\frac{1}{2}\sin 15^{\circ} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos 15^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ C. $\sin 75^{\circ} \cos 75^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$ D. $\sin^{2}75^{\circ} - \cos^{2}75^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据诱导公式、两角和的正弦公式，结合二倍角的正弦公式，余弦公式进行求解判断即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A： $\tan 165^{\circ} = \tan (180^{\circ} - 15^{\circ}) = -\tan 15^{\circ} = -\tan (45^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}) = -\frac{\tan 45^{\circ} - \tan 30^{\circ}}{1 + \tan 45^{\circ} \tan 30^{\circ}}$ ，
> >
> > $\Rightarrow \tan 165^{\circ} = -\frac{1 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}}{1 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}} = -2 + \sqrt{3}$ ，因此本选项等式不成立；
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{2} \sin 1 5 ^ {\circ} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos 1 5 ^ {\circ} = \frac {1}{2} \sin (4 5 ^ {\circ} - 3 0 ^ {\circ}) + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos (4 5 ^ {\circ} - 3 0 ^ {\circ})
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{1}{2} (\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{1}{2}) + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} (\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{1}{2}) = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$ ，因此本选项等式不成立；
> >
> > C: $\sin 75^{\circ} \cos 75^{\circ} = \cos 15^{\circ} \sin 15^{\circ} = \frac{1}{2} \sin 30^{\circ} = \frac{1}{4}$ , 因此本选项等式不成立；
> >
> > D: $\sin^2 75^\circ - \cos^2 75^\circ = -\cos 150^\circ = -\cos (180^\circ - 30^\circ) = \cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，因此本选项等式成立，
> >
> > 故选 **D**
