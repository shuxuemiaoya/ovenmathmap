> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据 $\sin 75^{\circ} = \sin (45^{\circ} + 30^{\circ})$ 利用两角和的正弦公式计算可得·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sin 75^{\circ} = \sin (45^{\circ} + 30^{\circ})$ $= \sin 45^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ} + \cos 45^{\circ}\sin 30^{\circ}$ $= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} +\frac{1}{2}\times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}.$
> >
> > 故选：D
> >
> > 15. 计算 $\sin \left(-\frac{7\pi}{12}\right) = (\quad)$ A. $\frac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}{4}$ B. $\frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$ C. $\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$ D. $-\frac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}{4}$
> >
> > 【答案】D
> >
> > 【分析】将 $\frac{7\pi}{12}$ 看成 $\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{3}$ ，根据诱导公式以及两角和的正弦公式，化简计算，即可得出答案。【详解】 $\sin \left(-\frac{7\pi}{12}\right) = -\sin \frac{7\pi}{12} = -\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\left(\sin \frac{\pi}{4} \cos \frac{\pi}{3} + \cos \frac{\pi}{4} \sin \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ $= -\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = -\frac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}{4}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
