> [!faq]- 《专题14 三角恒等变换高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由题意，根据 $\sin^{2}\alpha + \cos^{2}\alpha = 1$ 可得 $\frac{1}{\sin^{2}\alpha} + \frac{9}{\cos^{2}\alpha} = 10 + \frac{\cos^{2}\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha} + \frac{9\sin^{2}\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha}$ ，结合基本不等式计算即可求解.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，得 $\sin \alpha > 0, \cos \alpha > 0$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {9}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} = (\frac {1}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {9}{\cos^ {2} \alpha}) (\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 0 + \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {9 \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} \quad 1 0 + 2 \sqrt {\frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} \cdot \frac {9 \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha}} = 1 6
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{\cos^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha} = \frac{9\sin^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha}$ 即 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 即 $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{6}$ 时，等号成立·
> >
> > 故选 **D**
