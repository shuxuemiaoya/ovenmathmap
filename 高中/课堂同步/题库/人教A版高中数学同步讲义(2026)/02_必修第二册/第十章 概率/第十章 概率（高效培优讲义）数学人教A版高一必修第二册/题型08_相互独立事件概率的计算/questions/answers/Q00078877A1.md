> [!faq]- 《第十章 概率（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版高一必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{1}{6}$ (2) $\frac{1}{9}$ (3) $\frac{13}{18}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （ $^{1}$ ）结合对立事件的概率公式，根据独立事件的乘法概率公式求解即可；
> >
> > (2) 结合互斥事件的概率加法公式，根据独立事件的乘法概率公式求解即可；
> >
> > (3) 结合互斥事件的概率加法公式，根据独立事件的乘法概率公式求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设 $A_{i}, B_{i} (i = 1,2,3)$ 分别表示乙、甲在第 $i$ 次投篮投中
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} P \left(A _ {i}\right) = \frac {1}{2}, P \left(\overline {{A _ {i}}}\right) = 1 - \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{2}, P \left(B _ {i}\right) = \frac {1}{3}, P \left(\overline {{B _ {i}}}\right) = 1 - \frac {1}{3} = \frac {2}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > 所求的概率为
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(\overline {{{A _ {1}}}} B _ {1}\right) = P \left(\overline {{{A _ {1}}}}\right) P \left(B _ {1}\right) = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {1}{6}.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 所求的概率为
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(\overline {{{A _ {1}}}} \overline {{{B _ {1}}}} \overline {{{A _ {2}}}} B _ {2} + \overline {{{A _ {1}}}} \overline {{{B _ {1}}}} \overline {{{A _ {2}}}} \overline {{{B _ {2}}}} A _ {3}\right) = P \left(\overline {{{A _ {1}}}} \overline {{{B _ {1}}}} \overline {{{A _ {2}}}} B _ {2}\right) + P \left(\overline {{{A _ {1}}}} \overline {{{B _ {1}}}} \overline {{{A _ {2}}}} \overline {{{B _ {2}}}} A _ {3}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{9}.
> > $$
> >
> > (3) 所求的概率为
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(A _ {1} + \overline {{{A _ {1}}}} \overline {{{B _ {1}}}} A _ {2} + \overline {{{A _ {1}}}} \overline {{{B _ {1}}}} \overline {{{A _ {2}}}} \overline {{{B _ {2}}}} A _ {3}\right) = P (A _ {1}) + P (\overline {{{A _ {1}}}} \overline {{{B _ {1}}}} A _ {2}) + P (\overline {{{A _ {1}}}} \overline {{{B _ {1}}}} \overline {{{A _ {2}}}} \overline {{{B _ {2}}}} A _ {3})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1 3}{1 8}.
> > $$
