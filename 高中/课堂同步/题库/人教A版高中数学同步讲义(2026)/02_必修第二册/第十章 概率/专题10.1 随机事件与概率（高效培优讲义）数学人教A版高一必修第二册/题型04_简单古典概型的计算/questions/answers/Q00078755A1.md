> [!faq]- 《专题10.1 随机事件与概率（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版高一必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $^{(1)}$ 样本空间见解析； $\frac{3}{4}$ ； (2)第二次，第三次摸到红球的概率均为 $\frac{3}{4}$ ； (3)抽签中签的概率与抽签顺序无关
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）依据古典概型写出基本事件空间，再写出“第一次摸到红球”中包含的基本事件，从而求出概率；
> >
> > (2) 由古典概型求得概率；
> >
> > (3) 依据概率相同得到结论·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）将三个红球记为 $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$ ，一个黄球记为y，
> >
> > 从中不放回地依次随机摸出 $^{3}$ 个球，该实验的基本事件空间为：
> >
> > $$
> > \Omega = \left\{\left(r _ {1}, r _ {2}, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {1}, r _ {3}, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {1}, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {3}, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {1}, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {2}, r _ {1}\right), \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(r _ {1}, r _ {3}, y\right), \left(r _ {1}, y, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {1}, y\right), \left(r _ {3}, y, r _ {1}\right), \left(y, r _ {1}, r _ {3}\right), \left(y, r _ {3}, r _ {1}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(r _ {2}, r _ {3}, y\right), \left(r _ {2}, y, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {2}, y\right), \left(r _ {3}, y, r _ {2}\right), \left(y, r _ {2}, r _ {3}\right), \left(y, r _ {3}, r _ {2}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(r _ {1}, r _ {2}, y\right), \left(r _ {1}, y, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {1}, y\right), \left(r _ {2}, y, r _ {1}\right), \left(y, r _ {1}, r _ {2}\right), \left(y, r _ {2}, r _ {1}\right) \},
> > $$
> >
> > 共有 24 个基本事件，
> >
> > 设“第一次摸到红球”为事件 A ，则
> >
> > $$
> > A = \left\{\left(r _ {1}, r _ {2}, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {1}, r _ {3}, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {1}, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {3}, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {1}, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {2}, r _ {1}\right), \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(r _ {1}, r _ {2}, y\right), \left(r _ {1}, y, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {1}, y\right), \left(r _ {2}, y, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {1}, r _ {3}, y\right), \left(r _ {1}, y, r _ {3}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left. \left(r _ {3}, r _ {1}, y\right), \left(r _ {3}, y, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {3}, y\right), \left(r _ {2}, y, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {2}, y\right), \left(r _ {3}, y, r _ {2}\right) \right\}
> > $$
> >
> > 共有18个基本事件，
> >
> > 所以 $P(A)=\frac{n(A)}{n(\Omega)}=\frac{18}{24}=\frac{3}{4}$ ，即第一次摸到红球的概率为 $\frac{3}{4}$
> >
> > (2) 设“第二次摸到红球”为事件 B，则
> >
> > $$
> > B = \left\{\left(r _ {1}, r _ {2}, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {1}, r _ {3}, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {1}, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {3}, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {1}, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {2}, r _ {1}\right), \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(r _ {1}, r _ {2}, y\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {1}, y\right), \left(y, r _ {1}, r _ {2}\right), \left(y, r _ {2}, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {1}, r _ {3}, y\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {1}, y\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left. \left(y, r _ {1}, r _ {3}\right), \left(y, r _ {3}, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {3}, y\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {2}, y\right), \left(y, r _ {2}, r _ {3}\right), \left(y, r _ {3}, r _ {2}\right) \right\}
> > $$
> >
> > 共有18个基本事件，
> >
> > 所以 $P(B)=\frac{n(B)}{n(\Omega)}=\frac{18}{24}=\frac{3}{4}$ ，即第二次摸到红球的概率为 $\frac{3}{4}$
> >
> > 设“第三次摸到红球”为事件 C ，则
> >
> > $$
> > C = \left\{\left(r _ {1}, r _ {2}, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {1}, r _ {3}, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {1}, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {2}, r _ {3}, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {1}, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {3}, r _ {2}, r _ {1}\right), \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(r _ {1}, y, r _ {2}\right), \left(r _ {2}, y, r _ {1}\right), \left(y, r _ {1}, r _ {2}\right), \left(y, r _ {2}, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {1}, y, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {3}, y, r _ {1}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left. \left(y, r _ {1}, r _ {3}\right), \left(y, r _ {3}, r _ {1}\right), \left(r _ {2}, y, r _ {3}\right), \left(r _ {3}, y, r _ {2}\right), \left(y, r _ {2}, r _ {3}\right), \left(y, r _ {3}, r _ {2}\right) \right\}
> > $$
> >
> > 共有18个基本事件，
> >
> > 所以 $P(C)=\frac{n(C)}{n(\Omega)}=\frac{18}{24}=\frac{3}{4}$ ，即第三次摸到红球的概率为 $\frac{3}{4}$
> >
> > (3) 因为 $P(A) = P(B) = P(C)$ ，即第一、二、三次抽到红球的概率相同，
> >
> > 所以，抽签中签的概率与抽签顺序无关·
