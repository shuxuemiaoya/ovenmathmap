> [!faq]- 《高一数学下学期第一次月考_人教A版_高效培优_强化卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)2 (2)① $a \otimes b = \left|x_{1}y_{2} - y_{1}x_{2}\right|$ , ② 7 (3)9
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $a = (2,1)$ ，可得 $\left|\vec{a}\right| = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}$ ，则 $a \cdot b = |a||b|\cos \theta = 4 \Rightarrow \cos \theta = \frac{4}{\sqrt{5} \times 2} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$
> >
> > 由于 $\theta \in [0,\pi ]$ ，因此 $\sin \theta = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\theta} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$ ，其中 $\theta$ 为 $a,b$ 的夹角，
> >
> > $\vec{a} \otimes b = |a||b|\sin\theta = \sqrt{5} \times 2 \times \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}} = 2$ ；
> >
> > (2) ①由 $a = (x_{1}, y_{1})$ ， $b = (x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，可得 $\cos a, b = \frac{a \cdot b}{|a| \cdot |b|} = \frac{x_{1}x_{2} + y_{1}y_{2}}{\sqrt{x_{1}^{2} + y_{1}^{2}}\sqrt{x_{2}^{2} + y_{2}^{2}}}$ ，
> >
> > 结合 $a, b \in [0, \pi]$ ，故 $\sin \left\langle a, b \right\rangle = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \left\langle a, b \right\rangle} = \sqrt{1 - \left( \frac{x_1 x_2 + y_1 y_2}{\sqrt{x_1^2 + y_1^2} \sqrt{x_2^2 + y_2^2}} \right)^2} = \frac{\left|x_1 y_2 - y_1 x_2\right|}{\sqrt{x_1^2 + y_1^2} \sqrt{x_2^2 + y_2^2}}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > a \otimes b = | a | \cdot | b | \sin \left\langle a, b \right\rangle = \sqrt {x _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {1} ^ {2}} \times \sqrt {x _ {2} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2}} \times \frac {\left| x _ {1} y _ {2} - y _ {1} x _ {2} \right|}{\sqrt {x _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {1} ^ {2}} \times \sqrt {x _ {2} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2}}} = \left| x _ {1} y _ {2} - y _ {1} x _ {2} \right|
> > $$
> > - **②**：由 $A(2,1)$ ， $B(-1,2)$ ， $C(0,4)$ 可得 $AB = (-3,1)$ ， $BC = (1,2)$
> >
> > 故 $AB \otimes BC = |-3 \times 2 - 1 \times 1| = 7$
> >
> > (3) 由 $\vec{a} = \left(\frac{1}{\cos\alpha}, \frac{2}{\sin\alpha}\right), \vec{b} = \left(\frac{2}{\sin\alpha}, -\frac{1}{\cos\alpha}\right)$ ，结合（2）的结论可知：
> >
> > $$
> > \vec {a} \otimes \vec {b} = \left| - \frac {1}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} - \frac {4}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} \right| = \frac {1}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {4}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} = \left(\frac {1}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {4}{\sin^ {2} \alpha}\right) (\cos^ {2} \alpha + \sin^ {2} \alpha)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 5 + \frac {\sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} + \frac {4 \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha} \quad 5 + 2 \sqrt {\frac {\sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha} \cdot \frac {4 \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha}} = 9
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{\sin^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha} = \frac{4\cos^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha}$ ，等号成立，结合 $\alpha \in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，故 $\tan \alpha = \sqrt{2}$ 时取到等号，
> >
> > 因此 $a \otimes b$ 的最小值为9.
