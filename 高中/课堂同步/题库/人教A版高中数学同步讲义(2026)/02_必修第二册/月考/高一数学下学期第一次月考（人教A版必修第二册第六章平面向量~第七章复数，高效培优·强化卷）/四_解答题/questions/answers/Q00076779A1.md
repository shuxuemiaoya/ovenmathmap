> [!faq]- 《高一数学下学期第一次月考（人教A版必修第二册第六章平面向量~第七章复数，高效培优·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)-4 (2) $\frac{DM}{MB} = \frac{5}{6}$ (3) $\left|\frac{1}{2}AF+\frac{1}{2}AE\right|$ 的取值范围是 $\left[\frac{13\sqrt{5}}{10},\frac{\sqrt{41}}{2}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）当 $\lambda = \frac{1}{2}$ 时， $BE = \frac{1}{2} BC$ ，所以 $BC = BA + AD + DC = -AB + AD + \frac{1}{3} AB = -\frac{2}{3} AB + AD$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{BE}{B}=\frac{1}{2}\frac{BC}{BC}=\frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{2}{3}AB+\frac{AD}{AD}\right)=-\frac{1}{3}AB+\frac{1}{2}AD$
> >
> > $\square\square\square AE=\square\square AB+ \square\square BE=\square\square AB-\frac{1}{3}AB+\frac{1}{2}AD=\frac{2}{3}AB+\frac{1}{2}AD$
> >
> > 又 $BD = AD - AB$
> >
> > 所以 $AE \cdot BD = \left( \frac{2}{3} AB + \frac{1}{2} AD \right) \cdot \left( AD - AB \right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {2}{3} A B \cdot A D + \frac {1}{2} A D ^ {2} - \frac {2}{3} A B ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} A B \cdot A D = \frac {1}{2} \times 2 ^ {2} - \frac {2}{3} \times 3 ^ {2} = 2 - 6 = - 4
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 当 $\lambda=\frac{2}{3}$ 时， $\overline{BE}=\frac{2}{3}\overline{BC}$ ，所以 $\overline{BC}=\overline{BA}+\overline{AD}+\overline{DC}=-\overline{AB}+\overline{AD}+\frac{1}{3}\overline{AB}=-\frac{2}{3}\overline{AB}+\overline{AD}$
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{BE}=\frac{1}{2}\overline{BC}=\frac{2}{3}\left(-\frac{2}{3}\overline{AB}+\overline{AD}\right)=-\frac{4}{9}\overline{AB}+\frac{2}{3}\overline{AD}$
> >
> > $$
> > A E = A B + B E = A B - \frac {4}{9} A B + \frac {2}{3} A D = \frac {5}{9} A B + \frac {2}{3} A D
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 A, M, E 三点共线，所以存在 m，使 $AM = mAE = \frac{5}{9}mAB + \frac{2}{3}mAD$ ，
> >
> > 又因为 $B, M, D$ 三点共线，所以 $\frac{5}{9} m + \frac{2}{3} m = 1$ ，解得 $m = \frac{9}{11}$
> >
> > 所以 $AM=\frac{5}{11}AB+\frac{6}{11}AD$ ，所以 $\frac{DM}{MB}=\frac{11-6}{6}=\frac{5}{6}$ ;
> >
> > (3) 因为 $AF = AD + DF = AD + (1 - \lambda)DC = AD + \frac{1 - \lambda}{3}AB$
> >
> > $$
> > B C = B A + A D + D C = - A B + A D + \frac {1}{3} A B = - \frac {2}{3} A B + A D
> > $$
> >
> > 所以
> >
> > $$
> > B E = \lambda B C = \lambda \left(- \frac {2}{3} A B + A D\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \\ A E = A B + B E = A B + \lambda \left(- \frac {2}{3} A B + A D\right) = \left(1 - \frac {2}{3} \lambda\right) A B + \lambda A D \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{A F}{A E} + \frac{1}{2} \frac{A E}{A E} = \left( \frac{5}{6} - \frac{2}{3} \lambda \right) \frac{A B}{A B} + \left( 1 + \frac{\lambda}{2} \right) \frac{A D}{A D}$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A F} + \frac {1}{2} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A E} \right| ^ {2} = \left(\frac {5}{6} - \frac {2}{3} \lambda\right) ^ {2} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B} ^ {2} + 2 \left(\frac {5}{6} - \frac {2}{3} \lambda\right) \left(1 + \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B} \cdot \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D} + \left(1 + \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) ^ {2} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D} ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 9 \left(\frac {5}{6} - \frac {2}{3} \lambda\right) ^ {2} + 4 \left(1 + \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) ^ {2} = 5 \lambda^ {2} - 6 \lambda + \frac {4 1}{4} = 5 \left(\lambda - \frac {3}{5}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {1 6 9}{2 0},
> > $$
> >
> > 由题意知 $\lambda \in [0,1]$
> >
> > 所以当 $\lambda=\frac{3}{5}$ 时， $\left|\frac{\square\square\square}{AF}+\frac{1}{2}\frac{\square\square\square}{AE}\right|$ 取到最小值 $\frac{13\sqrt{5}}{10}$
> >
> > 当 $\lambda = 0$ 时， $\left|\frac{A\bar{F}}{2} +\frac{1}{2}\frac{\bar{A}\bar{E}}{AE}\right|$ 取到最大值 $\frac{\sqrt{41}}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|\frac{\square\square\square}{AF}+\frac{1}{2}\frac{\square\square\square}{AE}\right|$ 的取值范围是 $\left[\frac{13\sqrt{5}}{10},\frac{\sqrt{41}}{2}\right]$ .
