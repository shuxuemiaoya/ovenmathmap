> [!faq]- 《专题02 平面向量基本定理及坐标表示六大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $AC = -b + 2a$ (2) $\lambda = \frac{1}{2}$ (3) $-\frac{11}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $DC = AC - AD$ ， $AB = CB - CA$ ， $AB = 2DC$
> >
> > 所以 $\overset{\square\square\square}{CB}-\overset{\square\square\square}{CA}=2\left(\overset{\square\square\square}{AC}-\overset{\square\square\square}{AD}\right)$ ，化简为 $AC=-\overset{\square\square\square}{BC}+2\overset{\square\square\square}{AD}=-b+2a$
> >
> > (2) 因为 B, D, P 三点共线，所以 $\overline{AP} = k\overline{AB} + (1 - k)\overline{AD}$
> >
> > 因为 $\overline{DC} = \overline{AC} - \overline{AD}$ ， $\overline{AB} = 2\overline{DC}$ ，所以 $\overline{AC} = \overline{AD} + \frac{1}{2}\overline{AB}$
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A P} = \frac {3}{4} \stackrel {\rightarrow} {a} + \lambda \stackrel {\rightarrow} {b} = \frac {3}{4} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D} + \lambda \stackrel {\square \square \square} {B C} = \frac {3}{4} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D} + \lambda \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} - \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{AP}=\frac{3}{4}\overline{AD}+\lambda\left(\overline{AD}-\frac{1}{2}\overline{AB}\right)=-\frac{\lambda}{2}\overline{AB}+\left(\frac{3}{4}+\lambda\right)\overline{AD}$
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}-\frac{\lambda}{2}&=k\\ \frac{3}{4}+\lambda&=1-k\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\lambda=\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > (3) 因为点 E 是线段 AC 上的动点，设 $AE = tAC (0 \leq t \leq 1)$ ，因为 $AC = AD + DC = AD + \frac{1}{2}AB$
> >
> > 所以 $AE = tAD + \frac{t}{2}AB$
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{DE} = \overline{AE} -\overline{AD} = (t - 1)\overline{AD} +\frac{t}{2}\overline{AB}$ ， $\overline{BE} = \overline{AE} -\overline{AB} = tAD + \left(\frac{t}{2} -1\right)\overline{AB}$
> >
> > $$
> > D E \cdot B E = \left[ (t - 1) A D + \frac {t}{2} A B \right] \cdot \left[ t A D + \left(\frac {t}{2} - 1\right) A B \right] = \left(t ^ {2} - t\right) A D ^ {2} + \left(t ^ {2} - \frac {3}{2} t + 1\right) A B \cdot A D + \left(\frac {1}{4} t ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} t\right) A B ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(t ^ {2} - t\right) \times 4 + \left(t ^ {2} - \frac {3}{2} t + 1\right) \times 2 \times 4 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \left(\frac {1}{4} t ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} t\right) \times 1 6 = 1 2 t ^ {2} - 1 8 t + 4 = 1 2 \left(t - \frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {2} - \frac {1 1}{4},
> > $$
> >
> > 故当 $t = \frac{3}{4}$ 时， $\frac{\text{xxxx}}{DE \cdot BE}$ 取到最小值 $-\frac{11}{4}$
