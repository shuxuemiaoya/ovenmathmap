> [!faq]- 《专题6.3 平面向量基本定理及坐标表示（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $AC \cdot EF = \frac{2}{3}$ (2) $\left[\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2},\sqrt{5}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）当 $\lambda=\frac{2}{3}$ 时，依题意知， $BF=\frac{2}{3}BC$ ， $DE=\frac{1}{3}DC$ ， $DC=\frac{1}{2}AB$ 。
> >
> > $$
> > A C = A D + D C = A D + \frac {1}{2} A B
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > C B = A B - A C = \frac {1}{2} A B - A D
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $EF = AF - AE$ ， $\overline{AF} = AB + BF = AB + \frac{2}{3} BC = AB + \frac{2}{3}\left(AD - \frac{1}{2} AB\right) = \frac{2}{3}\left(AD + AB\right)$
> >
> > $AE = AD + DE = AD + \frac{1}{3} DC = AD + \frac{1}{6} AB$ .
> >
> > 所以 $EF = AF - AE = -\frac{1}{3} AD + \frac{1}{2} AB$
> >
> > 因此 $\overline{AC} \cdot \overline{EF} = \left( \frac{\overline{AD}}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \overline{AB} \right) \cdot \left( -\frac{1}{3} \overline{AD} + \frac{1}{2} \overline{AB} \right) = -\frac{1}{3} \overline{AD}^{2} + \frac{1}{4} \overline{AB}^{2} + \frac{1}{3} \overline{AB} \cdot \overline{AD}$
> >
> > 因为 $\overset{\cup\cup\cup}{AB}=2\overset{\cup\cup\cup}{DC}$ ， $\left|\overset{\cup\cup\cup}{AD}\right|=\left|\overset{\cup\cup\cup}{CD}\right|=1$ ， $AD \perp AB$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\left|AB\right|=2$ ， $AD\cdot AB=0$ ，所以 $AC\cdot EF=\frac{2}{3}$
> >
> > $\overrightarrow{AE}=\overrightarrow{AD}+\overrightarrow{DE}=\overrightarrow{AD}+\left(1-\lambda\right)\overrightarrow{DC}=\overrightarrow{AD}+\frac{\left(1-\lambda\right)}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$ (2) 由题意
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \\ A F = A B + B F = A B + \lambda B C = A B + \lambda \left(A D - \frac {1}{2} A B\right) = \lambda A D + \left(1 - \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) A B \end{array} .
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $\frac{AE}{2}+\frac{1}{2}AF=\left(1+\frac{\lambda}{2}\right)AD+\left(1-\frac{3\lambda}{4}\right)AB$
> >
> > 因为 $\left|AB\right|=2,\quad\left|AD\right|=1,\quad AD\cdot AB=0$
> >
> > 所以
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \overrightarrow {A E} + \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {A F} \right| ^ {2} = \left(1 + \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) ^ {2} \overrightarrow {A D} ^ {2} + 4 \left(1 - \frac {3 \lambda}{4}\right) ^ {2} \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2} + 2 \left(1 + \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) \left(1 - \frac {3 \lambda}{4}\right) \overrightarrow {A D} \cdot \overrightarrow {A B} = \left(1 + \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) ^ {2} \cdot \left| \overrightarrow {A D} \right| ^ {2} + \left(1 - \frac {3 \lambda}{4}\right) ^ {2} \cdot \left| \overrightarrow {A B} \right| ^ {2} = \frac {5}{2} \lambda^ {2} - 5 \lambda + 5 = \frac {5}{2} (\lambda - 1) ^ {2} + \frac {5}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 由题意知， $\lambda \in [0,1]$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|\frac{\overrightarrow{AE}}{2} +\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AF}\right|^2$ 的取值范围是 $\left[\frac{5}{2},5\right]$
> >
> > $\therefore \left|\frac{A E}{2} +\frac{1}{2} \frac{A F}{2}\right|$ 的取值范围是 $\left[\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2},\sqrt{5}\right]$
