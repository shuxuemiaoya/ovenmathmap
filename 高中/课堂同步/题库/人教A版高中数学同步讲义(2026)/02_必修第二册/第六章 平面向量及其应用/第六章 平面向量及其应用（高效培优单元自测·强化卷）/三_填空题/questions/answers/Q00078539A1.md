> [!faq]- 《第六章 平面向量及其应用（高效培优单元自测·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $AM = \frac{3}{4} AB + \frac{1}{4} AC$ , $BG = -\frac{2}{3} AB + \frac{1}{3} AC$ (2)3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意，D 为 BC 的中点，所以 $AD = \frac{1}{2} AB + \frac{1}{2} AC$
> >
> > 又 $M$ 为 $BD$ 的中点，所以 $\square \square \square \square = \frac{1}{2} AB + \frac{1}{2} AD = \frac{1}{2} AB + \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2} AB + \frac{1}{2} AC\right) = \frac{3}{4} AB + \frac{1}{4} AC$
> >
> > ∵ AG = 2GD，即 $AG = \frac{2}{3}AD$
> >
> > $$
> > B G = A G - A B = \frac {2}{3} A D - A B = \frac {2}{3} \times \left(\frac {1}{2} A B + \frac {1}{2} A C\right) - A B = - \frac {2}{3} A B + \frac {1}{3} A C
> > $$
> >
> > $AM=\frac{3}{4}AB+\frac{1}{4}AC$ $BG=-\frac{2}{3}AB+\frac{1}{3}AC$
> >
> > (2) 由 $AG = 2GD$ ， $AE = \lambda AB$ ， $AF = \mu AC(\lambda, \mu \in \mathbb{R}^{+})$
> >
> > 得 $\frac{AB}{\lambda} = \frac{1}{\lambda} \frac{AE}{AE}, \frac{AC}{\mu} = \frac{1}{\mu} AF$
> >
> > 所以 $AG=\frac{2}{3}AD=\frac{2}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2}AB+\frac{1}{2}AC\right)=\frac{1}{3}AB+\frac{1}{3}AC=\frac{1}{3\lambda}AE+\frac{1}{3\mu}AF$
> >
> > 因为 $E, F, G$ 三点共线，则 $\frac{1}{3\lambda} + \frac{1}{3\mu} = 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \lambda + 4 \mu = (\lambda + 4 \mu) \left(\frac {1}{3 \lambda} + \frac {1}{3 \mu}\right) = \frac {5}{3} + \frac {4 \mu}{3 \lambda} + \frac {\lambda}{3 \mu} \quad \frac {5}{3} + 2 \sqrt {\frac {4 \mu}{3 \lambda} \cdot \frac {\lambda}{3 \mu}} = 3
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{4\mu}{3\lambda} = \frac{\lambda}{3\mu}$ ，即 $\lambda = 1$ ， $\mu = \frac{1}{2}$ 时取等号所以 $\lambda + \mu$ 的最小值 $^{3}$ .
