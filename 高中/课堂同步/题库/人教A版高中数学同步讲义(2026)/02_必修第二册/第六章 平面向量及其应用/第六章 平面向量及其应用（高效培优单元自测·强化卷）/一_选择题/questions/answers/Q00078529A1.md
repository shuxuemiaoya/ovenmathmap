> [!faq]- 《第六章 平面向量及其应用（高效培优单元自测·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $MG = \lambda MN$ ，则 $AG = AM + MG = AM + \lambda MN = AM + \lambda (AN - AM)$
> >
> > $$
> > = (1 - \lambda) A M + \lambda A N = x (1 - \lambda) A B + y \lambda A C
> > $$
> >
> > 又因为 $G$ 是 $\mathrm{V}ABC$ 的重心，故 $\frac{\mathrm{u}\mathrm{u}\mathrm{r}}{AG} = \frac{1}{3}\frac{\mathrm{u}\mathrm{u}\mathrm{r}}{AB} +\frac{1}{3}\frac{\mathrm{u}\mathrm{u}\mathrm{r}}{AC}$
> >
> > 所以有 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x(1 - \lambda) = \frac{1}{3} \\ y\lambda = \frac{1}{3} \end{array} \right. \Rightarrow \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} = 3(1 - \lambda) + 3\lambda = 3.$
> >
> > 故选 **A**
