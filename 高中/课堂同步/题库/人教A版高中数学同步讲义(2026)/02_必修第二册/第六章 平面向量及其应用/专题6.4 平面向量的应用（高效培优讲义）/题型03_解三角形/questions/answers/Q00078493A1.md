> [!faq]- 《专题6.4 平面向量的应用（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $BN=\sqrt{7}$ $$ 1 1 \sqrt {9 1} $$ (2) 182
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意有： $AN=\frac{1}{2}AC=3$ ， $\angle BAC=60^{\circ}$ ，
> >
> > 在 $\triangle ABN$ 中，由余弦定理有： $BN^{2} = AN^{2} + AB^{2} - 2AN\cdot AB\cos \angle BAC$
> >
> > 所以 $BN^{2} = 9 + 4 - 2 \times 3 \times 2 \times \frac{1}{2} = 7$ ，所以 $BN = \sqrt{7}$ ；
> >
> > (2) 由题意有： $AM = AB + BM = AB + \frac{1}{2} BC = AB + \frac{1}{2}\left(AC - AB\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(AB + AC\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > B N = B A + A N = - A B + \frac {1}{2} A C,
> > $$
> >
> > 所以
> >
> > $$
> > A M \cdot B N = \frac {1}{2} \left(A B + A C\right) \cdot \left(- A B + \frac {1}{2} A C\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} \left(- A B ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} A B \cdot A C + \frac {1}{2} A C ^ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{2} \left(- \left| A B \right| ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} \left| A B \right| \left| A C \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \frac {1}{2} \left| A C \right| ^ {2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} \times \left(- 4 - \frac {1}{2} \times 2 \times 6 \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \times 3 6\right) = \frac {1 1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \left| A M \right| ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} \left(A B + A C\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} \left(A B ^ {2} + 2 A B \cdot A C + A C ^ {2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \left(\left| A B \right| ^ {2} + 2 \left| A B \right| \left| A C \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \left| A C \right| ^ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{4} \times \left(4 + 2 \times 2 \times 6 \times \frac {1}{2} + 3 6\right) = 1 3
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \left| A M \right| = \sqrt {1 3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {由} \left| \overrightarrow {B N} \right| ^ {2} = \left(- \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {A C}\right) ^ {2} = \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2} - \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {A C} + \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {A C} ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left| A B \right| ^ {2} - \left| A B \right| \left| A C \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \frac {1}{4} \left| A C \right| ^ {2} = 4 - 2 \times 6 \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{4} \times 3 6 = 7
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \left| \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {B N} \right| = \sqrt {7}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \cos \angle M P N = \cos \left\langle A M, B N \right\rangle = \frac {\square \square \square \square \square \square \square}{\left| A M \right| \left| B N \right|} = \frac {\frac {1 1}{2}}{\sqrt {1 3} \times \sqrt {7}} = \frac {1 1 \sqrt {9 1}}{1 8 2}.
> > $$
