> [!faq]- 《第六章 平面向量及其应用（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先根据向量垂直求出 $m$ ，再根据向量数量积公式求出两向量夹角的余弦，从而确定夹角的大小.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 已知 $a = (1,m),b = (-1,3)$ ，则 $2a - b = (3,2m - 3)$
> >
> > $\because (2a - b) \perp b, \therefore (2a - b) \cdot b = 3 \times (-1) + (2m - 3) \times 3 = 0$ ，解得 $m = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > . a \cdot b = 1 \times (- 1) + 2 \times 3 = 5 \quad | \vec {a} | = \sqrt {1 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {2}} = \sqrt {5}, | b | = \sqrt {(- 1) ^ {2} + 3 ^ {2}} = \sqrt {1 0}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \langle \vec {a}, \vec {b} \rangle = \frac {\vec {a} \cdot b}{| a | \cdot | b |} = \frac {5}{\sqrt {5} \times \sqrt {1 0}} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\langle a, b \right\rangle \in [ 0, \pi ] \quad \stackrel {\rightarrow} {\left\langle a, b \right\rangle} = \frac {\pi}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
