> [!faq]- 《专题6_4_平面向量的应用_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(2,2\sqrt{3})$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \tan(A+B)=\frac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B}$ ， $\therefore -\tan C=\frac{\tan A+\tan B}{1-\tan A\tan B}$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan A + \tan B = \tan C (\tan A \tan B - 1) = \frac {\sin C}{\cos C} \cdot \left(\frac {\sin A \sin B}{\cos A \cos B} - 1\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sin C}{\cos C} \cdot \frac {\sin A \sin B - \cos A \cos B}{\cos A \cos B} = \frac {- \sin C \cos (A + B)}{\cos A \cos B \cos C} = \frac {\sin C \cos C}{\cos A \cos B \cos C} = \frac {\sin C}{\cos A \cos B}
> > $$
> >
> > 由余弦定理 $b^{2}=a^{2}+c^{2}-2ac\cos B$ 得， $a^{2}+c^{2}-b^{2}=2ac\cos B$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {2 c ^ {2}}{a ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - b ^ {2}} = \frac {2 c ^ {2}}{2 a c \cos B} = \frac {c}{a \cos B} = \frac {\sin C}{\sin A \cos B},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \text {由} \sqrt {3} (\tan A + \tan B) = \frac {2 c ^ {2}}{a ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - b ^ {2}} \text {得，} \frac {\sqrt {3} \sin C}{\cos A \cos B} = \frac {\sin C}{\sin A \cos B}, \therefore \frac {\sqrt {3}}{\cos A} = \frac {1}{\sin A},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan A = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \quad , \because A \in \left(0, \frac {\pi}{2}\right) \quad , \therefore A = \frac {\pi}{6}.
> > $$
> >
> > 又由正弦定理得 $\frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} = \frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{2}{\frac{1}{2}} = 4$ ，∴ $b = 4\sin B, c = 4\sin C$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sqrt {3} b - c = 4 (\sqrt {3} \sin B - \sin C) = 4 [ \sqrt {3} \sin B - \sin (A + B) ] = 4 [ \sqrt {3} \sin B - \sin (B + \frac {\pi}{6}) ]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 4 \left(\sqrt {3} \sin B - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin B - \frac {1}{2} \cos B\right) = 4 \left(\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin B - \frac {1}{2} \cos B\right) = 4 \sin \left(B - \frac {\pi}{6}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > 是锐角三角形， $A=\frac{\pi}{6}$ ， $\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}&C=\frac{5\pi}{6}-B\\ &0<B<\frac{\pi}{2}\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow\frac{\pi}{3}<B<\frac{\pi}{2}$ ， $\because \triangle ABC$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore B - \frac {\pi}{6} \in \left(\frac {\pi}{6}, \frac {\pi}{3}\right), \quad \therefore \sin \left(B - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \in \left(\sin \frac {\pi}{6}, \sin \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \left(\frac {1}{2}, \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sqrt {3} b - c \in (2, 2 \sqrt {3})
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $(2,2\sqrt{3})$ .
