> [!faq]- 《专题6_4_平面向量的应用_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{6\sqrt{13}}{65}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ 点 P 在线段 OM 上，∴ 设 $OP=\lambda OM=(\lambda,2\lambda),\lambda\in[0,1]$
> >
> > 则 $PA = OA - OP = (1 - \lambda, 5 - 2\lambda)$
> >
> > $$
> > P B = O B - O P = (7 - \lambda , 1 - 2 \lambda)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P A \cdot P B = (1 - \lambda) (7 - \lambda) + (5 - 2 \lambda) (1 - 2 \lambda)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 5 \lambda^ {2} - 2 0 \lambda + 1 2 = 5 (\lambda - 2) ^ {2} - 8
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ 当 $\lambda = 0$ 时， $PA \cdot PB$ 取得最大值为 12
> >
> > 此时 $\overline{PA} = (1,5),\left|\overline{PA}\right| = \sqrt{26},\overline{PB} = (7,1),\left|\overline{PB}\right| = 5\sqrt{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos \angle A P B = \cos <   \frac {\text { u   r }}{P A}, \frac {\text { u   r }}{P B} > = \frac {\frac {\text { u   r }}{P A} \cdot \frac {\text { u   r }}{P B}}{\left| \frac {\text { u   r }}{P A} \right| \left| \frac {\text { u   r }}{P B} \right|} = \frac {1 2}{\sqrt {2 6} \cdot 5 \sqrt {2}} = \frac {6 \sqrt {1 3}}{6 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{6\sqrt{13}}{65}$
