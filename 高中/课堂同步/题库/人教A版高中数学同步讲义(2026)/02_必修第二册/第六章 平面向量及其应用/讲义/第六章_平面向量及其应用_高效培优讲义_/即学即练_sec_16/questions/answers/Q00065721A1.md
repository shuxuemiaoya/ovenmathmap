> [!faq]- 《第六章_平面向量及其应用_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-2\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 借助向量投影定义与数量积公式计算即可得
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{\boxed{ABC}\cdot\boxed{AB}}{\left|AB\right|} = \frac{\left|\boxed{AC}\right|\cdot\left|\boxed{AB}\right|\cos A}{\left|AB\right|} = \left|\boxed{AC}\right|\cos A = 4\times \left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) = -2\sqrt{2}.$
> >
> > 故答案为： $-2\sqrt{2}$
