> [!faq]- 《第六章_平面向量及其应用_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $12+\frac{8\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (2) $4+4\sqrt{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）利用两角和差余弦公式可求得 $\cos \angle DBC$ ，根据余弦定理可求得结果；
> >
> > (2) 利用两角和差正弦公式可求得 $\sin \angle DBC$ ，采用面积桥 $S_{\triangle ABC} = S_{\triangle ABD} + S_{\triangle DBC}$ ，结合三角形面积公式可构
> >
> > 造方程求得结果·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $\because \cos \angle ABC = -\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}, \angle ABC \in (0, \pi), \therefore \sin \angle ABC = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos \angle D B C = \cos (\angle A B C - \angle A B D) = \cos \angle A B C \cos \angle A B D + \sin \angle A B C \sin \angle A B D = - \frac {\sqrt {6}}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {3 - \sqrt {6}}{6},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore C D ^ {2} = B D ^ {2} + B C ^ {2} - 2 B D \cdot B C \cos \angle D B C = 4 + 1 6 - 1 6 \times \frac {3 - \sqrt {6}}{6} = 1 2 + \frac {8 \sqrt {6}}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 得： $\sin \angle DBC = \sin (\angle ABC - \angle ABD) = \sin \angle ABC \cos \angle ABD - \cos \angle ABC \sin \angle ABD$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {\sqrt {6}}{3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {3} + 3 \sqrt {2}}{6};
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because S _ {\triangle A B C} = S _ {\triangle A B D} + S _ {\triangle D B C}, \therefore \frac {1}{2} A B \cdot B C \sin \angle A B C = \frac {1}{2} A B \cdot B D \sin \angle A B D + \frac {1}{2} B C \cdot B D \sin \angle D B C,
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}AB=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}AB+\frac{2\sqrt{3}+6\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ，解得： $AB=4+4\sqrt{6}\cdot$
