> [!faq]- 《第六章_平面向量及其应用_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由 $\overline{BC} = 3\overline{BD}$ 得 $BC = \frac{3}{2} CD$ ，进而得 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{3}{2} S_{\triangle ACD} = 9$ ，再由 $\overline{AM} = 2\overline{MD}$ 得 $AD = 3MD$ ，进而可得 $S_{\triangle BCM} = \frac{1}{3} S_{\triangle ABC} = 3.$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $BC = 3BD$ ，所以 $BC = 3BD, BC = \frac{3}{2}CD$ ，所以 $\frac{S_{\triangle ACD}}{S_{\triangle ABC}} = \frac{CD}{BC} = \frac{2}{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{3}{2} S_{\triangle ACD} = \frac{3}{2} \times 6 = 9$ ，又 $AM = 2MD$ ，所以 AD = 3MD，
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{S_{\triangle BCM}}{S_{\triangle ABC}} = \frac{DM}{AD} = \frac{1}{3}$ ，所以 $S_{\triangle BCM} = \frac{1}{3} S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{3} \times 9 = 3$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**
