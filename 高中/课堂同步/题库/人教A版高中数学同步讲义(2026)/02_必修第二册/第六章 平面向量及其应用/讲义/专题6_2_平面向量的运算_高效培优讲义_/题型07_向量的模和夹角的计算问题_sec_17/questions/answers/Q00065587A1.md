> [!faq]- 《专题6_2_平面向量的运算_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\theta=\frac{2\pi}{3}$ ; (2) $t=-1$ 或 $t=-\frac{3}{2}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由已知 $\left(3a + 2b\right)\cdot \left(a - 3b\right) = 3a^2 -7a\cdot b - 6b^2 = 3\times 25 - 7a\cdot b - 6\times 4 = 86$
> >
> > $$
> > a \cdot b = - 5
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a \cdot b = - 5 = | a | | b | \cos \theta = 5 \times 2 \cos \theta , \cos \theta = - \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\theta \in [0,\pi ]$ ，所以 $\theta = \frac{2\pi}{3}$
> >
> > (2) $\left|a - tb\right|^2 = (a - tb)^2 = a^2 - 2ta \cdot b + t^2b^2 = 25 + 10t + 4t^2 = 19$
> >
> > 解得 $t = -1$ 或 $t = -\frac{3}{2}$
