## 【即学即练】

1. 化简 $(AB - MC) + (MA + BN) = (\quad)$ A. 0 B. CN C. BM D. AC

【答案】B

【详解】 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} \square & \square \\ AB & MC \end{array} \right) + \left( \begin{array}{cc} \square & \square \\ MA & BN \end{array} \right) = AB + BN + MA - MC = AN + MA - MC = MN - MC = CN$ .故选：B
