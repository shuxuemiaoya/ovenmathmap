> [!faq]- 《专题6_3_平面向量基本定理及坐标表示_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{3}{2}$ (2) $\sqrt{53}$ (3) $A(10,7)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $AE = AB + BE = e_1 + (\lambda + 1)e_2$
> >
> > 当 $A, E, C$ 三点共线时，存在实数 $\mu$ ，使得 $AE = \mu EC$
> >
> > 即 $e_{1}^{\square} + (\lambda + 1)e_{2}^{\square\square} = \mu\left(-2e_{1}^{\square} + e_{2}^{\square\square}\right) = -2\mu e_{1}^{\square\square\square} + \mu e_{2}^{\square\square}$
> >
> > 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}1&=-2\mu\\\lambda+1&=\mu\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\lambda=-\frac{3}{2}$ .
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 可知 $BE = -e_{1} - \frac{3}{2} e_{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore B C = \overline {{B E}} + \overline {{E C}} = - e _ {1} - \frac {3}{2} e _ {2} - 2 e _ {1} + e _ {2} = - 3 e _ {1} - \frac {1}{2} e _ {2} = (- 7, - 2),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| B C \right| = \sqrt {(- 7) ^ {2} + (- 2) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {5 3}.
> > $$
> >
> > (3) $AB = (6, 0)$ , BC = (-7, -2),
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \text {UUU} \\ A C = A B + B C = (- 1, - 2) \\ \therefore \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 设 $A(a,b)$ ，∴ $C(a-1,b-2)$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{c} \text {卍卍卍} \\ D C = (a - 4, b - 7) \\ \therefore \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 在平行四边形 ABCD 中， $AB = DC$ ，即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a-4&=6\\ b-7&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=10\\ b&=7\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > $A(10,7)$
