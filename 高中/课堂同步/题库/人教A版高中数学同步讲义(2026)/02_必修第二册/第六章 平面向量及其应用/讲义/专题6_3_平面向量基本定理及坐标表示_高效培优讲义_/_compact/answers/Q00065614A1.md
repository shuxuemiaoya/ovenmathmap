> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $a = (x,y),\lambda a = (\lambda x,\lambda y),F(\lambda a) = (\lambda x + \lambda y,\lambda x - \lambda y)$ ， $\lambda F(a) = (\lambda x + \lambda y,\lambda x - \lambda y)$ ，A正确；
> >
> > 若 $a / / b$ ，不妨记 $b = \lambda a$ ，由A选项， $F(b) = F(\lambda a) = \lambda F(a)$ ， $\therefore F(a) / / F(b)$ ，B正确；
> >
> > $A(-2,4), B(2,-2), C$ 为线段 AB 的中点， $C(0,1), BC = (-2,3), F(BC) = (1,-5)$ ，C 错误；
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {{\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup}} {O A} = (a, - 1) (a > 0), \stackrel {{\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup}} {O B} = F (\stackrel {{\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup}} {O A}) = (a - 1, a + 1), \stackrel {{\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup}} {A B} = (- 1, a + 2), \left| \stackrel {{\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup}} {O A} \right| ^ {2} = a ^ {2} + 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {O B} \right| ^ {2} = 2 a ^ {2} + 2 > \left| \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {O A} \right| ^ {2}, a > 0, \left| \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A B} \right| ^ {2} = a ^ {2} + 4 a + 5 > \left| O A \right| ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore O, A, B$ 构成等腰三角形，只可能 $|OB| = |AB|$ ，联立可解 $a = 2 + \sqrt{7}$ ，D 正确；
> >
> > 故选 **ABD**.
