> [!faq]- 《专题6_3_平面向量基本定理及坐标表示_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3\sqrt{13}}{13}/\frac{3}{13}\sqrt{13}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在VABC中， $AB=2\sqrt{2},AC=3,\angle A=135^{\circ}$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\overrightarrow{AC}=|\overrightarrow{AB}||\overrightarrow{AC}|\cos A=-6$
> >
> > 线段 $EF, BC$ 的中点分别为 $M, N$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A M} = \frac {1}{2} \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A F} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A E}\right) = \frac {1}{2} \left(y A C + x A B\right), \quad \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A N} = \frac {1}{2} \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \\ M N = A N - A M = \frac {1}{2} \left[ (1 - x) A B + (1 - y) A C \right] \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴两边平方得：
> >
> > $$
> > M N ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} \left[ (1 - x) ^ {2} A B ^ {2} + 2 (1 - x) (1 - y) A B \cdot A C + (1 - y) ^ {2} A C ^ {2} \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \left[ 8 (1 - x) ^ {2} - 1 2 (1 - x) (1 - y) + 9 (1 - y) ^ {2} \right] = 2 (1 - x) ^ {2} - 3 (1 - x) (1 - y) + \frac {9}{4} (1 - y) ^ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > $\because x + 3y = 1(x > 0, y > 0), \therefore 3y = 1 - x, 0 < y < \frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore M N ^ {2} = \frac {1 1 7}{4} y ^ {2} - \frac {2 7}{2} y + \frac {9}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 因为对称轴为 $y = \frac{3}{13} < \frac{1}{3}$ ，所以当 $y = \frac{3}{13}$ 时取得最小值，
> >
> > 最小值为 $\overrightarrow{MN}^{2}=\frac{117}{4}\times\left(\frac{3}{13}\right)^{2}-\frac{27}{2}\times\frac{3}{13}+\frac{9}{4}=\frac{9}{13}$ ，所以 $\left|\overrightarrow{MN}\right|$ 的最小值为 $\frac{3\sqrt{13}}{13}$ .
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{3\sqrt{13}}{13}$
