> [!faq]- 《专题03_平面向量的应用六大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\left|\begin{matrix}\overrightarrow{AM}\\\end{matrix}\right|=\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}$ ; $AM\cdot BC=-\frac{3}{2}$ ; (2) $\lambda = \frac{2}{3}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）当 $\lambda = \frac{1}{2}$ 时， $CM = \frac{1}{2} CB$ ，则 $M$ 为 $BC$ 的中点，
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{AM}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\overline{AC}+\overline{AB}\right)$ ，即 $\left|\overline{AM}\right|^{2}=\frac{1}{4}\left(\left|\overline{AC}\right|^{2}+\left|\overline{AB}\right|^{2}+2\left|\overline{AC}\right|\cdot\left|\overline{AB}\right|\cos\angle BAC\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \left(1 + 4 + 2 \times 1 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {7}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|\overrightarrow{AM}\right|=\frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A M} \cdot \stackrel {\square \square \square} {B C} = \frac {1}{2} \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B}\right) \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} - \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B}\right) = \frac {1}{2} \left(\left| \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} \right| ^ {2} - \left| \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B} \right| ^ {2}\right) = - \frac {3}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > (2)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \triangle A B C
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > B C ^ {2} = A B ^ {2} + A C ^ {2} - 2 A B \cdot A C \cos \angle B A C = 4 + 1 - 2 \times 1 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} = 3
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > B C = \sqrt {3}
> > $$
> >
> > 由正弦定理得 $\frac{BC}{\sin\angle BAC} = \frac{AB}{\sin\angle ACB}$ ，即 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sin60^{\circ}} = \frac{2}{\sin\angle ACB}$ ，解得 $\sin \angle ACB = 1$
> >
> > 因为 $\angle ACB\in (0,180^{\circ})$ ，所以 $\angle ACB = 90^{\circ}$ ，所以 $\angle ABC = 180^{\circ} - \angle BAC - \angle ACB = 30^{\circ}$
> >
> > 因为 $AM \perp CN$ ，所以 $AM \cdot CN = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square \square} {A M} \cdot \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C N} = \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} + \stackrel {\square \square \square \square} {C M}\right) \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {C B} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {B N}\right) = \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} + \lambda \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C B}\right) \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {C B} + \lambda \stackrel {\square \square \square} {B A}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A C} \cdot \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {C B} + \lambda A C \cdot \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {B A} + \lambda \left| \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {C B} \right| ^ {2} + \lambda^ {2} \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {C B} \cdot \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {B A}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - C A \cdot C B - \lambda A C \cdot A B + \lambda \left| C B \right| ^ {2} - \lambda^ {2} B C \cdot B A
> > $$
> >
> > $=-\lambda+3\lambda-3\lambda^{2}=-3\lambda^{2}+2\lambda=0$ ，解得 $\lambda=\frac{2}{3}$ 或 $\lambda=0$ （舍去），
> >
> > 故 $\lambda=\frac{2}{3}$ .
