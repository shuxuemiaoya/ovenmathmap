> [!faq]- 《专题01_平面向量的运算七大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A．外心 B．内心 C．重心 D．垂心
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 原式变形为 $\overline{AO}=\frac{\tan B}{\tan A+\tan B+\tan C}\overline{AB}+\frac{\tan C}{\tan A+\tan B+\tan C}\overline{AC}$
> >
> > $\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square$ $AO \cdot BC = \frac{\tan B}{\tan A + \tan B + \tan C} AB \cdot BC + \frac{\tan C}{\tan A + \tan B + \tan C} AC \cdot BC$ $= \frac{\tan B(-\cos B)}{\tan A + \tan B + \tan C} ac + \frac{\tan C \cos C}{\tan A + \tan B + \tan C} ab$ $= \frac{a}{\tan A + \tan B + \tan C} (-\sin B \times c + \sin C \times b) = \frac{-\sin B \times ac + \sin C \times ab}{\tan A + \tan B + \tan C} = 0$
> >
> > 所以 $AO \perp BC$ ，同理， $BO \perp AC$
> >
> > 所以 O 是 VABC 的垂心，
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
