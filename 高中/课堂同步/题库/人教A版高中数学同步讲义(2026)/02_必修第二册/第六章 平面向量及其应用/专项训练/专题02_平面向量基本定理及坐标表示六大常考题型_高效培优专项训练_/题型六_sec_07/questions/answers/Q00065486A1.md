> [!faq]- 《专题02_平面向量基本定理及坐标表示六大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)2 (2) $a \otimes b = \left|x_{1}y_{2} - x_{2}y_{1}\right|$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由已知 $a = (\sqrt{3}, 1)$ ，得 $\left|a\right| = 2$ ，由 $a \mathrm{g} b = \left|a\right|\left|b\right|\cos \theta = 4\cos \theta = 2\sqrt{3}$ ，可得 $\cos \theta = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
> >
> > 又 $0 \leq \theta \leq \pi$ ， $\therefore \sin \theta = \frac{1}{2}$ ， $a \otimes b = |a||b|\sin \theta = 2 \times 2 \times \frac{1}{2} = 2$ ；
> >
> > (2) $\left|a\right|^{\Gamma} = \sqrt{x_1^2 + y_1^2},\left|b\right|^{\Gamma} = \sqrt{x_2^2 + y_2^2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \theta = \frac {a g b}{| a | g | b |} = \frac {x _ {1} x _ {2} + y _ {1} y _ {2}}{\sqrt {x _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {1} ^ {2}} g \sqrt {x _ {2} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2}}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin^ {2} \theta = 1 - \left(\frac {x _ {1} x _ {2} + y _ {1} y _ {2}}{\sqrt {x _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {1} ^ {2}} \mathrm{g} \sqrt {x _ {2} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2}}}\right) ^ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $0 \leq \theta \leq \pi$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \theta = \frac {\left| x _ {1} y _ {2} - x _ {2} y _ {1} \right|}{\sqrt {x _ {1} ^ {2} + y _ {1} ^ {2}} \mathrm{g} \sqrt {x _ {2} ^ {2} + y _ {2} ^ {2}}} ，
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a \otimes b = \left| a \right| \left| b \right| \sin \theta = \left| x _ {1} y _ {2} - x _ {2} y _ {1} \right|.
> > $$
