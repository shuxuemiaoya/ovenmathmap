> [!faq]- 《第六章_平面向量及其应用_高效培优单元自测_提升卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $2\sqrt{21}$ (2) $\frac{\pi}{6}$ (3) $\pm\sqrt{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $\left|2a-b\right|=\sqrt{\left(2a-b\right)^{2}}=\sqrt{4a^{2}-4a\cdot b+b^{2}}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {4 \times 1 6 - 4 \times 4 \times 2 \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) + 4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \sqrt {2 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow {a}, a + \overrightarrow {b} \right\rangle = \frac {\overrightarrow {a} \cdot (\overrightarrow {a + b})}{| \overrightarrow {a} | | a + b |} = \frac {\overrightarrow {a ^ {2}} + a \cdot \overrightarrow {b}}{4 \sqrt {(\overrightarrow {a + b}) ^ {2}}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\stackrel {\rightarrow} {a ^ {2}} + a \cdot \stackrel {\rightarrow} {b}}{4 \sqrt {a ^ {2} + 2 a \cdot b + b ^ {2}}} = \frac {1 6 + 4 \times 2 \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right)}{4 \sqrt {1 6 + 2 \times 4 \times 2 \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) + 4}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1 2}{4 \sqrt {1 2}} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $\left\langle \vec{a}, a + \vec{b} \right\rangle = \frac{\pi}{6}$ ，所以 $a$ 与 $a + b$ 的夹角为 $\frac{\pi}{6}$ ；
> >
> > (3) 若向量 $2a - \lambda b$ 与 $\lambda a - 3b$ 平行，
> >
> > 则 $2a - \lambda b = k(\lambda a - 3b)$ ， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}k\lambda = 2\\ -3k = -\lambda \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} k = \frac {\sqrt {6}}{3} \\ \lambda = \sqrt {6} \end{array} \text {或} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} k = - \frac {\sqrt {6}}{3} \\ \lambda = - \sqrt {6} \end{array} \right. \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\lambda$ 的值为 $\pm\sqrt{6}$ .
