> [!faq]- 《第六章_平面向量及其应用_高效培优单元自测_强化卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left[-\frac{1}{16},\frac{1}{2}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $AB = 2, AC = 3, \angle BAC = 60^{\circ}$
> >
> > 所以 $AB \cdot AC = \left|AB\right|\left|AC\right| \cos \angle BAC = 3$ ， $AB^{2} = \left|AB\right|^{2} = 4, AC^{2} = \left|AC\right|^{2} = 9$
> >
> > 设 $\overline{DF}=\lambda\overline{DE}(0\leq\lambda\leq1)$
> >
> > 则 $\overline{BF} = \overline{BD} +\overline{DF} = -\frac{1}{2}\overline{AB} +\overline{\lambda DE} = -\frac{1}{2}\overline{AB} +\lambda (\overline{AE} -\overline{AD})$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{2} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B} + \lambda \left(\frac {1}{3} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} - \frac {1}{2} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B}\right) = \left(- \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{2} \lambda\right) \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B} + \frac {1}{3} \lambda \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C F} = \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C E} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {E F} = - \frac {2}{3} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} - (1 - \lambda) \stackrel {\square \square \square} {D E} = - \frac {2}{3} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} - (1 - \lambda) (\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A E} - \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {2}{3} \square A C - (1 - \lambda) \left(\frac {1}{3} \square A C - \frac {1}{2} \square A B\right) = \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{2} \lambda\right) \square A B + \left(\frac {1}{3} \lambda - 1\right) \square A C
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} \stackrel {\square \square \overline {{\square}}} {B F} \cdot \stackrel {\square \square \overrightarrow {\square}} {C F} = \left[ \left(- \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{2} \lambda\right) \stackrel {\square \square \overline {{\square}}} {A B} + \frac {1}{3} \lambda \stackrel {\square \square \overline {{\square}}} {A C} \right] \cdot \left[ \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{2} \lambda\right) \stackrel {\square \square \overline {{\square}}} {A B} + \left(\frac {1}{3} \lambda - 1\right) \stackrel {\square \square \overline {{\square}}} {A C} \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(\frac {1}{4} \lambda^ {2} - \frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {\square \square \square} A B ^ {2} + \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3} \lambda^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} \lambda\right) ^ {\square \square \square} A B \cdot A C + \left(\frac {1}{9} \lambda^ {2} - \frac {1}{3} \lambda\right) ^ {\square \square \square} A C ^ {2} = \lambda^ {2} - \frac {3}{2} \lambda + \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 对于 $y = \lambda^2 -\frac{3}{2}\lambda +\frac{1}{2}$ ，其开口向上，对称轴为 $\lambda = \frac{3}{4}$
> >
> > 所以 $y=\lambda^{2}-\frac{3}{2}\lambda+\frac{1}{2}$ 在 $\left[0,\frac{3}{4}\right)$ 上单调递减，在 $\left(\frac{3}{4},1\right]$ 上单调递增，
> >
> > 当 $\lambda = 0$ 时， $\overline{BF} \cdot \overline{CF} = \lambda^2 - \frac{3}{2}\lambda + \frac{1}{2}$ 取得最大值 $\frac{1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 当 $\lambda=\frac{3}{4}$ 时， $BF\cdot CF=\lambda^{2}-\frac{3}{2}\lambda+\frac{1}{2}$ 取得最小值 $-\frac{1}{16}$
> >
> > 所以 $BF \cdot CF$ 的取值范围是 $\left[-\frac{1}{16}, \frac{1}{2}\right]$ .
> >
> > 故答案为： $\left[-\frac{1}{16},\frac{1}{2}\right]$
