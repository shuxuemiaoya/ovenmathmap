> [!faq]- 《专题01 平面向量的运算七大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $a \perp b$ ，∴ $a \cdot b = 0$ 。
> >
> > $\because \left|a\right| = 1,\left|a + b\right| = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| a + b \right| ^ {2} = (a + b) ^ {2} = a ^ {2} + 2 a \cdot b + b ^ {2} = | a | ^ {2} + | b | ^ {2} = 4
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| b \right| ^ {2} = 4 - \left| \vec {a} \right| ^ {2} = 3, \therefore \left| b \right| = \sqrt {3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\sqrt{3}$
