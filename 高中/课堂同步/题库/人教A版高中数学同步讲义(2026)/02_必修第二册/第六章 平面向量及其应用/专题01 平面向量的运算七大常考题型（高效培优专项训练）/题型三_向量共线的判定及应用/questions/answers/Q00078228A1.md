> [!faq]- 《专题01 平面向量的运算七大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A．三边均不等的三角形 B．直角三角形 C．等边三角形 D．底边和腰不等的等腰三角形
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $2AB \cdot AC = 2\left|AB\right|\left|AC\right|\cos A = \left|AB\right|\left|AC\right|$ ，则 $\cos A = \frac{1}{2}, A \in (0, \pi)$ ，所以 $A = \frac{\pi}{3}$ ，由 $\left(\left|AC\right|AB + \left|AB\right|AC\right) \cdot BC = 0$ ，则 $\left(\left|AC\right|AB + \left|AB\right|AC\right) \cdot (AC - AB) = \left|AC\right|AB \cdot AC + \left|AB\right|AC^2 - \left|AC\right|AB^2 - \left|AB\right|AC \cdot AB$ $= \left|AC\right|^2\left|AB\right| \times \frac{1}{2} + \left|AB\right|\left|AC\right|^2 - \left|AC\right|\left|AB\right|^2 - \left|AB\right|^2\left|AC\right| \times \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {3}{2} \left| \begin{array}{c} \square \\ A B \end{array} \right| \left| \begin{array}{c} \square \\ A C \end{array} \right| ^ {2} - \left| \begin{array}{c} \square \\ A B \end{array} \right| ^ {2} \left| \begin{array}{c} \square \\ A C \end{array} \right| \times \frac {3}{2} = \frac {3}{2} \left| \begin{array}{c} \square \\ A B \end{array} \right| \left| \begin{array}{c} \square \\ A C \end{array} \right| \left(\left| \begin{array}{c} \square \\ A C \end{array} \right| - \left| \begin{array}{c} \square \\ A B \end{array} \right|\right) = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\left| \overline{A}C \right| = \left| \overline{A}B \right|$ ，所以 $\triangle ABC$ 为等边三角形；
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
