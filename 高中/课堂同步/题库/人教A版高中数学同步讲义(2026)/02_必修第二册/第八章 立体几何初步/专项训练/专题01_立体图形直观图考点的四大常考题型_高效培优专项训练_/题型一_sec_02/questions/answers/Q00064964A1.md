> > [!success]- **【答案】** ![](../../../images/questions/148d4dea23cd07b964845ca5363de02046226aeb69d49c1255a7c0bf86fea3fc.jpg) A.6 B.9 C.12 D.15
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据直观图还原原图形，然后可求出 $\triangle OAB$ 的面积.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\triangle OAB$ 的直观图可知原图中， $\angle AOB = 90^{\circ}, OA = 6, OB = 4$
> >
> > 所以 $\triangle OAB$ 的面积为 $\frac{1}{2}, 4, 6 = 12$ .
