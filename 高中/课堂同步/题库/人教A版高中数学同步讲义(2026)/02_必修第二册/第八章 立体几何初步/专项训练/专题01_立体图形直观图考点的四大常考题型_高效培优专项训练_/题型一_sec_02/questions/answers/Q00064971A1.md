> > ![](../../../images/questions/ceeb084893a1200b79295b723d1ada66324b9f1d6b954fe35a5b4fe57f9df809.jpg)
> >
> > 所以 $\vee ABC$ 为等腰三角形，有 $AB = AC > AD$
