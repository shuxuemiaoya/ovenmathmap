> [!faq]- 《专题8.3 简单几何体的表面积与体积（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 2:9
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据 $S_{\triangle B_1DE} = S_{\text{矩形}ABB_1A_1} - S_{\triangle A_1B_1D} - S_{\triangle ADE} - S_{\triangle EBB_1}$ ，求得 $S_{\triangle DEB_1} = \frac{2}{3} S_{\triangle AA_1B_1}$ ，再根据三棱锥的换底性可得
> >
> > $V_{C_{1}-AA_{1}B_{1}}=V_{A-A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}}=\frac{1}{3}V_{ABC-A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}}$ ，由此可得答案·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $S_{\triangle B_{1}DE} = S_{矩形ABB_{1}A_{1}} - S_{\triangle A_{1}B_{1}D} - S_{\triangle ADE} - S_{\triangle EBB_{1}}$
> >
> > $E$ 是 $AB$ 的三等分点（靠近点 $A$ ）， $D$ 是 $AA_{1}$ 的中点，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {\triangle A _ {1} B _ {1} D} = \frac {1}{2} S _ {\triangle A A _ {1} B _ {1}} \quad S _ {\triangle A D E} = \frac {1}{6} S _ {\triangle A A _ {1} B _ {1}} \quad S _ {\triangle E B B _ {1}} = \frac {2}{3} S _ {\triangle A B B _ {1}} = \frac {2}{3} S _ {\triangle A A _ {1} B _ {1}}
> > $$
> >
> > 又∵ $S_{矩形ABB_{1}A_{1}}=2S_{\triangle AA_{1}B_{1}}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {\triangle B _ {1} D E} = 2 S _ {\triangle A A _ {1} B _ {1}} - \frac {1}{2} S _ {\triangle A A _ {1} B _ {1}} - \frac {1}{6} S _ {\triangle A A _ {1} B _ {1}} - \frac {2}{3} S _ {\triangle A A _ {1} B _ {1}} = \frac {2}{3} S _ {\triangle A A _ {1} B _ {1}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore V _ {C _ {1} - D E B _ {1}} = \frac {2}{3} V _ {C _ {1} - A A _ {1} B _ {1}} = \frac {2}{3} V _ {A - A _ {1} B _ {1} C _ {1}} = \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} V _ {A B C - A _ {1} B _ {1} C _ {1}} = \frac {2}{9} V _ {A B C - A _ {1} B _ {1} C _ {1}}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ 三棱锥 $D-B_{1}C_{1}E$ 的体积与三棱柱 $ABC-A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$ 的体积之比为 2:9
> >
> > 故答案为：2:9.
