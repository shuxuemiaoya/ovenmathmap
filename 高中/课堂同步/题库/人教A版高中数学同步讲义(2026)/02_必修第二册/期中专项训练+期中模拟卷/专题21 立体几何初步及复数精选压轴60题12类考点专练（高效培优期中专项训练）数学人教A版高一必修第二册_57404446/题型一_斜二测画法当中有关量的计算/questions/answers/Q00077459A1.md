>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/cdb037c98ab0f81979c3d199432dc864b5492ea5730df4f6bd71570f6ecb8814.jpg) A.2 B. $2\sqrt{2}$ C.4 D.6
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 将直观图还原为原图，如图，
> >
> > ![](../../../images/questions/part-001/6fba26fac2b97dea8cc362ea171ffa9b2fb610283418ddfefa380da71bca5bb9.jpg)
> >
> > 在直观图中， $O'B' = A'B' = 2$ ，则 $O'A' = 2\sqrt{2}$
> >
> > 故在原图中， $OA = 2O'A' = 4\sqrt{2}$ ， $OB = O'B' = 2$
> >
> > 所以 $AB = \sqrt{OA^2 + OB^2} = \sqrt{32 + 4} = 6$ ，而 $AD = BC = B'C' = 4$
> >
> > 所以原四边形 ABCD 中最长边为 6.
