> [!faq]- 《专题22 解三角形精选压轴60题12类考点专练（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高一必修第二册_57404525》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $(-2,1)$ $$ \sqrt {3} $$ (2) 12
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）先利用余弦定理求得 A，再利用向量的几何意义可得向量 $BO \cdot AC = 2 - c$ ，用正弦定理可得
> >
> > $c=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{\tan B}+1$ ，结合角B的范围继而求解；
> >
> > (2) 用三角形面积公式结合条件求得 $S_{\triangle OAC} = \frac{1}{\tan B}, S_{\triangle OBC} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot \left(1 + \frac{1}{\tan^2 B}\right)$ ，面积作差转化为函数求最值
> >
> > 即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $(b+c+a)(b+c-a)=3bc$ ，
> >
> > 可化为 $b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2} = bc$
> >
> > 由余弦定理知， $\cos A = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc} = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 又 $0 < A < \pi$ ，所以 $A = \frac{\pi}{3}$
> >
> > 由 $BO \cdot AC = BO \cdot (BC - BA) = BO \cdot BC - BO \cdot BA$
> >
> > 因为 O 为锐角 $\vee ABC$ 外接圆圆心，
> >
> > 所以 $\overset{\square\square}{BO}\cdot\overset{\square\square}{BC}=\frac{1}{2}\overset{\square\square}{BC}^{2}=\frac{1}{2}a^{2},\overset{\square\square}{BO}\cdot\overset{\square\square}{BA}=\frac{1}{2}\overset{\square\square}{BA}^{2}=\frac{1}{2}c^{2},$
> >
> > 由余弦定理得，
> >
> > $$
> > a ^ {2} = b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - 2 b c = 4 + c ^ {2} - 2 c
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\overset{\square\square}{BO}\cdot\overset{\square\square}{AC}=\frac{1}{2}a^{2}-\frac{1}{2}c^{2}=\frac{1}{2}(4+c^{2}-2c)-\frac{1}{2}c^{2}=2-c$
> >
> > 由正弦定理得， $\frac{b}{\sin B}=\frac{c}{\sin C}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > c = \frac {b \sin C}{\sin B} = \frac {2 \sin (A + B)}{\sin B} = \frac {2 \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{3} + B\right)}{\sin B}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {3} \cos B + \sin B}{\sin B} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{\tan B} + 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {由} \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 0 <   B <   \frac {\pi}{2} \\ 0 <   \frac {2 \pi}{3} - B <   \frac {\pi}{2} \end{array} , \text {解得} \frac {\pi}{6} <   B <   \frac {\pi}{2} \right.,
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan B\in \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}, + \infty\right)$
> >
> > 则 $c = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\tan B} + 1 \in (1,4)$
> >
> > 所以 $BO \cdot AC = 2 - c \in (-2,1)$ .
> >
> > (2) 设 $\vee ABC$ 的外接圆半径为 R，
> >
> > 则 $OA = OB = OC = R$
> >
> > 且 $2R = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{2}{\sin B}$ 即 $R = \frac{1}{\sin B}$
> >
> > 因为 $\angle AOC = 2B, \angle BOC = 2A = \frac{2\pi}{3}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $S_{\triangle OAC} = \frac{1}{2} R^{2} \cdot \sin \angle AOC = \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{\sin^{2} B} \cdot \sin 2B = \frac{1}{\tan B}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\triangle O B C} = \frac {1}{2} R ^ {2} \cdot \sin \angle B O C = \frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {1}{\sin^ {2} B} \cdot \sin \frac {2 \pi}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} \cdot \frac {\sin^ {2} B + \cos^ {2} B}{\sin^ {2} B} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} \cdot \left(1 + \frac {1}{\tan^ {2} B}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $S_{\triangle OAC} - S_{\triangle OBC} = \frac{1}{\tan B} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \cdot \left(1 + \frac{1}{\tan^{2} B}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} \cdot \frac {1}{\tan^ {2} B} + \frac {1}{\tan B} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} \left(\frac {1}{\tan B} - \frac {2 \sqrt {3}}{3}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{1 2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{\tan B} = \frac {2 \sqrt {3}}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan B = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $\triangle OAC$ 和 $\triangle OBC$ 面积之差的最大值 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{12}$ .
