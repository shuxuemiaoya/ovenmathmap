> [!faq]- 《专题02 平面向量的运算（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高一必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > G 满足 $\overline{GA} + \overline{GB} + \overline{GC} = 0$ ，∴ G 为 $\vee ABC$ 的重心， $\therefore \overline{AG} = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2}(AB + AC) = \frac{1}{3}(AB + AC)$ 又∵ $AG = 3AM$ $\therefore BM = BA + AM = BA + \frac{1}{3}AG = BA + \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{3}(AB + AC) = \frac{BA - \frac{1}{9}BA + \frac{1}{9}AC}{}$ $=\frac{8}{9}BA+\frac{1}{9}\left(BC-BA\right)=\frac{7}{9}BA+\frac{1}{9}BC$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
