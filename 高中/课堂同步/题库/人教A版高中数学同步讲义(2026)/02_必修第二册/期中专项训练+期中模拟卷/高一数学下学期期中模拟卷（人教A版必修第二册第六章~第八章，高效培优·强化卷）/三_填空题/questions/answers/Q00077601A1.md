> [!faq]- 《高一数学下学期期中模拟卷（人教A版必修第二册第六章~第八章，高效培优·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) (i) $\left\{\begin{aligned}m=2,\\ n=-1.\end{aligned}\right.$ ; (ii) 平行，理由见解析 (2) $z_{2} = -1 + \mathrm{i}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）（i）由题意得 $b = ma + nc = (m + 2n - ni, n + mi + 2ni) = (z_1, -1)$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}m+2n-n\mathrm{i}=z_{1},\\ n+mi+2ni=-1,\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}m+2n-n\mathrm{i}=z_{1},\\ n=-1,\\ m+2n=0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}z_{1}=\mathrm{i},\\ n=-1,\\ m=2,\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}m=2,\\ n=-1.\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > (ii) 由 (i) 知 $b = (i, -1)$ ，所以 $a + b = (1 + i, -1 + i)$ ， $a - b = (1 - i, 1 + i)$ ，
> >
> > 因为 $\left(a + b\right) \cdot \left(a - b\right) = (1 + \mathrm{i})(1 + \mathrm{i}) + (-1 + \mathrm{i})(1 - \mathrm{i}) = 4\mathrm{i}$ ，得 $\left|\left(a + b\right) \cdot \left(a - b\right)\right| = 4$
> >
> > 因为 $\left|a+b\right|=\sqrt{(a+b)\cdot(a+b)}=\sqrt{(1+i,-1+i)\cdot(1+i,-1+i)}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {(1 + i) (1 - i) + (- 1 + i) (- 1 - i)} = \sqrt {(1 + i) (1 - i) + (1 - i) (1 + i)} = 2
> > $$
> >
> > 同理得 $\left|a-b\right|=\sqrt{(1-\mathrm{i})(1+\mathrm{i})+(1+\mathrm{i})(1-\mathrm{i})}=2$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|\left(a+b\right)\cdot\left(a-b\right)\right|=\left|\left(a+b\right)\right|\left|\left(a-b\right)\right|$ ，故 $a+b$ 与 a-b 平行。
> >
> > (2) 设 $z_{2} = \lambda + \mu i, (\lambda, \mu \in \mathbb{R})$
> >
> > 则 $a \cdot d = (1, \mathrm{i}) \cdot (1 + \mathrm{i}, z_2) = (1, \mathrm{i}) \cdot (1 + \mathrm{i}, \lambda + \mu \mathrm{i}) = 1 - \mathrm{i} + \mathrm{i}(\lambda - \mu \mathrm{i}) = 1 + \mu + (\lambda - 1) \mathrm{i}$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {得} \left| \vec {a} \cdot d \right| = \sqrt {\left(1 + \mu\right) ^ {2} + \left(\lambda - 1\right) ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {又} \left| a \right| = \sqrt {(1 , \mathrm{i}) \cdot (1 , \mathrm{i})} = \sqrt {1 + \mathrm{i} (- \mathrm{i})} = \sqrt {2}, \left| d \right| = \sqrt {(1 + \mathrm{i} , \lambda + \mu \mathrm{i}) \cdot (1 + \mathrm{i} , \lambda + \mu \mathrm{i})}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {(1 + \mathrm{i}) (1 - \mathrm{i}) + (\lambda + \mu \mathrm{i}) \cdot (\lambda - \mu \mathrm{i})} = \sqrt {2 + \lambda^ {2} + \mu^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > 若 $a$ 与 $d$ 平行，则 $\left|a\cdot d\right| = \left|a\right|\left|d\right|$ ，即 $\sqrt{\left(1 + \mu\right)^2 + \left(\lambda - 1\right)^2} = \sqrt{2}\sqrt{2 + \lambda^2 + \mu^2}$
> >
> > 化简整理得 $\left(\lambda + 1\right)^2 + \left(\mu - 1\right)^2 = 0$ ，所以 $\lambda = -1$ ， $\mu = 1$ ，所以 $z_2 = -1 + i$
