> [!faq]- 《高一数学下学期期中模拟卷（人教A版必修第二册第六章~第八章，高效培优·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{\pi}{3}$ (2) $4\sqrt{3}$ (3) $2\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $m \perp n$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $3\cos^{2}A - \sin^{2}A = 0 \Rightarrow 3\cos^{2}A = \sin^{2}A$
> >
> > 因为 $\vee ABC$ 是锐角三角形，
> >
> > 所以 $A \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，因此 $\cos A > 0, \sin A > 0$
> >
> > 所以由 $3\cos^{2}A=\sin^{2}A\Rightarrow\sqrt{3}\cos A=\sin A\Rightarrow\tan A=\sqrt{3}\Rightarrow A=\frac{\pi}{3}$ ;
> >
> > (2) 因为 $\forall ABC$ 的面积为 $3\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{2} bc\cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 3\sqrt{3}\Rightarrow bc = 12$
> >
> > 由余弦定理得，
> >
> > $$
> > a ^ {2} = b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - 2 b c \cdot \frac {1}{2} \Rightarrow a ^ {2} = (b + c) ^ {2} - 3 b c \Rightarrow b + c = \sqrt {a ^ {2} + 3 b c} = \sqrt {1 2 + 3 6} = 4 \sqrt {3};
> > $$
> >
> > (3) 由 $\overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} + \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AC} \Rightarrow \overrightarrow{AD}^2 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} + \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AC}\right)^2$
> >
> > $$
> > \Rightarrow \left| \overrightarrow {A D} \right| = \sqrt {\frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{9} \overrightarrow {A C} ^ {2} + 2 \times \left(\frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {A B}\right) \cdot \left(\frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A C}\right)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \Rightarrow \left| \stackrel {\square \square} {A D} \right| = \sqrt {\frac {1}{4} \times 4 ^ {2} + \frac {1}{9} \times 6 ^ {2} + 2 \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} \times 4 \times 6 \times \frac {1}{2}} = 2 \sqrt {3}.
> > $$
