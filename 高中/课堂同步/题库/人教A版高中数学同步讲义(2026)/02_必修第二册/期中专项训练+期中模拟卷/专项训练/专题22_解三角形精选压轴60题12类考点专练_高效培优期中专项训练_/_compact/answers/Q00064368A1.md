> [!faq]- 《专题22_解三角形精选压轴60题12类考点专练_高效培优期中专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用正弦定理和余弦定理结合三角变换公式可求三角函数式的值.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由正弦定理可得 $\frac{\sin A\sin B}{\sin^{2}C}=\frac{ab}{c^{2}}$ ，由余弦定理可得 $\cos C=\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}-c^{2}}{2ab}$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & \text {故} \frac {2 \tan A \tan B}{\tan C (\tan A + \tan B)} = \frac {2 \times \frac {\sin A}{\cos A} \times \frac {\sin B}{\cos B}}{\frac {\sin C}{\cos C} \left(\frac {\sin A}{\cos A} + \frac {\sin B}{\cos B}\right)} = \frac {2 \sin A \sin B}{\frac {\sin C}{\cos C} \times (\sin A \cos B + \sin B \cos A)} \\ & = \frac {2 \sin A \sin B \cos C}{\sin^ {2} C} = \frac {2 a b \times \frac {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2} - c ^ {2}}{2 a b}}{c ^ {2}} = \frac {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2} - c ^ {2}}{c ^ {2}} = \frac {2 0 2 4 c ^ {2}}{c ^ {2}} = 2 0 2 4. \end{array}
> > $$
