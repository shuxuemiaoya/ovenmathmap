> [!faq]- 《专题04_平面向量基本定理及坐标表示_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\lambda = \frac{3}{4}$ (2) $\frac{DM}{DB} = \frac{7}{10}$ (3) $\left[\frac{13\sqrt{5}}{10}, \frac{\sqrt{41}}{2}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）在直角梯形ABCD中，易得 $\angle ABC = \frac{\pi}{4}$ ， $BC = 2\sqrt{2}$
> >
> > 因为 $AE \cdot BC = (AB + \lambda BC) \cdot BC = AB \cdot BC + \lambda BC^2 = 0$
> >
> > 可得 $-3 \times 2\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + 8\lambda = 0$ ，所以 $\lambda = \frac{3}{4}$ .
> >
> > (2) 因为 $AE = AB + BE = AB + \lambda BC = AB + \lambda \left( BA + AD + DC \right) = AB - \lambda AB + \lambda AD + \frac{\lambda}{3} AB$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(1 - \frac {2}{3} \lambda\right) \overset {\square \square \dot {1}} {A B} + \lambda \overset {\square \square \dot {1}} {A D}
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $\lambda=\frac{1}{3}$ 时， $AE=\frac{7}{9}AB+\frac{1}{3}AD$
> >
> > 设 $AM = xAE$ ， $DM = yDB$
> >
> > 则 $AM = xAE = \frac{7}{9}xAB + \frac{1}{3}xAD$
> >
> > 又因为 $\overline{AM} = \overline{AD} + \overline{DM} = \overline{AD} + yDB = \overline{AD} + y\left(\overline{DA} + \overline{AB}\right) = yAB + (1 - y)\overline{AD}$
> >
> > 且 $\begin{array}{ll}\text{，} & \text{不共线，则}\left\{\frac{7}{9}x = y\right.\\ \left.\frac{1}{3}x = 1 - y\right.\end{array}$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x = \frac{9}{10}\\ y = \frac{7}{10} \end{array} \right.,$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{DM}{DB} = \frac{7}{10}$ .
> >
> > (3) 因为 $AF = AD + DF = AD + (1 - \lambda)DC = AD + \frac{1 - \lambda}{3}AB$ ， $AE = \lambda AD + \left(1 - \frac{2}{3}\lambda\right)AB$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{2}AF+\frac{1}{2}AE=\left(1+\frac{\lambda}{2}\right)AD+\left(\frac{5}{6}-\frac{2\lambda}{3}\right)AB$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \stackrel {\square} {A F} + \frac {1}{2} \stackrel {\square} {A E} \right| ^ {2} = \left(1 + \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) ^ {2} \left| \stackrel {\square} {A D} \right| ^ {2} + \left(\frac {5}{6} - \frac {2 \lambda}{3}\right) ^ {2} \left| \stackrel {\square} {A B} \right| ^ {2} = 4 \left(1 + \frac {\lambda}{2}\right) ^ {2} + 9 \left(\frac {5}{6} - \frac {2 \lambda}{3}\right) ^ {2} [ ]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = (\lambda + 2) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {5}{2} - 2 \lambda\right) ^ {2} = 5 \lambda^ {2} - 6 \lambda + \frac {4 1}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 由题意知， $\lambda\in[0,1]$
> >
> > 所以当 $\lambda=\frac{3}{5}$ 时， $\left|\frac{1}{2}AE+AF\right|$ 取到最小值 $\sqrt{5\times\left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^{2}-6\times\frac{3}{5}+\frac{41}{4}}=\frac{13\sqrt{5}}{10}$ ，
> > 当 $\lambda=0$ 时， $\left|\frac{1}{2}AE+AF\right|$ 取到最大值 $\frac{\sqrt{41}}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|\frac{1}{AF}+\frac{1}{2}\frac{1}{AE}\right|$ 的取值范围是 $\left[\frac{13\sqrt{5}}{10},\frac{\sqrt{41}}{2}\right]$ .
> >
