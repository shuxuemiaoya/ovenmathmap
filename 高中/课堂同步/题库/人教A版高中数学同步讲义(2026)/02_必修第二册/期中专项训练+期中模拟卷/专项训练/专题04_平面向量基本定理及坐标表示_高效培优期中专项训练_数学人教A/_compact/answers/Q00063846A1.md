> [!faq]- 《专题04_平面向量基本定理及坐标表示_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\overline{DE}=-\overline{AD}+\frac{1}{2}\overline{AB}$ ; $\overline{DC}=-\frac{1}{2}\overline{AD}+\overline{AB}$ (2) $x - y = \frac{3}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （ $^{1}$ ）因为 E 是 AB 的中点，由向量的线性运算法则，
> >
> > 可得： $DE = DA + AE = DA + \frac{1}{2} AB = -AD + \frac{1}{2} AB$
> >
> > $\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square$ $DC = DA + AB + BC = -AD + AB + \frac{1}{2} \square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square + \frac{1}{2} \square AD + AB$
> >
> > (2) 由 F, E, C 三点共线， $DF = xAB + yAD$
> >
> > 又因为 $\overline{DE} = -\overline{AD} + \frac{1}{2}\overline{AB}$ ， $\overline{DC} = -\frac{1}{2}\overline{AD} + \overline{AB}$ ，所以 $-\frac{4}{3}\left(\overline{DE} - \frac{1}{2}\overline{DC}\right) = \overline{AD}$ ， $-\frac{4}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2}\overline{DE} - \overline{DC}\right) = \overline{AB}$
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{DF}=xAB+yAD=x\left[-\frac{4}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2}DE-DC\right)\right]+y\times\left(-\frac{4}{3}\left(DE-\frac{1}{2}DC\right)\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(- \frac {2}{3} x - \frac {4}{3} y\right) D E + \left(\frac {4}{3} x + \frac {2}{3} y\right) D C
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $F, E, C$ 三点共线，所以 $\left(-\frac{2}{3} x - \frac{4}{3} y\right) + \left(\frac{4}{3} x + \frac{2}{3} y\right) = 1$
> >
> > 所以 $x - y = \frac{3}{2}$ .
