> [!faq]- 《专题20_平面向量精选压轴60题12类考点专练_高效培优期中专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{\sqrt{39}}{2}$ (2) $\frac{4\sqrt{91}}{91}$ (3) $\frac{2}{3}$ , 证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）运用中线长的向量表达式，结合数量积定义可解；
> >
> > (2) 转化为向量夹角余弦值可解；
> >
> > (3) 根据中线的性质及三点共线求证即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $M$ 为 $BC$ 的中点， $\therefore AM = \frac{1}{2} AB + \frac{1}{2} AC$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore A M ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} (A B + A C) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} \left(A B ^ {2} + 2 A B \cdot A C + A C ^ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{4} (4 + 2 5 + 2 \times 2 \times 5 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}) = \frac {3 9}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > A M \mid = \frac {\sqrt {3 9}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 因为 $BN = \frac{1}{2} AC - AB$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore B N ^ {2} = \left(\frac {1}{2} A C - A B\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} A C ^ {2} - A C \cdot A B + A B ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} \times 2 5 - 2 \times 5 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + 4 = \frac {2 1}{4},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \left|\overrightarrow{BN}\right| = \frac{\sqrt{21}}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos \angle M P N = \cos \left\langle A M, B N \right\rangle = \frac {A M \cdot B N}{| A M | | B N |} = \frac {\frac {1}{2} (A B + A C) \cdot \left(\frac {1}{2} A C - A B\right)}{| A M | | B N |} = \frac {4 \sqrt {9 1}}{9 1}.
> > $$
> >
> > (3) $t = \frac{2}{3}$ ，证明如下：
> >
> > $\overline{AP}=tAM=\frac{1}{2}t\left(AB+AC\right)=\frac{1}{2}tAB+AC\frac{1}{2}t$
> >
> > 因为 $B, P, N$ 三点共线，
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{AP} = \lambda \overline{AB} + (1 - \lambda) \overline{AN}$ ，又 $\overline{AN} = \frac{1}{2} \overline{AC}$
> >
> > 所以 $AP = \lambda AB + \frac{1 - \lambda}{2} AC$
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{t}{2}&=\lambda\\\frac{t}{2}&=\frac{1-\lambda}{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $t=\frac{2}{3}$
