>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ![](../../images/questions/b7a70964cccae776e7d56864ff1560da19b5111ed9bbfd5070d37e48b84ed747.jpg) A. 1 B. $\sqrt{2}$ C. $\sqrt{3}$ D. 2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图，连接AC，
> >
> > 由 $\left|\overline{OC}\right|=\left|\overline{OB}\right|$ ，得 $\angle ABC=\angle OCB=30^{\circ}$
> >
> > 因为 C 为半圆上的点，所以 $\angle ACB = 90^{\circ}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\left| \overline{AC} \right| = \frac{1}{2} \left| AB \right| = 1$
> >
> > ![](../../images/questions/eabca584639da071d7825467e6782ba855f61ca431b871b4c0ff96db6017ad8e.jpg)
> >
