> [!faq]- 《专题07_正弦定理_余弦定理_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高一必》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\left[-\frac{\pi}{6}+k\pi,\frac{\pi}{3}+k\pi\right](k\in\mathbf{Z})$ (2) (i) 2 ; (ii) $\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $f(x)=\sqrt{3}\sin\omega x\cos\omega x-\cos^{2}\omega x-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin2\omega x-\frac{1}{2}\cos2\omega x-1=\sin\left(2\omega x-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)-1$
> >
> > 由 $f(x)$ 的图象上相邻两条对称轴之间的距离为 $\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，有 $T = \frac{2\pi}{2\omega} = \pi$ ，得 $\omega = 1$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $f(x) = \sin \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) - 1$
> >
> > 令 $-\frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi \leq 2x - \frac{\pi}{6} \leq \frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi (k \in \mathbf{Z})$ ，解得 $-\frac{\pi}{6} + k\pi \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{3} + k\pi (k \in \mathbf{Z})$
> >
> > 所以函数 $f(x)$ 的单调递增区间为 $\left[-\frac{\pi}{6} + k\pi, \frac{\pi}{3} + k\pi\right](k \in \mathbf{Z})$
> >
> > (2) (i) 已知 $f(B) = \sin \left(2B - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) - 1 = 0$ ，由 $B \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，得 $B = \frac{\pi}{3}$ ，
> >
> > 由正弦定理 $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{c}{\sin C} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} = 2$ ，得 $a = 2\sin A, c = 2\sin C$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{a + c}{\sin A + \sin C} = \frac{2\sin A + 2\sin C}{\sin A + \sin C} = 2$
> >
> > (ii) 由 (i) 可知 $a = 2\sin A, c = 2\sin C$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\triangle A B C} = \frac {1}{2} a c \sin B = \sqrt {3} \sin A \sin C = \sqrt {3} \sin A \sin \left(\frac {2 \pi}{3} - A\right) = \frac {3}{2} \sin A \cos A + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin^ {2} A
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {3}{4} \sin 2 A - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} \cos 2 A + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin \left(2 A - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $\triangle ABC$ 是锐角三角形，有 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}0 < A < \frac{\pi}{2}\\ 0 < \frac{2\pi}{3} -A <   \frac{\pi}{2} \end{array} \right.$ ，得 $A\in \left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ， $2A - \frac{\pi}{6}\in \left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)$
> >
> > 则 $\sin \left(2A - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \in \left(\frac{1}{2}, 1\right]$ ，所以 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \sin \left(2A - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \in \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\right]$ ，
> >
> > 即 $\triangle ABC$ 面积的取值范围是 $\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\right]$
> >
