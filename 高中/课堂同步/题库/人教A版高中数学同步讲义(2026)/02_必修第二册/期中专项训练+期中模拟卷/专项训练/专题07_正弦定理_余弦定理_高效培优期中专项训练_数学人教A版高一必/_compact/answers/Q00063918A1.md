> [!faq]- 《专题07_正弦定理_余弦定理_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高一必》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)2 (2) $-\frac{3\sqrt{3}+\sqrt{7}}{8}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由余弦定理， $a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2}-2bc\cos A$ ，即 $a^{2}=1+2-2\times1\times\sqrt{2}\times\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\right)=4$
> >
> > 而 $a > 0$ ，所以 $a = 2$ ；
> >
> > (2) 因为 $\cos A = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$ ，所以 $\cos 2A = 2\cos^{2}A - 1 = 2 \times \left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\right)^{2} - 1 = -\frac{3}{4}$ ，
> >
> > 又 $A \in (0, \pi)$ ，所以 $\sin A > 0$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \sin 2 A = 2 \sin A \cos A = 2 \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} A} \cos A = 2 \sqrt {1 - \left(- \frac {\sqrt {2}}{4}\right) ^ {2}} \times \left(- \frac {\sqrt {2}}{4}\right) = - \frac {\sqrt {7}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \left(2 A - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos 2 A + \frac {1}{2} \sin 2 A = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \times \left(- \frac {3}{4}\right) + \frac {1}{2} \times \left(- \frac {\sqrt {7}}{4}\right) = - \frac {3 \sqrt {3} + \sqrt {7}}{8}.
> > $$
