> [!faq]- 《专题07_正弦定理_余弦定理_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高一必》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because b\cos A + a\cos B = 6$
> >
> > 由余弦定理有： $b \times \frac{b^{2} + c^{2} - a^{2}}{2bc} + a \times \frac{a^{2} + c^{2} - b^{2}}{2ac} = 6$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore c^{2} = 6c$ ，解得 $c = 6$
> >
> > 由 $\overline{BO}=x\overline{BA}+y\overline{BC}$ 得， $\overline{BO}\cdot\overline{BA}=\frac{1}{2}\left|\overline{BA}\right|^{2}=x\overline{BA}\cdot\overline{BA}+y\overline{BC}\cdot\overline{BA}$
> >
> > 即 $\overset{\square\square}{BO}\cdot\overset{\square\square}{BA}=\frac{1}{2}\left|\overset{\square\square}{BA}\right|^{2}=x\left|\overset{\square\square}{BA}\right|^{2}+y\left|\overset{\square\square}{BC}\right|\cdot\left|\overset{\square\square}{BA}\right|\cos B$
> >
> > $$
> > B O \cdot B C = \frac {1}{2} \left| B C \right| ^ {2} = x B A \cdot B C + y B C \cdot B C
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $\overline{BO} \cdot \overline{BC} = \frac{1}{2}\left|\overline{BC}\right|^2 = x\left|\overline{BC}\right| \cdot \left|\overline{BA}\right|\cos B + y\left|\overline{BC}\right|^2$
> >
> > 即： $18 = 36x + 24y$ ， $32 = 24x + 64y$ ，解得 $x = \frac{2}{9}$ ， $y = \frac{5}{12}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore x y = \frac {2}{9} \times \frac {5}{1 2} = \frac {5}{5 4}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
