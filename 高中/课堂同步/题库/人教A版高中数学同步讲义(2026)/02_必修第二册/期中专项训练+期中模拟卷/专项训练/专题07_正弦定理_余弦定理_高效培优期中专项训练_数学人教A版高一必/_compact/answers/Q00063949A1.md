> [!faq]- 《专题07_正弦定理_余弦定理_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高一必》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意有 $\cos\angle ABC=1-2\sin^{2}\frac{\angle ABC}{2}=1-\frac{2}{3}=\frac{1}{3}$ ，所以 $\sin\angle ABC=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}\angle ABC}=\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ，
> > 又 AD = 3DC, BD = 3，所以 $\frac{AD}{4} = \frac{3}{4} AC$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{AD} -\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{AC} -\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{3}{4}\left(\overrightarrow{AB} +\overrightarrow{BC}\right) - \overrightarrow{AB} = -\frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{AB} +\frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{BC} = \frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{BA} +\frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{BC}$ 所以 $\left|\overrightarrow{BD}\right|^2 = \left(\overrightarrow{BD}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{BA} +\frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{BC}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{16}\overrightarrow{BA}^2 +\frac{9}{16}\overrightarrow{BC}^2 +\frac{3}{8}\overrightarrow{BA}\cdot \overrightarrow{BC}$ $= \frac{1}{16}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|^2 +\frac{9}{16}\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|^2 +\frac{3}{8}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|\cos \angle ABC = \frac{1}{16}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|^2 +\frac{9}{16}\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|^2 +\frac{1}{8}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|$ $2\sqrt{\frac{1}{16}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|^2\times\frac{9}{16}\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|^2} +\frac{1}{8}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right| = \frac{1}{2}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|$
> >
> > 即 $\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|\leq2\left|\overrightarrow{BD}\right|^{2}=18$ ，当且仅当 $\frac{1}{16}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|^{2}=\frac{9}{16}\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|^{2}\Rightarrow\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|=3\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|=3\sqrt{6}$ 时，等号成立，
> > 所以 $S_{\triangle ABC}=\frac{1}{2}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|\sin\angle ABC=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{BC}\right|\leq\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}\times18=6\sqrt{2}$
> >
> > 故选：B.
> >
> > ## 考点 10 正余弦定理与三角函数性质的结合应用
