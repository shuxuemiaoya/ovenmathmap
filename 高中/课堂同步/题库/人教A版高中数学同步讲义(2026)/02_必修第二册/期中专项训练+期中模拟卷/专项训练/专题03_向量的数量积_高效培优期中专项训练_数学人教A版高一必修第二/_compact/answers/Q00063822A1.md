> [!faq]- 《专题03_向量的数量积_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高一必修第二》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\overline{AD}=\frac{3}{4}\overline{AB}+\frac{1}{4}\overline{AC}$ ， $\overline{BE}=\frac{1}{3}\overline{AC}-\overline{AB}$ (2)证明见解析 (3)证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $\overline{AC}=3\overline{AE}$ ，所以 $\overline{AE}=\frac{1}{3}\overline{AC}$ ，则 $\overline{BE}=\overline{AE}-\overline{AB}=\frac{1}{3}\overline{AC}-\overline{AB}$ 。
> >
> > 因为 $CD = 3DB$ ，所以 $BD = \frac{1}{4}BC$
> >
> > 则 $AD = AB + BD = AB + \frac{1}{4} BC = AB + \frac{1}{4}\left(AC - AB\right) = \frac{3}{4} AB + \frac{1}{4} AC$
> >
> > (2) 证明：由 (1) 可得
> >
> > $\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square=\left(\frac{3}{4}\square\square\square\square AB+\frac{1}{4}\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\right)\cdot\left(\frac{1}{3}\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square-AB\right)=\frac{1}{12}\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square2-\frac{3}{4}\square\square\square\square\square\square\square\square2.$
> >
> > 因为 $AB = 2$ ， $AC = 6$ ，所以 $\overline{AD} \cdot \overline{BE} = \frac{1}{12} \overline{AC}^2 - \frac{3}{4} \overline{AB}^2 = \frac{1}{12} \times 36 - \frac{3}{4} \times 4 = 0$ ，则 $AD \perp BE$ ：
> >
> > (3) 证明：因为 $A, F, D$ 三点共线，
> >
> > 所以 $BF = kBA + (1 - k)BD = -kAB + \frac{1 - k}{4}BC = \frac{1 - k}{4}AC - \frac{3k + 1}{4}AB$
> >
> > 因为 B, F, E 三点共线，所以 $\overline{BF} = \lambda \overline{BE} = \frac{1}{3} \lambda \overline{AC} - \lambda \overline{AB}$
> >
> > 则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} -\frac{3k + 1}{4} = -\lambda \\ \frac{1 - k}{4} = \frac{1}{3}\lambda \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\lambda = \frac{1}{2}$ ，即 $\square \square \square BF = \frac{1}{2} BE$
> >
> > 故 $F$ 是线段 $BE$ 的中点.
