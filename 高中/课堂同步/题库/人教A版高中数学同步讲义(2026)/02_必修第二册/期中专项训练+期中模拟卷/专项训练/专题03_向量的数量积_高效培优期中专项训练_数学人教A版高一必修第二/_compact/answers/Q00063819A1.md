> [!faq]- 《专题03_向量的数量积_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高一必修第二》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{4\sqrt{91}}{91}$ (2) -1, $\lambda = \frac{10}{11}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设 $AB = a$ ， $AC = b$ ， $\because M$ 、 $N$ 分别为 $BC$ 、 $AC$ 的中点， $\therefore AM = \frac{1}{2}a + \frac{1}{2}b$ ， $BN = -a + \frac{1}{2}b$ ， $\because |a| = 4, |b| = 10$ ， $a \cdot b = 4 \times 10 \times \frac{1}{2} = 20$ ， $\therefore |AM| = \sqrt{\left(\frac{1}{2}a + \frac{1}{2}b\right)^2} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{4}a^2 + \frac{1}{2}a \cdot b + \frac{1}{4}b^2}$ $= \sqrt{\frac{1}{4} \times 16 + \frac{1}{2} \times 20 + \frac{1}{4} \times 100} = \sqrt{39}$ ， $\left|\overrightarrow{BN}\right| = \sqrt{\left(-a + \frac{1}{2}b\right)^2} = \sqrt{a^2 - b \cdot a + \frac{1}{4}b^2} = \sqrt{16 - 20 + \frac{1}{4} \times 100} = \sqrt{21}$ ，
> > 又 $AM \cdot BN = \left(\frac{1}{2}a + \frac{1}{2}b\right) \cdot \left(-a + \frac{1}{2}b\right) = -\frac{1}{2}a^2 - \frac{1}{4}a \cdot b + \frac{1}{4}b^2$ $= -\frac{1}{2} \times 4^2 - \frac{1}{4} \times 20 + \frac{1}{4} \times 10^2 = 12$ ， $\therefore \cos \angle MPN = \frac{|AM| \cdot |BN|}{|AM| \cdot |BN|} = \frac{12}{\sqrt{39} \times \sqrt{21}} = \frac{4\sqrt{91}}{91}$ ，
> > 即 $\angle MPN$ 的余弦值为 $\frac{4\sqrt{91}}{91}$ ·
> > (2）设 $|NA| = x (0 < x < 10)$ ，
> > 则 $NA \cdot NB = NA \cdot (NA + AB) = NA^2 + NA \cdot AB = NA^2 - AN \cdot AB$ $= x^2 - 4x \cdot \frac{1}{2} = x^2 - 2x = (x - 1)^2 - 1$ ，
> > 所以当 $x = 1$ 即 $|NA| = 1$ 时， $NA \cdot NB$ 取最小值-1，即 $AN = \frac{1}{10}AC$ ， $\therefore BN = BA + AN = BA + \frac{1}{10}AC = BA + \frac{1}{10}(BC - BA) = \frac{9}{10}BA + \frac{1}{10}BC$ ， $\because BC = 2BM$ ， $BP = \lambda BN (0 \leq \lambda \leq 1)$ ， $\therefore BP = \lambda (\frac{9}{10}BA + \frac{1}{5}BM) = \frac{9\lambda}{10}BA + \frac{\lambda}{5}BM$
> >
> > ∵ A, P, M 三点共线，
> > ∴ $\frac{9\lambda}{10} + \frac{\lambda}{5} = 1$ ，解得 $\lambda = \frac{10}{11}$ ∴ $\lambda = \frac{10}{11}$ .
