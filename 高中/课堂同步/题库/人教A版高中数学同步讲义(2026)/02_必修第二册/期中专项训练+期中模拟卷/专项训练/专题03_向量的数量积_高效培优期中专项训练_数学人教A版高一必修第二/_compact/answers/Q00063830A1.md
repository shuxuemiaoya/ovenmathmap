> [!faq]- 《专题03_向量的数量积_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高一必修第二》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** BCD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because\left|a+b\right|=\sqrt{3}$ ， $\therefore\left(\vec{a}+b\right)^{2}=3$
> >
> > $\therefore a^{2} + 2a \cdot b + b^{2} = 3$ ，即 $1 + 2a \cdot b + 4 = 3$
> >
> > $\therefore a \cdot b = -1$ ，选项 B 正确·
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a \cdot (a - b) = a ^ {2} - a \cdot b = 1 + 1 = 2 \neq 0
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore a \perp (a - b)$ 不成立，选项 $^{A}$ 错误.
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \left\langle \vec {a}, \vec {b} \right\rangle = \frac {\vec {a} \cdot \vec {b}}{| a | | b |} = \frac {- 1}{1 \times 2} = - \frac {1}{2}, \left\langle \boldsymbol {a}, \boldsymbol {b} \right\rangle \in [ 0, \pi ]
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore a$ 与 $b$ 夹角为 $\frac{2\pi}{3}$ ，选项C正确·
> >
> > $\therefore \left(a - b\right)^{2} = a^{2} - 2a \cdot b + b^{2} = 1 + 2 + 4 = 7$
> >
> > $\therefore\left|a-b\right|=\sqrt{7}$ ，选项D正确.
> >
> > 故选：BCD.
