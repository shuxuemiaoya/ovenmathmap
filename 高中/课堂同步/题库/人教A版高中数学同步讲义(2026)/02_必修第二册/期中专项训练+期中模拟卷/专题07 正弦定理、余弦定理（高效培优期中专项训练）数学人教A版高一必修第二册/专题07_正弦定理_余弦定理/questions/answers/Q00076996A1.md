> [!faq]- 《专题07 正弦定理、余弦定理（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高一必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\frac{5}{3}$ B. $\frac{7}{3}$ C. $\frac{8}{3}$ D. $\frac{10}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $b^{2} - c^{2} = a^{2} - \frac{1}{2} ac$ ，可得 $a^2 +c^2 -b^2 = \frac{1}{2} ac$ ，由余弦定理， $\cos B = \frac{a^2 + c^2 - b^2}{2ac} = \frac{1}{4}$
> >
> > $$
> > \square \square \square B D = \square \square \square B A + \square \square \square A D = \square \square \square B A + \frac {2}{3} \square \square \square A C = \square \square \square B A + \frac {2}{3} (\square \square \square B C - \square \square B A) = \frac {1}{3} \square \square \square B A + \frac {2}{3} \square \square \square B C
> > $$
> >
> > 因 $AB = 4, BC = 3$ ，则 $\boxed{BA} \cdot \boxed{BC} = \left| \boxed{BA} \right| \cdot \boxed{BC} \cos B = 4 \times 3 \times \frac{1}{4} = 3$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\left|\frac{\square\square\square}{BD}\right|=\sqrt{\left(\frac{1}{3}\frac{\square\square\square}{BA}+\frac{2}{3}\frac{\square\square\square}{BC}\right)^{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{1}{9}\frac{\square\square\square}{BA^{2}}+\frac{4}{9}\frac{\square\square\square}{BA}\cdot\frac{\square\square\square}{BC}+\frac{4}{9}\frac{\square\square\square}{BC^{2}}}=\sqrt{\frac{16}{9}+\frac{12}{9}+4}=\frac{8}{3}.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
