> [!faq]- 《专题04 平面向量基本定理及坐标表示（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高一必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)证明见解析 (2) $k = -1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $AB=e_{1}+e_{2},BC=2e_{1}+8e_{2},CD=3\left(e_{1}-e_{2}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\text {UUU}} {B D} = \stackrel {\text {UUU}} {B C} + \stackrel {\text {UUU}} {C D} = 2 \stackrel {\text {U}} {e _ {1}} + 8 \stackrel {\text {U}} {e _ {2}} + 3 \left(\stackrel {\text {U}} {e _ {1}} - \stackrel {\text {U}} {e _ {2}}\right) = 5 \stackrel {\text {U}} {e _ {1}} + 5 \stackrel {\text {U}} {e _ {2}} = 5 \left(\stackrel {\text {U}} {e _ {1}} + \stackrel {\text {U}} {e _ {2}}\right) = 5 \stackrel {\text {UUU}} {A B}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{AB}, \overline{BD}$ 共线，且有公共点，所以 A, B, D 三点共线
> >
> > (2) 因为 $ke_{1} + e_{2}$ 和 $e_{1} + ke_{2}$ 是方向相反的两个向量，
> >
> > 所以存在实数 $\lambda$ ，使 $k e_1^{\sqcup} + e_2^{\sqcup} = \lambda \left(e_1^{\sqcup} + k e_2^{\sqcup}\right) = \lambda e_1^{\sqcup} + \lambda k e_2^{\sqcup}$ ，且 $\lambda < 0$
> >
> > 又 $\stackrel{\sqcup}{e_1},\stackrel{\sqcup}{e_2}$ 不共线，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}k = \lambda \\ 1 = \lambda k \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}k = 1\\ \lambda = 1 \end{array} \right.$ ，或 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}k = -1\\ \lambda = -1 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 因为 $\lambda < 0$ ，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} k = -1 \\ \lambda = -1 \end{array} \right.$ ，所以 $k = -1$
> >
