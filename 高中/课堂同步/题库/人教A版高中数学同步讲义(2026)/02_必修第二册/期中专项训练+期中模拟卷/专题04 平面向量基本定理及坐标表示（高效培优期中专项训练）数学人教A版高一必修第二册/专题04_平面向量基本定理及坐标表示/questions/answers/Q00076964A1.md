> [!faq]- 《专题04 平面向量基本定理及坐标表示（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高一必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $2x - y \in [-1, 1]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $P(\cos \theta ,\sin \theta),\theta \in \left[0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ ， $E(1,0),D(0,1),F\left(\frac{3}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)$
> >
> > 则 $AP = (\cos \theta, \sin \theta), AF = \left(\frac{3}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right), ED = (-1, 1)$
> >
> > 因为 $\stackrel{\cup\cup}{AP}=xED+yAF$ ，所以 $(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)=\left(-x+\frac{3}{2}y,x+\frac{1}{2}y\right)$
> >
> > 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} -x + \frac{3}{2} y = \cos \theta \\ x + \frac{1}{2} y = \sin \theta \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $x = \frac{3}{4}\sin \theta -\frac{1}{4}\cos \theta ,y = \frac{1}{2}\sin \theta +\frac{1}{2}\cos \theta$
> >
> > 所以 $2x - y = \sin \theta -\cos \theta = \sqrt{2}\sin \left(\theta -\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$
> >
> > 因为 $\theta -\frac{\pi}{4}\in \left[-\frac{\pi}{4},\frac{\pi}{4}\right]$ ，所以 $\sin \left(\theta -\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\in \left[-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right]$
> >
> > 即 $2x - y \in [-1, 1]$
