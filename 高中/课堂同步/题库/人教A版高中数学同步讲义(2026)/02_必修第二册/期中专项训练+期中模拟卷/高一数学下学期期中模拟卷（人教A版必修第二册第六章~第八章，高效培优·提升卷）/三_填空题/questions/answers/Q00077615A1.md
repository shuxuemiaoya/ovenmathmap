> [!faq]- 《高一数学下学期期中模拟卷（人教A版必修第二册第六章~第八章，高效培优·提升卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $|\omega| = \sqrt{7}$ $$ \left[ - \frac {7}{4}, \sqrt {6} + 1 \right] \tag {2} $$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）当 m=1 时， $z_{1}=1+\sqrt{3}i$
> >
> > 可得 $z_{1}^{2} = (1 + \sqrt{3}\mathrm{i})^{2} = 1 + 2\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i} + 3\mathrm{i}^{2} = 1 + 2\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i} - 3 = -2 + 2\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i}$
> >
> > $\omega=\frac{2}{z_{1}^{2}+2}-\frac{4}{3}\overline{z_{1}}-\frac{2}{3}$ ，其中 $\overline{z_{1}}=1-\sqrt{3}i$ ，可得：
> >
> > $$
> > \omega = \frac {2}{- 2 + 2 \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i} + 2} - \frac {4}{3} (1 - \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}) - \frac {2}{3} = \frac {2}{2 \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}} - \frac {4}{3} + \frac {4 \sqrt {3}}{3} \mathrm{i} - \frac {2}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \omega = - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \mathrm{i} - \frac {4}{3} + \frac {4 \sqrt {3}}{3} \mathrm{i} - \frac {2}{3} = - 2 + \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}
> > $$
> >
> > 可得 $|\omega| = \sqrt{(-2)^2 + (\sqrt{3})^2} = \sqrt{4 + 3} = \sqrt{7}$
> >
> > (2) 因为点 $Z_{1}$ 与点 $Z_{2}$ 为同一点，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}m &= \sqrt{2}\sin\left(\theta + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \\ \sqrt{3}m &= \lambda - \sin 2\theta\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 化简可得 $\sqrt{2}\sin(\theta+\frac{\pi}{4})=\sqrt{2}(\sin\theta\cos\frac{\pi}{4}+\cos\theta\sin\frac{\pi}{4})=\sin\theta+\cos\theta$ ，即 $m=\sin\theta+\cos\theta$
> >
> > 将 $m = \sin \theta +\cos \theta$ 代入 $\sqrt{3} m = \lambda -\sin 2\theta$ ，可得 $\lambda = \sqrt{3} (\sin \theta +\cos \theta) + \sin 2\theta$
> >
> > 令 $t = \sin \theta +\cos \theta$ ，根据辅助角公式可得 $t = \sin \theta +\cos \theta = \sqrt{2}\sin (\theta +\frac{\pi}{4})$ ，所以 $t\in [-\sqrt{2},\sqrt{2} ]$
> >
> > 将 $t = \sin \theta +\cos \theta$ 两边平方可得 $t^2 = (\sin \theta +\cos \theta)^2 = \sin^2\theta +2\sin \theta \cos \theta +\cos^2\theta = 1 + \sin 2\theta$ ，即 $\sin 2\theta = t^2 -1$
> >
> > 则 $\lambda = \sqrt{3}t + t^{2} - 1 = t^{2} + \sqrt{3}t - 1$ .
> >
> > 将 $\lambda = t^2 +\sqrt{3} t - 1$ 看作关于 $t$ 的二次函数，其对称轴为 $t = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，开口向上.
> >
> > 当 $t = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 时， $\lambda_{\min} = (-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})^{2} + \sqrt{3} \times (-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}) - 1 = \frac{3}{4} - \frac{3}{2} - 1 = -\frac{7}{4}$ ;
> >
> > 当 $t = \sqrt{2}$ 时， $\lambda_{\max} = (\sqrt{2})^2 + \sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{2} - 1 = 1 + \sqrt{6}$ .
> >
> > 所以 $\lambda$ 的取值范围是 $\left[-\frac{7}{4},1+\sqrt{6}\right]$ .
