> [!faq]- 《第七章 复数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $4x - i = (3 + i)y$ ，∴ $4x - i = 3y + yi$ ，∴ $\left\{\begin{aligned}4x &= 3y \\ y &= -1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，∴ $\left\{\begin{aligned}x &= -\frac{3}{4}\\ y &= -1\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| x + y \mathrm{i} \right| = \left| - \frac {3}{4} - \mathrm{i} \right| = \sqrt {\left(- \frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {2} + (- 1) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {\frac {9}{1 6} + 1} = \frac {5}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
