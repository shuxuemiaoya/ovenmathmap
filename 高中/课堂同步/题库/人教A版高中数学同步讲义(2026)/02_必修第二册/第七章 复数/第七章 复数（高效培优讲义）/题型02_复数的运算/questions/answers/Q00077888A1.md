> [!faq]- 《第七章 复数（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 首先化简得到 z = 2 ，再求 $\left|z\right|$ 即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\frac{z}{1 + \mathrm{i}} = 1 - \mathrm{i}$ ，则 $z = (1 + \mathrm{i})(1 - \mathrm{i}) = 2$ ，所以 $|z| = 2$
> >
> > 故选：C
> >
> > ## 方法技巧
> >
> > 设 $z_{1} = a + bi, z_{2} = c + di(a, b, c, d \in R)$ ，则
> > (1) $z_{1} \pm z_{2} = a \pm c + (b \pm d)i$ (2) $z_{1} \cdot z_{2} = ac - bd + (ad + bc)i$ $\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}} = \frac{ac + bd}{c^{2} + d^{2}} + \frac{bc - ad}{c^{2} + d^{2}} i (z_{2} \neq 0)$ (3)
