> [!faq]- 《专题01 复数概念的四大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=3\\ y&=-8\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=-8\\ y&=3\end{aligned}\right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由已知复数相等可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x+y&=-5\\ -xy&=24\end{aligned}\right.$ ，求解即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意， $\left\{\begin{aligned}x+y&=-5\\ -xy&=24\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=3\\ y&=-8\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=-8\\ y&=3\end{aligned}\right.$
