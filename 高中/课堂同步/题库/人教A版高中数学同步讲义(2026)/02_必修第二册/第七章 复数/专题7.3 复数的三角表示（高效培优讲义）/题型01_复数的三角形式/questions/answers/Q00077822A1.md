> [!faq]- 《专题7.3 复数的三角表示（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $1+i$ (2) $2\sqrt{3}-2i$ (3) $\cos\frac{\pi}{4}+i\sin\frac{\pi}{4}$ (4) $\cos\frac{11\pi}{6}+i\sin\frac{11\pi}{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （ $^{1}$ ）求出三角函数值展开后可得；
> >
> > (2) 结合诱导公式求出三角函数值展开后可得；
> >
> > (3) 先计算模长，再求辐角，然后可得；
> >
> > (4) 先计算模长，再求辐角，然后可得·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $\sqrt{2} (\cos \frac{\pi}{4} + i \sin \frac{\pi}{4}) = \sqrt{2} (\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} i) = 1 + i$
> >
> > $$
> > 4 (\cos \frac {1 1 \pi}{6} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {1 1 \pi}{6}) = 4 [ \cos (2 \pi - \frac {\pi}{6}) + \mathrm{i} \sin (2 \pi - \frac {\pi}{6}) ] = 4 (\cos \frac {\pi}{6} - \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {\pi}{6}) = 4 (\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} - \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{i}) = 2 \sqrt {3} - 2 \mathrm{i}
> > $$
> >
> > (3) 复数的模长为 1，辐角为 $\theta = \arctan \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\pi}{4}$ ，所以 $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} i = \cos \frac{\pi}{4} + i \sin \frac{\pi}{4}$ .
> >
> > (4) 复数的模长为 1，辐角为 $\theta = \arctan\left(\frac{-\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}}\right) = \frac{11\pi}{6}$ ， $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{1}{2}i = \cos\frac{11\pi}{6} + i\sin\frac{11\pi}{6}$ .
