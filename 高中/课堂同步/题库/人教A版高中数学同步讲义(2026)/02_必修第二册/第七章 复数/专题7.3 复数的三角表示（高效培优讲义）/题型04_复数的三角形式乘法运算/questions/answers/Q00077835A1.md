> [!faq]- 《专题7.3 复数的三角表示（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** } ^ {(1)} \sqrt {6} \left(\cos \frac {1 1 \pi}{1 2} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {1 1 \pi}{1 2}\right) \quad (2) 2 1 \left(\cos \frac {1 1 \pi}{1 2} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {1 1 \pi}{1 2}\right) \quad (3) 2 \left(\cos \frac {4 \pi}{3} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {4 \pi}{3}\right) $$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据复数三角形式的乘法公式和乘方公式计算即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {6} \left[ \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{1 2} + \frac {5 \pi}{6}\right) + \mathrm{i} \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{1 2} + \frac {5 \pi}{6}\right) \right] = \sqrt {6} \left(\cos \frac {1 1 \pi}{1 2} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {1 1 \pi}{1 2}\right)
> > $$
> > $$
> > \left(^ {2}\right) \text {原式} = 2 1 \left[ \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{6} + \frac {3 \pi}{4}\right) + \mathrm{i} \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{6} + \frac {3 \pi}{4}\right) \right] = 2 1 \left(\cos \frac {1 1 \pi}{1 2} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {1 1 \pi}{1 2}\right)
> > $$
> > (3) 原式 = 2 $\left(\cos\frac{4\pi}{3}+i\sin\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)$ .
