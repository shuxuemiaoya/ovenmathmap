> [!faq]- 《专题7.3 复数的三角表示（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)-30 $$ - 2 \left(\cos \frac {\pi}{1 2} + i \sin \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) \tag {2} $$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 直接利用复数的三角表示的运算法则结合三角恒等变换计算得到答案·
> >
> > $$
> > \text {
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （} ^ {1}) \text {原式} = 3 0 \left[ \cos \left(1 8 ^ {\circ} + 5 4 ^ {\circ} + 1 0 8 ^ {\circ}\right) + \mathrm{isin} \left(1 8 ^ {\circ} + 5 4 ^ {\circ} + 1 0 8 ^ {\circ}\right) \right] = 3 0 \left(\cos 1 8 0 ^ {\circ} + \mathrm{isin} 1 8 0 ^ {\circ}\right) = - 3 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(^ {2}\right) \text {原式} = 2 \left[ \cos \left(\frac {7 \pi}{4} - \frac {2 \pi}{3}\right) + \mathrm{i} \sin \left(\frac {7 \pi}{4} - \frac {2 \pi}{3}\right) \right] = 2 \left(\cos \frac {1 3 \pi}{1 2} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {1 3 \pi}{1 2}\right) = - 2 \left(\cos \frac {\pi}{1 2} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)
> > $$
