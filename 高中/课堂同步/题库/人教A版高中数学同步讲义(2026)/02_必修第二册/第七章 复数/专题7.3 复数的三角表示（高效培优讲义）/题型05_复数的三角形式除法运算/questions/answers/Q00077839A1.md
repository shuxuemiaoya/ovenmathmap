> [!faq]- 《专题7.3 复数的三角表示（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 复数化为三角形式，按三角形式的乘除运算法则，即可求解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > 2 \left(\cos \frac {2 \pi}{3} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {2 \pi}{3}\right) \times \sqrt {3} \left(\cos \frac {5 \pi}{6} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {5 \pi}{6}\right) = 2 \sqrt {3} \left(\cos \frac {3 \pi}{2} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {3 \pi}{2}\right) = - 2 \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i} \tag {1}
> > $$
> > $$
> > \left(^ {2}\right) \text {原式} = 3 \sqrt {2} \left[ \cos \left(1 6 0 ^ {\circ} - 2 5 ^ {\circ}\right) + \mathrm{i} \sin \left(1 6 0 ^ {\circ} - 2 5 ^ {\circ}\right) \right] = 3 \sqrt {2} \left(\cos 1 3 5 ^ {\circ} + \mathrm{i} \sin 1 3 5 ^ {\circ}\right) = 3 \sqrt {2} \left(- \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} + \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \mathrm{i}\right) = - 3 + 3 \mathrm{i}
> > $$
