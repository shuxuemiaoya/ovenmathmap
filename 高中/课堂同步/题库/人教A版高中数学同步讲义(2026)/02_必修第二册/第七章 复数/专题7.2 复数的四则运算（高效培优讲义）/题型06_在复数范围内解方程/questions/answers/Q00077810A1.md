> [!faq]- 《专题7.2 复数的四则运算（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 1
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据复数模求出复数 z，再由根与系数的关系求解 p, q 即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $z = 1 + b\mathrm{i}(b\in \mathbb{R})$
> >
> > 则 $|z| = \sqrt{1 + b^2} = \sqrt{3}$ ，解得 $b = \pm \sqrt{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $z = 1 + \sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}$ 或 $z = 1 - \sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}$
> >
> > 由题意可知， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}1 + \sqrt{2}\mathrm{i} + 1 - \sqrt{2}\mathrm{i} = -p\\ (1 + \sqrt{2}\mathrm{i})(1 - \sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}) = q \end{array} \right.\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l}p = -2\\ q = 3 \end{array} \right.\Rightarrow p + q = 1.$
> >
> > 故答案为： $^{1}$
