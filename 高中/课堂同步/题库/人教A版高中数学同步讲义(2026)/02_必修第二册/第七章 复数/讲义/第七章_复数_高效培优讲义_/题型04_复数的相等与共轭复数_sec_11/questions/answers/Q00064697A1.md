> [!faq]- 《第七章_复数_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $x = 4$ ， $y = -6$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由复数相等的条件列方程组求解.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：由 $\left(\frac{1}{2} x + y\right) + \left(5x + \frac{2}{3} y - 16\right)\mathrm{i} = -4$
> >
> > 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{1}{2} x + y = -4 \\ 5x + \frac{2}{3} y - 16 = 0 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 4 \\ y = -6 \end{array} \right.$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore x = 4 \quad y = - 6
> > $$
