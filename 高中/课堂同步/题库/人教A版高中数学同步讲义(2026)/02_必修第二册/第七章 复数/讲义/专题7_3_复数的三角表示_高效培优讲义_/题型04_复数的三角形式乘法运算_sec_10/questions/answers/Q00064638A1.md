> [!faq]- 《专题7_3_复数的三角表示_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{1}{32}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{32}i$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用三角形式的复数乘法、除法、乘方运算法则求解即可·
> >
> > $$
> > \text {
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为} \left[ 2 \left(\cos \frac {\pi}{3} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {\pi}{3}\right) \right] ^ {- 4} = \frac {1}{\left[ 2 \left(\cos \frac {\pi}{3} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {\pi}{3}\right) \right] ^ {4}} = \frac {1}{2 ^ {4} \left(\cos \frac {4}{3} \pi + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {4}{3} \pi\right)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\cos 0 + \mathrm{i} \sin 0}{1 6 \left(\cos \frac {4 \pi}{3} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {4 \pi}{3}\right)} = \frac {1}{1 6} \left[ \cos \left(- \frac {4 \pi}{3}\right) + \mathrm{i} \sin \left(- \frac {4 \pi}{3}\right) \right] = - \frac {1}{3 2} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3 2} \mathrm{i}.
> > $$
