> [!faq]- 《专题7_2_复数的四则运算_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a=\pm\frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}$ (2)不存在，理由见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （ $^{1}$ ）根据复数模的公式，列方程求解；
> >
> > (2) 先计算 $z_{1} \cdot z_{2}$ ，再根据复数的性质，列方程求解。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $Qz_{1}=a+2i,\quad z_{2}=1+3ai$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| z _ {1} \right| = \sqrt {a ^ {2} + 4} \quad \left| z _ {2} \right| = \sqrt {9 a ^ {2} + 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $\because \left|z_{1}\right| = \left|z_{2}\right|$ ， $\therefore \sqrt{a^2 + 4} = \sqrt{9a^2 + 1}$ ，即 $8a^{2} = 3$ ，解得 $a^2 = \frac{3}{8}$ ，即 $a = \pm \frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}$
> >
> > (2) $\because \mathrm{i}^2 = -1$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore z _ {1} \cdot z _ {2} = (a + 2 \mathrm{i}) (1 + 3 a \mathrm{i}) = a + 3 a ^ {2} \mathrm{i} + 2 \mathrm{i} - 6 a = - 5 a + (2 + 3 a ^ {2}) \mathrm{i}
> > $$
> >
> > $\because z_{1} \cdot z_{2} \in \mathbf{R}$ ， $\therefore z_{1} \cdot z_{2}$ 的虚部为 $0$ ， $2 + 3a^{2} = 0$ ，该方程无实数解，
> >
> > $\therefore$ 不存在实数 a，使得 $z_{1} \cdot z_{2} \in R$
> >
> > 2. 计算 $\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^6 \cdot \left(-\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^9 =$ \_\_\_\_.
> >
> > 【答案】-1
> >
> > 【分析】根据复数的乘方运算法则求解即可·
> >
> > 【详解】因为 $\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} +\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^3 = \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^3 +3\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2\left(\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\right) + 3\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^2 +\left(\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^3 = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{8} +\frac{9}{8}\mathrm{i} - \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{8} -\frac{1}{8}\mathrm{i} = \mathrm{i}$
> >
> > 所以 $\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}+\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^{6}=\mathrm{i}^{2}=-1$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \left(- \frac {1}{2} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \mathrm{i}\right) ^ {3} = \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {3} + 3 \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} \left(\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \mathrm{i}\right) + 3 \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) \left(\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \mathrm{i}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \mathrm{i}\right) ^ {3} = - \frac {1}{8} + \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{8} \mathrm{i} + \frac {9}{8} - \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{8} \mathrm{i} = 1
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\left(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^{9}=1$ .
> >
> > 所以 $\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} +\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^6\cdot \left(-\frac{1}{2} +\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\mathrm{i}\right)^9 = -1.$
> >
> > 故答案为：-1·
