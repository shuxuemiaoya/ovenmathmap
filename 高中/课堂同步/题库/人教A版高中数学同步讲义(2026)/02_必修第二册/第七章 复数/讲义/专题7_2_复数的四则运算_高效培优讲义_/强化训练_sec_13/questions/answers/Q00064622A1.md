> [!faq]- 《专题7_2_复数的四则运算_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. 若 $z_{1}^{2} \in R$ ，则 $z_{1} \in R$ B. 若 $\frac{z_{1}}{z_{2}} \in R$ ，则 $\overline{z_{1}} \cdot z_{2} \in R$ C. 若 $|z_{1}z_{2}| = 2z_{2}$ ，则 $z_{1}\overline{z_{1}} = 4$ D. 若 $z_{1}z_{2} = |z_{1}|^{2}$ ，则 $z_{1} = z_{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 举例说明判断 AD；利用复数运算及共轭复数、复数模的意义计算判断 BC.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 对于 A，取 $z_{1}=i$ ， $z_{1}^{2}=-1\in R$ ，而 $z_{1}\notin R$ ，A 错误；
> >
> > 对于 B，设 $z_{1}=x_{1}+y_{1}i, z_{2}=x_{2}+y_{2}i, x_{1}, y_{1}, x_{2}, y_{2} \in R, x_{2}^{2} + y_{2}^{2} \neq 0$
> >
> > $\frac{z_1}{z_2} = \frac{x_1 + y_1\mathrm{i}}{x_2 + y_2\mathrm{i}} = \frac{(x_1 + y_1\mathrm{i})(x_2 - y_2\mathrm{i})}{(x_2 + y_2\mathrm{i})(x_2 + y_2\mathrm{i})} = \frac{x_1x_2 + y_1y_2}{x_2^2 + y_2^2} +\frac{x_2y_1 - x_1y_2}{x_2^2 + y_2^2}\mathrm{i},$ 由 $z_{2}$
> >
> > 得 $x_{2}y_{1} - x_{1}y_{2} = 0$ ， $\overline{z_1} z_2 = (x_1 - y_1\mathrm{i})(x_2 + y_2\mathrm{i}) = x_1x_2 + y_1y_2 - (x_2y_1 - x_1y_2)\mathrm{i} = x_1x_2 + y_1y_2\in \mathbf{R}$ ，B正确；
> >
> > 对于 $C$ ，由 $\left|z_1z_2\right| = 2z_2$ 及 $z_{2}\neq 0$ ，设 $z_{1} = c + d\mathbf{i},c,d\in \mathbb{R}$ ， $z_{2} = a > 0$
> >
> > $\left|z_{1}z_{2}\right| = \left|a(c + di)\right| = a\sqrt{c^{2} + d^{2}} = 2a$ ，解得 $c^2 +d^2 = 4$
> >
> > 则 $z_{1}\overline{z_{1}} = (c + d\mathrm{i})(c - d\mathrm{i}) = c^{2} + d^{2} = 4$ ，C正确；
> >
> > 对于 D，取 $z_{1}=i, z_{2}=-i$ ， $z_{1}z_{2}=1=\left|z_{1}\right|^{2}$ ，而 $z_{1} \neq z_{2}$ ，D 错误·
> >
> > 故选 **BC**
