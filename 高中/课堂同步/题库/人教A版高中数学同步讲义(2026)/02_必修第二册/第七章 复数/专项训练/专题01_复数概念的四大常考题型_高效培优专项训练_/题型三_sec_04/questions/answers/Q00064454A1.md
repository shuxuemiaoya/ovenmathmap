> > [!note]- **【分析】**

>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：由 $z_{2} = b - 3\mathrm{i}$ 得 $\overline{z}_2 = b + 3\mathrm{i}$ ， $\because z_1 = \overline{z_2}$ ， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} -1 = b \\ a = 3 \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a = 3\\ b = -1 \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore a + b = 2$ ：
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
