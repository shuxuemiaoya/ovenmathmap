> [!faq]- 《专题01_复数概念的四大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \frac{1}{4} \\ y = -2 \end{array} \right.$ (2) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \frac{15}{7} \\ y = -\frac{3}{7} \end{array} \right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据复数相等即可得到方程组，解出即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}-4x+1&=0\\ y+2&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=\frac{1}{4}\\ y&=-2\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > (2) 由题意得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x - 2y = 3 \\ -(3x + y) = -6 \end{array} \right.$ , 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \frac{15}{7} \\ y = -\frac{3}{7} \end{array} \right.$ .
