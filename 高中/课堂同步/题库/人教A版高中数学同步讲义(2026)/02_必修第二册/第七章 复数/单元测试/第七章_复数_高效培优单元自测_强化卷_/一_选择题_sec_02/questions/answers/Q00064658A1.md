> [!faq]- 《第七章_复数_高效培优单元自测_强化卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** BD
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 应用特殊值法判断 A、C，根据共轭复数的性质及模的相关运算判断 B、D.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 取 $z_{1} = 1 + \mathrm{i}, z_{2} = -1 + \mathrm{i}$ ，满足 $\left|z_{1}\right| = \left|z_{2}\right|$ ，但它们的实部不相等，A错误；
> >
> > 由 $\left|\overline{z_{1}}\right|=\left|z_{1}\right|$ ， $\left|\overline{z_{2}}\right|=\left|z_{2}\right|$ ，所以 $\left|\overline{z_{1}}\right|=\left|\overline{z_{2}}\right|$ ，B正确；
> >
> > 取 $z_{1} = 1 + \mathrm{i}, z_{2} = -1 + \mathrm{i}, z_{3} = 1$ ，满足 $\left|z_{1}\right| = \left|z_{2}\right|$ ， $\left|z_{1} - z_{3}\right| = 1 \neq \left|z_{2} - z_{3}\right| = \sqrt{(-2)^{2} + 1^{2}} = \sqrt{5}$ ，C 错误；
> >
> > 设复数 $z_{t} = a + b\mathrm{i}, z_{s} = c + d\mathrm{i}, a, b, c, d \in \mathbf{R}$ ，则 $z_{t}z_{s} = (ac - bd) + (ad + bc)\mathrm{i}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| z _ {t} z _ {s} \right| = \sqrt {(a c - b d) ^ {2} + (a d + b c) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {a ^ {2} c ^ {2} + b ^ {2} d ^ {2} + a ^ {2} d ^ {2} + b ^ {2} c ^ {2}} = \sqrt {(a ^ {2} + b ^ {2}) (c ^ {2} + d ^ {2})} = \left| z _ {t} \right| \left| z _ {s} \right|,
> > $$
> >
> > $\left|z_{1}z_{3}\right|=\left|z_{1}\right|\left|z_{3}\right|,\left|z_{2}z_{3}\right|=\left|z_{2}\right|\left|z_{3}\right|$ ，所以 $\left|z_{1}z_{3}\right|=\left|z_{2}z_{3}\right|$ ，D正确.
> >
> > 故选 **BD**
