> [!faq]- 《专题7_3_离散型随机变量的数字特征_高效培优讲义_数学人教A版高二选》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)分布列见解析， $\frac{16}{25}$ (2) $m = 5, n = 1$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）由题意可知：进球个数 X 的可能取值为 0,1,2,3,4，求分布列，进而可得方差；
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 可知： $E(X)=2$ ， $D(X)=\frac{16}{25}$ ，根据期望和方差的性质列式求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意可知：进球个数 $X$ 的可能取值为 $0,1,2,3,4$ ，则有：
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = \left(1 - \frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {2} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1 6}{6 2 5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {1}{5} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {2} + \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{5} \times \frac {4}{5} \times \left(1 - \frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1 3 6}{6 2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \left(\frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {2} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {2} \times \left(1 - \frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {2} + C _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {4}{5} \times \left(1 - \frac {4}{5}\right) \times C _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{5} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{5}\right) = \frac {3 2 1}{6 2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = \left(\frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {2} \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{5} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{5}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {2} \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {4}{5} \times \left(1 - \frac {4}{5}\right) = \frac {1 3 6}{6 2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 4) = \left(\frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1 6}{6 2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $X$ 的分布列为：
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>4</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{16}{625}$ </td><td> $\frac{136}{625}$ </td><td> $\frac{321}{625}$ </td><td> $\frac{136}{625}$ </td><td> $\frac{16}{625}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > 所以 $E(X) = 0 \times \frac{16}{625} + 1 \times \frac{136}{625} + 2 \times \frac{321}{625} + 3 \times \frac{136}{625} + 4 \times \frac{16}{625} = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > D (X) = (0 - 2) ^ {2} \times \frac {1 6}{6 2 5} + (1 - 2) ^ {2} \times \frac {1 3 6}{6 2 5} + (2 - 2) ^ {2} \times \frac {3 2 1}{6 2 5} + (3 - 2) ^ {2} \times \frac {1 3 6}{6 2 5} + (4 - 2) ^ {2} \times \frac {1 6}{6 2 5} = \frac {1 6}{2 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 可知： $E(X)=2$ ， $D(X)=\frac{16}{25}$
> >
> > 若随机变量 $Y = mX + n(m > 0)$ ，且 $E(Y) = 11, D(Y) = 16$
> >
> > 可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&2m+n=11\\&\frac{16}{25}m^{2}=16\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $m=5, n=1$
