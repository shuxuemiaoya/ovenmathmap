> [!faq]- 《第七章_随机变量及其分布列_高效培优讲义_数学人教A版高二选择性必修第》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3}{8}/0.375$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据全概率公式求解小兰取出的是 $^{2}$ 个红球的概率 $P(A_{0})P(B|A_{0})+P(A_{1})P(B|A_{1})+P(A_{2})P(B|A_{2})$
> >
> > 进而求解小红从口袋中取出的也是 $^{2}$ 个红球的概率 $P(A_{2})P(B|A_{2})$ ，即可利用贝叶斯公式求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设小红取出2个球，其中红球的个数为 $i = 0,1,2$ 个的事件为 $A_{i}$ ，从小兰取出2个球，其中红球的个数
> >
> > 为 $^{2}$ 个的事件为 B ，
> >
> > 由题意可得： $P(A_{0})=\frac{C_{3}^{2}C_{3}^{0}}{C_{6}^{2}}=\frac{1}{5}$ ， $P(B|A_{0})=\frac{C_{4}^{0}C_{2}^{2}}{C_{6}^{2}}=\frac{1}{15}$ ；
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(A _ {1}\right) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {1} \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {1}}{\mathrm{C} _ {6} ^ {2}} = \frac {3}{5}, P (B \mid A _ {1}) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {0} \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2}}{\mathrm{C} _ {6} ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{5};
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(A _ {2}\right) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {0} \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2}}{\mathrm{C} _ {6} ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{5}, P (B \mid A _ {2}) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0} \mathrm{C} _ {4} ^ {2}}{\mathrm{C} _ {6} ^ {2}} = \frac {2}{5};
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} P \left(A _ {2} \mid B\right) = \frac {P \left(A _ {2}\right) P \left(B \mid A _ {2}\right)}{P \left(A _ {0}\right) P \left(B \mid A _ {0}\right) + P \left(A _ {1}\right) P \left(B \mid A _ {1}\right) + P \left(A _ {2}\right) P \left(B \mid A _ {2}\right)} = \frac {\frac {1}{5} \times \frac {2}{5}}{\frac {1}{5} \times \frac {1}{1 5} + \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {1}{5} + \frac {1}{5} \times \frac {2}{5}} = \frac {3}{8},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以小兰取出的是 $^{2}$ 个红球，则小红取出的也是 $^{2}$ 个红球的概率为 $\frac{3}{8}$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{3}{8}$
