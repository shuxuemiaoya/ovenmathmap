> [!faq]- 《第七章_随机变量及其分布列_高效培优讲义_数学人教A版高二选择性必修第》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{407}{435}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 令事件 $A_{i}$ 表示该箱玻璃杯有 i 只次品，i = 0, 1, 2，令事件 B 表示顾客买下这箱玻璃碗，计算
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(A _ {0}\right), P \left(A _ {1}\right), P \left(A _ {2}\right), P \left(B \mid A _ {0}\right), P \left(B \mid A _ {1}\right), P \left(B \mid A _ {2}\right) \text {, 再利用全概率公式即可求解}.
> > $$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 令事件 $A_{i}$ 表示该箱玻璃杯有 i 只次品，i = 0, 1, 2，令事件 B 表示顾客买下这箱玻璃碗，
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(A _ {0}\right) = 0. 7, P \left(A _ {1}\right) = 0. 2, P \left(A _ {2}\right) = 0. 1, P \left(B \mid A _ {0}\right) = 1, P \left(B \mid A _ {1}\right) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {2 9} ^ {5}}{\mathrm{C} _ {3 0} ^ {5}} = \frac {5}{6}, P \left(B \mid A _ {2}\right) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {2 8} ^ {5}}{\mathrm{C} _ {3 0} ^ {5}} = \frac {2 0}{2 9},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (B) = P \left(A _ {0}\right) P \left(B \mid A _ {0}\right) + P \left(A _ {1}\right) P \left(B \mid A _ {1}\right) + P \left(A _ {2}\right) P \left(B \mid A _ {2}\right) = 0. 7 \times 1 + 0. 2 \times \frac {5}{6} + 0. 1 \times \frac {2 0}{2 9} = \frac {4 0 7}{4 3 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{407}{435}$
