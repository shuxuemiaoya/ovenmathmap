> [!faq]- 《专题7.3 离散型随机变量的数字特征（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第三册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** <table><tr><td>X</td><td>-1</td><td>0</td><td>a</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{6}$ </td><td>b</td><td> $\frac{1}{4}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{4}$ </td></tr><tr><td>A. $\frac{155}{36}$ </td><td>B. $\frac{155}{72}$ </td><td></td><td>C. $\frac{155}{144}$ </td><td>D. $\frac{49}{144}$ </td></tr></table>
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据分布列的性质，结合期望和方差的运算性质进行求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由分布列可得 $\frac{1}{6} + b + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = 1 \Rightarrow b = \frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > E (1 2 X) = 7 \Rightarrow 1 2 E (X) = 7 \Rightarrow 1 2 \left(- \frac {1}{6} + 0 + \frac {1}{4} a + \frac {1}{4} \times 2\right) = 7 \Rightarrow a = 1
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $E(12X)=7\Rightarrow12E(X)=7\Rightarrow E(X)=\frac{7}{12}$
> >
> > $$
> > D (X) = \frac {1}{6} \times \left(- 1 - \frac {7}{1 2}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{3} \times \left(0 - \frac {7}{1 2}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \times \left(1 - \frac {7}{1 2}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \times \left(2 - \frac {7}{1 2}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1 5 5}{1 4 4},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $D(2X+3)=2^{2}\cdot D(X)=4\times\frac{155}{144}=\frac{155}{36}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **A**
