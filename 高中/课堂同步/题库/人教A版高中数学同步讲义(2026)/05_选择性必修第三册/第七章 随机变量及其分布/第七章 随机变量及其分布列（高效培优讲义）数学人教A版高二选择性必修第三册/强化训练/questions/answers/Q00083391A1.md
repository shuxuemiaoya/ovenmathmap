> [!faq]- 《第七章 随机变量及其分布列（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第三册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** = >
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据题意，解法 $^{1}$ 、求出数学期望和方差，根据基本不等式比较即可；解法 $^{2}$ 、根据期望相等，根据
> >
> > 数据离散程度判断方差即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解法 $^{1}$ ： $E(X)=\frac{1}{4}(x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}+x_{4})$
> >
> > $$
> > D (X) = \frac {1}{4} \left\{\left[ x _ {1} - E (X) \right] ^ {2} + \left[ x _ {2} - E (X) \right] ^ {2} + \left[ x _ {3} - E (X) \right] ^ {2} + \left[ x _ {4} - E (X) \right] ^ {2} \right\}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \left\{\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} x _ {i} ^ {2} - 4 [ E (X) ] ^ {2} \right\}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > E (Y) = \frac {1}{4} \left(\frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2} + \frac {x _ {2} + x _ {3}}{2} + \frac {x _ {3} + x _ {4}}{2} + \frac {x _ {4} + x _ {1}}{2}\right) = \frac {1}{4} \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2} + x _ {3} + x _ {4}\right) = E (X)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > D (Y) = \frac {1}{4} \left\{\left[ \frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2} - E (X) \right] ^ {2} + \left[ \frac {x _ {2} + x _ {3}}{2} - E (X) \right] ^ {2} + \left[ \frac {x _ {3} + x _ {4}}{2} - E (X) \right] ^ {2} + \left[ \frac {x _ {4} + x _ {1}}{2} - E (X) \right] ^ {2} \right\}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \left\{\left(\frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {x _ {2} + x _ {3}}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {x _ {3} + x _ {4}}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {x _ {4} + x _ {1}}{2}\right) ^ {2} - 4 [ E (X) ] ^ {2} \right\}.
> > $$
> >
> > 要比较 $D(X)$ 与 $D(Y)$ 的大小，只需比较 $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_i^2$ 与 $\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{x_2 + x_3}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{x_3 + x_4}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{x_4 + x_1}{2}\right)^2$ .
> >
> > 两者作差并化简，
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} x _ {i} ^ {2} - \left(\frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2}\right) ^ {2} - \left(\frac {x _ {2} + x _ {3}}{2}\right) ^ {2} - \left(\frac {x _ {3} + x _ {4}}{2}\right) ^ {2} - \left(\frac {x _ {4} + x _ {1}}{2}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{4} \left[ 2 x _ {1} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {2} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {3} ^ {2} + 2 x _ {4} ^ {2} + 2 \left(x _ {1} x _ {2} + x _ {2} x _ {3} + x _ {3} x _ {4} + x _ {4} x _ {1}\right) \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} \left[ \left(x _ {1} - x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} + \left(x _ {2} - x _ {3}\right) ^ {2} + \left(x _ {3} - x _ {4}\right) ^ {2} + \left(x _ {4} - x _ {1}\right) ^ {2} \right] ①,
> > $$
> >
> > 由于 $x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3}, x_{4}$ 为互不相等的正实数，故① > 0
> >
> > 即 $\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_i^2 > \left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{x_2 + x_3}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{x_3 + x_4}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{x_4 + x_1}{2}\right)^2$ ，所以 $D(X) > D(Y)$
> >
> > 解法 $^{2}$ ：易知 $E(X)=E(Y)$
> >
> > 由方差的定义知，方差刻画离散型随机变量取值的离散程度，而随机变量 $Y$ 的取值是随机变量 $X$ 的平均值，
> >
> > 更集中，从而 $D(X) > D(Y)$
