> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由表格数据，得 $\overline{x}=\frac{1}{5}\times(1+2+3+4+5)=3,\quad\overline{y}=\frac{1}{5}\times(59+61+64+68+73)=65$

> > 所以 $\sum_{i=1}^{5}\left(x_i - \overline{x}\right)\left(y_i - \overline{y}\right) = 35$ ， $\sum_{i=1}^{5}\left(x_i - \overline{x}\right)^2 = 10$
> >
> > 所以 $\hat{b}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\left(x_{i}-\bar{x}\right)\left(y_{i}-\bar{y}\right)}{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\left(x_{i}-\bar{x}\right)^{2}}=\frac{35}{10}=3.5$ ， $\hat{a}=\bar{y}-\hat{b}\bar{x}=65-3.5\times3=54.5$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {设} \quad , \text {则} \quad y = c + d x ^ {2} = c + d t \quad , \text {易得} \quad \overline {{t}} = \frac {1}{5} \times \left(1 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {2} + 3 ^ {2} + 4 ^ {2} + 5 ^ {2}\right) = 1 1, \hat {d} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} \left(t _ {i} - \bar {t}\right) \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right)}{\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} \left(t _ {i} - \bar {t}\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {2 1 7}{3 7 4} \approx 0. 6,
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\hat{c} = \overline{y} -\hat{d}\overline{t}\approx 65 - 0.6\times 11 = 58.4$
> >
> > 所以两种模型的回归方程分别为 $\hat{y}=3.5x+54.5,\quad\hat{y}=0.6x^{2}+58.4$
> >
> > (2) 对于 $\hat{y}=3.5x+54.5$ ，其残差平方和为 $\left(59-58\right)^{2}+\left(61-61.5\right)^{2}+\left(64-65\right)^{2}+\left(68-68.5\right)^{2}+\left(73-72\right)^{2}=3.5$ .
> >
> > 对于 $\hat{y} = 0.6x^{2} + 58.4$ ，其残差平方和为 $\left(59 - 59\right)^{2} + \left(61 - 60.8\right)^{2} + \left(64 - 63.8\right)^{2} + \left(68 - 68\right)^{2} + \left(73 - 73.4\right)^{2} = 0.24.$
> >
> > 因为 0.24 < 3.5，所以模型 $\hat{y} = 0.6x^{2} + 58.4$ 的拟合效果更好。
