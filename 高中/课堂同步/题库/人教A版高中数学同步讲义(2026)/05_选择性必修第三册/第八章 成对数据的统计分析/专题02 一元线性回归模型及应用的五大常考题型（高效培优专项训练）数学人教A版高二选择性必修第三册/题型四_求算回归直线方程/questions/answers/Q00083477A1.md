> [!faq]- 《专题02 一元线性回归模型及应用的五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第三册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ② ④
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由题意可得 $m + n = 5$ ，分别取 $m$ 与 $n$ 的值，由公式计算出 $b_{1}, a_{1}, b_{2}, a_{2}, r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}$ 的值，逐一分析四个结论即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知 $1+m+n+4=10$ ，即 $m+n=5\cdot\overline{x}=\frac{1+2+3+4}{4}=2.5,\overline{y}=2.5$ .
> > - **①**：若 m = 3.5 ，则 n = 1.5 ，
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) = (1 - 2. 5) (4 - 2. 5) + (2 - 2. 5) (3. 5 - 2. 5) + (3 - 2. 5) (1. 5 - 2. 5) + (4 - 2. 5) (1 - 2. 5) = - 5. 5,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} = (- 1. 5) ^ {2} + (- 0. 5) ^ {2} + 0. 5 ^ {2} + 1. 5 ^ {2} = 5,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2} = 1. 5 ^ {2} + 1 ^ {2} + (- 1) ^ {2} + (- 1. 5) ^ {2} = 6. 5,
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $b_{1} = \frac{-5.5}{5} = -1.1, a_{1} = 2.5 - (-1.1) \times 2.5 = 5.25$ ， $r_{1} = \frac{-5.5}{\sqrt{5} \times \sqrt{6.5}} \approx -\sqrt{0.93}$ .
> > - **②**：若 $m = 3$ ，则 $n = 2$ ，则
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) = (1 - 2. 5) (4 - 2. 5) + (2 - 2. 5) (3 - 2. 5) + (3 - 2. 5) (2 - 2. 5) + (4 - 2. 5) (1 - 2. 5) = - 5,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} = 5, \quad \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2} = 1. 5 ^ {2} + 0. 5 ^ {2} + (- 0. 5) ^ {2} + (- 1. 5) ^ {2} = 5,
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $b_{2} = \frac{-5}{5} = -1, a_{2} = 2.5 - (-1) \times 2.5 = 5$ ， $r_{2} = \frac{-5}{\sqrt{5} \times \sqrt{5}} = -1$ .
> > - **③**：若 m = 2.5 ，则 n = 2.5 ，
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) = (1 - 2. 5) (4 - 2. 5) + (2 - 2. 5) (2. 5 - 2. 5) + (3 - 2. 5) (2. 5 - 2. 5) + (4 - 2. 5) (1 - 2. 5) = - 4. 5,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} = 5, \quad \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {4} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2} = 1. 5 ^ {2} + (- 1. 5) ^ {2} = 4. 5,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} b _ {3} = \frac {- 4 . 5}{5} = - 0. 9, a _ {3} = 2. 5 - (- 0. 9) \times 2. 5 = 4. 7 5, r _ {3} = \frac {- 4 . 5}{\sqrt {5} \times \sqrt {4 . 5}} = - \sqrt {0 . 9}.
> > $$
> >
> > 由样本点的中心相同知三条回归直线交于同一个点，不可能围成封闭三角形，所以①不正确·
> >
> > 由以上计算可得 $a_{1} > a_{2} > a_{3}, b_{1} < b_{2} < b_{3}$ ，所以②正确，③不正确。
> >
> > 相关系数中， $\left|r_{2}\right|$ 最大，所以④正确·
> >
> > 故答案为：②④·
