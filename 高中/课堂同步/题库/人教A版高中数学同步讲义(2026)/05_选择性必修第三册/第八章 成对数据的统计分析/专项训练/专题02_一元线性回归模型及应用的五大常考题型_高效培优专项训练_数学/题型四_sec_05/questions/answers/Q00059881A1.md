> [!faq]- 《专题02_一元线性回归模型及应用的五大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_数学》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ACD
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用平均数的意义计算可判断 $^{A}$ ；由方差的性质计算可判断 $^{B}$ ；利用回归方程的最小二乘法计算
> >
> > 可判断 C；利用相关系数公式计算可判断 D.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 对于 A， $\overline{y}=\frac{y_{1}+y_{2}+y_{3}+\cdots+y_{n}}{n}=\frac{10x_{1}+10x_{2}+10x_{3}+\cdots+10x_{n}}{n}=10\overline{x}$ ，所以 A 正确，
> >
> > 对于 B，因为 Y = 10X ，所以由方差的性质可得 $\sigma_{2}^{2} = 100\sigma_{1}^{2}$ ，所以 B 错误，
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{C}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {为} \overline {{b}} _ {1} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right)}{\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2}}, \quad \overline {{b}} _ {2} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right)}{\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(1 0 x _ {i} - 1 0 \bar {x}\right) \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right)}{\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(1 0 x _ {i} - 1 0 \bar {x}\right) ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{10\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\left(x_{i} - \overline{x}\right)\left(z_{i} - \overline{z}\right)}{100\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\left(x_{i} - \overline{x}\right)^{2}} = \frac{\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\left(x_{i} - \overline{x}\right)\left(z_{i} - \overline{z}\right)}{10\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\left(x_{i} - \overline{x}\right)^{2}} = \frac{\bar{b}_{1}}{10}$ ，所以，所以C正确，
> >
> > $$
> > r _ {1} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right)}\sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2}} \sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right) ^ {2}}, r _ {2} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right)}{\sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2}} \sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right) ^ {2}}} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(1 0 x _ {i} - 1 0 \bar {x}\right) \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right)}\sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(1 0 x _ {i} - 1 0 \bar {x}\right) ^ {2}} \sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(z _ {i} - \bar {z}\right) ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{10\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\left(x_{i} - \overline{x}\right)\left(z_{i} - \overline{z}\right)}{\sqrt{100\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\left(x_{i} - \overline{x}\right)^{2}}\sqrt{\sum_{i = 1}^{n}\left(z_{i} - \overline{z}\right)^{2}}} = r_{1}$ ，所以D正确·
> >
> > 故选 **ACD**.
