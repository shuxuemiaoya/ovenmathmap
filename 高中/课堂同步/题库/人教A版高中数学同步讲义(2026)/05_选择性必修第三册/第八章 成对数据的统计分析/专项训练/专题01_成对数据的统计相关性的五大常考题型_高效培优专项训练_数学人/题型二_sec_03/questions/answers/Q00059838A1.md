> [!faq]- 《专题01_成对数据的统计相关性的五大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_数学人》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 0.98
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 运用给出的公式计算样本中心，再计算相关系数即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题设， $\overline{x}=\frac{1+2+3+4+5}{5}=3,\quad\sum_{i=1}^{5}(x_{i}-\overline{x})^{2}=\sum_{i=1}^{5}x_{i}^{2}-5\overline{x}^{2}=55-5\times9=10$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2} = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} y _ {i} ^ {2} - 5 \bar {y} ^ {2} \approx 4 0 9 5 4 - 5 \times 7 7 ^ {2} = 1 1 3 0 9
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $r=\frac{b\cdot\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{5}(x_{i}-\bar{x})^{2}}}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{5}(y_{i}-\bar{y})^{2}}}=\frac{32.88\times\sqrt{10}}{\sqrt{11309}}=\frac{328.8}{\sqrt{10\times11309}}\approx\frac{328.8}{336.3}\approx0.98$
> >
> > 故答案为：0.98·
