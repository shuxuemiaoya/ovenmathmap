> [!faq]- 《第八章_成对数据的统计分析_高效培优讲义_数学人教A版高二选择性必修第》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $0.96 / \frac{24}{25}$ $\vec{a}\cdot b==\sum_{i=1}^{10}x_{i}y_{i}-10\bar{x}\cdot\bar{y}=480$ ，故 $r=\cos<a,b>=0.96$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sum_{i = 1}^{10}x_{i} = 30,\sum_{i = 1}^{10}y_{i} = 600$ ，故 $\overline{x} = \frac{30}{10} = 3\overline{y} = \frac{600}{10} = 60$
> >
> > $$
> > \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} = (x _ {1} - \bar {x}, x _ {2} - \bar {x}, \dots , x _ {1 0} - \bar {x}) \cdot (y _ {1} - \bar {y}, y _ {2} - \bar {y}, \dots , y _ {1 0} - \bar {y}) = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {1 0} (x _ {i} - \bar {x}) (y _ {i} - \bar {y})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {1 0} x _ {i} y _ {i} - 1 0 \overline {{{x}}} \cdot \overline {{{y}}} = 2 2 8 0 - 1 0 \times 3 \times 6 0 = 4 8 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > r = \cos \left\langle \vec {a}, \vec {b} \right\rangle = \frac {\vec {a} \cdot \vec {b}}{| \vec {a} | \cdot | \vec {b} |} = \frac {4 8 0}{5 \times 1 0 0} = 0. 9 6.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为：0.96
