> [!faq]- 《专题8_2_一元线性回归模型及其应用_高效培优讲义_数学人教A版高二选》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)-0.98 (2)回归直线方程为 $\hat{y} = -0.59x + 6.87$ ，年利润2.74万元
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）求出 x, y，结合公式求出 r，即可得解；
> >
> > (2) 先利用最小二乘法求出回归直线方程，再将 $x = 7$ 代入计算，即可求解
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $\overline{x} = \frac{1}{5} (1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5) = 3$ ， $\overline{y} = \frac{1}{5} (6.4 + 5.5 + 5.0 + 4.8 + 3.8) = 5.1$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) = (1 - 3) (6. 4 - 5. 1) + (2 - 3) (5. 5 - 5. 1)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > + (3 - 3) (5. 0 - 5. 1) + (4 - 3) (4. 8 - 5. 1) + (5 - 3) (3. 8 - 5. 1) = - 5. 9
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} = (1 - 3) ^ {2} + (2 - 3) ^ {2} + (3 - 3) ^ {2} + (4 - 3) ^ {2} + (5 - 3) ^ {2} = 1 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2} = (6. 4 - 5. 1) ^ {2} + (5. 5 - 5. 1) ^ {2} + (5. 0 - 5. 1) ^ {2} + (4. 8 - 5. 1) ^ {2} + (3. 8 - 5. 1) ^ {2} = 3. 6 4
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > r = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right)}{\sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2}}} = \frac {- 5 . 9}{\sqrt {1 0 \times 3 . 6 4}} = \frac {- 5 . 9}{\sqrt {3 6 . 4}} \approx \frac {- 5 . 9}{6} \approx - 0. 9 8
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \hat {b} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right)}{\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {- 5 . 9}{1 0} = - 0. 5 9, \quad \hat {a} = \bar {y} - \hat {b} \bar {x} = 5. 1 - (- 0. 5 9) \times 3 = 6. 8 7,
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $y$ 关于 $x$ 的回归直线方程为 $\hat{y} = -0.59x + 6.87$
> >
> > 2026 年对应代码为 7，当 x = 7 时， $\hat{y} = -0.59 \times 7 + 6.87 = 2.74$ ，由此预测该产品 2026 年的年利润为 2.74 万元。
