> [!faq]- 《第八章 成对数据的统计分析（高效培优单元自测·提升卷）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第三册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 32
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用方差公式求出 $s_{\mathrm{A}}^{2}$ ， $s_{\mathrm{B}}^{2}$ ，利用平均数公式求出 $\overline{x}$ ， $\overline{y}$ ，利用
> >
> > $$
> > s ^ {2} = \frac {m}{m + n} \left[ s _ {1} ^ {2} + (\overline {{x}} _ {1} - \overline {{x}}) ^ {2} \right] + \frac {n}{m + n} \left[ s _ {2} ^ {2} + (\overline {{x}} _ {2} - \overline {{x}}) ^ {2} \right] = \frac {1}{m + n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m + n} (x _ {i} - \overline {{x}}) ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > 利用公式 $\hat{b}=\frac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^{25}x_{i}y_{i}-25\overline{xy}}{\sum\limits_{i=1}^{25}x_{i}^{2}-25\overline{x}^{2}}$ 求出 $\hat{b}$ ，由 $\hat{y}=\hat{b}x+\hat{a}$ ，利用公式 $\hat{s}^{2}=\hat{b}^{2}s^{2}\quad\hat{s}^{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $A, B$ 两种类型的数量比为 $2:3$ ，现按分层随机抽样的方法抽取容量为 $^{25}$ 的样本，
> >
> > 则 $A$ 两种类型的数量为 $m = 25 \times \frac{2}{5} = 10$ ， $B$ 两种类型的数量为 $n = 25 \times \frac{3}{5} = 15$
> >
> > 设 $A$ 类型样本的方差为 $s_A^2$ ， $B$ 类型样本的方差为 $s_B^2$ ，总样本的方差为 $s^2$
> >
> > $$
> > \overline {{{{x _ {A}}}}} = 5 \quad \overline {{{{y _ {A}}}}} = 8. 5 \quad \overline {{{{x _ {B}}}}} = 1 0 \quad \overline {{{{y _ {B}}}}} = 1 6
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because s ^ {2} = \frac {1}{m + n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m + n} \left(x _ {i} - \overline {{x}}\right) ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore s _ {A} ^ {2} = \frac {1}{1 0} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {1 0} \left(x _ {i} - \overline {{{x _ {A}}}}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{1 0} \times 2 0 = 2, \quad s _ {B} ^ {2} = \frac {1}{1 5} \sum_ {i = 1 1} ^ {2 5} \left(x _ {i} - \overline {{{x _ {B}}}}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{1 5} \times 3 0 = 2
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \overline {{x}} = \frac {m}{m + n} \cdot \overline {{x _ {A}}} + \frac {n}{m + n} \cdot \overline {{x _ {B}}}, \overline {{y}} = \frac {m}{m + n} \cdot \overline {{y _ {A}}} + \frac {n}{m + n} \cdot \overline {{y _ {B}}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \bar {x} = \frac {1 0}{2 5} \times 5 + \frac {1 5}{2 5} \times 1 0 = 8, \quad \bar {y} = \frac {1 0}{2 5} \times 8. 5 + \frac {1 5}{2 5} \times 1 6 = 1 3,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because s ^ {2} = \frac {m}{m + n} \left[ s _ {A} ^ {2} + \left(\overline {{x _ {A}}} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} \right] + \frac {n}{m + n} \left[ s _ {B} ^ {2} + \left(\overline {{x _ {B}}} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} \right],
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore s ^ {2} = \frac {1 0}{2 5} \times [ 2 + (5 - 8) ^ {2} ] + \frac {1 5}{2 5} \times [ 2 + (1 0 - 8) ^ {2} ] = \frac {2}{5} \times 1 1 + \frac {3}{5} \times 6 = 8,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because s ^ {2} = \frac {1}{m + n} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m + n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2}, \therefore \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {m + n} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} = (m + n) s ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {2 5} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} = 2 5 s ^ {2} = 2 5 \times 8 = 2 0 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {2 5} \left(x _ {i} - \bar {x}\right) ^ {2} = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {2 5} x _ {i} ^ {2} - 2 5 \bar {x} ^ {- 2}, \therefore \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {2 5} x _ {i} ^ {2} - 2 5 \bar {x} ^ {- 2} = 2 0 0.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because 2 5 \overline {{x y}} = 2 5 \times 8 \times 1 3 = 2 6 0 0, \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {2 5} x _ {i} y _ {i} = 3 0 0 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {2 5} x _ {i} y _ {i} - 2 5 \overline {{x}} \overline {{y}} = 3 0 0 0 - 2 6 0 0 = 4 0 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \hat {b} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {2 5} x _ {i} y _ {i} - 2 5 \overline {{x}} \overline {{y}}}{\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {2 5} x _ {i} ^ {2} - 2 5 \overline {{x}} ^ {- 2}} = \frac {4 0 0}{2 0 0} = 2,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \hat {y} = \hat {b} x + \hat {a}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \hat {s} ^ {2} = \hat {b} ^ {2} s ^ {2} = 4 \times 8 = 3 2, \quad \therefore y _ {\text {的预测值}} \hat {y} _ {\text {的方差估计值}} \hat {s} ^ {2} = 3 2.
> > $$
> >
