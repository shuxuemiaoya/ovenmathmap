> [!faq]- 《第八章 成对数据的统计分析（高效培优单元自测·强化卷）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第三册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)0.88(2) $\hat{y}=4.5t+19$ (3)55000 亩
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $\overline{t} = \frac{1}{7}\left(1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7\right) = 4$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(t _ {i} - \bar {t}\right) ^ {2} = (1 - 4) ^ {2} + (2 - 4) ^ {2} + (3 - 4) ^ {2} + (4 - 4) ^ {2} + (5 - 4) ^ {2} + (6 - 4) ^ {2} + (7 - 4) ^ {2} = 2 8
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {7} \left(t _ {i} - \overline {{t}}\right) ^ {2}} = 2 \sqrt {7}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > r = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {7} \left(t _ {i} - \bar {t}\right) \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right)}{\sqrt {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {7} \left(t _ {i} - \bar {t}\right) ^ {2} \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {7} \left(y _ {i} - \bar {y}\right) ^ {2}}} = \frac {1 2 6}{2 \sqrt {7} \times 2 7} \approx 0. 8 8
> > $$
> >
> > 即相关系数约为 0.88
> >
> > (2) 因为 $\hat{b}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{7}\left(t_{i}-\overline{t}\right)\left(y_{i}-\overline{y}\right)}{\sum_{i=1}^{7}\left(t_{i}-\overline{t}\right)^{2}}=\frac{126}{28}=4.5$
> >
> > $$
> > \hat {a} = \bar {y} - b \bar {t} = \frac {\sum_ {i = 1} ^ {7} y _ {i}}{7} - 4. 5 \times 4 = \frac {2 5 9}{7} - 1 8 = 1 9,
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\hat{y}=4.5t+19$
> >
> > (3) 当 t=8 时， $\hat{y}=4.5\times8+19=55$
> >
> > 该地区 2026 年绿化面积为 $100000 \times 55\% = 55000$ 亩。
