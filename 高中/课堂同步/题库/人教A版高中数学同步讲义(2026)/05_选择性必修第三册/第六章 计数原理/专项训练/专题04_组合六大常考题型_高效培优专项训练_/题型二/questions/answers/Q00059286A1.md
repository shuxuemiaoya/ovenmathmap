> [!faq]- 《专题04_组合六大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由两个连续自然数 $m+k$ 与 $m+k+1$ 的积，联想到可化为 $2C_{m+k+1}^{2}$ ，进一步运用
> >
> > $C_{r}^{r} + C_{r+1}^{r} + \cdots + C_{r+k}^{r} = C_{r+1}^{r+1} + C_{r+1}^{r} + \cdots + C_{r+k}^{r}$ ，反复运用基本的组合恒等式 $C_{n}^{m} + C_{n}^{m-1} = C_{n+1}^{m}$ 即可化简。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 证明： $\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(m+k)(m+k+1)=2\left(C_{m+1}^{2}+C_{m+2}^{2}+\cdots+C_{m+n}^{2}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} = 2 \left[ \left(\mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2} + \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} + \dots + \mathrm{C} _ {m} ^ {2} + \mathrm{C} _ {m + 1} ^ {2} + \dots + \mathrm{C} _ {m + n} ^ {2}\right) - \left(\mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2} + \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} + \dots + \mathrm{C} _ {m} ^ {2}\right) \right] \\ = 2 \left(\mathrm{C} _ {m + n + 1} ^ {3} - \mathrm{C} _ {m + 1} ^ {3}\right) = 2 \left[ \frac {(m + n + 1) !}{3 ! (m + n - 2) !} - \frac {(m + 1) !}{3 ! (m - 2) !} \right] \\ = 2 \left[ \frac {(m + n + 1) (m + n) (m + n - 1) (m + n - 2) !}{3 ! (m + n - 2) !} - \frac {(m + 1) m (m - 1) (m - 2) !}{3 ! (m - 2) !} \right] \\ = 2 \left[ \frac {(m + n + 1) (m + n) (m + n - 1)}{3 !} - \frac {(m + 1) m (m - 1)}{3 !} \right] \\ = \frac {m ^ {3} + n ^ {3} + 3 m ^ {2} n + 3 m n ^ {2} - m - n}{3} - \frac {m ^ {3} - m}{3} \\ = \frac {n ^ {3} + 3 m ^ {2} n + 3 m n ^ {2} - n}{3} = \frac {n}{3} \left(3 m ^ {2} + 3 m n + n ^ {2} - 1\right) \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 故原式成立.
