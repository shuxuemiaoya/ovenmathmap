> [!faq]- 《高二数学下学期第一次月考（人教A版选择性必修数列 导数，高效培优·提升卷）[56877320]》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 中， $a_1 = 1$ ，当 $n$ 2时， $a_{n} - a_{n - 1} = n$
> >
> > 则当 n 2 时， $a_{n}=a_{1}+(a_{2}-a_{1})+(a_{3}-a_{2})+\cdots+(a_{n}-a_{n-1})=1+2+3+\cdots+n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$
> >
> > 而 $a_1 = 1$ 满足上式，因此 $a_{n} = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2},\frac{1}{a_{n}} = \frac{2}{n(n + 1)} = 2(\frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1})$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = 2 \left[ \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{4}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) \right] = 2 \left(1 - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) = \frac {2 n}{n + 1}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $S_{2026}=\frac{2\times2026}{2027}=\frac{4052}{2027}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**
