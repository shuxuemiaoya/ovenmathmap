> [!faq]- 《高二数学下学期第一次月考_人教A版_高效培优_提升卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)4 (2) $n^{2}$ (3) $\frac{n}{n+1}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）∵ $a_{1}=1$ ， $\sqrt{a_{n+1}}-\sqrt{a_{n}}=\frac{1}{4}a_{2}$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore \sqrt{a_{2}} -\sqrt{a_{1}} = \frac{1}{4} a_{2}$ ，即 $\sqrt{a_2} -1 = \frac{1}{4} a_2$
> >
> > 解得 $a_{2} = 4$
> >
> > (2) 有 (1) 得 $\sqrt{a_{n+1}} - \sqrt{a_n} = \frac{1}{4} a_2 = 1$
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\sqrt{a_n}\right\}$ 是首项为1，公差为1的等差数列， $\therefore \sqrt{a_n} = n$ ，则 $a_{n} = n^{2}$
> >
> > (3) $\because \frac{1}{a_n + \sqrt{a_n}} = \frac{1}{n^2 + n} = \frac{1}{(n + 1)n} = \frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1}$
> >
> > $S_{n}=1-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{3}+\cdots+\frac{1}{n}-\frac{1}{n+1}=1-\frac{1}{n+1}=\frac{n}{n+1}$ ，故数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_{n}+\sqrt{a_{n}}}\right\}$ 的前n项和 $S_{n}=\frac{n}{n+1}$
