> [!faq]- 《高二数学下学期第一次月考_人教A版_高效培优_强化卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AB
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可知 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_{1} + a_{5} = a_{1} + (a_{1} + 4d) = 2a_{1} + 4d = 14\\ a_{4} - a_{2} = (a_{1} + 3d) - (a_{1} + d) = 2d = 4 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_1 = 3\\ d = 2 \end{array} \right.$ ，故A正确；
> >
> > 通项公式为 $a_{n}=a_{1}+(n-1)d=3+2(n-1)=2n+1$ ，故 B 正确；
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} b_{2} = a_{2} = 5 \\ b_{4} = a_{6} = 13 \end{array} \right.$ ，又因为 $b_{4} = b_{2}q^{2}$ ，即 $13 = 5q^{2}$ ， $\therefore q = \pm \frac{\sqrt{65}}{5}$ ，C选项错误；
> >
> > $b_{1}=\frac{b_{2}}{q}=\frac{5}{q},b_{3}=b_{2}\cdot q=5q$ ，设 $S_{n}$ 为数列 $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ 的前n项和，
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {4} = b _ {1} + b _ {2} + b _ {3} + b _ {4} = \frac {5}{q} + 5 + 5 q + 1 3 = 5 \left(q + \frac {1}{q}\right) + 1 8
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {当} q = \sqrt {\frac {1 3}{5}} \text {时}. q + \frac {1}{q} = \sqrt {\frac {1 3}{5}} + \sqrt {\frac {5}{1 3}} = \frac {1 8}{\sqrt {6 5}}, \therefore S _ {4} = 5 \times \frac {1 8}{\sqrt {6 5}} + 1 8 \neq 4 0;
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $q = -\sqrt{\frac{13}{5}}$ 时， $q + \frac{1}{q} = -\frac{18}{\sqrt{65}}$ 。 $S_4 = 5 \times \left(-\frac{18}{\sqrt{65}}\right) + 18 \neq -20$ ，故D错误。
> >
> > 故选 **AB**.
