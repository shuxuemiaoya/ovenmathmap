> [!faq]- 《高二数学下学期期中模拟卷（人教A版选择性必修第二册全部+选择性必修三第六、七章，高效培优·提升卷）_57272348》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可得： $\left\{\begin{aligned}m+n&=\frac{1}{2}\\ -2m+0+\frac{1}{3}+2n=0\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}m&=\frac{1}{3}\\ n&=\frac{1}{6}\end{aligned}\right.$ 则 $D(X)=\sum_{4}^{i=1}(x_{i}-EX)^{2}\cdot P_{i}=4\times\frac{1}{3}+0+1\times\frac{1}{3}+4\times\frac{1}{6}=\frac{7}{3}$ 所以 $D(3X+1)=9D(X)=9\times\frac{7}{3}=21$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
