> [!faq]- 《高二数学下学期期中模拟卷_人教A版_高效培优_强化卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 构造函数 $h(x)=\frac{f(x)}{\sin x},x\in(0,\frac{\pi}{2})$ ，则 $h'(x)=\frac{f'(x)\sin x-f(x)\cos x}{\sin^{2}x}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \because f ^ {\prime} (x) \sin x - f (x) \cos x <   0, x \in (0, \frac {\pi}{2}), \therefore h ^ {\prime} (x) <   0, x \in (0, \frac {\pi}{2})
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $h(x)$ 在 $x \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$ 上单调递减.
> >
> > 故有 $h(\frac{\pi}{6}) > h(\frac{\pi}{4}) > h(\frac{\pi}{3})$ ，即 $\frac{f(\frac{\pi}{6})}{\sin\frac{\pi}{6}} > \frac{f(\frac{\pi}{4})}{\sin\frac{\pi}{4}} > \frac{f(\frac{\pi}{3})}{\sin\frac{\pi}{3}}$
> >
> > 即 $2f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > \sqrt{2}f(\frac{\pi}{4}) > \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}f(\frac{\pi}{3})$ ①.
> >
> > 对于A：由①式可知 $2f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > \sqrt{2} f(\frac{\pi}{4})$ ，即 $f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} f(\frac{\pi}{4})$ ，因此 $f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > \sqrt{2} f(\frac{\pi}{4})$ 无法判断，故A错误；
> >
> > 对于 B、C：由①式可知 $2f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} f(\frac{\pi}{3})$ ，即 $\sqrt{3} f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > f(\frac{\pi}{3})$ ，故 $\sqrt{2} f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > f(\frac{\pi}{3})$ 无法判断，故 B 错误，C
> >
> > 正确；
> >
> > 对于 D：由①式可知 $2f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > \sqrt{2}f(\frac{\pi}{4})$ ，即 $\sqrt{2}f(\frac{\pi}{6}) > f(\frac{\pi}{4})$ ，故 D 错误.
> >
> > 故选：C.
> >
> > 4．数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 满足 $a_{1}=\frac{1}{3}$ ， $\frac{a_{n+1}}{n+1}=\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n}+n}(n\in\mathbb{N}^{*})$ ，若 $a_{1}+a_{1}a_{2}+a_{1}a_{2}a_{3}+\cdots+a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}\leq\frac{5}{6}$ 成立，则正整数n的
> >
> > 最大值为（ ）A.4 B.6 C.8 D.10
> >
> > 【答案】D
> >
> > 【详解】由 $\frac{a_{n + 1}}{n + 1} = \frac{a_n}{a_n + n}$ ，则 $\frac{n + 1}{a_{n + 1}} = \frac{a_n + n}{a_n} = 1 + \frac{n}{a_n}$ 即 $\frac{n + 1}{a_{n + 1}} -\frac{n}{a_n} = 1$
> >
> > 又 $\frac{1}{a_1} = 3$ ，故数列 $\left\{\frac{n}{a_n}\right\}$ 是以3为首项，1为公差的等差数列，
> >
> > $\frac{n}{a_n} = 3 + n - 1 = n + 2$ 故 $a_{n} = \frac{n}{n + 2}$
> >
> > 则 $a_{1}a_{2}\dots a_{n} = \frac{1}{3}\times \frac{2}{4}\times \frac{3}{5}\times \dots \times \frac{n}{n + 2} = \frac{2}{(n + 1)(n + 2)} = \frac{2}{n + 1} -\frac{2}{n + 2}$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {1} + a _ {1} a _ {2} + a _ {1} a _ {2} a _ {3} + \dots + a _ {1} a _ {2} \dots a _ {n} = \frac {2}{2} - \frac {2}{3} + \frac {2}{3} - \frac {2}{4} + \dots + \frac {2}{n + 1} - \frac {2}{n + 2} = 1 - \frac {2}{n + 2}
> > $$
> >
> > 令 $1 - \frac{2}{n + 2} \leq \frac{5}{6}$ ，解得 $n \leq 10$ ，故正整数 $n$ 的最大值为 $10$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
