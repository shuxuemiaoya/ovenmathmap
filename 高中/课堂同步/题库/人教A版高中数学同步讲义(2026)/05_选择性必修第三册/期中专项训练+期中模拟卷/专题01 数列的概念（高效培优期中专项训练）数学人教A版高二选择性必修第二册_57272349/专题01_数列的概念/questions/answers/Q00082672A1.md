> [!faq]- 《专题01 数列的概念（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册_57272349》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\frac{131}{132}$ B. $\frac{135}{132}$ C. $\frac{175}{132}$ D. $\frac{109}{66}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 n=1 时， $a_{1}=1$ .
> >
> > 当 $n$ 2时，利用递推关系： $S(n) - S(n - 1) = na_{n} = (2n - 1) - [2(n - 1) - 1] = 2$
> >
> > 因此， $a_{n}=\frac{2}{n}$ (n 2)·
> >
> > 当 n=1 时，项为 $\frac{a_{1}}{1+2}=\frac{1}{3}$ ,
> >
> > 当时，项为 $\frac{\frac{2}{n}}{n + 2} = \frac{2}{n(n + 2)} = \frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 2},$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {n = 2} ^ {1 0} \left(\frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 2}\right) = \left(\frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{3} + \dots + \frac {1}{1 0}\right) - \left(\frac {1}{4} + \frac {1}{5} + \dots + \frac {1}{1 2}\right) = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{1 1} - \frac {1}{1 2},
> > $$
> >
> > 将 $n = 1$ 的项与剩余项相加： $\frac{1}{3} +\left(\frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{3} -\frac{1}{11} -\frac{1}{12}\right) = \frac{1}{3} +\frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{3} -\frac{1}{11} -\frac{1}{12} = \frac{2}{3} +\frac{1}{2} -\frac{23}{132} = \frac{131}{132}.$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
