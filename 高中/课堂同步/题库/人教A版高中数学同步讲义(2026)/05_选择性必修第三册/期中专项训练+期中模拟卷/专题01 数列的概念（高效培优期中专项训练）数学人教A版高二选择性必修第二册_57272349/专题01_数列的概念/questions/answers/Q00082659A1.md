> > 解：∵ $a_{n+1}=\frac{n+1}{n}a_{n},\therefore\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}=\frac{n+1}{n}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {a _ {2}}{a _ {1}} = \frac {2}{1}, \frac {a _ {3}}{a _ {2}} = \frac {3}{2}, \frac {a _ {4}}{a _ {3}} = \frac {4}{3}; \dots , \frac {a _ {n}}{a _ {n - 1}} = \frac {n}{n - 1} n 2
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{a_n}{a_1} = \frac{n}{1} = n$ ， $n$ 2，
> >
> > $\because a_{1} = 2, \therefore a_{n} = 2n, n, 2,$
> >
> > 显然，当 $n = 1$ 时， $a_{1} = 2$ 满足 $a_{n} = 2n$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = 2 n, n \in \mathrm{N} ^ {*}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore a_{2020} = 2 \times 2022 = 4044$
