>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a_1 = \frac{1}{2}$ ， $a_{n + 1} = a_n(a_n + 1)$ ，故 $a_2 > 0$ ，依次有 $a_n > 0$
> >
> > 根据 $a_{n + 1} = a_n(a_n + 1)$ 可得 $\frac{1}{a_n} -\frac{1}{a_{n + 1}} = \frac{1}{a_n + 1}$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = b _ {1} + b _ {2} + \dots + b _ {n} = \frac {1}{a _ {1}} - \frac {1}{a _ {2}} + \frac {1}{a _ {2}} - \frac {1}{a _ {3}} + \dots + \frac {1}{a _ {n}} - \frac {1}{a _ {n + 1}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{a _ {1}} - \frac {1}{a _ {n + 1}} = 2 - \frac {1}{a _ {n + 1}}.
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $a_{n + 1} = a_n(a_n + 1)$ 可得 $b_{n} = \frac{1}{a_{n} + 1} = \frac{a_{n}}{a_{n + 1}}$
> >
> > 从而 $T_{n}=b_{1}b_{2}\times\cdots\times b_{n}=\frac{a_{1}}{a_{2}}\times\frac{a_{2}}{a_{3}}\times\cdots\times\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n+1}}=\frac{1}{2a_{n+1}}$
> >
> > 故 $S_{n}+2T_{n}=2-\frac{1}{a_{n+1}}+2\times\frac{1}{2a_{n+1}}=2$ ，
> >
> > 故答案为：2·
