> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n}=2^{n-1}$ (2) $2^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}}$ (3)3120
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）∵ $\left\{\ln a_{n}\right\}$ 是首项为0的等差数列，∴ $a_{1}=1$ ， $2\ln a_{2}=\ln a_{1}+\ln a_{3}$ ，∴ $a_{2}^{2}=a_{1}a_{3}=a_{3}$
> >
> > 又∵ $\left\{S_{n}+a_{1}\right\}$ 是等比数列，∴ $\left(S_{2}+a_{1}\right)^{2}=\left(S_{1}+a_{1}\right)\left(S_{3}+a_{1}\right)$
> >
> > $\therefore (a_{2} + 2)^{2} = 2(2 + a_{2} + a_{3})$ ，即 $a_2^2 +4a_2 + 4 = 4 + 2a_2 + 2a_3$
> >
> > $\therefore a_{2}^{2}=a_{3},\therefore a_{2}^{2}+4a_{2}+4=4+2a_{2}+2a_{2}^{2},$ 即 $a_{2}^{2}-2a_{2}=0$
> >
> > $\therefore a_{n}>0,\quad\therefore a_{2}=2,$ 解得
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{ \ln a_{n} \right\}$ 是等差数列，
> >
> > ∴ 当 n 2 时， $d = \ln a_{n} - \ln a_{n-1} = \ln a_{2} - \ln a_{1} = \ln 2 - \ln 1 = \ln 2$
> >
> > $\ln\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-1}}=\ln2,\quad\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-1}}=2$ 即 a\_{n-1} 为定值，
> >
> > ∴ 数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 为首项 $a_{1}=1$ ，公比 q=2 的等比数列
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{a_{n}\right\}_{\text{的通项公式为}} a_{n}=2^{n-1}$
> >
> > (2) $\therefore a_{n} = 2^{n - 1},\quad \therefore P_{n} = a_{1}\cdot a_{2}\cdot a_{3}\dots a_{n} = 2^{0}\times 2^{1}\times 2^{2}\times \dots \times 2^{n - 1} = 2^{0 + 1 + 2 + \dots +n - 1} = 2^{\frac{n(n - 1)}{2}}$
> >
> > (3) $\because a_{n} = 2^{n - 1}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b _ {n} = \log_ {2} a _ {2 n - 1} + \log_ {2} a _ {2 n} = \log_ {2} 2 ^ {2 n - 1 - 1} + \log_ {2} 2 ^ {2 n - 1} = 2 n - 2 + 2 n - 1 = 4 n - 3
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore b_{1}=1,\quad n\quad2$ 时， $b_{n}-b_{n-1}=(4n-3)-[4(n-1)-3]=4$
> >
> > 即 $\{b_n\}$ 是首项为1，公差为4的等差数列，
> >
> > $$
> > _ \mathrm{令} C _ {n} = \left(- 1\right) ^ {n} \cdot b _ {n} ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > C _ {2 n - 1} + C _ {2 n} = - b _ {2 n - 1} ^ {2} + b _ {2 n} ^ {2} = \left(b _ {2 n} + b _ {2 n - 1}\right) \left(b _ {2 n} - b _ {2 n - 1}\right) = 4 \left(b _ {2 n} + b _ {2 n - 1}\right) \left(n \in \mathrm{N} ^ {*}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 记 $\left\{C_{n}\right\}$ 的前n项和为 $T_{n}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {2 0} = \left(C _ {1} + C _ {2}\right) + \dots + \left(C _ {1 9} + C _ {2 0}\right) = 4 \left(b _ {1} + b _ {2} + \dots + b _ {2 0}\right) = 4 \times \frac {(1 + 7 7) \times 2 0}{2} = 3 1 2 0
> > $$
> >
> > ∴数列 $\left\{(-1)^{n}\cdot b_{n}^{2}\right\}$ 的前20项和为3120.
