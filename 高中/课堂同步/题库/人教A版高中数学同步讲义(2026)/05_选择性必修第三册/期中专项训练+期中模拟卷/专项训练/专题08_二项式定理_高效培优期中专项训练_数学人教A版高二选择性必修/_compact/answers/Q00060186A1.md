> [!faq]- 《专题08_二项式定理_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高二选择性必修》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可得 $\left(1+t+t^{2}\right)^{9}=\mathrm{M}_{18}^{0}+\mathrm{M}_{18}^{1}t+\mathrm{M}_{18}^{2}t^{2}+\cdots+\mathrm{M}_{18}^{18}t^{18}$
> >
> > 根据二项式定理可得 $(t-1)^{9}=C_{9}^{0}t^{9}-C_{9}^{1}t^{8}+C_{9}^{2}t^{7}+\cdots-C_{9}^{9}$
> >
> > 所以 $M_{18}^{0}C_{9}^{0}-M_{18}^{1}C_{9}^{1}+M_{18}^{2}C_{9}^{2}-M_{18}^{3}C_{9}^{3}+\cdots-M_{18}^{9}C_{9}^{9}$ 是 $(t-1)^{9}\left(1+t+t^{2}\right)^{9}=\left(t^{3}-1\right)^{9}$ 的展开式中 $t^{9}$ 项的系数，
> > 又二项展开式 $\left(t^{3}-1\right)^{9}$ 的通项为 $T_{r+1}=C_{9}^{r}\left(t^{3}\right)^{9-r}\left(-1\right)^{r}=\left(-1\right)^{r}C_{9}^{r}t^{27-3r}$
> >
> > 所以 $M_{18}^{0}C_{9}^{0}-M_{18}^{1}C_{9}^{1}+M_{18}^{2}C_{9}^{2}-M_{18}^{3}C_{9}^{3}+\cdots-M_{18}^{9}C_{9}^{9}=C_{9}^{6}$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
