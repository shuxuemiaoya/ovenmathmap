> [!faq]- 《专题15_计数原理及随机变量及其分布精选压轴70题14类考点专练_期中》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{1}{2}$ (2) <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>5</td><td>7</td><td>8</td><td>10</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{7}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{6}{24}$ </td></tr></table> 期望为6 (3) $\frac{29}{64}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）根据条件，利用互斥事件和相互独立事件的概率公式，即可求解；
> >
> > (2) 根据条件，求出 $X$ 可能的取值及相应的概率，即可得分布列，再由期望的计算公式，即可求解；
> >
> > （3）先求出乙同学的总得分的可能取值及相应的概率，设事件 $E$ 表示乙获胜，事件 $F$ 表示甲的总分不低
> >
> > 于 5 分，利用相互独立事件同时发生的概率公式求出 $P(E), P(EF)$ ，再由条件概率公式，即可求解.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设事件 D 表示乙同学三个问题中至少有两个问题回答正确，
> >
> > $$
> > P (D) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {3} + \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {3} \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {1}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) X 可能的取值有 0,2,3,5,7,8,10,
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = \left(1 - \frac {3}{4}\right) \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {1}{2 4}, P (X = 2) = \frac {3}{4} \times \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {3}{2 4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = \left(1 - \frac {3}{4}\right) \times \frac {2}{3} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {2}{2 4}, P (X = 5) = \left(1 - \frac {3}{4}\right) \times \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {3}{4} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {7}{2 4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 7) = \frac {3}{4} \times \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {3}{2 4}, P (X = 8) = \left(1 - \frac {3}{4}\right) \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {2}{2 4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1 0) = \frac {3}{4} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {6}{2 4}.
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $X$ 的分布列为：
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>5</td><td>7</td><td>8</td><td>10</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{7}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{24}$ </td><td> $\frac{6}{24}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > $$
> > E (X) = 0 \times \frac {1}{2 4} + 2 \times \frac {3}{2 4} + 3 \times \frac {2}{2 4} + 5 \times \frac {7}{2 4} + 7 \times \frac {3}{2 4} + 8 \times \frac {2}{2 4} + 1 0 \times \frac {6}{2 4} = \frac {1 4 4}{2 4} = 6.
> > $$
> >
> > (3) 记 $Y$ 为乙同学的总得分， $Y$ 可能的取值有 0,2,3,5,7,8,10
> >
> > 则 $P(Y = 0) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{8}, P(Y = 2) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{8}, P(Y = 3) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{8}$
> >
> > $$
> > P (Y = 5) = \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {3} + \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {1}{4}, P (Y = 7) = \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {1}{8}, P (Y = 8) = \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {1}{8}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (Y = 1 0) = \frac {1}{8},
> > $$
> >
> > 设事件 E 表示乙获胜，事件 F 表示甲的总分不低于 5 分，
> >
> > 法一：因为 $P(E)=\frac{1}{8}\times\frac{1}{24}+\frac{1}{8}\times\frac{4}{24}+\frac{1}{4}\times\frac{6}{24}+\frac{1}{8}\times\frac{13}{24}+\frac{1}{8}\times\frac{16}{24}+\frac{1}{8}\times\frac{18}{24}=\frac{64}{192}$
> >
> > $$
> > P (E F) = \frac {1}{8} \times \frac {7}{2 4} + \frac {1}{8} \times \frac {1 0}{2 4} + \frac {1}{8} \times \frac {1 2}{2 4} = \frac {2 9}{1 9 2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (F \mid E) = \frac {P (E F)}{P (E)} = \frac {2 9}{6 4}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {法二}: P (E) = \frac {1}{2 4} \times \frac {7}{8} + \frac {3}{2 4} \times \frac {6}{8} + \frac {2}{2 4} \times \frac {5}{8} + \frac {7}{2 4} \times \frac {3}{8} + \frac {3}{2 4} \times \frac {2}{8} + \frac {2}{2 4} \times \frac {1}{8} = \frac {6 4}{1 9 2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (E F) = \frac {7}{2 4} \times \frac {3}{8} + \frac {3}{2 4} \times \frac {2}{8} + \frac {2}{2 4} \times \frac {1}{8} = \frac {2 9}{1 9 2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (F \mid E) = \frac {P (E F)}{P (E)} = \frac {2 9}{6 4}
> > $$
