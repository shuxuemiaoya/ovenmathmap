> [!faq]- 《专题12_正态分布_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高二选择性必修第》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)71分； (2)5 ; (3)分布列详见解析； $E(X)=\frac{5}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $P(X > \mu -\sigma) = \frac{1}{2} +\frac{P(\mu - \sigma < X\leq\mu + \sigma)}{2}\approx 0.84135$
> >
> > 又 $\mu$ 的近似值为76.5， $\sigma = s$ 的近似值为5.5，
> >
> > 所以该校预期的平均成绩大约是 $76.5 - 5.5 = 71$ （分）
> >
> > (2) 由 $\mu \approx 76.5$ ，可得 $P(\xi > 76.5) = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 即从所有报考该试点高校的学生中随机抽取 $^{1}$ 人，
> >
> > 该学生笔试成绩高于 76.5 的概率为 $\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 所以随机变量 $\xi$ 服从二项分布 $\xi \sim \left(10, \frac{1}{2}\right)$ ，故 $E(\xi) = 10 \times \frac{1}{2} = 5$
> >
> > (3) X 的可能取值为 0,1,2,3,4
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2} \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{9},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2} \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {1}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2} \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) + \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2} \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1 3}{3 6},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2} \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) + \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{6},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 4) = \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2} \times \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{3 6},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $X$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>4</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{3}$ </td><td> $\frac{13}{36}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{6}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{36}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > 所以 $E(X)=0\times\frac{1}{9}+1\times\frac{1}{3}+2\times\frac{13}{36}+3\times\frac{1}{6}+4\times\frac{1}{36}=\frac{5}{3}$
> >
