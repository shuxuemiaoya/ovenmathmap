> [!faq]- 《专题12_正态分布_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高二选择性必修第》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\overline{x}=80$ ；0.1359 (2)① $\frac{7}{300}$ ; ② $\frac{4}{7}$ ; ③1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $\overline{x}=66\times0.1+77\times0.2+80\times0.48+88\times0.19+96\times0.03=80$
> >
> > 因为 $X \sim N(80,36)$ ，所以 $\sigma = 6$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > P (8 6 <   X \leq 9 2) = \frac {1}{2} P (\mu - 2 \sigma \leq X \leq \mu + 2 \sigma) - \frac {1}{2} P (\mu - \sigma \leq X \leq \mu + \sigma) \approx \frac {0 . 9 5 4 5 - 0 . 6 8 2 7}{2} = 0. 1 3 5 9
> > $$
> >
> > (2) ①设“抽取的零件为甲机床生产”记为事件 $A_{1}$ ,
> >
> > “抽取的零件为乙机床生产”记为事件 $A_{2}$ ，
> >
> > “抽取的零件为次品”记为事件 B，
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} P \left(A _ {1}\right) = \frac {2}{3}, P \left(A _ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{3}, P \left(B \mid A _ {1}\right) = 0. 0 2, P \left(B \mid A _ {2}\right) = 0. 0 3,
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $P(B) = P(A_1)P(B|A_1) + P(A_2)P(B|A_2) = \frac{2}{3}\times 0.02 + \frac{1}{3}\times 0.03 = \frac{0.07}{3} = \frac{7}{300}$
> >
> > $$
> > P \left(A _ {1} | B\right) = \frac {P \left(A _ {1} B\right)}{P (B)} = \frac {P \left(A _ {1}\right) P \left(B \mid A _ {1}\right)}{P (B)} = \frac {\frac {2}{3} \times 0 . 0 2}{\frac {0 . 0 7}{3}} = \frac {4}{7}; \tag {②}
> > $$
> > - **③**：由（1）及（2）①可知，这批零件是次品且性能指标在 $(86,92]$ 内的概率 $p=\frac{7}{300}\times0.1359$ ，
> > 且随机变量 $Y \sim B(300, p)$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $E(Y)=300p=300\times\frac{7}{300}\times0.1359=0.9513\approx1$
> >
> > 所以随机变量 Y 的数学期望为 1.
