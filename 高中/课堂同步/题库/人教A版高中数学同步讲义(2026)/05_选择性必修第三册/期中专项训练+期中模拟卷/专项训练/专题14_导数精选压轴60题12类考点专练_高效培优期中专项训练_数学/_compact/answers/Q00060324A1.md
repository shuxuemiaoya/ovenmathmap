> [!faq]- 《专题14_导数精选压轴60题12类考点专练_高效培优期中专项训练_数学》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 56
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $f(x)=x^{2}-3$ ， $x_{1}=3$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore f \left(x _ {1}\right) = f (3) = 3 ^ {2} - 3 = 6 \quad f ^ {\prime} (x) = 2 x
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore f ^ {\prime} \left(x _ {1}\right) = f ^ {\prime} (3) = 2 \times 3 = 6
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ 曲线 $y = f(x)$ 在 x = 3 处的切线方程为 $y - 6 = 6(x - 3)$ ，化简得 y = 6x - 12
> >
> > 令 $y = 0$ ，即 $6x - 12 = 0$ ，解得 $x = 2$ ，即 $x_{2} = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore f \left(x _ {2}\right) = f (2) = 2 ^ {2} - 3 = 1 \quad f ^ {\prime} \left(x _ {2}\right) = f ^ {\prime} (2) = 2 \times 2 = 4
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore$ 曲线 $y = f(x)$ 在 $x = 2$ 处的切线方程为 $y - 1 = 4(x - 2)$ ，化简得 $y = 4x - 7$
> >
> > 令 $y = 0$ ，即 $4x - 7 = 0$ ，解得 $x = \frac{7}{4}$ ，即 $x_{3} = \frac{7}{4}$
> >
> > $$
> > f \left(x _ {3}\right) = f \left(\frac {7}{4}\right) = \left(\frac {7}{4}\right) ^ {2} - 3 = \frac {1}{1 6}, f ^ {\prime} \left(x _ {3}\right) = f ^ {\prime} \left(\frac {7}{4}\right) = 2 \times \frac {7}{4} = \frac {7}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ 曲线 $y = f(x)$ 在 $x = \frac{7}{4}$ 处的切线方程为 $y - \frac{1}{16} = \frac{7}{2} \left( x - \frac{7}{4} \right)$ ，化简得 $y = \frac{7}{2} x - \frac{97}{16}$
> >
> > 令 y=0 ，即 $\frac{7}{2}x-\frac{97}{16}=0$ ，解得 $x=\frac{97}{56}$ ，即 $x_{4}=\frac{97}{56}$
> >
> > ∴进行三次迭代得到的 $x_{4}$ 的值为 $\frac{97}{56}$ .
