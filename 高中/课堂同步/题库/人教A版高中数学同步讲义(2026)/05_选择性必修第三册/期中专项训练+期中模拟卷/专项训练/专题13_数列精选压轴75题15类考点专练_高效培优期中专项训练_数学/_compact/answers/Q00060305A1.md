> [!faq]- 《专题13_数列精选压轴75题15类考点专练_高效培优期中专项训练_数学》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n}=15-2n$ ， $S_{n}=-n^{2}+14n$ (2) $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&14n-n^{2},n\leq7\\ &98-14n+n^{2},n&8\end{aligned}\right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）根据题意列式求解 $a_1, d$ ，进而可得结果；
> >
> > (2) 先求 $S_{n}$ ，讨论 $a_{n}$ 的符号去绝对值，结合 $S_{n}$ 运算求解.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 $d$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_2 = a_1 + d = 11\\ S_{10} = 10a_1 + 45d = 40 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 解得 $a_1 = 13, d = -2$ ，所以 $\left\{a_n\right\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_n = 13 + (n - 1) \cdot (-2) = 15 - 2n$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = \frac {n (1 3 + 1 5 - 2 n)}{2} = - n ^ {2} + 1 4 n
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 得 $S_{n} = 14n - n^{2}$ ，令 $a_{n} = 15 - 2n > 0$ ，解得 $n < \frac{15}{2}, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$
> >
> > 当 $n \leq 7$ 时，数列的前 $n$ 项均为正数，
> >
> > 则 $T_{n}=\left|a_{1}\right|+\left|a_{2}\right|+\cdots+\left|a_{n}\right|=a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{n}=S_{n}=14n-n^{2}$
> >
> > 当 $n = 8$ 时，数列的前7项为正数，从第8项至第 $n$ 项为负数，
> >
> > 则 $T_{n}=\left|a_{1}\right|+\left|a_{2}\right|+\cdots+\left|a_{n}\right|=\left(a_{1}+a_{2}+\cdots+a_{7}\right)-\left(a_{8}+\cdots+a_{n}\right)=S_{7}-\left(S_{n}-S_{7}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 S _ {7} - S _ {n} = 2 (1 4 \times 7 - 7 ^ {2}) - (1 4 n - n ^ {2}) = n ^ {2} - 1 4 n + 9 8
> > $$
> >
> > 综上， $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&14n-n^{2},n\leq7\\ &98-14n+n^{2},n&8.\end{aligned}\right.$
