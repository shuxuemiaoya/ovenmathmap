> [!faq]- 《专题13_数列精选压轴75题15类考点专练_高效培优期中专项训练_数学》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设等差数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为d， $\frac{S_{n}}{n}=\frac{na_{1}+\frac{1}{2}n(n-1)d}{n}=a_{1}+\frac{1}{2}(n-1)d$ 故数列 $\left\{\frac{S_n}{n}\right\}$ 也为等差数列，并且公差为 $\frac{d}{2}$
> >
> > 又 $\frac{S_{2018}}{2018} -\frac{S_{2016}}{2016} = 1$
> >
> > $\therefore d = 1$
> >
> > 又 $a_{1} = 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {S _ {n}}{n} = 1 + \frac {1}{2} (n - 1) = \frac {n + 1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = \frac {n (n + 1)}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {1}{S _ {n}} = \frac {2}{n (n + 1)} = 2 \left(\frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {1}{S _ {1}} + \frac {1}{S _ {2}} + \dots + \frac {1}{S _ {2 0 1 8}} = 2 \left(1 - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 0 1 8} - \frac {1}{2 0 1 9}\right) = 2 \left(1 - \frac {1}{2 0 1 9}\right) = \frac {4 0 3 6}{2 0 1 9}.
> > $$
