> [!faq]- 《专题05_导数的概念及运算_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高二选择》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设直线 $y = kx + b$ 与 $f(x)$ 的图象相切于点 $P_{1}(x_{1}, y_{1})$
> >
> > 与 $g(x)$ 的图象相切于点 $P_{2}(x_{2},y_{2})$ ,
> >
> > 又 $f^{\prime}(x) = \mathrm{e}^{x - 1},g^{\prime}(x) = \mathrm{e}^{x + 2023}$ ，且 $y_{1} = \mathrm{e}^{x_{1} - 1},y_{2} = \mathrm{e}^{x_{2} + 2023} - 2024.$
> >
> > 曲线 $y = f(x)$ 在点 $P_{1}(x_{1}, y_{1})$ 处的切线方程为 $y - e^{x_{1}-1} = e^{x_{1}-1}(x - x_{1})$ ,
> >
> > 曲线 $y = g(x)$ 在点 $P_{2}(x_{2},y_{2})$ 处的切线方程为 $y - \mathrm{e}^{x_2 + 2023} + 2024 = \mathrm{e}^{x_2 + 2023}(x - x_2).$
> >
> > 故 $\left\{ \begin{array}{c} \mathrm{e}^{x_1 - 1} = \mathrm{e}^{x_2 + 2023}\\ \mathrm{e}^{x_1 - 1} - x_1\mathrm{e}^{x_1 - 1} = \mathrm{e}^{x_2 + 2023} - x_2\mathrm{e}^{x_2 + 2023} - 2024 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 解得 $x_{1} - x_{2} = 2024$
> >
> > 故 $k = \frac{y_1 - y_2}{x_1 - x_2} = \frac{\mathrm{e}^{x_1 - 1} - \mathrm{e}^{x_2 + 2023} + 2024}{2024} = 1$
> >
> > 故答案为：1
