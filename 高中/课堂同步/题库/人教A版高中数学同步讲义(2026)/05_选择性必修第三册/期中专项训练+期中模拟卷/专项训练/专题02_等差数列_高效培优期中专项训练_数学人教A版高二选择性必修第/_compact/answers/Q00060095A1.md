> [!faq]- 《专题02_等差数列_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高二选择性必修第》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n}=8n-4,n\in N^{*}$ (2) $T_{n}=\frac{n}{16(2n+1)}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因 $\sqrt{S_n} = \sqrt{S_{n - 1}} +2$ ，则 $\sqrt{S_n} -\sqrt{S_{n - 1}} = 2$
> >
> > 则数列 $\left\{\sqrt{S_n}\right\}$ 是以 $\sqrt{S_1} = \sqrt{a_1} = 2$ 为首项，2为公差的等差数列，
> >
> > 则 $\sqrt{S_n} = 2n$ ，即 $S_{n} = 4n^{2}$
> >
> > 则 $a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n - 1} = 4n^{2} - 4(n - 1)^{2} = 8n - 4,n$ 2
> >
> > 又 $a_1 = 4$ 满足上式，故 $a_{n} = 8n - 4, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$
> >
> > 则数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n} = 8n - 4, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 知， $b_{n}=\frac{1}{a_{n}a_{n+1}}=\frac{1}{16(2n-1)(2n+1)}=\frac{1}{32}\left(\frac{1}{2n-1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = \frac {1}{3 2} \left(1 - \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{5} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 n - 1} - \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right) = \frac {1}{3 2} \left(1 - \frac {1}{2 n + 1}\right) = \frac {n}{1 6 (2 n + 1)}
> > $$
