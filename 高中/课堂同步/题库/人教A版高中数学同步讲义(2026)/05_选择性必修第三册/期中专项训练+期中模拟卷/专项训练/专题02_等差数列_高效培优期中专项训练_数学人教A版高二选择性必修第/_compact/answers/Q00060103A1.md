> [!faq]- 《专题02_等差数列_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高二选择性必修第》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{17}{29}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\{a_{n}\},\{b_{n}\}$ 为等差数列，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {a _ {2}}{b _ {4} + b _ {6}} + \frac {a _ {8}}{b _ {3} + b _ {7}} = \frac {a _ {2}}{b _ {1} + b _ {9}} + \frac {a _ {8}}{b _ {1} + b _ {9}} = \frac {a _ {2} + a _ {8}}{b _ {1} + b _ {9}} = \frac {a _ {1} + a _ {9}}{b _ {1} + b _ {9}} = \frac {\frac {a _ {1} + a _ {9}}{2} \times 9}{\frac {b _ {1} + b _ {9}}{2} \times 9} = \frac {S _ {9}}{T _ {9}} = \frac {2 \times 9 - 1}{3 \times 9 + 2} = \frac {1 7}{2 9}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{17}{29}$
