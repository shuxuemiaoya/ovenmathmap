> [!faq]- 《专题02_等差数列_高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教A版高二选择性必修第》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $n = 1$ 时， $a_1 = S_1 = \frac{3}{4}$ ；
> >
> > 当 $n$ 2时， $a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n - 1} = \frac{1}{4} n(n + 2) - \frac{1}{4}(n - 1)(n + 1) = \frac{2n + 1}{4}$
> >
> > $n = 1$ 也满足 $a_{1} = 1$ ；故 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n} = \frac{2n + 1}{4}$ .
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{a_k a_{k+1}} = \frac{16}{(2k + 1)(2k + 3)} = 8\left(\frac{1}{2k + 1} - \frac{1}{2k + 3}\right)$
> >
> > 则 $\sum_{k=1}^{10}\frac{1}{a_k a_{k+1}} = 8\left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{5} + \frac{1}{5} - \frac{1}{7} + \cdots + \frac{1}{21} - \frac{1}{23}\right) = 8\left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{23}\right) = \frac{160}{69}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**
