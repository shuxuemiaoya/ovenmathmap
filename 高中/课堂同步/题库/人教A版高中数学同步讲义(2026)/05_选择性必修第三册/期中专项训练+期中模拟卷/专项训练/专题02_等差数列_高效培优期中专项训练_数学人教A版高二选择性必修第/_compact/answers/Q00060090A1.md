>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ABD

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：∵ $a_{1} + a_{2} + a_{3} = S_{2020}$ ，∴ $a_{4} + a_{5} + \cdots + a_{2020} = \frac{2017(a_{4} + a_{2020})}{2} = 0$ ，
> > ∴ $a_{4} + a_{2020} = a_{1} + a_{2023} = 2a_{1012} = 0$
> >
> > $\because a_{1} > 0, a_{1012} = 0, \therefore$ 数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是递减数列，且公差 $d < 0$ ，故选项A、D正确，选项C错误；
> >
> > 又 $S_{2023}=\frac{2023\left(a_{1}+a_{2023}\right)}{2}=0$ ，选项 B 正确，
> >
> > 故选：ABD
