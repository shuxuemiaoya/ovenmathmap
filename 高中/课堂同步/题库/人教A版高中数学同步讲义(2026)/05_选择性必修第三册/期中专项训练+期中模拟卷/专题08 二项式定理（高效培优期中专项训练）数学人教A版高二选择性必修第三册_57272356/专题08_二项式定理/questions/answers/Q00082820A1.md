> [!faq]- 《专题08 二项式定理（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第三册_57272356》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 80
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left(1 + x^{2} + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{6} = \left[1 + \left(x^{2} + \frac{1}{x}\right)\right]^{6}$ 的展开式通项为 $C_6^r\cdot 1^{6 - r}\left(x^2 +\frac{1}{x}\right)^r (0\leq r\leq 6,r\in \mathbf{N})$
> >
> > $\left(x^{2} + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{r}$ 的展开式通项为 $\mathrm{C}_r^k (x^2)^{r - k}\cdot \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^k = \mathrm{C}_r^k x^{2r - 3k}\left(0\leq k\leq r\leq 6,k,r\in \mathbf{N}\right)$
> >
> > 所以， $\left(1 + x^2 +\frac{1}{x}\right)^6$ 的展开式通项为 $T_{r + 1,k + 1} = \mathrm{C}_6^r\mathrm{C}_r^k x^{2r - 3k}\left(0\leq k\leq r\leq 6,k,r\in \mathbf{N}\right)$
> >
> > 由 $2r - 3k = 3(0\leq k\leq r\leq 6,k,r\in \mathbf{N})$ 可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}k = 1\\ r = 3 \end{array} \right.$ 或 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}k = 3\\ r = 6 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 因此，展开式中 $x^{3}$ 的系数为 $\mathrm{C}_6^3\mathrm{C}_3^1 +\mathrm{C}_6^6\mathrm{C}_6^3 = 20\times 3 + 1\times 20 = 80$
> >
> > 故答案为：80.
