> [!faq]- 《专题05 导数的概念及运算（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册_57272353》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2} / 0.5$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $f^{\prime}(x) = a\left[(x - x_{1})(x - x_{2})\right]'(x - x_{3}) + a(x - x_{1})(x - x_{2})(x - x_{3})'$
> >
> > $$
> > = a \left[ 2 x - \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) \right] \left(x - x _ {3}\right) + a \left(x - x _ {1}\right) \left(x - x _ {2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $k_{2} = -2$ ，则 $a\left[2x_{2} - \left(x_{1} + x_{2}\right)\right]\left(x_{2} - x_{3}\right) + a\left(x_{2} - x_{1}\right)\left(x_{2} - x_{2}\right) = 2$
> >
> > 即 $a\left(x_{2} - x_{3}\right)\left(x_{2} - x_{1}\right) = -2$
> >
> > 又 $k_{1} = a(x_{1} - x_{2})(x_{1} - x_{3})$ ， $k_{3} = a(x_{3} - x_{1})(x_{3} - x_{2})$
> >
> > 由于 $x_{1}$ ， $x_{2}$ ， $x_{3}$ 均不相等，
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{k _ {1}} + \frac {1}{k _ {3}} = \frac {1}{a (x _ {1} - x _ {2}) (x _ {1} - x _ {3})} + \frac {1}{a (x _ {3} - x _ {1}) (x _ {3} - x _ {2})}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\left(x _ {3} - x _ {2}\right) - \left(x _ {1} - x _ {2}\right)}{a \left(x _ {1} - x _ {2}\right) \left(x _ {1} - x _ {3}\right) \left(x _ {3} - x _ {2}\right)} = \frac {x _ {3} - x _ {1}}{- 2 \left(x _ {1} - x _ {3}\right)} = \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{1}{2}$
