> [!faq]- 《专题02 等差数列（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册_57272350》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n}=2n+1$ ； (2)10.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设等差数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为d，∵ $a_{2}=5$ ，∴ $a_{1}+d=5$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \because a _ {9} + 7 = 2 a _ {6}, \therefore a _ {1} + 8 d + 7 = 2 (a _ {1} + 5 d)
> > $$
> >
> > 联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_{1} + d = 5\\ a_{1} + 8d + 7 = 2(a_{1} + 5d) \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_1 = 3\\ d = 2 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以 $a_{n}=a_{1}+(n-1)d=3+2(n-1)=2n+1$
> >
> > $\therefore\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的通项公式 $a_{n}=2n+1$ ;
> >
> > $$
> > (2) \because b _ {n} = a _ {n + 1} ^ {2} - a _ {n} ^ {2}, a _ {n} = 2 n + 1, \therefore b _ {n} = (2 n + 3) ^ {2} - (2 n + 1) ^ {2} = 8 (n + 1),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b _ {n + 1} = 8 (n + 2) \quad \therefore b _ {n + 1} - b _ {n} = 8
> > $$
> >
> > ∴数列 $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ 是以 $b_{1}=16$ 为首项，8为公差的等差数列，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {n} = \frac {n [ 1 6 + 8 (n + 1) ]}{2} = 4 n (n + 3)
> > $$
> >
> > $\because S_{m} > 432, \therefore 4m(m + 3) > 432, \therefore m^{2} + 3m - 108 > 0,$
> >
> > $\therefore(m-9)(m+12)>0$ , ∵ m 为正整数，∴ m>9
> >
> > ∴正整数m的最小值10.
