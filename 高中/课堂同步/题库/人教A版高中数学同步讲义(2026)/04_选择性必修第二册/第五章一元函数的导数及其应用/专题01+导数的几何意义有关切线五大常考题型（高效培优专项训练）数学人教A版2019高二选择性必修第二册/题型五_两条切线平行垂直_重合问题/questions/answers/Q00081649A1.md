> [!faq]- 《专题01+导数的几何意义有关切线五大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版2019高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}), B(x_{2},y_{2}), C(x_{3},y_{3}), D(x_{4},y_{4})$ ，利用导数的几何意义求出 $l_{1}$ ， $l_{2}$ 的方程，联立方程求解即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2}),C(x_{3},y_{3}),D(x_{4},y_{4})$
> >
> > 对 $C_1: y = 2 + x^2$ ，求导得 $y' = 2x$
> >
> > 所以 $C_1$ 在 $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ 处的切线方程为 $y - (2 + x_1^2) = 2x_1(x - x_1)$
> >
> > 即 $y = 2x_{1}x - x_{1}^{2} + 2$
> >
> > 对 $C_{2}: y = 2x - 2 - x^{2}$ ，求导得 $y' = 2 - 2x$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $C_2$ 在 $B(x_{2},y_{2})$ 处的切线方程为 $y - (2x_{2} - 2 - x_{2}^{2}) = (2 - 2x_{2})(x - x_{2})$
> >
> > 即 $y = (2 - 2x_{2})x + x_{2}^{2} - 2$
> >
> > 又因为 $l_{1}$ 是 $C_{1}$ 与 $C_{2}$ 的公切线，
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2x_{1} = 2 - 2x_{2} \\ -x_{1}^{2} + 2 = x_{2}^{2} - 2 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_{2} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{7}}{2} \\ x_{1} = \frac{1 - \sqrt{7}}{2} \end{array} \right.$ 或 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_{2} = \frac{1 - \sqrt{7}}{2} \\ x_{1} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{7}}{2} \end{array} \right.$ ，
> >
> > 不妨取 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_{2} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{7}}{2} \\ x_{1} = \frac{1 - \sqrt{7}}{2} \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 同理可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_{3} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{7}}{2} \\ x_{4} = \frac{1 - \sqrt{7}}{2} \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 从而可得 $l_{1}$ ，即 $AB$ 的方程为： $y = (1 - \sqrt{7})x + \frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}$
> >
> > 同理可得 $l_{2}$ ，即 $CD$ 的方程为： $y = (1 + \sqrt{7})x - \frac{\sqrt{7}}{2}$
> >
> > 由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = (1 - \sqrt{7})x + \frac{\sqrt{7}}{2} \\ y = (1 + \sqrt{7})x - \frac{\sqrt{7}}{2} \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $x = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 AB 与 CD 交点的横坐标是 $\frac{1}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
