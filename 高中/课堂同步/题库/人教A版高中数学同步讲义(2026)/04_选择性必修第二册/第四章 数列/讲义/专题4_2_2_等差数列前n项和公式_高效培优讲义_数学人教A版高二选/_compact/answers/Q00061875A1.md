> [!faq]- 《专题4_2_2_等差数列前n项和公式_高效培优讲义_数学人教A版高二选》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n}=2n-12$ ； (2) $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}-n^{2}+11n,n&\leq6\\ n^{2}-11n+60,n&>6\end{aligned}\right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）先将 $S_{9} = -18$ 用公式 $S_{n} = \frac{n(a_{1} + a_{n})}{2}$ 求出 $a_{5}$ 的值，将 $a_{1}^{2} + a_{3}^{2} = a_{9}^{2} + a_{11}^{2}$ 整理得到 $a_{5} + a_{7} = 0$ ，从而
> >
> > 得到 $a_{7}$ 的值，再求出公差 $d$ ，利用 $a_{n} = a_{5} + (n - 5)d$ 代入 $a_{5}$ 和 $d$ 求出 $a_{n}$ ；
> >
> > (2) 先由 $a_{n} = 2n - 12$ 求出 $a_{1}$ 和 $S_{n}$ ，再按照 $n \leq 6$ 和 $n > 6$ （ $n \in \mathbb{N}_{+}$ ）分情况讨论求出 $T_{n}$ 即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $Q S_{9} = -18$ ， $\therefore \frac{9(a_{1} + a_{9})}{2} = 9a_{5} = -18$ ， $\therefore a_{5} = -2$ ， $Q a_{1}^{2} + a_{3}^{2} = a_{9}^{2} + a_{11}^{2}$ ， $\therefore (a_{9}^{2} - a_{1}^{2}) + (a_{11}^{2} - a_{3}^{2}) = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left(a _ {9} - a _ {1}\right) \left(a _ {9} + a _ {1}\right) + \left(a _ {1 1} - a _ {3}\right) \left(a _ {1 1} + a _ {3}\right) = 0 \quad \therefore 8 d \left(a _ {9} + a _ {1}\right) + 8 d \left(a _ {1 1} + a _ {3}\right) = 0 \quad \because d \neq 0 \quad \therefore \left(a _ {9} + a _ {1}\right) + \left(a _ {1 1} + a _ {3}\right) = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > , \therefore 2 a _ {5} + 2 a _ {7} = 0, \therefore a _ {5} + a _ {7} = 0, Q a _ {5} = - 2, \therefore a _ {7} = 2, \therefore d = \frac {a _ {7} - a _ {5}}{7 - 5} = \frac {2 + 2}{2} = 2,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = a _ {5} + (n - 5) d = - 2 + (n - 5) \times 2 = 2 n - 1 2
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because a _ {n} = 2 n - 1 2, \therefore a _ {1} = - 1 0, \therefore S _ {n} = \frac {n (a _ {1} + a _ {n})}{2} = \frac {n (- 1 0 + 2 n - 1 2)}{2} = n ^ {2} - 1 1 n \tag {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore$ 当 $n \leq 6$ 且 $n \in \mathbb{N}_{+}$ 时， $a_{n} \leq 0$
> >
> > 当 n > 6 且 $n \in N_{+}$ 时， $a_{n} > 0$ ，
> >
> > ∴ 当 $n \leq 6$ 且 $n \in N_{+}$ 时，
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = - \left(a _ {1} + a _ {2} + \dots + a _ {n}\right) = - S _ {n} = - n ^ {2} + 1 1 n
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > n > 6 \text {且} n \in \mathrm{N} _ {+ \text {时}}, T _ {n} = - \left(a _ {1} + a _ {2} + \dots + a _ {6}\right) + \left(a _ {7} + a _ {8} + \dots + a _ {n}\right) = \left(a _ {1} + a _ {2} + \dots + a _ {n}\right) - 2 \left(a _ {1} + a _ {2} + \dots + a _ {6}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = n ^ {2} - 1 1 n - 2 S _ {6} = n ^ {2} - 1 1 n - 2 (6 ^ {2} - 1 1 \times 6) = n ^ {2} - 1 1 n + 6 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l} - n ^ {2} + 1 1 n, n \leq 6 \\ n ^ {2} - 1 1 n + 6 0, n > 6 \end{array} \right.
> > $$
