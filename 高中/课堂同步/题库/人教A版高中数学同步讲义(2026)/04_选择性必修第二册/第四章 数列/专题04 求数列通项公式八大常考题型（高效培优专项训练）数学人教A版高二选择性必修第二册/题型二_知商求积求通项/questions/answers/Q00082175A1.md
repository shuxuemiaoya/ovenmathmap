> [!faq]- 《专题04 求数列通项公式八大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_{n} = \frac{3^{2^{n + 1}} - 1}{16}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用累乘法来求通项公式，即 $a_{n}=a_{1}\times\frac{a_{2}}{a_{1}}\times\frac{a_{3}}{a_{2}}\times\cdots\times\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-1}}$ 的关系，逐步化简得出通项公式.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $n$ 2时， $\frac{a_n}{a_{n - 1}} = 1 + 3^{2^n}$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \frac {a _ {2}}{a _ {1}} \cdot \frac {a _ {3}}{a _ {2}} \dots \cdot \frac {a _ {n}}{a _ {n - 1}}, \\ = (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {2}}) (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {3}}) \dots (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {n}}) \\ = \frac {(1 - 3 ^ {2 ^ {2}}) (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {2}}) (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {3}}) \cdots (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {n}})}{1 - 3 ^ {2 ^ {2}}} \\ = \frac {(1 - 3 ^ {2 ^ {3}}) (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {3}}) \cdots (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {n}})}{1 - 3 ^ {2 ^ {2}}} \\ \frac {(1 - 3 ^ {2 ^ {n}}) (1 + 3 ^ {2 ^ {n}})}{1 - 3 ^ {2 ^ {2}}} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{1 - 3^{2^{n + 1}}}{1 - 3^{2^2}}$
> >
> > 所以当 $n$ 2时， $a_{n} = a_{1} \times \frac{a_{2}}{a_{1}} \times \frac{a_{3}}{a_{2}} \times \dots \times \frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1 - 3^{2^{n+1}}}{-80} = \frac{3^{2^{n+1}} - 1}{160}$ 。
> >
> > 经检验， $n = 1$ 也满足上式，
> >
> > 所以 $a_{n}=\frac{3^{2^{n+1}}-1}{160}$ .
