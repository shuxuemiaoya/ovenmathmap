> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意得， $a_{3}=a_{1}+a_{2}=3$ ， $a_{4}=a_{2}+a_{3}=5$ ， $a_{5}=a_{3}+a_{4}=8$ ， $a_{6}=a_{4}+a_{5}=13$ ，
> >
> > $a_{7} = a_{5} + a_{6} = 21$ $a_{8} = a_{6} + a_{7} = 34$ $a_{9} = a_{7} + a_{8} = 55$
> >
> > $$
> > (2) (i) S _ {n} + 2 = a _ {1} + a _ {2} + a _ {3} + a _ {4} + a _ {5} + \dots + a _ {n} + 2 = a _ {1} + 2 + a _ {2} + a _ {3} + a _ {4} + a _ {5} + \dots + a _ {n}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(a _ {1} + a _ {2}\right) + a _ {2} + a _ {3} + a _ {4} + a _ {5} + \dots + a _ {n} = \left(a _ {3} + a _ {2}\right) + a _ {3} + a _ {4} + a _ {5} + \dots + a _ {n}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(a _ {4} + a _ {3}\right) + a _ {4} + a _ {5} + \dots + a _ {n} = \left(a _ {5} + a _ {4}\right) + a _ {5} + a _ {6} + \dots + a _ {n} = \left(a _ {6} + a _ {5}\right) + a _ {6} + \dots + a _ {n}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \dots = \left(a _ {n - 1} + a _ {n - 2}\right) + a _ {n - 1} + a _ {n} = a _ {n} + a _ {n - 1} + a _ {n} = a _ {n + 1} + a _ {n} = a _ {n + 2}.

> > (3) 设 $a_{n+2}-ra_{n+1}=s\left(a_{n+1}-ra_{n}\right)$ ，对比 $a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+a_{n}$ 得， $\left\{\begin{aligned}rs&=-1\\ r+s&=1\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 解得 $r = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}, s = \frac{1 - \sqrt{5}}{2}$ 或 $r = \frac{1 - \sqrt{5}}{2}, s = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$ .
> >
> > 当 $r=\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2},s=\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}$ 时，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n + 1} - \frac {1 + \sqrt {5}}{2} a _ {n} = \left(\frac {1 - \sqrt {5}}{2}\right) ^ {n + 1} \tag {①}
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $r = \frac{1 - \sqrt{5}}{2}, s = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$ 时，同理可得 $a_{n+1} - \frac{1 - \sqrt{5}}{2} a_n = \left(\frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}\right)^{n+1}$ ②，
> > - **②**：- ①得， $a_{n}=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\left[\left(\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)^{n+1}-\left(\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)^{n+1}\right]$
