> [!faq]- 《专题04 求数列通项公式八大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_{n} = 3 - \frac{1}{n}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 将已知条件中的递推公式进行变形，再利用累加法即可求解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because a_{n + 1} = a_n + \frac{1}{n(n + 1)}$ ， $\therefore a_{n + 1} - a_n = \frac{1}{n(n + 1)} = \frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1}$
> >
> > ∴当n 2时，
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = \left(a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1}\right) + \left(a _ {n - 1} - a _ {n - 2}\right) + \dots + \left(a _ {2} - a _ {1}\right) + a _ {1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(\frac {1}{n - 1} - \frac {1}{n}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{n - 2} - \frac {1}{n - 1}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3}\right) + \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) + 2 = 3 - \frac {1}{n},
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $n = 1$ 时， $a_{1} = 3 - \frac{1}{1} = 2$ ，与 $a_{1} = 2$ 相符， $\therefore$ 数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的通项公式为 $a_{n} = 3 - \frac{1}{n}$ .
