> [!faq]- 《专题4.1 数列的概念（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 2020
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由题意可得 $F_{n+2} = F_{n+1} + F_{n}$ ，再结合累加法即可求解
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意， $F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_{n}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $F_{2022} \cdot F_{2021} = \left( F_{2021} + F_{2020} \right) \cdot F_{2021} = F_{2021}^{2} + F_{2021} \cdot F_{2020}$
> >
> > $$
> > F _ {2 0 2 1} \cdot F _ {2 0 2 0} = F _ {2 0 2 0} ^ {2} + F _ {2 0 2 0} \cdot F _ {2 0 1 9}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > F _ {2 0 2 0} \cdot F _ {2 0 1 9} = F _ {2 0 1 9} ^ {2} + F _ {2 0 1 9} \cdot F _ {2 0 1 8}
> > $$
> >
> > L
> >
> > $$
> > F _ {3} \cdot F _ {2} = F _ {2} ^ {2} + F _ {2} \cdot F _ {1} = F _ {2} ^ {2} + F _ {1} ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $F_{2022} \cdot F_{2021} = F_{2021}^{2} + F_{2020}^{2} + F_{2019}^{2} + \cdots + F_{2}^{2} + F_{1}^{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $F_{2022}^{2} - \left(F_{1}^{2} + F_{2}^{2} + \dots +F_{2021}^{2}\right) = F_{2022}^{2} - F_{2022}\cdot F_{2021} = F_{2022}\left(F_{2022} - F_{2021}\right) = F_{2022}\cdot F_{m}$
> >
> > 所以 $F_{2022}-F_{2021}=F_{m}$
> >
> > 所以 $F_{2022} = F_{2021} + F_{m}$
> >
> > 由 $F_{2022} = F_{2021} + F_{2020}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $F_{m}=F_{2020}$
> >
> > 所以 m = 2020 ，
> >
> > 故答案为：2020
