> [!faq]- 《第四章 数列（高效培优单元测试·强化卷）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n}=2n(2)(i)$ $T_{n}=2-\frac{n+2}{2^{n}}$ ; (ii) $\left(-1,\frac{3}{2}\right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 $d$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_1 + 4d = 10\\ 7a_1 + \frac{7\times 6}{2} d = 56 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $a_1 = d = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = a _ {1} + (n - 1) d = 2 + 2 (n - 1) = 2 n \quad ; \tag {5分}
> > $$
> >
> > $(2)(i)$ 由 $(1)$ 知 $b_{n}=\frac{a_{n}}{2^{n+1}}=\frac{2n}{2^{n+1}}=\frac{n}{2^{n}}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {2}{2 ^ {2}} + \frac {3}{2 ^ {3}} + \dots + \frac {n - 1}{2 ^ {n - 1}} + \frac {n}{2 ^ {n}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{2} T _ {n} = \frac {1}{2 ^ {2}} + \frac {2}{2 ^ {3}} + \frac {3}{2 ^ {4}} + \dots + \frac {n - 1}{2 ^ {n}} + \frac {n}{2 ^ {n + 1}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {1}{2} T _ {n} = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2 ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{2 ^ {3}} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 ^ {n}} - \frac {n}{2 ^ {n + 1}} = \frac {\frac {1}{2} \left[ 1 - \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n} \right]}{1 - \frac {1}{2}} - \frac {n}{2 ^ {n + 1}} = 1 - \frac {n + 2}{2 ^ {n + 1}},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore T_{n}=2-\frac{n+2}{2^{n}}$ ; (10 分)
> >
> > (ii) 由 (i) 得 $(-1)^{n} \lambda < 2 - \frac{n + 2}{2^{n}} + \frac{n}{2^{n}} = 2 - \frac{2}{2^{n}}$
> >
> > 设 $c_{n} = 2 - \frac{2}{2^{n}}$ ，则 $c_{n + 1} = 2 - \frac{2}{2^{n + 1}}$
> >
> > $\therefore c_{n + 1} - c_n = \left(2 - \frac{2}{2^{n + 1}}\right) - \left(2 - \frac{2}{2^n}\right) = \frac{1}{2^n} >0$ ，∴数列 $\{c_n\}$ 是递增数列，
> >
> > 当 $n$ 为偶数时， $\lambda < 2 - \frac{2}{2^n}$ 恒成立， $\therefore \lambda < 2 - \frac{2}{2^2} = \frac{3}{2}$
> >
> > 当 $n$ 为奇数时， $-\lambda < 2 - \frac{2}{2^n}$ 恒成立， $\therefore -\lambda < 2 - \frac{2}{2} = 1$ ， $\therefore \lambda > - 1$
> >
> > ∴实数 $\lambda$ 的取值范围为 $\left(-1,\frac{3}{2}\right)$ .(17 分)
