> [!faq]- 《专题4.3.1 等比数列的概念（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)1； (2) $\frac{19}{2}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）直接代入 $f(x) + f(\frac{1}{x})$ 化简即可；
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) $f(x) + f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = 1$ ，结合等比数列性质，即可求解
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为函数 $f(x)=\frac{1}{x+1}$
> >
> > 所以 $f(x)+f(\frac{1}{x})=\frac{1}{1+x}+\frac{1}{1+\frac{1}{x}}=\frac{1}{1+x}+\frac{x}{1+x}=1$
> >
> > (2) 因数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 是正项等比数列，且 $a_{10}=1$ ，则 $a_{1}a_{19}=a_{2}a_{18}=a_{3}a_{17}=\cdots=a_{10}^{2}=1$
> >
> > 所以 $f(a_{1}) + f(a_{19}) = f(a_{1}) + f(\frac{1}{a_{1}}) = 1$
> >
> > 同理 $f(a_{2}) + f(a_{18}) = f(a_{3}) + f(a_{17}) = \cdots = f(a_{10}) + f(a_{10}) = 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {令} S = f \left(a _ {1}\right) + f \left(a _ {2}\right) + f \left(a _ {3}\right) + \dots + f \left(a _ {1 8}\right) + f \left(a _ {1 9}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $S = f(a_{19}) + f(a_{18}) + f(a_{17}) + \cdots + f(a_{2}) + f(a_{1})$
> >
> > 则有 2S = 19 ，故 $S = \frac{19}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $f(a_{1}) + f(a_{2}) + f(a_{3}) + \cdots + f(a_{18}) + f(a_{19}) = \frac{19}{2}$
