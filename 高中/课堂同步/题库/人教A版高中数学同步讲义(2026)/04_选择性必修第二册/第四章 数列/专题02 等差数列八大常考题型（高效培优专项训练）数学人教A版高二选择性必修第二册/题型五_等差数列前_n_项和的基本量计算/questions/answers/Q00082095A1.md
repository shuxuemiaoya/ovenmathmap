> [!faq]- 《专题02 等差数列八大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 设等差数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为d，由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&S_{4}=2S_{2}\\ &a_{2}=2a_{1}+1\end{aligned}\right.$ 得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&4a_{1}+6d=2(2a_{1}+d)\\ &a_{1}+d=2a_{1}+1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解出 $a_{1},d$ ，进而求解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设等差数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为d，
> >
> > 由题意有 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&S_{4}=2S_{2}\\&a_{2}=2a_{1}+1\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow\left\{\begin{aligned}&4a_{1}+6d=2(2a_{1}+d)\\&a_{1}+d=2a_{1}+1\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow\left\{\begin{aligned}&a_{1}=-1\\&d=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $S_{15}=15a_{1}=-15$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
