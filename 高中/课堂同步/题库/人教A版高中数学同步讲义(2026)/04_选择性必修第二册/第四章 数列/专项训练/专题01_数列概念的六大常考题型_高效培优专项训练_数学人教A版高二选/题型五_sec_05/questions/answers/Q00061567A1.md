> [!faq]- 《专题01_数列概念的六大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_数学人教A版高二选》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用差比较法确定正确答案·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $a_1 = 1 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$ ; $a_2 = 2 \times \frac{4}{9} = \frac{8}{9}$ ; $a_3 = 3 \times \frac{8}{27} = \frac{8}{9} = a_2$ , $a_4 = 4 \times \frac{16}{81} = \frac{64}{81} < a_3$
> >
> > 当 $n$ 4时， $a_{n+1} - a_n = (n + 1) \cdot \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n+1} - n \cdot \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^n = \frac{-n + 2}{3} \cdot \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^n < 0$ ，所以 $a_{n+1} < a_n$ ，
> >
> > 所以数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 中的最大项的项数 2 或 3.
> >
> > 故选：C
> >
> > 38. 数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 、 $\{b_n\}$ 满足： $a_1 = 8$ ， $a_{n} - a_{n - 1} = 8n(n\in \mathbf{N}^{*},n - 2)$ ， $b_{n} = \sqrt{a_{n} + 1}\left(\frac{9}{10}\right)^{n}$ ，则数列 $\{b_n\}$ 的最大项是（）A.第7项 B.第9项C.第11项 D.第12项
> >
> > 【答案】B
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = 4 n ^ {2} + 4 n
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b _ {n} = (2 n + 1) \left(\frac {9}{1 0}\right) ^ {n}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} b _ {k} & b _ {k - 1} \\ b _ {k} & b _ {k + 1} \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > k
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1} = 8 n \quad a _ {n - 1} - a _ {n - 2} = 8 (n - 1) \quad \dots \quad a _ {2} - a _ {1} = 1 6
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} - a _ {1} = \frac {(1 6 + 8 n) (n - 1)}{2} = (4 n + 8) (n - 1)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = 4 n ^ {2} + 4 n (n - 2)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > n = 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b _ {n} = (2 n + 1) \left(\frac {9}{1 0}\right) ^ {n}
> > $$
> >
> > 令 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}b_{k} & b_{k - 1}\\ b_{k} & b_{k + 1} \end{array} \right.$ ，即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}(2k + 1)\bigg(\frac{9}{10}\bigg)^k & (2k - 1)\bigg(\frac{9}{10}\bigg)^{k - 1}\\ (2k + 1)\bigg(\frac{9}{10}\bigg)^k & (2k + 3)\bigg(\frac{9}{10}\bigg)^{k + 1} \end{array} \right.,$
> >
> > 解得 $\frac{17}{2} \leq k \leq \frac{19}{2}$ ， $k \in \mathbf{Z}$ ，故 $k = 9$ ，即第九项最大·
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
