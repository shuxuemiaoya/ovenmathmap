> [!faq]- 《专题04_求数列通项公式八大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_数学人教A版高》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_{n}=\frac{2}{n(n+1)}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据题意可得 $\frac{a_{n + 1}}{a_n} = \frac{n}{n + 2}$ ，再利用累乘法求解.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because a_{1} = 1$ ， $na_{n} = (n + 2)a_{n + 1}$ ，即 $\frac{a_{n + 1}}{a_n} = \frac{n}{n + 2}$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = a _ {1} \cdot \frac {a _ {2}}{a _ {1}} \cdot \frac {a _ {3}}{a _ {2}} \dots \cdot \frac {a _ {n}}{a _ {n - 1}} = 1 \cdot \frac {1}{3} \cdot \frac {2}{4} \cdot \frac {3}{5} \dots \cdot \frac {n - 2}{n} \cdot \frac {n - 1}{n + 1} = \frac {2}{n (n + 1)}
> > $$
> >
> > $a_{1}=1$ 满足上式，所以 $a_{n}=\frac{2}{n(n+1)}$ .
> >
> > 故答案为： $a_{n}=\frac{2}{n(n+1)}$
