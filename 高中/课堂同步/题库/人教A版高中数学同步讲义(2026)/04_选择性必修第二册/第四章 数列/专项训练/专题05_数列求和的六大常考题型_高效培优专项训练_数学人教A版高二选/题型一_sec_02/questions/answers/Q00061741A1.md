> [!faq]- 《专题05_数列求和的六大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_数学人教A版高二选》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)证明见解析， $a_{n}=2^{n}+2n+1$ (2) $S_{n} = n + 5 - \frac{2n + 5}{2^{n}}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）借助等差数列定义计算即可得；
> >
> > (2) 借助错位相减法计算即可得.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $a_{n} = a_{n - 1} + 2^{n - 1} + 2$ ，则 $a_{n} - 2^{n} = a_{n - 1} + 2^{n - 1} + 2 - 2^{n} = a_{n - 1} - 2^{n - 1} + 2$
> >
> > 故 $\left(a_{n}-2^{n}\right)-\left(a_{n-1}-2^{n-1}\right)=2$ ，又 $a_{1}-2^{1}=5-2=3$ ，
> >
> > 故数列 $\left\{a_{n} - 2^{n}\right\}$ 是以3为首项，2为公差的等差数列，
> >
> > 则 $a_{n} - 2^{n} = 3 + 2(n - 1)$ ，即 $a_{n} = 2^{n} + 2n + 1$
> >
> > (2) $b_{n} = \frac{a_{n}}{2^{n}} = \frac{2^{n} + 2n + 1}{2^{n}} = 1 + \frac{2n + 1}{2^{n}}$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {n} = 1 + \frac {3}{2} + 1 + \frac {5}{2 ^ {2}} + \dots + 1 + \frac {2 n + 1}{2 ^ {n}} = n + \frac {3}{2} + \frac {5}{2 ^ {2}} + \dots + \frac {2 n + 1}{2 ^ {n}}
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $\frac{1}{2}S_{n}=\frac{n}{2}+\frac{3}{2^{2}}+\frac{5}{2^{3}}+\cdots+\frac{2n+1}{2^{n+1}}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {n} - \frac {1}{2} S _ {n} = \frac {1}{2} S _ {n} = \frac {n}{2} + \frac {3}{2} + \frac {2}{2 ^ {2}} + \frac {2}{2 ^ {3}} + \dots + \frac {2}{2 ^ {n}} - \frac {2 n + 1}{2 ^ {n + 1}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {n}{2} + \frac {3}{2} + \frac {\frac {1}{2} \left(1 - \frac {1}{2 ^ {n - 1}}\right)}{1 - \frac {1}{2}} - \frac {2 n + 1}{2 ^ {n + 1}} = \frac {n}{2} + \frac {5}{2} - \frac {2 n + 5}{2 ^ {n + 1}},
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $S_{n} = n + 5 - \frac{2n + 5}{2^{n}}$
