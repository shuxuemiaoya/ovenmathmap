> [!faq]- 《专题05_数列求和的六大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_数学人教A版高二选》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n} = 2n - 12$ ; (2) $T_{n} = \begin{cases} -n^{2} + 11n, & n \leq 6 \\ n^{2} - 11n + 60, & n > 6 \end{cases}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）先将 $S_{9} = -18$ 用公式 $S_{n} = \frac{n(a_{1} + a_{n})}{2}$ 求出 $a_{5}$ 的值，将 $a_{1}^{2} + a_{3}^{2} = a_{9}^{2} + a_{11}^{2}$ 整理得到 $a_{5} + a_{7} = 0$ ，从
> >
> > 而得到 $a_{7}$ 的值，再求出公差 d，利用 $a_{n}=a_{5}+(n-5)d$ 代入 $a_{5}$ 和 d 求出 $a_{n}$ ；
> >
> > (2) 先由 $a_{n} = 2n - 12$ 求出 $a_{1}$ 和 $S_{n}$ ，再按照 $n \leq 6$ 和 $n > 6$ （ $n \in \mathbb{N}_{+}$ ）分情况讨论求出 $T_{n}$ 即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $Q S_{9} = -18$ ， $\therefore \frac{9(a_{1} + a_{9})}{2} = 9a_{5} = -18$ ， $\therefore a_{5} = -2$ ， $Q a_{1}^{2} + a_{3}^{2} = a_{9}^{2} + a_{11}^{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left(a _ {9} ^ {2} - a _ {1} ^ {2}\right) + \left(a _ {1 1} ^ {2} - a _ {3} ^ {2}\right) = 0 \quad \therefore \left(a _ {9} - a _ {1}\right) \left(a _ {9} + a _ {1}\right) + \left(a _ {1 1} - a _ {3}\right) \left(a _ {1 1} + a _ {3}\right) = 0 \quad \therefore 8 d \left(a _ {9} + a _ {1}\right) + 8 d \left(a _ {1 1} + a _ {3}\right) = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because d \neq 0 \quad \therefore (a _ {9} + a _ {1}) + (a _ {1 1} + a _ {3}) = 0 \quad \therefore 2 a _ {5} + 2 a _ {7} = 0 \quad \therefore a _ {5} + a _ {7} = 0 \quad Q a _ {5} = - 2 \quad \therefore a _ {7} = 2
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore d = \frac {a _ {7} - a _ {5}}{7 - 5} = \frac {2 + 2}{2} = 2, \therefore a _ {n} = a _ {5} + (n - 5) d = - 2 + (n - 5) \times 2 = 2 n - 1 2.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $\because a_{n} = 2n - 12, \therefore a_{1} = -10, \therefore S_{n} = \frac{n(a_{1} + a_{n})}{2} = \frac{n(-10 + 2n - 12)}{2} = n^{2} - 11n,$
> >
> > ∴当 $n\leq6$ 且 $n\in N_{+}$ 时， $a_{n}\leq0$ ，
> >
> > 当 $n > 6$ 且 $n\in \mathbf{N}_{+}$ 时， $a_{n} > 0$
> >
> > ∴当 $n\leq6$ 且 $n\in N_{+}$ 时，
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = - \left(a _ {1} + a _ {2} + \dots + a _ {n}\right) = - S _ {n} = - n ^ {2} + 1 1 n
> > $$
> >
> > 当 n > 6 且 $n \in N_{+}$ 时， $T_{n} = -(a_{1} + a_{2} + \cdots + a_{6}) + (a_{7} + a_{8} + \cdots + a_{n}) = (a_{1} + a_{2} + \cdots + a_{n}) - 2(a_{1} + a_{2} + \cdots + a_{6})$
> >
> > $$
> > = n ^ {2} - 1 1 n - 2 S _ {6} = n ^ {2} - 1 1 n - 2 (6 ^ {2} - 1 1 \times 6) = n ^ {2} - 1 1 n + 6 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {n} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l} - n ^ {2} + 1 1 n, n \leq 6 \\ n ^ {2} - 1 1 n + 6 0, n > 6 \end{array} \right..
> > $$
