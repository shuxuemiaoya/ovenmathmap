> [!faq]- 《第四章 数列（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{15}{8}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由裂项法即可求解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $a_{n + 1} - a_n = \frac{1}{n(n + 1)} = \frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1}$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {8} = \left(a _ {8} - a _ {7}\right) + \left(a _ {7} - a _ {6}\right) + \dots + \left(a _ {2} - a _ {1}\right) + a _ {1} = \left(\frac {1}{7} - \frac {1}{8} + \frac {1}{6} - \frac {1}{7} + \dots + 1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) + 1 = 2 - \frac {1}{8} = \frac {1 5}{8}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{15}{8}$
