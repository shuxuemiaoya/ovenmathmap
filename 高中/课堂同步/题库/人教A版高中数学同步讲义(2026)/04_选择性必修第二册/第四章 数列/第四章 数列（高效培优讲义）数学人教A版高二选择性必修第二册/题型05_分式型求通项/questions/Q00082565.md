A. $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n} + 1\right\}$ 为等比数列

B $a_{n + 1} > a_n$

D. $\left\{2^{n}a_{n}a_{n + 1}\right\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和 $T_{n} = 1 - \frac{1}{2^{n + 1} - 1}$
