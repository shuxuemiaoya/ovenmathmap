> [!faq]- 《第四章 数列（高效培优讲义）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 4050
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据 $a_1a_{2025} = 1$ 可得 $a_{n}a_{2026 - n} = 1, n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}, n \leq 2025$ ，结合函数得到当 $x \neq 0$ 时， $f(x) + f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = 4$ ，进而
> >
> > 结合倒序相加法求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 正数数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 是公比不等于 1 的等比数列， $a_{1}a_{2025}=1$
> >
> > 则 $a_{n}a_{2026 - n} = 1,n\in \mathbf{N}^{*},n\leq 2025$
> >
> > 由 $f(x) = \frac{4}{1 + x^2}$ ，当 $x \neq 0$ 时， $f(x) + f\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = \frac{4}{1 + x^2} + \frac{4}{1 + \left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^2} = \frac{4}{1 + x^2} + \frac{4x^2}{1 + x^2} = 4$ ，
> >
> > 于是 $f(a_{n}) + f(a_{2026-n}) = f(a_{n}) + f\left(\frac{1}{a_{n}}\right) = 4$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {令} T _ {2 0 2 5} = f \left(a _ {1}\right) + f \left(a _ {2}\right) + \dots + f \left(a _ {2 0 2 5}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $T_{2025}=f\left(a_{2025}\right)+f\left(a_{2024}\right)+\cdots+f\left(a_{1}\right)$
> >
> > 因此 $2T_{2025}=\left[f\left(a_{1}\right)+f\left(a_{2025}\right)\right]+\left[f\left(a_{2}\right)+f\left(a_{2024}\right)\right]+\cdots+\left[f\left(a_{2025}\right)+f\left(a_{1}\right)\right]=4\times2025$
> >
> > 所以 $T_{2025}=4050$ .
> >
> > 故答案为：4050.
