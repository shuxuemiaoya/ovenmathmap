> [!faq]- 《第四章 数列（高效培优单元测试·提升卷）数学人教A版高二选择性必修第二册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{2}{3},&n=1\\\frac{11-6n}{3^{n}},&n\quad2^{(2)}T_{n}=-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{n-1}{6n-5}\end{aligned}\right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题可得 $\frac{3n-1}{S_{n}}=3^{n}$ ，所以 $S_{n}=\frac{3n-1}{3^{n}}$
> >
> > 当 $n = 1$ 时， $a_{1} = S_{1} = \frac{2}{3}$ .
> >
> > 当 $n$ 2时， $a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n-1} = \frac{3n - 1}{3^{n}} - \frac{3(n - 1) - 1}{3^{n - 1}} = \frac{11 - 6n}{3^{n}}$ .
> >
> > 因为 $a_{1}=\frac{2}{3}$ 不满足上式， $\therefore a_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{2}{3},&n=1\\&\frac{11-6n}{3^{n}},&n\quad2\cdot(^{6}\text{分})\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 知， $c_{n} = -3^{n}a_{n} = \left\{\begin{array}{l}-2, n=1 \\6n-11, n=2\end{array}\right.$
> >
> > 当 $n = 1$ 时， $\frac{1}{c_n c_{n + 1}} = \frac{1}{c_1 c_2} = -\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 当 $n$ 2时， $\frac{1}{c_n c_{n+1}} = \frac{1}{(6n - 11)(6n - 5)} = \frac{1}{6}\left(\frac{1}{6n - 11} -\frac{1}{6n - 5}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {n} = \frac {1}{c _ {1} c _ {2}} + \frac {1}{c _ {2} c _ {3}} + \frac {1}{c _ {3} c _ {4}} + \dots + \frac {1}{c _ {n - 1} c _ {n}} + \frac {1}{c _ {n} c _ {n + 1}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{6} \left(1 - \frac {1}{7}\right) + \frac {1}{6} \left(\frac {1}{7} - \frac {1}{1 3}\right) + \dots + \frac {1}{6} \left(\frac {1}{6 n - 1 7} - \frac {1}{6 n - 1 1}\right) + \frac {1}{6} \left(\frac {1}{6 n - 1 1} - \frac {1}{6 n - 5}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{6} \left(1 - \frac {1}{6 n - 5}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {n - 1}{6 n - 5} (n - 2)
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $T_{1} = -\frac{1}{2}$ 满足上式， $\therefore T_{n} = -\frac{1}{2} + \frac{n-1}{6n-5}$ . (17 分)
