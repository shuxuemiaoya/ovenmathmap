> [!faq]- 《专题01 空间向量及其运算高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)15 (2) $\sqrt{29 + 4\sqrt{2}}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （ $^{1}$ ）根据空间向量的线性定理和向量的数量积定义进行求解即可·
> >
> > (2) 根据空间向量的线性定理、向量的数量积定义和向量的模进行求解即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意可得， $AB\cdot AD = 2\times 2\times \cos 45^{\circ} = 2\sqrt{2}$ ， $AA^{\prime}\cdot AB = AA^{\prime}\cdot AD = 3\times 2\times \cos 60^{\circ} = 3$
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{BB'} \cdot AC' = AA' \cdot \left(AB + AD + AA'\right) = AA' \cdot AB + AA' \cdot AD + AA'^2 = 3 + 3 + 3^2 = 15$ .
> >
> > (2) $A C ^ { 2 } = \left( A A ^ { \prime } + A B + A D \right) ^ { 2 }$
> >
> > $$
> > = \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A A ^ {\prime}} ^ {2} + \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A B} ^ {2} + \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A D} ^ {2} + 2 \left(\stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A A ^ {\prime}} \cdot \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A B} + \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A A ^ {\prime}} \cdot \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A D} + \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A B} \cdot \stackrel {\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup} {A D}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 3 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {2} + 2 \times (3 + 3 + 2 \sqrt {2}) = 2 9 + 4 \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以线段 $AC'$ 的长为 $\sqrt{29 + 4\sqrt{2}}$
