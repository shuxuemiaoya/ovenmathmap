> [!faq]- 《专题01 空间向量及其运算高频题型精准突破（八大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据空间向量的线性运算求得正确答案·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{O A}{O P}=OA+AP=OA+\frac{2}{3}AN$
> >
> > $$
> > = \square O A + \frac {2}{3} \left(\square O N - \square O A\right) = \frac {1}{3} \square O A + \frac {2}{3} \square O N
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{3} O A + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} O M = \frac {1}{3} O A + \frac {4}{9} O M
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{3} \square O A + \frac {4}{9} \times \frac {1}{2} (\square O B + \square O C) = \frac {1}{3} \square O A + \frac {2}{9} \square O B + \frac {2}{9} \square O C.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **B**
