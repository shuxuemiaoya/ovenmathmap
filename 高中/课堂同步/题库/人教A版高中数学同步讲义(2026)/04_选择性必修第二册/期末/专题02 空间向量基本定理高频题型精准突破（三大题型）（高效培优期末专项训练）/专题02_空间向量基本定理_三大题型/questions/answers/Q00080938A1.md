> [!faq]- 《专题02 空间向量基本定理高频题型精准突破（三大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ![](../../../images/questions/d0f473f258b61419ce38740a437e2ae29d532528831b7c148bfbc2da83dd1833.jpg) A. $BD_{1}$ 长为 $2\sqrt{3}$ B. 异面直线 $AC$ 与 $BD_{1}$ 所成角的余弦值为 $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ C. $A_{1}C \perp B_{1}D_{1}$ D. $AA_{1} \perp BD$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 以 $\left\{AB, AD, AA_1\right\}$ 为一组基底，将 $BD_{1}$ 用基底表示，得 $\left|BD_{1}\right|^{2} = \left(BD_{1}\right)^{2} = \left(-AB + AD + AA_{1}\right)^{2}$ ，利用数量积的运算即可求解，进而判断 $A$ ，先求 $AC \cdot BD_{1}$ ，利用向量的夹角公式即可判断 $B$ ，计算 $A_{1}C \cdot B_{1}D_{1}$ 和 $AA_{1} \cdot BD$ 即可判断 $CD$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意有： $BD_{1}=-AB+AD+AA_{1}$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \begin{array}{c} \sqcup \sqcup \sqcup \\ B D _ {1} \end{array} \right| ^ {2} = \binom{\sqcup \sqcup \sqcup}{B D _ {1}} ^ {2} = \left(- A B + A D + A A _ {1}\right) ^ {2} = \begin{array}{c} \sqcup \sqcup \sqcup \\ A B ^ {2} + A D ^ {2} + A A _ {1} ^ {2} - 2 A B \cdot A D - 2 A B \cdot A A _ {1} + 2 A D \cdot A A _ {1} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $= \left|\overline{AB}\right|^2 +\left|\overline{AD}\right|^2 +\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|^2 -2\left|\overline{AB}\right|\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|\cos 60^\circ +2\left|\overline{AD}\right|\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|\cos 60^\circ = 4\times 3 = 12$ ，所以 $\left|BD_1\right| = 2\sqrt{3}$ ，故A正确；
> >
> > $\overline{A}C=\overline{A}B+\overline{A}D$ ，所以 $\left|\overline{A}C\right|=2\sqrt{2}$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A C}} \cdot \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{B D}} _ {1} = \left(\stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A B}} + \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A C}}\right) \cdot \left(- \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A B}} + \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A D}} + \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A A}} _ {1}\right) = - \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A B}} ^ {2} + \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A B}} \cdot \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A D}} + \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A B}} \cdot \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A A}} _ {1} - \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A B}} \cdot \stackrel {{\text {UUU}}} {{A D}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > + A D ^ {2} + A D \cdot A A _ {1} = - \left| A B \right| ^ {2} + \left| A B \right| \left| A A _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \left| A D \right| ^ {2} + \left| A D \right| \left| A A _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - 4 + 2 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} + 4 + 2 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} = 4
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos AC, BD_{1} = \frac{AC \cdot BD}{|AC||BD_{1}|} = \frac{4}{2\sqrt{2} \times 2\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{6}$ ，故 B 错误；
> >
> > 由 $A_{1}C = AB + AD - AA_{1}$ ， $B_{1}D_{1} = BD = AD - AB$
> >
> > 所以 $A_{1}C \cdot B_{1}D_{1} = \left(AB + AD - AA_{1}\right) \cdot \left(-AB + AD\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = - A B ^ {2} + A B \cdot A D + A B \cdot A A _ {1} + A B \cdot A D + A D ^ {2} - A D \cdot A A _ {1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \square A B ^ {2} + \square A B \cdot \square A A _ {1} + \square A D ^ {2} - \square A D \cdot \square A A _ {1} = - \left| \square A B \right| ^ {2} + \left| \square A B \right| \left| \square A A _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \left| \square A D \right| ^ {2} - \left| \square A D \right| \left| \square A A _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $= -4 + 2 \times 2 \times \frac{1}{2} + 4 - 2 \times 2 \times \frac{1}{2} = 0$ ，所以 $A_{1}C \perp B_{1}D_{1}$ ，故 $^{C}$ 正确；
> >
> > $$
> > \left. \right. \overline {{A A}} _ {1} \cdot \overline {{B D}} = \overline {{A A}} _ {1} \cdot \left(\overline {{A D}} - \overline {{A B}}\right) = \overline {{A A}} _ {1} \cdot \overline {{A D}} - \overline {{A A}} _ {1} \cdot \overline {{A B}} = \left| \overline {{A D}} \right|\left| \overline {{A A}} _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - \left| \overline {{A B}} \right|\left| \overline {{A A}} _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} = 2 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} - 2 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $AA_{1} \perp BD$ ，故 D 正确；
> >
> > 故选 **ACD**.
