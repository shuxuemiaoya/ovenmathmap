> [!faq]- 《专题02 空间向量基本定理高频题型精准突破（三大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 待定系数法设 $p = x\left( \begin{array}{cc}1 & 1 \\ a + b \end{array} \right) + y\left( \begin{array}{cc}1 & 1 \\ b + c \end{array} \right) + z\left( \begin{array}{cc}1 & 1 \\ c + a \end{array} \right)$ ，结合所给定义及其在基底 $\{a,b,c\}$ 下的斜坐标计算即可得·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $p = x\left(a + b\right) + y\left(b + c\right) + z\left(c + a\right) = (x + z)a + (x + y)b + (y + z)c$ 又 $p = a - 2b + 5c$
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l}x + z = 1\\ x + y = -2\\ y + z = 5 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}x = -3\\ y = 1\\ z = 4 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 即 $p = -3\left(a + b\right) + \left(b + c\right) + 4\left(c + a\right)$ .
> >
> > 所以向量 $p$ 在基底 $\{a + b, b + c, c + a\}$ 下的斜坐标为 $(-3, 1, 4)$ .
> >
> > 故选：D.
