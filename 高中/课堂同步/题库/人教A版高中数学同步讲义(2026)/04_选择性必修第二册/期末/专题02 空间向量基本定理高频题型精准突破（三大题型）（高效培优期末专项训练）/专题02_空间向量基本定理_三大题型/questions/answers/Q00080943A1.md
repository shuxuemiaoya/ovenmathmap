> [!faq]- 《专题02 空间向量基本定理高频题型精准突破（三大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $CD=\frac{2}{3}AB-AC+\frac{1}{3}AA_{1}$ (2) $\frac{1}{4}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）利用空间向量基本定理得到 $CD=\frac{2}{3}AB-AC+\frac{1}{3}AA_{1}$
> >
> > (2) $\overline{CD}=\frac{2}{3}\overline{AB}-\overline{AC}+\frac{1}{3}\overline{AA}_{1}$ 两边平方，求出 $\overline{CD}^{2}=8$ ，得到 $\left|\overline{CD}\right|=2\sqrt{2}$ ，并求出
> >
> > $CD \cdot AC_{1} = \left( \frac{2}{3} AB - AC + \frac{1}{3} AA_{1} \right) \cdot \left( AC + AA_{1} \right) = -3$ ， $\left| AC_{1} \right| = 3\sqrt{2}$ ，利用异面直线向量夹角余弦公式求出答案.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $BD=\frac{1}{3}BA_{1}$
> >
> > $$
> > C D = C B + B D = C B + \frac {1}{3} B A _ {1} = A B - A C + \frac {1}{3} \left(A A _ {1} - A B\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{2}{3} AB - AC + \frac{1}{3} AA_{1}$
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 知， $\overline{CD}=\frac{2}{3}\overline{AB}-\overline{AC}+\frac{1}{3}\overline{AA}_{1}$ ，两边平方得
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C D} ^ {2} = \left(\frac {2}{3} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B} - \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A C} + \frac {1}{3} \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A A _ {1}}\right) ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {4}{9} A B ^ {2} + A C ^ {2} + \frac {1}{9} A A _ {1} ^ {2} - \frac {4}{3} A B \cdot A C - \frac {2}{3} A A _ {1} \cdot A C + \frac {4}{9} A B \cdot A A _ {1}
> > $$
> >
> > 因为三棱柱 $ABC - A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}$ 为直三棱柱， $AB = AC = AA_{1} = 3$
> >
> > 所以 $AB \perp AA_{1}, AA_{1} \perp AC$ ，故 $AB \cdot AA_{1} = AA_{1} \cdot AC = 0$
> >
> > $AB\cdot AC = \left|AB\right|\cdot \left|AC\right|\cos \frac{\pi}{3} = 3\times 3\cos \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{9}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $CD^{2}=4+9+1-\frac{4}{3}\times\frac{9}{2}=8$
> >
> > 故 $\left| \overline{CD} \right| = 2\sqrt{2}$ .
> >
> > 因为 $AC_{1} = \sqrt{AC^{2} + CC_{1}^{2}} = 3\sqrt{2}$ ，故 $\left|A C_1\right| = 3\sqrt{2}$
> >
> > 设直线 CD 与直线 $AC_{1}$ 所成角为 $\theta$
> >
> > $CD \cdot AC_{1} = \left( \frac{2}{3} AB - AC + \frac{1}{3} AA_{1} \right) \cdot \left( AC + AA_{1} \right)$ $= \frac{2}{3} AB \cdot AC + \frac{2}{3} AB \cdot AA_{1} - AC^{2} - AC \cdot AA_{1} + \frac{1}{3} AC \cdot AA_{1} + \frac{1}{3} AA_{1}^{2}$ $= \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{9}{2} - 9 + 3 = -3$ 所以 $\cos\theta = \left| \begin{array}{c} \overrightarrow{CD} \cdot AC \\ |CD| |AC_{1} \end{array} \right| = \frac{3}{2\sqrt{2} \times 3\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{4}$ ,
> >
> > 所以直线 CD 与直线 $AC_{1}$ 所成角的余弦值为 $\frac{1}{4}$ .
