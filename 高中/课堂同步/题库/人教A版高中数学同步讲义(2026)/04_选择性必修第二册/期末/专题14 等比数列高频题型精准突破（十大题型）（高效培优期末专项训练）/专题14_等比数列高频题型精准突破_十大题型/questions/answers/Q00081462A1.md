> [!faq]- 《专题14 等比数列高频题型精准突破（十大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\{a_{n} + b_{n}\}$ B. $\{a_{n} - b_{n}\}$ C. $\{a_{n}b_{n}\}$ D. $\left\{\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}}\right\}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据等比数列的定义与等比中项逐一判断即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设等比数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ ， $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ 是两个公比分别为 q, p，且 $p \neq q, p \neq 0, q \neq 0$ 对于 A，因为 $\left(a_{2} + b_{2}\right)^{2} = \left(a_{1}q + b_{1}p\right)^{2} = \left(a_{1}q\right)^{2} + \left(b_{1}p\right)^{2} + 2a_{1}b_{1}qp$ ， $\left(a_{1} + b_{1}\right)\left(a_{3} + b_{3}\right) = \left(a_{1} + b_{1}\right)\left(a_{1}q^{2} + b_{1}p^{2}\right) = \left(a_{1}q\right)^{2} + \left(b_{1}p\right)^{2} + a_{1}b_{1}\left(q^{2} + p^{2}\right)$ ，
> >
> > 因 $p^{2} + q^{2} > 2pq$ ，则 $\left(a_{2} + b_{2}\right)^{2} < \left(a_{1} + b_{1}\right)\left(a_{3} + b_{3}\right)$ ，故 $\left\{a_{n} + b_{n}\right\}$ 不是等比数列，即 A 错误；
> >
> > 对于 B，因为 $\left(a_{2} - b_{2}\right)^{2} = \left(a_{1}q - b_{1}p\right)^{2} = \left(a_{1}q\right)^{2} + \left(b_{1}p\right)^{2} - 2a_{1}qb_{1}p$ ， $\left(a_{1} - b_{1}\right)\left(a_{3} - b_{3}\right) = \left(a_{1} - b_{1}\right)\left(a_{1}q^{2} - b_{1}p^{2}\right) = \left(a_{1}q\right)^{2} + \left(b_{1}p\right)^{2} - a_{1}b_{1}\left(q^{2} + p^{2}\right)$ ，
> >
> > 与 A 同理， $\left(a_{2} - b_{2}\right)^{2} > \left(a_{1} - b_{1}\right)\left(a_{3} - b_{3}\right)$ ，故 $\left\{a_{n} - b_{n}\right\}$ 不是等比数列，即 B 错误；
> >
> > 对于 C，因为 $a_{n}b_{n} = a_{1}q^{n-1}b_{1}p^{n-1}, a_{n+1}b_{n+1} = a_{1}q^{n}b_{1}p^{n}$ ， $\frac{a_{n+1}b_{n+1}}{a_{n}b_{n}} = \frac{a_{1}q^{n}b_{1}p^{n}}{a_{1}q^{n-1}b_{1}p^{n-1}} = pq$ ，pq 是一个常数，所以 $\left\{a_{n}b_{n}\right\}$ 是等比数列，故 C 正确。
> >
> > 对于 D，因为 $\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}}=\frac{a_{1}q^{n-1}}{b_{1}p^{n-1}},\frac{a_{n+1}}{b_{n+1}}=\frac{a_{1}q^{n}}{b_{1}p^{n}}$ ， $\frac{\frac{a_{n+1}}{b_{n+1}}}{\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}}}=\frac{\frac{a_{1}q^{n}}{b_{1}p^{n}}}{\frac{a_{1}q^{n-1}}{b_{1}p^{n-1}}}=\frac{q}{p}$ ， $\frac{q}{p}$ 是一个常数，
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\frac{a_{n}}{b_{n}}\right\}$ 是等比数列，故 D 正确.
> >
> > 故选 **CD**.
