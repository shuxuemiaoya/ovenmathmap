> [!faq]- 《专题13 等差数列高频题型精准突破（十大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 设等差数列 $\left\{a_{n+1}-a_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为且，等差数列 $\left\{\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n+1}-a_{n}}\right\}$ 的公差为 $d_{1}$ 且 $d_{1}\neq0$ ，进而根据累加法得 $a_{n}=a_{1}+\frac{n(n-1)d}{2}+(n-1)c_{1}$ ， $\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n+1}-a_{n}}=d_{1}n+c_{2}$ ，进而整理得 $\frac{d}{2}n^{2}+\left(c_{1}-\frac{d}{2}\right)n+a_{1}-c_{1}=dd_{1}n^{2}+\left(c_{1}d_{1}+c_{2}d\right)n+c_{1}c_{2}$ ，故 $\frac{d}{2}=dd_{1}$ ，即 $d_{1}=\frac{1}{2}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：设等差数列 $\left\{a_{n+1}-a_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为d，且 $d\neq0$ ，则 $a_{n+1}-a_{n}=dn+c_{1}$ $\therefore a_{n}=a_{1}+\left[d+2d+\cdots+(n-1)d\right]+(n-1)c_{1}=a_{1}+\frac{n(n-1)d}{2}+(n-1)c_{1}$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{a_n}{a_{n+1} - a_n} = \frac{a_1 + \frac{n(n-1)d}{2} + (n-1)c_1}{dn + c_1}$ ， $\because \left\{\frac{a_n}{a_{n+1} - a_n}\right\}$ 为等差数列， $\therefore \frac{a_n}{a_{n+1} - a_n} = d_1n + c_2$ ，（且 $d_{1}$ 为公差） $\therefore \frac{d}{2} n^2 +\left(c_1 - \frac{d}{2}\right)n + a_1 - c_1 = dd_1n^2 +(c_1d_1 + c_2d)n + c_1c_2$ ， $\therefore \frac{d}{2} = dd_1$ ， $\because d\neq 0$ ， $\therefore d_{1} = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
