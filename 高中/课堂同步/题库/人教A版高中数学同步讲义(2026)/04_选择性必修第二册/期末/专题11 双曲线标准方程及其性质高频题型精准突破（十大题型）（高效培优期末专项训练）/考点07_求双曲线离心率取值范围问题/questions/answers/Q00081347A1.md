> [!faq]- 《专题11 双曲线标准方程及其性质高频题型精准突破（十大题型）（高效培优期末专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先应用双曲线定义结合正弦定理把离心率转化为角的正弦，再根据两角和差和辅助角公式化简，根据已知角范围求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在 $\triangle F_{1}PF_{2}$ 中，
> >
> > $$
> > e = \frac {c}{a} = \frac {2 c}{2 a} = \frac {\left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right|}{\left| P F _ {1} \right| - \left| P F _ {2} \right|} = \frac {\sin \angle F _ {1} P F _ {2}}{\sin \angle P F _ {2} F _ {1} - \sin \angle P F _ {1} F _ {2}} = \frac {\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}}{\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{3} + \theta\right) - \sin \theta} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cdot \frac {1}{\cos \left(\frac {\pi}{6} + \theta\right)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \theta \in \left(\frac {\pi}{1 2}, \frac {\pi}{6}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\pi}{6} + \theta \in \left(\frac {\pi}{4}, \frac {\pi}{3}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} +\theta\right)\in \left(\frac{1}{2},\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $e\in \left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2},\sqrt{3}\right)$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
