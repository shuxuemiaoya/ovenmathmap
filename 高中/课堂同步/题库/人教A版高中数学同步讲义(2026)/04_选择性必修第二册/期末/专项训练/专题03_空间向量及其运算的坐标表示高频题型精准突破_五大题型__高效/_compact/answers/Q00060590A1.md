>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > $a \perp b$ 即等价于 $a \cdot b = 0 \cdot$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a = (2, -3, 0), b = (m, 2, -1)$ 且 $a \perp b$ ，所以 $a \cdot b = 2m - 6 + 0 = 0$ ，解得 $m = 3$
