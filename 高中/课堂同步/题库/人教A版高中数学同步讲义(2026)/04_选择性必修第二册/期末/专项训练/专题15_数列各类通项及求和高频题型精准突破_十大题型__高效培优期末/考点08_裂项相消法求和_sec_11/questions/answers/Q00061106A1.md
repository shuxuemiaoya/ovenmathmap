> [!faq]- 《专题15_数列各类通项及求和高频题型精准突破_十大题型__高效培优期末》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n}=n$ (2)证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）利用 $S_{n}, a_{n}$ 的关系，得出数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是等差数列，进而可求得通项公式；
> >
> > (2) 利用裂项相消求和法可求得 $T_{n}$ ，进而可证得结论·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）当 $n = 1$ 时， $S_{1} = a_{1} = \frac{a_{1}(a_{1} + 1)}{2}$ ，得 $a_{1} = 1$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \because S _ {n} = \frac {a _ {n} (a _ {n} + 1)}{2}, \therefore a _ {n} ^ {2} - 2 S _ {n} + a _ {n} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 当 n 2 时， $a_{n-1}^{2}-2S_{n-1}+a_{n-1}=0$ ，
> >
> > 两式作差可得： $a_{n}^{2}-2S_{n}+a_{n}-\left(a_{n-1}^{2}-2S_{n-1}+a_{n-1}\right)=0$
> >
> > 即 $a_{n}^{2} - a_{n - 1}^{2} - a_{n} - a_{n - 1} = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left(a _ {n} + a _ {n - 1}\right) \left(a _ {n} - a _ {n - 1} - 1\right) = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 又∵ $a_{n}>0$ ，∴ $a_{n}-a_{n-1}=1(n\quad2)$ ，且 $a_{1}=1$ ，
> >
> > ∴数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 是以 $^{1}$ 为首项， $^{1}$ 为公差的等差数列，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = n.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $\because a_{n} = n, \therefore S_{n} = \frac{n(n + 1)}{2}, \therefore \frac{1}{S_{n}} = \frac{2}{n(n + 1)}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b _ {n} = \frac {2}{n (n + 1)} = 2 \left(\frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right).
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $T_{n}=b_{1}+b_{2}+b_{3}+\cdots+b_{n}$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \left[ \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3}\right) + \left(\frac {1}{3} - \frac {1}{4}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right) \right] = 2 \left(1 - \frac {1}{n + 1}\right).
> > $$
> >
> > $\because n\in\mathbf{N}^{*},\therefore\frac{1}{n+1}>0,\therefore T_{n}<2,$
> >
> > 又∵ $\left\{T_{n}\right\}$ 为递增数列，所以 $T_{n}\geq T_{1}=1$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 1 \leq T _ {n} <   2.
> > $$
