> [!faq]- 《专题10_椭圆标准方程及其性质高频题型精准突破_九大题型__高效培优期》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据题意可知 $PF_{1} \perp PF_{2}$ ，可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \left|PF_{1}\right| + \left|PF_{2}\right| = 2a = 4\sqrt{2} \\ \left|PF_{1}\right|^{2} + \left|PF_{2}\right|^{2} = \left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|^{2} = 4c^{2} = 16 \end{array} \right.$ ，然后可求 $\left|PF_{1}\right|\cdot \left|PF_{2}\right|$ 【详解】 $\because \overline{PF_1} \cdot \overline{PF_2} = 0$ ， $\therefore PF_1 \perp PF_2$ ，又椭圆 $C: \frac{x^2}{8} + \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \left|PF_{1}\right| + \left|PF_{2}\right| = 2a = 4\sqrt{2} \\ \left|PF_{1}\right|^2 + \left|PF_{2}\right|^2 = \left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|^2 = 4c^2 = 16 \end{array} \right.$ ， $|PF_1| \cdot |PF_2| = \frac{1}{2}\left[\left(|PF_1| + |PF_2|\right)^2 - \left(|PF_1|^2 + |PF_2|^2\right)\right] = 8.$
>
