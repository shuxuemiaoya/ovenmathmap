> [!faq]- 《专题01_空间向量及其运算高频题型精准突破_八大题型__高效培优期末专》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据空间向量的线性运算求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意， $\overline{OP}=\frac{1}{2}\overline{OG}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\overline{OA}+\overline{AG}\right)=\frac{1}{2}\left(\overline{OA}+\frac{2}{3}\overline{AD}\right)=\frac{1}{2}\overline{OA}+\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{1}{2}\left(\overline{AC}+\overline{AB}\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} \square O A + \frac {1}{6} \left(\square O C - O A + \square O B - O A\right) = \frac {1}{6} \square O A + \frac {1}{6} \square O B + \frac {1}{6} \square O C,
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **A**
