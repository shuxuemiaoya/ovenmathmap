> [!faq]- 《第二章_直线和圆的方程_高效培优单元测试_提升卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $B(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $C(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，且 $A(2,0)$ 由 $\overline{AB} = \overline{BC}$ 可得 $(x_{1} - 2,y_{1}) = (x_{2} - x_{1},y_{2} - y_{1})$ ，即 $x_{2} = 2x_{1} - 2,y_{2} = 2y_{1}$ 将 $B,C$ 代入圆 $O$ 方程可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x_1^2 +y_1^2 = 1\\ x_2^2 +y_2^2 = 1 \end{array} \right.$ 即 $\left(2x_{1} - 2\right)^{2} + \left(2y_{1}\right)^{2} = 1$ ，化简可得 $4x_{1}^{2} - 8x_{1} + 4 + 4y_{1}^{2} = 1$ ，将 $y_{1}^{2} = 1 - x_{1}^{2}$ 代入可得 $4x_{1}^{2} - 8x_{1} + 4 + 4\left(1 - x_{1}^{2}\right) = 1$ ，解得 $x_{1} = \frac{7}{8}$ 则 $y_{1}^{2} = 1 - \left(\frac{7}{8}\right)^{2} = \frac{15}{64}$ 所以 $\left|BC\right| = \left|AB\right| = \sqrt{\left(x_1 - 2\right)^2 + y_1^2} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{7}{8} - 2\right)^2 + \frac{15}{64}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}.$
> >
> > 故选 **D**
