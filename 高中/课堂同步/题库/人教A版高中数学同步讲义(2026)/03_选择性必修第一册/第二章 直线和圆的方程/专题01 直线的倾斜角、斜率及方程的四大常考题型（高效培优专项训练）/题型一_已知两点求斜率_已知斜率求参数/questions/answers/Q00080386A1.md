> [!faq]- 《专题01 直线的倾斜角、斜率及方程的四大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\left(-2\sqrt{3},-\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)$ (2) $\alpha = \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} \arctan \left(-\frac{2}{m}\right), & m < 0 \\ \frac{\pi}{2}, & m = 0 \\ \pi - \arctan \frac{2}{m}, & m > 0 \end{array} \right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）当 $\alpha=\left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ ，斜率 $k=-\frac{2}{m}=\tan\alpha\in\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3},\sqrt{3}\right)$ ，解得 $m\in\left(-2\sqrt{3},-\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)$ ;
> >
> > (2) i. $m = 0$ 时， $x = \frac{1}{2}$ ， $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{2}$ ；
> >
> > ii. 时， $y = -\frac{2}{m}x + \frac{1}{m}$ ，斜率 $k = -\frac{2}{m}$ ， $\alpha = \begin{cases} \arctan k, & k > 0 \\ \pi - \arctan (-k), & k < 0 \end{cases} = \begin{cases} \arctan \left(-\frac{2}{m}\right), & m < 0 \\ \pi - \arctan \frac{2}{m}, & m > 0 \end{cases}$ ，综上， $\alpha = \begin{cases} \arctan \left(-\frac{2}{m}\right), & m < 0 \\ \frac{\pi}{2}, & m = 0 \\ \pi - \arctan \frac{2}{m}, & m > 0 \end{cases}$
