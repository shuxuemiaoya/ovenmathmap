> [!faq]- 《专题2_3_直线的交点坐标与距离公式_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) a = 3 ; (2) 存在点 $P\left(\frac{1}{9}, \frac{37}{18}\right)$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）由两平行线间距离公式代入数据即可求解；
> >
> > (2) 由点在第一象限，结合点到线的距离公式列出等式求解即可·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）∵ $l_{2}:2x-y-\frac{1}{2}=0$
> >
> > 与 间的距离为 $d = \frac{\left|a - \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)\right|}{\sqrt{2^2 + (-1)^2}} = \frac{\left|a + \frac{1}{2}\right|}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{7\sqrt{5}}{10}$
> >
> > 即 $\left|a+\frac{1}{2}\right|=\frac{7}{2}$
> >
> > ∴ a = 3
> > ;
> >
> > (2) 假设存在，设点 $P(x_{0}, y_{0})$
> >
> > 由条件②知，点 $P$ 在与 $l_{1}, l_{2}$ 平行的直线 $l^{\prime} 2x - y + c = 0$ 上，
> >
> > 且 $\frac{|c - 3|}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{\left|c + \frac{1}{2}\right|}{\sqrt{5}}$
> >
> > $\therefore c = \frac{13}{2}$ 或 $c = \frac{11}{6}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 4 x _ {0} - 2 y _ {0} + 1 3 = 0 \quad 1 2 x _ {0} - 6 y _ {0} + 1 1 = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 由条件③知， $\frac{\left|2x_{0}-y_{0}+3\right|}{\sqrt{5}}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{5}}\frac{\left|x_{0}+y_{0}-1\right|}{\sqrt{2}}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left| 2 x _ {0} - y _ {0} + 3 \right| = \left| x _ {0} + y _ {0} - 1 \right|, \text {即} x _ {0} - 2 y _ {0} + 4 = 0 \text {或} 3 x _ {0} + 2 = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 因为点 $P$ 在第一象限， $\therefore x_0 > 0, 3x_0 + 2 = 0$ （舍）
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} {x _ {0} - 2 y _ {0} + 4 = 0} \\ {4 x _ {0} - 2 y _ {0} + 1 3 = 0 \text {或}} \end{array} \right. \left\{ \begin{array}{l} {x _ {0} - 2 y _ {0} + 4 = 0} \\ {1 2 x _ {0} - 6 y _ {0} + 1 1 = 0} \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_0 = -3 \\ y_0 = \frac{1}{2} \end{array} \right.$ （舍）， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_0 = \frac{1}{9} \\ y_0 = \frac{37}{18} \end{array} \right.$ ，
> >
> > 所以存在点 $P\left(\frac{1}{9},\frac{37}{18}\right)$ 同时满足①②③.
