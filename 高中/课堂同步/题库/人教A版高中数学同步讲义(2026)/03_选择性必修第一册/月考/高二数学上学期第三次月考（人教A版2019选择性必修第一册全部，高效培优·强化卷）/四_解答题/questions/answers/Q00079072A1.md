> [!faq]- 《高二数学上学期第三次月考（人教A版2019选择性必修第一册全部，高效培优·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a+b-c$ ; $\frac{1}{4}a-b-c(2)\sqrt{3}$ ; $\frac{\sqrt{37}}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）解：根据空间向量的线性运算法则，可得 $A_{1}C = AC - AA_{1} = AB + AD - AA_{1} = a + b - c$
> >
> > 因为 $\overline{AE} = \frac{1}{3}\overline{EB} = \frac{1}{3}\left(\overline{AB} -\overline{AE}\right)$ ，所以 $\overline{AE} = \frac{1}{4}\overline{AB}$
> >
> > 所以 $D_{1}E = AE - AD_{1} = \frac{1}{4} AB - AD - AA_{1} = \frac{1}{4} a - b - c$
> >
> > (2) 解：因为 $AC = AD_{1} = \sqrt{2}$ ，所以 $\left|\overline{A}\overline{C}\right| = \left|a + b\right| = \sqrt{2}$
> >
> > 可得 $a^2 + 2a \cdot b + b^2 = 1 + 2a \cdot b + 1 = 2$ ，解得 $a \cdot b = 0$ ，
> >
> > 同理可得 $b \cdot c = 0$
> >
> > 因为 $AC \cdot AD_{1} = (a + b) \cdot (b + c) = b^{2} + a \cdot b + a \cdot c + b \cdot c = 1 + a \cdot c = \frac{1}{2}$ ，可得 $a \cdot c = -\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|2a+c\right|=\sqrt{4a^{2}+4a\cdot c+c^{2}}=\sqrt{4-2+1}=\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > D _ {1} E = \left| \overrightarrow {D _ {1} E} \right| = \left| \frac {1}{4} a - b - c \right| = \sqrt {\left(\frac {1}{4} a - b - c\right) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {\frac {1}{1 6} a ^ {2} + b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} a \cdot b - a \cdot c + 2 b \cdot c}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {\frac {1}{1 6} + 1 + 1 - 0 + \frac {1}{4} + 0} = \frac {\sqrt {3 7}}{4}
> > $$
