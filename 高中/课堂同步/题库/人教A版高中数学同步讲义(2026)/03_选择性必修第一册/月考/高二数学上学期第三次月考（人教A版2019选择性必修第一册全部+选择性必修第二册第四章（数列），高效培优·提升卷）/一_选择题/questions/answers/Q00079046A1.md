> [!faq]- 《高二数学上学期第三次月考（人教A版2019选择性必修第一册全部+选择性必修第二册第四章（数列），高效培优·提升卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题设 $S_{\triangle F_1MF_2} = \frac{1}{2}\left|MF_1\right|\cdot \left|MF_2\right|\sin \angle F_1MF_2 = \frac{1}{2}\left|MF_1\right|\cdot \left|MF_2\right|\sin \frac{2\pi}{3} = \sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 可得 $\left|MF_1\right|\cdot \left|MF_2\right| = 4$ ，又 $M$ 为上顶点，则 $\left|MF_1\right| = \left|MF_2\right| = a = 2$
> >
> > 故 $\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right| = \sqrt{\left|MF_{1}\right|^{2} + \left|MF_{2}\right|^{2} - 2\left|MF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|MF_{2}\right|\cos\frac{2\pi}{3}} = 2\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $c = \sqrt{3}$ ，则 $b^{2} = a^{2} - c^{2} = 1$ ，故标准方程为 $\frac{x^2}{4} +y^2 = 1$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
