> [!faq]- 《第三章 圆锥曲线的方程（高效培优单元测试·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $x^{2}-\frac{y^{2}}{5}=1$ (2) $\left(\frac{5}{3},5\right)$ (3)证明见详解
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题知 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2c = 2\sqrt{6} \\ \frac{b^2}{4a^2} - \frac{b^2}{4b^2} = 1 \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a^2 = 1 \\ b^2 = 5 \end{array} \right. \\ a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 则 $\Omega$ 的方程为 $x^{2}-\frac{y^{2}}{5}=1$
> >
> > (2) 因为直线 $l: y = t(x + 1)$ 与 $\Omega$ 的右支交于点 $E(x_E, y_E)$ ( $x_E > 2$ )
> >
> > 所以 $x_{E}^{2}-\frac{y_{E}^{2}}{5}=1\Rightarrow y_{E}^{2}=5\left(x_{E}^{2}-1\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > t ^ {2} = \frac {y _ {E} ^ {2}}{\left(x _ {E} + 1\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {5 \left(x _ {E} ^ {2} - 1\right)}{\left(x _ {E} + 1\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {5 \left(x _ {E} - 1\right)}{x _ {E} + 1} = 5 \left(1 - \frac {2}{x _ {E} + 1}\right) \in \left(\frac {5}{3}, 5\right),
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $t^{2}$ 的取值范围为 $\left(\frac{5}{3},5\right)$ .
> >
> > (3) 证明：根据题意直线 $MN$ 斜率存在，
> >
> > 设 $y = kx + m$ ， $M(x_{1},y_{1}),N(x_{2},y_{2})$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = k x + m \\ x ^ {2} - \frac {y ^ {2}}{5} = 1 \end{array} \Rightarrow (5 - k ^ {2}) x ^ {2} - 2 k m x - m ^ {2} - 5 = 0 \right.,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x _ {1} + x _ {2} = \frac {2 k m}{5 - k ^ {2}}, x _ {1} x _ {2} = \frac {- m ^ {2} - 5}{5 - k ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y _ {1} + y _ {2} = k \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) + 2 m = \frac {2 k ^ {2} m}{5 - k ^ {2}} + 2 m = \frac {1 0 m}{5 - k ^ {2}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x _ {1} y _ {2} + x _ {2} y _ {1} = 2 k x _ {1} x _ {2} + m \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) = \frac {- 2 k m ^ {2} - 1 0 k}{5 - k ^ {2}} + \frac {2 k m ^ {2}}{5 - k ^ {2}} = \frac {- 1 0 k}{5 - k ^ {2}},
> > $$
> >
> > 由（1）知 $A\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\right), B(0, \sqrt{5})$ ，又 $\angle MAN$ 的平分线垂直于 $OB$ ，
> >
> > 所以直线 AM, AN 的斜率 $k_{1}, k_{2}$ 互为相反数，
> >
> > $$
> > \text {即} k _ {1} + k _ {2} = \frac {y _ {1} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}}{x _ {1} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}} + \frac {y _ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}}{x _ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}} = \frac {\left(x _ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right) \left(y _ {1} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right) + \left(x _ {1} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right) \left(y _ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right)}{\left(x _ {1} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right) \left(x _ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right)}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {x _ {2} y _ {1} + x _ {1} y _ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2} \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2} \left(y _ {1} + y _ {2}\right) + \frac {5}{2}}{\left(x _ {1} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right) \left(x _ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right)} = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} x _ {2} y _ {1} + x _ {1} y _ {2} - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2} \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2} \left(y _ {1} + y _ {2}\right) + \frac {5}{2} = \frac {- 1 0 k}{5 - k ^ {2}} - \frac {\sqrt {5} k m}{5 - k ^ {2}} - \frac {5 \sqrt {5} m}{5 - k ^ {2}} + \frac {5}{2} \\ = \frac {- 2 0 k - 2 \sqrt {5} k m - 1 0 \sqrt {5} m + 2 5 - 5 k ^ {2}}{2 (5 - k ^ {2})} = \frac {- 5 (k + 5) (k - 1) - 2 \sqrt {5} (k + 5)}{2 (5 - k ^ {2})} \\ = \frac {- 2 \sqrt {5} (k + 5) \left(\frac {\sqrt {5}}{2} k + m - \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2}\right)}{2 (5 - k ^ {2})} = 0, \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > ∵ M, N 异于点 A，∴ A 不在直线 MN 上，
> >
> > 即 $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} k + m \neq \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} \Rightarrow \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} k + m - \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} \neq 0$
> >
> > 所以 $k + 5 = 0 \Rightarrow k = -5$
> >
> > 即直线 $MN$ 的倾斜角为定值·
