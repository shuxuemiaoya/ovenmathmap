> [!faq]- 《第三章_圆锥曲线的方程_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $x^{2}+2y^{2}=20$ (2) $5\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设点 $P(x_0, y_0), M(x, y)$ ，则 $Q(x_0, 0)$ ，由 $\overline{QP} = \sqrt{2QM}$ ，可得 $0 = \sqrt{2}(x - x_0), y_0 = \sqrt{2}(y - 0)$ ，即 $x = x_0, y_0 = \sqrt{2}y$
> >
> > $$
> > \because x _ {0} ^ {2} + y _ {0} ^ {2} = 2 0, \therefore x ^ {2} + 2 y ^ {2} = 2 0
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 将曲线 C 与直线 l 联立得 $\left\{\begin{aligned} x^{2} + 2y^{2} &= 20, \\ y &= x + m, \end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 消去 $y$ 得 $3x^{2} + 4mx + 2m^{2} - 20 = 0$
> >
> > ∵ 直线 l 与曲线 C 交于 A, B 两点，设 $A(x_{1}, y_{1}), B(x_{2}, y_{2})$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \Delta = 1 6 m ^ {2} - 4 \times 3 \times (2 m ^ {2} - 2 0) > 0
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\because m\neq 0,\therefore 0 <   m^2 <  30,x_1 + x_2 = -\frac{4m}{3},x_1x_2 = \frac{2m^2 - 20}{3}$
> >
> > ∴ 点 O 到直线 AB: $x - y + m = 0$ 的距离 $d = \frac{|m|}{\sqrt{2}}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \left| A B \right| = \sqrt {1 + k ^ {2}} \left| x _ {1} - x _ {2} \right| = \sqrt {\left(1 + k ^ {2}\right) \left[ \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) ^ {2} - 4 x _ {1} x _ {2} \right]}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {2 \times \left(\frac {1 6 m ^ {2}}{9} - 4 \times \frac {2 m ^ {2} - 2 0}{3}\right)} = \sqrt {\frac {1 6}{9} (3 0 - m ^ {2})}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {\triangle O A B} = \frac {1}{2} \sqrt {\frac {1 6}{9} \left(3 0 - m ^ {2}\right)} \times \frac {| m |}{\sqrt {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\triangle O A B} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{3} \times \sqrt {m ^ {2} (3 0 - m ^ {2})} \leq \frac {\sqrt {2}}{3} \times \frac {m ^ {2} + (3 0 - m ^ {2})}{2} = 5 \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $m^2 = 30 - m^2$ ，即 $m^2 = 15$ 时取等号.
> >
> > ∴ $\text{三角形} OAB$ 面积的最大值为 $5\sqrt{2}$
