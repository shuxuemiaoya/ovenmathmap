> [!faq]- 《第三章_圆锥曲线的方程_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{x_1^2}{a^2} +\frac{y_1^2}{b^2} = 1\\ \frac{x_2^2}{a^2} +\frac{y_2^2}{b^2} = 1 \end{array} \right.$ 两式相减得 $\frac{(x_1 - x_2)(x_1 + x_2)}{a^2} +\frac{(y_1 - y_2)(y_1 + y_2)}{b^2} = 0$ 又 $\frac{y_1 - y_2}{x_1 - x_2} = -\frac{1}{2}$ ， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x_{1} + x_{2} = 2\\ y_{1} + y_{2} = 2 \end{array} \right.$ ，则 $\frac{2}{a^2} -\frac{2}{2b^2} = 0$
> >
> > 则 $a^{2}=2b^{2}$ ， $\frac{b}{a}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**
