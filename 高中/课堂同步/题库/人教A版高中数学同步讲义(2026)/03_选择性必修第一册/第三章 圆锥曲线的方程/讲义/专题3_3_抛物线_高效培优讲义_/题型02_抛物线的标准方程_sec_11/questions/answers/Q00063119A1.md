> [!faq]- 《专题3_3_抛物线_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $y^{2}=2x$ ；（2）① $2\sqrt{10}$ ；②见解析. $$ \frac {x ^ {2}}{1} - y ^ {2} = 1 $$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $4x^{2}-y^{2}=1$ ，化为标准形式： $\frac{1}{4}$
> >
> > $a^{2}=\frac{1}{4},a=\frac{1}{2}$ ，右顶点 $A\left(\frac{1}{2},0\right)$ ,
> >
> > 设抛物线的方程为 $y^{2}=2px$ ，焦点坐标为 $F\left(\frac{p}{2},0\right)$ ,
> >
> > 由于抛物线的焦点是双曲线的右顶点，所以 p = 1，
> >
> > $\therefore$ 抛物线 C 的方程 $y^{2}=2x$
> >
> > (2) $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y^{2} = 2x \\ y = x - 2, \text{消去} x, \text{得} y^{2} - 2y - 4 = 0, \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}), B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则 $y_{1}y_{2} = -4, y_{1} + y_{2} = 2,$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| y _ {1} - y _ {2} \right| = \sqrt {\left(y _ {1} + y _ {2}\right) ^ {2} - 4 y _ {1} y _ {2}} = \sqrt {2 0} = 2 \sqrt {5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y _ {1} = x _ {1} - 2, y _ {2} = x _ {2} - 2, x _ {1} - x _ {2} = y _ {1} - y _ {2}, x _ {1} = \frac {y _ {1} ^ {2}}{2}, x _ {2} = \frac {y _ {2} ^ {2}}{2}, x _ {1} x _ {2} = \frac {y _ {1} ^ {2} y _ {2} ^ {2}}{4} = 4,
> > $$
> > - **①**：$\left|AB\right|=\sqrt{\left(x_{1}-x_{2}\right)^{2}+\left(y_{1}-y_{2}\right)^{2}}=2\sqrt{10}$
> > - **②**：$OA \cdot OB = x_{1}x_{2} + y_{1}y_{2} = 4 - 4 = 0$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore O A \perp O B
> > $$
