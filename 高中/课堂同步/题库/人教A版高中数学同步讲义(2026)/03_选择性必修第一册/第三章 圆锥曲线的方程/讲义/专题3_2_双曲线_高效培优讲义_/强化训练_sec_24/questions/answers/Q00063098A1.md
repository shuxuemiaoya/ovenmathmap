> [!faq]- 《专题3_2_双曲线_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 抛物线 $x^{2} = 2y$ 的准线为直线 $l:y = -\frac{1}{2}$ ，与圆 $C:x^{2} + y^{2} = 1$ 联立得 $x = \pm \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
> >
> > 不妨设 $A\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\right),B\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\right)$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{OA} = \left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\right),\overrightarrow{OB} = \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, - \frac{1}{2}\right),$
> >
> > 故 $\cos \angle AOB = \frac{\square\square\square\square}{\square\square\square}\frac{\square\square\square}{\square\square\square}\frac{OA\cdot OB}{OA|\left|OB\right|} = \frac{-\frac{1}{2}}{1\times 1} = -\frac{1}{2},\angle AOB\in [0,\pi ],$ 故 $\angle AOB = \frac{2\pi}{3}$
> >
> > 故选 **B**
