> [!faq]- 《专题3.1 椭圆（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $PF_{1} \cdot PF_{2} = 0$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore PF_{1}\perp PF_{2}$
> >
> > 又椭圆 $C:\frac{x^{2}}{8}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$ ，
> >
> > 则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \left|PF_{1}\right| + \left|PF_{2}\right| = 2a = 4\sqrt{2}\\ \left|PF_{1}\right|^{2} + \left|PF_{2}\right|^{2} = \left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|^{2} = 4c^{2} = 16 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| P F _ {1} \right| \cdot \left| P F _ {2} \right| = \frac {1}{2} \left[ \left(\left| P F _ {1} \right| + \left| P F _ {2} \right|\right) ^ {2} - \left(\left| P F _ {1} \right| ^ {2} + \left| P F _ {2} \right| ^ {2}\right) \right] = 8.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
