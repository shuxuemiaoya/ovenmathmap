> [!faq]- 《专题3.1 椭圆（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} +\frac{y^2}{a^2 - 1} = 1$ 可知 $b = \sqrt{a^2 - 1},c = 1$ ，则 $F(1,0)$ ， $A(a,0)$ ， $B(0,\sqrt{a^2 - 1})$
> >
> > 由题意可设圆的方程为 $C \left( x - r \right)^{2} + \left( y - n \right)^{2} = r^{2}$ ，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&\left(1 - r\right)^{2} + \left(0 - n\right)^{2} = r^{2}\\ &\left(a - r\right)^{2} + \left(0 - n\right)^{2} = r^{2}\\ &\left(0 - r\right)^{2} + \left(\sqrt{3} - n\right)^{2} = r^{2}\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a = 3\\ n = \sqrt{3}\\ r = 2 \end{array} \right.$ ，所以 $B\left(0,2\sqrt{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\left|BF\right| = \sqrt{1^2 + \left(2\sqrt{2}\right)^2} = 3$
> >
> > 故答案为：3
