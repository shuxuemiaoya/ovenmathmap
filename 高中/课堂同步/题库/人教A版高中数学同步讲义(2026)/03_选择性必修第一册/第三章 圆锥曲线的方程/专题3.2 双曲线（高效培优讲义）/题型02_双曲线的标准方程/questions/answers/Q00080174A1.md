> [!faq]- 《专题3.2 双曲线（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{x^{2}}{9}-\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1$ (2)证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）根据题意得 $\frac{\sqrt{(x - 3\sqrt{2})^2 + y^2}}{\left|x - \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\right|} = \sqrt{2}$ ，则可得 $\sqrt{(x - 3\sqrt{2})^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{2}\left|x - \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\right|$
> >
> > 将上式两边平方，得 $\left(x - 3\sqrt{2}\right)^2 + y^2 = 2\left(x - \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^2$
> >
> > 整理得 $x^{2} - 6\sqrt{2} x + 18 + y^{2} = 2x^{2} - 6\sqrt{2} x + 9$ ，所以 $x^{2} - y^{2} = 9$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{x^2}{9} -\frac{y^2}{9} = 1$
> >
> > (2) 设点 M 坐标为 $\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)$ ，设曲线在点 M 处的切线方程为 $y - y_{0} = k(x - x_{0})$
> >
> > 与双曲线方程联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y - y_0 = k(x - x_0) \\ x^2 - y^2 = 9 \end{array} \right.$ ，消去 $y$ ，可得 $x^2 - \left[k(x - x_0) + y_0\right]^2 = 9$
> >
> > 整理得 $\left(1 - k^2\right)x^2 +\left(2k^2 x_0 + 2ky_0\right)x - k^2 x_0^2 -2ky_0x_0 + y_0^2 -9 = 0$
> >
> > 所以 $1 - k^2 \neq 0$ 且 $\Delta = (2k^2 x_0 + 2ky_0)^2 - 4(1 - k^2)(-k^2 x_0^2 - 2ky_0x_0 + y_0^2 - 9) = 0$
> >
> > 解得 $k=\frac{y_{0}}{x_{0}}$ ，代入 $y-y_{0}=k(x-x_{0})$ ，得 $y_{0}y-y_{0}^{2}=x_{0}x-x_{0}^{2}$
> >
> > 所以切线方程为 $x_0x - y_0y = 9$
> >
> > 与 $y = x$ 联立得 $x_{A} = \frac{9}{x_{0} - y_{0}}$ ，与 $y = -x$ 联立得 $x_{B} = \frac{9}{x_{0} + y_{0}}$
> >
> > 故 $S_{\triangle AOB} = \frac{1}{2}\left|\sqrt{2} x_A\cdot \sqrt{2} x_B\right| = \left|\frac{81}{x_0^2 - y_0^2}\right| = \frac{81}{9} = 9.$
