> [!faq]- 《第三章 圆锥曲线的方程（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设弦的两端点为 $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则 $x_{1} + x_{2} = 2,y_{1} + y_{2} = 1$ 因为 $\frac{1^2}{6} +\frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2}{3} < 1$ ，所以点 $\left(1,\frac{1}{2}\right)$ 在椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{6} +\frac{y^2}{3} = 1$ 内，
> >
> > 将 $A(x_{1},y_{1}), B(x_{2},y_{2})$ 代入椭圆得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{x_1^2}{6} + \frac{y_1^2}{3} = 1 \\ \frac{x_2^2}{6} + \frac{y_2^2}{3} = 1 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 两式相减得 $\frac{(x_{1}-x_{2})(x_{1}+x_{2})}{6}+\frac{(y_{1}-y_{2})(y_{1}+y_{2})}{3}=0$
> >
> > 即 $\frac{(x_1 - x_2)(x_1 + x_2)}{6} = -\frac{(y_1 - y_2)(y_1 + y_2)}{3}$
> >
> > 即 $-\frac{3(x_{1}+x_{2})}{6(y_{1}+y_{2})}=\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}$
> >
> > 即 $-\frac{3\times2}{6\times1}=\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}$
> >
> > $\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}=-1$ 即 $x_{1}-x_{2}$
> >
> > 所以弦所在的直线的斜率为 -1.
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
