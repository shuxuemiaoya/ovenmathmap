> [!faq]- 《第三章 圆锥曲线的方程（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为点 $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})$ 在椭圆 $C:\frac{x^{2}}{2}+y^{2}=1$ 上，
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{2}+y_{1}^{2}&=1\\ \frac{x_{2}^{2}}{2}+y_{2}^{2}&=1\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > <!-- source-part:2 pages:51-100 -->
> >
> > 因为直线 $OA$ ， $OB$ 的斜率之积为 $-\frac{1}{2}$ ，所以 $\frac{y_1}{x_1} \times \frac{y_2}{x_2} = -\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 可得 $\left(y_{1}y_{2}\right)^{2} = \left(1 - \frac{x_{1}^{2}}{2}\right)\left(1 - \frac{x_{2}^{2}}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{4}\left(x_{1}x_{2}\right)^{2}$ ，化简得 $x_{1}^{2} + x_{2}^{2} = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > x _ {1} ^ {2} - y _ {1} ^ {2} + x _ {2} ^ {2} - y _ {2} ^ {2} = x _ {1} ^ {2} - \left(1 - \frac {x _ {1} ^ {2}}{2}\right) + x _ {2} ^ {2} - \left(1 - \frac {x _ {2} ^ {2}}{2}\right) = \frac {3 x _ {1} ^ {2}}{2} + \frac {3 x _ {2} ^ {2}}{2} - 2
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {3}{2} \left(x _ {1} ^ {2} + x _ {2} ^ {2}\right) - 2 = 1
> > $$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
