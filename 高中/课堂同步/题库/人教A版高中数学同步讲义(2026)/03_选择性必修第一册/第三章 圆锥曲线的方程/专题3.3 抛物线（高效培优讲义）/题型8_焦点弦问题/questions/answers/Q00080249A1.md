> [!faq]- 《专题3.3 抛物线（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AC
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $A(x_{1}, y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，AB 的方程为 $y = k\left(x - \frac{p}{2}\right)$
> >
> > 由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y&=k\left(x-\frac{p}{2}\right)\\ y^{2}&=2px\end{aligned}\right.$ 可得， $k^{2}x^{2}-p\left(k^{2}+2\right)x+\frac{1}{4}k^{2}p^{2}=0$ 则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x_{1}+x_{2}&=\frac{p\left(k^{2}+2\right)}{k^{2}}\\ x_{1}\cdot x_{2}&=\frac{1}{4}p^{2}\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|AB\right|=x_{1}+x_{2}+p=\frac{p(k^{2}+2)}{k^{2}}+p=\frac{2p(k^{2}+1)}{k^{2}}$
> >
> > 同理可得 $\left|CD\right|==\frac{p\left(\frac{1}{k^{2}}+1\right)}{\frac{1}{k^{2}}}=2p\left(1+k^{2}\right)$ ，则有 $\frac{1}{\left|AB\right|}+\frac{1}{\left|CD\right|}=\frac{1}{2p}$ ，所以 $^{A}$ 正确；
> >
> > 若 $\left|AF\right|\cdot\left|BF\right|=\frac{4}{3}p^{2}$ ，由 $\left(x_{1}+\frac{p}{2}\right)\cdot\left(x_{2}+\frac{p}{2}\right)=x_{1}x_{2}+\frac{p}{2}\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right)+\frac{1}{4}p^{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x _ {1} + x _ {2} = \frac {p (k ^ {2} + 2)}{k ^ {2}} \\ x _ {1} \cdot x _ {2} = \frac {1}{4} p ^ {2} \quad \text {得} | A F | \cdot | B F | = \frac {1}{2} p ^ {2} + \frac {p}{2} \cdot \frac {p (k ^ {2} + 2)}{k ^ {2}} = \frac {p ^ {2} (1 + k ^ {2})}{k ^ {2}}, \text {即} \frac {p ^ {2} (1 + k ^ {2})}{k ^ {2}} = \frac {4}{3} p ^ {2}, \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $k = \sqrt{3}$ ，故B错误；
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square} {O A} \cdot \stackrel {\square \square \square} {O B} = x _ {1} \cdot x _ {2} + y _ {1} \cdot y _ {2} = \frac {1}{4} p ^ {2} + k ^ {2} \left(x _ {1} - \frac {p}{2}\right) \left(x _ {2} - \frac {p}{2}\right) = \frac {1}{4} p ^ {2} + k ^ {2} \left(x _ {1} x _ {2} - \frac {p}{2} \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) + \frac {1}{4} p ^ {2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{4} p ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} k ^ {2} p ^ {2} - \frac {p ^ {2} (k ^ {2} + 2)}{2} = - \frac {3}{4} p ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > 与 k 无关，同理 $OC \cdot OD = -\frac{3}{4} p^{2}$ ，故 $OA \cdot OB = OC \cdot OD$ ，故 C 正确；
> >
> > 因为 $AB \perp CD$ ，所以四边形 $ABCD$ 的面积
> >
> > $$
> > S = \frac {1}{2} | A B | \cdot | C D | = \frac {2 p (k ^ {2} + 1)}{k ^ {2}} \cdot 2 p (1 + k ^ {2}) = 2 p ^ {2} (k ^ {2} + \frac {1}{k ^ {2}} + 2) \quad 8 p ^ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $k^{2}=\frac{1}{k^{2}}$ ，即 k=1 时，等号成立，故 D 错误。
> >
> > 故选 **AC**.
