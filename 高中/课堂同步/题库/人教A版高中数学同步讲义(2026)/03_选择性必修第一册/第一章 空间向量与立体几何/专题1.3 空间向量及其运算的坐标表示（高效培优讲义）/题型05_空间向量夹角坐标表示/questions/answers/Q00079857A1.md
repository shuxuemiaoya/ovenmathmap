> [!faq]- 《专题1.3 空间向量及其运算的坐标表示（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先由题意结合向量数量积定义和模长坐标计算公式得到 $p + n = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 和 $p^2 + n^2 = 1$ ，再结合向量夹角余
> >
> > 弦公式即可计算求解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为空间两个单位向量 $OA = (m, n, 0), OB = (0, p, n), OB$ 与向量 $OC = (1, 1, 1)$ 的夹角等于 $\frac{\pi}{3}$ ，所以 $OB \cdot OC = p + n, |OB| = \sqrt{0^2 + p^2 + n^2} = 1, |OC| = \sqrt{1^2 + 1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{3}$ ，所以 $p + n = 1 \times \sqrt{3} \times \cos \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，结合 $p^2 + n^2 = 1$ 得 $np = \frac{(p + n)^2 - (p^2 + n^2)}{2} = -\frac{1}{8}$
> >
> > 则向量 $\overrightarrow{OA},\overrightarrow{OB}$ 夹角的余弦值为 $\cos\left\langle\overrightarrow{OA},\overrightarrow{OB}\right\rangle=\frac{\overrightarrow{OA}\cdot\overrightarrow{OB}}{\left|\overrightarrow{OA}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{OB}\right|}=np=-\frac{1}{8}$
> >
> > 故选：D.
> >
> > ## 方法技巧
> >
> > 若 $a = (a_{1}, a_{2}, a_{3}), b = (b_{1}, b_{2}, b_{3})$ ，则
> >
> > $$
> > | a | = \sqrt {a \cdot a} = \sqrt {a _ {1} ^ {2} + a _ {2} ^ {2} + a _ {3} ^ {2}}, | b | = \sqrt {b \cdot b} = \sqrt {b _ {1} ^ {2} + b _ {2} ^ {2} + b _ {3} ^ {2}} \tag {1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \langle \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} \rangle = \frac {\vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} _ {\rightarrow}}{| \vec {a} | \cdot | \vec {b} |} = \frac {a _ {1} b _ {1} + a _ {2} b _ {2} + a _ {3} b _ {3}}{\sqrt {a _ {1} ^ {2} + a _ {2} ^ {2} + a _ {3} ^ {2}} \cdot \sqrt {b _ {1} ^ {2} + b _ {2} ^ {2} + b _ {3} ^ {2}}} (a \neq 0, b \neq 0)
> > $$
> >
> > (2)
