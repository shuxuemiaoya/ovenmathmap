> [!faq]- 《专题1.2 空间向量基本定理（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由题意选 $\left\{\begin{array}{ccc}AB,AC,AA_{1}\end{array}\right\}$ 为一组基底，利用空间向量基本定理，先表示 AO，利用 $\left|AO\right|^{2}=AO^{2}=\frac{1}{4}\left(AA_{1}+AB+AC\right)^{2}$ 即可求解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意有：
> >
> > $\left|\overline{AO}\right|^2 = \overline{AO}^2 = \frac{1}{4}\left(\overline{AA_1} + \overline{AB} + \overline{AC}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}\left(\overline{AA_1}^2 + \overline{AB}^2 + \overline{AC}^2 + 2\overline{AA_1} \cdot \overline{AB} + 2\overline{AA_1} \cdot \overline{AC} + 2\overline{AB} \cdot \overline{AC}\right)$ $= \frac{1}{4}\left(\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|^2 + \left|\overline{AB}\right|^2 + \left|\overline{AC}\right|^2 + 2\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|\left|\overline{AB}\right|\cos 60^\circ + 2\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|\left|\overline{AC}\right|\cos 60^\circ + 2\left|\overline{AB}\right|\left|\overline{AC}\right|\cos 90^\circ\right) = \frac{1}{4}(4 + 1 + 1 + 2 + 2) = \frac{10}{4}$ 所以 $\left|\overline{AO}\right| = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
