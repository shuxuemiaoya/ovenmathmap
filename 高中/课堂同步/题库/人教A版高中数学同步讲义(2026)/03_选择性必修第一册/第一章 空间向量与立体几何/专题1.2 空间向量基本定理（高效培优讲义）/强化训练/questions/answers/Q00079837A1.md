> [!faq]- 《专题1.2 空间向量基本定理（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{5\sqrt{7}}{14}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先利用基底表示向量 $BC_{1}, CA_{1}$ ，再利用向量的夹角公式和同角三角函数的平方关系即可求解。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意有 $BC_{1} = BC + CC_{1} = AD + AA_{1}$ ， $CA_{1} = CB + BA + AA_{1} = -AB - AD + AA_{1}$
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{BC}_{1}\cdot\overline{CA}_{1}=\left(\overline{AD}+\overline{AA}_{1}\right)\cdot\left(-\overline{AB}-\overline{AD}+\overline{AA}_{1}\right)=-\overline{AD}\cdot\overline{AB}-\overline{AD}^{2}+$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \square \\ A D \cdot A A _ {1} - A A _ {1} \cdot A B - A D \cdot A A _ {1} + A A _ {1} ^ {2} = - | A B | | A D | \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - | A D | ^ {2} - | A A _ {1} | | A B | \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + | A A _ {1} | ^ {2} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - 2 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} - 4 - 2 \times 4 \times \frac {1}{2} + 1 6 = 6
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \overline {{B C}} _ {1} \right| ^ {2} = \left(\overline {{A D}} + \overline {{A A}} _ {1}\right) ^ {2} = \overline {{A D}} ^ {2} + 2 \overline {{A D}} \cdot \overline {{A A}} _ {1} + \overline {{A A}} _ {1} ^ {2} = \left| \overline {{A D}} \right| ^ {2} + 2 \left| \overline {{A D}} \right| \left| \overline {{A A}} _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \left| \overline {{A A}} _ {1} \right| ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 4 + 2 \times 2 \times 4 \times \frac {1}{2} + 1 6 = 2 8
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|B C_1\right| = 2\sqrt{7}$ ，又 $\left|CA_1\right|^2 = \left(-AB - AD + AA_1\right)^2 = AB^2 +AD^2 +AA_1^2 +2AB\cdot AD - 2AB\cdot AA_1 - 2AD\cdot AA_1$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left| \overline {{A B}} \right| ^ {2} + \left| \overline {{A D}} \right| ^ {2} + \left| \overline {{A A _ {1}}} \right| ^ {2} + 2 \left| \overline {{A B}} \right| \left| \overline {{A D}} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - 2 \left| \overline {{A B}} \right| \left| \overline {{A A _ {1}}} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - 2 \left| \overline {{A D}} \right| \left| \overline {{A A _ {1}}} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 4 + 4 + 1 6 + 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} - 2 \times 2 \times 4 \times \frac {1}{2} - 2 \times 2 \times 4 \times \frac {1}{2} = 1 2
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|CA_{1}\right|=2\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \cos B C _ {1}, C A _ {1} = \frac {B G \cdot C A _ {1}}{\left| B C _ {1} \right| \left| C A _ {1} \right|} = \frac {6}{2 \sqrt {7} \times 2 \sqrt {3}} = \frac {\sqrt {2 1}}{1 4},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {所以} \sin B C _ {1}, C A _ {1} = \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} B C _ {1} , C A _ {1}} = \sqrt {1 - \left(\frac {\sqrt {2 1}}{1 4}\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {5 \sqrt {7}}{1 4},
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{5\sqrt{7}}{14}$
