> [!faq]- 《第一章 空间向量与立体几何（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $OE=\frac{1}{3}a+\frac{1}{3}b+\frac{1}{3}c$ (2)证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）∵ D 为 BC 中点，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore O D = \frac {1}{2} (O B + O C)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because A E = A O + O E = \frac {2}{3} A D = \frac {2}{3} \left(A O + O D\right)
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ $OE = \frac{1}{3}OA + \frac{2}{3}OD = \frac{1}{3}OA + \frac{2}{3}\left[\frac{1}{2}(OB + OC)\right] = \frac{1}{3}a + \frac{1}{3}b + \frac{1}{3}c$
> >
> > (2) $\because OA = OB = OC$ 且 $\angle AOB = \angle BOC = \angle AOC = 90^{\circ}$
> >
> > $\therefore\triangle ABC$ 为等边三角形且 $OD \perp BC, AD \perp BC$
> >
> > $$
> > \because O D \cap A D = D, O D, A D \subset \text {平面} O A D
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore BC \perp$ 平面 OAD
> >
> > ∵ $OE \subset_{平面} OAD$
> >
> > $\therefore BC \perp OE$
> >
> > $$
> > \because A E = \frac {2}{3} A D
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore E_{为}VABC$ 重心，
> >
> > 同理可证 $AC \perp OE$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \because B C \cap A C = C, B C, A C \subset \text {平面} A B C
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore OE \perp$ $ABC$ 平面
