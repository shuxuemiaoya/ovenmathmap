> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \theta = \frac {(a + b) \cdot (a - b)}{| a + b | \cdot | a - b |} = \frac {- 3 + 3 + 0}{\sqrt {1 0} \times \sqrt {1 4}} = 0
> > $$
