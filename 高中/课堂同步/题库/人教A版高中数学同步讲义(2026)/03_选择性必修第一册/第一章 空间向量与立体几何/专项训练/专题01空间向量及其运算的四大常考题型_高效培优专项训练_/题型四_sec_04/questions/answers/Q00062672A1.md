> [!faq]- 《专题01空间向量及其运算的四大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a^{2}$ (2) $\pi - \arccos \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (3)垂直
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （ $^{1}$ ）根据数量积的定义直接计算，可得答案；
> >
> > (2) 求得向量 $\overline{AO}$ 的模，求出 $\overline{AO} \cdot \overline{CB}$ ，根据向量的夹角公式求得答案；
> >
> > (3) 计算 $\overset{\square\square}{AO}$ 与 $\overset{\square\square\square}{CD_{1}}$ 的数量积，根据结果，可得答案.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）正方体 $ABCD - A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}D_{1}$ 中， $CD_{1} = \sqrt{2} a, CD_{1}, CD = \frac{\pi}{4}$ ，
> >
> > 故 $CD_{1}\cdot CD=\sqrt{2}a\times a\times\cos\frac{\pi}{4}=a^{2};$
> >
> > (2) 由题意知， $AB \cdot AD = 0, AB \cdot AA_{1} = 0, AA_{1} \cdot AD = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > A O = A D + D O = A D + \frac {1}{2} (A B + A A _ {1})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > | \overrightarrow {A O} | = \sqrt {\left[ A D + \frac {1}{2} (A B + A A _ {1}) \right] ^ {2}} = \frac {\sqrt {6} a}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $AO\cdot CB = -AO\cdot AD = -[AD + \frac{1}{2}(AB + AA_1)]\cdot AD = -a^2$
> >
> > 故 $\cos\overrightarrow{AO},\overrightarrow{CB}=\frac{\overrightarrow{AO}\cdot\overrightarrow{CB}}{\left|\overrightarrow{AO}\right|\cdot\left|\overrightarrow{CB}\right|}=\frac{-a^{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}a\cdot a}=-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
> >
> > 故 $_{AO}^{uuu}$ 与 $_{CB}^{uuu}$ 的夹角的大小为 $\pi-\arccos\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ ;
> >
> > (3) 由题意， $AB \cdot AD = 0, AB \cdot AA_{1} = 0, AA_{1} \cdot AD = 0$
> >
> > $AO \cdot CD_{1} = [AD + \frac{1}{2}(AB + AA_{1})] \cdot (AA_{1} - AB)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} A A _ {1} ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} A B ^ {2} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $AO$ 与 $CD_{1}$ 垂直·
