> [!faq]- 《专题01空间向量及其运算的四大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a^{2}$ (2)垂直
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （ $^{1}$ ）根据数量积的定义直接计算即可；
> >
> > (2) 计算 $\overset{\square\square}{AO}$ 与 $\overset{\square\square\square}{CD_{1}}$ 的数量积，根据结果可得答案.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）正方体 $ABCD - A_1B_1C_1D_1$ 中， $\left|\overline{CD}_1\right| = \sqrt{2} a,\left|\overline{CD}\right| = a,\left\langle \overline{CD}_1,\overline{CD}\right\rangle = \frac{\pi}{4}$
> >
> > 故 $CD_{1}\cdot CD=\sqrt{2}a\times a\times\cos\frac{\pi}{4}=a^{2}$
> >
> > (2) 由题意， $AB \cdot AD = 0, AB \cdot AA_{1} = 0, AA_{1} \cdot AD = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A O} \cdot \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C D _ {1}} = \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {D O}\right) \cdot \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {C C _ {1}} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C D}\right) = \left[ \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D} + \frac {1}{2} \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {D C} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {D D _ {1}}\right) \right] \cdot \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {C C _ {1}} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C D}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left[ \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \\ A D + \frac {1}{2} \left(\square \square \square + \square \square \square_ {1}\right) \end{array} \right] \cdot \left( \begin{array}{l l} \square \square \square & \square \square \square \\ A A _ {1} - A B \end{array} \right) = \frac {1}{2} \square \square \square^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} \square B ^ {2} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $AO$ 与 $CD_{1}$ 垂直·
