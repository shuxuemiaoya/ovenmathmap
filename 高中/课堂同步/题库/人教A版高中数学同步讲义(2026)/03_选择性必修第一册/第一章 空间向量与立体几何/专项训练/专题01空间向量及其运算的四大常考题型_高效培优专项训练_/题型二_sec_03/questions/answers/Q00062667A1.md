> [!faq]- 《专题01空间向量及其运算的四大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\frac{1}{2}a+\frac{3}{8}b+\frac{1}{8}c$ (2) $-\frac{35}{8}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）根据空间向量的线性运算法，结合 DC = 3BD ，即可求解；
> >
> > (2) 由 $BC = OC - OB = -b + c$ ，得到 $OE \cdot BC = \left(\frac{1}{2} a + \frac{3}{8} b + \frac{1}{8} c\right) \cdot (-b + c)$ ，结合向量的数量积的运算公式，准确计算，即可求解。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）解：因为 DC = 3BD，由向量的线性运算法则，
> >
> > 可得： $OE = OA + AE = OA + \frac{1}{2} AD = OA + \frac{1}{2}(AB + BD) = OA + \frac{1}{2}(AB + \frac{1}{4} BC)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \square O A + \frac {1}{2} (\square O B - \square O A) + \frac {1}{2} (\square O C - \square O B) = \frac {1}{2} \square O A + \frac {3}{8} \square O B + \frac {1}{8} \square O C = \frac {1}{2} a + \frac {3}{8} b + \frac {1}{8} c.
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 解：由 $BC = OC - OB = -b + c$
> >
> > 所以 $OE \cdot BC = \left(\frac{1}{2}a + \frac{3}{8}b + \frac{1}{8}c\right) \cdot (-b + c)$ $= -\frac{1}{2}a \cdot b + \frac{1}{2}a \cdot c - \frac{3}{8}b^{2} + \frac{3}{8}b \cdot c - \frac{1}{8}b \cdot c + \frac{1}{8}c^{2}$ $= -\frac{1}{2} \times (4 \times 4 \times \frac{1}{2}) + \frac{1}{2} \times (4 \times 3 \times \frac{1}{2}) - \frac{3}{8} \times 16 + \frac{3}{8} \times (4 \times 3 \times \frac{1}{2}) - \frac{1}{8} \times (4 \times 3 \times \frac{1}{2}) + \frac{1}{8} \times 9$ $= -\frac{35}{8}$ .
