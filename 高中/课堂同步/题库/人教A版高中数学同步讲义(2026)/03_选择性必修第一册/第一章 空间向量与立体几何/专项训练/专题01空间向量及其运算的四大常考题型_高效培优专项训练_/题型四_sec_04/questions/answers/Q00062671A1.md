> [!faq]- 《专题01空间向量及其运算的四大常考题型_高效培优专项训练_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a^{2}$ (2) $-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (3)垂直
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （ $^{1}$ ）利用数量积的公式可得；
> >
> > (2) 先用 $AB, AD, AA_1$ 表示 $AO$ ，利用数量积运算律可得 $AO \cdot CB$ 、 $\left|AO\right|$ 进而利用公式可得 $AO$ 与 $CB$ 的夹角的余弦值.
> >
> > (3) 利用数量积运算律得 $AO \cdot CD_{1} = 0$ ，进而可得 AO 与 $CD_{1}$ 是否垂直。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）正方体 $ABCD - A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}D_{1}$ 中， $CD_{1} = \sqrt{2}a, \left\langle CD_{1}, CD \right\rangle = \frac{\pi}{4}$
> >
> > 故 $CD_{1}\cdot CD=\sqrt{2}a\times a\times\cos\frac{\pi}{4}=a^{2}$
> >
> > (2) 由题意知， $AB \cdot AD = 0, AB \cdot AA_{1} = 0, AA_{1} \cdot AD = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > A O = A D + D O = A D + \frac {1}{2} \left(A B + A A _ {1}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \overrightarrow {A O} \right| = \sqrt {\left[ \overrightarrow {A D} + \frac {1}{2} \left(\overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {A A _ {1}}\right) \right] ^ {2}} = \sqrt {\overrightarrow {A D} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {A A _ {1}} ^ {2}} = \frac {\sqrt {6} a}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \\ A O \cdot C B = - A O \cdot A D = - \left[ \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \\ A D + \frac {1}{2} \left(A B + A A _ {1}\right) \end{array} \right] \cdot A D = - a ^ {2} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $\cos\left\langle\overrightarrow{AO},\overrightarrow{CB}\right\rangle=\frac{\overrightarrow{AO}\cdot\overrightarrow{CB}}{\left|AO\right|\left|CB\right|}=\frac{-a^{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}a\cdot a}=-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
> >
> > (3) 由题意， $AB \cdot AD = 0, AB \cdot AA_{1} = 0, AA_{1} \cdot AD = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > A O \cdot C D _ {1} = \left[ A D + \frac {1}{2} \left(A B + A A _ {1}\right) \right] \cdot \left(A A _ {1} - A B\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} A A _ {1} ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} A B ^ {2} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $AO$ 与 $CD_{1}$ 垂直·
