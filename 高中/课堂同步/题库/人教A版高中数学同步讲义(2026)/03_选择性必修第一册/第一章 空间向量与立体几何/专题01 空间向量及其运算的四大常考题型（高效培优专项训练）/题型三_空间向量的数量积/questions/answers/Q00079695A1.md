> [!faq]- 《专题01 空间向量及其运算的四大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)2 (2) $\frac{5}{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）以 $AB, AD, AA'$ 为基底表示出 $AO$ 可求出 $x, y, z$ 的值，即可求得结果；
> >
> > (2) 根据向量数量积的运算律求得 AO 的长，再由向量夹角的计算公式可得结果·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为点 $O$ 为 $BC'$ 的中点
> >
> > 所以 $\overset{\square\square}{BO} = \frac{1}{2}\overset{\square\square}{BC'} = \frac{1}{2}\left(\overset{\square\square}{BB'} + \overset{\square\square}{BC}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\overset{\square\square}{AA'} + \overset{\square\square}{AD}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\overset{\square\square}{AD} + \frac{1}{2}\overset{\square\square}{AA'}$
> >
> > 所以 $AO = AB + BO = AB + \frac{1}{2} AD + \frac{1}{2} AA'$
> >
> > 所以 $x = 1$ ， $y = z = \frac{1}{2}$ 所以 $x + y + z = 1 + \frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{2} = 2$
> >
> > (2) 因为 $\left|\overrightarrow{AO}\right|^{2}=\left(\overrightarrow{AB}+\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AD}+\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AA'}\right)^{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \square \square^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \square \square^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \square \square^ {2} + \square \square \cdot \square A D + \square \square \cdot \square A ^ {\prime} + \frac {1}{2} \square \square \cdot \square A ^ {\prime}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 + \frac {1}{4} + \frac {1}{4} + 0 + 1 \times 1 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \frac {1}{2} \times 1 \times 1 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {9}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|AO\right|=\frac{3}{2}$ ;
> >
> > 因为 $AO \cdot DC = AO \cdot AB = AB^2 + \frac{1}{2} AB \cdot AD + \frac{1}{2} AB \cdot AA' = 1 + 0 + \frac{1}{2} \times 1 \times 1 \times \cos 60^\circ = \frac{5}{4}$
> >
> > 又 $\left|DC\right|=\left|AB\right|=1$ 。
> >
> > 所以 $\cos\left\langle\overrightarrow{AO},\overrightarrow{DC}\right\rangle=\frac{\overrightarrow{AO}\cdot\overrightarrow{DC}}{\left|\overrightarrow{AO}\right|\cdot\left|\overrightarrow{DC}\right|}=\frac{\frac{5}{4}}{\frac{3}{2}\times1}=\frac{5}{6}$ ;
> >
> > 所以直线 AO 与 DC 所成的角的余弦值为 $\frac{5}{6}$ .
