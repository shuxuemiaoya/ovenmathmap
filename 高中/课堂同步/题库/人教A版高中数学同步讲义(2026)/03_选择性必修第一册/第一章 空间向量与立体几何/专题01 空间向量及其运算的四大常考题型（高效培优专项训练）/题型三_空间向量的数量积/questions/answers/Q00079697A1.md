> [!faq]- 《专题01 空间向量及其运算的四大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)2 (2) $\sqrt{10}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）由向量的线性运算可得 $\overline{BD}_{1}=\overline{AA}_{1}+\overline{AD}-\overline{AB}$ ，由向量的数量积的运算律可得 $\overline{BD}_{1}\cdot\overline{AC}=2$
> >
> > (2) 由 $AC_{1} = AB + AD + AA_{1}$ 两边平方后可得 $\left|AC_{1}\right| = \sqrt{10}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）在平行六面体 $ABCD - A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}D_{1}$ 中， $BD_{1} = AD_{1} - AB = AA_{1} + AD - AB$
> >
> > 因为 $\overline{A}C=\overline{A}B+\overline{A}D,\quad AB=AD=1,\quad AA_{1}=2,\quad\angle BAD=\frac{\pi}{2},\quad\angle BAA_{1}=\angle DAA_{1}=\frac{\pi}{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{\mathrm{uu}}{AB} \cdot \frac{\mathrm{uu}}{AD} = 0$ ， $\frac{\mathrm{uu}}{AA_1} \cdot \frac{\mathrm{uu}}{AB} = \left|\frac{\mathrm{uu}}{AA_1}\right| \cdot \left|\frac{\mathrm{uu}}{AB}\right| \cos \left\langle \frac{\mathrm{uu}}{AA_1}, \frac{\mathrm{uu}}{AB}\right\rangle = 2 \times 1 \times \cos \frac{\pi}{3} = 1$
> >
> > $AA_{1} \cdot AD = \left| AA_{1} \right| \cdot \left| AD \right| \left\langle \cos AA_{1}, AD \right\rangle = 2 \times 1 \times \cos \frac{\pi}{3} = 1$
> >
> > 则 $BD_{1} \cdot AC = \left(AA_{1} + AD - AB\right) \cdot \left(AB + AD\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = A A _ {1} \cdot A B + A A _ {1} \cdot A D + A D \cdot A B + A D - A B - A B \cdot A D = 1 + 1 + 0 + 1 - 1 - 0 = 2
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 因为 $AC_{1} = AB + BC + CC_{1} = AB + AD + AA_{1}$
> >
> > 所以 $AC_{1}^{2}=\left(AB+AD+AA_{1}\right)^{2}=AB^{2}+AD^{2}+AA_{1}^{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > + 2 A B \cdot A D + 2 A B \cdot A A _ {1} + 2 A D \cdot A A _ {1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 + 1 + 4 + 2 \times 0 + 2 \times 1 + 2 \times 1 = 1 0
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $\left|A C_{1}\right| = \sqrt{10}$
