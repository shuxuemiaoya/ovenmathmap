> [!faq]- 《专题1_1_空间向量及其运算_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由 A, E, F, M 四点共面， $B_{1}A = xB_{1}E + yB_{1}F + (1 - x - y)B_{1}M$ ，由 $B_{1}M = \lambda MC_{1}$ ，求出 $\lambda$ 的值.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $A_{1}F=\frac{1}{3}A_{1}C_{1}$ ，E为 $BB_{1}$ 中点， $B_{1}M=\lambda MC_{1}$
> >
> > 又因为 $A,E,F,M$ 四点共面，设 $\overline{B_1A} = x\overline{B_1E} +y\overline{B_1F} +(1 - x - y)\overline{B_1M}$
> >
> > $$
> > \overline {{B _ {1} A _ {1}}} + \overline {{A _ {1} A}} = \frac {1}{2} x \overline {{B _ {1} B}} + y \left(\overline {{B _ {1} A _ {1}}} + \frac {1}{3} \overline {{A _ {1} C _ {1}}}\right) + (1 - x - y) \frac {\lambda}{\lambda + 1} \overline {{B _ {1} C _ {1}}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2} x B _ {1} B + y \left[ \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \\ B _ {1} A _ {1} \end{array} + \frac {1}{3} \left( \begin{array}{l l} \square \square \square & \square \square \square \\ B _ {1} C _ {1} - B _ {1} A _ {1} \end{array} \right) \right] + (1 - x - y) \frac {\lambda}{\lambda + 1} \square \square \square
> > $$
> >
> > $= \frac{1}{2} x B_{1}B + y\left(\frac{2}{3} B_{1}A_{1} + \frac{1}{3} B_{1}C_{1}\right) + (1 - x - y)\frac{\lambda}{\lambda + 1} B_{1}C_{1}$ 所以 $\begin{cases} 1 = \frac{2}{3}y \\ 1 = \frac{1}{2}x \\ \frac{1}{3}y + (1 - x - y)\frac{\lambda}{\lambda + 1} = 0 \end{cases}$ ，所以 $\begin{cases} x = 2 \\ y = \frac{3}{2} \cdot \text{故选：} A \\ \lambda = \frac{1}{4} \end{cases}$ .
