> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图，由已知， $\left|AB\right|=\left|AD\right|=\left|AA_{1}\right|=3$ ， $\angle DAB=\angle A_{1}AB=\angle A_{1}AD=60^{\circ}$

> > $\because AC_{1} = AB + AD + AA_{1}, \therefore \left|AC_{1}\right|^{2} = AC_{1}^{2} = \left(AB + AD + AA_{1}\right)^{2} = AB^{2} + AD^{2} + AA_{1}^{2} + 2AB \cdot AD + 2AB \cdot AA_{1} + 2AD \cdot AA_{1}$ $= \left|\overline{AB}\right|^2 + \left|\overline{AD}\right|^2 + \left|\overline{AA_1}\right|^2 + 2\left|\overline{AB}\right|\left|\overline{AD}\right|\cos 60^\circ + 2\left|\overline{AB}\right|\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|\cos 60^\circ + 2\left|\overline{AD}\right|\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|\cos 60^\circ$ $= 9 + 9 + 9 + 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times \frac{1}{2} + 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times \frac{1}{2} + 2 \times 3 \times 3 \times \frac{1}{2} = 54, \therefore \left|\overline{AC_1}\right| = 3\sqrt{6}$ ，即 $AC_{1} = 3\sqrt{6}$ ，
> >
