> [!faq]- 《专题1_2_空间向量基本定理_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 取空间向量的一个基底 $\{AB, AD, AA_{1}\}$ ，利用空间向量法求出异面直线的夹角余弦值。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在平行六面体 $ABCD - A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}D_{1}$ 中， $AC_{1} = AB + BC + CC_{1} = AB + AD + AA_{1}$ ， $BB_{1} = AA_{1}$ ，记 $AB = AD = AA_{1} = a$ ， $\angle A_{1}AB = \angle A_{1}AD = \angle BAD = 60^{\circ}$ 则 $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{AD} \cdot \overrightarrow{AA}_{1} = \overrightarrow{AA}_{1} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = a \times a \cos 60^{\circ} = \frac{a^{2}}{2}$ $\sqrt{a^{2} + a^{2} + a^{2} + 2 \times \frac{a^{2}}{2} + 2 \times \frac{a^{2}}{2} + 2 \times \frac{a^{2}}{2}} = \sqrt{6} a, |BB_{1}| = a,$ $\overrightarrow{AC_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{BB_{1}} = (\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AD} + AA_{1}) \cdot AA_{1} = \overrightarrow{AB} \cdot AA_{1} + \overrightarrow{AD} \cdot AA_{1} + AA_{1}^{2} = \frac{a^{2}}{2} + \frac{a^{2}}{2} + a^{2} = 2a^{2},$ 因此 $\cos\left\langle AC_{1}, BB_{1}\right\rangle=\frac{AC_{1}^{2} \cdot BB_{1}}{\left|AC_{1}\right|\left|BB_{1}\right|}=\frac{2a^{2}}{\sqrt{6}a \cdot a}=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3},$
> >
> > 所以直线 $AC_{1}$ 与 $BB_{1}$ 所成角的余弦值为 $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
> >
> > 故选 **C**
