> [!faq]- 《专题1_2_空间向量基本定理_高效培优讲义_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由空间向量线性运算，可得 $AC_{1} = AB + AD + AA_{1}$ ，利用数量积运算性质即可得出。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $AC_{1}=AC+CC_{1}=AB+AD+AA_{1}$
> >
> > 又 $AB = 1$ ， $AD = 1$ ， $AA_{1} = 2$ ， $\angle BAD = \angle BAA_{1} = \angle DAA_{1} = 60^{\circ}$ $\therefore AC^{2} = AB^{2} + AD^{2} + AA_{1}^{2} + 2\left(AB\cdot AD + AB\cdot AA_{1} + AD\cdot AA_{1}\right)$ $= 1^{2} + 1^{2} + 2^{2} + 2\times \left(1\times 1\times \frac{1}{2} +1\times 2\times \frac{1}{2} +1\times 2\times \frac{1}{2}\right) = 11$ $\therefore \left|AC_1\right| = \sqrt{11}$
> >
> > 故选 **A**
