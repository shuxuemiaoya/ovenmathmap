> [!faq]- 《专题03 空间向量的应用四大常考题型（高效培优专项训练）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $\sqrt{6}$ (2)证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）由题意可得 $\overline{AC_{1}}=\overline{AB}+\overline{AD}+\overline{AA_{1}}$ ，再平方即可得到答案；
> >
> > (2) 根据 $A_{1}C \square BD = 0$ ， $A_{1}C \square BB_{1} = 0$ ，可得 $A_{1}C \perp BD$ ， $A_{1}C \perp BB_{1}$ ，再利用线面垂直的判定即可证明.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $AC_{1}=AB+BC+CC_{1}=AB+AD+AA_{1}$
> >
> > 可得 $AC_{1}^{2} = AB^{2} + AD^{2} + AA_{1}^{2} + 2AB\square AD + 2AB\square AA_{1} + 2AD\square AA_{1}$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 ^ {2} + 1 ^ {2} + 1 ^ {2} + 2 \times 1 \times 1 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + 2 \times 1 \times 1 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + 2 \times 1 \times 1 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} = 6
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|A C_{1}\right|=\sqrt{6}$
> >
> > (2) $A_{1}C = AC - AA_{1} = AB + AD - AA_{1}$ , $BD = AD - AB$ , $BB_{1} = AA_{1}$ 所以 $A_{1}C\square BD=\left(AB+AD-AA_{1}\right)\square\left(AD-AB\right)$ $=AB\square AD+AD^{2}-AA_{1}\square AD-AB^{2}-AD\square AB+AA_{1}\square AB$ $=1\times1\times\cos60^{\circ}+1^{2}-1\times1\times\cos60^{\circ}-1^{2}-1\times1\times\cos60^{\circ}+1\times1\times\cos60^{\circ}=0$ 所以 $A_{1}C\perp BD$ ，所以 $A_{1}C\perp BD$ $A_{1}C\square BB_{1}=\left(AB+AD-AA_{1}\right)\square AA_{1}=AB\square AA_{1}+AD\square AA_{1}-AA_{1}^{2}$ $=1\times1\times\cos60^{\circ}+1\times1\times\cos60^{\circ}-1^{2}=0$ 所以 $A_{1}C\perp BB_{1}$ ，所以 $A_{1}C\perp BB_{1}$ ，又 $BB_{1}\cap BD=B$ ， $BB_{1},BD\subset$ 平面 $BDD_{1}B_{1}$ ，
> > 所以 $A_{1}C\perp$ 平面 $BDD_{1}B_{1}$ .
