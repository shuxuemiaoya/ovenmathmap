> [!faq]- 《专题1.1 空间向量及其运算（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left|a+b-2c\right|=\sqrt{(a+b-2c)^{2}}=\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}+4c^{2}+2a\cdot b-4a\cdot c-4b\cdot c}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {\left| a \right| ^ {2} + \left| b \right| ^ {2} + 4 | c | ^ {2} + 2 | a | \cdot | b | \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - 4 | a | \cdot | c | \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - 4 | b | \cdot | c | \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {1 + 1 + 4 + 2 \times 1 \times 1 \times \frac {1}{2} - 4 \times 1 \times 1 \times \frac {1}{2} - 4 \times 1 \times 1 \times \frac {1}{2}} = \sqrt {3}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\sqrt{3}$
