> [!faq]- 《专题1.1 空间向量及其运算（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a^{2}(2)$ 垂直
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）正方体 $ABCD - A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}D_{1}$ 中， $\left|\overline{CD}_{1}\right| = \sqrt{2} a, \left|\overline{CD}\right| = a, \left\langle \overline{CD}_{1}, \overline{CD}\right\rangle = \frac{\pi}{4}$ ，故
> >
> > $CD_{1} \cdot CD = \sqrt{2}a \times a \times \cos\frac{\pi}{4} = a^{2}$
> >
> > (2) 由题意， $AB \cdot AD = 0, AB \cdot AA_{1} = 0, AA_{1} \cdot AD = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A O} \cdot \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C D _ {1}} = \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {D O}\right) \cdot \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {C C _ {1}} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C D}\right) = \left[ \stackrel {\square \square \square} {A D} + \frac {1}{2} \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {D C} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {D D _ {1}}\right) \right] \cdot \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {C C _ {1}} + \stackrel {\square \square \square} {C D}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left[ \begin{array}{l} \square \square \square \\ A D + \frac {1}{2} \left(\square \square \square A B + \square \square \square A A _ {1}\right) \end{array} \right] \cdot \left( \begin{array}{l l} \square \square \square & \square \square \square \\ A A _ {1} - A B \end{array} \right) = \frac {1}{2} \square \square \square^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} A B ^ {2} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $\overset{\text{UUU}}{AO}$ 与 $\overset{\text{UUUU}}{CD_{1}}$ 垂直·
