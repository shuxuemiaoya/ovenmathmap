> [!faq]- 《专题1.1 空间向量及其运算（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a^{2}(2)-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ (3)垂直
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）正方体 $ABCD - A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}D_{1}$ 中， $CD_{1} = \sqrt{2} a, \left\langle CD_{1}, CD \right\rangle = \frac{\pi}{4}$ ，故 $CD_{1} \cdot CD = \sqrt{2} a \times a \times \cos \frac{\pi}{4} = a^{2}$ .
> >
> > (2) 由题意知， $AB \cdot AD = 0, AB \cdot AA_{1} = 0, AA_{1} \cdot AD = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > A O = A D + D O = A D + \frac {1}{2} \left(A B + A A _ {1}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \overrightarrow {A O} \right| = \sqrt {\left[ \overrightarrow {A D} + \frac {1}{2} \left(\overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {A A _ {1}}\right) \right] ^ {2}} = \sqrt {\overrightarrow {A D} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {A A _ {1}} ^ {2}} = \frac {\sqrt {6} a}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $AO\cdot CB = -AO\cdot AD = -\left[\frac{AO}{AD} +\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{AB}{AB} +\frac{AA_{1}}{A}\right)\right]\cdot AD = -a^{2}$ ，故 $\cos \left\langle AO,CB\right\rangle = \frac{\frac{AO\cdot CB}{|AO||CB|}}{\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}a\cdot a} = \frac{-a^2}{3}$
> >
> > (3) 由题意， $AB \cdot AD = 0, AB \cdot AA_{1} = 0, AA_{1} \cdot AD = 0$
> >
> > $= \frac{1}{2} AA_{1}^{2} - \frac{1}{2} AB^{2} = 0$ 故 $AO$ 与 $CD_{1}$ 垂直.
