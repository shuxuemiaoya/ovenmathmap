> [!faq]- 《专题1.1 空间向量及其运算（高效培优讲义）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $$ \begin{array}{c c} \text {uuu} & \\ A D _ {1} & \end{array} \qquad \begin{array}{c c} \text {uuuu} \\ B D _ {1} \end{array} $$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由题意，根据空间向量的线性运算分别计算即可求解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $A_{1}F_{1}-EF-BA+FF_{1}+CD+F_{1}A_{1}=AF+FE+AB+BB_{1}+CD+DC=AE+AB_{1}+0=AE+ED_{1}=AD_{1}.$ (2) $DE + E_{1}F_{1} + FD + BB_{1} + A_{1}E_{1} = DE + EF + FD + BB_{1} + B_{1}D_{1} = DF + FD + BD_{1} = 0 + BD_{1} = BD_{1}$
> >
> > 故答案为： $AD_{1};BD_{1}$
