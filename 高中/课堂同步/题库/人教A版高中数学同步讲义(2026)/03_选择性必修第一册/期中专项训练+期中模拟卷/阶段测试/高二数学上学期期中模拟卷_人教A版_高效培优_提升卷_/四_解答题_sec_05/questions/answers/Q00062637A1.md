> [!faq]- 《高二数学上学期期中模拟卷_人教A版_高效培优_提升卷_》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $OE=\frac{1}{3}a+\frac{1}{3}b+\frac{1}{3}c$ (2)证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）∵ D 为 BC 中点，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore O D = \frac {1}{2} (O B + O C)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because A E = A O + O E = \frac {2}{3} A D = \frac {2}{3} \left(A O + O D\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore OE = \frac{1}{3} OA + \frac{2}{3} OD = \frac{1}{3} OA + \frac{2}{3}\left[\frac{1}{2}(OB + OC)\right] = \frac{1}{3} a + \frac{1}{3} b + \frac{1}{3} c\quad ;$
> >
> > (2) $\because OA = OB = OC$ 且 $\angle AOB = \angle BOC = \angle AOC = 90^{\circ}$
> >
> > $\therefore \triangle ABC$ $OD \perp BC, AD \perp BC$ 为等边三角形且
> >
> > $$
> > \because O D \cap A D = D, O D, A D \subset \text {平面} O A D,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore BC \perp_{\text{平面}} OAD$
> >
> > ∵ $OE \subset_{平面} OAD$
> >
> > $\therefore BC \perp OE$
> >
> > ∵ AE = $\frac{2}{3}$ AD
> >
> > $\therefore E_{为}VABC_{重心}$
> >
> > 同理可证 $AC \perp OE$ ，
> >
> > ∵ $BC \cap AC = C, BC, AC \subset ABC$ 平面
> >
> > $\therefore OE \perp ABC$ 平面
