> [!faq]- 《专题10 椭圆标准方程及其性质全方位总结（九大题型）（高效培优期中专项训练）数学人教A版2019高二选择性必修第一册》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用焦点三角形的面积公式及椭圆的定义可得 $\angle F_{1}PF_{2} = \frac{\pi}{3}$ ，进一步得 $\triangle F_{1}PQ$ 为等边三角形，且 $PQ \perp x$ 轴，从而可得解·
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由椭圆的定义，得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|+\left|PF_{2}\right|=2a$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \angle F _ {1} P F _ {2} = \frac {\left| P F _ {1} \right| ^ {2} + \left| P F _ {2} \right| ^ {2} - 4 c ^ {2}}{2 \left| P F _ {1} \right| \left| P F _ {2} \right|} = \frac {\left(\left| P F _ {1} \right| + \left| P F _ {2} \right|\right) ^ {2} - 2 \left| P F _ {1} \right| \left| P F _ {2} \right| - 4 c ^ {2}}{2 \left| P F _ {1} \right| \left| P F _ {2} \right|}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {4 a ^ {2} - 4 c ^ {2} - 2 \left| P F _ {1} \right| \left| P F _ {2} \right|}{2 \left| P F _ {1} \right| \left| P F _ {2} \right|} = \frac {4 b ^ {2} - 2 \left| P F _ {1} \right| \left| P F _ {2} \right|}{2 \left| P F _ {1} \right| \left| P F _ {2} \right|}
> > $$
> >
> > 整理得： $2b^{2}=|PF_{1}||PF_{2}|(\cos\angle F_{1}PF_{2}+1)$ ，又 $S_{\triangle PF_{1}F_{2}}=\frac{1}{2}|PF_{1}||PF_{2}|\sin\angle F_{1}PF_{2}$ ， $S_{\triangle PF_{1}F_{2}}=\frac{b^{2}\sin\angle F_{1}PF_{2}}{\cos\angle F_{1}PF_{2}+1}=\frac{2b^{2}\sin\frac{\angle F_{1}PF_{2}}{2}\cos\frac{\angle F_{1}PF_{2}}{2}}{2\cos^{2}\frac{\angle F_{1}PF_{2}}{2}}=b^{2}\tan\frac{\angle F_{1}PF_{2}}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}b^{2}$
> >
> > 因此 $\tan\frac{\angle F_{1}PF_{2}}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ， $\angle F_{1}PF_{2}=\frac{\pi}{3}$ ，又 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=\left|PQ\right|$ ，则 $\triangle F_{1}PQ$ 为等边三角形，
> > 由椭圆对称性得 $PQ \perp x$ 轴，所以 $\frac{|PQ|}{|F_{1}F_{2}|} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
