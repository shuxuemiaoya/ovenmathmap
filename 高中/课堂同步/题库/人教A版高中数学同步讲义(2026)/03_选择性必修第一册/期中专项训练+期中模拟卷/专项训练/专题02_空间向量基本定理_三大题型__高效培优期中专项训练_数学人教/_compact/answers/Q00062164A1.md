> [!faq]- 《专题02_空间向量基本定理_三大题型__高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 设 $AE = a$ ，用基底表示出 $\overline{ME},\overline{DF}$ ，再由数量积的运算律化简可求出 $a$ ，即可得出答案
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > A E \perp
> > $$
> > $$
> > A B C D, A B, A D \subset_ {\text {底面}} A B C D
> > $$
> > 所以 $AD\cdot AB = 0$ ， $AD\cdot AE = 0$ ， $AB\cdot AE = 0$ ，设 $AE = a$
> > 因为 $ME = MA + AE = \frac{1}{2} \left( DA - DC \right) + AE = -\frac{1}{2} AD - \frac{1}{2} AB + AE$
> > $$
> > D F = D C + C F = A B + A E
> > $$
> > $$
> > \stackrel {\square \square \square} {M E} \cdot \stackrel {\square \square \square} {D F} = \left(- \frac {1}{2} A D - \frac {1}{2} A B + A E\right) \cdot \left(\stackrel {\square \square \square} {A B} + A E\right)
> > $$
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{2} A D \cdot A B - \frac {1}{2} A D \cdot A E - \frac {1}{2} A B ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} A B \cdot A E + A B \cdot A E + A E ^ {2}
> > $$
> > $= -\frac{1}{2} AB^{2} + AE^{2} = -\frac{1}{2} + a^{2} = \frac{7}{2}$ ，解得： $a = 2$
> > 故 $AE = 2\cdot$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
