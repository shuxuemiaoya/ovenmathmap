> [!faq]- 《专题02_空间向量基本定理_三大题型__高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $OQ = -\frac{2}{3} \vec{a} + \frac{1}{6} \vec{b} + \frac{1}{6} \vec{c}$ (2) $\left|OQ\right|_{\min}=\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）根据空间向量的线性运算结合图形计算即可；
> >
> > (2) 根据 $\left|OQ\right|^{2}=\left(-\frac{2}{3}a+\frac{1}{6}b+\frac{1}{6}c\right)^{2}$ ，结合数量积的运算律计算即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $OQ = OM + MQ = \frac{1}{2} OA + \frac{1}{3} MN = -\frac{1}{2} AO + \frac{1}{3}\left(MA + AB + BN\right)$ $= -\frac{1}{2} AO + \frac{1}{3}\left[-\frac{1}{2} AO + AB + \frac{1}{2}\left(AC - AB\right)\right]$ $= -\frac{1}{2} a + \frac{1}{3}\left[-\frac{1}{2} a + b + \frac{1}{2}\left(c - b\right)\right]$ $= -\frac{2}{3} a + \frac{1}{6} b + \frac{1}{6} c$
> >
> > $$
> > | \overrightarrow {O Q} | ^ {2} = \left(- \frac {2}{3} a + \frac {1}{6} b + \frac {1}{6} c\right) ^ {2} = \frac {4}{9} a ^ {2} + \frac {1}{3 6} b ^ {2} + \frac {1}{3 6} c ^ {2} - \frac {2}{9} a \cdot b - \frac {2}{9} a \cdot c + \frac {1}{1 8} b \cdot c
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {4}{9} + \frac {1}{3 6} + \frac {1}{3 6} \lambda^ {2} - \frac {2}{9} \times 1 \times 1 \times \frac {1}{2} - \frac {2}{9} \times 1 \times \lambda \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{3 6} \lambda^ {2} - \frac {1}{9} \lambda + \frac {1 3}{3 6}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{3 6} (\lambda - 2) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 故当 $\lambda=2$ 时， $\left|\stackrel{\square\square}{O}Q\right|^{2}$ 取得最小值 $\frac{1}{4}$ ，所以 $\left|\stackrel{\square\square}{O}Q\right|_{\min}=\frac{1}{2}$
