> [!faq]- 《专题02_空间向量基本定理_三大题型__高效培优期中专项训练_数学人教》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ACD
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 以 $\left\{AB, AD, AA_{1}\right\}$ 为一组基底，将 $BD_{1}$ 用基底表示，得 $\left|BD_{1}\right|^{2} = \left(BD_{1}\right)^{2} = \left(-AB + AD + AA_{1}\right)^{2}$ ，利用数量积的运算即可求解，进而判断 $A$ ，先求 $AC \cdot BD_{1}$ ，利用向量的夹角公式即可判断 $B$ ，计算 $A_{1}C \cdot B_{1}D_{1}$ 和 $AA_{1} \cdot BD$ 即可判断 $CD$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意有： $BD_{1}=-AB+AD+AA_{1}$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \left| \overline {{B D}} _ {1} \right| ^ {2} = \left(\overline {{B D}} _ {1}\right) ^ {2} = \left(- A B + A D + A A _ {1}\right) ^ {2} = \overline {{A B}} ^ {2} + \overline {{A D}} ^ {2} + \overline {{A A}} _ {1} ^ {2} - 2 A B \cdot A D - 2 A B \cdot A A _ {1} + 2 A D \cdot A A _ {1}
> > $$
> >
> > $= \left|\overline{AB}\right|^2 +\left|\overline{AD}\right|^2 +\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|^2 -2\left|\overline{AB}\right|\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|\cos 60^\circ +2\left|\overline{AD}\right|\left|\overline{AA_1}\right|\cos 60^\circ = 4\times 3 = 12$ ，所以 $\left|BD_1\right| = 2\sqrt{3}$ ，故A正确； $\overline{AC} = \overline{AB} +\overline{AD}$ ，所以 $\left|\overline{AC}\right| = 2\sqrt{2}$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{c} \text {UUU} \\ A C \cdot B D _ {1} = \left( \begin{array}{c c} \text {UUU} & \text {UUU} \\ A B + A C \end{array} \right) \cdot \left(- A B + A D + A A _ {1}\right) = - A B ^ {2} + A B \cdot A D + A B \cdot A A _ {1} - A B \cdot A D \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > + A D ^ {2} + A D \cdot A A _ {1} = - \left| A B \right| ^ {2} + \left| A B \right| \left| A A _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \left| A D \right| ^ {2} + \left| A D \right| \left| A A _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - 4 + 2 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} + 4 + 2 \times 2 \times \frac {1}{2} = 4
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos AC, BD_{1}=\frac{AC\cdot BD}{\left|AC\right|\left|BD_{1}\right|}=\frac{4}{2\sqrt{2}\times2\sqrt{3}}=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{6}$ ，故 B 错误；
> >
> > 由 $A_{1}C = AB + AD - AA_{1}$ ， $B_{1}D_{1} = BD = AD - AB$
> >
> > 所以 $A_{1}C \cdot B_{1}D_{1} = \left(AB + AD - AA_{1}\right) \cdot \left(-AB + AD\right)$
> >
> > $$
> > = - A B ^ {2} + A B \cdot A D + A B \cdot A A _ {1} + A B \cdot A D + A D ^ {2} - A D \cdot A A _ {1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - A B ^ {2} + A B \cdot A A _ {1} + A D ^ {2} - A D \cdot A A _ {1} = - \left| A B \right| ^ {2} + \left| A B \right| \left| A A _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} + \left| A D \right| ^ {2} - \left| A D \right| \left| A A _ {1} \right| \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $= -4 + 2 \times 2 \times \frac{1}{2} + 4 - 2 \times 2 \times \frac{1}{2} = 0$ ，所以 $A_{1}C \perp B_{1}D_{1}$ ，故 $C$ 正确；
> >
> > 由 $AA_{1}\cdot BD=AA_{1}\cdot\left(AD-AB\right)=AA_{1}\cdot AD-AA_{1}\cdot AB=\left|AD\right|\left|AA_{1}\right|\cos60^{\circ}-\left|AB\right|\left|AA_{1}\right|\cos60^{\circ}=2\times2\times\frac{1}{2}-2\times2\times\frac{1}{2}=0$ 所以 $AA_{1} \perp BD$ ，故 D 正确；
> >
> > 故选 **ACD**.
