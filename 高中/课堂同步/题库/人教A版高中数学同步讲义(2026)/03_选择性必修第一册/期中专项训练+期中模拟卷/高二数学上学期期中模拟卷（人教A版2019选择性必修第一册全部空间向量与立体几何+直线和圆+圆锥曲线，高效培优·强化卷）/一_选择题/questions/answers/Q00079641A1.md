> [!faq]- 《高二数学上学期期中模拟卷（人教A版2019选择性必修第一册全部空间向量与立体几何+直线和圆+圆锥曲线，高效培优·强化卷）》官方解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A. $\left(1,-\frac{1}{4}\right)$ B. $\left(-1,\frac{1}{4}\right)$ C. $\left(1,-\frac{1}{10}\right)$ D. $\left(-3,\frac{3}{10}\right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2}),M(x_{0},y_{0})$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {x ^ {2}}{5} + y ^ {2} = 1 \\ y = 2 x + m \end{array} \Rightarrow 2 1 x ^ {2} + 2 0 m x + 5 m ^ {2} - 5 = 0 \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x _ {1} + x _ {2} = - \frac {2 0 m}{2 1}, x _ {1} x _ {2} = \frac {5 m ^ {2} - 5}{2 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \Delta = 4 0 0 m ^ {2} - 8 4 (5 m ^ {2} - 5) = 4 2 0 - 2 0 m ^ {2} > 0, \Rightarrow m ^ {2} <   2 1 \text {, 即} - \sqrt {2 1} <   m <   \sqrt {2 1}
> > $$
> >
> > 又为线段 $AB$ 的中点，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x_0 = -\frac{10m}{21}\\ y_0 = \frac{m}{21} \end{array} \right.$ ，则的轨迹方程为 $y = -\frac{1}{10} x$ 所以 $\left(1, - \frac{1}{10}\right),\left(-3,\frac{3}{10}\right)$ 都在 $y = -\frac{1}{10} x$ 上，
> >
> > 又 $-\sqrt{21} < m < \sqrt{21}$ ，所以 $-\frac{10\sqrt{21}}{21} < x_{0} < \frac{10\sqrt{21}}{21}$ ，因为 $-3 < -\frac{10\sqrt{21}}{21}$ ，所以 D 错误.
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
