A. $P_{2}=\frac{4}{9}$ B. $P_{n}=\frac{2}{3}P_{n-1}+\frac{1}{3}P_{n-2}(n\geq3)$ C. $\left\{P_{n}-\frac{3}{4}\right\}$ 是等比数列
D. $P_{100}=\frac{1}{4}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{100}+\frac{3}{4}$
