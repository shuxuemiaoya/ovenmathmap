> > [!note]- **【分析】**

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【详解】根据题意得 $P(X=2)=C_{4}^{2}p^{2}(1-p)^{2}=\frac{8}{27}$ ,
> > 即 $p^2 (1 - p)^2 = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2\times \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2,$
> > 解得 $p = \frac{1}{3}$ 或 $p = \frac{2}{3}$ （舍去），故 $P(X = 1) = C_4^1 p(1 - p)^3 = \frac{32}{81}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**
