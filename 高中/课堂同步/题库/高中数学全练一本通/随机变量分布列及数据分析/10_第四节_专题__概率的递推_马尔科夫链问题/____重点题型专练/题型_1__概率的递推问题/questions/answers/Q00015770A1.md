> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $P_{n} = \frac{1}{38}\times \left(-\frac{4}{15}\right)^{n - 1} + \frac{9}{19}, n\in \mathbf{N}, n\geq 1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $C_1 =$ “第 $n- 1$ 次出现红球”， $C_2 =$ “第 $n- 1$ 次出现绿球”， $D=$ “第 $n$ 次出现红球”，则 $P(C_1) = P_{n - 1}, P(C_2) = 1 - P_{n - 1}, P(D|C_1) = \frac{1}{3}, P(D|C_2) = \frac{3}{5},$ 由全概率公式得 $P_{n} = P(D) = P(C_{1})P(D|C_{1}) + P(C_{2})P(D|C_{2})$ $= P_{n - 1}\times \frac{1}{3} +(1 - P_{n - 1})\times \frac{3}{5} = -\frac{4}{15}P_{n - 1} + \frac{3}{5}(n\in \mathbf{N}, n\geq 1).$ 即 $P_{n} = -\frac{4}{15}P_{n - 1} + \frac{3}{5}, n\in \mathbf{N}, n\geq 1,$ 所以 $P_{n} - \frac{9}{19} = -\frac{4}{15}\left(P_{n - 1} - \frac{9}{19}\right),P_{1} - \frac{9}{19} = \frac{1}{2} -\frac{9}{19} = \frac{1}{38},$
> >
> > 所以数列  \( \left\{P\_{n}-\frac{9}{19}\right\} \)  是首项为  \( \frac{1}{38} \) ，公比为  \( -\frac{4}{15} \)  的等比数列，
> > 所以  \( P\_{n}-\frac{9}{19}=\frac{1}{38}\times\left(-\frac{4}{15}\right)^{n-1} \) ，即  \( P\_{n}=\frac{1}{38}\times\left(-\frac{4}{15}\right)^{n-1}+\frac{9}{19} \) ， \( n\in N,n\geq1 \) 
> > 故答案为： \( P\_{n}=\frac{1}{38}\times\left(-\frac{4}{15}\right)^{n-1}+\frac{9}{19} \) ， \( n\in N,n\geq1 \)
