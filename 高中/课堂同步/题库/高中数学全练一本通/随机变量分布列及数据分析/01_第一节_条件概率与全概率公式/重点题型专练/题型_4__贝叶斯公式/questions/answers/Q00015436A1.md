> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $A_{i}=$ “王同学第 i 天去 A 餐厅就餐”， $B_{i}=$ “王同学第 i 天去 B 餐厅就餐”，i=1,2，
> >
> > 依题意， $P(B_{1})=\frac{1}{3}$ ， $P(A_{2}|A_{1})=\frac{4}{5}$ ， $P(A_{2}|B_{1})=\frac{3}{5}$ ，则 $P(A_{1})=\frac{2}{3}$ ，
> >
> > 由 $P(A_{2}|A_{1})=\frac{P(A_{2}A_{1})}{P(A_{1})}=\frac{4}{5}$ 有： $P(A_{2}A_{1})=\frac{8}{15}$ ，
> >
> > 因为 $A_{2}=A_{1}A_{2}\cup B_{1}A_{2}$ ，所以 $P(A_{2})=P(A_{1}A_{2}\cup B_{1}A_{2})=P(A_{1}A_{2})+P(B_{1}A_{2})$ $=P(A_{1})P(A_{2}|A_{1})+P(B_{1})P(A_{2}|B_{1})=\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{4}{5}+\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{3}{5}=\frac{11}{15}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $P(A_{1}|A_{2})=\frac{P(A_{2}A_{1})}{P(A_{2})}=\frac{\frac{8}{15}}{\frac{11}{15}}=\frac{8}{11}$ . 故选:B.
