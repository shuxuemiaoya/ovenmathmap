> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设取到甲袋为事件 A，则 $P(A) = P(\overline{A}) = \frac{1}{2}$ ,
> >
> > 设取到红球为事件 B，则 $P(B|A) = \frac{6}{10} = \frac{3}{5}$ , $P(B|\overline{A}) = \frac{8}{14} = \frac{4}{7}$ ,
> >
> > 由全概率公式可得 $P(B) = P(B|A)P(A) + P(B|\overline{A})P(\overline{A}) = \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{4}{7} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{41}{70}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)} = \frac{\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{1}{2}}{\frac{41}{70}} = \frac{21}{41}$ . 故选：D.
