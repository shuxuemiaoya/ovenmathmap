>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设事件 A：知道答案，事件 B：答对本题，
> >
> > 则 $P(\overline{A}) = 0.6$ , $P(B|A) = 0.9$ , $P(B|\overline{A}) = 0.2$ 则 $P(B) = P(B|A)P(A) + P(B|\overline{A})P(\overline{A}) = 0.9 \times 0.4 + 0.2 \times 0.6 = 0.48$ $P(\overline{A}|B) = \frac{P(\overline{AB})}{P(B)} = \frac{P(B|\overline{A})P(\overline{A})}{P(B)} = \frac{0.2\times 0.6}{0.48} = 0.25$ 故选：D
