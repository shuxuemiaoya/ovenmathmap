> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $B =$ “任取一个 $X$ 光片为次品”， $A_{i} = "X$ 光片为某厂生产”（甲、乙、丙厂依次对应 $i = 1,2,3$ ）则 $\Omega = A_1\cup A_2\cup A_3$ ，且 $A_{1},A_{2},A_{3}$ 两两互斥.  
> > 由题意可得： $P(A_{1}) = \frac{5}{10},P(A_{2}) = \frac{3}{10},P(A_{3}) = \frac{2}{10},$
> >
> > $P(B|A_{1})=\frac{1}{10},P(B|A_{2})=\frac{1}{15},P(B|A_{3})=\frac{1}{20}$ $P(B)=P(A_{1})P(B|A_{1})+P(A_{2})P(B|A_{2})+P(A_{3})P(B|A_{3})$ $=\frac{5}{10}\times\frac{1}{10}+\frac{3}{10}\times\frac{1}{15}+\frac{2}{10}\times\frac{1}{20}=\frac{2}{25}$ . 故选：A.
