> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $P(\overline{A})=\frac{1}{4}=1-P(A)$ ，则 $P(A)=\frac{3}{4}$ .
> > 则 $P(B)=P(A)P(B|A)+P(\overline{A})P(B|\overline{A})=\frac{3}{4}\times\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{4}\times\frac{1}{2}=\frac{5}{8}$ 故选：B.
