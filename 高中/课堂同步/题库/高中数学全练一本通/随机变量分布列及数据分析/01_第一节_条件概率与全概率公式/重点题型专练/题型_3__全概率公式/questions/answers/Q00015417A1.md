> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $A$ 表示“考生答对”， $B$ 表示“考生知道正确答案”，由全概率公式得 $P(A) = P(B)P(A|B) + P(\overline{B})P(A|\overline{B}) = \frac{1}{5}\times 1 + \frac{4}{5}\times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{2}{5},$ 故 $P(B|A) = \frac{P(AB)}{P(A)} = \frac{\frac{1}{5}}{\frac{2}{5}} = \frac{1}{2}.$
