> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $P(A_{1})=P(A_{1}|A_{3})=\frac{1}{4}, P(A_{2})=\frac{2}{3}, P(\overline{A}_{2}|A_{3})=\frac{2}{5}, P(\overline{A}_{2}|\overline{A}_{3})=\frac{1}{6}$ 所以 $P(\overline{A}_{2})=P(A_{3})P(\overline{A}_{2}|A_{3})+P(\overline{A}_{3})P(\overline{A}_{2}|\overline{A}_{3})$ $=P(A_{3})P(\overline{A}_{2}|A_{3})+(1-P(A_{3}))P(\overline{A}_{2}|\overline{A}_{3})$ 所以 $1-\frac{2}{3}=\frac{2}{5}P(A_{3})+\frac{1}{6}(1-P(A_{3}))$ ，解得 $P(A_{3})=\frac{5}{7}$ 因为 $P(\overline{A}_{3}|A_{1})=1-P(A_{3}|A_{1})=1-\frac{P(A_{1}A_{3})P(A_{3})}{P(A_{1})}=1-\frac{P(A_{1}|A_{3})P(A_{3})}{P(A_{1})}$ 所以 $P(A_{1}\overline{A}_{3})=P(\overline{A}_{3}|A_{1})P(A_{1})=P(A_{1})-P(A_{1}|A_{3})P(A_{3})=\frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{4}\times\frac{5}{7}=\frac{1}{14}$ .
> > 故选：A.
