> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $\frac{4}{9}$ ；（2）答案见解析.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1）设“甲投球一次命中”为事件 A，
> >
> > 则 $P(A) = \frac{1}{3}$ , $P(\overline{A}) = \frac{2}{3}$
> >
> > 故甲投球 2 次命中 1 次的概率为
> >
> > $$
> > P = P \left(A \cdot \overline {{A}}\right) + P (\overline {{A}}) P (A) = \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {4}{9}
> > $$
> >
> > （2）设“乙投球一次命中”为事件 $B$ 。由题意得 $P(B \cdot B) = p \cdot p = \frac{1}{16}$ ，解得 $p = \frac{1}{4}$ ，所以 $P(B) = \frac{1}{4}$ ， $P(\overline{B}) = \frac{3}{4}$
> >
> > 由题意得 $X$ 服从 $B\left(3, \frac{1}{4}\right)$ , 则
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = C _ {3} ^ {0} \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {0} \times \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {2 7}{6 4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = C _ {3} ^ {1} \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {1} \times \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {2 7}{6 4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = C _ {3} ^ {2} \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {1} = \frac {9}{6 4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = C _ {3} ^ {3} \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {3} \times \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {0} = \frac {1}{6 4}
> > $$
