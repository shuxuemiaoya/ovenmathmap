> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 答案见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可知 X 的可能取值有 0,1,2,3，且 $X \sim B\left(3, \frac{3}{4}\right)$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore P (X = k) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {k} \times \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {k} \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {3 - k}, k = 0, 1, 2, 3,
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $P(X=0)=C_{3}^{0}\times\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{0}\times\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)=\frac{1}{64}$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {1} \times \frac {3}{4} \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {9}{6 4},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{4} = \frac {2 7}{6 4},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {3} \times \left(\frac {3}{4}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {2 7}{6 4}.
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore X$ 的分布列为
