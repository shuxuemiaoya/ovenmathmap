> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1）分布列见解析， $\frac{8}{3}$ （2）k=3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）根据题意， $X \sim B(4, \frac{2}{3})$ ，X 可以取 0、1、2、3、4， $P(X=0)=\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{4}=\frac{1}{81}$ , $P(X=1)=C_{4}^{1}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{1}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{3}=\frac{8}{81}$ , $P(X=2)=C_{4}^{2}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{2}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2}=\frac{8}{27}$ , $P(X=3)=C_{4}^{3}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{3}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{1}=\frac{32}{81}$ $P(X=4)=\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{4}=\frac{16}{81}$
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>4</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{81}$ </td><td> $\frac{8}{81}$ </td><td> $\frac{8}{27}$ </td><td> $\frac{32}{81}$ </td><td> $\frac{16}{81}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > 故 $E(X) = 4 \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{3}$ (2) $P(X = k) = C_4^k\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^k\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{4 - k}$ 则: $\begin{cases} C_4^k\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^k \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{4 - k} \geq C_4^{k - 1}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k - 1} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{5 - k} \\ C_4^k\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^k \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{4 - k} \geq C_4^{k + 1}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{k + 1} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{3 - k} \end{cases}$ , 化简得 $\begin{cases} C_4^k\left(\frac{2}{3}\right) \geq C_4^{k - 1} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\right) \\ C_4^k \cdot \left(\frac{1}{3}\right) \geq C_4^{k + 1}\left(\frac{2}{3}\right) \end{cases}$ ,
> >
> > 继续化简得到 $\begin{cases} 2 \times \frac{4!}{k!(4 - k)!} \geq \frac{4!}{(k - 1)!(5 - k)!} \\ \frac{4!}{k!(4 - k)!} \geq 2 \times \frac{4!}{(k + 1)!(3 - k)!} \end{cases}$ , $\begin{cases} 2 \times \frac{1}{k} \geq \frac{1}{5 - k}. \\ \frac{1}{4 - k} \geq 2 \times \frac{1}{k + 1}. \end{cases}$ 解得: $\frac{7}{3} \leq k \leq \frac{10}{3}$ , 又由于 $k \in \mathbb{N}^*$ , 故 $k = 3$
