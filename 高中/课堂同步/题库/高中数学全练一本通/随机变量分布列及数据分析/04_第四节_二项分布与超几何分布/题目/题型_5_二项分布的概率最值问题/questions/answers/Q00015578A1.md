> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 每下落一层向左或向右落下等可能，概率均为 $\frac{1}{2}$ ，
> > 每一层均要乘以 $\frac{1}{2}$ ，共做 10 次选择，
> > 故 X 服从二项分布， $X \sim B\left(10, \frac{1}{2}\right)$ ，
> > 又 $E(X)=10 \times \frac{1}{2}=5$ ，
> > 令 $P(X=k_{0})$ 最大，
> >
> > 则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}P(X=k_{0})&\geq P(X=k_{0}-1)\\ P(X=k_{0})&\geq P(X=k_{0}+1)\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}C_{10}^{k_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{k_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{10-k_{0}}&\geq C_{10}^{k_{0}-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{k_{0}-1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{11-k_{0}}\\ C_{10}^{k_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{k_{0}}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{10-k_{0}}&\geq C_{10}^{k_{0}+1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{k_{0}+1}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{9-k_{0}}\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\frac{9}{2} \leq k_{0} \leq \frac{11}{2}$ ，又因为 $0 \leq k \leq 10, k \in Z$ ，所以 $k_{0} = 5$ ，
> > 所以 $P(X = k) \leq P(X = 5), k = 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots, 10$ $P(X=k)\leq P(X=k_{0})$ ，且 $k_{0}=5$ 。故选：B.
