>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设命中目标的子弹数为 X，则 $X \sim B(19, \frac{4}{5})$ ，有 $P(X=k)=C_{19}^{k}(\frac{4}{5})^{k}(\frac{1}{5})^{19-k}, k \in \mathbb{N}, k \leq 19$ 依题意，设 $P(X=k)$ 最大，显然 $P(X=0)$ ， $P(X=19)$ 都不是最大的，即有 $1 \leq k \leq 18$ 于是 $\left\{\begin{aligned}P(X=k)\geq P(X=k+1)\\ P(X=k)\geq P(X=k-1)\end{aligned}\right.$ ，即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}C_{19}^{k}(\frac{4}{5})^{k}(\frac{1}{5})^{19-k}\geq C_{19}^{k+1}(\frac{4}{5})^{k+1}(\frac{1}{5})^{18-k}\\ C_{19}^{k}(\frac{4}{5})^{k}(\frac{1}{5})^{19-k}\geq C_{19}^{k-1}(\frac{4}{5})^{k-1}(\frac{1}{5})^{20-k}\end{aligned}\right.$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{19!}{k!(19-k)!}\geq4\cdot\frac{19!}{(k+1)!(18-k)!}\\4\cdot\frac{19!}{k!(19-k)!}\geq\frac{19!}{(k-1)!(20-k)!}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，整理得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}k+1\geq4(19-k)\\4(20-k)\geq k\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $15 \leq k \leq 16$ ，
> >
> > 所以击中目标的子弹数最可能是15或16.故选：D
