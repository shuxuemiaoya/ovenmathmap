> > [!success]- **【答案】** B

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 依题意可得 $\frac{C_n^2 C_{12-n}^1}{C_{12}^3} = \frac{3n}{55}$ , 即 $\frac{\frac{n(n-1)(12-n)}{2}}{\frac{12 \times 11 \times 10}{6}} = \frac{3n}{55}$ , 整理得 $n^2 - 13n + 36 = 0$ , 解得 $n = 4$ 或 $9$ , 因为 $2 < n < 7$ , 所以 $n = 4$ . 故选：B.
