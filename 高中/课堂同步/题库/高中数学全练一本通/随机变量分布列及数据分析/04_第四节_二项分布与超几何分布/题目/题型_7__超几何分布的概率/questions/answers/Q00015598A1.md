> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可知 $X, Y$ 均服从超几何分布, 且 $X + Y = 4, Z = 2X + Y$ , 由 $|Z - 6| \leq 1$ , 得 $5 \leq Z \leq 7$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $Z = 5, Z = 6, Z = 7$ ,
> >
> > 因为 $P(Z = 5) = P(X = 1, Y = 3) = \frac{C_4^1 C_6^3}{C_{10}^4} = \frac{8}{21}$ , $P(Z = 6) = P(X = 2, Y = 2) = \frac{C_4^2 C_6^2}{C_{10}^4} = \frac{3}{7}$ , $P(Z = 7) = P(X = 3, Y = 1) = \frac{C_4^3 C_6^1}{C_{10}^4} = \frac{4}{35}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $P(|Z - 6| \leq 1) = P(Z = 5) + P(Z = 6) + P(Z = 7)$ $= \frac{8}{21} + \frac{3}{7} + \frac{4}{35} = \frac{97}{105}$ , 故选：B
