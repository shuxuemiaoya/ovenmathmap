> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意，得 $a+2a+3a=1$ ，则 $a=\frac{1}{6}$ ，所以 $E(\zeta)=-1\times\frac{1}{6}+0\times\frac{1}{3}+2\times\frac{1}{2}=\frac{5}{6}$ ， $E(|\zeta|)=1\times\frac{1}{6}+0\times\frac{1}{3}+2\times\frac{1}{2}=\frac{7}{6}$ ，所以 $D(\zeta) = \frac{1}{6} \times \left(-1 - \frac{5}{6}\right)^2 + \frac{1}{3} \times \left(0 - \frac{5}{6}\right)^2 + \frac{1}{2} \times \left(2 - \frac{5}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{53}{36}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > D \left(\left| \zeta \right|\right) = \frac {1}{6} \times \left(1 - \frac {7}{6}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{3} \times \left(0 - \frac {7}{6}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} \times \left(2 - \frac {7}{6}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {2 9}{3 6},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $D(2\zeta - 1) = 4D(\zeta) = 4 \times \frac{53}{36} = \frac{53}{9}$ , $D(2|\zeta| - 1) = 4D(|\zeta|) = \frac{29}{9}$ ,
> >
> > 即 $D(2\zeta-1)$ 最大, 故选:C.
