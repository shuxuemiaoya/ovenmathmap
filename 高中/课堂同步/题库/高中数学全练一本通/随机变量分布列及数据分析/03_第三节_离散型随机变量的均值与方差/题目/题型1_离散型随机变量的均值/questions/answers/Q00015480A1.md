> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 分布列见解析, $\frac{14}{15}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 随机变量 X 的所有可能取值为 0,1,2.
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = \left(1 - \frac {3}{5}\right) \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) = \frac {4}{1 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = \frac {3}{5} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{3}\right) + \left(1 - \frac {3}{5}\right) \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {8}{1 5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {1}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > 则随机变量 $X$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td>4/15</td><td>8/15</td><td>1/5</td></tr></table>
> >
> > 故 $E(X) = 0 \times \frac{4}{15} + 1 \times \frac{8}{15} + 2 \times \frac{1}{5} = \frac{14}{15}$ .
