> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意, 设 X 表示“所得分数”, 则 X 的可能取值为 -4, 1, 3, 6.
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = - 4) = \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} = \frac {1}{9}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {7}{1 8},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {7}{1 8},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 6) = \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {1}{9}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $X$ 的分布列为：
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>-4</td><td>1</td><td>3</td><td>6</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{7}{18}$ </td><td> $\frac{7}{18}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{9}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > 所以 $E(X)=(-4)\times\frac{1}{9}+1\times\frac{7}{18}+3\times\frac{7}{18}+6\times\frac{1}{9}=\frac{16}{9}$ .
