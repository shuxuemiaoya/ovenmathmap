> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1）答案见解析（2） $E(X)=\frac{6}{5},D(X)=\frac{9}{25}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）X 的可能取值为 0, 1, 2.
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {0} \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2}}{\mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{1 0}, P (X = 1) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {1} \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {1}}{\mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {2}} = \frac {3}{5}, P (X = 2) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0}}{\mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {2}} = \frac {3}{1 0},
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $X$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{10}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{5}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{10}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > （2）由（1）中分布列可得 $E(X)=0\times\frac{1}{10}+1\times\frac{3}{5}+2\times\frac{3}{10}=\frac{6}{5}$ , $D(X)=\left(0-\frac{6}{5}\right)^{2}\times\frac{1}{10}+\left(1-\frac{6}{5}\right)^{2}\times\frac{3}{5}+\left(2-\frac{6}{5}\right)^{2}\times\frac{3}{10}=\frac{9}{25}$ .
