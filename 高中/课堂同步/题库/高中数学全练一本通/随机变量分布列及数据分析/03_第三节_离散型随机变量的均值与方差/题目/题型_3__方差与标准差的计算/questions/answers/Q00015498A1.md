> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{15}{16}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ξ 的可能取值为 5,4,3,2 $P(\xi=5)=\frac{1}{4}\times\frac{1}{4}=\frac{1}{16}$ ， $P(\xi=4)=\frac{1}{4}\times\left(1-\frac{1}{4}\right)=\frac{3}{16}$ ， $P(\xi=3)=\left(1-\frac{1}{4}\right)\times\frac{1}{4}=\frac{3}{16}$ ， $P(\xi=2)=\left(1-\frac{1}{4}\right)\times\left(1-\frac{1}{4}\right)=\frac{9}{16}$ ，
> > 则 $E(\xi)=5\times\frac{1}{16}+4\times\frac{3}{16}+3\times\frac{3}{16}+2\times\frac{9}{16}=\frac{11}{4}$ ，
> > 则 $D(\xi)=\left(5-\frac{11}{4}\right)^{2}\times\frac{1}{16}+\left(4-\frac{11}{4}\right)^{2}\times\frac{3}{16}+\left(3-\frac{11}{4}\right)^{2}\times\frac{3}{16}+\left(2-\frac{11}{4}\right)^{2}\times\frac{9}{16}=\frac{15}{16}$
