> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 分布列见解析， $\frac{149}{324}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得，X 的可能取值为 0, 1, 2. $P(X=0)=\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{1}{3}=\frac{1}{9}$ , $P(X=1)=\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{2}{3}+\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{1}{4}=\frac{7}{18}$ , $P(X=2)=\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{3}{4}=\frac{1}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故 $X$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{7}{18}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{2}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > $$
> > E (X) = 0 \times \frac {1}{9} + 1 \times \frac {7}{1 8} + 2 \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {2 5}{1 8}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > D (X) = \left(0 - \frac {2 5}{1 8}\right) ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{9} + \left(1 - \frac {2 5}{1 8}\right) ^ {2} \times \frac {7}{1 8} + \left(2 - \frac {2 5}{1 8}\right) ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1 4 9}{3 2 4}.
> > $$
