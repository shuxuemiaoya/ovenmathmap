> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $2 - 2\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ξ服从两点分布,设成功的概率为p,
> >
> > 可得 $E(\xi)=p$ ， $D(\xi)=p(1-p)$ ，其中 0<p<1，
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{2D(\xi) - 1}{E(\xi)} = \frac{2p(1 - p) - 1}{p} = 2 - \left(2p + \frac{1}{p}\right) \leq 2 - 2\sqrt{2p \cdot \frac{1}{p}} = 2 - 2\sqrt{2}$ （当且
> >
> > $$
> > 2 p = \frac {1}{p}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > p = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{2D(\xi) - 1}{E(\xi)}$ 的最大值为 $2 - 2\sqrt{2}$ . 故答案为: $2 - 2\sqrt{2}$ .
