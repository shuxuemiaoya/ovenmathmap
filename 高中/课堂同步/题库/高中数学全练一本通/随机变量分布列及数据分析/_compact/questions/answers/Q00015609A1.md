> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{18}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意，X 满足超几何分布，且 X 的取值为 0,1,2，
> > 则 $P(X=0)=\frac{C_{3}^{0}C_{3}^{2}}{C_{6}^{2}}=\frac{1}{5},P(X=1)=\frac{C_{3}^{1}C_{3}^{1}}{C_{6}^{2}}=\frac{3}{5},P(X=2)=\frac{C_{3}^{2}C_{3}^{0}}{C_{6}^{2}}=\frac{1}{5},$ $E(X)=0\times\frac{1}{5}+1\times\frac{3}{5}+2\times\frac{1}{5}=1,D(X)=\frac{1}{5}\times(0-1)^{2}+\frac{3}{5}\times(1-1)^{2}+\frac{1}{5}\times(2-1)^{2}=\frac{2}{5}$ ，所以 $D(3X+2)=3^{2}D(X)=9\times\frac{2}{5}=\frac{18}{5}$ 。故答案为： $\frac{18}{5}$
