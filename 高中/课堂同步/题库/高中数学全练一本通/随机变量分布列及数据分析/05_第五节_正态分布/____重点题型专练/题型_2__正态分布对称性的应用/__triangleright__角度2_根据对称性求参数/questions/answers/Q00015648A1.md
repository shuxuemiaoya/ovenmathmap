> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\xi\sim B\left(9,\frac{1}{3}\right),\eta\sim N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$ 则 $E(\eta)=\mu,D(\xi)=9\times\frac{1}{3}\times\left(1-\frac{1}{3}\right)=2,$ 且 $E(\eta)=D(\xi), 即 μ=2, 可得 η~N(2,\sigma^{2}),$ 若 $P(\eta\leqslant2a+1)+P(\eta\leqslant2-a)=1,$ 则 $(2a+1)+(2-a)=2\mu, 即 a+3=4, 解得 a=1. 故选：C.$
