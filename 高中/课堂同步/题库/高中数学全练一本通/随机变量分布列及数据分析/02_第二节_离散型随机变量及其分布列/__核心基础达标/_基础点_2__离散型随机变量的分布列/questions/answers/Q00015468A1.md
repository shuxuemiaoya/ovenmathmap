> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $\frac{4}{9}$ ；（2）答案见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意解析几何题解答正确的概率是 $\frac{3}{5}$ ，立体几何题解答正确的概率为 $\frac{2}{3}$ ，
> >
> > 所以小明同学恰好有两道题解答正确的概率
> >
> > $$
> > P = \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {2}{3} \times (1 - \frac {2}{3}) + \frac {3}{5} \times (1 - \frac {2}{3}) \times \frac {2}{3} + (1 - \frac {3}{5}) \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} = \frac {6}{4 5} + \frac {6}{4 5} + \frac {8}{4 5} = \frac {4}{9};
> > $$
> >
> > （2）由题意得 X 的可能取值为 0, 1, 2, 3, 5,
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = \frac {2}{5} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {2}{4 5}, P (X = 1) = \frac {2}{5} \times \left(\frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {2}{3}\right) = \frac {8}{4 5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {3}{4 5} = \frac {1}{1 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = \frac {3}{5} \times \left(\frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{3} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {2}{3}\right) + \frac {2}{5} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} = \frac {2 0}{4 5} = \frac {4}{9},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 5) = \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} = \frac {1 2}{4 5} = \frac {4}{1 5},
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $X$ 的分布列为
