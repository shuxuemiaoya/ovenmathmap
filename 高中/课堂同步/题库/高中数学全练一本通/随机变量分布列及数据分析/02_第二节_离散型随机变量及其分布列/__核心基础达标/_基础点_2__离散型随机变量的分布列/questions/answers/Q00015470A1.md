> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $\frac{4}{5}$ ；（2）分布列见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由题意得,甲、乙两班级的出场序号均为偶数的概率 $P_{1}=\frac{A_{3}^{2}}{A_{6}^{2}}=\frac{1}{5}$
> >
> > 故甲、乙两班级的出场序号中至少有一个为奇数的概率 $P_{2}=1-P_{1}=\frac{4}{5}$ （2）易知 X 的可能取值为 0, 1, 2, 3, 4,
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = \frac {\mathrm{A} _ {2} ^ {2} \mathrm{A} _ {5} ^ {5}}{\mathrm{A} _ {6} ^ {6}} = \frac {1}{3}, P (X = 1) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {4} ^ {1} \mathrm{A} _ {2} ^ {2} \mathrm{A} _ {4} ^ {4}}{\mathrm{A} _ {6} ^ {6}} = \frac {4}{1 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {4} ^ {2} \mathrm{A} _ {2} ^ {2} \mathrm{A} _ {2} ^ {2} \mathrm{A} _ {3} ^ {3}}{\mathrm{A} _ {6} ^ {6}} = \frac {1}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {4} ^ {3} \mathrm{A} _ {3} ^ {3} \mathrm{A} _ {2} ^ {2} \mathrm{A} _ {2} ^ {2}}{\mathrm{A} _ {6} ^ {6}} = \frac {2}{1 5}, P (X = 4) = \frac {\mathrm{A} _ {2} ^ {2} \mathrm{A} _ {4} ^ {4}}{\mathrm{A} _ {6} ^ {6}} = \frac {1}{1 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $X$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>4</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{3}$ </td><td> $\frac{4}{15}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{5}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{15}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{15}$ </td></tr></table>
