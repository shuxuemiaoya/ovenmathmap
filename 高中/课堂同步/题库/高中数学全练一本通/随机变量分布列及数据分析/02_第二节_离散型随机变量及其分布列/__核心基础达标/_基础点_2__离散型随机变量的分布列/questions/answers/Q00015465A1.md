> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得取球次数 $\xi$ 是一随机变量. 则 $\xi=1,2,3,4,5$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 1) = \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 2) = \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {1}{4} = \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 3) = \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {3}{4} \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 4) = \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {3}{4} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 1) = \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {3}{4} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{1} = \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以若每次取出黑球不再放回, 取球次数 $\xi$ 的分布列为:
