【1】试用两种变换途径说明下列函数的变换方式.

(1) $y = \sin x \longrightarrow y = \sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ ;

(2) $y = \sin x \longrightarrow y = \sin \left(5x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ ;

(3) $y = \sin x \longrightarrow y = \sin \left( \frac{1}{2} x - \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$ ;

(4) $y = \cos x \longrightarrow y = \cos \left( \frac{2}{3} x + \frac{3\pi}{2} \right)$ ;

(5) $y = \cos x \longrightarrow y = 2\cos \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) - 1$ ;

(6) $y = \sin x \longrightarrow y = \frac{1}{2} \sin \left( \frac{1}{3} x - \frac{\pi}{3} \right) + 2$ .
