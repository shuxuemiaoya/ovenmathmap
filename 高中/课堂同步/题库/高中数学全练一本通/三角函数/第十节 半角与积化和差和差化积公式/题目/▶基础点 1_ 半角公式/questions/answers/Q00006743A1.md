> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ; $\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ ; $\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 解析： $\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos\alpha}{2}}=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\frac{1}{3}}{2}}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ , $\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos\alpha}{2}}=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1+\frac{1}{3}}{2}}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ , $\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos\alpha}{1+\cos\alpha}}=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\frac{1}{3}}{1+\frac{1}{3}}}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
