> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$ 解析: 由 $\sin \left( \theta + \frac{3}{2}\pi \right) = \frac{4}{5}$ , 得 $\cos \theta = -\frac{4}{5}$ ,
> 因为 $\theta \in \left( \frac{\pi}{2}, \pi \right)$ , 所以 $\frac{\theta}{2} \in \left( \frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$ ,
> 所以 $\cos \frac{\theta}{2} = \sqrt{\frac{1 + \cos\theta}{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{1 - \frac{4}{5}}{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{10}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$
