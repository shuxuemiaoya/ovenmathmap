> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{1}{2}$
> 解析：由三角函数定义可知：
> $\sin \theta = \frac{4}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}} = \frac{4}{5}, \cos \theta = \frac{3}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}} = \frac{3}{5}, \left( \theta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2} \right) \right),$ 则 $\tan \frac{\theta}{2} = \frac{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos \frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{2\sin \frac{\theta}{2}\cos \frac{\theta}{2}}{2\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{\sin\theta}{1 + \cos\theta} = \frac{1}{2}$ . 故答案为: $\frac{1}{2}$
