> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 因为 $\alpha$ 是锐角, 所以 $0 < \frac{\alpha}{2} < \frac{\pi}{4}$ ,
> 因为 $\sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 - \cos\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 - \frac{1}{3}}{2} = \frac{1}{3},\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 + \cos\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 + \frac{1}{3}}{2} = \frac{2}{3},$
> 所以 $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3},\cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
> $$
> \cos \left(\frac {\alpha}{2} + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \cos \frac {\alpha}{2} \cos \frac {\pi}{6} - \sin \frac {\alpha}{2} \sin \frac {\pi}{6} = \frac {\sqrt {6}}{3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{6}.
> $$
>
> 故选 **D**.
