> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 3
> 解析: 因为 $\frac{5}{2}\pi < \theta < 3\pi$ , $\sin \theta = \frac{3}{5}$ ,
> 所以 $\cos \theta = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2\theta} = -\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2} = -\frac{4}{5}$ ,
> 所以 $\tan \frac{\theta}{2} = \frac{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos \frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{2\sin \frac{\theta}{2}\cos \frac{\theta}{2}}{2\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{\sin\theta}{1 + \cos\theta} = \frac{\frac{3}{5}}{1 - \frac{4}{5}} = 3$ . 故答案为: 3
