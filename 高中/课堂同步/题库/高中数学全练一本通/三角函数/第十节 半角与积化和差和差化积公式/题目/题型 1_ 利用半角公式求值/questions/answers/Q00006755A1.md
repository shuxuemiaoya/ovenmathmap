> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：由 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right),\sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5}$
> 得 $\cos \alpha = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2\alpha} = -\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2} = -\frac{4}{5},$
> $$
> \because \frac {\pi}{2} <   \alpha <   \pi , \therefore \frac {\pi}{4} <   \frac {\alpha}{2} <   \frac {\pi}{2}, \cos \frac {\alpha}{2} > 0,
> $$
> $$
> \cos \frac {\alpha}{2} = \sqrt {\frac {1 + \cos \alpha}{2}} = \sqrt {\frac {1 + \left(- \frac {4}{5}\right)}{2}} = \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{1 0},
> $$
> 所以 $\cos \left(\pi -\frac{\alpha}{2}\right) = -\cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = -\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$ .
>
> 故选 **A**.
