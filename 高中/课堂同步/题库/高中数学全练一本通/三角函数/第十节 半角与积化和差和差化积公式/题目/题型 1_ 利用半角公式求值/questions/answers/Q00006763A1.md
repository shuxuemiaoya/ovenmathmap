> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 2
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题意知: $\sin\frac{\alpha}{2} - \cos\frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$ , 则 $\left( \sin\frac{\alpha}{2} - \cos\frac{\alpha}{2} \right)^2 = \frac{1}{5}$ , 可得 $1 - \sin\alpha = \frac{1}{5}$ , 即 $\sin\alpha = \frac{4}{5}$ ,
> 又 $\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha < \pi$ , 可得 $\cos\alpha = -\frac{3}{5}$ ,
> 由 $\tan\frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 - \cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha} = \frac{1 - (-\frac{3}{5})}{\frac{4}{5}} = 2$ . 故答案为: 2
