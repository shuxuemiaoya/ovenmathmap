> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** \( \frac{24}{7} \)
>
> **【解析】**
> 由  \( \sin\alpha + \sin\beta = \frac{1}{4} \) ,  \( \cos\alpha + \cos\beta = \frac{1}{3} \)  得， \( 2\sin\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} = \frac{1}{4} \) ,
> \( 2\cos\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} = \frac{1}{3} \) ,
> 两式相除得  \( \tan\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} = \frac{3}{4} \) ,
> 则  \( \tan(\alpha + \beta) = \frac{2\tan\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}}{1 - \tan^{2}\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}} = \frac{2 \times \frac{3}{4}}{1 - \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{2}} = \frac{24}{7} \) . 故答案为:  \( \frac{24}{7} \)
