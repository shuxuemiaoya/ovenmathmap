> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** -m
>
> **【解析】**
> 由 $m = cos^2\alpha - cos^2\beta = \frac{1 + cos 2\alpha}{2} - \frac{1 + cos 2\beta}{2}$ $= \frac{1}{2} (\cos 2\alpha - cos 2\beta) = -\sin (\alpha + \beta)\sin (\alpha - \beta)$ 所以 $sin (\alpha + \beta)\sin (\alpha - \beta) = -m$ ．故答案为：-m.
