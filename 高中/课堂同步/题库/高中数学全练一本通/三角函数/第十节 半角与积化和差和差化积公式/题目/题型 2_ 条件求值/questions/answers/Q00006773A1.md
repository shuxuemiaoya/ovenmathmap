> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> \( -\frac{5}{3} \)
> 解析：因为  \( \tan\alpha, \tan\beta \)  是一元二次方程  \( x^{2} + 3x - 4 = 0 \)  的 2 个根
> 所以  \( \tan\alpha + \tan\beta = -3 \) ,  \( \tan\alpha \cdot \tan\beta = -4 \)
> \( \frac{\cos2\alpha + \cos2\beta}{\sin2\alpha + \sin2\beta} = \frac{2\cos(\alpha + \beta)\cos(\alpha - \beta)}{2\sin(\alpha + \beta)\cos(\alpha - \beta)} = \cot(\alpha + \beta) \)
> \( = \frac{1}{\tan(\alpha + \beta)} = \frac{1 - \tan\alpha\tan\beta}{\tan\alpha + \tan\beta} = \frac{1 - (-4)}{-3} = -\frac{5}{3} \) ,
> 故答案为:  \( -\frac{5}{3} \) .
