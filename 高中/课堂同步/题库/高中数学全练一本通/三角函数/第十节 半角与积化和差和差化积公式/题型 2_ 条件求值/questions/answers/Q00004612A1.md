> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{15}$ 解析：因为 $\alpha - \beta = \frac{\pi}{3}$ ， $\cos\alpha + \cos\beta = 2\cos\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} = 2\cos\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}\cos\frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{1}{5}$ ，所以 $cos\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{15}$ . 故答案为： $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{15}$
