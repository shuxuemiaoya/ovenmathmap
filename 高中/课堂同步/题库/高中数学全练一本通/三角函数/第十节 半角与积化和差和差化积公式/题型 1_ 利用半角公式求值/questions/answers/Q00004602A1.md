> **【解析】**
> $\sqrt{3}$ 解析: 因为 $\frac{1 + \cos\theta}{\sin\theta} = \frac{2\cos^2\frac{\theta}{2}}{2\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\cos\frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{\cos\frac{\theta}{2}}{\sin\frac{\theta}{2}} = \frac{1}{\tan\frac{\theta}{2}}$ , 且 $\frac{1 + \cos\theta}{\sin\theta} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ .
所以 $\tan \frac{\theta}{2} = \sqrt{3}$ , 故答案为: $\sqrt{3}$ .
