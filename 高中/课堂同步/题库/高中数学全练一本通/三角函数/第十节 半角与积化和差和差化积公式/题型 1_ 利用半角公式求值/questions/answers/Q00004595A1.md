> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\because 5\pi <  \theta <  6\pi$ ， $\therefore \frac{\theta}{2}\in \left(\frac{5\pi}{2},3\pi\right),\frac{\theta}{4}\in \left(\frac{5\pi}{4},\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ ，故 $\sin \frac{\theta}{4} <  0$
$$
\cos \frac {\theta}{2} = a, \therefore \sin \frac {\theta}{4} = - \sqrt {\frac {1 - \cos \frac {\theta}{2}}{2}} = - \sqrt {\frac {1 - a}{2}}
$$
