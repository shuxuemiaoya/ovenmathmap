\( =\frac{\cos^{2}\alpha}{\frac{1+\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}-\frac{1-\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}}=\frac{\cos^{2}\alpha\sin\alpha}{2\cos\alpha}=\frac{1}{2}\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\frac{1}{4}\sin2\alpha= \)  右边，所以原恒等式成立.
（方法二）左边  \( =\frac{\cos^{2}\alpha\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}}{1-\tan^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}}=\frac{1}{2}\cos^{2}\alpha\tan\alpha=\frac{1}{2}\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\frac{1}{4}\sin2\alpha= \)  右边，所以，原等式成立.
