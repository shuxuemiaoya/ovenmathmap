> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 详见解析
> $$
> y = x ^ {2} \sin x \tag {1}
> $$
> $$
> f (- x) = (- x) ^ {2} \sin (- x) = - x ^ {2} \sin x = - f (x)
> $$
> $$
> f (- x) = \left| \sin (- x) \right| + \cos (- x) = f (x)
> $$
> 所以 $f(x) = |\sin x| + \cos x$ 是偶函数；
> $$
> f (- x) = \cos [ \sin (- x) ] = \cos (- \sin x) = \cos (\sin x) = f (x)
> $$
> $$
> y = \sin \left(\frac {3}{2} \pi - 2 x\right) = - \cos 2 x
> $$
> $$
> f (- x) = - \cos (- 2 x) = - \cos 2 x = f (x)
> $$
> $$
> y = \sin \left(\frac {3}{2} \pi - 2 x\right)
> $$
