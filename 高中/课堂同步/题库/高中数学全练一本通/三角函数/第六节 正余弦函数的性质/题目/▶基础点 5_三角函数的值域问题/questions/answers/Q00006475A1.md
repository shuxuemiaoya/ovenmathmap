> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\left[1,\frac32\right]$
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \left[ 1, \frac {3}{2} \right]
> $$
> $$
> 0 \leq x \leq \frac {\pi}{3}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {\pi}{6} \leq 2 x + \frac {\pi}{6} \leq \frac {5 \pi}{6}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {1}{2} \leq \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \leq 1
> $$
> $$
> 1 \leq \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + \frac {1}{2} \leq \frac {3}{2}
> $$
> $$
> x \in \left[ 0, \frac {\pi}{3} \right]
> $$
> $$
> \left[ 1, \frac {3}{2} \right]
> $$
