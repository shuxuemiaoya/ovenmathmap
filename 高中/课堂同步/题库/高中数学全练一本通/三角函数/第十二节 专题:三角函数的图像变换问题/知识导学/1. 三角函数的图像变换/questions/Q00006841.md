【3】说明下列函数的图像变换方式.

(1) $y = \sin 2x \longrightarrow y = \sin \left(2x - \frac{5\pi}{12}\right)$ ;

(2) $y = 3\cos 2x \longrightarrow y = 3\cos \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{4}\right);$

(3) $y = \sin \left(x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \longrightarrow y = 2\sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ ;

(4) $y = \sin \left(x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \longrightarrow y = \sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ ;

(5) $y = \sin \left( x - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) \longrightarrow y = \sin \left( \frac{x}{2} - \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$ ;

$$
(6) y = 2 \sin \left(2 x - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \longrightarrow y = 2 \sin \left(x - \frac {\pi}{3}\right).
$$
