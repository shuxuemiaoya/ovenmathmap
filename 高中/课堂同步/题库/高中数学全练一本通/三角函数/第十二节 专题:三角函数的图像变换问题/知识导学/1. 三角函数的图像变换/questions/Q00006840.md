【2】说明下列函数的图像变换方式.

(1) $y = \sin \left(x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \longrightarrow y = \sin x$ ;

(2) $y = \sin \left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) \longrightarrow y = \sin x$ ;

(3) $y = \sin \left( \frac{1}{2} x - \frac{\pi}{6} \right) \longrightarrow y = \sin x$ ;

(4) $y = 2\cos \left(3x - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) + 1 \longrightarrow y = \cos x$ .
