> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 答案见解析
> 解析：（1）由三角函数定义可知
> $$
> \cos \alpha = \frac {2}{\sqrt {2 ^ {2} + \sqrt {5} ^ {2}}} = \frac {2}{3}, \sin \alpha = \frac {\sqrt {5}}{\sqrt {2 ^ {2} + \sqrt {5} ^ {2}}} = \frac {\sqrt {5}}{3}; \tan \alpha = \frac {\sqrt {5}}{2};
> $$
> $$
> \cos \alpha = \frac {- 3}{\sqrt {(- 3) ^ {2} + 4 ^ {2}}} = - \frac {3}{5}, \sin \alpha = \frac {4}{\sqrt {(- 3) ^ {2} + 4 ^ {2}}} = \frac {4}{5}; \tan \alpha = \frac {4}{- 3} = - \frac {4}{3};
> $$
> （3）由三角函数定义可知
> $$
> \cos \alpha = \frac {- \sqrt {3}}{\sqrt {\left(- \sqrt {3}\right) ^ {2} + (- 1) ^ {2}}} = - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}, \sin \alpha = \frac {- 1}{\sqrt {\left(- \sqrt {3}\right) ^ {2} + (- 1) ^ {2}}} = - \frac {1}{2};
> $$
> $$
> \tan \alpha = \frac {- 1}{- \sqrt {3}} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}
> $$
> （4）由三角函数定义可知 $\cos\alpha=\frac{5}{\sqrt{(5)^{2}+(0)^{2}}}=1,\quad\sin\alpha=\frac{0}{\sqrt{(5)^{2}+(0)^{2}}}=0,\quad\tan\alpha=\frac{0}{5}=0;$
> $$
> - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
> $$
> 解析:由题设知： $P(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{1}{2})$ ，故 $\cos\alpha=-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 。故答案为： $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
