解析:如图,作出单位圆中的三角函数线,则有
$$
\cos \alpha = O M, \sin \alpha = M P, O P = 1,
$$
![](../../../../images/answers/913cb4b30e98e819c3c04a2555949a310293d2ad879ba0e8062acbde66dcc538.jpg)
在 Rt△OPM 中， $\left|OM\right|+\left|MP\right|>\left|OP\right|$
∴ $\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha > 1$
又 $\left|OM\right|^2 +\left|MP\right|^2 = \left|OP\right|^2 = 1$
$$
\therefore \left(\left| O M \right| + \left| M P \right|\right) ^ {2} \leq 2 \left(\left| O M \right| ^ {2} + \left| M P \right| ^ {2}\right) = 2 \text {即} \left| O M \right| + \left| M P \right| \leq \sqrt {2}
$$
当且仅当 $|OM| = |MP|$ 取等号，
$$
\therefore 1 <   \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha \leq \sqrt {2}
$$
