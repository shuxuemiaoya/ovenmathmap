> 5
> 解析： $f(-2)=a\sin(-4)+b\tan(-2)+3=-a\sin4-b\tan2+3=1$ ，故 $a\sin4+b\tan2=2$ $f(\pi+2)=a\sin2(\pi+2)+b\tan(\pi+2)+3=a\sin4+b\tan2+3=5$ 故答案为：5
