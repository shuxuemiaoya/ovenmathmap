> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1）分类讨论，答案见解析；（2）\(\frac{22}{25}\).
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）当\(\alpha\)为第二象限时：\(\cos \alpha = -\frac{4}{5}\).
> 当\(\alpha\)为第四象限时：\(\cos \alpha = \frac{4}{5}\).
> （2）\(2 + \sin \alpha \cos \alpha - \cos^2 \alpha = \frac{2 \sin^2 \alpha + \sin \alpha \cos \alpha + \cos^2 \alpha}{\sin^2 \alpha + \cos^2 \alpha}\)
> \(= \frac{2\tan^2\alpha + tan\alpha + 1}{\tan^2\alpha + 1} = \frac{22}{25}\).
