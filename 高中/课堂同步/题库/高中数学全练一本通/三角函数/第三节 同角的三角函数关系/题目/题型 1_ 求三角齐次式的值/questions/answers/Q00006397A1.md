> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> \(-\frac{2}{5}\)
> 解析：由题设\(\sin \theta \cdot \cos \theta = \frac{\sin \theta \cdot \cos \theta}{\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta} = \frac{\tan \theta}{\tan^2 \theta + 1} = -\frac{2}{5}\). （分子分母同除以\(\cos^2 \alpha\)）故答案为：\(-\frac{2}{5}\)
