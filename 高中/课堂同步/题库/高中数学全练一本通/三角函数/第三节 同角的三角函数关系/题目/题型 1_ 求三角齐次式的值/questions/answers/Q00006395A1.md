> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 2
> 解析：\(\because \frac{2 \sin \alpha - 3 \cos \alpha}{4 \sin \alpha - 9 \cos \alpha} = \frac{2 \tan \alpha - 3}{4 \tan \alpha - 9} = -1, \therefore 2 \tan \alpha - 3 = -4 \tan \alpha + 9\), 解得：\(\tan \alpha = 2\). 故答案为：2.
