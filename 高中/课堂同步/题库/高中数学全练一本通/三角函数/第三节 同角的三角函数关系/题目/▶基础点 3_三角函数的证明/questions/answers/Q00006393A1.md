> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $1 + \tan^{2}\alpha = 1 + \frac{\sin^{2}\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha} = \frac{\cos^{2}\alpha + \sin^{2}\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha} = \frac{1}{\cos^{2}\alpha}$ ，即证.
> (2) $\sin^4\alpha - \cos^4\alpha = (\sin^2\alpha - \cos^2\alpha)(\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha) = \sin^2\alpha - \cos^2\alpha$ ，即证.
> (2) $\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha=(\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha)(\sin\alpha+\cos\alpha)-\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha$ ，即证
> (3) $\frac{\sin\alpha}{1-\cos\alpha}\cdot\frac{\cos\alpha\cdot\tan\alpha}{1+\cos\alpha}=\frac{\sin\alpha}{1-\cos\alpha}\cdot\frac{\cos\alpha\cdot\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}}{1+\cos\alpha}=\frac{\sin^{2}\alpha}{(1-\cos\alpha)(1+\cos\alpha)}=$ $\frac{\sin^{2}\alpha}{1-\cos^{2}\alpha}=\frac{\sin^{2}\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha}=1$ . 即证.
> (4) 左边 $=\tan^{2}\theta-\sin^{2}\theta=\frac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta}-\sin^{2}\theta=\sin^{2}\theta\frac{1-\cos^{2}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta}=$ $=\sin^{2}\theta\frac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta}=\tan^{2}\theta\sin^{2}\theta=$ 右边，即证.
> (5) $\frac{1-\tan^{2}\theta}{1+\tan^{2}\theta}=\frac{1-\left(\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\right)^{2}}{1+\left(\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\right)^{2}}=\frac{\cos^{2}\theta-\sin^{2}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta+\sin^{2}\theta}=\cos^{2}\theta-\sin^{2}\theta$ ，即证.
> (6) 左边 $=\frac{\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\cdot\sin\theta}{\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}-\sin\theta}=\frac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\sin\theta-\cos\theta\cdot\sin\theta}=\frac{(1-\cos\theta)(1+\cos\theta)}{\sin\theta(1-\cos\theta)}=$ $\frac{1+\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}=$ 右边，即证.
> (7) 左边 $=\frac{(\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha)^{2}}{(\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha)(\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha)}=\frac{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha}=\frac{1-\tan\alpha}{1+\tan\alpha}=$ 右边，即证.
> (8) $\because \frac{\cos x}{1-\sin x}-\frac{1+\sin x}{\cos x}=\frac{\cos^{2}x-(1-\sin^{2}x)}{(1-\sin x)\cos x}=\frac{\cos^{2}x-\cos^{2}x}{(1-\sin x)\cos x}=0$ , $\therefore \frac{\cos x}{1-\sin x}=\frac{1+\sin x}{\cos x}$ ，即证.
> (9) $(\tan\alpha-\sin\alpha)\cdot(\tan\alpha+\sin\alpha)=\tan^{2}\alpha-\sin^{2}\alpha=$ $\tan^{2}\alpha-\tan^{2}\alpha\cdot\cos^{2}\alpha=$ $\tan^{2}\alpha(1-\cos^{2}\alpha)=$ $\tan^{2}\alpha\cdot\sin^{2}\alpha$ 所以 $\frac{\tan\alpha\sin\alpha}{\tan\alpha-\sin\alpha}=\frac{\tan\alpha+\sin\alpha}{\tan\alpha\cdot\sin\alpha}$ ，即证.
> (10) 左边
> $= \frac{\sin^2\alpha + 2\cos^2\alpha + 1}{\cos^2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha} = \frac{\sin^2\alpha + 2\cos^2\alpha + \sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha} = \frac{\frac{2\sin^2\alpha + 3\cos^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha}}{\frac{\cos^2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha}}$ $= \frac{3 + 2\tan^{2}\alpha}{1 - \tan^{2}\alpha} =$ 右边，即证.
