## 4. $\sin \theta, \cos \theta, \tan \theta$ 的知一求二问题

（1）已知 $\sin \theta$ （或 $\cos \theta$ ）求 $\tan \theta$ 常用以下方式求解

$$
\boxed {\sin^ {2} \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta = 1} \xrightarrow {} \boxed {\cos^ {2} \theta = 1 - \sin^ {2} \theta} \xrightarrow {} \boxed {\sin^ {2} \theta = 1 - \cos^ {2} \theta} \xrightarrow {} \boxed {\tan \theta = \frac {\sin \theta}{\cos \theta}}
$$

（2）已知 $\tan\theta$ 求 $\sin\theta$ （或 $\cos\theta$ ）常用以下方式求解

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\text {方法一}} \\ & {\tan \theta = \frac {\sin \theta}{\cos \theta}} \\ & {\text {方法二}} \end{array} \quad \begin{array}{r l} & {\sin^ {2} \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta = 1} \\ & {\tan^ {2} \theta = \frac {1 - \cos^ {2} \theta}{\cos^ {2} \theta}} \end{array} \quad \begin{array}{r l} & {\text {求} \sin^ {2} \theta , \cos^ {2} \theta} \\ & {\text {求} \sin \theta , \cos \theta} \end{array}
$$

当角 $\theta$ 的范围不确定且涉及开方时, 常因三角函数值的符号问题而对角 $\theta$ 分区间（象限）讨论.
