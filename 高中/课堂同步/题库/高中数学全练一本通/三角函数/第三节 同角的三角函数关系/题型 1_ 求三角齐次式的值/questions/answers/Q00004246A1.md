> **【解析】**
> \(\frac{1}{7}\)
解析：\(\because \tan \theta = \frac{4}{3}\),
\(\therefore \frac{\sin \theta - \cos \theta}{\sin \theta + \cos \theta} = \frac{\frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} - 1}{\frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} + 1} = \frac{\tan \theta - 1}{\tan \theta + 1} = \frac{\frac{4}{3} - 1}{\frac{4}{3} + 1} = \frac{1}{7}\). 故答案为：\(\frac{1}{7}\).
