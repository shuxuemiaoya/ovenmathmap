【3】(2023·全国·高一专题练习)

(1) $\sin (-\alpha)\cos (-\alpha -\pi)\tan (2\pi +\alpha);$

(2) $\sin \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{2}\right) \cdot \cos \left(\alpha - \frac{3\pi}{2}\right) \cdot \tan \left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)$ ;

(3) $\frac{\sin^2 (\alpha + \pi)\cos (\alpha + \pi)}{\tan (\pi - \alpha)\cos^3 (-\alpha - \pi)\tan (-\alpha - 2\pi)};$

(4) $\frac{\sin(180^{\circ}-\alpha)\sin(270^{\circ}-\alpha)\tan(180^{\circ}-\alpha)}{\sin(90^{\circ}+\alpha)\tan(180^{\circ}+\alpha)\tan(360^{\circ}-\alpha)};$

(5) $\frac{\cos(\alpha - \pi)}{\sin(\pi - \alpha)} \cdot \sin\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{2}\right) \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)$ ;

(6) $\frac{\sin(2\pi + \alpha)\cos(\pi - \alpha)\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)\cos\left(\frac{7\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{\cos(\pi - \alpha)\sin(3\pi - \alpha)\sin(-\pi + \alpha)\sin\left(\frac{5\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)};$

(7) $\frac{\tan(2\pi - \alpha)\sin(-2\pi - \alpha)\sin\left(\frac{3\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)}{\sin(\alpha - \pi)\cos\left(\frac{3\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)};$

(8) $\frac{-\sin(180^{\circ} + \alpha) + \sin(-\alpha)}{1 + \cos(-\alpha) + \cos(180^{\circ} - \alpha)};$

(9) $\frac{\cos\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{5\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)} \cdot \sin (\pi - \alpha) \cdot \cos (2\pi + \alpha)$ ;

(10) $\frac{\sin(-\alpha + k\pi)\cos(\alpha + k\pi)}{\sin[(k + 1)\pi + \alpha]\cos[(k + 1)\pi - \alpha]}$ .
