> **【答案】** $-m^2$
>
> **【解析】**
> $\sin 239^{\circ}\sin 121^{\circ} = \sin (270^{\circ} - 31^{\circ})\sin (90^{\circ} + 31^{\circ})$
> $$
> = (- \cos 3 1 ^ {\circ}) \cdot \cos 3 1 ^ {\circ} = - \cos^ {2} 3 1 ^ {\circ} = - m ^ {2}
> $$
