> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 解析：因为 $\alpha$ 为锐角，所以 $\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\in\left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)$ 且 $\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)>0\\ \sin^{2}\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)+\cos^{2}\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=1\end{aligned}\right.$ 得 $\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ ,
由诱导公式得 $\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha\right)=\sin\left[\frac{\pi}{2}-\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right]=\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ , $\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha\right)=\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ .
所以 $\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha\right)=\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha\right)}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha\right)}=\frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}}{\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
