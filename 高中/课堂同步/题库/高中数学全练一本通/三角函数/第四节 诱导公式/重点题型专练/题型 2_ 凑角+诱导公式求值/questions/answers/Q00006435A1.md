> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{91}{2}$
>
> **【解析】**
> ∵ $\sin^{2}1^{\circ}+\sin^{2}89^{\circ}=\sin^{2}1^{\circ}+\cos^{2}1^{\circ}=1$ $\sin^{2}2^{\circ}+\sin^{2}88^{\circ}=\sin^{2}2^{\circ}+\cos^{2}2^{\circ}=1$ $\sin^{2}44^{\circ}+\sin^{2}46^{\circ}=\sin^{2}44^{\circ}+\cos^{2}44^{\circ}=1$ ∴ 原式 = $44 + \sin^{2} 45^{\circ} + \sin^{2} 90^{\circ} = \frac{91}{2}$ $=44+\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^{2}+1=\frac{91}{2}$
