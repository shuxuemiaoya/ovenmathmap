> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** (1) $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ; (2) $-\frac{1}{2}$ ; (3) $-\frac{1}{3}$ ; (4) $\frac{12}{13}$ ; (5) $-\frac{1}{3}$ ; (6) $-\frac{1}{2}$ ; $$ \frac {1}{4}; (8) - \frac {3}{4}; (9) \frac {\sqrt {5}}{5}; (1 0) - \sqrt {3} $$
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）因为 $\left(\frac{5\pi}{6} +\alpha\right) + \left(\frac{\pi}{6} -\alpha\right) = \pi$ ，故 $\left(\frac{5\pi}{6} +\alpha\right) = \pi -\left(\frac{\pi}{6} -\alpha\right)$ ，则
> $$
> \cos \left(\frac {5 \pi}{6} + \alpha\right) = \cos \left[ \pi - \left(\frac {\pi}{6} - \alpha\right) \right] = - \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{6} - \alpha\right)
> $$
> 因为 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} -\alpha\right) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，故 $\cos \left(\frac{5\pi}{6} +\alpha\right) = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
> （2）因为 $\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \left(\frac{\pi}{3} -\alpha\right) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，所以 $\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{2} -\left(\frac{\pi}{3} -\alpha\right),$
> 所以 $\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left[\frac{\pi}{2} -\left(\frac{\pi}{3} -\alpha\right)\right] = \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} -\alpha\right) = -\frac{1}{2};$
> （3）因为 $\left(\alpha +\frac{7\pi}{6}\right) - \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \pi$
> 所以 $\left(\alpha +\frac{7\pi}{6}\right) = \pi +\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$
> 因为 $\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{1}{3}$ ,
> 所以 $\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{7\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left[\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \pi \right] = -\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = -\frac{1}{3};$
> （4）因为 $\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \left(\frac{\pi}{6} -\alpha\right) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，故 $\left(\frac{\pi}{6} -\alpha\right) = \frac{\pi}{2} -\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$
> 所以 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} -\alpha\right) = \cos \left[\frac{\pi}{2} -\left(\frac{\pi}{3} +\alpha\right)\right] = \sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{12}{13};$
> （5）因为 $\left(\alpha +\frac{7\pi}{6}\right) - \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{3\pi}{2}$ ，故 $\left(\alpha +\frac{7\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{3\pi}{2} +\left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$
> $$
> \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {7}{6} \pi\right) = \cos \left(\left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{3}\right) + \frac {3 \pi}{2}\right) = \sin \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = - \frac {1}{3}
> $$
> （6）因为 $\left(\alpha+\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)-\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，故 $\left(\alpha+\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{\pi}{2}+\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$
> $$
> \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {2 \pi}{3}\right) = \cos \left[ \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + \frac {\pi}{2} \right] = - \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = - \frac {1}{2}
> $$
> （7）因为 $\left(\alpha +\frac{5}{6}\pi\right) - \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，故 $\left(\alpha +\frac{5}{6}\pi\right) = \frac{\pi}{2} +\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ 因为 $\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{1}{4},$
> 所以 $\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{5}{6}\pi\right)=\cos\left[\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)+\frac{\pi}{2}\right]=-\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{4}$ ;
> （8）因为 $\left(\frac{6\pi}{7}-\alpha\right)-\left(-\frac{\pi}{7}-\alpha\right)=\pi$ ，所以 $\left(\frac{6\pi}{7}-\alpha\right)=\pi+\left(-\frac{\pi}{7}-\alpha\right)$ ， $\sin\left(\frac{6\pi}{7}-\alpha\right)=\sin\left[\left(-\frac{\pi}{7}-\alpha\right)+\pi\right]=-\sin\left(-\frac{\pi}{7}-\alpha\right)=-\frac{3}{4}$ ;
> （9）因为 $\alpha+\frac{4\pi}{3}-\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{3\pi}{2}$ ，所以 $\alpha+\frac{4\pi}{3}=\frac{3\pi}{2}+\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ ，
> 所以 $\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)=\cos\left[\frac{3\pi}{2}+\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right]=\sin\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ ;
> （10）因为 $\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)-\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，所以 $\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}$ 故 $\tan\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\tan\left[\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}\right]=-\frac{1}{\tan\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}=-\sqrt{3}$ .
