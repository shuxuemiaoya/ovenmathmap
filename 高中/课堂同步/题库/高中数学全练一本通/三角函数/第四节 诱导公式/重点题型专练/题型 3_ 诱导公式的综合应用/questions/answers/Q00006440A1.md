> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）证明见解析（2）证明见解析
> 解析。（1）在 ABC 中， $A=B+C=7$ 。则
> $$
> \triangle A B C
> $$
> $$
> A + B + C = \pi
> $$
> $$
> \cos (2 A + B + C) = \cos [ A + (A + B + C) ] = \cos (\pi + A) = - \cos A.
> $$
> 又∵ $\cos(B+C)=\cos(\pi-A)=-\cos A$
> $\therefore \cos (2A + B + C) = \cos (B + C)$
> （2）在 $\triangle ABC$ 中， $A + B + C = \pi$ ，则 $\frac{B + C}{2} = \frac{1}{2} (\pi -A)$
> $$
> \therefore \cos \left(\frac {B + C}{2} - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \cos \left[ \frac {1}{2} (\pi - A) - \frac {\pi}{4} \right] = \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - \frac {A}{2} - \frac {\pi}{4}\right)
> $$
> $$
> = \cos \left[ \frac {\pi}{2} - \left(\frac {A}{2} + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) \right] = \sin \left(\frac {A}{2} + \frac {\pi}{4}\right),
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \sin \left(\frac {A}{2} + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \cos \left(\frac {B + C}{2} - \frac {\pi}{4}\right).
> $$
