【8】（2021·高一课前预习）求证：

$$
\frac {2 \sin (\theta - \frac {3 \pi}{2}) \cos (\theta + \frac {\pi}{2}) - 1}{1 - 2 \sin^ {2} (\pi + \theta)} = \frac {\tan (9 \pi + \theta) + 1}{\tan (\pi + \theta) - 1}.
$$
