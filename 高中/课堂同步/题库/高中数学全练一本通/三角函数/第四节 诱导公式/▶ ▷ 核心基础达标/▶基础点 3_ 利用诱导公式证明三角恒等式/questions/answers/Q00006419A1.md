> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 证明见解析
> 解析：左边 $= \frac{-2\sin\theta\cos\theta - 1}{1 - 2\sin^2\theta} = \frac{-2\sin\theta\cos\theta - (\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta)}{\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta - 2\sin^2\theta}$
> $$
> = \frac {- (\sin \theta + \cos \theta) ^ {2}}{(\cos \theta + \sin \theta) (\cos \theta - \sin \theta)} = \frac {\sin \theta + \cos \theta}{\sin \theta - \cos \theta}.
> $$
> $$
> \text {边} = \frac {\tan (\pi + \theta) + 1}{\tan (\pi + \theta) - 1} = \frac {\tan \theta + 1}{\tan \theta - 1} = \frac {\frac {\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} + 1}{\frac {\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} - 1} = \frac {\sin \theta + \cos \theta}{\sin \theta - \cos \theta}
> $$
> ∴左边=右边, 故原等式成立.
