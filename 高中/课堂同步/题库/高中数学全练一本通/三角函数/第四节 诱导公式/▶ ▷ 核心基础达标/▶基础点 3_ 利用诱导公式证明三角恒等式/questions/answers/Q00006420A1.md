> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 当 $k = 2$ 时，
> 左边 $= \frac{\tan(2\pi - \alpha)\tan(2\pi + \alpha)}{\cos(4\pi - \alpha)\sin(5\pi + \alpha)} = \frac{-\tan\alpha \cdot \tan\alpha}{\cos\alpha \cdot (-\sin\alpha)} = \frac{\tan^2\alpha}{\cos\alpha \sin\alpha} = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos^3\alpha}$ ; 当 $k = 3$ 时，
> $$
> \text {左边} = \frac {\tan (3 \pi - \alpha) \tan (3 \pi + \alpha)}{\cos (6 \pi - \alpha) \sin (7 \pi + \alpha)} = \frac {- \tan \alpha \cdot \tan \alpha}{\cos \alpha \cdot (- \sin \alpha)} = \frac {\tan^ {2} \alpha}{\cos \alpha \sin \alpha} = \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos^ {3} \alpha};
> $$
> 综上， $k = 2$ 或 $k = 3$ 有原等式恒成立.
