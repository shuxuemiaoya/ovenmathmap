> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $\frac{5}{2}$ ；（2） $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ；（3） $-\frac{3}{4}$ ；（4） $\frac{5}{4}$ ；（5） $\frac{1}{4}$ ；（6）-1
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\sin 810^{\circ} + \tan 1125^{\circ} + \cos 420^{\circ}$
> $$
> = \sin (2 \times 3 6 0 ^ {\circ} + 9 0 ^ {\circ}) + \tan (3 \times 3 6 0 ^ {\circ} + 4 5 ^ {\circ}) + \cos (3 6 0 ^ {\circ} + 6 0 ^ {\circ})
> $$
> $$
> = \sin 9 0 ^ {\circ} + \tan 4 5 ^ {\circ} + \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} = 1 + 1 + \frac {1}{2} = \frac {5}{2}
> $$
> （2）原式= $\tan\left(360^{\circ}+45^{\circ}\right)-\sin\left(360^{\circ}+90^{\circ}\right)+\cos\left(2\times360^{\circ}+30^{\circ}\right)$
> $$
> = \tan 4 5 ^ {\circ} - \sin 9 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ} = 1 - 1 + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
> $$
> （3）原式 $= -\cos (180^{\circ} - 60^{\circ})\cdot \sin (180^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}) + \tan (135^{\circ} + 2\times 360^{\circ})$ $= -( - \cos 60^{\circ})\sin 30^{\circ} + \tan 135^{\circ}$ $= \cos 60^{\circ}\sin 30^{\circ} + \tan (180^{\circ} - 45^{\circ})$ $= \cos 60^{\circ}\sin 30^{\circ} - \tan 45^{\circ}$ $= \frac{1}{2}\times \frac{1}{2} -1 = -\frac{3}{4}.$
> （4）原式 $= \sin \left(2\pi +\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\cos \left(-4\pi +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \tan \left(-4\pi +\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\cdot \cos \left(4\pi +\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$
> $$
> = \sin \frac {\pi}{3} \cos \frac {\pi}{6} + \tan \frac {\pi}{4} \cos \frac {\pi}{3} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} + 1 \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {5}{4}
> $$
> $$
> = \sin \left(2 \pi + \frac {2 \pi}{3}\right) \cos \left(4 \pi + \frac {7 \pi}{6}\right) + \tan \left(- 6 \pi + \frac {\pi}{4}\right)
> $$
> $$
> = \sin \frac {2 \pi}{3} \cos \frac {7 \pi}{6} + \tan \frac {\pi}{4} = \sin \frac {\pi}{3} \left(- \cos \frac {\pi}{6}\right) + \tan \frac {\pi}{4} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \times \left(- \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}\right) + 1 = \frac {1}{4}
> $$
> (6) $\because \cos 179^{\circ} = \cos (180^{\circ} - 1^{\circ}) = -\cos 1^{\circ}$
> $$
> \cos 1 7 8 ^ {\circ} = \cos (1 8 0 ^ {\circ} - 2 ^ {\circ}) = - \cos 2 ^ {\circ}
> $$
> $\cos 91^{\circ} = \cos (180^{\circ} - 89^{\circ}) = -\cos 89^{\circ}$
> $$
> \therefore \text {   原式   } = \left(\cos 1 ^ {\circ} + \cos 1 7 9 ^ {\circ}\right) + \left(\cos 2 ^ {\circ} + \cos 1 7 8 ^ {\circ}\right) + \dots +
> $$
> $$
> \left(\cos 8 9 ^ {\circ} + \cos 9 1 ^ {\circ}\right) + \left(\cos 9 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 1 8 0 ^ {\circ}\right) = \cos 9 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 1 8 0 ^ {\circ} = 0 + (- 1) = - 1
> $$
