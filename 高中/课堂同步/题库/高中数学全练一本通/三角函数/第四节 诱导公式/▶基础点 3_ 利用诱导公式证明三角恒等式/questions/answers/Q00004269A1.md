> **【解析】**
> 证明见解析
解析：∵左边= $\frac{2\cos\theta\cdot(-\sin\theta)-1}{1-2\sin^{2}\theta}=\frac{-1-2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{1-2\sin^{2}\theta}$
$$
= \frac {- (\sin^ {2} \theta - 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta)}{\sin^ {2} \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta - 2 \sin^ {2} \theta}
$$
$$
= \frac {- (\sin \theta + \cos \theta) ^ {2}}{(\cos \theta + \sin \theta) (\cos \theta - \sin \theta)} = - \frac {\cos \theta + \sin \theta}{\cos \theta - \sin \theta} = \frac {\sin \theta + \cos \theta}{\sin \theta - \cos \theta} = \text {右边}.
$$
∴原式成立.
