> 解析:由题意知 $-90^{\circ} + 360^{\circ} \cdot k < \alpha < 360^{\circ} \cdot k (k \in Z)$ ,
> $$
> - 3 6 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot k <   - \alpha <   - 3 6 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot k + 9 0 ^ {\circ} (k \in Z)

> 2 7 0 ^ {\circ} - 3 6 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot k <   2 7 0 ^ {\circ} - \alpha <   3 6 0 ^ {\circ} - 3 6 0 ^ {\circ} \cdot k (k \in Z),
