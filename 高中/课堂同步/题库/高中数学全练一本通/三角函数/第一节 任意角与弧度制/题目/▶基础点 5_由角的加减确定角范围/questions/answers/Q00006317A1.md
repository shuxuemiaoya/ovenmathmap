> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $$ 5 0 ^ {\circ} < \alpha + \beta < 1 4 0 ^ {\circ}; - 5 0 ^ {\circ} < \alpha - \beta < 4 0 ^ {\circ} $$
>
> **【解析】**
> ∵ $30^{\circ}<\alpha<60^{\circ}$ ， $20^{\circ}<\beta<80^{\circ}$
> ∴ $50^{\circ}<\alpha+\beta<140^{\circ}$
> $$
> \because 2 0 ^ {\circ} <   \beta <   8 0 ^ {\circ}
> $$
> $$
> \therefore - 8 0 ^ {\circ} <   - \beta <   - 2 0 ^ {\circ}
> $$
> $\therefore \alpha - \beta = \alpha + (-\beta)$
> $$
> \therefore - 5 0 ^ {\circ} <   \alpha + (- \beta) <   4 0 ^ {\circ}
> $$
