> **【解析】**
> $\because \alpha = \frac{\pi}{12}, R = 2, \therefore l = |\alpha| \cdot R = \frac{\pi}{12} \times 2 = \frac{\pi}{6}$ ，故答案为： $\frac{\pi}{6}$ .
