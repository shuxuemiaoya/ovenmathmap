> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1）2；（2）-1；（3）2；（4）-2；
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\frac{3 - \sin 160^{\circ}}{1 + \sin^{2}35^{\circ}} = \frac{3 - \sin 20^{\circ}}{1 + \frac{1 - \cos 70^{\circ}}{2}} = \frac{3 - \sin 20^{\circ}}{\frac{3 - \sin 20^{\circ}}{2}} = 2$
> (2) $\frac{\sin^235^\circ - \frac{1}{2}}{\cos 10^\circ \cos 80^\circ} = \frac{\frac{1 - \cos^270^\circ}{2} - \frac{1}{2}}{\cos 10^\circ \sin 10^\circ} = \frac{-\cos 70^\circ}{\sin 20^\circ} = -1;$
> $$
> \frac {3 - \sin 1 7 0 ^ {\circ}}{1 + \sin^ {2} 4 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {3 - \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{1 + \frac {1 - \cos 8 0 ^ {\circ}}{2}} = \frac {3 - \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\left(\frac {3 - \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{2}\right)} = 2; \tag {3}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {\sin 8 0 ^ {\circ} + 1}{\sin^ {2} 5 ^ {\circ} - 1} = \frac {\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} + 1}{\frac {1 - \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{2} - 1} = 2 \times \frac {\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} + 1}{- \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} - 1} = - 2; \tag {4}
> $$
