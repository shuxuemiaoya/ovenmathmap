【4】化简求值：

(1) $\frac{2\cos^2\alpha}{\sin 2\alpha} \cdot \frac{1 - \cos 2\alpha}{\cos 2\alpha}$ ;

(2) $\frac{\cos 2\alpha}{1 + \sin 2\alpha} \cdot \frac{1 + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan \alpha}$ ;

(3) $\frac{2\cos^4x - 2\cos^2x + \frac{1}{2}}{2\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4} - x\right)\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi}{4} + x\right)};$

(4) $\sqrt{2 + 2\cos 8} + 2\sqrt{1 - \sin 8}$ ;

(5) $\frac{(\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha - 1)(\sin\alpha - \cos\alpha + 1)}{\sin2\alpha}$ ;

(6) $\frac{2\sin(\pi - \alpha) + \sin 2\alpha}{2\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2}};$

(7) $\frac{2\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\theta\right)\sin^2\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\theta\right)}{\frac{1}{2}-\cos^2\theta}$ ;

(8) $\sin^2 50^\circ + \cos 20^\circ \sin 10^\circ$ .
