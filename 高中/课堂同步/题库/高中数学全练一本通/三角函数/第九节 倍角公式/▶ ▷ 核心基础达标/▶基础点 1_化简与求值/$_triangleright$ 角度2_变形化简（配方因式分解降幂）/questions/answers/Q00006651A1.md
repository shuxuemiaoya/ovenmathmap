> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\tan 2\alpha$ （2）1；（3） $\frac{1}{2}\cos 2x$ ；（4） $-2\sin 4$ ；（5） $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2}$ ；（6） $2\sin \alpha$ ；（7） $-2$ ；（8） $\frac{3}{4}$ .
> $$
> \begin{array}{r l} & \frac {2 \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin 2 \alpha} \cdot \frac {1 - \cos 2 \alpha}{\cos 2 \alpha} = \frac {1 + \cos 2 \alpha}{\sin 2 \alpha} \cdot \frac {1 - \cos 2 \alpha}{\cos 2 \alpha} = \frac {1 - \cos^ {2} 2 \alpha}{\sin 2 \alpha \cos 2 \alpha} = \frac {\sin^ {2} 2 \alpha}{\sin 2 \alpha \cos 2 \alpha} \\ & = \frac {\sin 2 \alpha}{\cos 2 \alpha} = \tan 2 \alpha \end{array}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{1 + \sin 2 \alpha} \cdot \frac {1 + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan \alpha} = \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha}{(\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha) ^ {2}} \cdot \frac {1 + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan \alpha} \tag {2}
> $$
> $$
> \begin{array}{r l} = & \frac {\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha}{\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha} \cdot \frac {1 + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan \alpha} = \frac {1 - \tan \alpha}{1 + \tan \alpha} \cdot \frac {1 + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan \alpha} = 1; \\ & \frac {1}{2} (4 \cos^ {4} x - 4 \cos^ {2} x + 1) \end{array}
> $$
> （3）原式= $2\cdot\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right)}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right)}\cos^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-x\right)$
> $$
> = \frac {\left(2 \cos^ {2} x - 1\right) ^ {2}}{4 \cdot \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - x\right) \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - x\right)}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\left(2 \cos^ {2} x - 1\right) ^ {2}}{2 \cdot \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - 2 x\right)} = \frac {\cos^ {2} 2 x}{2 \cos 2 x} = \frac {1}{2} \cos 2 x
> $$
> （4）由于 $\frac{5\pi}{4} < 4 < \frac{3\pi}{2}$ ，所以 $\cos 4 < 0, \sin 4 < 0$ ，且 $\cos 4 > \sin 4$ ，
> 所以 $\sqrt{2 + 2\cos 8} + 2\sqrt{1 - \sin 8}$
> （5）原式 $= \frac{\left(2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2} - 2\sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2}\right)\left(2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2} + 2\sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2}\right)}{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}$
> $$
> = \frac {4 \sin^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2} \left(\cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2} - \sin^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)}{4 \sin \frac {\alpha}{2} \cos \frac {\alpha}{2} \cos \alpha} = \tan \frac {\alpha}{2}.
> $$
> $$
> \frac {2 \sin (\pi - \alpha) + \sin 2 \alpha}{2 \cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {2 \sin \alpha + \sin 2 \alpha}{2 \cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}} \tag {6}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {2 \sin \alpha + \sin 2 \alpha}{2 \cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {2 \sin \alpha (1 + \cos \alpha)}{2 \cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {2 \sin \alpha (1 + \cos \alpha)}{1 + \cos \alpha} = 2 \sin \alpha ,
> $$
> $$
> \frac {2 \tan \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \theta\right) \sin^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \theta\right)}{\frac {1}{2} - \cos^ {2} \theta} = \frac {2 \tan \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \theta\right) \cos^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \theta\right)}{\frac {1}{2} - \frac {1 + \cos 2 \theta}{2}} \tag {7}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {2 \frac {\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \theta\right)}{\cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \theta\right)} \cos^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \theta\right)}{- \frac {\cos 2 \theta}{2}} = \frac {2 \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \theta\right) \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \theta\right)}{- \frac {\cos 2 \theta}{2}}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - 2 \theta\right)}{- \frac {\cos 2 \theta}{2}} = \frac {\cos 2 \theta}{- \frac {\cos 2 \theta}{2}} = - 2.
> $$
> $$
> \sin^ {2} 5 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 2 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} = \cos^ {2} 4 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin 7 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} \tag {8}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \cos 8 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin 7 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \cos (7 0 ^ {\circ} + 1 0 ^ {\circ}) + \sin 7 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \left(\cos 7 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} - \sin 7 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}\right) + \sin 7 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} \left(\cos 7 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin 7 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}\right)
