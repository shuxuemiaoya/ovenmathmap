> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 证明: 由二倍角公式 $\sin2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta,\cos2\theta=\cos^{2}\theta-\sin^{2}\theta$ 以及 $\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta=1$ 可得,
> $$
> \begin{array}{l} \frac {\sin 2 \theta + 1}{\sin 2 \theta + \cos 2 \theta + 1} = \frac {2 \sin \theta \cos \theta + \sin^ {2} \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta}{2 \sin \theta \cos \theta + 2 \cos^ {2} \theta} \\ = \frac {(\sin \theta + \cos \theta) ^ {2}}{2 \cos \theta (\sin \theta + \cos \theta)} = \frac {\sin \theta + \cos \theta}{2 \cos \theta} \end{array}
> $$
> $= \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} +\frac{\cos\theta}{\cos\theta}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\big(\tan \theta +1\big)$ 得证.
