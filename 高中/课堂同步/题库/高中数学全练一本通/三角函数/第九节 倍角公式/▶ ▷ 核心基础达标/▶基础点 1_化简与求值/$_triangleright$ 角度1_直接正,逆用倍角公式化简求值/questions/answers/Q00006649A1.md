> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1）2；（2）1；（3）2；（4）- $\frac{1}{8}$
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）原式 $= \left(\frac{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}} -\frac{\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}}\right)\cdot \frac{2\sin^2\alpha}{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}$ $= \frac{\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2} - \sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cdot\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}}\cdot \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{2\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}\cdot \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = 2$ （2） $\sin 50^{\circ}\left(1 + \sqrt{3}\tan 10^{\circ}\right) = \sin 50^{\circ}\left(1 + \frac{\sqrt{3}\sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}}\right) = \sin 50^{\circ}\times \frac{\cos 10^{\circ} + \sqrt{3}\sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}}$ $= \sin 50^{\circ}\times \frac{2\sin(10^{\circ} + 30^{\circ})}{\cos 10^{\circ}} = \frac{2\sin 50^{\circ}\sin 40^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}} = \frac{2\sin 50^{\circ}\cos 50^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}} = \frac{\sin 100^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{\sin(90^{\circ} + 10^{\circ})}{\cos 10^{\circ}} = \frac{\cos 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}} = 1$ (3)
> $$
> \frac {2 \cos^ {2} \alpha - 1}{\tan \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) \sin^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)} = \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{\frac {\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right)}{\cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right)} \sin^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{\frac {\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right)}{\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)} \sin^ {2} \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)} = \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \alpha\right) \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)}.
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{\cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)} = \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{\frac {1}{2} \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + 2 \alpha\right)} = \frac {2 \cos 2 \alpha}{\cos 2 \alpha} = 2
> $$
> $$
> \cos \frac {\pi}{7} \cos \frac {2 \pi}{7} \cos \frac {4 \pi}{7} = \frac {\sin \frac {\pi}{7} \cos \frac {\pi}{7} \cos \frac {2 \pi}{7} \cos \frac {4 \pi}{7}}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}} \tag {4}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\frac {1}{2} \sin \frac {2 \pi}{7} \cos \frac {2 \pi}{7} \cos \frac {4 \pi}{7}}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} \sin \frac {4 \pi}{7} \cos \frac {4 \pi}{7}}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} \sin \frac {8 \pi}{7}}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {- \frac {1}{8} \sin \frac {\pi}{7}}{\sin \frac {\pi}{7}} = - \frac {1}{8}
> $$
