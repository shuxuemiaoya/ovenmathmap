> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 证明见解析
> 解析： $\frac{1 - \cos 4\alpha}{\sin 4\alpha}\cdot \frac{\cos 2\alpha}{1 + \cos 2\alpha} = \frac{2\sin^22\alpha}{2\sin 2\alpha\cos 2\alpha}\cdot \frac{\cos 2\alpha}{1 + \cos 2\alpha}.$
> $$
> = \frac {\sin 2 \alpha}{1 + \cos 2 \alpha} = \frac {2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{2 \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \tan \alpha
> $$
