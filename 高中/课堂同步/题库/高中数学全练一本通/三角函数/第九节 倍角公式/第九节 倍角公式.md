# 第九节 倍角公式

## 第九节 倍角公式

## 知识导学

1. 二倍角的正弦、余弦、正切公式

$$
\sin 2 \alpha = 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha
$$

$$
\cos 2 \alpha = \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha = 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \alpha = 2 \cos^ {2} \alpha - 1,
$$

$$
\tan 2 \alpha = \frac {2 \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha}
$$

## 2. 倍角公式的变形应用

（1）配方变形： $1 \pm \sin 2\alpha = \sin^{2}\alpha + \cos^{2}\alpha \pm \sin 2\alpha = (\sin\alpha \pm \cos\alpha)^{2}$

（2）因式分解变形： $\cos 2\alpha = \cos^2\alpha -\sin^2\alpha = (\cos \alpha +\sin \alpha)(\cos \alpha -\sin \alpha).$

（3）升幂公式： $1+\cos2\alpha=2\cos^{2}\alpha;1-\cos2\alpha=2\sin^{2}\alpha$

（4）降幂公式： $\cos^2\alpha = \frac{1 + \cos 2\alpha}{2};\sin^2\alpha = \frac{1 - \cos 2\alpha}{2};\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{2}\sin 2\alpha ;\tan^2\alpha = \frac{1 - \cos 2\alpha}{1 + \cos 2\alpha}$

## 3. 万能公式（用正切来表示正余弦的倍角）

$\sin 2\alpha = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha}$ 即 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha}$ .

$$
\cos 2 \alpha = \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha = \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha}{1 + \tan^ {2} \alpha} \text {即} \cos 2 \alpha = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha}{1 + \tan^ {2} \alpha}.
$$
