> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** \( \left(\frac{5}{4},\frac{3}{2}\right] \)
>
> **【解析】**
> \( \sin^{2}A+\sin^{2}C=\frac{1}{2}(1-\cos 2A)+\frac{1}{2}(1-\cos 2C) \)
> \( =1-\frac{1}{2}(\cos 2A+\cos 2C)=1-\frac{1}{2}[\cos 2A+\cos 2(\frac{2\pi}{3}-A)] \)
> \( =1-\frac{1}{2}(\frac{1}{2}\cos 2A-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin 2A)=1-\frac{1}{2}\cos(2A+\frac{\pi}{3}) \)
> △ABC为锐角三角形，
> ∴  \( 0 < A < \frac{\pi}{2} \)  且  \( 0 < C = \frac{2\pi}{3} - A < \frac{\pi}{2} \) ，∴  \( \frac{\pi}{6} < A < \frac{\pi}{2} \) ，
> ∴  \( \frac{2\pi}{3} < 2A + \frac{\pi}{3} < \frac{4\pi}{3} \)
> ∴  \( -1 \leqslant \cos\left(2A + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) < -\frac{1}{2} \) ，∴  \( \frac{5}{4} < 1 - \frac{1}{2}\cos\left(2A + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) \leqslant \frac{3}{2} \) ，
> 故  \( \sin^{2}A + \sin^{2}C \)  的取值范围为  \( \left(\frac{5}{4},\frac{3}{2}\right] \) .
