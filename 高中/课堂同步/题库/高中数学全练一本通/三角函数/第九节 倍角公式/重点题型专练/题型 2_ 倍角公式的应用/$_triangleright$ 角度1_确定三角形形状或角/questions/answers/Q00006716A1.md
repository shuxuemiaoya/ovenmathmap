> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $C = \frac{\pi}{3}$
> 解析:由题意得: $\frac{1+\cos2A}{2}-\frac{1+\cos2B}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin2A-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin2B$ ,
> $$
> \therefore \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin 2 A - \frac {1}{2} \cos 2 A = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin 2 B - \frac {1}{2} \cos 2 B, \therefore
> $$
> $$
> \sin \left(2 A - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left(2 B - \frac {\pi}{6}\right).
> $$
> $\because a\neq b,\therefore A\neq B,$ 又 $A+B\in(0,\pi),\therefore 2A-\frac{\pi}{6}+2B-\frac{\pi}{6}=\pi,$
> $$
> \therefore A + B = \frac {2 \pi}{3}, \therefore C = \frac {\pi}{3}
> $$
