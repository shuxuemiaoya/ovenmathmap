> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $A = \frac{\pi}{3}$
> 解析： $\because \cos A \cdot \sqrt{3} \sin A - \cos^{2} A = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \sin 2A - \frac{1 + \cos 2A}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$ ，
> $\therefore \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin 2A - \frac{1}{2}\cos 2A = 1$ 即 $\sin \left(2A - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = 1$
> 因为 $A \in (0, \pi)$ ，所以 $2A - \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{11\pi}{6}\right)$ ，
> 故 $2A - \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{2}, A = \frac{\pi}{3}$ .
