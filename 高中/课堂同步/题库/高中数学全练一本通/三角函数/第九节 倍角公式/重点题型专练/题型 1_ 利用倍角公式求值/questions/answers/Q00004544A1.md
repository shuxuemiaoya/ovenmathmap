> $\frac{7}{25}$
解析： $\sin \left(2\alpha +\frac{5\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left[\frac{\pi}{2} +2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right]$
$$
= \cos \left[ 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \right] = 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \frac {7}{2 5}
$$
