> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{5}{9}$
>
> **【解析】**
> $\cos \left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \cos \theta \cos \frac{\pi}{4} -\sin \theta \sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} (\cos \theta -\sin \theta) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3},$
> 所以
> $$
> \cos \theta - \sin \theta = \frac {2}{3}, (\cos \theta - \sin \theta) ^ {2} = \cos^ {2} \theta + \sin^ {2} \theta - 2 \cos \theta \sin \theta = 1 - \sin 2 \theta = \frac {4}{9}
> $$
> 所以 $\sin 2\theta = \frac{5}{9}$ 故答案为： $\frac{5}{9}$
