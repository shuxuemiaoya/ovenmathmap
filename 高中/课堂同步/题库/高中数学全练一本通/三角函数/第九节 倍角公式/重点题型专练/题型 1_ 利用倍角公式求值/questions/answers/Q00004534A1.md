> **【解析】**
> $\frac{4}{3}$ 解析：由 $\sin \alpha \cos \alpha - \cos^2\alpha = \frac{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha - \cos^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{\tan\alpha - 1}{\tan^2\alpha + 1}$ ，因为 $\sin \alpha \cos \alpha - \cos^2\alpha = -\frac{2}{5}$ ，可得 $\frac{\tan\alpha - 1}{\tan^2\alpha + 1} = -\frac{2}{5}$ ，解得 $\tan \alpha = -3$ 或 $\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$ ，又因为 $\alpha \in (-\frac{\pi}{8}, \frac{3\pi}{8})$ ，所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$ ，则 $\tan 2\alpha = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 - \tan^2\alpha} = \frac{2\times \frac{1}{2}}{1 - (\frac{1}{2})^2} = \frac{4}{3}$ . 故答案为： $\frac{4}{3}$ .
