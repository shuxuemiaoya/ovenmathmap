> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\cos (\beta -\alpha) = \cos \beta \cos \alpha +\sin \beta \sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5}$
> $$
> \tan \alpha \tan \beta = \frac {\sin \alpha \sin \beta}{\cos \alpha \cos \beta} = \frac {1}{2}, 2 \sin \alpha \sin \beta = \cos \alpha \cos \beta
> $$
> 所以 $\sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{5},\cos \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{2}{5}$
> 所以 $\cos 2(\alpha +\beta) = 2\cos^2 (\alpha +\beta) - 1 = 2(\cos \alpha \cos \beta -\sin \alpha \sin \beta)^2 -1$
> $= 2 \times \frac{1}{25} - 1 = -\frac{23}{25}$ .
>
> 故选 **D**
