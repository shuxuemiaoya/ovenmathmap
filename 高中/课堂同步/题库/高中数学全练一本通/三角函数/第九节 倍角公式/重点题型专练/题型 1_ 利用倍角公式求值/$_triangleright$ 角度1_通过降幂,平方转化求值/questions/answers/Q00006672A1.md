> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{9}{10}$
> 解析:由 $\alpha\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)$ ， $\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ ，得 $\cos\alpha=-\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$
> $$
> \therefore \sin 2 \alpha = 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 2 \times \frac {\sqrt {5}}{5} \times \left(- \frac {2 \sqrt {5}}{5}\right) = - \frac {4}{5}
> $$
> 所以 $\cos^{2}\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{1+\cos\left(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{2}=\frac{1-\sin2\alpha}{2}=\frac{1+\frac{4}{5}}{2}=\frac{9}{10}$
> 故答案为: $\frac{9}{10}$
