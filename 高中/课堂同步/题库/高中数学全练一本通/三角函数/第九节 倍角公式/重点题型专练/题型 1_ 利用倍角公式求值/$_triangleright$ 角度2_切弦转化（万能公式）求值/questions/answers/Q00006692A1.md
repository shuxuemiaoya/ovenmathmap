> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{3 + 4\sqrt{3}}{10}$
>
> **【解析】**
> \$\because\tan\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}} =\underline{\frac{1}{2}}\$, \$\therefore\$ \$\sin \alpha = 2\tan\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\$ \$\cos\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\$=\$=\frac{2\tan\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\$ \$\cos\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\$=\$=\frac{1+\tan^2\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\$ \$\cos^2\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\$=\$=\frac{4\tan\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\$ \$\cos\underline{\frac{\alpha}{2}}\$
> $\cos \alpha = \cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2} -\sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2} - \sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos^2\frac{\alpha}{2} + \sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{1 - \tan^2\frac{\alpha}{2}}{1 + \tan^2\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{1 - \frac{1}{4}}{1 + \frac{1}{4}} = \frac{3}{5}$ $\therefore \sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \alpha \cos \frac{\pi}{6} +\cos \alpha \sin \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{4}{5}\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} +\frac{3}{5}\times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3 + 4\sqrt{3}}{10}$
