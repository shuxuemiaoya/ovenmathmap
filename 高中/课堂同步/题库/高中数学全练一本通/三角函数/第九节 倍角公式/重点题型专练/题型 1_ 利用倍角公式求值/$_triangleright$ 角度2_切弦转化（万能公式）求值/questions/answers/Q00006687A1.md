> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：由 $\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{3}$ ，则 $\sin 2\alpha - \cos 2\alpha = \frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha - \cos^2\alpha + \sin^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha}$ $= \frac{2\tan\alpha - 1 + \tan^2\alpha}{\tan^2\alpha + 1} = \frac{2\times \frac{1}{3} - 1 + \frac{1}{9}}{\frac{1}{9} + 1} = -\frac{1}{5}$ .
>
> 故选 **A**.
