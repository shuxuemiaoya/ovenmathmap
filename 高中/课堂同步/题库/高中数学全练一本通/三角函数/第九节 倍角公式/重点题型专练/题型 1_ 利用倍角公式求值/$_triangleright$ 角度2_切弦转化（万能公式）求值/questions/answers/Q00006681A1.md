> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{7}{26}\sqrt{2}$
> 解析： $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{\tan^2\alpha + 1} = \frac{-\frac{4}{3}}{\frac{13}{9}} = -\frac{12}{13},$
> $$
> \cos 2 \alpha = \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = \frac {1 - \frac {4}{9}}{\frac {4}{9} + 1} = \frac {5}{1 3}
> $$
> 所以 $\sin (2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} (\sin 2\alpha +\cos 2\alpha) = -\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{26}$ 故答案为： $-\frac{7}{26}\sqrt{2}$
