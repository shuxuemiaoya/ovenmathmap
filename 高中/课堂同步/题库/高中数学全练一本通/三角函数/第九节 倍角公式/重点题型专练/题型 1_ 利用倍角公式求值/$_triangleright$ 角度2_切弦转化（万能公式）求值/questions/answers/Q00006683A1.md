> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 因为 $\tan(\pi+\theta)=2$ ，由诱导公式得： $\tan(\pi+\theta)=\tan\theta=2$
> 所以 $\sin 2\theta = \frac{2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta} = \frac{2\tan\theta}{\tan^2\theta + 1} = \frac{4}{5}$
> $$
> \cos 2 \theta = \frac {\cos^ {2} \theta - \sin^ {2} \theta}{\cos^ {2} \theta + \sin^ {2} \theta} = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \theta}{1 + \tan^ {2} \theta} = \frac {1 - 4}{1 + 4} = - \frac {3}{5},
> $$
> $\sin \left(2\theta + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \sin 2\theta \cos \frac{\pi}{4} + \cos 2\theta \sin \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \left(-\frac{3}{5}\right) \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$ . 故答
> 案为: $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$
