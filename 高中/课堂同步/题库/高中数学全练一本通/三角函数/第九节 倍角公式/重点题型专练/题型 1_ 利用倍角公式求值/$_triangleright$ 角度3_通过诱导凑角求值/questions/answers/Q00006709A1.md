> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 因为 $\theta\in\left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，则 $\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)$ ,
> 可知 $\cos\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)<0,\sin\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)>0$
> 且 $\cos \left(2\theta +\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 2\cos^2\left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) - 1 = -\frac{3}{5}$ ，解得 $\cos \left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5},$
> 则 $\sin \left(\theta +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\left(\theta + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)} = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$ ，可得
> $$
> \tan \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \frac {\sin \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{3}\right)}{\cos \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{3}\right)} = - 2
> $$
> 所以 $\tan\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\tan\left[\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}\right]=-\frac{1}{\tan\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)}=\frac{1}{2}$
