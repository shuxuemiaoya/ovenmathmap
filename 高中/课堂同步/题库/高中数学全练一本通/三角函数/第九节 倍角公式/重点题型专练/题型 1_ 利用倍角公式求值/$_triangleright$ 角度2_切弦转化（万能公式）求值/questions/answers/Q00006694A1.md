> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{56}{65}$
>
> **【解析】**
> $\because \tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$ $\therefore \sin \alpha = \frac{2\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}}{1 + \tan^2\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{2\times\frac{1}{2}}{1 + (\frac{1}{2})^2} = \frac{4}{5},\cos \alpha = \frac{1 - \tan^2\frac{\alpha}{2}}{1 + \tan^2\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{1 - (\frac{1}{2})^2}{1 + (\frac{1}{2})^2} = \frac{3}{5},$ $\because \alpha ,\beta \in (0,\pi),\cos \alpha >0:\therefore \alpha \in (0,\frac{\pi}{2}):\therefore \alpha -\beta \in (-\pi ,\frac{\pi}{2}),$ $\because \sin (\alpha -\beta) = \frac{5}{13} >0:\therefore \alpha -\beta \in (0,\frac{\pi}{2}),\therefore \cos (\alpha -\beta) = \frac{12}{13}$ $\therefore \cos \beta = \cos (-\beta) = \cos (\alpha -\beta -\alpha) = \cos (\alpha -\beta)\cos \alpha +\sin (\alpha -\beta)\sin \alpha$ $= \frac{12}{13}\times \frac{3}{5} +\frac{5}{13}\times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{56}{65}$
