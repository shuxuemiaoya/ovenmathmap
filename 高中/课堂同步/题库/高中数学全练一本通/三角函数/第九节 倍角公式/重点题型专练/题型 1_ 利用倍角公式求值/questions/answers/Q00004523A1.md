> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{4}{5}$
解析：∵ $\tan(\pi+\theta)=2,\therefore\tan\theta=2,$
$$
\cos \left(2 \theta + \frac {\pi}{2}\right) = - \sin 2 \theta = - \frac {2 \sin \theta \cos \theta}{\sin^ {2} \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta} = - \frac {2 \tan \theta}{1 + \tan^ {2} \theta} = - \frac {2 \times 2}{1 + 2 ^ {2}} = - \frac {4}{5}.
$$
故答案为: $-\frac{4}{5}$
