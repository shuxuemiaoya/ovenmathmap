> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 因为 $\tan\alpha=3,\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha=1$ 所以 $\frac{2 + \cos^2\alpha}{\sin 2\alpha} = \frac{2(\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha) + \cos^2\alpha}{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha} = \frac{2\tan^2\alpha + 3}{2\tan\alpha} = \frac{2\times 3^2 + 3}{2\times 3} = \frac{7}{2}$ .
>
> 故选 **D**.
