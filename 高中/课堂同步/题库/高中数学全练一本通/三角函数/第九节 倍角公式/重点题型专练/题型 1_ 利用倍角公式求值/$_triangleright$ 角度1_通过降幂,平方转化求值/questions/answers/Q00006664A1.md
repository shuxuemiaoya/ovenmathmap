> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\cos^{2}\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{1+\cos\left[2\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right]}{2}=\frac{1+\cos\left(2\alpha-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{2}$
> $= \frac{1 + \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - 2\alpha\right)}{2} = \frac{1 + \sin 2\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 + \frac{1}{4}}{2} = \frac{5}{8}$
>
> 故选 **A**.
