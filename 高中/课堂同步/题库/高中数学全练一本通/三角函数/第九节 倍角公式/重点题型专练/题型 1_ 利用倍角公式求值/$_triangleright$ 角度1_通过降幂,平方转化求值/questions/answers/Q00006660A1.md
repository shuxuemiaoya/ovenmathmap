> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\cos^2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left[\cos \left(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{2}\right) + 1\right] = \frac{1}{2}\left[-\sin 2\alpha +1\right].$
> $= \frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{2}{3} + 1\right) = \frac{1}{6}$
>
> 故选 **B**.
