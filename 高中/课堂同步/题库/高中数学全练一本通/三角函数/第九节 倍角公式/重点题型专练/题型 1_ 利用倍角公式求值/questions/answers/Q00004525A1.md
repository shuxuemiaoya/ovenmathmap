> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{3}{5}$
解析: $\because \tan\alpha\cdot\tan\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\tan\alpha\cdot\frac{\tan\alpha+1}{1-\tan\alpha}=\frac{2}{3}$ ,
∴ $3\tan^{2}\alpha + 5\tan\alpha - 2 = 0$
解得 $\tan\alpha=-2$ 或 $\tan\alpha=\frac{1}{3}$ （舍去），
所以 $\cos 2\alpha = \cos^2\alpha -\sin^2\alpha = \frac{\cos^2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha + \sin^2\alpha} = \frac{1 - \tan^2\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha} = -\frac{3}{5}.$
