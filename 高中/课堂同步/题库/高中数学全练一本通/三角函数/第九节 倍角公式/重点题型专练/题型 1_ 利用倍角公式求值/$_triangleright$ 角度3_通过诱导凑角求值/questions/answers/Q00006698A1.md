> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac13$
>
> **【解析】**
> $= -\cos 2\left(\alpha +\frac{2\pi}{7}\right) = 2\sin^2\left(\alpha +\frac{2\pi}{7}\right) - 1 = 2\times \left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\right)^2 -1 = \frac{1}{3}.$ 故答案为： $\frac{1}{3}$
