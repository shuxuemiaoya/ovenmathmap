> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\cos^2\left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1 + \cos\left(2\alpha - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{2} = \frac{1 + \sin 2\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 + 2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{2},$
> 因为角 $\alpha$ 的顶点在原点, 始边与 $x$ 轴的非负半轴重合, 终边经过点 $P(2, -1)$ ,
> 所以 $\sin \alpha = \frac{-1}{\sqrt{2^2 + (-1)^2}} = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}, \cos \alpha = \frac{2}{\sqrt{2^2 + (-1)^2}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}$
> 因此 $\cos^{2}\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{1+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{2}=\frac{1+2\times\left(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\right)\times\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}}{2}=\frac{1}{10}$ ，
>
> 故选 **A**
