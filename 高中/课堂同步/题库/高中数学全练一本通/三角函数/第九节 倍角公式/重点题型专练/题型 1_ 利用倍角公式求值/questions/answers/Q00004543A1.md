> **【解析】**
> $\frac{4}{5}$
解析： $\cos \left(2\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \cos \left[2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) - \pi \right] = -\cos \left(2\alpha +\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)$
$$
= - 2 \cos^ {2} \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) + 1
$$
由于 $\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$ ，所以 $\cos \left(2\alpha +\frac{2\pi}{3}\right) = 2\cos^2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) - 1 = -\frac{4}{5},$
所以 $\cos\left(2\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{4}{5}$
