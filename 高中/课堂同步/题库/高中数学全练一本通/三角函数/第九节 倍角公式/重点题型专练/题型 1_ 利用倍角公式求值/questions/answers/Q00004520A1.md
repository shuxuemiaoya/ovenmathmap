> **【解析】**
> $\frac{24}{25}$
解析: 因为 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角, 所以 $0 < \sin \alpha < 1, -1 < \cos \alpha < 0$ ,
$$
\sin \alpha \sqrt {\frac {1 - \cos \alpha}{1 + \cos \alpha}} + \cos \alpha \sqrt {\frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{1 + \sin \alpha}}
$$
$$
= \sin \alpha \sqrt {\frac {(1 - \cos \alpha) ^ {2}}{(1 + \cos \alpha) (1 - \cos \alpha)}} + \cos \alpha \sqrt {\frac {(1 - \sin \alpha) ^ {2}}{(1 + \sin \alpha) (1 - \sin \alpha)}}
$$
$$
= \sin \alpha \frac {1 - \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha} - \cos \alpha \frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = 1 - \cos \alpha - 1 + \sin \alpha = \frac {7}{5}
$$
所以 $\sin\alpha-\cos\alpha=\frac{7}{5}$ ，两边平方可得 $\sin2\alpha=-\frac{24}{25}$ ，
则 $\cos \left(\frac{3\pi}{2} - 2\alpha\right) = -\sin 2\alpha = \frac{24}{25}$ . 故答案为: $\frac{24}{25}$ .
