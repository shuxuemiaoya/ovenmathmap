> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: $\because \sin2\alpha=\frac{1}{3}$ ,
> $\therefore \cos^2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1 + \cos\left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{2} = \frac{1 - \sin 2\alpha}{2} = \frac{1 - \frac{1}{3}}{2} = \frac{1}{3}.$
>
> 故选 **B**.
