> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{3}{5}$
> 解析:由 $\frac{\tan\alpha}{\tan\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}=\frac{\tan\alpha}{\frac{\tan\alpha+1}{1-\tan\alpha}}=\frac{\tan\alpha(1-\tan\alpha)}{\tan\alpha+1}=-\frac{2}{3}$
> 得 $3\tan^2\alpha - 5\tan \alpha - 2 = 0$
> $$
> \tan \alpha = 2
> $$
> $$
> \tan \alpha = - \frac {1}{3}
> $$
> 因为 $\alpha$ 为锐角, 故 $\tan \alpha = 2$ .
> $\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\cos2\alpha=\frac{\cos2\alpha}{1}=\frac{\cos^{2}\alpha-\sin^{2}\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha+\sin^{2}\alpha}=\frac{1-\tan^{2}\alpha}{1+\tan^{2}\alpha}=\frac{1-4}{1+4}=-\frac{3}{5}$ 故答案为： $-\frac{3}{5}$
