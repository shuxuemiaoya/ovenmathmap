> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{5}{6}$
解析:由 $2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}=2\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)-\frac{\pi}{2}$ ,
$$
\sin \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left[ 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) - \frac {\pi}{2} \right] = - \cos \left[ 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \right]
$$
$$
= - \left[ 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \right] = - \left(1 - \frac {1}{6}\right) = - \frac {5}{6}.
$$
