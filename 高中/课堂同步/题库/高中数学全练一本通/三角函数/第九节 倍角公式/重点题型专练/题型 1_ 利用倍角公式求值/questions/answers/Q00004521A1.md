> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\sin2\alpha=2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha}=\frac{\frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha}}{\frac{\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha}}$ $=\frac{2\tan\alpha}{\tan^{2}\alpha+1}=\frac{2\times3}{3^{2}+1}=\frac{3}{5}$ . 故选：A.
