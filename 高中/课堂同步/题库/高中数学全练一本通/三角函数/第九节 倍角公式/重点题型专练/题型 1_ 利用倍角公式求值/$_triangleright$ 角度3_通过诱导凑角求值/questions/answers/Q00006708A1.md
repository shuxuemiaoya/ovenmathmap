> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{9}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 由 $0<\alpha<\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，得 $\frac{\pi}{8}<\alpha+\frac{\pi}{8}<\frac{5\pi}{8}$ ，
> 又 $\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{8}\right) = \frac{1}{3}$ ，所以
> $$
> \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{8}\right) = \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{8}\right)} = \sqrt {1 - \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}
> $$
> 因为
> $$
> \sin \left(\frac {3 \pi}{4} - 2 \alpha\right) = \sin \left[ \pi - 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{8}\right) \right] = \sin 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{8}\right) = 2 \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{8}\right) \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{8}\right)
> $$
> 所以 $2\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{8}\right)\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{8}\right) = 2\times \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}\times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{9}$
