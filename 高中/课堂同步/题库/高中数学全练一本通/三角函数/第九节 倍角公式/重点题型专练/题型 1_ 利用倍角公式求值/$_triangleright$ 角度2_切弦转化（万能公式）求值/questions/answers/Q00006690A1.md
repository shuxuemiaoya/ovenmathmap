> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> - $\frac{2}{5}$ 解析：因为 $\frac{1 - \tan\theta}{2 + \tan\theta} = 1$ ，所以 $\tan \theta = -\frac{1}{2}$ ，所以 $1 + \sin 2\theta - \cos 2\theta = 1 + 2\sin\theta\cos\theta - (1 - 2\sin^2\theta) = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta + 2\sin^2\theta = \frac{2\tan\theta\cos\theta + 2\tan^2\theta}{\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta} = \frac{2\tan\theta + 2\tan^2\theta}{\tan^2\theta + 1} = \frac{2\times (-\frac{1}{2}) + 2\times (-\frac{1}{2})^2}{(-\frac{1}{2})^2 + 1} = -\frac{2}{5}$ . 故答案为：- $\frac{2}{5}$
