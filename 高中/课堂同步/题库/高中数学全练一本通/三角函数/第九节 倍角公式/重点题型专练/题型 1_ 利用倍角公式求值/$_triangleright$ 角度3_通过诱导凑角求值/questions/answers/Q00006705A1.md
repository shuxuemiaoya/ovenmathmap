> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{7}{8}$
> 解析： $\because \cos \left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \sin \left(\frac{2\pi}{3} +x\right) = \cos \left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{2} +x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$
> $$
> = 2 \cos \left(x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \frac {1}{2}
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \cos \left(x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \frac {1}{4}
> $$
> $$
> \sin \left(2 x - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left[ 2 \left(x + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) - \frac {\pi}{2} \right] = - \cos \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{3}\right)
> $$
> $\cos \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = 2\cos^2 \left(x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) - 1 = -\frac{7}{8}$ ,
>
> 故选 **A**
