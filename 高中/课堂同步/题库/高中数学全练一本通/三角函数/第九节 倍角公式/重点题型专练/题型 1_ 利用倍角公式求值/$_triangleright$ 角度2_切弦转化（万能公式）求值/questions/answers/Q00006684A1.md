> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:由 $\tan\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=-3$ , 得 $\frac{\tan\alpha+1}{1-\tan\alpha}=-3$ , 解得 $\tan\alpha=2$
> 所以 $\cos^{2}\alpha + 2\sin2\alpha = \frac{\cos^{2}\alpha + 4\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha + \sin^{2}\alpha} = \frac{1 + 4\tan\alpha}{1 + \tan^{2}\alpha} = \frac{1 + 8}{1 + 4} = \frac{9}{5}$ .
>
> 故选 **A**.
