> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** (1) $x = \arcsin \frac{2}{5}$ ; (2) $-\arcsin \frac{1}{4}$ ; (3) $\pi + \arcsin \frac{1}{4}$ ; (4) $x = \arcsin \frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}$ 或 $x = \pi - \arcsin \frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}$ .
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）∵ $\sin x = \frac{2}{5}, x \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$
> ∴ $x = \arcsin \frac{2}{5}$
> (2) $\because \sin x = -\frac{1}{4}, -\frac{\pi}{2} < x < \frac{\pi}{2}, \therefore -\frac{\pi}{2} < x < 0$
> $\because \arcsin \frac{1}{4} \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$
> ∴ $x = -\arcsin \frac{1}{4}$ .
> (3) $\because \sin x = -\frac{1}{4}, \pi < x < \frac{3\pi}{2}$ ,
> $\because \arcsin\frac{1}{4} \in (0,\frac{\pi}{2})$
> ∴ $x = \pi + \arcsin \frac{1}{4}$ .
> (4) $\because \sin x = \frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}, x \in [0, \pi]$ ,
> $$
> \because \arcsin \frac {\sqrt {7}}{4} \in (0, \frac {\pi}{2})
> $$
> ∴ $x = \arcsin \frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}$ 或 $x = \pi - \arcsin \frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}$ .
