> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $x = \arccos \frac{1}{3}$ ；（2） $x = -\arccos \frac{2}{3}$ ；（3） $x = \pi +\arccos \frac{1}{3}$ (4) $\pi - \arccos \frac{4}{5}$ ; (5) $x = \arccos \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} - \pi$ .
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）∵ $\cos x=\frac{1}{3}>0,\therefore x\in(0,\frac{\pi}{2})$
> ∴ $x = \arccos \frac{1}{3}$ ;
> (2) $\because \cos x = \frac{2}{3}, x \in [-\pi, 0]$
> $\because \cos x = \frac{2}{3} > 0$
> $\therefore x \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{2}, 0\right]$ ,
> $\because \arccos \frac{2}{3} \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$
> ∴ $x = -\arccos \frac{2}{3}$ ;
> (3) $\because \cos x = -\frac{1}{3} < 0, \therefore x \in (\pi, \frac{3\pi}{2})$
> $\because \arccos \frac{1}{3} \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$
> 则 $x = \pi +\arccos {\frac{1}{3}}$
> (4) $\because \cos x = -\frac{4}{5}, \frac{\pi}{2} < x < \pi$
> $\because \arccos \frac{4}{5} \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$
> ∴ $x = \pi - \arccos \frac{4}{5}$ ;
> (5) $\because \cos x = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} < 0, \therefore x \in (-\pi, -\frac{\pi}{2})$
> $\because \arccos \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$
> $\therefore x = \arccos \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} - \pi .$
