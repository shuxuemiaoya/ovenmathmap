> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{3}{4}$
解析: 因为 $\tan(\alpha + \beta) = 3$ , $\tan\left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = -3$ ,
所以 $\tan \left(\beta -\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \tan \left[\left(\alpha +\beta\right) - \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right]$
$$
= \frac {\tan (\alpha + \beta) - \tan \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{4}\right)}{1 + \tan (\alpha + \beta) \tan \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{4}\right)} = \frac {3 + 3}{1 + 3 \times (- 3)} = - \frac {3}{4}.
$$
故答案为: $-\frac{3}{4}$
