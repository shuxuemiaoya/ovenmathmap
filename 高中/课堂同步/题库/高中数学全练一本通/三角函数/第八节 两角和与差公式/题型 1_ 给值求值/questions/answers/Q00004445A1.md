> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ 解析: 因为 $0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ , $-\frac{\pi}{2} < \beta < 0$ ,
所以 $\frac{\pi}{4} < \alpha + \frac{\pi}{4} < \frac{3\pi}{4}$ , $\frac{\pi}{4} < \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\beta}{2} < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ,
因为 $\cos (\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{1}{3}$ , $\cos (\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\beta}{2}) = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ ,
所以 $\sin (\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \sqrt{1 - (\frac{1}{3})^2} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ , $\sin (\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\beta}{2}) = \sqrt{1 - (\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3})^2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ,
所以 $\cos (\alpha + \frac{\beta}{2}) = \cos [(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}) - (\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\beta}{2})]$ $= \cos (\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4})\cos (\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\beta}{2}) + \sin (\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4})\sin (\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\beta}{2})$ $= \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} + \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ . 故答案为: $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$
