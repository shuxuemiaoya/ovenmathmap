> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\pi}{3}$ 解析:依题意 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&0<\beta<\frac{\pi}{4}\\ &\frac{\pi}{4}<\alpha<\frac{\pi}{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ ， $\left\{\begin{aligned}&0<2\beta<\frac{\pi}{2}\\ &\frac{\pi}{4}<\alpha<\frac{\pi}{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ ， $\left\{\begin{aligned}-\frac{\pi}{4}<-\beta<0\\-\frac{\pi}{2}<-2\beta<0\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{\pi}{4}<2\alpha-\beta<\pi\\-\frac{\pi}{4}<\alpha-2\beta<\frac{\pi}{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $\sin(2\alpha-\beta)=\sqrt{1-\left(-\frac{11}{14}\right)^{2}}=\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{14},\cos(\alpha-2\beta)=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{7}\right)^{2}}=\frac{1}{7}$ 所以 $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\cos[(2\alpha-\beta)-(\alpha-2\beta)]=\cos(2\alpha-\beta)\cos(\alpha-2\beta)+\sin(2\alpha-\beta)\sin(\alpha-2\beta)=-\frac{11}{14}\times\frac{1}{7}+\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{14}\times\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{7}=\frac{1}{2},$ 由于 $\frac{\pi}{4}<\alpha+\beta<\frac{3\pi}{4}$ ，所以 $\alpha+\beta=\frac{\pi}{3}$ .
故答案为： $\frac{\pi}{3}$
