> **【解析】**
> $\frac{3\pi}{4}$
解析： $\because \alpha, \beta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，且 $\alpha < \beta$ ， $\therefore \alpha - \beta \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, 0\right), 2\alpha \in (0, \pi)$ ， $\therefore \sin (\alpha - \beta) = -\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}, \sin 2\alpha = \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$ ， $\therefore \cos (\alpha + \beta) = \cos [2\alpha - (\alpha - \beta)] = \cos 2\alpha \cos (\alpha - \beta) + \sin 2\alpha \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10} \times \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} + \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10} \times \left(-\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\right) = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ . $\because \alpha + \beta \in (0, \pi)$ ， $\therefore \alpha + \beta = \frac{3\pi}{4}$ .
