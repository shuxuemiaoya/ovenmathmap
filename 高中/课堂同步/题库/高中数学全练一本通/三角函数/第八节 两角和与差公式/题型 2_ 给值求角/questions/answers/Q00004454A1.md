> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\pi}{4}$ 解析： $\because \alpha \in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right),\beta \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2},0\right),$ $\therefore \alpha -\beta \in (0,\pi).$ $\because \cos (\alpha -\beta) = \frac{3}{5},\therefore \sin (\alpha -\beta) = \frac{4}{5}.$ $\because \beta \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2},0\right),\sin \beta = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10},\therefore \cos \beta = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}.$ $\therefore \sin \alpha = \sin \left[(\alpha -\beta) + \beta \right]$ $= \sin (\alpha -\beta)\cos \beta +\cos (\alpha -\beta)\sin \beta$ $= \frac{4}{5}\times \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10} +\frac{3}{5}\times \left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},$ $\therefore \alpha = \frac{\pi}{4}.$
