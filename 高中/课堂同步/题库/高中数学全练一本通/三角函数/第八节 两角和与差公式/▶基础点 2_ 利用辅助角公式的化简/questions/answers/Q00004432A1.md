> 见解析
解析：（1） $y=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin x+\frac{1}{2}\cos x=\sin x\cos\frac{\pi}{6}+\cos x\sin\frac{\pi}{6}=\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ ， $\tan\varphi=\frac{b}{a}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ (2) $y=\sqrt{3}\sin x+\cos x$ , $=2(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin x+\frac{1}{2}\cos x)$ , $=2(\sin x\cos\frac{\pi}{6}+\cos x\sin\frac{\pi}{6})$ , $=2\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ , $\tan\varphi=\frac{b}{a}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ (3) $y=\sin x+\cos x=\sqrt{2}\left(\sin x\cdot\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}+\cos x\cdot\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$ $=\sqrt{2}\left(\sin x\cdot\cos\frac{\pi}{4}+\cos x\cdot\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\sqrt{2}\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ , $\tan\varphi=\frac{b}{a}=1$ (4) $y=\sqrt{2}\cos x-\sqrt{6}\sin x$ $=2\sqrt{2}\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin x+\frac{1}{2}\cos x\right)$ , $=2\sqrt{2}\left(\cos\frac{5\pi}{6}\sin x+\sin\frac{5\pi}{6}\cos x\right)$ , $=2\sqrt{2}\sin\left(x+\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)$ , $\tan\varphi=\frac{b}{a}=-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ (5) 提出系数 $\sqrt{\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{6}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{6}\right)^{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ , $y=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{6}\sin\frac{x}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{6}}{6}\cos\frac{x}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2}\sin\frac{x}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos\frac{x}{2}\right)$ $=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{3}\sin\frac{x}{2}+\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\cos\frac{x}{2}\right)$ $=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}\sin\left(\frac{x}{2}+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ , $\tan\varphi=\frac{b}{a}=\sqrt{3}$ (6) 提出系数 $\sqrt{12^{2}+5^{2}}=13$ , $y=12\sin x+5\cos x=13\left(\frac{12}{13}\sin x+\frac{5}{13}\cos x\right)$ $=13\left(\cos\varphi\sin x+\sin\varphi\cos x\right)$ $=13\sin(x+\varphi)$ , $\tan\varphi=\frac{b}{a}=\frac{5}{12}$ (7) $y=\sin(x+\frac{\pi}{3})-\sin x$ $=\sin x\cos\frac{\pi}{3}+\cos x\sin\frac{\pi}{3}-\sin x$ $=\frac{1}{2}\sin x+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos x-\sin x$ $=-\frac{1}{2}\sin x+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos x$ $=\cos\frac{2\pi}{3}\sin x+\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\cos x$ $=\sin\left(x+\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)$ , $\tan\varphi=\frac{b}{a}=-\sqrt{3}$ .
(8) $y=\sin x+\cos\left(x-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ $=\sin x+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos x+\frac{1}{2}\sin x$ $=\frac{3}{2}\sin x+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos x=\sqrt{3}\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ , $\tan\varphi=\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
