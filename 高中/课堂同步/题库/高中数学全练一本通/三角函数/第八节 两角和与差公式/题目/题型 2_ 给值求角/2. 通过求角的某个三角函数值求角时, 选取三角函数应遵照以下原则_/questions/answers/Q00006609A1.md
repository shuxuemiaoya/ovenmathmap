> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> - $\frac{\pi}{4}$ 解析：因为 $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{3}{5}$ ，所以 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{4}{5}$ 又由 $0 <   \alpha +\beta <  \pi$ 且 $\cos (\alpha +\beta) = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$ ，可得 $\sin (\alpha +\beta) = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$ $\sin (\alpha -\beta) = \sin [2\alpha -(\alpha +\beta)] = \sin 2\alpha \cos (\alpha +\beta) - \cos 2\alpha \sin (\alpha +\beta)$ $= \frac{4}{5}\times (-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}) - \frac{3}{5}\times \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},$ 因为α，β为锐角，可得α-β∈（-π/2，π/2），所以α-β=-π/4.
