> 解析：∵ $\alpha,\beta$ 为锐角， $\tan\alpha=\frac{3}{4},\cos(\alpha+\beta)=-\frac{4}{5}$ ∴ $\sin\alpha=\frac{3}{5},\cos\alpha=\frac{4}{5},\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\frac{3}{5}$ ∴ $\cos(2a+\beta)=\cos a\tan(\alpha+\beta)-s i n a s i n(a+\beta)$
> $= \frac{4}{5}\times \left(-\frac{4}{5}\right) - \frac{3}{5}\times \frac{3}{5} = -1$ 又 $2\alpha +\beta \in \left(0,\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ $\therefore 2\alpha +\beta = \pi$
