> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\pi}{4}$ 解析：因为 $\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{7},\tan \beta = \frac{1}{3}\Rightarrow \tan 2\beta = \frac{\frac{2}{3}}{1 - \frac{1}{9}} = \frac{3}{4}$ ，所以 $\tan (\alpha +2\beta) = \frac{\frac{1}{7} + \frac{3}{4}}{1 - \frac{1}{7}\times\frac{3}{4}} = \frac{4 + 21}{28 - 5} = 1$ ，且 $0 <   \tan 2\beta = \frac{3}{4} <  1\Rightarrow 2\beta \in (0,\frac{\pi}{2})$ 所以 $\alpha +2\beta \in (0,\frac{3\pi}{4})$ ，则 $\alpha +2\beta = \frac{\pi}{4}$
