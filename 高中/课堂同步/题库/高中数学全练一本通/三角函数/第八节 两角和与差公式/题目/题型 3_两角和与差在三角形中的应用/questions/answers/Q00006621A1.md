> **【答案】** 1
>
> **【解析】**
> $\tan C = \tan(\pi - A - B) = -\tan(A + B) = -\frac{\tan A + \tan B}{1 - \tan A\tan B} = -\frac{2 + 3}{1 - 2 \times 3} = 1$ 故答案为：1.
