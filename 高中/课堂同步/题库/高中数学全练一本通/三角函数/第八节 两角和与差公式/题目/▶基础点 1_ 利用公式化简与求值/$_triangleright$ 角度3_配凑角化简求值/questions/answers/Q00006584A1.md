> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$ ；（2） $\frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$ ；（3） $\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$ ；（4） $\frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\cos 75^{\circ} = \cos (45^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}) = \cos 45^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ} - \sin 45^{\circ}\sin 30^{\circ}$
> $$
> = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {6} - \sqrt {2}}{4}
> $$
> (2) $\sin 75^{\circ} = \sin (45^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} +\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$
> (3) $\sin 15^{\circ} = \sin (45^{\circ} - 30^{\circ}) = \sin 45^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ} - \cos 45^{\circ}\sin 30^{\circ}$
> $$
> \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cdot \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cdot \frac {1}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {6} - \sqrt {2}}{4}
> $$
> (4) $\sin 195^{\circ} = \sin (150^{\circ} + 45^{\circ}) = \sin 150^{\circ}\cos 45^{\circ} + \cos 150^{\circ}\sin 45^{\circ}$
> $$
> = \frac {1}{2} \cdot \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} + \left(- \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}\right) \cdot \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {2} - \sqrt {6}}{4}
> $$
