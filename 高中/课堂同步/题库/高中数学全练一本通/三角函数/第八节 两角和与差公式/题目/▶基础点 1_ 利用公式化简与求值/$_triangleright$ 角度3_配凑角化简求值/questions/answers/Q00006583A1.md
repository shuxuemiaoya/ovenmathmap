> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\frac{1}{2}$ ；（2） $\sqrt{3}$ ；（3） $\frac{1}{2}$ ；（4） $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ；（5） $2 + \sqrt{3}$ ；（6） $\frac{1}{2}$
> (7) $2 - \sqrt{3}$ ; (8) $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ .
> 解析：（1）原式 $= \frac{\sin(20^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}) - \sin 20^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}}$
> $$
> = \frac {\sin 2 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 2 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} - \sin 2 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 2 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\cos 2 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 2 0 ^ {\circ}} = \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {1}{2}.
> $$
> (2) $\frac{2\sin 80^{\circ} - \sin 20^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}} = \frac{2\sin(60^{\circ} + 20^{\circ}) - \sin 20^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{2\sin 60^{\circ}\cos 20^{\circ} + 2\cos 60^{\circ}\sin 20^{\circ} - \sin 20^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}}$
> $$
> = \frac {\sqrt {3} \cos 2 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \sin 2 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 2 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\sqrt {3} \cos 2 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 2 0 ^ {\circ}} = \sqrt {3}.
> $$
> （3）由 $\frac{\sin57^{\circ}-\sin27^{\circ}\cos30^{\circ}}{\cos27^{\circ}}=\frac{\sin(30^{\circ}+27^{\circ})-\sin27^{\circ}\cos30^{\circ}}{\cos27^{\circ}}$
> $$
> = \frac {\sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 2 7 ^ {\circ} + \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 2 7 ^ {\circ} - \sin 2 7 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 2 7 ^ {\circ}}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 2 7 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 2 7 ^ {\circ}} = \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {1}{2}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {\sin 5 8 ^ {\circ} - \sin 1 3 ^ {\circ} \cos 4 5 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 3 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\sin (4 5 ^ {\circ} + 1 3 ^ {\circ}) - \sin 1 3 ^ {\circ} \cos 4 5 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 3 ^ {\circ}} \tag {4}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\sin 4 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 1 3 ^ {\circ} + \cos 4 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 3 ^ {\circ} - \sin 1 3 ^ {\circ} \cos 4 5 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 3 ^ {\circ}},
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {\sin 4 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 1 3 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 3 ^ {\circ}} = \sin 4 5 ^ {\circ} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},
> $$
> (5) 原式
> $$
> = \frac {\sin (7 5 ^ {\circ} - 8 ^ {\circ}) + \cos 7 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 8 ^ {\circ}}{\cos (7 5 ^ {\circ} - 8 ^ {\circ}) - \sin 7 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 8 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\sin 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 ^ {\circ} - \cos 7 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 8 ^ {\circ} + \cos 7 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 8 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 ^ {\circ} + \sin 7 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 8 ^ {\circ} - \sin 7 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 8 ^ {\circ}}
> $$
> $$
> = \tan 7 5 ^ {\circ} = \tan (4 5 ^ {\circ} + 3 0 ^ {\circ}) = \frac {\tan 4 5 ^ {\circ} + \tan 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{1 - \tan 4 5 ^ {\circ} \tan 3 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {1 + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}}{1 - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}} = \frac {3 + \sqrt {3}}{3 - \sqrt {3}} = 2 + \sqrt {3}.
> $$
> （6）原式 $= \frac{2\sin(17^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}) - \sqrt{3}\sin17^{\circ}}{2\cos17^{\circ}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}\sin17^{\circ} + \cos17^{\circ} - \sqrt{3}\sin17^{\circ}}{2\cos17^{\circ}} = \frac{1}{2}$
> (7) $\frac{\sin 9^{\circ} + \sin 6^{\circ}\cos 15^{\circ}}{\cos 9^{\circ} - \sin 6^{\circ}\sin 15^{\circ}} = \frac{\sin (15^{\circ} - 6^{\circ}) + \sin 6^{\circ}\cos 15^{\circ}}{\cos (15^{\circ} - 6^{\circ}) - \sin 6^{\circ}\sin 15^{\circ}} = \frac{\sin 15^{\circ}\cos 6^{\circ}}{\cos 15^{\circ}\cos 6^{\circ}} = \tan 15^{\circ}$
> $$
> = \tan (4 5 ^ {\circ} - 3 0 ^ {\circ}) = \frac {1 - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}}{1 + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}} = \frac {3 - \sqrt {3}}{3 + \sqrt {3}} = \frac {(3 - \sqrt {3}) ^ {2}}{(3 + \sqrt {3}) (3 - \sqrt {3})} = \frac {1 2 - 6 \sqrt {3}}{6} = 2 - \sqrt {3}
> $$
> （8）原式 $= \sin (30^{\circ} - 10^{\circ}) + \sin (30^{\circ} + 10^{\circ}) + \sin 60^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}$ $= \sin 30^{\circ}\cos 10^{\circ} - \sin 10^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ} + \sin 30^{\circ}\cos 10^{\circ} + \sin 10^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ}+$ $\sin 60^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}$ $= \cos 10^{\circ} + \sin 60^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}$ $= \sin 60^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
