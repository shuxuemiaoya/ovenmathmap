> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$
> 解析: 因为 $\frac{\pi}{4}<\alpha<\frac{3\pi}{4}$ ，所以 $0<\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4}<\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，
> 又 $\sin \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{4}{5}$ ，所以 $\cos \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}\right)} = \frac{3}{5},$
> 所以 $\cos \alpha = \cos \left[\left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{4}\right) + \frac{\pi}{4}\right] = \cos \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\cos \frac{\pi}{4} -\sin \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\sin \frac{\pi}{4}$
> $$
> = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \left(\frac {3}{5} - \frac {4}{5}\right) = - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{1 0}
> $$
