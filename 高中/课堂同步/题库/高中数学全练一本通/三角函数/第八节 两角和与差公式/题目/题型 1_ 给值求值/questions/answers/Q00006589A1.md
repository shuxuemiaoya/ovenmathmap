> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$
> 解析: 因为 $\alpha$ 为锐角, 所以 $\alpha \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$ , 所以 $\alpha + \frac{\pi}{12} \in (\frac{\pi}{12}, \frac{7\pi}{12})$ ,
> 又因为 $\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{12}\right)=\frac{3}{5}$ ，所以 $\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{12}\right)=\frac{4}{5}$ ，
> 所以 $\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\cos\left(\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{12}\right)+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ $=\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{12}\right)\cos\frac{\pi}{4}-\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{12}\right)\sin\frac{\pi}{4}$ $=\frac{3}{5}\times\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}-\frac{4}{5}\times\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}=-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$ .
