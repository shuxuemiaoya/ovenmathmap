> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $-\frac{16}{65}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 因为 $\sin\alpha=\frac{4}{5}$ ， $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=-\frac{12}{13}$ ，且 $\alpha\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ， $\alpha+\beta\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)$ 。
> $$
> \cos \alpha = \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {3}{5}, \sin (\alpha + \beta) = \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} (\alpha + \beta)} = \frac {5}{1 3}
> $$
> 可得 $\cos \beta = \cos \left[\left(\alpha +\beta\right) - \alpha \right] = \cos (\alpha +\beta)\cos \alpha +\sin (\alpha +\beta)\sin \alpha$
> $$
> = \left(- \frac {1 2}{1 3}\right) \times \frac {3}{5} + \frac {5}{1 3} \times \frac {4}{5} = - \frac {1 6}{6 5}
> $$
> 即 $\cos\beta=-\frac{16}{65}$ . 故答案为: $-\frac{16}{65}$ .
