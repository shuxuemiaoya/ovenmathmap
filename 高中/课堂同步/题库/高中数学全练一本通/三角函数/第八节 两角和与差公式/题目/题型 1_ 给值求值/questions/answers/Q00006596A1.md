> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> } - \frac {5 6}{6 5}
> $$
> 解析: 因为 $\frac{\pi}{2}<\alpha<\frac{3\pi}{4}$ ， $\frac{\pi}{2}<\beta<\frac{3\pi}{4}$ ，则 $-\frac{3\pi}{4}<-\beta<-\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，故可得
> $$
> - \frac {\pi}{4} <   \alpha - \beta <   \frac {\pi}{4}
> $$
> 又 $\alpha >\beta$ ，则 $\alpha -\beta \in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{4}\right),\alpha +\beta \in \left(\pi ,\frac{3\pi}{2}\right);$
> 又 $\cos (\alpha -\beta) = \frac{12}{13}$ ，故可得
> $$
> \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac {5}{1 3}; \sin (\alpha + \beta) = - \frac {3}{5}, \cos (\alpha + \beta) = - \frac {4}{5}
> $$
> 则 $\sin 2\alpha = \sin \left[\left(\alpha +\beta\right) + \left(\alpha -\beta\right)\right]$
> $$
> = \sin (\alpha + \beta) \cos (\alpha - \beta) + \cos (\alpha + \beta) \sin (\alpha - \beta)
> $$
> $$
> = \left(- \frac {3}{5}\right) \times \frac {1 2}{1 3} + \left(- \frac {4}{5}\right) \times \frac {5}{1 3} = - \frac {5 6}{6 5}.
> $$
