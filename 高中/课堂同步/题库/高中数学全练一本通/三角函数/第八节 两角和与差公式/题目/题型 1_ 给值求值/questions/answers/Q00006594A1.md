> **【解析】**
> $\frac{33}{65}$
> 解析:∵锐角 $\alpha$ 满足 $\cos\alpha=\frac{12}{13}$ ,
> ∴ $\sin\alpha=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}\alpha}=\frac{5}{13}$ ，则 $\alpha\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ .
> $$
> \cos (2 \alpha + \beta) = \frac {3}{5}
> $$
> $$
> 2 \alpha + \beta \in \left(0, \frac {\pi}{2}\right), \sin (2 \alpha + \beta) = \sqrt {1 - \cos^ {2} (2 \alpha + \beta)} = \frac {4}{5}
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \sin (\alpha + \beta) = \sin [ (2 \alpha + \beta) - \alpha ] = \sin (2 \alpha + \beta) \cos \alpha - \cos (2 \alpha + \beta)
> $$
> $$
> \sin \alpha = \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {1 2}{1 3} - \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {5}{1 3} = \frac {3 3}{6 5}
> $$
