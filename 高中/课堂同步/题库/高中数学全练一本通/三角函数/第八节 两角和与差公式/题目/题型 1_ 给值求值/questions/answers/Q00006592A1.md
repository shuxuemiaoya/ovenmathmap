> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
>
> **【解析】**
> ∵ $\alpha$ 、 $\beta\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ， $-\beta\in\left(-\frac{\pi}{2},0\right)$ ，
> $$
> \therefore \sin \alpha = \sqrt {1 - \left(\frac {2 \sqrt {5}}{5}\right) ^ {2}} = \frac {\sqrt {5}}{5}, \alpha - \beta \in \left(- \frac {\pi}{2}, \frac {\pi}{2}\right)
> $$
> $$
> \because \sin (\alpha - \beta) = - \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{1 0} <   0,
> $$
> $\therefore \alpha -\beta \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2},0\right).$ $\therefore \cos (\alpha -\beta) = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}\right)^2} = \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}.$ $\therefore \cos \beta = \cos [\alpha -(\alpha -\beta)]$ $= \cos \alpha \cdot \cos (\alpha -\beta) + \sin \alpha \cdot \sin (\alpha -\beta)$ $= \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\times \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10} +\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}\times \left(-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$
