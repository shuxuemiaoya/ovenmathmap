> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{56}{65}$
> 解析：∵ $\alpha$ ， $\beta\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，
> $\therefore \alpha + \beta \in (0, \pi)$ , $\beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ .
> 又 $\because \cos (\alpha + \beta) = \frac{3}{5}$ , $\sin (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{5}{13}$ , $\therefore \sin (\alpha + \beta) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 (\alpha + \beta)} = \frac{4}{5}$ , $\cos (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4})} = \frac{12}{13}$ , $\therefore \cos (\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}) = \cos \left[ (\alpha + \beta) - \left( \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) \right]$ $= \cos (\alpha + \beta) \cos (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) + \sin (\alpha + \beta) \sin (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{12}{13} + \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{5}{13} = \frac{56}{65}$
