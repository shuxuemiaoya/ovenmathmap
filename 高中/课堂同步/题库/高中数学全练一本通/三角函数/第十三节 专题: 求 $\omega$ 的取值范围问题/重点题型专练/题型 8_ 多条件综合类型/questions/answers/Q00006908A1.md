> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\left(0,\frac{4}{3}\right]\cup\left[8,\frac{17}{2}\right]$ 解析：当 $x \in \left[\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{4}\right]$ 时， $\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3} \in \left[\frac{\pi}{6}\omega - \frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{4}\omega - \frac{\pi}{3}\right]$ ，
> 因为 $f(x)$ 在 $\left[\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{4}\right]$ 上单调递增，所以 $\left[\frac{\pi}{6}\omega-\frac{\pi}{3},\frac{\pi}{4}\omega-\frac{\pi}{3}\right]\subseteq[-\pi+2k\pi,2k\pi],k\in Z,$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\omega&\geq-4+12k\\ \omega&\leq\frac{4}{3}+8k\end{aligned}\right.,k\in Z,$ 即 $\omega\in[-4+12k,\frac{4}{3}+8k],k\in Z,$
> 因为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} -4 + 12k \leq \frac{4}{3} + 8k \\ \frac{4}{3} + 8k > 0 \end{array} \right.$ , $k \in \mathbf{Z}$ , 所以 $k \in \left(-\frac{2}{3}, \frac{4}{3}\right]$ , $k \in \mathbf{Z}$ , 故 $k = 0$ 或 1, 当 $k = 0$ 时, $\omega \in \left[-4, \frac{4}{3}\right]$ , 当 $k = 1$ 时 $\omega \in \left[8, \frac{28}{3}\right]$ ;
> 当 $x \in \left[\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{3}\right]$ 时, $\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3} \in \left[\frac{\pi}{4}\omega - \frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{3}\omega - \frac{\pi}{3}\right]$ , 因为 $f(x) \geq 0$ 恒成立,
> 所以 $\left[\frac{\pi}{4}\omega - \frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{3}\omega - \frac{\pi}{3}\right] \subseteq \left[-\frac{\pi}{2} + 2k_1\pi, \frac{\pi}{2} + 2k_1\pi\right], k_1 \in \mathbf{Z}$ ,
> 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \omega \geq -\frac{2}{3} + 8k_1 \\ \omega \leq \frac{5}{2} + 6k_1 \end{array} \right.$ , $k_1 \in \mathbf{Z}$ , 即 $\omega \in \left[-\frac{2}{3} + 8k_1, \frac{5}{2} + 6k_1\right], k_1 \in \mathbf{Z}$ ,
> 因为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} -\frac{2}{3} + 8k_1 \leq \frac{5}{2} + 6k_1 \\ \frac{5}{2} + 6k_1 > 0 \end{array} \right.$ , $k_1 \in \mathbf{Z}$ , 所以 $k_1 \in \left(-\frac{5}{12}, \frac{19}{12}\right], k_1 \in \mathbf{Z}$ ,
> 故 $k_1 = 0$ 或 1, 当 $k_1 = 0$ 时, $\omega \in \left[-\frac{2}{3}, \frac{5}{2}\right]$ , 当 $k_1 = 1$ 时 $\omega \in \left[\frac{22}{3}, \frac{17}{2}\right]$ ,
> 综上所述, 又因为 $\omega > 0$ , 当 $k = 0$ , $k_1 = 0$ 时, $\omega \in \left(0, \frac{4}{3}\right]$ ;
> 当 $k = 1$ , $k_1 = 1$ 时, $\omega \in \left[8, \frac{17}{2}\right]$ , 所以 $\omega \in \left(0, \frac{4}{3}\right] \cup \left[8, \frac{17}{2}\right]$
