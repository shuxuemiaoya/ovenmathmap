> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> f (x) = x ^ {2} - 6 x + 8, x \in [ 2, 4 ] \tag {2}
> $$
> $$
> : \because f (x + 2) = - f (x)
> $$
> $$
> \therefore f (x + 4) = - f (x + 2) = f (x).
> $$
> ∴ $f(x)$ 是周期为 4 的周期函数.
> $$
> (2) \because x \in [ 2, 4 ], \therefore - x \in [ - 4, - 2 ], \therefore 4 - x \in [ 0, 2 ]
> $$
> $$
> \therefore f (4 - x) = 2 (4 - x) - (4 - x) ^ {2} = - x ^ {2} + 6 x - 8.
> $$
> $$
> \because f (4 - x) = f (- x) = - f (x), \therefore - f (x) = - x ^ {2} + 6 x - 8,
> $$
> $$
> f (x) = x ^ {2} - 6 x + 8, x \in [ 2, 4 ].
> $$
