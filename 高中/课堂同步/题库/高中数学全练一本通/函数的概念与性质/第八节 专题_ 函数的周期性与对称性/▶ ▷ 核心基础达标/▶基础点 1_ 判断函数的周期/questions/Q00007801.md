【1】根据结论判断下列抽象函数的正周期：
① $f(x+2)=f(x)$ ; ② $f(x-3)=f(x)$ ;

$$
⑪ f (x) = \frac {1}{f (x - 4)};
$$

$$
f (x - 2) = \frac {1}{f (x + 3)}; \tag {⑫}
$$

$$
③ f (x + 6) - f (x) = 0;
$$

$$
④ f (x - 3) = f (x + 4);
$$

$$
f (x + 2) = \frac {2}{f (x - 4)}; \tag {13}
$$

$$
⑭ f (x) f (x + 3) = 1;
$$

$$
⑤ f (x + 4) = - f (x);
$$

$$
⑥ f (x) = - f (x - 2);
$$

$$
f (x + 1) f (x + 3) = 2 \tag {15}
$$

$$
f (x + 1) = - \frac {1}{f (x)}; \tag {16}
$$

$$
⑦ f (x) + f (x + 7) = 0;
$$

$$
⑧ f (x + 2) = - f (x + 7);
$$

$$
⑰ f (x - 1) = - \frac {1}{f (x + 3)};
$$

$$
⑱ f (x) = - \frac {3}{f (x + 3)};
$$

$$
⑨ f (x - 2) + f (x + 3) = 0;
$$

$$
⑩ f (x + 3) = \frac {1}{f (x)};
$$

$$
⑲ f (x) f (x + 2) = - 1;
$$

$$
⑳ f (x - 3) f (x + 2) = 2.
$$
