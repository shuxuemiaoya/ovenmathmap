> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $$
> x _ {1}, x _ {2} \in (- 1, + \infty)
> $$
> $$
> x _ {1} <   x _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> f \left(x _ {1}\right) - f \left(x _ {2}\right) = \frac {2 x _ {1} - 3}{x _ {1} + 1} - \frac {2 x _ {2} - 3}{x _ {2} + 1}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {(2 x _ {1} - 3) (x _ {2} + 1) - (2 x _ {2} - 3) (x _ {1} + 1)}{(x _ {1} + 1) (x _ {2} + 1)} = \frac {5 (x _ {1} - x _ {2})}{(x _ {1} + 1) (x _ {2} + 1)}
> $$
> $$
> x _ {1}, x _ {2} \in (- 1, + \infty), x _ {1} <   x _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> x _ {1} - x _ {2} <   0, x _ {1} + 1 > 0, x _ {2} + 1 > 0
> $$
> $$
> f \left(x _ {1}\right) - f \left(x _ {2}\right) <   0
> $$
> $$
> f \left(x _ {1}\right) <   f \left(x _ {2}\right)
> $$
