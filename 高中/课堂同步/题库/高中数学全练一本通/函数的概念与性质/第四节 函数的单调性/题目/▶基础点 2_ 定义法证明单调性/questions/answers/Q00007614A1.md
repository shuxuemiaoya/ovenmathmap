> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $$
> x _ {1}, x _ {2} \in (0, + \infty)
> $$
> $$
> x _ {1} <   x _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> f \left(x _ {1}\right) - f \left(x _ {2}\right) = \frac {1}{x _ {1} ^ {2}} - \frac {1}{x _ {2} ^ {2}} = \frac {x _ {2} ^ {2} - x _ {1} ^ {2}}{x _ {1} ^ {2} \cdot x _ {2} ^ {2}} = \frac {\left(x _ {2} + x _ {1}\right) \left(x _ {2} - x _ {1}\right)}{x _ {1} ^ {2} \cdot x _ {2} ^ {2}}
> $$
> $$
> \because x _ {1}, x _ {2} \in (0, + \infty)
> $$
> $$
> x _ {1} <   x _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> \therefore x _ {1} ^ {2} \cdot x _ {2} ^ {2} > 0, x _ {2} + x _ {1} > 0, x _ {2} - x _ {1} > 0
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \frac {\left(x _ {2} + x _ {1}\right) \left(x _ {2} - x _ {1}\right)}{x _ {1} ^ {2} \cdot x _ {2} ^ {2}} > 0
> $$
> $$
> f \left(x _ {1}\right) - f \left(x _ {2}\right) > 0
> $$
> $$
> \therefore f (x) = \frac {1}{x ^ {2}} \text {在} (0, + \infty)
> $$
