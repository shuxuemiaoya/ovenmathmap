> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \forall x _ {1}, x _ {2} \in (0, + \infty), x _ {1} <   x _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> f \left(x _ {1}\right) - f \left(x _ {2}\right) = - x _ {1} + \frac {2}{x _ {1}} + x _ {2} - \frac {2}{x _ {2}} = \left(x _ {2} - x _ {1}\right) + \frac {2 \left(x _ {2} - x _ {1}\right)}{x _ {1} x _ {2}} = \left(x _ {2} - x _ {1}\right) \left(1 + \frac {2}{x _ {1} x _ {2}}\right)
> $$
> $$
> x _ {1}, x _ {2} \in (0, + \infty), x _ {1} <   x _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> x _ {2} - x _ {1} > 0, \left(1 + \frac {2}{x _ {1} x _ {2}}\right) > 0
> $$
> $$
> f \left(x _ {1}\right) > f \left(x _ {2}\right)
> $$
> $$
> (0, + \infty)
> $$
