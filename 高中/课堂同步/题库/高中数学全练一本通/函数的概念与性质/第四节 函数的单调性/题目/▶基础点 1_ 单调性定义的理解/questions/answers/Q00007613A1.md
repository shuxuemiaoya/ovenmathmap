> 解析：对于 A，“无穷多个”不能代表“所有”“任意”，所以 A 是假命题；对于 B， $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}(x \neq 0)$ ，当 $x \in (-∞, 0)$ 时， $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$ 是单调递减函数，当 $x \in (0, +∞)$ 时， $f(x) = \frac{1}{x}$ 是单调递减函数，
> $$
> f (x) = \frac {1}{x}
> $$
> $$
> (- \infty , 0) \cup (0, + \infty)
> $$
> $$
> \text {于} \mathrm{C}, \forall x _ {1}, x _ {2} \in (a, b)
> $$
> $$
> \because \frac {f (x _ {1}) - f (x _ {2})}{x _ {1} - x _ {2}} <   0 (x _ {1} \neq x _ {2})
> $$
> $$
> \left[ f \left(x _ {1}\right) - f \left(x _ {2}\right) \right] \cdot \left(x _ {1} - x _ {2}\right) <   0
> $$
> $$
> \text {于} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}\right) - f \left(x _ {2}\right) > 0 \\ x _ {1} - x _ {2} <   0 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}\right) - f \left(x _ {2}\right) <   0 \\ x _ {1} - x _ {2} > 0 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}\right) > f \left(x _ {2}\right) \\ x _ {1} <   x _ {2} \end{array} \text {或} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} f \left(x _ {1}\right) <   f \left(x _ {2}\right) \\ x _ {1} > x _ {2} \end{array} \right. \right.
> $$
> $$
> \therefore f (x) \text {在} (a, b)
