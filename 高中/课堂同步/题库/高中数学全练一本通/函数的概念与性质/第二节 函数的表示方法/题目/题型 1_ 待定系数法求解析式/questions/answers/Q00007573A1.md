> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $f(x)=3x+1$ 或 $f(x)=-3x-2$ ;
> 解析: 设 $f(x)=kx+b$
> 所以 $f(f(x))=k(kx+b)+b=k^{2}x+kb+b=9x+4$
> 则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} k^2 = 9 \\ kb + b = 4 \end{array} \right. \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} k = 3 \\ b = 1 \end{array} \right.$ 或 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} k = -3 \\ b = -2 \end{array} \right.,$
> 故 $f(x)=3x+1$ 或 $f(x)=-3x-2$ ;
