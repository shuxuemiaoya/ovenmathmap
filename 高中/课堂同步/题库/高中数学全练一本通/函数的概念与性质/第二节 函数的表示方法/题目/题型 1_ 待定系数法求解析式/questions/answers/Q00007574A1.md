> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $f(x) = 2x + 5$
>
> **【解析】**
> 设 $f(x)=ax+b(a\neq0)$ ,
> $$
> 2 f (x + 3) - f (x - 2) = 2 [ a (x + 3) + b ] - [ a (x - 2) + b ]
> $$
> $$
> = 2 a x + 6 a + 2 b - a x + 2 a - b = a x + 8 a + b = 2 x + 2 1
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 2 \\ 8 a + b = 2 1 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> a = 2, b = 5
> $$
> $$
> f (x) = 2 x + 5
> $$
