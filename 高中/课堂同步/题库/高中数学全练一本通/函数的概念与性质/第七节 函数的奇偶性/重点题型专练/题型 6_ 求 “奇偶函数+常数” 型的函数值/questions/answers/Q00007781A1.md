> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 4
> 解析： $\because f(x) = \frac{2(x-1)^2}{x^2+1} = \frac{2(x^2+1)-4x}{x^2+1} = 2-\frac{4x}{x^2+1}$ ，
> $$
> g (x) = f (x) - 2 = - \frac {4 x}{x ^ {2} + 1}
> $$
> $$
> g (- x) = - \frac {- 4 x}{x ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {4 x}{x ^ {2} + 1} = - g (x)
> $$
> $\therefore$ 可令 $g(x) = f(x) - 2 = -\frac{x^2 + 1}{x^2 + 1}$ , 则 $g(-x) = -\frac{x^2 + 1}{x^2 + 1} = \frac{x^2 + 1}{x^2 + 1} = -g(x)$ , $\therefore g(x)$ 为定义在 $\mathbf{R}$ 上的奇函数, $\therefore g(x)_{\max} + g(x)_{\min} = 0$ , 则 $M - 2 + m - 2 = 0$ , $\therefore M + m = 4$ .
>
> 故选 **D**.
