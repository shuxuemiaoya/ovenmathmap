> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> F _ {1} (x) = f (x) + g (x)
> $$
> $$
> F _ {1} (- x) = f (- x) + g (- x) = - f (x) + g (x) \neq - F _ {1} (x)
> $$
> $$
> \therefore F _ {1} (x)
> $$
> $$
> F _ {1} (- x) \neq F _ {1} (x)
> $$
> $$
> F _ {2} (x) = f (x) g (x)
> $$
> $$
> F _ {2} (- x) = f (- x) g (- x) = - f (x) g (x) = - F _ {2} (x)
> $$
> $$
> F _ {2} (- x) \neq F _ {2} (x)
> $$
> $$
> : C f (- x) = - f (x)
> $$
