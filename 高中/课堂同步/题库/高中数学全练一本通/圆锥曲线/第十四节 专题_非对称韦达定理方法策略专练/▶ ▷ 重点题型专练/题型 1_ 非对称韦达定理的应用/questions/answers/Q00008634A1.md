> **【答案】** （1） $C:\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{2}=1$ ；（2）详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）略
> (2) $A(-2,0), B(2,0), D(1,0)$ ,
> 由题意得，直线 MN 可以垂直于 x 轴，但不平行于 x 轴；
> 令 $MN: x = my + 1; M(x_1, y_1), N(x_2, y_2)$ . $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x=my+1\\&\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{2}=1\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow\left(m^{2}+2\right)y^{2}+2my-3=0\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}&y_{1}+y_{2}=\frac{-2m}{m^{2}+2}(1)\\&y_{1}y_{2}=\frac{-3}{m^{2}+2}(2)\end{aligned}\right.$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{y_{1}}{x_{1}+2}&=\frac{y}{x+2}\\ \frac{y_{2}}{x_{2}-2}&=\frac{y}{x-2}\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow\left\{\begin{aligned}&x=\frac{4y_{2}(my_{1}+3)}{3y_{2}+y_{1}}-2\\ &y=\frac{4y_{1}y_{2}}{3y_{2}+y_{1}}\end{aligned}\right.$ (2)÷(1): $2my_{1}y_{2}=3(y_{1}+y_{2})$ $\therefore \frac{4y_{2}(my_{1}+3)}{3y_{2}+y_{1}}=\frac{4my_{1}y_{2}+12y_{2}}{3y_{2}+y_{1}}=\frac{6(y_{1}+y_{2})+12y_{2}}{3y_{2}+y_{1}}=\frac{6(y_{1}+3y_{2})}{3y_{2}+y_{1}}=6$ $\Rightarrow x=6-2=4$ . P 在定直线：x=4 上.
