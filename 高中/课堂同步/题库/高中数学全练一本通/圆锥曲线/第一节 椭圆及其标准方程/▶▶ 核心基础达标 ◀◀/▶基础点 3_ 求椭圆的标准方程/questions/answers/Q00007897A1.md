> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{y^2}{16} + x^2 = 1$
>
> **【解析】**
> 设椭圆的标准方程为 $\frac{y^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{x^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1 (a > b > 0)$ ，依题意得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{16}{a^{2}} + \frac{0}{b^{2}} &= 1 \\ 2c &= 2\sqrt{15} \\ a^{2} &= b^{2} + c^{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=4\\ b&=1\\ c&=\sqrt{15}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，所以该椭圆的标准方程为 $\frac{y^{2}}{16}+x^{2}=1$ .
