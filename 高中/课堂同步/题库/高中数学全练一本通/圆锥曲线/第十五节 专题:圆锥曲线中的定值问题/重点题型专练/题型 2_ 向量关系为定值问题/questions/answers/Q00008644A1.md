> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1）8；（2）证明见解析.
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）由双曲线 $C:x^{2} - \frac{y^{2}}{2} = 1$ 可得 $a = 1$ ， $b = \sqrt{2}$ ，所以 $c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} = \sqrt{1 + 2} = \sqrt{3}$ 所以 $F(-\sqrt{3},0)$ ，设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ， $k_{PF} = \frac{\sqrt{3} - 0}{0 - (-\sqrt{3})} = 1$ ，所以直线 $l$ 的方程为 $y = x + \sqrt{3}$ 由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}y = x + \sqrt{3}\\ 2x^{2} - y^{2} = 2 \end{array} \right.$ 联立得： $x^{2} - 2\sqrt{3} x - 5 = 0$ 所以 $x_{1} + x_{2} = 2\sqrt{3},x_{1}x_{2} = -5$ ， $|AF|\cdot |BF| = \sqrt{(x_1 + \sqrt{3})^2 + y_1^2}\sqrt{(x_2 + \sqrt{3})^2 + y_2^2} = 2\left|\left(x_1 + \sqrt{3}\right)(x_2 + \sqrt{3})\right|$ $= 2|x_{1}x_{2} + \sqrt{3}(x_{1} + x_{2}) + 3| = 2\times |-5 + \sqrt{3}\times 2\sqrt{3} +3| = 8.$
> （2）由题意知直线 l 的斜率存在，不妨设直线 $l: y = kx + \sqrt{3}$ ，
> 由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y=kx+\sqrt{3}\\ 2x^{2}-y^{2}=2\end{aligned}\right.$ 可得： $\left(k^{2}-2\right)x^{2}+2\sqrt{3}kx+5=0$ 所以 $x_{1}+x_{2}=\frac{2\sqrt{3}k}{2-k^{2}}$ , $x_{1}x_{2}=\frac{5}{k^{2}-2}$ , $\overrightarrow{MA}=\left(x_{1},y_{1}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$ , $\overrightarrow{MB}=\left(x_{2},y_{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$ , $\overrightarrow{MA}\cdot\overrightarrow{MB}=x_{1}x_{2}+\left(y_{1}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)\left(y_{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)=x_{1}x_{2}+\left(kx_{1}+\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)\left(kx_{2}+\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$ $=\left(1+k^{2}\right)x_{1}x_{2}+\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{4}k\left(x_{1}+x_{2}\right)+\frac{75}{16}$ $=\left(1+k^{2}\right)\frac{5}{k^{2}-2}+\frac{5\sqrt{3}k}{4}\cdot\frac{2\sqrt{3}k}{2-k^{2}}+\frac{75}{16}=\frac{35}{16}$ . 所以 $\overrightarrow{MA}\cdot\overrightarrow{MB}=\frac{35}{16}$ 为定值.
