> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> （1） $2\sqrt{2}x - y - 2\sqrt{2} = 0$ ；（2）证明见解析.
> 解析：（1）直线 $l$ 斜率不为 $0, F(1,0)$ ，设直线 $l: x = ty + 1$ ，
> 设 $A(x_1, y_1), B(x_2, y_2)$ ,
> 因为 $A$ 点在 $x$ 轴上方，所以 $y_1 > 0, y_2 < 0$ 由 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}x = ty + 1 \\ y^2 = 4x\end{array}\right.$ ，得 $y^2 - 4ty - 4 = 0$ $\therefore y_1 + y_2 = 4t, y_1y_2 = -4$ $\because \overrightarrow{AF} = 2\overrightarrow{FB} \Rightarrow (1 - x_1, y_1) = 2(x_2 - 1, y_2) \therefore -y_1 = 2y_2$ 由 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}y_1 + y_2 = 4t \\ -y_1 = 2y_2\end{array}\right. \Rightarrow \left\{\begin{array}{l}y_1 = 8t \\ y_2 = -4t\end{array}\right.$ 代入 $y_1y_2 = -4$ 因 $y_1 > 0$ ，所以 $t > 0$ ，解得 $t = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}$ 所以 $AB$ 所在直线方程为 $2\sqrt{2}x - y - 2\sqrt{2} = 0$ （2）设 $AB$ 中点为 $N(x_N, y_N)$ $\therefore y_N = \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} = 2t, x_N = 2t^2 + 1 \therefore N(2t^2 + 1, 2t)$ 所以 $AB$ 中垂线 $l': y - 2t = -t(x - 2t^2 - 1) \therefore D(2t^2 + 3, 0)$ $\therefore |DF| = |2t^2 + 3 - 1| = 2t^2 + 2$ $\because |AB| = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} = \sqrt{(ty_2 - ty_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$ $= \sqrt{1 + t^2}\sqrt{(y_1 + y_2)^2 - 4y_1y_2} = \sqrt{1 + t^2}\sqrt{16t^2 + 16}$ $= 4t^2 + 4$ $\therefore \frac{|AB|}{|DF|} = \frac{4t^2 + 4}{2t^2 + 2} = 2$ （定值）
