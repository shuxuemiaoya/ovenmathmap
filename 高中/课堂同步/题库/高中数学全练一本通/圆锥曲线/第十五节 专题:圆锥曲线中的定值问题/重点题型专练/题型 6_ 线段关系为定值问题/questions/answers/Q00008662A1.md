> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$ ；（2）是， $\frac{4}{3}$ .
> 解析：（1）由已知 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{a^{2}}+\frac{9}{4b^{2}}&=1\\ e=\frac{c}{a}=\frac{1}{2}\\ c^{2}&=a^{2}-b^{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=2\\ b&=\sqrt{3}\\ c&=1\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以椭圆 C 的方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$ （2）由（1）可知 $F(1,0)$ 依题意可知直线 l 的斜率不为 0，故可设直线 l 的方程为 $x=my+1$ 由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{x^{2}}{4}+\frac{y^{2}}{3}&=1\\ x=my+1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，消去 x
> 整理得 $(3m^{2}+4)y^{2}+6my-9=0$ 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}), B(x_{2},y_{2})$ 则 $y_{1}+y_{2}=\frac{-6m}{3m^{2}+4}, y_{1}y_{2}=\frac{-9}{3m^{2}+4}$ 不妨设 $y_{1}>0, y_{2}<0,$ $\left|AF\right|=\sqrt{(x_{1}-1)^{2}+y_{1}^{2}}=\sqrt{(my_{1}+1-1)^{2}+y_{1}^{2}}=\sqrt{m^{2}+1}\cdot\left|y_{1}\right|=\sqrt{m^{2}+1}\cdot y_{1},$ 同理 $\left|BF\right|=\sqrt{m^{2}+1}\cdot\left|y_{2}\right|=-\sqrt{m^{2}+1}\cdot y_{2}$ 所以 $\frac{1}{\left|AF\right|}+\frac{1}{\left|BF\right|}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{m^{2}+1}\cdot y_{1}}+\frac{1}{-\sqrt{m^{2}+1}\cdot y_{2}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{m^{2}+1}}\left(\frac{1}{y_{1}}-\frac{1}{y_{2}}\right)$ $=\frac{1}{\sqrt{m^{2}+1}}\cdot\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{y_{1}y_{2}}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{m^{2}+1}}\cdot\frac{-\sqrt{(y_{2}+y_{1})^{2}-4y_{1}y_{2}}}{y_{1}y_{2}}$ $=\frac{1}{\sqrt{m^{2}+1}}\cdot\frac{-\sqrt{\left(\frac{-6m}{3m^{2}+4}\right)^{2}-4\times\frac{-9}{3m^{2}+4}}}{-\frac{-9}{3m^{2}+4}}=\frac{4}{3}$ 即 $\frac{1}{\left|AF\right|}+\frac{1}{\left|BF\right|}=\frac{4}{3}.$
