> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1）\(\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{3} = 1\)；（2）是定值，理由见解析。
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）椭圆 \(\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1 (a > b > 0)\) 离心率为 \(\frac{1}{2}\)，即 \(e = \frac{c}{a} = \frac{1}{2}\)，\(\because\) 点 \(G(0, 2)\) 与椭圆的左、右顶点可以构成等腰直角三角形，\(\therefore a = 2\)，综上有：\(c = 1\)，\(b = \sqrt{3}\)，故椭圆方程为 \(\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{3} = 1\)，（2）由直线与椭圆交于 \(M, N\) 两点，联立方程：\(\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = kx + m \\ \frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{3} = 1 \\ x\_1 + x\_2 = \frac{-8km}{3 + 4k^2} \\ x\_1x\_2 = \frac{4(m^2 - 3)}{3 + 4k^2} \\ k\_{OM}, k\_{ON} = \frac{y\_1y\_2}{x\_1x\_2} = \frac{(kx\_1 + m)(kx\_2 + m)}{x\_1x\_2} = \frac{k^2x\_1x\_2 + mk(x\_1 + x\_2) + m^2}{x\_1x\_2} \\ = \frac{4k^2(m^2 - 3) - 8k^2m^2 + m^2(3 + 4k^2)}{4(m^2 - 3)} = \frac{3(m^2 - 4k^2)}{4(m^2 - 3)} = -\frac{3}{4}, \\ \therefore 2m^2 = 4k^2 + 3, \\ |MN| = \sqrt{1 + k^2}|x\_1 - x\_2| = \sqrt{1 + k^2} \cdot \frac{4\sqrt{3}\sqrt{4k^2 + 3 - m^2}}{3 + 4k^2} = \sqrt{1 + k^2} \cdot \frac{4\sqrt{3}|m|}{2m^2}, \\ 原点 \(O\) 到 \(l\) 的距离 \(d = \frac{|m|}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}}\)，\(\therefore S\_{\triangle OMN} = \frac{|MN|}{2} \cdot d = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{1 + k^2} \cdot \frac{4\sqrt{3}|m|}{2m^2} \cdot \frac{|m|}{\sqrt{1 + k^2}} = \sqrt{3}\) 为定值；
