> （2）由题意知：直线 $l$ 斜率存在，设 $l:y = kx + 1$ ， $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，
> 由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x^{2} = 4y\\ y = kx + 1 \end{array} \right.$ 得： $x^{2} - 4kx - 4 = 0$ ，则 $\Delta = 16k^2 +16 > 0$ ， $\therefore x_{1} + x_{2} = 4k$ ， $x_{1}x_{2} = -4$ ： $\because \overrightarrow{AD} = (-1 - x_1,2 - y_1)$ ， $\overrightarrow{BD} = (-1 - x_2,2 - y_2)$ $\therefore \overrightarrow{AD}\cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = (-1 - x_1)(-1 - x_2) + (2 - y_1)(2 - y_2) = 5 + (x_1 + x_2) + x_1x_2 - 2(y_1 + y_2) + y_1y_2$ $= 5 + (x_{1} + x_{2}) + x_{1}x_{2} - 2(k(x_{1} + x_{2}) + 2) + (kx_{1} + 1)(kx_{2} + 1)$ $= 2 + (1 - k)(x_{1} + x_{2}) + (k^{2} + 1)x_{1}x_{2} = 2 + 4k(1 - k) - 4(k^{2} + 1)$ $= -8k^{2} + 4k - 2 = -8\left(k - \frac{1}{4}\right)^{2} - \frac{3}{2}$ ：
> ∴当 $k = \frac{1}{4}$ 时， $\overrightarrow{AD}\cdot \overrightarrow{BD}$ 取得最大值 $-\frac{3}{2}$ ：
