> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}), B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x_{1}^{2}&=6y_{1}\\ x_{2}^{2}&=6y_{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，所以 $x_{1}^{2}-x_{2}^{2}=6y_{1}-6y_{2}$ ，整理得 $\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}=\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{6}$ .
> 因为弦 AB 的中点为 (1,4)，所以 $\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}=\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{6}=\frac{2}{6}=\frac{1}{3}$ ，即直线 l 的斜率为 $\frac{1}{3}$ .
>
> 故选 **C**
