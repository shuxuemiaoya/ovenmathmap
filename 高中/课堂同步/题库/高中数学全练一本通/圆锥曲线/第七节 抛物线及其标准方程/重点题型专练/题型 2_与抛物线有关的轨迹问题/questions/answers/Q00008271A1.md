> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $y^{2}=8x(x\geq0)$ 或 $y=0(x<0)$
> $$
> \mid x \mid + 2 = \sqrt {(x - 2) ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}
> $$
> $$
> x ^ {2} + 4 | x | + 4 = x ^ {2} - 4 x + 4 + y ^ {2}
> $$
> $$
> y ^ {2} = 4 | x | + 4 x
> $$
> $$
> x \geq 0
> $$
> $$
> y ^ {2} = 8 x
> $$
> $$
> y = 0
> $$
> $$
> x <   0
> $$
> $$
> y ^ {2} = 8 x (x \geq 0)
> $$
> $$
> y = 0 (x <   0)
> $$
