> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 由垂径定理可知， $k_{1}k_{2} = -\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}} = -\frac{3}{4}$ 解得 $\frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{3}{4}$ $\therefore e = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^2} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{3}{4}} = \frac{1}{2}$
