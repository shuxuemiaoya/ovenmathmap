> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 解析：法一：设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\\&\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y_{2}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，∵过点M(1,1)作斜率为 $-\frac{1}{2}$ 的直线与椭圆 $C:\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1(a>b>0)$ 相交于A，B两点，M是线段AB的中点，
> ∴两式相减可得 $\frac{2}{a^{2}}+\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)\cdot\frac{2}{b^{2}}=0$ ,
> ∴ $a=\sqrt{2}b,\therefore c=\sqrt{a^{2}-b^{2}}=b,\therefore e=\frac{c}{a}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
> 法二：由垂径定理得 $k_{OM}\cdot k_{AB}=-\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}},1\times\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)=-\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}$ ，解得 $a=\sqrt{2}b$ ∴ $c=\sqrt{a^{2}-b^{2}}=b$ ∴ $e=\frac{c}{a}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
