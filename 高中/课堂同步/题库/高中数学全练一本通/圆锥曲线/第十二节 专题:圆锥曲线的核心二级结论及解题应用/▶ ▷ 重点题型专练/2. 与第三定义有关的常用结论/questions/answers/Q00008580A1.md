> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{1}{4}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 设双曲线半焦距 $c$ ，由题意知 $e = \frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\Rightarrow \frac{c^2}{a^2} = \frac{a^2 + b^2}{a^2} = \frac{5}{4}\Rightarrow \frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{1}{4},$ 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),M(x_{0},y_{0})$ ，根据对称性可得 $B(-x_1, - y_1)$ 则 $\frac{x_1^2 - y_1^2}{a^2} = 1,\frac{x_0^2 - y_0^2}{a^2} = 1( x_0\neq \pm x_1,y_0\neq \pm y_1 )$ ，两式相减得 $\frac{x_1^2 - x_0^2}{a^2} -\frac{y_1^2 - y_0^2}{b^2} = 0$ ，即 $\frac{y_1^2 - y_0^2}{x_1^2 - x_0^2} = \frac{b^2}{a^2},$ 由斜率坐标公式得 $k_{1}\cdot k_{2} = \frac{y_{1} - y_{0}}{x_{1} - x_{0}}\cdot \frac{-y_{1} - y_{0}}{-x_{1} - x_{0}} = \frac{y_{0}^{2} - y_{1}^{2}}{x_{0}^{2} - x_{1}^{2}} = \frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}} = \frac{1}{4}$ ，所以 $k_{1}\cdot k_{2}$ 的值为 $\frac{1}{4}$ .故答案为： $\frac{1}{4}$
