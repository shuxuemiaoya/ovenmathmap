> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{3}{2}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题可知， $k_{AQ} = -k_{1}$ ，由垂径定理得 $k_{AQ}\cdot k_{QB} = \frac{a^2}{b^2}$ 即 $-k_{1}k_{2} = \frac{a^{2}}{b^{2}} = \frac{4}{5},$ $\therefore \frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{5}{4}\therefore e = \sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^2} = \sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^2} = \frac{3}{2}$
