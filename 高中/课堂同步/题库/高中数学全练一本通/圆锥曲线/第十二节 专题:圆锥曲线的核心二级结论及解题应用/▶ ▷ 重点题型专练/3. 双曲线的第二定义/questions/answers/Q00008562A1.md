> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{4}{3}$ 解析：设 $\angle BF_{1}O=\alpha$ ，则 $\angle AF_{1}O=\pi-\alpha$ .
> 由椭圆的焦半径角度式可知 $\left|BF_{1}\right|=\frac{b^{2}}{a-c\cos\alpha}=\frac{3}{2-\cos\alpha},\left|AF_{1}\right|=\frac{b^{2}}{a-c\cos(\pi-\alpha)}=\frac{3}{2+\cos\alpha}$ ,
> 从而 $\lambda=\frac{\left|AF_{1}\right|+\left|F_{1}B\right|}{\left|AF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|F_{1}B\right|}=\frac{\frac{3}{2+\cos\alpha}+\frac{3}{2-\cos\alpha}}{\frac{9}{4-\cos^{2}\alpha}}=\frac{\frac{12}{4-\cos^{2}\alpha}}{\frac{9}{4-\cos^{2}\alpha}}=\frac{4}{3}$ .
> 故答案为： $\frac{4}{3}$
