> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $|AF| = 2, |BF| = \frac{6}{5}, |AB| = \frac{16}{5}$ 解析:由椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{3} = 1$ 得 $c = 1$ ，又 $\theta = 120^\circ$ 所以根据焦半径公式 $|AF| = \frac{b^2}{a + c\cos\theta} = \frac{3}{2 - \frac{1}{2}} = 2$ ， $|BF| = \frac{b^2}{a - c\cos\theta} = \frac{3}{2 + \frac{1}{2}} = \frac{6}{5}$ , $|AB| = \frac{2ab^2}{|a^2 - c^2\cos^2\theta|} = \frac{2\times 2\times 3}{|4 - 1\times \frac{1}{4}|} = \frac{16}{5}$ .
