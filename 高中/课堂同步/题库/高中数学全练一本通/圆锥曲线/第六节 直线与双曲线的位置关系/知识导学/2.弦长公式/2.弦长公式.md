## 2.弦长公式

若直线 $l:y = kx + m$ 与双曲线 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} -\frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1(a > 0,b > 0)$ 交于 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ 两点，

则 $AB = \sqrt{1 + k^2}\left|x_1 - x_2\right| = \sqrt{1 + k^2}\sqrt{\left(x_1 + x_2\right)^2 - 4x_1x_2}$ 或 $AB = \sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{k^2}}\left|y_1 - y_2\right| = \sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{k^2}}\sqrt{\left(y_1 + y_2\right)^2 - 4y_1y_2}$ .
