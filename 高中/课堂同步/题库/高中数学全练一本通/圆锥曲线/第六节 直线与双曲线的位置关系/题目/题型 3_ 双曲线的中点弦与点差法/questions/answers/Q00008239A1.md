> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：由题意可得 $x_{1}+x_{2}=2, y_{1}+y_{2}=2$ ，且 $\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}=\frac{1}{2}$ ，
> 又因为 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\\ \frac{y_{2}^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，所以 $\frac{1}{a^{2}}(y_{1}+y_{2})(y_{1}-y_{2})+\frac{1}{b^{2}}(x_{1}+x_{2})(x_{2}-x_{1})=0$ ，
> 即有 $\frac{2}{a^{2}}(y_{1}-y_{2})=\frac{2}{b^{2}}(x_{1}-x_{2})$ 所以 $\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}=\frac{\frac{2}{b^{2}}}{\frac{2}{a^{2}}}=\frac{a^{2}}{b^{2}}=\frac{1}{2}$ 所以 $b^{2}=2a^{2}$ ，所以 $c^{2}=b^{2}+a^{2}=3a^{2}$ ，所以 $e^{2}=\frac{c^{2}}{a^{2}}=3$ ，所以 $e=\sqrt{3}$ 。
>
> 故选 **C**.
