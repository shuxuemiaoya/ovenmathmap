> **【解析】**
> $x-y+1=0$ 解析：设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}), B(x_{2},y_{2})$ 则有 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{8}-\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{16}=1\\&\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{8}-\frac{y_{2}^{2}}{16}=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，两式相减，得 $\frac{(x_{1}+x_{2})(x_{1}-x_{2})}{8}=\frac{(y_{1}+y_{2})(y_{1}-x_{2})}{16}$ 因为线段AB的中点为M(1,2)，所以 $x_{1}+x_{2}=2,y_{1}+y_{2}=4$ 因此由 $\frac{(x_{1}+x_{2})(x_{1}-x_{2})}{8}=\frac{(y_{1}+y_{2})(y_{1}-x_{2})}{16}\Rightarrow\frac{y_{1}-x_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}=1$ 即直线AB的斜率为1，方程为 $y-2=x-1\Rightarrow x-y+1=0$ 代入双曲线方程中，得 $y^{2}-4y-14=0$ 因为 $(-4)^{2}-4\times1\times(-14)>0$ ，所以线段AB存在，
>
> 故选 **C**
