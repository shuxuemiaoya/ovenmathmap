> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：设 $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\\ \frac{x_{2}^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y_{2}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\end{aligned}\right.$ 由点差法得 $\frac{(x_{1}+x_{2})(x_{1}-x_{2})}{a^{2}}-\frac{(y_{1}+y_{2})(y_{1}-y_{2})}{b^{2}}=0(x_{1}\neq x_{2})$ ∵ $M(-1,3)$ ，∴ $x_{2}+x_{2}=-2$ ， $y_{1}+y_{2}=6$ ，∴ $\frac{-2}{a^{2}}-\frac{6(y_{1}-y_{2})}{b^{2}(x_{1}-x_{2})}=0$ ，
> 又 $k_{AB}=\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}=-1$ ，∴ $b^{2}=3a^{2}$ ∴渐近线方程为 $y=\pm\frac{b}{a}x=\pm\sqrt{3}x$ 。
>
> 故选 **A**.
