> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 如图, 由 $A$ , $B$ 分别向抛物线的准线作垂线, 垂足为 $A'$ , $B'$ , 设直线 $AB$ 与抛物线的准线交点为 $M$ , 抛物线的准线与 $x$ 轴交于点 $N$ , 则 $\left|FN\right| = p = 2$ , 设 $\left|BF\right| = m (m > 0)$ , 则 $\left|AF\right| = 3\left|BF\right| = 3m, \left|AB\right| = \left|AF\right| + \left|BF\right| = 4m$ 由抛物线的定义, $\left|AA'\right| = \left|AF\right| = 3m, \left|BB'\right| = \left|BF\right| = m$ , 易知 $\triangle MBB' \sim \triangle MAA'$ , $\therefore \frac{\left|BB'\right|}{\left|AA'\right|} = \frac{\left|MB\right|}{\left|MA\right|} = \frac{\left|MB\right|}{\left|MB\right| + \left|AB\right|}, \therefore \frac{m}{3m} = \frac{\left|MB\right|}{\left|MB\right| + 4m}, \therefore \left|MB\right| = 2m,$ 又易知, $\triangle MBB' \sim \triangle MFN$ , $\therefore \frac{\left|BB'\right|}{\left|FN\right|} = \frac{\left|MB\right|}{\left|MF\right|} = \frac{\left|MB\right|}{\left|MB\right| + \left|BF\right|}, \therefore \frac{m}{2} = \frac{2m}{2m + m}, \therefore m = \frac{4}{3}, \therefore |AB| = 4m = \frac{16}{3}$ . 故答案为: $\frac{16}{3}$ .
