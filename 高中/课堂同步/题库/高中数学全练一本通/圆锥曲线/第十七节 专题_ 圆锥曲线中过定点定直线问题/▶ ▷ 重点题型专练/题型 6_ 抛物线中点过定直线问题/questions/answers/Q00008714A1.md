> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题意 $k_{AB} \neq 0$ ，设点 $A\left(\frac{y_1^2}{2}, y_1\right), B\left(\frac{y_2^2}{2}, y_2\right), C\left(\frac{y_3^2}{2}, y_3\right), D\left(\frac{y_4^2}{2}, y_4\right)$ 。直线 $AB$ 的方程为 $x = m_1 y + 1$ ，直线 $CD$ 的方程为 $x = m_2 y + 1$ 。由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y^2 = 2x \\ x = m_1 y + 1 \end{array} \right.$ 得 $y^2 - 2m_1 y - 2 = 0$ ， $\because \Delta = 4m_1^2 + 8 > 0$ 恒成立，由韦达定理得 $y_1 + y_2 = 2m_1, y_1 y_2 = -2$ ，同理有 $y_3 + y_4 = 2m_2, y_3 y_4 = -2$ ， $\therefore k_{AC} = \frac{y_3 - y_1}{\frac{y_3^2}{2} - \frac{y_1^2}{2}} = \frac{2}{y_1 + y_3}$ ， $\therefore l_{AC}: y = \frac{2}{y_1 + y_3}\left(x - \frac{y_1^2}{2}\right) + y_1$ ①同理可得 $l_{BD}: y = \frac{2}{y_2 + y_4}\left(x - \frac{y_2^2}{2}\right) + y_2$ $\because y_1 y_2 = -2, \therefore y_2 = \frac{-2}{y_1}$ ，同理 $\because y_3 y_4 = -2, \therefore y_4 = \frac{-2}{y_3}$ ， $\therefore l_{BD}: y = \frac{2}{-\frac{2}{y_1} - \frac{2}{y_3}}\left(x - \frac{2}{y_1^2}\right) - \frac{2}{y_1}$ ，即 $y = \frac{-y_1 y_3}{y_1 + y_3}\left(x - \frac{2}{y_1^2}\right) - \frac{2}{y_1}$ ②联立①②得 $\frac{2}{y_1 + y_3}\left(x - \frac{y_1^2}{2}\right) + y_1 = \frac{-y_1 y_3}{y_1 + y_3}\left(x - \frac{2}{y_1^2}\right) - \frac{2}{y_1}$ 整理得 $\frac{2 + y_1 y_3}{y_1 + y_3} x = \frac{2y_3}{(y_1 + y_3)y_1} + \frac{y_1^2}{y_1 + y_3} - \frac{2}{y_1} - y_1$ 化简得 $x = -1$ ，即点 $Q$ 在直线 $x = -1$ 上。
