> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \sqrt {3}, \therefore \angle B F _ {1} F _ {2} = \frac {\pi}{3}
> $$
> $$
> \left| B F _ {1} \right| = \left| B F _ {2} \right|
> $$
> $$
> \triangle B F _ {1} F _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> \left| B F _ {1} \right| = \left| B F _ {2} \right| = \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = 2 c, \angle B F _ {2} F _ {1} = \frac {\pi}{3}, \angle F _ {1} F _ {2} A = \frac {2 \pi}{3}
> $$
> 在 $\triangle AF_1F_2$ 中， $\tan \angle F_2F_1A = \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{11} > 0$ ，则 $\angle F_2F_1A$ 为锐角，
> 则 $\sin \angle F_{2}F_{1}A = \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{14},\cos \angle F_{2}F_{1}A = \frac{11}{14}$
> $$
> \sin A = \sin \left(\frac {2 \pi}{3} + \angle F _ {2} F _ {1} A\right) = \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{1 4}
> $$
> 由正弦定理， $\frac{\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|}{\sin A}=\frac{\left|AF_{1}\right|}{\sin\angle F_{1}F_{2}A}=\frac{\left|AF_{2}\right|}{\sin\angle F_{2}F_{1}A}$
> $$
> \therefore \frac {2 c}{\frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{1 4}} = \frac {\left| A F _ {1} \right|}{\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}} = \frac {\left| A F _ {2} \right|}{\frac {5 \sqrt {3}}{1 4}},
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \left| A F _ {1} \right| = \frac {1 4}{3} c, \left| A F _ {2} \right| = \frac {1 0}{3} c
> $$
> 由 $\left|AF_{1}\right|-\left|AF_{2}\right|=2a$ ，得 $\frac{4}{3}c=2a$ ，∴ $e=\frac{c}{a}=\frac{3}{2}$ 。故答案选：B。
