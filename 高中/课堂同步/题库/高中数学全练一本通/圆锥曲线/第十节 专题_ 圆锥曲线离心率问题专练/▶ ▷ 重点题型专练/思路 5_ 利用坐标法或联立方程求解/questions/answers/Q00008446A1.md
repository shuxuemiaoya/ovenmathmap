> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 设 $P(x_{1}, y_{1}), Q(x_{1}, -y_{1})$ ，则 $\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{y_{1}^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1$ ，
> 所以 $\frac{y_{1}}{x_{1}+a}\cdot\frac{-y_{1}}{x_{1}-a}=\frac{-y_{1}^{2}}{x_{1}^{2}-a^{2}}=\frac{b^{2}\left(1-\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}}\right)}{x_{1}^{2}-a^{2}}=-\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}=-\frac{1}{4}$ 所以 $\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}=\frac{1}{4}, e=\frac{c}{a}=\frac{\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}}{a}=\sqrt{1+\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}}=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$ .
>
> 故选 **C**.
