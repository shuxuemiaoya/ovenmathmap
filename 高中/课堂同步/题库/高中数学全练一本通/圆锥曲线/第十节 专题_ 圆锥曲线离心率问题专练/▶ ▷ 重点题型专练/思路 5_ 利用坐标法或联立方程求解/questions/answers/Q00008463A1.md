> **【解析】**
> 解析: 解: 设 $M(x_{1}, y_{1}), N(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ,解析：解：设 $(a_{1},y_{1}),(a_{2},y_{2})$ 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\\ \frac{y_{2}^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，两式相减得 $b^{2}(y_{1}+y_{2})(y_{1}-y_{2})+a^{2}(x_{1}+x_{2})(x_{1}-x_{2})=0$ 所以 $b^{2}(-2)(y_{1}-y_{2})+2a^{2}(x_{1}-x_{2})=0$ 所以 $-b^{2}\left(\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}}\right)+a^{2}=0$ 所以 $-2b^{2}+a^{2}=0$ 所以 $-2(a^{2}-c^{2})+a^{2}=0$ ，所以 $e=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
>
> 故选 **B**
