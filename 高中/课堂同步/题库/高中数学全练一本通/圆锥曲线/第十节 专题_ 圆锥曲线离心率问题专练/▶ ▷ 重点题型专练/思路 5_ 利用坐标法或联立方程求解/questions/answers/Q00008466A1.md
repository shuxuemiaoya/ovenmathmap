> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2},\sqrt{2}\right)$
>
> **【解析】**
> 设 $P(x_{0},y_{0})$ , $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ , 因为 A, B 关于原点对称, 所以 $B(-x_{1},-y_{1})$ ,
> ∴ $k_{PA}=\frac{y_{0}-y_{1}}{x_{0}-x_{1}}, k_{PB}=\frac{y_{0}+y_{1}}{x_{0}+x_{1}}, \therefore k_{PA} \cdot k_{PB} = \frac{y_{0}^{2} - y_{1}^{2}}{x_{0}^{2} - x_{1}^{2}}.$ 又因为点 P, A 都在双曲线上，所以 $\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y_{0}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1,\quad\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}}-\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$ 两式相减得: $\frac{x_{0}^{2}-x_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}}=\frac{y_{0}^{2}-y_{1}^{2}}{b^{2}}$
> $\therefore \frac{y_0^2 - y_1^2}{x_0^2 - x_1^2} = \frac{b^2}{a^2},$ $\therefore k_{PA}\cdot k_{PB} = \frac{b^2}{a^2}\in \left(\frac{1}{4},1\right),$ $\therefore \frac{c^2 - a^2}{a^2} = e^2 -1\in \left(\frac{1}{4},1\right),e^2\in \left(\frac{5}{4},2\right),$ $\therefore \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} <  e <   \sqrt{2} .$ 故答案为： $\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2},\sqrt{2}\right)$
