> **【解析】**
> 22
> $$
> \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = 2 \times \sqrt {9 + 1 6} = 1 0
> $$
> $$
> \left| \left| P F _ {1} \right| - \left| P F _ {2} \right| \right| = 6
> $$
> $$
> \left| P F _ {1} \right|: \left| P F _ {2} \right| = 1: 3
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \left| P F _ {1} \right| = 3, \left| P F _ {2} \right| = 9
> $$
