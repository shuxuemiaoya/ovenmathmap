> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 6
>
> **【解析】**
> $C:\frac{x^2}{4} -y^2 = 1$ ， $a^2 = 4,b^2 = 1$ ， $c^2 = 4 + 1 = 5$
> 易得双曲线的 2a=4, 焦距 $2c=2\sqrt{5}$ .
> 因为 $A, B$ 都在右支上，则 $\left|AF_1\right| = \left|AF_2\right| + 4, \left|BF_1\right| = \left|BF_2\right| + 4$
> $$
> \triangle A B F _ {1}
> $$
> $$
> \left| A B \right| + \left| A F _ {1} \right| + \left| B F _ {1} \right| = \left| A B \right| + \left| A F _ {2} \right| + \left| B F _ {2} \right| + 8 = 2 \left| A B \right| + 8 = 2 0
> $$
> $$
> \left| A B \right| = 6
> $$
