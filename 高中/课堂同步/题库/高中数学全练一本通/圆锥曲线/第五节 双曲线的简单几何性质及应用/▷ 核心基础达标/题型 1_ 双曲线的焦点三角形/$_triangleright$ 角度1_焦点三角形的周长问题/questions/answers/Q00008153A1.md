> **【解析】**
> 解析: 双曲线的标准方程为 $x^{2}-\frac{y^{2}}{8}=1$ ，所以 a=1, c=3, $\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|=6$ ，
> 因为 $\triangle PF_{1}F_{2}$ 是等腰三角形, 不妨设 $P$ 在双曲线的右支上, 则
> $$
> \left| P F _ {1} \right| = \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = 6
> $$
> $$
> \left| P F _ {2} \right| = \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = 6
> $$
> $$
> \left| P F _ {1} \right| = \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = 6 \text {时}
> $$
> $$
> \left| P F _ {2} \right| = 6 - 2 = 4
> $$
> $$
> 6 + 6 + 1 0 = 1 6
> $$
> $$
> \triangle P F _ {1} F _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> \left| P F _ {2} \right| = \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = 6
> $$
> $$
> \left| P F _ {1} \right| = 6 + 2 = 8
> $$
> $$
> \triangle P F _ {1} F _ {2}
> $$
> $$
> 6 + 6 + 1 0 = 1 6
> $$
