> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 详见解析
> 解析：令 $M(x_{1},y_{1}), N(x_{2},y_{2}); y=kx+m$ ;
> 则： $\left\{\begin{aligned}&y=kx+m\\&\frac{x^{2}}{2}+y^{2}=1\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow\left(1+2k^{2}\right)x^{2}+4kmx+2m^{2}-2=0$ $\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}&x_{1}+x_{2}=-\frac{4km}{1+2k^{2}}(1)\\&x_{1}x_{2}=\frac{2(m^{2}-1)}{1+2k^{2}}(2)\end{aligned}\right.$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{y-1}{x}&=\frac{y_{1}-1}{x_{1}}\\ \frac{y+1}{x}&=\frac{y_{2}+1}{x_{2}}\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow y=1-\frac{2kx_{1}x_{2}+2(m+1)mx_{1}}{(m-1)x_{2}-(m+1)x_{1}}$
> $\therefore \overrightarrow{OP} \cdot \overrightarrow{OQ} = my = m\left(1 - \frac{2kx_1x_2 + 2(m + 1)mx_1}{(m - 1)x_2 - (m + 1)x_1}\right) = m - \frac{2kmx_1x_2 + 2(m + 1)mx_1}{(m - 1)x_2 - (m + 1)x_1}$ (2) ÷ (1): $2kmx_1x_2 = -(m^2 - 1)(x_1 + x_2)$ (3) $\therefore \frac{2kmx_1x_2 + 2(m + 1)mx_1}{(m - 1)x_2 - (m + 1)x_1} = \frac{-(m^2 - 1)(x_1 + x_2) + 2(m + 1)mx_1}{(m - 1)x_2 - (m + 1)x_1}$ $= \frac{(1 - m)[(m + 1)x_1 - (m - 1)x_2]}{(m - 1)x_2 - (m + 1)x_1} = m - 1$ $\therefore \overrightarrow{OP} \cdot \overrightarrow{OQ} = m - (m - 1) = 1$
