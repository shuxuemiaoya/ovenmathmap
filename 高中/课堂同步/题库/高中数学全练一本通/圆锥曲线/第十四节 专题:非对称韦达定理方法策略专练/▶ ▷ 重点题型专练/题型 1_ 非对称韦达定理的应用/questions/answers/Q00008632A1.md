> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $\frac{x^{2}}{8}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$ （2）y=1
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\frac{x^{2}}{8}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$ （2）根据两点式方程 $AN:\frac{y-2}{x}=\frac{y_{2}-2}{x_{2}}\quad BM:\frac{y+2}{x}=\frac{y_{1}+2}{x_{1}}$ ，联立得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x=\frac{-2x_{1}x_{2}}{x_{1}-3x_{2}}\\&y=\frac{-2kx_{1}x_{2}-12x_{2}}{x_{1}-3x_{2}}-2\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore\frac{-2kx_{1}x_{2}-12x_{2}}{x_{1}-3x_{2}}=\frac{-2kx_{1}x_{2}-12x_{2}}{(x_{1}+x_{2})-4x_{2}}=\frac{-2k\times\frac{24}{1+2k^{2}}-12x_{2}}{\frac{-16k}{1+2k^{2}}-4x_{2}}$ $\therefore y=\frac{-2kx_{1}x_{2}-12x_{2}}{x_{1}-3x_{2}}=3$
