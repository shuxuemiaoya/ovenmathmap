> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:椭圆 $\frac{x^{2}}{25}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1$
> 则 $a^{2}=25, b^{2}=9$ ，所以 $c^{2}=16$ ，
> 则 $\left|PF_1\right| + \left|PF_2\right| = 2a = 10,\left|FF_2\right| = 2c = 8$
> 由余弦定理可知 $\cos\angle F_{1}PF_{2}=\frac{\left|PF_{1}\right|^{2}+\left|PF_{2}\right|^{2}-\left|FF_{2}\right|^{2}}{2\left|PF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|PF_{2}\right|}=\frac{1}{2}$
> 代入化简可得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|PF_{2}\right|=12$
> 则 $S_{\Delta PF_1F2} = \frac{1}{2}\left|PF_1\right|\cdot \left|PF_2\right|\cdot \sin \angle F_1PF_2 = \frac{1}{2}\times 12\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = 3\sqrt{3}$ ，
>
> 故选 **B**.
