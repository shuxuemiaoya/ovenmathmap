> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $30^{\circ}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 由椭圆方程知: a=2, b=1, $c=\sqrt{3}$ ;
> 若 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=\left|PF_{2}\right|$ , $\because\left|PF_{1}\right|+\left|PF_{2}\right|=2a$ , $\therefore\left|PF_{1}\right|=\left|PF_{2}\right|=a=2$ ,
> 又 $\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|=2c=2\sqrt{3}$ , $\therefore\cos\angle PF_{1}F_{2}=\frac{\left|PF_{1}\right|^{2}+\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|^{2}-\left|PF_{2}\right|^{2}}{2\left|PF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|}=\frac{12}{4\times2\sqrt{3}}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 又 $0^{\circ}<\angle PF_{1}F_{2}<180^{\circ}$ , $\therefore\angle PF_{1}F_{2}=30^{\circ}$ . 故答案为: $30^{\circ}$ .
