> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** AB
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \frac {x ^ {2}}{2 5} + \frac {y ^ {2}}{9} = 1
> $$
> $$
> c = \sqrt {2 5 - 9} = 4
> $$
> $$
> \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = 8
> $$
> 当 $AF_{1} \perp AF_{2}$ 时，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \left|AF_{1}\right| + \left|AF_{2}\right| = 10① \\ \left|AF_{1}\right|^{2} + \left|AF_{2}\right|^{2} = 64② \end{array} \right.$
> - **①**：平方减去②得 $\left|AF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|AF_{2}\right|=18$
> $$
> \therefore S _ {\triangle A F _ {1} F _ {2}} = \frac {1}{2} \times \left| A F _ {1} \right| \cdot \left| A F _ {2} \right| = 9
> $$
> 当 $AF_{1}\bot F_{1}F_{2}$ （或者 $AF_{2}\bot F_{1}F_{2}$ ）时， $F_{1}(-4,0)$
> 令 x=-4，则 $\frac{(-4)^{2}}{25}+\frac{y^{2}}{9}=1$ ，解得 $y=\pm\frac{9}{5}$ ，
> 则 $\left|AF_1\right| = \frac{b^2}{a} = \frac{9}{5}, S_{\triangle AF_1F_2} = \frac{1}{2}\times \frac{9}{5}\times 8 = \frac{36}{5}$ .
>
> 故选 **AB**.
