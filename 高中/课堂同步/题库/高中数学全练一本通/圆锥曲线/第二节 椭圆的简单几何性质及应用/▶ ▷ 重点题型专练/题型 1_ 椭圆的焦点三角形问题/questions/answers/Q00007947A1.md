>

> $$
> \because 2 c = 2, \therefore c = 1, \therefore b ^ {2} = 2
> $$
> ∴椭圆 C 的方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{3} + \frac{y^{2}}{2} = 1$ . 故答案为: $\frac{x^{2}}{3} + \frac{y^{2}}{2} = 1$
