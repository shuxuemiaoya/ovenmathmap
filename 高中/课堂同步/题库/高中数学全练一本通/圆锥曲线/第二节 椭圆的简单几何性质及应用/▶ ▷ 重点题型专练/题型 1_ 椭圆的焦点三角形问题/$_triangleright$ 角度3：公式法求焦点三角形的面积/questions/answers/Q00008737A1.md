> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 4
> 解析:由椭圆的定义可知, $\left|MF_{1}\right|+\left|MF_{2}\right|=6$
> $$
> \left| M F _ {1} \right| - \left| M F _ {2} \right| = 2
> $$
> 联立两式 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\left|MF_{1}\right|+\left|MF_{2}\right|=6\\ \left|MF_{1}\right|-\left|MF_{2}\right|=2\end{aligned}\right.$ ，可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\left|MF_{1}\right|=4\\ \left|MF_{2}\right|=2\end{aligned}\right.$
> 又 $\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right| = 2\sqrt{5}$
> 所以 $\left|MF_1\right|^2 +\left|MF_2\right|^2 = \left|F_1F_2\right|^2$
> 所以 $\triangle F_{1}F_{2}M$ 是以 $|MF_1|, |MF_2|$ 为直角边的直角三角形，
> 所以 $\triangle F_{1}F_{2}M$ 的面积为 $\frac{1}{2}\cdot \left|MF_1\right|\cdot \left|MF_2\right| = \frac{1}{2}\times 4\times 2 = 4$ .故答案为：4.
