> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 解析:依题意可知: $\triangle F_{1}PF_{2}$ 是直角三角形, $\angle F_{1}PF_{2}=60^{\circ},\angle PF_{2}F_{1}=30^{\circ}$ ,
> 所以 $\left|PF_{1}\right|\left|\left|PF_{2}\right|\left|\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|=1:2:\sqrt{3}\right.$ 所以 $e=\frac{c}{a}=\frac{2c}{2a}=\frac{\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|}{\left|PF_{1}\right|+\left|PF_{2}\right|}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{1+2}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ .故答案为: $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
