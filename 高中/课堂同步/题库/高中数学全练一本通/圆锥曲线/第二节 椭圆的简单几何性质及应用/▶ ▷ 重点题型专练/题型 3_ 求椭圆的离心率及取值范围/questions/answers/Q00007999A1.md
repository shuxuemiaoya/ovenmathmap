> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\left[\frac{3}{5},1\right)$
>
> **【解析】**
> 设椭圆的焦距为 $2c(c>0)$ ，由椭圆的定义可得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|+\left|PF_{2}\right|=2a$ ，又 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=4\left|PF_{2}\right|$ 可得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=\frac{8a}{5},\left|PF_{2}\right|=\frac{2a}{5}$ 由题意可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{8a}{5}\leq a+c\\&\frac{2a}{5}\geq a-c\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\frac{3}{5}\leq\frac{c}{a}<1$ $\therefore\frac{3}{5}\leq e<1$ 故答案为： $\left[\frac{3}{5},1\right)$
