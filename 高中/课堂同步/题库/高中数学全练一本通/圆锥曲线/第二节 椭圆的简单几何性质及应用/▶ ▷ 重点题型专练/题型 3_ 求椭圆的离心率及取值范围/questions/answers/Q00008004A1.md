> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 设 $P(x_{0}, y_{0})$ , $x_{0} \neq \pm a$ , $A(-a, 0)$ , $B(a, 0)$ ,
> $$
> k _ {A P} \cdot k _ {B P} = \frac {y _ {0}}{x _ {0} + a} \times \frac {y _ {0}}{x _ {0} - a} = \frac {y _ {0} ^ {2}}{x _ {0} ^ {2} - a ^ {2}} = - \frac {b ^ {2}}{a ^ {2}}
> $$
> 由题意可知， $-\frac{b^2}{a^2}\leq -\frac{2}{3}$ ，即 $\frac{a^2 - c^2}{a^2}\geq \frac{2}{3}$ 得 $\frac{c^2}{a^2}\leq \frac{1}{3}$
> 则 $e = \frac{c}{a}\in \left(0,\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right].$
>
> 故选 **B**
