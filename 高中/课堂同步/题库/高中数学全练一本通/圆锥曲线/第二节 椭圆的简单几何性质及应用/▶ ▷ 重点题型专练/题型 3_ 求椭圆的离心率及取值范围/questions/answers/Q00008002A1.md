> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:由题可得 $M(-a,0)$ ，设 $A(x_{0},y_{0}), B(-x_{0},y_{0})$ .
> $$
> k _ {A M} \cdot k _ {B M} = \frac {y _ {0}}{x _ {0} + a} \cdot \frac {y _ {0}}{a - x _ {0}} = \frac {y _ {0} ^ {2}}{a ^ {2} - x _ {0} ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{8}
> $$
> 又 $\frac{x_0^2}{a^2} +\frac{y_0^2}{b^2} = 1\Rightarrow \frac{y_0^2}{b^2} = \frac{a^2 - x_0^2}{a^2}\Rightarrow \frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{1}{8},$
> 则 $a^2 = 8b^2, c^2 = a^2 - b^2 = 7b^2$
> 则 $e^2 = \frac{c^2}{a^2} = \frac{7b^2}{8b^2} = \frac{7}{8}$ .
>
> 故选 **C**
