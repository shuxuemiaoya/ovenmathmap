> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 解析:由椭圆定义可得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|+\left|PF_{2}\right|=2a$ ，又 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=2\left|PF_{2}\right|$ 故 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=\frac{4a}{3},\left|PF_{2}\right|=\frac{2a}{3}$ 由余弦定理得 $\cos\angle F_{1}PF_{2}=\frac{\left|\left|F_{1}P\right|^{2}+\left|F_{2}P\right|^{2}-\left|\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|^{2}\right.}{2\left|\left|F_{1}P\right|\cdot\left|\left|F_{2}P\right|\right.}\right)=\frac{\frac{16a^{2}}{9}+\frac{4a^{2}}{9}-4c^{2}}{2\cdot\frac{4a}{3}\cdot\frac{2a}{3}}=\frac{\frac{20a^{2}}{9}-4c^{2}}{\frac{16a^{2}}{9}}$ 故 $\frac{\frac{20a^{2}}{9}-4c^{2}}{\frac{16a^{2}}{9}}=\frac{1}{2}$ ，故 $\frac{40a^{2}}{9}-8c^{2}=\frac{16a^{2}}{9}$ 解得 $\frac{c}{a}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，故离心率为 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 故答案为： $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
