> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
> 解析:根据题意可得 $B(b,0), F_{2}(0,c)$ ，设 $P(x,y)$ ，
> $$
> \because \overline {{{B F _ {2}}}} = 2 \overline {{{F _ {2} P}}}, \therefore (- b, c) = 2 (x, y - c), \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} - b = 2 x \\ c = 2 y - 2 c \end{array} , \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = - \frac {b}{2} \\ y = \frac {3 c}{2} \end{array} , \right. \right.
> $$
> 又点 $P(-\frac{b}{2}, \frac{3c}{2})$ 在椭圆 $C: \frac{y^2}{a^2} + \frac{x^2}{b^2} = 1 (a > b > 0)$ 上，
> $\therefore \frac{9c^2}{4a^2} +\frac{b^2}{4b^2} = 1,\therefore \frac{c^2}{a^2} = \frac{1}{3},\therefore$ 椭圆 $C$ 的离心率为 $\frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
> 故答案为: $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ .
