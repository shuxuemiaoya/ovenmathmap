> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:由题意,椭圆上存在点P,使得线段AP的垂直平分线过点F,即F点到P点与A点的距离相等,
> 又 $|FA|=\frac{a^{2}}{c}-c=\frac{b^{2}}{c},\left|PF\right|\in[a-c,a+c]$ , $\therefore\frac{b^{2}}{c}\in[a-c,a+c]$ $\therefore ac-c^{2}\leq b^{2}\leq ac+c^{2}$ $\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}&ac-c^{2}\leq a^{2}-c^{2}\\&ac+c^{2}\geq a^{2}-c^{2}\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}&e\leq1\\&2e^{2}+e-1\geq0\end{aligned}\right.$ ，又 $\because e\in(0,1)$ $\therefore e\in[\frac{1}{2},1)$ .
>
> 故选 **D**.
