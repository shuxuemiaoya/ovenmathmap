> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 由 $\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}+\frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}=1$ ，可得 $y^{2}=b^{2}\left(1-\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}\right)$
> $\therefore \left|PF\right| = \sqrt{\left(x - c\right)^2 + y^2} = \frac{c}{a}\left|x - \frac{a^2}{c}\right|$ 又 $-a\leq x\leq a$ $\therefore \left|PF\right| = \frac{c}{a}\left|x - \frac{a^2}{c}\right| = \frac{c}{a}\left(\frac{a^2}{c} -x\right) = a - \frac{c}{a} x\in [a - c,a + c]$ 即 $a - c\leq |PF|\leq a + c$
