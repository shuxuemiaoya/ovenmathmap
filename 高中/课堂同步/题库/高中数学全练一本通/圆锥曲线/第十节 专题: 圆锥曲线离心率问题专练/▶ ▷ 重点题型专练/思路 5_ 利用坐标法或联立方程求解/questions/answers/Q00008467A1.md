> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：设点 $P$ 、 $Q$ 的坐标分别是 $(x_{1},y_{1}),(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则点 $M$ 的坐标是 $\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2},\frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right),$ 从而 $k_{1} = \frac{y_{2} - y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}},k_{2} = \frac{y_{2} + y_{1}}{x_{2} + x_{1}},$ 由 $k_{1}k_{2} = 2$ ，得 $\frac{y_2^2 - y_1^2}{x_2^2 - x_1^2} = 2,$ 又 $\frac{x_1^2 - y_1^2}{a^2} = 1,\frac{x_2^2 - y_2^2}{a^2} = 1$ ，所以 $y_{1}^{2} = \frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}\big(x_{1}^{2} - a^{2}\big),y_{2}^{2} = \frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}\big(x_{2}^{2} - a^{2}\big),$ 于是 $y_{2}^{2} - y_{1}^{2} = \frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}\big(x_{2}^{2} - x_{1}^{2}\big)$ ，又 $\frac{y_2^2 - y_1^2}{x_2^2 - x_1^2} = 2$ ，得 $\frac{b^2}{a^2} = 2$ 即 $\frac{c^2 - a^2}{a^2} = 2$ ，解得 $c^2 = 3a^2$ ，从而 $e = \sqrt{3}$ .
>
> 故选 **B**.
