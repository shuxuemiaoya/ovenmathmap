> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{2} = 1$ ；（2）存在实数 $t = 2$ 。
> 解析：（1）由题意可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&e = \frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\\ &\frac{1}{2} \times 2c \times b = 2,\\ &a^2 = b^2 + c^2\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $a^2 = 4, b^2 = 2, c^2 = 2$ 。
> 故椭圆 $C$ 的标准方程为 $\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{2} = 1$ ；
> (2) 如图，由 (1) 可知 $F_1(-\sqrt{2},0), F_2(\sqrt{2},0)$ .
> 当直线 l 的斜率不存在时， $\left|AF_{1}\right|+\left|BF_{1}\right|=\frac{b^{2}}{a}=1$ ，则 $t=\frac{\left|AF_{1}\right|+\left|BF_{1}\right|}{\left|AF_{1}\right|\left|BF_{1}\right|}=2$ 当直线 l 的斜率存在时，设其斜率为 k，
> 则直线 l 的方程为 $y=k(x+\sqrt{2})$ ， $A(x_{1},y_{1})B(x_{2},y_{2})$ .
> 联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y&=k(x+\sqrt{2})\\ \frac{x^{2}}{4}&+\frac{y^{2}}{2}=1\end{aligned}\right.$ 整理得 $(2k^{2}+1)x^{2}+4\sqrt{2}k^{2}x+4k^{2}-4=0$ 则 $x_{1}+x_{2}=-\frac{4\sqrt{2}k^{2}}{2k^{2}+1}$ ， $x_{1}x_{2}=\frac{4k^{2}-4}{2k^{2}+1}$ 从而 $\left|x_{1}-x_{2}\right|=\sqrt{(x_{1}-x_{2})-4x_{1}x_{2}}=\frac{4\sqrt{k^{2}+1}}{2k^{2}+1}$ 故 $\left|AF_{1}\right|+\left|BF_{1}\right|=AB=\sqrt{k^{2}+1}\left|x_{1}-x_{2}\right|=\frac{4k^{2}+4}{2k^{2}+1}$ 由题意可得 $\left|AF_{1}\right|=\sqrt{k^{2}+1}\left|x_{1}+\sqrt{2}\right|,\left|BF_{1}\right|=\sqrt{k^{2}+1}\left|x_{2}+\sqrt{2}\right|$ 则 $\left|AF_{1}\right|\left|BF_{1}\right|=(k^{2}+1)\left|x_{1}x_{2}+\sqrt{2}(x_{1}+x_{2})+2\right|=\frac{2(k^{2}+1)}{2k^{2}+1}$ 因为 $\left|AF_{1}\right|+\left|BF_{1}\right|=t\left|AF_{1}\right|\left|BF_{1}\right|$ 所以 $t=\frac{\left|AF_{1}\right|+\left|BF_{1}\right|}{\left|AF_{1}\right|\left|BF_{1}\right|}=\frac{\frac{4k^{2}+4}{2k^{2}+1}}{\frac{2(k^{2}+1)}{2k^{2}+1}}=2$ 由题意可得 $\left|AF_{1}\right|=\sqrt{k^{2}+1}\left|x_{1}+\sqrt{2}\right|,\left|BF_{1}\right|=\sqrt{k^{2}+1}\left|x_{2}+\sqrt{2}\right|$ 则 $\left|AF_{1}\right|\left|BF_{1}\right|=(k^{2}+ 1)\left|x_{1}x_{2}+\sqrt{2}(x_{1}+x_{2})+ 2\right|=\frac{2(k^{2}+ 1)}{2k^{2}+ 1}$ 因为 $\left|AF_{1}\right|+\left|BF_{1}\right|=t\left|AF_{1}\right|\left|BF_{1}\right|$ 所以 $t=\frac{\left|\left|AF_{1}\right|\left|\left|BF_{1}\right|\right|\right|}{\left|\left|AF_{1}\right|\left|\left|BF_{1}\right|\right|\right|}=\frac{\frac{4k^{2}+4}{2k^{2}+1}}{\frac{2(k^{2}+ 1)}{2k^{2}+ 1}}=2$ 由题意可得 $\left|AF_{1}\right|=\sqrt{k^{2}+ 1}\left|x_{1}+\sqrt{2}\right|\left|x_{1}x_{2}+\sqrt{2}(x_{1}+x_{2})\right|=0\left|\frac{CF}{c}-\frac{y}{b}=1\right.$ 则  \( \left|\begin{matrix}
> A F \_{1}| & B F \_{1}| = (k^{2} + 1)
> A F \_{1}| + |B F \_{1}| = t |A F \_{1}| |B F \_{1}|
> A F \_{1}| + |B F \_{1}| &= t |A F \_{1}| |B F \_{1}|
> \end{matrix}\right. \)
