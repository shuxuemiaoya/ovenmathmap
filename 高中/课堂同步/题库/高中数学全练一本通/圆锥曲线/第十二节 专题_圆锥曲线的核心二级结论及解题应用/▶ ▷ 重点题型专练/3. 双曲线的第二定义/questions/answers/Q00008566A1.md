> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：设直线 AB 的倾斜角为 $\theta$ ,
> ∵ $\overrightarrow{AF}=4\overrightarrow{FB},\therefore\lambda=4,\because\left|e\cos\theta\right|=\left|\frac{\lambda-1}{\lambda+1}\right|,\therefore\left|e\cdot\frac{1}{2}\right|=\left|\frac{\lambda-1}{\lambda+1}\right|=\frac{4-1}{4+1}=\frac{3}{5}$ ,
> 解得 $e=\frac{6}{5}$ ，
>
> 故选 **B**.
