> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> D解析：设 $P(x_0,y_0),(x_0\neq \pm a),A(-a,0),B(a,0),$ ∴ $\frac{x_0^2}{a^2} -\frac{y_0^2}{b^2} = 1,$ ∴ $x_0^2 -a^2 = \frac{a^2}{b^2}y_0^2,$ ∴ $k_{1}\cdot k_{2} = \frac{y_{0}}{x_{0} + a}\cdot \frac{y_{0}}{x_{0} - a} = \frac{y_{0}^{2}}{x_{0}^{2} - a^{2}} = \frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}} = 8,$ ∴ $e = \frac{c}{a} = \sqrt{1 + \frac{b^2}{a^2}} = \sqrt{1 + 8} = 3.$
>
> 故选 **D**.
