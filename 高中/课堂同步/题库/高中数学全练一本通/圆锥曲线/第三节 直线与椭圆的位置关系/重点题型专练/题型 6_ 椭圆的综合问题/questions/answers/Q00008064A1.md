> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $\left(\frac{4}{4+3t^{2}},\frac{-3t}{4+3t^{2}}\right)$ （2）证明见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）由题意可得 $x_{1}\neq x_{2},t\neq0$ ，设 $P(x_{1},y_{1}),Q(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则 $P_{1}(x_{1},-y_{1})$ ，
> 联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=ty+1\\ \frac{x^{2}}{4}&+\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，得 $(4+3t^{2})y^{2}+6ty-9=0$ 则 $\Delta>0,y_{1}+y_{2}=-\frac{6t}{4+3t^{2}},y_{1}y_{2}=\frac{-9}{4+3t^{2}}$ $\therefore x_{1}+x_{2}=t(y_{1}+y_{2})+2=\frac{8}{4+3t^{2}}$ 故 PQ 中点坐标为 $\left(\frac{4}{4+3t^{2}},\frac{-3t}{4+3t^{2}}\right)$ .
> （2）直线 $PQ: y + y_{1} = \frac{y_{2} + y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}}(x - x_{1})$ 当 y=0 时， $x=x_{1}+\frac{y_{1}(x_{2}-x_{1})}{y_{2}+y_{1}}=ty_{1}+1+\frac{y_{1}(ty_{2}+1-ty_{1}-1)}{y_{2}+y_{1}}=\frac{2ty_{1}y_{2}}{y_{2}+y_{1}}+1$ $=\frac{2t\cdot\frac{-9}{4+3t^{2}}}{\frac{-6t}{4+3t^{2}}}+1=4$ ，所以直线 PQ 过定点 $(4,0)$ .
