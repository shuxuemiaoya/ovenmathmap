> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> 1: \because a x + y - a + 1 = 0
> $$
> $$
> a (x - 1) + y + 1 = 0
> $$
> $$
> \frac {x ^ {2}}{3} + \frac {y ^ {2}}{2} = 1 \therefore \frac {1 ^ {2}}{3} + \frac {(- 1) ^ {2}}{2} = 1
> $$
> $$
> 2: \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {x ^ {2}}{3} + \frac {y ^ {2}}{2} = 1 \\ a x + y - a + 1 = 0 \end{array} \Rightarrow (3 a ^ {2} + 2) x ^ {2} - 6 a (a - 1) x + 3 \left(a ^ {2} - 2 a - 1\right) = 0 \right.
> $$
> $$
> \Delta = 3 6 a ^ {2} (a - 1) ^ {2} - 1 2 \left(3 a ^ {2} + 2\right) \left(a ^ {2} - 2 a - 1\right) = 4 8 a ^ {2} + 4 8 a + 2 4 = 4 8 \left(a + \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} + 1 2 > 0
> $$
> 解析:由题知直线 $l: y = k(x + \sqrt{3})$ 过定点 $(- \sqrt{3}, 0)$ ,
> 因为直线 $l: y = k(x + \sqrt{3})$ 与椭圆 $C: \frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + y^{2} = 1 \quad (a > 1)$ 总有公共点，
> 所以点 $(- \sqrt{3}, 0)$ 在椭圆上或椭圆内，
> 所以 $\frac{3}{a^{2}} \leq 1$ ，由于 a > 1，所以 $a \geq \sqrt{3}$ 所以实数 a 的取值范围是 $\left[\sqrt{3}, +\infty\right)$
