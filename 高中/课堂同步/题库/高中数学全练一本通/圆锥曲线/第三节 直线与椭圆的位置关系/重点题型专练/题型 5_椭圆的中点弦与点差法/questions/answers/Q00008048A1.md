> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{4}{3}$ 解析：设 $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ 、 $B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，故有 $\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{9}+\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{4}=1$ ①， $\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{9}+\frac{y_{2}^{2}}{4}=1$ ②，
> 所以，两式作差得 $\frac{x_{2}^{2}-x_{1}^{2}}{9}+\frac{y_{2}^{2}-y_{1}^{2}}{4}=0$ ，即 $\frac{(x_{2}-x_{1})(x_{2}+x_{1})}{9}+\frac{(y_{2}-y_{1})(y_{2}+y_{1})}{4}=0$ 所以， $k_{AB}=\frac{y_{2}-y_{1}}{x_{2}-x_{1}}=-\frac{4(x_{1}+x_{2})}{9(y_{1}+y_{2})}$ 因为 AB 的中点为 $M\left(-1,\frac{1}{3}\right)$ ,
> 所以 $x_{1}+x_{2}=-2, y_{1}+y_{2}=\frac{2}{3}$
> 所以 $k_{AB} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = -\frac{4 \times (-2)}{9 \times \frac{2}{3}} = \frac{4}{3}$
>
> 故选 **A**
