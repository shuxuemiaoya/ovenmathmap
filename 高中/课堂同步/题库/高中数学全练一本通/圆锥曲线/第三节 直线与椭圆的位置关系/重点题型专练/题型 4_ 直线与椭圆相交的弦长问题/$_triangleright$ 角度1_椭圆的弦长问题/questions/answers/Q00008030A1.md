> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{5\sqrt{5}}{3}$ 解析：法一：联立 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}2x - y - 2 = 0\\ \frac{x^2}{5} +\frac{y^2}{4} = 1\end{array}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}x = 0\\ y = -2\end{array}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}x = \frac{5}{3}\\ y = \frac{4}{3}\end{array}\right.$ 不妨设 $A(0, - 2)$ ， $B\left(\frac{5}{3},\frac{4}{3}\right)$ $\therefore |AB| = \sqrt{\left(\frac{5}{3} - 0\right)^2 + \left(\frac{4}{3} + 2\right)^2} = \frac{5\sqrt{5}}{3}.$ 法二：联立 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}2x - y - 2 = 0\\ \frac{x^2}{5} +\frac{y^2}{4} = 1 \end{array}\right.$ ，消去 $y$ 得， $24x^{2} - 40x = 0$ 设点 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ 则 $x_{1} + x_{2} = \frac{5}{3},x_{1}x_{2} = 0$ ，∴ $\left|AB\right| = \sqrt{1 + 2^2}\sqrt{\left(x_1 + x_2\right)^2 - 4x_1x_2} = \sqrt{5}\times \frac{5}{3} = \frac{5\sqrt{5}}{3}$ 法三：联立 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}2x - y - 2 = 0\\ \frac{x^2}{5} +\frac{y^2}{4} = 1 \end{array}\right.$ ，消去 $x$ 得， $3y^{2} + 2y - 8 = 0$ 设点 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，则由根与系数的关系得 $y_{1} + y_{2} = -\frac{2}{3},y_{1}y_{2} = -\frac{8}{3}$ 所以 $|AB| = \sqrt{1 + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2}\sqrt{(y_1 + y_2)^2 - 4y_1y_2} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\times \sqrt{\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 - 4\times\left(-\frac{8}{3}\right)} = \frac{5\sqrt{5}}{3}.$
