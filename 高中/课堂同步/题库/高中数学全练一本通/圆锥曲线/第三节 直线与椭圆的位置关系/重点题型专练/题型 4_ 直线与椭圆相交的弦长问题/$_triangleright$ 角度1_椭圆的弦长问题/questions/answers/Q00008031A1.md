> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $y = x + 1$ 或 $y = -x + 1$ 解析：设 $M(x_{1},y_{1}),N(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，由 $\left\{\begin{array}{ll}y = kx + 1,\\ \frac{x^2}{2} +y^2 = 1, \end{array}\right.$ 消去 $y$ 并化简得 $(1 + 2k^{2})x^{2} + 4kx = 0,$ 所以 $x_{1} + x_{2} = -\frac{4k}{1 + 2k^{2}},x_{1}x_{2} = 0,$ 由 $|MN| = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ，得 $(x_{1} - x_{2})^{2} + (y_{1} - y_{2})^{2} = \frac{32}{9},$ 所以 $(1 + k^{2})(x_{1} - x_{2})^{2} = \frac{32}{9}$ 所以 $(1 + k^{2})\left[(x_{1} + x_{2})^{2} - 4x_{1}x_{2}\right] = \frac{32}{9},$ 即 $(1 + k^{2})\left(\frac{-4k}{1 + 2k^{2}}\right)^{2} = \frac{32}{9},$
> 化简得 $k^4 + k^2 - 2 = 0$ ，所以 $k^2 = 1$ ，所以 $k = \pm 1$ .
> 故直线方程为 $y = x + 1$ 或 $y = -x + 1$ .
