> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> ±1
> 解析：椭圆 $3x^2 + 4y^2 = 48$ ，即 $\frac{x^2}{16} + \frac{y^2}{12} = 1$ ，则 $a = 4, b = 2\sqrt{3}, c = 2$ ，左焦点为 $(-2,0)$ ,
> 设直线 $AB$ 为 $x = my - 2, A(x_1, y_1), B(x_2, y_2)$ ,
> 由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3x^2 + 4y^2 = 48 \\ x = my - 2 \end{array} \right.$ ，得 $3(my - 2)^2 + 4y^2 = 48$ ,
> 整理得 $(3m^2 + 4)y^2 - 12my - 36 = 0$ ,
> 因为 $\Delta = 144m^2 + 144(3m^2 + 4) > 0$ ,
> 所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y_1 + y_2 = \frac{12m}{3m^2 + 4} \\ y_1y_2 = \frac{-36}{3m^2 + 4} \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $|AB| = \sqrt{1 + m^2} \cdot \sqrt{(y_1 + y_2)^2 - 4y_1y_2} = \sqrt{1 + m^2} \cdot \sqrt{\left(\frac{12m}{3m^2 + 4}\right)^2 + \frac{144}{3m^2 + 4}} = \frac{48}{7}$ , $\frac{24(1 + m^2)}{3m^2 + 4} = \frac{48}{7}$ ，解得 $m = \pm 1$ ，所以直线 $AB$ 为斜率为 ±1，故答案为：±1.
