> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $x^{2}=4y$ （2）证明见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）设 $A(x_{1},y_{1})B(x_{2},y_{2})$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x^{2}=2py\\&y=x+\frac{p}{2}\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow y^{2}-3py+\frac{p^{2}}{4}=0,\quad y_{1}+y_{2}=3p,$ $\therefore\left|AB\right|=y_{1}+y_{2}+p=4p=8,\therefore p=2,x^{2}=4y;$ （2）由题得 $l_{1}$ 斜率一定存在，设 $l_{1}:y=kx+m,(m\neq0),M(x_{3},y_{3}),N(x_{4},y_{4}).$ 则 $\overrightarrow{OM}\cdot\overrightarrow{ON}=0,\quad x_{3}x_{4}+y_{3}y_{4}=0,$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}x^{2}=4y\\ y=kx+m\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow x^{2}-4kx-4m=0,\quad x_{3}+x_{4}=4k,\quad x_{3}\cdot x_{4}=-4m,\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore x_{3}x_{4}+y_{3}y_{4}=x_{3}x_{4}+(kx_{3}+m)(kx_{4}+m)=(k^{2}+1)x_{3}x_{4}+km(x_{3}+x_{4})+m^{2}=0,$ $\therefore m^{2}-4m=0,\quad m\neq0,\quad\therefore m=4,\quad\therefore l_{1}:y=kx+4,$ 恒过点 $(0,4)$ .
