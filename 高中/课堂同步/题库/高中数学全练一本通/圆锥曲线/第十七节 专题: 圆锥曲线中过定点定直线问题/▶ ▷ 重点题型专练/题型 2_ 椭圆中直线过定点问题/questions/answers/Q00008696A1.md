> **【解析】**
> （1） $\frac{x^{2}}{3}+y^{2}=1$ ；（2）存在，直线 l 过定点 $\left(0,-\frac{1}{2}\right)$ .

> 2 c = 2 \sqrt {2}, \because 1 <   c

> a ^ {2} - 1 = 2

> $$
> \frac {x ^ {2}}{3} + y ^ {2} = 1
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = k x + m \\ \frac {x ^ {2}}{3} + y ^ {2} = 1 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> \frac {x ^ {2}}{3} + (k x + m) ^ {2} = 1
> $$
> $$
> \left(\frac {1}{3} + k ^ {2}\right) x ^ {2} + 2 k m x + m ^ {2} - 1 = 0
> $$
> $$
> \Delta = 4 k ^ {2} m ^ {2} - 4 \left(\frac {1}{3} + k ^ {2}\right) (m ^ {2} - 1) = 4 \left(k ^ {2} - \frac {1}{3} m ^ {2} + \frac {1}{3}\right) > 0
> $$
> $$
> 3 k ^ {2} - m ^ {2} + 1 > 0
> $$
> 设 A 、 B 两点的坐标分别为 $(x_{1}, y_{1})$ 、 $(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ，
> 则 $x_{1} + x_{2} = -\frac{6km}{1 + 3k^{2}}$ ， $x_{1}x_{2} = \frac{3(m^{2} - 1)}{1 + 3k^{2}}$ ∴ $y_{1} + y_{2} = k(x_{1} + x_{2}) + 2m = \frac{2m}{1 + 3k^{2}}$ $y_{1}y_{2}=(kx_{1}+m)(kx_{2}+m)=k^{2}x_{1}x_{2}+km(x_{1}+x_{2})+m^{2}$ $=\frac{3k^{2}(m^{2}-1)}{1+3k^{2}}-\frac{6k^{2}m^{2}}{1+3k^{2}}+m^{2}=\frac{m^{2}-3k^{2}}{1+3k^{2}}$ ∵ 以 AB 为直径的圆过椭圆的上顶点 $Q(0,1)$ ，∴ $AQ \perp BQ$ ，即 $\overline{QA} \cdot \overline{QB} = 0$ 即 $\overline{QA} \cdot \overline{QB} = x_{1}x_{2} + (y_{1} - 1)(y_{2} - 1) = x_{1}x_{2} + y_{1}y_{2} - (y_{1} + y_{2}) + 1$ $=\frac{3(m^{2}-1)}{1+3k^{2}}+\frac{m^{2}-3k^{2}}{1+3k^{2}}-\frac{2m}{1+3k^{2}}+1=\frac{4m^{2}-2m-2}{1+3k^{2}}=0$ 化简得 $2m^{2}-m-1=0$ ，∴ m=1 或 m=- $\frac{1}{2}$ .
> 当 m=1 时，直线 l: y=kx+1 过定点 $Q(0,1)$ ，与已知矛盾.
> 当 $m=-\frac{1}{2}$ 时，满足 $3k^{2}-m^{2}+1>0$ ，此时直线 l 为 y=kx- $\frac{1}{2}$ 过定点 $(0,-\frac{1}{2})$ . ∴ 直线 l 过定点 $(0,-\frac{1}{2})$ .
