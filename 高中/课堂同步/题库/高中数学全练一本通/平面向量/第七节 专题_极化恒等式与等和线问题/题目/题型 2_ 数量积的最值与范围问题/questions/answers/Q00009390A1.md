> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 记 AB 的中点为 M, 连接 CM, 则 $CM = \frac{5}{2}$ 由极化恒等式可得： $\overrightarrow{PA}\cdot\overrightarrow{PB}=\left|\overrightarrow{PM}\right|^{2}-\frac{1}{4}\left|\overrightarrow{AB}\right|^{2}=\left|\overrightarrow{PM}\right|^{2}-\frac{25}{4}$ $\because\left|PM\right|_{\max}=\left|CM\right|+1=\frac{7}{2},\therefore\overrightarrow{PA}\cdot\overrightarrow{PB}=\left|\overrightarrow{PM}\right|^{2}-\frac{25}{4}=6$ $\because\left|PM\right|_{\max}=\left|CM\right|-1=\frac{3}{2},\therefore\overrightarrow{PA}\cdot\overrightarrow{PB}=\left|\overrightarrow{PM}\right|^{2}-\frac{25}{4}=-4$ 即 $\overrightarrow{PA}\cdot\overrightarrow{PB}\in[-4,6]$
>
> 故选 **D**
