> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} +\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AC},\overrightarrow{CG} = \frac{2}{5}\overrightarrow{AB} -\overrightarrow{AC};$ （2） $\frac{2}{9}$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A D} = \overrightarrow {B D} - \overrightarrow {B A} = \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {B C} + \overrightarrow {A B} = \frac {1}{3} (\overrightarrow {A C} - \overrightarrow {A B}) + \overrightarrow {A B} = \frac {2}{3} \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A C};
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {C G} = \overrightarrow {A G} - \overrightarrow {A C} = \frac {2}{5} \overrightarrow {A B} - \overrightarrow {A C}
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A O} = \lambda \overrightarrow {A D}
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A O} = \lambda (\overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {B D})
> $$
> $$
> = \lambda \left(\overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {B C}\right) = \lambda \left(\overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A C} - \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A B}\right) = \frac {2}{3} \lambda \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {\lambda}{3} \overrightarrow {A C}
> $$
> 由 G, O, C 三点共线, 存在唯一 $\mu \in R$ ,
> 使得 $\overrightarrow{AO} = \mu \overrightarrow{AG} + (1 - \mu) \overrightarrow{AC} = \frac{2\mu}{5} \overrightarrow{AB} + (1 - \mu) \overrightarrow{AC}$ ,
> 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}1-\mu=\frac{\lambda}{3}\\ \frac{2}{5}\mu=\frac{2\lambda}{3}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，则 $\mu=\frac{5}{6}$ ， $\lambda=\frac{1}{2}$ .
> 有 $\overrightarrow{AO} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} +\frac{1}{6}\overrightarrow{AC}$
> 在图2中，由 $E,O,F$ 三点共线，存在唯一 $m\in \mathbb{R}$ ，使得 $\overrightarrow{AO} = m\overrightarrow{AE} +(1 - m)\overrightarrow{AF} = mx\overrightarrow{AB} +(1 - m)y\overrightarrow{AC}$
> 有 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{3}&=mx\\ \frac{1}{6}&=(1-m)y\end{aligned}\right.$ ，则 $\frac{1}{3x}+\frac{1}{6y}=1,\because0<x<1,0<y<1,$
> $\therefore 1 = \frac{1}{3x} + \frac{1}{6y} \geq 2\sqrt{\frac{1}{3x} \cdot \frac{1}{6y}}$ ， $\therefore xy \geq \frac{2}{9}$ ，当且仅当 $\frac{1}{3x} = \frac{1}{6y}$ 即 $x = \frac{2}{3}$ ， $y = \frac{1}{3}$ 时， $xy$ 有最小值为 $\frac{2}{9}$ .
