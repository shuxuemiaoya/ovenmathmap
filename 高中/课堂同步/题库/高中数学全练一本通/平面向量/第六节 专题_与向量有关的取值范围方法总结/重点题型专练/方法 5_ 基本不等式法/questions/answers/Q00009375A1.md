> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:由于 B,C,D 三点共线,所以 $\lambda+\mu=1$ ,
> 所以 $\frac{2}{\lambda}+\frac{1}{\mu}=\left(\frac{2}{\lambda}+\frac{1}{\mu}\right)(\lambda+\mu)=3+\frac{2\mu}{\lambda}+\frac{\lambda}{\mu}$ $\geq3+2\sqrt{\frac{2\mu}{\lambda}\cdot\frac{\lambda}{\mu}}=3+2\sqrt{2}$ 当且仅当 $\frac{2\mu}{\lambda}=\frac{\lambda}{\mu},\lambda=\sqrt{2}\mu,\left\{\begin{aligned}\lambda=\sqrt{2}\mu\\ \lambda+\mu=1\end{aligned}\Rightarrow\lambda=2-\sqrt{2},\mu=\sqrt{2}-1\right.$ .
>
> 故选 **C**
