> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:由题可知 $\overrightarrow{AE}=\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB},\overrightarrow{DF}=\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{DA}$
> ![](../../../../../../images/answers/7a5106d1bf86e6d4fc8c9f635da2c021a421295e732c787829f323fa20514286.jpg)
> 设 $\overrightarrow{DG} = \lambda \overrightarrow{DE}, \overrightarrow{FG} = \mu \overrightarrow{FC}, \lambda, \mu \in (0,1)$ 则 $\overrightarrow{AG} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{DG} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \lambda \overrightarrow{DE} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \lambda (\overrightarrow{AE} - \overrightarrow{AD})$ $=\lambda\overrightarrow{AE}+(1-\lambda)\overrightarrow{AD}=\frac{1}{2}\lambda\overrightarrow{AB}+(1-\lambda)\overrightarrow{AD}$ 又 $\overrightarrow{AG}=\overrightarrow{AF}+\overrightarrow{FG}=\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AD}+\mu\overrightarrow{FC}=\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AD}+\mu(\overrightarrow{FD}+\overrightarrow{DC})$ $=\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AD}+\mu\left(\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AD}+\overrightarrow{AB}\right)=\left(\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{3}\mu\right)\overrightarrow{AD}+\mu\overrightarrow{AB}$ 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{2}\lambda &=\mu\\ 1-\lambda &=\frac{2}{3}+\frac{1}{3}\mu\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\lambda=\frac{2}{7},\mu=\frac{1}{7}$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{AG}=\frac{1}{7}\overrightarrow{AB}+\frac{5}{7}\overrightarrow{AD}$ .
>
> 故选 **A**.
