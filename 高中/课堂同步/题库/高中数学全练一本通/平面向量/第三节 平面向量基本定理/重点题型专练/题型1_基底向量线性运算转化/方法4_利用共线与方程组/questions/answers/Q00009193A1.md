> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：设 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \lambda \overrightarrow{AD}$ ， $\overrightarrow{EP} = \mu \overrightarrow{EC}$ ，
> 所以 $\overrightarrow{BP} = \overrightarrow{AP} - \overrightarrow{AB} = \lambda \overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AB} = \lambda (\overrightarrow{BD} - \overrightarrow{BA}) - \overrightarrow{AB}$ ，
> 又 $\overrightarrow{BD} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{BC}$ 所以 $\overrightarrow{BP}=\frac{\lambda}{3}\overrightarrow{BC}+\left(1-\lambda\right)\overrightarrow{BA}$ 因为 $\overrightarrow{BE}=\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BA}$ 所以 $\overrightarrow{BP}=\overline{BE}+\overline{EP}=\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BA}+\mu\overrightarrow{EC}=\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BA}+\mu\left(\overrightarrow{BC}-\overrightarrow{BE}\right)$ $=\frac{2}{3}(1-\mu)\overrightarrow{BA}+\mu\overrightarrow{BC}$ 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{\lambda}{3}&=\mu\\ \frac{2}{3}&-\frac{2}{3}\mu=1-\lambda\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\lambda&=\frac{3}{7}\\ \mu&=\frac{1}{7}\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $\overrightarrow{BP}=\frac{1}{7}\overrightarrow{BC}+\frac{4}{7}\overrightarrow{BA}=\frac{1}{7}\vec{a}+\frac{4}{7}\vec{b}$ ，
>
> 故选 **B**.
