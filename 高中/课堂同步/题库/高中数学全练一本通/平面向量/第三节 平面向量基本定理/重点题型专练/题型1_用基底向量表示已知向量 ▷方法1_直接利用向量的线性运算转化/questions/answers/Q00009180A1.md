> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 令 \(\triangle ABC\) 的边 \(BC, AC\) 上的中线 \(AD\) 与 \(BE\) 交于点 \(G\), 则 \(G\) 是 \(\triangle ABC\) 的重心, 则有 \(\overrightarrow{GD} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{a}, \overrightarrow{BG} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BE} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{b}\),
> 所以 \(\overrightarrow{BC} = 2\overrightarrow{BD} = 2(\overrightarrow{BG} + \overrightarrow{GD}) = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{a} + \frac{4}{3}\overrightarrow{b}\).
>
> 故选 **B**
