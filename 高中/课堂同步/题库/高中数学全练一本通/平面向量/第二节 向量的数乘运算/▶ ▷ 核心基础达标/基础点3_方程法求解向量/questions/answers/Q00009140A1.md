> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\vec{x} = 3\vec{a} +2\vec{b}$ ， $\vec{y} = 4\vec{a} +3\vec{b}$
>
> **【解析】**
> $\left\{\begin{aligned}&3\vec{x}-2\vec{y}=\vec{a},①\\&-4\vec{x}+3\vec{y}=\vec{b},②\end{aligned}\right.$
> - **①**：×4 + ② ×3，得 $\left(12\vec{x}-8\vec{y}\right)+\left(-12\vec{x}+9\vec{y}\right)=4\vec{a}+3\vec{b}$
> $$
> \vec {y} = 4 \vec {a} + 3 \vec {b}
> $$
> $$
> \vec {x} = \frac {1}{3} (\vec {a} + 2 \vec {y}) = \frac {1}{3} (\vec {a} + 8 \vec {a} + 6 \vec {b}) = 3 \vec {a} + 2 \vec {b}
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \vec {x} = 3 \vec {a} + 2 \vec {b}, \vec {y} = 4 \vec {a} + 3 \vec {b}
> $$
