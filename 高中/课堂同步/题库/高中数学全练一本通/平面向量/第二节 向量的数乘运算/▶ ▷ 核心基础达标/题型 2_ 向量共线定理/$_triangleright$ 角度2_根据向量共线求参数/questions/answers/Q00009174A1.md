> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{AG} -\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BC},\overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AG} +\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{BC}$ ；（2）1.
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）因为 $\overrightarrow{BG} = 2\overrightarrow{GC}$ ，则
> $$
> \overrightarrow {B G} = \frac {2}{3} \overrightarrow {B C}, \overrightarrow {A G} = \overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {B G} = \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {2}{3} (\overrightarrow {A C} - \overrightarrow {A B}) = \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {2}{3} \overrightarrow {A C}
> $$
> 即 $\overrightarrow{AB} + 2\overrightarrow{AC} = 3\overrightarrow{AG}$ ，又 $\overrightarrow{AC} -\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{BC}$
> 所以 $\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{AG} -\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BC},\overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AG} +\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{BC}$
> （2）由（1）及已知得 $\overrightarrow{AF}=\frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{AG}=\frac{3}{4}\left(\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB}+\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AC}\right)=\frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{AB}+\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AC}$
> 因为 $\overrightarrow{DB} = \lambda \overrightarrow{AD},\overrightarrow{EC} = \mu \overrightarrow{AE}$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{1}{1 + \lambda}\overrightarrow{AB},\overrightarrow{AE} = \frac{1}{1 + \mu}\overrightarrow{AC}$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {D E} = \overrightarrow {A E} - \overrightarrow {A D} = \frac {1}{1 + \mu} \overrightarrow {A C} - \frac {1}{1 + \lambda} \overrightarrow {A B}
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {D F} = \overrightarrow {A F} - \overrightarrow {A D} = \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {A C} + (\frac {1}{4} - \frac {1}{1 + \lambda}) \overrightarrow {A B},
> $$
> 又点 D, F, E 共线，即 $\overrightarrow{DE} // \overrightarrow{DF}$ ，即有 $\overrightarrow{DE} = t \overrightarrow{DF}, t \in R$ ，显然 $t \neq 0$
> 于是 $\frac{1}{1 + \mu}\overrightarrow{AC} -\frac{1}{1 + \lambda}\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{t}{2}\overrightarrow{AC} +\left(\frac{1}{4} -\frac{1}{1 + \lambda}\right)t\overrightarrow{AB}$ ，而向量 $\overrightarrow{AB},\overrightarrow{AC}$ 不共线，
> 因此 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{1}{1+\mu}&=\frac{t}{2}\\ \frac{1}{1+\lambda}&=(\frac{1}{1+\lambda}-\frac{1}{4})t\end{aligned}\right.$ ，则 $\frac{1}{1+\lambda}=(\frac{1}{1+\lambda}-\frac{1}{4})\cdot\frac{2}{1+\mu}$ ，整理得 $\lambda+2\mu=1$ ，
> 所以 $\lambda + 2\mu$ 为定值, 此定值为 1.
