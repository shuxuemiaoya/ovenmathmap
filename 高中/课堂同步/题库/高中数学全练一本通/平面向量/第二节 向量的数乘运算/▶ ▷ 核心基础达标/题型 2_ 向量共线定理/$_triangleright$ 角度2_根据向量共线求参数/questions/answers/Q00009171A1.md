> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 若 A, B, D 三点共线, 则存在实数 $\lambda$ , 使 $\overrightarrow{BD} = \lambda \overrightarrow{AB}$ ,
> $$
> \overrightarrow {B D} = \overrightarrow {C D} - \overrightarrow {C B} = (2 k - 1) \overrightarrow {e _ {1}} - (3 + k) \overrightarrow {e _ {2}},
> $$
> $$
> \therefore (2 k - 1) \overrightarrow {e _ {1}} - (3 + k) \overrightarrow {e _ {2}} = \lambda (\overrightarrow {e _ {1}} + 2 \overrightarrow {e _ {2}})
> $$
> $\because \vec{e}_{1}$ 与 $\vec{e}_{2}$ 是两个不共线向量，
> ∴ $2k - 1 = \lambda$ ，且 $-(3 + k) = 2\lambda$ ，解得 $k = -\frac{1}{5}$ ，
>
> 故选 **B**.
