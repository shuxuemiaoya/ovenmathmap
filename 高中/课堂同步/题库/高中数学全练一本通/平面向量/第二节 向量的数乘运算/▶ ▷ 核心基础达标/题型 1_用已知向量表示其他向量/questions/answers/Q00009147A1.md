> **【解析】**
> 解析: 因为 $\overrightarrow{DC}=2\overrightarrow{AB},\overrightarrow{BE}=2\overrightarrow{EC}$
> 所以 $\overrightarrow{DE} = \overrightarrow{DC} +\overrightarrow{CE} = \overrightarrow{DC} +\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{CB} = \overrightarrow{DC} +\frac{1}{3}\Bigl (\overrightarrow{DB} -\overrightarrow{DC}\Bigr)$ $= \overrightarrow{DC} +\frac{1}{3}\Bigl (\overrightarrow{DA} +\overrightarrow{AB} -\overrightarrow{DC}\Bigr)$ $= \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{DC} +\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{DA} +\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{DC} +\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{DA} +\frac{1}{6}\overrightarrow{DC} = \frac{5}{6}\vec{a} +\frac{1}{3}\vec{b}$ .
>
> 故选 **C**
