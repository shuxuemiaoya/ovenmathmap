> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $(\sqrt{2},\sqrt{2});\left(-\frac{3}{2},\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$
>
> **【解析】**
> 设点 $A(x,y),B(x_{0},y_{0})$ , $\because\left|\vec{a}\right|=2$ ，且 $\angle A O x=45^{\circ}$ , $\therefore x=2\cos45^{\circ}=\sqrt{2},y=2\sin45^{\circ}=\sqrt{2}$ . $\because\left|\vec{b}\right|=3,\angle xO B=90^{\circ}+30^{\circ}=120^{\circ}$ , $\therefore x_{0}=3\cos120^{\circ}=-\frac{3}{2},y_{0}=3\sin120^{\circ}=\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$ .
> 故 $\vec{a}=\overrightarrow{OA}=(\sqrt{2},\sqrt{2}),\vec{b}=\overrightarrow{OB}=\left(-\frac{3}{2},\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ .
> 故答案为: $\left(\sqrt{2},\sqrt{2}\right);\left(-\frac{3}{2},\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$
