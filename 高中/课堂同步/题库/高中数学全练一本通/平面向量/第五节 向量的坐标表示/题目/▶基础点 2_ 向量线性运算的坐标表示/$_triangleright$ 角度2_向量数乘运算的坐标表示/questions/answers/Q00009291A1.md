> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \vec {c} = - 2 \vec {a} + 2 \vec {b}. \tag {16}
> $$
> $$
> \vec {c} = x \vec {a} + y \vec {b}
> $$
> 则 $(10, -4) = x(-2,3) + y(3,1) = (-2x + 3y,3x + y)$
> $$
> \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 1 0 = - 2 x + 3 y \\ - 4 = 3 x + y \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> x = - 2, y = 2
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \vec {c} = - 2 \vec {a} + 2 \vec {b}.
> $$
