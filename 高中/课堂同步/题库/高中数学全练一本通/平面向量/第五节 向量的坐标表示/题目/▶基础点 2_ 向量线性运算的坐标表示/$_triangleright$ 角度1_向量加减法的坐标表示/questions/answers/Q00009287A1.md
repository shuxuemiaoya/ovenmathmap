> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** (1) (4,1) (2) (-2,1)
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）∵四边形ABCD是平行四边形，
> $$
> \therefore \overrightarrow {B C} = (3, 4) - (- 1, 3) = (4, 1)
> $$
> $$
> (2) \text { 设 } A (x, y), \text { 又 } D (2, 2),
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \overrightarrow {A D} = (2 - x, 2 - y),
> $$
> $$
> \text {又} \overrightarrow {A D} = \overrightarrow {B C},
> $$
> $$
> \therefore (2 - x, 2 - y) = (4, 1),
> $$
> 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}2-x=4\\ 2-y=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x=-2\\ y=1\end{aligned}\right.$
