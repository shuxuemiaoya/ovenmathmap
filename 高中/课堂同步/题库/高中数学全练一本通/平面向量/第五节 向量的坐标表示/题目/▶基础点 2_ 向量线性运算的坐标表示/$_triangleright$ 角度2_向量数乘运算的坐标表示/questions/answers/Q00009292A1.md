> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> $\left(\frac{15}{4},-\frac{5}{2}\right)$ .
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A D} = \overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {B C} + \overrightarrow {C D} = (3 \vec {i} - 2 \vec {j}) + (4 \vec {i} + \vec {j}) + (8 \vec {i} - 9 \vec {j}) = 1 5 \vec {i} - 1 0 \vec {j}
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A D} = 4 \vec {a}
> $$
> $$
> \vec {a} = \frac {1 5}{4} \vec {i} - \frac {5}{2} \vec {j}
> $$
> 因此 $\vec{a}$ 在基 $\left\{\vec{i},\vec{j}\right\}$ 下的坐标为 $\left(\frac{15}{4},-\frac{5}{2}\right)$ .
