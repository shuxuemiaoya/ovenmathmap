> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \overrightarrow {O P} = (a, 0), \overrightarrow {O A} = (3, - 1), \overrightarrow {O B} = (3, 2)
> $$
> 则 $(a,0) = (6, - 2) + (3\lambda ,2\lambda)$
> 则有 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a = 6 + 3\lambda \\ 0 = -2 + 2\lambda \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\lambda = 1\\ a = 9 \end{array} \right.$
