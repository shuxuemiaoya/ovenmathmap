> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 因为 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \lambda \left( \frac{\overrightarrow{AB}}{\left| \overrightarrow{AB} \right| \cos B} + \frac{\overrightarrow{AC}}{\left| \overrightarrow{AC} \right| \cos C} \right)$ ,
> $$
> \therefore \overrightarrow {A P} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} = \lambda \left(\frac {\overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C}}{\left| \overrightarrow {A B} \right| \cos B} + \frac {\overrightarrow {A C} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C}}{\left| \overrightarrow {A C} \right| \cos C}\right)
> $$
> $$
> = \lambda \left(- \frac {\left| \overrightarrow {A B} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow {B C} \right| \cos B}{\left| \overrightarrow {A B} \right| \cos B} + \frac {\left| \overrightarrow {A C} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow {B C} \right| \cos C}{\left| \overrightarrow {A C} \right| \cos C}\right) = 0
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \overrightarrow {A P} \perp \overrightarrow {B C}
> $$
