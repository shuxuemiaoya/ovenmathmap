> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $\sqrt{7}$ （2） $\frac{3\sqrt{21}}{14}$
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）解：由题知 $\because \angle BAC = 120^{\circ}, AB = AC = 4,$
> $\therefore \triangle ABC$ 是等腰三角形， $\angle ABC = \angle ACB = 30^{\circ}$
> 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中, 由余弦定理得:
> $$
> \cos \angle B A C = \frac {A B ^ {2} + A C ^ {2} - B C ^ {2}}{2 A B \times A C}
> $$
> 即 $\frac{32 - BC^2}{32} = -\frac{1}{2}$
> $\therefore BC = 4\sqrt{3}$
> $$
> \because D C = 3 B D
> $$
> $$
> \therefore B D = \sqrt {3}, C D = 3 \sqrt {3}
> $$
> 在 $\triangle ABD$ 中, 由余弦定理得:
> $$
> \cos \angle A B C = \frac {A B ^ {2} + B D ^ {2} - A D ^ {2}}{2 A B \times B D}
> $$
> 即 $\frac{4^2 + \sqrt{3}^2 - AD^2}{2 \times 4 \times \sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
> $$
> \therefore A D = \sqrt {7}
> $$
> （2）由（1）知， $AD=\sqrt{7},CD=3\sqrt{3},AC=4$
> 在 $\triangle ADC$ 中, 由余弦定理得:
> $$
> \cos \angle C D A = \frac {A D ^ {2} + C D ^ {2} - A C ^ {2}}{2 A D \times C D}
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {7 + 2 7 - 1 6}{2 \times \sqrt {7} \times 3 \sqrt {3}} = \frac {\sqrt {2 1}}{7},
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \sin \angle C D A = \frac {2 \sqrt {7}}{7},
> $$
> $$
> \because D H \perp A C, \angle B C A = 3 0 ^ {\circ},
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \angle C D H = 6 0 ^ {\circ},
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \cos \angle A D H = \cos (\angle C D A - \angle C D H)
> $$
> $$
> = \cos \angle C D H \times \cos \angle C D A + \sin \angle C D H \times \sin \angle C D A
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {2 1}}{7} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \times \frac {2 \sqrt {7}}{7}
> $$
> $= \frac{3\sqrt{21}}{14}$ ，故 $\cos \angle ADH = \frac{3\sqrt{21}}{14}$
