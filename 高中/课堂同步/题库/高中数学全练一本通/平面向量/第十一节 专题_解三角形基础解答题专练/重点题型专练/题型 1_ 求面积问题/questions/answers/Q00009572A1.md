> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $C = \frac{\pi}{3}$ ；（2） $\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）因为 $a \sin A \cos B + b \sin A \cos A = \sqrt{3} a \cos C$ 根据正弦定理得 $\sin A\sin A\cos B+\sin A\sin B\cos A=\sqrt{3}\sin A\cos C$ $\because \sin A \neq 0,$ $\therefore \sin A \cos B + \sin B \cos A = \sqrt{3} \cos C,$ 即 $\sin(A+B)=\sqrt{3}\cos C$ 即 $\sin C = \sqrt{3} \cos C$ . $\because \cos C \neq 0, \therefore \tan C = \sqrt{3}.$ 因为 $0 < C < \pi$ ，所以 $C = \frac{\pi}{3}$ .
> $$
> \overrightarrow {B A} \cdot \overrightarrow {A C} = b c \cos (\pi - A) = - b c \cos A = - 1
> $$
> $$
> b c \cos A = 1
> $$
> $$
> a ^ {2} = b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - 2 b c \cos A
> $$
> $$
> b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} = 9 + 2 b c \cos A = 1 1
> $$
> $$
> c ^ {2} = a ^ {2} + b ^ {2} - 2 a b \cos C
> $$
> $$
> b ^ {2} - c ^ {2} = 2 a b \cos C - a ^ {2} = 2 \times 3 \times b \times \cos \frac {\pi}{3} - 3 ^ {2} = 3 b - 9 \tag {②}
> $$
> $$
> 2 b ^ {2} - 3 b - 2 = 0
> $$
> $$
> b = 2 \text {(负舍)}
> $$
> $$
> \triangle A B C
> $$
> $$
> \frac {1}{2} a b \sin C = \frac {1}{2} \times 3 \times 2 \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{2}
> $$
