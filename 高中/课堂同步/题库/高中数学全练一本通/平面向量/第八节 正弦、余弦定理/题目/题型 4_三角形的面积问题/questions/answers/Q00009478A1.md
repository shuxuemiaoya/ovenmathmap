> **【解析】**
> 解析: 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中, $a = \sqrt{3}$ , $b = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , $C = 60^{\circ}$ ,
> $$
> S _ {\triangle A B C} = \frac {1}{2} a b \sin C = \frac {1}{2} \times \sqrt {3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{8}
> $$
