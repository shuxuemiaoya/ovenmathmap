> **【解析】**
> 解析:在△ABC中, 因为 $\frac{2a-c}{b}=\frac{\cos C}{\cos B}$ ，
> 由正弦定理, 可得 $\frac{2\sin A-\sin C}{\sin B}=\frac{\cos C}{\cos B}$ ，即
> $$
> 2 \sin A \cos B - \sin C \cos B = \sin B \cos C,
> $$
> $$
> 2 \sin A \cos B = \sin C \cos B + \sin B \cos C = \sin (B + C)
> $$
> $$
> B + C = \pi - A
> $$
> $$
> \sin (B + C) = \sin A
> $$
> $$
> A \in (0, \pi)
> $$
> $$
> \sin A > 0
> $$
> $$
> \cos B = \frac {1}{2}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {\pi}{6}
> $$
