> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：∵ $a = 2b\cos C$ ,
> 则 $\sin A = 2\sin B\cos C, \sin(B + C) = 2\sin B\cos C,$ $\therefore \sin(B - C) = 0, \therefore B = C.$ $\therefore \sin^{2}A=\sin^{2}B+\sin^{2}C,\therefore a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2},$ $\therefore \triangle ABC$ 为等腰直角三角形.
>
> 故选 **B**
