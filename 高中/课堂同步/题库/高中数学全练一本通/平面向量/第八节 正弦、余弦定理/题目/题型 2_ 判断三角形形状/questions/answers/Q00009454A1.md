> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:由正弦定理得: $\sin A\cos C + \sin C\cos A = \sin C$ , $\therefore \sin(A + C) = \sin C$ , $\therefore \sin B = \sin C$ , $\because$ 三角形内角和等于 $180^{\circ}$ , $\therefore B = C$ ,
>
> 故选 **C**.
