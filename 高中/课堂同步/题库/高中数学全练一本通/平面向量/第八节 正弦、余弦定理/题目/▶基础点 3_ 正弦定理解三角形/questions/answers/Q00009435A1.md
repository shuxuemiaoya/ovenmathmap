> **【答案】** C

> **【解析】**
> 解析:∵ $A=60^{\circ}$ , $a=4\sqrt{3}$ , $b=4\sqrt{2}$ 由正弦定理可得, $\frac{a}{\sin A}=\frac{b}{\sin B}$ ∴ $\sin B = \frac{b \sin A}{a} = \frac{4\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}}{4\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ∵a>b,∴A>B
> ∴B=45°故选:C.
