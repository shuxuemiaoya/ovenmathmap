> **【解析】**
> 等边三角形
解析: 方法一: 由正弦定理得 $\frac{\sin C}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{b}$ , $\because 2\cos A\sin B = \sin C$ , $\therefore \cos A = \frac{\sin C}{2\sin B} = \frac{c}{2b}$ , 由余弦定理的推论得 $\cos A = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc}$ $\therefore \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc} = \frac{c}{2b}$ , 化简得 $b^2 + c^2 - a^2 = c^2$ , $\therefore a = b$ ；又 $\because (a + b + c)(a + b - c) = 3ab$ , $\therefore (a + b)^2 - c^2 = 3ab$ ，化简得 $4b^2 - c^2 = 3b^2$ , $\therefore b = c$ , $\therefore a = b = c$ ，即 $\triangle ABC$ 是等边三角形. 方法二: $\because A + B + C = \pi$ , $\therefore \sin C = \sin(A + B)$ , 又 $2\cos A\sin B = \sin C$ , $\therefore 2\cos A\sin B = \sin(A + B)$ , $\therefore 2\cos A\sin B = \sin A\cos B + \cos A\sin B$ , $\therefore \sin A\cos B - \cos A\sin B = 0$ , $\therefore \sin(A - B) = 0$ ， $\because A, B \in (0, \pi)$ , $\therefore A - B \in (-\pi, \pi)$ , $\therefore A = B$ ，又 $\because (a + b + c)(a + b - c) = 3ab$ , $\therefore (a + b)^2 - c^2 = 3ab$ ，即 $a^2 + b^2 - c^2 = ab$ ，由余弦定理的推论得 $\cos C = \frac{a^2 + b^2 - c^2}{2ab} = \frac{ab}{2ab} = \frac{1}{2}$ 又 $C \in (0, \pi)$ , $\therefore C = \frac{\pi}{3}$ , 又 $A = B$ , $\therefore \triangle ABC$ 是等边三角形.
