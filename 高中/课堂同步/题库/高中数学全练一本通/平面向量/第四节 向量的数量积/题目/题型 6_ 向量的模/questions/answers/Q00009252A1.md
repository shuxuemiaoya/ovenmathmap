> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \left| \vec {a} - \vec {b} \right| = \sqrt {3}
> $$
> $$
> \left(\vec {a} - \vec {b}\right) ^ {2} = \vec {a} ^ {2} + \vec {b} ^ {2} - 2 \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} = 3
> $$
> $$
> \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} = - \frac {1}{2}
> $$
> $$
> \cos \left\langle \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} \right\rangle = \frac {\vec {a} \cdot \vec {b}}{\left| \vec {a} \right| \left| \vec {b} \right|} = - \frac {1}{2}
> $$
> $$
> 0 ^ {\circ} \leq \left\langle \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} \right\rangle \leq 1 8 0 ^ {\circ}
> $$
> $$
> \left\langle \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} \right\rangle = 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}
