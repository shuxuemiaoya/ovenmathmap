>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \frac {\pi}{4}, \left| \vec {a} \right| = \sqrt {2}, \left| \vec {b} \right| = 1
> $$
> $$
> \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} = \sqrt {2} \times 1 \times \sin \frac {\pi}{4} = 1
> $$
> $$
> \left| 3 \vec {a} + \vec {b} \right| = \sqrt {\left(3 \vec {a}\right) ^ {2} + 6 \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} + (\vec {b}) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {1 8 + 6 + 1} = 5
> $$
