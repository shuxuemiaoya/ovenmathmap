> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \left| \vec {a} - \vec {b} \right| ^ {2} = \left| \vec {a} \right| ^ {2} + \left| \vec {b} \right| ^ {2} - 2 \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} = 1 0, \therefore \left| \vec {a} \right| ^ {2} + \left| \vec {b} \right| ^ {2} = 1 4
> $$
> $$
> \because \left(\left| \vec {a} \right| - \left| \vec {b} \right|\right) ^ {2} = \left| \vec {a} \right| ^ {2} + \left| \vec {b} \right| ^ {2} - 2 \left| \vec {a} \right| \left| \vec {b} \right| = 4, \therefore \left| \vec {a} \right| \left| \vec {b} \right| = 5
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \cos \left\langle \vec {a}, \vec {b} \right\rangle = \frac {\vec {a} \cdot \vec {b}}{\left| \vec {a} \right| \left| \vec {b} \right|} = \frac {2}{5}
> $$
