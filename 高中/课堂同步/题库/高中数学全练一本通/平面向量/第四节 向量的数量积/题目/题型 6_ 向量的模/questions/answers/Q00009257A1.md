> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \frac {1}{2} \vec {b}
> $$
> $$
> \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} = \frac {1}{2} \vec {b} \cdot \vec {b} = \frac {1}{2} \vec {b} ^ {2}
> $$
> $$
> \left| 3 \vec {a} - \vec {b} \right| = \left| \vec {a} + \vec {b} \right|
> $$
> $$
> 9 \vec {a} ^ {2} - 6 \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} + \vec {b} ^ {2} = \vec {a} ^ {2} + 2 \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} + \vec {b} ^ {2}
> $$
> $$
> \vec {a} ^ {2} = \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b}
> $$
> $$
> \left| \vec {a} \right| = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \left| \vec {b} \right|
> $$
> $$
> \text {所以} \cos \left\langle {\vec {a}, {\vec {b}}} \right\rangle = {\frac {{\vec {a}} \cdot {\vec {b}}}{{\left| {\vec {a}} \right|} \cdot {\left| {\vec {b}} \right|}}} = {\frac {{\frac {1}{2}} {\left| {\vec {b}} \right|} ^ {2}}{{\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}} {\left| {\vec {b}} \right|} ^ {2}}} = {\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}
> $$
> $$
> 0 \leq \left\langle \vec {a}, \vec {b} \right\rangle \leq \pi
> $$
> $$
> \left\langle \vec {a}, \vec {b} \right\rangle = \frac {\pi}{4}
> $$
