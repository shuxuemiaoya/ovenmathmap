> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {B C}
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A D} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} = (2 \overrightarrow {A B} + 3 \overrightarrow {A C}) \cdot \overrightarrow {B C}
> $$
> $$
> = 2 \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} + 3 \overrightarrow {A C} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} = 2 | \overrightarrow {A B} | \cdot | \overrightarrow {B C} | \cdot \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} + 3 | \overrightarrow {A C} | \cdot | \overrightarrow {B C} | \cdot \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ}
> $$
> $$
> = - \left| \overrightarrow {B C} \right| ^ {2} + \frac {3}{2} \left| \overrightarrow {B C} \right| ^ {2} = \frac {1}{2} \left| \overrightarrow {B C} \right| ^ {2}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {\overrightarrow {A D} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C}}{| \overrightarrow {B C} | ^ {2}} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} = \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {B C}
> $$
> $$
> \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {B C}
> $$
