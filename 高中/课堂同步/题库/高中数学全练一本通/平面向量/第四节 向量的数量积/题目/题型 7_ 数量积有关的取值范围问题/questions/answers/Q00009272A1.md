> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> 解：（1）证明见解析；（2）k>1 或 k<0。
> $$
> \therefore \left| \vec {a} \right| = \left| \vec {b} \right| = \left| \vec {c} \right| = 1, \quad \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} = \left| \vec {a} \right| \left| \vec {b} \right| \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} = - \frac {1}{2}, \quad \vec {a} \cdot \vec {c} = \left| \vec {a} \right| \left| \vec {c} \right| \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} = - \frac {1}{2},
> $$
> $$
> \vec {b} \cdot \vec {c} = | \vec {b} | | \vec {c} | \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} = - \frac {1}{2}
> $$
> $$
> \because (\vec {a} - \vec {b}) \cdot \vec {c} = \vec {a} \cdot \vec {c} - \vec {b} \cdot \vec {c} = - \frac {1}{2} - \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) = 0
> $$
> $$
> \therefore (\vec {a} - \vec {b}) \perp \vec {c};
> $$
> $$
> (2) \because | k \vec {a} + \vec {b} | > | \vec {b} + \vec {c} |
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \left(k \vec {a} + \vec {b}\right) ^ {2} > \left(\vec {b} + \vec {c}\right) ^ {2}
> $$
> $$
> k ^ {2} \vec {a} ^ {2} + \vec {b} ^ {2} + 2 k \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} > \vec {b} ^ {2} + \vec {c} ^ {2} + 2 \vec {b} \cdot \vec {c}
> $$
> $$
> \therefore k ^ {2} + 1 - k > 1 + 1 - 1
> $$
> $$
> k ^ {2} - k > 0
> $$
> $$
> k <   0
> $$
> $$
> \text {为} k > 1
> $$
