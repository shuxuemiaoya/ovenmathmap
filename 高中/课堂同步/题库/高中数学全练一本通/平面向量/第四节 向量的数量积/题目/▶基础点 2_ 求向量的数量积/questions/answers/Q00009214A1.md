>
> **【解析】**
> -2
> 详解: $\because\left|AB\right|=\left|BC\right|=2$ , 向量 $\overrightarrow{AB}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{BC}$ 的夹角为 $120^{\circ}$
> $$
> \therefore \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} = | \overrightarrow {A B} | \cdot | \overrightarrow {B C} | \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} = 2 \times 2 \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) = - 2.
> $$
