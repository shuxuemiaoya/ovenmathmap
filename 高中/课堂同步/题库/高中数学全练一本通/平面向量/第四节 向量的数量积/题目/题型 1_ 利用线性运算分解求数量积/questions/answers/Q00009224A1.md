> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\overrightarrow{AP} = \frac{2}{3}\vec{a} -\vec{b}$ ；（2） $\frac{13}{9}$
> 详解：（1）因为 $\overrightarrow{BP} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{BC}$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{CP} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{CB}$
> 由题得 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \overrightarrow{CP} - \overrightarrow{CA} = \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{CB} - \overrightarrow{CA} = \frac{2}{3} \vec{a} - \vec{b}$ ;
> (2) 由已知得
> $$
> \overrightarrow {C Q} = \overrightarrow {C A} + \overrightarrow {A Q} = \overrightarrow {C A} + \frac {2}{3} \overrightarrow {A B} = \overrightarrow {C A} + \frac {2}{3} (\overrightarrow {C B} - \overrightarrow {C A}) = \frac {2}{3} \overrightarrow {C B} + \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {C A} = \frac {2}{3} \vec {a} + \frac {1}{3} \vec {b}
> $$
> $$
> \therefore \overrightarrow {A P} \cdot \overrightarrow {C Q} = \left(\frac {2}{3} \vec {a} - \vec {b}\right) \cdot \left(\frac {2}{3} \vec {a} + \frac {1}{3} \vec {b}\right) = \frac {4}{9} | \vec {a} | ^ {2} - \frac {4}{9} \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b} - \frac {1}{3} |
> $$
> $$
> = \frac {4}{9} \times 4 ^ {2} - \frac {4}{9} \times 4 \times 3 \times \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - \frac {1}{3} \times 3 ^ {2} = \frac {1 3}{9}
> $$
