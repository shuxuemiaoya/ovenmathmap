详解:由题意可得:  \( \left\{\begin{aligned}&7\overrightarrow{a}^{2}+16\overrightarrow{a}\cdot\overrightarrow{b}-15\overrightarrow{b}^{2}=0\\&7\overrightarrow{a}^{2}-33\overrightarrow{a}\cdot\overrightarrow{b}+20\overrightarrow{b}^{2}=0\end{aligned}\right. \) ，化简得  \( \left\{\begin{aligned}&\left|\vec{a}\right|^{2}=\frac{5}{7}\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}\\&\left|\vec{b}\right|^{2}=\frac{7}{5}\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}\end{aligned}\right. \)
因此  \( \cos\left\langle\vec{a},\vec{b}\right\rangle=\frac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{\left|\vec{a}\right||\vec{b}|}=1 \) ，所以  \( \vec{a} \)  与  \( \vec{b} \)  的夹角为 0.
