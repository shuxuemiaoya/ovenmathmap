> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 正项等比数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 中， $a_{2}a_{2023}=4$ 故 $a_{1}a_{2024}=a_{2}a_{2023}=a_{3}a_{2022}=\cdots=a_{1012}a_{1013}=4$ 故 $\log_{2}a_{1}+\log_{2}a_{2}+\cdots+\log_{2}a_{2024}=\log_{2}\left(a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{2023}a_{2024}\right)$ $=\log_{2}\left(a_{1}a_{2024}\right)\left(a_{2}a_{2023}\right)\cdots\left(a_{1012}a_{1013}\right)=\log_{2}4^{1012}=1012\log_{2}4=1012\times2=2024.$ 故选：B
