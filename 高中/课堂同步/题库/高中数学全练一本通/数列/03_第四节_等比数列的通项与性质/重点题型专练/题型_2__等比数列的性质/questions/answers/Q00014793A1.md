> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 为等比数列， $a_{2}\cdot a_{6}\cdot a_{10}=3\sqrt{3}$ $\therefore a_{6}^{2}\cdot a_{6}=3\sqrt{3},\therefore a_{6}=\sqrt{3},a_{4}\cdot a_{8}=a_{6}^{2}=3;$ $\because\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ 为等差数列， $b_{1}+b_{6}+b_{11}=6$ $\therefore3b_{6}=6,b_{6}=2,\therefore b_{3}+b_{9}=2b_{6}=4,$ $\therefore\frac{a_{4}\cdot a_{8}}{b_{3}+b_{9}}=\frac{3}{4}.$
