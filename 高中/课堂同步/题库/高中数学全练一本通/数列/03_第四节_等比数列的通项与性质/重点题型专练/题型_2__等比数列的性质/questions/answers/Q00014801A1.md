> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{31}{8}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 为等比数列, 所以 $\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\frac{1}{a_{3}}+\frac{1}{a_{4}}+\frac{1}{a_{5}}=\frac{1}{a_{1}}+\frac{1}{a_{5}}+\frac{1}{a_{2}}+\frac{1}{a_{4}}+\frac{1}{a_{3}}$ $= \frac{a_1 + a_5}{a_1a_5} +\frac{a_2 + a_4}{a_2a_4} +\frac{1}{a_3} = \frac{a_1 + a_2 + a_4 + a_5}{a_3^2} +\frac{1}{a_3} = \frac{27}{2}\times \frac{1}{4} +\frac{1}{2} = \frac{31}{8}.$ 故答案为： $\frac{31}{8}$
