> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_{n} = 3^{n - 1}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because 3a_n^2 +(3 - a_{n + 1})a_n - a_{n + 1} = 0,\therefore (3a_n - a_{n + 1})(a_n + 1) = 0,$ $\because a_n > 0,\therefore a_n + 1 > 0,\therefore a_{n + 1} = 3a_n,$ $\because a_1 = 1,\therefore a_n\neq 0,$ ∴数列 $\{a_n\}$ 是首项为1，公比为3的等比数列， $\therefore a_n = 3^{n - 1};$
