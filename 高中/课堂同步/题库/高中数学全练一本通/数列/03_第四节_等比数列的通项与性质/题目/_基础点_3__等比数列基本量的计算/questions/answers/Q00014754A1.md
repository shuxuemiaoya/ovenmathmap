> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设等比数列 $\{a_n\}$ 的公比是 $q$ ，
> >
> > 因为 $a_3 = 2, a_5 = 4$ ，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_3 = a_1 q^2 = 2 \\ a_5 = a_1 q^4 = 4 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a_1 = 1 \\ q = \pm \sqrt{2} \end{array} \right.$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $a_9 = a_1 q^8 = 1 \times (\pm \sqrt{2})^8 = 16$ 。故选：B。
