> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设正数等比数列 $\{a_n\}$ 的公比为 $q, q > 0$ ，由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2(a_3 + a_4) = a_2 a_3 \\ a_1 a_4 = 12a_2 \end{array} \right.$ ，得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2(a_1 q^2 + a_1 q^3) = a_1 q \cdot a_1 q^2 \\ a_1 \cdot a_1 q^3 = 12a_1 q \end{array} \right.$ ，整理得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 + 2q = a_1 q \\ a_1 q^2 = 12 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $a_1 = 3, q = 2$ ，所以 $a_5 = a_1 q^4 = 3 \times 2^4 = 48$ 。故选：D
