> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意, $a_{1},a_{3},a_{4}$ 成等比数列,则 $a_{3}^{2}=a_{1}a_{4}$ ,
> > 则 $(a_{1}+2d)^{2}=a_{1}(a_{1}+3d)\Rightarrow a_{1}^{2}+4a_{1}d+4d^{2}=a_{1}^{2}+3a_{1}d\Rightarrow4d^{2}=-a_{1}d$ $\because d\neq0,\therefore4d=-a_{1}$ ，则 $\frac{d}{a_{1}}=-\frac{1}{4}$ .故选:B.
