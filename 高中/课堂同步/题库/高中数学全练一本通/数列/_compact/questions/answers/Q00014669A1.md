> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{11}{27}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\frac{S_n}{T_n} = \frac{2n + 2}{5n + 3}$ ,且 $\frac{S_{21}}{T_{21}} = \frac{\frac{21(a_1 + a_{21})}{2}}{\frac{21(b_1 + b_{21})}{2}} = \frac{a_1 + a_{21}}{b_1 + b_{21}} = \frac{2a_{11}}{2b_{11}} = \frac{a_{11}}{b_{11}}$ ,
> >
> > 由等差数列前n项和的性质得 $\frac{a_2 + a_{20}}{b_7 + b_{15}} = \frac{2a_{11}}{2b_{11}} = \frac{a_{11}}{b_{11}} = \frac{S_{21}}{T_{21}} = \frac{2 \times 21 + 2}{5 \times 21 + 3} = \frac{11}{27}$ ,故答案为: $\frac{11}{27}$ .
