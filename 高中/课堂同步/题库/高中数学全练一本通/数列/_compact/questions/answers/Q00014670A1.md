> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{12}{7}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由等差数列性质
> >
> > 得, $\frac{a_3 + a_9}{3b_7 - b_9} = \frac{2a_6}{3(b_1 + 6d) - (b_1 + 8d)} = \frac{2a_6}{2(b_1 + 5d)} = \frac{2a_6}{2b_6} = \frac{a_6}{b_6}$ ,
> >
> > 等差数列 $\left\{a_n\right\}$ 前n项和满足 $S_{2n-1} = (2n - 1)a_n$ ，则 $S_{11} = 11a_6$ ，
> >
> > 等差数列 $\left\{b_n\right\}$ 前n项和满足 $T_{2n-1} = (2n - 1)b_n$ ，则 $T_{11} = 11b_6$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{a_6}{b_6} = \frac{S_{11}}{T_{11}} = \frac{2 \times 11 + 2}{11 + 3} = \frac{24}{14} = \frac{12}{7}$ .
> >
> > 故选:B.
