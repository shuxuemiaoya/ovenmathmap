> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3}{7}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (2n+3) $S_n = nT_n \Rightarrow \frac{S_n}{T_n} = \frac{n}{2n+3}$ ,
> >
> > 又 $S_9 = \frac{9}{2}(a_1 + a_9) = \frac{9}{2} \times 2a_5 = 9a_5$ , $T_9 = \frac{9}{2}(b_1 + b_9) = \frac{9}{2} \times 2b_5 = 9b_5$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{S_9}{T_9} = \frac{a_5}{b_5}$ ,又 $\frac{S_9}{T_9} = \frac{9}{2 \times 9 + 3} = \frac{3}{7}$ ,所以 $\frac{a_5}{b_5} = \frac{3}{7}$ .
