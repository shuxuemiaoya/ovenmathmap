> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{4}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\frac{S_n}{T_n} = \frac{7n + 1}{4n + 27}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{a_{11}}{b_{11}} = \frac{a_1 + a_{21}}{b_1 + b_{21}} = \frac{\frac{(a_1 + a_{21}) \times 21}{2}}{\frac{(b_1 + b_{21}) \times 21}{2}} = \frac{S_{21}}{T_{21}} = \frac{7 \times 21 + 1}{4 \times 21 + 27} = \frac{4}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故答案为: $\frac{4}{3}$ .
