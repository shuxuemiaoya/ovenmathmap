> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{21}{20}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\left\{a_n\right\},\left\{b_n\right\}$ 为等差数列，且 $\frac{S_n}{T_n} = \frac{2n + 3}{n + 1}$ ,
> >
> > 所以可设 $S_n = kn(2n + 3)$ ， $T_n = kn(n + 1)$ ,
> >
> > 则 $a_5 = S_5 - S_4 = 65k - 44k = 21k$ ， $b_{10} = T_{10} - T_9 = 10k \times 11 - 9k \times 10 = 20k$ ，
> >
> > \$\therefore \frac{a\_5}{b\_{10}} = \frac{21}{20}\$.
