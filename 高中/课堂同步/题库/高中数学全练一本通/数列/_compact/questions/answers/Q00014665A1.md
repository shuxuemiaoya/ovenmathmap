> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{72}{29}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由等差数列的性质及求和公式可得: $\frac{a_7}{b_7} = \frac{2a_7}{2b_7} = \frac{a_1 + a_{13}}{b_1 + b_{13}} = \frac{\frac{13(a_1 + a_{13})}{2}}{\frac{13(b_1 + b_{13})}{2}} = \frac{S_{13}}{T_{13}} = \frac{5 \times 13 + 7}{2 \times 13 + 3} = \frac{72}{29}$ .
> >
> > 故答案为: $\frac{72}{29}$
