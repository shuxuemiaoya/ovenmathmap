> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_{n}=\frac { 2 } { n+1 }$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because a_{n+1} = \frac{2a_n}{a_n + 2}, \therefore \frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = \frac{a_n + 2}{2a_n} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{a_n}, \therefore$ 数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n}\right\}$ 是首项为 $\frac{1}{a_1} = 1$ ，公差为 $\frac{1}{2}$ 的等差数列， $\therefore \frac{1}{a_n} = 1 + \frac{1}{2}(n-1) = \frac{n+1}{2}$ ， $\therefore a_n = \frac{2}{n+1}$ .
