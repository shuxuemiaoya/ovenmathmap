> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_n = 3^{n-1}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because a_{n+2} + 3a_n = 4a_{n+1}$ , $n \in \mathbb{N}^*$ , $\therefore a_{n+2} - a_{n+1} = 3(a_{n+1} - a_n)$ ,
> > 令 $b_n = a_{n+1} - a_n$ , $\therefore b_{n+1} = 3b_n$ ,
> > 又 $b_1 = a_2 - a_1 = 2$ , $\therefore$ 数列 $\{b_n\}$ 是以 2 为首项, 3 为公比的等比数列, $\therefore b_n = 2 \times 3^{n-1}$ , $n \in \mathbb{N}^*$ . $\because b_n = a_{n+1} - a_n = 2 \times 3^{n-1}$ , $^{34}$ $\therefore$ 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, $a_n = (a_n - a_{n-1}) + (a_{n-1} - a_{n-2}) + \cdots + (a_2 - a_1) + a_1$ $= b_{n-1} + b_{n-2} + \cdots + b_1 + a_1$ $= 2 \times 3^{n-2} + 2 \times 3^{n-3} + \cdots + 2 \times 3^0 + 1$ $= \frac{2(1 - 3^{n-1})}{1 - 3} + 1 = 3^{n-1}$ , 又 $a_1 = 1$ 也满足上式, 所以 $a_n = 3^{n-1}$ .
