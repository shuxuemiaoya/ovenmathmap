> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_n = n$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $a_n = n(a_{n+1} - a_n)$ , 得 $(n + 1)a_n = na_{n+1}$ ,
> >
> > 即 $\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{n + 1}{n}$ ,
> >
> > 则 $\frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{n}{n - 1}$ , $\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}} = \frac{n - 1}{n - 2}$ , $\frac{a_{n-2}}{a_{n-3}} = \frac{n - 2}{n - 3}$ , ..., $\frac{a_2}{a_1} = \frac{2}{1}$ , $n \geq 2$ ,
> >
> > 由累乘法可得 $\frac{a_n}{a_1} = n$ , 所以 $a_n = n$ , $n \geq 2$ ,
> >
> > 又 $a_1 = 1$ , 符合上式, 所以 $a_n = n$ .
