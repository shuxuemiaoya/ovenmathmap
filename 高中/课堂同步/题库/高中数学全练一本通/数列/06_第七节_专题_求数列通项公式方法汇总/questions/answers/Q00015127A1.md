> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_{n}=\frac{1}{2^{n}-1}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because\frac{1}{a_{n+1}}=\frac{2}{a_{n}}+1$ ，等式两边同时加1整理得 $\frac{1}{a_{n+1}}+1=2\left(\frac{1}{a_{n}}+1\right)$ 又 $\because a_{1}=1,\therefore\frac{1}{a_{1}}+1=2$ $\therefore\left\{\frac{1}{a_{n}}+1\right\}$ 是首项为2，公比为2的等比数列. $\therefore\frac{1}{a_{n}}+1=2^{n}$ ， $\therefore a_{n}=\frac{1}{2^{n}-1}$
