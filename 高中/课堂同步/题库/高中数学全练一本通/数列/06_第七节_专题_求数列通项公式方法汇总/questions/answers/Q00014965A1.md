> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_n = \frac{4042}{n(n + 1)}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $S_n = n^2 a_n$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $n \geq 2$ 时, $a_n = S_n - S_{n-1} = n^2 a_n - (n - 1)^2 a_{n-1}$ ,
> >
> > 即 $(n - 1)^2 a_{n-1} = (n^2 - 1)a_n$ ,
> >
> > 化简得 $\frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{n - 1}{n + 1}$ , 又 $a_1 = 2021$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $a_n = \frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} \times \frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}} \times \frac{a_{n-2}}{a_{n-3}} \times \cdots \times \frac{a_3}{a_2} \times \frac{a_2}{a_1} \times a_1$ $= \frac{n - 1}{n + 1} \times \frac{n - 2}{n} \times \frac{n - 3}{n - 1} \times \cdots \times \frac{2}{4} \times \frac{1}{3} \times 2021$ $= \frac{4042}{n(n + 1)}$ ,
> >
> > 检验 $n = 1$ 时也成立,
> >
> > 所以 $a_n = \frac{4042}{n(n + 1)}$ .
