> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_{n} = 2n - 1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为当 $n \geq 2$ 时， $nS_{n+1} + (n+1)S_{n-1} = (2n+1)S_{n} + 1$ 。所以 $n(S_{n+1} - S_{n}) = (n+1)(S_{n} - S_{n-1}) + 1$ ，即 $na_{n+1} - (n+1)a_{n} = 1$ ，所以 $\frac{a_{n+1}}{n+1} - \frac{a_{n}}{n} = \frac{1}{n(n+1)} = \frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+1}$ 。所以 $\frac{a_{2}}{n} = \left(\frac{a_{n}}{n} - \frac{a_{n-1}}{n-1}\right) + \left(\frac{a_{n-1}}{n-1} - \frac{a_{n-2}}{n-2}\right) + \cdots + \left(\frac{a_{3}}{3} - \frac{a_{2}}{2}\right) + \frac{a_{2}}{2} = \left(\frac{1}{n-1} - \frac{1}{n}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{n-2} - \frac{1}{n-1}\right) + \cdots + \left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\right) + \frac{3}{2} = 2 - \frac{1}{n}$ ，所以 $a_{n} = 2n - 1$ 。又 $a_{1} = 2 \times 1 - 1 = 1$ 满足上式，故 $a_{n} = 2n - 1$ 。
