> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_n = (n + 1)(\frac{1}{2})^n$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因 $(n + 2)a_n = 2(n + 1)a_{n+1}, n \in N^*$ , $a_2 = \frac{3}{4}$ , 取 $n = 1$ 代入解得 $a_1 = \frac{4}{3} a_2 = 1$ , 且 $\frac{2a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{n + 2}{n + 1}$ ,
> >
> > 对 $n$ 赋值, 并左右式分别相乘, 可
> >
> > 得: $\frac{2a_2}{a_1} \times \frac{2a_3}{a_2} \times \frac{2a_4}{a_3} \times \cdots, \frac{2a_n}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{3}{2} \times \frac{4}{3} \times \frac{5}{4} \times \cdots, \frac{n + 1}{n}$ ,
> >
> > 化简得： $2^{n-1}\frac{a_{n}}{a_{1}}=\frac{n+1}{2}$ ，解得： $a_{n}=(n+1)(\frac{1}{2})^{n}$ .
