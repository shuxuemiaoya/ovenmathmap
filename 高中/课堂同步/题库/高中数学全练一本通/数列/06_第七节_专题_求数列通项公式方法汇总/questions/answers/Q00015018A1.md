> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_n = 3 - 2(\frac{2}{3})^{n-1}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $a_{n+2} = \frac{5}{3}a_{n+1} - \frac{2}{3}a_n ⇒ a_{n+2} - a_{n+1} = \frac{2}{3}(a_{n+1} - a_n)$ , 故 $\{a_{n+1} - a_n\}$ 是以 $a_2 - a_1 = \frac{2}{3}$ 为首项, $\frac{2}{3}$ 为公比的等比数列, 即 $a_{n+1} - a_n = (\frac{2}{3})^n, ... \\a_2 - a_1 = \frac{2}{3}\end{cases}$ 全部相加, 利用等比数列求和得： $a_{n + 1} - a_1 = 2 - 2\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^n$ ， $a_{n} = 3 - 2\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n - 1}$
