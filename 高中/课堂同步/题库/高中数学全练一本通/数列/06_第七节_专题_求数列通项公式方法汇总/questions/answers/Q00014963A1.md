> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{n(n+1)}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a_{n}=\frac{n-1}{n+1}a_{n-1}(n\geqslant2),\quad a_{1}=\frac{1}{2},$ 所以 $a_{n}\neq0(n\in N^{*})$ ，所以当 n≥2 时， $\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-1}}=\frac{n-1}{n+1},$ 所以 $a_{n}=\left(\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-1}}\cdot\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}}\cdots\cdots\frac{a_{3}}{a_{2}}\cdot\frac{a_{2}}{a_{1}}\right)\cdot a_{1}$
> >
> > $= \left(\frac{n - 1}{n + 1} \times \frac{n - 2}{n} \times \cdots \times \frac{2}{4} \times \frac{1}{3}\right) \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{n(n + 1)} (n \geq 2)$ 当 $n = 1$ , $a_1 = \frac{1}{1 \times 2} = \frac{1}{2}$ 满足上式,
> >
> > 所以 $a_n = \frac{1}{n(n + 1)}$ . 故答案为: $\frac{1}{n(n + 1)}$
