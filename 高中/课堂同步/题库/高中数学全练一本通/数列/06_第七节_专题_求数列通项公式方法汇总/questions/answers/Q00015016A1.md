> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_n = \frac{2 + (-\frac{1}{2})^{n-1}}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a_n + a_{n+1} = 2a_{n+2}$ , 所以 $a_{n+2} - a_{n+1} = -\frac{1}{2}(a_{n+1} - a_n)$ ,
> > 又 $a_2 - a_1 = -\frac{1}{2} \neq 0$ ,
> > 所以数列 $\{a_{n+1} - a_n\}$ 是首项为 $-\frac{1}{2}$ , 公比为 $-\frac{1}{2}$ 的等比数列.
> > 所以 $a_{n+1} - a_n = -\frac{1}{2} \times (-\frac{1}{2})^{n-1} = (-\frac{1}{2})^n$ ,
> > 所以当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, $a_n = a_1 + (a_2 - a_1) + (a_3 - a_2) + \ldots + (a_n - a_{n-1})$ $= a_1 + (-\frac{1}{2}) + (-\frac{1}{2})^2 + \cdots + (-\frac{1}{2})^{n-1} = 1 + \frac{-1 + (-\frac{1}{2})^{n-1}}{3} = \frac{2 + (-\frac{1}{2})^{n-1}}{3}$ ,
> > 又当 $n=1$ 时, $a_1 = 1 = \frac{2 + (-\frac{1}{2})^{1-1}}{3}$ , 符合上式,
> > 所以对于任意正整数 $n$ 都有 $a_n = \frac{2 + (-\frac{1}{2})^{n-1}}{3}$ .
