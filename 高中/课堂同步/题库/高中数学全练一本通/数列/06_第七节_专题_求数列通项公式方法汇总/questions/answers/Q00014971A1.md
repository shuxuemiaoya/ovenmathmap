> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_{n}=\frac{2^{n}}{(2^{n}-1)(2^{n+1}-1)}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $(2^{n+2}-1)a_{n+1}=(2^{n+1}-2)a_{n}$ 得， $\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_{n}}=\frac{2^{n+1}-2}{2^{n+2}-1}=2\cdot\frac{2^{n}-1}{2^{n+2}-1}$ 则 $\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n-1}}\cdot\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}}\cdot\frac{a_{n-2}}{a_{n-3}}\cdots\frac{a_{2}}{a_{1}}=2\cdot\frac{2^{n-1}-1}{2^{n+1}-1}\cdot2\cdot\frac{2^{n-2}-1}{2^{n}-1}\cdot2\cdot\frac{2^{n-3}-1}{2^{n-1}-1}\cdots2\cdot\frac{2^{1}-1}{2^{3}-1}=$ $2^{n-1}\cdot\frac{3}{(2^{n+1}-1)(2^{n}-1)}$ 即 $\frac{a_{n}}{a_{1}}=\frac{3\cdot2^{n-1}}{(2^{n}-1)(2^{n+1}-1)}$ ，又 $a_{1}=\frac{2}{3}$ ，所以 $a_{n}=\frac{2^{n}}{(2^{n}-1)(2^{n+1}-1)}$ .
> > 故答案为： $a_{n}=\frac{2^{n}}{(2^{n}-1)(2^{n+1}-1)}$ .
