> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $a_n = -2^n \cdot n$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{2n}{n - 1}$ , 则 $\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_{n-2}} = \frac{2(n - 1)}{n - 2}$ , $\frac{a_{n-2}}{a_{n-3}} = \frac{2(n - 2)}{n - 3}$ , ..., $\frac{a_2}{a_1} = \frac{2\times 2}{1}$ ,
> >
> > 累乘得: $\frac{a_n}{a_1} = \frac{2n}{n - 1} \times \frac{2(n - 1)}{n - 2} \times \frac{2(n - 2)}{n - 3} \times \cdots \times \frac{2\times 2}{1} = n\cdot 2^{n-1}$ ,
> >
> > 又 $a_1 = -2$ , 故 $a_n = -2^n \cdot n$ .
> >
> > 检验 $n = 1$ 时也成立,
> >
> > 故 $a_n = -2^n \cdot n$ .
