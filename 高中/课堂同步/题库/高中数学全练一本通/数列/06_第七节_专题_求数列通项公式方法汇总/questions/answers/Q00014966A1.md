> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{(2n - 1)(2n + 1)}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由于数列 $\{a_n\}$ 中, $a_1 = \frac{1}{3}$ , 前 $n$ 项和 $S_n = n(2n - 1)a_n$ ,
> >
> > ∴ 当 $n \geq 2$ 时, $S_{n-1} = (n - 1)(2n - 3)a_{n-1}$ ,
> >
> > 两式相减可得: $a_n = n(2n - 1)a_n - (n - 1)(2n - 3)a_{n-1}$ $\therefore (2n + 1)a_n = (2n - 3)a_{n-1}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}} = \frac{2n - 3}{2n + 1}$ ,
> >
> > 因此 $a_n = a_1 \times \frac{a_2}{a_1} \times \frac{a_3}{a_2} \times \frac{a_4}{a_3} \times \cdots \times \frac{a_n}{a_{n-1}}$ $= \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{5} \times \frac{3}{7} \times \cdots \times \frac{2n - 5}{2n - 1} \times \frac{2n - 3}{2n + 1} = \frac{1}{(2n - 1)(2n + 1)}$ .
> >
> > 检验 $n = 1$ 时也成立,
> >
> > 故 $a_n = \frac{1}{(2n - 1)(2n + 1)}$ .
