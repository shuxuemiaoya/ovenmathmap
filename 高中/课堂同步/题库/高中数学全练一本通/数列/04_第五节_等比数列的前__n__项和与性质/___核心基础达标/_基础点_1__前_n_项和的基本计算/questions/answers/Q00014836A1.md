> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设等比数列 $\{a_n\}$ 的公比为 $q$ ，由 $a_8 = 3a_{11}$ 得 $a_8 = 3a_8q^3$ ，解得 $q^3 = \frac{1}{3}$ 所以 $\frac{S_{12}}{S_6} = \frac{\frac{a_1(1 - q^{12})}{1 - q}}{\frac{a_1(1 - q^6)}{1 - q}} = 1 + q^6 = 1 + \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{10}{9}$ 。故选：C.
