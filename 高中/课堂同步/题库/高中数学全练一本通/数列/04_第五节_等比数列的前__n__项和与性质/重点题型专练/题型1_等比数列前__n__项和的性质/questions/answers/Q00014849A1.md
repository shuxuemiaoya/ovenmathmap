> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 18
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $a_{1}=S_{1}=3\lambda-1,a_{2}=S_{2}-a_{1}=(9\lambda-1)-(3\lambda-1)=6\lambda$ , $a_{3}=S_{3}-S_{2}=18\lambda$ ,
> >
> > 故 $(6\lambda)^{2}=18\lambda\cdot(3\lambda-1)$ , 解之得 $\lambda=1$ 或 $\lambda=0$ （舍去）, 故 $a_{3}=18$ .
