> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $a_n=\frac{2}{3}n,(n\in N^*)$ （2）证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $2S_{n+1}+2S_n=3a_{n+1}^2①$ 当 $n\geq2$ 时， $2S_n+2S_{n-1}=3a_n^2②$ ①-②得： $2a_{n+1}+2a_n=3(a_{n+1}^2-a_n^2)$ $\because a_n>0,\therefore a_n+a_{n+1}>0,$ $\therefore a_{n+1}-a_n=\frac{2}{3},$ 对于②式令n=2，有 $2(\frac{2}{3}+a_2)+2\times\frac{2}{3}=3a_2^2,$
> >
> > $\because a_{2} > 0, \therefore a_{2} = \frac{4}{3}$ , 即数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是从第二项起, 首项为 $\frac{4}{3}$ , 公差为 $\frac{2}{3}$ 的等差数列. $\therefore$ 当 $n \geq 2$ 时, $a_{n} = \frac{4}{3} + (n - 2) \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{2}{3} n$ ,
> >
> > 又 $a_{1} = \frac{2}{3}$ 也满足上式, $\therefore a_{n} = \frac{2}{3} n, (n \in \mathbf{N}^{*})$ .
> >
> > (2) 当 $n \geq 2$ 时, $\frac{1}{n^{2}} < \frac{1}{(n - 1)n}$ ,
> >
> > 左边 $= \frac{9}{4} \left( \frac{1}{2^{2}} + \frac{1}{3^{2}} + \frac{1}{4^{2}} + \cdots + \frac{1}{n^{2}} \right) < \frac{9}{4} \left[ \frac{1}{1 \times 2} + \frac{1}{2 \times 3} + \frac{1}{3 \times 4} + \cdots + \frac{1}{(n - 1)n} \right]$ $= \frac{9}{4} \left( 1 - \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4} + \cdots + \frac{1}{(n - 1)} - \frac{1}{n} \right) = \frac{9}{4} \left( 1 - \frac{1}{n} \right) < \frac{9}{4}$ , $\therefore$ 当 $n \geq 2$ 时, $\frac{1}{a_{2}^{2}} + \frac{1}{a_{3}^{2}} + \cdots + \frac{1}{a_{n}^{2}} < \frac{9}{4}$ .
