> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 令 $a_{n} = \frac{1}{2^{n}-1}$ , 则 $\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n} = \frac{2^n - 1}{2^{n+1}-1} < \frac{2^n - 1}{2^{n+1}-2} = \frac{1}{2} \Rightarrow a_{n+1} < \frac{1}{2} a_n$ 又因为 $a_1 = 1, a_2 = \frac{1}{3}$ , 由于不等式右边分母为 3, 因此从第三项开始放缩,
> >
> > 得 $a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n < a_1 + a_2 + \frac{1}{2} a_2 + \cdots + (\frac{1}{2})^{n-2} a_2 = 1 + \frac{\frac{1}{3}\left( 1 - \frac{1}{2^{n-1}} \right)}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} < \frac{5}{3}$ 故不等式得证.
