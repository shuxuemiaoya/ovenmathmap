> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) 证明见解析, $a_n = n·2^n$ ; (2) 证明见解析.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) 证明: $\frac{a_{n+1}}{2^{n+1}} - \frac{a_n}{2^n} = \frac{2a_n + 2^{n+1}}{2^{n+1}} - \frac{a_n}{2^n} = \frac{2a_n}{2^{n+1}} + 1 - \frac{a_n}{2^n} = 1$ , $\therefore \left\{\frac{a_n}{2^n}\right\}$ 是首项为 $\frac{a_1}{2^!}= 1$ , 公差为 1 的等差数列, $\therefore \frac{a_n}{2^n}= 1 + (n-1)l=n$ , $\therefore a_n=n·2^n$ .
> >
> > (2) 证明: \$\because a\_n=n·2^n\$, \$\therefore a\_{n+1}=(n+1)·2^{n+1}\$, \$\therefore a\_{n+1}-a\_n=(n+2)·2^n\$,
> >
> > 当 \$n∈N^\*\text {时}\$, \$n+2>2\$, \$\therefore (n+2)·2^n > 2^{n+1}\$,
> >
> > \$\therefore \frac{1}{(n+2)·2^n}<\frac{1}{2^{n+1}}\$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{1}{a_2 - a_1} + \frac{1}{a_3 - a_2} + \frac{1}{a_4 - a_3} + \dots + \frac{1}{a_{n+1} - a_n} < \frac{1}{2^2} + \frac{1}{2^3} + \frac{1}{2^4} + \dots + \frac{1}{2^{n+1}} = \frac{\frac{1}{4}\left(1 - \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^n\right)}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^n\right) < \frac{1}{2}\cdot$
