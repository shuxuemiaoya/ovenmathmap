> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1) $a_{n} = 2\left( \sqrt{n} - \sqrt{n - 1} \right)$ (2) 证明见详解
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1) 因为 $S_{n} = \frac{a_{n}^{2} + 4}{2a_{n}}$ , 则 $2a_{n} S_{n} = a_{n}^{2} + 4$ , 且 $a_{n} > 0, S_{n} > 0$ ,
> >
> > 令 $n = 1$ , 则 $2S_{1}^{2} = S_{1}^{2} + 4$ , 可得 $S_{1}^{2} = 4$ ;
> >
> > 又因为 $2a_{n+1} S_{n+1} = a_{n+1}^{2} + 4$ , 则 $2(S_{n+1} - S_{n}) S_{n+1} = (S_{n+1} - S_{n})^{2} + 4$ ,
> >
> > 整理得 $S_{n+1}^{2} - S_{n}^{2} = 4$ ,
> >
> > 可知数列 $\left\{ S_{n}^{2} \right\}$ 是以首项为 4, 公差为 4 的等差数列, 则 $S_{n}^{2} = 4 + 4(n - 1) = 4 n$ ,
> >
> > 且 $S_{n} > 0$ , 可得 $S_{n} = 2\sqrt{n}$ ,
> >
> > 当 $n = 1$ 时, $a_{1} = 2$ ;
> >
> > 当 $n \geq 2$ 时, $a_{n} = S_{n} - S_{n-1} = 2\left( \sqrt{n} - \sqrt{n - 1} \right)$ ;
> >
> > 可知 $a_{1} = 2$ 符合上式, 所以 $a_{n} = 2\left( \sqrt{n} - \sqrt{n - 1} \right)$ .
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 可得: $b_{n} = \frac{36}{\left( S_{n}^{2}-6 \right)^{2}} = \frac{36}{\left( 4n-6 \right)^{2}} = \frac{9}{\left( 2n-3 \right)^{2}}$ ,
> >
> > 当 $n \geq 3$ 时, $b_{n} = \frac{9}{\left( 2n-3 \right)^{2}} < \frac{9}{\left( 2n-3 \right)\left( 2n-5 \right)} = \frac{9}{2}\left( \frac{1}{2n-5} - \frac{1}{2n-3} \right)$ ,
> >
> > 可得 $T_{n} = 9 + 9 + \frac{9}{2}\left( 1 - \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{5} + \cdots + \frac{1}{2n-5} - \frac{1}{2n-3} \right)$ $= 18 + \frac{9}{2}\left( 1 - \frac{1}{2n-3} \right) < 18 + \frac{9}{2} = \frac{45}{2}$ ;
> >
> > 且 $T_{1} = 9 < \frac{45}{2}, T_{2} = 9 + 9 = 18 < \frac{45}{2}$ ,
> >
> > 综上所述: $T_{n} < \frac{45}{2}$ .
