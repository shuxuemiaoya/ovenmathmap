> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 10
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由等差数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 有 $S_n,S_2n-S_n,S_3n-S_2n,S_4n-S_3n$ 成等差数列,
> > 设为d,
> > 则 $S_3n-S_n=S_3n-S_2n+S_2n-S_n=S_n+2d+S_n+d=2S_n+3d=5\dots d=d=1$ ,
> > 故 $S_4n=S_4n-S_3n+S_3n-S_2n+S_2n-S_n+S_n=4S_n+6d=10$ .
