> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (2023·福建漳州·高二福建省华安县第一中学校考阶段练习)已知数列 $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ 的通项 $b_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&2n-3,n=2k-1\\&4n+6,n=2k\end{aligned}\right.$ , $k\in N^{*}$ , 求数列 $\left\{b_{n}\right\}$ 的前 n 项和 $T_{n}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{7}{2}n,n\text{为偶数}\\&\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{5}{2}n-5,n\text{为奇数}\end{aligned}\right.$ 方法 $1:b_n=\left\{\begin{aligned}&2n-3,n=2k-1\\&4n+6,n=2k\end{aligned}\right.,k\in N^{*}$ ,
> > 当 n 为偶数时, $T_n=(b_1+b_3+\cdots+b_{n-1})+(b_2+b_4+\cdots+b_n) = -\frac{1+2(n-1)-3}{2}\cdot\frac{n}{2}+\frac{14+4n+6}{2}\cdot\frac{n}{2}=\frac{3}{2}n^2+\frac{7}{2}n$ ,
> > 当 n 为奇数时, 若 $n\geq3$ , 则 $T_n=(b_1+b_3+\cdots+b_n)+(b_2+b_4+\cdots+b_{n-1}) = -\frac{1+2n-3}{2}\cdot\frac{n+1}{2}+\frac{14+4(n-1)+6}{2}\cdot\frac{n-1}{2}=\frac{3}{2}n^2+\frac{5}{2}n-5$ ,
> > 显然 $T_{1}=b_{1}=-1$ 满足上式, 因此当 n 为奇数时, $T_{n}=\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{5}{2}n-5$ . $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{7}{2}n,n\text{为偶数}\\&\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{5}{2}n-5,n\text{为奇数}\end{aligned}\right.$ 方法 $2:b_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&2n-3,n=2k-1\\&4n+6,n=2k\end{aligned}\right.,k\in N^{*},$ 当 n 为偶数时, $b_{n-1}+b_n=2(n-1)-3+4n+6=6n+1$ , $T_{n}=\frac{13+(6n+1)}{2}\cdot\frac{n}{2}=\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{7}{2}n$ ,
> > 当 n 为奇数时, $T_{n}=T_{n+1}-b_{n+1}=\frac{3}{2}(n+1)^{2}+\frac{7}{2}(n+1)-[4(n+1)+6]=\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{5}{2}n-5$ .
> > 所以 $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{7}{2}n,n\text{为偶数}\\&\frac{3}{2}n^{2}+\frac{5}{2}n-5,n\text{为奇数}\end{aligned}\right.$
