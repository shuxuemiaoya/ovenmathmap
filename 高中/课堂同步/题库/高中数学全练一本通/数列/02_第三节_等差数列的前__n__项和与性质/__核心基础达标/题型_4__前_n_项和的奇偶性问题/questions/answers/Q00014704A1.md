> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $a_{n}=4n-3$ （2） $T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&3n^{2}-7n,n\text{为偶数}\\ &3n^{2}-5n+4,n\text{为奇数}\end{aligned}\right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）设等差数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的公差为 d， $\because b_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&2a_{n},n\text{为奇数}\\&a_{n}-9,n\text{为偶数}\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore b_{1}=2a_{1},b_{2}=a_{2}-9=a_{1}+d-9,\quad b_{3}=2a_{3}=2(a_{1}+2d)=2a_{1}+4d,$ $\because S_{4}=28,T_{3}=16,$ $\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}&4a_{1}+6d=28\\&2a_{1}+a_{1}+d-9+2a_{1}+4d=16\end{aligned}\right.$ 整理得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&2a_{1}+3d=14\\&a_{1}+d=5\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a_{1}=1,\\&d=4,\end{aligned}\right.$ $\therefore a_{n}=4n-3;$ （2）当 n 为偶数时， $T_{n}=b_{1}+b_{2}+b_{3}+\cdots+b_{n}$ $=2(a_{1}+a_{3}+\cdots+a_{n-1})+(a_{2}-9+a_{4}-9+\cdots+a_{n}-9)$ $=2\cdot\frac{n}{2}(a_{1}+a_{n-1})+\left[\frac{n}{2}(a_{2}+a_{n})-\frac{n}{2}\times9\right]$ $=\frac{n}{2}[1+4(n-1)-3]+\frac{n}{4}(5+4n-3-18)$ $=3n^{2}-7n;$ 当 n 为奇数时， $n\geq3,$ $T_{n}=T_{n-1}+b_{n}=T_{n-1}+2a_{n}$ $=3(n-1)^{2}-7(n-1)+2(4n-3)$ $=3n^{2}-5n+4,$ 当 n=1 时，上式也成立； $\therefore T_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&3n^{2}-7n,n\text{为偶数}\\&3n^{2}-5n+4,n\text{为奇数}\end{aligned}\right.$
