> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{3n^{2}+4n}{4},n\text{为偶数}\\ \frac{3n^{2}+6n+11}{4},n\text{为奇数}\end{aligned}\right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意，当 n 为偶数
> >
> > 时， $a_{n-1}+a_{n}=2(n-1)+3+n-2=3n-1$ ， $S_{n}=\frac{5+(3n-1)}{2}\cdot\frac{n}{2}=\frac{3n^{2}+4n}{4}$ ，
> > 当 n 为奇数时， $S_{n}=S_{n+1}-a_{n+1}=\frac{3(n+1)^{2}+4(n+1)}{4}-[(n+1)-2]=\frac{3n^{2}+6n+11}{4}$ 所以 $S_{n}=\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{3n^{2}+4n}{4},n\text{为偶数}\\ \frac{3n^{2}+6n+11}{4},n\text{为奇数}\end{aligned}\right.$
