> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{80}{81}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a_{n} = \frac{2n + 1}{n^{2}(n + 1)^{2}} = \frac{1}{n^{2}} -\frac{1}{(n + 1)^{2}}$ 所以 $S_{8} = 1 - \frac{1}{2^{2}} +(\frac{1}{2^{2}} -\frac{1}{3^{2}}) + \dots +( \frac{1}{8^{2}} -\frac{1}{9^{2}}) = 1 - \frac{1}{81} = \frac{80}{81}$ .故答案为： $\frac{80}{81}$
