> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1）$a_n = 2n$（2）$2n^2 + \frac{4^{n+1}-4}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > | (1) a _ {n} = 2 n (2) 2 n ^ {2} + \frac {4 ^ {n + 1} - 4}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = S _ {n} - S _ {n - 1} = n ^ {2} + n - (n - 1) ^ {2} - (n - 1) = 2 n
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {1} = S _ {1} = 2
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {n} = 2 n
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b _ {n} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 2 n, n \text {为奇数} \\ 2 ^ {n}, n \text {为偶数} \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > T _ {2 n} = (2 + 6 + 1 0 + \dots + 4 n - 2) + (2 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {4} + 2 ^ {6} + \dots + 2 ^ {2 n})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {n (2 + 4 n - 2)}{2} + \frac {4 (1 - 4 ^ {n})}{1 - 4} = 2 n ^ {2} + \frac {4 ^ {n + 1} - 4}{3}
> > $$
