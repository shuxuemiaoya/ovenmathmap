> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{19}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 函数 $f(x)=\frac{1}{x+1}$ ，当 x>0
> > 时， $f(x)+f(\frac{1}{x})=\frac{1}{1+x}+\frac{1}{1+\frac{1}{x}}=\frac{1}{1+x}+\frac{x}{1+x}=1$ 因数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是正项等比数列，且 $a_{10}=1$ ，则 $a_{1}a_{19}=a_{2}a_{18}=a_{3}a_{17}=\cdots=a_{10}^{2}=1$ $f(a_{1})+f(a_{19})=f(a_{1})+f(\frac{1}{a_{1}})=1$ ，同理 $f(a_{2})+f(a_{18})=f(a_{3})+f(a_{17})=\cdots=f(a_{10})+f(a_{10})=1$ 令 $S=f(a_{1})+f(a_{2})+f(a_{3})+\cdots+f(a_{18})+f(a_{19})$ 又 $S=f(a_{19})+f(a_{18})+f(a_{17})+\cdots+f(a_{2})+f(a_{1})$ 则有 2S=19， $S=\frac{19}{2}$ 所以 $f(a_{1})+f(a_{2})+f(a_{3})+\cdots+f(a_{18})+f(a_{19})=\frac{19}{2}$ . 故答案为： $\frac{19}{2}$
