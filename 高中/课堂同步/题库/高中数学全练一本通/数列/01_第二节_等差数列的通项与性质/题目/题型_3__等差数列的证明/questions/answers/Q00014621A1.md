> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设这三个数依次为 a-d, a, a+d,
> > 由题意可得: $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a-d+a+a+d=9,\\&a(a-d)=6(a+d)\end{aligned}\right.$ , 解得: $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a=3\\&d=-1\end{aligned}\right.$ ,
> > 所以这三个数依次为 4,3,2.
