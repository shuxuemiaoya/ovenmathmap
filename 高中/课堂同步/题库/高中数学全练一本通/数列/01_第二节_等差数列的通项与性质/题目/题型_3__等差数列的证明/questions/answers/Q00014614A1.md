> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析， $a_{n} = \frac{2}{n} +2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{1}{a_{n + 1} - 2} -\frac{1}{a_n - 2} = \frac{1}{4 - \frac{4}{a_n} - 2} -\frac{1}{a_n - 2} = \frac{a_n}{2a_n - 4} -\frac{1}{a_n - 2} = \frac{1}{2}$ 为常数，所以 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n - 2}\right\}$ 为公差为 $\frac{1}{2}$ 的等差数列，由于 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n - 2}\right\}$ 为公差为 $\frac{1}{2}$ 的等差数列，且首项为 $\frac{1}{2}$ 所以 $\frac{1}{a_n - 2} = \frac{1}{2} n,$ 所以 $a_{n} = \frac{2}{n} +2$
