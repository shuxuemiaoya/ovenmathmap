>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 6
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a_{4} + a_{7} = 2$ ，所以 $a_{5} + a_{6} = a_{4} + a_{7} = 2$ ，
> > 又 $a_{5}a_{6} = -8$ ，
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a_{5}=-2\\&a_{6}=4\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a_{5}=4\\&a_{6}=-2\end{aligned}\right.$ 又 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为递增等差数列，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a_{5}=-2\\&a_{6}=4\end{aligned}\right.$ ，则 $d=a_{6}-a_{5}=6$ .
