> > [!success] **【答案】** $$ \left\{\sqrt {2}, - \sqrt {2}, - \frac {9}{4} \right\} $$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：由题意,集合 $A=\left\{x\mid\frac{x+a}{x^{2}-2}=1\right\}$ 是单元素集,
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {x + a}{x ^ {2} - 2} = 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because x ^ {2} - 2 \neq 0 \Rightarrow x \neq \pm \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a = \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{x - \sqrt {2}} \Rightarrow x = \sqrt {2} + 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a = - \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {1}{x + \sqrt {2}} \Rightarrow x = - \sqrt {2} + 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a \neq \pm \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {x + a}{x ^ {2} - 2} = 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x ^ {2} - x - 2 - a = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \Delta = 1 + 4 (a + 2) = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a = - \frac {9}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{\sqrt {2}, - \sqrt {2}, - \frac {9}{4} \right\}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{\sqrt {2}, - \sqrt {2}, - \frac {9}{4} \right\}
> > $$
