> [!success]- 全练一本通
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $-8 \leq b \leq -3$ 当 $-2 \leq x_{2} \leq 2$ 时, $y_{2} = x - 1$ , $-3 \leq y_{2} \leq 1$ , 记 $A = \left\{ y_{2} \mid -3 \leq y_{2} \leq 1 \right\}$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $-2 \leq x_{1} \leq 2$ 时, $y_{1} = x^{2} + 2x + b + 1 = (x + 1)^{2} + b$ , $b \leq y_{1} \leq 9 + b$ ,
> >
> > 记 $B = \left\{ y_{1} \mid b \leq y_{1} \leq 9 + b \right\}$ .
> >
> > 因为对 $\forall x_{1} \in \{x \mid -2 \leq x \leq 2\}$ , $\exists x_{2} \in \{x \mid -2 \leq x \leq 2\}$ , 使得 $y_{1} = y_{2}$ 成立, 所以 $A \subseteq B$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&b \leq -3 \\&b + 9 \geq 1\end{aligned}\right.$ , 解得 $-8 \leq b \leq -3$ . 实数 b 的取值范围为 $-8 \leq b \leq -3$ .
