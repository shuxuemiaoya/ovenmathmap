>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：因为 a > 0, b > 0,
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{b^{2}}{a} + a + \frac{a^{2}}{b} + b \geq 2\sqrt{\frac{b^{2}}{a} \cdot a} + 2\sqrt{\frac{a^{2}}{b} \cdot b} = 2a + 2b$ ,
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{b^{2}}{a} = a, \frac{a^{2}}{b} = b$ , 即 a = b 时, 等号成立, 故 $\frac{b^{2}}{a} + \frac{a^{2}}{b} \geq a + b$ .
