> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：因为 a>0,b>0,c>0
> > 则 $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{b}\geqslant2\sqrt{\frac{1}{ab}}=2\sqrt{\frac{abc}{ab}}=2\sqrt{c},\frac{1}{b}+\frac{1}{c}\geqslant2\sqrt{\frac{1}{bc}}=2\sqrt{\frac{abc}{bc}}=2\sqrt{a}$ $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{c} \geqslant 2\sqrt{\frac{1}{ac}} = 2\sqrt{\frac{abc}{ac}} = 2\sqrt{b}$ ,
> >
> > 三式相加得： $2\left(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}\right) \geqslant 2\left(\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b} + \sqrt{c}\right)$ 所以 $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} \geqslant \sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b} + \sqrt{c}$ .
