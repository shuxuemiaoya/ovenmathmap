> > 解析：因为 $A=B$，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=x^2\\ y&=2y\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=2y\\ y&=x^2\end{aligned}\right.$，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=0\\ y&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=1\\ y&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=\frac{1}{2}\\ y&=\frac{1}{4}\end{aligned}\right.$，又集合中的元素需满足互异性，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=\frac{1}{2}\\ y&=\frac{1}{4}\end{aligned}\right.$，则 $x-y=\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{4}=\frac{1}{4}$。
