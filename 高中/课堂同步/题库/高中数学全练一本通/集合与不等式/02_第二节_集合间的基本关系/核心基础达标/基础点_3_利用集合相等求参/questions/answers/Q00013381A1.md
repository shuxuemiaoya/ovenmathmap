> > [!success] **【答案】** $1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：因为 $\{a,0,-1\}=\{a^2,ab,0\}$，所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=a^2\\ -1&=ab\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=ab\\ -1&=a^2\end{aligned}\right.$，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=1\\ b&=-1\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=-1\\ b&=1\end{aligned}\right.$，所以 $ab=-1$，所以 $(ab)^{2024}=(-1)^{2024}=1$，故答案为：$1$.
