> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：$M = \left\{x \mid x = m + \frac{1}{6}, m \in Z\right\} = \left\{x \mid x = \frac{6m + 1}{6}, m \in Z\right\} = \left\{x \mid x = \frac{3 \cdot 2m + 1}{6}, m \in Z\right\}$ .
> >
> > \( N=\left\{x\mid x=\frac{n}{2}-\frac{1}{3},n\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}=\left\{x\mid x=\frac{3(n-1)+1}{6},n\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}=\left\{x\mid x=\frac{3k+1}{6},k\in\mathbb{Z}\right\} \)
> >  \( P=\left\{x\mid x=\frac{p}{2}+\frac{1}{6},p\in\mathbb{Z}\right\}=\left\{x\mid x=\frac{3p+1}{6},p\in\mathbb{Z}\right\} \) ，故  \( M \subsetneq N=P \) 。
> >
> > 故选 **B**。
