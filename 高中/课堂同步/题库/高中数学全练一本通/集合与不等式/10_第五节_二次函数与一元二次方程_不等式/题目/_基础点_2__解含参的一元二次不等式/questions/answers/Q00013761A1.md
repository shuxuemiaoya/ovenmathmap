> > 当 $0 < x \leq 3$ 时，要使 $x^{2} + 2x + 1 \geq 2kx - 5$ ，
> > 分参得 $2k \leq x + \frac{6}{x} + 2$ ，即 $2k \leq \left(x + \frac{6}{x} + 2\right)_{\min}$
> > $$
> > x + \frac {6}{x} \geq 2 \sqrt {6}
> > $$
> > $$
> > x = \frac {6}{x}
> > $$
> > $$
> > x = \sqrt {6}
> > $$
> > $$
> > x + \frac {6}{x} + 2
> > $$
> > $$
> > 2 \sqrt {6} + 2
> > $$
> > $$
> > 2 k \leq 2 \sqrt {6} + 2, k \leq \sqrt {6} + 1
> > $$
> > $$
> > 1 \geq - 5
> > $$
> > $$
> > - 4 \leq x <   0
> > $$
> > $$
> > x ^ {2} + 2 x + 1 \geq 2 k x - 5
> > $$
> > $$
> > 2 k \geq x + \frac {6}{x} + 2
> > $$
> > $$
> > 2 k \geq \left(x + \frac {6}{x} + 2\right) _ {\mathrm{m}}
> > $$
> > 因为 $x + \frac{6}{x} = -(-x + \frac{6}{-x})\leq -2\sqrt{6}$ ，当且仅当 $-x = \frac{6}{-x}$
> > 即 $x = -\sqrt{6}$ 时等号成立，
> > 所以 $(x + \frac{6}{x})_{\max} = -2\sqrt{6}$
> > 所以 $2k \geq -2\sqrt{6} + 2$ ，即 $k \geq -\sqrt{6} + 1$
> > 综上 $-\sqrt{6} + 1 \leq k \leq \sqrt{6} + 1$
