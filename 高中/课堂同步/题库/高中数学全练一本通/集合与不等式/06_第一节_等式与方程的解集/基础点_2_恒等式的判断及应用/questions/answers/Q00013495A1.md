> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}; -\frac{9}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：因为 $x^2 + \frac{1}{2} x - 5 = a(2x - 1)(x + 1) + c = 2ax^2 + ax + c - a$ , 根据对应项系数相等 $\begin{cases} 2a = 1 \\ a = \frac{1}{2} \\ c - a = -5 \end{cases}$ , 解得 $\begin{cases} a = \frac{1}{2} \\ c = -\frac{9}{2} \end{cases}$ . 故答案为: $\frac{1}{2}; -\frac{9}{2}$ .
