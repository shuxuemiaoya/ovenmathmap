> [!success]- 全练一本通
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** ± $2\sqrt{2}-2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：由题意可得 $\Delta=m^{2}+4m>0$ ，解得 m>0 或 m<-4， $x_{1}+x_{2}=-m,x_{1}x_{2}=-m$ 则 $\left|x_{1}-x_{2}\right|=\sqrt{(x_{1}+x_{2})^{2}-4x_{1}x_{2}}=\sqrt{m^{2}+4m}=2$ ，解得 $m=\pm 2\sqrt{2}-2$ .
> > 故答案为：± $2\sqrt{2}-2$ .
