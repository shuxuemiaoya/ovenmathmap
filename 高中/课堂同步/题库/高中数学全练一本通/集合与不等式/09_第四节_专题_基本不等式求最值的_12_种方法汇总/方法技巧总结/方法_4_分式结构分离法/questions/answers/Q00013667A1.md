> > [!success] **【答案】** $2\sqrt{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：因为 x>0, y>0, $x+2y=5$ ,
> > 则 $\frac{(x+1)(2y+1)}{\sqrt{2xy}}=\frac{2xy+1+(x+2y)}{\sqrt{2xy}}=\frac{2xy+6}{\sqrt{2xy}}$ $=\sqrt{2xy}+\frac{6}{\sqrt{2xy}}\geq2\sqrt{\sqrt{2xy}\cdot\frac{6}{\sqrt{2xy}}} = 2\sqrt{6}$ 当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x+2y=5\\&\sqrt{2xy}=\frac{6}{\sqrt{2xy}}\\&x>0,y>0\end{aligned}\right.$ 时，即当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x=3\\&y=1\end{aligned}\right.$ 时，等号成立，故 $\frac{(x+1)(2y+1)}{\sqrt{2xy}}$ 最小值为 $2\sqrt{6}$ .
