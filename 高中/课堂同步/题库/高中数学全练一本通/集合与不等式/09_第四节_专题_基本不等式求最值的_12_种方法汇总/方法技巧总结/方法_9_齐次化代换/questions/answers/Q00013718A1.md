> > [!success] **【答案】** $2\sqrt{2}+2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：$\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+a}{ab}=\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+a(a+2b)}{ab}=\frac{2a^{2}+b^{2}+2ab}{ab}$ $=\frac{2a}{b}+\frac{b}{a}+2\geqslant2\sqrt{2}+2$ ，当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a+2b=1,\\&\frac{2a}{b}&=\frac{b}{a}.\\\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a=\frac{2\sqrt{2}-1}{7},\\&b=\frac{4-\sqrt{2}}{7}.\\\end{aligned}\right.$ ∴ 当 $a=\frac{2\sqrt{2}-1}{7},b=\frac{4-\sqrt{2}}{7}$ 时， $\frac{a^{2}+b^{2}+a}{ab}$ 最小值 $2\sqrt{2}+2$ .
