>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $2\sqrt{2}+2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：$\frac{x^{2}+y^{2}}{xy-y^{2}}=\frac{\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)^{2}+1}{\frac{x}{y}-1}$ , 设 $t=\frac{x}{y}$ , 则 t>1.
> > 于是 $\frac{x^{2}+y^{2}}{xy-y^{2}}=\frac{\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)^{2}+1}{\frac{x}{y}-1}=\frac{t^{2}+1}{t-1}=\frac{t^{2}-1+2}{t-1}=\frac{t^{2}-1}{t-1}+\frac{2}{t-1}=t+1+\frac{2}{t-1}$ ,
> > 令 $y=t+1+\frac{2}{t-1}(t>1)$ ，则 $y=t-1+\frac{2}{t-1}+2\geq2\sqrt{(t-1)\cdot\frac{2}{t-1}}+2=2+2\sqrt{2}$ ,
> > 当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&t>1\\ &t-1=\frac{2}{t-1}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，即 $t=\sqrt{2}+1$ ，也即 $x=(1+\sqrt{2})y$ 时， $\frac{x^{2}+y^{2}}{xy-y^{2}}$ 取到最小值 $2\sqrt{2}+2$ .
