>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：$\frac{x^{2}+2y}{xy}=\frac{x^{2}+2y\cdot(2x+y)}{xy}=\frac{x^{2}+2y^{2}+4xy}{xy}$ $\geq\frac{2\sqrt{x^{2}\times2y^{2}}+4xy}{xy}=\frac{2\sqrt{2}xy+4xy}{xy}=2\sqrt{2}+4$ ,
> >
> > 当且仅当 $x^{2}=2y^{2}$ , 即 $x=\frac{4-\sqrt{2}}{7}$ , $y=\frac{2\sqrt{2}-1}{7}$ 时, 等号成立.
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
