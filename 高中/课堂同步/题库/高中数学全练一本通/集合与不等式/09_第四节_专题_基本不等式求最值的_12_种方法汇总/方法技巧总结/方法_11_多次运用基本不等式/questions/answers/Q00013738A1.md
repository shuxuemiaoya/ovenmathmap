> > [!success] **【答案】** 4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：若 $a > 0, b > 0$ ，则 $(a + b)^2 + \frac{1}{ab} \geq (2\sqrt{ab})^2 + \frac{1}{ab} = 4ab + \frac{1}{ab} \geq 4$ ，当且仅当 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = b \\ 4ab = \frac{1}{ab} \end{array} \right.$ ，即 $a = b = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 时取等号，即所求的最小值为4，故答案为：4
