> [!success]- 全练一本通
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $4\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：$\because (a - b)b \leq \left(\frac{a - b + b}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{a^2}{4}$ ， $\therefore 2a^2 + \frac{1}{(a - b)b} \geq 2a^2 + \frac{4}{a^2} \geq 4\sqrt{2}$ ，当且仅当 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a - b = b \\ 2a^2 = \frac{4}{a^2} \end{array} \right.$ ，即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = \sqrt[4]{2} \\ b = \frac{\sqrt[4]{2}}{2} \end{array} \right.$ 时，等号成立，所以 $2a^2 + \frac{1}{(a - b)b}$ 的最小值是 $4\sqrt{2}$ ．故答案为： $4\sqrt{2}$
