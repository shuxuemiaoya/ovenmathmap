> [!success]- 全练一本通
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > a b > 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a ^ {2} + 4 b ^ {2} + \frac {1}{a b} \geq 2 \sqrt {a ^ {2} \times 4 b ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{a b}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 4 a b + \frac {1}{a b} \geq 2 \sqrt {4 a b \cdot \frac {1}{a b}} = 4
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $a^2 = 4b^2, 4ab = \frac{1}{ab}$ 即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 1 \\ b = \frac{1}{2} \end{array} \right.$ 或 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = -1 \\ b = -\frac{1}{2} \end{array} \right.$ 时取“=”．故答案为：4
