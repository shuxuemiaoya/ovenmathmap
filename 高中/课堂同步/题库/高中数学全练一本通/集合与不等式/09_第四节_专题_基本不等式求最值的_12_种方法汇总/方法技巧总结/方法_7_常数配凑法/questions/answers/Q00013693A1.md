> [!success]- 全练一本通
>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $1+2\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：若 a, b 为正数，则 $a+1>0, b>0$ $\frac{4}{a+1}+\frac{a}{b}=\frac{4}{a+1}+\frac{1-2b}{b}=\frac{4}{a+1}+\frac{1}{b}-2=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{4}{a+1}+\frac{2}{2b}\right)\cdot(a+1+2b)-2$ $=\frac{1}{2}\left(6+\frac{8b}{a+1}+\frac{a+1}{b}\right)-2\geq1+2\sqrt{2}$ ，
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{8b}{a+1}=\frac{a+1}{b}$ 时等号成立，
> >
> > 所以当 $a=3-2\sqrt{2}, b=\sqrt{2}-1$ 时， $\left(\frac{4}{a+1}+\frac{a}{b}\right)_{\min}=1+2\sqrt{2}$ .
