>
> > [!success] **【答案】** 18
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：∵ $2x+y+6=xy,\therefore y=\frac{2x+6}{x-1},x>1,$ $xy=\frac{x(2x+6)}{x-1}=\frac{2(x^{2}+3x)}{x-1}=\frac{2[x^{2}-1+3(x-1)+4]}{x-1}=2\left(x+1+\frac{4}{x-1}+3\right)=2\left(x-1+\frac{4}{x-1}+5\right)\geq2\times\left(2\sqrt{x-1\cdot\frac{4}{x-1}}+5\right)=18$ 当且仅当 x=3 时，等号成立，∴ xy 的最小值为18.
