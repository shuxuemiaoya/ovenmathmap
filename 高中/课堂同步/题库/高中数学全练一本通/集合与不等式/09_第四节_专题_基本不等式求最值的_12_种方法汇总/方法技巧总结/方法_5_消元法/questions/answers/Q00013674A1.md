> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：因为 $x > 0, y > 0, \frac{8}{x} + \frac{2}{y} = 1$，所以有 $x = \frac{8y}{y - 2} > 0$，且 $y - 2 > 0$，所以有 $\frac{4}{x - 8} + \frac{1}{y - 2} = \frac{4}{\frac{8y}{y - 2} - 8} + \frac{1}{y - 2} = \frac{y - 2}{4} + \frac{1}{y - 2}$，由基本不等式得 $\frac{4}{x - 8} + \frac{1}{y - 2} = \frac{y - 2}{4} + \frac{1}{y - 2} \geqslant 2\sqrt{\frac{y - 2}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{y - 2}} = 1$，当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x &= \frac{8y}{y - 2} > 0 \\ \frac{y - 2}{4} &= \frac{1}{y - 2}\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 $x = 16, y = 4$ 时，等号成立，故当且仅当 $x = 16, y = 4$ 时，$\frac{4}{x - 8} + \frac{1}{y - 2}$ 有最小值 $1$.
