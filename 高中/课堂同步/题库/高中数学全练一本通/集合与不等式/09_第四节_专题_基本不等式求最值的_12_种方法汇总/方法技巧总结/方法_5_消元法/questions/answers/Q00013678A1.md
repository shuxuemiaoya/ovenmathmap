> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：$\frac{1}{4a} +\frac{4a}{2a + b} = \frac{1}{4a} +\frac{4a}{a + 1} = \frac{1 + a}{4a} +\frac{4a}{a + 1} -\frac{1}{4}\geqslant 2\sqrt{\frac{1 + a}{4a}\cdot\frac{4a}{a + 1}} -\frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{4},$ 当且仅当 $\frac{1 + a}{4a} = \frac{4a}{a + 1}$ ，即 $a = \frac{1}{3},b = \frac{2}{3}$ 时取得等号.
