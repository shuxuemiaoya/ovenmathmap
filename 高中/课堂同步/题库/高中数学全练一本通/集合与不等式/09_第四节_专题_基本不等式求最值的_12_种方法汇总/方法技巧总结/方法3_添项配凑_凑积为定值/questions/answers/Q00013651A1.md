> > [!success] **【答案】** $$ 2 \sqrt {2} + 2 $$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：当 x>1 时, x-1>0,
> > 则 $2x + \frac{1}{x-1} = 2(x-1) + \frac{1}{x-1} + 2 \geq 2\sqrt{2(x-1) \cdot \frac{1}{x-1}} + 2 = 2\sqrt{2} + 2$ , 当且仅当 $2(x-1) = \frac{1}{x-1}(x>1)$ 时, 即当 $x = 1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 时, 等号成立, 所以当 x>1
> > 时, $2x + \frac{1}{x-1}$ 的最小值为 $2\sqrt{2} + 2$ .
