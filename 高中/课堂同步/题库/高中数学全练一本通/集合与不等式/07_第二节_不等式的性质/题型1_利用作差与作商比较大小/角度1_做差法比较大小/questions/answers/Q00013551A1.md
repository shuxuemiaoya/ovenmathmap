> > (3) $(x + 1)(x^{2} + \frac{x}{2} + 1) - (x + \frac{1}{2})(x^{2} + x + 1)$

> > $$
> > = x ^ {3} + \frac {3}{2} x ^ {2} + \frac {3}{2} x + 1 - \left(x ^ {3} + \frac {3}{2} x ^ {2} + \frac {3}{2} x + \frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {1}{2} > 0

> > $$
> > \left(x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}\right) ^ {2} - x y (x - y) ^ {2} = (x + y) ^ {2} (x - y) ^ {2} - x y (x - y) ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = (x - y) ^ {2} \left(x ^ {2} + x y + y ^ {2}\right) = (x - y) ^ {2} \left[ \left(x + \frac {y}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {3}{4} y ^ {2} \right] \geq 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left(x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}\right) ^ {2} \geq x y (x - y) ^ {2}
