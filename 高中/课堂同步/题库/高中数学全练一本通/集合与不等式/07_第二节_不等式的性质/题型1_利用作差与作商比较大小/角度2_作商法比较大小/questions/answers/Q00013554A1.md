> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：$\because a > b > 0$ $\therefore \frac{a^2 - b^2}{a^2 + b^2} >0,\frac{a - b}{a + b} >0$ $\therefore \frac{\overline{a^2 - b^2}}{\overline{a - b}} = \frac{(a + b)^2}{a^2 + b^2} = 1 + \frac{2ab}{a^2 + b^2} >1$ $\therefore \frac{a^2 - b^2}{a^2 + b^2} >\frac{a - b}{a + b}$ .
