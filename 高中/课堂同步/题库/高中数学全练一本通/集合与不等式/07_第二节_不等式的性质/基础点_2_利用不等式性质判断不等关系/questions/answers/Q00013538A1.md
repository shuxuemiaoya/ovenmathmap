> > (8) $\because \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a < b, \\ a < 0, \end{array} \right.\therefore a^2 > ab$ 。又 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a < b, \\ b < 0, \end{array} \right.\therefore ab > b^2,\quad \therefore a^2 > ab > b^2$ 故该命题为真命题；
