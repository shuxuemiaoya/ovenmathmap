>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $9\leq \frac{x^5}{y^5}\leq 32$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：$\frac{x^{5}}{y^{5}}=(\frac{x^{2}}{y})^{3}\frac{1}{xy^{2}}$
> >
> > $$
> > \because 3 \leq \frac {x ^ {2}}{y} \leq 4, \therefore 2 7 \leq (\frac {x ^ {2}}{y}) ^ {3} \leq 6 4
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because 2 \leq x y ^ {2} \leq 3, \therefore \frac {1}{3} \leq \frac {1}{x y ^ {2}} \leq \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 由不等式的性质得出 $9 \leq \left(\frac{x^2}{y}\right)^3 \frac{1}{xy^2} \leq 32$ ，即 $9 \leq \frac{x^5}{y^5} \leq 32$ 。
