>
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x\geq2,x\in N_{+}\\ &y\geq2,y\in N_{+}\\ &0.8\times5x+2\times4y\leq50\end{aligned}\right.$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：每种邮票至少买两套, 则有 $x \geq 2, x \in N_{+}, y \geq 2, y \in N_{+}$ , 又因为 50 元
> >
> > 钱买纪念邮票, 所以 $0.8 \times 5x + 2 \times 4y \leq 50$ , 故 $\left\{\begin{aligned} x &\geq 2, x \in N_{+} \\ y &\geq 2, y \in N_{+} \\ 0.8 &\times 5x + 2 \times 4y \leq 50 \end{aligned}\right.$
