> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{5}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{1}{2x} + \frac{x}{y+1} = \frac{1}{2x} + \frac{1-y}{y+1} = \frac{1}{2x} + \frac{-(y+1)+2}{y+1} = \frac{1}{2x} + \frac{4}{2y+2}-1$ $\geqslant \frac{(1+2)^2}{2x+2y+2} - 1 = \frac{5}{4}$ 当 $\frac{1}{2x} = \frac{2}{2y+2}$ 时, 即 $x = \frac{2}{3}, y = \frac{1}{3}$ , 有所求的最小值 $\frac{5}{4}$ .
