> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2} + \sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由权方和不等式可得: $1 = \frac{2}{a + 2} + \frac{1}{a + 2b} \geqslant \frac{(\sqrt{2} + 1)^2}{2a + 2b + 2}$ , 解得: $a + b \geqslant \frac{1}{2} + \sqrt{2}$ . 当 $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{a + 2} = \frac{1}{a + 2b}$ , 即 $a = \sqrt{2}, b = \frac{1}{2}$ , 等号成立.
