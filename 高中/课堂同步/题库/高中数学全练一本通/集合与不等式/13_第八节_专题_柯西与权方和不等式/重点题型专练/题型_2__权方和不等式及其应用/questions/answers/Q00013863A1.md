> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $3+2\sqrt{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由权方和不等式可得：
> > $\frac{1}{a - 1} + \frac{2}{b} = \frac{1}{a - 1} + \frac{(\sqrt{2})^2}{b} \geqslant \frac{(1 + \sqrt{2})^2}{a - 1 + b} = 3 + 2\sqrt{2}$ . 当 $\frac{1}{a - 1} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{b}$ 时, 等号成立.
