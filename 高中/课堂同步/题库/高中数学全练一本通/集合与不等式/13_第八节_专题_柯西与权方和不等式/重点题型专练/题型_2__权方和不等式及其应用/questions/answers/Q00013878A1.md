> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{6}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{1}{x^2 + x} +\frac{2}{2y^2 + y} = \frac{\frac{1}{x^2}}{1 + \frac{1}{x}} +\frac{\frac{2}{y^2}}{2 + \frac{1}{y}} = \frac{\frac{1}{x^2}}{1 + \frac{1}{x}} +\frac{\frac{4}{y^2}}{4 + \frac{2}{y}}$
> > $$
> > \frac {\frac {1}{x ^ {2}}}{1 + \frac {1}{x}} + \frac {\frac {4}{y ^ {2}}}{4 + \frac {2}{y}} \geq \frac {\left(\frac {1}{x} + \frac {2}{y}\right) ^ {2}}{1 + \frac {1}{x} + 4 + \frac {2}{y}} = \frac {1}{6}
> > $$
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{\frac{1}{x}}{1 + \frac{1}{x}} = \frac{\frac{2}{y}}{4 + \frac{2}{y}}$ 时等号成立.
