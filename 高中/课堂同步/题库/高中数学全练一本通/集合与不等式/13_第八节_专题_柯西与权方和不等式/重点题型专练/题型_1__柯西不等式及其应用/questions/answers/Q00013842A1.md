> > [!note]- **【分析】**

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由柯西不等式: $\left(x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}\right)\left(4+9+36\right)\geq(2x+3y+6z)^{2}\geq\frac{144}{49}$ , $\therefore x^{2} + y^{2} + z^{2} \geqslant \frac{144}{49}$ ，当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{x}{2}&=\frac{y}{3}=\frac{z}{6}\\ 2x+3y+6z&=12\end{aligned}\right.$ ，即 $x=\frac{24}{49},y=\frac{36}{49},z=\frac{72}{49}$ 取等号
