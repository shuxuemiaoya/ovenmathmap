> > [!success] **【答案】** $\{(0, 2)\}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：由题意可得 $M \cap N = \left\{(x, y) \mid \left\{\begin{aligned} y &= x + 2 \\ x &= 0 \end{aligned}\right\}$ ，解方程可得 x = 0, y = 2，故 $M \cap N = \{(0, 2)\}$ 。故答案为： $\{(0, 2)\}$
