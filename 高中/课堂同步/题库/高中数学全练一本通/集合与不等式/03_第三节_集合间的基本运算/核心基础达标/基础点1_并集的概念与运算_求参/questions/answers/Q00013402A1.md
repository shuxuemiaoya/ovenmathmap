> > [!success] **【答案】** $-\frac{1}{3} < m < \frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：由 $A \cup B = B$ , 则 $A \subseteq B$ , 故有 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 1 - m \times (-3) > 0 \\ 1 - m \times (-1) > 0 \\ 1 - m \times 2 > 0 \end{array} \right.$ , 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} m > -\frac{1}{3} \\ m > -1 \\ m < \frac{1}{2} \end{array} \right.$ , 即 $-\frac{1}{3} < m < \frac{1}{2}$ . 故答案为: $-\frac{1}{3} < m < \frac{1}{2}$ .
