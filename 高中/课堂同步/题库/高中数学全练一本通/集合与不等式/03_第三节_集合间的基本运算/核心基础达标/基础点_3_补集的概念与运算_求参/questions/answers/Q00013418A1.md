> > [!success] **【答案】** a=2
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：由题意得:①当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a+1&=3\\ a^{2}&+2a-3=a+3\end{aligned}\right.$ 时,解得:a=2代入检验,得 $U=\{2,3,5\}$ , $A=\{2,3\}$ , $C_{U}A=\{5\}$ ，满足条件
> > - **②**：当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a+3&=3\\ a^{2}&+2a-3=a+1\end{aligned}\right.$ 时,无解.综上所述,a=2.
