> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $(1+x)^{2n}$ 的展开式中 $x^{n-1}$ 的系数为 $C_{2n}^{n-1}$ .
> >
> > 又 $(1 + x)^{2n} = (1 + x)^{n}(1 + x)^{n}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left(\mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {0} + \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {1} x + \dots + \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {n} x ^ {n}\right) \cdot \left(\mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {0} + \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {1} x + \dots + \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {n} x ^ {n}\right),
> > $$
> >
> > 则等式右边整理后 $x^{n - 1}$ 的系数为
> >
> > $$
> > \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {0} \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {n - 1} + \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {1} \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {n - 2} + \dots + \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {n - 1} \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {0} = \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {0} \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {1} + \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {1} \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {2} + \dots + \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {n - 1} \mathrm{C} _ {n} ^ {n}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $C_{n}^{0}C_{n}^{1} + C_{n}^{1}C_{n}^{2} + \cdots + C_{n}^{n-1}C_{n}^{n} = C_{2n}^{n-1}$
> >
