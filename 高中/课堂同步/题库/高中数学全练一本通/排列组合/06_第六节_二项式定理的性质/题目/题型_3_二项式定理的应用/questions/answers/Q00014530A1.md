> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $99^{10}=(100-1)^{10}=C_{10}^{0}100^{10}+C_{10}^{1}100^{9}\left(-1\right)^{1}+\cdots+C_{10}^{9}100\left(-1\right)^{9}+C_{10}^{10}\left(-1\right)^{10}=$ $\left[C_{10}^{0}100^{10}+C_{10}^{1}100^{9}\left(-1\right)^{1}+\cdots+C_{10}^{9}100\left(-1\right)^{9}\right]+1=$ $1000\left[C_{10}^{0}100^{-7}-C_{10}^{1}100^{-6}+\cdots\left(-1\right)^{9}\right]+1,$ 所以 $99^{10}$ 除以1000的余数为1.
