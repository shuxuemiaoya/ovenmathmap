>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 31.681

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $1.996^5=(2-0.004)^5=C_5^ {o}\times 2^ {s}-C_5^ {i}\times 2^ {q}\times 0.004 ^ {i }+C_5^ {2}\times 2^ {3}\times 0.004 ^ {2}-C_5^ {3}\times 2^ {2}\times 0.004 ^ {3 }+C_5^ {4}\times 2\times 0.004 ^ {4}-C_5^ {s}\times 0.004 ^ {s}\approx 32-0.32+0.00128=31.68128$ ，将 $1.996^s$ 精确到 0.001，故近似值为31.681．故答案为：31.681.
