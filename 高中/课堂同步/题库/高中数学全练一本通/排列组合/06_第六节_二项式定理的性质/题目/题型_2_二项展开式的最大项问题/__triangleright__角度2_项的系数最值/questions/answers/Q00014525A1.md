> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【详解】由题意得二项式 $\left( \frac{x^{2}}{2}-x^{-\frac{1}{2}} \right)^{n}$ 展开式的通项公式
> > 为: $T_{r+1} = C_{n}^{r}\left( \frac{x^{2}}{2} \right)^{n-r}\left( -x^{-\frac{1}{2}} \right)^{r}= (-1)^{r}\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{n-r}\mathrm{C}_{n}^{r}\mathrm{x}^ {2n-\frac{5r}{2}}$ ,
> > 因为展开式中第 9 项为常数项, 故 $2n - \frac{5}{2}\times 8 = 0 \Rightarrow n = 10$ ,
> > 故第 $r + 1$ 项的系数绝对值为 $\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{10-r}\mathrm{C}_{10}^{r}$ ,
> > 设展开式中第 $r + 1$ 项的系数绝对值最大, 则有 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{10-r}\mathrm{C}_{10}^{r}\geq \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{9-r}\mathrm{C}_{10}^{r+1}\\ \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{10-r}\mathrm{C}_{10}^{r}\geq \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{11-r}\mathrm{C}_{10}^{r-1}\\ \end{array} \right.$ , $\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l}\frac{10!}{2r!(10-r)!}\geq \frac{10!}{(r+1)!(9-r)!}\\ \frac{10!}{r!(10-r)!}\geq \frac{10!}{2(r-1)!(11-r)!}\\ \end{array} \right.\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l}\frac{1}{2\times (10 - r)}\geq \frac{1}{r+1}\\ \frac{1}{r}\geq \frac{1}{2(11 - r)}\\ \end{array} \right.\Rightarrow \frac{19}{3}\leq r\leq \frac{22}{3}$ ,
> > 又因为 $r\in N^*$ , 故 $r=7$ , 所以第 8 项的系数绝对值最大,
> >
> > 故选 **C**
