> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left(x + \frac{y^2}{x}\right)(x - 2y)^4 = x\left(x - 2y\right)^4 + \frac{y^2}{x}\left(x - 2y\right)^4$ 因为 $\left(x - 2y\right)^4 = \left[x + (-2y)\right]^4 = C_4^0 x^4(-2y)^0 + C_4^1 x^3(-2y)^1 + C_4^2 x^2(-2y)^2 + C_4^3 x^1(-2y)^3 + C_4^4 x^0(-2y)^4 = x^4 - 8x^3 y + 24x^2 y^2 - 32xy^3 + 16y^4$ 所以 $\left(x + \frac{y^2}{x}\right)(x - 2y)^4$ 的展开式中 $x^2 y^3$ 项的为 $x \cdot (-32xy^3) + \frac{y^2}{x} \cdot (-8x^3 y) = -40x^2 y^3$ ，系数为-40。故选：B.
