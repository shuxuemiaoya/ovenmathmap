> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left(x-\frac{1}{x}+1\right)^{6}=\left[\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)+1\right]^{6}$ $=C_{6}^{0}\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)^{6}+C_{6}^{1}\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)^{5}+C_{6}^{2}\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)^{4}+C_{6}^{3}\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)^{3}$ $+C_{6}^{4}\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)^{2}+C_{6}^{5}\left(x-\frac{1}{x}\right)^{1}+C_{6}^{6},$ 可知含 $x^{4}$ 项的系数是 $C_{6}^{0}\cdot C_{6}^{1}\times(-1)+C_{6}^{2}\cdot C_{4}^{0}\times(-1)^{4}=9$ 。故选：C.
