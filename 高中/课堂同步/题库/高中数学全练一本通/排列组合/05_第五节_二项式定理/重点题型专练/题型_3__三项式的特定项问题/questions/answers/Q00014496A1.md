> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 12600
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\left(x^{2} + \frac{1}{x} + y^{3} + \frac{1}{y}\right)^{10} = \left[\left(x^{2} + \frac{1}{x}\right) + \left(y^{3} + \frac{1}{y}\right)\right]^{10}$ 的二项展开式 $T_{r+1} = C_{10}^{r}\left(x^{2} + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{10-r}\left(y^{3} + \frac{1}{y}\right)^{r}, r = 0, 1, \ldots, 10$ , $\left(x^{2} + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{10-r}$ 的二项展开式为 $T_{k+1}' = C_{10-r}^{k}\left(x^{2}\right)^{10-r-k}\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^{k} = C_{10-r}^{k}x^{20-2r-3k}, k = 0, 1, \ldots, 10 - r$ , $\left(y^{3} + \frac{1}{y}\right)^{m}$ 的二项展开式为 $T_{m+1}' = C_{r}^{m}\left(y^{3}\right)^{r-m}\left(\frac{1}{y}\right)^{m} = C_{r}^{m}y^{3r-4m}, m = 0, 1, \ldots, r$ ,
> >
> > 若展开式中的常数项满足，则可得 $\begin{cases}20 - 2r - 3k = 0 \\3r - 4m = 0 \\r, k, m \in N\end{cases}$ ，解得 $\begin{cases}r = 4 \\m = 3 \\k = 4\end{cases}$ 故常数项为： $C_{10}^{4} \cdot C_{6}^{4} \cdot C_{4}^{3} = 12600$ . 故答案为：12600.
