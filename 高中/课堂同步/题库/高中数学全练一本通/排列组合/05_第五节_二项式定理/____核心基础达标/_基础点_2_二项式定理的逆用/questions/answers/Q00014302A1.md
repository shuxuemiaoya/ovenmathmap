> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $x^4$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because 1 = C _ {4} ^ {0} = C _ {4} ^ {4}, 4 = C _ {4} ^ {1} = C _ {4} ^ {3}, 6 = C _ {4} ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \text {原式} = C _ {4} ^ {0} (x - 1) ^ {4} \cdot 1 ^ {0} + C _ {4} ^ {1} (x - 1) ^ {3} \cdot 1 ^ {1} + C _ {4} ^ {2} (x - 1) ^ {2} \cdot 1 ^ {2} + C _ {4} ^ {3} (x - 1) \cdot 1 ^ {3} + C _ {4} ^ {4} \cdot 1 ^ {4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \left[ (x - 1) + 1 \right] ^ {4} = x ^ {4}
> > $$
