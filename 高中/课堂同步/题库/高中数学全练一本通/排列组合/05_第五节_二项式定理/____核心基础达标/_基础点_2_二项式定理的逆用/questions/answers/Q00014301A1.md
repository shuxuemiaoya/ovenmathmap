> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $x^5 - 1$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 原式$$
> > = \mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {0} (x - 1) ^ {5} + \mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {1} (x - 1) ^ {4} + \mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {2} (x - 1) ^ {3} + \mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {3} (x - 1) ^ {2} + \mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {4} (x - 1) ^ {1} + \mathrm{C} _ {5} ^ {5} (x - 1) ^ {0} - 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = [ (x - 1) + 1 ] ^ {5} - 1 = x ^ {5} - 1
> > $$
