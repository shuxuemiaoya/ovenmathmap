>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $32x^{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 原式 $= C_5^0 (2x + 1)^5 -C_5^1 (2x + 1)^4 +C_5^2 (2x + 1)^3 -C_5^3 (2x + 1)^2 +C_5^4 (2x + 1) - C_5^5 (2x + 1)^0$ $= [(2x + 1) - 1]^{5} = (2x)^{5} = 32x^{5}$ .
