>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 证明: 因为
> >
> > $C_{n}^{k}\cdot C_{n - k}^{m - k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n - k)!}\cdot \frac{(n - k)!}{(m - k)!(n - m)!} = \frac{n!}{k!(m - k)!(n - m)!},$ $C_{n}^{m}\cdot C_{m}^{k} = \frac{n!}{m!(n - m)!}\cdot \frac{m!}{k!(m - k)!} = \frac{n!}{k!(n - m)!(m - k)!},$
> >
> > 所以 $C_{n}^{k} \cdot C_{n-k}^{m-k} = C_{n}^{m} \cdot C_{m}^{k}$ .
