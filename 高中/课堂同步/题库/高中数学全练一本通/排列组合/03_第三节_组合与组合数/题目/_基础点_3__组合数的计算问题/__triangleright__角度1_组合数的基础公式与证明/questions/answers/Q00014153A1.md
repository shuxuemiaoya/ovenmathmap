> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $C_{n}^{m}=\frac{n!}{m!(n-m)!}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {m + 1}{n - m} \cdot C _ {n} ^ {m + 1} = \frac {m + 1}{n - m} \cdot \frac {n !}{(m + 1) ! (n - m - 1) !} = \frac {m + 1}{(m + 1) !} \cdot \frac {n !}{(n - m) (n - m - 1) !} =
> > $$
> >
> > $\frac{n!}{m!(n-m)!}$ ，所以 $C_{n}^{m}=\frac{m+1}{n-m}\cdot C_{n}^{m+1}$
