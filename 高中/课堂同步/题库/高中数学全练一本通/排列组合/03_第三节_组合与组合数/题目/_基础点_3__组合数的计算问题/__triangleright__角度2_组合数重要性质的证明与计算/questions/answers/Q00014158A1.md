> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 左边 $= m \cdot \frac{n!}{m!(n - m)!} = \frac{m \cdot n!}{m(m - 1)!(n - m)!} = \frac{n!}{(m - 1)!(n - m)!}$ ；右边 $= n \cdot \frac{(n - 1)!}{(m - 1)![(n - 1) - (m - 1)]!} = \frac{n \cdot (n - 1)!}{(m - 1)!(n - m)!} = \frac{n!}{(m - 1)!(n - m)!}$ ；即左边 $=$ 右边，所以 $mC_{n}^{m} = nC_{n - 1}^{m - 1}(m,n\in \mathbf{N}_{+},n\geq 2)$ 。
