> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{5}{27}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{A_{8}^{5}+A_{8}^{4}}{A_{9}^{6}-A_{9}^{5}}=\frac{4A_{8}^{4}+A_{8}^{4}}{4A_{9}^{5}-A_{9}^{5}}=\frac{5A_{8}^{4}}{3A_{9}^{5}}=\frac{5A_{8}^{4}}{3\times9A_{8}^{4}}=\frac{5}{27}$ ；故答案为： $\frac{5}{27}$
