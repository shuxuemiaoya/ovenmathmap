> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \frac {A _ {n - 1} ^ {m - 1} \cdot A _ {n - m} ^ {n - m}}{A _ {n - 1} ^ {n - 1}} = \frac {(n - 1) !}{\lceil (n - 1) - (m - 1) \rceil !} \times (n - m)! \times \frac {1}{(n - 1) !} = \frac {(n - 1) !}{(n - m) !} \times (n - m)! \times \frac {1}{(n - 1) !} = 1
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为:1.
