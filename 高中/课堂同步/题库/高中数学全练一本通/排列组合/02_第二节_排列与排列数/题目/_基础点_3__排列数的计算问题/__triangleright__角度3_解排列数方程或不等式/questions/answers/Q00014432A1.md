> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\{3,4,5\}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $3A_{x}^{3} \leq 2A_{x+1}^{2} + 6A_{x}^{2}$ ，所以 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}3\times\frac{x!}{(x-3)!}\leq 2\times\frac{(x+1)!}{(x-1)!}+6\times\frac{x!}{(x-2)!},\\ x\geq 3,x\in N^{*}\end{array}\right.$ ，化简可得 $\left\{\begin{array}{l}\left(3x-2\right)(x-5)\leq 0\\ x\geq 3,x\in N^{*}\end{array}\right.$ ，解得 $x \in \{3,4,5\}$ ，所以不等式解集为 $\{3,4,5\}$ .
