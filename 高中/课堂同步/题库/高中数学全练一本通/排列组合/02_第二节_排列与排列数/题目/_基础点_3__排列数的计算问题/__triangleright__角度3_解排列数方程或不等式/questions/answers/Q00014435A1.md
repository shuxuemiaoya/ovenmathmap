> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 6
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because A_8^{m + 2} = \frac{8!}{(6 - m)!},A_8^m = \frac{8!}{(8 - m)!}$ $\therefore \frac{8!}{(6 - m)!} <  6\times \frac{8!}{(8 - m)!}$ $\therefore \frac{6}{(7 - m)(8 - m)} >1$ ，解得： $5 <   m <   10$ 又 $m + 2\leq 8$ ，即 $m\leq 6$ $\therefore 5 <   m\leq 6$ $\because m\in N^{*}$ $\therefore m = 6$
