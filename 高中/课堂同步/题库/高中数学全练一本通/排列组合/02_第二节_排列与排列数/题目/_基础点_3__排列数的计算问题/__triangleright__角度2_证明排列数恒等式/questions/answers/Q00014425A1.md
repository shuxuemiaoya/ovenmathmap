> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because A_{n+1}^{m}-A_{n}^{m}=\frac{(n+1)!}{(n+1-m)!}-\frac{n!}{(n-m)!}=\frac{n!}{(n-m)!}\cdot\left(\frac{n+1}{n+1-m}-1\right)$
> >
> > $= \frac{n!}{(n - m)!} \cdot \frac{m}{(n + 1 - m)} = m \cdot \frac{n!}{(n + 1 - m)!} = mA_{n}^{m-1}$ , $\therefore A_{n+1}^{m} - A_{n}^{m} = mA_{n}^{m-1}$ .
