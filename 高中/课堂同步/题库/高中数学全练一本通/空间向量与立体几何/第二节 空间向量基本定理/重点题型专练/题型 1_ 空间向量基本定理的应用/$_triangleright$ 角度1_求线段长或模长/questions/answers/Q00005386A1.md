> **【解析】**
> $\overline{BM} + \overline{MN} + \overline{ND}$ ; $\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$ . 解析: ∵矩形ABCD中, $AB = 1$ , $BC = \sqrt{3}$ , 则可得 $AM = \frac{1}{2}$ , $BM = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , $CN = \frac{1}{2}$ , $DN = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ , $MN = 1$ . 因为平面ABC与平面ACD垂直, $BM \perp AC$ , 平面 $ABC \cap$ 平面 $ACD = AC$ , 则 $BM \perp$ 平面 $ACD$ . 所以 $BM \perp ND$ .

由题可得 $\overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{BM} + \overrightarrow{MN} + \overrightarrow{ND}$ ,
所以 $\left|\overrightarrow{BD}\right|^{2}=\left(\overrightarrow{BM}+\overrightarrow{MN}+\overrightarrow{ND}\right)^{2}$ $\left|\overrightarrow{BM}\right|^{2}+\left|\overrightarrow{MN}\right|^{2}+\left|\overrightarrow{ND}\right|^{2}+2\left(\overrightarrow{BM}\cdot\overrightarrow{MN}+\overrightarrow{MN}\cdot\overrightarrow{ND}+\overrightarrow{BM}\cdot\overrightarrow{ND}\right)$ $\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^{2}+1^{2}+\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^{2}+2\times(0+0+0)=\frac{5}{2}$ 所以 $\left|\overrightarrow{BD}\right|=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$ . 故答案为： $\overrightarrow{BM}+\overrightarrow{MN}+\overrightarrow{ND}$ ; $\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$ .
