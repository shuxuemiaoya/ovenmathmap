> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\left[\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\sqrt{2}\right].$
>
> **【解析】**
> 由已知 $E$ 为棱 $B_{1}C_{1}$ 上的动点，设 $\overline{B_1E} = \lambda \overline{B_1C_1}(0\leq \lambda \leq 1).$ 因为 $\overrightarrow{AE} = \overrightarrow{AB_1} +\overrightarrow{B_1E} = \overrightarrow{AB_1} +\overrightarrow{\lambda B_1C_1} = \overrightarrow{AB} +\overrightarrow{BB_1} +\overrightarrow{\lambda B_1C_1}$ 所以 $\overrightarrow{AE}\cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = (\overrightarrow{AB} +\overrightarrow{BB_1} +\overrightarrow{\lambda B_1C_1})\cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AB}\cdot \overrightarrow{AC} +\overrightarrow{BB_1}\cdot \overrightarrow{AC} +\overrightarrow{\lambda B_1C_1}\cdot \overrightarrow{AC}\\ = 1\times \sqrt{2}\times \cos 45^{\circ} + \lambda \times 1\times \sqrt{2}\times \cos 45^{\circ} = 1 + \lambda$ 所以向量 $\overline{AE}$ 在向量 $\overline{AC}$ 方向上投影的数量为 $\frac{1 + \lambda}{\sqrt{2}}$ 又 $0\leqslant \lambda \leqslant 1,\therefore 1\leqslant 1 + \lambda \leqslant 2,\therefore \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\leqslant \frac{1 + \lambda}{\sqrt{2}}\leqslant \sqrt{2}$ 所以向量 $\overline{AE}$ 在向量 $\overline{AC}$ 方向上投影的数量的取值范围为 $\left[\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\sqrt{2}\right]$
