> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） \(\overline{\mathrm{{DM}}} = \vec{a} +\frac {1}{2}\vec {c},\) ， \(\overline{\mathrm{{A\_1C}}} = \vec {a} +\vec {b}-\vec {c},\) (2） \(|\overline{\mathrm{{DM}}}| = \sqrt {3};\quad |\overline{\mathrm{{A\_1C}}} | = \sqrt {2};\quad \frac {\sqrt {6}} {6}\)
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）由题意可得： \(\overline{\mathrm{{DM}}} = \overline{\mathrm{{DC}}} +\overline{\mathrm{{CM}}} = \overline{\mathrm{{DC}}} +\frac {1}{2}\overline{\mathrm{{CC\_1}}} = \vec {a} +\frac {1}{2}\vec {c},\)
> \(\overline{\mathrm{{A\_1C}}} = \overline{\mathrm{{A\_1B\_1}}} +\overline{\mathrm{{B\_1B}}} +\overline{\mathrm{{BC}}} = \vec {a} -\vec {c} +\vec {b} = \vec {a} +\vec {b}-\vec {c}.\)
> （2）由题意可得： \(\vec {a}\cdot \vec {b} = 0,\vec {a}\cdot \vec {c} = \vec {b}\cdot \vec {c} = 1\times 2\times \frac {1}{2} = 1,\)
> 因为 \(|\overline{\mathrm{{DM}}}| = \sqrt{\left(\vec {a} + \frac {1}{2}\vec {c}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\vec {a^2} + \vec {a}\cdot\vec {c} + \frac {1}{4}\vec {c^2}} = \sqrt {1 + 1 + \frac {1}{4}\times 4} = \sqrt {3},\)
> \(|\overline{\mathrm{{A\_1C}}} | = \sqrt{(a + b - c)^2} = \sqrt{\vec {a^2} + b^2 + c^2 + 2a\cdot b - 2a\cdot c - 2b\cdot c}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {}\\ {} \\ （2）因异面直线夹角为锐角或直角，所以异面直线DM与 \(A\_{1}C\) 所成角的余弦值 \(\frac {\sqrt {6}} {6}.\)
