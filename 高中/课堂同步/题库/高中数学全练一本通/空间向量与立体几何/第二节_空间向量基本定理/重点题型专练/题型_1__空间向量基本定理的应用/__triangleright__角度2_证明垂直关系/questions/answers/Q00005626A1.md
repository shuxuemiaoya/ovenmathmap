> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）因为E是AB的中点，F在OC上，且 \(\overline{\mathrm{{OF}}} = 2\overline{\mathrm{{FC}}}\)
> 所以 \(\overline{\mathrm{{OE}}} = \frac{1}{2} (\overline{\mathrm{{OA}}} +\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}}),\overline{\mathrm{{OF}}} = \frac{2}{3}\overline{\mathrm{{OC}}},\)
> 于是 \(\overline{\mathrm{{EF}}} = \overline{\mathrm{{OF}}} -\overline{\mathrm{{OE}}} = \frac{2}{3}\overline{\mathrm{{OC}}} -\frac{1}{2} (\overline{\mathrm{{OA}}} +\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}}) = -\frac{1}{2}\overline{\mathrm{{OA}}} -\frac{1}{2}\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}} +\frac{2}{3}\overline{\mathrm{{OC}}}.\)
> （2）由（1）得 \(\overline{\mathrm{{OE}}} = \frac{1}{2} (\overline{\mathrm{{OA}}} +\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}}),\overline{\mathrm{{BF}}} = \overline{\mathrm{{OF}}} -\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}} = \frac{2\cdot\overline{\mathrm{{OC}}} - \overline{\mathrm{{OB}}}}{3},\)
> 因此 \(|\overline{\mathrm{{OE}}} | = \frac{1}{2} |\overline{\mathrm{{OA}}} +\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}}| = \frac{1}{2}\times \sqrt{4 + 4 + 2\times 2\times 2\times 2} = \sqrt {5},\)
> \(|\overline{\mathrm{{BF}}} | = \left|\frac {2}{3}\overline{\mathrm{{OC}}} -\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}}\right| = \sqrt {\frac {4}{9}\times 4 + 4 - \frac {4}{3}\times 2\times 2\times 2} = \frac {2\sqrt {7}} {3},\)
> 又因为 \(\overline{\mathrm{{OE}}}\cdot \overline{\mathrm{{BF}}} = \frac{1}{2} (\overline{\mathrm{{OA}}} +\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}})\cdot \left(\frac {2}{3}\overline{\mathrm{{OC}}} -\overline{\mathrm{{OB}}}\right) = -\frac {5}{3},\)
> 所以向量 \(\overline{\mathrm{{OE}}}\) 与向量 \(\overline{\mathrm{{BF}}}\) 所成角的余弦值为
> \(= \frac{\overline{\mathrm{{OE}}}\cdot\overline{\mathrm{{BF}}}}{\left|\overline{\mathrm{{OE}}}\right|\left|\overline{\mathrm{{BF}}}\right|} = \frac{-\frac {5}{3}}{\sqrt {3}\times\frac {2\sqrt {7}} {3}} = -\frac {5\sqrt {21}} {42}.\)
