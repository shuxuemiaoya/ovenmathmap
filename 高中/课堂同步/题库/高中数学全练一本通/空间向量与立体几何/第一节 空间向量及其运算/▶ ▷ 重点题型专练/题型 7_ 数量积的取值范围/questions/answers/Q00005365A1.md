> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\left[\frac{1}{4},\frac{3}{4}\right]$
解析:如图所示,正四面体 D-ABC 的棱长是 1,E 是 AB 的中点,点 F 是线段 DC 上的动点（包含端点）,
![](../../../../../images/answers/538770c6c875a6c32591d1f0b911479d0ab7070e258259a423e7665006705a24.jpg)
所以 $\overrightarrow{EC} = \overrightarrow{AC} -\overrightarrow{AE} = \overrightarrow{AC} -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$ 又 $F,C,D$ 三点共线，所以 $\overrightarrow{AF} = \lambda \overrightarrow{AC} +(1 - \lambda)\overrightarrow{AD}\big(0\leq \lambda \leq 1\big)$ $\overline{EC}\cdot \overline{AF} = \left(\overline{AC} -\frac{1}{2}\overline{AB}\right)\cdot \left[\lambda \overline{AC} +(1 - \lambda)\overline{AD}\right]$ $= \lambda \overline{AC} ^2 +(1 - \lambda)\overline{AC}\cdot \overline{AD} -\frac{1}{2}\lambda \overline{AB}\cdot \overline{AC} -\frac{1}{2}(1 - \lambda)\overline{AB}\cdot \overline{AD}$ $= \lambda +(1 - \lambda)\cos 60^{\circ} - \frac{1}{2}\lambda \cos 60^{\circ} - \frac{1}{2}(1 - \lambda)\cos 60^{\circ}$ $= \frac{1}{2}\lambda +\frac{1}{4}$ ，则 $\overline{EC}\cdot \overline{AF}$ 的取值范围为 $\left[\frac{1}{4},\frac{3}{4}\right]$ .故答案为： $\left[\frac{1}{4},\frac{3}{4}\right].$
