> **【解析】**
> 证明见解析
解析：证明：因为 M 在 BD 上，且 $BM=\frac{1}{3}BD$ ，
所以 $\overline{MB}=\frac{1}{3}\overline{DB}=\frac{1}{3}\overline{DA}+\frac{1}{3}\overline{AB}.$ 同理 $\overline{AN}=\frac{1}{3}\overline{AD}+\frac{1}{3}\overline{DE}.$ 又 $\overline{CD}=\overline{BA}=-\overline{AB}$ ，
所以 $\overline{MN}=\overline{MB}+\overline{BA}+\overline{AN}=\left(\frac{1}{3}\overline{DA}+\frac{1}{3}\overline{AB}\right)+\overline{BA}+\left(\frac{1}{3}\overline{AD}+\frac{1}{3}\overline{DE}\right),$ $=\frac{\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\underline{\underline{\text{}2}}\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar {\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{\bar{-}}}^}}}}}}}}}}}}) } - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - }$ 又 CD 与 DE 不共线，所以 MN，CD，DE 共面。因为 MN 不在平面 CDE 内，所以 MN // 平面 CDE。
