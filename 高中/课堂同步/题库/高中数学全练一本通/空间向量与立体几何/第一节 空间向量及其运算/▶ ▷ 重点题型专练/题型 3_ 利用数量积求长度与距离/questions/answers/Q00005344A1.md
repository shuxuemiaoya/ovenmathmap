> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$ 解析：设 $AO = \vec{a}$ ， $AB = b$ ， $AC = c$ ， $\overline {DE} = DA + AB + BE = \frac {1}{2}OA + AB + \frac {1}{2}(AC - AB)$ $= -\frac {1}{2}\overline {AO} +\frac {1}{2}\overline {AB} +\frac {1}{2}\overline {AC}= -\frac {1}{2}\bar {a} +\frac {1}{2}\bar {b} +\frac {1}{2}\bar {c},$ $\left|\overline {DE}\right|=\sqrt{\left(-\frac {1}{2}\bar {a}+\frac {1}{2}\bar {b}+\frac {1}{2}\bar {c}\right)^2}= \frac {1}{2}\sqrt {(-\bar {a}+\bar {b}+\bar {c})^2}$ $= \frac {1}{2}\sqrt {\bar {a} ^ {2}+\bar {b} ^ {2}+\bar {c} ^ {2}- 2\bar {a} \cdot\bar {b}- 2\bar {a} \cdot\bar {c}+ 2\bar {b}\cdot\bar {c}}$ $= \frac {1}{2}\sqrt {1+1+9-2\times 1\times\cos60^\circ-2\times 1\times3\times\cos60^\circ+2\times 1\times3\times\cos60^\circ}$ $= \frac {1}{2}\sqrt {11-1-3+3}= \frac {\sqrt {10}}{2}$ ∴ $DE=\frac {\sqrt {10}}{2}$ 故答案为： $\frac {\sqrt {10}}{2}$
