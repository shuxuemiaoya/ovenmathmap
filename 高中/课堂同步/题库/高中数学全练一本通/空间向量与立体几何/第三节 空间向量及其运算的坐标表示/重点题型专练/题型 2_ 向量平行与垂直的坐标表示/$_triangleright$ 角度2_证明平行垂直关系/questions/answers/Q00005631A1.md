> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> （1）证明见解析（2）证明见解析
$A(0,0,0)$ 、 $B(1,0,0)$ 、 $C(1,1,0)$ 、 $D(0,1,0)$ 、 $A_{1}(0,0,1)$ 、 $B_{1}(1,0,1)$ 、 $C_{1}(1,1,1)$ 、 $D_{1}(0,1,1)$ ，由中点性质得 $E\left(1,1,\frac{1}{2}\right)$ 、 $F\left(1,\frac{1}{2},0\right)$ ， $G\left(\frac{1}{2},1,0\right)$ ， $H\left(\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2},1\right)$ .
$$
\overrightarrow {A B _ {1}} = (1, 0, 1), \overrightarrow {G E} = \left(\frac {1}{2}, 0, \frac {1}{2}\right), \overrightarrow {E H} = \left(- \frac {1}{2}, - \frac {1}{2}, \frac {1}{2}\right)
$$
$$
\overrightarrow {A B _ {1}} = 2 \overrightarrow {G E}, \overrightarrow {A B _ {1}} \cdot \overrightarrow {E H} = 1 \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) + 1 \times \frac {1}{2} = 0
$$
所以 $\overrightarrow{AB_1} // \overrightarrow{GE}$ , $\overrightarrow{AB_1} \perp \overrightarrow{EH}$ ,
$$
A B _ {1} / / G E, A B _ {1} \perp E H
$$
（2）因为 $\overrightarrow{A_1G} = \left(\frac{1}{2}, 1, -1\right)$ , $\overrightarrow{DF} = \left(1, -\frac{1}{2}, 0\right)$ , $\overrightarrow{DE} = \left(1, 0, \frac{1}{2}\right)$ ,
$$
\therefore \overrightarrow {A _ {1} G} \cdot \overrightarrow {D F} = \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{2} + 0 = 0
$$
$$
\overrightarrow {A _ {1} G} \cdot \overrightarrow {D E} = \frac {1}{2} + 0 - \frac {1}{2} = 0,
$$
$\therefore A_{1}G \perp DF, A_{1}G \perp DE.$
又 $DF \cap DE = D$ ，所以 $A_{1}G \perp$ 平面 $EFD$ .
