> **【解析】**
> $$
\overrightarrow {A B} = (0, 3, 2), \overrightarrow {B C} = (- 2, 0, 2)
$$
$$
\frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {B C} = (- 1, 0, 1)
$$
$$
\overrightarrow {A B} - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {B C} = (1, 3, 1)
$$
