> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 1
>
> **【解析】**
> ∵ $A(2,3,1), B(-1,1,-1), C(0,-1,1), D(1,1,x)$ ,
> ∴ $\overrightarrow{AB}=(-3,-2,-2)$ , $\overrightarrow{AC}=(-2,-4,0)$ , $\overrightarrow{AD}=(-1,-2,x-1)$ ,
> 又∵A,B,C,D四点共面，
> ∴由平面向量基本定理可知存在实数 $\lambda,\mu$ 使 $\overrightarrow{AD}=\lambda\overrightarrow{AB}+\mu\overrightarrow{AC}$ 成立，$\therefore (-1, -2, x - 1) = \lambda (-3, -2, -2) + \mu (-2, -4, 0)$ , $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} -1 = -3\lambda - 2\mu \\ -2 = -2\lambda - 4\mu \\ x - 1 = -2\lambda \end{array} \right.$ , 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 1 \\ \lambda = 0 \\ \mu = \frac{1}{2} \end{array} \right.$ , 故答案为: 1
