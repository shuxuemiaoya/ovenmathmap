> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> B (x, y, z)
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A B} = (x - 1, y - 1, z) = (6, 0, 2)
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{c} x - 1 = 6 \\ y - 1 = 0 \\ z = 2 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> : x = 7, y = 1, z = 2
> $$
> $$
> D (x, y, z)
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A B} = (2, - 5, 1) - (4, 1, 3) = (- 2, - 6, - 2), \overrightarrow {D C} = (3 - x, 7 - y, - 5 - z)
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A B} = \overrightarrow {D C}
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3 - x = - 2 \\ 7 - y = - 6 \\ - 5 - z = - 2 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> (5, 1 3, - 3)
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 5 \\ y = 1 3 \\ z = - 3 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> D (x, y, z)
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A D} = (x, y - 1, z), \overrightarrow {A B} = (3, 1, 2)
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 6 \\ y - 1 = 2 \\ z = 4 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {A D} = 2 \overrightarrow {A B}
> $$
> $$
> D (6, 3, 4)
> $$
> 解析: 设点 $C(x,y,z)$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{AC}=(x-2,y,z-1)$ ， $\overrightarrow{BC}=(x-10,y-4,z-13)$ ，
> 因为 $\overrightarrow{AC} = -\overrightarrow{BC}$ 所以 $(x - 2, y, z - 1) = -(x - 10, y - 4, z - 13)$ ,
> 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x-2=10-x\\ y=4-y\\ z-1=13-z\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得：x=6,y=2,z=7，所以C的坐标为(6,2,7).
> 故答案为:(6,2,7)
