> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\vec{n}=\left(\sqrt{2},-1,-1\right)$ 解析：因为 CD⊥平面 ABD, CD⊥BD，所以过 D 作平面 BC轴, 以 D 为原点, DB, DC 分别为 x 轴, y 轴建立如图所示空间直角坐标系.
设 AD = 1，则 BC = 3, AB = a, $BD^{2} + CD^{2} = 9$ , $BD^{2} = a^{2} + 1$ , $CD^{2} = a^{2} + 4$ ,
解得 $a=\sqrt{2}$ ， $A\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3},0,\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\right),C(0,\sqrt{6},0),E\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2},0\right)$
![](../../../../../images/answers/cdece12d08a7168f12513ac6521c137d6f2e1200e2813fab74056789743e339b.jpg)
$$
\overrightarrow {D A} = \left(\frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}, 0, \frac {\sqrt {6}}{3}\right), \overrightarrow {D E} = \left(\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}, \frac {\sqrt {6}}{2}, 0\right)
$$
设平面 $DAE$ 的法向量 $\vec{n} = (x,y,z)$ ，则有 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \vec{n} \cdot \overrightarrow{DA} = 0 \\ \vec{n} \cdot \overrightarrow{DE} = 0 \end{array} \right.$ ， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} x + \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} z = 0 \\ \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} x + \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2} y = 0 \end{array} \right.$ 故可得 $\vec{n} = (\sqrt{2}, -1, -1)$ .
