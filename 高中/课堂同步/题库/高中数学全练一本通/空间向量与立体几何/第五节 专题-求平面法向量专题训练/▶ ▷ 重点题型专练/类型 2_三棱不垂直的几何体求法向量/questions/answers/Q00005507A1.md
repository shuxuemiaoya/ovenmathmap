> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\vec{n}=\left(1,1,\sqrt{2}\right)$
解析: 取 BC 中点 E, 连接 AE, DE, 如图所示:
![](../../../../../images/answers/6cc0b02041bbcee082c61f61818fa98c45913deedf37c9f47857de6ebcfa3b69.jpg)
$\because \triangle ABC$ 是正三角形， $\therefore AB = AC, E$ 为 $BC$ 中点， $\therefore AE \perp BC$ ， $\because BD = CD, E$ 为 $BC$ 中点， $\therefore DE \perp BC$ ， $\because AE \cap DE = E, AE \subset$ 平面 $ADE, DE \subset$ 平面 $ADE$ ， $\therefore BC \perp$ 平面 $ADE$ ， $\because DE \perp BC$ ，
以 $E$ 为原点， $EC$ 方向为 $x$ 轴， $ED$ 方向为 $y$ 轴，过 $E$ 作垂直于平面 $BCD$ 的线为 $z$ 轴建立如图所示直角坐标系，
![](../../../../../images/answers/8649dbc75be2d05549e21d8bbf5ac9804f4de7783057b5b6afa5c507b40a1e1b.jpg)
∵ $AD = \sqrt{3}$ , BD = CD = 1,
∴ AB = AC = BC = $\sqrt{2}$ , $AE = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$ , $DE = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 则 $B\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0,0\right),C\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0,0\right),D\left(0,\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)$ ,
不妨设 $A(0,y,z)$ ,
∵ $AD = \sqrt{3}$ , $AC = \sqrt{2}$ ,
∴ $\left\{\begin{aligned}&\left(y-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^{2}+z^{2}=3\\&\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}=2\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y&=-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\\ z&=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ∴ $A\left(0,-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},1\right)$ 设平面 ACD 法向量为 $\vec{n}=(x,y,z)$ ,
∵ $\overrightarrow{CD}=\left(-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},0\right)\overrightarrow{AD}=\left(0,\sqrt{2},-1\right)$ ∴ $\left\{\begin{aligned}\vec{n}\cdot\overrightarrow{CD}&=0\\ \vec{n}\cdot\overrightarrow{AD}&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}y&=0\\ \sqrt{2}y-z&=0\end{aligned}\right.$ 取 y=1，则 x=1， $z=\sqrt{2}$ 则 $\vec{n}=\left(1,1,\sqrt{2}\right)$ .
