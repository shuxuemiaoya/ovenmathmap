> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\sqrt{30}}{3}$ 解析:依题意, $\overrightarrow{BA}=(1,1,2),\overrightarrow{BC}=(-2,2,4)$ , 则 $|\overrightarrow{BA}|=\sqrt{6}$ , $\frac{\overline{BC}}{|B\overline{C}|}=(-\frac{\sqrt{6}}{6},\frac{\sqrt{6}}{6},\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3})$ ,
所以 $AD=\sqrt{\left|\overrightarrow{BA}\right|^{2}-\left(\overrightarrow{BA}\cdot\frac{\overline{BC}}{\left|\overline{BC}\right|}\right)^{2}}=\sqrt{6-\left(\frac{2\sqrt{6}}{3}\right)^{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{30}}{3}$ .
