> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{\log_{2}e}{e}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\log_{2}x-k\cdot2^{kx}\geqslant0\Rightarrow\log_{2}x\geqslant k\cdot2^{kx}$ $\Rightarrow x\cdot\log_{2}x\geq kx\cdot2^{kx}\Rightarrow2^{\log_{2}x}\cdot\log_{2}x\geq kx\cdot2^{kx}$ 即 $\log_{2}x\geq kx\Rightarrow k\leq\left(\frac{\log_{2}x}{x}\right)_{\min}=\frac{\log_{2}e}{e}$
