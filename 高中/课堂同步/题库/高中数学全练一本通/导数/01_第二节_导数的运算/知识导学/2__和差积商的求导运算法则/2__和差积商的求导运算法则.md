## 2. 和差积商的求导运算法则

(1) $[f(x)\pm \mathrm{g}(x)]^{\prime} = f^{\prime}(x)\pm \mathrm{g}^{\prime}(x)$ ; (2) $[f(x)\cdot \mathrm{g}(x)]^{\prime} = f^{\prime}(x)\mathrm{g}(x) + f(x)\mathrm{g}^{\prime}(x)$ ;

(3) $\left[\frac{f(x)}{\mathrm{g}(x)}\right]' = \frac{f'(x)\mathrm{g}(x) - f(x)\mathrm{g}'(x)}{\mathrm{g}^2(x)};$ (4） $[cf(x)]' = cf'(x).$

运算法则的推广:

加减法推广: $\left[u(x)\pm v(x)\pm\cdots\pm w(x)\right]'=u'(x)\pm v'(x)\pm\cdots\pm w'(x)$

乘法推广： $[u(x)v(x)\bullet \dots \bullet w(x)]' = u'(x)v(x)\bullet \dots \bullet w(x) + u(x)v'(x)\bullet \dots \bullet w(x) + u(x)v(x)\bullet \dots \bullet w'(x)$
