> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** （1） $99(x+1)^{98}$ ；（2） $\frac{x+1}{(2x+1)\sqrt{2x+1}}$ ;
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (3) $2\sin (2x + 5) + (4x - 6)\cos (2x + 5)$ (4) $\frac{-3x\sin(3x - 2) - \cos(3x - 2)}{2x^2}$ ;
> >
> > (5) $6(3x+1)\ln(3x)+\frac{(3x+1)^{2}}{x}$
> >
> > (6) $y' = -2\mathrm{e}^{-2x}\sin \left(5x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) + 5\mathrm{e}^{-2x}\cos \left(5x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$
> >
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $y = (x + 1)^{99}$ ， $y' = 99(x + 1)^{98}$ .
> >
> > (2) $\left(\sqrt{2x + 1}\right)' = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2x + 1}} \cdot 2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2x + 1}}$
> >
> > $$
> > y = \frac {x}{\sqrt {2 x + 1}}, y ^ {\prime} = \frac {\sqrt {2 x + 1} - \frac {x}{\sqrt {2 x + 1}}}{2 x + 1} = \frac {2 x + 1 - x}{(2 x + 1) \sqrt {2 x + 1}} = \frac {x + 1}{(2 x + 1) \sqrt {2 x + 1}}.
> > $$
> >
> > (3) $y = (2x - 3)\sin (2x + 5)$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > y ^ {\prime} = 2 \sin (2 x + 5) + (2 x - 3) \times 2 \cos (2 x + 5)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \sin (2 x + 5) + (4 x - 6) \cos (2 x + 5).
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y = \frac {\cos (3 x - 2)}{2 x}
> > $$
> >
> > $y^{\prime} = \frac{-3\sin(3x - 2)\cdot 2x - 2\cos(3x - 2)}{4x^{2}} = \frac{-3x\sin(3x - 2) - \cos(3x - 2)}{2x^{2}}.$
> >
> > (5) $y=\left(3x+1\right)^{2}\ln\left(3x\right)$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > y ^ {\prime} = 2 (3 x + 1) \times 3 \times \ln (3 x) + (3 x + 1) ^ {2} \cdot \frac {1}{3 x} \times 3 = 6 (3 x + 1) \ln (3 x) + \frac {(3 x + 1) ^ {2}}{x}.
> > $$
> >
> > (6) $y' = (\mathrm{e}^{-2x})' \sin (5x + \frac{\pi}{4}) + \mathrm{e}^{-2x} [\sin (5x + \frac{\pi}{4})]'$
> >
> > $$
> > = - 2 \mathrm{e} ^ {- 2 x} \sin (5 x + \frac {\pi}{4}) + 5 \mathrm{e} ^ {- 2 x} \cos (5 x + \frac {\pi}{4})
> > $$
