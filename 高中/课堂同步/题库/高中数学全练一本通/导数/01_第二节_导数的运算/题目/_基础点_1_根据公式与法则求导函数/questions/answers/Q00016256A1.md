> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left(\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}^x} + 2x\right)\cos x - \left(\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}^x} + x^2\right)\sin x$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $F(x)=f(x)+g(x)=\frac{\sin x}{\mathrm{e}^{x}}+3+x^{2}\cos x$
> >
> > $$
> > F ^ {\prime} (x) = \left(\frac {\sin x}{\mathrm{e} ^ {x}}\right) ^ {\prime} + \left(x ^ {2} \cos x\right) ^ {\prime} = \frac {\cos x \mathrm{e} ^ {x} - \sin x \mathrm{e} ^ {x}}{\left(\mathrm{e} ^ {x}\right) ^ {2}} + 2 x \cos x - x ^ {2} \sin x
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\cos x - \sin x}{\mathrm{e} ^ {x}} + 2 x \cos x - x ^ {2} \sin x = \left(\frac {1}{\mathrm{e} ^ {x}} + 2 x\right) \cos x - \left(\frac {1}{\mathrm{e} ^ {x}} + x ^ {2}\right) \sin x.
> > $$
