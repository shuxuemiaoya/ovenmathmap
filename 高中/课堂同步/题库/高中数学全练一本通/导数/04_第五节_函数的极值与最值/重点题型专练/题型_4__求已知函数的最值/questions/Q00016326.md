【25】求下列函数的最值：

(1) $f(x) = 3x - x^3\left(-\sqrt{3}\leq x\leq 3\right);$

(2) $f(x) = x + \sqrt{1 - x^2}$ ;

(3) $f(x) = \sin 2x - x\left(-\frac{\pi}{2} \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ;

(4) $f(x) = x^4 - \ln x^4, x \in \left[-\mathrm{e}, -\frac{1}{\mathrm{e}}\right]$ .
