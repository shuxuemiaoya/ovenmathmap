> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(-4,0]\cup[2,3)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 函数 $y = f(x)$ 是偶函数, $\therefore f(-3) = f(3) = 0$ ∴由图可知:
> > 当 $x \in [-3, 3]$ 时, $f(x) \leq 0$ , $\therefore x \in [-4, 2]$ 时, $f(x + 1) \leq 0$ ,
> > 当 $x \in (-4, -3] \cup [3, 4)$ 时, $f(x) \geq 0$ , $\therefore x \in (-5, -4] \cup [2, 3)$ 时, $f(x+1) \geq 0$ ,
> > 当 $x \in (-4, 0]$ 时, $f'(x) \leq 0$ ; 当 $x \in [0, 4)$ 时, $f'(x) \geq 0$ , $\because f(x+1)\cdot f'(x)\geq0,\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}f(x+1)\geq0\\ f'(x)\geq0\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}f(x+1)\leq0\\ f'(x)\leq0\end{aligned}\right.$ ,
> > 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x\in(-5,-4]\cup[2,3]\\ x\in[0,4)\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x\in[-4,2]\\ x\in(-4,0]\end{aligned}\right.$ , $\therefore x\in[2,3)$ 或 $x\in(-4,0]$ . 故答案为: $(-4,0] \cup [2,3)$ .
