> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解析：由题意，可得 $\overline{x}=\frac{70\times50+80-60+70-90}{50}=70$ ，
> > 设收集的48个准确数据分别记为 $x_{1},x_{2},\cdots,x_{48}$ ，
> > 则 $75=\frac{1}{50}\left[\left(x_{1}-70\right)^{2}+\left(x_{2}-70\right)^{2}+\cdots+\left(x_{48}-70\right)^{2}+(60-70)^{2}+(90-70)^{2}\right]$ $=\frac{1}{50}\left[\left(x_{1}-70\right)^{2}+\left(x_{2}-70\right)^{2}+\cdots+\left(x_{48}-70\right)^{2}+500\right]$ , $s^{2}=\frac{1}{50}\left[\left(x_{1}-70\right)^{2}+\left(x_{2}-70\right)^{2}+\cdots+\left(x_{48}-70\right)^{2}+(80-70)^{2}+(70-70)^{2}\right]$ $=\frac{1}{50}\left[\left(x_{1}-70\right)^{2}+\left(x_{2}-70\right)^{2}+\cdots+\left(x_{48}-70\right)^{2}+100\right]<75$ ，所以 $s^{2}<75$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
