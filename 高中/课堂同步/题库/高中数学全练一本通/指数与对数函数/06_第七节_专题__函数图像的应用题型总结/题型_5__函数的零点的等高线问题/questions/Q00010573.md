A. 0 < m < 2
B. $x_{1} + x_{2} = x_{1}x_{2}$ C. $x_{3} + x_{4} = 6$ D. $\left(\frac{1}{x_{1}}+\frac{1}{x_{2}}\right)(x_{3}+x_{4})=8$
