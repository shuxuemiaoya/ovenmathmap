> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left(0,\frac{1}{3}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 $x_{1} \neq x_{2}$ 时, $\frac{f(x_{1}) - f(x_{2})}{x_{1} - x_{2}} < 0$ ,
> > $\therefore f(x)$ 是 R 上的减函数,
> > $$
> > \because f (x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \left(1 - 2 a\right) ^ {x}, x \leq 1 \\ \log_ {a} x + \frac {1}{3}, x > 1 \end{array} , \right.
> > $$
> > $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l}0 <   1 - 2a <   1\\ 1 - 2a\geq \frac{1}{3}\\ 0 <   a <   1 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $0 <   a\leq \frac{1}{3}$
> > 因此实数 a 的取值范围是 $\left(0, \frac{1}{3}\right]$ ,
> > 故答案为: $\left(0,\frac{1}{3}\right]$ .
