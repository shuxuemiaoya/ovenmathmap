> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 证明见解析.
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 本题未单列分析。
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\log_{a_{1}}b_{1}=\log_{a_{2}}b_{2}=\cdots=\log_{a_{n}}b_{n}=\lambda$
> > 故可得 $\frac{\lg b_{1}}{\lg a_{1}}=\frac{\lg b_{2}}{\lg a_{2}}=\cdots=\frac{\lg b_{n}}{\lg a_{n}}=\lambda$
> > $$
> > \lg b _ {1} = \lambda \lg a _ {1} = \lg a _ {1} ^ {\lambda}
> > $$
> > $$
> > b _ {1} = a _ {1} ^ {\lambda}
> > $$
> > 同理可得 $b_{2}=a_{2}^{\lambda},\cdots,b_{n}=a_{n}^{\lambda}$
> > 故可得 $b_{1}b_{2}\cdots b_{n}=\left(a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}\right)^{\lambda}$
> > 故 $\log_{a_{1}a_{2}\cdots a_{n}}(b_{1}b_{2}\cdots b_{n})=\lambda$ ，即证.
