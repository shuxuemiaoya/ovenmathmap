> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2} a + b$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\left(\frac{1}{7}\right)^{a}=\frac{1}{3}$ ，得 $a=\log_{\frac{1}{7}}\frac{1}{3}=\log_{7}3$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \log_ {4 9} 4 8 = \frac {1}{2} \log_ {7} 4 8 = \frac {1}{2} \left(\log_ {7} 3 + \log_ {7} 1 6\right) = \frac {1}{2} \left(\log_ {7} 3 + 2 \log_ {7} 4\right) = \frac {1}{2} a + b.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为: $\frac{1}{2}a+b$ .
