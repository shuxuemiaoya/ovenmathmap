>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** -1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because 2^x = 24^y = 3$ ， $\therefore x = \log_23$ ， $y = \log_{24}3$ ， $\therefore \frac{1}{x} = \log_32$ ， $\frac{1}{y} = \log_324$ ， $\therefore \frac{3y - x}{xy} = \frac{3}{x} - \frac{1}{y} = 3\log_32 - \log_324 = \log_38 - \log_324 = \log_3\frac{1}{3} = -1$ 。故答案为：-1
