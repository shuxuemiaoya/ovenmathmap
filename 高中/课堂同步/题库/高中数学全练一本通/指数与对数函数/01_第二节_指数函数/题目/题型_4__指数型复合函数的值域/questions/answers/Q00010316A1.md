> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left(0,\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right]\cup \left[\sqrt{2}, + \infty\right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当 x > 0 时 $\frac{x}{4} + \frac{1}{4x} \geq 2\sqrt{\frac{x}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{4x}} = \frac{1}{2}$ ，当且仅当 $\frac{x}{4} = \frac{1}{4x}$ ，即 x = 1 时取等号，
> >
> > $$
> > x <   0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {x}{4} + \frac {1}{4 x} = - \left[ \left(- \frac {x}{4}\right) + \frac {1}{- 4 x} \right] \leq 2 \sqrt {\left(- \frac {x}{4}\right) \cdot \frac {1}{- 4 x}} = - \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > - \frac {x}{4} = \frac {1}{- 4 x}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > x = - 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > t = \frac {x}{4} + \frac {1}{4 x}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > t \in \left(- \infty , - \frac {1}{2} \right] \cup \left[ \frac {1}{2}, + \infty\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {x}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}, f \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) = \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y = \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {t}, t \in \left(- \infty , - \frac {1}{2} \right] \cup \left[ \frac {1}{2}, + \infty\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y \in \left(0, \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \right] \cup \left[ \sqrt {2}, + \infty\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y = \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {\frac {x}{4} + \frac {1}{4 x}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(0, \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \right] \cup \left[ \sqrt {2}, + \infty\right)
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\left(0,\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right]\cup \left[\sqrt{2}, + \infty\right)$
