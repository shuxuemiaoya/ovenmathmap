> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $(-1,\frac{3}{2})$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $f(x)$ 的图象过点 $A(1,\frac{1}{2})$ ,
> > ∴ $f(\frac{1}{2}) = a^{1} = \frac{1}{2}$ ,
> >
> > $\therefore f(x) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^x$ $\because f(x) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^x$ 在R上单调递减 $\therefore f(2m^2 -1) <   f(m + 2)\Leftrightarrow 2m^2 -1 > m + 2$ 即 $2m^{2} - m - 3 > 0$ ： $m <   - 1$ 或 $m > \frac{3}{2}$ $\therefore m\in (-\infty , - 1)\cup \left(\frac{3}{2}, + \infty\right).$
