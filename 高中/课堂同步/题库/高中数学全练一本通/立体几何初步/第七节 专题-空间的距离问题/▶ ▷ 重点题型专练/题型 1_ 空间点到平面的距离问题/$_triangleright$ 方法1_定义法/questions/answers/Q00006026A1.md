> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:由题意可知
$$
E F = \sqrt {2}, E B _ {1} = F B _ {1} = \sqrt {5}, V _ {E - B _ {1} F C _ {1}} = \frac {1}{3} \times \left(\frac {1}{2} \times 2 \times 2\right) \times 1 = \frac {2}{3},
$$
设点 $C_1$ 到平面 $EFB_{1}$ 的距离为 $h$ ，因为 $V_{C_1 - B_1EF} = V_{E - B_1FC_1}$
所以 $\frac{1}{3} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \times \sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{5 - \frac{1}{2}} \right) \times h = \frac{2}{3}, h = \frac{4}{3}$ . 故选: D
