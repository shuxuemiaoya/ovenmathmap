> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 17
解析: 设圆台的高为 h, 依题意 $V=\frac{1}{3}(4\pi+36\pi+12\pi)h=104\pi$ , 解得 h=6.
![](../../../../../images/answers/c3bed84c3e8b8818aa56410182bcbee4cfc4c3e25692fc376910bdcb52179095.jpg)
设 $O_{1}O = x$ ，则 $2^{2} + x^{2} = 6^{2} + (6 - x)^{2}$ ，解得 $x = \frac{17}{3}$ 故 $\frac{OO_1}{OO_2} = \frac{\frac{17}{3}}{6 - \frac{17}{3}} = 17$ .故答案为：17.
