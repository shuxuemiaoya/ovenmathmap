> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{1}{3}$ 解析: 取 $BD$ 的中点 $O$ , 连接 $A_1O, C_1O, A_1C_1$ , 如图所示:
![](../../../../../images/answers/9a03caccdacc68a13f613f94183ec57bcc98d6b22080ace67a993b5a74f6b2a4.jpg)
因为长方体 $ABCD - A_1B_1C_1D_1$ 中 $AB = AD = AA_{1} = 4$ 所以 $BD = A_{1}C_{1} = A_{1}D = A_{1}B = BC_{1} = DC_{1} = \sqrt{4^{2} + 4^{2}} = 4\sqrt{2}$ 即 $BO = DO = 2\sqrt{2}$ 所以 $A_{1}O\bot BD$ ， $C_1O\bot BD$ ，且 $A_{1}O = C_{1}O = \sqrt{\left(4\sqrt{2}\right)^{2} - \left(2\sqrt{2}\right)^{2}} = 2\sqrt{6}$ 所以 $\angle A_1OC_1$ 为二面角 $A_{1} - BD - C_{1}$ 的平面角， $\cos \angle A_1OC_1 = \frac{\left(2\sqrt{6}\right)^2 + \left(2\sqrt{6}\right)^2 - \left(4\sqrt{2}\right)^2}{2\times\left(2\sqrt{6}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{3}.$ 故答案为： $\frac{1}{3}$
