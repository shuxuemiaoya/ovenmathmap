> **【解析】**
> $\frac{3}{4}$ 解析：取 $AB$ 的中点 $O$ ，连接 $VO$ 、 $OC$ ，如下图所示：
![](../../../../../images/answers/85c2fe04c169ad41aee0e0db4e7aa92c098a77f3d436803a37cbe6ecbc999dc9.jpg)
$\because VA=VB, O$ 为 AB 的中点, 则 $VO \perp AB$ , 且
$$
A V \perp B V, A B = 2 \sqrt {2}, \therefore V O = \frac {1}{2} A B = \sqrt {2}
$$
$$
C A = C B, O
$$
$$
O C \perp A B
$$
$$
O C = \sqrt {2}
$$
$$
A C \perp B C
$$
$$
V - A B - C
$$
$$
\angle V O C
$$
$$
\cos \angle V O C = \frac {V O ^ {2} + O C ^ {2} - V C ^ {2}}{2 V O \cdot O C} = \frac {3}{4}
$$
$$
V - A B - C
$$
$$
\frac {3}{4}
$$
$$
: \frac {3}{4}
$$
$$
\frac {\pi}{3}
$$
