由题意得 $BF = A_{1}M = \frac{1}{2} AB$
$$
B F \mathbin {/ /} A _ {1} M
$$
$$
\therefore
$$
$$
A _ {1} F B M
$$
$$
\therefore A _ {1} F \parallel B M
$$
又 $F_{1}, M$ 分别为 $C_{1}D_{1}$ ， $A_{1}B_{1}$ 的中点，则 $F_{1}M \parallel C_{1}B_{1}$ 且 $F_{1}M = C_{1}B_{1}$ 而 $C_{1}B_{1} \parallel BC$ 且 $C_{1}B_{1} = BC$ $\therefore F_{1}M \parallel BC$ 且 $F_{1}M = BC$ $\therefore$ 四边形 $F_{1}MBC$ 为平行四边形 $\therefore BM \parallel F_{1}C$ 又 $BM \parallel A_{1}F$ $\therefore A_{1}F \parallel F_{1}C$
$$
\parallel C E _ {1}
$$
∴ $\angle EA_{1}F$ 与 $\angle E_{1}CF_{1}$ 的两边分别平行，且方向都相反
∴ $\angle EA_{1}F = \angle E_{1}CF_{1}$ .
