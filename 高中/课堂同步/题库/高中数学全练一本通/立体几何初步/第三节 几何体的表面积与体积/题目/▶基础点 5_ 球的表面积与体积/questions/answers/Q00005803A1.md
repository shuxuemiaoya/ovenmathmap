> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $S_{\text{球}} = 4\pi$ ， $V_{\text{球}} = \frac{4}{3}\pi$
$$
R = 1
$$
$$
S _ {\text {球}} = 4 \pi R ^ {2} = 4 \pi \times 1 ^ {2} = 4 \pi
$$
$$
V _ {\text {球}} = \frac {4}{3} \pi R ^ {3} = \frac {4}{3} \pi
$$
