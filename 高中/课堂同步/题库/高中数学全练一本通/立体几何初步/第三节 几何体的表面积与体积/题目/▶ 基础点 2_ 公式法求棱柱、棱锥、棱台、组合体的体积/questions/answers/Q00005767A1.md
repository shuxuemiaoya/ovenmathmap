> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{7}{24}$ 解析: 多面体 $CMG - DAD_{1}$ 为三棱台,
又 $S_{\triangle CMG} = \frac{1}{2}\times \frac{1}{2}\times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8}, S_{\triangle DAD_1} = \frac{1}{2}\times 1\times 1 = \frac{1}{2}$ 高 $h = CD = 1$ 所以 $V_{CMG - DAD_1} = \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{8} +\sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{1}{8}}\right)\times 1 = \frac{7}{24}.$
