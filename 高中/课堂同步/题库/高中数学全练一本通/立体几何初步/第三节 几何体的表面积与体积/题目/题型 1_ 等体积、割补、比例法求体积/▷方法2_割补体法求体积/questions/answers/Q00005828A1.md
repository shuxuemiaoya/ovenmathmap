> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{8\sqrt{2}}{3}$
解析:如图,分别过点A,B作EF的垂线,垂足分别为G,H,连接DG,CH,
易得 $EG = HF = 1, AG = GD = BH = HC = \sqrt{3}$ .
取 AD 的中点 O, 连接 GO, 易得 $GO = \sqrt{2}$ ,
∴ $S_{\triangle ADG}=S_{\triangle BCH}=\frac{1}{2}\times\sqrt{2}\times2=\sqrt{2}$ ∴多面体的体积 $V=V_{三棱锥E-ADG}+V_{三棱锥F-BCH}+V_{三棱柱AGD-BHC}=2V_{三棱锥E-ADG}+V_{三棱柱AGD-BHC}$ $=\frac{1}{3}\times\sqrt{2}\times1\times2+\sqrt{2}\times2=\frac{8\sqrt{2}}{3}$
故选：A.
