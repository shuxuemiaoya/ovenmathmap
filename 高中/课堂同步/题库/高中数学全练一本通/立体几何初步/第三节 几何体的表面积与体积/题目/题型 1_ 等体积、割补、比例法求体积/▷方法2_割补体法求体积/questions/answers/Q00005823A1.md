> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{8\sqrt{2}}{3}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 可知正四棱锥的高为 $h=\sqrt{2^{2}-(\sqrt{2})^{2}}=\sqrt{2}$ ,
> 所以八面体体积为 $V=2V_{E-ABCD}=2\times\frac{1}{3}\times S_{ABCD}\cdot h=2\times\frac{1}{3}\times4\times\sqrt{2}=\frac{8\sqrt{2}}{3}$ .
> 故答案为： $\frac{8\sqrt{2}}{3}$
