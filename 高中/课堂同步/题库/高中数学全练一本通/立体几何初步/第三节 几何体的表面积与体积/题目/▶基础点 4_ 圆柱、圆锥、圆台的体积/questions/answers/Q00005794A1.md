> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{27}{\pi}$ 或 $\frac{27}{2\pi}$ .
$$
2 \pi r = 6
$$
$$
r = \frac {3}{\pi}, l = 3
$$
$$
V _ {\text {圆柱}} = \pi r ^ {2} l = \pi \times \left(\frac {3}{\pi}\right) ^ {2} \times 3 = \frac {2 7}{\pi}
$$
$$
2 \pi r = 3
$$
$$
r = \frac {3}{2 \pi}, l = 6
$$
$$
V _ {\text {圆柱}} = \pi r ^ {2} l = \pi \times \left(\frac {3}{2 \pi}\right) ^ {2} \times 6 = \frac {2 7}{2 \pi}
$$
$$
\frac {2 7}{\pi} \text {或} \frac {2 7}{2 \pi}
$$
