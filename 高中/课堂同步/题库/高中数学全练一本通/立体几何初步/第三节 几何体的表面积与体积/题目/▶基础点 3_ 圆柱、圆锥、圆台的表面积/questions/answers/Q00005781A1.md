> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $27\pi$
>
> **【解析】**
> 设 $OA = OB = r,SA = SB = l$ ，由题意得： $\pi l = 2\pi r = 6\pi$ ，则 $l = 6$ 所以 $S_{\text{侧}} = \pi rl = 18\pi ,S_{\text{底}} = 9\pi ,S_{\text{表}} = S_{\text{侧}} + S_{\text{底}} = 27\pi .$
