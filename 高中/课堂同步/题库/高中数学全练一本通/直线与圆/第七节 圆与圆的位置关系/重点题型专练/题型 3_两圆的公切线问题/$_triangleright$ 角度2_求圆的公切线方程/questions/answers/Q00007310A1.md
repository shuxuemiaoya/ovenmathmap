> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\left(x+\frac{13}{5}\right)^{2}+\left(y-\frac{6}{5}\right)^{2}=\frac{4}{5}.$ 解析：过直线 $2x+y+4=0$ 和圆 $x^{2}+y^{2}+2x-4y+1=0$ 的交点的圆系方程可设为 $x^{2}+y^{2}+2x-4y+1+\lambda(2x+y+4)=0(\lambda\in R)$ ，
> 即 $[x-(\lambda+1)]^{2}+\left(y+\frac{\lambda-4}{2}\right)^{2}=\frac{5}{4}\lambda^{2}-4\lambda+4$ 圆的半径为 $\sqrt{\frac{5}{4}\lambda^{2}-4\lambda+4}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{5\lambda^{2}-16\lambda+16}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{5\left(\lambda-\frac{8}{5}\right)^{2}+\frac{16}{5}}$ 故当 $\lambda=\frac{8}{5}$ 时对应圆的半径最小，且最小半径为 $\frac{2}{5}\sqrt{5}$ .
> ∴ 所求圆的方程为 $\left(x+\frac{13}{5}\right)^{2}+\left(y-\frac{6}{5}\right)^{2}=\frac{4}{5}.$
