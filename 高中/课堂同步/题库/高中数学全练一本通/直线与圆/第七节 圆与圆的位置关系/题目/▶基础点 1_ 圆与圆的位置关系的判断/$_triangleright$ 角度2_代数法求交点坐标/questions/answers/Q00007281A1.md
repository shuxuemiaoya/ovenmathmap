> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** 证明见解析,切点为 $\left(\frac{19}{5},-\frac{7}{5}\right)$
>
> **【解析】**
> 证明:圆 $C_{1}:x^{2}+y^{2}-6x+4y+12=0$ ①,即圆 $C_{1}:(x-3)^{2}+(y+2)^{2}=1$ , 圆心 $C_{1}(3,-2)$ , 半径 $R_{1}=1$ ;
> 圆 $C_{2}:x^{2}+y^{2}-14x-2y+34=0$ ②,即圆 $C_{2}:(x-7)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}=16$ , 圆心 $C_{2}(7,1)$ , 半径 $R_{2}=4$ ,
> 所以 $\left|C_{1}C_{2}\right|=\sqrt{(7-3)^{2}+(1+2)^{2}}=5$ , 所以 $\left|C_{1}C_{2}\right|=R_{2}+R_{1}=5$ , 所以两圆相外切;
> - **①**：- ② 得 $8x+6y-22=0$ , 即 $4x+3y-11=0$ ,
> 则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&4x+3y-11=0\\ &x^{2}+y^{2}-6x+4y+12=0\end{aligned}\right.$ , 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x=\frac{19}{5}\\ &y=-\frac{7}{5}\end{aligned}\right.$ , 即切点坐标为 $\left(\frac{19}{5},-\frac{7}{5}\right)$
