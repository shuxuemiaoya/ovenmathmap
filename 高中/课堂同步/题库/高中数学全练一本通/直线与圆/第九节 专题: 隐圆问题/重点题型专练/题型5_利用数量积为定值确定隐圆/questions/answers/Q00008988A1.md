> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> P (x, y)
> $$
> $$
> M (- c, 0), N (c, 0)
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {M P} = (x + c, y), \overrightarrow {N P} = (x - c, y)
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {M P} \cdot \overrightarrow {N P} = 4 \text {可得:} (x + c) (x - c) + y ^ {2} = 4
> $$
> $$
> x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = 4 + c ^ {2}
> $$
