> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\sqrt{14}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}), B(x_{2},y_{2})$ .
> 联立方程组 $\begin{cases}x^{2}+(y-2)^{2}=4,\\x-y+1=0,\end{cases}$ 消去 y，整理得 $2x^{2}-2x-3=0$ .
> 此方程根的判别式 $\Delta=(-2)^{2}-4\times2\times(-3)=28>0$ .
> 所以 $x_{1}+x_{2}=1, x_{1}x_{2}=-\frac{3}{2}$ .
> 所以 $|AB|=\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+(y_{2}-y_{1})^{2}}=\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}+\left[(x_{2}+1)-(x_{1}+1)\right]^{2}}=\sqrt{2}\sqrt{(x_{2}-x_{1})^{2}}=\sqrt{2}\sqrt{(x_{1}+x_{2})^{2}-4x_{1}x_{2}}=\sqrt{2}\sqrt{1-4\times(-\frac{3}{2})}=\sqrt{14}$ .
> 所以 $|AB|=\sqrt{14}$ .
