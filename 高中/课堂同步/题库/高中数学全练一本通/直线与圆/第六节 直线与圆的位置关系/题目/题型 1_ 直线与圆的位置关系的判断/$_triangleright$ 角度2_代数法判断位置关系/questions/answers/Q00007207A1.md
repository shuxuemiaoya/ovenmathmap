> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x - m y + 2 = 0 \\ x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = 4 \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> m \neq 0
> $$
> $$
> (m ^ {2} + 1) y ^ {2} - 4 m y + 4 = 0
> $$
> $$
> \Delta = 1 6 m ^ {2} - 1 6 (m ^ {2} + 1) = 1 6 m ^ {2} \geq 0
> $$
