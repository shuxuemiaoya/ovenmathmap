> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $2\left(x+\frac{1}{4}+\frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}i\right)\left(x+\frac{1}{4}-\frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}i\right)$
>
> **【解析】**
> $2x^{2}+x+1=2\left(x^{2}+\frac{1}{2}x+\frac{1}{2}\right)=2\left[\left(x+\frac{1}{4}\right)^{2}+\frac{7}{16}\right]=2\left[\left(x+\frac{1}{4}\right)^{2}-\frac{7}{16}i^{2}\right]$ $=2\left(x+\frac{1}{4}+\frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}i\right)\left(x+\frac{1}{4}-\frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}i\right)$
