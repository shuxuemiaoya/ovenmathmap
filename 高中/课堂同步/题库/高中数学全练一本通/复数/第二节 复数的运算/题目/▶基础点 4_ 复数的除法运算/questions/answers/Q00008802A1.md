> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：由题意得 $z_{1} = 1 - i,z_{2} = 1 + i$ $\frac{z_2}{z_1} +1 = \frac{1 + i}{1 - i} +1 = \frac{(1 + i)^2}{(1 + i)(1 - i)} +1 = \frac{2i}{2} +1 = 1 + i,$ 则 $|\underline{\underline{\mathsf{{z_ {i}}}^{-}}}+\underline{\underline{\mathsf{{l}}}^{-}}|=\left|\mathbf{l}+\mathbf{l}\right|=\sqrt{\mathbf{\Omega}}.$
>
> 故选 **C**.
