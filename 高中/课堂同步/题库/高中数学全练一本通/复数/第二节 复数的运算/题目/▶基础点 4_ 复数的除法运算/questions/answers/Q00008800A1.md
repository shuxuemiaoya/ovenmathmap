> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{6}{5}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 由(3+4i) $z = 5\mathrm{i}$ ，得 $z = \frac{5\mathrm{i}}{3 + 4\mathrm{i}} = \frac{5\mathrm{i}\cdot(3 - 4\mathrm{i})}{(3 + 4\mathrm{i})(3 - 4\mathrm{i})} = \frac{20 + 15\mathrm{i}}{25} = \frac{4}{5} +\frac{3}{5}\mathrm{i},$ ， $z = \frac{4}{5} -\frac{3}{5}\mathrm{i},$ 所以 $|z - z| = (\frac{4}{5} +\frac{3}{5}\mathrm{i}) - (\frac{4}{5} -\frac{3}{5}\mathrm{i})| = |\frac{6}{5}\mathrm{i}| = \frac{6}{5}$ 故答案为：6/5
