> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $-\sqrt{2} +\sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 依题意得 $\left(-1+\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i}\right)\left(\cos\frac{4\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{z_{2}}{\left(\cos\frac{3\pi}{4}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)}$ 所以 $z_{2}=(-1+\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i})\left(\cos\frac{4\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)\left(\cos\frac{3\pi}{4}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ $=2\left(\cos\frac{2\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)\left(\cos\frac{4\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)\left(\cos\frac{3\pi}{4}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ $=2\left[\cos\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}+\frac{4\pi}{3}+\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)+\mathrm{i}\sin\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}+\frac{4\pi}{3}+\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)\right]$ $=2\left(\cos\frac{11\pi}{4}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{11\pi}{4}\right)=-\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}$
