## 5. 复数三角形式的乘法公式推广

$$
\begin{array}{l} z _ {1} z _ {2} z _ {3} \dots z _ {n} = r _ {1} (\cos \theta_ {1} + i \sin \theta_ {1}) \cdot r _ {2} (\cos \theta_ {2} + i \sin \theta_ {2}) \dots r _ {n} (\cos \theta_ {n} + i \sin \theta_ {n}) \\ = r _ {1} r _ {2} \dots r _ {n} [ \cos (\theta_ {1} + \theta_ {2} + \dots + \theta_ {n}) + i \sin (\theta_ {1} + \theta_ {2} + \dots + \theta_ {n}) ] \end{array}
$$
