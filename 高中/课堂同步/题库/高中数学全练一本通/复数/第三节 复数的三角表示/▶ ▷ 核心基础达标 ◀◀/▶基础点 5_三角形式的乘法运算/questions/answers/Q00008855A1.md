> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\because z_{2}=2\sqrt{2}\left(\sin30^{\circ}-\mathrm{i}\cos30^{\circ}\right)=2\sqrt{2}\left(\cos300^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin300^{\circ}\right)$ , $\therefore z_{1}\cdot z_{2}=\sqrt{2}\left(\cos60^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin60^{\circ}\right)\cdot2\sqrt{2}\left(\cos300^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin300^{\circ}\right)$ $=4\left[\cos(60^{\circ}+300^{\circ})+\mathrm{i}\sin(60^{\circ}+300^{\circ})\right]=4\left(\cos360^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin360^{\circ}\right)$ $=4\left(\cos0^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin0^{\circ}\right)$ .
>
> 故选 **D**.
