> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $-4\sqrt{2}+4\sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}$
>
> **【解析】**
> $8\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{2}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{\pi}{2}\right)\times\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{4}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ $=8\left[\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+\mathrm{i}\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right]=8\cos\frac{3\pi}{4}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{3\pi}{4}=-4\sqrt{2}+4\sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}$ .
> 故答案为： $-4\sqrt{2}+4\sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}$ .
