> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\left(\cos75^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin75^{\circ}\right)\left(\cos15^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin15^{\circ}\right)=\left(\sin15^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\cos15^{\circ}\right)\left(\cos15^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin15^{\circ}\right)$ $=\sin15^{\circ}\cos15^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\cos^{2}15^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\sin^{2}15^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}^{2}\sin15^{\circ}\cos15^{\circ}$ $=\sin15^{\circ}\cos15^{\circ}+\mathrm{i}\left(\cos^{2}15^{\circ}+\sin^{2}15^{\circ}\right)-\sin15^{\circ}\cos15^{\circ}=\mathrm{i}$
>
> 故选 **D**
