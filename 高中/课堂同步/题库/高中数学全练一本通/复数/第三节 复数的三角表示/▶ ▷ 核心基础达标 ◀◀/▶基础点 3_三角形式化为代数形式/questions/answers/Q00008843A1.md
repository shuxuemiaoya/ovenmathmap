> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\sqrt{3}-\mathrm{i}$
>
> **【解析】**
> $\sqrt{3}-i$
> $$
> z = 2 \left(\cos \frac {\pi}{6} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sqrt {3} + \mathrm{i}
> $$
> $$
> \overline {{z}} = \sqrt {3} - \mathrm{i}
> $$
> $$
> \sqrt {3} - \mathrm{i}
> $$
