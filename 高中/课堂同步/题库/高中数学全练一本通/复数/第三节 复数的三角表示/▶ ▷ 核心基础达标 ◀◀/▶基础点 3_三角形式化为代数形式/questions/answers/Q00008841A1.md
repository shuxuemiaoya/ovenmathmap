> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $$ - \frac {3}{2} \sqrt {2} + \frac {3}{2} \sqrt {6} i \tag {10} $$
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题得 $3\sqrt{2}\left(\cos\frac{2\pi}{3}+i\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)=3\sqrt{2}\left(-\frac{1}{2}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}i\right)=-\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{2}+\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{6}i$ 故答案为： $-\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{2}+\frac{3}{2}\sqrt{6}i$
