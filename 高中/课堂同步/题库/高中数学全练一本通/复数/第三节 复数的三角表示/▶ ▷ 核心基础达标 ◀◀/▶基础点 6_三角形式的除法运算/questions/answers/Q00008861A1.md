> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $-\frac{1}{32}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{32}\mathrm{i}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 因为 $\left[2\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right]^{-4}=\frac{1}{\left[2\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right]^{4}}$ $=\frac{1}{2^{4}\left(\cos\frac{4}{3}\pi+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{4}{3}\pi\right)}=\frac{\cos0+\mathrm{i}\sin0}{16\left(\cos\frac{4\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)}$ $=\frac{1}{16}\left[\cos\left(-\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)+\mathrm{i}\sin\left(-\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)\right]=-\frac{1}{32}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{32}\mathrm{i}$
