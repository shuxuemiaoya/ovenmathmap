> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $\sqrt{2}\cos\left(2\theta-\frac{\pi}{12}\right)+\sqrt{2}\mathrm{i}\sin\left(2\theta-\frac{\pi}{12}\right)$ ；（2） $\cos\theta+\mathrm{i}\sin\theta$ ； （3） $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\left(\cos\frac{29\pi}{6}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{29\pi}{6}\right)$
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\frac{(1-\sqrt{3})i)(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)}{(1-i)(\cos\theta-i\sin\theta)}=\frac{2\left[\cos\left(-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)+\mathrm{i}\sin\left(-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right](\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)}{\sqrt{2}\left[\cos\left(-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+\mathrm{i}\sin\left(-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right]\left[\cos(-\theta)+i\sin(-\theta)\right]}$ $=\frac{2\left[\cos\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)+i\sin\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right]}{\sqrt{2}\left[\cos\left(-\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+i\sin\left(-\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right]}$ $=\sqrt{2}\cos\left[\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)-\left(-\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right]+\sqrt{2}i \sin\left[\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)-\left(-\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\right]$ $=\sqrt{2}\cos\left(2\theta-\frac{\pi}{12}\right)+\sqrt{2}i \sin\left(2\theta-\frac{\pi}{12}\right)$ （2） $\frac{(\cos7\theta+i\nosin7\rho)(\cos2\rho+i\nosin2\rho)}{(\cos5\rho+i\nosin5\rho)(\cos3\rho+i\nosin3\rho)}=\frac{\cos(7\rho+2\rho)+i\nosin(7\rho+2\rho)}{\cos(5\rho+3\rho)+i\nosin(5\rho+3\rho)}$ $=\frac{\cos9\rho+i\nosin9\rho}{\cos8\rho+i\nosin8\rho}=\cos(9\rho-8\rho)+i\nosin(9\rho-8\rho)=cosh+\mathrm{i}sin\rho$ （3） $\frac{\sqrt{5}\left(\cos\frac{5\pi}{4}+i\nosin\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)^{2}\cdot \sqrt{2}\left(\cos\frac{7\pi}{3}+i\nosin\frac{7\pi}{3}\right)}{\sqrt{3}\left(\cos\frac{7\pi}{3}+i\nosin\frac{7\pi}{3}\right)\cdot \sqrt{5}\left(\cos\frac{7\pi}{3}-i\nosin\frac{7\pi}{3}\right)}$ $= \frac{\left(\cos \frac{5 \pi}{4}+i \sin \frac{5 \pi}{4}\right)^{2} \cdot \sqrt{2} \left(\cos \frac{7 \pi}{3}+i \sin \frac{7 \pi}{3}\right)}{\sqrt{3}}$ $= \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} \left(\cos \frac{5 \pi}{2}+i \sin \frac{5 \pi}{2}\right) \cdot \left(\cos \frac{7 \pi}{3}+i \sin \frac{7 \pi}{3}\right)$ $= \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} \left[\cos \left(\frac{5 \pi}{2}+\frac{7 \pi}{3}\right)+i \sin \left(\frac{5 \pi}{2}+\frac{7 \pi}{3}\right)\right]= \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} \left(\cos \frac{29 \pi}{6}+i \sin \frac{29 \pi}{6}\right)$
