【31】计算（结果用复数的三角表示）
(1) $\frac{(1-\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i})(\cos\theta+\mathrm{i}\sin\theta)}{(1-\mathrm{i})(\cos\theta-\mathrm{i}\sin\theta)}$ ;

(2) $\frac{(\cos 7\theta + i\sin 7\theta)(\cos 2\theta + i\sin 2\theta)}{(\cos 5\theta + i\sin 5\theta)(\cos 3\theta + i\sin 3\theta)};$

(3) $\frac{\sqrt{5}\left(\cos\frac{5\pi}{4}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)^2\cdot\sqrt{2}\left(\cos\frac{7\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{7\pi}{3}\right)}{\sqrt{3}\left(\cos\frac{7\pi}{3}+\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{7\pi}{3}\right)\cdot\sqrt{5}\left(\cos\frac{7\pi}{3}-\mathrm{i}\sin\frac{7\pi}{3}\right)}.$
