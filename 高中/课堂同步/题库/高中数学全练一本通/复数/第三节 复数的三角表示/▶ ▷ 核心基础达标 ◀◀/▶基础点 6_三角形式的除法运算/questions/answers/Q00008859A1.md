> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析： $2\times-\left[\sqrt{2}\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{5}+\mathrm{i}sin\frac{\pi}{5}\right)\right]=\frac{2(\cos0+\mathrm{i}sin0)}{\sqrt{2}\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{5}+\mathrm{i}sin \frac{\pi}{5}\right)}$ $= \frac{2}{\sqrt{2}} \left[\cos(0-\frac {\pi }{5}) + \mathrm{i}sin(0-\frac {\pi }{5}) \right]= \sqrt { 2 } \left[\cos(- \frac {\pi }{5}) + \mathrm{i}sin(- \frac {\pi }{5}) \right]$ ,
>
> 故选 **C**.
