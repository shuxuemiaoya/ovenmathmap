> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** （1） $\frac{1}{2}\left(\cos \frac{7\pi}{4} +\mathrm{i}\sin \frac{7\pi}{4}\right)$ ；（2） $\frac{1}{2}\left(\cos \frac{4\pi}{3} +\mathrm{i}\sin \frac{4\pi}{3}\right)$
>
> **【解析】**
> （1） $\frac{1}{2}\left(\cos \frac{\pi}{4} -\mathrm{i}\sin \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\mathrm{i}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\mathrm{i},$
> 其中 $r = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{2},$
> 故三角形式为 $\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\mathrm{i}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\cos \frac{7\pi}{4} +\mathrm{i}\sin \frac{7\pi}{4}\right)$
> (2) $-\frac{1}{2}\left(\cos \frac{\pi}{3} + i \sin \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} i\right) = -\frac{1}{4} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} i,$
> 其中 $r = \sqrt{\left(-\frac{1}{4}\right)^2 + \left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)^2} = \frac{1}{2}$
> 故三角形式为 $\frac{1}{2}\left(-\frac{1}{2} -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\mathrm{i}\right) = \frac{1}{2}\left(\cos \frac{4\pi}{3} +\mathrm{i}\sin \frac{4\pi}{3}\right);$
