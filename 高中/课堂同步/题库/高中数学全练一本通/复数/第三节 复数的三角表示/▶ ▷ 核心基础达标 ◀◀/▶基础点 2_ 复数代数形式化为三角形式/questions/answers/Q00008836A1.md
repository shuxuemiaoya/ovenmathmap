> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: $\because r=\sqrt{\left(\sqrt{3}\right)^{2}+1^{2}}=2$ ,
> $$
> \cos \theta = \frac {1}{2}, \sin \theta = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
> $$
> 又 $\theta \in [0,2\pi)$ ，∴ $\theta = \frac{\pi}{3}$
> ∴ $1 + \sqrt{3}i = 2\left(\cos\frac{\pi}{3} + i\sin\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$
>
> 故选 **B**.
