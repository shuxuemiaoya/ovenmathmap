> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $\frac{7\pi}{4}$
>
> **【解析】**
> $z$ 的辐角主值是 $\frac{\pi}{4}$
> 则 $z = r\left(\cos \frac{\pi}{4} +\mathrm{i}\sin \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = r\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} +\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\mathrm{i}\right),r > 0$
> 所以共轭复数 $\overline{z} = r\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\mathrm{i}\right) = r\left(\cos \frac{7\pi}{4} +\sin \frac{7\pi}{4}\mathrm{i}\right),$
> $$
> \frac {7 \pi}{4}
> $$
