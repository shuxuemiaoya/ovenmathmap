> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** -1或 $-\frac{5}{3}$
>
> **【解析】**
> 若 $z_{1} = z_{2}$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}m^2 -2 = \cos 2\theta \\ m + 2 = \sin \theta \end{array} \right.$ ，又 $\cos 2\theta = 1 - 2\sin^2\theta$ 则 $m^2 -2 = 1 - 2(m + 2)^2$ ，解得 $m = -1$ 或 $m = -\frac{5}{3}$ .故答案为：-1或 $-\frac{5}{3}$
