> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $$ a = 1, b = 3 $$ $$ a = - 1, b = - 3 $$
>
> **【解析】**
> （1）由题意可知 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a - 3b = 5\\ 2a + 3b = 1 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $a = 2,b = -1$
> （2）由题意可知 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a^2 -b^2 = -8\\ 2ab = 6 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $a = 1,b = 3$ 或 $a = -1,b = -3$
