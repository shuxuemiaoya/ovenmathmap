> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** (1) $x=4$ 或 $x=-3$；(2) $m=-1$，最小值为 $\sqrt{5}$
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> x = - 3; \tag {2}
> $$
> $$
> m = - 1
> $$
> $$
> \sqrt {5}
> $$
> $$
> m = 1  , \text {得}   z = 3 + 4 i    ,    \overline {{{z}}} = 3 - 4 i
> $$
> $$
> \left| \overline {{z}} \right| = \left| x + (x - 1) i \right|
> $$
> $$
> \sqrt {3 ^ {2} + (- 4) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {x ^ {2} + (x - 1) ^ {2}}
> $$
> $$
> x ^ {2} - x - 1 2 = 0
> $$
> $$
> x = 4 \text {或} x = - 3
> $$
> $$
> \left| \overline {{z}} \right| = \sqrt {(1 + 2 m) ^ {2} + [ - (3 + m) ] ^ {2}} = \sqrt {5 m ^ {2} + 1 0 m + 1 0} = \sqrt {5 (m + 1) ^ {2} + 5} \geq \sqrt {5}
> $$
> $$
> \sqrt {5}
> $$
