> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** $z_2=1-\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i}$ 或 $z_2=-1+\sqrt{3}\mathrm{i}$
>
> **【解析】**
> $$
> 1 - \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {O Z _ {1}} = (\sqrt {3}, 1)
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {O Z _ {1}} \perp \overrightarrow {O Z _ {2}}
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {O Z _ {2}} = (x, y)
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {O Z _ {1}} \cdot \overrightarrow {O Z _ {2}} = \sqrt {3} x + y = 0
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 1 \\ y = - \sqrt {3} \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = - 1 \\ y = \sqrt {3} \end{array} \right.
> $$
> $$
> \left| O Z _ {1} \right| = \left| O Z _ {2} \right|
> $$
> $$
> x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = 4
> $$
> $$
> z _ {2} = 1 - \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}
> $$
> $$
> \overrightarrow {O Z _ {2}} = (1, - \sqrt {3}) \text {或} \overrightarrow {O Z _ {2}} = (- 1, \sqrt {3})
> $$
> $$
> z _ {2} = - 1 + \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}
> $$
> $$
> 1 - \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}
> $$
> $$
> - 1 + \sqrt {3} \mathrm{i}
> $$
