> [!faq]- 《指数函数的应用》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 令 $y = \frac{2^x - 1}{2^x + 1}$ ，得 $2^{x} = -\frac{y + 1}{y - 1}$
> >
> > $\because 2^{x}>0,\quad\therefore-\frac{y+1}{y-1}>0,$ 解得 -1<y<1.
> >
> > $\therefore f(x)$ 的值域为 $(-1,1)$ .
> >
> > (2)【问答题】 $\because f(-x) = \frac{2^{-x} - 1}{2^{-x} + 1} = \frac{1 - 2^x}{1 + 2^x} = -f(x)$ ，定义域为 $R$
> >
> > $\therefore f(x)$ 是奇函数.
> >
> > (3)【问答题】 $f(x)=\frac{(2^{x}+1)-2}{2^{x}+1}=1-\frac{2}{2^{x}+1},$
> >
> > 设 $x_{1}, x_{2}$ 是 $R$ 上任意两个实数，且 $x_{1} < x_{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > f (x _ {1}) - f (x _ {2}) = \frac {2}{2 ^ {x _ {2}} + 1} - \frac {2}{2 ^ {x _ {1}} + 1} = \frac {2 (2 ^ {x _ {1}} - 2 ^ {x _ {2}})}{(2 ^ {x _ {1}} + 1) (2 ^ {x _ {2}} + 1)},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because x _ {1} <   x _ {2}, \therefore 2 ^ {x _ {2}} > 2 ^ {x _ {1}} > 0,
> > $$
> >
> > 从而 $2^{x_1} + 1 > 0, 2^{x_2} + 1 > 0, 2^{x_1} - 2^{x_2} < 0,$
> >
> > $\therefore f(x_{1}) - f(x_{2}) < 0$ ，即 $f(x_{1}) < f(x_{2})$
> >
> > $\therefore f(x)$ 为 $R$ 上的增函数.
