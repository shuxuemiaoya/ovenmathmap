> [!faq]- 《诱导公式(1)》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【正确答案】 $\because f(\alpha) = \frac{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)\sin\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{\cos(-\pi - \alpha)\tan(\pi - \alpha)} = \frac{(-\sin\alpha)(-\cos\alpha)}{(-\cos\alpha)(-\tan\alpha)} = \cos\alpha$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore f \left(\frac {2 0 1 7 \pi}{3}\right) = \cos \left(6 7 2 \pi + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \cos \frac {\pi}{3} = \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
