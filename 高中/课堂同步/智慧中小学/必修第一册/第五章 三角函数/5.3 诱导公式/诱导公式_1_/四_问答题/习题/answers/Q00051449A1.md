> [!faq]- 《诱导公式(1)》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【正确答案】 $\frac{cos(2\pi - \theta)}{cos(\pi + \theta)sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \theta\right) - sin\left(\frac{3\pi}{2} + \theta\right)} + \frac{cos(\pi - \theta)}{cos\theta\left[sin\left(\frac{3\pi}{2} - \theta\right) - 1\right]}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\cos \theta}{(- \cos \theta) \cos \theta - (- \cos \theta)} + \frac {- \cos \theta}{\cos \theta (- \cos \theta - 1)} = \frac {1}{1 - \cos \theta} + \frac {1}{1 + \cos \theta} = \frac {2}{\sin^ {2} \theta} = 2 + \frac {2}{\tan^ {2} \theta} = 2 + \frac {2}{2 ^ {2}} = \frac {5}{2}
> > $$
