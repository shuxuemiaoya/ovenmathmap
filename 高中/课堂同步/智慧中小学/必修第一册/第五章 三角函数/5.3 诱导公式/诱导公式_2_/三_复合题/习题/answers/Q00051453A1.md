> [!faq]- 《诱导公式(2)》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 又 $\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}m=\frac{m}{\sqrt{3+m^{2}}}$ ，所以 $m=\pm\sqrt{5}$ .
> >
> > 当 $m = \sqrt{5}$ 时， $\cos \alpha = \frac{-\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3 + m^2}} = -\frac{\sqrt{6}}{4},$ $\tan \alpha = \frac{m}{-\sqrt{3}} = -\frac{\sqrt{15}}{3};$
> >
> > 当 $m = -\sqrt{5}$ 时， $\cos \alpha = \frac{-\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3 + m^2}} = -\frac{\sqrt{6}}{4},$ $\tan \alpha = \frac{m}{-\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{15}}{3}.$
> >
> > (2)【问答题】 $\frac{\cos(\pi + \alpha)\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)\cos\left(\frac{11\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{\cos(\pi - \alpha)\sin(-\pi - \alpha)\sin\left(\frac{9\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)} = \frac{-\cos\alpha \cdot (-\sin\alpha) \cdot (-\sin\alpha)}{-\cos\alpha \cdot \sin\alpha \cdot \cos\alpha} = \tan \alpha = \pm \frac{\sqrt{15}}{3}$ .
