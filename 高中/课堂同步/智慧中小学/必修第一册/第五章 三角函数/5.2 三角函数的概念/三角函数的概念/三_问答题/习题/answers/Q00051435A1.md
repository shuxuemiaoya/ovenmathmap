> [!faq]- 《三角函数的概念》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ①当 $m > 0$ 时， $r = \sqrt{m^2 + 8m^2} = 3m$ ，
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \sin \theta = \frac {2 \sqrt {2} m}{3 m} = \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}, \cos \theta = \frac {m}{3 m} = \frac {1}{3}, \tan \theta = \frac {2 \sqrt {2} m}{m} = 2 \sqrt {2}.
> > $$
> > - **②**：当 $m < 0$ 时， $r = \sqrt{m^2 + 8m^2} = -3m,$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \theta = \frac {2 \sqrt {2} m}{- 3 m} = - \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}, \cos \theta = \frac {m}{- 3 m} = - \frac {1}{3}, \tan \theta = \frac {2 \sqrt {2} m}{m} = 2 \sqrt {2}.
> > $$
