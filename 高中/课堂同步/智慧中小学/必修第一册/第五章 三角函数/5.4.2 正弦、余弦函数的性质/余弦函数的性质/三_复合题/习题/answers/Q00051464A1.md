> [!faq]- 《余弦函数的性质》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $2k\pi - \frac{\pi}{2} \leqslant 2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \leqslant 2k\pi + \frac{\pi}{2}(k \in \mathbb{Z})$ ，得 $k\pi - \frac{\pi}{3} \leqslant x \leqslant k\pi + \frac{\pi}{6}(k \in \mathbb{Z})$
> >
> > 所以 $f(x)$ 的单调增区间是 $\left[k\pi -\frac{\pi}{3},k\pi +\frac{\pi}{6}\right](k\in Z);$
> >
> > (2)【问答题】 $\because x\in [0,m],2x + \frac{\pi}{6}\in \left[\frac{\pi}{6},2m + \frac{\pi}{6}\right],f(x)\in \left[\frac{l}{2},1\right],$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {\pi}{2} \leqslant 2 m + \frac {\pi}{6} \leqslant \frac {5 \pi}{6}, \quad \therefore \frac {\pi}{6} \leqslant m \leqslant \frac {\pi}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > 故实数 $m$ 的取值范围为 $\left[\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{3}\right]$ .
