> [!faq]- 《简单的三角变换（1）》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【正确答案】证明：左边 $= \frac{2\sin 2x\cos 2x}{2\cos^2 2x} \cdot \frac{\cos 2x}{2\cos^2 x} \cdot \frac{\cos x}{2\cos^2 \frac{x}{2}} = \frac{\sin 2x}{4\cos x\cos^2 \frac{x}{2}} = \frac{2\sin x\cos x}{4\cos x\cos^2 \frac{x}{2}} = \frac{\sin x}{2\cos^2 \frac{x}{2}} = \frac{2\sin \frac{x}{2}\cos \frac{x}{2}}{2\cos^2 \frac{x}{2}} = \tan$ $\frac{x}{2} =$ 右边
