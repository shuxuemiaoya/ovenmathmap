> [!faq]- 《余弦和正切公式（1）》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【正确答案】 $\because \sin (\alpha +\beta) = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5},\quad \therefore \cos (\alpha +\beta) = \pm \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5},\quad \because \alpha ,\beta$ 为锐角， $\therefore \pi >\alpha +\beta >\alpha >0,$ $\therefore \sin \alpha = \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$ ，又 $y = \cos x$ 在[0，π]上单调递减， $\therefore \cos (\alpha +\beta) <   \cos \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10},\quad \therefore \cos (\alpha +\beta) = -\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5},$ $\therefore \cos \beta = \cos [(\alpha +\beta) - \alpha ] = \cos (\alpha +\beta)\cos \alpha +\sin (\alpha +\beta)\sin \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}\Big(-\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\Big) + \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}\cdot \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}.$
