> [!faq]- 《正切公式》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【正确答案】 $\because f(x)=2\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\cos x-\frac{1}{2}=\sqrt{3}\sin x\cos x+\cos^{2}x-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin 2x+\frac{1}{2}\cos 2x=\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$
> >
> > ， $f(\alpha) = \frac{1}{3}$ ，可得 $\sin \left(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{1}{3},\quad \because \alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{5\pi}{12}\right)$ ，可得 $2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\in \left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right),$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = - \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right)} = - \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}, \quad \therefore \cos 2 \alpha = \cos \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6} - \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \cos \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \cos \frac {\pi}{6} + \sin 2 \alpha .
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \sin \frac {\pi}{6} = - \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1 - 2 \sqrt {6}}{6}.
> > $$
