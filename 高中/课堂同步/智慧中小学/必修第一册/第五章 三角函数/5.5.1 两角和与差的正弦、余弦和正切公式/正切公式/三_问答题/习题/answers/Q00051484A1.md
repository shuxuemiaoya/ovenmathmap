> [!faq]- 《正切公式》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【正确答案】 $\because \cos \alpha = \frac{4}{5}, \therefore \cos 2\alpha = 2\cos^2\alpha - 1 = \frac{7}{25}.$ $\because \alpha, \beta \in (0, \pi)$ , $\cos \alpha = \frac{4}{5} > 0,$ $\therefore 0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}, -\pi < \alpha - \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}.$ 又 $\because \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{5}{13}, \therefore \alpha - \beta \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2}), \therefore \cos (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{12}{13}.$ $\therefore \sin 2\alpha = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 2 \times \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{24}{25}.$ $\therefore \sin (\alpha + \beta) = \sin [2\alpha - (\alpha - \beta)] = \sin 2\alpha \cos (\alpha - \beta) - \cos 2\alpha \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{24}{25} \times \frac{12}{13} - \frac{7}{25} \times \frac{5}{13} = \frac{253}{325}.$
