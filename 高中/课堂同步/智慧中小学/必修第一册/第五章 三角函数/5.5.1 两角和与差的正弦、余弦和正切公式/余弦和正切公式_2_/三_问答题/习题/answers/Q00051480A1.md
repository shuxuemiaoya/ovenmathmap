> [!faq]- 《余弦和正切公式（2）》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【正确答案】∵ $2\alpha-\beta=2(\alpha-\beta)+\beta$ ，又 $\tan(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{1}{2}$ ，∴ $\tan2(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{2\tan(\alpha-\beta)}{1-\tan^{2}(\alpha-\beta)}=\frac{4}{3}$
> >
> > 故 $\tan(2\alpha-\beta)=\tan[2(\alpha-\beta)+\beta]=\frac{\tan2(\alpha-\beta)+\tan\beta}{1-\tan2(\alpha-\beta)\tan\beta}=\frac{\frac{4}{3}-\frac{1}{7}}{1+\frac{4}{3}\times\frac{1}{7}}=1$ ，又 $\because\tan\alpha=\tan[(\alpha-\beta)+\beta]=\frac{\tan(\alpha-\beta)+\tan\beta}{1-\tan(\alpha-\beta)+\tan\beta}=\frac{1}{3}<1,$
> >
> > 且 $0 < \alpha < \pi, \therefore 0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{4}, \therefore 0 < 2\alpha < \frac{\pi}{2},$
> >
> > 又 $tan\beta=-\frac{1}{7}$ ，且 $\beta\in(0,\pi)\Rightarrow\beta\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)\Rightarrow-\beta\in\left(-\pi,-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ .
> >
> > $\therefore 2\alpha - \beta \in (-\pi, 0)$ . 又 $tan(2\alpha - \beta) = 1$ , $\therefore 2\alpha - \beta = -\frac{3\pi}{4}$ .
