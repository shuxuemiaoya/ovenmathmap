> [!faq]- 《函数表示法》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ∵ $-\pi < -1$ ，∴ $f(-\pi) = -\pi + 2$ .
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because -1 < \frac{3}{2} < 2, \therefore f\left(\frac{3}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{4} > 2.$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore f \left(f \left(\frac {3}{2}\right)\right) = f \left(\frac {9}{4}\right) = 2 \times \frac {9}{4} = \frac {9}{2}.
> > $$
