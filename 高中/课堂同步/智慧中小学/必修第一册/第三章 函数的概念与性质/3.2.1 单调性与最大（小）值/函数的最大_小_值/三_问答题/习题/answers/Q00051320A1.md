> [!faq]- 《函数的最大（小）值》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $y=\frac{x^{2}+8}{x-1}=\frac{x^{2}-1+9}{x-1}=(x+1)+\frac{9}{x-1}=(x-1)+\frac{9}{x-1}+2.$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because x > 1, \therefore x - 1 > 0, \therefore (x - 1) + \frac{9}{x - 1} + 2 \geq 2\sqrt{(x - 1) \cdot \frac{9}{x - 1}} + 2 = 8.$ 当且仅当 $x - 1 = \frac{9}{x - 1}$ , 即 $x = 4$ 时等号成立. $\therefore y$ 有最小值8.
