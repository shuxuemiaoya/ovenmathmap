> [!faq]- 《单调性》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\forall_{x_{1}}, x_{2}\in(0,1)$ ，且 $x_{1}<x_{2}$ ，则
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > f _ {(x _ {1})} - f _ {(x _ {2})} = \left(x _ {1} + \frac {1}{x _ {1}}\right) - \left(x _ {2} + \frac {1}{x _ {2}}\right) = (x _ {1} - x _ {2}) + \left(\frac {1}{x _ {1}} - \frac {1}{x _ {2}}\right) = (x _ {1} - x _ {2}) + \frac {x _ {2} - x _ {1}}{x _ {1} x _ {2}} = (x _ {1} - x _ {2}) \cdot \frac {x _ {1} x _ {2} - 1}{x _ {1} x _ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $\because 0 < x_{1} < x_{2} < 1, \therefore x_{1} - x_{2} < 0, 0 < x_{1}x_{2} < 1, x_{1}x_{2} - 1 < 0, \therefore f(x_{1}) - f(x_{2}) > 0$ 即 $f(x_{1}) > f(x_{2})$ . $\therefore f(x)$ 在 $(0, 1)$ 上单调递减.
