> [!faq]- 《一元回归方程的应用》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} (x _ {i} - \bar {x}) ^ {2} = (- 2) ^ {2} + (- 1) ^ {2} + 0 + 1 + 2 ^ {2} = 1 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} (x _ {i} - \overline {{x}}) (y _ {i} - \overline {{y}}) = \sum_ {i = 1} ^ {5} x _ {i} y _ {i} - 5 \overline {{x}} \overline {{y}} = 4 7 0,
> > $$
> >
> > 得 $\hat{b} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_i - \bar{x})^2} = \frac{470}{10} = 47,$ $\hat{a} = \overline{y} -\hat{b}\overline{x} = 132 - 47\times 3 = -9,$
> >
> > 所求线性回归方程为 $\hat{y}=47x-9;$
> >
> > (2)【问答题】将2025年对应的年份编号 x=9 代入线性回归方程得 $\hat{y}=47\times9-9=414$ ，故预测2025年该市电动自行车的数量为414万辆.
