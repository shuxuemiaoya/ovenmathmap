> [!faq]- 《随机变量及其分布_二项分布与超几何分布、正态分布》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > X的可能取值为1，2，3，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore P (X = 1) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {4} ^ {I} \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {2}}{\mathrm{C} _ {6} ^ {3}} = \frac {1}{5}, P (X = 2) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {4} ^ {2} \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {I}}{\mathrm{C} _ {6} ^ {3}} = \frac {3}{5}, P (X = 3) = \frac {\mathrm{C} _ {4} ^ {3} \mathrm{C} _ {2} ^ {0}}{\mathrm{C} _ {6} ^ {3}} = \frac {1}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ X 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{5}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{5}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{5}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > Y的可能取值为0，1，2，3，且 $Y \sim B\left(3, \frac{2}{3}\right)$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore P (Y = 0) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {0} \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {0} \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {1}{2 7}, \quad P (Y = 1) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {1} \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {1} \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {2}{9},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P _ {(Y = 2)} = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {2} \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {1} = \frac {4}{9}, \quad P _ {(Y = 3)} = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {3} \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {3} \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {0} = \frac {8}{2 7},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore Y$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>Y</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{27}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{4}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{8}{27}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > (2) $E(X)=1\times\frac{1}{5}+2\times\frac{3}{5}+3\times\frac{1}{5}=2$
> >
> > (2)【问答题】由
> > （1）有 $E(X)=1\times\frac{1}{5}+2\times\frac{3}{5}+3\times\frac{1}{5}=2$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore D (X) = E (X ^ {2}) - (E (X)) ^ {2} = 1 ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{5} + 2 ^ {2} \times \frac {3}{5} + 3 ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{5} - 2 ^ {2} = \frac {2}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > 而 $E(Y) = 3 \times \frac{2}{3} = 2$ ， $D(Y) = 3 \times \frac{2}{3} \times \left(1 - \frac{2}{3}\right) = \frac{2}{3}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore E _ {(X)} = E _ {(Y)}, \quad D _ {(X)} <   D _ {(Y)}
> > $$
> >
> > 故两人平均答对的题数相等，说明实力相当；但甲答对题数的方差比乙小，说明甲发挥较为稳定，因此推荐甲参加比赛更加合适.
