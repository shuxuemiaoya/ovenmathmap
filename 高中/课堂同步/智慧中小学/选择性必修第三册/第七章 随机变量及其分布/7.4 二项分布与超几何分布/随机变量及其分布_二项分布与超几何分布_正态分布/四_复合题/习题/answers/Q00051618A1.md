> [!faq]- 《随机变量及其分布_二项分布与超几何分布、正态分布》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 】解：设甲投中的次数为 $X$ ，乙投中的次数为 $Y$ ，
> >
> > 则根据题意， $X,Y$ 的可能取值均为 $0,1,2,3$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $P(X = 0) = \mathbf{C}_3^0\left(\frac{l}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{l}{8},$ $P(Y = 0) = \mathbf{C}_3^0\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^3 = \frac{8}{27}$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = \mathbf {C} _ {3} ^ {I} \biggl (\frac {l}{2} \biggr) ^ {3} = \frac {3}{8}, P (Y = 1) = \mathbf {C} _ {3} ^ {I} \bigl (\frac {l}{3} \bigr) \bigl (\frac {2}{3} \bigr) ^ {2} = \frac {4}{9},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} (\frac {1}{2}) ^ {2} (\frac {1}{2}) = \frac {3}{8}, P (Y = 2) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} (\frac {1}{3}) ^ {2} (\frac {2}{3}) = \frac {2}{9},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = (\frac {1}{2}) ^ {3} = \frac {1}{8}, P (Y = 3) = (\frac {1}{3}) ^ {3} = \frac {1}{2 7},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以，乙赢的概率为 $P = P(Y = 1)^{P}(X = 0) + P(Y = 2)^{P}(X \leqslant 1) + P(Y = 3)^{P}(X \leqslant 2)$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {4}{9} \times \frac {1}{8} + \frac {2}{9} \times (\frac {1}{8} + \frac {3}{8}) + \frac {1}{2 7} \times (\frac {1}{8} + \frac {3}{8} + \frac {3}{8}) = \frac {4 3}{2 1 6}
> > $$
> >
> > (2)【问答题
> > 】解：根据题意，X 的可能取值均为 0, 1, 2, 3，且 $X \sim B\left(3, \frac{1}{2}\right)$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $P(X = 0) = \mathbf{C}_3^0\left(\frac{l}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{l}{8}$ ， $P(X = 1) = \mathbf{C}_3^l\left(\frac{l}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{3}{8}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \mathbf {C} _ {3} ^ {2} (\frac {l}{2}) ^ {2} (\frac {l}{2}) = \frac {3}{8}, P (X = 3) = (\frac {l}{2}) ^ {3} = \frac {l}{8},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以， $X$ 的分布列为：
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{1}{8}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{8}$ </td><td> $\frac{3}{8}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{8}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > 所以， $E(X) = 1\times \frac{3}{8} +2\times \frac{3}{8} +3\times \frac{1}{8} = \frac{3}{2}$
