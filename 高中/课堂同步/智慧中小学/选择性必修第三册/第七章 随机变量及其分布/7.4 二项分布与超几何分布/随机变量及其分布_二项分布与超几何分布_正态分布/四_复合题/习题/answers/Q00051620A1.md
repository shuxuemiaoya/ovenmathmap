> [!faq]- 《随机变量及其分布_二项分布与超几何分布、正态分布》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 又由于每次取到黑球的概率均为 $\frac{2}{10} = \frac{1}{5}$ ，3 次取球可看成 3 次独立重复试验，
> >
> > 故随机变量服从二项分布 $XB(3, \frac{l}{5})$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 0) = C _ {3} ^ {0} \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {0} \left(\frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {6 4}{1 2 5}, P (X = 1) = C _ {3} ^ {1} \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {1} \left(\frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {2 3} = \frac {4 8}{1 2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = C _ {3} ^ {2} \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {2} \left(\frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {1} = \frac {1 2}{1 2 5}, P (X = 3) = C _ {3} ^ {3} \left(\frac {1}{5}\right) ^ {3} \left(\frac {4}{5}\right) ^ {0} = \frac {1}{1 2 5},
> > $$
> >
> > 则随机变量 $X$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td>64/125</td><td>48/125</td><td>12/125</td><td>1/125</td></tr></table>
> >
> > (2)【问答题】到黑球 Y 的可能的取值为 0, 1, 2, Y 服从超几何分布,
> >
> > $$
> > P (Y = 0) = \frac {C _ {2} ^ {0} C _ {8} ^ {3}}{C _ {1 0} ^ {3}} = \frac {7}{1 5}, P (Y = 1) = \frac {C _ {2} ^ {1} C _ {8} ^ {2}}{C _ {1 0} ^ {3}} = \frac {7}{1 5}, P (Y = 2) = \frac {C _ {2} ^ {2} C _ {8} ^ {1}}{C _ {1 0} ^ {3}} = \frac {1}{1 5},
> > $$
> >
> > 则随机变量 $Y$ 的分布列为
> >
> > <table><tr><td>Y</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{7}{15}$ </td><td> $\frac{7}{15}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{15}$ </td></tr></table>
