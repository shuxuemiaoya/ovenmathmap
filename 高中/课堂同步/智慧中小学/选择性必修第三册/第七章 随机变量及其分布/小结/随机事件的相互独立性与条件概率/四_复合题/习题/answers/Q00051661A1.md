> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (2)【问答题】依题意 $X\sim B\left(3,\frac{1}{3}\right)$ ，则X的可能取值为0，1，2，3，
> >
> > 所以 $P(X=0)=\mathrm{C}_{3}^{0}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{0}\times\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{3}=\frac{8}{27};\quad P(X=1)=\mathrm{C}_{3}^{1}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{1}\times\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{2}=\frac{4}{9};$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {2} \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {1} = \frac {2}{9}; P (X = 3) = \mathrm{C} _ {3} ^ {3} \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {3} \times \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {0} = \frac {1}{2 7}.
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $X$ 的分布列为：
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>0</td><td>1</td><td>2</td><td>3</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{8}{27}$ </td><td> $\frac{4}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{2}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{27}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > 所以 $E(X)=3\times\frac{1}{3}=1$ .
