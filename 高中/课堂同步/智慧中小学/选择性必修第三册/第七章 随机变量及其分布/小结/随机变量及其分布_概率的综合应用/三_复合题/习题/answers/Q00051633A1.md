> [!faq]- 《随机变量及其分布_概率的综合应用》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设李华 “基础理论合格” 为事件 $A_{1}$ ，“基础理论补考后成绩合格” 为事件 $A_{2}$ ，
> >
> > “实践操作考试成绩合格”为事件 $B_{1}$ ，“实践操作考试补考后成绩合格”为事件 $B_{2}$ .
> >
> > 李华恰好3次考试通过的概率为： $P = P_{(A_1\overline{B_1} B_2)} + P_{(\overline{A_1} A_2B_1)} = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{5}{18}$
> >
> > (2)【问答题】 $\frac{8}{3}$
> >
> > 由题意知， $\xi$ 可能取得的值为：2，3，4， $P(\xi = 2) = P(A_1B_1) + P(\overline{A_1A_2}) = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{9}$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 3) = P (A _ {1} \overline {{B _ {1}}} B _ {2}) + P (\overline {{A _ {1}}} A _ {2} B _ {1}) + P (A _ {1} \overline {{B _ {1}}} \overline {{B _ {2}}}) = \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {4}{9}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (\xi = 4) = P (\overline {{A _ {1}}} A _ {2} \overline {{B _ {1}}} B _ {2}) + P (\overline {{A _ {1}}} A _ {2} \overline {{B _ {1}}} \overline {{B _ {2}}}) = \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1}{9}
> > $$
> >
> > 分布列（如表）
> >
> > <table><tr><td>ξ</td><td>2</td><td>3</td><td>4</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td> $\frac{4}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{4}{9}$ </td><td> $\frac{1}{9}$ </td></tr></table>
> >
> > 故 $E\xi = 2\times \frac{4}{9} +3\times \frac{4}{9} +4\times \frac{1}{9} = \frac{8}{3}.$
