>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 证明： $A_{n+1}^{m}-A_{n}^{m}=mA_{n}^{m-1}$
> >
> > 【正确答案】 $\because A_{n + 1}^{m} - A_{n}^{m} = \frac{(n + 1)!}{(n + 1 - m)!} -\frac{n!}{(n - m)!} = \frac{n!}{(n - m)!}\cdot \left(\frac{n + 1}{n + 1 - m} -1\right) = \frac{n!}{(n - m)!}\cdot \frac{m}{(n + 1 - m)} = m\cdot$ $\frac{n!}{(n + 1 - m)!} = mA_n^{m - 1},\quad \therefore A_{n + 1}^m -A_n^m = mA_n^{m - 1}.$
