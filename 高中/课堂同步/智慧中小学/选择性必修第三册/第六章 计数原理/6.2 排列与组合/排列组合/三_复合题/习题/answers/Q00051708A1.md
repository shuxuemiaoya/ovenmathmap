> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > C _ {8} ^ {5} + C _ {9 9} ^ {9 9} \cdot C _ {1 0 0} ^ {9 8} = C _ {8} ^ {3} + 1 \times C _ {1 0 0} ^ {2} = \frac {8 \times 7 \times 6}{3 \times 2 \times 1} + \frac {1 0 0 \times 9 9}{2 \times 1} = 5 6 + 4 9 5 0 = 5 0 0 6
> > $$

> > (3)【问答题】 $n^{2}+n$
> >
> > $C_{n}^{n - 1}\cdot C_{n + 1}^{n} = C_{n}^{n - 1}\cdot (C_{n}^{n} + C_{n}^{n - 1}) = C_{n}^{l}\cdot (1 + C_{n}^{l}) = n^{2} + n$ （或原式 $= C_n^I\cdot C_{n + 1}^I = n(n + 1) = n^2 +n$ ）.
