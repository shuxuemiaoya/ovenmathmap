> [!faq]- 《数列小结（2)》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n + 1} - a _ {n} = \frac {(n + 1) ^ {2}}{(n + 1) ^ {2} + 1} - \frac {n ^ {2}}{n ^ {2} + 1} = \frac {(n + 1) ^ {2} (n ^ {2} + 1) - n ^ {2} [ (n + 1) ^ {2} + 1 ]}{[ (n + 1) ^ {2} + 1 ] (n ^ {2} + 1)} = \frac {2 n + 1}{[ (n + 1) ^ {2} + 1 ] (n ^ {2} + 1)} > 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore a_{n} < a_{n + 1}, \therefore$ 数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是递增数列；
> >
> > (2)【问答题】数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 有界.
> >
> > 理由如下： $\because |a_n| = \left|\frac{n^2}{n^2 + 1}\right| = \left|1 - \frac{1}{n^2 + 1}\right| < 1$ ，即 $|a_n| < 1$ 恒成立， $\therefore$ 数列 $\{a_n\}$ 有界.
