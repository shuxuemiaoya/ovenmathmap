> >
> > $$
> > \because a _ {1} = 1, a _ {3} a _ {4} = \frac {1}{3 2}, \therefore q ^ {5} = \frac {1}{3 2}, \therefore q = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n - 1}; S _ {n} = \frac {1 - \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n}}{1 - \frac {1}{2}} = 2 - \left(\frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {n - 1}
> > $$
> >
> > (2)【问答题
> > 】解： $\because b_{n} = \log_{2}a_{n} = \log_{2}\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{n - 1} = 1 - n,$

> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {1}{b _ {n + 1} b _ {n + 2}} = \frac {1}{n (n + 1)} = \frac {1}{n} - \frac {1}{n + 1},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore T _ {1 0 0} = 1 - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3} + \dots + \frac {1}{1 0 0} - \frac {1}{1 0 1} = 1 - \frac {1}{1 0 1} = \frac {1 0 0}{1 0 1}.
