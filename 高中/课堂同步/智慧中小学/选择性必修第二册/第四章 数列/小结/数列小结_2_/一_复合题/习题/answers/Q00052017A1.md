> [!faq]- 《数列小结（2)》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {2} = 3 _ {a _ {1}} - 2,
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\because a_{2} + S_{2} = 31, \therefore a_{2} + a_{1} + a_{2} = 2a_{2} + a_{1} = 2(3a_{1} - 2) + a_{1} = 7a_{1} - 4 = 31,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {I} = 5, \quad \therefore a _ {I} - 2 ^ {I} = 3 \neq 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {a _ {n + 1} - 2 ^ {n + 1}}{a _ {n} - 2 ^ {n}} = \frac {3 a _ {n} - 2 ^ {n} - 2 ^ {n + 1}}{a _ {n} - 2 ^ {n}} = \frac {3 (a _ {n} - 2 ^ {n})}{a _ {n} - 2 ^ {n}} = 3,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \{a_{n} - 2^{n}\}$ 是首项为 $a_{1} - 2^{1} = 3$ ，公比为3的等比数列；
> >
> > (2)【问答题】由
> > （1）可知 $a_{n} - 2^{n} = 3^{n}$ ，即 $a_{n} = 2^{n} + 3^{n}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {n} = \frac {2 (1 - 2 ^ {n})}{1 - 2} + \frac {3 (1 - 3 ^ {n})}{1 - 3} = 2 ^ {n + 1} + \frac {3 ^ {n + 1}}{2} - \frac {7}{2}.
> > $$
