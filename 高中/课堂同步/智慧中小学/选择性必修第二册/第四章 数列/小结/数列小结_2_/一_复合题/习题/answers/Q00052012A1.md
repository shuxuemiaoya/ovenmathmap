> [!faq]- 《数列小结（2)》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 】解：由题意，设等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的公差为 $d$ ，则 $3a_{I} + 3d = 18,3a_{I} + 12d = 54$ ，解得 $a_{I} = 2,d = 4$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a _ {n} = 2 + 4 (n - 1) = 4 n - 2, n \in N ^ {*};
> > $$
> >
> > (2)【问答题
> > 】解： $\because b_{n}=\frac{4}{a_{n}\cdot a_{n+1}}=\frac{4}{(4n-2)(4n+2)}=\frac{1}{(2n-1)(2n+1)}=\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{2n-1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right),\quad\therefore S_{n}=\frac{1}{2}$ $\left[\left(1-\frac{1}{3}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{5}\right)+\left(\frac{1}{5}-\frac{1}{7}\right)+\cdots+\left(\frac{1}{2n-1}-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)\right]=\frac{1}{2}\left(1-\frac{1}{2n+1}\right)=\frac{n}{2n+1}.$ 【提示】
