> [!faq]- 《数列概念2》答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 可得 $\frac{a_{n+1}}{2^{n+1}} -\frac{a_n}{2^n} = 1,$
> >
> > 则数列 $\left\{\frac{a_n}{2^n}\right\}$ 是首项为 $\frac{a_1}{2} = 1$ ，公差为1的等差数列，
> >
> > 则 $\frac{a_n}{2^n} = 1 + n - 1 = n$ ，即 $a_{n} = n\cdot 2^{n}$
> >
> > (2)【问答题】 $b_{n}=\frac{n+2}{na_{n+1}}=\frac{n+2}{n(n+1)\cdot2^{n+1}}=\frac{1}{n\cdot2^{n}}-\frac{1}{(n+1)\cdot2^{n+1}}$
> >
> > $T_{n} = \frac{1}{1\cdot 2} -\frac{1}{2\cdot 2^{2}} +\frac{1}{2\cdot 2^{2}} -\frac{1}{3\cdot 2^{3}} +\dots +\frac{1}{n\cdot 2^{n}} -\frac{1}{(n + 1)\cdot 2^{n + 1}} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot \frac{1}{(n + 1)\cdot 2^{n + 1}}.$
