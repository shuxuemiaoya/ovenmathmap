> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 直线$l$的方程为$\frac{x}{a} + \frac{y}{b}$*=*1,即*bx+ay-ab=*0*.*
> > 由点到直线的距离公式,且*a\>*1,得点(1,0)到直线$l$的距离$d_{1}$*=*$\frac{b\text{(}a\text{-}1\text{)}}{\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}}}$,点(*-*1,0)到直线$l$的距离$d_{2}$*=*$\frac{b\text{(}a + 1\text{)}}{\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}}}$,∴*s=d*~1~*+d*~2~*=*$\frac{2ab}{\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}}} = \frac{2ab}{c}$*.*
> > 由$s$≥$\frac{4}{5}$*c*,得$\frac{2ab}{c} \geq \frac{4}{5}$*c*,即5*a*$\sqrt{c^{2}\text{-}a^{2}}$≥2$c^{2}$*.*
> > ∵*e=*$\frac{c}{a}$,∴5$\sqrt{e^{2}\text{-}1}$≥2$e^{2}$,∴25($e^{2}$*-*1)≥4$e^{4}$,即4$e^{4}$*-*25$e^{2}$*+*25≤0,
> > ∴$\frac{5}{4}$≤$e^{2}$≤5(*e\>*1)*.*∴$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$≤$e$≤$\sqrt{5}$,即$e$的取值范围为$\left\lbrack \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\text{,}\sqrt{5} \right\rbrack$*.*
