> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 不妨设$A$($a$,0),点$P$在第一象限内,由题意知,点$P$的横坐标是$\frac{a}{2}$,
> > 设*P*$\left( \frac{a}{2}\text{,}y \right)$,由点$P$在椭圆上,得$\frac{\left( \frac{a}{2} \right)^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}$*=*1,$y^{2}$*=*$\frac{3}{4}$$b^{2}$,即*P*$\left( \frac{a}{2},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}b \right)$*.*
> > 因为∠*OPA=*120°,所以∠*POA=*30°,故tan∠*POA=*$\frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}b}{\frac{a}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$,所以*a=*3*b*,
> > 所以*e=*$\frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{a^{2}\text{-}b^{2}}}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{\text{(}3b\text{)}^{2}\text{-}b^{2}}}{3b} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$*.*
