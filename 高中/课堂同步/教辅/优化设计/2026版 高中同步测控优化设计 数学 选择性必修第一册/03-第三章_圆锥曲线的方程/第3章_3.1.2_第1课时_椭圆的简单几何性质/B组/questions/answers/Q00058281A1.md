> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设$P$($x$,$y$),由∠*APO=*90°知,点$P$在以*OA*为直径的圆上*.*
> > 圆的方程是$\left( x\text{-}\frac{a}{2} \right)^{2}$*+y*^2^*=*$\left( \frac{a}{2} \right)^{2}$,
> > ∴$y^{2}$*=ax-x*^2^*.*①
> > 又点$P$在椭圆上,故$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}$*=*1*.*②
> > 把①代入②得$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{ax\text{-}x^{2}}{b^{2}}$*=*1,∴($a^{2}$*-b*^2^)$x^{2}$*-a*^3^*x+a*^2^$b^{2}$*=*0*.*故(*x-a*)\[($a^{2}$*-b*^2^)*x-ab*^2^\]*=*0*.*
> > 又$x$≠$a$,$x$≠0,∴*x=*$\frac{ab^{2}}{a^{2}\text{-}b^{2}}$*.*
> > ∵0*\<x\<a*,∴0*\<*$\frac{ab^{2}}{a^{2}\text{-}b^{2}}$*\<a*,∴2$b^{2}$*\<a*^2^,∴$a^{2}$*\<*2$c^{2}$*.*
> > ∴*e\>*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*.*又0*\<e\<*1,故所求的椭圆离心率的取值范围是$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*\<e\<*1*.*
