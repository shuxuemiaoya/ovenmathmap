> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)若∠$F_{1}$*AB=*90°,则△$AOF_{2}$为等腰直角三角形,
> > *OA=OF*~2~,即*b=c.*所以*a=*$\sqrt{2}$*c*,*e=*$\frac{c}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*.*
> > (2)由题知$A$(0,$b$),$F_{1}$(*-c*,0),$F_{2}$($c$,0),其中,*c=*$\sqrt{a^{2}\text{-}b^{2}}$,设$B$($x$,$y$)*.*
> > 由$\overrightarrow{AF_{2}}$*=*2$\overrightarrow{F_{2}B}$⇔($c$,*-b*)*=*2(*x-c*,$y$),解得*x=*$\frac{3c}{2}$,*y=-*$\frac{b}{2}$,即*B*$\left( \frac{3c}{2}\text{,-}\frac{b}{2} \right)$*.*
> > 将点$B$坐标代入$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}$*=*1,得$\frac{\frac{9}{4}c^{2}}{a^{2}} + \frac{\frac{b^{2}}{4}}{b^{2}}$*=*1,即$\frac{9c^{2}}{4a^{2}} + \frac{1}{4}$*=*1,解得$a^{2}$*=*3$c^{2}$*.*①
> > 又由$\overrightarrow{AF_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(*-c*,*-b*)·$\left( \frac{3c}{2}\text{,-}\frac{3b}{2} \right) = \frac{3}{2}$⇒$b^{2}$*-c*^2^*=*1,即有$a^{2}$*-*2$c^{2}$*=*1*.*②
> > 由①②解得$c^{2}$*=*1,$a^{2}$*=*3,从而有$b^{2}$*=*2*.*
> > 椭圆方程为$\frac{x^{2}}{3} + \frac{y^{2}}{2}$*=*1*.*
