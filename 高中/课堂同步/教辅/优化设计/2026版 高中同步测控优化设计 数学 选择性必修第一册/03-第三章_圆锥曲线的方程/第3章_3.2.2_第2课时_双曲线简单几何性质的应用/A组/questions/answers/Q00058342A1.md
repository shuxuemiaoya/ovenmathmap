> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{x^{2}}{2} - \frac{y^{2}}{5}$*=*1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知中点坐标为$\left( - \frac{2}{3}, - \frac{5}{3} \right)$,设双曲线方程为$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{y^{2}}{7\text{-}a^{2}}$*=*1,$M$($x_{1}$,$y_{1}$),$N$($x_{2}$,$y_{2}$),
> > 则$\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{y_{1}^{2}}{7\text{-}a^{2}}$*=*1,①
> > $\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{y_{2}^{2}}{7\text{-}a^{2}}$*=*1,②
> > ①*-*②,得$\frac{\text{(}x_{1} + x_{2}\text{)(}x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}\text{)}}{a^{2}} = \frac{\text{(}y_{1} + y_{2}\text{)(}y_{1}\text{-}y_{2}\text{)}}{7\text{-}a^{2}}$,
> > 即$\frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{y_{1} + y_{2}} = \frac{a^{2}}{7\text{-}a^{2}} \cdot \frac{y_{1}\text{-}y_{2}}{x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}}$,得$\frac{\text{-}\frac{4}{3}}{\text{-}\frac{10}{3}} = \frac{a^{2}}{7\text{-}a^{2}}$,解得$a^{2}$*=*2,故双曲线方程为$\frac{x^{2}}{2} - \frac{y^{2}}{5}$*=*1*.*
