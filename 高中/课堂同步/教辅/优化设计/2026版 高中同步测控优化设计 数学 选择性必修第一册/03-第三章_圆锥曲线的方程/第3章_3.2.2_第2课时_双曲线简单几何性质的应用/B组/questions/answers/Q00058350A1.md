> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)设双曲线$C$的方程为$\frac{x^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{y^{2}}{b^{2}}$*=*1(*a\>*0,*b\>*0),
> > 由已知得*a=*$\sqrt{3}$,*c=*2*.*又因为$a^{2}$*+b*^2^*=c*^2^,所以$b^{2}$*=*1,故双曲线$C$的方程为$\frac{x^{2}}{3}$*-y*^2^*=*1*.*
> > (2)将*y=kx+*$\sqrt{2}$代入$\frac{x^{2}}{3}$*-y*^2^*=*1中,得(1*-*3$k^{2}$)$x^{2}$*-*6$\sqrt{2}$*kx-*9*=*0*.*
> > 由直线$l$与双曲线$C$交于$A$,$B$两点得,$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 1\text{-}3k^{2} \neq 0\text{,} \\
> > \Delta = \text{(-}6\sqrt{2}k\text{)}^{2} + 36\text{(}1\text{-}3k^{2}\text{)} > 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 即$k^{2}$≠$\frac{1}{3}$,且$k^{2}$*\<*1*.*①
> > 设$A$(*x~A~*,*y~A~*),$B$(*x~B~*,*y~B~*),
> > 则*x~A~+x~B~=*$\frac{6\sqrt{2}k}{1\text{-}3k^{2}}$,*x~A~x~B~=*$\frac{\text{-}9}{1\text{-}3k^{2}}$,
> > 由$\overrightarrow{OA} \cdot \overrightarrow{OB}$*\>*2,得*x~A~x~B~+y~A~y~B~\>*2,
> > 而*x~A~x~B~+y~A~y~B~=x~A~x~B~+*(*kx~A~+*$\sqrt{2}$)(*kx~B~+*$\sqrt{2}$)*=*($k^{2}$*+*1)*x~A~x~B~+*$\sqrt{2}$*k*(*x~A~+x~B~*)*+*2*=*($k^{2}$*+*1)·$\frac{\text{-}9}{1\text{-}3k^{2}} + \frac{\sqrt{2}k\text{·}6\sqrt{2}k}{1\text{-}3k^{2}}$*+*2*=*$\frac{3k^{2} + 7}{3k^{2}\text{-}1}$,
> > 于是$\frac{3k^{2} + 7}{3k^{2}\text{-}1}$*\>*2,解此不等式得$\frac{1}{3}$*\<k*^2^*\<*3*.*②
> > 由①②得$\frac{1}{3}$*\<k*^2^*\<*1*.*
> > 故$k$的取值范围是$\left( \text{-}1\text{,-}\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \right) \cup \left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\text{,}1 \right)$*.*
