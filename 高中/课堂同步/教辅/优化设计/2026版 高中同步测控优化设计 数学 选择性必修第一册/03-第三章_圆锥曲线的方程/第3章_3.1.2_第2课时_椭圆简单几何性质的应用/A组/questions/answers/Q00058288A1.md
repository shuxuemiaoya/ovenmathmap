> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** *x+*2*y-*3*=*0
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 易知此弦所在直线的斜率存在,所以设斜率为$k$,弦的端点坐标为($x_{1}$,$y_{1}$),($x_{2}$,$y_{2}$),
> > 则$\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{4} + \frac{y_{1}^{2}}{2}$*=*1,①
> > $\frac{x_{2}^{2}}{4} + \frac{y_{2}^{2}}{2}$*=*1,②
> > ①*-*②,得$\frac{\text{(}x_{1} + x_{2}\text{)(}x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}\text{)}}{4} + \frac{\text{(}y_{1} + y_{2}\text{)(}y_{1}\text{-}y_{2}\text{)}}{2}$*=*0*.*
> > ∵$x_{1}$*+x*~2~*=*2,$y_{1}$*+y*~2~*=*2,∴$\frac{x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}}{2}$*+y*~1~*-y*~2~*=*0,
> > ∴*k=*$\frac{y_{1}\text{-}y_{2}}{x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > ∴此弦所在的直线方程为*y-*1*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$(*x-*1),即*x+*2*y-*3*=*0*.*
