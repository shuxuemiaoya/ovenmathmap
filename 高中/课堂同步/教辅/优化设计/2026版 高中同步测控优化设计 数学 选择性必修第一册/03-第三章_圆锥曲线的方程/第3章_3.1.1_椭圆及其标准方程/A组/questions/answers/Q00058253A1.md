> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left\{ m\left| m > \frac{1}{2}\text{,}\text{且}m \neq 1 \right.\  \right\}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由方程$\frac{x^{2}}{m} + \frac{y^{2}}{2m\text{-}1}$*=*1表示椭圆,知$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > m > 0\text{,} \\
> > 2m\text{-}1 > 0\text{,} \\
> > m \neq 2m\text{-}1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得*m\>*$\frac{1}{2}$,且$m$≠1*.*
