> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设所求抛物线的标准方程为$x^{2}$*=*2*py*(*p\>*0),
> > 设$A$($x_{0}$,$y_{0}$),由题知*M*$\left( 0\text{,-}\frac{p}{2} \right)$*.*
> > ∵*\|AF\|=*3,∴$y_{0}$*+*$\frac{p}{2}$*=*3,
> > ∵*\|AM\|=*$\sqrt{17}$,∴$x_{0}^{2} + \left( y_{0} + \frac{p}{2} \right)^{2}$*=*17,
> > ∴$x_{0}^{2}$*=*8,代入方程$x_{0}^{2}$*=*2$py_{0}$,得8*=*2*p*$\left( 3\text{-}\frac{p}{2} \right)$,解得*p=*2或*p=*4*.*
> > ∴所求抛物线的标准方程为$x^{2}$*=*4*y*或$x^{2}$*=*8*y.*
