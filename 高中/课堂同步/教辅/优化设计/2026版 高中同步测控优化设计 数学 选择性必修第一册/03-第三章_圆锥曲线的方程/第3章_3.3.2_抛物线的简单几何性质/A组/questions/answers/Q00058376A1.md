> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{5\sqrt{2}}{4}　\left( \frac{1}{2}\text{,}1 \right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设点$P$($x_{0}$,$y_{0}$)是$y^{2}$*=*2*x*上任一点,则点$P$到直线*x-y+*3*=*0的距离为*d=*$\frac{\text{|}x_{0}\text{-}y_{0} + 3\text{|}}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\left| \frac{y_{0}^{2}}{2}\text{-}y_{0} + 3 \right|}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\text{|(}y_{0}\text{-}1\text{)}^{2} + 5\text{|}}{2\sqrt{2}}$,当$y_{0}$*=*1时,$d_{min}$*=*$\frac{5}{2\sqrt{2}} = \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{4}$,此时$x_{0}$*=*$\frac{1}{2}$,故点$P$的坐标为$\left( \frac{1}{2}\text{,}1 \right)$*.*
