> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设直线与抛物线的交点为$A$($x_{1}$,$y_{1}$),$B$($x_{2}$,$y_{2}$)*.*
> > 由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > y^{2} = 2mx\text{,} \\
> > y = x\text{-}4\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$得$x^{2}$*-*2(4*+m*)*x+*16*=*0,
> > $x_{1}$*+x*~2~*=*2(4*+m*),$x_{1}$$x_{2}$*=*16,
> > 所以弦长为$\sqrt{\text{(}1 + k^{2}\text{)(}x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}\text{)}^{2}}$
> > *=*$\sqrt{2\text{[}4\text{(}4 + m\text{)}^{2}\text{-}4 \times 16\text{]}}$
> > *=*2$\sqrt{2\text{(}m^{2} + 8m\text{)}}$*.*
> > 由2$\sqrt{2\text{(}m^{2} + 8m\text{)}}$*=*6$\sqrt{2}$,
> > 得*m=*1或*m=-*9*.*
> > 经检验,*m=*1或*m=-*9均符合题意*.*
> > 故所求抛物线的标准方程为$y^{2}$*=*2*x*或$y^{2}$*=-*18*x.*
