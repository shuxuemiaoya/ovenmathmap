> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** 3*x-*2*y+*12*=*0
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设$A$($a$,0),$B$(0,$b$)*.*
> > 由题意得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \frac{a + 0}{2} = \text{-}2\text{,} \\
> > \frac{0 + b}{2} = 3\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = \text{-}4\text{,} \\
> > b = 6\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 因此,直线$l$的方程为$\frac{x}{\text{-}4} + \frac{y}{6}$*=*1,即3*x-*2*y+*12*=*0*.*
