> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设圆$C_{2}$的圆心$C_{2}$($x$,$y$),则$C_{2}$($x$,$y$)与圆$C_{1}$的圆心(*-*1,1)关于直线*x-y-*1*=*0对称,
> > 从而$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \frac{y\text{-}1}{x + 1} \times 1 = \text{-}1\text{,} \\
> > \frac{x\text{-}1}{2}\text{-}\frac{y + 1}{2}\text{-}1 = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = 2\text{,} \\
> > y = \text{-}2\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$即$C_{2}$(2,*-*2)*.*
> > 圆$C_{2}$的半径等于圆$C_{1}$的半径,即为1*.*
> > 故圆$C_{2}$的方程为(*x-*2)^2^*+*(*y+*2)^2^*=*1*.*
