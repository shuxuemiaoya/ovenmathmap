> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为圆心在直线$l_{1}$上,所以设圆心坐标为$C$($a$,*a-*1),半径为*r.*
> > 点$C$到直线$l_{2}$的距离$d_{1}$*=*$\frac{\text{|}4a + 3\text{(}a\text{-}1\text{)} + 14\text{|}}{5} = \frac{\text{|}7a + 11\text{|}}{5}$*.*
> > 点$C$到直线$l_{3}$的距离$d_{2}$*=*$\frac{\text{|}3a + 4\text{(}a\text{-}1\text{)} + 10\text{|}}{5} = \frac{\text{|}7a + 6\text{|}}{5}$*.*
> > 由题意得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \frac{\text{|}7a + 11\text{|}}{5} = r\text{,} \\
> > \left( \frac{\text{|}7a + 6\text{|}}{5} \right)^{2} + 3^{2} = r^{2}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*a=*2,*r=*5*.*
> > 故所求圆的方程是(*x-*2)^2^*+*(*y-*1)^2^*=*25*.*
