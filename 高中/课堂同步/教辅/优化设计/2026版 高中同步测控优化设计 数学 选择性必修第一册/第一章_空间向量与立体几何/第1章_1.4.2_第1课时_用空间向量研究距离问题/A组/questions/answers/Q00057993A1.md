> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 以$D$为原点,建立空间直角坐标系,如图所示,
> > ![id:2147514543;FounderCES](../../images/image9.jpeg)
> > 则$A_{1}$(1,0,1),$D_{1}$(0,0,1),$D$(0,0,0),$A$(1,0,0),$C$(0,2,0)*.*
> > (1)证明:设$E$(1,$y$,0)(0≤$y$≤2),
> > 则$\overrightarrow{D_{1}E}$*=*(1,$y$,*-*1),$\overrightarrow{A_{1}D}$*=*(*-*1,0,*-*1)*.*
> > 由于$\overrightarrow{D_{1}E} \cdot \overrightarrow{A_{1}D}$*=*0,故$D_{1}$*E*⊥$A_{1}$*D.*
> > (2)$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(*-*1,2,0),$\overrightarrow{D_{1}A}$*=*(1,0,*-*1)*.*
> > 设平面$ACD_{1}$的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{AC} = 0\text{,} \\
> > \mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{D_{1}A} = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 即\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \text{-}x + 2y = 0\text{,} \\
> > x\text{-}z = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > y = \frac{x}{2}\text{,} \\
> > z = x\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 可取**$n$*=*(2,1,2)*.*
> > 当$E$为*AB*的中点时,$E$(1,1,0),则$\overrightarrow{AE}$*=*(0,1,0),
> > 所以点$E$到平面$ACD_{1}$的距离为$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{AE}\text{·}n\text{|}}{\text{|}n\text{|}} = \frac{1}{3}$*.*
