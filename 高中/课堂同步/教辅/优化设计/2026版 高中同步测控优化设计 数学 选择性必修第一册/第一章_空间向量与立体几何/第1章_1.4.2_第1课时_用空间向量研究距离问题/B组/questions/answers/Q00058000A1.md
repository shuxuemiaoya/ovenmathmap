> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 直线*PA*上存在点$M$满足题意*.*以$A$为原点,*AB*,*AD*,*AP*所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,
> > 建立如图所示的空间直角坐标系,则$A$(0,0,0),$P$(0,0,2),$C$(1,1,0),$D$(0,2,0),
> > ![id:2147514620;FounderCES](../../images/image20.jpeg)
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{PC}$*=*(1,1,*-*2),$\overrightarrow{PD}$*=*(0,2,*-*2)*.*
> > 设$M$(0,0,$z_{0}$)(0≤$z_{0}$≤2),又设平面*PCD*的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > n\text{·}\overrightarrow{PC} = 0\text{,} \\
> > n\text{·}\overrightarrow{PD} = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 即\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x + y\text{-}2z = 0\text{,} \\
> > 2y\text{-}2z = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = z\text{,} \\
> > y = z\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 取*z=*1,则*x=*1,*y=*1*.*所以,**$n$*=*(1,1,1)是平面*PCD*的一个法向量*.*
> > 又因为$\overrightarrow{MP}$*=*(0,0,2*-z*~0~),所以点$M$到平面*PCD*的距离*d=*$\frac{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{MP}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{|}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$(2*-z*~0~)*.*
> > 令*d=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$,可得$z_{0}$*=*1*.*
> > 所以存在点$M$,且$M$(0,0,1)是线段*AP*的中点*.*
> > 综上可知,线段*AP*的中点到平面*PCD*的距离为$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$*.*
