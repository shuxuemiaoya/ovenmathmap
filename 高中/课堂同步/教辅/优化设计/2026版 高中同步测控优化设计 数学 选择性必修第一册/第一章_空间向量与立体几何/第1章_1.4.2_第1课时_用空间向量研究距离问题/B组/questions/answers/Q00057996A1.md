> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知$OO_{1}$⊥平面*ABCD*,所以$OO_{1}$⊥*OA*,$OO_{1}$⊥*OB.*
> > 又*OA*⊥*OB*,则以$O$为原点,建立空间直角坐标系*Oxyz*,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147514557;FounderCES](../../images/image11.jpeg)
> > 因为底面*ABCD*是边长为4,∠*DAB=*60°的菱形,
> > 所以*OA=*2$\sqrt{3}$,*OB=*2*.*
> > 则$A$(2$\sqrt{3}$,0,0),$B$(0,2,0),$C$(*-*2$\sqrt{3}$,0,0),$O_{1}$(0,0,3),
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{O_{1}B}$*=*(0,2,*-*3),$\overrightarrow{O_{1}C}$*=*(*-*2$\sqrt{3}$,0,*-*3)*.*
> > 设平面$O_{1}$*BC*的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),
> > 则**$n$**⊥$\overrightarrow{O_{1}B}$,**$n$**⊥$\overrightarrow{O_{1}C}$,
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2y\text{-}3z = 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}2\sqrt{3}x\text{-}3z = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > y = \frac{3}{2}z\text{,} \\
> > x = \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}z\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以可取**$n$*=*(*-*$\sqrt{3}$,3,2)*.*
> > 因为$E$是$O_{1}$*A*的中点,
> > 所以*E*$\left( \sqrt{3}\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{3}{2} \right)$*.*
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{EO_{1}} = \left( \text{-}\sqrt{3}\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{3}{2} \right)$*.*
> > 所以点$E$到平面$O_{1}$*BC*的距离为$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{EO_{1}}\text{·}\mathbf{n}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{|}} = \frac{3}{2}$*.*
