> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** 2$\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 建立空间直角坐标系*Axyz*,如图所示,
> > ![id:2147514578;FounderCES](../../images/image14.jpeg)
> > 则$A$(0,0,0),$B$(6,0,0),$C_{1}$(4,2,2$\sqrt{2}$),$D$(0,4,0),
> > 于是$\overrightarrow{BA}$*=*(*-*6,0,0),$\overrightarrow{BC_{1}}$*=*(*-*2,2,2$\sqrt{2}$)*.*
> > 向量$\overrightarrow{BA}$的单位向量**$u$*=*(*-*1,0,0),取**$a$*=*$\overrightarrow{BC_{1}}$*=*(*-*2,2,2$\sqrt{2}$)*.*
> > 所以点$C_{1}$到直线*AB*的距离为$\sqrt{\mathbf{a}^{2}\text{-(}\mathbf{a}\text{·}\mathbf{u}\text{)}^{2}}$*=*2$\sqrt{3}$*.*
