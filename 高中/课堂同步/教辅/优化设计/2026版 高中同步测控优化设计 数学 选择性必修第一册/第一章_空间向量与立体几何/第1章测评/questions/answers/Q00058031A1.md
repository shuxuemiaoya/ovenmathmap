>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵平面*PAB*⊥平面*ABCD*,平面*PAB*∩平面*ABCD=AB*,*AB*⊥*PA*,且*PA*⊂平面*PAB*,
> > ∴*PA*⊥平面*ABCD.*
> > 又*AB*⊥*AD*,
> > ∴可建立空间直角坐标系*Axyz*,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147515141;FounderCES](images/image14.jpeg)
> > 设*BC=*4,*AP=λ*(*λ\>*0),则$A$(0,0,0),$D$(0,2,0),$E$(2,1,0),$C$(2,4,0),$P$(0,0,*λ*)*.*
> > (1)证明:∵$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(2,4,0),$\overrightarrow{AP}$*=*(0,0,*λ*),$\overrightarrow{DE}$*=*(2,*-*1,0),
> > ∴$\overrightarrow{DE} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC}$*=*4*-*4*+*0*=*0,$\overrightarrow{DE} \cdot \overrightarrow{AP}$*=*0*.*
> > ∴*DE*⊥*AC*,*DE*⊥*AP.*
> > 又*AC*∩*AP=A*,
> > ∴*DE*⊥平面*PAC.*
> > 又*DE*⊂平面*PED*,
> > ∴平面*PED*⊥平面*PAC.*
> > (2)由(1)知,平面*PAC*的一个法向量是$\overrightarrow{DE}$*=*(2,*-*1,0),$\overrightarrow{PE}$*=*(2,1,*-λ*)*.*
> > 设直线*PE*与平面*PAC*所成的角为*θ*,
> > 则sin *θ=\|*cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{PE},\overrightarrow{DE}$*\>\|=*$\left| \frac{4\text{-}1}{\sqrt{5} \times \sqrt{5 + \lambda^{2}}} \right| = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$,解得*λ=*±2*.*
> > ∵*λ\>*0,
> > ∴*λ=*2,即$P$(0,0,2)*.*
> > 设平面*PCD*的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),则**$n$**·$\overrightarrow{DC}$*=*0,**$n$**·$\overrightarrow{DP}$*=*0*.*
> > ∵$\overrightarrow{DC}$*=*(2,2,0),$\overrightarrow{DP}$*=*(0,*-*2,2),
> > ∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2x + 2y = 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}2y + 2z = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > ∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > y = \text{-}x\text{,} \\
> > z = \text{-}x\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 取**$n$*=*(1,*-*1,*-*1),
> > 则cos*\<*$n$**,$\overrightarrow{DE}$*\>=*$\frac{\mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{DE}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{||}\overrightarrow{DE}\text{|}} = \frac{2 + 1}{\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{5}} = \frac{\sqrt{15}}{5}$*.*
> > 设平面*PAC*与平面*PCD*的夹角为*θ*,则cos *θ=\|*cos*\<*$n$**,$\overrightarrow{DE}$*\>\|=*$\frac{\sqrt{15}}{5}$*.*
> > ∴平面*PAC*与平面*PCD*的夹角的余弦值为$\frac{\sqrt{15}}{5}$*.*
