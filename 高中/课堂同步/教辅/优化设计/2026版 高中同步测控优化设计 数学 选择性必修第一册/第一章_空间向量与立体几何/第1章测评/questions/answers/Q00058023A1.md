> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 取*AD*的中点$O$,连接*OP*,*OC.*
> > ![id:2147515064;FounderCES](images/image3.jpeg)
> > 由题意知*OP*⊥*AD*,因为平面*PAD*⊥平面*ABCD*,平面*PAD*∩平面*ABCD=AD*,所以*OP*⊥平面*ABCD.*以$O$为原点,*OD*所在直线为$x$轴,过点$O$,平行于*DC*的直线为$y$轴,*OP*所在直线为$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系,如图所示*.*设*AD=*2*a*,*AB=*2*b*,则$P$(0,0,$\sqrt{3}$*a*),$C$($a$,2*b*,0),于是$\overrightarrow{OC}$*=*($a$,2*b*,0),$\overrightarrow{PC}$*=*($a$,2*b*,*-*$\sqrt{3}$*a*)*.*
> > ∵*OP*⊥平面*ABCD*,
> > ∴*PC*与平面*ABCD*所成的角即∠*PCO.*
> > ∴cos∠*PCO=*$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{OC}\text{·}\overrightarrow{PC}\text{|}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{OC}\text{||}\overrightarrow{PC}\text{|}} = \frac{a^{2} + 4b^{2}}{\sqrt{a^{2} + 4b^{2}} \times \sqrt{4a^{2} + 4b^{2}}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$,可得*b=*$\sqrt{2}$*a.*
> > ∴$\frac{AB}{AD} = \frac{2b}{2a} = \sqrt{2}$*.*
