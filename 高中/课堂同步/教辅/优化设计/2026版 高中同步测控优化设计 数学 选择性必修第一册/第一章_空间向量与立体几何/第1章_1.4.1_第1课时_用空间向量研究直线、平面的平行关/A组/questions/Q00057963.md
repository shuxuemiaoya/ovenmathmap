9. 如图,*ABEDFC*为多面体,平面*ABED*与平面*ACFD*垂直,点$O$在线段*AD*上, *OA=*1,*OD=*2,△*OAB*,△*OAC*,△*ODE*,△*ODF*都是正三角形*.*求证:直线*BC*∥*EF.*
![id:2147513840;FounderCES](../images/image2.jpeg)
证明:过点$F$作*FQ*⊥*AD*,交*AD*于点$Q$,连接*QE.*
由平面*ABED*⊥平面*ACFD*,平面*ABED*∩平面*ACFD=AD*,*FQ*⊂平面*ACFD*,得*FQ*⊥平面*ABED.*因为△*ODF*,△*ODE*是正三角形,所以$Q$为*OD*的中点,所以*EQ*⊥*OD.*以$Q$为原点,$\overrightarrow{QE},\overrightarrow{QD},\overrightarrow{QF}$的方向分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴的正方向,建立空间直角坐标系,如图所示*.*
![id:2147513847;FounderCES](../images/image3.jpeg)
由已知得$E$($\sqrt{3}$,0,0),$F$(0,0, $\sqrt{3}$),*B*$\left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, - \frac{3}{2},0 \right)$,*C*$\left( 0\text{,-}\frac{3}{2}\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)$,
所以$\overrightarrow{BC} = \left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right),\overrightarrow{EF}$*=*(*-*$\sqrt{3}$,0, $\sqrt{3}$)*.*
因为$\overrightarrow{EF}$*=*2$\overrightarrow{BC}$,且$B$∉*EF*,所以*BC*∥*EF.*
10. 在长方体*ABCD-A*~1~$B_{1}$$C_{1}$$D_{1}$中,*DA=*2,*DC=*3,$DD_{1}$*=*4,$M$,$N$,$E$,$F$分别为棱$A_{1}$$D_{1}$,$A_{1}$$B_{1}$,$D_{1}$$C_{1}$,$B_{1}$$C_{1}$的中点*.*求证:平面*AMN*∥平面*EFBD.*
证明:(方法一)建立空间直角坐标系*Dxyz*,如图所示,
则$A$(2,0,0),$B$(2,3,0),$M$(1,0,4),$N$(2,$\frac{3}{2}$,4),$E$(0,$\frac{3}{2}$,4),$F$(1,3,4),
![id:2147513854;FounderCES](../images/image4.jpeg)
∴$\overrightarrow{MN} = \left( 1\text{,}\frac{3}{2}\text{,}0 \right),\overrightarrow{EF} = \left( 1\text{,}\frac{3}{2}\text{,}0 \right),\overrightarrow{AM}$*=*(*-*1,0,4),$\overrightarrow{BF}$*=*(*-*1,0,4)*.*
∵$\overrightarrow{MN} = \overrightarrow{EF}$,
∴*MN*∥*EF.*
又*EF*⊂平面*EFBD*,*MN*⊄平面*EFBD*,
∴*MN*∥平面*EFBD.*
同理,可证*AM*∥平面*EFBD.*
又*MN*⊂平面*AMN*,*AM*⊂平面*AMN*,且*MN*∩*AM=M*,
∴平面*AMN*∥平面*EFBD.*
(方法二)建立空间直角坐标系*Dxyz*,如方法一,
则$A$(2,0,0),$M$(1,0,4),*N*$\left( 2\text{,}\frac{3}{2}\text{,}4 \right)$,$D$(0,0,0),*E*$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{3}{2}\text{,}4 \right)$,$F$(1,3,4),
∴$\overrightarrow{AM}$*=*(*-*1,0,4),$\overrightarrow{AN} = \left( 0\text{,}\frac{3}{2}\text{,}4 \right),\overrightarrow{DE} = \left( 0\text{,}\frac{3}{2}\text{,}4 \right),\overrightarrow{DF}$*=*(1,3,4)*.*
设平面*AMN*的法向量为**$n$**~1~*=*($x_{1}$,$y_{1}$,$z_{1}$),则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
n_{1}\text{·}\overrightarrow{AM} = 0\text{,} \\
n_{1}\text{·}\overrightarrow{AN} = 0\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
\text{-}x_{1} + 4z_{1} = 0\text{,} \\
\frac{3}{2}y_{1} + 4z_{1} = 0\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\ \therefore\left\{ \begin{matrix}
x_{1} = 4z_{1}\text{,} \\
y_{1} = \text{-}\frac{8}{3}z_{1}\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
∴可取**$n$**~1~*=*$\left( 1\text{,-}\frac{2}{3}\text{,}\frac{1}{4} \right)$*.*
∴**$n$**~1~*=*$\left( 1\text{,-}\frac{2}{3}\text{,}\frac{1}{4} \right)$为平面*AMN*的一个法向量*.*
同理,可得平面*EFBD*的一个法向量为**$n$**~2~*=*$\left( \frac{3}{2}\text{,-}1\text{,}\frac{3}{8} \right)$*.*
∵**$n$**~1~*=*$\frac{2}{3}$**$n$**~2~,∴**$n$**~1~∥**$n$**~2~*.*
∴平面*AMN*∥平面*EFBD.*
