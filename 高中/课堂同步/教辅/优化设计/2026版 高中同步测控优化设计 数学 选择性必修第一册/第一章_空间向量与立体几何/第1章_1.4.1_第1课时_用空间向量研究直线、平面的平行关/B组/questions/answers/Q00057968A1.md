> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\text{,}1 \right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图,设*AC*与*BD*相交于点$O$,连接*OE.*
> > ![id:2147513868;FounderCES](../../images/image6.jpeg)
> > 因为*AM*∥平面*BDE*,且*AM*⊂平面*ACEF*,平面*ACEF*∩平面*BDE=OE*,所以*AM*∥*EO.*
> > 又$O$是正方形*ABCD*对角线的交点,且*ACEF*是矩形,
> > 所以$M$为线段*EF*的中点*.*
> > 在空间直角坐标系中,$E$(0,0,1),$F$($\sqrt{2},\sqrt{2}$,1)*.*
> > 因此点$M$的坐标为$\left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\text{,}1 \right)$*.*
