> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设正方形*ABCD*的边长为1,则*AP=AD=AB=*1,设$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*$a$**,$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*$b$**,$\overrightarrow{AP}$*=*$c$**,
> > 则$\overrightarrow{EF} = \overrightarrow{EA} + \overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{DF}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$**$c$*+*$b$*+*$\frac{1}{2}$**$a$**,$\overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AB}$*=*$b$*-*$a$**,
> > $\overrightarrow{EF} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD}$*=*$\left( - \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{c} + \mathbf{b} + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a} \right)$·(**$b$-$a$**)*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$**$c$**·**$b$*+*$\frac{1}{2}$**$c$**·**$a$*+*$b$**^2^*-*$a$**·**$b$*+*$\frac{1}{2}$**$a$**·**$b$*-*$\frac{1}{2}$**$a$**^2^*=*$\frac{1}{2}$,
> > *\|*$\overrightarrow{EF}$*\|=*$\sqrt{(\text{-}\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{c} + \mathbf{b} + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a})\ ^{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$,*\|*$\overrightarrow{BD}$*\|=*$\sqrt{2}$*.*
> > 所以cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{EF},\overrightarrow{BD}$*\>=*$\frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\text{·}\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{6}$*.*
