> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图,连接*SO*,*AC*,*OB*,由于棱锥是正四棱锥,因此底面四边形*ABCD*是正方形,从而*OA*,*OB*,*OS*两两垂直,以$O$为原点,*OA*,*OB*,*OS*所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系*.*
> > ![id:2147513586;FounderCES](../../images/image2.jpeg)
> > 因为侧棱长为$\sqrt{2}$,底面边长为$\sqrt{3}$,$E$为*SA*的中点,
> > 所以*A*$\left( \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\text{,}0\text{,}0 \right)$,*S*$\left( 0\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$,*C*$\left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\text{,}0\text{,}0 \right)$,*B*$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\text{,}0 \right)$,*E*$\left( \frac{\sqrt{6}}{4},0,\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} \right)$*.*
> > (1)$\overrightarrow{CE} = \left( \frac{3\sqrt{6}}{4}\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} \right)$,
> > 所以*\|*$\overrightarrow{CE}$*\|=*$\sqrt{\left( \frac{3\sqrt{6}}{4} \right)^{2} + 0^{2} + \left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} \right)^{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{14}}{2}$,
> > 即*CE=*$\frac{\sqrt{14}}{2}$*.*
> > (2)因为$\overrightarrow{BE} = \left( \frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}\text{,-}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} \right),\overrightarrow{SC}$*=*$\left( - \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2},0, - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$,
> > 所以cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{BE},\overrightarrow{SC}$*\>=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{BE}\text{·}\overrightarrow{SC}}{\overset{\text{　}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{BE}\text{||}\overrightarrow{SC}\text{|}}} = \frac{\text{-}1}{\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2}}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 故异面直线*BE*和*SC*所成角的余弦值为$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > (3)证明:因为$G$在*SC*上,所以$\overrightarrow{SG}与\overrightarrow{SC}$共线,
> > 所以可设$\overrightarrow{SG}$*=λ*$\overrightarrow{SC} = \left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\lambda\text{,}0\text{,-}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\lambda \right)$,*λ*∈*$R$*,
> > 则$\overrightarrow{OG} = \overrightarrow{OS} + \overrightarrow{SG} = \left( 0\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$*+*![](../../images/image3.jpeg)*-*$\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$*λ*,0,*-*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*λ*![](../../images/image4.jpeg)*=*$\left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\lambda\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\text{(}1\text{-}\lambda\text{)} \right)$*.*
> > 因为*OG*⊥*SC*,即$\overrightarrow{OG}\bot\overrightarrow{SC}$,所以$\overrightarrow{OG} \cdot \overrightarrow{SC}$*=*0*.*
> > 所以$\frac{3}{2}$*λ-*$\frac{1}{2}$(1*-λ*)*=*0,解得*λ=*$\frac{1}{4}$*.*
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{OG} = \left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{8}\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{8} \right)$*.*
> > 又$\overrightarrow{BE} = \left( \frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}\text{,-}\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} \right)$,
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{OG} \cdot \overrightarrow{BE}$*=-*$\frac{6}{32}$*+*0*+*$\frac{6}{32}$*=*0*.*
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{OG}\bot\overrightarrow{BE}$,即*OG*⊥*BE.*
