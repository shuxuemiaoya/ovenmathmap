> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{2}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 依题意,建立空间直角坐标系*Dxyz*,如图所示,
> > 则$A$(1,0,0),*M*$\left( 1\text{,}\frac{1}{2}\text{,}1 \right)$,$C$(0,1,0),*N*$\left( 1\text{,}1\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$,
> > ![id:2147514922;FounderCES](../../images/image3.jpeg)
> > 从而$\overrightarrow{AM} = \left( 0\text{,}\frac{1}{2}\text{,}1 \right),\overrightarrow{CN} = \left( 1\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$*.*
> > 由于cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{AM},\overrightarrow{CN}$*\>=*$\frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}} = \frac{2}{5}$,故异面直线*AM*与*CN*所成角的余弦值为*\|*cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{AM},\overrightarrow{CN}$*\>\|=*$\frac{2}{5}$*.*
