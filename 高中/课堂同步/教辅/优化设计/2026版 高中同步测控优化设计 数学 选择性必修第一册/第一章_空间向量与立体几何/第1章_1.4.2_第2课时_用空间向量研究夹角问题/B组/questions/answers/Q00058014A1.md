> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{12}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 平面*Oxy*的一个法向量为**$n$*=*(0,0,1)*.*
> > 设平面*α*的法向量为**$u$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),则有$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \text{-}3x + 4y = 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}3x + az = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 即3*x=*4*y=az.*所以可取**$u$*=*$\left( \frac{a}{3}\text{,}\frac{a}{4}\text{,}1 \right)$*.*
> > 由题意得*\|*cos*\<*$n$**,**$u$*\>\|=*$\frac{1}{\sqrt{\frac{a^{2}}{9} + \frac{a^{2}}{16} + 1}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$,
> > 已知*a\>*0,故*a=*$\frac{12}{5}$*.*
