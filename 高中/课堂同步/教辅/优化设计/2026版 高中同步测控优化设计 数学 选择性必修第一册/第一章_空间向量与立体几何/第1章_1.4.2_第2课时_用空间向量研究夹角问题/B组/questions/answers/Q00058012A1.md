> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** BCD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图,正方形*ABCD*沿对角线*BD*折成直二面角,取*BD*中点$O$,连接*AO*,*CO*,
> > ![id:2147514992;FounderCES](../../images/image13.jpeg)
> > 则以$O$为原点,*OC*所在直线为$x$轴,*OD*所在直线为$y$轴,*OA*所在直线为$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系,
> > 设*OC=*1,则$A$(0,0,1),$B$(0,*-*1,0),$C$(1,0,0),$D$(0,1,0),$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*(0,1,*-*1),$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=*(1,1,0),
> > *\|*cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{AD},\overrightarrow{BC}$*\>\|=*$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{AD}\text{·}\overrightarrow{BC}\text{|}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{AD}\text{||}\overrightarrow{BC}\text{|}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{2}$,
> > 所以异面直线*AD*与*BC*所成的角为60°,故A不正确;
> > $\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(1,0,*-*1),$\overrightarrow{BD}$*=*(0,2,0),
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{AC} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD}$*=*0,所以*AC*⊥*BD*,故B正确;
> > 设平面*ACD*的一个法向量为**$t$*=*($x_{1}$,$y_{1}$,$z_{1}$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > t\text{·}\overrightarrow{AC} = x_{1}\text{-}z_{1} = 0\text{,} \\
> > t\text{·}\overrightarrow{AD} = y_{1}\text{-}z_{1} = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$取$z_{1}$*=*1,得**$t$*=*(1,1,1),$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=*(1,1,0),
> > 设*BC*与平面*ACD*所成角为*θ*,则sin *θ=\|*cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{BC}$,**$t$*\>\|=*$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{BC}\text{·}\mathbf{t}\text{|}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{BC}\text{||}\mathbf{t}\text{|}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$,故C正确;
> > 平面*BCD*的一个法向量**$n$*=*(0,0,1),$\overrightarrow{BA}$*=*(0,1,1),$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=*(1,1,0),
> > 设平面*ABC*的法向量**$m$*=*($x_{2}$,$y_{2}$,$z_{2}$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > m\text{·}\overrightarrow{BA} = y_{2} + z_{2} = 0\text{,} \\
> > m\text{·}\overrightarrow{BC} = x_{2} + y_{2} = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 取$x_{2}$*=*1,得**$m$*=*(1,*-*1,1),
> > 设平面*ABC*与平面*BCD*的夹角为*θ*,
> > 则cos *θ=\|*cos*\<*$m$**,**$n$*\>\|=*$\frac{\text{|}\mathbf{m}\text{·}\mathbf{n}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{m}\text{||}\mathbf{n}\text{|}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$,
> > 所以sin *θ=*$\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$,所以tan *θ=*$\sqrt{2}$,
> > 所以平面*ABC*与平面*BCD*的夹角的正切值是$\sqrt{2}$,故D正确*.*
