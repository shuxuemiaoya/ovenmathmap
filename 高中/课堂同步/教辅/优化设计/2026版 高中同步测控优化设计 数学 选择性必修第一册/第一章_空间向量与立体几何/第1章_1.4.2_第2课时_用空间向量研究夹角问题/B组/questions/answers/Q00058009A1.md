> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 以$D$为原点,*DA*,*DC*,$DD_{1}$所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147514964;FounderCES](../../images/image9.jpeg)
> > 由题意知,$A_{1}$(1,0,2),$E$(1,1,1),$D_{1}$(0,0,2),$A$(1,0,0),
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{A_{1}E}$*=*(0,1,*-*1),$\overrightarrow{D_{1}E}$*=*(1,1,*-*1),$\overrightarrow{EA}$*=*(0,*-*1,*-*1)*.*
> > 设平面$A_{1}$$ED_{1}$的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > n\text{·}\overrightarrow{A_{1}E} = 0\text{,} \\
> > n\text{·}\overrightarrow{D_{1}E} = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 可得平面$A_{1}$$ED_{1}$的一个法向量为**$n$*=*(0,1,1)*.*
> > 因为cos*\<*$n$**,$\overrightarrow{EA}$*\>=*$\frac{\mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{EA}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{||}\overrightarrow{EA}\text{|}} = \frac{\text{-}2}{\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2}}$*=-*1,
> > 所以*\<*$n$**,$\overrightarrow{EA}$*\>=*180°*.*
> > 所以直线*AE*与平面$A_{1}$$ED_{1}$所成的角为90°*.*
