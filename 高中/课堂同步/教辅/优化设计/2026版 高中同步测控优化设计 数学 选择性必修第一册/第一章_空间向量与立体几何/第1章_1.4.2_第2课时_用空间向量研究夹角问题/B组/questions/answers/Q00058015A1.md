> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知,*AP*,*AB*,*AD*两两垂直*.*以$A$为原点,*AD*,*AB*,*AP*所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系,如图所示,
> > ![id:2147515027;FounderCES](../../images/image18.jpeg)
> > 则$A$(0,0,0),$B$(0,2,0),$C$(2,2,0),$D$(1,0,0),$P$(0,0,2),$M$(0,1,1)*.*
> > (1)证明:$\overrightarrow{AM}$*=*(0,1,1),$\overrightarrow{PD}$*=*(1,0,*-*2),$\overrightarrow{CD}$*=*(*-*1,*-*2,0)*.*
> > 设平面*PCD*的法向量**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \overrightarrow{PD}\text{·}\mathbf{n} = 0\text{,} \\
> > \overrightarrow{CD}\text{·}\mathbf{n} = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 即\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x\text{-}2z = 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}x\text{-}2y = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = 2z\text{,} \\
> > y = \text{-}z\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 取*z=*1,则*x=*2,*y=-*1*.*所以**$n$*=*(2,*-*1,1)是平面*PCD*的一个法向量*.*
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{AM}$·**$n$*=*0,所以$\overrightarrow{AM}$⊥**$n$*.*
> > 又*AM*⊄平面*PCD*,所以*AM*∥平面*PCD.*
> > (2)由题意知$\overrightarrow{DN}$*=λ*$\overrightarrow{DC}$*=λ*(1,2,0),0≤*λ*≤1*.*
> > 连接*AN*,因为$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*(1,0,0),所以$\overrightarrow{AN} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{DN}$*=*(1,0,0)*+λ*(1,2,0)*=*(1*+λ*,2*λ*,0)*.*
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{MN} = \overrightarrow{AN} - \overrightarrow{AM}$*=*(1*+λ*,2*λ*,0)*-*(0,1,1)*=*(1*+λ*,2*λ-*1,*-*1)*.*
> > 又平面*PAB*的一个法向量为**$m$*=*(1,0,0),
> > 所以cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{MN}$,**$m$*\>=*$\frac{1 + \lambda}{\sqrt{\text{(}1 + \lambda\text{)}^{2} + \text{(}2\lambda\text{-}1\text{)}^{2} + 1}} = \frac{1 + \lambda}{\sqrt{5\lambda^{2}\text{-}2\lambda + 3}}$*\>*0,
> > 设直线*MN*与平面*PAB*所成的角为*θ*,
> > 则sin *θ=\|*cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{MN}$,**$m$*\>\|=*$\frac{1 + \lambda}{\sqrt{5\lambda^{2}\text{-}2\lambda + 3}} = \frac{1 + \lambda}{\sqrt{5\text{(}1 + \lambda\text{)}^{2}\text{-}12\text{(}1 + \lambda\text{)} + 10}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5\text{-}\frac{12}{1 + \lambda} + 10\left( \frac{1}{1 + \lambda} \right)^{2}}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{10\left( \frac{1}{1 + \lambda}\text{-}\frac{3}{5} \right)^{2} + \frac{7}{5}}}$*.*
> > 所以,当$\frac{1}{1 + \lambda} = \frac{3}{5}$,即*λ=*$\frac{2}{3}$时,sin *θ*取得最大值,也就是直线*MN*与平面*PAB*所成的角最大*.*
