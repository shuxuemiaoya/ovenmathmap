7. 如图,在四棱柱*ABCD-A*~1~$B_{1}$$C_{1}$$D_{1}$中,底面*ABCD*和侧面$BCC_{1}$$B_{1}$都是矩形,$E$是*CD*的中点,$D_{1}$*E*⊥*CD*,*AB=*2*BC=*2*.*
![id:2147515006;FounderCES](../images/image15.jpeg)
(1)求证:平面$CC_{1}$$D_{1}$*D*⊥底面*ABCD*;
(2)若平面$BCC_{1}$$B_{1}$与平面$BED_{1}$所成的锐二面角的大小为$\frac{\pi}{3}$,求线段$ED_{1}$的长度*.*
(1)证明:因为底面*ABCD*和侧面$BCC_{1}$$B_{1}$都是矩形,所以*AD*⊥*CD*,*AD*⊥$DD_{1}$,
又*CD*∩$DD_{1}$*=D*,*CD*,$DD_{1}$⊂平面$CDD_{1}$$C_{1}$,
所以*AD*⊥平面$CDD_{1}$$C_{1}$,又$D_{1}$*E*⊂平面$CDD_{1}$$C_{1}$,
所以*AD*⊥$D_{1}$*E*,又*CD*⊥$D_{1}$*E*,且*CD*∩*AD=D*,*CD*,*AD*⊂平面*ABCD*,
故$D_{1}$*E*⊥平面*ABCD*,又$D_{1}$*E*⊂平面$CC_{1}$$D_{1}$*D*,
则平面$CC_{1}$$D_{1}$*D*⊥平面*ABCD.*
(2)解:如图,取*AB*的中点$F$,连接*EF*,则四边形*EFBC*为正方形,所以*EF*⊥*CD*,故以$E$为坐标原点,建立空间直角坐标系*Exyz*,
![id:2147515013;FounderCES](../images/image16.jpeg)
设$ED_{1}$*=a*,则$E$(0,0,0),$F$(1,0,0),$B$(1,1,0),$C$(0,1,0),$C_{1}$(0,2,$a$),
所以$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=*(*-*1,0,0),$\overrightarrow{CC_{1}}$*=*(0,1,$a$),$\overrightarrow{FC}$*=*(*-*1,1,0),
设平面$BCC_{1}$$B_{1}$的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),则有$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
\mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{BC} = 0\text{,} \\
\mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{CC_{1}} = 0\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\ 即\left\{ \begin{matrix}
\text{-}x = 0\text{,} \\
y + az = 0\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
令*z=*1,则**$n$*=*(0,*-a*,1),
连接*FC*,因为*FC*⊥*BE*,
又*FC*⊥$D_{1}$*E*,*BE*∩$D_{1}$*E=E*,*BE*,$D_{1}$*E*⊂平面$BED_{1}$,所以*FC*⊥平面$BED_{1}$,
故$\overrightarrow{FC}$*=*(*-*1,1,0)为平面$BD_{1}$*E*的一个法向量,
所以*\|*cos*\<*$n$**,$\overrightarrow{FC}$*\>\|=*$\frac{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{FC}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{||}\overrightarrow{FC}\text{|}} = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}\text{·}\sqrt{a^{2} + 1}}$,
因为平面$BCC_{1}$$B_{1}$与平面$BED_{1}$所成的锐二面角的大小为$\frac{\pi}{3}$,
所以$\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}\text{·}\sqrt{a^{2} + 1}}$*=*cos$\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{1}{2}$,解得*a=*1*.*
所以$ED_{1}$*=*1*.*
