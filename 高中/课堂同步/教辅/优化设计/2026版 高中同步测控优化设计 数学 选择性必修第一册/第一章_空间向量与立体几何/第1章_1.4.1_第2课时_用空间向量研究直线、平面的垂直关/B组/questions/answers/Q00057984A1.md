> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 分别以*DA*,*DC*,$DD_{1}$所在直线为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147514248;FounderCES](../../images/image17.jpeg)
> > 则$D$(0,0,0),$A$($a$,0,0),$B$($a$,$a$,0),$C$(0,$a$,0),$A_{1}$($a$,0,$a$),$C_{1}$(0,$a$,$a$)*.*设$E$(0,$a$,$e$),$e$∈\[0,*a*\]*.*
> > (1)证明:由$B$,$D$的坐标,得$\overrightarrow{BD}$*=*(*-a*,*-a*,0)*.*
> > 由$A_{1}$,$E$的坐标,得$\overrightarrow{A_{1}E}$*=*(*-a*,$a$,*e-a*)*.*
> > ∵$\overrightarrow{A_{1}E} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD}$*=a*^2^*-a*^2^*=*0*.*∴$\overrightarrow{A_{1}E}\bot\overrightarrow{BD}$*.*∴$A_{1}$*E*⊥*BD.*
> > (2)$E$为$CC_{1}$的中点,理由如下:
> > 取*BD*的中点$O$,连接$A_{1}$*O*,*OE*,
> > 则*O*$\left( \frac{a}{2}\text{,}\frac{a}{2}\text{,}0 \right),\overrightarrow{OE} = \left( \text{-}\frac{a}{2}\text{,}\frac{a}{2}\text{,}e \right)$,
> > $\overrightarrow{OA_{1}} = \left( \frac{a}{2}\text{,-}\frac{a}{2}\text{,}a \right)$*.*
> > ∵$A_{1}$*B=A*~1~$D$,$O$为*BD*中点,
> > ∴$A_{1}$*O*⊥*BD.*
> > 又平面$A_{1}$*BD*⊥平面*EBD*,平面$A_{1}$*BD*∩平面*EBD=BD*,且$A_{1}$*O*⊂平面$A_{1}$*BD*,
> > ∴$A_{1}$*O*⊥平面*EBD.*
> > 又*OE*⊂平面*EBD*,
> > ∴$A_{1}$*O*⊥*OE.*
> > ∴$\overrightarrow{OA_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{OE}$*=*0,
> > 即*-*$\frac{a^{2}}{4} - \frac{a^{2}}{4}$*+ae=*0,
> > 得*e=*$\frac{a}{2}$,即*E*$\left( 0\text{,}a\text{,}\frac{a}{2} \right)$*.*
> > ∴当$E$为$CC_{1}$的中点时,平面$A_{1}$*BD*⊥平面*EBD.*
