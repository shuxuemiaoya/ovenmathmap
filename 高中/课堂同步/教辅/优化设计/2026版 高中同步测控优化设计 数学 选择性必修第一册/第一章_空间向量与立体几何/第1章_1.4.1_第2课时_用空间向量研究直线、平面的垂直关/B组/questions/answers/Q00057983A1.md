> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $a$或2*a*
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 建立空间直角坐标系*Bxyz*,如图所示,
> > ![id:2147514234;FounderCES](../../images/image15.jpeg)
> > 则$B_{1}$(0,0,3*a*),*D*$\left( \frac{\sqrt{2}a}{2},\frac{\sqrt{2}a}{2},3a \right)$,$C$(0,$\sqrt{2}$*a*,0)*.*
> > 设$E$($\sqrt{2}$*a*,0,$z$)(0≤$z$≤3*a*),则$\overrightarrow{CE}$*=*($\sqrt{2}$*a*,*-*$\sqrt{2}$*a*,$z$),$\overrightarrow{B_{1}E}$*=*($\sqrt{2}$*a*,0,*z-*3*a*)*.*
> > 分析知$B_{1}$*D*⊥*CE*,则要使*CE*⊥平面$B_{1}$*DE*,只需$\overrightarrow{CE}\bot\overrightarrow{B_{1}E}$,
> > 则$\overrightarrow{CE} \cdot \overrightarrow{B_{1}E}$*=*0,即2$a^{2}$*+z*^2^*-*3*az=*0,
> > 得*z=a*或2*a.*
> > 故*AE=a*或2*a.*
