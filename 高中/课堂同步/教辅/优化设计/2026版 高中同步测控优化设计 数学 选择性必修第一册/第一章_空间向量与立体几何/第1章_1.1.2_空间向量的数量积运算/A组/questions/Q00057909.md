9. 如图,在四棱锥*P-ABCD*中,底面*ABCD*为菱形,侧棱*PA*⊥底面*ABCD*,求证:*PC*⊥*BD.*
![id:2147513009;FounderCES](../images/image4.jpeg)
证明:∵$\overrightarrow{PC} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AP},\overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AB}$,
∴$\overrightarrow{PC} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD}$*=*($\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AP}$)·($\overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AB}$)*=*($\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AD}$)·($\overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AB}$)*-*$\overrightarrow{AP}$·($\overrightarrow{AD} - \overrightarrow{AB}$)
*=*${\overrightarrow{AD}}^{2} - {\overrightarrow{AB}}^{2} - \overrightarrow{AP} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{AP} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}$*.*
∵底面*ABCD*为菱形,
∴*AD=AB*,∴${\overrightarrow{AD}}^{2} - {\overrightarrow{AB}}^{2}$*=*0*.*
∵侧棱*PA*⊥底面*ABCD*,
∴*PA*⊥*AB*,*PA*⊥*AD*,
∴$\overrightarrow{AP} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{AP} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}$*=*0,
∴$\overrightarrow{PC} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = {\overrightarrow{AD}}^{2} - {\overrightarrow{AB}}^{2} - \overrightarrow{AP} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{AP} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}$*=*0,∴*PC*⊥*BD.*
