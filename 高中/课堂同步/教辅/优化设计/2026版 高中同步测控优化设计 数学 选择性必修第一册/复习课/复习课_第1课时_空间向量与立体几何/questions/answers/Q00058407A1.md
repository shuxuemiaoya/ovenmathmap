> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 以$D$为原点,*DA*,*DC*,$DD_{1}$所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系(图略),则$A$(2,0,0),$B$(2,2,0),$C$(0,2,0),$C_{1}$(0,2,1)*.*
> > ∵$\overrightarrow{BC_{1}}$*=*(*-*2,0,1),$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(*-*2,2,0),且$\overrightarrow{AC}$为平面$BB_{1}$$D_{1}$*D*的一个法向量*.*
> > ∴cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{BC_{1}},\overrightarrow{AC}$*\>=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{BC_{1}}\text{·}\overrightarrow{AC}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{BC_{1}}\text{||}\overrightarrow{AC}\text{|}} = \frac{4}{\sqrt{5} \times \sqrt{8}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}$*.*
