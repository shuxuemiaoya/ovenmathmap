> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意,以*AB*,*AD*,*AP*所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴建立空间直角坐标系(图略),则$B$(2,0,0),$D$(0,2,0),$C$(2,2,0),$P$(0,0,4),所以$\overrightarrow{PD}$*=*(0,2,*-*4),$\overrightarrow{PC}$*=*(2,2,*-*4),设$\overrightarrow{PH}$*=t*$\overrightarrow{PC}$,则$\overrightarrow{PH}$*=*(2*t*,2*t*,*-*4*t*),所以$\overrightarrow{AH} = \overrightarrow{AP} + \overrightarrow{PH}$*=*(2*t*,2*t*,4*-*4*t*),因为过点$A$的平面垂直于侧棱*PD*,交侧棱*PC*于点$H$,所以$\overrightarrow{PD}\bot\overrightarrow{AH}$,所以(0,2,*-*4)·(2*t*,2*t*,4*-*4*t*)*=*0,所以2*×*2*t-*4(4*-*4*t*)*=*0,所以*t=*$\frac{4}{5}$,所以点$H$分线段*PC*的比$\frac{PH}{HC}$*=*4*.*
