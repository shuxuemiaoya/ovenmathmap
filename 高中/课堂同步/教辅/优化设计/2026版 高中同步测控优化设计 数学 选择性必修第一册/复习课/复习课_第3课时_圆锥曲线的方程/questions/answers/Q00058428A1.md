> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** *y=*±$x$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设过$F_{2}$($c$,0)与渐近线*bx-ay=*0垂直的直线为$l$,则$l$的方程为*y=-*$\frac{a}{b}$(*x-c*),
> > 则由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > bx\text{-}ay = 0\text{,} \\
> > y = \text{-}\frac{a}{b}\text{(}x\text{-}c\text{)}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \frac{a^{2}}{c}\text{,} \\
> > y = \frac{ab}{c}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$所以$H$的坐标为$\left( \frac{a^{2}}{c},\frac{ab}{c} \right)$,
> > 又△$F_{1}$$HF_{2}$的面积为$b^{2}$,所以$S_{\text{△}F_{1}HF_{2}} = \frac{1}{2}$×2*c*×$\frac{ab}{c}$*=b*^2^,解得*a=b*,
> > 所以双曲线$C$的渐近线方程为*y=*±*x.*
