> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left( \text{-}\frac{1}{2}\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知,点$B$的坐标为($x$,*-x*),
> > ∵*AB*⊥*OB*,
> > ∴$x$≠0,且$\frac{\text{-}x\text{-}1}{x} \cdot \frac{\text{-}x}{x}$*=-*1,解得*x=-*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > ∴点$B$的坐标为$\left( \text{-}\frac{1}{2}\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$*.*
