> > ∴圆心$C$(1,2),半径*r=*$\sqrt{5}$*.*
> > ∴圆$C$的方程为(*x-*1)^2^*+*(*y-*2)^2^*=*5*.*
> > 设$P$($x$,$y$),$M$($x_{0}$,$y_{0}$)*.*
> > ∵线段*MN*的中点为$P$,
> > ∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \frac{x_{0} + 4}{2}\text{,} \\
> > y = \frac{y_{0}\text{-}2}{2}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 则\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x_{0} = 2x\text{-}4\text{,} \\
> > y_{0} = 2y + 2\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$

> > ∴(2*x-*5)^2^*+*(2*y*)^2^*=*5,即$\left( x\text{-}\frac{5}{2} \right)^{2}$*+y*^2^*=*$\frac{5}{4}$*.*
> > 故线段*MN*的中点$P$的轨迹方程为$\left( x\text{-}\frac{5}{2} \right)^{2}$*+y*^2^*=*$\frac{5}{4}$*.*
