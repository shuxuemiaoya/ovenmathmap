> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** (*x+*2)^2^*+*$\left( y\text{-}\frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} = \frac{25}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得线段*AB*的中点$\left( \text{-}2\text{,}\frac{3}{2} \right)$为圆的圆心,直径*\|AB\|=*5*.*
> > 故以$\left( \text{-}2\text{,}\frac{3}{2} \right)$为圆心,$\frac{5}{2}$为半径的圆的标准方程为(*x+*2)^2^*+*$\left( y\text{-}\frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} = \frac{25}{4}$*.*
