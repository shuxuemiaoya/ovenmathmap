> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)(方法一)直线*AB*的斜率*k=*$\frac{5\text{-}0}{1\text{-}6}$*=-*1,
> > 所以线段*AB*的垂直平分线$m$的斜率为1*.*
> > 线段*AB*的中点坐标为$\left( \frac{6 + 1}{2},\frac{0 + 5}{2} \right)$,即$\left( \frac{7}{2},\frac{5}{2} \right)$*.*
> > 因此,直线$m$的方程为*y-*$\frac{5}{2}$*=x-*$\frac{7}{2}$,即*x-y-*1*=*0*.*
> > 因为圆心在直线$l$上,所以圆心是直线$m$与直线$l$的交点*.*
> > 方程组$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x\text{-}y\text{-}1 = 0\text{,} \\
> > 2x\text{-}7y + 8 = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 得\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = 3\text{,} \\
> > y = 2\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以圆心坐标为$C$(3,2)*.*
> > 从而半径*r=\|CA\|=*$\sqrt{13}$,
> > 所以圆$C$的方程是(*x-*3)^2^*+*(*y-*2)^2^*=*13*.*
> > (方法二)设圆$C$的方程为(*x-a*)^2^*+*(*y-b*)^2^*=r*^2^*.*
> > 由题意得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \text{(}6\text{-}a\text{)}^{2} + \text{(}0\text{-}b\text{)}^{2} = r^{2}\text{,} \\
> > \text{(}1\text{-}a\text{)}^{2} + \text{(}5\text{-}b\text{)}^{2} = r^{2}\text{,} \\
> > 2a\text{-}7b + 8 = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 3\text{,} \\
> > b = 2\text{,} \\
> > r^{2} = 13\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以圆$C$的方程是(*x-*3)^2^*+*(*y-*2)^2^*=*13*.*
> > (2)设线段*PQ*的中点$M$($x$,$y$),$P$($x_{0}$,$y_{0}$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \frac{x_{0} + 8}{2}\text{,} \\
> > y = \frac{y_{0} + 0}{2}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 得\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x_{0} = 2x\text{-}8\text{,} \\
> > y_{0} = 2y\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 将点$P$(2*x-*8,2*y*)代入圆$C$的方程,得(2*x-*8*-*3)^2^*+*(2*y-*2)^2^*=*13,
> > 即线段*PQ*的中点$M$的轨迹方程为$\left( x\text{-}\frac{11}{2} \right)^{2}$*+*(*y-*1)^2^*=*$\frac{13}{4}$*.*
