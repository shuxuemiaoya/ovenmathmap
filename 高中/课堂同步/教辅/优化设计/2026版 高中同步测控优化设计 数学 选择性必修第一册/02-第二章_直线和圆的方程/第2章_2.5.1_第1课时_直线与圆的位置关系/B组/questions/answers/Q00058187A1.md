> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $x^{2}$*+*(*y+*1)^2^*=*18
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x + y + 1 = 0\text{,} \\
> > x\text{-}y\text{-}1 = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = 0\text{,} \\
> > y = \text{-}1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$即圆心$C$的坐标为(0,*-*1)*.*
> > 圆心$C$到*AB*所在直线的距离*d=*$\frac{\text{|-}4\text{-}11\text{|}}{\sqrt{3^{2} + 4^{2}}} = \frac{15}{5}$*=*3*.*
> > 已知*\|AB\|=*6,由勾股定理,得半径*r=*$\sqrt{3^{2} + 3^{2}}$*=*3$\sqrt{2}$,所以,圆的方程为$x^{2}$*+*(*y+*1)^2^*=*18*.*
