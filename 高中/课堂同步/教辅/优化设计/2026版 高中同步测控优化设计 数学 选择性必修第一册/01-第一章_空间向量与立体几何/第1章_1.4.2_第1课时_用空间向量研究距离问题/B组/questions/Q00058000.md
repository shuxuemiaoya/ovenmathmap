6. 如图,已知三棱柱*ABC-A*~1~$B_{1}$$C_{1}$的侧棱垂直于底面,∠*BAC=*90°,*AB=AC=AA*~1~*=*1,$E$,$F$分别是棱$C_{1}$*C*,*BC*的中点*.*
![id:2147514599;FounderCES](../images/image17.jpeg)
(1)求证:$B_{1}$*F*⊥平面*AEF*;
(2)求点$F$到平面$EAB_{1}$的距离*.*
(1)证明:三棱柱*ABC-A*~1~$B_{1}$$C_{1}$的侧棱垂直于底面,∠*BAC=*90°,
故以$A$为原点,*AB*,*AC*,$AA_{1}$所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,
建立空间直角坐标系,如图*.*
![id:2147514606;FounderCES](../images/image18.jpeg)
因为*AB=AC=AA*~1~*=*1,$E$,$F$分别是棱$C_{1}$*C*,*BC*的中点,
所以$B_{1}$(1,0,1),*F*$\left( \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2},0 \right)$,$A$(0,0,0),*E*$\left( 0,1,\frac{1}{2} \right)$,则$\overrightarrow{B_{1}F}$*=*$\left( - \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2}, - 1 \right)$,$\overrightarrow{AE}$*=*$\left( 0,1,\frac{1}{2} \right)$,$\overrightarrow{AF}$*=*$\left( \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2},0 \right)$,
所以$\overrightarrow{B_{1}F} \cdot \overrightarrow{AE}$*=*0,$\overrightarrow{B_{1}F} \cdot \overrightarrow{AF}$*=*0,所以$B_{1}$*F*⊥*AE*,$B_{1}$*F*⊥*AF*,
又*AE*∩*AF=A*,*AE*,*AF*⊂平面*AEF*,
所以$B_{1}$*F*⊥平面*AEF.*
(2)解:由(1)知$\overrightarrow{AB_{1}}$*=*(1,0,1),$\overrightarrow{AE}$*=*$\left( 0,1,\frac{1}{2} \right)$,
设平面$EAB_{1}$的法向量*$m$**=*($a$,$b$,$c$),
则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
\mathbf{m}\text{·}\overrightarrow{AB_{1}} = a + c = 0\text{,} \\
\mathbf{m}\text{·}\overrightarrow{AE} = b + \frac{1}{2}c = 0\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
取*a=*2,得**$m$*=*(2,1,*-*2)是平面$EAB_{1}$的一个法向量*.*
又$\overrightarrow{AF}$*=*$\left( \frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2},0 \right)$,所以点$F$到平面$EAB_{1}$的距离*d=*$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{AF}\text{·}\mathbf{m}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{m}\text{|}} = \frac{1}{2}$*.*
