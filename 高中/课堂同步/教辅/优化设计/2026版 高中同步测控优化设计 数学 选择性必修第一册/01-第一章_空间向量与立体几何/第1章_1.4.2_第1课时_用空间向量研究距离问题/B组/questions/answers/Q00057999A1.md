> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{2}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 建立空间直角坐标系$D_{1}$*xyz*,如图所示,
> > ![id:2147514592;FounderCES](../../images/image16.jpeg)
> > 则$A_{1}$(1,0,0),$B_{1}$(1,1,0),$D_{1}$(0,0,0),*N*$\left( \frac{1}{2},1,1 \right)$,
> > ∴$\overrightarrow{D_{1}B_{1}}$*=*(1,1,0),$\overrightarrow{B_{1}N} = \left( \text{-}\frac{1}{2}\text{,}0\text{,}1 \right)$*.*
> > ∵$E$,$F$,$M$,$N$分别是所在棱的中点,
> > ∴易证平面$A_{1}$*EF*∥平面$B_{1}$$NMD_{1}$,
> > ∴平面$A_{1}$*EF*与平面$B_{1}$$NMD_{1}$间的距离即为点$A_{1}$到平面$B_{1}$$NMD_{1}$的距离*.*
> > 设平面$B_{1}$$NMD_{1}$的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$)*.*
> > 由**$n$**·$\overrightarrow{D_{1}B_{1}}$*=*0,**$n$**·$\overrightarrow{B_{1}N}$*=*0,
> > 得平面$B_{1}$$NMD_{1}$的一个法向量**$n$*=*(2,*-*2,1)*.*
> > 又$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B_{1}}$*=*(0,1,0),∴点$A_{1}$到平面$B_{1}$$NMD_{1}$的距离为$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{A_{1}B_{1}}\text{·}\mathbf{n}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{|}} = \frac{2}{3}$*.*
