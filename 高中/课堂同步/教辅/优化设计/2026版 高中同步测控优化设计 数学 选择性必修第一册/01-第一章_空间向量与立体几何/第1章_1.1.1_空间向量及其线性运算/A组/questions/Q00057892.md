10. 如图所示,在正方体*ABCD-A*~1~$B_{1}$$C_{1}$$D_{1}$中,$E$,$F$分别为$BB_{1}$和$A_{1}$$D_{1}$的中点,证明:向量$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B},\overrightarrow{B_{1}C},\overrightarrow{EF}$是共面向量*.*
![id:2147512741;FounderCES](../images/image3.jpeg)
证明:$\overrightarrow{EF} = \overrightarrow{EB_{1}} + \overrightarrow{B_{1}A_{1}} + \overrightarrow{A_{1}F}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{B_{1}B} + \overrightarrow{B_{1}A_{1}} + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{B_{1}C_{1}},$
$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B} = \overrightarrow{A_{1}B_{1}} + \overrightarrow{A_{1}A}$*=-*$\overrightarrow{B_{1}A_{1}} + \overrightarrow{B_{1}B},\overrightarrow{B_{1}C} = \overrightarrow{B_{1}C_{1}} + \overrightarrow{B_{1}B}$*.*
假设存在实数$x$,$y$,使得$\overrightarrow{EF}$*=x*$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B}$*+y*$\overrightarrow{B_{1}C}$,
即*-*$\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{B_{1}B} + \overrightarrow{B_{1}A_{1}} + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{B_{1}C_{1}}$*=x*(*-*$\overrightarrow{B_{1}A_{1}} + \overrightarrow{B_{1}B}$)*+y*($\overrightarrow{B_{1}C_{1}} + \overrightarrow{B_{1}B}$)*=-x*$\overrightarrow{B_{1}A_{1}}$*+*(*x+y*)$\overrightarrow{B_{1}B}$*+y*$\overrightarrow{B_{1}C_{1}}$*.*
∵$\overrightarrow{B_{1}A_{1}},\overrightarrow{B_{1}B},\overrightarrow{B_{1}C_{1}}$不共面,
∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
\text{-}x = 1\text{,} \\
x + y = \text{-}\frac{1}{2}\text{,} \\
y = \frac{1}{2}\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
x = \text{-}1\text{,} \\
y = \frac{1}{2}\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
∴$\overrightarrow{EF}$*=-*$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B} + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{B_{1}C}$*.*
由向量共面的充要条件知,$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B},\overrightarrow{B_{1}C},\overrightarrow{EF}$是共面向量*.*
