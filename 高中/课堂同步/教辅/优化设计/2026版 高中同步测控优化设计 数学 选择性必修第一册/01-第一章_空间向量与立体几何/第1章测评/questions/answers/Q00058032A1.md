> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 存在*.*在△*PAD*中,因为*PA=PD*,$O$为*AD*的中点,所以*PO*⊥*AD*,
> > 又平面*PAD*⊥平面*ABCD*,平面*PAD*∩平面*ABCD=AD*,且*PO*⊂平面*PAD*,
> > 所以*PO*⊥平面*ABCD.*
> > 由题意可建立空间直角坐标系*Oxyz*,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147515155;FounderCES](images/image16.jpeg)
> > 由已知得$A$(0,*-*1,0),$B$(1,*-*1,0),$C$(1,0,0),$D$(0,1,0),$P$(0,0,1),
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{CP}$*=*(*-*1,0,1),$\overrightarrow{CD}$*=*(*-*1,1,0)*.*
> > 假设存在点$Q$,使得它到平面*PCD*的距离为$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$*.*
> > 设$Q$(0,$y$,0)(*-*1≤$y$≤1),则$\overrightarrow{CQ}$*=*(*-*1,$y$,0)*.*
> > 设平面*PCD*的法向量为**$n$*=*($x_{0}$,$y_{0}$,$z_{0}$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{CP} = 0\text{,} \\
> > \mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{CD} = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 即\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \text{-}x_{0} + z_{0} = 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}x_{0} + y_{0} = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > z_{0} = x_{0}\text{,} \\
> > y_{0} = x_{0}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 可取**$n$*=*(1,1,1)*.*
> > 所以点$Q$到平面*PCD*的距离为
> > $\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{CQ}\text{·}\mathbf{n}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{|}} = \frac{\text{|-}1 + y\text{|}}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$,
> > 得*y=-*$\frac{1}{2}$或*y=*$\frac{5}{2}$(舍去)*.*
> > 此时*AQ=*$\frac{1}{2}$,*QD=*$\frac{3}{2}$*.*
> > 所以存在点$Q$满足题意,此时$\frac{AQ}{QD} = \frac{1}{3}$*.*
