19. (17分)如图①所示,在Rt△*ABC*中,∠*C=*90°,*BC=*3,*AC=*6,$D$,$E$分别是*AC*,*AB*上的点,且*DE*∥*BC*,*DE=*2,将△*ADE*沿*DE*折起到△$A_{1}$*DE*的位置,使$A_{1}$*C*⊥*CD*,如图②所示*.*
![id:2147515162;FounderCES](images/image17.jpeg)
图①
![id:2147515169;FounderCES](images/image18.jpeg)
图②
(1)求证:$A_{1}$*C*⊥平面*BCDE*;
(2)若$M$是$A_{1}$*D*的中点,求直线*CM*与平面$A_{1}$*BE*所成角的大小;
(3)线段*BC*上是否存在点$P$,使平面$A_{1}$*DP*与平面$A_{1}$*BE*垂直?并说明理由*.*
(1)证明:∵*AC*⊥*BC*,且*DE*∥*BC*,
∴*DE*⊥*AC.*
∴*DE*⊥$A_{1}$*D*,*DE*⊥*DC.*
又$A_{1}$*D*∩*CD=D*,
∴*DE*⊥平面$A_{1}$*DC.*
∵$A_{1}$*C*⊂平面$A_{1}$*DC*,
∴*DE*⊥$A_{1}$*C.*
又$A_{1}$*C*⊥*CD*,且*DE*∩*CD=D*,
∴$A_{1}$*C*⊥平面*BCDE.*
(2)解:以$C$为原点,*CB*,*CD*,$CA_{1}$所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系*Cxyz*,如图所示,则$A_{1}$(0,0,2$\sqrt{3}$),$D$(0,2,0),$M$(0,1,$\sqrt{3}$),$B$(3,0,0),$E$(2,2,0)*.*
![id:2147515176;FounderCES](images/image19.jpeg)
设平面$A_{1}$*BE*的法向量**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),则**$n$**·$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B}$*=*0,**$n$**·$\overrightarrow{BE}$*=*0*.*又$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B}$*=*(3,0,*-*2$\sqrt{3}$),$\overrightarrow{BE}$*=*(*-*1,2,0),
∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
3x\text{-}2\sqrt{3}z = 0\text{,} \\
\text{-}x + 2y = 0\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
x = 2y\text{,} \\
z = \sqrt{3}y\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
取*y=*1,则*x=*2,*z=*$\sqrt{3}$*.*
∴**$n$*=*(2,1,$\sqrt{3}$)是平面$A_{1}$*BE*的一个法向量*.*
设直线*CM*与平面$A_{1}$*BE*所成的角为*θ.*
∵$\overrightarrow{CM}$*=*(0,1,$\sqrt{3}$),∴sin *θ=\|*cos*\<*$n$**,$\overrightarrow{CM}$*\>\|=*$\left| \frac{\mathbf{n}\text{·}\overrightarrow{CM}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{||}\overrightarrow{CM}\text{|}} \right| = \frac{4}{\sqrt{8} \times \sqrt{4}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*.*
∴直线*CM*与平面$A_{1}$*BE*所成的角为$\frac{\pi}{4}$*.*
(3)解:线段*BC*上不存在点$P$,使平面$A_{1}$*DP*与平面$A_{1}$*BE*垂直*.*
理由如下:假设存在点$P$满足条件,设其坐标为($p$,0,0),其中$p$∈\[0,3\]*.*
设平面$A_{1}$*DP*的法向量为**$m$*=*($x_{1}$,$y_{1}$,$z_{1}$),
则**$m$**·$\overrightarrow{A_{1}D}$*=*0,**$m$**·$\overrightarrow{DP}$*=*0*.*又$\overrightarrow{A_{1}D}$*=*(0,2,*-*2$\sqrt{3}$),$\overrightarrow{DP}$*=*($p$,*-*2,0),
∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
2y_{1}\text{-}2\sqrt{3}z_{1} = 0\text{,} \\
px_{1}\text{-}2y_{1} = 0\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
y_{1} = \frac{p}{2}x_{1}\text{,} \\
z_{1} = \frac{p}{2\sqrt{3}}x_{1}\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
取$x_{1}$*=*2,则$y_{1}$*=p*,$z_{1}$*=*$\frac{p}{\sqrt{3}}$*.*
∴**$m$*=*$\left( 2\text{,}p\text{,}\frac{p}{\sqrt{3}} \right)$为平面$A_{1}$*DP*的一个法向量*.*
平面$A_{1}$*DP*⊥平面$A_{1}$*BE*,
当且仅当**$m$**·**$n$*=*0,即4*+p+p=*0,解得*p=-*2,与$p$∈\[0,3\]矛盾*.*
∴线段*BC*上不存在点$P$,使平面$A_{1}$*DP*与平面$A_{1}$*BE*垂直*.*
