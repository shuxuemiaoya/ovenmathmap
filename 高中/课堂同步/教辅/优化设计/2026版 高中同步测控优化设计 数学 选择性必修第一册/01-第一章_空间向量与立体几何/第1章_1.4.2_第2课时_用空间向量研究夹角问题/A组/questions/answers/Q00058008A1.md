> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 以$A$为原点,建立空间直角坐标系*Axyz*,如图所示,
> > ![id:2147514957;FounderCES](../../images/image8.jpeg)
> > 则$A$(0,0,0),$B$(2,0,0),$C$(0,2,0),$D$(1,1,0),$A_{1}$(0,0,4) ,$C_{1}$(0,2,4)*.*
> > (1)因为$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B}$*=*(2,0,*-*4),$\overrightarrow{C_{1}D}$*=*(1,*-*1,*-*4), 所以cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B},\overrightarrow{C_{1}D}$*\>=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{A_{1}B}\text{·}\overrightarrow{C_{1}D}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{A_{1}B}\text{||}\overrightarrow{C_{1}D}\text{|}} = \frac{18}{\sqrt{20} \times \sqrt{18}} = \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$*.*
> > 设异面直线$A_{1}$*B*与$C_{1}$*D*所成的角为*θ*,则cos *θ=\|*cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B},\overrightarrow{C_{1}D}$*\>\|=*$\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$*.*
> > 所以异面直线$A_{1}$*B*与$C_{1}$*D*所成角的余弦值为$\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$*.*
> > (2)设平面$ADC_{1}$的法向量为**$n$**~1~*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),则**$n$**~1~·$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*0,**$n$**~1~·$\overrightarrow{AC_{1}}$*=*0*.*
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*(1,1,0),$\overrightarrow{AC_{1}}$*=*(0,2,4),所以*x+y=*0,*y+*2*z=*0*.*所以可取**$n$**~1~*=*(2,*-*2,1)*.*
> > 取平面$ABA_{1}$的一个法向量为**$n$**~2~*=*(0,1,0),设平面$ADC_{1}$与平面$ABA_{1}$的夹角为*θ*,
> > 则cos *θ=\|*cos*\<*$n$**~1~,**$n$**~2~*\>\|=*$\frac{\text{|}\mathbf{n}_{\mathbf{1}}\text{·}\mathbf{n}_{\mathbf{2}}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}_{\mathbf{1}}\text{||}\mathbf{n}_{\mathbf{2}}\text{|}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{9} \times \sqrt{1}} = \frac{2}{3}$*.*所以sin *θ=*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$*.*
> > 因此,平面$ADC_{1}$与平面$ABA_{1}$的夹角的正弦值为$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$*.*
