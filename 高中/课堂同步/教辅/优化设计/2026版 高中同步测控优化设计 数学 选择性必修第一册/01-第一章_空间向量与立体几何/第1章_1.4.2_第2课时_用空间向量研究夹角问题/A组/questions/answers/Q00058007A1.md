> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵四边形*ACDE*是正方形,
> > ∴*EA*⊥*AC.*
> > ∵平面*ACDE*⊥平面*ABC*,平面*ACDE*∩平面*ABC=AC*,且*EA*⊂平面*ACDE*,
> > ∴*EA*⊥平面*ABC.*
> > 以$A$为原点,以过点$A$,且平行于*BC*的直线为$x$轴,以*AC*,*AE*所在直线分别为$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系*Axyz*,如图所示*.*设*EA=AC=BC=*2,
> > 则$A$(0,0,0),$B$(2,2,0),$C$(0,2,0),$E$(0,0,2)*.*
> > ![id:2147514943;FounderCES](../../images/image6.jpeg)
> > ∵$M$是正方形*ACDE*的对角线的交点,∴$M$(0,1,1)*.*
> > (1)证明:$\overrightarrow{AM}$*=*(0,1,1),$\overrightarrow{EC}$*=*(0,2,*-*2),$\overrightarrow{CB}$*=*(2,0,0),
> > ∵$\overrightarrow{AM} \cdot \overrightarrow{EC}$*=*0,$\overrightarrow{AM} \cdot \overrightarrow{CB}$*=*0,∴$\overrightarrow{AM}\bot\overrightarrow{EC},\overrightarrow{AM}\bot\overrightarrow{CB}$*.*
> > ∴*AM*⊥*EC*,*AM*⊥*CB.*
> > 又*EC*∩*CB=C*,∴*AM*⊥平面*EBC.*
> > (2)∵*AM*⊥平面*EBC*,
> > ∴$\overrightarrow{AM}$为平面*EBC*的一个法向量*.*
> > ∵$\overrightarrow{AM}$*=*(0,1,1),$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(2,2,0),
> > ∴cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{AB},\overrightarrow{AM}$*\>=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{AB}\text{·}\overrightarrow{AM}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{AB}\text{||}\overrightarrow{AM}\text{|}} = \frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > ∴*\<*$\overrightarrow{AB},\overrightarrow{AM}$*\>=*60°*.*
> > ∴直线*AB*与平面*EBC*所成的角为30°*.*
