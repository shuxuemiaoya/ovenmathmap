> > [!success] **【答案】** 90°
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设正三棱柱的棱长为2*.*
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{AB_{1}} = \overrightarrow{BB_{1}} - \overrightarrow{BA},\overrightarrow{BM} = \overrightarrow{BC} + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BB_{1}}$,
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{AB_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{BM}$*=*($\overrightarrow{BB_{1}} - \overrightarrow{BA}$)·$\left( \overrightarrow{BC} + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BB_{1}} \right)$*=*0*+*2*-*2*-*0*=*0,所以$\overrightarrow{AB_{1}}\bot\overrightarrow{BM}$*.*
> > 所以异面直线$AB_{1}$与*BM*的夹角为90°*.*
