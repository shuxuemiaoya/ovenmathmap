> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 以$D$为原点,线段*DA*的长为单位长度,*DA*,*DP*,*DC*所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系*Dxyz*,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147514157;FounderCES](../../images/image4.jpeg)
> > 依题意有$D$(0,0,0),$Q$(1,1,0),$C$(0,0,1),$P$(0,2,0),则$\overrightarrow{DQ}$*=*(1,1,0),$\overrightarrow{DC}$*=*(0,0,1),$\overrightarrow{PQ}$*=*(1,*-*1,0)*.*
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{PQ} \cdot \overrightarrow{DQ}$*=*0,$\overrightarrow{PQ} \cdot \overrightarrow{DC}$*=*0,
> > 所以*PQ*⊥*DQ*,*PQ*⊥*DC.*
> > 又*DQ*∩*DC=D*,所以*PQ*⊥平面*DCQ.*
> > 又*PQ*⊂平面*PQC*,所以平面*PQC*⊥平面*DCQ.*
