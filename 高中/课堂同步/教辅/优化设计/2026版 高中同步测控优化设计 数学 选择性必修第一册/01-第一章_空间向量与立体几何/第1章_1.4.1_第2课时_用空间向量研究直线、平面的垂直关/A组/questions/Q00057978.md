证明:以$D$为原点,线段*DA*的长为单位长度,建立空间直角坐标系*Dxyz*,如图所示*.*
![id:2147514171;FounderCES](../images/image6.jpeg)
设$E$($a$,0,0),其中*a\>*0,则$C$(2*a*,0,0),$A$(0,1,0),$B$(2*a*,1,0),$P$(0,0,1),*F*$\left( a\text{,}\frac{1}{2}\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$,
所以$\overrightarrow{EF} = \left( 0\text{,}\frac{1}{2}\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right),\overrightarrow{PB}$*=*(2*a*,1,*-*1),$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(2*a*,0,0)*.*
因为$\overrightarrow{EF} \cdot \overrightarrow{PB}$*=*0,$\overrightarrow{EF} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB}$*=*0,
所以*EF*⊥*PB*,*EF*⊥*AB.*
又*PB*∩*AB=B*,
所以*EF*⊥平面*PAB.*
