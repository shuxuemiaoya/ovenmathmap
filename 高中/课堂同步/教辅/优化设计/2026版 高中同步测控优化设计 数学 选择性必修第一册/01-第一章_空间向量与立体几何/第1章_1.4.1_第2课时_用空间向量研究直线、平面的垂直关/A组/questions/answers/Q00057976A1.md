> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{\pi}{2}或\frac{\pi}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵*OP*⊥*OQ*,∴$\overrightarrow{OP}\bot\overrightarrow{OQ}$,∴$\overrightarrow{OP} \cdot \overrightarrow{OQ}$*=*0*.*
> > ∴cos $x$·(2cos *x+*1)*-*(2cos 2*x+*2)*=*0*.*
> > ∴2cos^2^*x-*cos *x=*0,解得cos *x=*0或cos *x=*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 又$x$∈\[0,π\],∴*x=*$\frac{\pi}{2}$或*x=*$\frac{\pi}{3}$*.*
