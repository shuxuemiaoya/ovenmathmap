8. 如图,正四面体*VABC*的高*VD*的中点为$O$,*VC*的中点为*M.*
![id:2147513367;FounderCES](../images/image11.jpeg)
(1)求证:*AO*,*BO*,*CO*两两垂直;
(2)求*DM*和*AO*所成角的大小*.*
设$\overrightarrow{VA}$*=*$a$**,$\overrightarrow{VB}$*=*$b$**,$\overrightarrow{VC}$*=*$c$**,正四面体的棱长为1*.*
(1)证明:$\overrightarrow{VD} = \frac{1}{3}$(**$a$+$b$+$c$**),$\overrightarrow{AO} = \overrightarrow{VO} - \overrightarrow{VA} = \frac{1}{6}$(**$b$*+*$c$*-*5**$a$**),
$\overrightarrow{BO} = \overrightarrow{VO} - \overrightarrow{VB} = \frac{1}{6}$(**$a$*+*$c$*-*5**$b$**),$\overrightarrow{CO} = \overrightarrow{VO} - \overrightarrow{VC} = \frac{1}{6}$(**$a$+$b$-**5**$c$**)*.*
因为$\overrightarrow{AO} \cdot \overrightarrow{BO} = \frac{1}{36}$(**$b$+$c$-**5**$a$**)·(**$a$+$c$-**5**$b$**)*=*$\frac{1}{36}$(18**$a$**·**$b$-**9**\|$a$***\|*^2^)*=*$\frac{1}{36}$(18*×*1*×*1*×*cos 60°*-*9)*=*0,
所以$\overrightarrow{AO}\bot\overrightarrow{BO}$,
即*AO*⊥*BO.*
同理,*AO*⊥*CO*,*BO*⊥*CO.*
所以*AO*,*BO*,*CO*两两垂直*.*
(2)解:$\overrightarrow{DM} = \overrightarrow{DV} + \overrightarrow{VM}$*=-*$\frac{1}{3}$(**$a$+$b$+$c$**)*+*$\frac{1}{2}$**$c$*=*$\frac{1}{6}$(**-**2**$a$-**2**$b$+$c$**),则*\|*$\overrightarrow{DM}$*\|=*$\sqrt{\left\lbrack \frac{1}{6}\text{(}\text{-}2\mathbf{a}\text{-}2\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}\text{)} \right\rbrack^{2}} = \frac{1}{2}$*.*
*\|*$\overrightarrow{AO}$*\|=*$\sqrt{\left\lbrack \frac{1}{6}\text{(}\mathbf{b} + \mathbf{c}\text{-}5\mathbf{a}\text{)} \right\rbrack^{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*.*
因为$\overrightarrow{DM} \cdot \overrightarrow{AO} = \frac{1}{6}$(*-*2**$a$-**2**$b$+$c$**)· $\frac{1}{6}$(**$b$+$c$-**5**$a$**)*=*$\frac{1}{4}$,
所以cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{DM},\overrightarrow{AO}$*\>=*$\frac{\frac{1}{4}}{\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*.*
所以*\<*$\overrightarrow{DM},\overrightarrow{AO}$*\>=*$\frac{\pi}{4}$*.*
故*DM*和*AO*所成角的大小为$\frac{\pi}{4}$*.*
