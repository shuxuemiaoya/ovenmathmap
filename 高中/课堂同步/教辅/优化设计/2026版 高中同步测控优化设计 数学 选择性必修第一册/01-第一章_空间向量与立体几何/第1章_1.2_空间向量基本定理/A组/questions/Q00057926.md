9. 如图,在直三棱柱*ABC-A\'B\'C\'*中,*AC=BC=AA\'*,∠*ACB=*90°,$D$,$E$分别为*AB*,*BB\'*的中点*.*
![id:2147513332;FounderCES](../images/image6.jpeg)
(1)求证:*CE*⊥*A\'D*;
(2)求*CE*与*AC\'*所成角的余弦值*.*
(1)证明:设$\overrightarrow{CA}$*=*$a$**,$\overrightarrow{CB}$*=*$b$**,$\overrightarrow{CC\text{'}}$*=*$c$**,则{**$a$**,**$b$**,**$c$**}构成空间的一个基底*.*
根据题意,*\|*$a$*\|=\|*$b$*\|=\|*$c$*\|*,且**$a$**·**$b$=$b$**·**$c$=$c$**·**$a$*=*0*.*
∵$\overrightarrow{CE}$*=*$b$*+*$\frac{1}{2}$**$c$**,$\overrightarrow{A\text{'}D}$*=-*$c$*+*$\frac{1}{2}$**$b$*-*$\frac{1}{2}$**$a$**,
∴$\overrightarrow{CE} \cdot \overrightarrow{A\text{'}D}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$**$c$**^2^*+*$\frac{1}{2}$**$b$**^2^*=*0,
∴$\overrightarrow{CE}\bot\overrightarrow{A\text{'}D}$*.*∴*CE*⊥*A\'D.*
(2)解:∵$\overrightarrow{AC\text{'}}$*=-*$a$*+*$c$**,$\overrightarrow{CE}$*=*$b$*+*$\frac{1}{2}$**$c$**,
∴*\|*$\overrightarrow{AC\text{'}}$*\|=*$\sqrt{2}$*\|*$a$*\|*,*\|*$\overrightarrow{CE}$*\|=*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$*\|*$a$*\|*,
又$\overrightarrow{AC\text{'}} \cdot \overrightarrow{CE}$*=*(*-*$a$*+*$c$**)·$\left( b + \frac{1}{2}c \right) = \frac{1}{2}$**$c$**^2^*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*\|*$a$*\|*^2^,
∴cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{AC\text{'}},\overrightarrow{CE}$*\>=*$\frac{\frac{1}{2}\text{|}a\text{|}^{2}}{\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}\text{|}a\text{|}^{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$*.*
故$E$与*AC\'*所成角的余弦值为$\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$*.*
