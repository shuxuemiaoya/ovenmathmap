> > [!success] **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设平面*ABC*的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(0,*-*1,1),$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=*(*-*1,1,0),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \overrightarrow{AB}\text{·}n = \text{-}y + z = 0\text{,} \\
> > \overrightarrow{BC}\text{·}n = \text{-}x + y = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*x=y=z.*
> > 因为单位向量的模为1,所以只有选项B正确*.*
