> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** 2∶3∶(*-*4)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\overrightarrow{AB} = \left( 1\text{,-}3\text{,-}\frac{7}{4} \right),\overrightarrow{AC} = \left( \text{-}2\text{,-}1\text{,-}\frac{7}{4} \right)$*.*
> > 因为**$a$**·$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*0,**$a$**·$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*0,
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x\text{-}3y\text{-}\frac{7}{4}z = 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}2x\text{-}y\text{-}\frac{7}{4}z = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 则\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \frac{2}{3}y\text{,} \\
> > z = \text{-}\frac{4}{3}y\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以$x$∶$y$∶*z=*$\frac{2}{3}$*y*∶$y$∶$\left( \text{-}\frac{4}{3}y \right)$*=*2∶3∶(*-*4)*.*
