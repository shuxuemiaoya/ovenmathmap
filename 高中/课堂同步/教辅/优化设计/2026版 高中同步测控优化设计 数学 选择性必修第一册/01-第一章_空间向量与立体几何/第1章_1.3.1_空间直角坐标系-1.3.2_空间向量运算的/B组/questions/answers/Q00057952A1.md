> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)设$D$($x$,$y$,$z$),则$\overrightarrow{DB}$*=*(*-x*,1*-y*,*-z*),$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(*-*1,0,2),$\overrightarrow{DC}$*=*(*-x*,*-y*,2*-z*),$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(*-*1,1,0)*.*
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{DB} \parallel \overrightarrow{AC},\overrightarrow{DC} \parallel \overrightarrow{AB}$,所以存在实数$m$,$n$,使得
> > $\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \overrightarrow{DB} = m\overrightarrow{AC}\text{,} \\
> > \overrightarrow{DC} = n\overrightarrow{AB}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 即\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \text{(}\text{-}x\text{,}1\text{-}y\text{,-}z\text{)} = m\text{(}\text{-}1\text{,}0\text{,}2\text{)}\text{,} \\
> > \text{(}\text{-}x\text{,-}y\text{,}2\text{-}z\text{)} = n\text{(}\text{-}1\text{,}1\text{,}0\text{)}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \text{-}1\text{,} \\
> > y = 1\text{,} \\
> > z = 2\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$即$D$(*-*1,1,2)*.*
> > (2)依题意$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(*-*1,1,0),$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(*-*1,0,2),$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=*(0,*-*1,2)*.*
> > 假设存在实数*α*,*β*,使得$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=α*$\overrightarrow{AB}$*+β*$\overrightarrow{BC}$成立,则有(*-*1,0,2)*=α*(*-*1,1,0)*+β*(0,*-*1,2)*=*(*-α*,*α-β*,2*β*)*.*
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \alpha = 1\text{,} \\
> > \alpha\text{-}\beta = 0\text{,} \\
> > 2\beta = 2\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*α=*1,*β=*1*.*
> > 故存在*α=β=*1,使得$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=α*$\overrightarrow{AB}$*+β*$\overrightarrow{BC}$成立*.*
