> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设直线$l$的方程为*y=k*(*x+*1),$A$($x_{1}$,$y_{1}$),$B$($x_{2}$,$y_{2}$)*.*
> > 由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > y^{2} = 4x\text{,} \\
> > y = k\text{(}x + 1\text{)}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$联立得$k^{2}$$x^{2}$*+*2($k^{2}$*-*2)*x+k*^2^*=*0,
> > ∴$x_{1}$*+x*~2~*=-*$\frac{2\text{(}k^{2}\text{-}2\text{)}}{k^{2}}$,

> > ∴$\left( \text{-}1 + \frac{2}{k^{2}}\text{-}1 \right)^{2} + \left( \frac{2}{k} \right)^{2}$*=*4,解得*k=*±1*.*
