> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{3\sqrt{5}}{10}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 以*AB*,*AD*,$AA_{1}$所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴、$z$轴,建立空间直角坐标系,如图所示,
> > ![id:2147518705;FounderCES](images/image4.jpeg)
> > 则$A_{1}$(0,0,1),*E*$\left( 1\text{,}0\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$,$D$(0,1,0),*F*$\left( \frac{1}{2}\text{,}1\text{,}0 \right)$,$D_{1}$(0,1,1),
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{A_{1}E} = \left( 1\text{,}0\text{,-}\frac{1}{2} \right),\overrightarrow{A_{1}D_{1}}$*=*(0,1,0)*.*
> > 设平面$A_{1}$$D_{1}$*E*的法向量为**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > n\text{·}\overrightarrow{A_{1}E} = 0\text{,} \\
> > n\text{·}\overrightarrow{A_{1}D_{1}} = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 即\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x\text{-}\frac{1}{2}z = 0\text{,} \\
> > y = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > z = 2x\text{,} \\
> > y = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 取**$n$*=*(1,0,2)*.*
> > 连接$A_{1}$*F*,则$\overrightarrow{A_{1}F} = \left( \frac{1}{2}\text{,}1\text{,-}1 \right)$,所以点$F$到平面$A_{1}$$D_{1}$*E*的距离*d=*$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{A_{1}F}\text{·}\mathbf{n}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{|}} = \frac{\left| \frac{1}{2}\text{-}2 \right|}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{3\sqrt{5}}{10}$*.*
