> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由题意知*AD*,*DC*,$DD_{1}$两两垂直,故可建立空间直角坐标系*Dxyz*,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147518719;FounderCES](images/image6.jpeg)
> > 由已知条件可得$A$(1,0,0),$C$(0,$\sqrt{3}$,0),$B$(1,2$\sqrt{3}$,0),$E$(0,$\sqrt{3}$,1),$A_{1}$(1,0,2),
> > (1)$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(0,2$\sqrt{3}$,0),$\overrightarrow{AE}$*=*(*-*1,$\sqrt{3}$,1)*.*
> > 设**$n$*=*($x$,$y$,$z$)是平面*ABE*的法向量,
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > n\text{·}\overrightarrow{AB} = 0\text{,} \\
> > n\text{·}\overrightarrow{AE} = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 即\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2\sqrt{3}y = 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}x + \sqrt{3}y + z = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > y = 0\text{,} \\
> > z = x\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 取*x=*1,则*z=*1*.*
> > 所以,**$n$*=*(1,0,1)是平面*ABE*的一个法向量*.*
> > 又因为$\overrightarrow{AA_{1}}$*=*(0,0,2),所以点$A_{1}$到平面*ABE*的距离为$\frac{\text{|}\overrightarrow{AA_{1}}\text{·}\mathbf{n}\text{|}}{\text{|}\mathbf{n}\text{|}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$*.*
> > 因为易证$A_{1}$$B_{1}$∥平面*ABE*,所以直线$A_{1}$$B_{1}$到平面*ABE*的距离即为点$A_{1}$到平面*ABE*的距离*.*所以直线$A_{1}$$B_{1}$到平面*ABE*的距离为$\sqrt{2}$*.*
> > (2)$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=*(*-*1,*-*$\sqrt{3}$,0),$\overrightarrow{CE}$*=*(0,0,1),
> > 设平面*BEC*的法向量为**$m$*=*($x_{0}$,$y_{0}$,$z_{0}$),则**$m$**·$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=*0,**$m$**·$\overrightarrow{CE}$*=*0*.*
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \text{-}x_{0}\text{-}\sqrt{3}y_{0} = 0\text{,} \\
> > z_{0} = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x_{0} = \text{-}\sqrt{3}y_{0}\text{,} \\
> > z_{0} = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 取**$m$*=*($\sqrt{3}$,*-*1,0)*.*
> > 所以cos*\<*$m$**,**$n$*\>=*$\frac{\mathbf{m}\text{·}\mathbf{n}}{\text{|}\mathbf{m}\text{||}\mathbf{n}\text{|}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2 \times \sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}$*.*
> > 设平面*ABE*与平面*BEC*的夹角为*θ*,
> > 则cos *θ=\|*cos*\<*$m$**,**$n$*\>\|=*$\frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}$*.*
> > 所以平面*ABE*与平面*BEC*的夹角的余弦值为$\frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}$*.*
