> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图,对于A,因为$E$,$F$分别是$A_{1}$$D_{1}$和$C_{1}$$D_{1}$的中点,
> > 故*EF*∥$A_{1}$$C_{1}$,故$A_{1}$$C_{1}$∥平面*CEF*,A正确*.　*
> > 对于B,建立空间直角坐标系,如图所示,设正方体*ABCD-A*~1~$B_{1}$$C_{1}$$D_{1}$的棱长为2,
> > 则$\overrightarrow{B_{1}D}$*=*(*-*2,*-*2,*-*2),$\overrightarrow{FC}$*=*(0,1,*-*2)*.*
> > ![id:2147518698;FounderCES](images/image3.jpeg)
> > 故$\overrightarrow{B_{1}D} \cdot \overrightarrow{FC}$*=*0*-*2*+*4*=*2≠0*.*故$\overrightarrow{B_{1}D},\overrightarrow{FC}$不互相垂直,
> > 又*CF*⊂平面*CEF*,故$B_{1}$*D*⊥平面*CEF*不成立,B错误*.*
> > 对于C,同B中空间直角坐标系,
> > 有$\overrightarrow{CE}$*=*(1,*-*2,2),$\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{DA} + \overrightarrow{DD_{1}} - \overrightarrow{DC} = \frac{1}{2}$(2,0,0)*+*(0,0,2)*-*(0,2,0)*=*(1,*-*2,2),
> > 故$\overrightarrow{CE} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{DA} + \overrightarrow{DD_{1}} - \overrightarrow{DC}$成立,C正确*.*
> > 对于D,同B中空间直角坐标系,有$\overrightarrow{CE}$*=*(1,*-*2,2),$\overrightarrow{AD_{1}}$*=*(*-*2,0,2),
> > 所以cos*\<*$\overrightarrow{CE},{\overrightarrow{AD}}_{1}$*\>=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{CE}\text{·}\overrightarrow{AD_{1}}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{CE}\text{||}\overrightarrow{AD_{1}}\text{|}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{6}$,故D正确*.*
