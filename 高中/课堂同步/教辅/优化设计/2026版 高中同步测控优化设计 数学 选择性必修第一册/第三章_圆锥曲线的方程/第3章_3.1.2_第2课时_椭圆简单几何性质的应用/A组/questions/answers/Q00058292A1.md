> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)设动点$P$的坐标是($x$,$y$),
> > 由题意得,*k~PA~*·*k~PB~=-*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 即$\frac{y}{x + \sqrt{2}} \cdot \frac{y}{x\text{-}\sqrt{2}}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$,化简整理得$\frac{x^{2}}{2}$*+y*^2^*=*1*.*
> > 故点$P$的轨迹方程$C$是$\frac{x^{2}}{2}$*+y*^2^*=*1($x$≠±$\sqrt{2}$)*.*
> > (2)设直线$l$与曲线$C$的交点$M$($x_{1}$,$y_{1}$),$N$($x_{2}$,$y_{2}$),
> > 由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > y = kx + 1\text{,} \\
> > \frac{x^{2}}{2} + y^{2} = 1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$得(1*+*2$k^{2}$)$x^{2}$*+*4*kx=*0*.*
> > ∴$x_{1}$*+x*~2~*=*$\frac{\text{-}4k}{1 + 2k^{2}}$,$x_{1}$·$x_{2}$*=*0*.*
> > *\|MN\|=*$\sqrt{1 + k^{2}} \cdot \sqrt{\text{(}x_{1} + x_{2}\text{)}^{2}\text{-}4x_{1}\text{·}x_{2}} = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}$,
> > 整理得$k^{4}$*+k*^2^*-*2*=*0,解得$k^{2}$*=*1或$k^{2}$*=-*2(舍)*.*
> > ∴*k=*±1,经检验符合题意*.*
> > ∴直线$l$的方程是*y=*±*x+*1,即*x-y+*1*=*0或*x+y-*1*=*0*.*
