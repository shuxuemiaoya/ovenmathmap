>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)因为双曲线的焦点在$y$轴上,所以可设双曲线的标准方程为$\frac{y^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{x^{2}}{b^{2}}$*=*1(*a\>*0,*b\>*0)*.*
> > 由题设知,*a=*2$\sqrt{5}$,点$A$(2,*-*5)在双曲线上,
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 2\sqrt{5}\text{,} \\
> > \frac{25}{a^{2}}\text{-}\frac{4}{b^{2}} = 1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a^{2} = 20\text{,} \\
> > b^{2} = 16\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故所求双曲线的标准方程为$\frac{y^{2}}{20} - \frac{x^{2}}{16}$*=*1*.*
> > (2)椭圆$\frac{x^{2}}{27} + \frac{y^{2}}{36}$*=*1的两个焦点为$F_{1}$(0,*-*3),$F_{2}$(0,3),双曲线与椭圆的一个交点为($\sqrt{15}$,4)(或(*-*$\sqrt{15}$,4))*.*
> > 设双曲线的标准方程为$\frac{y^{2}}{a^{2}} - \frac{x^{2}}{b^{2}}$*=*1(*a\>*0,*b\>*0),
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \frac{4^{2}}{a^{2}}\text{-}\frac{\text{(}\sqrt{15}\text{)}^{2}}{b^{2}} = 1\text{,} \\
> > a^{2} + b^{2} = 3^{2}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a^{2} = 4\text{,} \\
> > b^{2} = 5\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故所求双曲线的标准方程为$\frac{y^{2}}{4} - \frac{x^{2}}{5}$*=*1*.*
