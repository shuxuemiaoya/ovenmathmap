> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** ±1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 不妨设点$B$在第一象限,则$A_{1}$(*-a*,0),*B*$\left( c\text{,}\frac{b^{2}}{a} \right)$,$A_{2}$($a$,0),*C*$\left( c\text{,-}\frac{b^{2}}{a} \right)$,
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B} = \left( a + c\text{,}\frac{b^{2}}{a} \right),\overrightarrow{A_{2}C} = \left( c\text{-}a\text{,-}\frac{b^{2}}{a} \right)$*.*
> > 因为$A_{1}$*B*⊥$A_{2}$*C*,所以$\overrightarrow{A_{1}B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A_{2}C}$*=*0,所以$c^{2}$*-a*^2^*-*$\frac{b^{4}}{a^{2}}$*=*0,整理得,$\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}$*=*1,即$\frac{b}{a}$*=*1,
> > 所以渐近线的斜率为±1*.*
