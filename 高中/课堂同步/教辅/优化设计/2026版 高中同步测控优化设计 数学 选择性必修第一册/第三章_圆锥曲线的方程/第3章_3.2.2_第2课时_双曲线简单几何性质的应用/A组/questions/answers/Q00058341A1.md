> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{16\sqrt{3}}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图,由双曲线的方程得,两焦点分别为$F_{1}$(*-*3,0),$F_{2}$(3,0)*.*
> > ![id:2147517974;FounderCES](../../images/image3.jpeg)
> > 因为直线*AB*的倾斜角是30°,且直线过左焦点,所以直线*AB*的方程是*y=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$(*x+*3),
> > 联立,得方程组$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > y = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\text{(}x + 3\text{)}\text{,} \\
> > 2x^{2}\text{-}y^{2} = 6\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$消去$y$,得5$x^{2}$*-*6*x-*27*=*0,解这个方程得$x_{1}$*=*3,$x_{2}$*=-*$\frac{9}{5}$,分别代入直线*AB*的方程,得$y_{1}$*=*2$\sqrt{3}$,$y_{2}$*=*$\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{5}$,
> > 所以$A$,$B$的坐标分别为(3,2$\sqrt{3}$),$\left( \text{-}\frac{9}{5}\text{,}\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{5} \right)$*.*
> > 所以*\|AB\|=*$\sqrt{\text{(}x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}\text{)}^{2} + \text{(}y_{1}\text{-}y_{2}\text{)}^{2}}$*=*$\sqrt{\left( 3 + \frac{9}{5} \right)^{2} + \left( 2\sqrt{3}\text{-}\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{5} \right)^{2}}$*=*$\frac{16\sqrt{3}}{5}$*.*
