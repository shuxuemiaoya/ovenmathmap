> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *k~PA~*·*k~PB~*为定值*.*理由如下:
> > ∵点$P$在椭圆$\frac{x^{2}}{25} + \frac{y^{2}}{16}$*=*1上,
> > ∴$y^{2}$*=*16×$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{x^{2}}{25} \right)$*=*16×$\frac{25\text{-}x^{2}}{25}$*.*①
> > ∵点$P$的纵坐标$y$≠0,∴$x$≠±5*.*
> > ∴*k~PA~=*$\frac{y}{x + 5}$,*k~PB~=*$\frac{y}{x\text{-}5}$*.*
> > ∴*k~PA~*·*k~PB~=*$\frac{y}{x + 5} \cdot \frac{y}{x\text{-}5} = \frac{y^{2}}{x^{2}\text{-}25}$,②
> > 将①代入②得,*k~AP~*·*k~PB~=*$\frac{16 \times \frac{25\text{-}x^{2}}{25}}{x^{2}\text{-}25}$*=-*$\frac{16}{25}$*.*
> > ∴*k~PA~*·*k~PB~*为定值,这个定值是*-*$\frac{16}{25}$*.*
