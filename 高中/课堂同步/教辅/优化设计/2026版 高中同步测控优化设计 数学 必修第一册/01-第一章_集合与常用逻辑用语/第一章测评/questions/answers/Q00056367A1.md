> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)当*a=*$\frac{1}{2}$时,*B=*$\left\{ x\left| \frac{1}{2} < x < \frac{9}{4} \right.\  \right\}$,
> > 则∁*~U~B=*$\left\{ x\left| x \leq \frac{1}{2}\text{,}\text{或}x \geq \frac{9}{4} \right.\  \right\}$*.*
> > 故(∁*~U~B*)∩*A=*$\left\{ x\left| \frac{9}{4} \leq x < 3 \right.\  \right\}$*.*
> > (2)由命题$p$:$x$∈$A$,命题$q$:$x$∈$B$,$q$是$p$的必要条件,得$p$⇒$q$,即$A$⊆$B$,
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a \leq 2\text{,} \\
> > a^{2} + 2 \geq 3\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$得$a$≤*-*1或1≤$a$≤2*.*
> > 故实数$a$的取值范围为{*a\|a*≤*-*1或1≤$a$≤2}*.*
