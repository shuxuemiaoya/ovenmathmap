> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)若*A=*⌀,则$A$∩*B=*⌀成立*.*
> > 此时2*a+*1*\>*3*a-*5,即*a\<*6;
> > 若$A$≠⌀,如图,把集合$A$,$B$在数轴上表示出来,
> > ![id:2147492777;FounderCES](../../images/image6.jpeg)
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2a + 1 \leq 3a\text{-}5\text{,} \\
> > 2a + 1 \geq \text{-}1\text{,} \\
> > 3a\text{-}5 \leq 16\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得6≤$a$≤7*.*
> > 综上,满足$A$∩*B=*⌀的实数$a$的取值范围是{*a\|a*≤7}*.*
> > (2)因为$A$⊆($A$∩$B$),
> > 所以$A$∩*B=A*,即$A$⊆*B.*
> > 显然*A=*⌀满足条件,此时*a\<*6*.*
> > 若$A$≠⌀,如图,把集合$A$,$B$在数轴上表示出来,
> > ![id:2147492784;FounderCES](../../images/image7.jpeg)
> > 则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2a + 1 \leq 3a\text{-}5\text{,} \\
> > 3a\text{-}5 < \text{-}1
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 或\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2a + 1 \leq 3a\text{-}5\text{,} \\
> > 2a + 1 > 16\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2a + 1 \leq 3a\text{-}5\text{,} \\
> > 3a\text{-}5 < \text{-}1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$a$∈⌀;
> > 由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2a + 1 \leq 3a\text{-}5\text{,} \\
> > 2a + 1 > 16\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*a\>*$\frac{15}{2}$*.*
> > 综上,满足$A$⊆($A$∩$B$)的实数$a$的取值范围是$\left\{ a\left| a < 6\text{或}a > \frac{15}{2} \right.\  \right\}$*.*
