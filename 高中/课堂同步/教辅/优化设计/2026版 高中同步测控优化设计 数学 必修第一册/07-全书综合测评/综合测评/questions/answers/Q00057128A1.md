> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2f\text{(}x\text{)} + f\text{(-}x\text{)} = \text{(}2\text{-}m\text{)}log_{4}\text{(}1\text{-}x\text{)} + \text{(}1\text{-}2m\text{)}log_{4}\text{(}1 + x\text{),} \\
> > 2f\text{(-}x\text{)} + f\text{(}x\text{)} = \text{(}2\text{-}m\text{)}log_{4}\text{(}1 + x\text{)} + \text{(}1\text{-}2m\text{)}log_{4}\text{(}1\text{-}x\text{),}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 可得$f$($x$)*=*log~4~(1*-x*)*-m*log~4~(1*+x*),$x$∈(*-*1,1)*.*
> > 当*m=*1时,$f$($x$)*=-f*(*-x*),此时$f$($x$)为奇函数;当*m=-*1时,$f$($x$)*=f*(*-x*),此时$f$($x$)为偶函数;当$m$≠1,且$m$≠*-*1时,$f$($x$)是非奇非偶函数*.*
> > (2)由(1)可知当*m=*1时,$f$($x$)是奇函数*.*此时$f$($x$)*=*log~4~(1*-x*)*-*log~4~(1*+x*)*=*log~4~$\frac{1\text{-}x}{1 + x}$*.*
> > 由于方程$f$(2*^-x^*)*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*x+*$\frac{a}{2}$在*x\>*0时有实根,
> > 即log~4~$\frac{2^{x}\text{-}1}{2^{x} + 1} - \frac{1}{2}$*x=*$\frac{1}{2}$*a*,
> > 即log~2~$\frac{2^{x}\text{-}1}{2^{x} + 1}$*-x=a*在*x\>*0时有实根*.*
> > 令2*^x^=t*,*t\>*1,设函数$g$($t$)*=*log~2~$\frac{t\text{-}1}{t + 1}$*-*log~2~$t$,*t\>*1,只需求函数$g$($t$)的值域*.*
> > 可知$g$($t$)*=*log~2~$\frac{t\text{-}1}{t^{2} + t}$*=*log~2~$\frac{1}{t\text{-}1 + \frac{2}{t\text{-}1} + 3}$,*t\>*1,
> > 因为*t-*1*+*$\frac{2}{t\text{-}1}$*+*3≥3*+*2$\sqrt{2}$,当且仅当*t=*$\sqrt{2}$*+*1时,等号成立,取得最小值3*+*2$\sqrt{2}$,
> > 所以$g$($t$)≤log~2~(3*-*2$\sqrt{2}$),
> > 所以$a$≤log~2~(3*-*2$\sqrt{2}$),
> > 即实数$a$的取值范围是(*-∞*,log~2~(3*-*2$\sqrt{2}$)\]*.*
