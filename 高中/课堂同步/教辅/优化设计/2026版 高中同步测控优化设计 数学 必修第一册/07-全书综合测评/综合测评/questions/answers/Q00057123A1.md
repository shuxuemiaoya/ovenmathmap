> > [!success] **【答案】** \[*-*1,*+∞*)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由于函数$f$($x$)*=$log_{a}$*(*-x+*1)(*a\>*0,且$a$≠1)在区间\[*-*2,0\]上的值域是\[*-*1,0\],
> > 当*a\>*1时,$f$($x$)*=$log_{a}$*(*-x+*1)在区间\[*-*2,0\]上单调递减,
> > 故$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > f\text{(-}2\text{)} = log_{a}3 = 0\text{,} \\
> > f\text{(}0\text{)} = log_{a}1 = \text{-}1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$无解;
> > 当0*\<a\<*1时,$f$($x$)*=$log_{a}$*(*-x+*1)在区间\[*-*2,0\]上单调递增,
> > 故$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > f\text{(-}2\text{)} = log_{a}3 = \text{-}1\text{,} \\
> > f\text{(}0\text{)} = log_{a}1 = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得*a=*$\frac{1}{3}$*.*
> > 因为$g$($x$)*=*$\left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{x + m}$*-*3的图象不经过第一象限,
> > 所以$g$(0)*=*$\left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{m}$*-*3≤0,
> > 得$m$≥*-*1,即$m$的取值范围是\[*-*1,*+∞*)*.*
