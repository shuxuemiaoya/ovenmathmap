> > [!success] **【答案】** BC
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (方法一)由$x^{2}$*+y*^2^*-xy=*1,得$\left( x\text{-}\frac{y}{2} \right)^{2} + \left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}y \right)^{2}$*=*1*.*
> > 令$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x\text{-}\frac{y}{2} = cos\theta\text{,} \\
> > \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}y = sin\theta
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$(*θ*为参数),
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin\theta + cos\theta\text{,} \\
> > y = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin\theta
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$(*θ*为参数),
> > 故*x+y=*$\sqrt{3}$sin *θ+*cos *θ=*2sin$\left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$∈\[*-*2,2\],故选项A错误,B正确;
> > $x^{2}$*+y*^2^*=*$\left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin\theta + cos\theta \right)^{2} + \left( \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\sin\theta \right)^{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$sin 2*θ-*$\frac{1}{3}$cos 2*θ+*$\frac{4}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$sin(2*θ-φ*)*+*$\frac{4}{3} \in \left\lbrack \frac{2}{3}\text{,}2 \right\rbrack\left( \text{其中}\tan\varphi = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} \right)$,故选项D不正确,C正确*.*故选BC*.*
> > (方法二)由基本不等式可得*xy*≤$\frac{x^{2} + y^{2}}{2}$,
> > 则*xy+*$\frac{x^{2} + y^{2}}{2} \leq \frac{x^{2} + y^{2}}{2} + \frac{x^{2} + y^{2}}{2}$*=x*^2^*+y*^2^,
> > 即$\frac{\text{(}x + y\text{)}^{2}}{2}$≤$x^{2}$*+y*^2^*.*
> > *∴x*^2^*+y*^2^*-xy*≥$\frac{\text{(}x + y\text{)}^{2}}{2}$*-xy=*$\frac{x^{2} + y^{2}}{2} \geq \frac{\text{(}x + y\text{)}^{2}}{4}$*.*
> > 又$x^{2}$*+y*^2^*-xy=*1,*∴x*^2^*+y*^2^≤2,(*x+y*)^2^≤4,
> > *∴\|x+y\|*≤2*.∴*选项B,C正确*.*
> > 取*x=y=*1,则$x^{2}$*+y*^2^*-xy=*1,且*x+y=*2,因此选项A不正确*.*
> > 取*x=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$,*y=-*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$,则$x^{2}$*+y*^2^*-xy=*1,且$x^{2}$*+y*^2^*=*$\frac{2}{3}$,因此选项D不正确*.*
> > 故正确选项为B和C*.*
