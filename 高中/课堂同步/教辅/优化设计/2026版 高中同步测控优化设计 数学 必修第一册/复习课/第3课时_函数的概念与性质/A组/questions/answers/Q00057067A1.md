> > 设解析式为*y=kx+b*($k$≠0)*.*

> > \text{-}k + b = 0\text{,} \\
> > b = 1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 得\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > k = 1\text{,} \\
> > b = 1\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$故*y=x+*1;
> > 当*x\>*0时,设解析式为*y=a*(*x-*2)^2^*-*1,
> > 因为图象过点(4,0),
> > 所以0*=a*(4*-*2)^2^*-*1,得*a=*$\frac{1}{4}$*.*
> > 因此$f$($x$)*=*$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x + 1\text{,-}1 \leq x \leq 0\text{,} \\
> > \frac{1}{4}\text{(}x\text{-}2\text{)}^{2}\text{-}1\text{,}x > 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > (2)当*-*1≤$x$≤0时,$y$∈\[0,1\];
> > 当*x\>*0时,$y$∈\[*-*1,*+∞*)*.*
