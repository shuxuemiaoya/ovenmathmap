> > (2)*∵α*∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\pi \right)$,*β*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,*∴α+β*∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\frac{3\pi}{2} \right)$,
> > 又sin(*α+β*)*=-*$\frac{3}{5}$,*∴*cos(*α+β*)*=-*$\frac{4}{5}$*.*
> > *∴*sin *β=*sin\[(*α+β*)*-α*\]*=*sin(*α+β*)cos *α-*cos(*α+β*)sin *α=-*$\frac{3}{5} \times \left( \text{-}\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3} \right) - \left( \text{-}\frac{4}{5} \right) \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4 + 6\sqrt{2}}{15}$*.*
