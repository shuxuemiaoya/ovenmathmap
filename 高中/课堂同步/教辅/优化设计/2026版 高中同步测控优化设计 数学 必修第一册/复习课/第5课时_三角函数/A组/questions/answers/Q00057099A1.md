> > [!success] **【答案】** *-*$\frac{4}{3}　\frac{28}{25}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由tan$\frac{\alpha}{2}$*=*2,得tan *α=*$\frac{2tan\frac{\alpha}{2}}{1\text{-}tan^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{2 \times 2}{1\text{-}2^{2}}$*=-*$\frac{4}{3}$*.*
> > 故sin^2^*α-*sin *α*cos *α=*$\frac{sin^{2}\alpha\text{-}\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{sin^{2}\alpha + cos^{2}\alpha} = \frac{tan^{2}\alpha\text{-}\tan\alpha}{tan^{2}\alpha + 1} = \frac{\frac{16}{9}\text{-}\left( \text{-}\frac{4}{3} \right)}{\frac{16}{9} + 1} = \frac{28}{25}$*.*
