>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)要使函数$f$($x$)有意义,则有$\frac{1 + x}{1\text{-}x}$*\>*0,
> > 得*-*1*\<x\<*1,
> > 故函数$f$($x$)的定义域为(*-*1,1)*.*
> > (2)函数$f$($x$)为奇函数*.*理由如下:
> > *∵f*($x$)的定义域为(*-*1,1),关于原点对称,
> > 对任意的$x$∈(*-*1,1),有$f$(*-x*)*=*$\frac{2^{\text{-}x}\text{-}1}{2^{\text{-}x} + 1}$*+*log~2~$\frac{1\text{-}x}{1 + x} = \frac{1\text{-}2^{x}}{1 + 2^{x}}$*-*log~2~$\frac{1 + x}{1\text{-}x}$*=-f*($x$),
> > *∴f*($x$)为奇函数*.*
> > (3)要求函数$g$($x$)的零点,即求方程$g$($x$)*=*0的解*.*
> > 由$f$(1*-x*^2^)*+f*$\left( \frac{3x}{2} \right)$*=*0及$f$($x$)为奇函数,可得*f*$\left( \frac{3x}{2} \right)$*=-f*(1*-x*^2^)*=f*($x^{2}$*-*1),任取$x_{1}$,$x_{2}$∈(*-*1,1),且$x_{1}$*\<x*~2~,
> > $f$($x_{1}$)*-f*($x_{2}$)*=*$\left( \frac{2^{x_{1}}\text{-}1}{2^{x_{1}} + 1} + log_{2}\frac{1 + x_{1}}{1\text{-}x_{1}} \right) - \left( \frac{2^{x_{2}}\text{-}1}{2^{x_{2}} + 1} + log_{2}\frac{1 + x_{2}}{1\text{-}x_{2}} \right) = \frac{2\text{(}2^{x_{1}}\text{-}2^{x_{2}}\text{)}}{\text{(}2^{x_{1}} + 1\text{)(}2^{x_{2}} + 1\text{)}}$*+*log~2~$\left( \frac{1 + x_{1}}{1\text{-}x_{1}}\text{·}\frac{1\text{-}x_{2}}{1 + x_{2}} \right)$*.*
> > *∵-*1*\<x*~1~*\<x*~2~*\<*1,$\frac{2\text{(}2^{x_{1}}\text{-}2^{x_{2}}\text{)}}{\text{(}2^{x_{1}} + 1\text{)(}2^{x_{2}} + 1\text{)}}$*\<*0,
> > (1*-x*~1~)(1*+x*~2~)*-*(1*+x*~1~)(1*-x*~2~)*=*2($x_{2}$*-x*~1~)*\>*0,
> > *∴*0*\<*$\frac{1 + x_{1}}{1\text{-}x_{1}} \cdot \frac{1\text{-}x_{2}}{1 + x_{2}}$*\<*1,
> > *∴*log~2~$\left( \frac{1 + x_{1}}{1\text{-}x_{1}}\text{·}\frac{1\text{-}x_{2}}{1 + x_{2}} \right)$*\<*0,
> > *∴f*($x_{1}$)*-f*($x_{2}$)*\<*0,即$f$($x_{1}$)*\<f*($x_{2}$),
> > *∴f*($x$)在定义域(*-*1,1)内为增函数,
> > *∴*由*f*$\left( \frac{3x}{2} \right)$*=f*($x^{2}$*-*1),
> > 得$\frac{3x}{2}$*=x*^2^*-*1,
> > 得*x=*2或*x=-*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 验证当*x=*2时,1*-x*^2^*\<-*1,不符合题意,
> > 当*x=-*$\frac{1}{2}$时,符合题意*.*
> > *∴*函数$g$($x$)的零点为*x=-*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
