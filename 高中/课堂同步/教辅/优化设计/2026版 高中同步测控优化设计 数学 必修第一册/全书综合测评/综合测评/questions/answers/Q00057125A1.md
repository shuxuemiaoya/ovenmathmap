> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)设图象的一个最高点为($x_{1}$,1),相邻的最低点为($x_{2}$,*-*1),则*\|x*~1~*-x*~2~*\|=*$\frac{T}{2}$($T$为最小正周期,*T\>*0)*.*
> > *∵*$\sqrt{\text{(}x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}\text{)}^{2} + \text{(}1 + 1\text{)}^{2}} = \sqrt{4 + \pi^{2}}$*.*
> > *∴*$\frac{T^{2}}{4}$*+*4*=*4*+*π^2^,
> > *∴T=*2π*=*$\frac{2\pi}{\text{|}\omega\text{|}}$*.*
> > 又*ω\>*0,
> > *∴ω=*1,即$f$($x$)*=*sin(*x+φ*)*.*
> > *∵f*($x$)是偶函数,
> > *∴φ=k*π*+*$\frac{\pi}{2}$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > *∵*0≤*φ*≤π,
> > *∴φ=*$\frac{\pi}{2}$*.*
> > 即$f$($x$)*=*sin$\left( x + \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$*=*cos *x.*
> > (2)*∵*tan *α+*$\frac{1}{\tan\alpha}$*=*5,
> > *∴*$\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} + \frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$*=*5,
> > *∴*sin *α*cos *α=*$\frac{1}{5}$*.*
> > 则$\frac{\sqrt{2}f\left( 2\alpha\text{-}\frac{\pi\text{ }}{4} \right)\text{-}1}{1\text{-}\tan\alpha} = \frac{\sqrt{2}\cos\left( 2\alpha\text{-}\frac{\pi\text{ }}{4} \right)\text{-}1}{1\text{-}\tan\alpha} = \frac{cos2\alpha + sin2\alpha\text{-}1}{\frac{\cos\alpha\text{-}\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}} = \frac{\text{(}2sin\alpha\cos\alpha\text{-}2sin^{2}\alpha\text{)}\cos\alpha}{\cos\alpha\text{-}\sin\alpha}$*=*2sin *α*cos *α=*$\frac{2}{5}$*.*
