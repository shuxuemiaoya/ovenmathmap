> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由已知原方程判别式*Δ*≥0,即(*-a*)^2^*-*4*a*≥0,则$a$≥4或$a$≤0*.*
> > 又$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \sin\theta + cos\theta = a\text{,} \\
> > \sin\theta\cos\theta = a\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$(sin *θ+*cos *θ*)^2^*=*1*+*2sin *θ*cos *θ*,
> > 所以$a^{2}$*-*2*a-*1*=*0,
> > 得*a=*1*-*$\sqrt{2}$或*a=*1*+*$\sqrt{2}$(舍去)*.*
> > 所以sin *θ+*cos *θ=*sin *θ*cos *θ=*1*-*$\sqrt{2}$*.*
> > (1)cos$\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{-}\theta \right)$*+*sin$\left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right)$*=*sin *θ+*cos *θ=*1*-*$\sqrt{2}$*.*
> > (2)tan(π*-θ*)*-*$\frac{1}{\tan\theta}$*=-*tan *θ-*$\frac{1}{\tan\theta}$*=-*(tan *θ+*$\frac{1}{\tan\theta}$)*=-*$\left( \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} + \frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta} \right)$*=-*$\frac{1}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}$*=-*$\frac{1}{1\text{-}\sqrt{2}} = \sqrt{2}$*+*1*.*
