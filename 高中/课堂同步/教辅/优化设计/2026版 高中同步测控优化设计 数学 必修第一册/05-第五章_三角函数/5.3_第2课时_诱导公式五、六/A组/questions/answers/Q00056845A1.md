> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** *-*2*　*$\frac{1}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为角*α*的终边过点$P$(1,*-*2),
> > 所以tan *α=*$\frac{\text{-}2}{1}$*=-*2,
> > 所以$\frac{\sin\text{(}\pi\text{-}\alpha\text{)} + cos\text{(-}\alpha\text{)}}{2cos\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{-}\alpha \right)\text{-}\sin\left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha \right)} = \frac{\sin\alpha + cos\alpha}{2sin\alpha\text{-}\cos\alpha} = \frac{\tan\alpha + 1}{2tan\alpha\text{-}1} = \frac{\text{-}2 + 1}{2 \times \text{(-}2\text{)-}1} = \frac{1}{5}$*.*
