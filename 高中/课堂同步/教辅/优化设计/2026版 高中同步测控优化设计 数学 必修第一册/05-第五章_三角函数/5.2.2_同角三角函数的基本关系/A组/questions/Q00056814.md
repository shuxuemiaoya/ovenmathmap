10. 求证:$\frac{\sin\alpha}{1\text{-}\cos\alpha} = \frac{1 + cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$*.*
证明:*∵*左边*=*$\frac{\sin\alpha}{1\text{-}\cos\alpha} = \frac{\sin\alpha\text{(}1 + cos\alpha\text{)}}{\text{(}1\text{-}\cos\alpha\text{)(}1 + cos\alpha\text{)}} = \frac{\sin\alpha\text{(}1 + cos\alpha\text{)}}{1\text{-}cos^{2}\alpha} = \frac{\sin\alpha\text{(}1 + cos\alpha\text{)}}{sin^{2}\alpha} = \frac{1 + cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$*=*右边,
*∴*原等式成立*.*
