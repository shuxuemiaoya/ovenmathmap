> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵*tan *α+*$\frac{1}{\tan\alpha}$*=*3,*∴*$\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} + \frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}$*=*3,
> > 即$\frac{sin^{2}\alpha + cos^{2}\alpha}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}$*=*3*.*
> > *∴*sin *α*cos *α=*$\frac{1}{3}$*.*
