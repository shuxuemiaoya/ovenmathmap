> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)因为*α*是第三象限角,cos *α=-*$\frac{3}{4}$,
> > 所以sin *α=-*$\sqrt{1\text{-}cos^{2}\alpha}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{7}}{4}$*.*
> > 所以sin 2*α=*2sin *α*cos *α=*2*×*$\left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{4} \right) \times \left( \text{-}\frac{3}{4} \right) = \frac{3\sqrt{7}}{8}$*.*
> > (2)因为*β*∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\pi \right)$,sin *β=*$\frac{2}{3}$,
> > 所以cos *β=-*$\sqrt{1\text{-}sin^{2}\beta}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$*.*
> > 因为cos *α=-*$\frac{3}{4}$,
> > 所以cos 2*α=*2cos^2^*α-*1*=*2*×*$\frac{9}{16}$*-*1*=*$\frac{1}{8}$*.*
> > 所以cos(2*α+β*)*=*cos 2*α*cos *β-*sin 2*α*sin *β=*$\frac{1}{8} \times \left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3} \right) - \frac{3\sqrt{7}}{8} \times \frac{2}{3}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{5} + 6\sqrt{7}}{24}$*.*
