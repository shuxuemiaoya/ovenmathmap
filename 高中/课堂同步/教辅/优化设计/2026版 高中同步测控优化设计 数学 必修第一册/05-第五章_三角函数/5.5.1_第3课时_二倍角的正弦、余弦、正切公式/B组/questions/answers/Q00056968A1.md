> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}　\frac{4}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由sin^2^*α+*sin 2*α=*1,得$\frac{sin^{2}\alpha + 2sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{sin^{2}\alpha + cos^{2}\alpha}$*=*1,
> > *∴*$\frac{tan^{2}\alpha + 2tan\alpha}{tan^{2}\alpha + 1}$*=*1,解得tan *α=*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > *∴*sin 2*α=*$\frac{2sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{sin^{2}\alpha + cos^{2}\alpha} = \frac{2tan\alpha}{tan^{2}\alpha + 1} = \frac{2 \times \frac{1}{2}}{\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} + 1} = \frac{4}{5}$*.*
