> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{11\pi}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > cos$\frac{x}{2}$*=*sin$\left( \frac{x}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,将*y=*sin($\frac{x}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2}$)的图象上所有的点向左平移*φ*(*φ\>*0)个单位长度得*y=*sin$\left( \frac{x}{2} + \frac{\varphi}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$的图象*.*
> > 令$\frac{\varphi}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2}$*=*2*k*π*+*$\frac{\pi}{3}$($k$∈*$Z$*),
> > 得*φ=*4*k*π*-*$\frac{\pi}{3}$($k$∈*$Z$*),
> > 故当*k=*1时,*φ=*$\frac{11\pi}{3}$,
> > 即为*φ*的最小正值*.*
