> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{3}　\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由cos(π*+α*)*=-*$\frac{1}{3}$,得*-*cos *α=-*$\frac{1}{3}$,则cos *α=*$\frac{1}{3}$*.*
> > 又$\frac{3\pi}{2}$*\<α\<*2π,故sin(2π*-α*)*=-*sin *α=*$\sqrt{1\text{-}cos^{2}\alpha} = \sqrt{1\text{-}\left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{2}} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$*.*
