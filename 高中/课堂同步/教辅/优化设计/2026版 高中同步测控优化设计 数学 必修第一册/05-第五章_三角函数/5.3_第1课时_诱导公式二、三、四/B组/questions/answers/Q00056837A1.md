> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** *-*$\frac{\sqrt{1\text{-}m^{2}}}{m}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由$\frac{\pi}{6}$*\<α\<*$\frac{2\pi}{3}$,可得*α+*$\frac{\pi}{3} \in \left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\pi \right)$*.*
> > 因为cos$\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*=m*,
> > 所以sin$\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \sqrt{1\text{-}cos^{2}\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)} = \sqrt{1\text{-}m^{2}}$,
> > 所以tan$\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{1\text{-}m^{2}}}{m}$*.*
> > 所以tan$\left( \frac{2\pi}{3}\text{-}\alpha \right)$*=*tan$\left\lbrack \pi\text{-}\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) \right\rbrack$*=-*tan(*α+*$\frac{\pi}{3}$)*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{1\text{-}m^{2}}}{m}$*.*
