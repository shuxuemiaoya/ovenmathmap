> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{7}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由已知得sin\[(*α-β*)*+β*\]*=*$\frac{3}{5}$,即sin *α=*$\frac{3}{5}$*.*又因为*α*∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\pi \right)$,所以cos *α=-*$\frac{4}{5}$,
> > 于是tan *α=-*$\frac{3}{4}$,故tan$\left( \alpha\text{-}\frac{3\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{\tan\alpha\text{-}\tan\text{ }\frac{3\pi}{4}}{1 + tan\alpha\tan\text{ }\frac{3\pi}{4}} = \frac{\text{-}\frac{3}{4}\text{-(-}1\text{)}}{1 + \left( \text{-}\frac{3}{4} \right) \times \text{(-}1\text{)}} = \frac{1}{7}$*.*
