> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为tan$\left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{2}$,所以tan *θ=*tan![](../../images/image1.jpeg)*θ+*$\frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\pi}{4}$![](../../images/image2.jpeg)*=*$\frac{\tan\left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)\text{-}\tan\frac{\pi}{4}}{1 + tan\left( \theta + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)\tan\frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}\text{-}1}{1 + \frac{1}{2}}$*=-*$\frac{1}{3}$*.*
> > 由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \tan\theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} = \text{-}\frac{1}{3}\text{,} \\
> > sin^{2}\theta + cos^{2}\theta = 1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 且*θ*为第二象限角,可得cos *θ=-*$\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$*.*
