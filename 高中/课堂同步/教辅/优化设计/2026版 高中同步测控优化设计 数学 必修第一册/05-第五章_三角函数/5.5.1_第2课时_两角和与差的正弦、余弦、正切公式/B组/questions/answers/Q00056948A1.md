> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为sin(*α+β*)*=*$\frac{3}{5}$,sin(*α-β*)*=-*$\frac{2}{3}$,
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \sin\alpha\cos\beta + cos\alpha\sin\beta = \frac{3}{5}\text{,} \\
> > \sin\alpha\cos\beta\text{-}\cos\alpha\sin\beta = \text{-}\frac{2}{3}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \sin\alpha\cos\beta = \text{-}\frac{1}{30}\text{,} \\
> > \cos\alpha\sin\beta = \frac{19}{30}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以$\frac{\tan\alpha}{\tan\beta} = \frac{\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}}{\frac{\sin\beta}{\cos\beta}} = \frac{\sin\alpha\cos\beta}{\cos\alpha\sin\beta} = \frac{\text{-}\frac{1}{30}}{\frac{19}{30}}$*=-*$\frac{1}{19}$*.*
