> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{7}{8}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵*角*α*与角*β*均以*Ox*为始边,它们的终边关于$x$轴对称,
> > *∴*sin *α=-*sin *β=*$\frac{1}{4}$,cos *α=*cos *β=*$\frac{\sqrt{15}}{4}$*.*
> > *∴*cos(*α-β*)*=*cos *α*cos *β+*sin *α*sin *β=*$\frac{\sqrt{15}}{4} \times \frac{\sqrt{15}}{4} + \frac{1}{4} \times \left( \text{-}\frac{1}{4} \right) = \frac{14}{16} = \frac{7}{8}$*.*
