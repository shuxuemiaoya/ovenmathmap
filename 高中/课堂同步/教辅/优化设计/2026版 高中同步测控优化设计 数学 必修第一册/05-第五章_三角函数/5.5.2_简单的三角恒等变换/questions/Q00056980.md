10. 求证:tan$\frac{3x}{2}$*-*tan$\frac{x}{2} = \frac{2sinx}{\cos x + cos2x}$*.*
证明:*∵*左边*=*tan$\frac{3x}{2}$*-*tan$\frac{x}{2} = \frac{\sin\frac{3x}{2}}{\cos\frac{3x}{2}} - \frac{\sin\frac{x}{2}}{\cos\frac{x}{2}} = \frac{\sin\frac{3x}{2}\cos\frac{x}{2}\text{-}\cos\frac{3x}{2}\sin\frac{x}{2}}{\cos\frac{3x}{2}\cos\frac{x}{2}} = \frac{\sin\left( \frac{3x}{2}\text{-}\frac{x}{2} \right)}{\cos\frac{3x}{2}\cos\frac{x}{2}} = \frac{\sin x}{\cos\frac{3x}{2}\cos\frac{x}{2}} = \frac{2sinx}{\cos\left( \frac{3x}{2} + \frac{x}{2} \right) + cos\left( \frac{3x}{2}\text{-}\frac{x}{2} \right)} = \frac{2sinx}{\cos x + cos2x}$*=*右边,
*∴*原等式成立*.*
