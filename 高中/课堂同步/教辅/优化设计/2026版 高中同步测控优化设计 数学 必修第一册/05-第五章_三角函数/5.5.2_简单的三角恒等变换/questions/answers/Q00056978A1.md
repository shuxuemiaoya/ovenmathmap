> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为1*-*2sin^2^$\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$*=*cos$\left( 2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$*=-*sin 2*α*,
> > 所以sin^2^$\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{3}{4}$*.*
> > 因为*α*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,所以*α+*$\frac{\pi}{4} \in \left( \frac{\pi}{4}\text{,}\frac{3\pi}{4} \right)$*.*
> > 所以sin$\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$*.*
