> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)*∵f*($x$)*=*$\sqrt{3}$sin *x*cos *x+*cos^2^*x-*$\frac{1}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$sin 2*x+*$\frac{1}{2}$cos 2*x=*sin$\left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$,
> > *∴*函数$f$($x$)的最小正周期为*T=*$\frac{2\pi}{2}$*=*π*.*
> > 由*-*$\frac{\pi}{2}$*+*2*k*π≤2*x+*$\frac{\pi}{6} \leq \frac{\pi}{2}$*+*2*k*π,$k$∈*$Z$*,
> > 得*-*$\frac{\pi}{3}$*+k*π≤$x$≤$\frac{\pi}{6}$*+k*π,$k$∈*$Z$**.*
> > 故函数$f$($x$)的单调递增区间为\[*-*$\frac{\pi}{3}$*+k*π,$\frac{\pi}{6}$*+k*π\] ($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > (2)将$f$($x$)的图象所有点的横坐标伸长到原来的2倍(纵坐标不变),得到$g$($x$)*=*sin$\left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$的图象*.*
> > *∵*0≤$x$≤$\frac{\pi}{2}$*.∴*$\frac{\pi}{6}$≤*x+*$\frac{\pi}{6} \leq \frac{2\pi}{3}$*.*
> > *∴*$\frac{1}{2}$≤sin$\left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$≤1*.∴*$\frac{1}{2}$≤$g$($x$)≤1*.*
> > *∴*关于$x$的方程$g$($x$)*-k=*0在区间$\left\lbrack 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right\rbrack$上有实数解,即$g$($x$)的图象与直线*y=k*有交点*.*
> > *∴*$\frac{1}{2}$≤$k$≤1*.∴k*的取值范围为$\left\lbrack \frac{1}{2}\text{,}1 \right\rbrack$*.*
