> > [!success] **【答案】** 6*　*4
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设这两个自然数分别为$x$,$y$,则4*x+*9*y=*60,
> > $\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} = \left( \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} \right)\left( \frac{4x + 9y}{60} \right) = \frac{1}{60}$(13*+*$\frac{4x}{y} + \frac{9y}{x}$)≥$\frac{1}{60}$*×*(13*+*12)*=*$\frac{5}{12}$,
> > 当且仅当$\frac{4x}{y} = \frac{9y}{x}$,且4*x+*9*y=*60,
> > 即*x=*6,且*y=*4时,等号成立,
> > 故应分别填上6,4*.*
