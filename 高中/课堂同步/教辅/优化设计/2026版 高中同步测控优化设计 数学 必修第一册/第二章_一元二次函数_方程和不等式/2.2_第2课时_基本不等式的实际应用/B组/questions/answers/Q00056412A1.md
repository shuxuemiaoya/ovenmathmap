> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由题意可得,1万件产品的年生产成本为(32*Q+*3)万元,
> > 每件产品的销售价为$\frac{32Q + 3}{Q}$*×*150%*+*$\frac{x}{Q}$*×*50%,
> > 即年销售收入为![](../../images/image2.jpeg)$\frac{32Q + 3}{Q}$*×*150%*+*$\frac{x}{Q}$*×*50%![](../../images/image3.jpeg)·*Q=*$\frac{3}{2}$(32*Q+*3)*+*$\frac{1}{2}$*x.*
> > 即年利润*W=*$\frac{3}{2}$(32*Q+*3)*+*$\frac{1}{2}$*x-*(32*Q+*3)*-x=*$\frac{1}{2}$(32*Q+*3*-x*)*=*$\frac{\text{-}x^{2} + 98x + 35}{2\text{(}x + 1\text{)}}$($x$≥0)*.*
> > (2)令*x+*1*=t*($t$≥1),则
> > *W=*$\frac{\text{-(}t\text{-}1\text{)}^{2} + 98\text{(}t\text{-}1\text{)} + 35}{2t}$*=*50*-*$\left( \frac{t}{2} + \frac{32}{t} \right)$*.*
> > 因为$t$≥1,
> > 所以$\frac{t}{2} + \frac{32}{t}$≥2$\sqrt{\frac{t}{2}\text{·}\frac{32}{t}}$*=*8,
> > 即$W$≤42,当且仅当$\frac{t}{2} = \frac{32}{t}$,
> > 即*t=*8时,等号成立,$W$有最大值42,此时*x=*7*.*
> > 故当年广告费为7万元时,企业年利润最大,最大值为42万元*.*
