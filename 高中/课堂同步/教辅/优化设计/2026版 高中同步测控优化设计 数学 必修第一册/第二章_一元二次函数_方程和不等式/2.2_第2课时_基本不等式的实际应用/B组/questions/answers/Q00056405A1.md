> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为正实数$a$,$b$满足*a+b=*1,
> > 所以*a+b*≥2$\sqrt{ab}$,可得0*\<ab*≤$\frac{1}{4}$,
> > 当且仅当*a=b*时,取等号*.*
> > 则$\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = \frac{1}{ab}$≥4,
> > 即$\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}$有最小值4*.*
> > 由0*\<*$\sqrt{ab} \leq \frac{1}{2}$,可知$\sqrt{ab}$有最大值$\frac{1}{2}$,
> > 即$\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b} = \sqrt{a + b + 2\sqrt{ab}} = \sqrt{1 + 2\sqrt{ab}} \leq \sqrt{1 + 2 \times \frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{2}$,
> > 故$\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}$有最大值$\sqrt{2}$*.*
> > 由$a^{2}$*+b*^2^≥2*ab*,可得2($a^{2}$*+b*^2^)≥(*a+b*)^2^*=*1,
> > 则$a^{2}$*+b*^2^≥$\frac{1}{2}$,当且仅当*a=b=*$\frac{1}{2}$时,$a^{2}$*+b*^2^取得最小值$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
