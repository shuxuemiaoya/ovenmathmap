> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{x}{1 + x^{2}} \leq \frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵*$\frac{x}{1 + x^{2}} - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{2x\text{-}1\text{-}x^{2}}{2\text{(}1 + x^{2}\text{)}} = \frac{\text{-(}x\text{-}1\text{)}^{2}}{2\text{(}1 + x^{2}\text{)}}$≤0,
> > *∴*$\frac{x}{1 + x^{2}} \leq \frac{1}{2}$*.*
