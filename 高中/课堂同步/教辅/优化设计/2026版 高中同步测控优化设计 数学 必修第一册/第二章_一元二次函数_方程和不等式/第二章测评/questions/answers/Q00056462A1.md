> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由3∈$M$,知$\frac{3a\text{-}5}{9\text{-}a}$*\<*0,解得*a\<*$\frac{5}{3}$或*a\>*9;
> > 若5∈$M$,则$\frac{5a\text{-}5}{25\text{-}a}$*\<*0,
> > 得*a\<*1或*a\>*25*.*
> > 则由5∉$M$,知1≤$a$≤25,因此所求$a$的取值范围是1≤*a\<*$\frac{5}{3}$或9*\<a*≤25*.*
> > (2)当*a=*4时,原不等式$\frac{4x\text{-}5}{x^{2}\text{-}4}$*\<*0*.*
> > $\frac{4x\text{-}5}{x^{2}\text{-}4}$*\<*0⇔$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 4x\text{-}5 > 0\text{,} \\
> > x^{2}\text{-}4 < 0
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 或\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 4x\text{-}5 < 0\text{,} \\
> > x^{2}\text{-}4 > 0
> > \end{matrix} \right.\  \Leftrightarrow \left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x > \frac{5}{4}\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}2 < x < 2
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 或\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x < \frac{5}{4}\text{,} \\
> > x < \text{-}2\text{或}x > 2
> > \end{matrix} \right.\  \Leftrightarrow \frac{5}{4}$*\<x\<*2或*x\<-*2*.*
> > 故*M=*$\left\{ x\left| x < \text{-}2\text{,}\text{或}\frac{5}{4} < x < 2 \right.\  \right\}$*.*
