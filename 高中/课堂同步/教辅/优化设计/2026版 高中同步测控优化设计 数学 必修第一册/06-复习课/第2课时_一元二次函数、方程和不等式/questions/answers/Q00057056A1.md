> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 作差:$\frac{1}{1 + x}$*-*(1*-x*)*=*$\frac{x^{2}}{1 + x}$*.*
> > ①当*x=*0时,*∵*$\frac{x^{2}}{1 + x}$*=*0,*∴*$\frac{1}{1 + x}$*=*1*-x*;
> > ②当1*+x\<*0,即*x\<-*1时,
> > *∵*$\frac{x^{2}}{1 + x}$*\<*0,*∴*$\frac{1}{1 + x}$*\<*1*-x*;
> > ③当1*+x\>*0,且$x$≠0,即*-*1*\<x\<*0或*x\>*0时,
> > *∵*$\frac{x^{2}}{1 + x}$*\>*0,*∴*$\frac{1}{1 + x}$*\>*1*-x.*
