> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 将点(4,2)的坐标代入$f$($x$)*=x^α^*,得2*=*4*^α^*,*α=*$\frac{1}{2}$,所以$f$($x$)*=*$x^{\frac{1}{2}}$*.*
> > 显然$f$($x$)在定义域\[0,*+∞*)内为增函数,所以A中说法正确;
> > 因为$f$($x$)的定义域为\[0,*+∞*),所以$f$($x$)不具有奇偶性,所以B中说法不正确;
> > 当*x\>*1时,$\sqrt{x}$*\>*1,即$f$($x$)*\>*1,所以C中说法正确;当0*\<x*~1~*\<x*~2~时,\[$\frac{f\text{(}x_{1}\text{)} + f\text{(}x_{2}\text{)}}{2}$\]^2^*-*\[*f*$\left( \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2} \right)$\]^2^*=*$\left( \frac{\sqrt{x_{1}} + \sqrt{x_{2}}}{2} \right)^{2} - \left( \sqrt{\frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2}} \right)^{2} = \frac{x_{1} + x_{2} + 2\sqrt{x_{1}x_{2}}}{4} - \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2} = \frac{2\sqrt{x_{1}x_{2}}\text{-}x_{1}\text{-}x_{2}}{4}$*=-*$\frac{\text{(}\sqrt{x_{1}}\text{-}\sqrt{x_{2}}\text{)}^{2}}{4}$*\<*0,所以![](../../images/image4.jpeg)$\frac{f\text{(}x_{1}\text{)} + f\text{(}x_{2}\text{)}}{2}$![](../../images/image5.jpeg)^2^*\<*${\lbrack f\left( \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2} \right)\rbrack}^{2}$,又$\frac{f\text{(}x_{1}\text{)} + f\text{(}x_{2}\text{)}}{2}$*\>*0,*f*$\left( \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2} \right)$*\>*0,所以$\frac{f\text{(}x_{1}\text{)} + f\text{(}x_{2}\text{)}}{2}$*\<f*$\left( \frac{x_{1} + x_{2}}{2} \right)$成立,所以D中说法正确*.*故选ACD*.*
