> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $f$($x$)*=*$\frac{ax + 1}{x + 2}$*=a+*$\frac{1\text{-}2a}{x + 2}$,
> > 设任意$x_{1}$,$x_{2}$∈(*-*2,*+∞*),且$x_{1}$*\<x*~2~,
> > 则$f$($x_{1}$)*-f*($x_{2}$)*=*$\frac{1\text{-}2a}{x_{1} + 2} - \frac{1\text{-}2a}{x_{2} + 2} = \frac{\text{(}1\text{-}2a\text{)(}x_{2}\text{-}x_{1}\text{)}}{\text{(}x_{2} + 2\text{)(}x_{1} + 2\text{)}}$*.*
> > 由*-*2*\<x*~1~*\<x*~2~,得$x_{2}$*-x*~1~*\>*0,($x_{2}$*+*2)($x_{1}$*+*2)*\>*0*.*
> > ①若*a\<*$\frac{1}{2}$,则1*-*2*a\>*0,$f$($x_{1}$)*-f*($x_{2}$)*\>*0,即$f$($x_{1}$)*\>f*($x_{2}$),故$f$($x$)在区间(*-*2,*+∞*)内单调递减;
> > ②若*a\>*$\frac{1}{2}$,则1*-*2*a\<*0,$f$($x_{1}$)*-f*($x_{2}$)*\<*0,即$f$($x_{1}$)*\<f*($x_{2}$),故$f$($x$)在区间(*-*2,*+∞*)内单调递增*.*
> > 综上,当*a\<*$\frac{1}{2}$时,$f$($x$)在区间(*-*2,*+∞*)内单调递减;
> > 当*a\>*$\frac{1}{2}$时,$f$($x$)在区间(*-*2,*+∞*)内单调递增*.*
