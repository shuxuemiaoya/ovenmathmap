> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 要使函数有意义,应满足$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x + 2 \geq 0\text{,} \\
> > 6\text{-}2x \geq 0\text{,} \\
> > \sqrt{6\text{-}2x}\text{-}1 \neq 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 即$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x \geq \text{-}2\text{,} \\
> > x \leq 3\text{,} \\
> > x \neq \frac{5}{2}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*-*2≤$x$≤3,且$x$≠$\frac{5}{2}$,
> > 故函数的定义域为{*x*$\left| \text{-}2 \leq x < \frac{5}{2} \right.\$,或$\frac{5}{2}$*\<x*≤3}*.*
> > 用区间可表示为$\left\lbrack \text{-}2\text{,}\frac{5}{2} \right) \cup \left( \frac{5}{2}\text{,}3 \right\rbrack$*.*
