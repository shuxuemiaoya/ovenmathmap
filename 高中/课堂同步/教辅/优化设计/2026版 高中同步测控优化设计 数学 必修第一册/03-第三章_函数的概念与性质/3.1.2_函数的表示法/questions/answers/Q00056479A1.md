> > (2)由(1)中图象,结合函数$M$($x$)的定义,可得$M$($x$)的图象,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147524158;FounderCES](images/image7.jpeg)
> > 结合图象,得函数$M$($x$)*=*$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x^{2}\text{-}4x + 3\text{,}x \leq 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}x + 3\text{,}0 < x < 1\text{,} \\
> > \frac{3}{2}x + \frac{1}{2}\text{,}1 \leq x < 5\text{,} \\
> > x^{2}\text{-}4x + 3\text{,}x \geq 5\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 且最小值在*x=*1处取得,最小值是2,故值域为\[2,*+∞*)*.*
