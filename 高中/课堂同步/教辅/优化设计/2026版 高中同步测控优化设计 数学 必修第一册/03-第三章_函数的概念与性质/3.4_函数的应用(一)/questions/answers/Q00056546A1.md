> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{32\sqrt{3}}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设梯形的上底长是$x$ m,则下底长是1 m,
> > 根据题意,得$S$($x$)*=*$\frac{4}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot \frac{\text{(}3\text{-}x\text{)}^{2}}{1\text{-}x^{2}}$(0*\<x\<*1),
> > 令3*-x=t*,则$t$∈(2,3),$\frac{1}{t} \in \left( \frac{1}{3}\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$,
> > 则*S=*$\frac{4}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot \frac{t^{2}}{\text{-}t^{2} + 6t\text{-}8} = \frac{4}{\sqrt{3}} \cdot \frac{1}{\text{-}\frac{8}{t^{2}} + \frac{6}{t}\text{-}1}$,
> > 故当$\frac{1}{t} = \frac{3}{8}$,即*x=*$\frac{1}{3}$时,$S$有最小值,最小值是$\frac{32\sqrt{3}}{3}$*.*
