> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵α*是第三象限角,cos *α=-*$\frac{4}{5}$,
> > *∴*sin *α=-*$\frac{3}{5}$,
> > *∴*$\frac{1 + tan\frac{\alpha}{2}}{1\text{-}\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2} + sin\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}\text{-}\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2} + sin\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}\text{-}\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}} \cdot \frac{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2} + sin\frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos\frac{\alpha}{2} + sin\frac{\alpha}{2}} = \frac{1 + sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{1\text{-}\frac{3}{5}}{\text{-}\frac{4}{5}}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
