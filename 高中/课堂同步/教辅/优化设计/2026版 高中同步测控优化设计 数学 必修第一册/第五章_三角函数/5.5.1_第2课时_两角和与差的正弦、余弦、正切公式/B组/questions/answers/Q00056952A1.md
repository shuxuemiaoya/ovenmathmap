> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)因为*α*为第四象限角,cos *α=*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$,
> > 所以sin *α=-*$\sqrt{1\text{-}cos^{2}\alpha}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{7}}{3}$*.*
> > 所以sin$\left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \frac{1}{2}$sin *α+*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$cos *α=*$\frac{1}{2} \times \left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{7}}{3} \right) + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{6}\text{-}\sqrt{7}}{6}$*.*
> > (2)因为*α*∈$\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}0 \right)$,*β*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,
> > 所以*α-β*∈(*-*π,0)*.*又因为cos(*α-β*)*=*$\frac{4}{5}$,
> > 所以sin(*α-β*)*=-*$\sqrt{1\text{-}cos^{2}\text{(}\alpha\text{-}\beta\text{)}}$*=-*$\frac{3}{5}$*.*
> > 所以cos *β=*cos\[*α-*(*α-β*)\]*=*cos *α*cos(*α-β*)*+*sin *α*sin(*α-β*)*=*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} + \frac{\sqrt{7}}{3} \times \frac{3}{5} = \frac{4\sqrt{2} + 3\sqrt{7}}{15}$*.*
