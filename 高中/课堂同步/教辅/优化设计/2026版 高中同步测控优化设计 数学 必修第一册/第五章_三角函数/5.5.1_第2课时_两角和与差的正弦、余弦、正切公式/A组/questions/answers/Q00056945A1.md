> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可知cos *α=*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$,cos *β=*$\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$*.*
> > *∵α*,*β*为锐角,*∴*sin *α=*$\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$,sin *β=*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$,
> > *∴*tan *α=*7,tan *β=*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > (1)tan(*α+β*)*=*$\frac{\tan\alpha + tan\beta}{1\text{-}\tan\alpha\tan\beta} = \frac{7 + \frac{1}{2}}{1\text{-}7 \times \frac{1}{2}}$*=-*3*.*
> > (2)tan(*α+*2*β*)*=*tan\[(*α+β*)*+β*\]*=*$\frac{\tan\text{(}\alpha + \beta\text{)} + tan\beta}{1\text{-}\tan\text{(}\alpha + \beta\text{)}\tan\beta} = \frac{\text{-}3 + \frac{1}{2}}{1\text{-(-}3\text{)} \times \frac{1}{2}}$*=-*1,
> > *∵α*,*β*为锐角,*∴*0*\<α+*2*β\<*$\frac{3\pi}{2}$,*∴α+*2*β=*$\frac{3\pi}{4}$*.*
