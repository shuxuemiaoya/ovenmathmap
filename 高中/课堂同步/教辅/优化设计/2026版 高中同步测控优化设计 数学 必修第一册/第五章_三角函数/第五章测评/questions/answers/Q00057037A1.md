> > (1)由最低点为*M*$\left( \frac{2\pi}{3}\text{,-}2 \right)$,得*A=*2*.*

> > 得$\frac{T}{2} = \frac{\pi}{2}$($T$为$f$($x$)的最小正周期),即*T=*π*.*
> > 故*ω=*$\frac{2\pi}{T} = \frac{2\pi}{\pi}$*=*2*.*
> > 由点*M*$\left( \frac{2\pi}{3}\text{,-}2 \right)$在图象上,
> > 得2sin$\left( 2 \times \frac{2\pi}{3} + \varphi \right)$*=-*2,
> > 即sin$\left( \frac{4\pi}{3} + \varphi \right)$*=-*1,故$\frac{4\pi}{3}$*+φ=*2*k*π*-*$\frac{\pi}{2}$($k$∈*$Z$*),
> > 得*φ=*2*k*π*-*$\frac{11\pi}{6}$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*又*φ*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,

> > (2)由$x$∈$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{12}\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right\rbrack$,得2*x+*$\frac{\pi}{6} \in \left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{3}\text{,}\frac{7\pi}{6} \right\rbrack$*.*
