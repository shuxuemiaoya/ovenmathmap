> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $f$($x$)*=*sin *x+*$\sqrt{3}$cos *x=*2sin$\left( x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*.*
> > 对于A,当*x=*$\frac{\pi}{6}$时,*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$*=*2sin$\left( \frac{\pi}{6} + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*=*2,故函数$f$($x$)的图象不关于点$\left( \frac{\pi}{6}\text{,}0 \right)$对称*.*故A不正确*.*
> > 对于B,由*α*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{3} \right)$,可得*α+*$\frac{\pi}{3} \in \left( \frac{\pi}{3}\text{,}\frac{2\pi}{3} \right)$,则$f$(*α*)∈($\sqrt{3}$,2\],不存在$f$(*α*)*=*1,故B不正确*.*
> > 对于C,若函数$f$(*x+α*)的图象关于$y$轴对称,则*α+*$\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{2}$*+k*π,$k$∈*$Z$*,故*α=*$\frac{\pi}{6}$*+k*π,$k$∈*$Z$**.*
> > 当*k=*0时,*α=*$\frac{\pi}{6}$,满足*α*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{3} \right)$,故C正确*.*
> > 对于D,由$f$(*x+α*)*=f*(*x+*3*α*),*α*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{3} \right)$,可知2*α*是$f$($x$)的一个周期,又$f$($x$)的最小正周期为2π,
> > 故2*α=*2*k*π,$k$∈*$Z$*,且$k$≠0,
> > 即*α=k*π,$k$∈*$Z$*,且$k$≠0,
> > 此时不存在*α*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{3} \right)$,使*α=k*π,$k$∈*$Z$*,且$k$≠0成立,故D不正确*.*
