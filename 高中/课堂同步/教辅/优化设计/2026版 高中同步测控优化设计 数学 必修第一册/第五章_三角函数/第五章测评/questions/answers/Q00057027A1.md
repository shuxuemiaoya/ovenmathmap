> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 不妨设*ω\>*0,由题中函数图象可知,其最小正周期*T=*2*×*$\left( \frac{5}{4}\text{-}\frac{1}{4} \right)$*=*2,
> > 所以$\frac{2\pi}{\omega}$*=*2,解得*ω=*π*.*
> > 所以$f$($x$)*=*cos(π*x+φ*)*.*
> > 由题中图象可知,当*x=*$\frac{1}{2} \times \left( \frac{1}{4} + \frac{5}{4} \right) = \frac{3}{4}$时,$f$($x$)取得最小值,
> > 即*f*$\left( \frac{3}{4} \right)$*=*cos$\left( \frac{3\pi}{4} + \varphi \right)$*=-*1,
> > 得$\frac{3\pi}{4}$*+φ=*2*k*π*+*π($k$∈*$Z$*),
> > 得*φ=*2*k*π*+*$\frac{\pi}{4}$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > 令*k=*0,得*φ=*$\frac{\pi}{4}$,所以$f$($x$)*=*cos$\left( \pi x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$*.*
> > 令2*k*π≤π*x+*$\frac{\pi}{4}$≤2*k*π*+*π($k$∈*$Z$*),
> > 得2*k-*$\frac{1}{4}$≤$x$≤2*k+*$\frac{3}{4}$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > 所以函数$f$($x$)*=*cos$\left( \pi x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$的单调递减区间为$\left\lbrack 2k\text{-}\frac{1}{4}\text{,}2k + \frac{3}{4} \right\rbrack$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > 故选D*.*
