> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** \[$\sqrt{3}$*+*1,4\]
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为*y=*$\frac{2sin\left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)}{\cos x} = \frac{2\left( \sin x\cos\frac{\pi}{6} + cosx\sin\frac{\pi}{6} \right)}{\cos x} = \frac{\sqrt{3}\sin x + cosx}{\cos x} = \sqrt{3}$tan *x+*1,
> > 而函数*y=*tan $x$在区间$\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$内单调递增,又$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{4}\text{,}\frac{\pi}{3} \right\rbrack \subseteq \left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,
> > 所以*y=*$\sqrt{3}$tan *x+*1在区间$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{4}\text{,}\frac{\pi}{3} \right\rbrack$上也单调递增,
> > 故$\sqrt{3}$*+*1*=*$\sqrt{3}$tan$\frac{\pi}{4}$*+*1≤$\sqrt{3}$tan *x+*1≤$\sqrt{3}$tan$\frac{\pi}{3}$*+*1*=*4,即所求函数的值域为\[$\sqrt{3}$*+*1,4\]*.*
