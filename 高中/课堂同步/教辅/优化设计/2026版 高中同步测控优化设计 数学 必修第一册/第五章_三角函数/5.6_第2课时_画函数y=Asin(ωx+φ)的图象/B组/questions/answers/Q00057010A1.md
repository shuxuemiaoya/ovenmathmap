> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left( \frac{\pi}{12}\text{,}0 \right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当*y=*0时,sin$\left( 4x + \frac{2\pi}{3} \right)$*=*0,
> > *∴*4*x+*$\frac{2\pi}{3}$*=k*π($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > *∴x=*$\frac{k\pi}{4} - \frac{\pi}{6}$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*取*k=*0,
> > 则*x=-*$\frac{\pi}{6}$;取*k=*1,则*x=*$\frac{\pi}{12}$*.*
> > *∴*离原点最近的交点坐标是$\left( \frac{\pi}{12}\text{,}0 \right)$*.*
