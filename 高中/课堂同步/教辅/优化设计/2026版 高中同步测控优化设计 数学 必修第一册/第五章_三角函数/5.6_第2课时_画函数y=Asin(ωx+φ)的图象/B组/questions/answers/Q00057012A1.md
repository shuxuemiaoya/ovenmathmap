> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为$x$∈$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{8}\text{,}\frac{9\pi}{8} \right\rbrack$,所以0≤2*x-*$\frac{\pi}{4}$≤2π*.*
> > 列表如下:
> > | 2*x-*$\frac{\pi}{4}$ | 0 | $\frac{\pi}{2}$ | π | $\frac{3\pi}{2}$ | 2π |
> > |-----------|-----|------|------|------|------|
> > | $x$ | $\frac{\pi}{8}$ | $\frac{3\pi}{8}$ | $\frac{5\pi}{8}$ | $\frac{7\pi}{8}$ | $\frac{9\pi}{8}$ |
> > | 2sin$\left( 2x\text{-}\frac{\pi}{4} \right)$ | 0 | 2 | 0 | *-*2 | 0 |
> > 描点、连线作图如下:
> > ![id:2147494579;FounderCES](../../images/image10.jpeg)
> > 由*y=*2sin$\left( 2x\text{-}\frac{\pi}{4} \right)$*\>*1,得sin$\left( 2x\text{-}\frac{\pi}{4} \right) > \frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 又2*x-*$\frac{\pi}{4}$∈\[0,2π\],所以$\frac{\pi}{6}$*\<*2*x-*$\frac{\pi}{4} < \frac{5\pi}{6}$,
> > 得$\frac{5\pi}{24}$*\<x\<*$\frac{13\pi}{24}$*.*
> > 所以当$x$∈$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{8}\text{,}\frac{9\pi}{8} \right\rbrack$时,图象在直线*y=*1上方时所对应的$x$的取值范围为$\left( \frac{5\pi}{24}\text{,}\frac{13\pi}{24} \right)$*.*
