> > 在同一平面直角坐标系中画出*y=*sin $x$,$x$∈$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{3}\text{,}\pi \right\rbrack$与*y=*$\frac{1\text{-}a}{2}$的图象,由图象可知,当$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \leq \frac{1\text{-}a}{2}$*\<*1,即*-*1*\<a*≤1*-*$\sqrt{3}$时,*y=*sin $x$,$x$∈$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{3}\text{,}\pi \right\rbrack$的图象与*y=*$\frac{1\text{-}a}{2}$的图象有两个交点,即关于$x$的方程sin *x=*$\frac{1\text{-}a}{2}$在区间$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{3}\text{,}\pi \right\rbrack$上有两个实根*.*所以实数$a$的取值范围是$\left\lbrack \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{,}1 \right)$*.*
