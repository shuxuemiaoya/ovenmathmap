> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)因为*a\>*0,令2*k*π*-*$\frac{\pi}{2}$≤2*x+*$\frac{\pi}{3}$≤2*k*π*+*$\frac{\pi}{2}$($k$∈*$Z$*),得$k$π*-*$\frac{5\pi}{12}$≤$x$≤$k$π*+*$\frac{\pi}{12}$($k$∈*$Z$*),
> > 所以$f$($x$)的单调递增区间是![](../../images/image2.jpeg)$k$π*-*$\frac{5\pi}{12}$,$k$π*+*$\frac{\pi}{12}$![](../../images/image3.jpeg) ($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > (2)当$x$∈$\left\lbrack 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{4} \right\rbrack$时,$\frac{\pi}{3}$≤2*x+*$\frac{\pi}{3} \leq \frac{5\pi}{6}$,则$\frac{1}{2}$≤sin$\left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$≤1*.*又因为$f$($x$)的值域为\[1,3\],
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a > 0\text{,} \\
> > a + b = 3\text{,} \\
> > \frac{1}{2}a + b = 1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 4\text{,} \\
> > b = \text{-}1\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
