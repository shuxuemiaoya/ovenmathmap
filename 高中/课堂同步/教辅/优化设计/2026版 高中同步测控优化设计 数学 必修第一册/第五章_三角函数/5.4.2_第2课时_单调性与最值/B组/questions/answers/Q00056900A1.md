> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由2*k*π*-*$\frac{\pi}{2}$≤*ωx*≤2*k*π*+*$\frac{\pi}{2}$($k$∈*$Z$*),
> > 得$\frac{2k\pi}{\omega} - \frac{\pi}{2\omega}$≤$x$≤$\frac{2k\pi}{\omega} + \frac{\pi}{2\omega}$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > 所以函数$f$($x$)*=*sin *ωx*(*ω\>*0)的单调递增区间为$\left\lbrack \frac{2k\pi}{\omega}\text{-}\frac{\pi}{2\omega}\text{,}\frac{2k\pi}{\omega} + \frac{\pi}{2\omega} \right\rbrack$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > 又因为函数$f$($x$)在区间$\left\lbrack \text{-}\frac{2\pi}{3}\text{,}\frac{5\pi}{6} \right\rbrack$上单调递增,且存在唯一$x_{0}$∈\[0,π\],使得$f$($x_{0}$)*=*1,
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \left\lbrack \text{-}\frac{2\pi}{3}\text{,}\frac{5\pi}{6} \right\rbrack \subseteq \left\lbrack \text{-}\frac{\pi}{2\omega}\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2\omega} \right\rbrack\text{,} \\
> > \frac{\pi}{2\omega}\text{∈}\text{[}0\text{,}\pi\text{],}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\frac{1}{2}$≤*ω*≤$\frac{3}{5}$,故选A*.*
