> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)因为sin *θ*,cos *θ*为方程2$x^{2}$*-bx+*$\frac{1}{4}$*=*0的两根,
> > 所以*Δ=b*^2^*-*2≥0,且$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \sin\theta + cos\theta = \frac{b}{2}\text{,}\text{①} \\
> > \sin\theta\cos\theta = \frac{1}{8}\text{.}\text{②}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 将①式两边平方,②式代入整理,得$\frac{b^{2}}{4}$*=*1*+*$\frac{1}{4}$,解得*b=±*$\sqrt{5}$,此时*Δ=*5*-*2*\>*0*.*
> > 又sin *θ+*cos *θ=*$\frac{b}{2}$*\>*0,所以*b=*$\sqrt{5}$*.*
> > (2)由(1)得sin *θ+*cos *θ=*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$,*θ*∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{4}\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,
> > 故sin *θ\>*cos *θ.*又sin *θ*cos *θ=*$\frac{1}{8}$,
> > 所以sin *θ-*cos *θ=*$\sqrt{1\text{-}2sin\theta\cos\theta} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$,
> > 所以$\frac{\sin\theta + cos\theta}{\cos\theta\text{-}\sin\theta}$*=-*$\frac{\sin\theta + cos\theta}{\sin\theta\text{-}\cos\theta}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2} \times \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{15}}{3}$*.*
