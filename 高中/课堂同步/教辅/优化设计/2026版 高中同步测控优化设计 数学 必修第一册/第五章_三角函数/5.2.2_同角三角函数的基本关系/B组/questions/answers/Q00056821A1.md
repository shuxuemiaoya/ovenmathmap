> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 将sin *θ+*cos *θ=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}\text{-}1}{2}$的两边平方,
> > 得1*+*2sin *θ*cos *θ=*1*-*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$,即sin *θ*cos *θ=-*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$*.*
> > 故sin *θ*cos *θ=*$\frac{\sin\theta\cos\theta}{sin^{2}\theta + cos^{2}\theta} = \frac{\tan\theta}{1 + tan^{2}\theta}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$,
> > 得tan *θ=-*$\sqrt{3}$或tan *θ=-*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$*.*
> > 因为*θ*∈(0,π),0*\<*sin *θ+*cos *θ=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}\text{-}1}{2}$*\<*1,
> > 所以*θ*∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\pi \right)$,且*\|*sin *θ\|\>\|*cos *θ\|.*
> > 由*\|*tan *θ\|\>*1,得tan *θ=-*$\sqrt{3}$*.*
