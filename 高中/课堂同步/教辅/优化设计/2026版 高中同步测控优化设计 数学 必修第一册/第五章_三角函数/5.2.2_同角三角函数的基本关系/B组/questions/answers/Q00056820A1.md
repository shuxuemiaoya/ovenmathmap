> > [!success] **【答案】** 10
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据角*α*的终边经过点$P$(3,4),利用三角函数的定义,可得tan *α=*$\frac{4}{3}$*.*
> > 故$\frac{\sin\alpha + 2cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha\text{-}\cos\alpha} = \frac{\tan\alpha + 2}{\tan\alpha\text{-}1} = \frac{\frac{4}{3} + 2}{\frac{4}{3}\text{-}1} = \frac{\frac{10}{3}}{\frac{1}{3}}$*=*10*.*
