> > [!success] **【答案】** *-*$\frac{4}{25}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵*cos *α=-*$\frac{3}{5}$*\<*0,tan *α\>*0,
> > *∴α*是第三象限角,且sin *α=-*$\frac{4}{5}$*.*
> > *∴*$\frac{\sin\alpha cos^{2}\alpha}{1\text{-}\sin\alpha} = \frac{\sin\alpha\text{(}1\text{-}sin^{2}\alpha\text{)}}{1\text{-}\sin\alpha}$*=*sin *α*(1*+*sin *α*)*=*$\left( \text{-}\frac{4}{5} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{4}{5} \right)$*=-*$\frac{4}{25}$*.*
