> > [!success] **【答案】** AB
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵*sin *α=*$\frac{4}{5}$,且*α*为锐角,
> > *∴*cos *α=*$\sqrt{1\text{-}sin^{2}\alpha} = \sqrt{1\text{-}\left( \frac{4}{5} \right)^{2}} = \frac{3}{5}$,
> > *∴*tan *α=*$\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{\frac{4}{5}}{\frac{3}{5}} = \frac{4}{3}$,
> > sin *α+*cos *α=*$\frac{4}{5} + \frac{3}{5} = \frac{7}{5}$,
> > sin *α-*cos *α=*$\frac{4}{5} - \frac{3}{5} = \frac{1}{5}$*.*
> > 故选AB*.*
