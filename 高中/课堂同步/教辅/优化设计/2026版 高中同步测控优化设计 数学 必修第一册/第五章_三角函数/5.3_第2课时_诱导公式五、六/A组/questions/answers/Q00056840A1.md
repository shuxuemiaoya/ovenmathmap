> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > sin$\left( \pi\text{-}\alpha \right)$*=*sin *α*,故A正确*.*
> > cos$\left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha \right)$ *=-*sin *α*,故B错误*.*
> > cos$\left( \alpha\text{-}\frac{3\pi}{2} \right)$*=*cos$\left( \frac{3\pi}{2}\text{-}\alpha \right)$*=-*sin *α*,故C正确*.*
> > sin$\left( \frac{5\pi}{2}\text{-}\alpha \right)$*=*sin$\left( 2\pi + \frac{\pi}{2}\text{-}\alpha \right)$*=*cos *α*,故D正确*.*
