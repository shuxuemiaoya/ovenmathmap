> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *y=*2sin^2^*x=*1*-*cos 2*x.*
> > 将函数*y=*(sin *x+*cos $x$)^2^*=*1*+*sin 2*x*的图象向右平移$\frac{\pi}{4}$个单位长度,得到函数*y=*1*+*sin 2$\left( x\text{-}\frac{\pi}{4} \right)$*=*1*+*sin$\left( 2x\text{-}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$*=*1*-*cos 2*x*的图象,故A正确*.*

> > 将函数*y=*2sin^2^$\left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$*=*1*-*cos$\left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$的图象向右平移$\frac{\pi}{6}$个单位长度,得到函数*y=*1*-*cos\[2(*x-*$\frac{\pi}{6}$)*+*$\frac{\pi}{3}$\]*=*1*-*cos 2*x*的图象,故C正确,D不正确*.*
