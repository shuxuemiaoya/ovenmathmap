> > \text{-}\frac{\pi}{4}\omega \geq \text{-}\frac{\pi}{2}\text{,} \\
> > \frac{2\pi}{3}\omega \leq \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}

> > 得0*\<ω*≤$\frac{3}{4}$*.*
> > 所以*ω*的取值范围为$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{3}{4} \right\rbrack$*.*

> > $g$($x$)*=*2sin$\left\lbrack 2\left( x + \frac{\pi}{6} \right) \right\rbrack$*+*1*=*2sin$\left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*+*1*.*
> > 由$g$($x$)*=*0得,sin$\left( 2x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$,
> > 得*x=k*π*-*$\frac{\pi}{4}$或*x=k*π*-*$\frac{7\pi}{12}$,$k$∈*$Z$*,

> > 故若*y=g*($x$)在区间\[$a$,*b*\]上至少含有30个零点,则*b-a*的最小值为14*×*$\frac{2\pi}{3}$*+*15*×*$\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{43\pi}{3}$*.*
