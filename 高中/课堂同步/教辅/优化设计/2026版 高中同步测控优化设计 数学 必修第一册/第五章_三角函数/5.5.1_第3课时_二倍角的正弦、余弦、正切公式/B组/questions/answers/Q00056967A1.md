> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \sin\alpha = \frac{2}{3}\sin\beta\text{,}\text{　　　}\text{①} \\
> > \cos\alpha = 1\text{-}\frac{2}{3}\cos\beta\text{,}\text{②}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > ①^2^*+*②^2^得cos *β=*$\frac{1}{3}$,代入②式,得cos *α=*$\frac{7}{9}$*.*
> > 由*α*,*β*均为锐角,知sin *β=*$\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$,sin *α=*$\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{9}$*.*
> > 所以tan *β=*2$\sqrt{2}$,tan *α=*$\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{7}$,
> > tan 2*β=*$\frac{2tan\beta}{1\text{-}tan^{2}\beta}$*=-*$\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{7}$,
> > tan(*α+*2*β*)*=*$\frac{\tan\alpha + tan2\beta}{1\text{-}\tan\alpha tan2\beta}$*=*0*.*
> > 又*α+*2*β*∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{3\pi}{2} \right)$,故*α+*2*β=*π*.*
