> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当$x$∈$\left\lbrack \frac{5\pi}{2}\text{,}3\pi \right\rbrack$时,3π*-x*∈$\left\lbrack 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right\rbrack$*.*
> > *∵*当$x$∈$\left\lbrack 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right\rbrack$时,$f$($x$)*=*1*-*sin $x$,
> > *∴f*(3π*-x*)*=*1*-*sin(3π*-x*)*=*1*-*sin *x.*
> > 又$f$($x$)是以π为周期的偶函数,
> > *∴f*(3π*-x*)*=f*(*-x*)*=f*($x$),
> > *∴*当$x$∈$\left\lbrack \frac{5\pi}{2}\text{,}3\pi \right\rbrack$时,$f$($x$)*=*1*-*sin *x.*
