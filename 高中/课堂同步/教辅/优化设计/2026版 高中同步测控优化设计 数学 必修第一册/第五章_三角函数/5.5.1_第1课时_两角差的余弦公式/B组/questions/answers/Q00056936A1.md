> > *∴*cos 2*β=*cos\[(*α+β*)*-*(*α-β*)\]
> > *=*cos(*α+β*)cos(*α-β*)*+*sin(*α+β*)sin(*α-β*)
> > *=*$\frac{12}{13} \times \left( \text{-}\frac{12}{13} \right) + \left( \text{-}\frac{5}{13} \right) \times \frac{5}{13}$*=-*1*.*
> > 又*α+β*∈$\left( \frac{3\pi}{2}\text{,}2\pi \right)$,*α-β*∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\pi \right)$,
> > *∴*2*β*∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{2}\text{,}\frac{3\pi}{2} \right)$*.*
