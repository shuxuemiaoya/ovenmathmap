> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为sin(3π*-θ*)*=*sin *θ*,sin$\left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \theta \right)$*=*cos *θ*,
> > 所以sin *θ=*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$cos *θ.*
> > 因为sin^2^*θ+*cos^2^*θ=*1,
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \sin\theta = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}\text{,} \\
> > \cos\theta = \frac{2}{3}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 或\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \sin\theta = \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}\text{,} \\
> > \cos\theta = \text{-}\frac{2}{3}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以cos$\left( \theta\text{-}\frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \frac{1}{2}$cos *θ+*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$sin *θ=±*$\left( \frac{1}{3} + \frac{\sqrt{15}}{6} \right)$*.*
