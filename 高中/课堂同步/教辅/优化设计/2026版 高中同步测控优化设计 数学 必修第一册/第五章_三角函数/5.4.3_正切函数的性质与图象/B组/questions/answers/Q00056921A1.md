> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由*ω=*$\frac{1}{2}$,知函数$f$($x$)的最小正周期*T=*$\frac{\pi}{\omega} = \frac{\pi}{\frac{1}{2}}$*=*2π*.*令$\frac{x}{2} - \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{k\pi}{2}$($k$∈*$Z$*),得*x=k*π*+*$\frac{2\pi}{3}$($k$∈*$Z$*),
> > 故$f$($x$)的图象的对称中心是$\left( k\pi + \frac{2\pi}{3}\text{,}0 \right)$($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > (2)令$\frac{x}{2} - \frac{\pi}{3}$*=*0,则*x=*$\frac{2\pi}{3}$;
> > 令$\frac{x}{2} - \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{4}$,则*x=*$\frac{7\pi}{6}$;
> > 令$\frac{x}{2} - \frac{\pi}{3}$*=-*$\frac{\pi}{4}$,则*x=*$\frac{\pi}{6}$;
> > 令$\frac{x}{2} - \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{2}$,则*x=*$\frac{5\pi}{3}$;
> > 令$\frac{x}{2} - \frac{\pi}{3}$*=-*$\frac{\pi}{2}$,则*x=-*$\frac{\pi}{3}$*.*
> > 故函数*y=*tan$\left( \frac{x}{2}\text{-}\frac{\pi}{3} \right)$的图象与$x$轴的一个交点坐标是$\left( \frac{2\pi}{3}\text{,}0 \right)$,在这个交点左、右两侧相邻的两条渐近线方程分别是*x=-*$\frac{\pi}{3}$,*x=*$\frac{5\pi}{3}$,从而得到函数*y=f*($x$)在一个周期$\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{3}\text{,}\frac{5\pi}{3} \right)$内的简图如图所示*.*
