> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知40*-*24*=*(88*-*24)*×*$\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{20}{h}}$,
> > 即$\frac{1}{4} = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{20}{h}}$,
> > 得*h=*10*.*
> > 故原式可化为*T-*24*=*(88*-*24)*×*$\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{10}}$*.*
> > 当*T=*32时,代入上式,得32*-*24*=*(88*-*24)*×*$\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{10}}$,
> > 即$\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{\frac{t}{10}} = \frac{8}{64} = \frac{1}{8} = \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{3}$,解得*t=*30*.*
> > 因此,需要30 min,可降温到32 ℃*.*
