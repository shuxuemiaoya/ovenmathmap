> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)指数函数$f$($x$)*=a^x^*(*a\>*0,且$a$≠1)的反函数为$g$($x$)*=$log_{a}$x*(*a\>*0,且$a$≠1,*x\>*0)*.*
> > (2)由$g$($x$)≤log*~a~*(2*-*3*x*),得log*~a~x*≤log*~a~*(2*-*3*x*)*.*
> > 若*a\>*1,则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x > 0\text{,} \\
> > 2\text{-}3x > 0\text{,} \\
> > x \leq 2\text{-}3x\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得0*\<x*≤$\frac{1}{2}$;
> > 若0*\<a\<*1,则$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x > 0\text{,} \\
> > 2\text{-}3x > 0\text{,} \\
> > x \geq 2\text{-}3x\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\frac{1}{2}$≤*x\<*$\frac{2}{3}$*.*
> > 综上所述,当*a\>*1时,不等式的解集为$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right\rbrack$;
> > 当0*\<a\<*1时,不等式的解集为$\left\lbrack \frac{1}{2}\text{,}\frac{2}{3} \right)$*.*
