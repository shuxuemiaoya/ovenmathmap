> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left( \frac{1}{3}\text{,}\frac{2}{3} \right)$∪(1,*+∞*)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知log*~a~*(3*a-*1)*\>*0*=$log_{a}$*1*.*
> > 当*a\>*1时,*y=$log_{a}$x*在区间(0,*+∞*)内是增函数,
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 3a\text{-}1 > 1\text{,} \\
> > 3a\text{-}1 > 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*a\>*$\frac{2}{3}$*.*所以*a\>*1*.*
> > 当0*\<a\<*1时,*y=$log_{a}$x*在区间(0,*+∞*)内是减函数,所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 3a\text{-}1 < 1\text{,} \\
> > 3a\text{-}1 > 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\frac{1}{3}$*\<a\<*$\frac{2}{3}$*.*所以$\frac{1}{3}$*\<a\<*$\frac{2}{3}$*.*
> > 综上所述,实数$a$的取值范围是$\left( \frac{1}{3}\text{,}\frac{2}{3} \right)$∪(1,*+∞*)*.*
