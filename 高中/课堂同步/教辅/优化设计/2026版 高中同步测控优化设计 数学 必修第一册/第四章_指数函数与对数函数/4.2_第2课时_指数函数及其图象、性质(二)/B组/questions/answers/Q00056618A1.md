> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left\lbrack \text{-}\frac{3}{4}\text{,} + \text{∞} \right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设*t=$2^{x}$*,*∵x*∈(*-∞*,1\],
> > *∴*0*\<t*≤2*.*
> > *∴*原函数有意义等价于1*+t+at*^2^≥0在区间(0,2\]上恒成立,
> > 即$a$≥*-*$\frac{t + 1}{t^{2}}$在区间(0,2\]上恒成立,
> > 设$f$($t$)*=-*$\frac{1 + t}{t^{2}}$,
> > 则$f$($t$)*=-*$\frac{1 + t}{t^{2}}$*=-*$\left( \frac{1}{t} + \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} + \frac{1}{4}$*.*
> > *∵*0*\<t*≤2,*∴*$\frac{1}{t} \in \left\lbrack \frac{1}{2}\text{,} + \text{∞} \right)$,
> > *∴f*($t$)≤$f$(2)*=-*$\frac{3}{4}$,
> > *∴a*≥*-*$\frac{3}{4}$*.*
