> > 且$g$(*-x*)*=*$\frac{2^{\text{-}x}\text{-}1}{2^{\text{-}x} + 1} = \frac{1\text{-}2^{x}}{1 + 2^{x}}$*=-*$\frac{2^{x}\text{-}1}{2^{x} + 1}$*=-g*($x$),
> > 所以函数$g$($x$)是奇函数*.*
