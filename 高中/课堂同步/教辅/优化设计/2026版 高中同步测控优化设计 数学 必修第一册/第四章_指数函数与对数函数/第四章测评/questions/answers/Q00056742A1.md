> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)$f$($x$)*=*$\left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{\text{|}x\text{|-}a} = \left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{x\text{-}a}\text{,}x \geq 0\text{,} \\
> > \left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{\text{-}x\text{-}a}\text{,}x < 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以$f$($x$)在区间(0,*+∞*)内单调递减,在区间(*-∞*,0)内单调递增*.*
> > (2)因为*\|x\|-a*的最小值为*-a*,
> > 所以$f$($x$)*=*$\left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{\text{|}x\text{|-}a}$的最大值为$\left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{\text{-}a}$,
> > 即$\left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{\text{-}a} = \frac{9}{4}$,故*a=*2*.*
