> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{x}　\left\lbrack \frac{1}{9}\text{,}27 \right\rbrack$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设$f$($x$)*=a^x^*(*a\>*0,且$a$≠1),将点(*-*1,3)的坐标代入,得*a^-^*^1^*=*3,解得*a=*$\frac{1}{3}$,所以$f$($x$)*=*$\left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{x}$*.*因为$f$($x$)*=*$\left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{x}$在区间\[*-*3,2\]上单调递减,所以$f$($x$)的取值范围为$\left\lbrack \frac{1}{9}\text{,}27 \right\rbrack$*.*
