> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}或\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ①当*a\>*1时,$f$($x$)*=a^x^*在区间\[0,2\]上单调递增,此时$f$($x$)~max~*=f*(2)*=a*^2^,$f$($x$)~min~*=f*(0)*=*1,所以$a^{2}$*-*1*=*$\frac{1}{4}$,所以*a=*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$*.*
> > ②当0*\<a\<*1时,$f$($x$)*=a^x^*在区间\[0,2\]上单调递减,此时$f$($x$)~max~*=f*(0)*=*1,$f$($x$)~min~*=f*(2)*=a*^2^,所以1*-a*^2^*=*$\frac{1}{4}$,所以*a=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$*.*综上①②可知,*a=*$\frac{\sqrt{5}}{2}$或*a=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$*.*
