> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)当*a=-*1时,$f$($x$)*=*lo$g_{\frac{1}{2}}$($x^{2}$*+x+*1)*.*
> > 因为$x^{2}$*+x+*1*=*$\left( x + \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} + \frac{3}{4} \geq \frac{3}{4}$,
> > 所以lo$g_{\frac{1}{2}}$($x^{2}$*+x+*1)≤lo$g_{\frac{1}{2}}\frac{3}{4}$*=*2*-*log~2~3,
> > 因此$f$($x$)的值域为(*-∞*,2*-*log~2~3\]*.*
> > 又*t=x*^2^*+x+*1在区间$\left( \text{-}\text{∞}\text{,-}\frac{1}{2} \right\rbrack$上单调递减,在区间$\left\lbrack \text{-}\frac{1}{2}\text{,} + \text{∞} \right)$内单调递增,*y=*lo$g_{\frac{1}{2}}$*t*在定义域内单调递减,故$f$($x$)的单调递增区间为$\left( \text{-}\text{∞}\text{,-}\frac{1}{2} \right\rbrack$,单调递减区间为$\left\lbrack \text{-}\frac{1}{2}\text{,} + \text{∞} \right)$*.*
> > (2)令*u=x*^2^*-ax-a=*$\left( x\text{-}\frac{a}{2} \right)^{2} - \frac{a^{2}}{4}$*-a*,
> > 因为$f$($x$)在区间$\left( \text{-}\text{∞}\text{,-}\frac{1}{2} \right)$内单调递增,
> > 又*y=*lo$g_{\frac{1}{2}}$*u*在定义域上为减函数,
> > 所以*u=x*^2^*-ax-a*在区间$\left( \text{-}\text{∞}\text{,-}\frac{1}{2} \right)$内单调递减,
> > 且*u\>*0在区间$\left( \text{-}\text{∞}\text{,-}\frac{1}{2} \right)$内恒成立*.*
> > 因此$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \frac{a}{2} \geq \text{-}\frac{1}{2}\text{,} \\
> > \frac{1}{4} + \frac{a}{2}\text{-}a \geq 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*-*1≤$a$≤$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 故实数$a$的取值范围是$\left\lbrack \text{-}1\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right\rbrack$*.*
