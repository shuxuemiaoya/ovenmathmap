> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{a^{\frac{1}{2}}\text{-}b^{\frac{1}{2}}}{a^{\frac{1}{2}} + b^{\frac{1}{2}}} = \frac{\text{(}a^{\frac{1}{2}}\text{-}b^{\frac{1}{2}}\text{)}^{2}}{\text{(}a^{\frac{1}{2}} + b^{\frac{1}{2}}\text{)(}a^{\frac{1}{2}}\text{-}b^{\frac{1}{2}}\text{)}} = \frac{\text{(}a + b\text{)-}2\text{(}ab\text{)}^{\frac{1}{2}}}{a\text{-}b}$*.*①
> > *∵a*,$b$是$x^{2}$*-*12*x+*9*=*0的两根,
> > *∴a+b=*12,*ab=*9*.*②
> > *∴*(*a-b*)^2^*=*(*a+b*)^2^*-*4*ab=*12^2^*-*4*×*9*=*108*.*
> > 又*a\<b*,*∴a-b=-*6$\sqrt{3}$*.*③
> > 将②③代入①,得$\frac{a^{\frac{1}{2}}\text{-}b^{\frac{1}{2}}}{a^{\frac{1}{2}} + b^{\frac{1}{2}}} = \frac{12\text{-}2 \times 9^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\text{-}6\sqrt{3}}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$*.*
