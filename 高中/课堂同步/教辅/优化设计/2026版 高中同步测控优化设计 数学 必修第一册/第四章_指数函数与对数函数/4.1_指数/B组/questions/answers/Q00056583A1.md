> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为$f$($m$)*=*$\frac{3^{m}\text{-}1}{3^{m} + 1} = \frac{3}{4}$,所以$f$(*-m*)*=*$\frac{3^{\text{-}m}\text{-}1}{3^{\text{-}m} + 1} = \frac{1\text{-}3^{m}}{1 + 3^{m}}$*=-*$\frac{3^{m}\text{-}1}{3^{m} + 1}$*=-*$\frac{3}{4}$*.*
