> > B中,三人中恰有2人及格的概率$P_{2}$*=*$\frac{4}{5} \times \frac{3}{5} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{7}{10} \right) + \frac{4}{5} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{3}{5} \right) \times \frac{7}{10} + \left( 1\text{-}\frac{4}{5} \right) \times \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{7}{10} = \frac{113}{250}$,
> > 故B正确;
