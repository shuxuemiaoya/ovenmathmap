> > 则$a_{1}$*=\|**$a$**\|*cos 45°*=*2$\times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \sqrt{2}$,
> > $a_{2}$*=\|**$a$**\|*sin 45°*=*2$\times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \sqrt{2}$,
> > $b_{1}$*=\|**$b$**\|*cos 120°*=*3$\times \left( \text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right)$*=-*$\frac{3}{2}$,
> > $b_{2}$*=\|**$b$**\|*sin 120°*=*3$\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$,
