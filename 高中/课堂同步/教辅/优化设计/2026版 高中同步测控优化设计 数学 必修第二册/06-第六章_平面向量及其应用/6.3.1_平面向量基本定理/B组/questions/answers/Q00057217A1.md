> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\overrightarrow{OP} = \overrightarrow{OM} + \overrightarrow{MP} = \overrightarrow{ON} + \overrightarrow{NP}.$
> > 设$\overrightarrow{MP}$*=m*$\overrightarrow{MB},\overrightarrow{NP}$*=n*$\overrightarrow{NA}$($m$,$n$为实数),
> > 则$\overrightarrow{OP} = \overrightarrow{OM}$*+m*$\overrightarrow{MB} = \frac{1}{3}$*$a$**+m*$\left( b\text{-}\frac{1}{3}a \right) = \frac{1}{3}$(1*-m*)*$a$**+m**$b$*,$\overrightarrow{OP} = \overrightarrow{ON}$*+n*$\overrightarrow{NA} = \frac{1}{2}$*$b$**+n*(*$a$**-*$\frac{1}{2}$*$b$*)*=*$\frac{1}{2}$(1*-n*)*$b$**+n**$a$**.*
> > 由*$a$*,*$b$*不共线,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \frac{1}{3}\text{(}1\text{-}m\text{)} = n\text{,} \\
> > \frac{1}{2}\text{(}1\text{-}n\text{)} = m\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > n = \frac{1}{5}\text{,} \\
> > m = \frac{2}{5}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故$\overrightarrow{OP} = \frac{1}{5}$*$a$**+*$\frac{2}{5}$*$b$**.*
