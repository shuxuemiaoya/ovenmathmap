> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{3}{4}$*$a$**+*$\frac{1}{4}$*$b$*
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 以$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=**$a$*,$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=**$b$*作为以点$A$为公共起点的一组基底,则$\overrightarrow{AN} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{DN} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{DB} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{3}{4}(\overrightarrow{AB} - \overrightarrow{AD}$)*=*$\frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{3}{4}$*$a$**+*$\frac{1}{4}$*$b$**.*
