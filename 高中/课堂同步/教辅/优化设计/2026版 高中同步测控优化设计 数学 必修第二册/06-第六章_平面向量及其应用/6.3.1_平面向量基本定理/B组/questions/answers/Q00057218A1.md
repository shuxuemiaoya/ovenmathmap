> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)根据题意得$\overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AD}$*=**$b$*,$\overrightarrow{CE} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{CD} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BA}$*=-*$\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AB}$*=-*$\frac{2}{3}$*$a$*,即$\overrightarrow{BE} = \overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{CE}$*=**$b$**-*$\frac{2}{3}$*$a$**.*
> > ![id:2147498014;FounderCES](../../images/image12.jpeg)
> > (2)假设在线段*BC*上存在一点$F$满足*AF*⊥*BE*,
> > 设$\overrightarrow{BF}$*=t*$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=t**$b$*(0≤$t$≤1),
> > 则$\overrightarrow{AF} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BF}$*=**$a$**+t**$b$*,
> > 因为在边长为1的菱形*ABCD*中,∠*DAB=*60°,*\|**$a$**\|=\|**$b$**\|=*1,
> > 所以*$a$*·*$b$**=\|**$a$**\|\|**$b$**\|*cos 60°*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 因为*AF*⊥*BE*,
> > 所以$\overrightarrow{AF} \cdot \overrightarrow{BE}$*=*(*$a$**+t**$b$*)·$\left( b\text{-}\frac{2}{3}a \right)$*=*(1*-*$\frac{2}{3}$*t*)*$a$*·*$b$**-*$\frac{2}{3}$*$a$*^2^*+t**$b$*^2^*=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{2}{3}t \right) \times \frac{1}{2} - \frac{2}{3}$*+t=*0,
> > 得*t=*$\frac{1}{4}$,
> > 从而$\overrightarrow{AF}$*=**$a$**+*$\frac{1}{4}$*$b$*,
> > 故*\|*$\overrightarrow{AF}$*\|=*$\sqrt{\overset{\text{　}}{{\overrightarrow{AF}}^{2}}} = \sqrt{a^{2} + \frac{1}{2}a\text{·}b + \frac{1}{16}b^{2}}$*=*$\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{16}} = \frac{\sqrt{21}}{4}$*.*
> > *∴*在线段*BC*上存在一点$F$满足*AF*⊥*BE*,此时4*\|*$\overrightarrow{BF}$*\|=\|*$\overrightarrow{BC}$*\|*,*\|*$\overrightarrow{AF}$*\|=*$\frac{\sqrt{21}}{4}$*.*
