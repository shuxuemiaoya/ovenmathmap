9. 在四边形*ABCD*中,$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=**$a$**+*2*$b$*,$\overrightarrow{BC}$*=-*4*$a$**-**$b$*,$\overrightarrow{CD}$*=-*5*$a$**-*3*$b$*,证明这个四边形为梯形*.*
证明:$\because\overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BC} + \overrightarrow{CD}$*=*(*$a$**+*2*$b$*)*+*(*-*4*$a$**-**$b$*)*+*(*-*5*$a$**-*3*$b$*)*=-*8*$a$**-*2*$b$**=*2(*-*4*$a$**-**$b$*),
$\therefore\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*2$\overrightarrow{BC}$,$\therefore\overrightarrow{AD}与\overrightarrow{BC}$共线,且*\|*$\overrightarrow{AD}$*\|=*2*\|*$\overrightarrow{BC}$*\|.*
$\because\overrightarrow{AD},\overrightarrow{BC}$所在直线不重合,
*∴AD*∥*BC*,且*AD=*2*BC.*
*∴*四边形*ABCD*是以*AD*,*BC*为两条底边的梯形*.*
