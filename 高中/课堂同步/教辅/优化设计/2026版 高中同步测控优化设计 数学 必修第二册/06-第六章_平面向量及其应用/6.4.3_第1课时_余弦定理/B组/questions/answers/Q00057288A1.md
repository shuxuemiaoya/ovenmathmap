> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)*∵*(*a+b+c*)(*b+c-a*)*=*3*bc*,
> > *∴a*^2^*=b*^2^*+c*^2^*-bc.*
> > *∵a*^2^*=b*^2^*+c*^2^*-*2*bc*cos $A$,
> > *∴*2cos *A=*1,*∴*cos *A=*$\frac{1}{2}.$
> > *∵A*∈(0,π),*∴A=*$\frac{\pi}{3}.$
> > (2)在△*ABC*中,$a^{2}$*=b*^2^*+c*^2^*-*2*bc*cos $A$,且*a=*$\sqrt{3}$,
> > 则($\sqrt{3}$)^2^*=b*^2^*+c*^2^*-*2*bc*$\cdot \frac{1}{2}$*=b*^2^*+c*^2^*-bc.①*
> > *∵b+c=*2$\sqrt{3}$,与*①*联立,解得*bc=*3,
> > $\therefore\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > b + c = 2\sqrt{3}\text{,} \\
> > bc = 3\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > *∴b=c=*$\sqrt{3}$,
> > 于是*a=b=c=*$\sqrt{3}$,即△*ABC*为等边三角形*.*
