> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** 等边三角形
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由余弦定理的推论及cos *A=*$\frac{1}{2}$,
> > 得$\frac{b^{2} + c^{2}\text{-}a^{2}}{2bc} = \frac{1}{2}$,即$b^{2}$*+c*^2^*-a*^2^*=bc.*
> > *∵b+c=*2*a*,*∴a=*$\frac{b + c}{2}$,
> > *∴b*^2^*+c*^2^*-*$\left( \frac{b + c}{2} \right)^{2}$*=bc*,即(*b-c*)^2^*=*0,
> > *∴b=c*,于是*a=b=c.*
> > *∴*△*ABC*为等边三角形*.*
