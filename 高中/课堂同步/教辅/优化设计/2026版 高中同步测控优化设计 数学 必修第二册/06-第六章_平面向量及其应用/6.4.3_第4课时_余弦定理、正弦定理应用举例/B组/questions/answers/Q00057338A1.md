> > *AC=*$\sqrt{AB^{2} + BC^{2}\text{-}2AB\text{·}BC\text{·}cos120\text{°}}$*=*$\sqrt{400\text{(}\sqrt{6}\text{-}\sqrt{2}\text{)}^{2} + \text{(}40\sqrt{2}\text{)}^{2}\text{-}2 \times 20\text{(}\sqrt{6}\text{-}\sqrt{2}\text{)} \times 40\sqrt{2} \times \left( \text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right)}$ *=*40$\sqrt{3}.$
> > 由正弦定理,得$\frac{BC}{\sin\angle BAC} = \frac{AC}{\sin\angle ABC}$,得
> > sin∠*BAC=*$\frac{BC\sin\angle ABC}{AC} = \frac{40\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}}{40\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$
> > 即∠*BAC=*45°,75°*-*∠*BAC=*30°*.*
