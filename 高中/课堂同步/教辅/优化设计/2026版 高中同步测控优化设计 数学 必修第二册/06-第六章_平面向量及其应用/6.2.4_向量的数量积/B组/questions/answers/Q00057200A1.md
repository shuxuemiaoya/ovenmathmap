> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)*∵*(*$a$**-**$b$*)·(*$a$**+**$b$*)*=*$\frac{1}{2}$,*∴\|**$a$**\|*^2^*-\|**$b$**\|*^2^*=*$\frac{1}{2}.$
> > *∵\|**$a$**\|=*1,*∴\|**$b$**\|=* $\sqrt{\text{|}a\text{|}^{2}\text{-}\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$
> > 设*$a$*与*$b$*的夹角为*θ*,则cos *θ=*$\frac{a\text{·}b}{\text{|}a\text{||}b\text{|}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{1 \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$
> > *∵*0°≤*θ*≤180°,*∴θ=*45°*.*
> > (2)*∵*(*$a$**-**$b$*)^2^*=**$a$*^2^*-*2*$a$*·*$b$**+**$b$*^2^*=*$\frac{1}{2}$,*∴\|**$a$**-**$b$**\|=*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$
> > *∵*(*$a$**+**$b$*)^2^*=**$a$*^2^*+*2*$a$*·*$b$**+**$b$*^2^*=*$\frac{5}{2}$,*∴\|**$a$**+**$b$**\|=*$\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}.$
> > 设*$a$**-**$b$*与*$a$**+**$b$*的夹角为*α*,则
> > cos *α=*$\frac{\text{(}a\text{-}b\text{)·(}a + b\text{)}}{\text{|}a\text{-}b\text{||}a + b\text{|}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}.$
