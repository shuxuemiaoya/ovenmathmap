> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{3\pi}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵\|**$a$**\|=*$\sqrt{2}$,*$a$*·(*$a$**+**$b$*)*=*1,
> > *∴**$a$*^2^*+**$a$*·*$b$**=*2*+**$a$*·*$b$**=*1,*∴**$a$*·*$b$**=-*1*.*
> > 设*$a$*,*$b$*的夹角为*θ*,则cos *θ=*$\frac{a\text{·}b}{\text{|}a\text{||}b\text{|}} = \frac{\text{-}1}{\sqrt{2} \times 1}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$,又*θ*∈\[0,π\],*∴θ=*$\frac{3\pi}{4}.$
