> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由正弦定理$\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B}$,
> > 得sin *B=*$\frac{b\sin A}{a} = \frac{2sin30\text{°}}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$
> > *∵*0°*\<B\<*180°,*∴B=*45°或*B=*135°.
> > 当*B=*45°时,
> > *C=*180°*-*(*A+B*)*=*180°*-*(30°*+*45°)*=*105°*.*
> > $\because\frac{c}{\sin C} = \frac{a}{\sin A}$,
> > *∴c=*$\frac{a\sin C}{\sin A} = \frac{\sqrt{2}sin105\text{°}}{sin30\text{°}} = \frac{\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}}{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{3}$*+*1;
> > 当*B=*135°时,
> > *C=*180°*-*(*A+B*)*=*180°*-*(30°*+*135°)*=*15°,
> > *∴c=*$\frac{a\sin C}{\sin A} = \frac{\sqrt{2}sin15\text{°}}{sin30\text{°}} = \frac{\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{6}\text{-}\sqrt{2}}{4}}{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{3}$*-*1*.*
> > 综上可得,*B=*45°,*C=*105°,*c=*$\sqrt{3}$*+*1或*B=*135°,*C=*15°,*c=*$\sqrt{3}$*-*1*.*
