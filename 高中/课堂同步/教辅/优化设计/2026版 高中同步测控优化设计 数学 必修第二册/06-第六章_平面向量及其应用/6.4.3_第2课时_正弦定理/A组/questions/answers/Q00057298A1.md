> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由正弦定理及*a*cos *C+*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$*c=b*,
> > 得sin *A*cos *C+*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$sin *C=*sin *B.*
> > 因为sin *B=*sin(*A+C*)*=*sin *A*cos *C+*cos *A*sin $C$,
> > 所以$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$sin *C=*cos *A*sin *C.*
> > 因为sin $C$≠0,所以cos *A=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$
> > 因为0*\<A\<*π,所以*A=*$\frac{\pi}{6}.$
> > (2)由正弦定理,得sin *B=*$\frac{b\sin A}{a} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$
> > 因为0*\<B\<*π,所以*B=*$\frac{\pi}{3}$或*B=*$\frac{2\pi}{3}.$
> > *①*当*B=*$\frac{\pi}{3}$时,由*A=*$\frac{\pi}{6}$,得*C=*$\frac{\pi}{2}$,
> > 因为*c=*$\frac{b\sin C}{\sin B}$,所以*c=*2;
> > ②当*B=*$\frac{2\pi}{3}$时,由*A=*$\frac{\pi}{6}$,得*C=*$\frac{\pi}{6}$,即*c=a=*1*.*
> > 综上可得*c=*1或*c=*2*.*
