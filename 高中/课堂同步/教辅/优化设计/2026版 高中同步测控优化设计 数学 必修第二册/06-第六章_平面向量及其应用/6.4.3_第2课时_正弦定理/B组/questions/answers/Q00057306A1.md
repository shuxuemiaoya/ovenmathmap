> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设$R$为△*ABC*外接圆的半径*.*
> > *∵a=*2*b*sin $A$,
> > *∴*2*R*sin *A=*4*R*sin *B*sin *A.*
> > *∵*sin $A$≠0,*∴*sin *B=*$\frac{1}{2}.$
> > 又$B$为锐角,*∴B=*$\frac{\pi}{6}.$
> > 令*y=*cos *A+*sin *C=*cos *A+*sin\[π*-*(*B+A*)\]*=*cos *A+*sin$\left( \frac{\pi}{6} + A \right)$*=*cos *A+*sin$\frac{\pi}{6}$cos *A+*cos$\frac{\pi}{6}$sin $A$
> > *=*$\frac{3}{2}$cos *A+*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$sin *A=*$\sqrt{3}$sin$\left( A + \frac{\pi}{3} \right).$
> > 由△*ABC*为锐角三角形,知$\frac{\pi}{2}$*-B\<A\<*$\frac{\pi}{2}$,
> > 则$\frac{\pi}{3}$*\<A\<*$\frac{\pi}{2}.$
> > 即$\frac{2\pi}{3}$*\<A+*$\frac{\pi}{3} < \frac{5\pi}{6}$,得$\frac{1}{2}$*\<*sin$\left( A + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) < \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$
> > 得$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} < \sqrt{3}$sin$\left( A + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) < \frac{3}{2}$,即$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$*\<y\<*$\frac{3}{2}.$
> > 故cos *A+*sin $C$的取值范围是$\left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{,}\frac{3}{2} \right).$
