> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由余弦定理,知cos *A=*$\frac{b^{2} + c^{2}\text{-}a^{2}}{2bc}$,
> > 则$\frac{b^{2} + c^{2}\text{-}a^{2}}{\cos A}$*=*2*bc=*2,得*bc=*1*.*
> > (2)由正弦定理,有$\frac{\sin A\cos B\text{-}\sin B\cos A}{\sin A\cos B + sinB\cos A} - \frac{\sin B}{\sin C}$*=*1*.*①
> > 在△*ABC*中,*A+B+C=*π,则sin *C=*sin\[π*-*(*A+B*)\]*=*sin(*A+B*)*=*sin *A*cos *B+*cos *A*sin *B.*
> > ①式变形为sin *A*cos *B-*sin *B*cos *A-*sin *B=*sin *A*cos *B+*cos *A*sin $B$,即*-*sin *B=*2sin *B*cos *A.*
> > *∵*0*\<B\<*π,*∴*sin $B$≠0*.*则cos *A=-*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > *∵*0*\<A\<*π,*∴A=*$\frac{2\pi}{3}$*.*
> > 由(1)知*bc=*1,故$S$~△*ABC*~*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*bc*sin *A=*$\frac{1}{2}$*×*1*×*sin$\frac{2\pi}{3} = \frac{1}{2}$*×*1*×*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$*.*
> > *∴A=B=C=*60°,△*ABC*为正三角形*.*
