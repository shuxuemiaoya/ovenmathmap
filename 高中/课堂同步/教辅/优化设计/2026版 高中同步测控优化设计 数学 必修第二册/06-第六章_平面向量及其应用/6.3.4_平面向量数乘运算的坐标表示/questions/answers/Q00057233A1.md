> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left( \frac{4}{3}\text{,}\frac{2}{3} \right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设点$C$($x$,$y$),则$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(*x-*2,$y$),$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(*-*2,2),
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{AC} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB}$,所以(*x-*2,$y$)*=*$\left( \text{-}\frac{2}{3}\text{,}\frac{2}{3} \right)$,
> > 得*x=*$\frac{4}{3}$,*y=*$\frac{2}{3}$,即*C*$\left( \frac{4}{3}\text{,}\frac{2}{3} \right).$
