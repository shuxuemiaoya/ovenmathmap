8. 如图,$P$,$Q$是△*ABC*的边*BC*上两点,且*BP=QC.*求证:$\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AP} + \overrightarrow{AQ}.$
![id:2147494390;FounderCES](../images/image11.jpeg)
证明:$\because\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{AP} + \overrightarrow{PB},\overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AQ} + \overrightarrow{QC}$,
$\therefore\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AP} + \overrightarrow{PB} + \overrightarrow{AQ} + \overrightarrow{QC}.$
$\because\overrightarrow{PB}与\overrightarrow{QC}$大小相等,方向相反,
$\therefore\overrightarrow{PB} + \overrightarrow{QC}$*=***0**,$\therefore\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AP} + \overrightarrow{AQ}$*+***0***=*$\overrightarrow{AP} + \overrightarrow{AQ}.$
