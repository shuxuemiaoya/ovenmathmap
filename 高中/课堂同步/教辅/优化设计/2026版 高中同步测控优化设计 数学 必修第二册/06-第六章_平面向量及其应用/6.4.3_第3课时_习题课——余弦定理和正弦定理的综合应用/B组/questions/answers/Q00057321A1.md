> > [!note]- **【解析】**

> > 又由余弦定理,得$c^{2}$*=a*^2^*+b*^2^*-*2*ab*cos $C$,
> > *∴*2*ab-*6*=*2*ab*cos $C$,*∴ab=*$\frac{3}{1\text{-}\cos C}$,
> > *∴S*~△*ABC*~*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*ab*sin *C=*$\frac{3sinC}{2\text{(}1\text{-}\cos C\text{)}} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$,
> > *∴*$\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{3}$cos *C=*sin $C$,*∴*sin(*C+*60°)*=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$,
> > 又$C$为△*ABC*的内角,*∴C=*60°*.*
