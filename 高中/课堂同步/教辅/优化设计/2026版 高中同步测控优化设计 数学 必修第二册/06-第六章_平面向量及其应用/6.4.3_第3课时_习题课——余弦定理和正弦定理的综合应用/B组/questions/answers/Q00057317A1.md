> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵S*~△*ABC*~*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*AB*·*BC*sin *B=*$\frac{1}{2}$,
> > *∴*sin *B=*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*.*
> > 又$B$为△*ABC*的内角,*∴B=*45°或*B=*135°*.*
> > 若*B=*45°,则由余弦定理,得$AC^{2}$*=AB*^2^*+BC*^2^*-*2*AB*·*BC*cos *B=*1*+*2*-*2*×*$\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*=*1,
> > 此时△*ABC*为直角三角形,不合题意,舍去;
> > 若*B=*135°,则由余弦定理,得$AC^{2}$*=AB*^2^*+BC*^2^*-*2*AB*·*BC*cos *B=*1*+*2*-*2*×*1*×*$\sqrt{2} \times \left( \text{-}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$*=*5,
> > 即*AC=*$\sqrt{5}$*.*
