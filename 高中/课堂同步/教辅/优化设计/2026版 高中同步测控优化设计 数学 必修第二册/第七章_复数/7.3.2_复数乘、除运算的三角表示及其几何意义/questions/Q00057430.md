8. 求证:
(1)\[$r$(cos *θ+*isin *θ*)\]^2^*=r*^2^(cos 2*θ+*isin 2*θ*);
(2)\[$r$(cos *θ+*isin *θ*)\]^3^*=r*^3^(cos 3*θ+*isin 3*θ*)*.*
证明:(1)\[$r$(cos *θ+*isin *θ*)\]^2^*=r*^2^(cos *θ+*isin *θ*)^2^*=r*^2^(cos^2^*θ-*sin^2^*θ+*2icos *θ*sin *θ*)*=r*^2^(cos 2*θ+*isin 2*θ*),
故待证式成立*.*
(2)\[$r$(cos *θ+*isin *θ*)\]^3^*=*\[$r$(cos *θ+*isin *θ*)\]^2^·\[$r$(cos *θ+*isin *θ*)\]*=r*^2^(cos 2*θ+*isin 2*θ*)·$r$(cos *θ+*isin *θ*)
*=r*^3^\[cos(2*θ+θ*)*+*isin(2*θ+θ*)\]*=r*^3^(cos 3*θ+*isin 3*θ*),故待证式成立*.*
