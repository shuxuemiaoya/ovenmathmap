> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)因为$\frac{1}{z} = \frac{cos0 + isin0}{10\left( \cos\frac{\pi}{3} + isin\frac{\pi}{3} \right)}$
> > *=*$\frac{1}{10}\left\lbrack \cos\left( 0\text{-}\frac{\pi}{3} \right) + isin\left( 0\text{-}\frac{\pi}{3} \right) \right\rbrack$
> > *=*$\frac{1}{10}\left\lbrack \cos\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{3} \right) + isin\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{3} \right) \right\rbrack$,
> > 所以$\frac{1}{z}$的模为$\frac{1}{10}$,辐角为*-*$\frac{\pi}{3}$*+*2*k*π($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
> > (2)因为复数2$\left( \sin\frac{3\pi}{4} + icos\frac{3\pi}{4} \right) = \sqrt{2} - \sqrt{2}$i,模*r=*2,在复平面内对应的点在第四象限,且cos *θ=*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$,
> > 取*θ=-*$\frac{\pi}{4}$,所以2(sin$\frac{3\pi}{4}$*+*icos$\frac{3\pi}{4}$)*=*2\[cos$\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{4} \right)$*+*isin$\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{4} \right)$\]*.*
> > $\frac{1}{z} = \frac{cos0 + isin0}{2\left\lbrack \cos\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{4} \right) + isin\left( \text{-}\frac{\pi}{4} \right) \right\rbrack}$*=*$\frac{1}{2}$(cos$\frac{\pi}{4}$*+*isin$\frac{\pi}{4}$)*.*
> > 所以$\frac{1}{z}$的模为$\frac{1}{2}$,辐角为$\frac{\pi}{4}$*+*2*k*π($k$∈*$Z$*)*.*
