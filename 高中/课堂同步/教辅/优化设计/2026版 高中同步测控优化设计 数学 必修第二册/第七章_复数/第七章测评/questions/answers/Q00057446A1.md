> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设*z=x+y*i($x$,$y$∈*$R$*),
> > *∵z+*2i*=x+*(*y+*2)i是实数,
> > *∴y=-*2*.*
> > *∵*$\frac{z}{2\text{-}i} = \frac{x\text{-}2i}{2\text{-}i} = \frac{1}{5}$(*x-*2i)(2*+*i)*=*$\frac{1}{5}$(2*x+*2)*+*$\frac{1}{5}$(*x-*4)i是实数,
> > *∴x=*4,
> > *∴z=*4*-*2i*.*
> > *∵*(*z+a*i)^2^*=*(12*+*4*a-a*^2^)*+*8(*a-*2)i,
> > 根据条件,可知$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 12 + 4a\text{-}a^{2} > 0\text{,} \\
> > 8\text{(}a\text{-}2\text{)} > 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得2*\<a\<*6*.*
> > *∴*实数$a$的取值范围是(2,6)*.*
