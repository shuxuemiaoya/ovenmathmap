> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)设*z=a+b*i($a$,$b$∈*$R$*),
> > 由已知条件得,$a^{2}$*+b*^2^*=*2,①
> > *∵z*^2^*=a*^2^*-b*^2^*+*2*ab*i,
> > *∴*2*ab=*2*.*②
> > 由*①②*解得*a=b=*1或*a=b=-*1,
> > 即*z=*1*+*i或*z=-*1*-$i$.*
> > (2)当*z=*1*+*i时,$z^{2}$*=*(1*+*i)^2^*=*2i,*z-z*^2^*=*1*-*i,
> > 即点$A$(1,1),$B$(0,2),$C$(1,*-*1),
> > 得$S$~△*ABC*~*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*\|AC\|*·1*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*×*2*×*1*=*1;
> > 当*z=-*1*-*i时,$z^{2}$*=*(*-*1*-*i)^2^*=*2i,*z-z*^2^*=-*1*-*3i*.*
> > 即点$A$(*-*1,*-*1),$B$(0,2),$C$(*-*1,*-*3),
> > 得$S$~△*ABC*~*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*\|AC\|*·1*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*×*2*×*1*=*1*.*
> > 故△*ABC*的面积为1*.*
