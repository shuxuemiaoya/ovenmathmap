> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** ACD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A项中,*∵*(*x+*i)(1*-*i)*=*(*x+*1)*+*(1*-x*)i,
> > *∴*(*x+*1)*+*(1*-x*)i*=y.*
> > $\therefore\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x + 1 = y\text{,} \\
> > 1\text{-}x = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = 1\text{,} \\
> > y = 2\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$故A正确*.*
> > B项中,由A项知,*x+y*i*=*1*+*2i,所以*\|x+y*i*\|=*$\sqrt{5}$,故B不正确*.*
> > C项中,设*y=b*i($b$∈*$R$*),*∵*(*x+*i)(1*-*i)*=*(*x+*1)*+*(1*-x*)i,*∴*(*x+*1)*+*(1*-x*)i*=y=b*i*.*
> > $\therefore\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x + 1 = 0\text{,} \\
> > 1\text{-}x = b\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ \therefore\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \text{-}1\text{,} \\
> > b = 2\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > *∴x=-*1,*y=*2i,故C正确*.*
> > D项中,由C项知,*x+y*i*=-*1*+*2i*×$i$=-*1*-*2*=-*3,故D正确*.*
