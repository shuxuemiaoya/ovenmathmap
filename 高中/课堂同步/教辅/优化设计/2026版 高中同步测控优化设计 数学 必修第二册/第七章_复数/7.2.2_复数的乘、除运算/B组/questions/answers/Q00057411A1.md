> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** 2*　-*1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵a*,$b$∈*$R$*,且$\frac{a}{1\text{-}i}$*=*1*-b*i,
> > 则*a=*(1*-b*i)(1*-*i)*=*(1*-b*)*-*(1*+b*)i,
> > *∴*$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 1\text{-}b\text{,} \\
> > 0 = 1 + b\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 2\text{,} \\
> > b = \text{-}1\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
