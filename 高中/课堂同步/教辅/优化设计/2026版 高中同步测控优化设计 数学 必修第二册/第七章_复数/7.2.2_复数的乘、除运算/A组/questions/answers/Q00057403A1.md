> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** *±*i
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设*z=a+b*i($a$,$b$∈*$R$*),则$\overline{z}$*=a-b*i,
> > 由*z+*$\overline{z}$*=*4,$z$·$\overline{z}$*=*8,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2a = 4\text{,} \\
> > a^{2} + b^{2} = 8\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 2\text{,} \\
> > b = \pm 2\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 即*z=*2*+*2i,$\overline{z}$*=*2*-*2i或*z=*2*-*2i,$\overline{z}$*=*2*+*2i,
> > $\frac{\overline{z}}{z} = \frac{2\text{-}2i}{2 + 2i}$*=-*i或$\frac{\overline{z}}{z} = \frac{2 + 2i}{2\text{-}2i}$*=$i$.*故$\frac{\overline{z}}{z}$*=±$i$.*
