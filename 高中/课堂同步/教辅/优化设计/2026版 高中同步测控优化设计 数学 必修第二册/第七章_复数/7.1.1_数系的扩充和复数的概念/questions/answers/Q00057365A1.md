> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵M*∪*P=P*,
> > *∴M*⊆$P$,
> > 即($m^{2}$*-*2*m*)*+*($m^{2}$*+m-*2)i*=-*1或($m^{2}$*-*2*m*)*+*($m^{2}$*+m-*2)i*=*4i*.*
> > 由($m^{2}$*-*2*m*)*+*($m^{2}$*+m-*2)i*=-*1,
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > m^{2}\text{-}2m = \text{-}1\text{,} \\
> > m^{2} + m\text{-}2 = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*m=*1;
> > 由($m^{2}$*-*2*m*)*+*($m^{2}$*+m-*2)i*=*4i,
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > m^{2}\text{-}2m = 0\text{,} \\
> > m^{2} + m\text{-}2 = 4\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得*m=*2*.*
> > 综上可知,*m=*1或*m=*2*.*
