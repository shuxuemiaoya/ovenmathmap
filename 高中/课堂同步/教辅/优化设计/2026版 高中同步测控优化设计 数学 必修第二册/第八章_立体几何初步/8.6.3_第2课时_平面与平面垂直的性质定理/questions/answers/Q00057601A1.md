> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图,取*AB*的中点$E$,连接*PE.*

> > *∵PA=PB*,
> > *∴PE*⊥*AB.*
> > 又平面*PAB*⊥平面*ABC*,且交线为*AB*,
> > *∴PE*⊥平面*ABC.*
> > 连接*CE*,则*PE*⊥*CE.*

> > *∴AB=*2$\sqrt{7}.$
> > *∴PE=*$\sqrt{PA^{2}\text{-}AE^{2}} = \sqrt{6}$,
> > *CE=*$\sqrt{BE^{2} + BC^{2}} = \sqrt{43}.$
> > *∴PC=*$\sqrt{PE^{2} + CE^{2}}$*=*7*.*
