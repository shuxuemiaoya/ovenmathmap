> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由于$BB_{1}$∥$DD_{1}$,故$BB_{1}$与平面$ACD_{1}$所成的角等于$DD_{1}$与平面$ACD_{1}$所成的角*.*
> > 如图所示,过点$D$作*DO*⊥平面$ACD_{1}$,垂足为$O$,连接$D_{1}$*O*,则∠$DD_{1}$*O*即为所求的角*.*
> > ![id:2147499864;FounderCES](../../images/image11.jpeg)
> > 利用等体积法,不妨设正方体的棱长为1,$V_{D\text{-}AD_{1}C} = V_{D_{1}\text{-}ADC},S_{\text{△}ACD_{1}} = \frac{1}{2}$*AC*·$AD_{1}$sin 60°*=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$,
> > 则*DO=*$\frac{S_{\text{△}ACD}\text{·}DD_{1}}{S_{\text{△}ACD_{1}}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$
> > 故sin∠$DD_{1}$*O=*$\frac{DO}{DD_{1}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$,从而cos∠$DD_{1}$*O=*$\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}.$
> > 故选D*.*
