> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设圆锥的母线长为$l$ cm,圆台上、下底面的半径分别为$r$ cm,$R$ cm*.*
> > ![id:2147497426;FounderCES](images/image6.jpeg)
> > $\because\frac{l\text{-}10}{l} = \frac{r}{R}$,$\therefore\frac{l\text{-}10}{l} = \frac{1}{4}$,解得*l=*$\frac{40}{3}.$
> > 故圆锥的母线长为$\frac{40}{3}$ cm*.*
