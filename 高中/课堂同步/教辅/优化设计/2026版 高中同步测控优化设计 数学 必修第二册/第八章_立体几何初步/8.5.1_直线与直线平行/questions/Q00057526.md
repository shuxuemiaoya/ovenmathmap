8. 如图,在四面体*A-BCD*中,$E$,$F$,$G$,$H$分别是所在棱上的点,且*AE∶EB=AH∶HD=m*,*CF∶FB=CG∶GD=n.*
![id:2147498857;FounderCES](images/image8.jpeg)
(1)证明:$E$,$F$,$G$,$H$四点共面;
(2)当$m$,$n$满足什么条件时,四边形*EFGH*是平行四边形?
(1)证明:*∵AE∶EB=AH∶HD*,*∴EH*∥*BD.*
*∵CF∶FB=CG∶GD*,
*∴FG*∥*BD*,*∴EH*∥*FG*,*∴E*,$F$,$G$,$H$四点共面*.*
(2)解:当且仅当*EH*![](images/image3.png)*FG*时,四边形*EFGH*为平行四边形*.*(1)中已证*EH*∥*FG.*
$\because\frac{EH}{BD} = \frac{AE}{AE + EB} = \frac{m}{m + 1}$,*∴EH=*$\frac{m}{m + 1}$*BD.*
同理*FG=*$\frac{n}{n + 1}$*BD.*由*EH=FG*,得*m=n.*
故当*m=n*时,四边形*EFGH*为平行四边形*.*
9. 如图,△*ABC*和△*A\'B\'C\'*的对应顶点的连线*AA\'*,*BB\'*,*CC\'*交于同一点$O$,且$\frac{AO}{A\text{'}O} = \frac{BO}{B\text{'}O} = \frac{CO}{C\text{'}O} = \frac{2}{3}.$
![id:2147498864;FounderCES](images/image9.jpeg)
(1)证明:*AB*∥*A\'B\'*,*AC*∥*A\'C\'*,*BC*∥*B\'C\'.*
(2)求$\frac{S_{\text{△}ABC}}{S_{\text{△}A\text{'}B\text{'}C\text{'}}}$的值*.*
(1)证明:*∵AA\'*与*BB\'*相交于点$O$,且$\frac{AO}{A\text{'}O} = \frac{BO}{B\text{'}O}$,
*∴AB*∥*A\'B\'.*
同理*AC*∥*A\'C\'*,*BC*∥*B\'C\'.*
(2)解:*∵AB*∥*A\'B\'*,*AC*∥*A\'C\'*,且*AB*和*A\'B\'*,*AC*和*A\'C\'*的方向分别相反,
*∴*∠*BAC=*∠*B\'A\'C\'.*
同理∠*ABC=*∠*A\'B\'C\'*,因此△*ABC*∽△*A\'B\'C\'.*
又$\frac{AB}{A\text{'}B\text{'}} = \frac{AO}{A\text{'}O} = \frac{2}{3}$,$\therefore\frac{S_{\text{△}ABC}}{S_{\text{△}A\text{'}B\text{'}C\text{'}}} = \left( \frac{2}{3} \right)^{2} = \frac{4}{9}.$
