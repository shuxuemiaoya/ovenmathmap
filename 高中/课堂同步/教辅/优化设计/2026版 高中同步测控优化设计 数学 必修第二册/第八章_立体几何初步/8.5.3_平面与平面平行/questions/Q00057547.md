10. 如图,$B$为△*ACD*所在平面外一点,$M$,$N$,$G$分别为△*ABC*,△*ABD*,△*BCD*的重心*.*
![id:2147499386;FounderCES](images/image10.jpeg)
(1)求证:平面*MNG*∥平面*ACD*;
(2)求$S$~△*MNG*~*∶S*~△*DCA*~*.*
(1)证明:如图,连接*BM*,*BN*,*BG*并延长,分别交*AC*,*AD*,*CD*于点$P$,$F$,*H.*
![id:2147499393;FounderCES](images/image11.jpeg)
*∵M*,$N$,$G$分别为△*ABC*,△*ABD*,△*BCD*的重心,
$\therefore\frac{BM}{MP} = \frac{BN}{NF} = \frac{BG}{GH}$*=*2*.*
连接*PF*,*FH*,*PH*,有*MN*∥*PF*,*NG*∥*FH.*
*∵MN*⊄平面*ACD*,*PF*⊂平面*ACD*,
*∴MN*∥平面*ACD.*
同理,*NG*∥平面*ACD.*
*∵MN*∩*NG=N*,*∴*平面*MNG*∥平面*ACD.*
(2)解:由(1)可得$\frac{MG}{PH} = \frac{BG}{BH} = \frac{2}{3}$,
*∴MG=*$\frac{2}{3}$*PH.*
又*PH=*$\frac{1}{2}$*AD*,*∴MG=*$\frac{1}{3}$*AD.*
同理*NG=*$\frac{1}{3}$*AC*,*MN=*$\frac{1}{3}$*CD.*
*∴*△*MNG*∽△*DCA*,其相似比为1*∶*3*.*
*∴S*~△*MNG*~*∶S*~△*DCA*~*=*1*∶*9*.*
