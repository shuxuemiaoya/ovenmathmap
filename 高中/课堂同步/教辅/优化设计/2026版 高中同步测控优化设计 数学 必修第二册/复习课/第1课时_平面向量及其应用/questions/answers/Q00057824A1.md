> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{21}}{7}$*　*3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由正弦定理$\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B}$,得$\frac{\sqrt{7}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} = \frac{2}{\sin B}$,
> > 即sin *B=*$\frac{\sqrt{21}}{7}$*.*
> > 由余弦定理的推论,得cos *A=*$\frac{b^{2} + c^{2}\text{-}a^{2}}{2bc}$,得$\frac{1}{2} = \frac{4 + c^{2}\text{-}7}{4c}$,即*c=*3*.*
