>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)因为向量$\overrightarrow{BA}$对应的复数为1*+*2i,向量$\overrightarrow{BC}$对应的复数为3*-*i,所以向量$\overrightarrow{AC}$对应的复数为(3*-*i)*-*(1*+*2i)*=*2*-*3i*.*
> > 又因为$\overrightarrow{OC} = \overrightarrow{OA} + \overrightarrow{AC}$,所以点$C$对应的复数为(2*+*i)*+*(2*-*3i)*=*4*-*2i*.*
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{BC}$,所以向量$\overrightarrow{AD}$对应的复数为3*-$i$.*
> > 即$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*(3,*-*1)*.*
> > 设$D$($x$,$y$),则$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*(*x-*2,*y-*1)*=*(3,*-*1),
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x\text{-}2 = 3\text{,} \\
> > y\text{-}1 = \text{-}1\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = 5\text{,} \\
> > y = 0\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以点$D$对应的复数为5*.*
> > (2)因为$\overrightarrow{BA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC}$*=\|*$\overrightarrow{BA}$*\|\|*$\overrightarrow{BC}$*\|*cos $B$,
> > 所以cos *B=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{BA}\text{·}\overrightarrow{BC}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{BA}\text{||}\overrightarrow{BC}\text{|}} = \frac{3\text{-}2}{\sqrt{5} \times \sqrt{10}} = \frac{1}{5\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$*.*
> > 因为0*\<B\<*π,所以sin *B=*$\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$,所以$S$~▱*ABCD*~*=\|*$\overrightarrow{BA}$*\|\|*$\overrightarrow{BC}$*\|*sin *B=*$\sqrt{5} \times \sqrt{10} \times \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$*=*7,
> > 所以平行四边形*ABCD*的面积为7*.*
