> > [!success] **【答案】** *-*2i
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设*z=b*i($b$∈*$R$*,$b$≠0),因为$\frac{z + 2}{1\text{-}i} = \frac{bi + 2}{1\text{-}i} = \frac{\text{(}bi + 2\text{)(}1 + i\text{)}}{\text{(}1\text{-}i\text{)(}1 + i\text{)}} = \frac{2\text{-}b + \text{(}b + 2\text{)}i}{2} = \frac{2\text{-}b}{2} + \frac{b + 2}{2}$i是实数,所以*b+*2*=*0,*b=-*2,所以*z=-*2i*.*
