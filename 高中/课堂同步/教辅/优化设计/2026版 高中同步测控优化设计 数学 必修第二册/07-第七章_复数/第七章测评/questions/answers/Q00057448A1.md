> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由$z_{1}$*+*$\frac{1}{z_{2}} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$i,得
> > cos *α+*isin *α+*$\frac{1}{\cos\beta\text{-}isin\beta} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$i,
> > 则cos *α+*isin *α+*cos *β+*isin *β=*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$i,
> > 即(cos *α+*cos *β*)*+*i(sin *α+*sin *β*)*=*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$i*.*
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \cos\alpha + cos\beta = \frac{1}{2}\text{,} \\
> > \sin\alpha + sin\beta = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \cos\alpha = \frac{1}{2}\text{-}\cos\beta\text{,} \\
> > \sin\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{-}\sin\beta\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 即cos^2^*α+*sin^2^*α=*$\left( \frac{1}{2}\text{-}\cos\beta \right)^{2} + \left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{-}\sin\beta \right)^{2}$*=*1,
> > 整理,得cos *β=*1*-*$\sqrt{3}$sin *β*,代入sin^2^*β+*cos^2^*β=*1,
> > 可解得sin *β=*0或sin *β=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$*.*
> > 当sin *β=*0时,cos *β=*1,cos *α=-*$\frac{1}{2}$,sin *α=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$;
> > 当sin *β=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$时,cos *β=-*$\frac{1}{2}$,cos *α=*1,sin *α=*0*.*
> > 故$z_{1}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$i,$z_{2}$*=*1或$z_{1}$*=*1,$z_{2}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$i*.*
