> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** 4i
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设*m=b*i($b$∈*$R$*,且$b$≠0),
> > 则$x^{2}$*+*(2*-*i)*x+*(2*b*i*-*4)i*=*0,
> > 化简得($x^{2}$*+*2*x-*2*b*)*+*(*-x-*4)i*=*0,
> > 即$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x^{2} + 2x\text{-}2b = 0\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}x\text{-}4 = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \text{-}4\text{,} \\
> > b = 4\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故*m=*4i*.*
