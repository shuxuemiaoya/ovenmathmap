> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵AD*⊥*BC*,
> > *∴BD*⊥*AD*,*CD*⊥*AD*,
> > *∴*折叠后,∠*BDC*为平面*ABD*与平面*ACD*所成二面角的平面角*.*
> > 又平面*ABD*⊥平面*ACD*,*∴*∠*BDC=*90°*.*
> > 又折叠前,*AB=AC=*1,∠*BAC=*90°,
> > *∴BD+CD=*$\sqrt{AB^{2} + AC^{2}} = \sqrt{2}$,
> > *∴BD=CD=*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$
> > 折叠后,连接*BC*,
> > 在Rt△*BDC*中,*BC=*$\sqrt{BD^{2} + CD^{2}}$*=*1*.*
