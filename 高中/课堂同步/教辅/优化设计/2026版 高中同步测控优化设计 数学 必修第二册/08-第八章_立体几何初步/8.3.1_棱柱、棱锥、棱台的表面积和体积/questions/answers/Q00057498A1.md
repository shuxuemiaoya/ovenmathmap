> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)如图,设正三棱柱的高为$h$ cm,底面边长为$x$ cm,
> > 则$\frac{15\text{-}h}{15} = \frac{x}{12}$,得*x=*$\frac{4}{5}$(15*-h*)*.*①
> > ![id:2147498022;FounderCES](images/image7.jpeg)
> > 因为$S$~三棱柱侧~*=*3*x*·*h=*120,所以*xh=*40*.*②
> > ①②,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = 4\text{,} \\
> > h = 10
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 或\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = 8\text{,} \\
> > h = 5\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故正三棱柱的高为10 cm或5 cm*.*
> > (2)由棱锥的性质,得$\frac{S_{S\text{-}A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}\text{侧}}}{S_{S\text{-}ABC\text{侧}}} = \left( \frac{15\text{-}10}{15} \right)^{2} = \frac{1}{9}或\frac{S_{S\text{-}A_{1}B_{1}C_{1}\text{侧}}}{S_{S\text{-}ABC\text{侧}}} = \left( \frac{15\text{-}5}{15} \right)^{2} = \frac{4}{9}.$
