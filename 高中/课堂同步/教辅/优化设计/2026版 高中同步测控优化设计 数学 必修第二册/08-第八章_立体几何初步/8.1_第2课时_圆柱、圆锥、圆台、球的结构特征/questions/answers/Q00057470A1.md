> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ cm
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 过圆锥的顶点$S$和正方体底面的一条对角线*CD*作圆锥的截面,得圆锥的轴截面*SEF*、正方体的对角面$CDD_{1}$$C_{1}$,如图所示*.*
> > ![id:2147497405;FounderCES](images/image3.jpeg)
> > 设正方体的棱长为$x$ cm,
> > 则$CC_{1}$*=x* cm,$C_{1}$$D_{1}$*=*$\sqrt{2}$*x* cm,作*SO*⊥*EF*于点$O$,
> > 则*SO=*$\sqrt{2}$ cm,*OE=*1 cm*.*
> > *∵*△$ECC_{1}$∽△*ESO*,
> > $\therefore\frac{CC_{1}}{SO} = \frac{EC_{1}}{EO}$,即$\frac{x}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1\text{-}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}x}{1}$,
> > *∴x=*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$,即内接正方体的棱长为$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ cm*.*
