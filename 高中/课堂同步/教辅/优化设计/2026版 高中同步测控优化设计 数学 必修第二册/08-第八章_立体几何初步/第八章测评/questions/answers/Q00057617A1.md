> > [!success] **【答案】** 垂直*　*$\frac{2\sqrt{13}}{13}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵PA*⊥*AB*,*PA*⊥*BC*,*AB*∩*BC=B*,
> > *∴PA*⊥平面*ABCD.*
> > 又*BD*⊂平面*ABCD*,
> > *∴PA*⊥*BD.*
> > *∵*四边形*ABCD*为正方形,
> > *∴BD*⊥*AC.*
> > *∵PA*∩*AC=A*,
> > *∴BD*⊥平面*PAC.*
> > ![id:2147496743;FounderCES](images/image14.jpeg)
> > 如图,设*AC*∩*BD=O*,连接*PO*,
> > 则*BD*⊥*PO.*
> > *∴*∠*POA*为二面角*P-BD-A*的平面角*.*
> > 又*AB=*2$\sqrt{2}$,
> > *∴AC=*4,
> > *∴AO=*2*.*
> > *∴PA=*$\sqrt{PC^{2}\text{-}AC^{2}} = \sqrt{5^{2}\text{-}4^{2}}$*=*3*.*
> > *∴PO=*$\sqrt{PA^{2} + AO^{2}} = \sqrt{3^{2} + 2^{2}} = \sqrt{13}$,
> > *∴*cos∠*POA=*$\frac{AO}{PO} = \frac{2\sqrt{13}}{13}.$
