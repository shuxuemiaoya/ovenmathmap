> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在△*BCD*中,*BC=*31,*BD=*20,*CD=*21,由余弦定理,得cos∠*BDC=*$\frac{DB^{2} + DC^{2}\text{-}BC^{2}}{2DB\text{·}DC} = \frac{20^{2} + 21^{2}\text{-}31^{2}}{2 \times 20 \times 21}$*=-*$\frac{1}{7}$,
> > ![id:2147496279;FounderCES](../../images/image17.jpeg)
> > 即cos∠*ADC=*$\frac{1}{7}$,sin∠*ADC=*$\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{7}.$
> > 在△*ACD*中,由条件知*CD=*21,*A=*60°,
> > 则sin∠*ACD=*sin(60°*+*∠*ADC*)*=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \times \frac{1}{7} + \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{7} = \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{14}.$
> > 由正弦定理,得$\frac{AD}{\sin\angle ACD} = \frac{CD}{\sin A}$,解得*AD=*$\frac{21}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}} \times \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{14}$*=*15*.*故这时此车距离$A$城15 km*.*
