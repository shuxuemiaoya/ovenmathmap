> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*2*$a$*,$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*2*$a$**+**$b$*,
> > 所以*$b$**=*$\overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{BC}$,
> > 所以*\|**$b$**\|=\|*$\overrightarrow{BC}$*\|=*2,故A错误;
> > 因为$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*2*$a$*,所以*\|*$\overrightarrow{AB}$*\|=*2*\|**$a$**\|=*2,
> > 所以*\|**$a$**\|=*1,故B正确;
> > 因为*$a$**=*$\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}$,*$b$**=*$\overrightarrow{BC}$,
> > 所以*$a$*与*$b$*不平行,故C错误;
> > 因为4*$a$**+**$b$**=*$\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC},\overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AB}$,
> > 所以(4*$a$**+**$b$*)$\cdot \overrightarrow{BC}$*=*($\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC}$)·($\overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AB}$)*=*${\overrightarrow{AC}}^{2} - {\overrightarrow{AB}}^{2}$*=*0,
> > 所以(4*$a$**+**$b$*)$\bot\overrightarrow{BC}$,故D正确*.*
