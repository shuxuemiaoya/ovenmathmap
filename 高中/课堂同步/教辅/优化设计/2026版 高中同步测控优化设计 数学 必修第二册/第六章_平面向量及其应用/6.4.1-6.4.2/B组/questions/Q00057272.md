8. 已知△*ABC*是等腰直角三角形,∠*ABC=*90°,$D$是*BC*边的中点,*BE*⊥*AD*,垂足为点$E$,延长*BE*交*AC*于点$F$,连接*DF*,求证:∠*ADB=*∠*FDC.*
证明:如图,以$B$为原点,*BC*,*BA*所在直线分别为$x$轴、$y$轴建立平面直角坐标系,
![id:2147495788;FounderCES](../images/image13.jpeg)
设$A$(0,2),$C$(2,0),则$D$(1,0),$\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(2,*-*2)*.*
设$\overrightarrow{AF}$*=λ*$\overrightarrow{AC}$(*λ*∈*$R$*),则$\overrightarrow{BF} = \overrightarrow{BA} + \overrightarrow{AF}$*=*(0,2)*+*(2*λ*,*-*2*λ*)*=*(2*λ*,2*-*2*λ*)*.*
因为$\overrightarrow{DA}$*=*(*-*1,2),且$\overrightarrow{BF}\bot\overrightarrow{DA}$,所以$\overrightarrow{BF} \cdot \overrightarrow{DA}$*=*0,
所以*-*2*λ+*2(2*-*2*λ*)*=*0,解得*λ=*$\frac{2}{3}.$
所以$\overrightarrow{BF} = \left( \frac{4}{3}\text{,}\frac{2}{3} \right).$
所以$\overrightarrow{DF} = \overrightarrow{BF} - \overrightarrow{BD} = \left( \frac{1}{3}\text{,}\frac{2}{3} \right).$
又$\overrightarrow{DC}$*=*(1,0),
所以cos∠*ADB=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{DA}\text{·}\overrightarrow{DB}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{DA}\text{||}\overrightarrow{DB}\text{|}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$,
cos∠*FDC=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{DF}\text{·}\overrightarrow{DC}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{DF}\text{||}\overrightarrow{DC}\text{|}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$,
又∠*ADB*,∠*FDC*∈(0,π),所以∠*ADB=*∠*FDC.*
