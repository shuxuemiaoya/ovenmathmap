> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because\overrightarrow{AC} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BC}$,*∴A*为*BC*的中点,$\overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{BA}$,
> > 设点$C$(*x~C~*,*y~C~*),则(*x~C~-*2,*y~C~+*1)*=*(1,*-*5),
> > *∴*点$C$的坐标为(3,*-*6)*.*
> > 又*\|*$\overrightarrow{CE}$*\|=*$\frac{1}{4}|\overrightarrow{ED}$*\|*,且点$E$在*DC*的延长线上,
> > $\therefore\overrightarrow{CE}$*=-*$\frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{ED}.$
> > 设点$E$($x$,$y$),则(*x-*3,*y+*6)*=-*$\frac{1}{4}$(4*-x*,*-*3*-y*),得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x\text{-}3 = \text{-}\frac{1}{4}\text{(}4\text{-}x\text{),} \\
> > y + 6 = \text{-}\frac{1}{4}\text{(-}3\text{-}y\text{),}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \frac{8}{3}\text{,} \\
> > y = \text{-}7\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故点$E$的坐标是$\left( \frac{8}{3}\text{,-}7 \right).$
