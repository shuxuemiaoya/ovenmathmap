> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (方法一)*∵*sin *A=*$\frac{\sin B + sinC}{\cos B + cosC}$,
> > 且*A+B+C=*π,
> > *∴*sin *A*cos *B+*sin *A*cos *C=*sin(*A+C*)*+*sin(*A+B*),
> > *∴*sin *A*cos *B+*sin *A*cos *C=*sin *A*cos *C+*cos *A*sin *C+*sin *A*cos *B+*cos *A*sin $B$,*∴*cos $A$(sin *C+*sin $B$)*=*0,
> > 又sin *C+*sin $B$≠0,*∴*cos *A=*0*.*
> > *∵*0*\<A\<*π,*∴A=*$\frac{\pi}{2}$,*∴*△*ABC*为直角三角形*.*
> > (方法二)由正弦定理、余弦定理及题设条件,可得*a=*$\frac{b + c}{\frac{a^{2} + c^{2}\text{-}b^{2}}{2ac} + \frac{a^{2} + b^{2}\text{-}c^{2}}{2ab}}$,
> > 化简得(*b+c*)($b^{2}$*+c*^2^*-a*^2^)*=*0,
> > *∵b+c*≠0,*∴b*^2^*+c*^2^*-a*^2^*=*0,*∴b*^2^*+c*^2^*=a*^2^,
> > *∴*△*ABC*为直角三角形*.*
