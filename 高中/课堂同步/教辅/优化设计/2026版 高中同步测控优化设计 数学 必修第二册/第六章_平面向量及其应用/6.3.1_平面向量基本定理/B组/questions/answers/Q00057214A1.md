> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}$

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图,由题意知,$D$为*AB*的中点,$\overrightarrow{BE} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BC}$,
> > ![id:2147495147;FounderCES](../../images/image6.jpeg)
> > 则$\overrightarrow{DE} = \overrightarrow{DB} + \overrightarrow{BE} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} + \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{BC} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} + \frac{2}{3}(\overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AB}$)*=-*$\frac{1}{6}\overrightarrow{AB} + \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AC}.$
> > 即*λ*~1~*=-*$\frac{1}{6}$,*λ*~2~*=*$\frac{2}{3}.$
> > 故*λ*~1~*+λ*~2~*=-*$\frac{1}{6} + \frac{2}{3} = \frac{1}{2}.$
