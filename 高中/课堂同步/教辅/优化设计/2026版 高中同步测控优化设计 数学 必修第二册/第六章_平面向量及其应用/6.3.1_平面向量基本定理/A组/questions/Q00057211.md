9. 设*$e$*~1~,*$e$*~2~是不共线的非零向量,且*$a$**=**$e$*~1~*-*2*$e$*~2~,*$b$**=**$e$*~1~*+*3*$e$*~2~*.*
(1)证明:{*$a$*,*$b$*}可以作为一个基底;
(2)以{*$a$*,*$b$*}为基底,求向量*$c$**=*3*$e$*~1~*-**$e$*~2~的分解式;
(3)若4*$e$*~1~*-*3*$e$*~2~*=λ**$a$**+u**$b$*,求*λ*,$u$的值*.*
(1)证明:假设*$a$**=λ**$b$*(*λ*∈*$R$*),则*$e$*~1~*-*2*$e$*~2~*=λ*(*$e$*~1~*+*3*$e$*~2~)*.*
由*$e$*~1~,*$e$*~2~不共线,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
\lambda = 1\text{,} \\
3\lambda = \text{-}2\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
故*λ*不存在,即*$a$*与*$b$*不共线,可以作为一个基底*.*
(2)解:设*$c$**=m**$a$**+n**$b$*($m$,$n$∈*$R$*),则3*$e$*~1~*-**$e$*~2~*=m*(*$e$*~1~*-*2*$e$*~2~)*+n*(*$e$*~1~*+*3*$e$*~2~)*=*(*m+n*)*$e$*~1~*+*(*-*2*m+*3*n*)*$e$*~2~*.*
因为*$e$*~1~,*$e$*~2~不共线,
所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
m + n = 3\text{,} \\
\text{-}2m + 3n = \text{-}1\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
m = 2\text{,} \\
n = 1\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
故*$c$**=*2*$a$**+**$b$**.*
(3)解:由4*$e$*~1~*-*3*$e$*~2~*=λ**$a$**+u**$b$*,得4*$e$*~1~*-*3*$e$*~2~*=λ*(*$e$*~1~*-*2*$e$*~2~)*+u*(*$e$*~1~*+*3*$e$*~2~)*=*(*λ+u*)*$e$*~1~*+*(*-*2*λ+*3*u*)*$e$*~2~,
得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
\lambda + u = 4\text{,} \\
\text{-}2\lambda + 3u = \text{-}3\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
\lambda = 3\text{,} \\
u = 1\text{.}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
