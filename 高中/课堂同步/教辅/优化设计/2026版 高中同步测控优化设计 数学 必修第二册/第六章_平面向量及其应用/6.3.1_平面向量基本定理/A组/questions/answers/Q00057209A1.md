> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** *$p$**=-*$\frac{7}{4}$*$m$**+*$\frac{13}{8}$*$n$*
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设*$p$**=x**$m$**+y**$n$*,则3*$a$**+*2*$b$**=x*(2*$a$**-*3*$b$*)*+y*(4*$a$**-*2*$b$*)*=*(2*x+*4*y*)*$a$**+*(*-*3*x-*2*y*)*$b$*,
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 2x + 4y = 3\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}3x\text{-}2y = 2\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x = \text{-}\frac{7}{4}\text{,} \\
> > y = \frac{13}{8}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故*$p$**=-*$\frac{7}{4}$*$m$**+*$\frac{13}{8}$*$n$**.*
