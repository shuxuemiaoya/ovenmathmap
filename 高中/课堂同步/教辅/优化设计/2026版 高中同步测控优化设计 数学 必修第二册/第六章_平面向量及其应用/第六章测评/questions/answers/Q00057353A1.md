> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{12\sqrt{2}}{5}　\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在△*ABD*中,由正弦定理,得$\frac{AB}{\sin\angle ADB} = \frac{BD}{\sin\angle BAC}$*.*
> > 因为*AB=*4,∠*ADB=*$\frac{3\pi}{4}$,*AC=*$\sqrt{AB^{2} + BC^{2}}$*=*5,
> > 所以sin∠*BAC=*$\frac{BC}{AC} = \frac{3}{5}$,cos∠*BAC=*$\frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{4}{5}$,
> > 所以*BD=*$\frac{12\sqrt{2}}{5}$*.*
> > cos∠*ABD=*cos(∠*BDC-*∠*BAC*)*=*cos$\frac{\pi}{4}$·cos∠*BAC+*sin$\frac{\pi}{4}$sin∠*BAC=*$\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$*.*
