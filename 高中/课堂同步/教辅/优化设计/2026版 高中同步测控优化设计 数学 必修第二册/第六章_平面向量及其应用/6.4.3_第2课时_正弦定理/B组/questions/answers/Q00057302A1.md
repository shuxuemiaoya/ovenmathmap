> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由正弦定理,得*b=*2*R*sin $B$,*c=*2*R*sin $C$,*a=*2*R*sin $A$($R$为△*ABC*外接圆的半径),
> > 则3(2*R*sin $B$)cos *A=*2*R*sin *C*cos *A+*2*R*sin *A*cos $C$,
> > 则有3sin *B*cos *A=*sin(*C+A*)*=*sin *B.*
> > *∵*sin $B$≠0,*∴*cos *A=*$\frac{1}{3}$*\>*0*.*
> > *∵A*∈(0,π),*∴A*为锐角,
> > *∴*sin *A=*$\sqrt{1\text{-}cos^{2}A} = \sqrt{1\text{-}\frac{1}{9}} = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$,
> > 则有tan *A=*$\frac{\sin A}{\cos A} = \frac{\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}}{\frac{1}{3}}$*=*2$\sqrt{2}.$
