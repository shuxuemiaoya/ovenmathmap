> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{\pi}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *∵*2*b*cos *B=a*cos *C+c*cos $A$,
> > *∴*由正弦定理得2sin *B*cos *B=*sin *A*cos *C+*sin *C*cos $A$,
> > *∴*2sin *B*cos *B=*sin(*A+C*)*=*sin(π*-B*)*=*sin *B.*
> > 又sin $B$≠0,*∴*cos *B=*$\frac{1}{2}.$
> > *∵*0*\<B\<*π,*∴B=*$\frac{\pi}{3}.$
