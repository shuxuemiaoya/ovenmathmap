> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{63}{65}　\frac{21}{13}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由cos *A=*$\frac{4}{5}$,cos *C=*$\frac{5}{13}$,可得sin *A=*$\frac{3}{5}$,sin *C=*$\frac{12}{13}$,sin *B=*sin(*A+C*)*=*sin *A*cos *C+*cos *A*sin *C=*$\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{5}{13} + \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{12}{13} = \frac{63}{65}$,由正弦定理可得*b=*$\frac{a\sin B}{\sin A} = \frac{1 \times \frac{63}{65}}{\frac{3}{5}} = \frac{21}{13}$*.*
