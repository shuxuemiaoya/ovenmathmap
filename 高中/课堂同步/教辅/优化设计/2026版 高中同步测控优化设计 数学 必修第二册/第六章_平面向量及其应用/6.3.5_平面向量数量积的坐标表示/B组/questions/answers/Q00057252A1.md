> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由*\|k**$a$**+**$b$**\|=*$\sqrt{3}$*\|**$a$**-k**$b$**\|*,得($k$*$a$**+**$b$*)^2^*=*3(*$a$**-k**$b$*)^2^,
> > *∴k*^2^*$a$*^2^*+*2*k**$a$*·*$b$**+**$b$*^2^*=*3*$a$*^2^*-*6*k**$a$*·*$b$**+*3$k^{2}$*$b$*^2^,
> > *∴*($k^{2}$*-*3)*$a$*^2^*+*8*k**$a$*·*$b$**+*(1*-*3$k^{2}$)*$b$*^2^*=*0*.*
> > *∵\|**$a$**\|=*1,*\|**$b$**\|=*1,*∴k*^2^*-*3*+*8*k**$a$*·*$b$**+*1*-*3$k^{2}$*=*0*.*
> > *∴**$a$*·*$b$**=*$\frac{2k^{2} + 2}{8k} = \frac{k^{2} + 1}{4k}$(*k\>*0)*.*
> > (2)*$a$*·*$b$**=*$\frac{k^{2} + 1}{4k} = \frac{1}{4}\left( k + \frac{1}{k} \right)$(*k\>*0),
> > 由基本不等式可得*k+*$\frac{1}{k} \geq$`<!-- -->`{=html}2,当且仅当*k=*1时等号成立,即当*k=*1时,*$a$*·*$b$*的最小值为$\frac{1}{2}.$
> > 设此时*$a$*与*$b$*的夹角为*θ*,
> > 则cos *θ=*$\frac{a\text{·}b}{\text{|}a\text{||}b\text{|}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{1 \times 1} = \frac{1}{2}$,因为*θ*∈\[0,π\],
> > 所以*θ=*$\frac{\pi}{3}.$
