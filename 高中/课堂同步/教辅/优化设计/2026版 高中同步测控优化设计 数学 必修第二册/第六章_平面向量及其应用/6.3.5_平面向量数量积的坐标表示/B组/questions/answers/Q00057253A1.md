> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)当*α=*$\frac{\pi}{4}$时,*$b$**=*$\left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$,*$a$*·*$b$**=*$\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}$,
> > 则*\|**$m$**\|=*$\sqrt{\text{(}a + tb\text{)}^{2}} = \sqrt{5 + t^{2} + 2ta\text{·}b}$*=*$\sqrt{t^{2} + 3\sqrt{2}t + 5} = \sqrt{\left( t + \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)^{2} + \frac{1}{2}}$,
> > 故当*t=-*$\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}$时,*\|**$m$**\|*取得最小值*.*
> > (2)假设存在满足条件的实数*t.*
> > 由条件得cos$\frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{\text{(}a\text{-}b\text{)·(}a + tb\text{)}}{\text{|}a\text{-}b\text{||}a + tb\text{|}}$,*∵**$a$*⊥*$b$*,
> > *∴\|**$a$**-**$b$**\|=*$\sqrt{\text{(}a\text{-}b\text{)}^{2}} = \sqrt{6}$,*\|**$a$**+t**$b$**\|=*$\sqrt{\text{(}a + tb\text{)}^{2}} = \sqrt{5 + t^{2}}$,(*$a$**-**$b$*)·(*$a$**+t**$b$*)*=*5*-t*,
> > $\therefore\frac{5\text{-}t}{\sqrt{6} \times \sqrt{5 + t^{2}}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$
> > *∴t*^2^*+*5*t-*5*=*0,且*t\<*5,解得*t=*$\frac{\text{-}5 \pm 3\sqrt{5}}{2}.$
> > *∴*存在*t=*$\frac{\text{-}5 \pm 3\sqrt{5}}{2}$满足条件*.*
