> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left( \frac{1}{2}\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设*$b$**=*($x$,$y$)*.*
> > *∵\|**$b$**\|=*$\sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2}}$*=*1,
> > *∴x*^2^*+y*^2^*=*1*.*
> > *∵**$a$*·*$b$**=*$\sqrt{3}$*x+y=*$\sqrt{3}$,
> > *∴x*^2^*+*\[$\sqrt{3}$(1*-x*)\]^2^*=*1,即2$x^{2}$*-*3*x+*1*=*0*.*
> > 得$x_{1}$*=*1,$x_{2}$*=*$\frac{1}{2}$,相应有$y_{1}$*=*0,$y_{2}$*=*$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$*.*
> > *∵*当*$b$**=*(1,0)时,与$x$轴平行,
> > *∴**$b$**=*$\left( \frac{1}{2}\text{,}\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)$*.*
