> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)*∵**$a$*∥*$b$*,*∴*3*x-*4*×*9*=*0,解得*x=*12*.*
> > *∵**$a$*⊥*$c$*,*∴*3*×*4*+*4*y=*0,解得*y=-*3*.*
> > 即*$b$**=*(9,12),*$c$**=*(4,*-*3)*.*
> > (2)设*$m$*与*$n$*的夹角为*θ.*
> > *∵**$m$**=*2*$a$**-**$b$**=*(*-*3,*-*4),*$n$**=**$a$**+**$c$**=*(7,1),
> > *∴*cos *θ=*$\frac{m\text{·}n}{\text{|}m\text{||}n\text{|}} = \frac{\text{-}3 \times 7 + \text{(-}4\text{)} \times 1}{\sqrt{\text{(-}3\text{)}^{2} + \text{(-}4\text{)}^{2}} \times \sqrt{7^{2} + 1^{2}}}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$,
> > *∵θ*∈\[0,π\],*∴θ=*$\frac{3\pi}{4}.$
