10. 已知三个点$A$(2,1),$B$(3,2),$D$(*-*1,4)*.*
(1)求证:*AB*⊥*AD*;
(2)若四边形*ABCD*为矩形,求点$C$的坐标及矩形*ABCD*两对角线所夹的锐角的余弦值*.*
(1)证明:*∵A*(2,1),$B$(3,2),$D$(*-*1,4),
$\therefore\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(1,1),$\overrightarrow{AD}$*=*(*-*3,3)*.*
又$\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD}$*=*1*×*(*-*3)*+*1*×*3*=*0,
$\therefore\overrightarrow{AB}\bot\overrightarrow{AD}$,*∴AB*⊥*AD.*
(2)解:$\because\overrightarrow{AB}\bot\overrightarrow{AD}$,四边形*ABCD*为矩形,
$\therefore\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{DC}.$
设点$C$的坐标为($x$,$y$),则$\overrightarrow{DC}$*=*(*x+*1,*y-*4)*.*
又$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*(1,1),$\therefore\left\{ \begin{matrix}
x + 1 = 1\text{,} \\
y\text{-}4 = 1\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
x = 0\text{,} \\
y = 5\text{,}
\end{matrix} \right.\$
*∴*点$C$的坐标为(0,5)*.*
$\therefore\overrightarrow{AC}$*=*(*-*2,4),$\overrightarrow{BD}$*=*(*-*4,2),
*∴\|*$\overrightarrow{AC}$*\|=*2$\sqrt{5}$,*\|*$\overrightarrow{BD}$*\|=*2$\sqrt{5},\overrightarrow{AC} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD}$*=*8*+*8*=*16*.*
设$\overrightarrow{AC}与\overrightarrow{BD}$的夹角为*θ*,
则cos *θ=*$\frac{\overrightarrow{AC}\text{·}\overrightarrow{BD}}{\text{|}\overrightarrow{AC}\text{||}\overrightarrow{BD}\text{|}} = \frac{16}{2\sqrt{5} \times 2\sqrt{5}} = \frac{4}{5}.$
故矩形*ABCD*的两条对角线所夹的锐角的余弦值为$\frac{4}{5}.$
