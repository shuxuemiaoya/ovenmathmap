> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because\overrightarrow{OA}$*=**$a$*,$\overrightarrow{OB}$*=t**$b$*,$\overrightarrow{OC} = \frac{1}{3}$(*$a$**+**$b$*),
> > $\therefore\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{OB} - \overrightarrow{OA}$*=t**$b$**-**$a$*,
> > $\overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{OC} - \overrightarrow{OA} = \frac{1}{3}$(*$a$**+**$b$*)*-**$a$**=*$\frac{1}{3}$*$b$**-*$\frac{2}{3}$*$a$**.*
> > *∵A*,$B$,$C$三点共线,
> > *∴*存在实数*λ*,使$\overrightarrow{AB}$*=*$\lambda\overrightarrow{AC}$,即$t$*$b$**-**$a$**=λ*($\frac{1}{3}$*$b$**-*$\frac{2}{3}$*$a$*)*.*
> > *∵**$a$*,*$b$*不共线,$\therefore\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > t = \frac{1}{3}\lambda\text{,} \\
> > \text{-}1 = \text{-}\frac{2}{3}\lambda\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \lambda = \frac{3}{2}\text{,} \\
> > t = \frac{1}{2}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > *∴*当*t=*$\frac{1}{2}$时,$A$,$B$,$C$三点共线*.*
