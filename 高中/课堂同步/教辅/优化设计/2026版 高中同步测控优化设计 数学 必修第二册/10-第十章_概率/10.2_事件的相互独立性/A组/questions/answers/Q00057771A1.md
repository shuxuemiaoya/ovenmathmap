> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{11}{24}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 甲解出,而乙、丙不能解出为事件$A_{1}$,则$P$($A_{1}$)*=*$\frac{1}{2} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{4}$,
> > 乙解出,而甲、丙不能解出为事件$A_{2}$,
> > 则$P$($A_{2}$)*=*$\frac{1}{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{8}$,
> > 丙解出,而甲、乙不能解出为事件$A_{3}$,
> > 则$P$($A_{3}$)*=*$\frac{1}{4} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right) = \frac{1}{12}$*.*
> > 甲、乙、丙三人独立解答此题只有一人解出的概率为$P$($A_{1}$*+A*~2~*+A*~3~)*=*$\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{8} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{11}{24}$*.*
