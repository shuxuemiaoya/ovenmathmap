11. 已知*S~n~*为数列{*a~n~*}的前$n$项和,2*S~n~+*2*n=*3*a~n~*($n$∈*$N$**^\*^*)*.*
(1)求数列{*a~n~*}的通项公式;
(2)设*b~n~=*$\frac{1 + a_{n}}{a_{n}\text{·}a_{n + 1}}$,数列{*b~n~*}的前$n$项和为*T~n~*,证明*T~n~\<*$\frac{1}{4}$*.*
(1)解:∵2*S~n~+*2*n=*3*a~n~*,
∴2*S~n+~*~1~*+*2(*n+*1)*=*3*a~n+~*~1~,两式相减得*a~n+~*~1~*=*3*a~n~+*2,
∴*a~n+~*~1~*+*1*=*3(*a~n~+*1)*.*
∵2$S_{1}$*+*2*=*3$a_{1}$,
∴$a_{1}$*=*2*.*
∴数列{*a~n~+*1}是以3为首项,3为公比的等比数列,
∴*a~n~+*1*=$3^{n}$*,
∴*a~n~=$3^{n}$-*1*.*
(2)证明:∵*b~n~=*$\frac{3^{n}}{\text{(}3^{n}\text{-}1\text{)(}3^{n + 1}\text{-}1\text{)}} = \frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{1}{3^{n}\text{-}1} - \frac{1}{3^{n + 1}\text{-}1} \right)$,
∴*T~n~=*$\frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{1}{3 - 1} - \frac{1}{3^{2} - 1} + \frac{1}{3^{2} - 1} - \frac{1}{3^{3} - 1} + \ldots + \frac{1}{3^{n} - 1} - \frac{1}{3^{n + 1} - 1} \right)$*=*$\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{3^{n + 1}\text{-}1} < \frac{1}{4}$*.*
