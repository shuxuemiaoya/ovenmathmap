> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设$g$($x$)*=*$\frac{f\text{(}x\text{)}}{\cos x}$,
> > 则*g\'*($x$)*=*$\frac{f\text{'(}x\text{)}\cos x + f\text{(}x\text{)}\sin x}{cos^{2}x}$*\<*0,
> > ∴$g$($x$)在区间$\left\lbrack 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$内单调递减*.*
> > ∴$\frac{f\text{(}ln2\text{)}}{\cos\text{(}ln2\text{)}}$*=g*(ln 2)*\<g*(ln 1)*=g*(0)*=*0*.*
> > ∴$f$(ln 2)*\<*0*.*
> > ∵0*\<*$\frac{\pi}{6} < \frac{\pi}{4} < \frac{\pi}{3} < \frac{\pi}{2}$,
> > ∴0*\>*$\frac{f\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right)}{\cos\text{ }\frac{\pi}{6}} > \frac{f\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)}{\cos\text{ }\frac{\pi}{4}} > \frac{f\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)}{\cos\text{ }\frac{\pi}{3}}$,
> > 即0*\>*$\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}f\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right) > \sqrt{2}f\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$*\>*2*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*.*
> > ∴*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right) > \sqrt{2}f\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$,*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*\<*0,*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$*\<*0*.*
> > ∴*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right) > \sqrt{3}f\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*\>*2*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$,*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right) > \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{2}}f\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right) > \frac{2}{\sqrt{2}}f\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$,即*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right) > \sqrt{2}f\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$*.*故选A*.*
