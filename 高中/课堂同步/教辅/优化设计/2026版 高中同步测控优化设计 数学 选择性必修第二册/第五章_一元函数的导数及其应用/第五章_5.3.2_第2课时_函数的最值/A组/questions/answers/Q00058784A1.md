> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{\pi}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *f\'*($x$)*=*$\frac{1}{2}$*-*sin *x.*
> > 已知$x$∈$\left\lbrack 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right\rbrack$,令*f\'*($x$)*=*0,可得*x=*$\frac{\pi}{6}$*.*
> > 当$x$∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{6} \right)$时,*f\'*($x$)*\>*0,函数$f$($x$)单调递增;
> > 当$x$∈$\left( \frac{\pi}{6}\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$时,*f\'*($x$)*\<*0,函数$f$($x$)单调递减*.*
> > 因此函数$f$($x$)在区间$\left\lbrack 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right\rbrack$上先单调递增后单调递减,则$f$($x$)的最小值在区间端点处取得*.*
> > ∵$f$(0)*=*1,*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{2} \right) = \frac{\pi}{4}$,
> > ∴$f$($x$)~min~*=f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{2} \right) = \frac{\pi}{4}$*.*
