> > \end{matrix} \right.\$

> > 3 \times 2^{2} + 6a \times 2 + 3b = 0\text{,} \\

> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = \text{-}1\text{,} \\
