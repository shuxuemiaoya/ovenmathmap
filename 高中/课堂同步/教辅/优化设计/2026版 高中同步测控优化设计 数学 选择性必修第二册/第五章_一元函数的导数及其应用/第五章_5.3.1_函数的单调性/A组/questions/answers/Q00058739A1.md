> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 构造函数$g$($x$)*=f*($x$)cos $x$,$x$∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,则*g\'*($x$)*=*cos $x$·*f\'*($x$)*-*sin $x$·$f$($x$)*.*
> > ∵sin $x$·$f$($x$)*\<*cos $x$·*f\'*($x$),$x$∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$,
> > ∴*g\'*($x$)*=*cos $x$·*f\'*($x$)*-*sin $x$·$f$($x$)*\>*0*.*
> > ∴$g$($x$)在区间$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{\pi}{2} \right)$内单调递增*.*
> > ∴*g*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*\>g*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$,即*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$cos$\frac{\pi}{3}$*\>f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$cos$\frac{\pi}{4}$,
> > 即*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right) > \sqrt{2}f\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$,故A正确*.*
> > ∴$g$(1)*\<g*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$,即$f$(1)cos 1*\<f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$cos $\frac{\pi}{3}$,即*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{3} \right)$*\>*2cos 1·$f$(1),故B正确*.*
> > ∴$g$(1)*\>g*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$,即$f$(1)cos 1*\>f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$cos$\frac{\pi}{4}$,即$\sqrt{2}$*f*(1)cos 1*\>f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$,故C正确*.*
> > ∴*g*$\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$*\<g*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$,即*f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$cos $\frac{\pi}{6}$*\<f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$cos $\frac{\pi}{4}$,
> > 即$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}f\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right) < \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}f\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$,即$\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}f\left( \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$*\<f*$\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$,故D错误*.*
> > 故选ABC*.*
