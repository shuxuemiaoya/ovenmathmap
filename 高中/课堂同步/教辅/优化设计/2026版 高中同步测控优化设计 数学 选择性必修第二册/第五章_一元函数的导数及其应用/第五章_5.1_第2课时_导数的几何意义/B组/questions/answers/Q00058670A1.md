>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设直线$l$与曲线$C$相切于点$P$($x_{0}$,$y_{0}$)*.*
> > 设函数$f$($x$)*=y=x*^3^*-x*^2^*+*1,
> > 则*f\'*($x$)*=*$\lim_{\Delta x\text{→}0}\frac{f\text{(}x + \Delta x\text{)-}f\text{(}x\text{)}}{\Delta x}$*=*$\lim_{\Delta x\text{→}0}\frac{\text{(}x + \Delta x\text{)}^{3}\text{-(}x + \Delta x\text{)}^{2} + 1\text{-(}x^{3}\text{-}x^{2} + 1\text{)}}{\Delta x}$*=*3$x^{2}$*-*2*x.*
> > 由题意知,切线的斜率*k=*1,即3$x_{0}^{2}$*-*2$x_{0}$*=*1,解得$x_{0}$*=-*$\frac{1}{3}$或$x_{0}$*=*1*.*
> > 于是切点的坐标为$\left( \text{-}\frac{1}{3}\text{,}\frac{23}{27} \right)$或(1,1)*.*
> > 当切点为$\left( \text{-}\frac{1}{3}\text{,}\frac{23}{27} \right)$时,由$\frac{23}{27}$*=-*$\frac{1}{3}$*+a*,得*a=*$\frac{32}{27}$*.*
> > 当切点为(1,1)时,由1*=*1*+a*,得*a=*0(舍去)*.*
> > 故$a$的值为$\frac{32}{27}$,切点坐标为$\left( \text{-}\frac{1}{3}\text{,}\frac{23}{27} \right)$*.*
