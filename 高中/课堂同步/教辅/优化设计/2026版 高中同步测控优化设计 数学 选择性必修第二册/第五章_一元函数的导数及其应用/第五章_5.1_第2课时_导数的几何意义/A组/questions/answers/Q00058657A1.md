> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** *y=*5*x-*1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 曲线*y=f*($x$)在点(1,$f$(1))处的切线的斜率*k=*$\lim_{\Delta x\text{→}0}\frac{3\text{(}1 + \Delta x\text{)} + \text{(}1 + \Delta x\text{)}^{2}\text{-}3\text{-}1^{2}}{\Delta x}$*=*5*.*
> > ∵$f$(1)*=*4,
> > ∴切线方程为*y-*4*=*5(*x-*1),即*y=*5*x-*1*.*
