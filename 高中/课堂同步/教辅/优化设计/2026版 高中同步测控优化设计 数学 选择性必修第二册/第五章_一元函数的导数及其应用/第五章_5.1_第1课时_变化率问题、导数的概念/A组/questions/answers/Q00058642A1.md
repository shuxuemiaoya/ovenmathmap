> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由导数的定义知,*f\'*($x_{0}$)*=*$\lim_{\Delta x\text{→}0}\frac{\text{(}x_{0} + \Delta x\text{)}^{2}\text{-}x_{0}^{2}}{\Delta x}$*=*2$x_{0}$,*g\'*($x_{0}$)*=*$\lim_{\Delta x\text{→}0}\frac{\text{(}x_{0} + \Delta x\text{)}^{3}\text{-}x_{0}^{3}}{\Delta x}$*=*3$x_{0}^{2}$*.*
> > 因为*f\'*($x_{0}$)*+*2*=g\'*($x_{0}$),所以2$x_{0}$*+*2*=*3$x_{0}^{2}$,即3$x_{0}^{2}$*-*2$x_{0}$*-*2*=*0*.*
> > 得$x_{0}$*=*$\frac{1\text{-}\sqrt{7}}{3}$或$x_{0}$*=*$\frac{1 + \sqrt{7}}{3}$*.*
