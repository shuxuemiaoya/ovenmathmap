> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设直线*y=kx+b*与曲线*y=*ln *x+*2和*y=*ln(*x+*1)的切点分别为($x_{1}$,ln $x_{1}$*+*2)和($x_{2}$,ln($x_{2}$*+*1))*.*
> > 切线的斜率分别为*y\'*$\text{|}_{x = x_{1}} = \frac{1}{x_{1}}$,*y\'*$\text{|}_{x = x_{2}} = \frac{1}{x_{2} + 1}$,
> > 则切线方程分别为*y-*ln $x_{1}$*-*2*=*$\frac{1}{x_{1}}$(*x-x*~1~),*y-*ln($x_{2}$*+*1)*=*$\frac{1}{x_{2} + 1}$(*x-x*~2~),
> > 即*y=*$\frac{x}{x_{1}}$*+*ln $x_{1}$*+*1,*y=*$\frac{x}{x_{2} + 1} - \frac{x_{2}}{x_{2} + 1}$*+*ln($x_{2}$*+*1)*.*这两条直线表示同一条直线*y=kx+b*,
> > 所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \frac{1}{x_{1}} = \frac{1}{x_{2} + 1}\text{,} \\
> > \ln\text{ }x_{1} + 1 = ln\text{(}x_{2} + 1\text{)-}\frac{x_{2}}{x_{2} + 1}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > x_{1} = \frac{1}{2}\text{,} \\
> > x_{2} = \text{-}\frac{1}{2}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 将$x_{1}$*=*$\frac{1}{2}$代入切线方程*y=*$\frac{x}{x_{1}}$*+*ln $x_{1}$*+*1,得*y=*2*x+*1*-*ln 2,则*b=*1*-*ln 2*.*
