> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)设等差数列{*a~n~*}的公差为$d$,
> > 则$S_{3}$*=*3×1*+*$\frac{3 \times 2}{2}$*d=*12,解得*d=*3*.*
> > 故$a_{24}$*=*1*+*23×3*=*70,$S_{7}$*=*7×1*+*$\frac{7 \times 6}{2}$×3*=*70*.*
> > (2)由(1)知,*a~m~=*1*+*(*m-*1)×3*=*3*m-*2,
> > *S~n~=n*×1*+*$\frac{n\text{(}n\text{-}1\text{)}}{2}$×3*=*$\frac{3n^{2}\text{-}n}{2}$*.*
> > ∵*a~m~=S~n~*,∴3*m-*2*=*$\frac{3n^{2}\text{-}n}{2}$,∴*m=*$\frac{3n^{2}\text{-}n + 4}{6} = \frac{3n^{2} + 3n\text{-}4n + 4}{6} = \frac{n\text{(}n + 1\text{)}}{2} - \frac{2\text{(}n\text{-}1\text{)}}{3}$*.*
> > ∵$m$,$n$均为正整数,又$\frac{n\text{(}n + 1\text{)}}{2}$为正整数,
> > ∴只需$\frac{2\text{(}n\text{-}1\text{)}}{3}$为整数,且$\frac{n\text{(}n + 1\text{)}}{2} > \frac{2\text{(}n\text{-}1\text{)}}{3}$即可,
> > 即*n-*1*=*3*k*($k$∈*$N$*),∴*n=*3*k+*1,$k$∈*$N$*,
> > ∴所有$n$的值构成的集合为{*n\|n=*3*k+*1,$k$∈*$N$*}*.*
