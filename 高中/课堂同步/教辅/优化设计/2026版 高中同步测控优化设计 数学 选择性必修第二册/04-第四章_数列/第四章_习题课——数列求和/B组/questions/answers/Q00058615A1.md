> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)设等差数列{*a~n~*}的公差为*d.*
> > 因为$a_{3}$*=*7,$a_{5}$*+a*~7~*=*26,所以$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1} + 2d = 7\text{,} \\
> > 2a_{1} + 10d = 26\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1} = 3\text{,} \\
> > d = 2\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 所以*a~n~=*3*+*2(*n-*1)*=*2*n+*1,*S~n~=*3*n+*$\frac{n\text{(}n\text{-}1\text{)}}{2}$×2*=n*^2^*+*2*n*,
> > 即*a~n~=*2*n+*1,*S~n~=n*^2^*+*2*n.*
> > (2)由(1)知*a~n~=*2*n+*1,
> > 所以*b~n~=*$\frac{1}{a_{n}^{2}\text{-}1} = \frac{1}{\text{(}2n + 1\text{)}^{2}\text{-}1} = \frac{1}{4} \cdot \frac{1}{n\text{(}n + 1\text{)}} = \frac{1}{4}\left( \frac{1}{n}\text{-}\frac{1}{n + 1} \right)$,
> > 所以*T~n~=*$\frac{1}{4}\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\text{-}\frac{1}{3} + \text{…} + \frac{1}{n}\text{-}\frac{1}{n + 1} \right) = \frac{1}{4}\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{n + 1} \right) = \frac{n}{4\text{(}n + 1\text{)}}$,
> > 即数列{*b~n~*}的前$n$项和*T~n~=*$\frac{n}{4\text{(}n + 1\text{)}}$*.*
