> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)^{8}\left( 2\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 用{*a~n~*}表示每次取出的纯酒精,$a_{1}$*=*1,加水后浓度为$\frac{a\text{-}1}{a}$*=*1*-*$\frac{1}{a}$,$a_{2}$*=*1*-*$\frac{1}{a}$,加水后浓度为$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)\left( \frac{a\text{-}1}{a} \right) = \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)^{2}$,$a_{3}$*=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)^{2}$,
> > 依次类推,$a_{9}$*=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)^{8}$,$a_{10}$*=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)^{9}$*.*
> > 故$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)^{8} + \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)^{9} = \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)^{8}\left( 2\text{-}\frac{1}{a} \right)$*.*
