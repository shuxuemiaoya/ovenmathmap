> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当*n=k*($k$≥2,$k$∈*$N$**^\*^*)时,左边*=*$\frac{1}{k + 1} + \frac{1}{k + 2}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k}}$,
> > 当*n=k+*1时,左边*=*$\frac{1}{k + 2} + \frac{1}{k + 3}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k}} + \frac{1}{2^{k} + 1}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k + 1}}$,
> > 故由*n=k*到*n=k+*1时不等式左边的变化是增加了$\frac{1}{2^{k} + 1}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k + 1}} - \frac{1}{k + 1}$*.*
