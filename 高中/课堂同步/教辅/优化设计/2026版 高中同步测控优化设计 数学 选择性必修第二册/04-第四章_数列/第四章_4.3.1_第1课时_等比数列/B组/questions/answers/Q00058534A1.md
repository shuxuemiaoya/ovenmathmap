> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** (*-*2)^*n-*1^
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当*n=*1时,$S_{1}$*=*$\frac{2}{3}$$a_{1}$*+*$\frac{1}{3}$,解得$a_{1}$*=*1*.*
> > 当$n$≥2时,*a~n~=S~n~-S~n-~*~1~*=*$\frac{2}{3}$*a~n~+*$\frac{1}{3}$*-*$\left( \frac{2}{3}a_{n - 1} + \frac{1}{3} \right)$*=*$\frac{2}{3}$(*a~n~-a~n-~*~1~),∴*a~n~=-*2*a~n-~*~1~,即$\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n\text{-}1}}$*=-*2,
> > ∴{*a~n~*}是以1为首项的等比数列,其公比为*-*2,
> > ∴*a~n~=*1×(*-*2)^*n-*1^,即*a~n~=*(*-*2)^*n-*1^*.*
