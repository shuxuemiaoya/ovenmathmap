> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设{*a~n~*}的公比为$q$,由已知可得$q$≠1,则奇数项也构成等比数列,其公比为$q^{2}$,首项为$a_{1}$,
> > $S$~2*n*~*=*$\frac{a_{1}\text{(}1\text{-}q^{2n}\text{)}}{1\text{-}q}$,$S$~奇~*=*$\frac{a_{1}\text{[}1\text{-(}q^{2}\text{)}^{n}\text{]}}{1\text{-}q^{2}}$*.*
> > 由题意得$\frac{a_{1}\text{(}1\text{-}q^{2n}\text{)}}{1\text{-}q} = \frac{3a_{1}\text{(}1\text{-}q^{2n}\text{)}}{1\text{-}q^{2}}$,即1*+q=*3,*q=*2*.*
