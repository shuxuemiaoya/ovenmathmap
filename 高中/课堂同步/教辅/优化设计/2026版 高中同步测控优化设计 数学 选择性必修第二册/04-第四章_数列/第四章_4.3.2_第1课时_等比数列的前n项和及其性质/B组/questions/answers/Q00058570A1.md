> > (1)设等差数列{*a~n~*}的公差为$d$,
> > ∵等差数列{*a~n~*}满足$a_{5}$*=*13,$a_{1}$*+a*~3~*=*8,
> > ∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1} + 4d = 13\text{,} \\
> > 2a_{1} + 2d = 8\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得$a_{1}$*=*1,*d=*3,
> > ∴*a~n~=*1*+*3(*n-*1)*=*3*n-*2*.*

> > ∵在等比数列{*b~n~*}中,$b_{1}$*=a*~1~*=*1,$b_{3}$*=a*~4~*-*1*=*9,
> > ∴$q^{2}$*=*$\frac{b_{3}}{b_{1}}$*=*9,解得*q=*±3*.*
