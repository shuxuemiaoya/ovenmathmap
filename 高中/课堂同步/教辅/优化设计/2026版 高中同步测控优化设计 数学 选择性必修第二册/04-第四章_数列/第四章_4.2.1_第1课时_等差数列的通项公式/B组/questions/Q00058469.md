∴$\frac{2}{c + a} = \frac{1}{b + c} + \frac{1}{a + b}$*.*
∴2(*b+c*)(*a+b*)*=*(*a+c*)(*a+c+*2*b*),

9. 已知数列{*a~n~*}满足$a_{1}$*=*4,*a~n~=*4*-*$\frac{4}{a_{n\text{-}1}}$($n$≥2),令*b~n~=*$\frac{1}{a_{n}\text{-}2}$*.*
(1)求证:数列{*b~n~*}是等差数列;
(2)求数列{*a~n~*}的通项公式*.*
(1)证明:∵*a~n~=*4*-*$\frac{4}{a_{n\text{-}1}}$($n$≥2),
∴*a~n+~*~1~*-*2*=*2*-*$\frac{4}{a_{n}} = \frac{2\text{(}a_{n}\text{-}2\text{)}}{a_{n}}$($n$≥1)*.*
∴$\frac{1}{a_{n + 1}\text{-}2} = \frac{a_{n}}{2\text{(}a_{n}\text{-}2\text{)}} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{a_{n}\text{-}2}$($n$≥1),即*b~n+~*~1~*-b~n~=*$\frac{1}{2}$($n$≥1),
∴{*b~n~*}为等差数列*.*
(2)解:∵$\left\{ \frac{1}{a_{n}\text{-}2} \right\}$为等差数列,
∴$\frac{1}{a_{n}\text{-}2} = \frac{1}{a_{1}\text{-}2}$*+*(*n-*1)×$\frac{1}{2} = \frac{n}{2}$*.*∴*a~n~=*2*+*$\frac{2}{n}$*.*
∴数列{*a~n~*}的通项公式为*a~n~=*2*+*$\frac{2}{n}$*.*
