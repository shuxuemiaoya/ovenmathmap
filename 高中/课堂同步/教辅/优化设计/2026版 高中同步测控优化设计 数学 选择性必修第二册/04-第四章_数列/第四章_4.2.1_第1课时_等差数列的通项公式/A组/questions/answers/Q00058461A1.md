> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)数列$\left\{ \frac{1}{a_{n}} \right\}$是等差数列*.*理由如下:
> > ∵$a_{1}$*=*2,*a~n+~*~1~*=*$\frac{2a_{n}}{a_{n} + 2}$,
> > ∴$\frac{1}{a_{n + 1}} = \frac{a_{n} + 2}{2a_{n}} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{a_{n}}$,∴$\frac{1}{a_{n + 1}} - \frac{1}{a_{n}} = \frac{1}{2}$,
> > 即$\left\{ \frac{1}{a_{n}} \right\}$是首项为$\frac{1}{a_{1}} = \frac{1}{2}$,公差*d=*$\frac{1}{2}$的等差数列*.*
> > (2)由(1)可知$\frac{1}{a_{n}} = \frac{1}{a_{1}}$*+*(*n-*1)*d=*$\frac{n}{2}$,故*a~n~=*$\frac{2}{n}$*.*
