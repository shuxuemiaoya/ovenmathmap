> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设该数列的公比为*q.*
> > 由已知,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1}q\text{-}a_{1} = 2\text{,} \\
> > 4a_{1}q = 3a_{1} + a_{1}q^{2}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 化简得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1}\text{(}q\text{-}1\text{)} = 2\text{,} \\
> > q^{2}\text{-}4q + 3 = 0\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1} = 1\text{,} \\
> > q = 3
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$(*q=*1舍去)*.*
> > 故首项$a_{1}$*=*1,公比*q=*3*.*
