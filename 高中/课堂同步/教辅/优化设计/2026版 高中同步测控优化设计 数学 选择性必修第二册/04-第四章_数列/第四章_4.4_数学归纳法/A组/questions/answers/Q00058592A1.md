> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 猜想:1*+*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{n}\text{-}1} > \frac{n}{2}$($n$∈*$N$**^\*^*)*.*
> > 证明:(1)当*n=*1时,不等式成立;
> > (2)假设当*n=k*($k$∈*$N$**^\*^*)时,不等式成立,即1*+*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k}\text{-}1} > \frac{k}{2}$,
> > 则当*n=k+*1时,左边*=*1*+*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k}\text{-}1} + \frac{1}{2^{k}}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k + 1}\text{-}1} > \frac{k}{2} + \frac{1}{2^{k}}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k + 1}\text{-}1}$,
> > 其中$\frac{1}{2^{k}} + \frac{1}{2^{k} + 1}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k + 1}\text{-}1}$共有2*^k^*项,$\frac{1}{2^{k}} + \frac{1}{2^{k} + 1}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k + 1}\text{-}1} > \frac{2^{k}}{2^{k + 1}\text{-}1} > \frac{2^{k}}{2^{k + 1}} = \frac{1}{2}$,
> > 所以1*+*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k}\text{-}1} + \frac{1}{2^{k}}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k + 1}\text{-}1} > \frac{k}{2} + \frac{1}{2^{k}}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{k + 1}\text{-}1} > \frac{k + 1}{2}$,
> > 即当*n=k+*1时,不等式成立,由(1)(2)可知,对任意$n$∈*$N$**^\*^*,不等式成立*.*
