> > [!success] **【答案】** 2*　*3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{n} = a_{1} + \text{(}n\text{-}1\text{)} \times \frac{1}{2} = 3\text{,} \\
> > S_{n} = na_{1} + \frac{n\text{(}n\text{-}1\text{)}}{4} = \frac{15}{2}\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得$n^{2}$*-*13*n+*30*=*0,解得*n=*3或*n=*10*.*
> > 当*n=*3时,$a_{1}$*=*2*\>*0;
> > 当*n=*10时,$a_{1}$*=-*$\frac{3}{2}$*\<*0,不合题意,舍去,
> > 故$a_{1}$*=*2,*n=*3*.*
