> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)当*x\>c*时,*p=*$\frac{2}{3}$,*y=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{2}{3} \right)$·$x$·3*-*$\frac{2}{3}$·$x$·$\frac{3}{2}$*=*0;
> > 当0*\<x*≤$c$时,*p=*$\frac{1}{6\text{-}x}$,*y=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{6\text{-}x} \right)$·$x$·3*-*$\frac{1}{6\text{-}x}$·$x$·$\frac{3}{2} = \frac{3x\text{(}9\text{-}2x\text{)}}{2\text{(}6\text{-}x\text{)}}$*.*
> > ∴日盈利额$y$(单位:万元)与日产量$x$(单位:万件)的函数关系为*y=f*($x$)*=*$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \frac{3x\text{(}9\text{-}2x\text{)}}{2\text{(}6\text{-}x\text{)}}\text{,}0 < x \leq c\text{,} \\
> > 0\text{,}x > c
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$($c$为常数,且0*\<c\<*6)*.*
> > (2)由(1)知,当*x\>c*时,日盈利额为0*.*
> > 当0*\<x*≤$c$时,∵*y=*$\frac{3x\text{(}9\text{-}2x\text{)}}{2\text{(}6\text{-}x\text{)}}$,
> > ∴*y\'=*$\frac{3}{2} \cdot \frac{\text{(}9\text{-}4x\text{)(}6\text{-}x\text{)} + 9x\text{-}2x^{2}}{\text{(}6\text{-}x\text{)}^{2}} = \frac{3\text{(}x\text{-}3\text{)(}x\text{-}9\text{)}}{\text{(}6\text{-}x\text{)}^{2}}$*.*
> > 令*y\'=*0,得*x=*3或*x=*9(舍去)*.*
> > 若0*\<c\<*3,∵*y\'\>*0,∴函数在区间(0,*c*\]内单调递增,∴当*x=c*时,$y$~最大值~*=*$\frac{3c\text{(}9\text{-}2c\text{)}}{2\text{(}6\text{-}c\text{)}}$*.*
> > 若3≤*c\<*6,∵当$x$∈(0,3)时,*y\'\>*0,当$x$∈(3,$c$)时,*y\'\<*0,
> > ∴函数在区间(0,3)内单调递增,在区间(3,$c$)内单调递减*.*
> > ∴当*x=*3时,$y$~最大值~*=*$\frac{9}{2}$*.*
> > 综上,若0*\<c\<*3,则当日产量为$c$万件时,日盈利额最大;若3≤*c\<*6,则当日产量为3万件时,日盈利额最大*.*
