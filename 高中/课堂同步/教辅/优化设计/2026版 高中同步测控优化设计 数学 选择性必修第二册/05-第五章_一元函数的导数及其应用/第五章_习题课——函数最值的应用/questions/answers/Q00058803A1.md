> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 当$x$∈(0,1\]时,不等式$ax^{3}$*-*3*x+*1≥0恒成立,即$a$≥$\frac{3x\text{-}1}{x^{3}}$*.*
> > 设$g$($x$)*=*$\frac{3x\text{-}1}{x^{3}}$,$x$∈(0,1\],
> > 则*g\'*($x$)*=*$\frac{3x^{3}\text{-(}3x\text{-}1\text{)·}3x^{2}}{x^{6}}$*=-*$\frac{6\left( x\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right)}{x^{4}}$*.*
> > 令*g\'*($x$)*=*0,解得*x=*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 当$x$∈$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$时,*g\'*($x$)*\>*0,函数$g$($x$)在区间$\left( 0\text{,}\frac{1}{2} \right)$内单调递增;
> > 当$x$∈$\left( \frac{1}{2}\text{,}1 \right)$时,*g\'*($x$)*\<*0,函数$g$($x$)在区间$\left( \frac{1}{2}\text{,}1 \right\rbrack$内单调递减*.*
> > 所以,当*x=*$\frac{1}{2}$时,函数$g$($x$)取得最大值$g$($x$)~max~*=g*$\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)$*=*4*.*
> > 所以实数$a$的取值范围是\[4,*+∞*)*.*
