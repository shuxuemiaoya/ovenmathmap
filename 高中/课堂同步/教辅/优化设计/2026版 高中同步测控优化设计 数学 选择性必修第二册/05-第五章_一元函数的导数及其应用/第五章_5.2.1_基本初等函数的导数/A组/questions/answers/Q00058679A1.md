> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知,平行于直线*x+*2*y-*4*=*0的直线与曲线*y=*$\frac{1}{x}$(*x\<*0)相切的切点即为所求*.*
> > 设切点$P$($x_{0}$,$y_{0}$),由*y\'=-*$\frac{1}{x^{2}}$,得*y\'*$\text{|}_{x = x_{0}}$*=-*$\frac{1}{x_{0}^{2}}$*.*
> > ∵直线*x+*2*y-*4*=*0的斜率为*-*$\frac{1}{2}$,
> > ∴*-*$\frac{1}{x_{0}^{2}}$*=-*$\frac{1}{2}$,解得$x_{0}$*=*$\sqrt{2}$或$x_{0}$*=-*$\sqrt{2}$*.*
> > ∵*x\<*0,∴$x_{0}$*=-*$\sqrt{2}$,从而$y_{0}$*=-*$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$*=-*$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$*.*
> > ∴点$P$的坐标为$\left( \text{-}\sqrt{2}\text{,-}\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$*.*
