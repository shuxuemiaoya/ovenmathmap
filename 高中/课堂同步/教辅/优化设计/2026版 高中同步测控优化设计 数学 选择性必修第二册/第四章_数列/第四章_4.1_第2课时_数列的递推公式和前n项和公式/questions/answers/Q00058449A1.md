> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)∵*a~n~=a~n-~*~1~*+a~n-~*~2~($n$≥3),且$a_{1}$*=*1,$a_{2}$*=*2,
> > ∴$a_{3}$*=a*~2~*+a*~1~*=*3,$a_{4}$*=a*~3~*+a*~2~*=*3*+*2*=*5,

> > $a_{1}$*=*1,$a_{2}$*=*2,$a_{3}$*=*3,$a_{4}$*=*5,$a_{5}$*=*8*.*
> > (2)∵*b~n~=*$\frac{a_{n}}{a_{n + 1}}$,且$a_{1}$*=*1,$a_{2}$*=*2,$a_{3}$*=*3,$a_{4}$*=*5,$a_{5}$*=*8,

> > 故$b_{1}$*=*$\frac{1}{2}$,$b_{2}$*=*$\frac{2}{3}$,$b_{3}$*=*$\frac{3}{5}$,$b_{4}$*=*$\frac{5}{8}$*.*
