> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > *S~n~*是数列{*a~n~*}的前$n$项和,且$a_{1}$*=-*1,*a~n+~*~1~*=S~n~S~n+~*~1~,则*S~n+~*~1~*-S~n~=S~n~S~n+~*~1~,
> > 整理得$\frac{1}{S_{n + 1}} - \frac{1}{S_{n}}$*=-*1(常数),所以数列$\left\{ \frac{1}{S_{n}} \right\}$是以$\frac{1}{S_{1}}$*=-*1为首项,*-*1为公差的等差数列*.*
> > 故C正确;
> > 所以$\frac{1}{S_{n}}$*=-*1*-*(*n-*1)*=-n*,故*S~n~=-*$\frac{1}{n}$*.*
> > 所以当$n$≥2时,*a~n~=S~n~-S~n-~*~1~*=*$\frac{1}{n\text{-}1} - \frac{1}{n}$(首项不符合通项),
> > 故*a~n~=*$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \text{-}1\text{,}n = 1\text{,} \\
> > \frac{1}{n\text{-}1}\text{-}\frac{1}{n}\text{,}n \geq 2\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$故B正确,A不正确;
> > 所以$\frac{1}{S_{1}} + \frac{1}{S_{2}}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{S_{100}}$*=-*(1*+*2*+*3*+*...*+*100)*=-*5 050,故D正确*.*
