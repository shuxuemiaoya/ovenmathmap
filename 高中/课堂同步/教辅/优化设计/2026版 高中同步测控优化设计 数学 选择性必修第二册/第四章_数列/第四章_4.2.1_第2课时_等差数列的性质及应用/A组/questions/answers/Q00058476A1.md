> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{4}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由$x$,$a_{1}$,$a_{2}$,$y$成等差数列,可知$a_{2}$*-a*~1~*=*$\frac{y\text{-}x}{3}$*.*由$x$,$b_{1}$,$b_{2}$,$b_{3}$,$y$成等差数列,可知$b_{2}$*-b*~1~*=*$\frac{y\text{-}x}{4}$*.*
> > 故$\frac{a_{1}\text{-}a_{2}}{b_{1}\text{-}b_{2}} = \frac{a_{2}\text{-}a_{1}}{b_{2}\text{-}b_{1}} = \frac{4}{3}$*.*
