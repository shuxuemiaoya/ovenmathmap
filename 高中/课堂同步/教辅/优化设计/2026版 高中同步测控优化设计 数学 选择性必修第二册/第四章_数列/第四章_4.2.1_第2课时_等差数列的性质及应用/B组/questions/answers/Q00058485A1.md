> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 法一:设这四个数分别为$a$,$b$,$c$,$d$,根据题意,得
> > $\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > b\text{-}a = c\text{-}b = d\text{-}c\text{,} \\
> > a + b + c + d = 26\text{,} \\
> > bc = 40\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 2\text{,} \\
> > b = 5\text{,} \\
> > c = 8\text{,} \\
> > d = 11
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 或\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 11\text{,} \\
> > b = 8\text{,} \\
> > c = 5\text{,} \\
> > d = 2\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故这四个数分别为2,5,8,11或11,8,5,2*.*
> > 法二:设此等差数列的首项为$a_{1}$,公差为$d$,根据题意,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1} + \text{(}a_{1} + d\text{)} + \text{(}a_{1} + 2d\text{)} + \text{(}a_{1} + 3d\text{)} = 26\text{,} \\
> > \text{(}a_{1} + d\text{)(}a_{1} + 2d\text{)} = 40\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 化简,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 4a_{1} + 6d = 26\text{,} \\
> > a_{1}^{2} + 3a_{1}d + 2d^{2} = 40\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1} = 2\text{,} \\
> > d = 3
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 或\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1} = 11\text{,} \\
> > d = \text{-}3\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故这四个数分别为2,5,8,11或11,8,5,2*.*
> > 法三:设这四个数分别为*a-*3*d*,*a-d*,*a+d*,*a+*3*d*,根据题意,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > \text{(}a\text{-}3d\text{)} + \text{(}a\text{-}d\text{)} + \text{(}a + d\text{)} + \text{(}a + 3d\text{)} = 26\text{,} \\
> > \text{(}a\text{-}d\text{)(}a + d\text{)} = 40\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 化简,得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > 4a = 26\text{,} \\
> > a^{2}\text{-}d^{2} = 40\text{,}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$解得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = \frac{13}{2}\text{,} \\
> > d = \pm \frac{3}{2}\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 故这四个数分别为2,5,8,11或11,8,5,2*.*
