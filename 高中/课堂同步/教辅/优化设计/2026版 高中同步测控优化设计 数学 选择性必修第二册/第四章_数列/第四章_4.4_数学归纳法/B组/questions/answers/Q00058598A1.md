> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** *-*$\frac{4}{5}$*　-*$\frac{n + 1}{n + 2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由于$a_{1}$*=-*$\frac{2}{3}$,满足*S~n~+*$\frac{1}{S_{n}}$*+*2*=a~n~*($n$≥2),令*n=*2,则$S_{2}$*+*$\frac{1}{S_{2}}$*+*2*=a*~2~,化为$a_{1}$*+*$\frac{1}{S_{2}}$*+*2*=*0,
> > 故*-*$\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{S_{2}}$*+*2*=*0,解得$S_{2}$*=-*$\frac{3}{4}$*.*
> > 令*n=*3,$S_{3}$*+*$\frac{1}{S_{3}}$*+*2*=a*~3~,化为$S_{2}$*+*$\frac{1}{S_{3}}$*+*2*=*0,即*-*$\frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{S_{3}}$*+*2*=*0*.*
> > 得$S_{3}$*=-*$\frac{4}{5}$*.*
> > 猜想:*S~n~=-*$\frac{n + 1}{n + 2}$*.*
> > 下面用数学归纳法证明:
> > (1)当*n=*1时,$S_{1}$*=-*$\frac{2}{3}$,结论成立;
> > (2)假设当*n=k*($k$∈*$N$**^\*^*)时结论成立,即*S~k~=-*$\frac{k + 1}{k + 2}$,
> > 当*n=k+*1时,*S~k+~*~1~*+*$\frac{1}{S_{k + 1}}$*+*2*=a~k+~*~1~*=S~k+~*~1~*-S~k~*,*S~k+~*~1~*=-*$\frac{1}{S_{k} + 2}$*=-*$\frac{k + 2}{k + 3}$*=-*$\frac{\text{(}k + 1\text{)} + 1}{\text{(}k + 1\text{)} + 2}$*.*
> > 即当*n=k+*1时,结论也成立*.*
> > 由(1)(2)可得,对任意的正整数$n$,结论都成立*.*
