> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}$*　*3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设等比数列{*a~n~*}的公比为$q$,
> > 由题意可知,*q\>*0且$q$≠1*.*
> > 由已知得$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a_{1}\text{-}a_{1}q^{4} = 90\text{,}\text{①} \\
> > a_{1}q\text{-}a_{1}q^{3} = 36\text{,}\text{②}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
> > 又*a~n~\>*0,所以由$\frac{\text{①}}{\text{②}}$可得$\frac{1 + q^{2}}{q} = \frac{5}{2}$,
> > 即2$q^{2}$*-*5*q+*2*=*0,解得*q=*2或*q=*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 若*q=*2,则$a_{1}$*=-*6,不合题意,舍去,
> > 若*q=*$\frac{1}{2}$,则$a_{1}$*=*96,故$a_{5}$*=*96×$\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{4}$*=*6,$a_{7}$*=a*~5~$q^{2}$*=*$\frac{3}{2}$,$a_{5}$与$a_{7}$的等比中项为$\sqrt{a_{5}a_{7}}$*=*3*.*
