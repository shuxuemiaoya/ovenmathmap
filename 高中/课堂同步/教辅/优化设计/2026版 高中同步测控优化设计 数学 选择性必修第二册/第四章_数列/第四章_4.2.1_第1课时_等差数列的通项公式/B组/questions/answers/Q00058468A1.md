> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵*a\>*0,*b\>*0,lg $a$与lg $b$的等差中项为0,
> > ∴lg *a+*lg *b=*2×0,解得*ab=*1*.*
> > 则$\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}$≥2$\sqrt{\frac{1}{ab}}$*=*2,当且仅当$\frac{1}{a} = \frac{1}{b}$时取等号*.*
> > ∴$\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}$的最小值是2*.*
