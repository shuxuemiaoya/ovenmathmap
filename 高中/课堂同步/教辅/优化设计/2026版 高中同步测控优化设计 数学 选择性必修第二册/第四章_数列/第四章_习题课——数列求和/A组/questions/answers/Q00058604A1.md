> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** 2*-*$\frac{1}{2^{n\text{-}1}} - \frac{n}{2^{n}}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 令*S~n~=*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{2}{2^{2}} + \frac{3}{2^{3}}$*+*...*+*$\frac{n}{2^{n}}$,①
> > 则$\frac{1}{2}$*S~n~=*$\frac{1}{2^{2}} + \frac{2}{2^{3}}$*+*...*+*$\frac{n\text{-}1}{2^{n}} + \frac{n}{2^{n + 1}}$*.*②
> > ①*-*②,得$\frac{1}{2}$*S~n~=*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2^{2}} + \frac{1}{2^{3}}$*+*...*+*$\frac{1}{2^{n}} - \frac{n}{2^{n + 1}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}\left\lbrack 1\text{-}\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{n} \right\rbrack}{1\text{-}\frac{1}{2}} - \frac{n}{2^{n + 1}}$*=*1*-*$\frac{1}{2^{n}} - \frac{n}{2^{n + 1}}$,故*S~n~=*2*-*$\frac{1}{2^{n\text{-}1}} - \frac{n}{2^{n}}$*.*
