> > 则$f$(*n+*1)*-f*($n$)*=*$\frac{1}{2n + 1} + \frac{1}{2n + 2} - \frac{1}{n + 1} = \frac{1}{2n + 1} - \frac{1}{2n + 2}$*\>*0,
