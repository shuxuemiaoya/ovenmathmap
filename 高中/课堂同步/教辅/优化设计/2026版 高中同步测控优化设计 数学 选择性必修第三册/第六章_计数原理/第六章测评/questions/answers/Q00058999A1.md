> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{21}{16}　\frac{21}{2}$$x^{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意,($x^{2}$*+*$\frac{1}{2x}$)^9^的展开式的通项为*T~r+~*~1~*=*$C_{9}^{r}$·($x^{2}$)^9*-r*^·$\left( \frac{1}{2x} \right)^{r} = C_{9}^{r}\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{r}$·$x$^18*-*3*r*^*.*
> > 故当18*-*3*r=*0,即*r=*6时,取得常数项为$C_{9}^{6} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{6} = \frac{21}{16}$*.*
> > 又$C_{9}^{0} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{0}$*=*1,$C_{9}^{1} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{1} = \frac{9}{2},C_{9}^{2} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2}$*=*9,$C_{9}^{3} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{3} = \frac{21}{2},C_{9}^{4} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{4} = \frac{63}{8} > C_{9}^{5} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{5} = \frac{63}{16}$,
> > 后面由于$\frac{1}{2}$的指数越来越大,故数值越来越小*.*
> > 所以系数最大的是$C_{9}^{3}\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{3} = \frac{21}{2}$,
> > 故系数最大的项是$C_{9}^{3}\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{3}$$x^{9}$*=*$\frac{21}{2}$$x^{9}$*.*
