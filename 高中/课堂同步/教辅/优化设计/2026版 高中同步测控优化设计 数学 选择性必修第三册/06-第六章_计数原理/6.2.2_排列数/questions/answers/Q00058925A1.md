> > [!success] **【答案】** *x=*5
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为$A_{x}^{5} + A_{x}^{4}$*=*4$A_{x}^{3}$,所以$\frac{x\text{!}}{\text{(}x\text{-}5\text{)!}} + \frac{x\text{!}}{\text{(}x\text{-}4\text{)!}}$*=*4·$\frac{x\text{!}}{\text{(}x\text{-}3\text{)!}}$,所以1*+*$\frac{1}{x\text{-}4} = \frac{4}{\text{(}x\text{-}3\text{)(}x\text{-}4\text{)}}$,即$x^{2}$*-*6*x+*5*=*0,解得*x=*5或*x=*1(舍去)*.*
