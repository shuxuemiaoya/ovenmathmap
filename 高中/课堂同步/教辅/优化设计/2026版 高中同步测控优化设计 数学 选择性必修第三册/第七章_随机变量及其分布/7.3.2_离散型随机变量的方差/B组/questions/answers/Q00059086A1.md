> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得,*a+c=*2*b*,*a+b+c=*1,解得*b=*$\frac{1}{3}$*.*
> > $E$($Y$)*=na+*(*n+*1)*b+*(*n+*2)*c=n*(*a+b+c*)*+b+*2*c=n+b+*2*c*,$E$($Y^{2}$)*=n*^2^*a+*(*n+*1)^2^*b+*(*n+*2)^2^$c$,
> > 则$D$($Y$)*=E*($Y^{2}$)*-*($E$($Y$))^2^*=n*^2^*a+*(*n+*1)^2^*b+*(*n+*2)^2^*c-*(*n+b+*2*c*)^2^*=-*4$c^{2}$*+*$\frac{8}{3}$*c+*$\frac{2}{9} = {\text{-}4\left( c\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right)}^{2} + \frac{2}{3}\left( 0 \leq c \leq \frac{2}{3} \right)$,所以$D$($Y$)与$n$无关,且当*c=*$\frac{1}{3}$时,$D$($Y$)有最大值$\frac{2}{3}$*.*
