> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)$X$可能的取值为0,1,2,3,4,且$X$服从参数为*N=*8,*M=*4,*n=*4的超几何分布*.*
> > $P$(*X=*0)*=*$\frac{C_{4}^{0}C_{4}^{4}}{C_{8}^{4}} = \frac{1}{70}$,
> > $P$(*X=*1)*=*$\frac{C_{4}^{1}C_{4}^{3}}{C_{8}^{4}} = \frac{8}{35}$,
> > $P$(*X=*2)*=*$\frac{C_{4}^{2}C_{4}^{2}}{C_{8}^{4}} = \frac{18}{35}$,
> > $P$(*X=*3)*=*$\frac{C_{4}^{3}C_{4}^{1}}{C_{8}^{4}} = \frac{8}{35}$,
> > $P$(*X=*4)*=*$\frac{C_{4}^{4}C_{4}^{0}}{C_{8}^{4}} = \frac{1}{70}$*.*
> > 故$X$的分布列为
> > | $X$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
> > |:-----:|:--------:|:--------:|:--------:|:--------:|:--------:|
> > | $P$ | $$\frac{1}{70}$$ | $$\frac{8}{35}$$ | $$\frac{18}{35}$$ | $$\frac{8}{35}$$ | $$\frac{1}{70}$$ |
> > $X$的数学期望为$E$($X$)*=*0×$\frac{1}{70}$*+*1×$\frac{8}{35}$*+*2×$\frac{18}{35}$*+*3×$\frac{8}{35}$*+*4×$\frac{1}{70}$*=*2*.*

> > 品种乙的每公顷产量的样本平均数和样本方差分别为
> > ${\overline{x}}_{\text{乙}} = \frac{1}{8}$×(419*+*403*+*412*+*418*+*408*+*423*+*400*+*413)*=*412,
