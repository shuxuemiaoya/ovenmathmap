> > 设事件*A=*"取得蓝色玻璃球",*B=*"取得E型玻璃球"*.*
> > (方法1)因为$P$($A$)*=*$\frac{11}{16}$,$P$(*AB*)*=*$\frac{4}{16} = \frac{1}{4}$,
> > 所以$P$(*B\|A*)*=*$\frac{P\text{(}AB\text{)}}{P\text{(}A\text{)}} = \frac{\frac{1}{4}}{\frac{11}{16}} = \frac{4}{11}$*.*
> > (方法2)因为$n$($A$)*=*11,$n$(*AB*)*=*4,
> > 所以$P$(*B\|A*)*=*$\frac{n\text{(}AB\text{)}}{n\text{(}A\text{)}} = \frac{4}{11}$*.*
