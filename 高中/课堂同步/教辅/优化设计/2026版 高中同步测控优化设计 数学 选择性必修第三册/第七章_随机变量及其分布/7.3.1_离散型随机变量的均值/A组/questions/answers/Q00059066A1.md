> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设"甲解出该题"为事件$A$,"乙解出该题"为事件*B.*由题意知,$X$的可能取值为0,1,2*.*
> > $P$(*X=*0)*=P*($\overline{A}$)$P$($\overline{B}$)*=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{2}{3} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{4}{5} \right) = \frac{1}{15}$,
> > $P$(*X=*1)*=P*(*A*$\overline{B}$)*+P*($\overline{A}$*B*)*=P*($A$)$P$($\overline{B}$)*+P*($\overline{A}$)$P$($B$)*=*$\frac{2}{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{4}{5} \right) + \left( 1\text{-}\frac{2}{3} \right) \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{2}{5}$,
> > $P$(*X=*2)*=P*($A$)$P$($B$)*=*$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{8}{15}$*.*
> > 所以解出该题的人数$X$的分布列为
> > | $X$ |        0         |        1        |        2         |
> > |:---:|:----------------:|:---------------:|:----------------:|
> > | $P$ | $$\frac{1}{15}$$ | $$\frac{2}{5}$$ | $$\frac{8}{15}$$ |
> > 故$E$($X$)*=*0×$\frac{1}{15}$*+*1×$\frac{2}{5}$*+*2×$\frac{8}{15} = \frac{22}{15}$*.*
