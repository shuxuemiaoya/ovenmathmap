> > [!success] **【答案】** 185
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设父亲的身高为$x$ cm,儿子的身高为$y$ cm,则
> > | $x$ | 173 | 170 | 176 |
> > |-----|-----|-----|-----|
> > | $y$ | 170 | 176 | 182 |
> > $\overline{x}$*=*173,$\overline{y}$*=*176,$\overset{\text{\textasciicircum}}{b} = \frac{0 \times \text{(-}6\text{)} + \text{(-}3\text{)} \times 0 + 3 \times 6}{0^{2} + 9 + 9}$*=*1,
> > $\overset{\text{\textasciicircum}}{a} = \overline{y} - \overset{\text{\textasciicircum}}{b}\ \overline{x}$*=*176*-*1×173*=*3,
> > 故$\overset{\text{\textasciicircum}}{y}$*=x+*3,当*x=*182时,$\overset{\text{\textasciicircum}}{y}$*=*185*.*
