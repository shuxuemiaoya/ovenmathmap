> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)$X$的可能取值为10,20,100,*-*200*.*
> > 由题意得$P$(*X=*10)*=*$C_{3}^{1} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{1} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} = \frac{3}{8}$,
> > $P$(*X=*20)*=*$C_{3}^{2} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right)^{1} = \frac{3}{8}$,
> > $P$(*X=*100)*=*$C_{3}^{3} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right)^{0} = \frac{1}{8}$,
> > $P$(*X=-*200)*=*$C_{3}^{0} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{0} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right)^{3} = \frac{1}{8}$*.*
> > 所以$X$的分布列为
> > | $X$ |       10        |       20        |       100       |     *-*200      |
> > |:---:|:---------------:|:---------------:|:---------------:|:---------------:|
> > | $P$ | $$\frac{3}{8}$$ | $$\frac{3}{8}$$ | $$\frac{1}{8}$$ | $$\frac{1}{8}$$ |
> > $X$的均值$E$($X$)*=*10×$\frac{3}{8}$*+*20×$\frac{3}{8}$*+*100×$\frac{1}{8}$*-*200×$\frac{1}{8}$*=-*$\frac{5}{4}$*.*
