> [!success]- 优化设计
> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{4}{27}　\frac{98}{81}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ①恰好摸4次停止的概率*p=*$C_{3}^{1} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right)^{2} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{4}{27}$*.*
> > ②由题意得$X$的可能取值为0,1,2*.*
> > $P$(*X=*0)*=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right)^{4} = \frac{16}{81}$,
> > $P$(*X=*1)*=*$C_{4}^{1} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right)^{3} = \frac{32}{81}$,
> > $P$(*X=*2)*=*$C_{2}^{2} \times \left( \frac{1}{3} \right)^{2} + C_{2}^{1} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right) \times \frac{1}{3} + C_{3}^{1} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right)^{2} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{33}{81}$*.*
> > 故随机变量$X$的均值$E$($X$)*=*0×$\frac{16}{81}$*+*1×$\frac{32}{81}$*+*2×$\frac{33}{81} = \frac{98}{81}$*.*
