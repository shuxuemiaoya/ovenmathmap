> > 由题可知,$P$(*A\|B*)*=*$\frac{P\text{(}AB\text{)}}{P\text{(}B\text{)}}$,则$P$(*AB*)*=P*(*A\|B*)·$P$($B$)*=*$\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{2}{15} = \frac{1}{10}$,对于A,$P$(*B\|A*)*=*$\frac{P\text{(}AB\text{)}}{P\text{(}A\text{)}} = \frac{\frac{1}{10}}{\frac{4}{15}} = \frac{3}{8}$,故A正确;对于B,$P$($\overline{A}$*\|B*)*=*$\frac{P\text{(}\overline{A}B\text{)}}{P\text{(}B\text{)}} = \frac{P\text{(}B\text{)-}P\text{(}AB\text{)}}{P\text{(}B\text{)}} = \frac{\frac{2}{15}\text{-}\frac{1}{10}}{\frac{2}{15}} = \frac{1}{4}$,故B正确;对于C,$P$($A$∪$B$)*=P*($A$)*+P*($B$)*-P*(*AB*)*=*$\frac{4}{15} + \frac{2}{15} - \frac{1}{10} = \frac{3}{10}$,故C正确;对于D,$P$($\overline{A} \cup \overline{B}$)*=*1*-P*(*AB*)*=*1*-*$\frac{1}{10} = \frac{9}{10}$,故D错误.故选ABC*.*
