> > [!success] **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 随机变量$X$的所有可能取值为0,1,2,3*.*
> > $P$(*X=*0)*=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{4}$,
> > $P$(*X=*1)*=*$\frac{1}{2} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{4} \right) + \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right) \times \frac{1}{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{4} \right) + \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right) \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right) \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{11}{24}$,
> > $P$(*X=*2)*=*$\left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{2} \right) \times \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{2} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{3} \right) \times \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \left( 1\text{-}\frac{1}{4} \right) = \frac{1}{4}$,
> > $P$(*X=*3)*=*$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{24}$*.*
> > 所以,随机变量$X$的分布列为
> > | $X$ |        0        |         1         |        2        |        3         |
> > |:---:|:---------------:|:-----------------:|:---------------:|:----------------:|
> > | $P$ | $$\frac{1}{4}$$ | $$\frac{11}{24}$$ | $$\frac{1}{4}$$ | $$\frac{1}{24}$$ |
> > 随机变量$X$的均值$E$($X$)*=*0×$\frac{1}{4}$*+*1×$\frac{11}{24}$*+*2×$\frac{1}{4}$*+*3×$\frac{1}{24} = \frac{13}{12}$*.*
