> > [!success] **【答案】** $\frac{5}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得$P$(*X=*0)*=*$\frac{1}{12}$*=*(1*-p*)^2^×$\frac{1}{3}$,解得*p=*$\frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 随机变量$X$的可能取值为0,1,2,3*.*
> > $P$(*X=*0)*=*$\frac{1}{12}$,
> > $P$(*X=*1)*=*$\frac{2}{3} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2}$*+*2×$\frac{1}{3} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} = \frac{1}{3}$,
> > $P$(*X=*2)*=*$\frac{2}{3} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2}$×2*+*$\frac{1}{3} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} = \frac{5}{12}$,
> > $P$(*X=*3)*=*$\frac{2}{3} \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} = \frac{1}{6}$*.*
> > 因此$E$($X$)*=*0×$\frac{1}{12}$*+*1×$\frac{1}{3}$*+*2×$\frac{5}{12}$*+*3×$\frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{3}$*.*
