> > $P$(*X=*0)*=*$\frac{C_{5}^{2} + C_{15}^{2} + C_{20}^{2}}{C_{40}^{2}} = \frac{61}{156}$,
> > $P$(*X=*1)*=*$\frac{C_{5}^{1}C_{15}^{1} + C_{15}^{1}C_{20}^{1}}{C_{40}^{2}} = \frac{25}{52}$,

> > | $X$ |         0          |         1         |        2         |

> > 所以$X$的均值$E$($X$)*=*0×$\frac{61}{156}$*+*1×$\frac{25}{52}$*+*2×$\frac{5}{39} = \frac{115}{156}$*.*
