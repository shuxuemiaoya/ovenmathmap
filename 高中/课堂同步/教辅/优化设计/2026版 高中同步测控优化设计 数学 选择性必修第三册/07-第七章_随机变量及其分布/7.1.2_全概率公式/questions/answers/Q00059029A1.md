> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设事件$A_{1}$*=*"乘火车来",$A_{2}$*=*"乘轮船来",$A_{3}$*=*"乘汽车来",$A_{4}$*=*"乘飞机来",*B=*"迟到"*.*
> > 由题意得$P$($A_{1}$)*=*$\frac{3}{10}$,$P$($A_{2}$)*=*$\frac{1}{5}$,$P$($A_{3}$)*=*$\frac{1}{10}$,$P$($A_{4}$)*=*$\frac{2}{5}$,$P$(*B\|A*~1~)*=*$\frac{1}{4}$,$P$(*B\|A*~2~)*=*$\frac{1}{3}$,$P$(*B\|A*~3~)*=*$\frac{1}{12}$,$P$(*B\|A*~4~)*=*0*.*
> > 由贝叶斯公式,得
> > $P$($A_{1}$*\|B*)*=*$\frac{P\text{(}A_{1}\text{)}P\text{(}B\text{|}A_{1}\text{)}}{\overset{4}{\underset{k = 1}{\text{∑}}}P\text{(}A_{k}\text{)}P\text{(}B\text{|}A_{k}\text{)}} = \frac{1}{2}$*.*
> > 类似地,可得$P$($A_{2}$*\|B*)*=*$\frac{4}{9}$,$P$($A_{3}$*\|B*)*=*$\frac{1}{18}$,$P$($A_{4}$*\|B*)*=*0*.*
> > 由上述计算结果可以推断此人迟到乘火车来的可能性最大*.*
