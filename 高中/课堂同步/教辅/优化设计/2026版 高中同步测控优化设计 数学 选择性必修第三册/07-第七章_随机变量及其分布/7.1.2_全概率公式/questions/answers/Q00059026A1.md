> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设*B=*"任取1件产品是合格品",*A~i~=*"产品取自第$i$批规格",*i=*1,2,则*Ω=A*~1~∪$A_{2}$,且$A_{1}$,$A_{2}$互斥*.*
> > 由题意得$P$($A_{1}$)*=*0*.*3,$P$($A_{2}$)*=*0*.*7,$P$(*B\|A*~1~)*=*0*.*97,$P$(*B\|A*~2~)*=*0*.*94*.*
> > (1)由全概率公式,得
> > $P$($B$)*=P*($A_{1}$)$P$(*B\|A*~1~)*+P*($A_{2}$)$P$(*B\|A*~2~)*=*0*.*3×0*.*97*+*0*.*7×0*.*94*=*0*.*949*.*
> > (2)$P$($A_{1}$*\|B*)*=*$\frac{P\text{(}A_{1}B\text{)}}{P\text{(}B\text{)}} = \frac{P\text{(}A_{1}\text{)}P\text{(}B\text{|}A_{1}\text{)}}{P\text{(}B\text{)}} = \frac{0\text{.}3 \times 0\text{.}97}{0\text{.}949} = \frac{291}{949}$*.*
