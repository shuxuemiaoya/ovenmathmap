> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由已知得$X$的可能取值为0,1,且服从两点分布*.*
> > $P$(*X=*0)*=*$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4}$,
> > $P$(*X=*1)*=*$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4}$,
> > 则$E$($X$)*=*$\frac{3}{4}$,$D$($X$)*=*$\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{16}$*.*
> > 故选ABCD*.*
