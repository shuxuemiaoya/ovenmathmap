> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)$X$的分布列为
> > | $X$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
> > |:------:|:-----:|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|:-----:|
> > | $P$ | $$\frac{1}{2}$$ | $$\frac{1}{20}$$ | $$\frac{1}{10}$$ | $$\frac{3}{20}$$ | $$\frac{1}{5}$$ |
> > $E$($X$)*=*0×$\frac{1}{2}$*+*1×$\frac{1}{20}$*+*2×$\frac{1}{10}$*+*3×$\frac{3}{20}$*+*4×$\frac{1}{5} = \frac{3}{2}$*.*
> > $D$($X$)*=*$\left( 0\text{-}\frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} \times \frac{1}{2} + \left( 1\text{-}\frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} \times \frac{1}{20} + \left( 2\text{-}\frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} \times \frac{1}{10} + \left( 3\text{-}\frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} \times \frac{3}{20} + \left( 4\text{-}\frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} \times \frac{1}{5} = \frac{11}{4}$*.*
> > (2)由$D$($Y$)*=D*(*aX+b*)*=a*^2^$D$($X$),即$a^{2}$×$\frac{11}{4}$*=*11,得*a=*±2*.*∵$E$($Y$)*=E*(*aX+b*)*=aE*($X$)*+b*,
> > ∴当*a=*2时,由1*=*2×$\frac{3}{2}$*+b*,得*b=-*2;
> > 当*a=-*2时,由1*=-*2×$\frac{3}{2}$*+b*,得*b=*4*.*
> > ∴$\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = 2\text{,} \\
> > b = \text{-}2
> > \end{matrix} \right.\ 或\left\{ \begin{matrix}
> > a = \text{-}2\text{,} \\
> > b = 4\text{.}
> > \end{matrix} \right.\$
