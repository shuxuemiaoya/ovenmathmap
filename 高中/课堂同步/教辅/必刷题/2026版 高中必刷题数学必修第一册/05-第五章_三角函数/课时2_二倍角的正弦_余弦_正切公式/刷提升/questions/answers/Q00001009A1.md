> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 因为 $\alpha \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \beta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \tan \alpha = \frac{\cos 2\beta}{1 - \sin 2\beta},$ 所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\cos^2\beta - \sin^2\beta}{\cos^2\beta + \sin^2\beta - 2\sin\beta\cos\beta} = \frac{(\cos\beta - \sin\beta)(\cos\beta + \sin\beta)}{(\cos\beta - \sin\beta)^2},$ 因为 $\cos \beta - \sin \beta \neq 0,$ 所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\cos\beta + \sin\beta}{\cos\beta - \sin\beta} = \frac{1 + \tan\beta}{1 - \tan\beta} = \tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta\right).$ 因为 $\alpha \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \beta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ ，所以 $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{4} + \beta,$ 即 $\alpha - \beta = \frac{\pi}{4}$ .
>
> 故选 **B**.
