> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 当 $k$ 为偶数时，设 $k = 2n, n \in \mathbf{Z}$ ，则原式 $= \frac{\sin[(2n + 1)\pi + \theta]\cos[(2n + 1)\pi - \theta]}{\sin(2n\pi - \theta)\cos(2n\pi + \theta)} = \frac{\sin(\pi + \theta) \cdot \cos(\pi - \theta)}{-\sin\theta \cdot \cos\theta} = \frac{-\sin\theta \cdot (-\cos\theta)}{-\sin\theta \cdot \cos\theta} = -1.$
