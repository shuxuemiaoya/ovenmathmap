> $\frac{\pi - \sqrt{3}}{8}$ 【解析】莱洛三角形的周长为 $\frac{\pi}{2}$ , 可得弧长 $\widehat{AB} = \widehat{BC} = \widehat{AC} = \frac{\pi}{6}$ , 则等边三角点悟: 其对应的圆心角都为 $\frac{\pi}{3}$ 形的边长 $AB = BC = AC = \frac{\frac{\pi}{6}}{\frac{\pi}{3}} = \frac{1}{2}$ .
![](高中/课堂同步/教辅/必刷题/2026版%20高中必刷题数学必修第一册/images/answers/part-002/522cc0bf3879ead2dbfb0208cbd770c4f0132f77a70d27706e7460e1b6d2771c.jpg)
故分别以点 $A, B, C$ 为圆心，圆弧 $BC, AC, AB$ 所对的扇形面积均为 $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{6} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{\pi}{24}$ ，等边 $\triangle ABC$ 的面积 $S = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{16}$ ，所以莱洛三角形的面积是 $3 \times \frac{\pi}{24} - 2 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{16} = \frac{\pi - \sqrt{3}}{8}$ .
