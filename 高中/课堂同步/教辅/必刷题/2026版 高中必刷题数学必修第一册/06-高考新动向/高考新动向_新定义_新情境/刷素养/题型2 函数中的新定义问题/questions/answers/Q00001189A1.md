> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【解析】**
> 【解】(1)选择条件①:∵角α是第一象限角, $\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ ,
∴ $\cos\alpha=\sqrt{1-\sin^{2}\alpha}=\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\right)^{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ,
故 $\tan\alpha=\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=\sqrt{2}$
选择条件②：∵ $\tan^{2}\alpha+\sqrt{2}\tan\alpha-4=(\tan\alpha+2\sqrt{2})(\tan\alpha-\sqrt{2})=0$ ,
∴ $\tan\alpha=\sqrt{2}$ 或 $\tan\alpha=-2\sqrt{2}$ ,
又角 $\alpha$ 是第一象限角，∴ $\tan\alpha=\sqrt{2}$ .
(2)选择条件①：
由(1)可知， $\cos\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3},\cos(\alpha+\pi)\cos(\alpha-3\pi)-2\sqrt{2}\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)\sin\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ $=(-\cos\alpha)\cdot(-\cos\alpha)-2\sqrt{2}\sin\alpha\cdot(-\cos\alpha)$ $=\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)^{2}-2\sqrt{2}\times\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\times\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)=\frac{5}{3}.$ 选择条件②：
由(1)可知， $\tan\alpha=\sqrt{2},\cos(\alpha+\pi)\cos(\alpha-3\pi)-2\sqrt{2}\cos\left(\alpha+\frac{3\pi}{2}\right)\sin\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ $=(-\cos\alpha)\cdot(-\cos\alpha)-2\sqrt{2}\sin\alpha\cdot(-\cos\alpha)$ $=\cos^{2}\alpha+2\sqrt{2}\sin\alpha\cdot\cos\alpha$ $=\frac{\cos^{2}\alpha+2\sqrt{2}\sin\alpha\cdot\cos\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha}$ 功思：可对比两种解法，体会三角恒等变换的灵活性 $=\frac{1+2\sqrt{2}\tan\alpha}{\tan^{2}\alpha+1}=\frac{1+2\sqrt{2}\times\sqrt{2}}{(\sqrt{2})^{2}+1}=\frac{5}{3}.$
