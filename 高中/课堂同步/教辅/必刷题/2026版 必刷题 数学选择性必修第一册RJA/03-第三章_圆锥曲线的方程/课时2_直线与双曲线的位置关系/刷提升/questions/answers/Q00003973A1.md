> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【解析】**
> $\left(\pm\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3},0\right)$ 【解析】显然直线MN的斜率存在且不为0,设直线MN的方程为 $y=kx+2$ ,则 $Q\left(-\frac{2}{k},0\right)$ ,
由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y&=kx+2,\\ 3x^{2}-y^{2}&=3\end{aligned}\right.$ 消去y并整理得 $(3-k^{2})\cdot x^{2}-4kx-7=0$ , $\Delta=16k^{2}+28(3-k^{2})>0$ ,则 $0<k^{2}<7$ 且 $k^{2}\neq3$ . 设 $M(x_{1},y_{1}),N(x_{2},y_{2})$ ,
于是 $x_{1}+x_{2}=\frac{4k}{3-k^{2}},x_{1}x_{2}=-\frac{7}{3-k^{2}},\overrightarrow{PQ}=$ $\left(-\frac{2}{k},-2\right),\overrightarrow{QM}=\left(x_{1}+\frac{2}{k},y_{1}\right),\overrightarrow{QN}=$ $\left(x_{2}+\frac{2}{k},y_{2}\right)$ ,
由 $\overrightarrow{PQ} = \mu \overrightarrow{QM} = \lambda \overrightarrow{QN}$ ，得 $-\frac{2}{k} =$ $\mu \left(x_{1} + \frac{2}{k}\right) = \lambda \left(x_{2} + \frac{2}{k}\right)$ ，则 $\mu =$ $-\frac{2}{kx_1 + 2},\lambda = -\frac{2}{kx_2 + 2},\mu +\lambda = -\frac{2}{kx_1 + 2} -$ $\frac{2}{kx_2 + 2} = -\frac{2k(x_1 + x_2) + 8}{k^2x_1x_2 + 2k(x_1 + x_2) + 4} =$ $-\frac{2k\cdot\frac{4k}{3 - k^2} + 8}{-\frac{7k^2}{3 - k^2} + 2k\cdot\frac{4k}{3 - k^2} + 4} = \frac{8}{k^2 - 4}$ 而 $\mu +\lambda =$ 4，因此 $\frac{8}{k^2 - 4} = 4$ ，解得 $k = \pm \sqrt{6}$ ，所以点 $Q$ 的坐标为 $\left(\pm \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3},0\right)$
