> **【解析】**
> $4\sqrt{3}$ 【解析】：直线 $F_{2}M$ 的斜率为 $\sqrt{3},\therefore \tan \angle MF_2F_1 = -\sqrt{3},$ $\because 0 <   \angle MF_2F_1 <   \pi ,\therefore \angle MF_2F_1 = \frac{2\pi}{3},$ $\therefore \cos \angle MF_2F_1 = -\frac{1}{2}.\because \frac{|MF_2|}{|F_1F_2|} =$ $\frac{3}{5},|F_1F_2| = 2c,\therefore |MF_2| = \frac{6}{5} c,$ $\because |MF_1| + |MF_2| = 2a = 16,$ $\therefore |MF_1| = 16 - \frac{6}{5} c.$ 在 $\triangle F_1F_2M$ 中，由余弦定理得 $|MF_1|^2 = |MF_2|^2 +|F_1F_2|^2 -2|MF_2|\cdot$ $|F_1F_2|\cos \angle MF_2F_1,$ 即 $\left(16 - \frac{6}{5} c\right)^2 = \left(\frac{6}{5} c\right)^2 +4c^2 -2\times \frac{6}{5} c\times$ $2c\times \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)$ ，整理得 $c^2 +6c - 40 = 0$ ，解得 $c = 4$ 或 $c = -10$ （舍）.所以 $b = \sqrt{a^2 - c^2} = \sqrt{8^2 - 4^2} = 4\sqrt{3}.$
