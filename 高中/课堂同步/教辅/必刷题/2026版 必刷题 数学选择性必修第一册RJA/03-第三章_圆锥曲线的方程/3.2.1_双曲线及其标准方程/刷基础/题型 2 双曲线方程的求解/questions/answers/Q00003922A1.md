> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{x^{2}}{3}-\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$ 【解析】设双曲线的方程为 $mx^{2}-ny^{2}=1(mn>0)$ ,
→ 点悟：不清楚焦点的位置时，这样设方程避免了讨论
代入点 $A\left(2,\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\right),B(3,-2\sqrt{2})$ 的坐标可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&4m-\frac{4}{3}n=1,\\ &9m-8n=1,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&m=\frac{1}{3},\\ &n=\frac{1}{4},\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以双曲线的标准方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{3}-\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$ .
$= -\frac{1}{t}\cdot \frac{2y_1y_2 + (y_1 + y_2)}{y_1y_2 + (y_1 + y_2) + 1}$ $= -\frac{1}{t}\cdot \frac{2\times\frac{t^2 - 4t}{t^2 + 4} + \frac{4t - 2t^2}{t^2 + 4}}{\frac{t^2 - 4t}{t^2 + 4} + \frac{4t - 2t^2}{t^2 + 4} + 1} = 1,$ 即 $|QC| + |QD| = |QC|\cdot |QD|$
即 $|QC|+|QD|=|QC|\cdot|QD|$ ，所以 $|QC|+|QD|-|QC|\cdot|QD|$ 为定值0.
