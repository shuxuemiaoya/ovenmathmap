> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 设双曲线方程为 $mx^{2} + ny^{2} = 1$ (mn < 0),
则有 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&(2\sqrt{2})^{2}m+(\sqrt{3})^{2}n=1,\\&(-2\sqrt{3})^{2}m+(-\sqrt{6})^{2}n=1,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}m=\frac{1}{4},\\ n=-\frac{1}{3},\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以双曲线的标准方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{4}-\frac{y^{2}}{3}=1$ .
>
> 故选 **A**.
