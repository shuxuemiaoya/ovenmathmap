> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题知抛物线 $y^{2} = 4x$ 的焦点 $F(1,0)$ ，设 $A(x_{1},y_{1})(y_{1} < 0)$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})(x_{1} \neq x_{2})$ ，则 $A_{1}(x_{1}, -y_{1})$ . 因为直线 $AB$ 的斜率为 1，所以直线 $AB$ 的方程为 $y = x - 1$ . 联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y &= x - 1,\\ y^2 &= 4x,\end{aligned}\right.$ 消去 $y$ 整理得 $x^{2} - 6x +$
$1 = 0, \Delta > 0$ ，则 $x_{1} + x_{2} = 6, x_{1}x_{2} = 1$ . 直线 $A_{1}B$ 的斜率 $k = \frac{y_{2} - (-y_{1})}{x_{2} - x_{1}} = \frac{y_{2} + y_{1}}{x_{2} - x_{1}} = \frac{x_{1} + x_{2} - 2}{\sqrt{(x_{1} + x_{2})^{2} - 4x_{1}x_{2}}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，故直线 $A_{1}B$ 的斜率为 $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
>
> 故选 **C**.
