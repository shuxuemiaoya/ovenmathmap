> **【解析】**
> $1 + \sqrt{2}$ 【解析】由题意知，点 $C$ 的坐标为 $\left(\frac{a}{2}, - a\right)$ ，点 $F$ 的坐标为 $\left(\frac{a}{2} + b, b\right)$ . $\because C, F$ 两点都在抛物线 $y^{2} = 2px$ 上， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} a^{2} = 2p \cdot \frac{a}{2}, \\ b^{2} = 2p \cdot \left(\frac{a}{2} + b\right), \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore a^{2} - b^{2} + 2ab = 0$ ，即 $1 - \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^{2} + \frac{2b}{a} = 0$ ，解得 $\frac{b}{a} = 1 + \sqrt{2}$ 或 $\frac{b}{a} = 1 - \sqrt{2}$ . $\because 0 < a < b, \therefore \frac{b}{a} = 1 + \sqrt{2}$ .
