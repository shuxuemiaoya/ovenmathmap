> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** ABD
>
> **【解析】**
> 建立如图所示的空间直角坐标系, 则 $A(0,0,0), C_1(1,1,1), A_1(0,0,1), B(1,0,0), D(0,1,0)$ ,
$B_{1}(1,0,1),O\left(\frac{1}{2},\frac{1}{2},1\right). \overrightarrow{OA_{1}} =$ $\left(-\frac{1}{2}, - \frac{1}{2},0\right),\overrightarrow{AB} = (1,0,0),\overrightarrow{AD} =$ $(0,1,0),\overrightarrow{AA_1} = (0,0,1).$
![](高中/课堂同步/教辅/必刷题/2026版%20必刷题%20数学选择性必修第一册RJA/images/answers/part-001/15b249cbd084930f98acc17bb44ae0f9433fbca0afb9ca6d5552abd2bcaf16f7.jpg)
对于 A, $\overrightarrow{AC_{1}} = (1, 1, 1)$ , $\overrightarrow{BD} = (-1, 1, 0)$ , $\overrightarrow{A_{1}B} = (1, 0, -1)$ , 所以 $\overrightarrow{AC_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = 0$ , $\overrightarrow{AC_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{A_{1}B} = 0$ , 所以 $AC_{1} \perp BD$ , $AC_{1} \perp A_{1}B$ , 又 $BD \cap A_{1}B = B$ , $BD, A_{1}B \subset$ 平面 $A_{1}BD$ , 所以 $AC_{1} \perp$ 平面 $A_{1}BD$ , A 正确; 对于 B, 易知 $\overrightarrow{AB_{1}} = (1, 0, 1)$ 为平面 $A_{1}BCD_{1}$ 的一个法向量, $\overrightarrow{BB_{1}} = (0, 0, 1)$ , 所以 $\cos\langle\overrightarrow{BB_{1}}, \overrightarrow{AB_{1}}\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} \times 1} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ , 所以直线 $BB_{1}$ 与平面 $A_{1}BCD_{1}$ 所成的角的正弦值为 $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ , 所以直线 $BB_{1}$ 与平面 $A_{1}BCD_{1}$ 所成的角为 $\frac{\pi}{4}$ , B 正确; 对于 C, 由题可得 OE // 平面 $A_{1}BCD_{1}$ , 故直线 OE 到平面 $A_{1}BCD_{1}$ 的距离为 $\frac{|\overrightarrow{OA_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB_{1}}|}{|\overrightarrow{AB_{1}}|} = \frac{\left|(-\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}, 0) \cdot (1, 0, 1)\right|}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$ ,
故 C 错误; 对于 D, $\overrightarrow{AP} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} + \frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AA_{1}} = \left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{4}, \frac{2}{3}\right), \overrightarrow{AD} = (0, 1, 0)$ , 所以 $\frac{|\overrightarrow{AP} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD}|}{|\overrightarrow{AD}|} = \frac{\frac{3}{4}}{1} = \frac{3}{4}$ ,
所以点 P 到直线 AD 的距离为 $\sqrt{|\overrightarrow{AP}|^{2}-\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{2}}=\frac{5}{6}$ , D 正确.
>
> 故选 **ABD**.
