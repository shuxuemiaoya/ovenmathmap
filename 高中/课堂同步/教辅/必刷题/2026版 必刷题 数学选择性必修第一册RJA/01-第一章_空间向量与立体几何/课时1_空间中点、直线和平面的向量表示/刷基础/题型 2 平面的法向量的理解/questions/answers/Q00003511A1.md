> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题知 $\overrightarrow{AB}=(-3,-1,0)$ , $\overrightarrow{AC}=(-2,0,1)$ ，设平面 ABC 的法向量为 $\boldsymbol{n}=(x,y,z)$ ，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\boldsymbol{n}&\perp\overrightarrow{AB},\\ \boldsymbol{n}&\perp\overrightarrow{AC}\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow$
$\left\{ \begin{array}{l} n \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = 0, \\ n \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = 0 \end{array} \right. \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{array}{l} -3x - y = 0, \\ -2x + z = 0, \end{array} \right.$ 令 $x = 1$ ，得 $n = (1, -3, 2)$ .
>
> 故选 **B**.
>
