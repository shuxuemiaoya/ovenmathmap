> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> ∵ $a + b + c = 0$ , ∴ $a + c = -b$ , $b + c = -a$ , ∴ $(a + c)^{2} = a^{2} + 2a \cdot c + c^{2} = 41 + 2a \cdot c = 25$ , $(b + c)^{2} = b^{2} + 2b \cdot c + c^{2} = 50 + 2b \cdot c = 16$ , ∴ $a \cdot c = -8$ , $b \cdot c = -17$ , ∴ $\frac{(a - b) \cdot c}{|c|} \cdot \frac{c}{|c|} = \frac{a \cdot c - b \cdot c}{|c|^{2}} \cdot c = \frac{9}{25}c$ .
>
> 故选 **C**.
