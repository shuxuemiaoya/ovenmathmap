> **【解析】**
> 依题意， $a,b,c$ 共面，所以存在 $x,y$ ，使得 $\pmb {c} = x\pmb {a} + y\pmb{b}$ ，即（0,2， $1) = (2x,kx,3x) + (2y,5y,0) = (2x+$ $2y,kx + 5y,3x)$ ，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}2x + 2y = 0,\\ kx + 5y = 2,\\ 3x = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x = \frac{1}{3},\\ y = -\frac{1}{3},\\ k = 11. \end{array} \right.$
>
> 故选 **A**.
