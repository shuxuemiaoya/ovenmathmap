> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】 $\frac{1+\tan 190^{\circ}}{1-\tan 370^{\circ}} - \frac{2\cos 70^{\circ}}{\sin 40^{\circ}} =$ $\frac{1+\tan 10^{\circ}}{1-\tan 10^{\circ}} - \frac{2\sin 20^{\circ}}{\sin 40^{\circ}} = \frac{1 + \frac{\sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}}}{1 - \frac{\sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ}}} -$ $\frac{2\sin 20^{\circ}}{2\sin 20^{\circ}\cos 20^{\circ}} = \frac{\cos 10^{\circ} + \sin 10^{\circ}}{\cos 10^{\circ} - \sin 10^{\circ}} -$ $\frac{1}{\cos 20^{\circ}} = \frac{(\cos 10^{\circ} + \sin 10^{\circ})^{2}}{\cos^{2}10^{\circ} - \sin^{2}10^{\circ}} - \frac{1}{\cos 20^{\circ}} =$ $\frac{1 + \sin 20^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}} - \frac{1}{\cos 20^{\circ}} = \tan 20^{\circ}.$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
