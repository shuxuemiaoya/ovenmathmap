> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)因为点 $A(-8,6)$ 为角 $\alpha$ 终边上一点，所以 $\sin \alpha = \frac{6}{\sqrt{(-8)^2 + 6^2}} = \frac{3}{5}$ ， $\cos \alpha = \frac{-8}{\sqrt{(-8)^2 + 6^2}} = -\frac{4}{5}$ ，所以 $\sin 2\alpha = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 2 \times \frac{3}{5} \times \left(-\frac{4}{5}\right) = -\frac{24}{25}$ . (2)因为点 $A$ 在第一象限，所以 $\alpha \in \left(2k\pi, \frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi\right), k \in \mathbf{Z}$ ，所以 $\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left(\frac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi, \frac{2\pi}{3} + 2k\pi\right), k \in \mathbf{Z}$ . 因为 $\cos \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{4}{5}$ ，所以 $\sin \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)} = \frac{3}{5}$ . 所以 $\sin \alpha = \sin \left[\left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \frac{\pi}{6}\right] = \sin \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \cos \frac{\pi}{6} - \cos \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \sin \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3} - 4}{10}$ .
