> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】由 x>1 可得 x-1>0，所以 $2-\frac{4}{x-1}=-1-\left[3(x-1)+\frac{4}{x-1}\right]\leqslant-1-2\sqrt{3(x-1)\cdot\frac{4}{x-1}}=-1-4\sqrt{3}$ 当且仅当 $3(x-1)=\frac{4}{x-1}$ ，即 $x=1+\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ 或 [272]
> >
> > 所以 $\tan \alpha +\frac{1}{\tan\alpha} = -\frac{4}{3} +\frac{1}{-\frac{4}{3}} = -\frac{25}{12}$ 选条件③：因为 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha = \frac{7}{5},\\ \sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5}, & \text{或}\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\sin \alpha = \frac{4}{5}.\\ \cos \alpha = -\frac{4}{5} \end{array} \right.\\ \cos \alpha = -\frac{4}{5} \end{array} \right.$ 或 $\cos \alpha = -\frac{3}{5},$ 又 $|\sin \alpha | > |\cos \alpha |$ ，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\sin \alpha = \frac{4}{5},\\ \cos \alpha = -\frac{3}{5}. \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > ## 模块综合测试
> >
> > $x = 1 - \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ (舍去)时，等号成立.所以 $2 - 3x - \frac{4}{x - 1}$ 的最大值是 $-4\sqrt{3} -1$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
