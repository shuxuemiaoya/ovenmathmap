> > 【解】(1)因为 $\alpha$ 是第二象限角，所以 $\sin \alpha > 0, \cos \alpha < 0.$ 选条件①：因为 $\sin \alpha, \cos \alpha$ 是关于 $x$ 的方程 $25x^{2} - 5x - 12 = 0$ 的两个实根，所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{5}, \\ \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = -\frac{12}{25}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \sin \alpha = \frac{4}{5}, \\ \cos \alpha = -\frac{3}{5}. \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = -\frac{4}{3}$ ，所以 $\tan \alpha +$ $\frac{1}{\tan \alpha} = -\frac{4}{3} + \frac{1}{-\frac{4}{3}} = -\frac{25}{12}$ . 选条件②: 因为 $P(x,2)$ 为角 $\alpha$ 终边上一点, 且 $\cos \alpha = \frac{2}{5} x$ , 所以 $x < 0$ , 且 $\cos \alpha = \frac{2}{5} x = \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + 2^2}}$ , 解得 $x = -\frac{3}{2}$ . 所以 $\cos \alpha = \frac{2}{5} \times \left(-\frac{3}{2}\right) = -\frac{3}{5}$ , 所以 $\sin \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \alpha} = \frac{4}{5}$ , 所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = -\frac{4}{3}$ ,
