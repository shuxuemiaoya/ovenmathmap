> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】若 $S$ 中有3个元素, 不妨设 $S = \{a, b, c\} (a < b < c)$ , 则由条件①得 $ab, ac, bc \in T$ , 由条件②得 $\frac{b}{a}, \frac{c}{a}, \frac{c}{b} \in S$ , 在 $\frac{b}{a}, \frac{c}{a}, \frac{c}{b}$ 中显然 $\frac{c}{a}$ 最大. 分两种情况讨论: 当 $\frac{c}{a} = c$ 时, $a = 1, \therefore \frac{b}{a} = b, \frac{c}{b} \neq c$ , 若 $\frac{c}{b} = a$ , 则有 $b = c$ , 与题设矛盾, $\therefore \frac{c}{b} = b$ , 即 $c = b^2$ , 此时 $S = \{1, b, b^2\}$ , $T = \{b, b^2, b^3\}$ , $S \cup T = \{1, b, b^2, b^3\}$ , 有4个元素; 当 $\frac{c}{a} = b$ 时, $c = ab$ , 若 $\frac{b}{a} = b$ , 则 $a = 1, c = b$ , 与题设矛盾, $\therefore \frac{b}{a} = a, \therefore b = a^2, c = a^3$ , 此时 $S = \{a, a^2, a^3\}, \therefore a^3, a^4, a^5 \in T$ , $\therefore T = \{a^3, a^4, a^5\}$ 或 $\{a^2, a^3, a^4, a^5\}$ 或 $\{a^3, a^4, a^5, a^6\}$ , $\therefore S \cup T = \{a, a^2, a^3, a^4, a^5\}$ , 有5个元素或 $S \cup T = \{a, a^2, a^3, a^4, a^5, a^6\}$ , 有6个元素, $\therefore$ 当 $S$ 中有3个元素时, $S \cup T$ 有4个或5个或6个元素, 故 C, D 错误. 若 $S$ 中有4个元素, 不妨设 $S = \{a, b, c, d\} (a < b < c < d)$ , 则由条件①可得 $ab, ac, ad, bc, bd, cd \in T$ , 由条件②可得 $\frac{b}{a}, \frac{c}{a}, \frac{d}{a}, \frac{c}{b}, \frac{d}{b}, \frac{d}{c}, \frac{cd}{ab}, \frac{bd}{ac} \in S$ , 显然 $\frac{cd}{ab}$ 最大, 而 $\frac{b}{a}, \frac{c}{a}, \frac{d}{a}$ 分别对应从小到大的3个元素, $\because S$ 中只有4个元素, $\therefore$ 必有 $\frac{b}{a} = a, \frac{c}{a} = b, \frac{d}{a} = c, \frac{cd}{ab} = d$ , 由此可得 $b = a^2, c = a^3, d = a^4, \therefore S = \{a, a^2, a^3, a^4\}$ , $T = \{a^3, a^4, a^5, a^6, a^7\}$ , $S \cup T = \{a, a^2, a^3, a^4, a^5, a^6, a^7\}$ , 有7个元素, $\therefore$ 当 $S$ 中有4个元素时, $S \cup T$ 有7个元素, 故 A 正确, B 错误. 故选 A.
