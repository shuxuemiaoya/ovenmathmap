> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】 $\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos\alpha}{2}}-\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos\alpha}{2}}=\sqrt{\cos^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}}-\sqrt{\sin^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}}=\left|\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}\right|-$ → 点悟：根据原式 = $\sqrt{\frac{1+2\cos^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}-1}{2}}-\sqrt{\frac{1-\left(1-2\sin^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}\right)}{2}}$ 得到 $\left|\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\right|$ , ∵ $3\pi<\alpha<4\pi$ , ∴ $\frac{3\pi}{2}<\frac{\alpha}{2}<2\pi$ ,
> > ∴ $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} < 0, \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} > 0.$ ∴ 原式 = $\sin \frac{\alpha}{2} + \cos \frac{\alpha}{2} = \sqrt{2} \sin \left( \frac{\alpha}{2} + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
