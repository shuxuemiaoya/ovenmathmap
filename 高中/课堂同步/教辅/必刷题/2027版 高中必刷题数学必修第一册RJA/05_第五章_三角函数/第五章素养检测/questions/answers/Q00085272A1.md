> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $-\frac{1}{2}$ 【解析】 $\because \alpha \in \left[\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{3}\right]$ , $\therefore \cos \frac{\pi}{3} \leqslant \cos \alpha \leqslant \cos \frac{\pi}{6}$ , 即 $\frac{1}{2} \leqslant \cos \alpha \leqslant \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ . 又 $\beta - \alpha = \pi + 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}$ , $\therefore \cos \beta = \cos (\alpha + \pi + 2k\pi) = \cos (\alpha + \pi) = -\cos \alpha, k \in \mathbf{Z}, \therefore -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \leqslant \cos \beta \leqslant -\frac{1}{2}, \therefore \cos \beta$ 的最大值为 $-\frac{1}{2}$ .
