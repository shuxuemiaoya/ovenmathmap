> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D 【解析】由题意得 $\tan \alpha = \frac{BC}{AB} = \frac{5 \times \frac{0.618}{1 + 0.618 + 1}}{8 \times \frac{0.618}{1 + 0.618 + 1}} = \frac{5}{8}$ ，故 $\frac{\cos 2\alpha}{\sin 2\alpha} = \frac{1}{\tan 2\alpha} = \frac{1 - \tan^{2}\alpha}{2\tan \alpha} = \frac{1 - \left(\frac{5}{8}\right)^{2}}{2 \times \frac{5}{8}} = \frac{39}{80}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
