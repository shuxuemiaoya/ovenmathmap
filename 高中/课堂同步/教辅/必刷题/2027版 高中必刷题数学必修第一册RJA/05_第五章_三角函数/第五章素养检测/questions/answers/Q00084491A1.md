> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $2\sqrt{2} - 2$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\alpha \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ , 得 $\cos \alpha > 0$ , 由 $\beta \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ , 得 $-1 < \sin \beta < 1, 0 < 1 - \sin \beta < 2$ . 由 $(1 + \cos 2\alpha)(1 + \sin \beta) = \sin 2\alpha \cos \beta$ , 得 $2\cos^2\alpha (1 + \sin \beta) = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha \cos \beta$ , 则 $\tan \alpha = \frac{1 + \sin \beta}{\cos \beta}$ , $\tan^2\alpha - \sin \beta = \frac{(1 + \sin \beta)^2}{\cos^2\beta} - \sin \beta = \frac{(1 + \sin \beta)^2}{1 - \sin^2\beta} - \sin B = \frac{1 + \sin \beta}{1 - \sin \beta} - \sin \beta = \frac{2}{1 - \sin \beta} + 1 - \sin \beta - 2$ $\geqslant 2\sqrt{\frac{2}{1 - \sin \beta} \cdot (1 - \sin \beta)} - 2 = 2\sqrt{2} - 2$ , 当且仅当 $\frac{2}{1 - \sin \beta} = 1 - \sin \beta$ , 即 $\sin \beta = 1 - \sqrt{2}$ 时取等号, 所以 $\tan^2\alpha - \sin \beta$ 的最小值为 $2\sqrt{2} - 2$ .
