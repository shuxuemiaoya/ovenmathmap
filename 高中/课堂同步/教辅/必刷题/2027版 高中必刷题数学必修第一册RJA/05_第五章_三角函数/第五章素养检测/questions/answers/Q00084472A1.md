> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】因为 $\cos 2047^{\circ} = \cos (5 \times 360^{\circ} + 247^{\circ}) = \cos 247^{\circ} = \cos (180^{\circ} + 67^{\circ}) = -\cos 67^{\circ} = -\cos (90^{\circ} - 23^{\circ}) = -\sin 23^{\circ}$ ，故 $\sin 22^{\circ} \cos 23^{\circ} - \cos 22^{\circ} \cos 2047^{\circ} = \sin 22^{\circ} \cos 23^{\circ} + \cos 22^{\circ} \sin 23^{\circ} = \sin 45^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
