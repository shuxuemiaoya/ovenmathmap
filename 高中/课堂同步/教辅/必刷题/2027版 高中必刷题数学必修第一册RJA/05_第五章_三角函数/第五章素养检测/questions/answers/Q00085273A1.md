> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\theta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ , $\tan \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} = \frac{1}{3}$ , 所以 $\cos \theta = 3\sin \theta$ . 又因为 $\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta = 1$ , 解得 $\sin^2\theta = \frac{1}{10}$ , 即 $\sin \theta = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}, \cos \theta = \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$ , 所以 $\sin \theta - \cos \theta = -\frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}$ .
