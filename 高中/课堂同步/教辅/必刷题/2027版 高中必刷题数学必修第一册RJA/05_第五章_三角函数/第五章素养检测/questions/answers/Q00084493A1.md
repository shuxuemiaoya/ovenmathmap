> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)根据任意角三角函数的定义可得 $\tan\alpha=\frac{2}{1}=2.$ (2) 因为 $P(1,2)$ 为角 $\alpha$ 终边上一点，所以 $r=OP=\sqrt{1^{2}+2^{2}}=\sqrt{5}$ ，
> > 所以 $\sin\alpha=\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}=\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5},\cos\alpha=\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}.$ 所以 $\sin^{2}\alpha+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha-\cos^{2}\alpha=\frac{20}{25}+2\times\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}\times\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}-\frac{5}{25}=\frac{35}{25}=\frac{7}{5}.$ (3) $\frac{\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{5\pi}{2}\right)\cos(2024\pi-\alpha)}{\cos(\pi-\alpha)}+$ $\frac{\cos\left(\alpha-\frac{2023\pi}{2}\right)\sin(\pi-\alpha)}{\sin(\pi+\alpha)}$
> >
> > $= \frac{\sin\left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\cos(-\alpha)}{\cos(\pi - \alpha)} +$ $\frac{\cos\left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{2}\right)\sin(\pi - \alpha)}{\sin(\pi + \alpha)} = \frac{\cos\alpha\cos\alpha}{-\cos\alpha} -\frac{\sin\alpha\sin\alpha}{-\sin\alpha} = -\cos \alpha +\sin \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} +\frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}.$
