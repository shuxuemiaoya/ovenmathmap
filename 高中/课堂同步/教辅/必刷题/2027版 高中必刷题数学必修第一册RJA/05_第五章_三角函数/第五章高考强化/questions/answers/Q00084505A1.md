>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C 【解析】因为 $\frac{\sin\theta(1+\sin2\theta)}{\sin\theta+\cos\theta}=\frac{\sin\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}}{\sin\theta+\cos\theta}=\underbrace{\sin\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)}_{=}\frac{\sin^{2}\theta+\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta}=\frac{\frac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta}+\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}{\frac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta}+1}=$ 巧思：也可利用 $\sin\theta=-2\cos\theta$ 化为正弦或余弦求解 $\frac{\tan^{2}\theta+\tan\theta}{\tan^{2}\theta+1}=\frac{4-2}{4+1}=\frac{2}{5}$ ，故选 C.
