> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】∵ $\frac{\cos\alpha}{\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha}=\frac{1}{1-\tan\alpha}=\sqrt{3}$ ∴ $\tan \alpha = 1 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ , $\tan\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{\tan\alpha+\tan\frac{\pi}{4}}{1-\tan\alpha\tan\frac{\pi}{4}}=2\sqrt{3}-1$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
