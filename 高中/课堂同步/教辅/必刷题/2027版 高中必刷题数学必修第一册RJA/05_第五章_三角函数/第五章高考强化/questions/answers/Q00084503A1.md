> > [!success]- **【答案】** B

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B
> > 思路导引 $\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{1}{3},\cos\alpha\sin\beta=\frac{1}{6}\rightarrow\sin\alpha\cos\beta$ 的值 $\rightarrow\sin(\alpha+\beta)$ 的值
> > 二倍角公式 $\cos(2\alpha+2\beta)$ 的值
> > 【解析】∵ $\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta-\cos\alpha\sin\beta=\frac{1}{3}$ , $\cos\alpha\sin\beta=\frac{1}{6}$ , ∴ $\sin\alpha\cos\beta=\frac{1}{3}+$ $\cos\alpha\sin\beta=\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{6}=\frac{1}{2}.$ ∴ $\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta=\frac{1}{2}+$ $\frac{1}{6}=\frac{2}{3},\therefore\cos(2\alpha+2\beta)=\cos[2(\alpha+\beta)]=1-$ $2\sin^{2}(\alpha+\beta)=1-2\times\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{2}=\frac{1}{9}$ ，故选B.
> >
> > 代 1.C 【解析】由已知等式, 得 $\sin \alpha \cos \beta + \sin \beta \cos \alpha + \cos \alpha \cos \beta - \sin \alpha \sin \beta = 2\sqrt{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} (\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha) \sin \beta$ , 整理得 $\sin \alpha \cos \beta - \sin \beta \cos \alpha + \cos \alpha \cos \beta + \sin \alpha \sin \beta = 0$ , 即 $\sin (\alpha - \beta) + \cos (\alpha - \beta) = 0$ , 所以 $\tan (\alpha - \beta) = -1$ , 故选 C.
