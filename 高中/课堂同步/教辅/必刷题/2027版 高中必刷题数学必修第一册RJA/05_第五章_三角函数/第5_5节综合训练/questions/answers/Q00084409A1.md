> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{1}{16}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由和差化积公式得 $f(x) =$ $\cos 3x - \cos 2x = -2\sin \frac{3x + 2x}{2}\sin \frac{3x - 2x}{2} =$ $-2\sin \frac{5x}{2}\sin \frac{x}{2},$ 令 $-2\sin \frac{5x}{2}\sin \frac{x}{2} = 0,$ 当 $\sin \frac{x}{2} = 0$ 时， $\because x\in (0,\pi),\therefore \frac{x}{2}\in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，此时无解；当 $\sin \frac{5x}{2} = 0$ 时， $\because x\in (0,\pi),\therefore \frac{5x}{2}\in$ $\left(0,\frac{5\pi}{2}\right)$ ，则 $\frac{5x}{2} = \pi$ 或 $\frac{5x}{2} = 2\pi$ ，解得 $x = \frac{2\pi}{5}$ 或 $x = \frac{4\pi}{5},\therefore x_{1} = \frac{2\pi}{5},x_{2} = \frac{4\pi}{5}.$ $\cos \frac{x_1}{2}\cos x_1\cos 2x_1\cos 4x_1$ $= \cos \frac{\pi}{5}\cos \frac{2\pi}{5}\cdot \cos \frac{4\pi}{5}\cos \frac{8\pi}{5}$
> >
> > $\begin{aligned} & = \frac{\sin \frac{\pi}{5}\cos \frac{\pi}{5}\cos \frac{2\pi}{5}\cos \frac{4\pi}{5}\cos \frac{8\pi}{5}}{\sin \frac{\pi}{5}}\\ & = \frac{\sin \frac{2\pi}{5}\cos \frac{2\pi}{5}\cos \frac{4\pi}{5}\cos \frac{8\pi}{5}}{2\sin \frac{\pi}{5}}\\ & = \frac{\sin \frac{4\pi}{5}\cos \frac{4\pi}{5}\cos \frac{8\pi}{5}}{4\sin \frac{\pi}{5}}\\ & = \frac{\sin \frac{8\pi}{5}\cos \frac{8\pi}{5}}{8\sin \frac{\pi}{5}} = \frac{\sin \frac{16\pi}{5}}{16\sin \frac{\pi}{5}} = -\frac{1}{16}. \end{aligned}$
