> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{12}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可得 $3\cos \alpha + 2\sin \alpha = c, 3\cos \beta + 2\sin \beta = c$ ，两式作差可得， $3(\cos \alpha - \cos \beta) + 2(\sin \alpha - \sin \beta) = 0$ ，由和差化积公式可得 $-6\sin \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}\sin \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} + 4\cos \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}\sin \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} = 0$ ，整理得 $\sin \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} \cdot \left(2\cos \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} - 3\sin \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}\right) = 0$ ，所以 $\sin \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} = 0$ 或 $2\cos \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} - 3\sin \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} = 0$ ，即 $\sin \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} = 0$ 或 $\tan \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} = \frac{2}{3}$ . 当 $\sin \frac{\alpha - \beta}{2} = 0$ 时， $\alpha - \beta = 2m\pi, m \in \mathbf{Z}$ ，与题设 $\alpha - \beta \neq k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}$ 矛盾，舍去；当 $\tan \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} = \frac{2}{3}$ 时， $\tan (\alpha + \beta) = \frac{2\tan\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}}{1 - \tan^2\frac{\alpha + \beta}{2}} = \frac{2\times\frac{2}{3}}{1 - \frac{4}{9}} = \frac{12}{5}$ .
