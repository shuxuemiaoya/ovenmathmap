> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10},\alpha$ 是第二象限角，可知 $\cos\alpha=-\sqrt{1-\sin^{2}\alpha}=-\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$ ，
> > 所以 $\tan\alpha=\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=-\frac{1}{3}$ ,
> > 所以 $\tan 2\alpha = \frac{2\tan \alpha}{1 - \tan^{2}\alpha} = \frac{-\frac{2}{3}}{1 - \frac{1}{9}} = -\frac{3}{4}.$ 9. $-\frac{11}{5}$ 【解析】因为 $\tan\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=$ $\frac{\sin\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)}{\cos\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{2}\right)} = \frac{-\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha} = 2$ ，所以 $\cos \alpha = -2\sin \alpha$ ，
> > 则 $3\sin 2\alpha + \sin^2\alpha = \frac{3\sin 2\alpha + \sin^2\alpha}{1} = \frac{6\sin \alpha \cos \alpha + \sin^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{6\sin \alpha (-2\sin \alpha) + \sin^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + (-2\sin \alpha)^2} = \frac{-12\sin^2\alpha + \sin^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + 4\sin^2\alpha} = \frac{-11\sin^2\alpha}{5\sin^2\alpha} = -\frac{11}{5}$ .
