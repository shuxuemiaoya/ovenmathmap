> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{17\sqrt{2}}{50}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\theta$ 为锐角， $\cos (\theta + 15^{\circ}) = \frac{3}{5}, \therefore \sin (\theta + 15^{\circ}) = \frac{4}{5}.$ $\therefore \sin (2\theta + 30^{\circ}) = 2\sin (\theta + 15^{\circ}) \cos (\theta + 15^{\circ}) = \frac{24}{25},$ $\cos (2\theta + 30^{\circ}) = 2\cos^{2} (\theta + 15^{\circ}) - 1 = 2 \times \frac{9}{25} - 1 = -\frac{7}{25}.$ $\therefore \cos (2\theta - 15^{\circ}) = \cos (2\theta + 30^{\circ} - 45^{\circ}) = \cos (2\theta + 30^{\circ}) \cos 45^{\circ} + \sin (2\theta + 30^{\circ}) \sin 45^{\circ} = -\frac{7}{25} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{24}{25} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{17\sqrt{2}}{50}.$ 易错警示 不能将“ $2\theta - 15^{\circ}$ ”转化为特殊角及已知角的和、差，导致无法求解或错解.
