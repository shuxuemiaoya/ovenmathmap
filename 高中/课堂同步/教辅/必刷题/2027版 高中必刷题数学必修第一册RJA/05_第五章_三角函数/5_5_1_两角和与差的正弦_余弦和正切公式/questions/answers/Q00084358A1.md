> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)若 $\alpha$ 为锐角， $\tan \alpha = 2$ ，即 $\frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = 2$ ，又 $\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha = 1$ ，两式联立解得 $\sin \alpha = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}, \cos \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ .
> >
> > 又 $\beta$ 为锐角，所以 $-\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha - \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ，由 $\sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$ ，可得 $\cos (\alpha - \beta) = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2(\alpha - \beta)} = \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}$ ,
> >
> > 则 $\tan (\alpha - \beta) = \frac{1}{3}$ ，故 $\tan \beta = \tan [\alpha - (\alpha - \beta)] = \frac{\tan \alpha - \tan (\alpha - \beta)}{1 + \tan \alpha \tan (\alpha - \beta)} = \frac{2 - \frac{1}{3}}{1 + 2 \times \frac{1}{3}} = 1$ .
> >
> > (2)由 $f(\alpha) = \frac{\sin (\alpha - \pi)\tan (5\pi + \alpha)\cos (\pi + \alpha)}{\tan (2\pi - \alpha)\cos (-\frac{3\pi}{2} - \alpha)} = \frac{-\sin \alpha\tan \alpha(-\cos \alpha)}{-\tan \alpha\sin \alpha} = -\cos \alpha,$
> >
> > 又 $\alpha$ 是第三象限角，且 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = 2,$ $\sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1$ ，两式联立解得 $\cos \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5},$ 所以 $f(\alpha) = -\cos \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
