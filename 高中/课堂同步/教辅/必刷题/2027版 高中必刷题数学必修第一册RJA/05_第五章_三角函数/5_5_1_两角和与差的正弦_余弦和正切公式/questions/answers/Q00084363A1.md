> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{13}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because 8\cos(2\alpha+\beta)+5\cos\beta=8[\cos(\alpha+\beta)\cos\alpha-\sin(\alpha+\beta)\sin\alpha]+5[\cos(\alpha+\beta)\cos\alpha+\sin(\alpha+\beta)\sin\alpha]=13\cos(\alpha+\beta)\cos\alpha-3\sin(\alpha+\beta)\sin\alpha=0,$ $\therefore 3\sin(\alpha+\beta)\sin\alpha=13\cos(\alpha+\beta)\cos\alpha,$ $\therefore \tan(\alpha+\beta)\tan\alpha=\frac{13}{3}.$ 易错警示 不能利用“ $2\alpha+\beta=(\alpha+\beta)+\alpha,\beta=(\alpha+\beta)-\alpha$ ”展开三角函数式,导致错解.
