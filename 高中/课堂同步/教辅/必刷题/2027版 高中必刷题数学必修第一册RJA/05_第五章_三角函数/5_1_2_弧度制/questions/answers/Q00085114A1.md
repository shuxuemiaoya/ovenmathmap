> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\alpha < \gamma < \beta$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可得 $\beta = 3 \times \left(\frac{180}{\pi}\right)^{\circ} \approx 3 \times 57.30^{\circ} = 171.9^{\circ}, \gamma = \frac{5\pi}{6} \times \left(\frac{180}{\pi}\right)^{\circ} = 150^{\circ}$ , 所以 $\alpha < \gamma < \beta$ .
