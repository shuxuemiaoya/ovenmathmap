> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-2\tan \alpha$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\frac{2\sin(2\pi-\alpha)\cos(\pi+\alpha)\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\alpha\right)\cos\left(\frac{11\pi}{2}-\alpha\right)}{\cos(\pi-\alpha)\sin(3\pi-\alpha)\sin(-\pi-\alpha)\sin\left(\frac{9\pi}{2}+\alpha\right)}\frac{2(-\sin\alpha)(-\cos\alpha)(-\sin\alpha)(-\sin\alpha)}{-\cos\alpha\sin\alpha\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}=-2\tan\alpha.$
