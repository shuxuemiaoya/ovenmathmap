> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 原式 $= \sin (90^{\circ} + 5^{\circ}) + \cos (180^{\circ} + 5^{\circ}) + \tan (180^{\circ} + 60^{\circ}) = \cos 5^{\circ} + (-\cos 5^{\circ}) + \tan 60^{\circ} = \tan 60^{\circ} = \sqrt{3}$ .
