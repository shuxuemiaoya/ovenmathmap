> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D 【解析】由题意 $\frac{\cos(-585^{\circ})}{\tan585^{\circ}+\sin570^{\circ}}=\frac{\cos(135^{\circ}-360^{\circ}\times2)}{\tan(45^{\circ}+180^{\circ}\times3)+\sin(210^{\circ}+360^{\circ})}$ $= \frac{\cos 135^{\circ}}{\tan 45^{\circ} + \sin 210^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{\cos(45^{\circ} + 90^{\circ})}{\tan 45^{\circ} + \sin(30^{\circ} + 180^{\circ})}$ $= \frac{-\sin 45^{\circ}}{\tan 45^{\circ} - \sin 30^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{1 - \frac{1}{2}} = -\sqrt{2}$ ，故选D.
