> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C 【解析】因为 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角, 所以 $2k\pi+\frac{\pi}{2}<\alpha<2k\pi+\pi,k\in\mathbb{Z}$ ,
> > 所以 $2k\pi+\frac{\pi}{6}<\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}<2k\pi+\frac{2\pi}{3},k\in\mathbb{Z}$ ,
> > 所以 $\sin\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)>0,$ 又因为 $\cos\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{2}{3},$ 所以 $\sin\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)}=\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}.$ 所以 $\cos\left(\frac{11\pi}{6}-\alpha\right)=\cos\left[\frac{3\pi}{2}-\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right]=-\sin\left(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=-\frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
