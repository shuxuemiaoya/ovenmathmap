> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{7}{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{17\pi}{6}\right)=\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{5\pi}{6}+2\pi\right)=\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)$ ，而 $2\alpha+\frac{5\pi}{6}=2\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)+\frac{\pi}{2}$ ,
> > 于是 $\sin\left(2\alpha+\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)=\sin\left[2\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)+\frac{\pi}{2}\right]=$ $\cos\left[2\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right]=2\cos^{2}\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)-1=2\times\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{2}-1=-\frac{7}{9}.$
