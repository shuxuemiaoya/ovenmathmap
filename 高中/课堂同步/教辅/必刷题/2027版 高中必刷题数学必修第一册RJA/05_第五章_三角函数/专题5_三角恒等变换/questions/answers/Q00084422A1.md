> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】因为 $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{3}{5}$ ，所以 $\cos^{2}\alpha = \frac{1 + \cos 2\alpha}{2} = \frac{1}{2} \times \left(1 + \frac{3}{5}\right) = \frac{4}{5}$ , $\sin^{2}\alpha=1-\cos^{2}\alpha=\frac{1}{5}$ 所以 $\sin^{4}\alpha+\cos^{4}\alpha=\left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^{2}=\frac{17}{25}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
