> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)由题知 $g(x)=\sin^{4}x+\cos^{4}x=(\sin^{2}x+\cos^{2}x)^{2}-2\sin^{2}x\cos^{2}x=1-\frac{1}{2}\sin^{2}2x.$ 因为 $x \in \left[\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{3\pi}{8}\right]$ ，所以 $2x \in \left[\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right]$ ，
> > 故 $\sin 2x \in \left[\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, 1\right]$ ,
> > 从而 $\frac{1}{2}\leqslant1-\frac{1}{2}\sin^{2}2x\leqslant\frac{3}{4}$ ,
> > 即 $g(x)\in\left[\frac{1}{2},\frac{3}{4}\right]$ ,
> > 所以 $g(x)$ 在 $\left[\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{3\pi}{8}\right]$ 上的最大值为 $\frac{3}{4}$ ,
> > 最小值为 $\frac{1}{2}$ .
> > (2) $f\left(\frac{\pi}{180}\right)+f\left(\frac{2\pi}{180}\right)+f\left(\frac{3\pi}{180}\right)+\cdots+f\left(\frac{88\pi}{180}\right)+f\left(\frac{89\pi}{180}\right)$ $=g\left(\frac{\pi}{180}\right)+g\left(\frac{2\pi}{180}\right)+g\left(\frac{3\pi}{180}\right)+\cdots+g\left(\frac{44\pi}{180}\right)+\sin^{4}\frac{45\pi}{180}$ $=44-\frac{1}{2}\left(\sin^{2}\frac{2\pi}{180}+\sin^{2}\frac{4\pi}{180}+\sin^{2}\frac{6\pi}{180}+\cdots+\sin^{2}\frac{88\pi}{180}\right)+\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^{4}$ $=44-\frac{1}{2}\times22+\frac{1}{4}$ $=\frac{133}{4}.$
