> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\sin\theta\cos\theta<0,\\ \sin\theta-\cos\theta>0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\sin\theta>0,\\ \cos\theta<0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以角 $\theta$ 是第二象限角.
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
