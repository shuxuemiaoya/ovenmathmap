> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{57}}{2} + 4$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $OP^2 = 16\cos^2\left(t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + 9\cos^2\left(t + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ $= 16\cos^2\left(t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + 9\cos^2\left[\left(t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ $= 16\cos^2\left(t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) + 9\sin^2\left(t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ $= 9 + 7\cos^2\left(t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ . 当 $t \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{3}\right]$ 时， $t - \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{6}\right]$ ， $\cos \left(t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \in \left[\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, 1\right], \cos^2\left(t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \in \left[\frac{3}{4}, 1\right]$ ， $\therefore OP^2 \in \left[\frac{57}{4}, 16\right]$ ， $\therefore |OP|_{\min} = \frac{\sqrt{57}}{2}, |OP|_{\max} = 4.$ 所求之和为 $\frac{\sqrt{57}}{2} + 4.$
