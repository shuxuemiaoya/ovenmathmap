>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】由题意可知, 函数 $f(x)$ 的最小正周期 $T = \frac{\pi}{\omega} = \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 解得 $\omega = 2$ , 则 $f(x) = \tan 2x$ , 故 $f\left(\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \tan \frac{2\pi}{3} = \tan \left(\pi - \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\tan \frac{\pi}{3} = -\sqrt{3}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
