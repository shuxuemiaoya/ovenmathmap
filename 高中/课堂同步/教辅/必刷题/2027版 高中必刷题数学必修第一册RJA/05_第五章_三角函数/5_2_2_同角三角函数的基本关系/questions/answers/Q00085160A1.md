> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{17}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \sin \theta, \cos \theta$ 是关于 $x$ 的方程 $3x^{2}-x+t=0 (t \in \mathbf{R})$ 的两根， $\therefore \sin \theta+\cos \theta=\frac{1}{3}.$ $\because (\sin \theta+\cos \theta)^{2}+(\sin \theta-\cos \theta)^{2}=2,$ $\therefore (\cos \theta-\sin \theta)^{2}=2-\frac{1}{9}=\frac{17}{9},$ $\therefore \cos \theta-\sin \theta=\pm\frac{\sqrt{17}}{3}.$
> >
> > $\because \theta$ 是第二象限角， $\therefore \sin \theta >0,\cos \theta < 0,$ $\therefore \cos \theta -\sin \theta < 0,\therefore \cos \theta -\sin \theta = -\frac{\sqrt{17}}{3}.$
