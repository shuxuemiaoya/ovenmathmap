> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C【解析】∵ $\alpha$ 为第二象限角，
> > . $\tan \alpha \cdot \sqrt{\frac{1}{\sin^{2}\alpha} - 1} = \tan \alpha$ $\sqrt{\frac{1-\sin^{2}\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha}}=\tan\alpha\times\frac{-\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}=-1$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
