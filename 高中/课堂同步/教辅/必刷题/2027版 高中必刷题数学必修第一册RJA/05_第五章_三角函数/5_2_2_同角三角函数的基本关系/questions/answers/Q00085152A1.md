>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $f(\tan x)=\frac{1}{\cos^{2}x}=\frac{\sin^{2}x+\cos^{2}x}{\cos^{2}x}=$ $\tan^{2}x+1$ , 当 $\tan x=-\sqrt{3}$ 时, $f(-\sqrt{3})=3+1=4$ 巧思： $\tan x = -\sqrt{3}$ ，则 $\cos x = \pm \frac{1}{2}$ , $f(-\sqrt{3})=\frac{1}{\left(\pm-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}}=4$
