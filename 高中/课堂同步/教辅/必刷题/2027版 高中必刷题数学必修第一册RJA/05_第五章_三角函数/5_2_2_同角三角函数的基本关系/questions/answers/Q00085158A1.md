> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】因为 $\tan\theta+\frac{1}{\tan\theta}=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}+\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}=\frac{\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}=\frac{1}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}=6$ ，所以 $\sin\theta\cos\theta=\frac{1}{6}$ ，
> > 所以 $(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}=1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\frac{4}{3}.$ 又 $\theta \in (0, \pi)$ , $\sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac{1}{6} > 0$ , $\sin \theta > 0$ ,
> > 则 $\cos \theta > 0$ ,
> > 所以 $\sin\theta+\cos\theta=\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
