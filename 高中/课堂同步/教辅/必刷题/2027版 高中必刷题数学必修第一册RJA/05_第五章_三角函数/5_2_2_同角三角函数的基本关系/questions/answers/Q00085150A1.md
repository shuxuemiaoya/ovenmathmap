> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A【解析】因为 $\frac{2\sin\alpha - \cos\alpha}{\cos\alpha + 2\sin\alpha} = \frac{2\tan\alpha - 1}{1 + 2\tan\alpha} = \frac{3}{5}$ ，所以 $\tan \alpha = 2$ ，可得 $2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha + 3\cos^2\alpha = \frac{2\sin\alpha \cos\alpha + 3\cos^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{2\tan\alpha + 3}{\tan^2\alpha + 1} = \frac{2\times 2 + 3}{4 + 1} = \frac{7}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
