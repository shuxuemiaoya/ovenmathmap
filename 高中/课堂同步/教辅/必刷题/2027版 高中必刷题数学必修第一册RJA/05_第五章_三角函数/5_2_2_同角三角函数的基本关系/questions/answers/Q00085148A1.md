> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B【解析】因为 $20^{\circ}$ 是第一象限角，余弦值为正数，所以 $\cos 20^{\circ} = \sqrt{1 - \sin^{2}20^{\circ}} = \sqrt{1 - m^{2}}$ ，则 $\tan 20^{\circ} = \frac{\sin 20^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}} = \frac{m}{\sqrt{1 - m^{2}}}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
