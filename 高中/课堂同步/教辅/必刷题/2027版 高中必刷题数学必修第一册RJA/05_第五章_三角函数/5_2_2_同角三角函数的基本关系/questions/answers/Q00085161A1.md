> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because 1 + 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = (\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha)^2, 1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = (\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)^2,$ $\rightarrow$ 黑板：遇到 $1 \pm 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha$ 的形式可考虑用 $\sin^2 \alpha + \cos^2 \alpha$ 替换 1，从而化成完全平方式的形式 $\therefore \sqrt{1 + 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha} = |\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha|$ ， $\sqrt{1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha} = |\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha|$ 。
> >
> > 又 $\because \alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right), \therefore \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha > 0,$ $\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha > 0,$ $\therefore \frac{\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha + \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = 4,$ $\therefore \sin \alpha = 2\cos \alpha,$ $\therefore \frac{\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha}{2\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha} = \frac{2\cos \alpha - \cos \alpha}{4\cos \alpha + \cos \alpha} = \frac{1}{5}.$
