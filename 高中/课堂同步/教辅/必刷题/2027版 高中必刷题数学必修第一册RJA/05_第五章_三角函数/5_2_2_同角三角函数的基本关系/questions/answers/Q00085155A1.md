> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \sin^{2} 2x + \cos^{2} 2x = 1$ ， $\therefore \cos^{2} 2x = 1 - \sin^{2} 2x = (1 + \sin 2x)(1 - \sin 2x), \therefore \frac{1 + \sin 2x}{\cos 2x} = \frac{\cos 2x}{1 - \sin 2x} = \frac{1}{2}$ .
