> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D 【解析】由题意可知， $f(x)$ 的最大值为 5，∵ 函数 $f(x)=3\cos(\omega x+\varphi)+2\left(\omega>0,|\varphi|<\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ 的图象与直线 y=5 相邻两个交点间的距离为 $\frac{\pi}{2},\therefore T = \frac{\pi}{2},\omega = \frac{2\pi}{\frac{\pi}{2}} = 4,$ $\rightarrow$ 敬黑板：若三角函数最大值为 $a$ 则其图象与直线 $y = a$ 的两相邻交点间的距离为一个周期即 $f(x) = 3\cos (4x + \varphi) + 2.$ $\forall x\in \left[-\frac{\pi}{12},\frac{\pi}{16}\right],f(x)\geqslant 2$ 恒成立， $\cos (4x + \varphi)\geqslant 0$ 对 $x\in \left[-\frac{\pi}{12},\frac{\pi}{16}\right]$ 成立. $\because x\in \left[-\frac{\pi}{12},\frac{\pi}{16}\right],\therefore 4x + \varphi \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{3} +\varphi ,$ $\frac{\pi}{4} +\varphi ]$ $\because |\varphi | <   \frac{\pi}{2},\therefore -\frac{5\pi}{6} <  -\frac{\pi}{3} +\varphi <  \frac{\pi}{6}, - \frac{\pi}{4} <$ $\frac{\pi}{4} +\varphi <\frac{3\pi}{4},\ldots \left\{ \begin{array}{l} - \frac{\pi}{3} +\varphi \geqslant - \frac{\pi}{2},\\ \frac{\pi}{4} +\varphi \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $-\frac{\pi}{6}\leqslant \varphi \leqslant \frac{\pi}{4}.$
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
