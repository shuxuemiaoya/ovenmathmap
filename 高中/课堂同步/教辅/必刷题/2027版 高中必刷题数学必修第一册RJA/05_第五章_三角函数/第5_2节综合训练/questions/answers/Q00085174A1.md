> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{4}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \sin \alpha = \frac{2m - 5}{m + 1},\cos \alpha =$ $-\frac{m}{m + 1},\therefore$ 由 $\sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1$ ，可得 $\left(\frac{2m - 5}{m + 1}\right)^2 +\left(-\frac{m}{m + 1}\right)^2 = 1,$ 解得 $m = 4$ 或 $m = \frac{3}{2}.$ 又 $\because \alpha$ 为第三象限角， $\therefore \sin \alpha < 0,\cos \alpha < 0,$ 把 $m$ 的值代入检验得 $m = \frac{3}{2},$ 追坑：求得的 $m$ 的值要代入检验，必须符合前提 $\alpha$ 为第三象限角 $\therefore \sin \alpha = -\frac{4}{5},\cos \alpha = -\frac{3}{5},$ 可得 $\tan \alpha =$ $\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{4}{3}.$
