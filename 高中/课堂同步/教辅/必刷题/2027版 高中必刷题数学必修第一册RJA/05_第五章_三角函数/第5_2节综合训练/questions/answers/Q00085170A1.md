> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】∵ $\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}\beta}+\sqrt{1-\sin^{2}\beta}=|\sin\beta|+|\cos\beta|=\sin\beta-\cos\beta,$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}\sin\beta&\geqslant0,\\ \cos\beta&\leqslant0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 又 $\beta\in[0,2\pi),\therefore\frac{\pi}{2}\leqslant\beta\leqslant\pi,\therefore\cos\beta\in[-1,0].$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
