> > 【解】(1) $\because A < 0, \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} -A + b = 3, \\ A + b = -1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} A = -2, \\ b = 1. \end{array} \right.$ 设 $f(x)$ 的最小正周期为 $T$ , 则 $\frac{1}{2} T = \frac{5\pi}{6}$ , $\left(-\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \pi, \therefore T = 2\pi, \omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} = 1.$ 当 $x = \frac{5\pi}{6}$ 时, $f(x) = 3, \therefore \frac{5\pi}{6} + \varphi = -\frac{\pi}{2} + 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}$ , 又 $\because 0 < \varphi < \pi, \therefore \varphi = \frac{2\pi}{3}$ . $\therefore f(x) = -2\sin\left(x + \frac{2\pi}{3}\right) + 1.$ (2) 由 (1) 知 $f(x) = -2\sin\left(x + \frac{2\pi}{3}\right) + 1,$ $\therefore f(x) = 2\sin\left(x - \frac{\pi}{3}\right) + 1, \therefore g(x) = -2\cos\frac{x}{2} + 1.$ 令 $-\pi + 2k\pi \leqslant \frac{1}{2} x \leqslant 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}$ , 解得 $-2\pi + 4k\pi \leqslant x \leqslant 4k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}$ . $\therefore g(x)$ 的单调递减区间为 $[-2\pi + 4k\pi, 4k\pi], k \in \mathbf{Z}$ .
> >
> > 令 $\frac{1}{2} x = k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}, \therefore x = 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}$ . $\therefore g(x)$ 图象的对称轴方程为 $x = 2k\pi, k \in \mathbf{Z}$ .
