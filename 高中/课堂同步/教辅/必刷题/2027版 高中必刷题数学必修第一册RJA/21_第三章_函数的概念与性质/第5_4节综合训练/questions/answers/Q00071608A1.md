> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AB
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > AB 【解析】A 选项, 由 $\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha\right)=\frac{3}{5}$ ,
> > 得 $\sin\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\sin\left[\frac{\pi}{2}-\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha\right)\right]=$ $\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\alpha\right)=\frac{3}{5}$ ，A 正确；
> > B 选项，由 $\cos\left(\frac{9\pi}{2}-\alpha\right)=\cos\left(2\pi\times2+\frac{\pi}{2}-\alpha\right)=\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\alpha\right)=\sin\alpha=\frac{7}{8}$ ，得 $\sin(3\pi+$ $\alpha) = -\sin \alpha = -\frac{7}{8}, B$ 正确；
> > C选项，由 $\sin \alpha = \frac{4}{5}, \alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right)$ ，得 $\cos \alpha = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2 \alpha} = -\frac{3}{5}, \tan \alpha = \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = -\frac{4}{3}$ ，则 $\tan (\pi + \alpha) = \tan \alpha = -\frac{4}{3} \neq -\frac{3}{4}, C$ 错误；
> > D选项，因为 $\alpha \in (0, \pi)$ ，所以 $\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \in \left(\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{4\pi}{3}\right)$ ，又 $\sin \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\frac{1}{3} < 0$ ，所以 $\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} \in \left(\pi, \frac{4\pi}{3}\right)$ ，所以 $\cos \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = -\sqrt{1 - \left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)^2} = -\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ，所以 $\cos \left(\frac{2\pi}{3} - \alpha\right) = \cos \left[\pi - \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)\right] = -\cos \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}, D$ 错误.
> >
> > 故选 **AB**.
