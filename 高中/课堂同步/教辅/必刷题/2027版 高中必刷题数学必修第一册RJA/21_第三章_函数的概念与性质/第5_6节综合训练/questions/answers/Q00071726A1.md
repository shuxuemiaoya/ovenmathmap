> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】由题意得 $g(x)=f\left(x+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=$ $\sin\left[2\left(x+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)-\frac{\pi}{6}\right]-2\cos\left[2\left(x+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)-\frac{\pi}{6}\right]$ $=\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)-2\cos\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{2}\right)=\cos2x+$ $2\sin 2x=\sqrt{5}\sin(2x+\varphi)$ ,
> > 其中 $\varphi$ 为锐角，且 $\tan \varphi = \frac{1}{2}$ .
> >
> > 当 $0 \leqslant x \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2}$ 时， $\varphi \leqslant 2x + \varphi \leqslant \pi + \varphi$ 。因为方程 $g(x) = a$ 在 $\left[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ 内的两根为 $x_1, x_2$ ，根据正弦函数图象的对称性可得 $(2x_1 + \varphi) + (2x_2 + \varphi) = \pi$ ，所以 $x_1 + x_2 = \frac{\pi}{2} - \varphi$ 。所以 $\tan (x_1 + x_2) = \tan \left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \varphi\right) = \frac{\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \varphi\right)}{\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \varphi\right)} = \frac{\cos \varphi}{\sin \varphi} = \frac{1}{\tan \varphi} = 2.$
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
