> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AB
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > AB 【解析】对于 A, $\cos\frac{\pi}{12}\cos\frac{\pi}{6}-\sin\frac{\pi}{12}\sin\frac{\pi}{6}=\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{12}+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=\cos\frac{\pi}{4}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，故 A 正确；
> > 对于 B, $\frac{\tan\frac{\pi}{8}}{1-\tan^{2}\frac{\pi}{8}}=\frac{1}{2}\tan\frac{\pi}{4}=\frac{1}{2}\times1=\frac{1}{2}$ ，故 B 正确；
> > 对于 C, $\frac{1-\tan\frac{\pi}{12}}{1+\tan\frac{\pi}{12}}=\frac{\tan\frac{\pi}{4}-\tan\frac{\pi}{12}}{1+\tan\frac{\pi}{4}\tan\frac{\pi}{12}}=$ $\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\frac{\pi}{12}\right)=\tan\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，故 C 错误；
> > 对于 D, $\cos^{4}\frac{\pi}{8}-\sin^{4}\frac{\pi}{8}=\left(\cos^{2}\frac{\pi}{8}-\sin^{2}\frac{\pi}{8}\right)\left(\cos^{2}\frac{\pi}{8}+\sin^{2}\frac{\pi}{8}\right)=\cos\frac{\pi}{4}\times1=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\neq\frac{1}{2}$ ，故 D 错误.
> >
> > 故选 **AB**.
