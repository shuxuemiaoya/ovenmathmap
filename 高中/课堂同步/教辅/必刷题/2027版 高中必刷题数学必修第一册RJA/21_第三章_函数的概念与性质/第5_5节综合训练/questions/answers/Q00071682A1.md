> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C【解析】因为 $\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) - \sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5},$ 所以 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos \alpha -\frac{1}{2}\sin \alpha -\sin \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos \alpha -$ $\frac{3}{2}\sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5},$ 所以 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin \alpha -\frac{1}{2}\cos \alpha = \sin \left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{6}\right) =$ $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{5}$ ，则 $\sin \left(2\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \cos \left(2\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = 1-$ $2\sin^2\left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = 1 - 2\times \frac{3}{25} = \frac{19}{25}.$
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
