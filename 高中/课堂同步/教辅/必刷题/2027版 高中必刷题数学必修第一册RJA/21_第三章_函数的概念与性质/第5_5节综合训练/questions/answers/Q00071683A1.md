> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C 【解析】因为 $\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)=\cos\frac{\pi}{4}$ $\cos\alpha-\sin\frac{\pi}{4}\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}(\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha),$ $\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)=\frac{1}{3},$ 所以 $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}(\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha)=\frac{1}{3}$ ，故 $\cos\alpha-\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ，
> >
> > 两边平方，可得 $\cos^{2}\alpha-2\cos\alpha\sin\alpha+\sin^{2}\alpha=\frac{2}{9}$ ，又 $\cos^{2}\alpha+\sin^{2}\alpha=1$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\frac{7}{9}$ .
> >
> > 因为 $\frac{1}{\tan\alpha}+\tan\alpha=\frac{\cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha}+\frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}=$ $\frac{\cos^2\alpha + \sin^2\alpha}{\sin\alpha \cdot \cos\alpha} = \frac{1}{\sin\alpha \cdot \cos\alpha},$ 所以 $\frac{1}{\tan\alpha} + \tan \alpha = \frac{18}{7}.$ 故选C.
> >
> > 多种解法 令 $\beta = \frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha$ ，则 $\cos \beta = \frac{1}{3}$ ， $\alpha = \beta - \frac{\pi}{4}, \tan \alpha = \tan \left(\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{\tan \beta - 1}{1 + \tan \beta}$ ，所以 $\frac{1}{\tan \alpha} + \tan \alpha = \frac{1 + \tan \beta}{\tan \beta - 1} + \frac{\tan \beta - 1}{1 + \tan \beta} = \frac{2 (\tan^2 \beta + 1)}{\tan^2 \beta - 1}$ . 又 $\cos \beta = \frac{1}{3}$ ，所以 $\tan^2 \beta = \frac{\sin^2 \beta}{\cos^2 \beta} = \frac{1 - \cos^2 \beta}{\cos^2 \beta} = 8$ ，所以 $\frac{1}{\tan \alpha} + \tan \alpha = \frac{18}{7}$ .
