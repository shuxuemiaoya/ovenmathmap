> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C 【解析】因为 $\frac{1 + \sin 2\alpha}{1 - 2\sin^2\alpha}$ $= \frac{\sin^2\alpha + 2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha + \cos^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha}$ $= \frac{(\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha)^2}{(\cos\alpha + \sin\alpha)(\cos\alpha - \sin\alpha)}$ $= \frac{\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha}{\cos\alpha - \sin\alpha}$ $= \frac{\tan\alpha + 1}{1 - \tan\alpha},$ 所以 $\frac{\tan\alpha + 1}{1 - \tan\alpha} = 7$ ，解得 $\tan \alpha = \frac{3}{4}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
