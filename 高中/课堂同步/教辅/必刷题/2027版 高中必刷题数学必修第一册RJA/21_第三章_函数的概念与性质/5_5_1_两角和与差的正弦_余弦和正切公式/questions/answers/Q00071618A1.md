> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C 【解析】∵ $\sin 54^{\circ} = \sin (90^{\circ} - 36^{\circ}) = \cos 36^{\circ}$ ,
> > ∴ $\sin 36^{\circ}\cos 24^{\circ} + \sin 54^{\circ}\sin 24^{\circ} = \sin 36^{\circ}\cos 24^{\circ} + \cos 36^{\circ}\sin 24^{\circ} = \sin (36^{\circ} + 24^{\circ}) = \sin 60^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
