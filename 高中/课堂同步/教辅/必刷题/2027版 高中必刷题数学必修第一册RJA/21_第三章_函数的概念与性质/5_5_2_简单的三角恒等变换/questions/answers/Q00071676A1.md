> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【原始答案】** $\frac{1}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) = \frac{3}{2} \cos 2\alpha$ ，
> > →巧思：也可由 $= \frac{1 + \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan \alpha} = \frac{3}{2} \times \frac{\cos^2 \alpha - \sin^2 \alpha}{\cos^2 \alpha + \sin^2 \alpha} = \frac{3}{2} \times \frac{1 - \tan^2 \alpha}{1 + \tan^2 \alpha}$ 求解
> > 可得 $\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)} = \frac{3}{2} \cos 2\alpha = \frac{3}{2} \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\right.$ $2\alpha$ ，即 $\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)}{\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)} = 3 \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}+\alpha\right)$ 。
> >
> > $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)$ .
> >
> > 由于 $\alpha \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ , 故 $\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ , 则 $\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) \neq 0$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)} = 3\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right)$ , 则 $\cos^2 \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \alpha\right) = \frac{1}{3}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1 + \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{2} + 2\alpha\right)}{2} = \frac{1 - \sin 2\alpha}{2} = \frac{1}{3}$ , 解得 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{1}{3}$ .
> >
> > 【解】(1) $f(x) = 2\sin^2 x + 2\sqrt{3}\sin (2\pi + x)\cos (\pi - x) = 2\sin^2 x - 2\sqrt{3}\sin x\cos x = 1 - \cos 2x - \sqrt{3}\sin 2x = 1 - 2\sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ .
> >
> > 所以 $f\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = 1 - 2\sin \left(2\times \frac{\pi}{6} + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = -1$ .
> >
> > (2) 因为 $f(\alpha) = -\frac{1}{5}$ , 所以 $1 - 2\sin (2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}) = -\frac{1}{5}$ , 即 $\sin \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{3}{5}$ , 展开得 $\rightarrow$ 心: 可继续求解 $\cos \left(2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ , 然后根据 $\cos 2\alpha = \cos (2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6} - \frac{\pi}{6})$ 利用差角的余弦公式求解 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin 2\alpha + \frac{1}{2}\cos 2\alpha = \frac{3}{5}$ , 即 $5\sqrt{3}\sin 2\alpha + 5\cos 2\alpha = 6$ , 所以 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{6 - 5\cos 2\alpha}{5\sqrt{3}}$ .
> >
> > 又 $\sin^2 2\alpha + \cos^2 2\alpha = 1$ , 所以 $\left(\frac{6 - 5\cos 2\alpha}{5\sqrt{3}}\right)^2 + \cos^2 2\alpha = 1$ , 化简得 $100\cos^2 2\alpha - 60\cos 2\alpha - 39 = 0$ ,
> >
> > 解得 $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{60 \pm \sqrt{3600 + 400 \times 39}}{200} = \frac{3 \pm 4\sqrt{3}}{10}$ , 所以 $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{3 + 4\sqrt{3}}{10}$ 或 $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{3 - 4\sqrt{3}}{10}$ .
> >
> > 又因为 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{5\pi}{12}\right)$ , 所以 $2\alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{5\pi}{6}\right)$ , 所以 $-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} < \cos 2\alpha < \frac{1}{2}$ , 而 $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{3 + 4\sqrt{3}}{10} > \frac{1}{2}$ , 舍去,
> >
> > 而 $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{3 - 4\sqrt{3}}{10}$ 符合题意, 所以 $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{3 - 4\sqrt{3}}{10}$ .
> >
> > ★易错点 不能利用角之间的特殊关系求值
