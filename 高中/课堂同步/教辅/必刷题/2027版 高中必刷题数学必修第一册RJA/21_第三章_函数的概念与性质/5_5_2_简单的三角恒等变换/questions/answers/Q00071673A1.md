> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D 【解析】因为 $\sin 112.5^{\circ} = \sin (90^{\circ} + 22.5^{\circ}) = \cos 22.5^{\circ}, \sin 67.5^{\circ} = \sin (90^{\circ} - 22.5^{\circ}) = \cos 22.5^{\circ}$ ,
> > 所以 $\sin 112.5^{\circ}+\sin 67.5^{\circ}=2\cos 22.5^{\circ}$ .
> > 根据半角公式可得 $\cos 22.5^{\circ}=\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos45^{\circ}}{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{1+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2}}}{2}$ 所以 $\sin 112.5^{\circ} + \sin 67.5^{\circ} = 2\cos 22.5^{\circ} = \sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2}}$ .
> > 故选 D.
> >
> > 5 B 【解析】由 $\tan^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}=$ $\frac{3\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\alpha\right)+\sin\alpha-3}{\sin\alpha},$ 得 $\tan^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}=\frac{3\cos\alpha+\sin\alpha-3}{\sin\alpha}=$ $\frac{2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}-6\sin^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}}{2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}}=1-3\tan\frac{\alpha}{2},$ 则 $\tan\alpha=\frac{2\tan\frac{\alpha}{2}}{1-\tan^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}}=\frac{2}{3}$ ，则 $\cos2\alpha=$ $\frac{\cos^{2}\alpha-\sin^{2}\alpha}{\cos^{2}\alpha+\sin^{2}\alpha}=\frac{1-\tan^{2}\alpha}{1+\tan^{2}\alpha}=\frac{5}{13}.$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
