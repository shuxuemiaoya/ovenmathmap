> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】由题意可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}0.5&=a^{kt_{1}},\\ 0.25&=a^{k(t_{1}+t_{2})}\end{aligned}\right.$ 则 $0.25=a^{k(t_{1}+t_{2})}=a^{kt_{1}}a^{kt_{2}}=0.5a^{kt_{2}}$ ,
> > 则 $a^{kt_{2}}=0.5=a^{kt_{1}}$ ，解得 $t_{1}=t_{2}$ ，
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
