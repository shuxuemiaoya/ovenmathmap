> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】 $\log_{2}\frac{y_{1}+y_{2}}{2}=\log_{2}\frac{2^{x_{1}}+2^{x_{2}}}{2}\geqslant$ $\log_{2}\sqrt{2^{x_{1}}\cdot2^{x_{2}}}=\log_{2}2^{\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}}=\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2},\because x_{1}\neq x_{2},\therefore$ 等号取不到，即 $\log_{2}\frac{y_{1}+y_{2}}{2}>\frac{x_{1}+x_{2}}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
