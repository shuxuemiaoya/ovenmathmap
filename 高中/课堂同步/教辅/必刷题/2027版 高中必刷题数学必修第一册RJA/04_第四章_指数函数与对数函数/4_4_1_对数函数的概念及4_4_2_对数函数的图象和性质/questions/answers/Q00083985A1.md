> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $[2, +\infty)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $t = \sqrt{x^2 + ax + 5}$ , 则 $\log_{\frac{1}{a}}(t + 1) \cdot \log_5(t^2 + 1) + \log_a 3 = 0$ . $\therefore \log_5(t^2 + 1) = -\frac{\log_a 3}{\log_{\frac{1}{a}}(t + 1)} = \log_{t + 1} 3$ , 其只有一根 $t = 2$ , 则 $x^2 + ax + 5 = 4$ , 即 $x^2 + ax + 1 = 0$ 有解, $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \Delta = a^2 - 4 \geqslant 0, \\ a > 0 \text{且} a \neq 1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $a \in [2, +\infty)$ .
