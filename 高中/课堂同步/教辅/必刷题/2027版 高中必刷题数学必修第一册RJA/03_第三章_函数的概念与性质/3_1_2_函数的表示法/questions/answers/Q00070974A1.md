> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{8}{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 令 $\frac{2x}{x + 1} = t (t \neq 2)$ , 则 $x = \frac{t}{2 - t}$ , 将其代入 $f\left(\frac{2x}{x + 1}\right) = x^2 - 1$ 中得 $f(t) = \left(\frac{t}{2 - t}\right)^2 - 1$ , 即 $f(x) = \left(\frac{x}{2 - x}\right)^2 - 1$ , 则 $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = -\frac{8}{9}$ . 多种解法 令 $\frac{2x}{x + 1} = \frac{1}{2}$ , 解得 $x = \frac{1}{3}$ , 则 $f\left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 - 1 = -\frac{8}{9}$ .
