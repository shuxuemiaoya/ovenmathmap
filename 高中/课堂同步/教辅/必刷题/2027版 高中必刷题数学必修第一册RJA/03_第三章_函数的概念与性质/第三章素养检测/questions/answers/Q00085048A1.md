> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)【解】 $f(x)$ 在 $(1,+\infty)$ 上单调递增，证明如下： $\forall x_{1},x_{2}\in(1,+\infty)$ ，且 $x_{1}<x_{2}$ ，则 $f(x_{1})-f(x_{2})=\left(x_{1}^{2}+\frac{2}{x_{1}}\right)-\left(x_{2}^{2}+\frac{2}{x_{2}}\right)=\left(x_{1}^{2}-x_{2}^{2}\right)+\left(\frac{2}{x_{1}}-\frac{2}{x_{2}}\right)=\left(x_{1}-x_{2}\right)\left[(x_{1}+x_{2})-\frac{2}{x_{1}x_{2}}\right]$ $\because 1<x_{1}<x_{2},\therefore x_{1}-x_{2}<0,x_{1}x_{2}>1,x_{1}+x_{2}>2>\frac{2}{x_{1}x_{2}},$ $x_{1}+x_{2}-\frac{2}{x_{1}x_{2}}>0,\therefore f(x_{1})-f(x_{2})<0,$ $\therefore f(x)$ 在 $(1,+\infty)$ 上单调递增.
> > (2)【证明】由 $f(m)=f(n)$ 得 $m^{2}+\frac{2}{m}=n^{2}+\frac{2}{n},$ 化简得 $m^{2}-n^{2}=\frac{2}{n}-\frac{2}{m}=\frac{2(m-n)}{mn},$ 又 $m-n\neq0,\therefore mn(m+n)=2,$ $\therefore m+n>2\sqrt{mn},$ $\therefore 2=mn(m+n)>2(\sqrt{mn})^{3},$ $\therefore mn<1.$ (3)【解】不妨设存在满足题意的实数a,b, $\because 0\notin[a,b],\therefore a<b<0$ 或0<a<b,
