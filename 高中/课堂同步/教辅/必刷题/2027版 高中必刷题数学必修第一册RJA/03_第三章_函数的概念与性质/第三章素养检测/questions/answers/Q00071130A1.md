>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】因为函数 $f(x)$ 在 R 上单调递
> > 减,所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&m\leqslant\frac{m^{2}+m}{2},\\ &m^{2}-(m^{2}+m)m\geqslant-\frac{1}{2}m,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}m&\leqslant0\text{或}m\geqslant1,\\ m&\leqslant-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\text{或}0\leqslant m\leqslant\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},\end{aligned}\right.$ 则 $m \in \left(-\infty, -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right] \cup \{0\}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
