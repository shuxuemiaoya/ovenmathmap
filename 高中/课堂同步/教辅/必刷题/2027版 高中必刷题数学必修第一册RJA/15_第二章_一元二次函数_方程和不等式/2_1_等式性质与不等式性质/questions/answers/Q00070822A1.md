> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $p \geqslant 4q$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $p = m^2 - m + 2 = \left(m - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{7}{4} > 0, q = \frac{1}{m^2 + m + 2} = \frac{1}{\left(m + \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{7}{4}} > 0$ ，所以 $\frac{p}{q} = (m^2 - m + 2) = (m^2 + m + 2)^2 - m^2 = m^4 + 3m^2 + 4 \geqslant 4$ ，故 $p \geqslant 4q$ ，当且仅当 $m = 0$ 时取等号。
