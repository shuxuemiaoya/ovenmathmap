> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】∵ $2x + 3y + 6xy = \frac{5}{4}$ ，∴ $(2y + 1)(3y + 1) = \frac{9}{4}$ ，又∵ x > 0, y > 0, ∴ $2x + 1 > 0$ , $3y + 1 > 0$ ，故 $2x + 3y = (2x + 1) + (3y + 1) - 2 \geqslant 2\sqrt{(2x + 1)(3y + 1)} - 2 = 1$ ，当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&2x+1=3y+1,\\ &(2x+1)(3y+1)=\frac{9}{4},\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x=\frac{1}{4},\\ &y=\frac{1}{6}\end{aligned}\right.$ 时等号成立. 故选 A.
> > 多种解法 （消元法）∵ $2x + 3y + 6xy = \frac{5}{4}$ ,
> > ∴ $2x(1+3y)=\frac{5}{4}-3y$ , 又∵ y>0, ∴ $1+3y>0$ ,
> > ∴ $2x=\frac{\frac{5}{4}-3y}{1+3y}=\frac{9}{4(1+3y)}-1$ , ∴ $2x+3y=\frac{9}{4(1+3y)}-1+3y=\frac{9}{4(1+3y)}+1+3y-2 \geqslant 2\sqrt{\frac{9}{4(1+3y)}\cdot(1+3y)}-2=1$ , 当且仅当 $\frac{9}{4(1+3y)}=1+3y$ , 即 $y=\frac{1}{6}$ 时等号成立.
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
