> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 【解】(1)由 $x > 0, y > 0, x + 8y = xy$ 可得 $\frac{8}{x} + \frac{1}{y} = 1$ , 所以 $x + 2y = (x + 2y)\left(\frac{8}{x} + \frac{1}{y}\right) = \frac{16y}{x} + \frac{x}{y} + 10 \geqslant 2\sqrt{\frac{16y}{x} \cdot \frac{x}{y}} + 10 = 18$ , 当且仅当 $\frac{16y}{x} = \frac{x}{y}, \frac{8}{x} + \frac{1}{y} = 1$ , 即 $x = 4y = 12$ 时等号成立. 因此 $x + 2y$ 的最小值为 18. (2) 因为 $x > 1$ , 所以 $x - 1 > 0, \frac{4}{x - 1} > 0$ , 所以 $y = \frac{x^2 + 3x}{x - 1} = \frac{(x - 1)(x + 4) + 4}{x - 1} = x + 4 + \frac{4}{x - 1} = x - 1 + \frac{4}{x - 1} + 5 \geqslant 2\sqrt{(x - 1) \cdot \frac{4}{x - 1}} + 5 = 9$ , 当且仅当 $x - 1 = \frac{4}{x - 1}$ , 即 $x = 3$ 时等号成立. 因此 $y = \frac{x^2 + 3x}{x - 1}$ 的最小值为 9. (3) $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + 4ab + \frac{7}{2}bc + \frac{7}{2}ac$ $= (a + b + c)^2 + 2ab + \frac{3}{2}bc + \frac{3}{2}ac$ $= 1 + 2ab + \frac{3}{2}bc + \frac{3}{2}ac$ $= 1 + 2ab + \frac{3}{2}c(a + b)$ $\leqslant 1 + \frac{1}{2}(a + b)^2 + \frac{3}{2}c(a + b)$ $= 1 + \frac{1}{2}(1 - c)^2 + \frac{3}{2}c(1 - c)$
> >
> > $= -c^{2} + \frac{1}{2} c + \frac{3}{2} = -\left(c - \frac{1}{4}\right)^{2} + \frac{25}{16},$ 故当且仅当 $\begin{cases} a = b, \\ c = \frac{1}{4}, \\ a + b + c = 1, \end{cases}$ 即 $\begin{cases} a = b = \frac{3}{8}, \\ c = \frac{1}{4} \end{cases}$ 时， $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 + 4ab + \frac{7}{2} bc + \frac{7}{2} ac$ 取最大值 $\frac{25}{16}$ .
