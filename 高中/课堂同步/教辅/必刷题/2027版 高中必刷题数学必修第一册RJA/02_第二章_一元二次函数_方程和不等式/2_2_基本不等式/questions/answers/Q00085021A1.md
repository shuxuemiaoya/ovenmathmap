> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C 【解析】∵ $2a+b=6, a>0, b>0$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{a} + \frac{2}{b} = \frac{1}{6}\left(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{2}{b}\right)(2a + b) = \frac{1}{6} \times \left(4 + \frac{4a}{b} + \frac{b}{a}\right) \geqslant \frac{1}{6} \times (4 + 2\sqrt{4}) = \frac{4}{3}$ ,
> > 当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}b&=2a,\\ 2a+b&=6,\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=\frac{3}{2},\\ b&=3\end{aligned}\right.$ 时取等号 $\therefore \frac{1}{a}+\frac{2}{b}$ 的最小值为 $\frac{4}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
