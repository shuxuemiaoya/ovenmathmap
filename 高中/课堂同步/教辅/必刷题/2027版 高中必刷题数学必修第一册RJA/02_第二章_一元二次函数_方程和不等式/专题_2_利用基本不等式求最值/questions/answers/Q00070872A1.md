> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > C 【解析】因为 $-2 < x < \frac{4}{3}$ , 所以 $4 - 3x > 0$ , $4 + 2x > 0$ .
> > 设 $4 - 3x = a > 0, 4 + 2x = b > 0$ ,
> > 则 $2a + 3b = 20$ , 即 $\frac{a}{10} + \frac{3b}{20} = 1$ ,
> > 故 $\frac{2}{4 - 3x} + \frac{3}{4 + 2x} = \frac{2}{a} + \frac{3}{b} = \left(\frac{2}{a} + \frac{3}{b}\right)$ $\left(\frac{a}{10} + \frac{3b}{20}\right) = \frac{1}{5} + \frac{3b}{10a} + \frac{3a}{10b} + \frac{9}{20} \geqslant \frac{13}{20} + 2\sqrt{\frac{3b}{10a} \cdot \frac{3a}{10b}} = \frac{5}{4}$ ,
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{3b}{10a} = \frac{3a}{10b}$ , 即 $a = b \Rightarrow 4 - 3x = 4 + 2x \Rightarrow x = 0$ 时等号成立, 则 $\frac{2}{4 - 3x} + \frac{3}{4 + 2x}$ 的最小值为 $\frac{5}{4}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **C**.
