> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D 【解析】由题意可得 $\left|PF_{1}\right| + \left|PF_{2}\right| = 2a_{1}, \left|PF_{1}\right| - \left|PF_{2}\right| = 2a_{2}$ ，两式相减得 $2a_{1} - 2a_{2} = 2\left|PF_{2}\right| = \left|F_{1}F_{2}\right| = 2c$ ，所以 $\frac{1}{e_{1}} - \frac{1}{e_{2}} = 1$ ，即 $e_{1} = \frac{e_{2}}{e_{2} + 1}$ ，所以 $e_{1} + e_{2} = \frac{e_{2}}{e_{2} + 1} + e_{2} = \frac{e_{2}^{2} + 2e_{2}}{e_{2} + 1} = (e_{2} + 1) - \frac{1}{e_{2} + 1}$ ，令 $e_{2} + 1 = k$ ，则 k > 2, $e_{1} + e_{2} = k - \frac{1}{e_{2} + 1}$ ，设 $f(k) = k - \frac{1}{k}$ ，且函数 $f(k)$ 在 [174]
> >
> > $(2,+\infty)$ 上单调递增，则 $f(k)>f(2)=\frac{3}{2}$ ，所以 $e_{1}+e_{2}$ 的取值范围是 $\left(\frac{3}{2},+\infty\right)$ .
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
