> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】由题设知 $S_{\triangle F_{1}MF_{2}} = \frac{1}{2}|MF_{1}| \cdot |MF_{2}| \sin \angle F_{1}MF_{2} = \frac{1}{2}|MF_{1}| \cdot |MF_{2}| \sin \frac{2\pi}{3} = \sqrt{3}$ ,
> > 可得 $|MF_{1}|\cdot|MF_{2}|=4$ ，又 $|MF_{1}|=$ $|MF_{2}|$ ，则 $|MF_{1}|=|MF_{2}|=a=2$ 故 $|F_{1}F_{2}|=$ $\sqrt{|MF_{1}|^{2}+|MF_{2}|^{2}-2|MF_{1}|\cdot|MF_{2}|\cos\frac{2\pi}{3}}=2\sqrt{3}$ 所以 $c=\sqrt{3}$ ，所以 $b^{2}=a^{2}-c^{2}=1$ ，故 C 的标准方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{4}+y^{2}=1$ 。故选 A.
> >
> > 二级结论 已知椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} +\frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1(a>$ $b > 0)$ 上一点 $M$ ， $\angle F_1MF_2 = \theta$ ，则 $S_{\triangle F_1MF_2} = b^2\cdot \tan \frac{\theta}{2} = c\cdot |y_M|$ 证明：由椭圆定义得 $|MF_1|+$ $|MF_2| = 2a$ 等号左、右两边平方得 $|MF_1|^2 +$ $|MF_2|^2 +2|MF_1|\cdot |MF_2| = 4a^2$ ，①由余弦定理得 $|MF_1|^2 +|MF_2|^2 -$ $2|MF_1|\cdot |MF_2|\cos \theta = 4c^2$ ，②①-②得 $|MF_1|\cdot |MF_2| = \frac{2b^2}{1 + \cos\theta},$ 故 $S_{\triangle F_1MF_2} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot |MF_1|\cdot |MF_2|\sin \theta =$ $\frac{1}{2}\cdot \frac{2b^2}{1 + \cos\theta}\cdot \sin \theta = b^2\cdot \tan \frac{\theta}{2}.$ 另外 $S_{\triangle F_1MF_2} = \frac{1}{2}\times 2c\cdot |y_M| = c\cdot |y_M|$
