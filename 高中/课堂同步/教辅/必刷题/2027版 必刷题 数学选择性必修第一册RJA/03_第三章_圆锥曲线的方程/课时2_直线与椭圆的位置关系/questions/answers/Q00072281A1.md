> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{2} +y^2 = 1$ 中， $a^2 =$ $2,b^{2} = 1$ ，所以 $c^2 = a^2 -b^2 = 1$ ，即 $c = 1$ 故左焦点为 $F_{1}(-1,0)$ ，而 $\tan 60^{\circ} =$ $\sqrt{3}$ ，故直线 $l$ 的方程为 $y = \sqrt{3}(x + 1)$ 联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\frac{x^2}{2} +y^2 = 1, & \\ y = \sqrt{3} (x + 1), & \end{array} \right.$ 消去 $y$ 整理得 $7x^{2} + 12x + 4 = 0,\Delta = 12^{2} - 4\times 7\times 4 > 0,$ 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，由根与系数的关系得 $x_{1} + x_{2} = -\frac{12}{7},$ $x_{1}x_{2} = \frac{4}{7},$ 则由弦长公式得 $|AB| = \sqrt{1 + (\sqrt{3})^2}\times$ $\sqrt{\left(-\frac{12}{7}\right)^2 - 4\times\frac{4}{7}} = \frac{8\sqrt{2}}{7}.$ 故选B.
