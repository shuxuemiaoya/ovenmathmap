> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由抛物线 $C: x^{2} = 6y$ ，得焦点 $F\left(0, \frac{3}{2}\right)$ ，由题意可设 $A(x_{1}, y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ， $M(x_{0}, y_{0})$ ，由直线 AB 的斜率为 -2，得 $\frac{y_{1}-y_{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}} = \frac{\frac{1}{6}x_{1}^{2} - \frac{1}{6}x_{2}^{2}}{x_{1}-x_{2}} = \frac{1}{6} \cdot \frac{(x_{1}-x_{2})(x_{1}+x_{2})}{x_{1}-x_{2}} = \frac{1}{6}(x_{1}+x_{2}) = -2$ ，解得 $x_{1}+x_{2} = -12$ ，由直线 MA 与直线 MB 的斜率之和为 1，得 $\frac{y_{1}-y_{0}}{x_{1}-x_{0}} + \frac{y_{2}-y_{0}}{x_{2}-x_{0}} = \frac{\frac{1}{6}x_{1}^{2} - \frac{1}{6}x_{0}^{2}}{x_{1}-x_{0}} + \frac{\frac{1}{6}x_{2}^{2} - \frac{1}{6}x_{0}^{2}}{x_{2}-x_{0}} = \frac{1}{6} \cdot \left[\frac{(x_{1}-x_{0})(x_{1}+x_{0})}{x_{1}-x_{0}} + \frac{(x_{2}-x_{0})(x_{2}+x_{0})}{x_{2}-x_{0}}\right] = \frac{1}{6}(x_{1}+x_{0}+x_{2}+x_{0}) = \frac{1}{6}(2x_{0}-12) = 1$ ，解得 $x_{0} = 9$ ，所以 $y_{0} = \frac{1}{6}x_{0}^{2} = \frac{27}{2}$ ，可得 $|MF| = y_{0} + \frac{3}{2} = 15$ .
