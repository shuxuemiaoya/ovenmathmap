> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由抛物线定义可得, $6+\frac{p}{2}=8$ ,解得p=4,所以抛物线C的方程为 $y^{2}=8x$ .
> > (2)由题意可知,直线 l 的斜率不为0,设其方程为 $x=my+2,M(x_{1},y_{1})$ , $N(x_{2},y_{2})$ ,
> >
> > 联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=my+2,\\ y^{2}&=8x,\end{aligned}\right.$ 得 $y^{2}-8my-16=0$ ，则 $\Delta=64m^{2}+64>0,y_{1}+y_{2}=8m,y_{1}y_{2}=-16.$ 因为 $\overrightarrow{MF}=4\overrightarrow{FN}$ ，所以 $(2-x_{1},-y_{1})=4(x_{2}-2,y_{2})$ ，所以 $y_{1}=-4y_{2}$ ，联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y_{1}&=-4y_{2},\\ y_{1}y_{2}&=-16\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $y_{1}=8,y_{2}=-2$ 或 $y_{1}=-8,y_{2}=2$ ，所以 $8m=y_{1}+y_{2}=\pm6\Rightarrow m=\pm\frac{3}{4}$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{FN}\cdot\overrightarrow{FM}=(x_{2}-2,y_{2})\cdot(x_{1}-2,y_{1})=(x_{1}-2)(x_{2}-2)+y_{1}y_{2}$ ，又 $x_{1}=my_{1}+2,x_{2}=my_{2}+2$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{FN}\cdot\overrightarrow{FM}=m^{2}y_{1}y_{2}+y_{1}y_{2}=(m^{2}+1)y_{1}y_{2}=\left(\frac{9}{16}+1\right)\times(-16)=-25.$
