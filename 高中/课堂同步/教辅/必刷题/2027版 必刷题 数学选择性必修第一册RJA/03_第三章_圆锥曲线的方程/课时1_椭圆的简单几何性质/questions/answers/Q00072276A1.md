> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题知 $a=\sqrt{2}, b=c=1$ ，则 $F_{1}(-1,0), F_{2}(1,0)$ 。设 $P(x_{0},y_{0})$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{PF_{1}} = (-1-x_{0}, -y_{0}), \overrightarrow{PF_{2}} = (1-x_{0}, -y_{0}), \therefore \overrightarrow{PF_{1}} + \overrightarrow{PF_{2}} = (-2x_{0}, -2y_{0})$ ，∴ $|\overrightarrow{PF_{1}} + \overrightarrow{PF_{2}}| = \sqrt{4x_{0}^{2} + 4y_{0}^{2}} = 2\sqrt{2 - 2y_{0}^{2} + y_{0}^{2}} = 2\sqrt{-y_{0}^{2} + 2}$ 。∵ 点 P 在椭圆上，∴ $0 \leqslant y_{0}^{2} \leqslant 1$ ，∴ 当 $y_{0}^{2} = 1$ 时， $|\overrightarrow{PF_{1}} + \overrightarrow{PF_{2}}|$ 取得最小值 2。故选 C.
