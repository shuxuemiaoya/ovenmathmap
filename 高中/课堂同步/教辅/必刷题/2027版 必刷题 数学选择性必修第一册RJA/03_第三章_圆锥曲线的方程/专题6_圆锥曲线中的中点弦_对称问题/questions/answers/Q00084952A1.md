> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题易知 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}c^2 = a^2 +b^2,\\ e = \frac{c}{a} = 2, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $b^{2} = 3a^{2}$ ，∴双曲线的方程为 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} -$ $\frac{y^2}{3a^2} = 1.$ 由题知 $A,B$ 关于原点对称，设 $P(x_0,y_0),A(x_1,y_1),B(-x_1, - y_1)$ ，由得 $\frac{x_1^2}{a^2} -\frac{y_1^2}{3a^2} = 1,$ 得 $\frac{y_1^2 - y_0^2}{x_1^2 - x_0^2} = 3.\because$ 直线 $PA,$ $\frac{x_0^2}{a^2} -\frac{y_0^2}{3a^2} = 1,$ $PB$ 的斜率分别为 $k_{1},k_{2}$ ，且 $1\leqslant k_{1}\leqslant$ $\sqrt{2},\therefore k_{1} = \frac{y_{1} - y_{0}}{x_{1} - x_{0}},k_{2} = \frac{-y_{1} - y_{0}}{-x_{1} - x_{0}} = \frac{y_{1} + y_{0}}{x_{1} + x_{0}},$ $k_{1}k_{2}=\frac{y_{1}-y_{0}}{x_{1}-x_{0}}\cdot\frac{y_{1}+y_{0}}{x_{1}+x_{0}}=\frac{y_{1}^{2}-y_{0}^{2}}{x_{1}^{2}-x_{0}^{2}}=3,\therefore k_{2}=$ $\frac{3}{k_{1}}\in\left[\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2},3\right]$ . 故选 B.
> > 快解 由双曲线的第三定义可得 $k_{1}k_{2}=\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}=e^{2}-1=3$ ，且 $k_{1}\in[1,\sqrt{2}]$ , $\therefore k_{2}=\frac{3}{k_{1}}\in\left[\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2},3\right]$ . 故选 B.
> >
> > 二级结论 双曲线的第三定义及其推论
> > 平面内与两定点 $A_{1}(-a,0)$ , $A_{2}(a,0)$ ( $a\neq0$ ) 连线的斜率之积为 $\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}$ 或 $e^{2}-1$ (e>1) 的点的轨迹为双曲线（不包含实轴端点）.
> > 推论: 双曲线上的点 P 与两个关于原点对称的定点 $M(x_{1},y_{1})$ , $N(-x_{1}, -y_{1})$ 连线的斜率之积为 $\frac{b^{2}}{a^{2}}$ 或 $e^{2}-1$ .
