>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $B_{1}A_{2}$ 的中点坐标为 $M\left(\frac{a}{2},\frac{b}{2}\right)$ ，所以直线 $OM$ 的方程为 $y = \frac{b}{a} x.$ 则由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}y = \frac{b}{a} x,\\ \frac{x^2}{a^2} +\frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}x_1 = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} a,\\ y_1 = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} b \end{array} \right.$ 或 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}x_{2} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} a,\\ y_{2} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} b. \end{array} \right.$ 由题意和椭圆的对称性知 $\frac{a^2 + b^2}{2} = \left(\frac{3}{2} b\right)^2$ ，得 $\frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{2}{7}$ ，则 $e=$ $\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{b}{a}\right)^2} = \frac{\sqrt{35}}{7}$ 故选A.
