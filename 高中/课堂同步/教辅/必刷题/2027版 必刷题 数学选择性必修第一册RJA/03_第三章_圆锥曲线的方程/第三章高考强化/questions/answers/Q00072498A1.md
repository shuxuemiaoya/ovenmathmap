> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可设 $P(a, y_0)(y_0 > 0)$ , $\because F(-c, 0), A(a, 0), k_{PF} = \frac{1}{3}, S_{\triangle PFA} = \frac{3}{2}, e = \frac{1}{2},$ $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{y_0}{a + c} = \frac{1}{3}, \\ \frac{1}{2}(a + c) \cdot y_0 = \frac{3}{2}, \\ \frac{c}{a} = \frac{1}{2}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} a = 2, \\ c = 1, \\ y_0 = 1, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore b^2 = a^2 - c^2 = 3.$ $\therefore$ 椭圆的方程为 $\frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{3} = 1.$ (2)【证明】由(1)知 $P(2, 1)$ , 易知直线 $PB$ 的斜率一定存在, 设直线 $PB$ 的方程为 $y = k(x - 2) + 1$ , 联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = k(x - 2) + 1, \\ \frac{x^2}{4} + \frac{y^2}{3} = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 得 $(3 + 4k^2)x^2 + 8k(1 - 2k)x + 8(2k^2 - 2k - 1) = 0①$ , 直线 $PB$ 与椭圆有唯一公共点, $\therefore \Delta = 96(2k + 1) = 0$ , 解得 $k = -\frac{1}{2}$ ,代入①中得 $x^{2}-2x+1=0$ ，解得 x=1，
> > 则 $B\left(1,\frac{3}{2}\right)$ .
> > 解法一: 又∵ $F(-1,0)$ , $A(2,0)$ ,
> > ∴ $\overrightarrow{FB}=\left(2,\frac{3}{2}\right),\overrightarrow{FP}=(3,1)$ ,
> > ∴ $\cos\angle BFP = \cos\langle\overrightarrow{FB},\overrightarrow{FP}\rangle = \frac{\overrightarrow{FB}\cdot\overrightarrow{FP}}{|\overrightarrow{FB}||\overrightarrow{FP}|}=\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}.$ 在 Rt $\triangle FAP$ 中， $\cos\angle AFP=\frac{|FA|}{|FP|}=$ $\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}.$ ∴ $\angle AFP = \angle BFP$ ,
> > ∴ PF 平分 $\angle AFB$ .

> > 解法二：∵ $B\left(1,\frac{3}{2}\right)$ , $F(-1,0)$ , $\therefore k_{BF}=\frac{\frac{3}{2}-0}{1+1}=\frac{3}{4}$ ，即 $\tan\angle BFA=\frac{3}{4}$ . $\because \tan \angle PFA = \frac{1}{3}, \therefore \tan 2 \angle PFA = \frac{2 \times \frac{1}{3}}{1 - \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2} = \frac{3}{4},$ $\therefore \tan \angle BFA = \tan 2 \angle PFA, \therefore \angle BFA = 2 \angle PFA,$ 故 PF 平分 $\angle AFB.$
