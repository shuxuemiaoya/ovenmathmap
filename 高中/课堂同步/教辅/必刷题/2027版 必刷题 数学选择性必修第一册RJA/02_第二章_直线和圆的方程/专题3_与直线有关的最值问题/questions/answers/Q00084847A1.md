> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{1}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设点 $A(a, ka), a < 0$ , $B(b, kb), b > 0$ , 则直线 $AN: y = \frac{ka - 1}{a} x + 1, BM: y = \frac{kb}{b - 1}(x - 1)$ , 则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{ka - 1}{a} > 0, \\ \frac{kb}{b - 1} > 0, \\ \frac{ka - 1}{a} < \frac{kb}{b - 1}, \end{array} \right.$ 且a < 0, b > 0，则 $\left\{\begin{aligned}0 &< ka < 1, \\ 0 &< b < 1, \\ b &+ ak &>1.\end{aligned}\right.$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}y &= \frac{ka-1}{a}x + 1, \\ y &= \frac{kb}{b-1}(x - 1),\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得点 C 的坐标为 $\left(\frac{a(kb + b - 1)}{b + ka - 1}, \frac{kb(ka + a - 1)}{b + ka - 1}\right)$ . 由于 $|AB| = \sqrt{1 + k^{2}} \cdot (b - a)$ ，点 C 到直线 AB 的距离 $d = \frac{-k(b - a)}{\sqrt{1 + k^{2}}(b + ka - 1)}$ ，故 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{-k \cdot (b - a)^{2}}{2(b + ak - 1)}$ . 则 $S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{-k \cdot (b - a)^{2}}{2(b + ak - 1)} = 3$ ，即 $k = \frac{6(1 - b)}{6a + (b - a)^{2}}$ ，由于 $\frac{k_{AN}}{k_{BM}} = \frac{(ka - 1)(b - 1)}{kab} = \frac{6ab + (b - a)^{2}}{6ab} = \frac{1}{6} \times \left[4 - \left(\frac{b}{-a} + \frac{-a}{b}\right)\right] \leqslant \frac{1}{3}$ ，当且仅当 b = -a 时取等号.
> >
> > 避坑：a < 0，使用基本不等式时要加负号使其满足“一正”的条件
