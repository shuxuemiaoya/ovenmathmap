> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B【解析】由 $x - 2my = 0$ 以及 $(2x - 6)m + y - 4 = 0$ 得 $A(0,0),B(3,4)$ 由 $1\times 2m + (-2m)\times 1 = 0$ 得两条动直线始终垂直，则 $|PA|^2 +|PB|^2 = |AB|^2 = 3^2 +4^2 = 25$ ，则 $\frac{1}{|PA|^2} +\frac{4}{|PB|^2} =$ $\frac{1}{25}\left(\frac{1}{|PA|^2} + \frac{4}{|PB|^2}\right)(|PA|^2 + |PB|^2) = \frac{1}{25}\left(5 + \frac{|PB|^2}{|PA|^2} + \frac{4|PA|^2}{|PB|^2}\right) \geqslant \frac{1}{25}\left(5 + 2\sqrt{\frac{|PB|^2}{|PA|^2} \cdot \frac{4|PA|^2}{|PB|^2}}\right) = \frac{1}{25} \times (5 + 4) = \frac{9}{25}$ ，当且仅当 $|PB|^2 = 2|PA|^2$ ，即 $|PB|^2 = \frac{50}{3}, |PA|^2 = \frac{25}{3}$ 时等号成立，故 $\frac{1}{|PA|^2} + \frac{4}{|PB|^2}$ 的最小值是 $\frac{9}{25}$ . 故选 B.
