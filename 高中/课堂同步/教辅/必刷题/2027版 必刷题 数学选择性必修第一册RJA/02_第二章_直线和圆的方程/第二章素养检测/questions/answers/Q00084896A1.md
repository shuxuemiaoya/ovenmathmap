> > (1)设圆 C 的标准方程为 $(x-a)^{2}+(y-b)^{2}=r^{2}$ ,
> > 由已知可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&a^{2}+b^{2}=r^{2},\\ &(1-a)^{2}+(-1-b)^{2}=r^{2},\\ &b=0,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a=1,\\ b=0,\\ r=1,\end{aligned}\right.$
