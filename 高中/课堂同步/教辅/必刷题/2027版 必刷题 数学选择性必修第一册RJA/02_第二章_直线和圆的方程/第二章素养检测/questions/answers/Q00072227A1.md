>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设圆 $O_{1}, O_{2}, O_{3}, O_{4}$ 的半径分别为 $r_{1}, r_{2}, r_{3}, r_{4}$ , 由题意可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} r_{1} = r_{2}, \\ r_{1} + r_{2} = |O_{1}O_{2}| = 4, \\ r_{1} + r_{3} = |O_{1}O_{3}| = \frac{5}{2}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} r_{1} = r_{2} = 2, \\ r_{3} = \frac{1}{2}, \end{array} \right.$ 又因为 $2\left(\frac{1}{r_1r_2} + \frac{1}{r_1r_3} + \frac{1}{r_1r_4} + \frac{1}{r_2r_3} + \frac{1}{r_2r_4} + \frac{1}{r_3r_4}\right) = \frac{1}{r_1^2} + \frac{1}{r_2^2} + \frac{1}{r_3^2} + \frac{1}{r_4^2}$ , 即 $2\left(\frac{1}{4} + 1 + \frac{1}{2r_4} + 1 + \frac{1}{2r_4} + \frac{2}{r_4}\right) = \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{\frac{1}{4}} + \frac{1}{r_4^2}$ , 解得 $r_4 = \frac{1}{6}$ . 由 $|O_{1}O_{4}| = |O_{2}O_{4}|$ , 可知点 $O_{4}$ 在线段 $O_{1}O_{2}$ 的垂直平分线上, 即 $y$ 轴上, 设 $O_{4}(0, a)$ , 则由题意可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} |O_{1}O_{4}| = \sqrt{4 + a^{2}} = 2 + \frac{1}{6}, \\ |O_{3}O_{4}| = \left|\frac{3}{2} - a\right| = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{6}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $a = \frac{5}{6}$ , 即圆 $O_{4}$ 的圆心 $O_{4}$ ( $0, \frac{5}{6}$ ), 半径 $r_{4} = \frac{1}{6}$ , 所以圆 $O_{4}$ 的标准方程为 $x^{2} + \left(y - \frac{5}{6}\right)^{2} = \frac{1}{36}$ .
