> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B【解析】设线段 $AB$ 上靠近点 $A$ 的三等分点为 $C(x,y,z)$ 根据题意可得出 $\overrightarrow{AC} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB}$ 即 $(x - 1,y,z + 1) = \frac{1}{3} (3,3,3) = (1,1,1)$ 所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x - 1 = 1,\\ y = 1,\\ z + 1 = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}x = 2,\\ y = 1,\\ z = 0, \end{array} \right.$ 即点 $C(2,1,0)$
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
