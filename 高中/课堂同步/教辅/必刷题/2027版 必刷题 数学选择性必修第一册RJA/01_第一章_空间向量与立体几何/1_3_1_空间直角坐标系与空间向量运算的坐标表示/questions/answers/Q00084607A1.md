> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可知， $e_{1}, e_{2}, e_{3}$ 共面，设 $e_{2} = m e_{1} + n e_{3} (m, n \in \mathbf{R})$ ，即 $(2, 4, \lambda) = m(1, 1, 0) + n(2, 3, 1)$ ，即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&m+2n=2,\\ &m+3n=4,\text{解得}\end{aligned}\right.\left\{\begin{aligned}&m=-2,\\ &n=2,\quad\text{故选C.}\\ &\lambda=2.\end{aligned}\right.$
