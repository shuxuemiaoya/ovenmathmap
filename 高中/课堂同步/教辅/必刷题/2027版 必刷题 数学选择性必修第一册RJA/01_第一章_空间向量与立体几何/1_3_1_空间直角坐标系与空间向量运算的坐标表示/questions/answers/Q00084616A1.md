>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 已知 $M(\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{3}, -2)$ ， $N(2 + \sqrt{2}, 1 + \sqrt{3}, -4)$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{MN} = (2, 1, -2)$ 。因为 $c \parallel \overrightarrow{MN}, c = (2, 2t + 1, -2)$ ，所以 $\frac{2}{2} = \frac{2t + 1}{1} = \frac{-2}{-2}$ ，解得 t = 0。故选 A.
