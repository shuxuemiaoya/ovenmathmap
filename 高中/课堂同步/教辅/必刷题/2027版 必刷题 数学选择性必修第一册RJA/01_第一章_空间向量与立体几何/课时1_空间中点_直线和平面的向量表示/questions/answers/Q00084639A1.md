> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意, 若取 $a = \overrightarrow{OA} = (1, 2, 1)$ , $b = \overrightarrow{OB} = (2, 0, -1)$ , 则由题中方法得 $n = (-2, 3, -4)$ . 若取 $a = \overrightarrow{OA} = (1, 2, 1)$ , $b = \overrightarrow{BO} = (-2, 0, 1)$ , 则由题中方法得 $n = (2, -3, 4)$ . 所以 $|n| = \sqrt{(-2)^{2} + 3^{2} + (-4)^{2}} = \sqrt{2^{2} + (-3)^{2} + 4^{2}} = \sqrt{29}$ . 故选 BD.
