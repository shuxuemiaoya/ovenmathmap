> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x\times1+1\times y+1\times1=0,\\ \frac{1}{2}&=\frac{y}{-4}=\frac{1}{2},\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=1,\\ y&=-2,\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以
> > 向量 $\boldsymbol{a}=(1,1,1),\boldsymbol{b}=(1,-2,1)$ ，所以 $\boldsymbol{a}+\boldsymbol{b}=(2,-1,2)$ ，所以 $\left|\boldsymbol{a}+\boldsymbol{b}\right|=\sqrt{2^{2}+\left(-1\right)^{2}+2^{2}}=3.$ 故选 B.
