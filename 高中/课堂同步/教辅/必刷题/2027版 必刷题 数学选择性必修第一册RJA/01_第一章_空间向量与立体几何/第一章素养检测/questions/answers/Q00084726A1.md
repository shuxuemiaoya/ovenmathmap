> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > (1)由已知,得 $\overrightarrow{DC}=\overrightarrow{AC}-\overrightarrow{AD}=b-\frac{1}{2}a,\overrightarrow{CR}=\overrightarrow{AP}=c$ , 则 $\overrightarrow{DR}=\overrightarrow{DC}+\overrightarrow{CR}=\overrightarrow{DC}+\overrightarrow{AP}=b-\frac{1}{2}a+c.$ (2) 因为三棱锥 P-ABC 是棱长为 2 的正四面体, 所以 $|a| = |b| = |c| = 2$ , 所以 $a \cdot b = a \cdot c = b \cdot c = 2 \times 2 \times \cos 60^{\circ} = 2$ . 因为 $\overrightarrow{DR} = b - \frac{1}{2} a + c$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $|\overrightarrow{DR}| = \sqrt{\left(\boldsymbol{b} - \frac{1}{2}\boldsymbol{a} + \boldsymbol{c}\right)^2} =$ $\sqrt{\boldsymbol{b}^2 + \frac{1}{4}\boldsymbol{a}^2 + \boldsymbol{c}^2 - \boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b} - \boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{c} + 2\boldsymbol{b} \cdot \boldsymbol{c}} =$ $\sqrt{4 + 1 + 4 - 2 - 2 + 4} = 3.$ (3)由三棱锥 $P - ABC$ 是正三棱锥，设 $|\boldsymbol{a}| = x, |\boldsymbol{c}| = y$ ，则 $|\boldsymbol{b}| = x$ ，所以 $\cos \angle BAP = \cos \angle CAP = \frac{x^2 + y^2 - y^2}{2xy} = \frac{x}{2y}$ ，则 $\cos \langle \boldsymbol{a}, \boldsymbol{c} \rangle = \cos \langle \boldsymbol{b}, \boldsymbol{c} \rangle = \frac{x}{2y}$ ，则 $\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b} = x^2 \cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2} x^2, \boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{c} = \boldsymbol{b} \cdot \boldsymbol{c} = xy \cdot \frac{x}{2y} = \frac{1}{2} x^2.$ 因为 $\overrightarrow{DR} = b - \frac{1}{2} a + c$ ，所以 $|\overrightarrow{DR}| = \sqrt{\left(\boldsymbol{b} - \frac{1}{2}\boldsymbol{a} + \boldsymbol{c}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\boldsymbol{b}^2 + \frac{1}{4}\boldsymbol{a}^2 + c^2 - \boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b} - \boldsymbol{a} \cdot c + 2\boldsymbol{b} \cdot c} = \sqrt{x^2 + \frac{1}{4}x^2 + y^2 - \frac{1}{2}x^2 - \frac{1}{2}x^2 + x^2} = \sqrt{\frac{5}{4}x^2 + y^2},$ $\overrightarrow{DR} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = (\boldsymbol{b} - \frac{1}{2}\boldsymbol{a} + \boldsymbol{c}) \cdot \boldsymbol{b} = b^2 - \frac{1}{2}\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b} + b \cdot c = x^2 - \frac{1}{4}x^2 + \frac{1}{2}x^2 = \frac{5}{4}x^2.$ 设异面直线 $DR$ 与 $AC$ 所成的角为 $\theta$ ，则 $\cos \theta = \frac{|\overrightarrow{DR} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC}|}{|\overrightarrow{DR}| |\overrightarrow{AC}|} =$ $\frac{\frac{5}{4}x^2}{\sqrt{\frac{5}{4}x^2 + y^2} \cdot x} >\frac{1}{2}$ ，解得 $\frac{x}{2y} >\frac{\sqrt{5}}{10}$ ，所以 $\cos \langle a, c\rangle$ 的取值范围是 $\left(\frac{\sqrt{5}}{10}, 1\right)$ .避坑：不要遗漏< $a, c>$ 的取值范围是 $\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$
