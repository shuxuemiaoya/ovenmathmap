> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意知, $AA_{1}$ , AB, AD 两两垂直, 故 $a \cdot b = a \cdot c = b \cdot c = 0$ . 又 $|a| = 1, |b| = 2, |c| = 3$ , 所以 $(a + b) \cdot (b - c) = a \cdot b - a \cdot c + b^{2} - b \cdot c = b^{2} = 4$ . 故选 C.
