> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由于 i, j, k 是两两垂直的单位向量，故 $i \cdot j = j \cdot k = i \cdot k = 0$ ，且 $|i| = |j| = |k| = 1$ ，则 $5a \cdot 3b = 15 \times (3i + 2j - k) \cdot (i - j + 2k) = 15 \times (3 - 2 - 2) = -15$ 。故选 A.
