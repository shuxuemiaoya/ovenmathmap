> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】∵二面角 $\alpha-l-\beta$ 的大小为 $\frac{\pi}{3}, AC \perp l, BD \perp l, \therefore \langle \overrightarrow{AC}, \overrightarrow{BD} \rangle = \frac{\pi}{3}, \langle \overrightarrow{CA}, \overrightarrow{BD} \rangle = \frac{2\pi}{3}.$ 由题意得 $\overrightarrow{CD}=\overrightarrow{CA}+\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{BD}$ ，故 $\overrightarrow{CD^{2}}=(\overrightarrow{CA}+\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{BD})^{2}=\overrightarrow{CA^{2}}+\overrightarrow{AB^{2}}+\overrightarrow{BD^{2}}+2\overrightarrow{CA}\cdot\overrightarrow{AB}+2\overrightarrow{CA}\cdot\overrightarrow{BD}+2\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\overrightarrow{BD},$ ∴ $49 = 9 + |\overrightarrow{AB}|^{2} + 16 + 0 + 2 \times 3 \times 4 \times \cos \frac{2\pi}{3} + 0, \therefore |\overrightarrow{AB}| = 6,$ 故线段 AB 的长为 6.
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
