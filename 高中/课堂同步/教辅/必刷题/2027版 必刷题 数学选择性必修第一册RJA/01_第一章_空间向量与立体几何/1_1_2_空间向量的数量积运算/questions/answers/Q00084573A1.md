> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D 【解析】在空间四边形 ABCD 中， $BD = \sqrt{3} CD, \angle ADC = \frac{\pi}{6}, \angle ADB = \frac{\pi}{3}$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{DA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{DA} \cdot (\overrightarrow{DC} - \overrightarrow{DB}) = \overrightarrow{DA} \cdot \overrightarrow{DC} - \overrightarrow{DA} \cdot \overrightarrow{DB} = |\overrightarrow{DA}| |\overrightarrow{DC}| \cdot \cos \angle ADC - |\overrightarrow{DA}| |\overrightarrow{DB}| \cos \angle ADB = |\overrightarrow{DA}| |\overrightarrow{DC}| \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - |\overrightarrow{DA}| \cdot \sqrt{3} |\overrightarrow{DC}| \cdot \frac{1}{2} = 0$ ，所以 $\cos \langle \overrightarrow{DA}, \overrightarrow{BC} \rangle = \frac{\overrightarrow{DA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC}}{|\overrightarrow{DA}| |\overrightarrow{BC}|} = 0.$
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
