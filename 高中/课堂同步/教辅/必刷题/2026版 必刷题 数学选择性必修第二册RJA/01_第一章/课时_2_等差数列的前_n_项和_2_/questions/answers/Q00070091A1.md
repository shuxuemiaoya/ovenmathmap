> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】∵ $a_{n} \cdot b_{n} = 1$ ,
> >
> > ∴ $b_{n} = \frac{1}{n^{2} + 3n + 2} = \frac{1}{(n + 1)(n + 2)} = \frac{1}{n + 1} - \frac{1}{n + 2}$ .
> >
> > 设数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的前 n 项和为 $S_{n}$ ，则
> >
> > 敲黑板：把分母因式分解为 $(n + 1) \cdot (n + 2)$ 的形式即可裂项 $S_{100} = \left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4}\right) + \cdots + \left(\frac{1}{100} - \frac{1}{101}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{101} - \frac{1}{102}\right) = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{102} = \frac{25}{51}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
