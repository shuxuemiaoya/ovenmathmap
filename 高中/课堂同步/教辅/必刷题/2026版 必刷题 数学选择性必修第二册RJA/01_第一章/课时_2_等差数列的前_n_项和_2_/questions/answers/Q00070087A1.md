> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{19}{18}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $a_{n} = k_{1}n + b_{1}, b_{n} = k_{2}n + b_{2}$ （ $k_{1}, k_{2}, b_{1}, b_{2} \in \mathbb{R}$ ），则 $S_{n} = \frac{n(a_{1} + a_{n})}{2} = \frac{n(k_{1}n + k_{1} + 2b_{1})}{2}$ ， $T_{n} = \frac{n(b_{1} + b_{n})}{2} = \frac{n(k_{2}n + k_{2} + 2b_{2})}{2}$ . 故 $\frac{S_{n}}{T_{n}} = \frac{k_{1}n + k_{1} + 2b_{1}}{k_{2}n + k_{2} + 2b_{2}} = \frac{2n + 1}{n + 3}$ ，则 $\frac{k_{1}}{k_{1} + 2b_{1}} = 2, \frac{k_{2}}{k_{2} + 2b_{2}} = \frac{1}{3}$ ，且 $\frac{k_{1}}{k_{2}} = 2$ . 故 $b_{1} = -\frac{1}{4} k_{1}, k_{2} = b_{2}, k_{1} = 2k_{2}$ ，则 $a_{n} = k_{1}n - \frac{1}{4} k_{1}, b_{n} = \frac{1}{2} k_{1}n + \frac{1}{2} k_{1}$ ，则 $\frac{a_5}{b_8} = \frac{5k_1 - \frac{1}{4}k_1}{4k_1 + \frac{1}{2}k_1} = \frac{19}{18}$ .
