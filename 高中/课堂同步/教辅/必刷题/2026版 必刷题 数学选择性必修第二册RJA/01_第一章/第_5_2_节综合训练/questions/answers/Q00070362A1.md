> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ABD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ABD 【解析】对于 A, 由题意可知 $AE = BF = EF\sin 15^{\circ}, BE = AH = EF\cos 15^{\circ}$ , 则 $\frac{EF}{AB} = \frac{EF}{EF\sin 15^{\circ} + EF\cos 15^{\circ}} = \frac{1}{\sin 15^{\circ} + \cos 15^{\circ}}$ 而 $\sin 15^{\circ} = \sin 60^{\circ}\cos 45^{\circ} - \cos 60^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > $\sin 45^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$ , 则 $\cos 15^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$ , 所以 $a_{2} = EF = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} AB = \sqrt{6}$ , 故 A 正确; 对于 B, 由上可知 $AE = \sqrt{6} \times \frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{3 - \sqrt{3}}{2}, AH = \sqrt{6} \times \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{3 + \sqrt{3}}{2}$ , 所以 $S_{1} = \frac{1}{2} AE \cdot AH = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{3 - \sqrt{3}}{2} \times \frac{3 + \sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{3}{4}$ , 故 B 正确; 对于 C, 易知 $\triangle MNF \sim \triangle EFB$ , 此后对应三角形均相似, 而相似比 $\frac{MN}{EF} = \frac{MN}{MN\sin 15^{\circ} + MN\cos 15^{\circ}} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ , 即 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是首项为 3, 公比为 $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ 的等比数列, $\{S_{n}\}$ 是首项为 $\frac{3}{4}$ , 公比为 $\left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\right)^{2} = \frac{2}{3}$ 的等比点悟: 边长的公比为 $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ , 故对应比例变化的三角形面积的公比为 $\left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\right)^{2}$ 数列, 故 C 错误; 对于 D, 由上可得 $a_{n} = 3 \cdot \left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\right)^{n-1}$ , 则 $a_{n}^{2} = 9 \cdot \left(\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}\right)^{2n-2} = 9 \cdot \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n-1}$ , 则 $T_{n} = \frac{9 \times \left[1 - \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n}\right]}{1 - \frac{2}{3}} = 27 - 27 \times \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n}$ , 显然 $y = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n}$ 单调递减, 又 $n \in \mathbb{N}^{*}$ , 所以 $0 < \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{n} \leqslant \frac{2}{3}$ 恒成立, 所以 $T_{n}$ 满足 $9 \leqslant T_{n} < 27$ , 故 D 正确.
> >
> > 故选 **ABD**.
