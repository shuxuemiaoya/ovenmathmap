> > [!success]- **【答案】** D

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D 【解析】已知 $\frac{S_n}{T_n} = \frac{3n + 2}{4n + 5}$ , 不妨设 $S_n = (3n + 2)nk, T_n = (4n + 5)nk (k \neq 0)$ . 因为 $P, B, C$ 三点共线, 所以 $\frac{a_2 + a_6}{b_3} + \lambda = 1$ , 所以 $\frac{2a_4}{b_3} + \lambda = 1, \frac{a_4}{b_3} = \frac{2a_4}{2b_3} = \frac{a_1 + a_7}{b_1 + b_5} = \frac{\frac{a_1 + a_7}{2} \times 7}{\frac{b_1 + b_5}{2} \times 5} \times \frac{5}{7} = \frac{S_7}{T_5} \times \frac{5}{7} = \frac{7k(21 + 2)}{5k(20 + 5)} \times \frac{5}{7} = \frac{23}{25}$ , 所以 $\frac{2a_4}{b_3} + \lambda = \frac{46}{25} + \lambda = 1$ , 解得 $\lambda = -\frac{21}{25}$ ,
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
