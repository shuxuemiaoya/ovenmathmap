> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}+a_{4}&=18,\\ a_{2}a_{3}&=a_{1}a_{4}=32,\end{aligned}\right.$ 可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=2,\\ a_{4}&=16\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=16,\\ a_{4}&=2.\end{aligned}\right.$ 又因为数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是递增的等比数列，
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a_{1}&=2,\\ a_{4}&=16,\end{aligned}\right.$ 则公比 $q=2,a_{n}=2^{n}$ ， $S_{k+6}-S_{k}=a_{k+1}+a_{k+2}+\cdots+a_{k+6}=\frac{2^{k+1}(1-2^{6})}{1-2}=2^{k+7}-2^{k+1}$ ，所以 $S_{k+6}-S_{k}=2^{k+7}-2^{k+1}=2^{11}-2^{5}$ ，所以 k=4。
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
