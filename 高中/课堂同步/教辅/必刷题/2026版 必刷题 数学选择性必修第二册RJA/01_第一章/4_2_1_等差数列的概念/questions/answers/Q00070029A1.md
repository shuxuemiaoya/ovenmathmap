> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 16
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由等差中项的定义可得 $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} = 1$ ，故 $a + 9b = (a + 9b) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}\right) = 1 + \frac{a}{b} + \frac{9b}{a} + 9 \geqslant 10 + 2\sqrt{\frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{9b}{a}} = 16$ （当且仅当 a = 4, b = $\frac{4}{3}$ 时取等号）.
