> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > D 【解析】∵数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足 $a_{1}=2$ ， $a_{n+1}a_{n}=a_{n}-1$ ，易知 $a_{n}\neq0,\therefore a_{n+1}=1-\frac{1}{a_{n}},\therefore a_{2}=1-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{2},a_{3}=1-2=-1,$ $a_{4}=1-(-1)=2,a_{5}=1-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{1}{2},$
> >
> > 故选 **D**.
