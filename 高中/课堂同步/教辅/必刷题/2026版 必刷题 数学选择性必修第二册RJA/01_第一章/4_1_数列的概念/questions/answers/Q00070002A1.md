> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B【解析】由 $a_{n + 1} = a_n + \frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1}$ 可得 $a_{n + 1} - a_n = \frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1},$ $\therefore$ 当 $n\geqslant 2$ 时， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}a_2 - a_1 = 1 - \frac{1}{2},\\ a_3 - a_2 = \frac{1}{2} -\frac{1}{3},\\ \dots \\ a_n - a_{n - 1} = \frac{1}{n - 1} -\frac{1}{n}, \end{array} \right.$ $\therefore a_{n} - a_{1} = 1 - \frac{1}{2} +\frac{1}{2} -\frac{1}{3} +\dots +\frac{1}{n - 1}-$ $\frac{1}{n} = 1 - \frac{1}{n} = \frac{n - 1}{n},\therefore a_n = 1 + \frac{n - 1}{n} = \frac{2n - 1}{n}.$ 经验证， $a_1$ 也符合上式.
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
