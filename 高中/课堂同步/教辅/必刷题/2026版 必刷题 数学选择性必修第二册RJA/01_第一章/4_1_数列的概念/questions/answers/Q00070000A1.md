> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{50}{17}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a_{n+1} - a_n = \frac{1}{2n-1} - \frac{1}{2n-3} = \frac{-2}{(2n-1)(2n-3)}$ , 当 $n=1$ 时, $a_2 - a_1 = 2 > 0$ ; 当 $n \geqslant 2$ 时, $a_{n+1} - a_n < 0$ . 敲黑板: 求绝对值数列 $\{|a_{n+1} - a_n|\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和时, 必须判断 $(a_{n+1} - a_n)$ 的正负所以 $|a_2 - a_1| + |a_3 - a_2| + \cdots + |a_{10} - a_9| = a_2 - a_1 + a_2 - a_3 + a_3 - a_4 + \cdots + a_9 - a_{10} = 2a_2 - a_1 - a_{10} = 2 - (-1) - \frac{1}{2 \times 10-3} = 3 - \frac{1}{17} = \frac{50}{17}$ .
