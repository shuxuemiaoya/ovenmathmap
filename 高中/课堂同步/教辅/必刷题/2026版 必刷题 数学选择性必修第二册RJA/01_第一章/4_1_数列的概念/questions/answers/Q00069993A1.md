> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > B 【解析】设数列 2, $-\frac{4}{3}, \frac{6}{5}, -\frac{8}{7}, \cdots$ 为数列 $\{a_n\}$ , 观察可得 $a_1 = \frac{2}{1}, a_2 = -\frac{4}{3}, a_3 = \frac{6}{5}, a_4 = -\frac{8}{7}, \cdots$ , 归纳可得其通项公式为 $a_{n}=(-1)^{n-1}\frac{2n}{2n-1}$ , 所以第 8 项是 $a_{8}=(-1)^{8-1}\times\frac{2\times8}{2\times8-1}=-\frac{16}{15}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **B**.
