> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A 【解析】数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足 $a_{n+1}=$ $\left\{\begin{aligned}&2a_{n},0\leqslant a_{n}\leqslant\frac{1}{2},\\ &2a_{n}-1,\frac{1}{2}<a_{n}<1,\end{aligned}\right.$ $a_{1}=\frac{3}{5}$ ,
> >
> > 则 $a_{2}=2a_{1}-1=\frac{1}{5},a_{3}=2a_{2}=\frac{2}{5},a_{4}=$ $2a_{3}=\frac{4}{5},a_{5}=2a_{4}-1=\frac{3}{5}=a_{1}$ ,
> >
> > 因此继续下去会循环,数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 是周期为4的周期数列,
> >
> > 所以 $a_{10}=a_{2+2\times4}=a_{2}=\frac{1}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选 **A**.
