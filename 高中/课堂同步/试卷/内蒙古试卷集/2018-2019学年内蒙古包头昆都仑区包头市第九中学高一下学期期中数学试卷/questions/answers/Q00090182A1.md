> [!faq]- 2018-2019学年内蒙古包头昆都仑区包头市第九中学高一下学期期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析 (2) $(n-1)\cdot2^{n+1}+2-\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $\because a_{n+1} = 2a_n + 1, \therefore (a_{n+1} + 1) = 29(a_n + 1),$ $\because a_1 + 1 = 2 \neq 0, \therefore a_n + 1 \neq 0, \therefore \frac{a_{n+1} + 1}{a_n + 1} = 2, \therefore \{a_n + 1\}$ 是以2为公比,2为首项的等比数列， $\therefore a_n + 1 = 2^n, \therefore a_n = 2^n - 1.$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {(2) \quad \because a _ {n} = 2 ^ {n} - 1, \therefore b _ {n} = \log_ {2} (a _ {n} + 1) = \log_ {2} 2 ^ {n} = n,} \\ & {\quad \therefore b _ {n} \cdot a _ {n} = n \cdot (2 ^ {n} - 1) = n \cdot 2 ^ {n} - n,} \\ & {\quad \text {记}} \\ & {\quad A = 1 \times 2 ^ {1} + 2 \times 2 ^ {2} + \dots + n \cdot 2 ^ {n}, \therefore 2 A = 1 \times 2 ^ {2} + \dots + (n - 1) \cdot 2 ^ {n} + n \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1},} \\ & {\quad ,} \\ & {\quad \therefore - A = A - 2 A = 2 + 2 ^ {2} + \dots + 2 ^ {n} - n \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1} = \frac {2 (1 - 2 ^ {n})}{1 - 2} - n \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1} = 1 - n \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1} - 2,} \\ & {\quad , \therefore A = n - 1 \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1} + 2,} \\ & {\quad S _ {n} = A - (1 + 2 + \dots + n) = (n - 1) \cdot 2 ^ {n + 1} + 2 - \frac {n (n + 1)}{2}.} \end{array}
> > $$
