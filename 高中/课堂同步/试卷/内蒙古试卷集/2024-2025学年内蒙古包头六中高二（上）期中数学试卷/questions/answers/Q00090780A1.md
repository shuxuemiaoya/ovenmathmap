> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头六中高二（上）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $120^{\circ}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {A B} = (- 2, - 1, 3), \overrightarrow {C A} = (- 1, 3, - 2),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos <   \overrightarrow {A B}, \overrightarrow {C A} > = \frac {(- 2) \times (- 1) + (- 1) \times 3 + 3 \times (- 2)}{\sqrt {1 4} \cdot \sqrt {1 4}} = \frac {- 7}{1 4} = - \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \theta = <   \overrightarrow {A B}, \overrightarrow {C A} > = 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为 $120^{\circ}$
