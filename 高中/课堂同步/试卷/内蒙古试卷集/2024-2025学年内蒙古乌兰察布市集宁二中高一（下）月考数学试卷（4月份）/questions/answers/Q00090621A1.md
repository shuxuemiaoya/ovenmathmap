> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古乌兰察布市集宁二中高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {a} = (2, 0), \overrightarrow {b} = (1, \sqrt {3})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {b} = 2, | \overrightarrow {a} | = 2, | \overrightarrow {b} | = \sqrt {1 + 3} = 2,
> > $$
> >
> > 对 $A$ 选项， $\because \overrightarrow{a}\cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 2,\therefore A$ 选项正确；
> >
> > 对 $B$ 选项， $\because |\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{b}| = |(3,\sqrt{3})| = \sqrt{9 + 3} = 2\sqrt{3},$
> >
> > 又 $|\overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b}| = |(1, - \sqrt{3})| = \sqrt{1 + 3} = 2,$
> >
> > $|\overrightarrow{a}+\overrightarrow{b}|\neq|\overrightarrow{a}-\overrightarrow{b}|,\therefore B$ 选项错误；
> >
> > 对 $C$ 选项， $\because \cos <  \overrightarrow{a},\overrightarrow{b} > = \frac{\overrightarrow{a}\cdot\overrightarrow{b}}{|\overrightarrow{a}|\cdot|\overrightarrow{b}|} = \frac{2}{2\times 2} = \frac{1}{2},$
> >
> > 又 $< \overrightarrow{a},\overrightarrow{b} >\in [0,\pi ],\therefore < \overrightarrow{a},\overrightarrow{b} >=\frac{\pi}{3},\therefore C$ 选项错误；
> >
> > 对 $D$ 选项， $\because \overrightarrow{b}$ 在 $\overrightarrow{a}$ 方向上的投影向量为 $(\frac{\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{\overrightarrow{a}^2})\overrightarrow{a} = (\frac{2}{2^2})\overrightarrow{a} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a}$
> >
> > ∴ D选项正确.
> >
> > 故选：AD.
