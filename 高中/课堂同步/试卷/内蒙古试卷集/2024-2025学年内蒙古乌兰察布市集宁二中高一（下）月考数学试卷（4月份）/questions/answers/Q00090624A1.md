> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古乌兰察布市集宁二中高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{13}{85}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\alpha$ ， $\beta$ 为锐角，且 $sin\alpha = \frac{8}{17}$ ，cosβ= $\frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2\alpha} = \frac{15}{17}$ ， $\sin \beta = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\beta} = \frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos (\alpha + \beta) = \cos \alpha \cos \beta - \sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac {1 5}{1 7} \times \frac {3}{5} - \frac {8}{1 7} \times \frac {4}{5} = \frac {1 3}{8 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{13}{85}$
