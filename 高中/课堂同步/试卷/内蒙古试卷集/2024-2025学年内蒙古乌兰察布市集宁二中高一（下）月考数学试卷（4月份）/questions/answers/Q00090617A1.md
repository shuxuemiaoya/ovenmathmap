> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古乌兰察布市集宁二中高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\alpha \in (0, \frac{\pi}{3})$ ，则 $\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6} \in (\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{2})$ ，所以 $\sin (\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}) > 0$
> >
> > 且 $\sin (\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}) = \sqrt{1 - cos^2(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6})} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{9}{25}} = \frac{4}{5},$
> >
> > 而
> >
> > $$
> > \sin (\alpha + \frac {5 \pi}{1 2}) = \sin [ (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}) + \frac {\pi}{4} ] = \sin (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}) \cos \frac {\pi}{4} + \cos (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}) \sin \frac {\pi}{4} = \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > + \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} = \frac {7 \sqrt {2}}{1 0}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A.
