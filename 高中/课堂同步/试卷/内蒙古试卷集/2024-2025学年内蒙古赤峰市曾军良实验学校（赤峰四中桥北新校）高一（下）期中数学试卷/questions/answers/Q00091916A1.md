> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰市曾军良实验学校（赤峰四中桥北新校）高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $\overrightarrow{a} = (-1, \sqrt{3})$ , $\overrightarrow{b} = (m, \sqrt{3})$ ，且 $\overrightarrow{a}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{b}$ 的夹角为 $\frac{\pi}{3}$ .
> >
> > 可得 $\overrightarrow{a} \bullet \overrightarrow{b} = -1 \times m + \sqrt{3} \times \sqrt{3} = 3 - m, |\overrightarrow{a}| = 2, |\overrightarrow{b}| = \sqrt{3 + m^2}$ ,
> >
> > 则 $3 - m = 2 \times \sqrt{m^2 + 3} \times \cos \frac{\pi}{3}$ ，即 $3 - m = 2 \times \sqrt{m^2 + 3} \times \frac{1}{2} = \sqrt{m^2 + 3}$ .
> >
> > (2)由（1）可知 $\overrightarrow{b} = (1, \sqrt{3})$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {a} + \lambda \overrightarrow {b} = (- 1, \sqrt {3}) + \lambda (1, \sqrt {3}) = (- 1 + \lambda , \sqrt {3} + \sqrt {3} \lambda)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {a} + 2 \overrightarrow {b} = (- 1, \sqrt {3}) + 2 (1, \sqrt {3}) = (1, 3 \sqrt {3}).
> > $$
> >
> > 因为向量 $\overrightarrow{a} +\lambda \overrightarrow{b}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{a} +2\overrightarrow{b}$ 所成的角是锐角，
> >
> > 所以 $(\overrightarrow{a} + \lambda \overrightarrow{b}) \bullet (\overrightarrow{a} + 2\overrightarrow{b}) > 0$ 且 $\overrightarrow{a} + \lambda \overrightarrow{b}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{a} + 2\overrightarrow{b}$ 不共线.
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \left(\overrightarrow {a} + \lambda \overrightarrow {b}\right) \bullet \left(\overrightarrow {a} + 2 \overrightarrow {b}\right) = (- 1 + \lambda) \times 1 + (\sqrt {3} + \sqrt {3} \lambda) \times 3 \sqrt {3} = - 1 + \lambda + 9 + 9 \lambda = 1 0 \lambda \\ + 8 > 0 \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $\lambda > -\frac{4}{5}$ .
> >
> > 若两向量共线，则 $\frac{-1 + \lambda}{1} = \frac{\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{3}\lambda}{3\sqrt{3}}$ ，化简得 $3\sqrt{3} (-1 + \lambda) = \sqrt{3} +\sqrt{3}\lambda$ ，解得 $\lambda = 2$ 所以当两向量不共线时， $\lambda \neq 2$
> >
> > 综上，实数 $\lambda$ 的取值范围是 $(- \frac{4}{5}, 2) \cup (2, +\infty)$ .
