>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 因为 $a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2}-bc$
> >
> > 则 $\cos A=\frac{b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}}{2bc}=\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 则 $A=\frac{\pi}{3}.$
> >
> > (2)由 $a^{2}=b^{2}+c^{2}-bc$ 得， $a^{2}=(b+c)^{2}-3bc$ ，
> >
> > 因 $a = \sqrt{2}, b + c = 2$ ，则 $2 = 4 - 3bc$ ，即 $bc = \frac{2}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\triangle A B C} = \frac {1}{2} b c \sin A = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{6}.
> > $$
