> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰市曾军良实验学校（赤峰四中桥北新校）高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \overrightarrow{a} = (1,0),\overrightarrow{b} = (1,1),(\overrightarrow{a} +\lambda \overrightarrow{b})\perp \overrightarrow{a},$ $\therefore \overrightarrow{a} +\lambda \overrightarrow{b} = (1 + \lambda ,\lambda).$ $\therefore (\overrightarrow{a} +\lambda \overrightarrow{b})\bullet \overrightarrow{a} = 0,$ $\therefore (1 + \lambda ,\lambda)\cdot (1,0) = 0.$ 即 $1 + \lambda = 0$ ，则 $\lambda = -1$ 故选：B
