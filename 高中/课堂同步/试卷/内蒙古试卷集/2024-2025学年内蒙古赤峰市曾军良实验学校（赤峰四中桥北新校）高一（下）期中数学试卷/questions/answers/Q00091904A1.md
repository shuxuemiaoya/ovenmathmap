> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰市曾军良实验学校（赤峰四中桥北新校）高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because 0 <   x <   \pi , \therefore x - \frac {\pi}{6} \in \left(- \frac {\pi}{6}, \frac {5 \pi}{6}\right).
> > $$
> >
> > 又∵方程 $f(x)=\frac{4}{5}$ 的解为 $x_{1},x_{2}(0<x_{1}<x_{2}<\pi)$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {x _ {1} + x _ {2}}{2} = \frac {2 \pi}{3}, x _ {1} + x _ {2} = \frac {4 \pi}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \sin (x_{1} + x_{2}) = \sin \frac{4\pi}{3} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$
> >
> > 故选：A.
