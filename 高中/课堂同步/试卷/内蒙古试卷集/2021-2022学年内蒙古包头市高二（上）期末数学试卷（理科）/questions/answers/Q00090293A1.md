> [!faq]- 2021-2022学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷（理科）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $f(n) = -1 + 3 - 5 + \cdots + (-1)^n (2n - 1)$ , $\therefore f(k) = -1 + 3 - 5 + \cdots + (-1)^k (2k - 1)$ , $\therefore f(k + 1) = -1 + 3 - 5 + \cdots + (-1)^k (2k - 1) + (-1)^{n+1} (2k + 1)$ , $\therefore f(k + 1) - f(k) = (-1)^{k+1} (2k + 1).$ 故选：D.
