> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古兴安盟科尔沁右翼前旗二中高一（下）月考数学试卷（6月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{7}{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 已知 $\sin(\alpha-\frac{\pi}{8})=\frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - 2 \alpha\right) = \cos \left(2 \alpha - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \cos \left[ 2 \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{8}\right) \right] = 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{8}\right) = 1 - 2 \times \frac {1}{9} = \frac {7}{9}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{7}{9}$ .
