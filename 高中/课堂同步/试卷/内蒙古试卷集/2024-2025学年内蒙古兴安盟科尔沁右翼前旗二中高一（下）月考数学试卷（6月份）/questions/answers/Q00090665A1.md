> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古兴安盟科尔沁右翼前旗二中高一（下）月考数学试卷（6月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)因为 $f(x)=2\sin x[\cos(x+\frac{\pi}{3})+\cos x]$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {= 2 \sin x (\frac {1}{2} \cos x - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin x + \cos x)} \\ & {= 3 \sin x \cos x - \sqrt {3} \sin^ {2} x} \\ & {= \frac {3}{2} \sin 2 x - \sqrt {3} \cdot \frac {1 - \cos 2 x}{2}} \\ & {= \sqrt {3} (\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin 2 x + \frac {1}{2} \cos 2 x) - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}} \\ & {= \sqrt {3} \sin (2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}) - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2},} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $f(x)$ 的最小正周期为 $T=\frac{2\pi}{2}=\pi$ ;
> >
> > (2) $x\in[0,\frac{\pi}{2}]$ 时， $2x+\frac{\pi}{6}\in[\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{7\pi}{6}]$ ，令 $2x+\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，解得 $x=\frac{\pi}{6}$
> >
> > 即 $x = \frac{\pi}{6}$ 时， $f(x)$ 取得最大值为 $\sqrt{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ .
