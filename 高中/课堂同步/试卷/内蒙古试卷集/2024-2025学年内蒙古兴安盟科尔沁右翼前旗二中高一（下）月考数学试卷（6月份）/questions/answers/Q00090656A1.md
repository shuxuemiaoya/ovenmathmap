> > [!note]- **【解析】**

> > M Q B = M N
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore P A \parallel M N
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{PM}{MC} = \frac{AN}{NC} = \frac{AQ}{BC} = \frac{1}{2}.$
