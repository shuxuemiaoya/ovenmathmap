> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰二中高一（下）第二次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)因为 $\sin 75^{\circ} = \sin (45^{\circ} + 30^{\circ}) = \sin 45^{\circ}\cos 30^{\circ} + \cos 45^{\circ}\sin 30^{\circ}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} + \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {6} + \sqrt {2}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 在△BCD中，∠CBD=45°，∠BCD=60°，∠BDC=75°，CD=60m，
> >
> > 由正弦定理得 $\frac{BC}{\sin\angle BDC} = \frac{CD}{\sin\angle CBD}$ ，即 $\frac{BC}{\frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}} = \frac{60}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}$
> >
> > 可得 $BC = (30 + 30\sqrt{3})m$
> >
> > (2)由(1)知， $BC = (30 + 30\sqrt{3})m$
> >
> > $Rt\triangle ABC$ 中， $AB = BC\tan \angle ACB = BC\tan 30^{\circ} = (30 + 30\sqrt{3})\times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} = (10\sqrt{3} +30)m.$
