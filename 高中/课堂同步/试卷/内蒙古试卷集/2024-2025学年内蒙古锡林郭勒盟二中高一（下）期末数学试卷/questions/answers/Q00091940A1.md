> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古锡林郭勒盟二中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {a} = (2, - 1), \overrightarrow {b} = (1, 1)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {a} + k \overrightarrow {b} = (2, - 1) + k (1, 1) = (2 + k, k - 1),
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\overrightarrow{c} = (-5,1)$ ，且 $(\overrightarrow{a} + k\overrightarrow{b}) / / \overrightarrow{c}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 1 \times (2 + k) - (- 5) \times (k - 1) = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > 解得： $k=\frac{1}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选：B.
