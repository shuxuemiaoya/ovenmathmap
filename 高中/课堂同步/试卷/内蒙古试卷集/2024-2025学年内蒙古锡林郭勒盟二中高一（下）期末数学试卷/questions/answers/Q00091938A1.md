> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古锡林郭勒盟二中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {a} = (2, 3), \overrightarrow {b} = (3, 2),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b} = (2, 3) - (3, 2) = (- 1, 1),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b} | = \sqrt {(- 1) ^ {2} + 1 ^ {2}} = \sqrt {2}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A.
