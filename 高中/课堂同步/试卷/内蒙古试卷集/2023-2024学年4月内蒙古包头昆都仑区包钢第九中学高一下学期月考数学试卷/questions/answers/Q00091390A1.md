>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用诱导公式及和角的正弦公式逆用求解即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \sin 2 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 8 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 2 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 7 0 ^ {\circ} = \sin 2 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 2 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} = \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A
