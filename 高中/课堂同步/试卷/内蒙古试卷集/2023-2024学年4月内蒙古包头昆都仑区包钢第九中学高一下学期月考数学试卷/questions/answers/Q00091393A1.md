> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年4月内蒙古包头昆都仑区包钢第九中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用二倍角公式及两角差的正弦公式化简，再根据正弦函数的性质判断即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > a = \frac {1}{2} \cos 6 ^ {\circ} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin 6 ^ {\circ} = \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 6 ^ {\circ} - \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ} \sin 6 ^ {\circ} = \sin (3 0 ^ {\circ} - 6 ^ {\circ}) = \sin 2 4 ^ {\circ},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b = \frac {2 \tan 1 3 ^ {\circ}}{1 - \tan^ {2} 1 3 ^ {\circ}} = \tan 2 6 ^ {\circ} = \frac {\sin 2 6 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 2 6 ^ {\circ}} > \frac {\sin 2 6 ^ {\circ}}{1} = \sin 2 6 ^ {\circ},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > c = \sqrt {\frac {1 - \cos 5 0 ^ {\circ}}{2}} = \sqrt {\frac {1 - (1 - 2 \sin^ {2} 2 5 ^ {\circ})}{2}} = \sqrt {\sin^ {2} 2 5 ^ {\circ}} = \sin 2 5 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > 因为当 $0^{\circ} < x < 90^{\circ}$ 时， $y = \sin x$ 为增函数，
> >
> > 所以 $\sin 26^{\circ} > \sin 25^{\circ} > \sin 24^{\circ}$ ，故 $a < c < b$ .
> >
> > 故选：C.
