> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年4月内蒙古包头昆都仑区包钢第九中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用平面向量的线性运算计算可得结果.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意有：
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {B E} = \overrightarrow {B A} + \overrightarrow {A E} = \overrightarrow {B A} + \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A D} = \overrightarrow {B A} + \frac {1}{3} (\overrightarrow {B D} - \overrightarrow {B A}) = \overrightarrow {B A} + \frac {1}{3} (\frac {\overrightarrow {B C}}{2} - \overrightarrow {B A}) = \frac {2}{3} \overrightarrow {B A} + \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {B C}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
