> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** BCD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 等差数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 的前 $n$ 项和为 $S_{n}$ ，公差为 $d$ 且满足 $a_{1} > 0$ ， $S_{8} = S_{11}$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore S_{11} - S_8 = a_{11} + a_{10} + a_9 = 3a_{10} = 0, \therefore a_{10} = 0$ ，故 $D$ 对；
> >
> > $S_{19} = \frac{19(a_1 + a_{19})}{2} = 19a_{10} = 0$ ，故 $C$ 对；
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {1 0} = a _ {1} + 9 d = 0, \therefore a _ {1} = - 9 d > 0, \therefore d <   0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore S _ {n} = n a _ {1} + \frac {n (n - 1)}{2} d = - 9 n d + \frac {d}{2} n ^ {2} - \frac {d}{2} n = \frac {d}{2} \left(n - \frac {1 9}{2}\right) ^ {2} - \frac {1 9 ^ {2}}{8} d,
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $d < 0$ ，∴当 $n = 9$ 或 $n = 10$ ， $S_{n}$ 取得最大值，故 $\pmb{A}$ 错， $\pmb{B}$ 对.
> >
> > 故选：BCD.
> >
