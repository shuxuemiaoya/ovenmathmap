>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意，斐波那契数列1，1，2，3，5，8，…，
> >
> > $$
> > \bar {a} a _ {3} = a _ {1} + a _ {2} = 1 + 1 = 2, a _ {4} = a _ {2} + a _ {3} = 2 + 3 = 5, a _ {5} = a _ {3} + a _ {4} = 3 + 5 = 8,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {6} = a _ {4} + a _ {5} = 5 + 8 = 1 3, a _ {7} = a _ {5} + a _ {6} = 8 + 1 3 = 2 1, a _ {8} = a _ {6} + a _ {7} = 1 3 + 2 1 = 3 4
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {9} = a _ {7} + a _ {8} = 2 1 + 3 4 = 5 5.
