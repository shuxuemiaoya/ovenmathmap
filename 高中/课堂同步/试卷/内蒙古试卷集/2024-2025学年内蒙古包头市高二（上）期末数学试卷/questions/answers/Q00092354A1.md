> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{7}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 满足 $a_2 = 3$ ， $a_{n + 1} = \frac{1 + a_n}{1 - a_n}$
> >
> > 可得 $a_{2}=\frac{1+a_{1}}{1-a_{1}}=3$ ，
> >
> > 解得 $a_{1}=\frac{1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {3} = \frac {\overline {{1}} + a _ {2}}{1 - a _ {2}} = \frac {1 + 3}{1 - 3} = - 2, a _ {4} = \frac {1 + a _ {3}}{1 - a _ {3}} = \frac {1 - 2}{1 + 2} = - \frac {1}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {5} = \frac {1 + a _ {4}}{1 - a _ {4}} = \frac {1 - \frac {1}{3}}{1 + \frac {1}{3}} = \frac {1}{2} = a _ {1}, a _ {6} = \frac {1 + a _ {5}}{1 - a _ {5}} = \frac {1 + \frac {1}{2}}{1 - \frac {1}{2}} = 3 = a _ {2}, \ldots ,
> > $$
> >
> > 所以数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 前8项的和为 $2\left(\frac{1}{2} + 3 - 2 - \frac{1}{3}\right) = \frac{7}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{7}{3}$
