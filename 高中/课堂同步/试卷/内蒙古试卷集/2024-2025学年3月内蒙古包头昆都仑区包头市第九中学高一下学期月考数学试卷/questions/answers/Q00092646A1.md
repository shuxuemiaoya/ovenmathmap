> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年3月内蒙古包头昆都仑区包头市第九中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > （1） $f(x) = \sqrt{3}\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{3} +x\right)\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{3} -x\right) + \sin x\cos x$ $= \sqrt{3}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos x + \frac{1}{2}\sin x\right)\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos x - \frac{1}{2}\sin x\right) + \frac{1}{2}\sin^2 x$ $= \sqrt{3}\left(\frac{3}{4}\cos^2 x - \frac{1}{4}\sin^2 x\right) + \frac{1}{2}\sin 2x = \sqrt{3}\left(\frac{3}{4}\times \frac{1 + \cos 2x}{2} -\frac{1}{4}\times \frac{1 - \cos 2x}{2}\right) + \frac{1}{2}\sin 2x$ $= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} +\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos 2x + \frac{1}{2}\sin 2x = \sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ 令 $\frac{\pi}{2} +2k\pi \leqslant 2x + \frac{\pi}{3}\leqslant \frac{3\pi}{2} +2k\pi (k\in \mathbb{Z})$ ，得 $\frac{\pi}{12} +k\pi \leqslant x\leqslant \frac{7\pi}{12} +k\pi (k\in \mathbb{Z})$ 即 $f(x)$ 的单调递减区间为 $\left[\frac{\pi}{12} +k\pi ,\frac{7\pi}{12} +k\pi \right](k\in \mathbb{Z}).$
> >
> > (2) 根据题意可得 $g(x) = \sin\left(4x + \frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ . 因为存在 $x_{1}, x_{2} \in \left[-\frac{\pi}{12}, \frac{\pi}{12}\right]$ ，使得不等式 $f(x_{1}) + m \geqslant g(x_{2})$ 有解，所以 $m \geqslant g(x_{2})_{\min} - f(x_{1})_{\max}$ . 当 $-\frac{\pi}{12} \leqslant x_{2} \leqslant \frac{\pi}{12}$ 时， $0 \leqslant 4x_{2} + \frac{\pi}{3} \leqslant \frac{2\pi}{3}$ , $g(x_{2})_{\min} = g\left(-\frac{\pi}{12}\right) = \sin 0 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ ，当 $-\frac{\pi}{12} \leqslant x_{1} \leqslant \frac{\pi}{12}$ 时， $\frac{\pi}{6} \leqslant 2x_{1} + \frac{\pi}{3} \leqslant \frac{\pi}{2}$ , $f(x_{1})_{\max} = f\left(\frac{\pi}{12}\right) = \sin \frac{\pi}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} = 1 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ 所以 $m \geqslant -1$ ，即 $m$ 的取值范围为 $[-1, +\infty)$ .
