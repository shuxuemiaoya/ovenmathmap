> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年3月内蒙古包头昆都仑区包头市第九中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** -7
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\cos (\alpha +\beta) = \frac{4}{5},\cos (\alpha -\beta) = -\frac{3}{5},$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \alpha \cos \beta -\sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{4}{5},\cos \alpha \cos \beta +\sin \alpha \sin \beta = -\frac{3}{5},$
> >
> > 两式相加，得 $\cos \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{1}{10}$
> >
> > 两式相减，得 $\sin \alpha \sin \beta = -\frac{7}{10}$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan \alpha \tan \beta = -7$
> >
> > 故答案为：-7.
