> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年3月内蒙古包头昆都仑区包头市第九中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析 (2) $2 - \sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）将已知式中的切化弦，通分后利用辅助角公式，再利用二倍角公式和诱导公式化简即可；
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\sin 5 0 ^ {\circ} \left(1 + \sqrt {3} \tan 1 0 ^ {\circ}\right) = \sin 5 0 ^ {\circ} (1 + \frac {\sqrt {3} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}}) = \sin 5 0 ^ {\circ} \times \frac {\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ} + \sqrt {3} \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}}} \\ & {= \sin 5 0 ^ {\circ} \times \frac {2 \sin (3 0 ^ {\circ} + 1 0 ^ {\circ})}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {2 \sin 4 0 ^ {\circ} \cos 4 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\sin 8 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 1 0 ^ {\circ}} = 1} \\ & {(2)} \\ & {\frac {2 \cos^ {2} 4 2 ^ {\circ} + \sin 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 1 ^ {\circ} - 1}{\cos 6 ^ {\circ} - \cos 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 1 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\cos 8 4 ^ {\circ} + \sin 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 1 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 6 ^ {\circ} - \cos 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 1 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\sin 6 ^ {\circ} + \sin 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 1 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 6 ^ {\circ} - \cos 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 1 ^ {\circ}}} \\ & {= \frac {\sin (8 1 ^ {\circ} - 7 5 ^ {\circ}) + \sin 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 1 ^ {\circ}}{\cos (8 1 ^ {\circ} - 7 5 ^ {\circ}) - \cos 7 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 8 1 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\sin 8 1 ^ {\circ} \cos 7 5 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 8 1 ^ {\circ} \sin 7 5 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\cos 7 5 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 7 5 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {\cos (4 5 ^ {\circ} + 3 0 ^ {\circ})}{\sin (4 5 ^ {\circ} + 3 0 ^ {\circ})}} \\ & {= \frac {\cos 4 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ} - \sin 4 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 4 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos 4 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ}} = 2 - \sqrt {3}} \end{array}
> > $$
