> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年3月内蒙古包头昆都仑区包头市第九中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用诱导公式结合二倍角的余弦公式可求得所求代数式的值.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，则
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{6} - 2 \alpha\right) = \sin \left[ \frac {\pi}{2} - 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \right] = \cos \left[ 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \right]
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = 1 - 2 \times \left(- \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {1}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：C.
