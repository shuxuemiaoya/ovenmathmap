19. 求证:

$$
(1) \frac {1 + \sin 4 \theta - \cos 4 \theta}{1 + \sin 4 \theta + \cos 4 \theta} = \frac {2 \tan \theta}{1 - \tan^ {2} \theta}.
$$

$$
(2) \frac {2 (\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha)}{1 + \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha} = \frac {\cos \alpha}{1 + \sin \alpha} - \frac {\sin \alpha}{1 + \cos \alpha}.
$$
