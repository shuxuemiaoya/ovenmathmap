> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古牙克石林业一中高一（下）第三次模拟数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)根据条件： $cos^2\alpha + cos^2\beta - 2cos\alpha cos\beta = \frac{1}{4}$ ①， $sin^2\alpha + sin^2\beta - 2sin\alpha sin\beta = \frac{1}{9}$ ②，则①+②得： $2 - 2cos(\alpha - \beta) = \frac{13}{36}$ ，所以 $cos(\alpha - \beta) = \frac{59}{72}$ ；  
> > (2)因为 $\tan \alpha = 2$ ，所以 $\frac{\sin 2\alpha}{\cos 2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha} = \frac{2\sin\alpha cos\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha - 2\sin^2\alpha} = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 - 2\tan^2\alpha} = \frac{2\times 2}{1 - 2\times 4} = -\frac{4}{7}$ .
