> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)由题可知： $cosA = -\frac{1}{3}$ ，则 $sinA = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ 且 $\frac{a}{sinA} = \frac{c}{sinC}$ ，可得 $c = \frac{asinC}{sinA}$
> >
> > 又 $a\sin C = 4\sqrt{2}$ ，所以 $c = \frac{4\sqrt{2}}{\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}} = 6$ .
> >
> > (2)作BC边上的高AM，如图：

> >
> > 则 $BC = a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A \Rightarrow a = 9$
