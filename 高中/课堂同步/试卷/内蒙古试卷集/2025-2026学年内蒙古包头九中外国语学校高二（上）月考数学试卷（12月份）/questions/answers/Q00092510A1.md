> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头九中外国语学校高二（上）月考数学试卷（12月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** BD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设椭圆与双曲线的焦距为 $\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|=2c$
> >
> > 由题意得， $c^2 = a_1^2 -b_1^2 = a_2^2 +b_2^2$
> >
> > 由椭圆的定义得： $\left|PF_{1}\right|+\left|PF_{2}\right|=2a_{1}$
> >
> > 由双曲线的定义得： $\left|PF_{1}\right|-\left|PF_{2}\right|=2a_{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\left|PF_{1}\right|=a_{1}+a_{2},\left|PF_{2}\right|=a_{1}-a_{2}\neq0$ ，即 $a_{1}\neq a_{2}$ ，故选项A错误，选项B正确；
> >
> > 在 $\triangle OPF_{2}$ 中， $|OP| = |PF_2| = a_1 - a_2$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \angle OF_2P = \frac{\frac{1}{2}|OF_2|}{|PF_2|} = \frac{c}{2(a_1 - a_2)}$
> >
> > 在 $\triangle PF_{1}F_{2}$ 中，由余弦定理得，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \angle F _ {1} F _ {2} P = \frac {\left| P F _ {2} \right| ^ {2} + \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| ^ {2} - \left| P F _ {1} \right| ^ {2}}{2 \cdot \left| P F _ {2} \right| \cdot \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right|} = \frac {(a _ {1} - a _ {2}) ^ {2} + (2 c) ^ {2} - (a _ {1} + a _ {2}) ^ {2}}{2 \times (a _ {1} - a _ {2}) \times 2 c}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {4 c ^ {2} - 4 a _ {1} a _ {2}}{4 c \times (a _ {1} - a _ {2})}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > , \frac {4 c ^ {2} - 4 a _ {1} a _ {2}}{4 c \times (a _ {1} - a _ {2})} = \frac {c}{2 (a _ {1} - a _ {2})}, \text {可得} c ^ {2} = 2 a _ {1} a _ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \angle F _ {1} P F _ {2} = \frac {\left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| ^ {2} - \left| P F _ {2} \right| ^ {2} - \left| P F _ {1} \right| ^ {2}}{2 \cdot \left| P F _ {2} \right| \cdot \left| F _ {1} P \right|} = \frac {4 c ^ {2} - 2 a _ {1} ^ {2} - 2 a _ {2} ^ {2}}{2 (a _ {1} ^ {2} - a _ {2} ^ {2})} = \frac {4 a _ {1} a _ {2} - a _ {1} ^ {2} - a _ {2} ^ {2}}{a _ {1} ^ {2} - a _ {2} ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > <   \frac {2 a _ {1} a _ {2}}{a _ {1} ^ {2} - a _ {2} ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > ，所以 $PF_{1}\perp PF_{2}$ 不成立，所以C错误.
> >
> > $e_1 \cdot e_2 = \frac{c^2}{a_1 a_2} = \frac{2a_1 a_2}{a_1 a_2} = 2$ ，所以 $D$ 正确.
> >
> > 故选：BD.
