> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头九中外国语学校高二（上）月考数学试卷（12月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\overrightarrow{b} = (1, x, y)$ , $\overrightarrow{a} // \overrightarrow{b}$ , $\overrightarrow{a} = (-1, 0, -1)$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{\pmb{b}} = t\overrightarrow{\pmb{a}}$ ，即 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}1 = -t\\ x = 0\\ y = -t \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}t = -1\\ x = 0\\ y = 1 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以 $x+y=1$ .
> >
> > 故选：A.
