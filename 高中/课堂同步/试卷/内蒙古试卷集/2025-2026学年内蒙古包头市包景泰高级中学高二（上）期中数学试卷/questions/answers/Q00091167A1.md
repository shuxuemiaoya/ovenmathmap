> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头市包景泰高级中学高二（上）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得 $3(a-4)+3(b+1)=0$ ，故 $a+b=3$ ，
> >
> > 因为 $a > 0, b > 0$ ，由基本不等式得
> >
> > $$
> > {\frac {2}{a}} + {\frac {1}{2 b}} = {\frac {1}{3}} ({\frac {2}{a}} + {\frac {1}{2 b}}) (a + b) = {\frac {1}{3}} (2 + {\frac {1}{2}} + {\frac {2 b}{a}} + {\frac {a}{2 b}}) \geq {\frac {1}{3}} ({\frac {5}{2}} + 2 {\sqrt {{\frac {2 b}{a}} \bullet {\frac {a}{2 b}}}}) = {\frac {1}{3}} \times {\frac {9}{2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {3}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $\frac{2b}{a} = \frac{a}{2b}$ ，即 $a = 2$ ， $b = 1$ 时，等号成立.
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{3}{2}$ .
