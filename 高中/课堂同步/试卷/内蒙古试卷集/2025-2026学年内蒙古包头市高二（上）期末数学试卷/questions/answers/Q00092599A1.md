> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $^{•}$ .椭圆的焦点在x轴，顶点在y轴，
> >
> > 抛物线 $C_2: y = x^2 - 1$ 与坐标轴的交点分别为 $(-1, 0)$ , $(1, 0)$ , $(0, -1)$ ,
> >
> > $\therefore$ 椭圆的焦点为 $(-1,0)$ ， $(1,0)$ ，顶点为 $(0,-1)$
> >
> > 则 $c = 1, b = 1, a = \sqrt{2}$
> >
> > $\therefore$ 椭圆 $S_{1}$ 的标准方程为 $\frac{x^2}{2} + y^2 = 1$
> >
> > (2)设 $C_{2}$ 与y轴的交点为K，过坐标原点O的直线l与 $C_{2}$ 相交于点A，B，直线KA，KB分别与 $C_{1}$ 相交于点D，E.
> >
> > (i)证明：由题意得直线l的斜率存在，设直线l的方程为y=kx，
> >
> > 联立直线与抛物线 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y&=kx\\ y&=x^{2}-1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，得 $x^{2}-kx-1=0$ ，
> >
> > $\Delta = k^2 + 4 > 0$ ，设 $A(x_1, y_1), B(x_2, y_2)$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| x _ {1} + x _ {2} = k, x _ {1} x _ {2} = - 1 \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because k _ {K A} = \frac {y _ {1} + 1}{x _ {1}}, k _ {K B} = \frac {y _ {2} + 1}{x _ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore k _ {K A} \cdot k _ {K B} = \frac {y _ {1} + 1}{x _ {1}} \cdot \frac {y _ {2} + 1}{x _ {2}} = \frac {x _ {1} ^ {2}}{x _ {1}} \cdot \frac {x _ {2} ^ {2}}{x _ {2}} = x _ {1} x _ {2} = - 1
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore K A \bot K B, \therefore K D \bot K E
> > $$
> >
> > (ii)记 $\triangle KAB$ ， $\triangle KDE$ 的面积分别是 $S_{1}$ ， $S_{2}$
> >
> > 由(i)可知设 $k_{KA}=k_{1}$ ， $k_{KB}=-\frac{1}{k_{1}}$ ，
> >
> > 则直线KA:y=k1x-1
> >
> > 联立 $KA$ 与抛物线 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = k_{1}x - 1 \\ y = x^{2} - 1 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=0\\ y&=-1\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=k_{1}\\ y&=k_{1}^{2}-1\end{aligned}\right.,\therefore A(k_{1},k_{1}^{2}-1)$
> >
> > 则 $|KA|=\sqrt{k_{1}^{2}+1}|k_{1}|$ ，同理 $|KB|=\sqrt{\frac{1}{k_{1}^{2}}+1}|\frac{1}{k_{1}}|=\frac{\sqrt{k_{1}^{2}+1}}{k_{1}^{2}}$
> >
> > 又直线与椭圆联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} y = k_{1}x - 1 \\ x^{2} + 2y^{2} - 2 = 0 \end{array} \right.$ ，得 $(1 + 2k_{1}^{2})x^{2} - 4k_{1}x = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore x _ {D} = \frac {4 k _ {1}}{1 + 2 k _ {1} ^ {2}}, | K D | = \sqrt {k _ {1} ^ {2} + 1} \big | \frac {4 k _ {1}}{1 + 2 k _ {1} ^ {2}} \big |,
> > $$
> >
> > 同理， $|KE| = \sqrt{\frac{1}{k_1^2} + 1} |\frac{4\frac{1}{k_1}}{1 + 2\frac{1}{k_1^2}} | = \frac{\sqrt{1 + k_1^2}}{|k_1|} |\frac{4k_1}{k_1^2 + 2}| = \frac{4\sqrt{1 + k_1^2}}{k_1^2 + 2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {S _ {1}}{S _ {2}} = \frac {\frac {1}{2} | K A | | K B |}{\frac {1}{2} | L D | | K E |} = \frac {\frac {k _ {1} ^ {2} + 1}{| k _ {1} |}}{\frac {1 6 (1 + k _ {1} ^ {2}) | k _ {1} |}{(1 + 2 k _ {1} ^ {2}) (k _ {1} ^ {2} + 2)}} = \frac {(1 + 2 k _ {1} ^ {2}) (2 + k _ {1} ^ {2})}{1 6 k _ {1} ^ {2}} = \frac {1}{1 6} (\frac {2 k _ {1} ^ {4} + 5 k _ {1} ^ {2} + 2)}{k _ {1} ^ {2}})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{1 6} \left(2 k _ {1} ^ {2} + \frac {2}{k _ {1} ^ {2}} + 5\right) \geqslant \frac {9}{1 6},
> > $$
> >
> > 当且仅当 $k_{1}=\pm1$ 时，取等号，
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{S_1}{S_2}$ 的最小值为 $\frac{9}{16}$ ，
> >
> > 此时A点坐标为 $(1,0)$ ，则直线l的方程为y=0.
