> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意可得 $a_{n}=1+2+3+\ldots+n=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{a_n} = 2\left(\frac{1}{n} -\frac{1}{n + 1}\right)$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{a_1} +\frac{1}{a_2} +\frac{1}{a_3} +\dots +\frac{1}{a_{2026}}$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 (1 - \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 0 2 6} - \frac {1}{2 0 2 7})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 2 \left(1 - \frac {1}{2 0 2 7}\right) = 2 \times \frac {2 0 2 6}{2 0 2 7} = \frac {4 0 5 2}{2 0 2 7}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
