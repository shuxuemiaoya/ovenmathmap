> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可得 $\overrightarrow{BB_1} \cdot \overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{BB_1} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} = 0$
> >
> > 设AB=1，则 $BB_{1}=\sqrt{2}$ ，
> >
> > 可得 $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} = |\overrightarrow{AB}| \cdot |\overrightarrow{BC}|\cos 120^{\circ} = 1 \times 1 \times \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = -\frac{1}{2}$ ,
> >
> > 因为
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {A B _ {1}} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C _ {1}} = (\overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {B B _ {1}}) \cdot (\overrightarrow {B B _ {1}} + \overrightarrow {B C}) = \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {B B _ {1}} + \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} + \overrightarrow {B B _ {1}} ^ {2} + \overrightarrow {B B _ {1}} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 0 - \frac {1}{2} + 2 + 0 = \frac {3}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 由题意可得 $\left|AB_{1}\right|=\left|BC_{1}\right|=\sqrt{1+2}=\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $AB_{1}$ 与 $BC_{1}$ 所成角的余弦值为 $\left|\cos \right| < \overrightarrow{AB_1}$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {B C _ {1}} > | = \left| \frac {\overrightarrow {A B _ {1}} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C _ {1}}}{\left| \overrightarrow {A B _ {1}} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow {B C _ {1}} \right|} \right| = \left| \frac {\frac {3}{2}}{\sqrt {3} \times \sqrt {3}} \right| = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $AB_{1}$ 与 $BC_{1}$ 所成角的大小为 $60^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 故选：B.
