> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头市高二（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)根据题意可得 $X$ 的所有可能取值为 1, 3, 5,
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 1) = C _ {5} ^ {3} \times \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {3} \times \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {2} + C _ {5} ^ {3} \times \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {2} \times \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {3} = \frac {4 0}{8 1}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 3) = C _ {5} ^ {4} \times \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {1} \times \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {4} + C _ {5} ^ {4} \times \left(\frac {1}{3}\right) ^ {4} \times \left(\frac {2}{3}\right) ^ {1} = \frac {1 0}{2 7},
> > $$
> >
> > $P(X = 5) = C_{5}^{5}\times \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{5} + C_{5}^{5}\times \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{5} = \frac{11}{81},$
> >
> > 所以 $X$ 的分布列为：
> >
> > <table><tr><td>X</td><td>1</td><td>3</td><td>5</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td>40/81</td><td>10/27</td><td>11/81</td></tr></table>
> >
> > 所以 $E(X)=1\times\frac{40}{81}+3\times\frac{10}{27}+5\times\frac{11}{81}=\frac{185}{81}$ ;
> >
> > (2)根据题意可得 $P(B|\sum_{k = 0}^{n - 2}A_k) = 0$ ， $P(B|A_{n - 1}) = \frac{1}{4}$ ， $P(B|A_n) = \frac{3}{4}$ ， $P(B|\sum_{k = n + 1}^{2n - 1}A_k) = 1$
> >
> > (3)证明：由题意可得：
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {{P _ {n + 1} = C _ {2 n - 1} ^ {n - 1} p ^ {n - 1} (1 - p) ^ {n} \cdot p ^ {2} + C _ {2 n - 1} ^ {n} p ^ {n} (1 - p) ^ {n - 1} \cdot [ 1 - (1 - p) ^ {2} ]}} \\ & {{+ [ P _ {n} - C _ {2 n - 1} ^ {n} p ^ {n} (1 - p) ^ {n - 1} ]}} \\ & {{= P _ {n} + C _ {2 n - 1} ^ {n - 1} p ^ {n - 1} (1 - p) ^ {n} \cdot p ^ {2} - C _ {2 n - 1} ^ {n} p ^ {n} (1 - p) ^ {n - 1} \cdot (1 - p) ^ {2}}} \\ & {{= P _ {n} + C _ {2 n - 1} ^ {n} p ^ {n} (1 - p) ^ {n} (2 p - 1),}} \\ & {{\mathrm{所以} P _ {n + 1} - P _ {n} = C _ {2 n - 1} ^ {n} p ^ {n} (1 - p) ^ {n} (2 p - 1),}} \\ & {{\mathrm{当} \frac {2}{3} <   p <   1 \mathrm{时}, P _ {n + 1} - P _ {n} > 0,}} \\ & {{\frac {P _ {n + 2} - P _ {n + 1}}{P _ {n + 1} - P _ {n}} = \frac {C _ {2 n + 1} ^ {n + 1} p ^ {n + 1} (1 - p) ^ {n + 1} (2 p - 1)}{C _ {2 n - 1} ^ {n} p ^ {n} (1 - p) ^ {n} (2 p - 1)} = \frac {4 n + 2}{n + 1} p (1 - p) <   4 p (1 - p) <   1,}} \\ & {{\mathrm{因为} P _ {n + 1} - P _ {n} > 0,}} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $P_{n + 2} - P_{n + 1} <   P_{n + 1} - P_n.$
