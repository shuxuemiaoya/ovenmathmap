> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头市高二（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \triangle ABC$ 中， $a = 2$ ， $b = \sqrt{2}$ ， $c = \sqrt{3} +1$
> >
> > $\therefore$ 由余弦定理得 $cosC = \frac{2^2 + (\sqrt{2})^2 - (\sqrt{3} + 1)^2}{2 \times 2 \times \sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \cos {\frac {7 \pi}{1 2}} = \cos (\frac {\pi}{3} + \frac {\pi}{4}) = \cos \frac {\pi}{3} \cos \frac {\pi}{4} - \sin \frac {\pi}{3} \sin \frac {\pi}{4} = \frac {1}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {2} - \sqrt {6}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴结合 $C\in(0,\pi)$ ，可得 $C=\frac{7\pi}{12}$ .
> >
