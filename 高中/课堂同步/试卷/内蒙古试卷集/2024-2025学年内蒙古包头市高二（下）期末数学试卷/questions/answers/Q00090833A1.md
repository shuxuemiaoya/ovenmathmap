> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头市高二（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据 $\sin 2\alpha = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{2}{3}$ ，可得 $(\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha)^2 = 1 + 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 1 + \frac{2}{3} = \frac{5}{3} \dots$ ①，
> >
> > $$
> > \text {且} (\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha) ^ {2} = 1 - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 1 - \frac {2}{3} = \frac {1}{3} \dots ②
> > $$
> >
> > 结合 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4},\frac{\pi}{2}\right),\sin \alpha >\cos \alpha$ ，解得 $sin\alpha +cos\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{15}}{3}$ ，且 $sin\alpha -cos\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > t a n (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{4}) = \frac {t a n \alpha + 1}{1 - t a n \alpha} = \frac {s i n \alpha + c o s \alpha}{c o s \alpha - s i n \alpha} = \frac {\frac {\sqrt {1 5}}{3}}{- \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}} = - \sqrt {5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D.
