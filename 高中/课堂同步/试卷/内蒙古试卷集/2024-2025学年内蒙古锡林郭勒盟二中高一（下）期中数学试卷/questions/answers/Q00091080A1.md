> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古锡林郭勒盟二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \sin 2 \theta = - \cos \left(2 \theta + \frac {\pi}{2}\right) = - \cos 2 \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = - \left(1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{4}\right)\right) = 2 \times \left(\frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {2} - 1 = - \frac {7}{8},
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D.
