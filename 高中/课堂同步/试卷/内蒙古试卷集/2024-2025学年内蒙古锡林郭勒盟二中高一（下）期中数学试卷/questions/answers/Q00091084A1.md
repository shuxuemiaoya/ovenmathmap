> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古锡林郭勒盟二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \alpha , \beta \in (0, \pi), \frac {\tan \alpha}{\tan \beta} = - \frac {1}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 0 <   \alpha <   \frac {\pi}{2}, \frac {\pi}{2} <   \beta <   \pi \text {或} 0 <   \beta <   \frac {\pi}{2}, \frac {\pi}{2} <   \alpha <   \pi ,
> > $$
> >
> > ①当 $0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ， $\frac{\pi}{2} < \beta < \pi$ 时，则 $-\pi < \alpha - \beta < 0$ ， $\sin (\alpha - \beta) < 0$ ，不符合题意，
> >
> > ②当 $0 < \beta < \frac{\pi}{2}$ ， $\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha < \pi$ 时，则 $0 < \alpha - \beta < \pi$ ， $\sin (\alpha - \beta) > 0$ ，符合题意，
> >
> > $$
> > \because \sin (\alpha - \beta) = \frac {5}{6}, \frac {\tan \alpha}{\tan \beta} = - \frac {1}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \alpha \cos \beta - \cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac {5}{6}, \frac {\sin \alpha \cos \beta}{\cos \alpha \sin \beta} = - \frac {1}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \alpha \cos \beta = \frac {1}{6}, \cos \alpha \sin \beta = - \frac {2}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin (\alpha + \beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta + \cos \alpha \sin \beta = - \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because 0 <   \beta <   \frac {\pi}{2}, \frac {\pi}{2} <   \alpha <   \pi , \therefore \frac {\pi}{2} \alpha + \beta <   \frac {3 \pi}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \alpha + \beta = \frac {7 \pi}{6}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
