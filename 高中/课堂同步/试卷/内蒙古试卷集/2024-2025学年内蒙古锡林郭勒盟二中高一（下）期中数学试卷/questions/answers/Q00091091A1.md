> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古锡林郭勒盟二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \text {(1)} \sqrt {6 \frac {1}{4}} + (\frac {8}{2 7}) ^ {- \frac {1}{3}} + 0. 1 2 5 ^ {0} \\ = \sqrt {\frac {2 5}{4}} + [ (\frac {2}{3}) ^ {3} ] ^ {- \frac {1}{3}} + 1 \\ = \frac {5}{2} + (\frac {2}{3}) ^ {- 1} + 1 \\ = \frac {5}{2} + \frac {3}{2} + 1 \\ = 5; \\ (2) \because x ^ {\frac {1}{2}} + x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} = 4, \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore x + x^{-1} = (x^{\frac{1}{2}} + x^{-\frac{1}{2}})^2 - 2 = 14,$ $\therefore x^{2} + x^{-2} = (x + x^{-1})^{2} - 2 = 14^{2} - 2 = 194,$ $\therefore \frac{x + x^{-1} + 4}{x^{2} + x^{-2} - 200} = \frac{14 + 4}{194 - 200} = -3.$
