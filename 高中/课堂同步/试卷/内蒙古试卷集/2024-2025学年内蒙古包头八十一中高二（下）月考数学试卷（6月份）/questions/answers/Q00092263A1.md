> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头八十一中高二（下）月考数学试卷（6月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意可知，随机事件 $M$ ， $N$ 满足 $M\subseteq N$ ， $\therefore P(MN) = P(M) = \frac{1}{19}$
> >
> > $$
> > \because P (\bar {N}) = \frac {1 7}{1 9}, \therefore P (N) = 1 - P (\bar {N}) = \frac {2}{1 9}, \therefore P (M | N) = \frac {P (M N)}{P (N)} = \frac {\frac {1}{1 9}}{\frac {2}{1 9}} = \frac {1}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A.
