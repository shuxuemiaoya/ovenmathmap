> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特一中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{2 - \sqrt{3}}{3}$ ；
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha\right) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ， $\therefore \cos \left(\frac{5}{6}\pi + \alpha\right) = -\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha\right) = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ . $\cos^2 \left(\frac{4\pi}{3} + \alpha\right) = \frac{1 + \cos \left(\frac{2\pi}{3} + 2\alpha\right)}{2} = \frac{1 - \cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - 2\alpha\right)}{2} = 1 - \cos^2 \left(\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha\right) = 1$ $-\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{2}{3}$ $\therefore \cos \left(\frac{5}{6}\pi + \alpha\right) + \cos^2 \left(\frac{4\pi}{3} + \alpha\right) = \frac{2 - \sqrt{3}}{3}$ .  
> > 故答案为： $\frac{2 - \sqrt{3}}{3}$ ；
