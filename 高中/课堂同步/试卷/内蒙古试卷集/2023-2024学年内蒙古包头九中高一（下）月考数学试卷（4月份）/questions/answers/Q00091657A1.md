> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古包头九中高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\begin{array}{rl} & {\sin 20^{\circ}\sin 80^{\circ} + \cos 20^{\circ}\sin 170^{\circ}}\\ & {= \sin 20^{\circ}\sin 80^{\circ} + \cos 20^{\circ}\cos 80^{\circ}}\\ & {= cos (80^{\circ} - 20^{\circ}) = \frac{1}{2}.} \end{array}$
> >
> > 故选：A.
