> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古包头九中高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 依题意， $\overrightarrow{a} \bullet \overrightarrow{b} = |\overrightarrow{a}| \bullet |\overrightarrow{b}| \cos \frac{2\pi}{3} = 4 \times 8 \times \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = -16$
> >
> > 所以 $|\overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b}| = \sqrt{(\overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b})^2} = \sqrt{\overrightarrow{a}^2 - 2\overrightarrow{a}\bullet\overrightarrow{b} + \overrightarrow{b}^2}$ $= \sqrt{4^2 - 2\times(-16) + 8^2} = 4\sqrt{7}.$
> >
> > (2)依题意， $(\overrightarrow{a} + k\overrightarrow{b})\bullet (k\overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b}) = k\overrightarrow{a}^2 +(k^2 -1)\overrightarrow{a}\bullet \overrightarrow{b} -k\overrightarrow{b}^2$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 6 k - 1 6 \left(k ^ {2} - 1\right) - 6 4 k = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 化为 $k^{2}+3k-1=0$ ，解得 $k=\frac{-3\pm\sqrt{13}}{2}$ .
