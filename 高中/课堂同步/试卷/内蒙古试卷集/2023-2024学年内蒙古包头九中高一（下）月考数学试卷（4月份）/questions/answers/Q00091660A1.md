> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古包头九中高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > a = \frac {1}{2} \cos 6 ^ {\circ} - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \sin 6 ^ {\circ} = \sin (3 0 ^ {\circ} - 6 ^ {\circ}) = \sin 2 4 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > b = \frac {2 \tan 1 3 ^ {\circ}}{1 - \tan^ {2} 1 3 ^ {\circ}} = \tan 2 6 ^ {\circ} = \frac {\sin 2 6 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 2 6 ^ {\circ}} > \frac {\sin 2 6 ^ {\circ}}{1} = \sin 2 6 ^ {\circ},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > c = \sqrt {\frac {1 - \cos 5 0 ^ {\circ}}{2}} = \sin 2 5 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \sin 2 4 ^ {\circ} <   \sin 2 5 ^ {\circ} <   \sin 2 6 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a <   c <   b.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：C.
