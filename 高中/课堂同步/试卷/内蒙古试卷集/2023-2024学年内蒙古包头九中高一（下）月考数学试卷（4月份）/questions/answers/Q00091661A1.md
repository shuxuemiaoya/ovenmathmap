> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古包头九中高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {P A} \cdot \overrightarrow {P B} = \overrightarrow {P B} \cdot \overrightarrow {P C} = \overrightarrow {P C} \cdot \overrightarrow {P A},
> > $$
> >
> > 则由 $\overrightarrow{PA} \cdot \overrightarrow{PB} = \overrightarrow{PB} \cdot \overrightarrow{PC}$ 得：
> >
> > $\overrightarrow{PB} \cdot (\overrightarrow{PC} - \overrightarrow{PA}) = 0$ ，即 $\overrightarrow{PB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = 0$ ， $\therefore PB \perp AC$
> >
> > 同理 $PA \perp BC$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > P C \perp A B,
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $P$ 是垂心
> >
> > 故选：D.
