> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年3月内蒙古包头九原区包头市第九中学外国语学校高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）利用同角三角函数基本关系结合角的象限，求出 $\sin\alpha$ 、 $\cos\beta$ ，再代入两角差的余弦公式计算 $\cos(\alpha-\beta)$ 的值.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > （1）由题意可知 $\sin \alpha = -\sqrt{1 - \cos^2\alpha} = -\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \beta = - \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} \beta} = - \frac {4}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以
> >
> > $$
> > \cos (\alpha - \beta) = \cos \alpha \cos \beta + \sin \alpha \sin \beta = - \frac {4}{1 5} - \frac {6 \sqrt {2}}{1 5} = - \frac {4 + 6 \sqrt {2}}{1 5}
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 因为 $\tan\alpha=\frac{1}{7},\sin\beta=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10},\quad\alpha,\beta\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \beta = \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10},\tan \beta = \frac{1}{3},2\beta \in (0,\pi),$
> >
> > 则 $\tan 2\beta = \frac{2\tan\beta}{1 - \tan^2\beta} = \frac{2\times\frac{1}{3}}{1 - \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2} = \frac{3}{4} >0$ ，所以 $0 <   2\beta <  \frac{\pi}{2},$
> >
> > 故 $0 < \alpha + 2\beta < \pi,$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan (\alpha + 2 \beta) = \frac {\tan \alpha + \tan 2 \beta}{1 - \tan \alpha \tan 2 \beta} = \frac {\frac {1}{7} + \frac {3}{4}}{1 - \frac {1}{7} \times \frac {3}{4}} = 1,
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $\alpha + 2\beta = \frac{\pi}{4}$ .
