> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年3月内蒙古包头九原区包头市第九中学外国语学校高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{1}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 运用二倍角进行化简，将其转为齐次式即可求出结果.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 注意到 $1 + \sin 2\theta = \sin^{2}\theta + \cos^{2}\theta + 2\sin\theta\cos\theta = (\sin\theta + \cos\theta)^{2}$ ，则 $\frac{\cos\theta(1+\sin2\theta)}{\sin\theta+\cos\theta}=\frac{\cos\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}}{\sin\theta+\cos\theta}=\frac{\cos\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)}{1}=\frac{\cos\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)}{\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta}$ ，又 $\sin\theta+2\cos\theta=0\Rightarrow\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=-2$ ，则 $\frac{\cos\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)}{\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta}=\frac{\tan\theta+1}{\tan^{2}\theta+1}=-\frac{1}{5}.$ 故答案为： $-\frac{1}{5}.$
