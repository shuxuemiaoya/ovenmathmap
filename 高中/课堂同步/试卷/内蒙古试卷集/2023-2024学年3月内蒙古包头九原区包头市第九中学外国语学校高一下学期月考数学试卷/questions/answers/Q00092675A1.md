> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年3月内蒙古包头九原区包头市第九中学外国语学校高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析 $$ (2) \frac {\sin a + 2 \cos \alpha}{2 \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha} = \frac {1}{7}, \sin 2 \alpha = - \frac {3}{5} $$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > (1) 直接利用诱导公式化简即可；
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \text {   (1)   由   } f (x) = \frac {\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - x\right) \cos \left(\frac {3 \pi}{2} + x\right) \tan (\pi - x)}{\cos (3 \pi - x) \sin (\pi + x)},
> > $$
> >
> > 可知 $f(x) = \frac{\cos x\sin x(-\tan x)}{(-\cos x)(- \sin x)} = -\tan x$
> >
> > (2) 由 $f(\alpha) = 3$ ，得 $-\tan \alpha = 3$ 即 $\tan \alpha = -3$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > {\frac {\sin a + 2 \cos \alpha}{2 \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha}} = {\frac {\tan a + 2}{2 \tan \alpha - 1}} = {\frac {1}{7}},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \text {则} \sin 2 \alpha = 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = {\frac {2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha}} = {\frac {2 \tan \alpha}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1}} = - {\frac {3}{5}}
> > $$
