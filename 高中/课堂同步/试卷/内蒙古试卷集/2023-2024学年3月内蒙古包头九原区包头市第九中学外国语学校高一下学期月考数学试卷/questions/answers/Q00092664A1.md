> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年3月内蒙古包头九原区包头市第九中学外国语学校高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 判断角的范围，根据同角的三角函数关系式求得 $\cos \left(\frac{3\pi}{4} +\alpha\right)$ $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} +\beta\right)$ ，再根据两角和的余弦公式即可求得答案.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $0 <   \alpha <  \frac{\pi}{4},\frac{\pi}{4} <  \beta <  \frac{3\pi}{4}$ ，所以 $\frac{3\pi}{4} <  \frac{3\pi}{4} +\alpha <  \pi$ $\frac{\pi}{2} <  \frac{\pi}{4} +\beta <  \pi ,$ 又因为 $\sin \left(\frac{3\pi}{4} +\alpha\right) = \frac{5}{13},\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} +\beta\right) = \frac{3}{5},$ 所以 $\cos \left(\frac{3\pi}{4} +\alpha\right) = -\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{5}{13}\right)^2} = -\frac{12}{13},\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} +\beta\right) = -\frac{4}{5},$ 故 $\cos (\alpha +\beta) = -\cos \left(\frac{3\pi}{4} +\alpha +\frac{\pi}{4} +\beta\right) = \sin \left(\frac{3\pi}{4} +\alpha\right)\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} +\beta\right) - \cos \left(\frac{3\pi}{4} +\alpha\right)\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{4} +\beta\right)$ $= \frac{5}{13}\times \frac{3}{5} -\frac{12}{13}\times \frac{4}{5} = -\frac{33}{65},$ 故选：B.
