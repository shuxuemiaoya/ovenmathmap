> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 若 $\sin\alpha=\frac{1}{3}$ ,
> >
> > 由于 $\sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1$ ， $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}$
> >
> > 可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}cos\alpha = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}, & tan\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\\ cos\alpha = -\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}, & tan\alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > (2)若 $\tan \alpha = 2$
> >
> > 可得 $\frac{\sin^2\alpha - \cos^2\alpha}{\cos^2\alpha - 2\sin\alpha \bullet \cos\alpha} = \frac{\tan^2\alpha - 1}{1 - 2\tan\alpha} = \frac{2^2 - 1}{1 - 2 \times 2} = -1.$
