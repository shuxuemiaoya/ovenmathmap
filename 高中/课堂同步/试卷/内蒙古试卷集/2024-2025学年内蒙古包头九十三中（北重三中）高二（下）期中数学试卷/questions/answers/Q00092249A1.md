> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头九十三中（北重三中）高二（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 6
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $f(x) = a \cos x$ , $g(x) = x^{2} + bx + 3$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore f ^ {\prime} (x) = - a \sin x, g ^ {\prime} (x) = 2 x + b,
> > $$
> >
> > $\because$ 曲线 $f(x) = a\cos x$ 与曲线 $g(x) = x^{2} + bx + 3$ 在交点 $(0,m)$ 处有公切线，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore m = f (0) = a = g (0) = 3 \text {且} f ^ {\prime} (0) = 0 = g ^ {\prime} (0) = b,
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $a = m = 3, b = 0, \therefore a + b + m = 3 + 0 + 3 = 6.$
> >
> > 故答案为：6.
