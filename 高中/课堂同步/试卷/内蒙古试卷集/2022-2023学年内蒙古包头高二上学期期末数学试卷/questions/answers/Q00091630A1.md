> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古包头高二上学期期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析 1
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由中垂线性质知， $|NB| = |NM|$
> >
> > 所以 $||NB| - |NA|| = |AM| = 2\sqrt{3} < |AB|$
> >
> > 所以点 $N$ 的轨迹是以 $A$ 、 $B$ 为焦点，实轴长为 $2\sqrt{3}$ 的双曲线
> >
> > 设此双曲线方程为 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} -\frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1(a > 0,b > 0)$ ，则 $a = \sqrt{3},a^2 +b^2 = 4,\therefore b^2 = 1$
> >
> > 所以点N的轨迹方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{3}-y^{2}=1$
> >
> > (2) 设 $C(x_{1}, y_{1}), D(x_{2}, y_{2})$ 可得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{x_1^2}{3} - y_1^2 = 1 \\ \frac{x_2^2}{3} - y_2^2 = 1 \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 两式相减得 $\frac{1}{3} (x_1 + x_2)(x_1 - x_2) - (y_1 + y_2)(y_1 - y_2) = 0$
> >
> > 由题意 $x_{1} + x_{2} = 3, y_{1} + y_{2} = 1$ ，所以 $k_{AB} = \frac{y_{1} - y_{2}}{x_{1} - x_{2}} = 1$
> >
> > 直线 $CD$ 方程为 $\pmb {y} - \frac{1}{2} = 1\times \left(x - \frac{3}{2}\right),\pmb {y} = \pmb {x} - \pmb{1},$
> >
> > 由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y&=x-1\\ \frac{x^{2}}{3}-y^{2}&=1\end{aligned}\right.$ ，得 $x^{2}-3x+3=0$ $\because \$\Delta = -3$
