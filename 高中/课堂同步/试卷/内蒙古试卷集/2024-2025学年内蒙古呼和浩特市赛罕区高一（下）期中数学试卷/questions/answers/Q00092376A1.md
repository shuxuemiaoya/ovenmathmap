> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特市赛罕区高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\overrightarrow{a} = (-2, -1), \overrightarrow{b} = (-1, -1)$
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = -1 \times (-2) + (-1) \times (-1) = 3$ ， $|\overrightarrow{a}| = \sqrt{(-2)^2 + (-1)^2} = \sqrt{5}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > | \overrightarrow {b} | = \sqrt {(- 1) ^ {2} + (- 1) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {2},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos\langle\overrightarrow{a},\overrightarrow{b}\rangle=\frac{\overrightarrow{a}\cdot\overrightarrow{b}}{|\overrightarrow{a}||\overrightarrow{b}|}=\frac{3}{\sqrt{5}\times\sqrt{2}}=\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}.$
> >
> > 故选：D.
