> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特市赛罕区高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)因为 $\overrightarrow{OP} = (-5, 12)$ ，所以 $|\overrightarrow{OP}| = \sqrt{(-5)^2 + 12^2} = 13$
> >
> > 设 $\angle xOP = \theta$ ，由三角函数的定义可得： $\cos \theta = -\frac{5}{13}$ ， $sin\theta = \frac{12}{13}$ 设 $P^{\prime}(x_{1},y_{1})$
> >
> > 若绕原点O顺时针旋转120°
> >
> > $$
> > \left| x _ {1} = 1 3 \cos \left(\theta - 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}\right) = 1 3 \left(\cos \theta \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin \theta \sin 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}\right) \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 3 \left[ - \frac {5}{1 3} \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) + \frac {1 2}{1 3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \right] = \frac {5}{2} + 6 \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y _ {1} = 1 3 \sin (\theta - 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}) = 1 3 (\sin \theta \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \cos \theta \sin 1 2 0 ^ {\circ})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 3 \left[ \frac {1 2}{1 3} \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) - \left(- \frac {5}{1 3}\right) \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \right] = - 6 + \frac {5 \sqrt {3}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $P'\left(\frac{5}{2}+6\sqrt{3},-6+\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ ;
> >
> > 若绕原点O逆时针旋转120°
> >
> > $$
> > \left| x _ {1} = 1 3 \cos \left(\theta + 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}\right) = 1 3 \left(\cos \theta \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} - \sin \theta \sin 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}\right) \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 3 \left[ - \frac {5}{1 3} \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) - \frac {1 2}{1 3} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \right] = \frac {5}{2} - 6 \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y _ {1} = 1 3 \sin (\theta + 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}) = 1 3 (\sin \theta \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos \theta \sin 1 2 0 ^ {\circ})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = 1 3 \left[ \frac {1 2}{1 3} \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) + \left(- \frac {5}{1 3}\right) \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \right] = - 6 - \frac {5 \sqrt {3}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $P'\left(\frac{5}{2}-6\sqrt{3},-6-\frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$ ;
> >
> > 综上可得： $P^{\prime}(\frac{5}{2} +6\sqrt{3}, - 6 + \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2})$ 或 $P^{\prime}(\frac{5}{2} -6\sqrt{3}, - 6 - \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{2}).$
> >
> > (2)因为向量 $\overrightarrow{OP} = (-5, 12)$ 绕原点 $O$ 旋转 $120^{\circ}$ 到 $\overrightarrow{OP'}$ 的位置，
> >
> > 所以 $\left|\overrightarrow{OP}\right|=13,\left|\overrightarrow{OP^{\prime}}\right|=13,\angle POP^{\prime}=120^{\circ}$
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\triangle O P P ^ {\prime}} = \frac {1}{2} | \overrightarrow {O P} | | \overrightarrow {O P ^ {\prime}} | s i n \angle P O P ^ {\prime} = \frac {1}{2} \times 1 3 \times 1 3 \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {1 6 9 \sqrt {3}}{4}.
> > $$
