> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷（文科）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\left|MF_{1}\right| + \left|MF_{2}\right| = 2a$ , $\left|MF_{1}\right| = 2\left|MF_{2}\right|$ ,
> > ∴ $\left|MF_{1}\right|=\frac{4a}{3}$ , $\left|MF_{2}\right|=\frac{2a}{3}$ ,
> > 又 $MF_{1}\perp MF_{2}$
> >
> > $\therefore |MF_1|^2 + |MF_2|^2 = |F_1F_2|^2$ ，即 $\frac{16a^2}{9} + \frac{4a^2}{9} = 4c^2$ ， $\therefore e^2 = \frac{c^2}{a^2} = \frac{5}{9}$ ， $\because 0 < e < 1$ ， $\therefore e = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$ ，
> > 故选： $D$ .
