> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷（文科）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left(-\frac{5}{2},\frac{3\sqrt{7}}{2}\right)$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{25} +\frac{y^2}{21} = 1$ ，则 $a = 5$ ， $b^{2} = 21$ ， $c = \sqrt{a^2 - b^2} = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore F _ {1} (- 2, 0), F _ {2} (2, 0), | P F _ {1} | = \left| F _ {1} F _ {2} \right| = 2 c = 4
> > $$
> >
> > 由题意设 $P(x,y)$ ， $x < 0$ ， $y > 0$ ， $\left|PF_1\right| = \left|F_1F_2\right|$
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l}(x + 2)^{2} + y^{2} = 16\\ \frac{x^{2}}{25} +\frac{y^{2}}{21} = 1 \end{array} \right.,$ 解得 $x = -\frac{5}{2},y = \frac{3\sqrt{7}}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore P (- \frac {5}{2}, \frac {3 \sqrt {7}}{2})
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\left(-\frac{5}{2},\frac{3\sqrt{7}}{2}\right)$ .
