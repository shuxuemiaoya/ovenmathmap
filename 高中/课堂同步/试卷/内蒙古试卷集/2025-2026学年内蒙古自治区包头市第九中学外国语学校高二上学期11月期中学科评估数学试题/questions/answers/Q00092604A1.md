>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > （1）由图可知 $\overrightarrow{DE} = \overrightarrow{DB} +\overrightarrow{BC} +\overrightarrow{CE}$ ，因为 $D$ 是棱 $AB$ 的中点，所以 $\overrightarrow{DB} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{PB} -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{PA} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{b} -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a},\overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{PC} -\overrightarrow{PB} = \overrightarrow{c} -\overrightarrow{b},$ 因为 $\overrightarrow{PE} = k\overrightarrow{PC}$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{CE} = (k - 1)\overrightarrow{PC} = (k - 1)\overrightarrow{c}.$ 故 $\overrightarrow{DE} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{b} -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{c} -\overrightarrow{b} +(k - 1)\overrightarrow{c} = -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a} -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{b} +k\overrightarrow{c}.$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {D E} = - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {a} - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {b} + k \overrightarrow {c}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {D E} \cdot \overrightarrow {P C} = (- \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {a} - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {b} + k \overrightarrow {c}) \cdot \overrightarrow {c} = - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {c} - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {b} \cdot \overrightarrow {c} + k \overrightarrow {c} ^ {2} = 0 (\ast)
> > $$
> >
> > 因为正三棱锥 $P - ABC, AB = 2\sqrt{3}, PA = 3$ ，所以 $\left|\overrightarrow{a}\right| = \left|\overrightarrow{b}\right| = \left|\overrightarrow{c}\right| = 3$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \angle A P B = \cos \angle B P C = \cos \angle C P A = \frac {3 ^ {2} + 3 ^ {2} - (2 \sqrt {3}) ^ {2}}{2 \times 3 \times 3} = \frac {1}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {c} = \overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {b} = \overrightarrow {b} \cdot \overrightarrow {c} = 3 \times 3 \times \frac {1}{3} = 3
> > $$
> >
> > 则由（ $\star$ ）可得： $-\frac{1}{2} \times 3 - \frac{1}{2} \times 3 + 9k = 0$ ，解得 $k = \frac{1}{3}$ .
> >
> > （3）由（2）可知 $\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} = \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} = 3$ ，又 $\overrightarrow{DE} = -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a} - \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{b} + k\overrightarrow{c}$ 则 $\left|\overrightarrow{DE}\right|^2 = \frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{a}^2 + \frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{b}^2 + k^2\overrightarrow{c}^2 + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - k\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} - k\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{c}$ $= \frac{9}{4} + \frac{9}{4} + 9k^2 + \frac{3}{2} - 3k - 3k = 9k^2 - 6k + 6 = (3k - 1)^2 + 5 \geqslant 5$ 故 $\left|\overrightarrow{DE}\right| \geqslant \sqrt{5}$ ，当 $k = \frac{1}{3}$ 时， $\left|\overrightarrow{DE}\right|$ 取得最小值 $\sqrt{5}$ .
