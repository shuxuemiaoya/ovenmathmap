> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古包头高一下学期期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{7}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 首先将 $\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{5}$ 两边平方即可求出 $2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha$ ，再求出 $\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha$ ，最后利用二倍角公式及两角差的正弦公式计算可得.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{5}$ ，且 $\alpha \in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right),$
> >
> > 以 $(\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)^2 = \frac{1}{25}$ , $\sin \alpha > 0$ , $\cos \alpha > 0$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $\sin^2\alpha - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha + \cos^2\alpha = \frac{1}{25}$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=\frac{24}{25}$ ,
> >
> > 所以
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha = \sqrt {(\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {\sin^ {2} \alpha + 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {7}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{\cos 2\alpha}{\sqrt{2}\sin\left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4}\right)} = \frac{\cos^2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha}{\sqrt{2}\sin\alpha\cos\frac{\pi}{4} - \sqrt{2}\cos\alpha\sin\frac{\pi}{4}}$ $= \frac{(\cos\alpha + \sin\alpha)(\cos\alpha - \sin\alpha)}{\sin\alpha - \cos\alpha} = -(\cos\alpha + \sin\alpha) = -\frac{7}{5}.$ 故答案为： $-\frac{7}{5}$
