> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意得
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = \frac {1}{c s c x} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{s e c x} = \sin x + \sqrt {3} \cos x = 2 (\sin x \cos \frac {\pi}{3} + \cos x \sin \frac {\pi}{3}) = 2 \sin (x + \frac {\pi}{3}),
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $\frac{\pi}{4} \leqslant x \leqslant \frac{\pi}{3}$ , $\frac{7\pi}{12} \leqslant x + \frac{\pi}{3} \leqslant \frac{2\pi}{3}$ , 所以当 $x = \frac{\pi}{4}$ 时, $f(x)$ 有最大值,
> >
> > 最大值为 $2\sin \frac{7\pi}{12} = 2\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{4} +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = 2(\sin \frac{\pi}{4}\cos \frac{\pi}{3} +\cos \frac{\pi}{4}\sin \frac{\pi}{3}) = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{2}.$
> >
> > 故选：D.
