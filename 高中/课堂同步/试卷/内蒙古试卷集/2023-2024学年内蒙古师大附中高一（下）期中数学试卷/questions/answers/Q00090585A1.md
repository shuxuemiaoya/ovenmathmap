> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 3
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 已知 $\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\alpha\right)=\frac{1}{3}=\frac{1-tan\alpha}{1+tan\alpha},\therefore\tan\alpha=\frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\cos 2 \alpha}{1 - \sin 2 \alpha} = \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha + \sin^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha}{1 - 2 \tan \alpha + \tan^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \frac {1}{4}}{1 - 1 + \frac {1}{4}} = 3,
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为：3.
