> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a > 0$ ，故 $\sin \alpha = \frac{-4a}{\sqrt{(3a)^2 + (-4a)^2}} = -\frac{4}{5}$ ，cos $\alpha = \frac{3a}{\sqrt{(3a)^2 + (-4a)^2}} = \frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \alpha + 2 \cos \alpha = - \frac {4}{5} + 2 \times \frac {3}{5} = \frac {2}{5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A.
