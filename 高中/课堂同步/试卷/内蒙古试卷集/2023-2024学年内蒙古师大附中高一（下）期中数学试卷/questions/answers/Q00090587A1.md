> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 因为 $\tan\alpha = 2$ ,
> > 所以 $\frac{cos(\frac{\pi}{2}+\alpha)}{sin(\pi-\alpha)-cos\alpha}=\frac{-sin\alpha}{sin\alpha-cos\alpha}=\frac{-tan\alpha}{tan\alpha-1}=-2.$
> >
> > (2)因为 $\beta$ 为钝角，
> >
> > 由 $cos2\beta = 1 - 2sin^2\beta = \frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 得 $\sin\beta=\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \beta = - \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} \beta} = - \sqrt {1 - (\frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{1 0}) ^ {2}} = - \frac {3 \sqrt {1 0}}{1 0},
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $tan\beta=\frac{sin\beta}{cos\beta}=-\frac{1}{3}$ ,
> >
> > 又因为 $tan2\alpha=\frac{2tan\alpha}{1-tan^{2}\alpha}=-\frac{4}{3}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \tan (2 \alpha - \beta) = \frac {\tan 2 \alpha - \tan \beta}{1 + \tan 2 \alpha \bullet \tan \beta} = \frac {- \frac {4}{3} - (- \frac {1}{3})}{1 + (- \frac {4}{3}) \times (- \frac {1}{3})} = - \frac {9}{1 3}.
> > $$
