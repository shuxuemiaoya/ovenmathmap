> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古兴安盟科尔沁右翼前旗二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)已知两个复向量 $\overrightarrow{\alpha} = (z_1, z_2)$ ， $\overrightarrow{\beta} = (z_3, z_4)$ 的积记作 $\overrightarrow{\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow{\beta}$ ，定义为 $\overrightarrow{\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow{\beta} = z_1 \overline{z_3} + z_2 \overline{z_4}$ ；又 $\overrightarrow{\alpha} = (2, 3)$ ， $\overrightarrow{\beta} = (1 - 2i, 3i)$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow {\beta} = 2 (1 + 2 i) + 3 \cdot (- 3 i) = 2 - 5 i.
> > $$
> >
> > (2)已知 $\overrightarrow{\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow{\beta} = z_1 \overline{z_3} + z_2 \overline{z_4}$ ; 复向量 $\overrightarrow{\alpha}$ 的模定义为 $|\overrightarrow{\alpha}| = \sqrt{\overrightarrow{\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow{\alpha}}$ ,
> >
> > 又 $\vec{\alpha} = (2 + i, i)$
> >
> > 则 $|\vec{\alpha} | = \sqrt{\vec{\alpha} \cdot \vec{\alpha}} = \sqrt{(2 + i)(2 - i) + i \cdot (-i)} = \sqrt{6}.$
> >
> > (3) $\overrightarrow{\alpha} = (1 + i, 2 - i)$ 与 $\overrightarrow{\beta} = (i, m)$ ，则
> >
> > $$
> > | \overrightarrow {\alpha} | = \sqrt {\overrightarrow {\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow {\alpha}} = \sqrt {(1 + i) (1 - i) + (2 - i) (2 + i)} = \sqrt {7}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > | \overrightarrow {\beta} | = \sqrt {i \cdot (- i) + m ^ {2}} = \sqrt {m ^ {2} + 1},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow {\beta} = (1 + i) \cdot (- i) + (2 - i) \cdot m = (2 m + 1) - (m + 1) i,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > | \overrightarrow {\alpha} \cdot \overrightarrow {\beta} | = \sqrt {(2 m + 1) ^ {2} + (m + 1) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {5 m ^ {2} + 6 m + 2},
> > $$
> >
> > 若 $\overrightarrow{\alpha} = (1 + i,2 - i)$ 与 $\overrightarrow{\beta} = (i,m)$ 能平行，则 $|\overrightarrow{\alpha}\cdot \overrightarrow{\beta}| = |\overrightarrow{\alpha} ||\overrightarrow{\beta} |,$
> >
> > 即 $\sqrt{5m^2 + 6m + 2} = \sqrt{7}\cdot \sqrt{m^2 + 1}$ ，整理得 $2m^{2} - 6m + 5 = 0$
> >
> > $\Delta = (-6)^{2} - 4 \times 2 \times 5 = -4 < 0$ ，即方程 $2m^{2} - 6m + 5 = 0$ 无实数解，
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{\alpha} = (1 + i, 2 - i)$ 与 $\overrightarrow{\beta} = (i, m)$ 不平行.
