> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos a = \frac {x}{r} = - \frac {1}{3}, \sin a = \frac {y}{r} = - \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}, \tan a = \frac {y}{x} = 2 \sqrt {2}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
