> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{2}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {c} = \overrightarrow {a} \bullet (2 \overrightarrow {a} - \sqrt {5} \overrightarrow {b}) = 2 \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} - \sqrt {5} \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} = 2
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {c} ^ {2} = (2 \overrightarrow {a} - \sqrt {5} \overrightarrow {b}) ^ {2} = 4 \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} - 4 \sqrt {5} \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} + 5 \overrightarrow {b} ^ {2} = 9,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | \overrightarrow {c} | = 3
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos <   \overrightarrow {a}, \overrightarrow {c} > = \frac {\overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {c}}{| \overrightarrow {a} | | \overrightarrow {c} |} = \frac {2}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{2}{3}$
