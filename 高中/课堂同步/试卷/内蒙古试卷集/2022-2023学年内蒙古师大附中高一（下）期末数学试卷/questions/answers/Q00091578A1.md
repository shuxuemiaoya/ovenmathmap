> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{7}{9}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $sin\left(\frac{\pi}{12} -\frac{\alpha}{2}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，可得 $\cos (\alpha -\frac{\pi}{6}) = 1 - 2\sin^2 (\frac{\pi}{12} -\frac{\alpha}{2}) = \frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - 2\alpha\right) = 2\cos^2 \left(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) - 1 = -\frac{7}{9}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \cos \left[ \frac {\pi}{2} - \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) \right] = \cos \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = - \frac {7}{9}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $-\frac{7}{9}$ .
