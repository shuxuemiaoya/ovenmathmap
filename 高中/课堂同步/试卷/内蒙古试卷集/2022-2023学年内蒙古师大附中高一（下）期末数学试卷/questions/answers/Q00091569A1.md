> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\because \tan \theta = 2, \text {则} \sin^ {2} \theta + \sin \theta \cos \theta - 2 \cos^ {2} \theta} \\ & {= \frac {\sin^ {2} \theta + \sin \theta \cos \theta - 2 \cos^ {2} \theta}{\sin^ {2} \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta} = \frac {\tan^ {2} \theta + \tan \theta - 2}{\tan^ {2} \theta + 1} = \frac {4 + 2 - 2}{4 + 1} = \frac {4}{5},} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A.
