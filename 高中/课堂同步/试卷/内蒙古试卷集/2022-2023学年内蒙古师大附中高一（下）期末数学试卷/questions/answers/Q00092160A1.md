> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {(1) f (x) = 2 \sqrt {3} \sin x \cos x + 2 \cos^ {2} x - 1} \\ & {= \sqrt {3} s i n 2 x + \cos 2 x} \\ & {= 2 \sin (2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}) ,} \\ & {\text {当} 2 x + \frac {\pi}{6} = \frac {\pi}{2} + 2 k \pi , k \in Z ,} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 即 $x=\frac{\pi}{6}+k\pi$ ， $k\in Z$ 时， $f(x)$ 的最大值为2；
> >
> > (2)由（1）及题意得 $f(\frac{A}{2})=2\sin(A+\frac{\pi}{6})=2$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin (A + \frac {\pi}{6}) = 1, \therefore A + \frac {\pi}{6} = \frac {\pi}{2}, A = \frac {\pi}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > 由余弦定理得 $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc\cos A$ ，所以 $b^2 + c^2 - bc = 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \sqrt {\frac {1 6 S}{\sqrt {3}}} = \sqrt {\frac {1 6 \bullet \frac {1}{2} \bullet b c s i n A}{\sqrt {3}}} = 2 \sqrt {b c},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b ^ {3} + c ^ {3} - \sqrt {\frac {1 6 S}{\sqrt {3}}} = (b + c) (b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - b c) - 2 \sqrt {b c}
> > $$
> >
> > $= b + c - 2\sqrt{bc} = (\sqrt{b} - \sqrt{c})^2 \geqslant 0$ ，当且仅当 $b = c$ 时等号成立，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b ^ {3} + c ^ {3} \geqslant \sqrt {\frac {1 6 S}{\sqrt {3}}}.
> > $$
