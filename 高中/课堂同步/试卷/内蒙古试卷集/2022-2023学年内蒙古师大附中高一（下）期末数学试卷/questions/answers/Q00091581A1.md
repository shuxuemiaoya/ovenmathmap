> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > $$
> > (I) \because | \overrightarrow {a} | = 2, | \overrightarrow {b} | = 1, <   \overrightarrow {a}, \overrightarrow {b} > = 1 2 0 ^ {\circ},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | \overrightarrow {a} - 2 \overrightarrow {b} | = \sqrt {(\overrightarrow {a} - 2 \overrightarrow {b}) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {\overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} - 4 \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} + 4 \overrightarrow {b} ^ {2}} = \sqrt {4 + 4 + 4} = 2 \sqrt {3};
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > (\Pi) \because (\overrightarrow {a} + t \overrightarrow {b}) \perp (2 \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b}),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\therefore (\overrightarrow {a} + t \overrightarrow {b}) \bullet (2 \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b}) = 2 \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} + (2 t - 1) \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} - t \overrightarrow {b} ^ {2} = 8 - (2 t - 1) - t = 0, \text {解得}} \\ & {t = 3.} \end{array}
> > $$
