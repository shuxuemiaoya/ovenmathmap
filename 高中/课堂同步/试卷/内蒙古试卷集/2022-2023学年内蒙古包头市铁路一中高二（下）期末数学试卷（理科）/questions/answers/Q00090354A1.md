> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古包头市铁路一中高二（下）期末数学试卷（理科）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $\because \overline{x} = \frac{2 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 8}{5} = 5, \overline{y} = \frac{30 + 40 + 60 + 50 + 70}{5} = 50,$ $\therefore \hat{b} = \frac{2 \times 30 + 4 \times 40 + 5 \times 60 + 6 \times 50 + 8 \times 70 - 5 \times 5 \times 50}{2^2 + 4^2 + 5^2 + 6^2 + 8^2 - 5 \times 5^2} = 6.5,$ $\therefore \hat{a} = 50 - 6.5 \times 5 = 17.5,$ $\therefore$ 线性回归方程是 $\hat{y} = 6.5x + 17.5;$ (2)由题意可得 $\mu = 5, \sigma = 1.3,$ $\because P(5 - 2.6 < X < 5 + 2.6) = P(\mu - 2\sigma < X < \mu + 2\sigma) = 0.9545,$ $\therefore P(X \leqslant 2.4) = P(X \geqslant 7.6) = \frac{1 - 0.9545}{2} = 0.02275,$ $P(X > 2.4) = 1 - P(X \leqslant 2.4) = 1 - 0.02275 = 0.97725,$ $\because P(5 - 1.3 < X < 5 + 1.3) = 0.6827,$ $\therefore P(X > 6.3) = P(X < 3.7) = \frac{1 - 0.6827}{2} = 0.15865,$ $\therefore P(2.4 < X \leqslant 6.3) = 0.97725 - 0.15865 = 0.8186,$
> >
> > 设每位员工单日获得奖金为 $Y$ （元），则 $Y$ 的分布列为：
> >
> > <table><tr><td>Y</td><td>0</td><td>200</td><td>500</td></tr><tr><td>P</td><td>0.02275</td><td>0.8186</td><td>0.15865</td></tr></table>
> >
> > ∴每位员工单日获得奖金的数学期望为：
> >
> > $$
> > E (Y) = 0 \times 0. 0 2 2 7 5 + 2 0 0 \times 0. 8 1 8 6 + 5 0 0 \times 0. 1 5 8 6 5 = 2 4 3. 0 4 5 \approx 2 4 3 (\text {元}) 。
> > $$
