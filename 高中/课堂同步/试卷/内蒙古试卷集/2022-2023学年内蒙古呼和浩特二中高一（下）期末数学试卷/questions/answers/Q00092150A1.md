> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古呼和浩特二中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{7}{25}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵α为锐角，
> > ∴ $\frac{\pi}{8}<\alpha+\frac{\pi}{8}<\frac{5\pi}{8}$ 又∵ $cos(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{8})>0$ ∴ $\frac{\pi}{8}<\alpha+\frac{\pi}{8}<\frac{\pi}{2}$ ∴ $sin(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{8})=\sqrt{1-cos^{2}(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{8})}=\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$ ∴ $cos(2\alpha-\frac{\pi}{4})=cos[(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4})-\frac{\pi}{2}]=cos[\frac{\pi}{2}-(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4})]=sin(2\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4})=2sin(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{8})cos(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{8})=2\times\frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}\times\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}=\frac{7}{25}.$ 故答案为： $\frac{7}{25}.$
