> >
> > $$
> > C D = C M \cdot \sin 6 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {A B \bullet s i n 4 5 ^ {\circ} \bullet s i n 6 0 ^ {\circ}}{s i n 1 5 ^ {\circ} \bullet s i n 3 0 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {(1 5 \sqrt {3} - 1) \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}}{\frac {\sqrt {6} - \sqrt {2}}{4} \times \frac {1}{2}} = 3 0 \sqrt {3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D.
