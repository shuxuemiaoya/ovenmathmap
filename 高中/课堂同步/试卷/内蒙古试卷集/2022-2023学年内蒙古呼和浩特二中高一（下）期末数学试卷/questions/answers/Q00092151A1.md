> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古呼和浩特二中高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $\because (\vec{a} - \vec{b}) \cdot (\vec{a} + \vec{b}) = \frac{1}{2}, \therefore \vec{a}^2 - \vec{b}^2 = \frac{1}{2}$ .
> > 又 $\because |\vec{a}| = 1, \therefore 1^2 - |\vec{b}|^2 = \frac{1}{2}$ , 解得 $|\vec{b}| = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ . $\because \vec{a} \bullet \vec{b} = \frac{1}{2}$ , $\therefore \cos < \vec{a}, \vec{b} > = \frac{\vec{a} \bullet \vec{b}}{|\vec{a}||\vec{b}|} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{1 \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ , $\therefore \vec{a}$ 与 $\vec{b}$ 的夹角为 $\frac{\pi}{4}$ ;
> > (2)由 (1) 可得 $|\vec{a} - \vec{b}| = \sqrt{\vec{a}^2 + \vec{b}^2 - 2\vec{a} \bullet \vec{b}} = \sqrt{1^2 + (\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2})^2 - 2 \times \frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ , $|\vec{a} + \vec{b}| = \sqrt{\vec{a}^2 + \vec{b}^2 + 2\vec{a} \bullet \vec{b}} = \sqrt{1^2 + (\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2})^2 + 2 \times \frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos <   \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b}, \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} > = \frac {(\overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b}) \bullet (\overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b})}{| \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b} | | \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} |} = \frac {\frac {1}{2}}{\frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{2}} = \frac {\sqrt {5}}{5}.
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{b}$ 的夹角的余弦值为 $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$
