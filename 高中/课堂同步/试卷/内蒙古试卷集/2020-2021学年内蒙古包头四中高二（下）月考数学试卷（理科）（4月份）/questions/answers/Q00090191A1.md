> [!faq]- 2020-2021学年内蒙古包头四中高二（下）月考数学试卷（理科）（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由双曲线 $\Gamma : \frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1 (a > 0, b > 0)$ ，可知其渐近线方程为 $y = \frac{b}{a} x$ 或 $y = -\frac{b}{a} x$
> >
> > 设切点为 $(x_0, y_0)$ ，由 $y = \ln x + \ln 2e$ ，得 $y' = \frac{1}{x}$
> >
> > $\because$ 双曲线 $\Gamma$ 的一条渐近线与函数 $y = \ln x + \ln 2e$ 的图象相切，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {1}{x _ {0}} = \frac {b}{a} \\ \frac {b}{a} x _ {0} = l n x _ {0} + l n 2 e \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {1}{x _ {0}} = - \frac {b}{a} \\ - \frac {b}{a} x _ {0} = l n x _ {0} + l n 2 e \end{array} \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $x_0 = \frac{1}{2}$ ， $y_0 = 1$ ， $\therefore y_0 = \frac{b}{a} x_0$ ， $\therefore \frac{b}{a} = 2$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore e = \frac {c}{a} = \frac {\sqrt {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2}}}{a} = \sqrt {1 + (\frac {b}{a}) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A.
