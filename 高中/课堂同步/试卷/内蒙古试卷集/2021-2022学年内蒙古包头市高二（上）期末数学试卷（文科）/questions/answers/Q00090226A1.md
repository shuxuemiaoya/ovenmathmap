> [!faq]- 2021-2022学年内蒙古包头市高二（上）期末数学试卷（文科）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $\because r = \sqrt{2}$ ,
> > ∴曲线 $C_{1}$ 的极坐标方程为 $\rho=\sqrt{2}$ $\therefore\left\{\begin{aligned}\rho^{2}&=4cos2\theta\\ \rho&=\sqrt{2}\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow\cos2\theta=\frac{1}{2}$ 即 $2cos^{2}\theta-1=\frac{1}{2}$ ，得 $cos^{2}\theta=\frac{3}{4}$ $\therefore \cos \theta = \pm \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
> >
> > 当 $\cos \theta = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 时， $\sin \theta = \pm \frac{1}{2}$ ，当 $\cos \theta = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 时， $\sin \theta = \pm \frac{1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 分别代入 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \sqrt{2} \cos \theta \\ y = \sqrt{2} \sin \theta \end{array} \right.$ ，可得四个交点坐标分别为：
> >
> > $$
> > \left(\frac {\sqrt {6}}{2}, \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\right), \left(\frac {\sqrt {6}}{2}, - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\right), \left(- \frac {\sqrt {6}}{2}, \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\right), \left(- \frac {\sqrt {6}}{2}, - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > (2)依题意，设 $A(x,y)$ ，则由对称性可知，矩形ABCD的面积 $S = 4xy$ ∴ $S = 4xy = 4\cdot \rho \cos \theta \cdot \rho \sin \theta = 2\rho^2\sin 2\theta$ ，代入 $\rho^2 = 4\cos 2\theta$ ∴ $S = 8\sin 2\theta \cos 2\theta = 4\sin 4\theta \leqslant 4$ （当且仅当 $\sin 4\theta = 1$ 时取等号），故矩形ABCD面积的最大值为4.
