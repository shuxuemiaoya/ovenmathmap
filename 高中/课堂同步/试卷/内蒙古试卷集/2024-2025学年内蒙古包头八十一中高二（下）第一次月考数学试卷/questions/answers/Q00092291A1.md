> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头八十一中高二（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{2e}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设曲线 $f(x) = x^2$ 与 $g(x) = \ln (ax)(a > 0)$ 的切点分别为 $(x_{1},x_{1}^{2})$ ， $(x_{2},\ln (ax_{2}))$ ， $\because f^{\prime}(x) = 2x,g^{\prime}(x) = \frac{1}{x},\therefore k_{1} = 2x_{1},k_{2} = \frac{1}{x_{2}},$ $\therefore y - x_1^2 = 2x_1(x - x_1),y - ln(ax_2) = \frac{1}{x_2} (x - x_2),$ $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{c}2x_{1} = \frac{1}{x_{2}}\\ x_{1}^{2} + ln(ax_{2}) - 1 = 0 \end{array} \right.$ ， $\frac{1}{4x_2^2} +ln(ax_2) - 1 = 0$ ，即 $1 - lna = \frac{1}{4x_2^2} +lnx_2$ ，令 $h(x) = \frac{1}{4x^2} +lnx$ ，则 $h^{\prime}(x) = \frac{2x^{2} - 1}{2x^{3}}$ 当 $0 <   x <   \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 时， $h^\prime (x) <   0,h(x)$ 单调递减；当 $x > \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 时， $h^\prime (x) > 0,h(x)$ 单调递增， $\therefore h(x)\geq h(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}) = \frac{1}{2} +ln\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，即 $1 - lna\geq \frac{1}{2} +ln\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，即 $lna\leq ln\sqrt{2e}$ ，即 $0 <   a\leq \sqrt{2e}.$ 故答案为： $\sqrt{2e}$
