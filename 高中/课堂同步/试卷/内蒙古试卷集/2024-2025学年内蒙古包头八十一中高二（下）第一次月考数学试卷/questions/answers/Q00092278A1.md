> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头八十一中高二（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$ ，得 $f^{\prime}(x) = \frac{1}{2} x^{-\frac{1}{2}}$
> >
> > $\therefore f^{\prime}(8) = \frac{1}{2}\times (8)^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{8},$
> >
> > 故选：C.
