> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头九十六中高二（上）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $A(3,0), B(0,4), C(4,7), D(11,6)$
> >
> > $$
> > k _ {A B} = \frac {0 - 4}{3 - 0} = - \frac {4}{3}, k _ {C D} = \frac {6 - 7}{1 1 - 4} = - \frac {1}{7}, k _ {B C} = \frac {7 - 4}{4 - 0} = \frac {3}{4}, k _ {A D} = \frac {6 - 0}{1 1 - 3} = \frac {3}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because k _ {B C} = k _ {A D}, k _ {A B} \neq k _ {C D}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ BC//AD，AB与CD不平行，
> >
> > 则四边形ABCD为梯形，
> >
> > 又 $k_{AD}\cdot k_{AB}=-1$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore A D \perp A B
> > $$
> >
> > ∴四边形ABCD为直角梯形.
> >
> > 故选：D.
