> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特一中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 将已知等式两边平方，得 $(\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha)^2 = 1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{3}{4}$
> >
> > 可得 $sin\alpha cos\alpha = \frac{1}{8}$
> >
> > 可得 $tan\alpha + \frac{1}{tan\alpha} = \frac{sin\alpha}{cos\alpha} + \frac{cos\alpha}{sin\alpha} = \frac{sin^2\alpha + cos^2\alpha}{sin\alpha cos\alpha} = \frac{1}{sin\alpha cos\alpha} = 8.$
> >
> > 故选：A.
