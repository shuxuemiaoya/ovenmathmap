> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特一中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 若 $tanB + tanC + \sqrt{3}tanBtanC = \sqrt{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan (B + C) = \frac {\tan B + \tan C}{1 - \tan B \tan C} = \frac {\sqrt {3} - \sqrt {3} \tan B \tan C}{1 - \tan B \tan C} = \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $B+C=\frac{\pi}{3}$ ，
> >
> > 则 $A = \frac{2\pi}{3}$
> >
> > 故选：C.
