> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特一中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sin 40^{\circ}\cos 70^{\circ} - \cos 40^{\circ}\sin 70^{\circ} = \sin (40^{\circ} - 70^{\circ}) = \sin (-30^{\circ}) = -\frac{1}{2}.$
> >
> > 故选：C.
