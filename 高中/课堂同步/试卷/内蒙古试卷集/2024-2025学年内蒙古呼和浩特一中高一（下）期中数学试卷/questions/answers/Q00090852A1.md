> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特一中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\frac{5sin\alpha - 3cos\alpha}{sin\alpha + cos\alpha} = tan\alpha$
> >
> > 得 $\frac{5\tan\alpha - 3}{\tan\alpha + 1} = \tan\alpha$ ，解得 $\tan\alpha = 1$ 或 3.
> >
> > $$
> > \because \alpha \in \left(\frac {\pi}{4}, \frac {\pi}{2}\right), \therefore \tan \alpha = 3
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \tan 2 \alpha = \frac {2 \tan \alpha}{1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {6}{1 - 9} = - \frac {3}{4}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D.
