> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年青海省海南州高级中学高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)向量 $\overrightarrow{a} = (1, 3)$ , $\overrightarrow{b} = (1, -2)$ .
> >
> > $$
> > | \cos \theta = \frac {\overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {b}}{| \overrightarrow {a} | \cdot | \overrightarrow {b} |} = \frac {(1 , 3) \cdot (1 , - 2)}{\sqrt {1 + 9} \times \sqrt {1 + 4}} = \frac {1 - 6}{\sqrt {1 0} \times \sqrt {5}} = - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 因为 $\theta \in [0,\pi]$ ，所以 $\theta = \frac{3\pi}{4}$
> >
> > $$
> > (2) \boldsymbol {k} \overrightarrow {\boldsymbol {a}} + \overrightarrow {\boldsymbol {b}} = k (1, 3) + (1, - 2) = (k + 1, 3 k - 2)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {a} + k \overrightarrow {b} = (1, 3) + k (1, - 2) = (1 + k, 3 - 2 k)
> > $$
> >
> > 由于 $k\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{b}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{a} +k\overrightarrow{b}$ 互相平行，故 $(k + 1)(3 - 2k) - (3k - 2)(1 + k) = 0$
> >
> > 解得 $k = 1$ 或-1
> >
> > 经检验，均满足要求.
