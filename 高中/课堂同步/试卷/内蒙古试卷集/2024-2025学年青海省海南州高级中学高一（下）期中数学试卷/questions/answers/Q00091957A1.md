>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\sin 105 = \sin (45^\circ + 60^\circ) = \sin 45^\circ \cos 60^\circ + \sin 60^\circ \cos 45^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ $= \frac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}{4}$ 故选： $D$ .
