> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年青海省海南州高级中学高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{5}$ ，得 $(\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha)^2 = \frac{1}{25}$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac {1}{2 5}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \sin 2\alpha = 1 - \frac{1}{25} = \frac{24}{25}.$
> >
> > 故选：C.
