> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析

> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $\overrightarrow{EF} = \overrightarrow{AF} - \overrightarrow{AE} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AD} - \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{b} - \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a}$ $\overrightarrow{EG}=\overrightarrow{EB}+\overrightarrow{BG}=\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{AF}=\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB}+\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AD}=\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a}+\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{b}$ (2) $EF \perp EG$ ，证明：由（1）得， $\overrightarrow{EF} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{b} - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{a}$ ， $\overrightarrow{EG} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{b} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{a}$ ，$\therefore \overrightarrow{EF} \bullet \overrightarrow{EG} = (\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{b} - \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a}) \cdot (\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{b} + \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{a}) = \frac{1}{9}\overrightarrow{b}^2 - \frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{a}^2 = \frac{1}{9} \times \frac{9}{4}\overrightarrow{a}^2 - \frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{a}^2 = 0,$ $\therefore \overrightarrow{EF} \perp \overrightarrow{EG},$ $\therefore EF \perp EG.$
