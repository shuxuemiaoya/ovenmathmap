> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰实验中学高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{19}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由向量 $\overrightarrow{a}$ ， $\overrightarrow{b}$ 的夹角为 $\frac{5\pi}{6}$ ， $|\overrightarrow{a}| = \sqrt{3}$ ， $|\overrightarrow{b}| = 1$
> >
> > 则 $\overrightarrow{a} \bullet \overrightarrow{b} = |\overrightarrow{a}||\overrightarrow{b}|cos\frac{5\pi}{6} = -\frac{3}{2}$
> >
> > 则
> >
> > $$
> > | \mathbf {3} \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} | = \sqrt {\left(\mathbf {3} \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b}\right) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {9 | \overrightarrow {a} | ^ {2} + 6 \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} + | \overrightarrow {b} | ^ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sqrt {9 \times 3 + 6 \times \sqrt {3} \times 1 \times (\frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}) + 1} = \sqrt {1 9}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\sqrt{19}$
