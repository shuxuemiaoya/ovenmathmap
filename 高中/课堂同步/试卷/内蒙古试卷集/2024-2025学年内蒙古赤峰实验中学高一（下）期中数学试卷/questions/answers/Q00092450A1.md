> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰实验中学高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \cos (\frac {\pi}{6} - \alpha) = \frac {3}{5}, \therefore \sin [ \frac {\pi}{2} - (\frac {\pi}{6} - \alpha) ] = \sin (\frac {\pi}{3} + \alpha) = \cos (\frac {\pi}{6} - \alpha) = \frac {3}{5},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin (\alpha - \frac {2 \pi}{3}) = \sin (\pi - \alpha + \frac {2 \pi}{3}) = - \sin (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}) = - \frac {3}{5}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：C.
