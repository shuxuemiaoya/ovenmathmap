>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 由已知得 $\overrightarrow{a} \bullet \overrightarrow{b} = |\overrightarrow{a}| \cdot |\overrightarrow{b}|\cos 120^{\circ} = 4 \times 2 \times \left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = -4$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore (2 \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b}) ^ {2} = 4 \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} - 4 \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} + \overrightarrow {b} ^ {2} = 6 4 + 1 6 + 4 = 8 4,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore |2\overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b}| = 2\sqrt{21}.$
> >
> > (2) $\because (\overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b})^2 = \overrightarrow{a}^2 + 2\overrightarrow{a} \bullet \overrightarrow{b} + \overrightarrow{b}^2 = 16 - 8 + 4 = 12,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} | = 2 \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {a} \bullet (\overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b}) = \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} + \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} = 1 6 - 4 = 1 2.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos <   \overrightarrow {a}, \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} > = \frac {\overrightarrow {a} \bullet (\overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b})}{| \overrightarrow {a} | \bullet | \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} |} = \frac {1 2}{4 \times 2 \sqrt {3}} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ $\overrightarrow{a}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b}$ 的夹角为 $\frac{\pi}{6}$ .
