> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\left(\frac{5}{2},\frac{19}{6}\right]$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $|\sqrt{3} sin(\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3})| = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，得 $|\sin (\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3})| = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 即 $\sin(\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3}) = \pm\frac{1}{2}$ ,
> >
> > 由 $sin(\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3}) = \pm \frac{1}{2}$ ，得 $\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3} = \pm \frac{\pi}{6} + 2k\pi, k \in Z$ 或 $\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3} = \pm \frac{5\pi}{6} + 2k\pi, k \in Z$
> >
> > 由题意知 $sin(\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3}) = \pm \frac{1}{2}$ 在 $(0, \pi)$ 上恰好存在6个实数根，
> >
> > 又因 $\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3} > -\frac{\pi}{3}$
> >
> > 故 $\omega x - \frac{\pi}{3}$ 的可能取值为 $-\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{5\pi}{6}, \frac{7\pi}{6}, \frac{11\pi}{6}, \frac{13\pi}{6}$ ,
> >
> > 故 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\omega\pi-\frac{\pi}{3}>\frac{13\pi}{6}\\ \omega\pi-\frac{\pi}{3}\leqslant\frac{17\pi}{6}\end{aligned}\right.$ ，即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\omega>\frac{5}{2}\\ \omega\leqslant\frac{19}{6}\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 得 $\frac{5}{2} < \omega \leqslant \frac{19}{6}$
> >
> > 所以 $\omega$ 的取值范围为 $(\frac{5}{2}, \frac{19}{6}]$ .
> >
> > 故答案为： $\left(\frac{5}{2},\frac{19}{6}\right]$ .
