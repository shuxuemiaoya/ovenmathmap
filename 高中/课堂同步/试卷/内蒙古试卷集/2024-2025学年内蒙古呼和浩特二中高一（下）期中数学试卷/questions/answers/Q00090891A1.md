> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $\alpha \in \left(-\frac{\pi}{2}, 0\right)$ ，且 $\sqrt{2}\cos2\alpha = \sin\left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ ,
> > ∴ $\sqrt{2}(\cos^{2}\alpha - \sin^{2}\alpha) = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} (\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha)$ , ∴ $\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha = 0$ , 或 $\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$ ,
> > ∴ $\tan \alpha = -1$ , 或 $\sin 2\alpha = \frac{3}{4}$ (舍去), ∴ $\alpha = -\frac{\pi}{4}$ ,
> > 则 $\sin2\alpha=\sin(-\frac{\pi}{2})=-1$ 故选：C.
