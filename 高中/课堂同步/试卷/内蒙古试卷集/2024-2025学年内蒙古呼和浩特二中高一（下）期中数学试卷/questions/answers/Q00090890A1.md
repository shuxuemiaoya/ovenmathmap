> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意知， $M\left(\frac{1}{2},1\right)$ ，设 $N(x,-1)$ ，且 $\overrightarrow{OM}\cdot\overrightarrow{ON}=0$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{x}{2} - 1 = 0$ ，解得 $x = 2$
> >
> > $\therefore$ 根据图象得， $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{\omega}{2} + \varphi = \frac{\pi}{2} \\ 2\omega + \varphi = \frac{3\pi}{2} \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $\omega = \frac{2\pi}{3}, \varphi = \frac{\pi}{6}$ .
> >
> > 故选：A.
