> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $A = 75^{\circ}$ ， $B = 45^{\circ}$ 得， $C = 180^{\circ} - A - B = 60^{\circ}$
> >
> > 由正弦定理得， $\frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}$
> >
> > 则 $c = \frac{bsinC}{sinB} = \frac{4\times sin60^{\circ}}{sin45^{\circ}} = 2\sqrt{6}$
> >
> > 故选：D.
