> >
> > 设直线的方程为 $x = ty + 8$ ， $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})$
> >
> > 联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{c}x = ty + 8\\ y^2 = 8x \end{array} \right.$ ，得 $y^{2} - 8xy - 64 = 0$
> >
> > 所以 $y_{1}y_{2}=-64$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\overline{OA} \cdot \overline{OB} = x_1x_2 + y_1y_2 = \frac{1}{64}(y_1y_2)^2 + y_1y_2 = \frac{1}{64} \cdot (-64)^2 - 64 = 0$ 所以 $OA \perp OB$ .
