> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头一中高二（上）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** (1,3,7)
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $P(x,y,z)$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {A P} = (x - 1, y - 3, z - 1), 2 \overrightarrow {A B} = (0, 0, 6),
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x-1&=0\\ y-3&=0\\ z-1&=6\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得x=1，y=3，z=7.
> >
> > 故答案为：(1,3,7).
