> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古乌兰察布市集宁二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {a} = (2, 4), \overrightarrow {b} = (- 1, 2), \overrightarrow {c} = \overrightarrow {a} - 6 \overrightarrow {b},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {c} = (2, 4) - 6 (- 1, 2) = (8, - 8),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | c | = \sqrt {8 ^ {2} + (- 8) ^ {2}} = 8 \sqrt {2}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D.
