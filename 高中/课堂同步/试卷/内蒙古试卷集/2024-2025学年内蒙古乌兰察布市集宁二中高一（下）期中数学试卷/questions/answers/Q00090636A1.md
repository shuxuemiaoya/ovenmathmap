> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古乌兰察布市集宁二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\triangle ABC$ 中， $sinB - sinA = \frac{1}{3} sinC$ ， $4\sin A - 3\sin B = 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b - a = \frac {1}{3} c, 4 a - 3 b = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a = \frac {3 b}{4}, c = a = \frac {3 b}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos A = \frac {b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}{2 b c} = \frac {b ^ {2} + \frac {9 b ^ {2}}{1 6} - \frac {9 b ^ {2}}{1 6}}{2 b \times \frac {3 b}{4}} = \frac {2}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
