> >
> > （I）由 $cosB = \frac{b + 2c}{2a}$ . 得 $cosB = \frac{b + 2c}{2a} = \frac{a^2 + c^2 - b^2}{2ac}$ .
> >
> > 即 $bc + 2c^2 = a^2 + c^2 - b^2$
> >
> > 得 $b^{2}+c^{2}-a^{2}=-bc$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos A = \frac {b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - a ^ {2}}{2 b c} = \frac {- b c}{2 b c} = - \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $A=120^{\circ}$
> >
> > (Ⅱ)(i)设b=2,c=3.则
> >
> > $$
> > a ^ {2} = b ^ {2} + c ^ {2} - 2 b c \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} = 4 + 9 - 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times \left(- \frac {1}{2}\right) = 1 3 + 6 = 1 9,
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $a=\sqrt{19}.$
> >
> > (ii) $\because \cos B = \frac{b + 2c}{2a} = \frac{2 + 6}{2\sqrt{19}} = \frac{4}{\sqrt{19}}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos 2 B = 2 \cos^ {2} B - 1 = 2 \times \frac {1 6}{1 9} - 1 = \frac {1 3}{1 9}.
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $\sin 2B = \frac{8\sqrt{3}}{19}$
> >
> > 则
> >
> > $$
> > \cos (2 B - A) = \cos (2 B - 1 2 0 ^ {\circ}) = \cos 2 B \cos 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin 2 B \sin 1 2 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {1 3}{1 9} \times (- \frac {1}{2}) + \frac {8 \sqrt {3}}{1 9}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
