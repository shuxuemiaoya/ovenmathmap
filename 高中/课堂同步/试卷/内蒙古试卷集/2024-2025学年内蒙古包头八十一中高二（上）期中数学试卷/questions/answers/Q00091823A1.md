> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头八十一中高二（上）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据题意可得 $c = 1$ ，设 $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})$
> >
> > 则 $\frac{x_1^2}{a^2} +\frac{y_1^2}{b^2} = 1$ ， $\frac{x_2^2}{a^2} +\frac{y_2^2}{b^2} = 1$ ，两式相减可得：
> >
> > $\frac{1}{a^2} + \frac{1}{b^2} \bullet \frac{y_1 - y_2}{x_1 - x_2} \bullet \frac{y_1 + y_2}{x_1 + x_2} = 0$ ，又 $\frac{y_1 - y_2}{x_1 - x_2} = k_{FP}$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{1}{a^2} + \frac{1}{b^2} \bullet \frac{-b - 0}{0 - (-1)} \bullet \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{1} = 0, \therefore a^2 = 2b,$ 又 $a^2 = b^2 + c^2 = b^2 + 1$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b ^ {2} + 1 = 2 b, \therefore b = 1, a ^ {2} = 2
> > $$
> >
> > ∴该椭圆的方程为 $\frac{x^{2}}{2}+y^{2}=1.$
> >
> > 故选：D.
