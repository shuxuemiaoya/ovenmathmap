> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头八十一中高二（上）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AD
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 对于 $A, \because \overrightarrow{a} = (2, 0, -1), \overrightarrow{b} = (-4, 0, 2)$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {b} = 2 \overrightarrow {a}
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $l_{1}$ ， $l_{2}$ 是两条不重合直线，∴ $l_{1} \parallel l_{2}$ ，即A正确；
> >
> > 对于 $B$ ， $\because \overrightarrow{c} \bullet \overrightarrow{m} = 6 - 4 - 2 = 0$ ， $\therefore \overrightarrow{c} \perp \overrightarrow{m}$
> >
> > 即 $l \parallel \alpha$ 或 $l \subset \alpha$ ，故B错误；
> >
> > 对于C，设直线l与平面 $\alpha$ 所成角为 $\theta$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > | \sin \theta = | \cos \langle \overrightarrow {d}, \overrightarrow {n} \rangle | = \frac {| \overrightarrow {d} \bullet \overrightarrow {n} |}{| \overrightarrow {d} | \bullet | \overrightarrow {n} |} = \frac {1}{\sqrt {2} \times \sqrt {2}} = \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \because \theta \in [ 0, \frac {\pi}{2} ], \therefore \theta = \frac {\pi}{6}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴直线l与平面 $\alpha$ 所成角的大小为 $\frac{\pi}{6}$ ，故C错误；
> >
> > 对于 $D, \because \overrightarrow{u} = (2, 2, -1), \overrightarrow{v} = (-3, 4, 2)$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {u} \bullet \overrightarrow {v} = - 6 + 8 - 2 = 0
> > $$
> >
> > 则 $\overrightarrow{\pmb{u}}\perp \overrightarrow{\pmb{v}}$ ，故 $\alpha \bot \beta$ ，故 $D$ 正确.
> >
> > 故选：AD.
