> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古名校联盟高一（下）质检数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $x = \log_424$ ， $y = 3\log_43 = \log_43^3 = \log_4^{27}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore y > x, \text {令} f (z) = 4 ^ {z} + 8 ^ {z}
> > $$
> >
> > $f(z)$ 为增函数，
> >
> > $$
> > f (y) = f (\log 4 2 7) = 4 ^ {\log_ {4} 2 7} + 8 ^ {\log_ {4} 2 7} = 2 7 + 2 7 \times \left(2 ^ {\log_ {2} 2 7}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} = 2 7 + 2 7 \times 3 \sqrt {3} > 1 5 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > f (x) = f \left(\log_ {4} 2 4\right) = 2 4 + 8 ^ {\log_ {4} 2 4} = 2 4 + 2 4 \times 2 \sqrt {6} <   1 5 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore y > z > x.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
