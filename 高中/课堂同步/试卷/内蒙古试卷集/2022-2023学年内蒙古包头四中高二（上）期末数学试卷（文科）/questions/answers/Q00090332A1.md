> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古包头四中高二（上）期末数学试卷（文科）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)由题意知， $a = 2$ ， $b = 1$ ， $c = \sqrt{3}$
> >
> > 所以 $F_{1}(-\sqrt{3},0), F_{2}(\sqrt{3},0)$ ，设 $P(m,n)(m > 0,n > 0)$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {P F _ {1}} \cdot \overrightarrow {P F _ {2}} = (- \sqrt {3} - m, - n) \cdot (\sqrt {3} - m, - n) = m ^ {2} + n ^ {2} - 3 = - \frac {5}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\frac{m^2}{4} + n^2 = 1$ ，解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} m = 1 \\ n = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > 所以 $P(1, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})$ ;
> >
> > (2)显然 $x = 0$ 不满足题意，设直线 $l$ 的方程为 $y = kx + 2$ ，设 $A(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $B(x_{2},y_{2})$
> >
> > 则 $\Delta = (16k)^2 - 4(4k^2 + 1) \times 12 > 0$ ，解得 $k^2 > \frac{3}{4}$ ①，
> >
> > 由韦达定理可得， $x_{1} + x_{2} = -\frac{16k}{4k^{2} + 1}$ ， $x_{1}x_{2} = \frac{12}{4k^{2} + 1}$
> >
> > $$
> > \left| y _ {1} y _ {2} = \left(k x _ {1} + 2\right) \left(k x _ {2} + 2\right) = k ^ {2} x _ {1} x _ {2} + 2 k \left(x _ {1} + x _ {2}\right) + 4 \right.
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\angle AOB$ 为锐角，则 $\cos \angle AOB > 0$ ，即 $\overrightarrow{OA} \cdot \overrightarrow{OB} > 0$ ， $x_{1}x_{2} + y_{1}y_{2} > 0$
> >
> > $$
> > x _ {1} x _ {2} + y _ {1} y _ {2} = y _ {1} y _ {2} = (1 + k ^ {2}) x _ {1} x _ {2} + 2 k (x _ {1} + x _ {2}) + 4 = \frac {1 2 (k ^ {2} + 1)}{4 k ^ {2} + 1} - \frac {1 6 k \cdot 2 k}{4 k ^ {2} + 1} + 4
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {4 (4 - k ^ {2})}{4 k ^ {2} + 1} > 0
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $0 < k^{2} < 4$ ，②
> >
> > 由①②，解得-2<k<- $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 或 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ <k<2，
> >
> > 所以实数 $k$ 的取值范围为 $(-2, -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}) \cup (\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, 2)$ .
