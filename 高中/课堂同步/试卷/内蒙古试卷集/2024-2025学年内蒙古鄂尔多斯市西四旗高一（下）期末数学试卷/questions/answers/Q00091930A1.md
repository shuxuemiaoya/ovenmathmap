> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古鄂尔多斯市西四旗高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{13}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由三角函数的定义，可得 $\tan \theta = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\tan (\theta -\frac{\pi}{6}) = \frac{tan\theta - tan\frac{\pi}{6}}{1 + tan\theta tan\frac{\pi}{6}} = \frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}}{1 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\times\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{9},$
> >
> > 可得 $tan(2\theta-\frac{\pi}{3})=\frac{2tan(\theta-\frac{\pi}{6})}{1-tan^{2}(\theta-\frac{\pi}{6})}=\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{13}.$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{13}$ .
