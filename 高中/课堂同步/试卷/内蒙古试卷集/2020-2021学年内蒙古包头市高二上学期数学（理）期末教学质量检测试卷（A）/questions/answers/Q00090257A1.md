> [!faq]- 2020-2021学年内蒙古包头市高二上学期数学（理）期末教学质量检测试卷（A）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because a_{n+1} = \frac{2a_n}{2 + a_n}$ , 且 $a_1 = 2 \neq 0$ , $\therefore a_n \neq 0$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = \frac{2 + a_n}{2a_n}$ , 即 $\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} = \frac{1}{a_n} + \frac{1}{2}$ , 即 $\frac{1}{a_{n+1}} - \frac{1}{a_n} = \frac{1}{2}$ , $\therefore$ 数列 $\left\{\frac{1}{a_n}\right\}$ 是以 $\frac{1}{a_1} = \frac{1}{2}$ 为首项, 以 $\frac{1}{2}$ 为公差的等差数列, $\therefore \frac{1}{a_n} = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}(n-1) = \frac{n}{2}$ , $\therefore a_n = \frac{2}{n}$ .
> > 故选：B。
