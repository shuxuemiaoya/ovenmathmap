> [!faq]- 2020-2021学年内蒙古包头市高二上学期数学（理）期末教学质量检测试卷（A）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because 0 <   k <   9
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 2 5 <   2 5 + k <   3 4, 0 <   9 - k <   9
> > $$
> >
> > 在曲线 $\frac{x^2}{25} -\frac{y^2}{9} = 1$
> >
> > 不妨令 $a_1 = 5, b_1 = 3, \therefore c_1 = \sqrt{a_1^2 + b_1^2} = \sqrt{34}$
> >
> > 在曲线 $\frac{x^2}{25 + k} -\frac{y^2}{9 - k} = 1(0 <   k <   9)$ 中，
> >
> > 不妨令 $a_{2}=\sqrt{25+k}$ ， $b_{2}=\sqrt{9-k}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore c _ {2} = \sqrt {a _ {2} ^ {2} + b _ {2} ^ {2}} = \sqrt {3 4}
> > $$
> >
> > A. $\because a_{1} = 5, a_{2} = \sqrt{25 + k}$ ,
> >
> > $\therefore 2a_{1} \neq 2a_{2}$ ，故A不正确；
> >
> > B. $e_1 = \frac{c_1}{a_1} = \frac{\sqrt{34}}{5}$
> >
> > $$
> > e _ {2} = \frac {c _ {2}}{a _ {2}} = \frac {\sqrt {3 4}}{\sqrt {2 5 + k}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore e _ {1} \neq e _ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故B不正确；
> >
> > C. 曲线 $\frac{x^2}{25} - \frac{y^2}{9} = 1$ 的渐近线方程为 $y = \pm \frac{b_1}{a_1} x = \pm \frac{3}{5} x$ ，曲线 $\frac{x^2}{25 + k} - \frac{y^2}{9 - k} = 1 (0 < k < 9$
> >
> > ）的渐近线方程为 $y=\pm\frac{b_{2}}{a_{2}}x=\pm\frac{\sqrt{9-k}}{\sqrt{25+k}}x$ ，故C不正确；
> >
> > D. $\because c_{1}=c_{2}=\sqrt{34}$ , $\therefore 2c_{1}=2c_{2}$ 故选：D。
