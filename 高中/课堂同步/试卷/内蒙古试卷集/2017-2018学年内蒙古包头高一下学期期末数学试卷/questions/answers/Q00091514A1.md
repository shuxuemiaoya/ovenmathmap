> [!faq]- 2017-2018学年内蒙古包头高一下学期期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{5+12\sqrt{3}}{26}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\alpha$ 为锐角，且 $\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \frac{5}{13},$
> >
> > 则： $\frac{\pi}{3} <  \alpha +\frac{\pi}{3} <  \frac{5\pi}{6},$
> >
> > 所以： $\sin \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2\left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)} = \frac{12}{13},$
> >
> > 所以： $\cos \alpha = \cos \left[\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{3}\right) - \frac{\pi}{3}\right]$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} {= \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) \cos \frac {\pi}{3} + \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) \sin \frac {\pi}{3},} \\ {= \frac {5}{1 3} \cdot \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1 2}{1 3} \cdot \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}} \\ {= \frac {5 + 1 2 \sqrt {3}}{2 6}.} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{5+12\sqrt{3}}{26}$ .
