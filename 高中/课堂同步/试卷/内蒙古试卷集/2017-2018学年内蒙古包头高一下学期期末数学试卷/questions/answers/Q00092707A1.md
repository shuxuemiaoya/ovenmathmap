> [!faq]- 2017-2018学年内蒙古包头高一下学期期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析 （2）证明见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1） $\because a_{n + 1} = a_n + 2^n +1,$ $\therefore (a_{n + 1} - 2^{n + 1}) - (a_n - 2^n) = a_{n + 1} - a_n - 2^n = 1,$ 又 $\because a_1 = 2$ $\therefore a_1 - 2 = 0,$ $\therefore$ 数列 $\{a_n - 2^n\}$ 为首项为0，公差为1的等差数列.
> >
> > (2) 由 (1) 知: $a_{n} - 2^{n} = n - 1$ , $\therefore a_{n} + 1 - n = 2^{n}$ , $\therefore b_{n} = \log_{2}(a_{n} + 1 - n) = \log_{2}2^{n} = n$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{b_1b_3} + \frac{1}{b_2b_4} + \frac{1}{b_3b_5} + \dots + \frac{1}{b_n \cdot b_{n+2}}$ $= \frac{1}{2}\left[\left(1 - \frac{1}{3}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{4}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{5}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac{1}{n-1} - \frac{1}{n+1}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{n} - \frac{1}{n+2}\right)\right]$ $= \frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{n+1} - \frac{1}{n+2}\right)$ $= \frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{n+1} + \frac{1}{n+2}\right)$ . $\because n \in \mathbf{N}^*$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{n+1} + \frac{1}{n+2}\right) > 0$ , $\therefore \frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{1}{n+1} + \frac{1}{n+2}\right) < \frac{3}{4}$ , $\therefore \frac{1}{b_1b_3} + \frac{1}{b_2b_4} + \frac{1}{b_3b_5} + \dots + \frac{1}{b_n \cdot b_{n+2}} < \frac{3}{4}$ .
