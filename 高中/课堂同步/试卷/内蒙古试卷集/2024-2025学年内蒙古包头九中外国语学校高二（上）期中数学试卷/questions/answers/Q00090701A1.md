> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头九中外国语学校高二（上）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{11}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\overrightarrow{AB} \bullet \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{AB} \bullet \overrightarrow{AA'} = \overrightarrow{AA'} \bullet \overrightarrow{AD} = 2 \times 2 \times \cos 60^\circ = 2$ ，且 $\overrightarrow{AM} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BM} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \frac{1}{2} (\overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{AA'})$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > | \overrightarrow {A M} | ^ {2} = (\overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {1}{2} (\overrightarrow {A D} + \overrightarrow {A A ^ {\prime}})) ^ {2} = \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2} + \overrightarrow {A B} \bullet \overrightarrow {A D} + \overrightarrow {A B} \bullet \overrightarrow {A A ^ {\prime}} + \frac {1}{4} | \overrightarrow {A D} | ^ {2} + \frac {1}{4} | \overrightarrow {A A ^ {\prime}} | ^ {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > + \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {A D} \bullet \overrightarrow {A A ^ {\prime}} = 4 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 1 1
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $AM=\sqrt{11}$ .
> >
> > 故答案为： $\sqrt{11}$ .
