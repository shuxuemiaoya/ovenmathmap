>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设直线l的倾斜角为 $\theta,\theta\in[0,\pi)$ .
> >
> > $\therefore \tan \theta = \frac{2\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{3}}{-2 - 1} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \theta = \frac {5 \pi}{6}.
> > $$
