> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头九中外国语学校高二（上）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设直线l与平面 $\alpha$ 的夹角为 $\theta$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > | \sin \theta = | \cos <   \overrightarrow {s}, \overrightarrow {n} > | = \frac {| \overrightarrow {s} \bullet \overrightarrow {n} |}{| \overrightarrow {s} | \bullet | \overrightarrow {n} |} = \frac {| 1 \times 1 + 1 \times (- 3) + 2 \times 0 |}{\sqrt {6} \times \sqrt {1 0}} = \frac {\sqrt {1 5}}{1 5}
> > $$
> >
> > 而 $\theta \in [0, \frac{\pi}{2}]$
> >
> > 所以 $cos\theta=\sqrt{1-sin^{2}\theta}=\sqrt{1-(\frac{\sqrt{15}}{15})^{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{210}}{15}.$
> >
> > 故选：D.
