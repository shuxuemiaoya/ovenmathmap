> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头昆都仑区包头市第六中学高一下学期期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 73
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 由题意求出AOB中的角和边，然后根据正弦定理求出AO的值.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 题意可得在 $\triangle AOB$ 中 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \angle OAB = 30^{\circ}, \\ \angle ABO = 45^{\circ}, \\ AB = 100\text{米}, \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $\angle AOB = 105^{\circ}$ ,
> >
> > 利用正弦定理可得 $\frac{AB}{\sin\angle AOB} = \frac{AO}{\sin\angle ABO}$
> >
> > 化简得
> >
> > $$
> > A O = \frac {A B \cdot \sin \angle A B O}{\sin \angle A O B} = \frac {1 0 0 \times \sin 4 5 ^ {\circ}}{\sin 1 0 5 ^ {\circ}} = \frac {1 0 0 \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}{\sin (6 0 ^ {\circ} + 4 5 ^ {\circ})} = \frac {1 0 0 \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}}{\frac {1}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2}} = 1 0 0 (\sqrt {3} - 1) \approx 7 3
> > $$
> >
> > 在Rt $\triangle ABC$ 中,可得PO = AO $\approx$ 73米.
> >
> > 故答案为：73
