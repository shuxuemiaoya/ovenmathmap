> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头昆都仑区包头市第六中学高一下学期期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析 (2) $\frac{5\sqrt{7}}{14}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）根据向量的加法法则容易得出 $\overrightarrow{AC}$ ，根据三角形相似求出 $\frac{AG}{AC}$ 的比值即可得出 $\overrightarrow{AG}$ ;
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）在平行四边形ABCD中，点E是AB的中点，所以DC//AE，且 $\frac{AE}{CD}=\frac{1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\triangle AGE \sim \triangle CGD$ ，所以 $\frac{AG}{GC} = \frac{AE}{CD} = \frac{1}{2}$ ，即 $AG = \frac{1}{3} AC$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {A C} = \overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {A D} = \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b}, \overrightarrow {A G} = \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A C} = \frac {1}{3} (\overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b})
> > $$
> >
> > (2) 由 $\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 2 \times 4 \times \frac{1}{2} = 4, \left|\overrightarrow{a}\right| = 4, \left|\overrightarrow{b}\right| = 2,$
> >
> > 于是 $\left|\overrightarrow{AC}\right|^{2}=\left(\overrightarrow{a}+\overrightarrow{b}\right)^{2}=\overrightarrow{a}^{2}+\overrightarrow{b}^{2}+2\overrightarrow{a}\cdot\overrightarrow{b}=16+4+8=28,$ $\therefore\left|\overrightarrow{AC}\right|=2\sqrt{7},$
> >
> > $$
> > \text { 又 } \overrightarrow {A C} \cdot \overrightarrow {A B} = \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} + \overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {b} = 2 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \backslash ; \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow {A C}, \overrightarrow {A B} \right\rangle = \frac {\overrightarrow {A C} \cdot \overrightarrow {A B}}{\left| \overrightarrow {A C} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow {A B} \right|} = \frac {2 0}{2 \sqrt {7} \times 4} = \frac {5 \sqrt {7}}{1 4}.
> > $$
