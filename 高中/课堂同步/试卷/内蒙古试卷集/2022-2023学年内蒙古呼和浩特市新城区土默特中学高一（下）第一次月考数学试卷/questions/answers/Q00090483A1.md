>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $\because OP = \sqrt{5}$ , 由三角函数的定义得 $sin\alpha = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$ , $\tan \alpha = -2$ ;
> >
> > $$
> > \because f (\alpha) = \frac {\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + \alpha\right) \tan (\pi + \alpha) + 2 \cos (\pi - \alpha)}{\sin \alpha + \cos (- \alpha)} = \frac {\cos \alpha \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} - 2 \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha} = \frac {\sin \alpha - 2 \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore f (\alpha) = \frac {\tan \alpha - 2}{\tan \alpha + 1} = \frac {- 2 - 2}{- 1} = 4.
> > $$
