> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古呼和浩特市新城区土默特中学高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：如图所示
> >
> > 作 $OD = 3OC$ ，以 $OA$ ， $OD$ 为邻边作平行四边形 $OAED$
> >
> > 连接AD，OE，交于点M，OE交AC于点N.
> >
> > $$
> > \because \text {满足} \overrightarrow {O A} + 2 \overrightarrow {O B} + 3 \overrightarrow {O C} = \overrightarrow {0}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {O A} + 3 \overrightarrow {O C} = - 2 \overrightarrow {O B},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {O E} = - 2 \overrightarrow {O B},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {O C}{A E} = \frac {O N}{N E} = \frac {1}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {O N} = \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {O E} = - \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {O B},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore O N = \frac {1}{2} O B = \frac {1}{3} B N,
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ $\triangle ABC$ 的面积与 $\triangle AOC$ 的面积之比是 3:1.
> >
> > 故选：A.
