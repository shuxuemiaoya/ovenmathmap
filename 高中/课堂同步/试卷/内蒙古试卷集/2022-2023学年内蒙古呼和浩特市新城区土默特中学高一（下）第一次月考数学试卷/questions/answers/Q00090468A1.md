> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古呼和浩特市新城区土默特中学高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \frac {\pi}{2} <   \alpha <   \pi , 3 \sin 2 \alpha = 2 \cos \alpha
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \alpha = \frac {1}{3}, \cos \alpha = - \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos (\alpha - \pi) = - \cos \alpha = - (- \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3}) = \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：C.
