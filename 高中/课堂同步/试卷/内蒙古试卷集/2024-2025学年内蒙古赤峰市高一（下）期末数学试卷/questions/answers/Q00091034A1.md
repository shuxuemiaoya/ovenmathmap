> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰市高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{7}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 已知 $|\overrightarrow{a}| = 2, |\overrightarrow{b}| = 3, \overrightarrow{a}, \overrightarrow{b}$ 的夹角为 $120^{\circ}$ ， $\therefore \overrightarrow{a} \bullet \overrightarrow{b} = |\overrightarrow{a}| |\overrightarrow{b}| \cos 120^{\circ} = 2 \times 3 \times (-\frac{1}{2}) = -3.$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore | \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} | = \sqrt {\overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} + \overrightarrow {b} ^ {2} + 2 \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b}} = \sqrt {2 ^ {2} + 3 ^ {2} + 2 \times (- 3)} = \sqrt {7}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为 $\sqrt{7}$ .
