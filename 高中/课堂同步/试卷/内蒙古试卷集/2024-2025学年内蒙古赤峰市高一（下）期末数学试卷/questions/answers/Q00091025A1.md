> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰市高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据 $\tan \theta = \frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta} = \frac{4}{3}$ ，且 $\sin^2\theta +\cos^2\theta = 1$ ，解得 $\sin^2\theta = \frac{16}{25},\cos^2\theta = \frac{9}{25}$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos 2\theta = \cos^2\theta -\sin^2\theta = -\frac{7}{25}.$
> >
> > 故选：D.
