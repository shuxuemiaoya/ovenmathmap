> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古部分名校高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $sin\alpha cos\beta + cos\alpha sin\beta = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10}$ ，①
> >
> > $sin\alpha cos\beta - cos\alpha sin\beta = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$ , ②
> >
> > 则①+②得 $\sin\alpha\cos\beta=\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{5}$ ，
> >
> > ①-②得 $\cos\alpha\sin\beta=\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{10}$ ，
> >
> > 故 $sin2\alpha sin2\beta = 2sin\alpha cos\alpha \times 2sin\beta cos\beta = 4 \times \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{5} \times \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{10} = \frac{24}{25}$ .
> >
> > 故选：D.
