> [!faq]- 2018-2019学年内蒙古包头青山区包头市北重三中高一下学期期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $\angle BAD = \theta$ ，则 $0 < \theta < 60^{\circ}$ ， $\because BC = 3BD, AD = 2,$ $\therefore S_{\triangle ABD} = \frac{1}{3}S_{\triangle ABC},$ $\therefore \frac{1}{2}AB \cdot AD \cdot \sin \theta = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{1}{2}AB \cdot AC \cdot \sin 60^{\circ},$ $\therefore AC = 4\sqrt{3}\sin \theta,$ 同理可得 $AB = 2\sqrt{3}\sin(60^{\circ} - \theta),$ $\therefore S_{\triangle ABC} = \frac{1}{2}AB \cdot AC \cdot \sin 60^{\circ}$ $= \frac{1}{2} \times 2\sqrt{3}\sin(60^{\circ} - \theta) \times 4\sqrt{3}\sin \theta \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ $= 6\sqrt{3}\sin \theta \sin(60^{\circ} - \theta)$ $= 3\sqrt{3}\left(\sqrt{3}\sin \theta \cos \theta - \sin^{2}\theta\right)$ $= 3\sqrt{3}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin 2\theta + \frac{1}{2}\cos 2\theta - \frac{1}{2}\right)$ $= 3\sqrt{3}\sin(2\theta + 30^{\circ}) - \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2},$ $\because 0 < \theta < 60^{\circ},$ $\therefore 30^{\circ} < 2\theta + 30^{\circ} < 150^{\circ},$ $\therefore \frac{1}{2} < \sin(2\theta + 30^{\circ}) \leqslant 1,$ $\therefore 3\sqrt{3}\sin(2\theta + 30^{\circ}) - \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} \leqslant \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2},$ 当 $\theta = 30^{\circ}$ 时取等号，
> >
> > 故 $\triangle ABC$ 面积的最大值为 $\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}.$ 故答案为： $\frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}.$
