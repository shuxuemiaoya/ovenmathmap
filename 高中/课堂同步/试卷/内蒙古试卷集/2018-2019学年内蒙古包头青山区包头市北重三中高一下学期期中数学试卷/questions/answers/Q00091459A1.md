> [!faq]- 2018-2019学年内蒙古包头青山区包头市北重三中高一下学期期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵等差数列 $\left\{a_{n}\right\}$ 的前n项和为 $S_{n}$ ，且 $a_{1}+a_{4}=4,\quad a_{2}+a_{5}=8$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 a _ {1} + 3 d = 4 \\ 2 a _ {1} + 5 d = 8 \end{array} \right.,
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $a_{1}=-1,\ d=2,$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \frac {S _ {2 0 1 9}}{2 0 1 9} = \frac {2 0 1 9 \times (- 1) + \frac {2 0 1 9 \times 2 0 1 8}{2} \times 2}{2 0 1 9} = 2 0 1 7.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选B.
