C 若 $a \subset \alpha, b \subset \beta, a \parallel \beta, b \parallel \alpha$ , 则 $\alpha \parallel \beta$

D 若 $a \subset \alpha, b \subset \beta, a \parallel \beta, a \perp b$ ，则 $\alpha \perp \beta$
