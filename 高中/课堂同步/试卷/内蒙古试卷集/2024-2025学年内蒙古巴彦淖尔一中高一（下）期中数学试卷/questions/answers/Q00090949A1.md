> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古巴彦淖尔一中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $2\theta+\frac{\pi}{3}=2(\theta-\frac{\pi}{3})+\pi$ ， $cos(\theta-\frac{\pi}{3})=\frac{2}{3}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos (2 \theta + \frac {\pi}{3}) = \cos [ 2 (\theta - \frac {\pi}{3}) + \pi ] = - \cos 2 (\theta - \frac {\pi}{3}) = - [ 2 \cos^ {2} (\theta - \frac {\pi}{3}) - 1 ] = \frac {1}{9}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：A.
