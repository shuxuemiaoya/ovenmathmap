> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头市经纬中学高二（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because y = \ln (x + b)$ 的导函数为 $y^\prime = \frac{1}{x + b}$
> >
> > 设直线 $y=x+a$ 与曲线 $y=\ln(x+b)$ 相切于点 $P(m,n)$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l}n = m + a\\ n = ln(m + b),\text{解得} b = a + 1,\\ 1 = \frac{1}{m + b} \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore a ^ {2} + b ^ {2} = a ^ {2} + (a + 1) ^ {2} = 2 \left(a + \frac {1}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore$ 当 $a = -\frac{1}{2}$ , $b = \frac{1}{2}$ 时, $a^2 + b^2$ 取得最小值 $\frac{1}{2}$ .
> >
> > 故选：A.
