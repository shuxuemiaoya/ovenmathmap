> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头市经纬中学高二（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解；因为函数 $f(x)$ 在 $x = x_0$ 处的导数为3，
> >
> > 所以 $f'(x_0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x_0 + \Delta x) - f(x_0)}{\Delta x} = 3$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \lim _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {f \left(x _ {0} + \Delta x\right) - f \left(x _ {0}\right)}{2 \Delta x} = \frac {1}{2} \cdot \lim _ {\Delta x \rightarrow 0} \frac {f \left(x _ {0} + \Delta x\right) - f \left(x _ {0}\right)}{\Delta x} = \frac {1}{2} \times 3 = \frac {3}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
