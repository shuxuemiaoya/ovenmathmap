> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古赤峰市元宝山一中、新红旗中学联考高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {f (x) = c o s x c o s (x - \frac {\pi}{6}) + \sqrt {3} s i n ^ {2} x - \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{4} = c o s x (c o s x \cdot \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} + s i n x \cdot \frac {1}{2}) + \sqrt {3}} \\ & {\cdot \frac {1 - c o s 2 x}{2} - \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{4}} \\ & {= \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cdot \frac {1 + c o s 2 x}{2} + \frac {1}{4} s i n 2 x + \sqrt {3} \cdot \frac {1 - c o s 2 x}{2} - \frac {3 \sqrt {3}}{4} = \frac {1}{4} s i n 2 x - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{4} c o s 2 x} \\ & {= \frac {1}{2} s i n (2 x - \frac {\pi}{3})} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $f(x)$ 的最小正周期为 $\frac{2\pi}{2} = \pi$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > 2 k \pi - \frac {\pi}{2} \leqslant 2 x - \frac {\pi}{3} \leqslant 2 k \pi + \frac {\pi}{2} (k \in Z) \Rightarrow k \pi - \frac {\pi}{1 2} \leqslant x \leqslant k \pi + \frac {5 \pi}{1 2} (k \in Z)
> > $$
> >
> > 所以函数的单调递增区间为 $[k\pi -\frac{\pi}{12},k\pi +\frac{5\pi}{12} ](k\in Z);$
> >
> > (2)当 $x\in[\frac{\pi}{12},\frac{\pi}{2}]$ 时， $2x-\frac{\pi}{3}\in[-\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{2\pi}{3}]$ ，
> >
> > 所以当 $2x - \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{2}$ 时，即当 $x = \frac{5\pi}{12}$ 时，函数 $f(x)$ 有最大值 $\frac{1}{2}$ .
