> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古赤峰市元宝山一中、新红旗中学联考高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 已知 $\tan (\alpha - 2\beta) = -2$ ， $\tan (2\alpha - \beta) = 3$
> >
> > 则
> >
> > $$
> > \tan (\alpha + \beta) = \tan [ (2 \alpha - \beta) - (\alpha - 2 \beta) ] = \frac {\tan (2 \alpha - \beta) - \tan (\alpha - 2 \beta)}{1 - \tan (2 \alpha - \beta) \bullet \tan (\alpha - 2 \beta)} = \frac {3 - (- 2)}{1 + 3 \times (- 2)}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
