> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古赤峰市元宝山一中、新红旗中学联考高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} f (x) = \cos x (2 \sqrt {3} \sin x + \cos x) - \sin^ {2} x = 2 \sqrt {3} \sin x \cos x + \cos^ {2} x - \sin^ {2} x = \sqrt {3} \sin 2 x \\ + \cos 2 x = 2 \sin (2 x + \frac {\pi}{6}) \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore f(x)$ 的最小正周期 $T = \frac{2\pi}{2} = \pi .$
> >
> > (2) $\because 0 \leqslant x \leqslant m, \therefore \frac{\pi}{6} \leq 2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \leq 2m + \frac{\pi}{6}$ ,
> >
> > $\because f(x)$ 在区间 $[0, m]$ 上的值域为 $[1, 2]$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{\pi}{2} \leq 2m + \frac{\pi}{6} \leq \frac{5\pi}{6}$ , 得 $\frac{\pi}{6} \leq m \leq \frac{\pi}{3}$ ,
> >
> > 即 $m$ 的取值范围为 $[\frac{\pi}{6},\frac{\pi}{3} ]$
