> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古赤峰市元宝山一中、新红旗中学联考高一（下）月考数学试卷（4月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 因为 $\sin\alpha = \frac{3}{5}$ ，且 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \alpha = - \sqrt {1 - \sin^ {2} \alpha} = - \frac {4}{5}, \tan \alpha = \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = - \frac {3}{4}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > {\frac {s i n (2 \pi - \alpha) + c o s (3 \pi + \alpha)}{s i n ({\frac {\pi}{2}} - \alpha) - s i n (\pi - \alpha)}} = {\frac {- s i n \alpha - c o s \alpha}{c o s \alpha - s i n \alpha}} = {\frac {- {\frac {3}{5}} - (- {\frac {4}{5}})}{- {\frac {4}{5}} - {\frac {3}{5}}}} = - {\frac {1}{7}}.
> > $$
