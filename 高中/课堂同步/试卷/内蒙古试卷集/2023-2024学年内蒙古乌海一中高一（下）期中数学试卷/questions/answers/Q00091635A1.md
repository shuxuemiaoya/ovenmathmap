> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古乌海一中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because z = \frac {3 i - 1}{2 i + 1} = \frac {(3 i - 1) (1 - 2 i)}{(2 i + 1) (1 - 2 i)} = \frac {5 + 5 i}{5} = 1 + i,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore |\bar{z}| = |1 - i| = \sqrt{2}.$
> >
> > 故选：B.
