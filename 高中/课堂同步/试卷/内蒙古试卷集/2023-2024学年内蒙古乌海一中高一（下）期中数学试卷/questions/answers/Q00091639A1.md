> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古乌海一中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $a = 2, b = \sqrt{6}, B = 2A$
> >
> > 由正弦定理得， $\frac{2}{\sin A} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{\sin B} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{\sin 2A} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2\sin A\cos A}$
> >
> > 因为 $\sin A > 0$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos A = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{4}$
> >
> > 则 $\sin A = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 A} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{3}{8}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{4}$ .
> >
> > 故选：B.
