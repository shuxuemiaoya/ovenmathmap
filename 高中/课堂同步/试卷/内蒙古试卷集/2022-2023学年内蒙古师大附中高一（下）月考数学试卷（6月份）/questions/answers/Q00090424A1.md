> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古师大附中高一（下）月考数学试卷（6月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} + (\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha) = \frac{\pi}{2}$ , $\therefore \sin (\alpha + \frac{\pi}{3}) = \cos (\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha) = \frac{1}{3}$ , $\therefore \sin (2\alpha + \frac{\pi}{6}) = \cos (\frac{\pi}{3} - 2\alpha) = 2\cos^2 (\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha) - 1 = 2 \times \frac{1}{9} - 1 = -\frac{7}{9}$ , 故选： $B$ .
