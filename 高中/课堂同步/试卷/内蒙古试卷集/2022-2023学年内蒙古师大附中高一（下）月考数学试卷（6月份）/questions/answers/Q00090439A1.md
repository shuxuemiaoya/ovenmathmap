> >
> > 又因为 $\cos(\alpha+\beta)=-\frac{\sqrt{5}}{15}$ ，
> >
> > 所以 $\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sqrt{1-\cos^{2}(\alpha+\beta)}=\frac{2\sqrt{55}}{15}$
> >

> > \sin (2 \alpha + \beta) = \sin [ (\alpha + \beta) + \alpha ] = \sin (\alpha + \beta) \cos \alpha + \sin \alpha \cos (\alpha + \beta)
> > $$
