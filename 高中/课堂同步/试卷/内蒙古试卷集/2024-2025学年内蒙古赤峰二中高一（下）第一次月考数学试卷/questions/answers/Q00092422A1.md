> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰二中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 因为 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角，所以 $\cos \alpha < 0$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \text {所以} \sqrt {\frac {1 + \sin \alpha}{1 - \cos \alpha}} - \sqrt {\frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{1 + \sin \alpha}} = \sqrt {\frac {1 + \sin \alpha}{1 - \sin \alpha} \bullet \frac {1 + \sin \alpha}{1 + \sin \alpha}} - \sqrt {\frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{1 + \sin \alpha} \bullet \frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{1 - \sin \alpha}} \\ = \frac {1 + \sin \alpha}{| \cos \alpha |} - \frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{| \cos \alpha |} = \frac {1 + \sin \alpha}{- \cos \alpha} - \frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{- \cos \alpha} = \frac {- 1 - \sin \alpha + 1 - \sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = - 2 \tan \alpha ; \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > (2)由 $\frac{2\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha}{\sin\alpha - 2\cos\alpha} = \frac{1}{2}$ ，得 $3\sin \alpha = -4\cos \alpha$ ，所以 $\tan \alpha = -\frac{4}{3}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > { \begin{array}{l} {{\text {所以} {\frac {1}{2}} \sin^ {2} \alpha - 3 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha - 2 \cos^ {2} \alpha = {\frac {{\frac {1}{2}} s i n ^ {2} \alpha - 3 s i n \alpha c o s \alpha - 2 c o s ^ {2} \alpha}{s i n ^ {2} \alpha + c o s ^ {2} \alpha}},}} \\ {{= {\frac {{\frac {1}{2}} s i n ^ {2} \alpha - 3 t a n \alpha - 2}{1 + t a n ^ {2} \alpha}}}} \\ {{= {\frac {{\frac {1}{2}} \times (- {\frac {4}{3}}) ^ {2} - 3 \times (- {\frac {4}{3}}) - 2}{1 + (- {\frac {4}{3}}) ^ {2}}}}} \\ {{= {\frac {2 6}{2 5}}.}} \end{array} }
> > $$
