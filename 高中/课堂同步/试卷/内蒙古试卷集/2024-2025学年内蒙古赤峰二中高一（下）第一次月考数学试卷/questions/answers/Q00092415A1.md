> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰二中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\overrightarrow{AD} = 3\overrightarrow{DB}$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{AB}$
> >
> > 因为 $C$ 、 $P$ 、 $D$ 三点共线，
> >
> > 所以可设 $\overrightarrow{CP} = k\overrightarrow{CD}$ ，即 $\overrightarrow{AP} -\overrightarrow{AC} = k(\overrightarrow{AD} -\overrightarrow{AC})$
> >
> > 又因为 $\overrightarrow{AP} = x\overrightarrow{AC} +\frac{3}{5}\overrightarrow{AB}$
> >
> > 所以 $(x - 1)\overrightarrow{AC} +\frac{3}{5}\overrightarrow{AB} = k(\frac{3}{4}\overrightarrow{AB} -\overrightarrow{AC})$
> >
> > 又 $\overrightarrow{AC}$ ， $\overrightarrow{AB}$ 为不共线的非零向量，
> >
> > 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x-1&=-k\\ \frac{3}{5}&=\frac{3}{4}k\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}k&=\frac{4}{5}\\ x&=\frac{1}{5}\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \frac{1}{5}\overrightarrow{AC} +\frac{3}{5}\overrightarrow{AB},$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {A P} \bullet \overrightarrow {C D} = \overrightarrow {A P} \bullet (\overrightarrow {A D} - \overrightarrow {A C}) = (\frac {1}{5} \overrightarrow {A C} + \frac {3}{5} \overrightarrow {A B}) \bullet (\frac {3}{4} \overrightarrow {A B} - \overrightarrow {A C})
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = - \frac {1}{5} \times 4 ^ {2} + \frac {9}{2 0} \times 5 ^ {2} - \frac {9}{2 0} \times 4 \times 5 \cos \frac {\pi}{3} = \frac {7 1}{2 0}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：B.
