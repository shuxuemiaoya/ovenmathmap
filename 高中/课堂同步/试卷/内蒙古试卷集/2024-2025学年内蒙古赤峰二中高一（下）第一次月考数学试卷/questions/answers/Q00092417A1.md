> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰二中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AB
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {a} = (- 3, 2), \overrightarrow {b} = (2, 1), \overrightarrow {c} = (\lambda , - 1), (\overrightarrow {a} + 2 \overrightarrow {b}) \perp \overrightarrow {c},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {a} + 2 \overrightarrow {b} = (1, 4)
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \left( \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right) \bullet \overrightarrow{c} = \lambda - 4 = 0$ ，故 $\lambda = 4$ ，选项 $A$ 正确.
> >
> > $$
> > B. \because t \overrightarrow {b} + \overrightarrow {c} = (2 t, t) + (\lambda , - 1) = (2 t + \lambda , t - 1), \overrightarrow {a} = t \overrightarrow {b} + \overrightarrow {c},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2t + \lambda = -3 \\ t - 1 = 2 \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $t = 3$ ， $\lambda = -9$ ，故 $\lambda + t = -6$ ，选项 $B$ 正确.
> >
> > C.由题意得， $\overrightarrow{a}\cdot\overrightarrow{b}=-4,\left|\overrightarrow{b}\right|=\sqrt{5}$
> >
> > ∴ $\overrightarrow{a}$ 在 $\overrightarrow{b}$ 方向上的投影向量为 $\frac{\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{|\overrightarrow{b}|^{2}} \overrightarrow{b} = -\frac{4}{5} \overrightarrow{b} = \left(-\frac{8}{5}, -\frac{4}{5}\right)$ ，选项 C 错误.
> >
> > $D.$ 由题意得， $\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{b} = (-1,3),2\overrightarrow{b} +\overrightarrow{c} = (4 + \lambda ,1)$
> >
> > $\because$ 向量 $\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{b}$ 与向量 $2\overrightarrow{b} +\overrightarrow{c}$ 的夹角为锐角，
> >
> > $\therefore (\overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b}) \bullet (2\overrightarrow{b} + \overrightarrow{c}) = -(4 + \lambda) + 3 > 0$ ，解得 $\lambda < -1$
> >
> > 当向量 $\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{b}$ 与向量 $2\overrightarrow{b} +\overrightarrow{c}$ 共线时，由 $-1\times 1 - 3(4 + \lambda) = 0$ 得 $\lambda = -\frac{13}{3}$
> >
> > ∴ $\lambda$ 的取值范围是 $(-∞, -\frac{13}{3}) ∪ (-\frac{13}{3}, -1)$ ，选项 D 错误.
> >
> > 故选：AB.
