> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古巴彦淖尔市高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 已知甲、乙、丙投篮命中的概率分别为 $\frac{2}{3}$ , $\frac{3}{5}$ , $\frac{1}{2}$ ,
> >
> > 设甲、乙、丙三人各投篮一次，甲、乙、丙投篮命中分别为事件A，B，C，
> >
> > $P(A) = \frac{2}{3}, P(B) = \frac{3}{5}, P(C) = \frac{1}{2}$ , 则 $X = 2$ 为事件 $AB\overline{C} + A\overline{B}C + \overline{A}BC$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > P (X = 2) = P \left(A B \bar {C} + A \bar {B} C + \bar {A} B C\right) = P \left(A B \bar {C}\right) + P \left(A \bar {B} C\right) + P (\bar {A} B C)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {2}{3} \times \frac {3}{5} \times \left(1 - \frac {1}{2}\right) + \frac {2}{3} \times \left(1 - \frac {3}{5}\right) \times \frac {1}{2} + \left(1 - \frac {2}{3}\right) \times \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {1 3}{3 0}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：C.
