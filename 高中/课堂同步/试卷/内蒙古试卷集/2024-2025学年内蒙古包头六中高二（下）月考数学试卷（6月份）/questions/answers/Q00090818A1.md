> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头六中高二（下）月考数学试卷（6月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题知 $|\overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b} | = \sqrt{\overrightarrow{a}^2 + \overrightarrow{b}^2 - 2\overrightarrow{a}\cdot\overrightarrow{b}} = 2\sqrt{3},$
> >
> > $$
> > (\overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b}) \cdot \overrightarrow {a} = \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} - \overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {b} = 2,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \langle \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b}, \overrightarrow {a} \rangle = \frac {(\overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b}) \cdot \overrightarrow {a}}{| \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b} | | \overrightarrow {a} |} = \frac {2}{2 \sqrt {3}} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{3}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ .
