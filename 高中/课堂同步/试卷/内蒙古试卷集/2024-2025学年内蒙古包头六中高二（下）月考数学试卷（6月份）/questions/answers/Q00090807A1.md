> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头六中高二（下）月考数学试卷（6月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because z = \frac {2}{1 + 3 i},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore |z| = \left|\frac{2}{1 + 3i}\right| = \frac{|2|}{|1 + 3i|} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{1 + 9}} = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{5}.$
> >
