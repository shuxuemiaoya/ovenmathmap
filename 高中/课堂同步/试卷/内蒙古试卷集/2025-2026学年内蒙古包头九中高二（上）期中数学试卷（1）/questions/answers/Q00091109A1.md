> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头九中高二（上）期中数学试卷（1）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\frac{1}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可得三棱锥 $A - BCD$ 为正四面体，则 $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AC} \cdot \overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 则
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{r l} & {\overrightarrow {A E} \cdot \overrightarrow {C F} = \frac {1}{2} (\overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {A C}) \cdot (\overrightarrow {A F} - \overrightarrow {A C}) = \frac {1}{4} (\overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {A C}) \cdot (\overrightarrow {A D} - 2 \overrightarrow {A C})} \\ & {= \frac {1}{4} (\overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {A D} + \overrightarrow {A C} \cdot \overrightarrow {A D} - 2 \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {A C} - 2 \overrightarrow {A C} ^ {2}) = - \frac {1}{2}} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $-\frac{1}{2}$ .
