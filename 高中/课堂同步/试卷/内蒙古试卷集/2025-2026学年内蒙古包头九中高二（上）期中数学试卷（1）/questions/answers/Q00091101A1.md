> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头九中高二（上）期中数学试卷（1）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 根据对称性不妨设 $M$ ， $N$ 都在 $\pmb{x}$ 轴上方，联立 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}y = -\frac{b}{a} x\\ y = \frac{b}{a} (x + c) \end{array} \right.$ ，解得 $M(-\frac{a^2}{c},\frac{ab}{c})$ 又 $F(-c,0)$ ，且 $\overrightarrow{FM} = \frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{FN}$ ，设 $N(x,y)$ 则 $(- \frac{a^2}{c} +c,\frac{ab}{c}) = \frac{1}{4} (x + c,y)$ 又 $N$ 在双曲线 $C:\frac{x^2}{a^2} -\frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1(a > 0,b > 0)$ 上， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{ll} - \frac{a^2}{c} +c = \frac{1}{4} (x + c)\\ \frac{ab}{c} = \frac{1}{4} y \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}x = \frac{3c^2 - 4a^2}{c}\\ y = \frac{4ab}{c} \end{array} \right.$ 又 $N$ 在双曲线 $C:\frac{x^2}{a^2} -\frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1(a > 0,b > 0)$ 上， $\therefore \frac{\frac{(3c^2 - 4a^2)^2}{c^2}}{a^2} -\frac{\frac{16a^2b^2}{c^2}}{b^2} = 1,$ $\therefore \frac{9c^4 - 24a^2c^2 + 16a^4 - 16a^4}{a^2c^2} = 1,$ $\therefore 9c^4 = 25a^2c^2,\therefore 9c^2 = 25a^2,$ $\therefore 9(a^{2} + b^{2}) = 25a^{2},$ $\therefore 9b^{2} = 16a^{2},\therefore 3b = 4a,\therefore \frac{b}{a} = \frac{4}{3},$ $\therefore$ 双曲线 $C$ 的渐近线方程为 $y = \pm \frac{4}{3} x.$
> >
> > 故选：D.
