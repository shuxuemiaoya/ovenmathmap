> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古呼和浩特市新城区土默特中学高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > $$
> > f (\alpha) = \frac {\sin (3 \pi - \alpha) \cos (2 \pi - \alpha) \sin \left(\frac {3 \pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{\cos (\pi - \alpha) \sin (- \pi - \alpha)} = \frac {\sin \alpha \bullet \cos \alpha \bullet (- \cos \alpha)}{- \cos \alpha \bullet \sin \alpha} = \cos \alpha .
> > $$
> >
> > (2) $\alpha$ 是第二象限角，且 $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \alpha\right) = -\sin \alpha = -\frac{1}{3}, \therefore \sin \alpha = \frac{1}{3}$ ，
> >
> > $\because \alpha$ 是第二象限角， $\therefore f(\alpha) = cos\alpha = -\sqrt{1 - sin^2\alpha} = -\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}.$
