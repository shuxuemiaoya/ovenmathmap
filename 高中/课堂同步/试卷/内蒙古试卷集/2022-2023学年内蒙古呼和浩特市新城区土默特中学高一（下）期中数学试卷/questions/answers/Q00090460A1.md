> > （1）向量 $\overrightarrow{a} = (1,2)$ ， $\overrightarrow{b} = (2,k)$ ， $\overrightarrow{c} = (8,7)$ $\therefore \overrightarrow{b} +\overrightarrow{c} = (10,k + 7)$ 令 $1\times (k + 7) - 2\times 10 = 0$ ，解得 $k = 13$ $\therefore$ 当 $k = 13$ 时， $\overrightarrow{a}\mathbin {//}(\overrightarrow{b} +\overrightarrow{c})$ (2)当 $k = 1$ 时， $\overrightarrow{b} = (2,1)$ 设 $\overrightarrow{c} = m\overrightarrow{a} +n\overrightarrow{b}$ 即 $(8,7) = (m + 2n,2m + n)$ $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l}m + 2n = 8\\ 2m + n = 7 \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $m = 2,n = 3$
