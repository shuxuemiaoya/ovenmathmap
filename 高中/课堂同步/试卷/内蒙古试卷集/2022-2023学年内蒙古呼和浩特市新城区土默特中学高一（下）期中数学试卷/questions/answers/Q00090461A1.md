> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古呼和浩特市新城区土默特中学高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $\because \tan \alpha = 3$ ，原式分子分母同除以 $\cos \alpha$ 可得，
> >
> > 原式 $= \frac{4\tan\alpha - 1}{3\tan\alpha + 5} = \frac{4 \times 3 - 1}{3 \times 3 + 5} = \frac{11}{14}$ .
> >
> > (2) $\because \tan \alpha = 3$ ，原式的分子分母同除以 $\cos^2 \alpha$ 可得：
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\tan^ {2} \alpha - 2 \tan \alpha - 1}{4 - 3 \tan^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {9 - 2 \times 3 - 1}{4 - 3 \times 3 ^ {2}} = - \frac {2}{2 3}.
> > $$
> >
> > (3) $\because \tan \alpha = 3$ ，用“1”的代换可得
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\frac {3}{4} \sin^ {2} \alpha + \frac {1}{2} \cos^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {\frac {3}{4} \tan^ {2} \alpha + \frac {1}{2}}{\tan^ {2} \alpha + 1} = \frac {\frac {3}{4} \times 9 + \frac {1}{2}}{9 + 1} = \frac {2 9}{4 0}.
> > $$
