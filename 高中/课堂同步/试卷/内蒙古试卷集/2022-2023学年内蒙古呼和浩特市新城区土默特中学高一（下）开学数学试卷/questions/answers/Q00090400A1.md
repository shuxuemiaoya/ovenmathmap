> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古呼和浩特市新城区土默特中学高一（下）开学数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $\because$ 集合 $M = \{x \mid -3 \leqslant x < 4\}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > N = \left\{x \mid x ^ {2} - 2 x - 8 \leqslant 0 \right\} = \left\{x \mid - 2 \leqslant x \leqslant 4 \right\},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore M \cup N = \{x \mid - 3 \leqslant x \leqslant 4 \}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > M \cap N = \{x \mid - 2 \leqslant x <   4 \}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D.
