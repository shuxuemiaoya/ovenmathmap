> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古名校联盟高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) ①∵ A(2,0), B(10,0), C(11,3), D(10,6),
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {B A} = (- 8, 0), \overrightarrow {B C} = (1, 3), \overrightarrow {D A} = (- 8, - 6), \overrightarrow {D C} = (1, - 3)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos \angle A B C = \frac {\overrightarrow {B A} \bullet \overrightarrow {B C}}{\overrightarrow {| B A |} \overrightarrow {| B C |}} = \frac {- 8}{8 \sqrt {1 0}} = - \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{1 0},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \angle A D C = \frac {\overrightarrow {D A} \bullet \overrightarrow {D C}}{\overrightarrow {| D A |} | \overrightarrow {D C |}} = \frac {1 0}{1 0 \sqrt {1 0}} = \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{1 0},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \cos \angle ABC + \cos \angle ADC = 0;$
> >
> > ②由PA = PB = PC = PD知，点P为四边形ABCD外接圆的圆心，
> >
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {A B} = (8, 0), \overrightarrow {B C} = (0, 6), \therefore \overrightarrow {A B} \bullet \overrightarrow {B C} = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore AB \perp BC$ ，四边形ABCD外接圆的圆心为AD的中点，
> >
> > ∴点P的坐标为(6,3);
> >
> > (2)由两点间的距离公式可得， $AB = 8$ ， $BC = CD = \sqrt{10}$ ， $AD = 10$ ∴过 $C$ 点的直线将四边形ABCD分成周长相等的两部分， $\therefore \overrightarrow{EC} = 9\overrightarrow{AE}$
> >
> > 设 $E$ 的坐标为 $(x, y)$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{EC} = (10 - x, 6 - y), \overrightarrow{AE} = (x - 2, y)$ ， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 10 - x = 9(x - 2) \\ 6 - y = 9y \end{array} \right.$ ， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = \frac{14}{5} \\ y = \frac{3}{5} \end{array} \right.$ ， $\therefore$ 点 $E$ 的坐标为 $(\frac{14}{5}, \frac{3}{5})$ 。
