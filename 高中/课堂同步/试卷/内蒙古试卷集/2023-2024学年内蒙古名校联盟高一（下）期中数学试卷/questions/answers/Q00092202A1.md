> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古名校联盟高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 因为 BC = 2， $\angle BCD = \frac{\pi}{6}$ ， $\triangle BCD$ 的面积为 $\frac{\sqrt{3} + 1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > S _ {\triangle B C D} = \frac {1}{2} B C \bullet C D \bullet \sin \angle B C D = \frac {\sqrt {3} + 1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 解得 $CD=\sqrt{3}+1$ ;
> >
> > 在 $\triangle BCD$ 中，由余弦定理得 $BD^{2} = BC^{2} + CD^{2} - 2BC\cdot CD\cos \angle BCD = 2$ 所以 $BD = \sqrt{2}$
> >
> > (2)在 $\triangle ABD$ 中，由正弦定理得 $\frac{AD}{\sin\angle ABD} = \frac{BD}{\sin\angle BAD}$
> >
> > 解得 $sin\angle BAD = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$ ， $cos\angle BAD = \frac{\sqrt{14}}{4}$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin \angle A D B = \sin (\angle A B D + \angle B A D)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \sin \angle A B D \cos \angle B A D + \cos \angle A B D \sin \angle B A D
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} (\frac {\sqrt {1 4}}{4} + \frac {\sqrt {2}}{4}) = \frac {1 + \sqrt {7}}{4}.
> > $$
