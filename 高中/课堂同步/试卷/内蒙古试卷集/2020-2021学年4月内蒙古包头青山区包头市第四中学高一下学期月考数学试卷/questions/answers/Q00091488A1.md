> [!faq]- 2020-2021学年4月内蒙古包头青山区包头市第四中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{3},\left(\frac{\pi}{6}-x\right)+\left(x+\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{\pi}{2}$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \cos (\frac {\pi}{6} - x) = \sin (x + \frac {\pi}{3}) = \frac {1}{3};
> > $$
> >
> > $\cos \left(\frac{\pi}{3} - 2x\right) = \cos 2\left(\frac{\pi}{6} - x\right) = 2\cos^2 \left(\frac{\pi}{6} - x\right) - 1 = -\frac{7}{9}$ ，因此正确答案
> >
> > 为A.
