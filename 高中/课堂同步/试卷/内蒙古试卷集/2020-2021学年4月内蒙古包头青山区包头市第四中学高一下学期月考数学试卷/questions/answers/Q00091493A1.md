> [!faq]- 2020-2021学年4月内蒙古包头青山区包头市第四中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $-\sqrt{3}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $4\sin 80^{\circ} - \frac{\cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}} = \frac{4\sin 80^{\circ}\sin 10^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{4\sin 10^{\circ}\cos 10^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{2\sin 20^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{2\sin(30^{\circ} - 10^{\circ}) - \cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{2\sin 30^{\circ}\cos 10^{\circ} - 2\cos 30^{\circ}\sin 10^{\circ} - \cos 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{-2\cos 30^{\circ}\sin 10^{\circ}}{\sin 10^{\circ}} = -\sqrt{3},$ 故答案为： $-\sqrt{3}.$
