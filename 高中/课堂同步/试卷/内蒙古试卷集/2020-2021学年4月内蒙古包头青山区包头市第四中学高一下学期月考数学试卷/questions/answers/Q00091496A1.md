> [!faq]- 2020-2021学年4月内蒙古包头青山区包头市第四中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $\frac{\pi}{2} < \beta < \pi$ 知： $\frac{\pi}{4} < \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} < \frac{3\pi}{4}$ , $\because \cos \left(\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{1}{3}$ , 则 $\sin \left(\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ , $\therefore \tan \left(\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = 2\sqrt{2}$ , 而 $\tan \beta = \tan \left[(\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) + \frac{\pi}{4}\right]$ , $\therefore \tan \beta = \frac{\tan (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) + \tan \frac{\pi}{4}}{1 - \tan (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) \tan \frac{\pi}{4}} = \frac{2\sqrt{2} + 1}{1 - 2\sqrt{2}} = -\frac{9 + 4\sqrt{2}}{7}$ ,
> >
> > (2) 由 $\cos \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \cos [(\beta + \alpha) - (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4})]$ , $\therefore \cos \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \cos (\beta + \alpha) \cos (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) + \sin (\beta + \alpha) \sin (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4})$ ,
> >
> > 由 $0 < \alpha < \frac{\pi}{2} < \beta < \pi$ 知： $\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha + \beta < \frac{3\pi}{2}$ , $\therefore$ 通过题意，得 $\cos (\beta + \alpha) = -\frac{3}{5}$ , 结合 (1) 有 $\sin (\beta - \frac{\pi}{4}) = \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ , $\therefore \cos \left(\alpha + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) = -\frac{3}{5} \times \frac{1}{3} + \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3} = \frac{8\sqrt{2} - 3}{15}$ .
