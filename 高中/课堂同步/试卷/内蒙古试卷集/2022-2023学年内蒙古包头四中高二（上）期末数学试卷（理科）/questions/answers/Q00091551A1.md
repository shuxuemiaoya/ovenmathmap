> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古包头四中高二（上）期末数学试卷（理科）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设 $P$ 为第一象限的交点， $\left|PF_1\right| = m$ 、 $\left|PF_2\right| = n$
> >
> > 则 $m + n = 2a_{1}$ 、 $m - n = 2a_{2}$
> >
> > 解得 $m=a_{1}+a_{2}$ 、 $n=a_{1}-a_{2}$ ，
> >
> > 在 $\triangle PF_{1}F_{2}$ 中，由余弦定理得： $\cos \angle F_{1}PF_{2} = \frac{m^{2} + n^{2} - 4c^{2}}{2mn} = \frac{1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore m^{2} + n^{2} - mn = 4c^{2}$ 即 $(a_{1} + a_{2})^{2} + (a_{1} - a_{2})^{2} - (a_{1}^{2} - a_{2}^{2}) = 4c^{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > a _ {1} ^ {2} + 3 a _ {2} ^ {2} = 4 c ^ {2}, \therefore \frac {a _ {1} ^ {2}}{c ^ {2}} + \frac {3 a _ {2} ^ {2}}{c ^ {2}} = 4
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{1}{e_1^2} + \frac{3}{e_2^2} = 4$ ，由柯西不等式可得：
> >
> > $$
> > (\frac {1}{e _ {1} ^ {2}} + \frac {3}{e _ {2} ^ {2}}) [ 2 ^ {2} + (\sqrt {3}) ^ {2} ] \geqslant (\frac {1}{e _ {1}} \times 2 + \frac {\sqrt {3}}{e _ {2}} \times \sqrt {3}) ^ {2} = (\frac {2}{e _ {1}} + \frac {3}{e _ {2}}) ^ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{2}{e_1} + \frac{3}{e_2} \leqslant 2\sqrt{7}$ ，当且仅当 $\frac{1}{2e_1} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{3}e_2}$ 时等号成立，
> >
> > 故选：B.
