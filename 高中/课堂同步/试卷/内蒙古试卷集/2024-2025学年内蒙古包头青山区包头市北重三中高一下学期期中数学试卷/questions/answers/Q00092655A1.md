> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头青山区包头市北重三中高一下学期期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析 (2)① $\left\{\begin{array}{l}\sin\alpha=-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}\\ \cos\alpha=\frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}\end{array}\right.;$ ② $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > （1）根据二倍角正切公式列式求解即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）由 $\tan 2\alpha = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 - \tan^2\alpha} = -\frac{3}{4},$
> >
> > 得 $3\tan^{2}\alpha-8\tan\alpha-3=(3\tan\alpha+1)(\tan\alpha-3)=0$ ，解得 $\tan\alpha=-\frac{1}{3}$ 或3.
> >
> > (2) ①由题意得 $\tan \alpha = -\frac{1}{3}$ , 且 $\sin \alpha < 0, \cos \alpha > 0$ .
> >
> > 由 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = -\frac{1}{3},\\ \sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1, \end{array} \right.$ 得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\sin \alpha = -\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10},\\ \cos \alpha = \frac{3\sqrt{10}}{10}. \end{array} \right.$
> >
> > $$
> > \cos \left(2 \alpha - \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \cos 2 \alpha + \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \sin 2 \alpha = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \left(2 \cos^ {2} \alpha - 1\right) + \sqrt {2} \sin \alpha \cos \alpha
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \left(2 \times \frac {9}{1 0} - 1\right) + \sqrt {2} \times \left(- \frac {\sqrt {1 0}}{1 0}\right) \times \frac {3 \sqrt {1 0}}{1 0} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{1 0}.
> > $$
