> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头青山区包头市北重三中高一下学期期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\sqrt{15}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用向量数量积求模即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \left| \overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} \right| = \sqrt {\overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} + 2 \overrightarrow {a} \cdot \overrightarrow {b} + \overrightarrow {b} ^ {2}} = \sqrt {4 + 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times \frac {1}{6} + 9} = \sqrt {1 5}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为: $\sqrt{15}$ .
