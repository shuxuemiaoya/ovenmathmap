> [!faq]- 2017-2018学年3月内蒙古包头青山区包头市北重三中高一下学期理科月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由正弦定理可得：
> >
> > $$
> > \boldsymbol {a} = 2 \boldsymbol {R} \sin A = 2 \times 1 \times \sin \frac {\pi}{3} = \sqrt {3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin B = \frac {\boldsymbol {b}}{2 R} = \frac {2}{2 \times 1} = \frac {1}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 由 $a=\sqrt{3}>1=b$ ，可得B为锐角，从而解得： $B=\frac{\pi}{6}$ 故解得： $C = \pi -A - B = \pi -\frac{\pi}{3} -\frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{\pi}{2},$
> >
> > $$
> > \boldsymbol {S} _ {\triangle A B C} = \frac {1}{2} \boldsymbol {a b} \sin C = \frac {1}{2} \times \sqrt {3} \times 1 \times \sin \frac {\pi}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：C.
