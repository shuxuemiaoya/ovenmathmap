> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰四中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** AB
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > y = \cos 2 x = \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {5 \pi}{1 2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y = \sin \left(2 x - \frac {\pi}{3}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > y = \cos 2 x = \sin \left(2 x + \frac {\pi}{2}\right)
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {7 \pi}{1 2}
> > $$
> >
> > 可得 $y = \sin (2x + \frac{7\pi}{6} + \frac{\pi}{2}) = \sin (2x + \frac{5\pi}{3}) = \sin (2x + \frac{5\pi}{3} - 2\pi) = \sin (2x - \frac{\pi}{3})$ 的图象故选：AB.
