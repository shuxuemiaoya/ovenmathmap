> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰四中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > ∵ $cos(\frac{\pi}{6}-\alpha)=\frac{3}{5}, \therefore sin[\frac{\pi}{2}-(\frac{\pi}{6}-\alpha)]=\sin(\frac{\pi}{3}+\alpha)=cos(\frac{\pi}{6}-\alpha)=\frac{3}{5}$ 则 $sin(\alpha-\frac{2\pi}{3})=\sin(\pi-\alpha+\frac{2\pi}{3})=-\sin(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3})=-\frac{3}{5}$ 故选：C.
