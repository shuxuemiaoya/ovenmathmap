> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古赤峰二中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because a = \sqrt {3}, b = \sqrt {2}, B = 4 5 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore$ 由正弦定理 $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B}$ 得： $\sin A = \frac{asinB}{b} = \frac{\sqrt{3} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \because \sqrt {3} > \sqrt {2}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore 4 5 ^ {\circ} <   A <   1 8 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > ∴ A 的度数为 $60^{\circ}$ 或 $120^{\circ}$ .
> >
> > 故选：C.
