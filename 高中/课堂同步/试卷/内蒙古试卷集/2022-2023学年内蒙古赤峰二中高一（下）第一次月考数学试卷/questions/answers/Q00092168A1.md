> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古赤峰二中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 已知 $b \cos A = c$ ，由正弦定理 $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C} = 2R$ ，
> >
> > 得 $2R\sin B\cos A = 2R\sin C$ ，又 $\sin C = \sin (A + B)$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin B\cos A = \sin (A + B) = \sin A\cos B + \cos A\sin B$ ，即 $\sin A\cos B = 0$
> >
> > 可得 $\sin A = 0$ 或 $\cos B = 0$ ，因为 $0 < A < \pi, 0 < B < \pi$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin A \neq 0$ ，则 $\cos B = 0$ ，即 $B = \frac{\pi}{2}$ .
> >
> > (2)由（1）可知△ABC为直角三角形，若 $\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\overrightarrow{AC}<\frac{1}{2}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {A B} \bullet \overrightarrow {A C} = | \overrightarrow {A B} | \bullet | \overrightarrow {A C} | \bullet \cos A = | \overrightarrow {A B} | ^ {2} = c ^ {2} <   \frac {1}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $0<c^{2}<\frac{1}{2}$ ，即 $0<c<\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，则 $C\in(0,\frac{\pi}{4})$ ，
> >
> > 在 $Rt\triangle ABC$ 中， $a^2 + c^2 = b^2 = 1$ ， $c = \sin C$ ， $a = \cos C$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{a} +\frac{1}{c} = \frac{1}{cosC} +\frac{1}{sinC} = \frac{sinC + cosC}{sinCcosC}$
> >
> > $$
> > t = \sin C + \cos C = \sqrt {2} \sin (C + \frac {\pi}{4}), t \in (1, \sqrt {2})
> > $$
> >
> > 又因为 $t^2 = (\sin C + \cos C)^2 = 1 + 2\sin C\cos C$
> >
> > 所以 $\sin C\cos C=\frac{t^{2}-1}{2}$ ，所以 $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{c}=\frac{2t}{t^{2}-1}$ ， $t\in(1,\sqrt{2})$ ，
> >
> > 令 $y=\frac{2t}{t^{2}-1}=\frac{2}{t-\frac{1}{t}}$ ， $t\in(1,\sqrt{2})$ ，因为 $t-\frac{1}{t}$ 在 $t\in(1,\sqrt{2})$ 上单调递增，
> >
> > 所以 $y=\frac{2}{t-\frac{1}{t}}$ 在 $t\in(1,\sqrt{2})$ 上单调递减，所以 $y>\frac{2}{\sqrt{2}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}}=2\sqrt{2}$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{1}{a} +\frac{1}{c}$ 的取值范围为 $(2\sqrt{2}, + \infty)$
> >
> > (3) $\triangle ABC$ 的外接圆的半径 $r = |OA| = |OC| = \frac{1}{2} b = \frac{1}{2}$ ，设 $P(m, n)$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \left| P O \right| ^ {2} = m ^ {2} + n ^ {2}, \left| P N \right| ^ {2} = \left| P M \right| ^ {2} = \left| P O \right| ^ {2} - \left| O N \right| ^ {2} = m ^ {2} + n ^ {2} - \frac {1}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {P M} \bullet \overrightarrow {P N} = | \overrightarrow {P M} | \bullet | \overrightarrow {P N} | \bullet c o s \angle M P N = | \overrightarrow {P M} | \bullet | \overrightarrow {P N} | \bullet (2 c o s ^ {2} \angle N P O - 1)
> > $$
> >
> > 而 $cos^2\angle NPO = \frac{|PN|^2}{|PO|^2} = \frac{m^2 + n^2 - \frac{1}{4}}{m^2 + n^2}$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {P M} \bullet \overrightarrow {P N} = (m ^ {2} + n ^ {2} - \frac {1}{4}) (2 \bullet \frac {m ^ {2} + n ^ {2} - \frac {1}{4}}{m ^ {2} + n ^ {2}} - 1),
> > $$
> >
> > 令 $t=m^{2}+n^{2}$ ，则
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {P M} \bullet \overrightarrow {P N} = (t - \frac {1}{4}) (2 \bullet \frac {t - \frac {1}{4}}{t} - 1) = (t - \frac {1}{4}) (1 - \frac {1}{2 t}) = t + \frac {1}{8 t} - \frac {3}{4} \geq 2 \sqrt {t \bullet \frac {1}{8 t}} - \frac {3}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} - \frac {3}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > ，当且仅当 $t=\frac{1}{8t}$ ，即 $t=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}$ 时取等号，
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{PM} \bullet \overrightarrow{PN}$ 的最小值为 $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - \frac{3}{4}$ .
