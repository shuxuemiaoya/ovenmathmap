> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古赤峰二中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \vert \overrightarrow {A B} - t \overrightarrow {A C} \vert^ {2} = \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2} + t ^ {2} \overrightarrow {A C} ^ {2} - 2 t \overrightarrow {A B} \bullet \overrightarrow {A C} = c ^ {2} + b ^ {2} t ^ {2} - 2 t b c c o s A
> > $$
> >
> > 当 $t = \frac{2bccosA}{2b^2} = \frac{ccosA}{b}$ 时， $|\overrightarrow{AB} - t\overrightarrow{AC}|^2$ 取最小值，则 $c^2 - c^2 cos^2 A = \frac{9}{25} c^2$
> >
> > 所以 $cos^{2}A=\frac{16}{25}$ ，又 $\angle BAC$ 为锐角，
> >
> > 故cosA = $\frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 因为 $|\overrightarrow{AC}| = 2|\overrightarrow{AB}|$ ，所以 $b = 2c$
> >
> > 所以 $cosA = \frac{b^2 + c^2 - a^2}{2bc} = \frac{5c^2 - a^2}{4c^2} = \frac{4}{5}$ ，得 $a = \frac{3}{\sqrt{5}} c$
> >
> > 所以 $cosB = \frac{a^2 + c^2 - b^2}{2ac} = \frac{\frac{9}{5}c^2 + c^2 - 4c^2}{2 \times \frac{3}{\sqrt{5}}c^2} = -\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选：D.
