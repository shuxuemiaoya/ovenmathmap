> [!faq]- 2022-2023学年内蒙古赤峰二中高一（下）第一次月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{7}{25}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} + \theta\right) = \frac{3}{5}$ ，得 $-\sin \theta = \frac{3}{5}$ ，即 $\sin \theta = -\frac{3}{5}$ .
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \cos 2 \theta = 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \theta = 1 - 2 \times (- \frac {3}{5}) ^ {2} = \frac {7}{2 5}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{7}{25}$
