> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特市回民区高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{l} \cos 1 0 5 ^ {\circ} = \cos (4 5 ^ {\circ} + 6 0 ^ {\circ}) = \cos 4 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 6 0 ^ {\circ} - \sin 4 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 6 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \\ = \frac {\sqrt {2} - \sqrt {6}}{4} \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > 故答案为： $\frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$
> >
> > 2、 $\alpha$ ， $\beta$ 均为锐角，且 $\tan \alpha = 7$ ， $\cos \beta = \frac{2\sqrt{5}}{5}$ ，
> >
> > $\therefore \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + tan^2\alpha}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{50}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}, \sin \alpha = \sqrt{1 - sin^2\alpha} = \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10},$ $\cos 2\beta = 2\cos^2\beta - 1 = \frac{8}{5} - 1 = \frac{3}{5}, \sin 2\beta = \sqrt{1 - cos^22\beta} = \frac{4}{5},$ $\therefore 2\beta \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2}),$ $\therefore \cos (\alpha + 2\beta) = \cos \alpha \cos 2\beta - \sin \alpha \sin 2\beta = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{10} \times \frac{3}{5} - \frac{7\sqrt{2}}{10} \times \frac{4}{5} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2},$ $\therefore \alpha + 2\beta = \frac{3\pi}{4}.$ 故答案为： $\frac{3\pi}{4}$ .
