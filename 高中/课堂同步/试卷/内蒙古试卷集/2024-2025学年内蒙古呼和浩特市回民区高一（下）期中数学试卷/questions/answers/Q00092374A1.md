> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特市回民区高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 扇形BOC面积为 $\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\pi}{3} \times 1 = \frac{\pi}{6}$ , 又 $\widehat{BC} = \frac{\pi}{3}$ , 则 $\angle BOC = \frac{\pi}{3}$ ,
> >
> > 三角形BOC面积为： $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\times 1^2 = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$ ，弓形面积为 $\frac{\pi}{6} -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$
> >
> > (2)设 $\angle BOA = \theta (0 <   \theta <  \frac{\pi}{2})$ ，则 $\angle AOC = \alpha = \theta +\frac{\pi}{2}$
> >
> > 由题意可得， $cos\theta=\frac{4}{5}$ ， $sin\theta=\frac{3}{5}$
> >
> > 则 $sin\alpha = sin(\theta + \frac{\pi}{2}) = cos\theta = \frac{4}{5}$ , $cos\alpha = cos(\theta + \frac{\pi}{2}) = -sin\theta = -\frac{3}{5}$ ,
> >
> > 即 $C(-\frac{3}{5}, \frac{4}{5})$ ;
> >
> > (3)设 $\angle BOA = \beta$ ，因 $OB$ 延长线上一点为 $D(4,3)$
> >
> > 则 $cos\beta=\frac{4}{\sqrt{4^{2}+3^{2}}}=\frac{4}{5}$ ， $sin\beta=\frac{3}{\sqrt{4^{2}+3^{2}}}=\frac{3}{5}$ ，得 $B(\frac{4}{5},\frac{3}{5})$ .
> >
> > 设OE与单位圆交于F，则 $F(\cos(\beta+\frac{\pi}{3}),\sin(\beta+\frac{\pi}{3}))$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \cos (\beta + \frac {\pi}{3}) = \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {1}{2} - \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {4 - 3 \sqrt {3}}{1 0}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin (\beta + \frac {\pi}{3}) = \frac {3}{5} \times \frac {1}{2} + \frac {4}{5} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = \frac {3 + 4 \sqrt {3}}{1 0}.
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $OE = OD = 5$ ，则 $E(5cos(\beta +\frac{\pi}{3}),5sin(\beta +\frac{\pi}{3})) = (\frac{4 - 3\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{3 + 4\sqrt{3}}{2})$
> >
> > 即 $E(\frac{4-3\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{3+4\sqrt{3}}{2}).$
