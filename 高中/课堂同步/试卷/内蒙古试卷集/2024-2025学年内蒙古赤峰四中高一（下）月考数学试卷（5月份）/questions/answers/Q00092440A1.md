> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 由图知： $A = \sqrt{2}$ ， $\frac{T}{4} = \frac{7\pi}{12} - \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{4}$
> >
> > 所以 $T = \pi, \omega = \frac{2\pi}{T} = 2$
> >
> > 又函数图象经过点 $\left(\frac{\pi}{3}, 0\right)$ ,
> >
> > 所以 $2 \times \frac{\pi}{3} + \varphi = \pi$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > \varphi = \frac {\pi}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > 故 $f(x)=\sqrt{2}\sin(2x+\frac{\pi}{3})$ .
> >
> > (2)由图知：函数 $f(x)$ 在 $[0, \frac{\pi}{12}]$ ， $[\frac{7\pi}{12}, \pi]$ 上单调递增，在 $(\frac{\pi}{12}, \frac{7\pi}{12})$ 上单调递减.
> >
> > (3)已知 $\alpha \in (0,\pi)$ , $f\left(\frac{\alpha}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{2}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > f (\frac {\alpha}{2}) = \sqrt {2} \sin (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}) = \frac {1}{2} \Rightarrow \sin (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}) = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{4} <   \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha + \frac{\pi}{3} < \pi$
> >
> > 所以 $\cos(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{3})=-\frac{\sqrt{14}}{4}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > c o s \alpha = c o s [ (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}) - \frac {\pi}{3} ] = c o s (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}) c o s \frac {\pi}{3} + s i n (\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}) s i n \frac {\pi}{3} = \frac {\sqrt {6} - \sqrt {1 4}}{8}
> > $$
