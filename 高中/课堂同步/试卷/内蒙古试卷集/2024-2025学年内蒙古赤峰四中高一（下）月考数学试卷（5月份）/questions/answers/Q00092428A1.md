> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古赤峰四中高一（下）月考数学试卷（5月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 在 $\triangle PAB$ 中， $\angle PAB = 30^{\circ}$ ， $\angle APB = 45^{\circ} - 30^{\circ} = 15^{\circ}$ ， $AB = 80$
> >
> > $$
> > \sin 1 5 ^ {\circ} = \sin (4 5 ^ {\circ} - 3 0 ^ {\circ}) = \sin 4 5 ^ {\circ} \cos 3 0 ^ {\circ} - \cos 4 5 ^ {\circ} \sin 3 0 ^ {\circ}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} - \frac {\sqrt {2}}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} = \frac {\sqrt {6} - \sqrt {2}}{4}
> > $$
> >
> > 由正弦定理得： $\frac{PB}{\sin 30^{\circ}} = \frac{AB}{\sin 15^{\circ}}$
> >
> > 所以 $PB = 40(\sqrt{6} +\sqrt{2})$
> >
> > 所以树的高度为 $PB\sin 45^{\circ} = 40 \times (\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}) \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = (40 + 40\sqrt{3})m.$
> >
> > 故选：A.
