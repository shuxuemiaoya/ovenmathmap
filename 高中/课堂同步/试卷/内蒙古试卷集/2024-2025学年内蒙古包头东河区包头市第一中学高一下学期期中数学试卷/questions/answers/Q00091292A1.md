> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头东河区包头市第一中学高一下学期期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 利用平面向量的加法，减法和数乘得到 $\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{6}{5}\overrightarrow{AM} -\frac{3}{5}\overrightarrow{AN}$ ，再利用待定系数法求解.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：因为
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {A B} = \overrightarrow {A M} + \overrightarrow {M B} = \overrightarrow {A M} + \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {D A} = \overrightarrow {A M} + \frac {1}{2} (\overrightarrow {D N} + \overrightarrow {N A}) = \overrightarrow {A M} + \frac {1}{2} (\frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A B} - \overrightarrow {A N})
> > $$
> >
> > 所以 $\frac{5}{6}\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{AM} -\frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AN}$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{6}{5}\overrightarrow{AM} -\frac{3}{5}\overrightarrow{AN},$
> >
> > 所以 $\lambda = \frac{6}{5}$ , $\mu = -\frac{3}{5}$ , $\lambda + \mu = \frac{3}{5}$ .
> >
> > 故选：D
