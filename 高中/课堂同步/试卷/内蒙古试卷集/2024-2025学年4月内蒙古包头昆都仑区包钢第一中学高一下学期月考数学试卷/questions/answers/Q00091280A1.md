> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年4月内蒙古包头昆都仑区包钢第一中学高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac{4}{5}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 先利用诱导公式可知 $\sin \beta = \cos \alpha$ ，然后结合 $\sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1$ 可求得 $\sin \alpha$ ，从而可求得 $\sin \beta$ ，进而求得答案.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：由题意得： $\because \alpha +\beta = \frac{\pi}{2}$ $\therefore \sin \beta = \sin (\frac{\pi}{2} -\alpha) = \cos \alpha$ 由题意得： $3\sin \alpha -\sin \beta = \sqrt{10}$ $\therefore 3\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha = \sqrt{10}$ $\therefore \cos \alpha = 3\sin \alpha -\sqrt{10}$ 由 $\sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha = 1$ 可知： $\sin^2\alpha +\left(3\sin \alpha -\sqrt{10}\right)^2 = 1$ $\therefore 10\sin^2\alpha -6\sqrt{10}\sin \alpha +9 = 0$ ，即 $(\sqrt{10}\sin \alpha -3)^2 = 0$ $\therefore \sqrt{10}\sin \alpha -3 = 0$ ，即 $\sin \alpha = \frac{3}{10}\sqrt{10}$ $\therefore \sin \beta = 3\sin \alpha -\sqrt{10} = -\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$ 故可知 $\cos 2\beta = 1 - 2\sin^2\beta = 1 - 2\times \left(-\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{5}$ 故答案为： $\frac{4}{5}$
