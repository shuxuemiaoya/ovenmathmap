> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特市高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** B
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\frac{a}{\cos A} = \frac{b}{\cos B}$ ，得 $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{\cos A}{\cos B}$
> >
> > 又 $\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B},\therefore \frac{a}{b} = \frac{\sin A}{\sin B}.$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{\sin A}{\sin B} = \frac{\cos A}{\cos B} \therefore \sin A \cos B = \cos A \sin B,$
> >
> > $\sin (A - B) = 0, A = B.$ 同理 $B = C$
> >
> > ∴ $\triangle ABC$ 是等边三角形.
> >
> > 故选B.
