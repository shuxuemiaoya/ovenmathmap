>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > （1）由题意可知， $\overrightarrow{a} \bullet \overrightarrow{b} = |\overrightarrow{a}||\overrightarrow{b}|cos\theta = 3\sqrt{3} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{9}{2}$
> >
> > $$
> > | \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b} | = \sqrt {\overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} - 2 \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} + \overrightarrow {b} ^ {2}} = \sqrt {9 - 2 \times \frac {9}{2} + 3} = \sqrt {3};
> > $$
> >
> > (2)易知 $\vec{a} \bullet (\vec{a} - \vec{b}) = \vec{a}^2 - \vec{a} \bullet \vec{b} = 9 - \frac{9}{2} = \frac{9}{2}$ ,
> >
> > $$
> > c o s \langle \overrightarrow {a}, \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b} \rangle = \frac {\overrightarrow {a} \bullet (\overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b})}{| \overrightarrow {a} | | \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {b} |} = \frac {\frac {9}{2}}{3 \sqrt {3}} = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2},
> > $$
> >
> > 又 $\langle \overrightarrow{a},\overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b}\rangle \in [0,\pi ]$
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{a}$ 与 $\overrightarrow{a} -\overrightarrow{b}$ 的夹角为 $\frac{\pi}{6}$
