> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古呼和浩特市高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** BC
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意可得， $\cot \frac{5\pi}{4} = \frac{1}{\tan \frac{5\pi}{4}} = 1$ ， $A$ 错误；
> >
> > $\sin \alpha \cdot \csc \alpha = \frac{y}{r} \times \frac{r}{y} = 1, B$ 正确；
> >
> > $$
> > \sec^ {2} \alpha + \csc^ {2} \alpha = \frac {r ^ {2}}{x ^ {2}} + \frac {r ^ {2}}{y ^ {2}} = \frac {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}{x ^ {2}} + \frac {x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}}{y ^ {2}} = 2 + \frac {y ^ {2}}{x ^ {2}} + \frac {x ^ {2}}{y ^ {2}} \geq 2 + 2 \sqrt {\frac {y ^ {2}}{x ^ {2}} \bullet \frac {x ^ {2}}{y ^ {2}}} = 4
> > $$
> >
> > ，当且仅当 $x^{2}=y^{2}$ 时取等号，C正确；
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {c o t ^ {2} \alpha - 1}{2 c o s \alpha} = \frac {\frac {x ^ {2}}{y ^ {2}} - 1}{2 \times \frac {x}{y}} = \frac {x ^ {2} - y ^ {2}}{2 x y}, \text {又} r ^ {2} = x ^ {2} + y ^ {2},
> > $$
> >
> > $\cos 2\alpha = \cos^2\alpha -\sin^2\alpha = \frac{x^2}{r^2} -\frac{y^2}{r^2} = \frac{x^2 - y^2}{r^2} = \frac{x^2 - y^2}{x^2 + y^2}\neq \frac{cot^2\alpha - 1}{2cos\alpha},D$ 错误.
> >
> > 故选：BC.
