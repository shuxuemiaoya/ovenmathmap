>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) $f(\alpha)=\frac{sin(-\alpha)\bullet cos^{2}(\pi-\alpha)\bullet cos(\frac{\pi}{2}-\alpha)}{cos(\frac{3\pi}{2}+\alpha)\bullet sin(-\pi-\alpha)\bullet sin(\alpha-\frac{5\pi}{2})}$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {- s i n \alpha \bullet c o s ^ {2} \alpha \bullet s i n \alpha}{s i n \alpha \bullet s i n \alpha \bullet (- c o s \alpha)} = c o s \alpha
> > $$
> >
> > (2)由 $f(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4})=\frac{2}{3}$ 可得 $cos(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{4})=\frac{2}{3}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > f \left(\frac {3 \pi}{4} - \alpha\right) = \cos \left(\frac {3 \pi}{4} - \alpha\right) = \cos \left[ \pi - \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) \right] = - \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) = - \frac {2}{3}.
> > $$
