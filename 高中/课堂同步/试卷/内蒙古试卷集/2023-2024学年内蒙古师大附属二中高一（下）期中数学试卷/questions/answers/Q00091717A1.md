> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古师大附属二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1) 设点 $B$ 坐标为 $B(x, y)$ ，则 $y = \sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5}$ ，
> >
> > 因为点 $B$ 在第二象限，所以 $\pmb{x} = \cos \alpha = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2\alpha} = -\sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2} = -\frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 点B坐标为 $B(-\frac{4}{5}, \frac{3}{5})$ .
> >
> > (2)由诱导公式可得 $\frac{\sin(\pi + \alpha) + \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{4\tan(\pi - \alpha)} = \frac{-\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha}{-4\tan\alpha}$
> >
> > 由（1）知 $sin\alpha = \frac{3}{5}$ ， $cos\alpha = -\frac{4}{5}$
> >
> > 所以 $tan\alpha=\frac{sin\alpha}{cos\alpha}=-\frac{3}{4}$ ，
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\sin (\pi + \alpha) + \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{4 \tan (\pi - \alpha)} = \frac {- \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha}{- 4 \tan \alpha} = \frac {- \frac {7}{5}}{4 \times \frac {3}{4}} = - \frac {7}{1 5}.
> > $$
