> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古师大附属二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > \because \overrightarrow {a} \perp (\overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b}),
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {a} \bullet (\overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b}) = 0,
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {a} ^ {2} + \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} = 0
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \overrightarrow {a} \bullet \overrightarrow {b} = - 1
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \overrightarrow{a}$ 在 $\overrightarrow{b}$ 上的投影数量为 $\frac{\overrightarrow{a} \bullet \overrightarrow{b}}{|\overrightarrow{b}|} = -\frac{1}{2}.$
> >
> > 故选：D.
