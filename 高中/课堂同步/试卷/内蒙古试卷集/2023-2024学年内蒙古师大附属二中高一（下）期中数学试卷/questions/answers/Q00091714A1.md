> [!faq]- 2023-2024学年内蒙古师大附属二中高一（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $\frac {\sqrt {7}}{2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由 $\frac{cos2\alpha}{sin(\alpha - \frac{\pi}{4})} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，得 $\frac{cos^2\alpha - sin^2\alpha}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}sin\alpha - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}cos\alpha} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，
> >
> > 即 $-\frac{sin\alpha + cos\alpha}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}} = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ ，得 $\sin \alpha +\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$
> >
> > 两边平方得： $1 + 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{4}$ ，∴ $2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = -\frac{3}{4}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore \sin \alpha - \cos \alpha = \sqrt {(\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha) ^ {2}} = \sqrt {1 - 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha} = \sqrt {1 + \frac {3}{4}} = \frac {\sqrt {7}}{2}.
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \frac {\sqrt {7}}{2}
> > $$
