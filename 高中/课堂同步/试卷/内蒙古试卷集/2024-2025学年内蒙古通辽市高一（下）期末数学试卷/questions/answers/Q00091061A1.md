> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古通辽市高一（下）期末数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** C
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 由题意， $\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{b} = (3,2) + (0, - 1) = (3,1)$
> >
> > 则 $(\overrightarrow{a} +\overrightarrow{b})\cdot \overrightarrow{a} = (3,1)\cdot (3,2) = 3\times 3 + 1\times 2 = 11.$
> >
> > 故选：C.
