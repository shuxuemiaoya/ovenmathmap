> > (1) $z_{1} \cdot z_{2} = (2 + ai)(4 - 3i) = 3a + 8 + (4a - 6)i$
> >
> > $\because z_{1} \cdot z_{2}$ 是实数，

> > $\therefore 4a - 6 = 0$ ，解得 $a = \frac{3}{2}$

> > $$
> >
> > $\because \frac{z_1}{z_2}$ 是纯虚数，
> >
> > $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l}8 - 3a = 0\\ 4a + 6\neq 0 \end{array} \right.,$ 解得 $a = \frac{8}{3}$
> >
> > $$
