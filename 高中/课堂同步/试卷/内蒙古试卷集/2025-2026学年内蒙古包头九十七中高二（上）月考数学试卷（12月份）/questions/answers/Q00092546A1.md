> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头九十七中高二（上）月考数学试卷（12月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** A
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 依题意， $\overrightarrow{AC} = (-1, - 1,2),\overrightarrow{AB} = (1,1, - 1)$ ，则
> >
> > $$
> > \cos <   \overrightarrow {A C}, \overrightarrow {A B} > = \frac {\overrightarrow {A C} \bullet \overrightarrow {A B}}{| \overrightarrow {A C} | | \overrightarrow {A B} |} = \frac {- 1 - 1 - 2}{\sqrt {6} \bullet \sqrt {3}} = - \frac {2 \sqrt {2}}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore \sin < \overrightarrow{AC}, \overrightarrow{AB} > = \sqrt{1 - (-\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3})^2} = \frac{1}{3}$
> >
> > ∴点C到直线AB的距离为d=|AC|sin< $\overrightarrow{AC}$ ， $\overrightarrow{AB}>=\sqrt{6}\times\frac{1}{3}=\frac{\sqrt{6}}{3}$ .
> >
> > 故选：A.
