>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 详见解析
> >
> > (1)由椭圆的定义知， $2a=8,\therefore a=4$
> >
> > 又∵椭圆的离心率 $e=\frac{c}{a}=\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\therefore c=2\sqrt{3}$
> >
> > $$
> > \therefore b ^ {2} = a ^ {2} - c ^ {2} = 1 6 - 1 2 = 4
> > $$
> >
> > $\therefore$ 椭圆 $C$ 的标准方程为 $\frac{x^2}{16} +\frac{y^2}{4} = 1.$
> >
> > (2) $\because M(2,1)$ 为椭圆 $\frac{x^{2}}{16}+\frac{y^{2}}{4}=1$ 内一点，∴直线l与椭圆必交于A，B两点，
> >
> > 设 $A(x_{1},y_{1}),B(x_{2},y_{2})$ ，当 $x_{1} = x_{2}$ 时，不合题意，故 $x_{1}\neq x_{2}$
> >
> > $\because M(2,1)$ 为线段 $AB$ 的中点， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac{x_1 + x_2}{2} = 2 \\ \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2} = 1 \end{array} \right.$ ， $\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_1 + x_2 = 4 \\ y_1 + y_2 = 2 \end{array} \right.$ ，
> >
> > 又∵A，B均在椭圆上，∴ $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{x_{1}^{2}}{16}+\frac{y_{1}^{2}}{4}&=1\\ \frac{x_{2}^{2}}{16}+\frac{y_{2}^{2}}{4}&=1\end{aligned}\right.$
> >
> > 两式相减，得 $\frac{x_1^2 - x_2^2}{16} +\frac{y_1^2 - y_1^2}{4} = 0$ ，即 $\frac{(x_1 + x_2)(x_1 - x_2)}{16} = -\frac{(y_1 + y_2)(y_1 - y_2)}{4}$
> >
> > $\therefore \frac{4(x_1 - x_2)}{16} = -\frac{2(y_1 - y_2)}{4}, \therefore \frac{y_1 - y_2}{x_1 - x_2} = -\frac{1}{2}$ , 即 $k_{AB} = -\frac{1}{2}$ ,
> >
> > ∴直线l的方程为y-1=- $\frac{1}{2}$ (x-2)，即 $x+2y-4=0$ .
