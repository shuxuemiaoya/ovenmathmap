> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头九十七中高二（上）月考数学试卷（12月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 设椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{16} +\frac{y^2}{4} = 1$ 上的点 $P(4\cos \theta ,2\sin \theta)$
> >
> > 则点 $P$ 到直线 $\pmb{x} + 2\pmb{y} - \sqrt{2} = 0$ 的距离
> >
> > $$
> > d = \frac {\left| 4 \cos \theta + 4 \sin \theta - \sqrt {2} \right|}{\sqrt {5}} = \frac {\left| 4 \sqrt {2} \sin \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{4}\right) - \sqrt {2} \right|}{\sqrt {5}} d _ {\max} = \frac {\left| - 4 \sqrt {2} - \sqrt {2} \right|}{\sqrt {5}} = \sqrt {1 0};
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D.
