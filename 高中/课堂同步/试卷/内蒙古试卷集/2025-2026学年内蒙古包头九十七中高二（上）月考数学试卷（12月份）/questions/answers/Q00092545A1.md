> [!faq]- 2025-2026学年内蒙古包头九十七中高二（上）月考数学试卷（12月份）解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 如图， $PE = \frac{1}{4} PC$ ，所以
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {P E} = \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {P C} = \frac {1}{4} (\overrightarrow {A C} - \overrightarrow {A P}) = \frac {1}{4} (\overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {A D} - \overrightarrow {A P}) = \frac {1}{4} (\overrightarrow {a} + \overrightarrow {b} - \overrightarrow {c}), \text {又}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {P B} = \overrightarrow {A B} - \overrightarrow {A P} = \overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {c}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {B E} = \overrightarrow {P E} - \overrightarrow {P B} = \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {a} + \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {b} - \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {c} - (\overrightarrow {a} - \overrightarrow {c}) = - \frac {3}{4} \overrightarrow {a} + \frac {1}{4} \overrightarrow {b} + \frac {3}{4} \overrightarrow {c}.
> > $$
> >
> > 故选：D.
