> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年内蒙古包头市景泰高级中学高二（下）期中数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** D
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > $$
> > E (X) = 0 \times \frac {1}{3} + a \times \frac {1}{3} + 1 \times \frac {1}{3} = \frac {a + 1}{3},
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > D (X) = (\frac {a + 1}{3}) ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{3} + (a - \frac {a + 1}{3}) ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{3} + (1 - \frac {a + 1}{3}) ^ {2} \times \frac {1}{3}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > = \frac {1}{2 7} [ (a + 1) ^ {2} + (2 a - 1) ^ {2} + (a - 2) ^ {2} ] = \frac {2}{9} (a ^ {2} - a + 1) = \frac {2}{9} (a - \frac {1}{2}) ^ {2} + \frac {1}{6}
> > $$
> >
> > $\because 0 < a < 1, \therefore D(X)$ 先减小后增大
> >
> > 故选：D.
