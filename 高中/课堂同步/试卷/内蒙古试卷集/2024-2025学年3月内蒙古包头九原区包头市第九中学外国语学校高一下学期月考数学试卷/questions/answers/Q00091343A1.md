> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年3月内蒙古包头九原区包头市第九中学外国语学校高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** 详见解析 (2) $\overrightarrow{MN} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{e_1} -\frac{1}{6}\overrightarrow{e_2}$
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据向量的加法与减法计算即可.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > （1）因为 $\overrightarrow{BM} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{BC}$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{CM} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{CB}$
> >
> > 所以 $\overrightarrow{CM} = \frac{2}{3}\times \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} = \frac{1}{3}\left(\overrightarrow{OB} -\overrightarrow{OA}\right) = -\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{e_1} +\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{e_2},$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {C N} = \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {C D} = \frac {1}{3} \times \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {O D} = \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {O D} = \frac {1}{6} (\overrightarrow {O A} + \overrightarrow {O B}) = \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {O A} + \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {O B} = \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {e _ {1}} + \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {e _ {2}}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \overrightarrow {M N} = \overrightarrow {C N} - \overrightarrow {C M} = \left(\frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {e _ {1}} + \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {e _ {2}}\right) - \left(- \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {e _ {1}} + \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {e _ {2}}\right) = \frac {1}{2} \overrightarrow {e _ {1}} - \frac {1}{6} \overrightarrow {e _ {2}}
> > $$
