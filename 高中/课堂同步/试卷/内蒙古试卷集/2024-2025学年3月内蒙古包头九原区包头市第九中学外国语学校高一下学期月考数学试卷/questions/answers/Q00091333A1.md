> [!faq]- 2024-2025学年3月内蒙古包头九原区包头市第九中学外国语学校高一下学期月考数学试卷解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** ACD
>
> > [!note]- **【分析】**
> > 根据三角恒等变换，化简求值.
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > A. $\tan 25^{\circ} + \tan 35^{\circ} + \sqrt{3} \tan 25^{\circ} \tan 35^{\circ}$ $= \tan 60^{\circ} (1 - \tan 25^{\circ} \tan 35^{\circ}) + \sqrt{3} \tan 25^{\circ} \tan 35^{\circ} = \tan 60^{\circ} = \sqrt{3}$ , 故A正确;
> > B. $\cos 85^{\circ} \cos 25^{\circ} - \sin 85^{\circ} \sin 25^{\circ} = \cos(85^{\circ} + 25^{\circ}) = \cos 110^{\circ} \neq \sqrt{3}$ , 故B错误;
> > C. $\frac{\sin 15^{\circ} + \cos 15^{\circ}}{\cos 15^{\circ} - \sin 15^{\circ}} = \frac{\tan 15^{\circ} + 1}{1 - \tan 15^{\circ}} = \frac{\tan 15^{\circ} + \tan 45^{\circ}}{1 - \tan 15^{\circ} \tan 45^{\circ}} = \tan(15^{\circ} + 45^{\circ}) = \sqrt{3}$ , 故C正确;
> > D. $\frac{\sin 40^{\circ} + \sin 80^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}} = \frac{\sin(60^{\circ} - 20^{\circ}) + \sin(60^{\circ} + 20^{\circ})}{\cos 20^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{2 \sin 60^{\circ} \cos 20^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}} = \sqrt{3}$ , 故D正确.
> > 故选：ACD
