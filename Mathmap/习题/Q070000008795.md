例4 化简

$$
\frac {\sin (2 \pi - \alpha) \cos (\pi + \alpha) \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + \alpha\right) \cos \left(\frac {1 1 \pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{\cos (\pi - \alpha) \sin (3 \pi - \alpha) \sin (- \pi - \alpha) \sin \left(\frac {9 \pi}{2} + \alpha\right)}.
$$
