(1) $\frac{x-1}{3x^2}, \frac{2}{ax}$ ;

(2) $\frac{3a}{2a-b}, -\frac{1}{b-2a}$ ;

(3) $\frac{1}{a-3}, \frac{2}{a^2+6a+9}$ ;

(4) $\frac{1}{x^2-4}, \frac{x}{4-2x}$ 。
