(1) $\left\{\begin{array}{l} x = -2, \\ y = 6; \end{array}\right.$

(2) $\left\{\begin{array}{l} x = 3, \\ y = 4; \end{array}\right.$

(3) $\left\{\begin{array}{l} x = 4, \\ y = 3; \end{array}\right.$

(4) $\left\{\begin{array}{l} x = 6, \\ y = -2。 \end{array}\right.$
