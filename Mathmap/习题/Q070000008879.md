5. 求证：

$$
\sin \theta - \sin \varphi = 2 \cos \frac {\theta + \varphi}{2} \sin \frac {\theta - \varphi}{2}; \tag {1}
$$

$$
(2) \cos \theta + \cos \varphi = 2 \cos \frac {\theta + \varphi}{2} \cos \frac {\theta - \varphi}{2};
$$

$$
\cos \theta - \cos \varphi = - 2 \sin \frac {\theta + \varphi}{2} \sin \frac {\theta - \varphi}{2}. \tag {3}
$$
