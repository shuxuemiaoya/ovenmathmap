分析：漏斗由两个多面体组成，其容积就是两个多面体的体积和.

解：由题意知

$$
\begin{array}{r l} V _ {\text {长方体} A B C D - A ^ {\prime} B ^ {\prime} C ^ {\prime} D ^ {\prime}} & = 1 \times 1 \times 0. 5 \\ & = 0. 5 (\mathrm{m} ^ {3}), \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} V _ {\text {棱锥} P - A B C D} & = \frac {1}{3} \times 1 \times 1 \times 0. 5 \\ & = \frac {1}{6} (\mathrm{m} ^ {3}). \end{array}
$$

所以这个漏斗的容积

$$
\begin{array}{r} V = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{6} = \frac {2}{3} \\ \approx 0. 6 7 (\mathrm{m} ^ {3}). \end{array}
$$
