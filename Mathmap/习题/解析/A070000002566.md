> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> $\sqrt{3}\sin15^{\circ}+\sin75^{\circ}=\sqrt{3}\sin15^{\circ}+\cos15^{\circ}=2\sin(15^{\circ}+30^{\circ})=$ → 点悟： $\sqrt{3}\sin15^{\circ}+\cos15^{\circ}=2\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin15^{\circ}+\frac{1}{2}\cos15^{\circ}\right)=2\left(\cos30^{\circ}\cdot\sin15^{\circ}+\sin30^{\circ}\cos15^{\circ}\right)$ $2\sin45^{\circ}=\sqrt{2}$ .
>
> 故选 **C**.
