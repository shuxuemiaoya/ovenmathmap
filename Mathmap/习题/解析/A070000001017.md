> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> $(a+b-2c)^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}+4c^{2}+2a\cdot b-4a\cdot c-4b\cdot c=1+1+4+2\times1\times1\times\frac{1}{2}-4\times1\times1\times\frac{1}{2}-4\times1\times1\times\frac{1}{2}=$ 3, 所以 $|a+b-2c|=\sqrt{3}$ .
>
> 故选 **B**.
