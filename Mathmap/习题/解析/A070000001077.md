> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> ∵ $n = (1, 0, -2)$ , $a = (-2, 0, 4)$ , $b = (4, 3, 2)$ , ∴ $a = -2n$ , $a \cdot b = -2 \times 4 + 0 \times 3 + 4 \times 2 = 0$ , 即 $a // n$ , $a \perp b$ , 所以 $l \perp \alpha$ , $l \perp m$ .
>
> 故选 **C**.
