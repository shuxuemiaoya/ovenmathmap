> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：（1） $a^7\div a^4 = a^{7 - 4} = a^3;$
> >
> > (2) $(-x)^{6} \div (-x)^{3} = (-x)^{6 - 3} = (-x)^{3} = -x^{3}$ ;
> >
> > (3) $(xy)^{4} \div (xy) = (xy)^{4 - 1} = (xy)^{3} = x^{3}y^{3}$ ;
> >
> > (4) $b^{2m+2} \div b^{2} = b^{2m+2-2} = b^{2m}$ .
