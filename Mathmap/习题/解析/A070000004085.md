> [!faq]- 答案与解析
>
> > [!success]- **【答案】** $2^{x^{\prime}} < 3^{\sqrt{2}} < 2^{\pi} < \pi^{\sqrt{5}}$
>
> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 因为 $(3^{\sqrt{2}})^{\sqrt{6}} = 9^{\sqrt{3}}, (2^{\sqrt{3}})^{\sqrt{6}} = 8^{\sqrt{2}}$ , 则由 $y = x^{\sqrt{3}}$ 在 $(0, +\infty)$ 上单调递增, $y = 8^{\pi}$ 在 $\mathbf{R}$ 上单调递增, 知 $9^{\sqrt{3}} > 8^{\sqrt{3}} > 8^{\sqrt{2}}$ , 故 $3^{\sqrt{2}} > 2^{\sqrt{3}}$ , 又由函数 $f(x) = a^{x}(a > 1)$ 为增函数, 得 $3^{\sqrt{2}} < 3^{\frac{3}{2}} = \sqrt{27} < 2^{3} < 2^{\pi}$ , 则 $2^{\sqrt{3}} < 3^{\sqrt{2}} < 2^{\pi} < 2^{3.2} = 8 \cdot 2^{\frac{1}{5}} < \pi^{2} \cdot \pi^{\frac{1}{5}} = \pi^{2.2} < \pi^{\sqrt{5}}$ .
> >
> > 点悟: 底数不变, 比较指数或指数不变, 比较底数
