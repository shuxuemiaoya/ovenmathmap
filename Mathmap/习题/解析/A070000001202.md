>
> **【解析】**
> ∵ 过点 $M(-2, a)$ , $N(a, 4)$ 的直线的斜率 $k = \frac{4 - a}{a - (-2)} = \frac{1}{2}$ ，解得 $a = 2, \therefore M(-2, 2)$ , $N(2, 4)$ , $\therefore |MN| = \sqrt{(-2 - 2)^{2} + (2 - 4)^{2}} = 2\sqrt{5}$ .
>
> 故选 **B**.
