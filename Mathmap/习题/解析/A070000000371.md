> > [!note]- **【解析】**
> > 解：(1) $\frac{x}{x - y} + \frac{y}{y - x} = \frac{x}{x - y} - \frac{y}{x - y} = \frac{x - y}{x - y} = 1;$
> >
> > (2) $\frac{a^2}{a - 1} -\frac{1 - 2a}{1 - a} = \frac{a^2}{a - 1} +\frac{1 - 2a}{a - 1} = \frac{a^2 - 2a + 1}{a - 1} = \frac{(a - 1)^2}{a - 1} = a - 1.$
