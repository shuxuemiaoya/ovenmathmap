> [!faq]- 人教A版高中数学·必修第一册
> 解：原式
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{l} = \frac {(- \sin \alpha) (- \cos \alpha) (- \sin \alpha) \cos \left[ 5 \pi + \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - \alpha\right) \right]}{\left(- \cos \alpha\right) \sin (\pi - \alpha) [ - \sin (\pi + \alpha) ] \sin \left[ 4 \pi + \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + \alpha\right) \right]} \\ = \frac {- \sin^ {2} \alpha \cos \alpha \left[ - \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - \alpha\right) \right]}{(- \cos \alpha) \sin \alpha [ - (- \sin \alpha) ] \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)} \\ = - \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = - \tan \alpha . \end{array}
> $$
