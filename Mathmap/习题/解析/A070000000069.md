解：原式 $= 2\left[\cos \left(\frac{4\pi}{3} -\frac{5\pi}{6}\right) + \mathrm{i}\sin \left(\frac{4\pi}{3} -\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)\right]$

$$
= 2 \left(\cos \frac {\pi}{2} + \mathrm{i} \sin \frac {\pi}{2}\right) = 2 (0 + \mathrm{i}) = 2 \mathrm{i}.
$$
