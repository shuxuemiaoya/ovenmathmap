> [!faq]- 高中选择性必修 第二册·人教A版数学
> 解：(1) $y'=(x^{3}\mathrm{e}^{x})'$ $=(x^{3})'\mathrm{e}^{x}+x^{3}(\mathrm{e}^{x})'$ $=3x^{2}\mathrm{e}^{x}+x^{3}\mathrm{e}^{x}.$
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{r l} y ^ {\prime} & = \left(\frac {2 \sin x}{x ^ {2}}\right) ^ {\prime} \\ & = \frac {(2 \sin x) ^ {\prime} x ^ {2} - 2 \sin x (x ^ {2}) ^ {\prime}}{(x ^ {2}) ^ {2}} = \frac {2 x ^ {2} \cos x - 4 x \sin x}{x ^ {4}} \\ & = \frac {2 x \cos x - 4 \sin x}{x ^ {3}}. \end{array} \tag {2}
> $$
