18.[天津四校2025高二期末联考]已知数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ 为等差数列,数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 为等比数列,且 $a_{4} = 7, a_{1} = 1, a_{1} + b_{3} = a_{2}^{2}, a_{2}b_{3} = 4a_{3} + b_{2}, n \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$ . (1)求数列 $\{a_{n}\}$ , 数列 $\{b_{n}\}$ 的通项公式; (2)设 $C_{n} = \begin{cases} k - 1, & n = b_{k-1}, \\ C_{n-1} + \frac{a_{n} + 1}{n}, & b_{k-1} < n < b_{k}, \end{cases}$ $k \in \mathbf{N}^{*}$ , $k \geqslant 2$ . ①证明:当 $k \geqslant 2, n = b_{k+1} - 1$ 时, $C_{n} \geqslant b_{k+1}$ ; ② $C_{1} = 1$ , 求 $\sum_{i=1}^{2^{n+1}} C_{i}$ .
