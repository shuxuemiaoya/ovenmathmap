解方程组：

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3 x + 5 y = 21, \\ 2 x - 5 y = - 11 _ {\circ} \end{array} \right.\tag{①}
$$

②

因为两个方程中的 $5 y$ 和 $-5 y$ 互为相反数，两个方程相加，可以得到 $5x = 10$

将 $x = 2$ 代入①，得

$$
\begin{array}{r l} 5 x & = 10, \\ x & = 2 _ {\circ} \\ 6 + 5 y & = 21, \\ y & = 3 _ {\circ} \end{array}
$$

所以方程组 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 3x + 5y = 21, \\ 2x - 5y = -11 \end{array} \right.$ 的解是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 2, \\ y = 3. \end{array} \right.$

解方程组:

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 x - 5 y = 7, \\ 2 x + 3 y = - 1 _ {\circ} \end{array} \right.\tag{①}
$$

②

解：②-①，得

$$
8 y = - 8,
$$

$$
y = - 1 _ {\circ}
$$

将 $y = -1$ 代入①，得

$$
\begin{array}{c} {2 x + 5 = 7,} \\ {x = 1 _ {\circ}} \end{array}
$$

所以原方程组的解是 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=1,\\ y&=-1.\end{aligned}\right.$

解方程组:

$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2 x + 3 y = 12, \\ 3 x + 4 y = 17 _ {\circ} \end{array} \right.\tag{①}
$$

②

能否使两个方程中 $x$ (或 $y$ ) 的系数相等 (或相反) 呢?

解：①×3，得

$$
6 x + 9 y = 36 _ {\circ}\tag{③}
$$

②×2，得

$$
6 x + 8 y = 34 _ {\circ}\tag{④}
$$

③-④，得

$$
y = 2 _ {\circ}
$$

将 $y = 2$ 代入①，得

$$
x = 3 _ {\circ}
$$

所以原方程组的解是 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = 3, \\ y = 2 \text{。} \end{array} \right.$
