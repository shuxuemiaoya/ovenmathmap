2. 把下列复数表示成代数形式:

(1) $3\sqrt{2}\left(\cos \frac{\pi}{4} +\mathrm{i}\sin \frac{\pi}{4}\right);$

(2) $8\left(\cos \frac{11\pi}{6} + \mathrm{i}\sin \frac{11\pi}{6}\right)$ ;

(3) $9(\cos \pi + \mathrm{i}\sin \pi)$ ;

(4) $6\left(\cos \frac{4\pi}{3} + \mathrm{i}\sin \frac{4\pi}{3}\right)$ .
