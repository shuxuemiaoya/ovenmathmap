我们来证明性质 2:
由两个实数大小关系的基本事实知
$\begin{array}{l} \left. \begin{array}{l} a > b \Rightarrow a - b > 0 \\ b > c \Rightarrow b - c > 0 \end{array} \right\} \Rightarrow (a - b) + (b - c) > 0 \\ \Rightarrow a - c > 0 \Rightarrow a > c. \\ \end{array}$
