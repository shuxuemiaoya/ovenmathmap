> **【解析】**
> - $\frac{7}{5}$ 解析：由题设， $3\sin \alpha = 1 - 2\sin^2\frac{\alpha}{2} = \cos \alpha$ ，则 $\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{3}$ ，又 $\sin 2\alpha + \cos 2\alpha = 2\sin \alpha\cos \alpha + \cos^2\alpha - \sin^2\alpha = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha} + \frac{1 - \tan^2\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha} = \frac{7}{5}$ .
