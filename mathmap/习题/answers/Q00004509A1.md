> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析： $\cos (\alpha -\frac{\pi}{2}) = \sin \alpha = -\frac{3}{5}$
因为 $\alpha \in (-\frac{\pi}{2}, 0)$
所以 $\cos \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2\alpha} = \frac{4}{5}$
则 $\sin 2\alpha = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = 2\times (-\frac{3}{5})\times \frac{4}{5} = -\frac{24}{25}$ 故选：C.
