> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 设 $a + 5b = m(2a + b) + n(a - b)$ 其中 $m, n \in \mathbb{R}$ , 则 $a + 5b = (2m + n)a + (m - n)b$ , 所以 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} 2m + n = 1, \\ m - n = 5, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} m = 2, \\ n = -3, \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $a + 5b = 2(2a + b) - 3(a - b)$ .
>
> 故选 **C**.
