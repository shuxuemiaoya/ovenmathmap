> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 依题意得 $AB \parallel F_{1}F_{2}$ ，如图，连接 $AF_{2}$ .
![](../../../../../images/answers/part-002/b5a266da19bdd95d7997254c12951ea29f0d5cd427276fed43c6504bb03f3855.jpg)
由椭圆性质知， $2c = |F_{1}F_{2}| = |AF_{1}|>$ a-c，解得 $e = \frac{c}{a} >\frac{1}{3},|AF_2| = 2a-$ 敖黑板：椭圆上一点到焦点的距离的最小值为长轴端点到相邻焦点的距离 $|AF_1| = 2a - 2c,$ 在 $\triangle AF_1F_2$ 中， $\angle F_2AF_1 = \angle AF_2F_1 =$ $\angle BAF_{2} = \frac{1}{2}\angle BAF_{1} <   \frac{\pi}{6},$ 所以 $\cos \angle F_2AF_1 = \frac{\frac{1}{2}|\Delta F_2|}{|\Delta F_1|} = \frac{a - c}{2c}>$ $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，解得 $e = \frac{c}{a} <  \frac{1}{\sqrt{3} + 1} = \frac{\sqrt{3} - 1}{2},$ 所以椭圆 $C$ 的离心率 $e$ 的取值范围是 $\left(\frac{1}{3},\frac{\sqrt{3} - 1}{2}\right)$ .
>
> 故选 **B**.
