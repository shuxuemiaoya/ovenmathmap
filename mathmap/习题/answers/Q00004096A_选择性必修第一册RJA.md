> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 设直线 $y = x + m$ 与 x 轴交于点 $M(-m, 0)$ ，直线方程与椭圆方程联立得 $\frac{4x^{2}}{3} + 2mx + m^{2} - 1 = 0, \Delta = (2m)^{2} - 4 \cdot \frac{4}{3} \cdot (m^{2} - 1) > 0$ ，解得 -2 < m < 2。设 $F_{1}(-\sqrt{2}, 0), F_{2}(\sqrt{2}, 0)$ 到得 $-\frac{\sqrt{30}}{3}<k<\frac{\sqrt{30}}{3}$ 且 $k\neq\pm\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$ .故直线l的斜率k的范围为 $\left\{k\mid-\frac{\sqrt{30}}{3}<k<\frac{\sqrt{30}}{3}\right.$ 且 $k\neq\pm\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\Bigg\}.$ (2)设 $C(x_{0},y_{0})(x_{0}^{2}\neq3)$ ，由题意知 $A_{2}(\sqrt{3},0)$ ，则令 $m=k_{A_{2}C}=\frac{y_{0}}{x_{0}-\sqrt{3}}$ ，
所以直线 $A_{2}C$ 的方程为 $y=m(x-\sqrt{3})$ ，联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}y=m(x-\sqrt{3}),\\ x^{2}+y^{2}=3,\end{aligned}\right.$ 得 $(m^{2}+1)x^{2}-2\sqrt{3}m^{2}x+3m^{2}-3=0.$ 所以 $x_{M}=\frac{\sqrt{3}(m^{2}-1)}{m^{2}+1},y_{M}=\frac{-2\sqrt{3}m}{m^{2}+1}.$ 由于C，D两点关于原点对称，所以 $D(-x_{0},-y_{0})$ ，
令直线 $A_{2}D$ 的斜率为n，则 $n=k_{A_{2}D}=\frac{-y_{0}}{-x_{0}-\sqrt{3}}=\frac{y_{0}}{x_{0}+\sqrt{3}}.$ 所以 $mn=\frac{y_{0}}{x_{0}-\sqrt{3}}\cdot\frac{y_{0}}{x_{0}+\sqrt{3}}=\frac{y_{0}^{2}}{x_{0}^{2}-3}$ ，又 $\frac{x_{0}^{2}}{3}-y_{0}^{2}=1,$ 所以 $mn=\frac{y_{0}^{2}}{x_{0}^{2}-3}=\frac{y_{0}^{2}}{3y_{0}^{2}}=\frac{1}{3}$ ，即 $n=\frac{1}{3m}.$ 所以 $x_{N}=\frac{\sqrt{3}(n^{2}-1)}{n^{2}+1}=$ $\frac{\sqrt{3}(-9m^{2}+1)}{9m^{2}+1},y_{N}=-\frac{2\sqrt{3}n}{n^{2}+1}=\frac{-6\sqrt{3}m}{9m^{2}+1},$ 所以 $k_{2}=\frac{y_{N}-y_{M}}{x_{N}-x_{M}}=\frac{-2m}{3m^{2}+1}=$ $\frac{-2y_{0}(x_{0}-\sqrt{3})}{3y_{0}^{2}+x_{0}^{2}+3-2\sqrt{3}x_{0}}=\frac{-2y_{0}(x_{0}-\sqrt{3})}{x_{0}^{2}-3+x_{0}^{2}+3-2\sqrt{3}x_{0}}=-\frac{y_{0}}{x_{0}}.$ 又 $k_{1}=\frac{y_{0}}{x_{0}}$ ，所以 $k_{1}+k_{2}=0.$
直线 $AB$ 的距离分别为 $d_{1}, d_{2}$ , 由题意得, $2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} \cdot |AB| \cdot d_{2} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot |AB| \cdot d_{1}$ , 所以 $d_{1} = 2d_{2}$ . 由三角形相似可得, $\frac{d_{1}}{d_{2}} = \frac{|F_{1}M|}{|F_{2}M|} = \frac{|\sqrt{2} + m|}{|\sqrt{2} + m|} = 2$ , 解得

> **✨ 巧思**
> 由点到直线的距离公式得 $d_{1} = \frac{|\sqrt{2} + m|}{\sqrt{2}}, d_{2} = \frac{|\sqrt{2} + m|}{\sqrt{2}}$ $m = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ 或 $m = -3\sqrt{2}$ . 因为 $-2 < m < 2$ , 所以 $m = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}$ , 故选 C.
