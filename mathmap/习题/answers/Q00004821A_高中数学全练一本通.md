> **【答案】** D
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析:由于 $\overrightarrow{AE}=\frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB},\overrightarrow{AF}=\frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AC},P$ 在射线 EF 上，设 $\overrightarrow{EP}=k\overrightarrow{EF},(k\geq0)$
![](../../../../images/answers/33272fc00b6f3736580d454c947fde33a19c7705965d7c4fb3714b32335323ef.jpg)
$$
\overrightarrow {A P} = \overrightarrow {A E} + \overrightarrow {E P} = \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A B} + k \overrightarrow {E F} = \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A B} + k (\overrightarrow {A F} - \overrightarrow {A E})
$$
$$
= \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A B} + k (\frac {2}{3} \overrightarrow {A C} - \frac {1}{3} \overrightarrow {A B}) = \frac {1}{3} (1 - k) \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {2 k}{3} \overrightarrow {A C}
$$
$$
= \frac {1}{3} (1 - k) \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {2 k}{3} (\overrightarrow {A B} + \overrightarrow {B C}) = \frac {1}{3} (1 + k) \overrightarrow {A B} + \frac {2 k}{3} \overrightarrow {B C}
$$
$$
\overrightarrow {A P} = \overrightarrow {A B} + m \overrightarrow {B C}
$$
$$
\left\{ \begin{array}{l} \frac {1}{3} (1 + k) = 1 \\ \frac {2 k}{3} = m \end{array} \right.
$$
$$
k = 2, m = \frac {4}{3}
$$
