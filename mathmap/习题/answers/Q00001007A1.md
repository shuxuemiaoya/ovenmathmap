> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> ∵ $\sin\alpha=2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}=2\times\frac{4}{5}\times\left(-\frac{3}{5}\right)=-\frac{24}{25}<0,\cos\alpha=\cos^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}-\sin^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}=\left(-\frac{3}{5}\right)^{2}-\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^{2}=-\frac{7}{25}<0,\therefore$ 角 $\alpha$ 是第三象限角.
>
> 故选 **C**.
