> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{3}{5}$
解析:已知
$$
\tan \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) = 2, \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{3} - 2 \theta\right) = \sin \left[ \frac {\pi}{2} - 2 \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) \right] = \cos 2 \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)
$$
$$
= \cos^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) - \sin^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)
$$
$$
= \frac {\cos^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) - \sin^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)}{\cos^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) + \sin^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)} = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)}{1 + \tan^ {2} \left(\theta + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right)} = \frac {1 - 4}{1 + 4} = - \frac {3}{5}.
$$
故答案为: $-\frac{3}{5}$
