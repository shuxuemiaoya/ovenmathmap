> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{7}{25}$
解析:由题可得,
$$
\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{3} - \alpha\right) + \sin \alpha = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos \alpha - \frac {1}{2} \sin \alpha + \sin \alpha = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \cos \alpha + \frac {1}{2} \sin \alpha
$$
$$
= \sin \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = \frac {3}{5}
$$
所以
$$
\sin \left(2 \alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left[ 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) - \frac {\pi}{2} \right] = - \cos 2 \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = 2 \sin^ {2} \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{3}\right) - 1
$$
$$
= 2 \times \left(\frac {3}{5}\right) ^ {2} - 1 = - \frac {7}{2 5}.
$$
