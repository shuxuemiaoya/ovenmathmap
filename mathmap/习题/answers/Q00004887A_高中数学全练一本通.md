> **【解析】**
> 0
详解：∵ $\overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{BC} - \overrightarrow{BA}$ ,
$$
\overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {A C} = \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot (\overrightarrow {B C} - \overrightarrow {B A}) = \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} - \overrightarrow {A B} \cdot \overrightarrow {B A} = - \overrightarrow {B A} \cdot \overrightarrow {B C} + \overrightarrow {A B} ^ {2}
$$
又 $\overrightarrow{BA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} = 36$ ， $AB = 6$ 即 $\overrightarrow{AB}^2 = |\overrightarrow{AB}|^2 = 36$
$\therefore\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\overrightarrow{AC}=-36+36=0$ ．故答案为：0.
