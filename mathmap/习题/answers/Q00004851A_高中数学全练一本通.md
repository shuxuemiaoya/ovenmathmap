> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{1}{18}\vec{b}+\frac{7}{27}\vec{c}$ 解析: 设 $\vec{a}=x\vec{b}+y\vec{c}$ ，由题意可知 $-\vec{l}_{1}+3\vec{l}_{2}=x(4\vec{l}_{1}+2\vec{l}_{2})+y(-3\vec{l}_{1}+12\vec{l}_{2})$ ，整理得 $-\vec{l}_{1}+3\vec{l}_{2}=(4x-3y)\vec{l}_{1}+(2x+12y)\vec{l}_{2}$ .
由平面向量基本定理得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&4x-3y=-1\\ &2x+12y=3\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&x=-\frac{1}{18}\\ &y=\frac{7}{27}\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $\vec{a}=-\frac{1}{18}\vec{b}+\frac{7}{27}\vec{c}$ .
