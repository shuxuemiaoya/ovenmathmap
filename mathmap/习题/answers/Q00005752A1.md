> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 20
解析: 因为斜二测直观图为矩形 $A^{\prime}B^{\prime}C^{\prime}D^{\prime}$ $A^{\prime}O^{\prime}=O^{\prime}B^{\prime}=2$ , $B^{\prime}C^{\prime}=2$ ,
则 $O'C'=\sqrt{(O'B')^{2}+(B'C')^{2}}=2\sqrt{2}$ ,
![](../../../../../images/answers/476816b320e6f5f8eca9faeb812bf736da9382b0aa0134dc913897c9ec4d683b.jpg)
可得原图 ABCD 中， $AB = AO + OB = A'O' + O'B' = 4$ ， $OC = 2O'C' = 4\sqrt{2}$ , $BC = \sqrt{OB^{2} + OC^{2}} = \sqrt{2^{2} + (4\sqrt{2})^{2}} = 6$ 四边形 ABCD 的周长 $2(AB + BC) = 2 \times (4 + 6) = 20$ . 故答案为:20.
