> **【解析】**
> $\frac{1}{2}$
解析: 因为 $\alpha\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right),\frac{\cos2\alpha}{1+\tan^{2}\alpha}=\frac{3}{8}$ ,
可得 $3\left(1 + \tan^2\alpha\right) = 8\times \frac{\sin^2\alpha - \cos^2\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = 8\times \frac{1 - \tan^2\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha}$
可得 $3\left(1 + \tan^2\alpha\right)^2 = 8 - 8\tan^2\alpha$
解得 $\tan^2\alpha = \frac{1}{3}$ ，因为 $\alpha \in \left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
所以 $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{6}$ ，所以 $\cos \left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \cos \frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{1}{2}$
