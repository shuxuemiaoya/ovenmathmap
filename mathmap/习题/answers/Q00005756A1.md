> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 160
解析:如图,设底面对角线 AC=a,BD=b,交点为 O,
![](../../../../../images/answers/76df7dadc02182e3b8c206de647857601a2bd303920c632a8f0f159490731ee1.jpg)
体对角线 $A_{1}C=15, B_{1}D=9$
所以 $a^2 + 5^2 = 15^2, b^2 + 5^2 = 9^2 \Rightarrow a^2 = 200, b^2 = 56$
∵该直四棱柱的底面是菱形，
$$
A B ^ {2} = \left(\frac {A C}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \left(\frac {B D}{2}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2}}{4} = 6 4 \Rightarrow A B = 8
$$
因此直四棱柱的侧面积为 $4 \times 8 \times 5 = 160$
