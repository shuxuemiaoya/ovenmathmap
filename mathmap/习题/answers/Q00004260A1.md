> **【解析】**
> $\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha = \frac{7}{5},\tan \alpha = -\frac{3}{4}$
解析: 因为 $\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha = -\frac{1}{5}$ ，则 $1 + 2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha = \frac{1}{25}$ ，
则 $\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = -\frac{12}{25}$ 且 $\alpha \in (0,\pi)$ ，则 $\sin \alpha > 0, \cos \alpha < 0$
所以 $\left(\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha\right)^2 = 1 - 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{49}{25}$ 又 $\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha >0$
所以 $\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha = \frac{7}{5}$ ，则 $\sin \alpha = \frac{3}{5},\cos \alpha = -\frac{4}{5}$ ，则 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = -\frac{3}{4}$
