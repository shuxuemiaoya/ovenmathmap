> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 4
解析： $\overrightarrow{PM}=-\frac{1}{15}\overrightarrow{PA}+\frac{2}{15}\overrightarrow{PB}+\frac{4}{15}\overrightarrow{PC}$ ，故 $15\overrightarrow{PM}=-\overrightarrow{PA}+2\overrightarrow{PB}+4\overrightarrow{PC},3\overrightarrow{PM}=-\frac{1}{5}\overrightarrow{PA}+\frac{2}{5}\overrightarrow{PB}+\frac{4}{5}\overrightarrow{PC},$
![](../../../../../images/answers/39ff55b7355b8c7321c20dd70c4f7d88db107f9f84aa2109b2c080247a276b5e.jpg)
不妨令 $\overrightarrow{PH} = 3\overrightarrow{PM}$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{PH} = -\frac{1}{5}\overrightarrow{PA} +\frac{2}{5}\overrightarrow{PB} +\frac{4}{5}\overrightarrow{PC}$ ，又 $-\frac{1}{5} +\frac{2}{5} +\frac{4}{5} = 1$ ，故点 $H,A,B,C$ 共面，故 $V_{M - ABC} = \frac{2}{3} V_{P - ABC} = \frac{2}{3}\times 6 = 4$ .故答案为：4.
