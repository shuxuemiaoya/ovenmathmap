> **【解析】**
> ∵ a-b 与 a 垂直，∴ $(a-b) \cdot a = 0, \therefore a \cdot a - a \cdot b = |a|^{2} - |a||b| \cdot \cos\langle a, b \rangle = 1 - 1 \times \sqrt{2} \times \cos\langle a, b \rangle = 0, \therefore \cos\langle a, b \rangle = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.$ $\because 0^{\circ} \leqslant \langle a, b \rangle \leqslant 180^{\circ}, \therefore \langle a, b \rangle = 45^{\circ}.$
