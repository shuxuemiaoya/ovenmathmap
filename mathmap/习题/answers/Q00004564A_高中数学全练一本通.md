> **【解析】**
> $B = \frac{\pi}{3}$ 解析: 因为 $\cos\frac{A+C}{2} = \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \frac{B}{2}\right) = \sin\frac{B}{2}$ ,
所以, $\cos B - \cos\frac{A+C}{2} = 1 - 2\sin^{2}\frac{B}{2} - \sin\frac{B}{2} = 0$ , 即 $2\sin^{2}\frac{B}{2} + \sin\frac{B}{2} - 1 = 0$ .
因为 $0 < B < \pi$ , 则 $0 < \frac{B}{2} < \frac{\pi}{2}$ , 所以, $0 < \sin\frac{B}{2} < 1$ , 解得 $\sin\frac{B}{2} = \frac{1}{2}$ ,
所以, $\frac{B}{2} = \frac{\pi}{6}$ , 因此 $B = \frac{\pi}{3}$ .
