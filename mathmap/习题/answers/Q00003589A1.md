> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 由题可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x\times1+1\times y+1\times1&=0,\\ \frac{1}{2}&=\frac{y}{-4}=\frac{1}{2},\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=1,\\ y&=-2,\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以
向量 $\boldsymbol{a}=(1,1,1),\boldsymbol{b}=(1,-2,1)$ ，所以 $\boldsymbol{a}+\boldsymbol{b}=(2,-1,2)$ ，所以 $\left|\boldsymbol{a}+\boldsymbol{b}\right|=\sqrt{2^{2}+\left(-1\right)^{2}+2^{2}}=3.$
>
> 故选 **B**.
