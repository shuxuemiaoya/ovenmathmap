> **【解析】**
> (1) $-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$ ; (2) $63/65$ 解析: (1) 因为 $\frac{\pi}{4} < \alpha < \frac{3\pi}{4}$ , 所以 $-\frac{\pi}{2} < \frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha < 0$ , 又 $\cos (\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha) = \frac{3}{5}$ , 则 $\sin (\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha) = \sqrt{1 - [\cos (\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha)]^2} = -\frac{4}{5}$ ,
所以 $cos\alpha = cos[\frac{\pi}{4} - (\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha)] = cos\frac{\pi}{4}\cos(\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha) + sin\frac{\pi}{4}\sin(\frac{\pi}{4} - \alpha)$ $= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} × 3/5 + 3/2 × (-5/5) = -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{10}$ .
(2) 因为 $0 < \beta < 0/4$ , 所以 $0 < 0/4 - 0 < 0/4$ , 又 $sin(\frac{\pi}{4} - 0) = 5/13$ ,
则 $\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\beta\right)=\sqrt{1-\left[\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\beta\right)\right]^{2}}=\frac{12}{13}$ 所以 $\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\sin\left[\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\beta\right)-\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\alpha\right)\right]$ $=\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\beta\right)\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\alpha\right)-\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\beta\right)\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}-\alpha\right)$ $=\frac{5}{13}\times\frac{3}{5}-\frac{12}{13}\times\left(-\frac{4}{5}\right)=\frac{63}{65}$
