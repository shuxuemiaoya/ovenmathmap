> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $$
\overrightarrow {C B} = \vec {a}, \overrightarrow {C D} = \vec {b}, \overrightarrow {C A} = \vec {c}, \vec {a}, \vec {b}, \vec {c}
$$
$$
\overrightarrow {A F} = \overrightarrow {A C} + \overrightarrow {C F} = - \vec {c} + \frac {1}{2} \vec {a}
$$
$$
\overrightarrow {C E} = \frac {1}{2} (\overrightarrow {C A} + \overrightarrow {C D}) = \frac {1}{2} \vec {c} + \frac {1}{2} \vec {b}
$$
$$
A F, C E
$$
$$
\cos \theta = \left| \frac {\overrightarrow {A F} \cdot \overrightarrow {C E}}{\left| \overrightarrow {A F} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow {C E} \right|} \right| = \left| \frac {- \frac {1}{2} \vec {c} ^ {- 2} - \frac {1}{2} \vec {b} \cdot \vec {c} + \frac {1}{4} \vec {a} \cdot \vec {c} + \frac {1}{4} \vec {a} \cdot \vec {b}}{\left| - \vec {c} + \frac {1}{2} \vec {a} \right| \cdot \left| \frac {1}{2} \vec {c} + \frac {1}{2} \vec {b} \right|} \right| = \left| \frac {- 2}{\sqrt {3} \cdot \sqrt {3}} \right| = \frac {2}{3}
$$
