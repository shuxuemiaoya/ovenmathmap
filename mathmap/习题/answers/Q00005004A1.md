> $\left[-\frac{3}{4},-\frac{23}{64}\right]$ 解析: 取 AC 的中点 O, 以 O 为原点, 直线 AC 为 x 轴, 建立如图所示的平面直角坐标系,
![](../../../../../images/answers/d006f5bc6c916bd40a4a30a1a4dc8cb4246880138da1f66093306b3031962ceb.jpg)
则： $A\left(-\frac{1}{2},0\right),B\left(0,\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),N\left(-\frac{1}{4},\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)$ ，设 $M(x,0), - \frac{1}{2}\leq x\leq \frac{1}{2}$ $\therefore \overline{BM} = \left(x, - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right),\overline{MN} = \left(-\frac{1}{4} -x,\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right),$ $\therefore \overrightarrow{BM}\cdot \overrightarrow{MN} = -x^2 -\frac{1}{4} x - \frac{3}{8} = -\left(x + \frac{1}{8}\right)^2 -\frac{23}{64}$ ，且 $-\frac{1}{2}\leq x\leq \frac{1}{2}$ $\therefore x = \frac{1}{2}$ 时， $\overrightarrow{BM}\cdot \overrightarrow{MN}$ 取最小值 $-\frac{3}{4};x = -\frac{1}{8}$ 时， $\overrightarrow{BM}\cdot \overrightarrow{MN}$ 取最大值 $-\frac{23}{64},\therefore \overrightarrow{BM}\cdot \overrightarrow{MN}$ 的取值范围是 $\left[-\frac{3}{4}, - \frac{23}{64}\right],$
