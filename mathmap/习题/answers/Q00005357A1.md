> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 在四面体中, 因为 $\angle ABD = \angle BDC = 90^{\circ}, AC = 2BD$ ,
设 AC = 2, BD = 1，且 $\overrightarrow{AB} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{BD} \cdot \overrightarrow{DC} = 0$ ， $\overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BD} + \overrightarrow{DC}$ ，
则 $\overrightarrow{AC} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = (\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BD} + \overrightarrow{DC}) \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = |\overrightarrow{BD}|^{2}$ , $\overrightarrow{BD}$ 在 $\overrightarrow{AC}$ 上的投影向量为 $\frac{\overrightarrow{BD} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC}}{|\overrightarrow{AC}|} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{AC}}{|\overrightarrow{AC}|} = \frac{|\overrightarrow{BD}|^{2}}{|\overrightarrow{AC}|^{2}} \cdot \overrightarrow{AC} = \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{AC}$ . 故选:B
