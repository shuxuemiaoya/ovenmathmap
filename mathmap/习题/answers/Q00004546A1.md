> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{7}{8}$
解析: 因为 $\sin\left(x+\frac{\pi}{12}\right)=-\frac{1}{4}$ ，所以
$$
\cos \left(\frac {5 \pi}{6} - 2 x\right) = \cos \left(\pi - \frac {\pi}{6} - 2 x\right) = - \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{6} + 2 x\right) = - \left[ 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \left(x + \frac {\pi}{1 2}\right) \right]
$$
$$
= - \left[ 1 - 2 \left(- \frac {1}{4}\right) ^ {2} \right] = - \frac {7}{8}
$$
