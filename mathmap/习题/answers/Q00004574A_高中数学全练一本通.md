> **【解析】**
> \( \left[2\sqrt{2}-2,1\right) \)
解析： \( \frac{1}{\tan A}+\frac{1}{\tan B}=\frac{\cos A}{\sin A}+\frac{\cos B}{\sin B}=\frac{\sin B\cos A+\sin A\cos B}{\sin A\sin B} \)
\( =\frac{\sin C}{\sin A\sin B}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2\sin A\sin B} \)
其  \( 2\sin A\sin B=2\sin A\sin\left(\frac{3\pi}{4}-A\right)=2\sin A\left(\sin\frac{3\pi}{4}\cos A-\cos\frac{3\pi}{4}\sin A\right) \)
\( =\sqrt{2}\sin A\cos A+\sqrt{2}\sin^{2}A=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\sin 2A-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cos 2A+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}=\sin\left(2A-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \)
所以  \( \frac{1}{\tan A}+\frac{1}{\tan B}=\frac{2}{\sqrt{2}\sin\left(2A-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+1} \)
因为 △ABC 为锐角三角形，故  \( A\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right) \) ， \( B=\frac{3\pi}{4}-A\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right) \) ，
解得  \( A\in\left(\frac{\pi}{4},\frac{\pi}{2}\right) \) ，所以  \( 2A - \frac{\pi}{4} \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right) \) ，
所以  \( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}<\sin\left(2A-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\leqslant1, 2<\sqrt{2}\sin\left(2A-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)+1\leqslant\sqrt{2}+1 \) ，
从而  \( 2\sqrt{2}-2\leqslant\frac{1}{\tan A}+\frac{1}{\tan B}<1 \)  故答案为： \( \left[2\sqrt{2}-2,1\right) \)
