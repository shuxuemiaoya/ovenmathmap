> **【解析】**
> 由椭圆定义可得 $\left|PF_{1}\right| + \left|PF_{2}\right| = 2a = 8, \left|F_{1}F_{2}\right| = 2c = 2\sqrt{16-9} = 2\sqrt{7}.$ 又因为 $\angle F_{1}PF_{2}=60^{\circ}$ ，所以由余弦定理可得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|^{2}+\left|PF_{2}\right|^{2}-2\left|PF_{1}\right|\left|PF_{2}\right|\cdot\cos60^{\circ}=\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|^{2}$ ，
即 $\left(\left|PF_{1}\right|+\left|PF_{2}\right|\right)^{2}-3\left|PF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|PF_{2}\right|=\left|F_{1}F_{2}\right|^{2}=28,$ 解得 $\left|PF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|PF_{2}\right|=12,$ 则 $\triangle F_{1}PF_{2}$ 的面积为 $\frac{1}{2}\left|PF_{1}\right|\cdot\left|PF_{2}\right|\sin60^{\circ}=3\sqrt{3}$ .
>
> 故选 **D**.

> **📚 二级结论**
> 已知椭圆 $\frac{x^2}{a^2} +\frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1(a>$ $b > 0)$ 上一点 $M,\angle F_1MF_2 = \theta$ ，则 $S_{\triangle F_1MF_2} = b^2\cdot \tan \frac{\theta}{2} = c\cdot |y_M|$ 证明：由椭圆定义得 $|MF_1|+$ $|MF_2| = 2a$ ，左、右两边平方得 $|MF_1|^2 +|MF_2|^2 +$ $2|MF_1|\cdot |MF_2| = 4a^2$ ，①由余弦定理得 $|MF_1|^2 +|MF_2|^2 -$ $2|MF_1|\cdot |MF_2|\cos \theta = 4c^2$ ，②①-②得 $|MF_1|\cdot |MF_2| = \frac{2b^2}{1 + \cos\theta},$ 故 $S_{\triangle F_1MF_2} = \frac{1}{2}\cdot |MF_1|\cdot |MF_2|\sin \theta =$ $\frac{1}{2}\cdot \frac{2b^2}{1 + \cos\theta}\cdot \sin \theta = b^2\cdot \tan \frac{\theta}{2}.$ 另外 $S_{\triangle F_1MF_2} = \frac{1}{2}\times 2c\cdot |y_M| = c\cdot |y_M|$
