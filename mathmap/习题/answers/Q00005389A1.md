> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 证明见解析
解析：（1）设 $\overrightarrow{VA} = \vec{a}, \overrightarrow{VB} = \vec{b}, \overrightarrow{VC} = \vec{c}$ ，正四面体的棱长为 1，
因为 $\overrightarrow{VD} = \overrightarrow{VB} + \overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{VB} + \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{1}{2} (\overrightarrow{BA} + \overrightarrow{BC}) = \overrightarrow{VB} + \frac{1}{3} (\overrightarrow{VA} - \overrightarrow{VB} + \overrightarrow{VC} - \overrightarrow{VB})$
$= \frac{1}{3}\left(\overrightarrow{VA} +\overrightarrow{VB} +\overrightarrow{VC}\right) = \frac{1}{3}\left(\vec{a} +\vec{b} +\vec{c}\right),$ $\overrightarrow{AO} = \overrightarrow{VO} -\overrightarrow{VA} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{VD} -\overrightarrow{VA} = \frac{1}{6}\left(\vec{a} +\vec{b} +\vec{c}\right) - \vec{a} = \frac{1}{6}\left(\vec{b} +\vec{c} -\vec{5a}\right),$ $\overrightarrow{BO} = \overrightarrow{VO} -\overrightarrow{VB} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{VD} -\overrightarrow{VB} = \frac{1}{6}\left(\vec{a} +\vec{b} +\vec{c}\right) - \vec{b} = \frac{1}{6}\left(\vec{a} +\vec{c} -\vec{5b}\right),$ $\overrightarrow{CO} = \overrightarrow{VO} -\overrightarrow{VC} = \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{VD} -\overrightarrow{VC} = \frac{1}{6}\left(\vec{a} +\vec{b} +\vec{c}\right) - \vec{c} = \frac{1}{6}\left(\vec{a} +\vec{b} -\vec{x}\right),$ 所以 $\overrightarrow{AO}\cdot \overrightarrow{BO} = \frac{1}{36}\left(\vec{b} +\vec{c} -5\vec{a}\right)\cdot \left(\vec{a} +\vec{c} -5\vec{b}\right) = \frac{1}{36}\left(18\vec{a}\cdot \vec{b} -9|\vec{a}|^2\right)$ $= \frac{1}{36}\left(18\times 1\times 1\times \cos \frac{\pi}{3} -9\right) = 0$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{AO}\perp \overrightarrow{BO}$ ，即 $AO\bot BO$ 同理， $AO\bot CO$ ， $BO\bot CO$ ，所以 $AO$ ， $BO$ ，CO两两垂直.
