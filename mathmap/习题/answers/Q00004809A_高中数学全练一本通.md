> 解析:如图, $\overrightarrow{CD} = -\frac{1}{2}\vec{a}$ , $\overrightarrow{BC} = \overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AB} = \vec{b} - \vec{a}$ ,
所以 $\overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{BC} +\overrightarrow{CD} = \vec{b} -\vec{a} -\frac{1}{2}\vec{a} = \vec{b} -\frac{3}{2}\vec{a}$ .故选：A.
