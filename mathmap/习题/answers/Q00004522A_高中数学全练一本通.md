> **【解析】**
> 解析: 因为 $\alpha\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ， $\sin2\alpha=\cos^{2}\alpha$ ，所以 $\cos\alpha\neq0$ 且
$$
2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \cos^ {2} \alpha
$$
解得 $\tan \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$
$$
\cos 2 \alpha = \cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha = \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\cos^ {2} \alpha + \sin^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha}{1 + \tan^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \frac {1}{4}}{1 + \frac {1}{4}} = \frac {3}{5}.
$$
故选:D
