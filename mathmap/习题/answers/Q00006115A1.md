> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{1}{3}$
解析: 过 A 作 AO⊥BD, 交 BD 于 O, 连结 PO,
![](../../../../../images/answers/e0dbb647251234f0454116bbc6bf31b898ea68c88fbda4f0658a2287eb653424.jpg)
$\because$ 矩形ABCD的两边 $\mathrm{AB} = 3$ ， $\mathrm{AD} = 4$ ， $\mathrm{PA}\perp$ 平面ABCD，且 $\mathrm{PA} = \frac{4}{5}$ $\therefore \mathrm{BD} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$ ，PO⊥BD， $\therefore \angle \mathrm{POA}$ 是二面角A-BD-P的平面角， $\because \frac{1}{2}\times \mathrm{BD}\times \mathrm{AO} = \frac{1}{2}\times \mathrm{AB}\times \mathrm{AD},$ $\therefore \mathrm{AO} = \frac{\mathrm{AB}\times\mathrm{AD}}{\mathrm{BD}} = \frac{12}{5},$ $\therefore \tan \angle \mathrm{POA} = \frac{\mathrm{PA}}{\mathrm{AO}} = \frac{\frac{4}{5}}{\frac{12}{5}} = \frac{1}{3}.$
