> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> 解析:如图1,延长CN与 $AA_{1}$ 交于点H,连结MH,与 $A_{1}B_{1}$ 交于点G,
连结 NG,GM，则四边形 CNGM 为所求截面，
其中 $CN=\sqrt{CC_{1}^{2}+C_{1}N^{2}}=\sqrt{3^{2}+3^{2}}=3\sqrt{2}$ $CM=\sqrt{BC^{2}+BM^{2}}=\sqrt{4^{2}+2^{2}}=2\sqrt{5}$
![](../../../../../images/answers/b4e0aa85af260edf2610cf1df5146fb768b2a313463841d34b60d712fbade01f.jpg)
图1
如图2， $\triangle A_{1}HN\sim \triangle C_{1}CN$ ，所以 $\frac{A_1H}{CC_1} = \frac{A_1N}{NC_1} = \frac{1}{3}$ 即 $A_{1}H = \frac{1}{3} CC_{1} = 1$
![](../../../../../images/answers/7cd2db3067f30f7a1a6461cad0fd91ac6af70a4052373e63c7d692bf3baadb27.jpg)
图2
如图1, 若 $A_{1}H = BM = 1$ ，则 $\triangle A_{1}HG \cong \triangle B_{1}MG$ ，所以 $A_{1}G = B_{1}G$
即点 G 是 $A_{1}B_{1}$ 的中点,
所以 $GM = \sqrt{B_1G^2 + B_1M^2} = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}$
$\triangle A_{1}GN$ 中， $\angle A_{1} = 60^{\circ},A_{1}G = 2,A_{1}N = 1$
所以 $NG = \sqrt{A_{1}N^{2} + A_{1}G^{2} - 2A_{1}N \cdot A_{1}G \cdot \cos60^{\circ}} = \sqrt{1 + 4 - 2 \times 1 \times 2 \times \frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{3}$ ，所以四边形 CNGM 的周长为 $3\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{5} + \sqrt{3} = 3\sqrt{2} + 3\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{3}$ 。故选：B
