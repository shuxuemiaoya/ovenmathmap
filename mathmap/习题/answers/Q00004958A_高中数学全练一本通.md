> **【解析】**
> 解析: 因为
$$
k \vec {b} - \vec {a} = (3 k, - k) - (1, 2) = (3 k - 1, - k - 2), 2 \vec {a} + \vec {b} = (2, 4) + (3, - 1) = (5, 3)
$$
且 $(k\vec{b} -\vec{a}) / / (2\vec{a} +\vec{b})$ ，所以 $(3k - 1)\times 3 - 5(-k - 2) = 0$ ，即 $14k = -7$
解得 $k = -\frac{1}{2}$ .故选：B
