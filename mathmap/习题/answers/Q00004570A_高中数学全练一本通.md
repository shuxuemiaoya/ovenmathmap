> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> (0, $\frac{1}{4}$ ]
解析: 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中, 因为 $\angle C = \frac{2\pi}{3}$ , 可得 $A + B = \frac{\pi}{3}$ , 所以 $B = \frac{\pi}{3} - A$ ,
则 $\sin A \cdot \sin B = \sin A \cdot \sin (\frac{\pi}{3} - A) = \sin A (\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos A - \frac{1}{2}\sin A)$ $= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin A\cos A - \frac{1}{2}\sin^2A = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\sin 2A + \frac{1}{4}\cos 2A - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2}\sin (2A + \frac{\pi}{6}) - \frac{1}{4}$ , 因为 $\angle C = \frac{2\pi}{3}$ , 可得 $0 < A < \frac{\pi}{3}$ , 则 $\frac{\pi}{6} < 2A + \frac{\pi}{6} < \frac{5\pi}{6}$ , 所以 $\sin (2A + \frac{\pi}{6}) \in (\frac{1}{2}, 1]$ ,
可得 $\frac{1}{2}\sin (2A + \frac{\pi}{6}) \in (\frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{2}]$ , 所以 $\frac{1}{2}\sin (2A + \frac{\pi}{6}) - \frac{1}{4} \in (0, \frac{1}{4}]$ .
故答案为: (0, $\frac{1}{4}$ ).
