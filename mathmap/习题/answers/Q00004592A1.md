> **【解析】**
> 解析：∵角 $\alpha$ 是第二象限角，且终边经过点 $(-3,4)$ ，
$$
\therefore \sin \alpha = \frac {4}{5}, \cos \alpha = - \frac {3}{5},
$$
$$
\therefore \tan \frac {\alpha}{2} = \frac {\sin \frac {\alpha}{2}}{\cos \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {2 \cdot \sin \frac {\alpha}{2} \cdot \cos \frac {\alpha}{2}}{2 \cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {\sin \alpha}{1 + \cos \alpha} = \frac {\frac {4}{5}}{1 + \left(- \frac {3}{5}\right)} = 2
$$
