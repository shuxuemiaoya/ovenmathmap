> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析：可设菱形的边长为 1，则 $BE = CF = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ， $BD = 1$ ；
线段 $AD, BD$ 的中点分别为 $E, F$ ； $\therefore \overline{BE} = \frac{1}{2} (\overline{BA} + \overline{BD})$ ， $\overline{CF} = \frac{1}{2} (\overline{BD} - 2\overline{BC}) = \frac{1}{2} (\overline{BD} - 2\overline{BC})$ ； $\therefore \overrightarrow{BE} \cdot \overrightarrow{CF} = \frac{1}{4} (\overrightarrow{BA} + \overrightarrow{BD}) \cdot (\overrightarrow{BD} - 2\overrightarrow{BC}) = \frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{BA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} - \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BA} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC} + \frac{1}{4}\overrightarrow{BD}^2 - \frac{1}{2}\overrightarrow{BD} \cdot \overrightarrow{BC}$ $= \frac{1}{8} - \frac{1}{2}\cos <\overrightarrow{BA},\overrightarrow{BC}> + \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{8} - \frac{1}{2}\cos <\overrightarrow{BA},\overrightarrow{BC}>;$ $\therefore \cos <\overrightarrow{BE},\overrightarrow{CF}> = \frac{\overrightarrow{BE} \cdot \overrightarrow{CF}}{\left|\overrightarrow{BE}\right|\left|\overrightarrow{CF}\right|} = \frac{\frac{1}{8} - \frac{1}{2}\cos <\overrightarrow{BA},\overrightarrow{BC}>}{\frac{3}{4}};$ 由图看出 $-\frac{1}{2}<\cos<\overrightarrow{BA},\overrightarrow{BC}><1$ ；
$\therefore -\frac{1}{2} < \cos < \overline{BE}, \overline{CF} > < \frac{1}{2}$ ； $\therefore \frac{\pi}{3} << \overline{BE}, \overline{CF} > < \frac{2\pi}{3}$ ；
又因为异面直线夹角范围是 $\left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ ，即异面直线 $BE$ 与 $CF$ 所成角的取值范围是 $\left(\frac{\pi}{3}, \frac{\pi}{2}\right]$ . 故选：C.
