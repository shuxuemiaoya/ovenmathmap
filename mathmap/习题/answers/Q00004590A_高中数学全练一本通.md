> **【解析】**
> \( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \)
解析：因为  \( 0<\frac{5\pi}{12}<\frac{\pi}{2} \) ，所以  \( \sin\frac{5\pi}{12}>0 \) 、 \( \cos\frac{5\pi}{12}>0 \) ，所以
\( \sin\frac{5\pi}{12}-\cos\frac{5\pi}{12}=\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos\frac{5\pi}{6}}{2}}-\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos\frac{5\pi}{6}}{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{1-\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)}{2}}-\sqrt{\frac{1+\left(-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)}{2}}= \)
\( \sqrt{\frac{2+\sqrt{3}}{4}}-\sqrt{\frac{2-\sqrt{3}}{4}}=\sqrt{\frac{4+2\sqrt{3}}{8}}-\sqrt{\frac{4-2\sqrt{3}}{8}}= \)
\( \sqrt{\frac{\left(\sqrt{3}+1\right)^{2}}{8}}-\sqrt{\frac{\left(\sqrt{3}-1\right)^{2}}{8}}=\frac{\sqrt{3}+1}{2\sqrt{2}}-\frac{\sqrt{3}-1}{2\sqrt{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \)
