> **【解析】**
> $\cos (\arcsin x) = \sqrt{1 - x^2}$ ， $\tan (\arcsin x) = \frac{x}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}$
$$
t = \arcsin x
$$
$$
x = \sin t
$$
$\cos t = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 t} = \sqrt{1 - x^2}$ 即 $\cos (\arcsin x) = \sqrt{1 - x^2}$
$\tan t=\frac{\sin t}{\cos t}=\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}$ ，即 $\tan(\arcsin x)=\frac{x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}$
