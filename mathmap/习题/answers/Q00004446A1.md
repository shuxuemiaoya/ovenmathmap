> **【解析】**
> $\frac{7\sqrt{5}}{27}$ 解析: $\alpha \in (\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi)$ , $\beta \in (0, \frac{\pi}{2})$ , $\frac{\alpha}{2} \in (\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2}), \frac{\beta}{2} \in (0, \frac{\pi}{4})$ , $-\beta \in (-\frac{\pi}{2}, 0), -\frac{\beta}{2} \in (-\frac{\pi}{4}, 0)$ , $\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2} \in (\frac{\pi}{4}, \pi), \frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta \in (-\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2})$ , $\cos (\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2}) = -\frac{1}{9}$ , $\sin (\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta) = \frac{2}{3}$ ,
所以 $\sin (\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2}) = \sqrt{1 - \cos^2 (\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2})} = \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{9}$ , $\cos (\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta) = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 (\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta)} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{3}$ ,
所以 $\cos \frac{\alpha + \beta}{2} = \cos [(\alpha - \frac{\beta}{2}) - (\frac{\alpha}{2} - \beta)]$ $= -\frac{1}{9} \times \frac{\sqrt{5}}{3} + \frac{4\sqrt{5}}{9} \times \frac{2}{3} = \frac{7\sqrt{5}}{27}$ .
