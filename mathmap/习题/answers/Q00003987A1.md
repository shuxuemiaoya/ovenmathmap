> 【解】(1)设双曲线的半焦距长为c,由题意可知,C的一条渐近线方程为bx-ay=0,右焦点为(c,0),∴右焦点到渐近线的距离 $d=\frac{|bc|}{\sqrt{a^{2}+b^{2}}}$ 1,解得b=1,由离心率 $e=\frac{c}{a}=\sqrt{2}$ ,又 $a^{2}+b^{2}=c^{2}$ ,解得a=1,∴双曲线C的方程为 $x^{2}-y^{2}=1$ .
(2)设直线l的方程为 $x=ty+3(t\neq\pm1)$ , $P(x_{1},y_{1})$ , $Q(x_{2},y_{2})$ , $x_{1}>0$ , $x_{2}>0$ ,联立 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=ty+3,\\ x^{2}-y^{2}&=1\end{aligned}\right.\Rightarrow(t^{2}-1)y^{2}+6ty+8=0$ , $\Delta=4t^{2}+32>0$ 恒成立, $y_{1}+y_{2}=\frac{-6t}{t^{2}-1}$ , $y_{1}y_{2}=\frac{8}{t^{2}-1}$ ,
∵直线l过点(3,0)且与双曲线C的右支交于两点, $\therefore y_{1}y_{2}=\frac{8}{t^{2}-1}<0$ ,解得-1<t<1. $\frac{S_{1}+S_{2}}{S_{2}}=\frac{S_{\triangle APQ}}{S_{\triangle AMN}}=\frac{\frac{1}{2}\cdot|AP|\cdot|AQ|\cdot\sin\angle PAQ}{\frac{1}{2}\cdot|AM|\cdot|AN|\cdot\sin\angle MAN}=(x_{1}+\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\cdots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,\ldots,$
1) $(x_{2}+1)=(ty_{1}+4)(ty_{2}+4)=t^{2}y_{1}y_{2}+$ $\rightarrow$ 点悟: $\frac{|AP|\cdot|AQ|}{|AM|\cdot|AN|}=$ $\frac{\sqrt{1+k_{AP}^{2}}|x_{1}+1|\cdot\sqrt{1+k_{AQ}^{2}}|x_{2}+1|}{\sqrt{1+k_{AP}^{2}}|0+1|\cdot\sqrt{1+k_{AQ}^{2}}|0+1|}=(x_{1}+1)$ 1) $(x_{2}+1)$ $4t(y_{1}+y_{2})+16=\frac{8t^{2}}{t^{2}-1}-\frac{24t^{2}}{t^{2}-1}+16=$ $\frac{16}{1-t^{2}}\in[16,+\infty),\therefore\frac{S_{1}}{S_{2}}\in[15,+\infty).$
