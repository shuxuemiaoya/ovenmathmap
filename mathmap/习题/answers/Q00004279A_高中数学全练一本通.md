> $-\frac{m}{\sqrt{1 - m^2}}$
解析： $\because \cos 140^{\circ} = \cos (180^{\circ} - 40^{\circ}) = -\cos 40^{\circ} = m$
$$
\therefore \cos 4 0 ^ {\circ} = - m
$$
$$
\therefore \sin 5 0 ^ {\circ} = \sin (9 0 ^ {\circ} - 4 0 ^ {\circ}) = \cos 4 0 ^ {\circ} = - m
$$
$$
\therefore \cos 5 0 ^ {\circ} = \sqrt {1 - m ^ {2}}
$$
$$
\therefore \tan 5 0 ^ {\circ} = \frac {\sin 5 0 ^ {\circ}}{\cos 5 0 ^ {\circ}} = - \frac {m}{\sqrt {1 - m ^ {2}}}
$$
