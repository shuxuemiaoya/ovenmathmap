解析：（1） $\cos 57^{\circ}\cos 3^{\circ} - \sin 57^{\circ}\sin 3^{\circ} = \cos (57^{\circ} + 3^{\circ}) = \cos 60^{\circ} = \frac{1}{2}$
(2) $\sin 12^{\circ} \sin 18^{\circ} - \cos 12^{\circ} \cos 18^{\circ} = -\cos (12^{\circ} + 18^{\circ}) = -\cos 30^{\circ} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(3) $\sin 20^{\circ}\cos 40^{\circ} + \cos 20^{\circ}\sin 40^{\circ} = \sin (20^{\circ} + 40) = \sin 60^{\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(4) $\frac{\sin(\alpha - 150^\circ) + \cos(\alpha - 120^\circ)}{\cos\alpha}$ $= \frac{\sin\alpha \cos 150^\circ - \cos\alpha \sin 150^\circ + \cos\alpha \cos 120^\circ + \sin\alpha \sin 120^\circ}{\cos\alpha}$ $= \frac{-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \sin\alpha - \frac{1}{2} \cos\alpha - \frac{1}{2} \cos\alpha + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha}$ $= \frac{-\cos\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = -1.$
（5）原式 $= \tan (74^{\circ} + 76^{\circ}) = \tan 150^{\circ} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}$
(6) $\frac{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{6} + \alpha\right) - \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{6} - \alpha\right)}{\sin\alpha}$
$$
\frac {\sin \frac {\pi}{6} \cos \alpha + \cos \frac {\pi}{6} \sin \alpha - \left(\sin \frac {\pi}{6} \cos \alpha - \cos \frac {\pi}{6} \sin \alpha\right)}{}
$$
$$
\frac {\sin \left(\frac {\pi}{6} + \alpha\right) - \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{6} - \alpha\right)}{\sin \alpha} = = \frac {\sqrt {3} \sin \alpha}{\sin \alpha} = \sqrt {3},
$$
(7) $\frac{1 + \tan 15^{\circ}}{1 - \tan 15^{\circ}} = \frac{\tan 45^{\circ} + \tan 15^{\circ}}{1 - \tan 45^{\circ} \tan 15^{\circ}} = \tan (45^{\circ} + 15^{\circ}) = \tan 60^{\circ} = \sqrt{3}$ ;
$$
\frac {1 + \tan 1 5 ^ {\circ}}{1 - \tan 1 5 ^ {\circ}} = \sqrt {3}
$$
