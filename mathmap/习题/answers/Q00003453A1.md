> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** B
>
> **【解析】**
> ∵二面角 $\alpha-l-\beta$ 的大小为 $\frac{\pi}{3}, AC \perp l, BD \perp l, \therefore \langle \overrightarrow{AC}, \overrightarrow{BD} \rangle = \frac{\pi}{3}, \langle \overrightarrow{CA}, \overrightarrow{BD} \rangle = \frac{2\pi}{3}.$ 由题意得 $\overrightarrow{CD}=\overrightarrow{CA}+\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{BD}$ ，所以 $\overrightarrow{CD^{2}}=(\overrightarrow{CA}+\overrightarrow{AB}+\overrightarrow{BD})^{2}=\overrightarrow{CA}^{2}+\overrightarrow{AB}^{2}+\overrightarrow{BD}^{2}+2\overrightarrow{CA}\cdot\overrightarrow{AB}+2\overrightarrow{CA}\cdot\overrightarrow{BD}+2\overrightarrow{AB}\cdot\overrightarrow{BD},\therefore49=9+|\overrightarrow{AB}|^{2}+16+0+2\times3\times4\times\cos\frac{2\pi}{3}+0,\therefore|\overrightarrow{AB}|=6,$ 故线段 AB 的长为 6.
>
> 故选 **B**.
