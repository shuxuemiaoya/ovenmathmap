> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> \( (-∞,1/3)∪(1/3,4/7) \)
详解: 向量  \( λ\underline{\bar{a}}+\bar{\bar{b}} \)  与向量  \( \bar{\bar{a}}-3\bar{\bar{b}} \)  所成角为钝角
则  \( (\lambda\bar{\bar{a}}+\bar{\bar{b}})\cdot(\bar{\bar{a}}-3\bar{\bar{b}})<0 \)  且  \( λ\underline{\bar{a}}+\bar{\bar{b}} \)  与  \( \bar{\bar{a}}-3\underline{\bar{b}} \)  不共线
\( (\lambda\underline{\bar{a}}+\bar{\bar{b}})\cdot(\bar{\bar{a}}-3\underline{\bar{b}})=λ\underline{\bar{a}}^{2}+(1-3λ)\bar{\bar{a}}\cdot\underline{\bar{b}}-3\underline{\bar{b}}^{2}=4λ-(1-3λ)-3<0 \)
得  \( \lambda<-\frac {4}{7} \) ，又  \( \lambda/\frac {1}{7}\neq-\frac {1}{7},\lambda\notimes-\frac {1}{7} \)
故 λ 的取值范围是  \( (-∞,1/3)∪(1/3,4/7) \)
故答案为： \( (-∞, \frac{1}{3}) ∪ (\frac{1}{3}, \frac{4}{7}) \)
