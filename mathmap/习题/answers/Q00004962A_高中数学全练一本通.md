> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> （1） $M\left(-\frac{1}{2}, - 1\right)$ ；（2） $\frac{3}{7}$
解析：（1）设 $B(x_{1},y_{1})$ ， $\because \overrightarrow{AB} = (4,3)$ ， $A(-1, - 2)$
$$
\therefore \left(x _ {1} + 1, y _ {1} + 2\right) = (4, 3)
$$
$$
\therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x _ {1} + 1 = 4 \\ y _ {1} + 2 = 3 \end{array} , \right. \therefore \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x _ {1} = 3 \\ y _ {1} = 1 \end{array} , \right.
$$
$\therefore B(3,1)$ ，同理可得 $D(-4,-3)$ ，
设 BD 的中点 $M(x_{2}, y_{2})$ ,
则 $x_{2} = \frac{3 - 4}{2} = -\frac{1}{2}, y_{2} = \frac{1 - 3}{2} = -1$
$\therefore M\left(-\frac{1}{2}, - 1\right)$
$$
\overrightarrow {P B} = (3, 1) - (2, y) = (1, 1 - y), \overrightarrow {B D} = (- 4, - 3) - (3, 1) = (- 7, - 4), \tag {2}
$$
∵P,B,D三点共线，∴ $\overrightarrow{PB}//\overrightarrow{BD}$
$\therefore-4+7\times(1-y)=0$ ，解得 $y=\frac{3}{7}$ .
