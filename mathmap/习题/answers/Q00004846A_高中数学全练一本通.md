> **【解析】**
> 解析: 因为 \(2\overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{DC}\), \(\overrightarrow{AB} = \overrightarrow{c}\), \(\overrightarrow{AC} = \overrightarrow{b}\),
所以 \(\overrightarrow{BD} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{BC} = \frac{1}{3} (\overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AB}) = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{b} - \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{c}\),
所以 \(\overrightarrow{AD} = \overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{c} + \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{b} - \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{c} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{b} + \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{c}\), 故选: D
