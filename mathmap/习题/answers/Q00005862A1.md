> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\frac{\pi a^{2}}{6}$
![](../../../../../../images/answers/fc754dc71dda31ee60e7905dcb639d2faf21cbd85a156c73c9bf7a294fcae03a.jpg)
连接 $OA$ 、 $OB$ 、 $OC$ 、 $OD$ ，将四棱锥分成四个小棱锥，正四面体的四个面面积相等，
易知小棱锥的高是内切球的半径 $r$ ，
由 $V_{A - BCD} = V_{O - ABC} + V_{O - ABD} + V_{O - ACD} + V_{O - BCD} = 4V_{O - ABC}$ 由于 $BE = \frac{2}{3} BF = \frac{2}{3}\times \frac{\sqrt{3}a}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}a}{3}$ ，所以 $AE = \sqrt{AB^2 - BE^2} = \sqrt{a^2 - \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}a}{3}\right)^2} = \frac{\sqrt{6}a}{3}$ 得 $\frac{1}{3}\times AE\times S_{\triangle BCD} = 4\times \frac{1}{3}\times r\times S_{\triangle ABC}\Rightarrow 4r = AE = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} a$ $\Rightarrow r = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{12} a\Rightarrow S = 4\pi r^2 = \frac{\pi a^2}{6}$ ，故答案为： $\frac{\pi a^2}{6}$
