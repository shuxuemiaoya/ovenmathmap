> **【解析】**
> $\frac{3}{5}$
解析： $\sin \left(2\alpha -\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \left[2\left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right) + \frac{\pi}{2}\right] = \cos 2\left(\alpha -\frac{\pi}{3}\right)$
$$
= 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \left(\alpha - \frac {\pi}{3}\right) = 1 - 2 \times \left(\frac {\sqrt {5}}{5}\right) ^ {2} = \frac {3}{5},
$$
