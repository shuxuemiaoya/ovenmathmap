> **【解析】**
> $\frac{4 - 3\sqrt{3}}{10}$ 解析： $\because \tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4} +\alpha\right) = \frac{1 + \tan\alpha}{1 - \tan\alpha} = -\frac{1}{3}\Rightarrow \tan \alpha = -2$ $\therefore \sin 2\alpha = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha} = -\frac{4}{5},\cos 2\alpha = \frac{1 - \tan^2\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha} = -\frac{3}{5}$ $\therefore \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{3} -2\alpha\right) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\cos 2\alpha -\frac{1}{2}\sin 2\alpha = \frac{4 - 3\sqrt{3}}{10}$
