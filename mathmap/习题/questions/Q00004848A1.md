> **【答案】** A
>
> **【解析】**
> 解析: 因为 \(D, E\) 分别是 \(AB, AC\) 的中点, 所以 \(F\) 是 \(\triangle ABC\) 的重心, 则 \(\overrightarrow{DF} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{DC}\),
所以 \(\overrightarrow{AF} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{DF} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{DC} = \overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{1}{3}\left(\overrightarrow{AC} - \overrightarrow{AD}\right)\)
\(= \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AD} + \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AC} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AB} + \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AC}\),
又因为 \(\overrightarrow{AF} = \lambda\overrightarrow{AB} + \mu\overrightarrow{AC}\), 且 \(\overrightarrow{AB}\) 与 \(\overrightarrow{AC}\) 不共线,
所以 \(\lambda = \frac{1}{3}, \mu = \frac{1}{3}\), 则 \(\lambda + \mu = \frac{2}{3}\), 故选: A.
