> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{3}{10}$
解析： $\sin (\frac{\pi}{2} -\alpha)\cdot \cos (\frac{\pi}{2} +\alpha) = -\frac{\sin 2\alpha}{2}$
$\because \sin 2\alpha = \frac{2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\sin^2\alpha + \cos^2\alpha} = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 + \tan^2\alpha} = \frac{3}{5},$
$\therefore \sin (\frac{\pi}{2} -\alpha)\cdot \cos (\frac{\pi}{2} +\alpha) = -\frac{3}{10}$
