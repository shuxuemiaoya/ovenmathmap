> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> $\alpha -\beta = 90^{\circ} + k\cdot 360^{\circ}$ ， $k\in \mathbb{Z}$
解析: 因为角 $\alpha$ 与 $x + 45^{\circ}$ 具有相同的终边,
所以 $\alpha = x + 45^{\circ} + k_{1}\cdot 360^{\circ},k_{1}\in Z$
又角 $\beta$ 与 $x-45^{\circ}$ 具有相同的终边,
所以 $\beta = x - 45^{\circ} + k_{2}\cdot 360^{\circ},k_{2}\in \mathbb{Z}$
所以 $\alpha - \beta = 90^{\circ} + (k_{1} - k_{2}) \cdot 360^{\circ}$ ， $k_{1}, k_{2} \in Z$ ，
令 $k = k_{1} - k_{2}$ ， $k\in \mathbb{Z}$
则 $\alpha -\beta = 90^{\circ} + k\cdot 360^{\circ}$ ， $k\in \mathbb{Z}$
故答案为: $\alpha - \beta = 90^{\circ} + k \cdot 360^{\circ}$ , $k \in Z$ .
$$
\left[ 1 0 \right] (1) \left\{\beta | \beta = - 4 5 ^ {\circ} + n \cdot 1 8 0 ^ {\circ}, n \in \mathbf {Z} \right\}; (2) \left\{\beta | \beta = 4 5 ^ {\circ} + n \cdot 9 0 ^ {\circ}, n \in \mathbf {Z} \right\}
$$
解析：（1）由题图易知，在 $0^{\circ} \sim 360^{\circ}$ 范围内，终边在直线 $y = -x$ 上的角有两个，即 $135^{\circ}$ 和 $315^{\circ}$ ，
因此，终边在直线 $y = -x$ 上的角的集合为 $S = \{\beta | \beta = 135^\circ + k \cdot 360^\circ, k \in \mathbf{Z}\} \cup \{\beta | \beta = 315^\circ + k \cdot 360^\circ, k \in \mathbf{Z}\}$ $= \{\beta | \beta = -45^\circ + (2k + 1) \cdot 180^\circ, k \in \mathbf{Z}\} \cup \{\beta | \beta = -45^\circ + 2(k + 1) \cdot 180^\circ, k \in \mathbf{Z}\}$ $= \{\beta | \beta = -45^\circ + n \cdot 180^\circ, n \in \mathbf{Z}\}$ ；
（2）同理可得终边在直线 y = x 上的角的集合为
$$
\left\{\beta \mid \beta = 4 5 ^ {\circ} + k \cdot 1 8 0 ^ {\circ}, k \in \mathbf {Z} \right\} = \left\{\beta \mid \beta = 4 5 ^ {\circ} + 2 k \cdot 9 0 ^ {\circ}, k \in \mathbf {Z} \right\}
$$
$$
S = \left\{\beta \mid \beta = - 4 5 ^ {\circ} + n \cdot 1 8 0 ^ {\circ}, n \in \mathbf {Z} \right\}
$$
$$
= \left\{\beta \mid \beta = - 4 5 ^ {\circ} + 2 k \cdot 9 0 ^ {\circ}, k \in \mathbf {Z} \right\} = \left\{\beta \mid \beta = 4 5 ^ {\circ} + (2 k - 1) \cdot 9 0 ^ {\circ}, k \in \mathbf {Z} \right\}
$$
所以终边在直线 $y = x$ 上和在直线 $y = -x$ 上的角的集合为
$$
S = \left\{\beta \mid \beta = 4 5 ^ {\circ} + 2 k \cdot 9 0 ^ {\circ}, k \in \mathbf {Z} \right\} \cup \left\{\beta \mid \beta = 4 5 ^ {\circ} + (2 k - 1) \cdot 9 0 ^ {\circ}, k \in \mathbf {Z} \right\}
$$
$$
= \left\{\beta \mid \beta = 4 5 ^ {\circ} + n \cdot 9 0 ^ {\circ}, n \in \mathbf {Z} \right\}
$$
解析: 令 k=0 , 得 $45^{\circ} \leq \alpha \leq 90^{\circ}$ ，则 B 选项中的阴影部分区域符合题意.
故选:B.
