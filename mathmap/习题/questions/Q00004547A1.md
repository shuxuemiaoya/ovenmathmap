> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{1}{3}$
解析： $\sin \left(\frac{\pi}{6} -2\alpha\right) = \sin \left[\frac{\pi}{2} -2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\right] = \cos 2\left(\alpha +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)$
$$
= 1 - 2 \sin^ {2} \left(\alpha + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = 1 - 2 \times \left(\frac {\sqrt {6}}{3}\right) ^ {2} = - \frac {1}{3}
$$
