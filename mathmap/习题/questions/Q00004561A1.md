> **【解析】**
> $B = \frac{2\pi}{3}$
解析：∵ $\sqrt{3}\sin B-2\cos^{2}\frac{A+C}{2}=0$
可得 $\sqrt{3}\sin B - \left[\cos (A + C) + 1\right] = 0$
即 $\sqrt{3}\sin B - (-\cos B + 1) = 0$
$$
a \sin x + b \cos x = \sqrt {a ^ {2} + b ^ {2}} \sin (x + \varphi)
$$
$$
(\tan \varphi = \frac {b}{a})
$$
$$
\because \sqrt {3} \sin B + \cos B = 1
$$
∴ $2\sin\left(B+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=1$ ，由于 $0<B<\pi$ 。解得 $B=\frac{2\pi}{3}$ 。
