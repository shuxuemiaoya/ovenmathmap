> **【解析】**
> 解析: 由 \(\overrightarrow{AD} = \frac{1}{2} (\overrightarrow{AB} + \overrightarrow{AC})\), 得 \(D\) 为 \(BC\) 的中点, 由 \(\overrightarrow{AE} = \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{AD}\), 得 \(\overrightarrow{ED} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AD}\), 所以 \(\overrightarrow{DC} = \overrightarrow{BD} = \overrightarrow{ED} + \overrightarrow{BE} = \frac{2}{3}\overrightarrow{AD} + \overrightarrow{BE}\), 故选: B.
