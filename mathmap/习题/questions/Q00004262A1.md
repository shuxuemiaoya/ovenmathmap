> **【解析】**
> $\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$
解析: 因为 $\tan\theta + \frac{1}{\tan\theta} = 6$ ,
所以 $\tan\theta>0$ , 又 $\theta\in(0,\pi)$
所以 $\theta\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ,
$$
\tan \theta + \frac {1}{\tan \theta} = 6 \Rightarrow \frac {\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} + \frac {1}{\frac {\sin \theta}{\cos \theta}} = 6 \Rightarrow \frac {\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} + \frac {\cos \theta}{\sin \theta} = 6 \Rightarrow \frac {\sin^ {2} \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta}{\sin \theta \cos \theta} = 6
$$
$$
\Rightarrow 6 \sin \theta \cos \theta = 1 \Rightarrow 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac {1}{3} \Rightarrow 1 + 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac {4}{3}
$$
$$
\Rightarrow \sin^ {2} \theta + \cos^ {2} \theta + 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac {4}{3} \Rightarrow (\sin \theta + \cos \theta) ^ {2} = \frac {4}{3}
$$
因为 $\theta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以由 $\left(\sin \theta + \cos \theta\right)^2 = \frac{4}{3} \Rightarrow \sin \theta + \cos \theta = \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ ，
故答案为： $\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$
