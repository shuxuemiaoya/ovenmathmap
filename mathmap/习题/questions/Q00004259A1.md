> **【解析】**
> $\frac{35}{12}$
解析: 因为 $\sin\alpha + \cos\alpha = -\frac{1}{5}$ ,
所以 $\left(\sin \alpha +\cos \alpha\right)^2 = \frac{1}{25}$ ，所以 $\sin^2\alpha +\cos^2\alpha +2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha = \frac{1}{25},$
所以 $\sin\alpha\cos\alpha=-\frac{12}{25}$ ,
因为 $\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha < \pi$ ，所以 $\sin \alpha > 0, \cos \alpha < 0$ ，
所以 $\sin \alpha -\cos \alpha = \sqrt{\left(\sin\alpha - \cos\alpha\right)^2} = \sqrt{1 - 2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha} = \sqrt{1 + \frac{24}{25}} = \frac{7}{5},$
$\frac{1}{\sin\alpha} - \frac{1}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{\cos\alpha - \sin\alpha}{\sin\alpha\cos\alpha} = \frac{-\frac{7}{5}}{-\frac{12}{25}} = \frac{35}{12}$ 故答案为： $\frac{35}{12}$
