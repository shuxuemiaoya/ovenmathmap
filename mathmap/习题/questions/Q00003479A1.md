> [!faq]- 必刷题解析
> **【答案】** C
>
> **【解析】**
> 因为 $a, b, c$ 共面，所以可设 $c = ma + nb (m, n \in \mathbb{R})$ 。又 $a = (1, 1, 0), b = (-1, 0, 2), c = (x, 2, 5)$ ，则 $(x, 2, 5) = (m - n, m, 2n)$ ，则 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l} x = m - n, \\ 2 = m, \\ 5 = 2n, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $x = -\frac{1}{2}$ ，
>
> 故选 **C**.
