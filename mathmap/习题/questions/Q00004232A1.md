> [!faq]- 全练一本通解析
> **【解析】**
> (1) $x\in \left[2k\pi +\frac{\pi}{3},2k\pi +\frac{2\pi}{3}\right](k\in \mathbf{Z})$ (2） $x\in \left(2k\pi +\frac{\pi}{4},2k\pi +\frac{3\pi}{4}\right]\cup \left[2k\pi +\frac{5\pi}{4},2\hbar \pi +\frac{7\pi}{4}\right)(k\in \mathbf{Z}).$
解析：（1）∵ $2\sin x - \sqrt{3} \geq 0$ , ∴ $\sin x \geq \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ ，在单位圆中作出满足该不等式的角的集合，如图所示，可得 $x \in \left[2k\pi + \frac{\pi}{3}, 2k\pi + \frac{2\pi}{3}\right] (k \in \mathbf{Z})$ .
![](../../../../images/answers/656d135552ffaf6ff76bb5e7922f38c43b2f8dd087bdfbab35e3c685d0d622c5.jpg)
(2) $\because \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 1 - \sqrt{2} \cos x > 0 \\ 1 + \sqrt{2} \cos x \geq 0 \end{array} \right. \therefore -\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \leq \cos x < \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ , 在单位圆中作出满足该不等式的角的集合, 如图所示, 可得 $x \in \left(2k\pi + \frac{\pi}{4}, 2k\pi + \frac{3\pi}{4} \right] \cup \left[2k\pi + \frac{5\pi}{4}, 2k\pi + \frac{7\pi}{4}\right) (k \in \mathbf{Z})$ .
