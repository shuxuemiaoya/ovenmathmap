> **【解析】**
> （1）\(\tan \alpha = -\frac{1}{2}\)；（2）\(-\frac{3}{2}\).
解析：（1）由\(2 \sin \alpha + \cos \alpha = 0\)，可知\(\cos \alpha \neq 0\)，所以\(\frac{2 \sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} + \frac{\cos \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = 0\)，得\(2 \tan \alpha + 1 = 0\)，解得\(\tan \alpha = -\frac{1}{2}\),
（2）因为\(\cos \alpha \neq 0\)，所以\(\frac{\sin^2 \alpha + 2 \cos^2 \alpha}{3 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha} = \frac{\tan^2 \alpha + 2}{3 \tan \alpha} = \frac{\frac{1}{4} + 2}{-\frac{3}{2}} = -\frac{3}{2}\)
