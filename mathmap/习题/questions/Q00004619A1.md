> **【解析】**
> 直角三角形
解析：因为  \( \cos2A - \cos2B = -2\sin(A + B)\sin(A - B) = 2\sin^{2}C \) ,
所以  \( \sin(A - B) + \sin(A + B) = 0 \) ,
所以  \( \sin(A - B) + \sin(A + B) = 2\sin A\cos B = 0 \) ,
所以  \( \cos B = 0 \) , 又  \( \angle B \in (0, \pi) \) , 所以  \( \angle B = \frac{\pi}{2} \) . 故  \( \triangle ABC \)  为直角三角形.
