> **【解析】**
> \( \cos\frac{\pi}{8}=\frac{\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2}}}{2} \) ， \( \tan\frac{\pi}{8}=\sqrt{2}-1 \)
解析： \( \cos\frac{\pi}{8}=\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos\frac{\pi}{4}}{2}}=\sqrt{\frac{1+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{2}}=\frac{\sqrt{2+\sqrt{2}}}{2} \)
\( \tan\frac{\pi}{8}=\frac{1-\cos\frac{\pi}{4}}{\sin\frac{\pi}{4}}=\frac{1-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}=\sqrt{2}-1 \)
