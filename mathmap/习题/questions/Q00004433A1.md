> **【解析】**
> $-\frac{56}{65}$
解析: 因为 $0 \leq \alpha \leq \frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{\pi}{2} \leq \beta \leq \pi, \sin \alpha = \frac{12}{13}, \sin \beta = \frac{4}{5}$ ,
所以 $\cos \alpha = \sqrt{1 - \sin^2\alpha} = \frac{5}{13}$ ， $\cos \beta = -\sqrt{1 - \sin^2\beta} = -\frac{3}{5}$
所以 $\sin (\alpha -\beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta -\cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{12}{13}\times (-\frac{3}{5}) - \frac{5}{13}\times \frac{4}{5} = -\frac{56}{65}.$
故答案为: $-\frac{56}{65}$
