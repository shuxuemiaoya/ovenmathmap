> **【解析】**
> (1) $-\tan\alpha$ ; (2) 3.
解析：（1） $f(\alpha)=\frac{(-\sin\alpha)(-\cos\alpha)(-\sin\alpha)\cos\left[5\pi+\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\alpha\right)\right]}{(-\cos\alpha)\sin(\pi-\alpha)[-\sin(\pi+\alpha)]\sin\left[4\pi+\left(\frac{\pi}{2}+\alpha\right)\right]}$
$$
= \frac {- \sin^ {2} \alpha \cos \alpha \left[ - \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - \alpha\right) \right]}{(- \cos \alpha) \sin \alpha [ - (- \sin \alpha) ] \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + \alpha\right)} = - \frac {\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = - \tan \alpha .
$$
(2) 因为 $f(\alpha) = -2$ ，所以 $\tan \alpha = 2$ ，
$$
\therefore \frac {\sin \alpha + \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha - \cos \alpha} = \frac {\tan \alpha + 1}{\tan \alpha - 1} = \frac {3}{1} = 3
$$
