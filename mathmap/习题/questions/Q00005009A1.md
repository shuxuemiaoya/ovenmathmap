> **【解析】**
> 30； $\frac{23}{8}$
![](../../../../../images/answers/e0e66f2392f8daab9177d748cba9974823b475b5c35104b844fa38a48a7c7b8b.jpg)
根据题意, 以 $A$ 为坐标原点, 建立如图所示平面直角坐标系,
因为正方形的边长为 6 , 且 $\overrightarrow{DE} = 2\overrightarrow{EC}, M$ 是 $BC$ 中点,
则 $B(6,0), D(0,6), M(6,3), E(4,6)$ ,
则 $\overrightarrow{BD} = (-6,6), \overrightarrow{ME} = (-2,3)$ ,
所以 $\overrightarrow{ME} \cdot \overrightarrow{BD} = (-6) \times (-2) + 6 \times 3 = 30$ ;
设 $\overrightarrow{DP} = \lambda \overrightarrow{DB}$ , 其中 $\lambda \in [0,1]$ ,
则 $\overrightarrow{DP} = \lambda \overrightarrow{DB} = \lambda (6,-6) = (6\lambda,-6\lambda)$ , 则 $P(6\lambda,6-6\lambda)$ ,
所以 $\overrightarrow{PE} = (4-6\lambda,6\lambda), \overrightarrow{PM} = (6-6\lambda,6\lambda-3)$ ,
则 $\overrightarrow{PE} \cdot \overrightarrow{PM} = (4-6\lambda)(6-6\lambda) + 6\lambda(6\lambda-3) = 6(12\lambda^2 - 13\lambda + 4), \lambda \in [0,1]$ ,
其中 $6(12\lambda^2 - 13\lambda + 4) = 6\left[12\left(\lambda - \frac{13}{24}\right)^2 + \frac{23}{48}\right], \lambda \in [0,1]$ ,
当 $\lambda = \frac{13}{24}$ 时, 有最小值为 $\frac{23}{8}$ .
所以 $\overrightarrow{PE} \cdot \overrightarrow{PM}$ 的最小值是 $\frac{23}{8}$ . 故答案为: $30; \frac{23}{8}$
