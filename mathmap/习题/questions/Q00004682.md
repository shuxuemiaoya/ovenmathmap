【4】说明下列函数的图像变换方式.

(1) $y = \cos 3x \longrightarrow y = \sin 3x$ ;

(2) $y = \sin \left(x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right) \longrightarrow y = \cos x$ ;

(3) $y = 2\cos x \longrightarrow y = 2\sin \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{4}\right)$ ;

(4) $y = \sin \left(x - \frac{\pi}{3}\right) \longrightarrow y = \cos \left(2x - \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ ;

(5) $y = \sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \longrightarrow y = \cos \left(3x - \frac{\pi}{3}\right)$ ;

(6) $y = \cos \left(x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right) \longrightarrow y = 2\sin \left(2x + \frac{\pi}{4}\right).$
