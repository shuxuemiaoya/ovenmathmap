设 $\overrightarrow{AP} = \vec{a}, \overrightarrow{AQ} = \vec{b}$ ，则 $\overrightarrow{AC} = 2\vec{a} + \frac{4}{3}\vec{b}, \overrightarrow{PQ} = \vec{b} - \vec{a}$ .
设 $\overrightarrow{AM} = \lambda \overrightarrow{AC}, \overrightarrow{PM} = \mu \overrightarrow{PQ}$ 则 $\overrightarrow{AM}=2\lambda\vec{a}+\frac{4}{3}\lambda\vec{b}$ $\overrightarrow{PM}=\mu\vec{b}-\mu\vec{a}$ 因为 $\overrightarrow{AM}=\overrightarrow{AP}+\overrightarrow{PM}=\vec{a}+\mu\vec{b}-\mu\vec{a}=(1-\mu)\vec{a}+\mu\vec{b}$ 所以 $\left\{\begin{aligned}&2\lambda=1-\mu\\&\frac{4}{3}\lambda=\mu\end{aligned}\right.$ ，解得 $\lambda=\frac{3}{10},\mu=\frac{2}{5}$ 所以 $\overrightarrow{AM}=\frac{3}{10}\overrightarrow{AC}$ ，即 $\frac{AM}{AC}=\frac{\left|\overrightarrow{AM}\right|}{\left|\overrightarrow{AC}\right|}=\frac{3}{10}$ . 故选：C.
