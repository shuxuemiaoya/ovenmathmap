> **【解析】**
> $\left(\frac{3}{4}, 0\right)$ 【解析】根据离心率为 $\sqrt{17}$ , 可知 $\sqrt{17} = \frac{c}{a} \Rightarrow 1 + \frac{b^2}{a^2} = 17 \Rightarrow \frac{b^2}{a^2} = 16$ . 设 $A(x_1, y_1), B(x_2, y_2), x_1 \neq x_2$ , 则 $\frac{x_1^2}{a^2} - \frac{y_1^2}{b^2} = 1, \frac{x_2^2}{a^2} - \frac{y_2^2}{b^2} = 1$ , 两式相减得 $\frac{x_1^2 - x_2^2}{a^2} - \frac{y_1^2 - y_2^2}{b^2} = 0$ , 故 $\frac{b^2}{a^2} = \frac{y_1^2 - y_2^2}{x_1^2 - x_2^2} = \frac{y_1 - y_2}{x_1 - x_2} \cdot \frac{y_1 + y_2}{x_1 + x_2} \Rightarrow \frac{b^2}{a^2} = k_{AB} \cdot k_{OM}$ , 故 $16 = k_{AB} \cdot 2$ , 故 $k_{AB} = 8$ , 故直线 $AB$ 的方程为 $y = 8(x - 1) + 2$ , 令 $y = 0$ , 则 $x = \frac{3}{4}$ , 故直线 $AB$ 与 $x$ 轴的交点为 $\left(\frac{3}{4}, 0\right)$ , 故 $|NA| + |NB| \geqslant |AB|$ , 当且仅当 $A, N, B$ 三点共线时取等号, $|NF_1| + |NF_2| \geqslant |F_1F_2|$ , 当且仅当 $F_1, F_2, N$ 三点共线时取等号, 故 $|NA| + |NB| + |NF_1| + |NF_2| \geqslant |F_1F_2| + |AB|$ , $|F_1F_2|$ , $|AB|$ 为定值, 当且仅当 $N$ 是 $F_1F_2$ 和 $AB$ 的交点时取等号, 故 $N\left(\frac{3}{4}, 0\right)$ .
